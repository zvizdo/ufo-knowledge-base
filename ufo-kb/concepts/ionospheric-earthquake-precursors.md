# Ionospheric Earthquake Precursors

The observation that major earthquakes are preceded by detectable electromagnetic signatures in the Earth's ionosphere, specifically **spikes in total electron count (TEC)**, in the hours or days before a seismic event.

## Mechanism

- Tectonic plates under stress prior to rupture act "like a giant semiconductor" — the rock generates piezoelectric EM energy as stress builds
- This EM energy propagates upward through the atmosphere into the ionosphere
- The ionospheric TEC can be measured from space by satellites that monitor space weather
- Multiple peer-reviewed studies confirm this pattern

## Research Programs

- **ESA INSPIRE program**: The European Space Agency documented ionospheric earthquake precursors; the program name is cited by [[tim-gallaudet]] as a searchable reference
- **Precursor company**: A private company Gallaudet is advising/working with that uses satellite-based TEC monitoring as an earthquake early warning system
- **Japan and Northern California pilots**: Gallaudet is pursuing operational earthquake early warning pilots in these two locations using TEC monitoring

## Bureaucratic Obstacle

Gallaudet approached the US Geological Survey (USGS) about funding R&D on ionospheric earthquake precursors. USGS responded categorically: "Our policy is that earthquakes cannot be predicted." The USGS defines "prediction" narrowly (precise time, location, and magnitude) and refused to engage with a broader "early warning" / "probabilistic hazard" framing. This bureaucratic blockage is analogous to other institutional resistance patterns Gallaudet describes in the UAP domain — agencies defining problems narrowly to avoid engaging with anomalous data.

## UAP Connection

This concept appears in the same interview as Gallaudet's UAP research. The connection is indirect but thematic: in both areas (UAP ocean phenomena and earthquake precursors), Gallaudet argues that government agencies are systematically refusing to analyze data that is already in their possession. The USGS case is the non-UAP illustration of the same pattern.
