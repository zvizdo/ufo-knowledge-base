# Quantum Foam

John Wheeler's (1955) characterization of spacetime at the Planck scale (~10⁻³⁵ m, 10⁻⁴⁴ s): at this resolution, the smooth geometry of general relativity dissolves into a turbulent, probabilistic froth of virtual particle pairs, topology fluctuations, and micro-wormholes. Quantum foam is the most fundamental layer of physical reality — the substrate from which the smooth spacetime of everyday experience emerges.

In the UAP context, quantum foam is invoked as the substrate in which two distinct phenomena are proposed to operate:

1. **Eric Davis / Vallée**: Space and time are "arbitrary" emergent epiphenomena of something deeper — quantum foam. NHI operating at or near this level would not be constrained by the space and time coordinates humans experience. See [[spacetime-as-epiphenomenon]].

2. **Dave Rossi**: The quantum vacuum (which, at the Planck scale, is quantum foam) permanently retains the potential fields of every past state. This is the physical substrate for the vacuum memory model — the "saved game" layer exists in the quantum foam. See [[vacuum-memory-time-model]].
