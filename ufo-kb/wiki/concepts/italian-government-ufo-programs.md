# Italian Government UFO Programs

Italy has the earliest alleged government UAP recovery documented in the KB: the [[1933-magenta-crash]], which prompted [[benito-mussolini]] to create [[gabinetto-rs33]] — claimed to be the world's first formally constituted government UAP investigation body. Italian government engagement with UAP continued, by some accounts, through the 1950s via the successor [[rs-33]] committee.

**Gabinetto RS/33 (1933)**: Mussolini's special investigation body was convened following the recovery of a disc-shaped craft near Magenta, Lombardy, in June 1933. RS/33 investigated the craft, concluded it was non-human in origin (contradicting Mussolini's public position that it was a foreign aircraft), and managed its storage at [[sia-marchetti]] aerospace facilities near Vergiate. The Stefani news agency imposed a complete media blackout on Mussolini's personal orders.

**Project McGregor (1945)**: The OSS under [[allen-dulles]] conducted [[project-mcgregor]] to retrieve the stored Magenta craft from Italian custody before German forces could extract it — making the Italian UAP program the target of the final major OSS operation of WWII. If the chain of custody account is accurate, the Italian government's 12-year stewardship of recovered non-human technology ended with a covert American retrieval.

**RS-33 (1950s)**: According to Marconi family testimony, a successor committee — also called RS-33 — was reconstituted in the 1950s around the [[1953-varese-case]], with [[guglielmo-marconi]] as president and [[enrico-fermi]] as vice-president.

**Significance**: Italy's UAP program history, if even partially accurate, predates the US Roswell-centered narrative by 14 years and represents the earliest documented instance of a government recovering and attempting to study non-human technology.
