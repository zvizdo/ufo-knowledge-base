# USO Electromagnetic Interference Pattern

A statistical finding by [[richard-dolan]] in his ~670-case USO database (see [[usos-volume-1]]): the prevalence of electromagnetic interference (EM effects) in USO encounters is not random but scales systematically with the intimacy of the encounter.

## The Three-Tier Pattern

| Encounter type | EM interference prevalence |
|---|---|
| All USO cases | ~10% |
| Military USO encounters | ~20% (2× baseline) |
| USO cases with missing time, entity sighting, or being-contact | ~40% (4× baseline) |

## Dolan's Interpretation

> "The thing that these EM encounters — they're not accidental. They're kind of a tool, maybe a weapon, like they're not it's not a random thing."

If EM effects were simply a byproduct of UAP propulsion or field generation, one would expect a relatively constant incidence across all encounter types. Instead, the doubling in military cases and quadrupling in high-intimacy cases suggests:

1. **EM as a weapon**: the phenomenon can selectively deploy electromagnetic effects against military platforms (disabling weapons systems, communications) when militarily engaged
2. **EM as a side effect of deeper engagement**: entity contact and abduction cases involve the same high-EM signature, suggesting close proximity to the craft's operational envelope triggers EM
3. **Both**: the effects may be both an inadvertent byproduct of operating envelope and a selectively applied countermeasure

## Paradigm Cases

- **[[baltic-sea-anomaly]] (2011–2025)**: Reproducible GPS loss, ROV/drone shutdown, and compass deviation on every Ocean X expedition including Stockholm University's own research vessel — the captain confirmed GPS failure on university-grade equipment while directly over the object. Temperature anomaly (near 0°C vs. expected 4–5°C) and phantom radar returns also recorded. The EM interference scales with proximity. This is the most extensively documented civilian reproducible EM interference case in the KB. See [[dennis-asberg]] and [[aGqFOxuYRT0]].
- **[[1971-uss-jfk-orb]]**: complete communications blackout + weapons offline on a US carrier; orb-type USO
- **[[1973-pascagoula-abduction]]**: entity contact case with adjacent USO incident in the same area within weeks (the adjacent USO likely involved EM, per Dolan's volume 2 discussion)
- **[[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown]]**: UAP monitoring nuclear test then shot down by X-ray pulse — inverse EM interaction (human EM weapon affects UAP, not the reverse)

## Connection to Orb/Sphere Shape

[[richard-dolan]] notes that a large proportion of USO encounters involve orb- or sphere-shaped objects, and that these cases cluster in the high-EM tier. This suggests either that orb-type UAPs specifically deploy EM as a mechanism, or that the sphere shape is associated with closer-range engagement that triggers EM effects.
