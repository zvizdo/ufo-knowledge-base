# Exodus Force

The "Exodus force" is the working label [[charles-buhler]] and [[andrew-arjima]] use for the anomalous directional thrust produced by their asymmetric electrostatic configurations. Buhler is careful to distinguish it from anti-gravity (which implies an opposing gravitational coupling) and from electrogravitics (which he considers "pretentious" given the absence of confirmed gravity coupling). The name reflects the team's framing of the force as humanity's escape from chemical propulsion.

Technically, Buhler describes it as comprising two components:
1. **Electrostatic pressure force** — a surface effect from the pressure of the electric field on the geometry of the device
2. **Divergence in the E-field force** — a volume effect from gradients in the electric field across asymmetric dielectric structures

## How It Differs from the Biefield-Brown Effect Label

The Exodus force is the same underlying phenomenon as the [[biefeld-brown-effect]] — an asymmetric capacitor under high voltage producing measurable thrust. The distinction is framing: "Biefeld-Brown effect" carries a century of contested history and the unresolved question of whether it is electrogravitic (coupling to gravity) or purely electrostatic. "Exodus force" is Buhler's neutral label pending theoretical resolution.

From the evidence gathered, Buhler believes the force is **purely electrostatic in manifestation** (no gravity coupling confirmed), **quantum mechanical in origin** (not explainable by classical Maxwell electrostatics), and **distinct from ionic wind** (survives vacuum and Faraday-cage enclosure at all tested levels).

## Buhler's QED Theory

Starting from Coulomb's law derived via second-order QED perturbation theory (the standard result), Buhler applies **third-order time-independent perturbation theory** to the two-charge system and finds:

- 12 Feynman diagram terms (vs. 4 in second order)
- Several terms involve a charge emitting or absorbing a **scalar photon** without a corresponding partner absorption/emission at the other charge
- This **asymmetric scalar-photon exchange** implies a net momentum imbalance — the theoretical seed of the force
- An asymmetry between the two charges' interaction naturally arises when the geometry is asymmetric

[[david-chester]]'s critique: scalar virtual photons are internal diagram lines, not physical radiation; QED normally conserves momentum; the "unmatched" scalar photons need a physical destination to satisfy conservation. Chester's constructive suggestion: explore Dirac spinor equations of motion at the classical level (which contain ℏ and α) and look at whether renormalization corrections affect the self-energy in a directional way.

The fine structure constant **α ≈ 1/137** appearing in Buhler's experimental force scaling is cited as a key indicator of quantum mechanical origin — one of the few garage-accessible experiments where α shows up.

## Casimir Effect Analogy

Buhler repeatedly draws the comparison to the **Casimir effect** — two uncharged conducting plates in proximity attract due to vacuum fluctuation pressure, requiring no power input. Similarly, the Exodus force appears to persist after the charging event as long as charge remains trapped in the dielectric. The force is a property of the field configuration, not of ongoing current flow. This analogy is why Buhler considers a classical explanation insufficient: the Casimir effect also requires quantum-vacuum physics beyond classical electromagnetism.

## Relationship to Other Propulsion Concepts

- **Ionic wind**: Related superficially; experimentally distinguishable by direction (devices move with ionic wind), vacuum behavior (ionic wind vanishes in vacuum; Exodus force grows), and scale weight test (ionic wind shows scale weight change from reaction; Exodus force in sealed enclosure shows no such change).
- **EmDrive / Q-thruster**: [[harold-white]]'s Eagle Works program also claimed anomalous thrust in closed cavities. Different physical mechanism claimed (microwave resonance vs. electrostatic DC), but both confront the same "momentum from nowhere" challenge.
- **Casimir-chip thruster**: [[harold-white]]'s more recent work (1.5 kV on a Casimir-effect chip) is cited by [[jesse-michaels]] as convergent with Buhler's results.
- **[[biefeld-brown-effect]]**: Historical ancestor; the Exodus force is the contemporary experimental realization.

## Open Questions

- What carries the "missing" momentum when the force is produced? (Chester's primary challenge to Buhler's QED theory)
- Is there any gravity coupling, or is this purely electrostatic? (Buhler is agnostic but skeptical of a gravity coupling)
- Can the force be scaled to Earth-gravity unity? What materials and geometries are optimal?
- Does the fine structure constant α appearing in scaling reflect a loop-level QED correction, or a classical electrostatic ratio effect?
