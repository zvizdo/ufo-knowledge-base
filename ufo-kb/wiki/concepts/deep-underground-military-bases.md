# Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs)

Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs) is the generic term for alleged classified subterranean installations that may house UAP-related research, reverse engineering programs, or other black-budget activities beyond normal congressional oversight.

## Evidence Base

### Richard Sauder's Research

[[richard-sauder]] is the primary civilian researcher who has documented underground base construction using public records — utility contracts, boring machine procurements, topographic anomalies, and declassified facility planning documents. Sauder published books mapping known and suspected underground installations in the United States. His methodology grounds DUMB claims in physical infrastructure evidence rather than witness testimony alone.

[[richard-dolan]] references Sauder's work as one of the more grounded approaches to the question of where classified programs physically operate.

### Katherine Austin Fitts and the Funding Connection

[[catherine-austin-fitts]], former Assistant Secretary of HUD, connects DUMBs to the financial anomalies she discovered while investigating Pentagon accounting:

- [[donald-rumsfeld]] announced $2.3 trillion in unaccounted Pentagon expenditures on September 10, 2001 — buried by the next day's events
- Fitts argues the unaccountability of this money is not accidental: the financial system is deliberately designed to allow funds to vanish into programs — including underground facilities — without triggering congressional oversight
- Her "Myth of the Rule of Law" paper examines how legal structures ostensibly protecting public money actually enable this flow

Dolan synthesizes Fitts's financial forensics with his [[breakaway-civilization]] framework: the underground bases may be physical nodes of a separate civilization that has diverged from the surface world in capability.

### Naval Surface Warfare Center (Crane, Indiana)

[[jesse-michaels]] (the host in [[GQlWf54K_7Y]]) briefly mentions the Naval Surface Warfare Center at Crane, Indiana, as having unusual underground facilities — Dolan expresses interest in discussing it further off-air. This is a single-source, uncorroborated claim but is logged for follow-up.

## Theoretical Significance

The DUMB concept is the physical infrastructure layer of the [[breakaway-civilization]] thesis. If classified groups have achieved dramatically divergent technological capability, they require physical spaces where that work can be conducted. Underground installations provide:
- Physical separation from surface-world observation
- Defensible perimeters
- Infrastructure for advanced manufacturing and research
- Plausible deniability (can be constructed under routine facility expansion contracts)

## Dulce as the Paradigm DUMB Myth

The [[dulce-underground-base]] claim — that a joint human-alien base operates beneath Archeleta Mesa near [[dulce-new-mexico]] — is the paradigm case for the DUMB mythology in UFO discourse. Its origin is substantially traceable to:
1. [[myrna-hansen]]'s hypnotic regression (early 1980s), enmeshed with the AFOSI disinformation operation against [[paul-benowitz]]
2. Active AFOSI amplification via [[rick-doty]], who redirected Benowitz's attention toward Dulce using staged props
3. [[john-lear]]'s propagation of the "Dulce firefight" — which he privately admitted was fabricated: "I made that up"

Greg Bishop's investigation (*[[project-beta]]*) documents how the Dulce myth was substantially manufactured by a documented intelligence operation. This makes Dulce both the best-known and most epistemically suspect DUMB claim. (Greg Bishop, [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])
