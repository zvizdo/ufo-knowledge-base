# Independent Aquifer

Brackish water in the [[osiris-shaft|Osiris Shaft]] at Giza that does not match the chemistry of Nile groundwater; evidence of a separate ancient water system beneath the plateau and key to the waterproofed-shafts argument.

The Osiris Shaft contains brackish water whose chemistry does not match Nile-derived groundwater, indicating a separate water source. Combined with Biondi's observation that the deep tubular shafts beneath Giza are themselves waterproof (water level outside the shafts cannot infiltrate inside), the independent-aquifer evidence supports a reading of the substructure as engineered with deliberate hydraulic isolation — relevant to both the chemical-manufacturing and underground-city hypotheses.
