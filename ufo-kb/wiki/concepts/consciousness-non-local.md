# Consciousness as Non-Local

The thesis that individual human consciousness is not produced by individual brains but is instead a shared or field-like phenomenon — an underlying medium in which individual perspectives participate. On this model, apparent anomalies (telepathy, remote viewing, NHI communication, post-death survival) are not exotic exceptions but predictable features of a substrate that is fundamentally non-local.

## Core Claim

Conventional neuroscience assumes consciousness is an emergent product of brain activity: no brain, no consciousness. The non-local thesis inverts or transcends this: the brain is a filter or transducer of consciousness rather than its source. Individual minds are local expressions of a wider field, and under certain conditions (autism, meditation, psychedelics, NHI contact, death approach), the filtering is reduced and the wider field becomes accessible.

## Evidence Streams Referenced in This KB

### Autistic Savant Telepathy
[[autistic-savant-telepathy]] — [[diane-powell]]'s controlled experiments document non-verbal autistic children accessing specific information held in others' minds. The most parsimonious non-local explanation: autistic neural architecture reduces the default filter on the shared consciousness field.

### Remote Viewing — Stargate Program
[[stargate-program]], run by [[hal-puthoff]] and Russell Targ at SRI, documented controlled remote viewing under CIA/DIA funding. Subjects perceived locations and events beyond normal sensory range at statistically significant rates. Institutionally the strongest evidence that non-local access is real and reproducible.

### NHI Telepathic Communication
[[nhi-telepathic-communication]] — [[harold-malmgren]] experienced apparent information transmission while handling UAP debris at Los Alamos. [[thomas-townsend-brown]] and [[nikola-tesla]] reported similar non-acoustic information reception. If NHI can transmit into human consciousness, the channel must already exist as part of the underlying structure.

### Past-Lives Research
[[ian-stevenson]]'s cases: children accessing verifiable information about lives they did not live. If accurate, requires memory storage and retrieval that is not brain-local.

### Ancient Mystery Traditions
[[brian-muraresku]]'s *[[the-immortality-key]]*: psychedelic sacraments in the Eleusinian Mysteries and early Christianity were designed to produce death-like experiences demonstrating consciousness continuity. The ancient "technology" for accessing the non-local field is arguably the earliest documented evidence stream.

### Turing's Parapsychology Passage
[[martin-shkreli]] (in [[2Mb6NPPjCyg]]) cites Alan Turing's "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" (1950): among Turing's candidate refutations of the claim that machines can think, the one he took most seriously was parapsychology — specifically that ESP-capable humans might outperform machines on random event generators, with an additional experimenter effect making controlled testing impossible. Shkreli endorses the underlying claim: "I do think there is [a mind-over-matter effect]." Turing's willingness to treat ESP as the strongest theoretical objection to machine consciousness is a remarkable early endorsement of non-local mind by one of the founders of computational theory.

## UAP Connection

Non-local consciousness is a load-bearing concept for understanding UAP phenomena: if NHI communicate telepathically, navigate via consciousness, or are themselves non-physical entities rather than corporeal beings in craft, then understanding UAP requires a model of consciousness that is not brain-bound. This is the link between the "hard" UAP cases (recovered materials, radar tracks) and the "soft" cases (contactee experiences, channeled messages, the gateway process).

### TV Receiver Analogy — Paul Smith and Herb Metz (eQIMantuasQ)

[[paul-smith]] and [[herb-metz]] independently arrive at the same **transmission/receiver model** in [[eQIMantuasQ]]:

> "That TV is not generating the program you're watching — that's coming over the airwaves. Pull out one of those plugs and the picture gets distorted. Pull out a different plug and it gets distorted in a different way. That's exactly what you might be doing with the brain when you're shutting pieces down and seeing it affect consciousness. That is evidence to show that physicalism isn't necessarily right." — Paul Smith

This analogy maps directly onto the William James "transmission" thesis (see [[transmission-theory-consciousness]]) but is drawn from direct operational experience (Stargate remote viewing) and laboratory data (PEAR REGs) rather than philosophical reasoning. Smith adds the **binding problem** as supporting evidence: no existing neuroscience model explains how disparate sensory pathways (optic, auditory, motor coordination) are unified into the single continuous "movie" of experience. His point: if we don't understand how the brain binds the ordinary stream of consciousness, assuming it *generates* consciousness is unjustified.

### Dean Radin's Mind-Matter Interaction Evidence
[[dean-radin]] has published approximately 100 peer-reviewed papers demonstrating statistically significant effects of human consciousness on the output of Random Event Generators. [[bob-maguire]] endorses this body of work as conclusive. Critically, in all the REG experiments, **time and distance are not factors** — exactly the spatial and temporal non-locality required for quantum-consciousness models. This is the most robust empirical evidence stream for non-local mind-matter interaction in the academic literature.

### Penrose-Hameroff Quantum Consciousness
[[bob-maguire]] endorses a version of the Penrose-Hameroff model: if the brain maintains quantum coherence (in microtubules or similar structures) and then the coherent state decoheres, the individual is "rendering reality in real time." Crucially: if decoherence is ongoing inside the mind and you're responsible for the rendering, then your ability to affect matter isn't "shooting particles out at the material world" — you're affecting the rendering of the material world from inside the rendering process. **Distance and time become non-factors.** This is the same non-locality that shows up in the REG experiments.

### Bob Maguire's Consciousness-Physics Bridge
[[bob-maguire]] has found a mathematical mapping from [[donald-hoffman]]'s consciousness model (implemented in **decorated permutations** connected to the amplituhedron) to [[stephen-wolfram]]'s **hypergraph physics** (from which GR and QFT can be derived computationally). If this mapping holds, it is the beginning of a formal unification of consciousness theory and physics — grounding non-local consciousness in mathematical structures that also produce spacetime and quantum field theory.

## Open Questions

- What is the mechanism by which autism reduces the filter (if it does) — structural, functional, developmental?
- Does the non-local field have structure, directionality, or memory — or is it isotropic?
