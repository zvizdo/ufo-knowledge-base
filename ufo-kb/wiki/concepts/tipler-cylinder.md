# Tipler Cylinder

An exact GR solution due originally to W. J. van Stockum (1937) and rediscovered/popularized by **Frank Tipler** (1974). For an infinitely long, sufficiently dense rotating cylinder of matter, the spacetime around the cylinder admits **closed timelike curves** — paths along which a traveler returns to their own past.

The Tipler cylinder is referenced in UAP discourse as one of the small handful of **mathematically rigorous** time-travel mechanisms inside general relativity. Its appearance in the literature underwrites the [[time-travel-uap]] / [[future-humans-hypothesis]] argument: closed timelike curves are not science fiction but mathematically permitted GR objects whose engineering remains the question.

The standard objections — infinite length, weak-energy-condition violations for finite-length variants — are cited in [[mick-west]]-tier rebuttals.
