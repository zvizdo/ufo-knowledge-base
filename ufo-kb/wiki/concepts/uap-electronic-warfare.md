# UAP Electronic Warfare

Documented pattern of UAP incidents where the unknown contact is associated with disruption of military electronic systems, including active noise jamming, radar interference, IFF suppression, and weapons/sensor failures.

## Documented Cases

- **D58 (Oct 2020, DCA mission)**: Chevron-shaped contact; noise jamming confirmed by two observers; radar lock obtained but contact unidentifiable; KINGPIN controller directed ID but could not classify; jamming received during attempted intercept.
- **Pantex nuclear facility (2020s)**: Jellyfish UAP observed during weapons work; weapons system reportedly "jammed" (see [[dow-uap-d23-mission-report-arabian-gulf-2023-10-31]] for Arabian Gulf parallel).
- **USAF Blue Book era**: Multiple pilot reports of aircraft system anomalies during close UAP passes.

## Phenomenological Significance

Active noise jamming attributed to a UAP contact that was simultaneously tracked on radar and confirmed visually (as in D58) implies a platform with deliberate EA capability rather than passive observational behavior. This places such incidents in a more adversarial or militarily capable category than ambient-presence sightings.
