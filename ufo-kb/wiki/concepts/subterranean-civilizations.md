# Subterranean Civilizations

The hypothesis that significant populations — including potentially non-human intelligent beings — have historically inhabited and may currently inhabit large subterranean tunnel and cave systems worldwide. Proposed as a framework for explaining the Nazca mummies, ancient mysteries, and certain UFO hotspots.

## Evidence / Instances

**Nazca, Peru (primary):** [[michael-mazzola]] and multiple anonymous sources believe the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] were found in a large subterranean tunnel system — not the small cave Leandro shows tourists. Local sources near the tunnel systems report strange noises at night, beings that don't walk like humans, and other anomalous observations — suggesting a potentially live situation.

**Derinkuyu / Turkey:** [[graham-hancock]] documents massive ant-farm-scale subterranean cities in modern Turkey (Derinkuyu and similar sites) — hundreds of rooms, dug deep beneath the earth. Interpreted by some as constructed to hide from a cataclysm. Jesse Michaels: if a breakaway civilization lived there hundreds of thousands of years ago and was "different from us," occasional surface visits might explain persistent contact lore.

**Taiос Caves, Ecuador:** Cave system at the edge of the Ecuadorian Amazon associated with legends of a metallic library made of gold. Neil Armstrong took a BBC film crew to search for it in 1976. Why would the first man on the moon seek alien artifacts on Earth?

**Dulce, New Mexico / S4 / Area 51:** Deep Underground Military Bases (DUMBs) documented by researchers like Richard Sauder; some rumors involve non-human beings (Phil Schneider, Bob Lazar, Dan Burish). See [[deep-underground-military-bases]].

## Connections to Nazca Tridactyls

Chilean researcher David Velasco proposes the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] represent a global subterranean species. The Nazca region is over 1,000 square miles with only 80,000 people — ideal for a secluded subterranean species to remain undiscovered. Local reports of anomalous beings near tunnels at night add to this hypothesis.

The Hopi "ant people" legend maps directly: wise underground beings with three fingers who helped humans survive catastrophes.

## Lazarus Species Angle

Jesse raises the concept of Lazarus species — species presumed extinct that are rediscovered. Some biologists believe Homo floresiensis may still survive in Indonesia (Orang Pendek). If a population of tridactyl hominids survived in isolated underground tunnel systems for thousands of years, their bodies preserved in diatomaceous earth would be from both ancient and more recent individuals — consistent with the 700–18,800-year carbon date range spanning over 1,000 years.
