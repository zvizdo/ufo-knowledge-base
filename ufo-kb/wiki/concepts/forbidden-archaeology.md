# Forbidden Archaeology

**Forbidden archaeology** is a term coined by researchers Michael Cremo and Richard Thompson in their 1993 book *Forbidden Archeology: The Hidden History of the Human Race*. The field catalogs physical anomalies — artifacts found in geological strata inconsistent with their supposed technology level, skeletal remains suggesting anatomically modern humans existed far earlier than consensus dates, and structures with precision engineering attributed to primitive cultures.

## Key Claims

- Mainstream archaeology systematically excludes anomalous evidence that challenges its consensus timeline
- Physical artifacts (Ooparts — Out-Of-Place Artifacts) exist whose provenance, if genuine, requires revising human chronological models
- The suppression is partly institutional (academic gatekeeping) and partly epistemological (evidence that doesn't fit the paradigm is re-classified or ignored)
