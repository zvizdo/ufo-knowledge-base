# Grey Aliens

The dominant entity type in modern abduction and contact literature. Characterized as short (3.5–5 feet), grey or whitish-grey skin, oversized domed head, large dark almond-shaped eyes without visible pupils, small nose and mouth, thin elongated limbs, and no visible external ear structures.

## Origin of the Archetype

The "Grey" as a cultural archetype crystallized through the Betty and Barney Hill abduction account (1961) and subsequent publicity. Earlier accounts describe similar entities but without the Grey label. By the 1980s–1990s, the Grey dominated UFO iconography globally — a cultural saturation that researchers like Jacques Vallée and Diana Pasulka have noted as itself potentially anomalous or engineered.

## Sub-types

- **Small Greys**: the most commonly reported; potentially robotic or drone-like per some witnesses
- **Tall Greys**: reported as taller, more authoritative figures; Charles Hall's description of "small grays" at Indian Springs
