# Higher-Dimensional Reality Access

Hypothesis that UAP, NHI entities, and certain consciousness phenomena involve access to — or intrusion from — dimensions of spacetime beyond the four standard dimensions. In this framework, NHI exist in a higher-dimensional manifold and can choose to manifest in (or intrude into) 3+1 dimensional spacetime at will, explaining apparent violations of classical physics (instant appearance/disappearance, wall penetration, shape-shifting).

## Key Proponents

- [[jacques-vallee]] — interdimensional hypothesis as his primary UAP model
- [[andrew-gallimore]] — the DMT state as a window into higher-dimensional reality
- Various [[alien-information-theory]] framings — information from higher dimensions encoded in contact experiences

## Variants

- **String-theory adjacent**: Extra compactified dimensions in M-theory; NHI in the bulk
- **Consciousness-based**: Higher dimensions are accessed via consciousness alteration (DMT, meditation, near-death), not physics
- **Control system**: Vallée's framing — NHI use higher-dimensional access to *manage* human reality (not merely observe it)
