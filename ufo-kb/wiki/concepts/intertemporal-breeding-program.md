# Intertemporal Breeding Program

A specific variant of the [[hybrid-program]] claim: reproductive procedures reported in UAP abduction accounts are not hybridization experiments by alien entities but a **genetic banking operation** conducted by [[future-humans-hypothesis|future Homo sapiens]] who have returned to the ancestral past to collect viable gametes before human reproductive capacity collapses irreversibly.

## The Problem in the Future (Motive)

[[michael-p-masters]] anchors the motive in current measurable trends:

- **60% reduction in sperm count** in developed-world males over the past 40 years
- **50% reduction globally** in the same period
- Below 40 million sperm per milliliter, fertility problems begin; the global average is approaching that threshold
- Pesticide exposure (documented correlation in four US cities) and other environmental factors drive the decline
- Broader domestication of the human species (reduction in testosterone, pro-social selection) compounds the trajectory

If this trend continues 8,000 years into the future, reproductive viability may have collapsed. Future humans must return to the past — when gametes were still abundant and genetically diverse — to preserve their lineage.

## The Mechanism

[[jim-penniston]]'s recalled contact message at [[1980-rendlesham-forest]]:
> "We are you. We come from 8,000 years in the future. We are having problems with reproduction. We are taking these gametes."

They explicitly framed the collection as self-preservation: "we want to maintain genetic diversity and not get wiped off the planet."

[[michael-p-masters]] adds (from PEER abductee accounts):
- Male abductees almost always had **sperm extracted**
- Female abductees had **developing fetuses implanted, then removed** (fetal monitoring and harvesting)
- **Egg extraction** is commonly reported
- The program appears to have been most active in the **1970s–90s**; reports have declined since

## Key Cases Cited

- **[[jim-penniston]] / [[1980-rendlesham-forest]]**: direct verbal statement of motive by future humans
- **[[antonio-villas-boas]] (1957)**: sexual intercourse with a female NHI — earliest documented sexual contact; entity appeared to deliberately collect viable material
- **[[barney-hill]]**: reluctantly disclosed semen extraction during his abduction experience
- **[[amy-rylance]]**: described spending 5–7 days on a craft with a physiologically fully human male; consistent with assisted reproduction rather than alien research
- **Multiple CE4 cases**: Roger Leir, Leo Sprinkle, Budd Hopkins, and David Jacobs all documented reproductive procedures as the most common abduction theme

## Why Self-Preservation, Not Hybridization

Masters distinguishes the intertemporal model from the classic hybrid program:

1. **Classic hybrid program**: NHI aliens seek to create a human-alien hybrid species
2. **Intertemporal model**: Future humans collect ancestral gametes to maintain a viable human lineage going forward — *self-preservation, not creation of a new species*

The chemical decontamination procedures (antiseptic rinses many abductees report before being taken aboard) make sense in this framework: future humans may have eliminated ancient pathogens and must protect themselves from ancestral disease exposure — the decontamination is **for them, not for the abductee**.

## Decline in Modern Reports

Masters notes a striking pattern: reproductive procedure reports are concentrated in 1970s–90s accounts and are relatively rare in modern abduction literature. Possible explanations:
- The program reached sufficient genetic diversity collection and concluded or scaled back
- Different future factions with different protocols may now be active
- Changed public awareness may affect reporting

## Open Questions

- Do the antiseptic rinse protocols reported by contactees correlate with specific pathogen histories?
- Is the decline in modern reports evidence of program completion or change in operational methodology?
