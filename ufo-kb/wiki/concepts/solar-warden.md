# Solar Warden

The informal name for an alleged classified US military space program, popularized by Gary McKinnon's 2001–2002 claims that he accessed US military computer networks and found evidence of a fleet of space-faring vessels. McKinnon described finding spreadsheets listing "non-terrestrial officers" and ship-to-ship transfer logs that did not reference any ship in the public military registry.

The term "Solar Warden" did not appear in McKinnon's original testimony — he described seeing the evidence but the program name was added later by researchers interpreting his account.

## Open Questions

- Is Solar Warden the same as any of the black programs described by other whistleblowers (TR-3B, ARV programs, or the programs referenced by Thomas Castello)?
