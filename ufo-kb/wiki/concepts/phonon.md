# Phonon

Quantized elastic micro-vibration wave in a solid material; the physical basis of Filippo Biondi's SAR Doppler tomography technique for detecting subterranean structure beneath the Giza plateau.

A phonon is a quantized vibrational mode in a crystalline lattice or solid medium. In Biondi's [[satellite-sar]] application, satellite radar pulses induce surface micro-vibrations whose elastic response carries information about subsurface voids and density anomalies. Each internal void or boundary has a characteristic phonon response, allowing reconstruction of internal geometry from sequences of micro-vibration snapshots — a fundamentally different mechanism than [[muon-tomography]] attenuation imaging.
