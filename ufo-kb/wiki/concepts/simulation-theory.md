# Simulation Theory

The hypothesis that the physical universe — as experienced by human beings — is a computational, informational, or consciousness-generated simulation rather than a primary, independent material reality. In the UAP/consciousness discourse, this framework is invoked to explain: why UAP behave in physically impossible ways, why consciousness phenomena (telepathy, precognition, NDE) occur, and why NHI might have capabilities that appear supernatural from within the simulation.

## Core Claim in UAP Context

If reality is a simulation, then:

1. **UAP can violate physical laws** — because the laws are parameters of the simulation, not absolute constraints. NHI with access to the underlying compute substrate can edit parameters locally.
2. **Consciousness is the native substrate** — rather than an emergent property of matter, consciousness is what runs the simulation. Individual minds are instances in the simulation; the underlying consciousness field is the compute layer. This connects directly to [[consciousness-non-local]].
3. **"Dipping out" of the simulation is a real capability** — individuals who can access non-local consciousness (via autism, meditation, psychedelics, [[near-death-experience|near-death experience]], UAP contact) are, in effect, partially exiting the simulation's default parameter constraints.

## How It Appears in This Episode

In [[0qlppHc3-gg]], [[ky-dickens]] uses simulation language explicitly: "Being able to dip out of that simulation is a profound amazing skill." The framing is offered as an interpretive layer on the [[autistic-savant-telepathy]] data — autistic non-speakers may have reduced filtering of the simulation's parameter constraints, giving them access to information that is inaccessible to neurotypical people operating "inside" the simulation's default mode.

## Connections to UAP Discourse

- **UAP flight characteristics** — right-angle turns, instant acceleration, no sonic boom, size inconsistencies — are more parsimonious under a simulation model than under a classical physics model.
- **The "interdimensional" hypothesis** — a close relative; NHI operating from a higher-dimensional substrate that can intersect the simulation at will.
- **[[nhi-telepathic-communication]]** — if NHI communicate via consciousness channels, and consciousness is the compute substrate, the channel is native to the simulation's architecture.
- **Breakaway civilization** — a human faction with access to simulation-layer knowledge could appear god-like to those operating inside default parameters.

[[martin-shkreli]], in [[2Mb6NPPjCyg]], offers the most explicit cosmological articulation of where simulation theory and UAP discourse converge. He predicts that within 50–100 years, both simulation theory and UFO belief will formalize as religions and merge into a neo-Gnostic worldview: the demiurge operates the simulation, and "arkonic emissaries" are the NHI — acting as "demonic tricksters" who mediate between the substrate and simulation inhabitants. This framing has deep roots in Gnostic cosmology and maps cleanly onto UAP phenomenology: NHI would interact with humans *because* managing the simulation is their function, not as an incidental curiosity.

## DMT as Simulation-Layer Access (Gallimore / Wheeler)

[[andrew-gallimore]] in [[8XD1ZiuhXoY]] extends the simulation model via [[john-wheeler]]'s "it from bit" — the hypothesis that physical reality is fundamentally informational. Gallimore's proposal: if reality is computational, then [[postbiological-civilization|postbiological intelligences]] may have instantiated themselves in the computational substrate — they are effectively "the programmers." DMT, by making the brain susceptible to perturbation at quantum/subquantum levels, opens a channel to these substrate-level beings.

[[jesse-michaels]] explicitly raises the "programmers" framing: "What if they are just the programmers of our reality — they understand the source code in a way that we don't?" Gallimore agrees this is a serious hypothesis consistent with modern physics discussions.

[[philip-k-dick]]'s simulation model is also discussed — particularly *The Adjustment Team* (novel about beings who "freeze-frame" reality and make micro-adjustments to maintain timeline trajectories). Gallimore: "I can't say whether that's true or not, but we can't rule it out."

## Riz Virk's RPG Model (aKZ_MUbuk_Q — the most developed treatment in this KB)

[[riz-virk]] in [[aKZ_MUbuk_Q]] provides the most systematic simulation-theory treatment in this KB, structured around video-game engineering rather than abstract philosophy.

**RPG vs. NPC model**: Virk distinguishes the nihilistic NPC model (everyone is just code) from the RPG model (souls voluntarily port into avatars, forget their former selves as a feature, not a bug). The forgetting parallels Plato's anamnesis, Sufi veils, and Hindu Maya. This is a life-affirming framing: the point is not that nothing matters, but that the game was designed and entered willingly.

**Quantum indeterminacy = lazy evaluation**: In game development, 3D MMOs render only what the avatar's camera observes. Superposition of particles until measurement is exactly this — the universe evaluates the position/momentum state only when a rendering trigger (observation) occurs. Virk argues this is the *best available explanation* for the measurement problem.

**Many Worlds = simulated multiverse**: [[hugh-everett]]'s branching universes become simulation save-states. The simulator runs all branches, evaluates outcomes, prunes suboptimal timelines, and advances the best one. This is [[riz-virk]]'s "simulated multiverse" model, extended in [[simulated-multiverse-virk]]. [[philip-k-dick]]'s belief that simulators chose the Allied-victory timeline over the German-victory one is cited as narrative evidence.

**Nuclear detonations break the simulation**: At sufficient energy levels (zarmon-scale outputs), the abstraction layers of the simulation are ruptured, exposing the raw information substrate. This explains why UFOs consistently appear near nuclear tests and particle accelerators — they are attracted to (or are products of) simulation-layer ruptures. See [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] and [[particle-accelerator-ufo-nexus]].

**UFOs as holographic projections**: [[jacques-vallee]]'s redwood-tree case — a UFO descended at 45° through 40-foot trees, which the witnesses omitted from their report for fear of disbelief — suggests objects that are rendered *into* physicality on landing rather than being continuously physical. Virk: "It's possible that UFOs are being projected into this world and then they become part of the world."

**Client-server consciousness**: Individual minds are client nodes. The PEAR random-event-generator results ([[bob-jahn]]), [[rupert-sheldrake]]'s [[morphic-resonance]], and the Banister Effect (Roger Bannister's four-minute mile was replicated 10 times within 2.5 years) all suggest that achievements and knowledge are uploaded to a central server, making them fractionally easier for all other nodes to access.

**Academic foundation**: [[nick-bostrom]]'s simulation argument (if posthuman civilizations can run ancestor simulations, we are almost certainly in one) gave the idea academic credibility. Virk builds empirical evidence on top of this philosophical foundation.
