# CE-5 Protocols

CE-5 (Close Encounter of the Fifth Kind) protocols are techniques for human-initiated, voluntary contact with non-human intelligence. The designation extends [[j-allen-hynek]]'s [[close-encounter-cases]] typology beyond CE4 (abduction) to a tier of bilateral, intentional communication.

**Steven Greer and CSETI**: The primary developer of CE-5 protocols is [[steven-greer]], through his organization CSETI (Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence, founded 1991). Greer's CE-5 methodology combines:
- Group meditation and coherent intention
- Light signaling (flashlights, lasers) in patterns
- Toning and sound signaling
- Remote viewing-adjacent protocols for "remote contact"
- Structured observation with documentation

**Claims and epistemics**: Greer claims consistent success in CE-5 events — producing UAP appearances in response to group protocols. The KB treats these claims with appropriate epistemics: positive UAP sightings at CE-5 events could reflect genuine response, confirmation bias (interpretating ordinary lights as response), or atmospheric phenomena. Independent replication with rigorous controls has not been conducted.

**Psionic interface connection**: [[psionic-uap-interface]] cites CE-5 protocols as evidence for the thesis that UAP/NHI respond to consciousness-based signals — that the interface is not electromagnetic but psychic or intentional. This connects CE-5 to the broader KB cluster of consciousness-UAP interaction claims.

**Contactee lineage**: CE-5 protocols extend the historical [[contactee]] tradition (1950s figures who claimed voluntary ongoing contact with "Space Brothers") into a systematic methodology with consciousness-science framing rather than purely spiritual framing.
