# NHI as Information Source

The thesis that encounters with non-human intelligences (NHI) function primarily as **information-delivery events** — the NHI acting as teachers, transmitters, or triggers of insight rather than (or in addition to) physical entities with physical craft.

## Variants of the Thesis

1. **Direct download model** — NHI impart technical or spiritual knowledge directly into a contact's mind (Tesla, Marconi, and Jack Parsons are retroactively placed in this lineage).
2. **Synchronistic trigger model** — NHI encounters catalyze latent human capacities for discovery without literal transmission.
3. **Channeling / mediated contact** — NHI communicate through intermediaries (mediums, automatic writing, hypnosis sessions).
4. **Peer-reviewed conduit** — Scientists or remote viewers receive anomalous information they later interpret as externally sourced.
