# Pre Dynastic Egypt

Period preceding pharaonic Egypt (~10000–3000 BCE); in alternative-archaeology readings of Giza, the era when the deepest substructures were constructed by an advanced population during the [[saharan-humid-period|Saharan Humid Period]].

Pre-dynastic Egypt is the long stretch before the unification of Upper and Lower Egypt under Narmer (~3100 BCE). [[robert-schoch]]'s [[sphinx-water-erosion-theory|Sphinx water-erosion analysis]] and Biondi's deep-tube SAR results both push origin dates substantially earlier than the conventional 4th-Dynasty (~2500 BCE) framing — into a [[pre-dynastic-egypt]] window that overlaps the [[saharan-humid-period]] (8500–5300 BCE) when the Sahara was inhabitable.
