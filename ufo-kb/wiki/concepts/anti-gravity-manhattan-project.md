# The Anti-Gravity Manhattan Project

**Claim**: The United States secretly pursued a Manhattan Project–scale anti-gravity and advanced propulsion program in the 1950s and 1960s, concealed from the public (and from allied nations) behind [[project-blue-book]]'s debunking front.

## Evidence Base

### Documentary
- **[[1971-australian-jio-memo]]** (Harry Turner, Australian JIO): Concludes Blue Book was a front; names [[j-robert-oppenheimer]], [[edward-teller]], [[freeman-dyson]] as deeply involved in anti-gravity programs; lists [[bell-aircraft]], [[martin-corporation]], [[lear-corporation]] as corporate participants
- **[[1956-g-engines-article]]**: Bell Aircraft president [[lawrence-bell]] publicly claims nuclear-fuel gravity cancellation work; Martin VP [[george-trimble]] predicts "Manhattan Project" timeline
- **[[twining-memo]] postscript** (1947): Twining explicitly calls for a "Manhattan Project–style" isolated secret effort to develop UAP-equivalent craft

### Institutional
- [[martin-corporation]]'s **RIAS** (Research Institute for Advanced Study): Dedicated anti-gravity division, employing [[lewis-whitten]], nearly hiring [[bryce-dewitt]]
- [[wright-patterson]]'s **Aerospace Research Laboratory**: Josh Goldberg's group, whose "sole mandate" was to develop a theoretical framework for anti-gravity
- **1957 [[unc-chapel-hill]] conference**: Sponsored by Wright Air Development Center, attended by top physicists, with aerospace contractor (RIAS) representation — the Air Force funding theoretical gravity research

### Experimental
- **[[townsend-brown-gravitator]]**: Witnessed and apparently validated by [[edward-teller]] ("I don't know how this works"), [[curtis-lemay]], [[bill-lear]], [[victor-brandus]] (Wright Field technical rep)
- Wright Field apparently assigned a technical consultant (Charles Wyatt Miller) to live with the Brown family and follow them

### Personnel Transfers
- [[operation-paperclip]]: [[victor-schauberger]], [[richard-miethe]], [[henry-coanda]] — Nazi anti-gravity researchers brought to Wright-Patterson
- [[avrocar-project]]: CIA/British saucer program (1952) taken over by WADC; conventional propulsion, but saucer-shaped, possibly a cover

### Nuclear Secrecy Overlay
- The [[atomic-energy-act-classification]] (1954) automatically classifies any material with radiological properties, including UAP
- [[robert-sarbacher]]: "UFO secrecy is classified two levels above the hydrogen bomb"
- [[david-grusch]]: Oppenheimer designed this classification overlay

## The Suppression Mechanism

The program's concealment involved two layers:
1. **Active debunking**: [[hp-robertson]]'s 1952 Robertson Panel mandated that Blue Book explain away UFO reports; [[edward-condon]]'s 1966–69 Condon Committee was the final nail in public UFO research credibility
2. **Legal classification**: The 1954 AEC Act made any recovered UAP material automatically nuclear-classified; defense contractors guarding this material were legally prohibited from disclosure

## Three Explanations for WADC's Anti-Gravity Interest

1. **Nazi tech transfer**: Kammler-Stab researchers via Operation Paperclip
2. **Townsend Brown's experiments**: Apparently functional electrogravitic effects witnessed at senior levels
3. **Roswell materials**: Recovered craft transported from Fort Worth to Wright-Patterson; WADC as the US's premier reverse-engineering facility
