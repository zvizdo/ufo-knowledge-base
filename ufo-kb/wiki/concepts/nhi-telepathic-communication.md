# NHI Telepathic Communication

Source summary: [[09KP8XVf5nY]]

## Core Thesis

Recovered UAP materials — and possibly non-human entities themselves — transmit information to human beings via a non-acoustic, non-electromagnetic channel that functions similarly to what is colloquially described as telepathy. Multiple independent historical threads support this pattern. The US government not only encountered this phenomenon but recognized it as real, treated it as a subject of scientific investigation, and ultimately institutionalized the pursuit of such non-local consciousness effects through classified research programs.

## Primary Evidence Streams

### Harald Malmgren at Los Alamos — [[bluegill-uap-debris]]
[[harold-malmgren]] handled debris recovered from the UAP downed during [[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown]] at Los Alamos National Laboratory. While holding the material, he heard words in his head — clearly, without acoustic basis. He subsequently forgot the specific words, but reported the experience as unambiguous and distinct from imagination or internal monologue.

Lawrence Geist of the Atomic Energy Commission (AEC) was present and observed Malmgren's reaction. Crucially, Geist treated the reaction not with surprise but with the demeanor of someone **administering a test** — implying that the telepathic effect was a known property of the material, expected to occur, and that Malmgren's response was being evaluated. The institutional foreknowledge implied by Geist's reaction is as significant as Malmgren's own experience.

Malmgren believes the words, though consciously forgotten, may have been subconsciously retained and may have shaped aspects of his subsequent thinking and work — a possibility he raised explicitly in his final interviews, per [[09KP8XVf5nY]].

### Nikola Tesla — Colorado Springs
[[nikola-tesla]], during his Colorado Springs experiments in 1899, claimed to receive communications from extraterrestrial intelligences via shortwave-type radio. His contemporaries dismissed this as eccentricity, but the claim should be read in the context of his electromagnetic research: Tesla was working with frequencies and field effects that were entirely unprecedented, and the "communication" he described may have been an early encounter with the same non-local effect Malmgren experienced sixty years later via physical debris.

### Thomas Townsend Brown
[[thomas-townsend-brown]] reportedly slept next to a "shortwave radio" device and claimed regular contact with entities he called "space brothers." Brown was not a marginal figure — he was a serious physicist whose electrogravitics work attracted US Navy attention. His communications claim is consistent with the pattern of technically sophisticated individuals encountering apparent non-human contact through their work with unusual electromagnetic or gravitational phenomena.

### The Stargate Program — Hal Puthoff and Russell Targ
The US government's most formal institutionalization of non-local consciousness research was [[stargate-program]], run at Stanford Research Institute (SRI) by [[hal-puthoff]] and Russell Targ. The program investigated remote viewing — the apparent ability of human subjects to perceive locations and events beyond sensory range — under rigorous experimental conditions, funded by the CIA and DIA.

The program's existence and partial success suggests the government had accumulated sufficient evidence of non-local consciousness effects to justify sustained classified investment. The UAP-telepathy encounters documented above provide one plausible origin for that institutional interest: officials who had experienced or witnessed the Los Alamos debris effect and similar phenomena needed a scientific framework and research infrastructure to understand and potentially weaponize or operationalize the capability.

### Autistic Savant Telepathy — Ky Dickens / Diane Powell

A structurally parallel thread with no NHI component: [[diane-powell]] (Harvard-trained neuroscientist) documented non-verbal autistic children achieving 90%+ hit rates in controlled telepathy experiments — accessing specific information (random numbers, images, objects) held in a facilitator's mind, with blindfolds and physical dividers ruling out sensory cueing. [[ky-dickens]]'s *[[telepathy-tapes]]* documentary extended Powell's work, including the "Talk on the Hill" — a reported telepathic communication network used by non-speaking autistic individuals. See [[autistic-savant-telepathy]] for full detail.

This thread does not involve NHI as a proximate cause, but it is directly relevant: it demonstrates that human capacity for non-local information access is real and measurable independently of UAP contact. If autistic individuals with unusual neural architecture can access what appears to be a shared consciousness field, the mechanism Malmgren encountered (information from UAP debris transmitted via the same channel) is no longer singular or exotic — it is a property of a channel that human beings also access in other contexts.

### Dan Sherman — Project Preserve Destiny (1991–1994)

The most operationally specific government-institutionalized NHI telepathic communication account in the KB. [[dan-sherman]], a USAF ELINT specialist, claims he was trained and deployed under [[project-preserve-destiny]] — an NSA program nested inside a conventional ELINT black project — as an "intuitive communicator" (IC). He received transmissions from two distinct alien entities (self-named "Spock" and "Bones") for approximately two years at overseas classified detachments.

Key phenomenological details from Sherman's account:
- **The channel is felt physically but not localizable:** *"You can feel it in your brain. You can feel it — you can't localize it. It's not a localization, but I can feel like it's in that area of my brain right there."*
- **Communication has a "tapestry" quality:** Each alien has a distinct signature or flavor; Sherman could distinguish Spock from Bones the way one voice differs from another on a phone call
- **Shutdowns are crisp:** When disallowed subjects were raised, communication terminated abruptly — no confusion or ambiguity, just cessation
- **Content is organic, not mechanical:** Sherman resists the word "download" — *"downloading kind of denotes some sort of machine. It was just a thought. It was a thought that came."* He prefers "receiving"
- **Biofeedback training:** A sine wave monitor visually confirmed IC contact — the wave shifted when Sherman's mental state crossed the threshold. This externalized the initially invisible phenomenon into visible proof
- **Two modes of communication:** Sherman developed a "closer plane" for informal or personal questions with Bones, distinct from the standard formal communication channel

**Critical institutional parallel:** PPD is to NHI communication what [[stargate-program]] is to human non-local perception — a government-funded, institutionalized research and operational program built around a phenomenon that conventional science cannot yet explain. PPD adds a dimension Stargate lacks: the ICs were *genetically pre-conditioned* by NHI before being selected (see [[nhi-genetic-modification]]).

**Late-stage mission shift to abduction monitoring:** Toward the end of Sherman's service, transmissions appeared to carry real-time abduction data — location potentials, pain levels, and recall indicators. This implies PPD was at least partly used to monitor NHI abduction activity. Whether this was for intelligence collection, to protect abductees, or for other purposes is unknown.

### Mario Woods at November 5 Silo — 1977

[[mario-woods]] received a telepathic transmission during the [[1977-ellsworth-woods-abduction]] that he describes with unusual precision:

> "The words 'do not fear' traveled through my body like a vibration — a hiss. A telepathic… I don't know. It got to every part of you. You feel it radiate through you."

Key properties of the transmission:
- No acoustic component
- Received as a wave-like vibration through the body
- Unambiguous: "There's no misinterpretation of someone speaking to you — you know exactly what's being said"
- No lag or "huh?" response possible: "their language is... direct rude access"
- Repeated: "do not fear" recurred as a sustained message
- Described by Mario as recognizing the communication the way a fetus in the womb recognizes speech from outside — felt as familiar, not alien

The tall Gray (one chest-and-head taller than the three small Grays) was the entity apparently associated with the transmission, though attribution under extreme stress is uncertain.

Under hypnotic regression, a second communication occurred during the wrist procedure: the tall Gray communicated "we don't hurt you" when Woods expressed pain.

This is among the most detailed on-record descriptions of NHI telepathic transmission mechanics from a military witness with Q clearance — not a civilian contactee.

### Kirsan Ilyumzhinov — 1997 Moscow Spacecraft Encounter

[[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]]'s account in [[IFaXi-NrPV0]] adds a firsthand case with unusually high institutional credibility (sitting head of state, corroborated by three witnesses).

During his [[1997-ilyumzhinov-encounter]], he was aboard a spacecraft for approximately one hour. The NHI beings (tall, humanoid, in yellow garments) **"were not reminded me of my meetings with Baba Vanga… She was answering before the translator had time to finish asking the question. It was the same situation with those beings."** He asked questions; they answered through thoughts. He was speaking aloud; they responded without spoken language.

Ilyumzhinov also compares this to **Atlantis** — the legendary civilization where, he says, people also communicated through thoughts — and to an Australian Aboriginal tribe reportedly capable of communicating over distances without technology.

The comparison to [[baba-vanga]] is structurally significant: she operated through the same apparent channel — knowing the content of questions before they were spoken — suggesting the mechanism is not unique to NHI but exists on a spectrum of consciousness access that both human psychics and NHI share.

The NHI also showed Ilyumzhinov mathematical formulas "like in space — conjured up one after another, various symbols" — suggesting that conceptual information was transmitted pictorially or symbolically rather than linguistically. He could not recall the symbols after returning, consistent with the pattern described in the Malmgren case (information initially clear, subsequently difficult to retain).

## Pattern Analysis

Across these four streams, a consistent structure emerges:

1. An individual with technical or scientific background encounters an unusual electromagnetic/gravitational/material artifact
2. The individual experiences apparent non-acoustic information transmission
3. The information is often initially clear but subsequently difficult to retain or articulate
4. Institutional observers (when present) treat the experience as expected rather than anomalous

The AEC's foreknowledge implied by Lawrence Geist's behavior at Los Alamos is the strongest evidence that this was not incidental: the US government had a working model of the telepathic effect and was testing humans against it by the early 1960s at the latest.
