# Tethys Sea

Ancient ocean that covered the Giza region in deep prehistory; its hydrothermal vent activity deposited the iron ore and rare-earth-bearing veins central to the pyramid-as-chemical-manufacturing thesis.

The Tethys Sea was a Mesozoic-era ocean that covered much of the present-day Mediterranean and North African region. In the chemical-manufacturing reading of Giza, Tethys hydrothermal vents deposited iron ore and rare-earth-bearing mineral veins in the limestone bedrock of the plateau — the geological resource the [[pyramid-purpose-debate|pyramid complex]] was allegedly engineered to extract.
