# Government Suppression of UAP Evidence

Government suppression of UAP evidence refers to the documented and alleged pattern by which state actors have classified, discredited, obstructed, or actively countered civilian and official awareness of UAP phenomena. It spans classification orders, active disinformation, institutional bad faith, and the systematic destruction or disappearance of records.

## Modes of Suppression

**Classification / FOIA Obstruction** — [[john-greenwald]] at [[black-vault]] demonstrates that FOIA response rates for UAP materials are declining, not improving, despite public disclosure momentum. Law enforcement exemptions are now being used to block UAP FOIA requests, implying an active hidden investigation. See [[immaculate-constellation]], [[whistleblower-protection-gap]].

**Active Disinformation** — The [[paul-benowitz]]/[[richard-doty]] operation is the clearest documented case: AFOSI deliberately drove a civilian researcher to psychiatric breakdown using false UFO information. The [[flood-the-zone-disinfo]] concept describes the broader technique. See also [[bill-moore]].

**Institutional Discrediting** — [[edward-ruppelt]]'s 1956→1960 reversal on UFOs under institutional pressure; NICAP's destruction from within; academic peer review suppression of UAP science. See [[astronomical-data-suppression]], [[navy-ufo-rivalry]].

**Peru / Nazca Mummy Suppression** — The Peruvian Ministry of Culture conflated tourist figurines with real mummy specimens in official statements (which became Reuters debunks), raided press conferences, and made 7 attempts to seize bodies from [[university-of-ika]]. A government actor using fabricated counter-evidence to suppress analysis of potentially genuine evidence. See [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]], [[HxQN2tkQHs8]].

**Record Loss** — NASA "lost" Kecksburg-related records after acknowledging they existed; multiple missing radar tapes and film reels across UAP history. See [[1965-kecksburg]], [[astronomical-data-suppression]].

## Why Suppression Is Maintained

Theories:
- **Technology monopoly** — reverse-engineered propulsion represents an energy and military advantage too valuable to share
- **Societal stability** — fear of public reaction to confirmation of NHI presence
- **Institutional inertia** — classification structures that became self-perpetuating
- **Breakaway civilization protection** — the suppressing body is protecting its own interests, not the state's

See [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]], [[breakaway-civilization]], [[disclosure-narratives]].
