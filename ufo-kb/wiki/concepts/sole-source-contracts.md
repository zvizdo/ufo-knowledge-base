# Sole-Source Contracts (Black Program Contracting)

The contracting mechanism used for classified SAP-level programs where competitive bidding would compromise security by exposing program specifications, performance requirements, and deliverables to multiple companies.

## The Mechanism

[[eric-davis]] explains in [[LnAiNChnuEQ]]:

Standard government contracting uses:
- **RFP (Request for Proposals)** — companies compete based on published specs
- **BAA (Broad Agency Announcement)** — even broader public solicitation (how Bigelow Aerospace learned about and bid for AAWSAP)

For SAP-level programs, competitive bidding is not used because publishing specs would compromise security. Instead:

> "The government agency has the contract guy and the program manager guy and maybe one other authority over all that they're going to call up their favorite contractor who they know or believe could successfully execute the project and they'll say okay we got we want to give you a contract here's how much money and here's how many years."

The "Beltway Bandits" (legacy defense firms) "need money so they will always say yes."

## Pass-Through Companies

Money for sole-source black contracts is typically channeled through a front (pass-through) company rather than directly from the agency to the contractor. This makes budget auditing difficult:

> "Usually they set up a fret company to pass the money through."

However, Davis emphasizes this does not prevent contractor-government communication. The program still has contract monitoring and oversight — the government customer knows who the contractor is, and the contractor knows who their customer is. The pass-through only obscures the financial trail, not the working relationship.

## IRAD Fraud Allegation Context

[[david-grusch]] alleged in congressional testimony that at least one contractor artificially inflated a program budget so they could siphon the excess into **IRAD (Internal Research and Development)** — essentially using overperformance on a black contract to fund unauthorized research. [[lue-elizondo]] repeated this allegation.

Davis says he has no direct knowledge of this but explains the two contract vehicle types: **Cost Plus Fee** and **Fixed Price**. IRAD fraud would most plausibly occur under Cost Plus contracts (where cost overruns are reimbursed), which is also the structure that creates massive overruns in public programs (Artemis, Orion, Starliner).

## Authorization Status

Davis confirms: "Those sole source contracts would they be authorized contracts then or have a transfer of authority those sole source contracts would they be traceable? No."

This means sole-source black contracts are **not traceable** through standard budget oversight channels — the pass-through company is the visible transaction endpoint.
