# Scalar Waves

Wave solutions that emerge from the full original form of James Clerk Maxwell's electromagnetic equations but were dropped when Oliver Heaviside reformulated those equations into the compact four-vector calculus form used today. The scalar-wave hypothesis holds that Heaviside's simplification — mathematically convenient and sufficient for most engineering applications — discarded a physically real class of electromagnetic phenomena.

In the UFO/UAP/consciousness-research context, scalar waves are proposed as:
1. A candidate mechanism for non-local information transfer (telepathy, NHI contact)
2. A candidate propulsion/field-manipulation mechanism for UAP behavior
3. A connection between biological structures (DNA) and macroscopic field physics

## The Maxwell → Heaviside Transition

Maxwell originally published 20–21 equations describing the electromagnetic field. In the late 19th century, Heaviside (and independently Hertz) reformulated these into four vector equations — the form taught in physics today. The simplification dropped terms representing **scalar and vector potentials without accompanying conventional E and B fields** present — the so-called pure-gauge or "longitudinal" terms.

Within this dropped space, [[constantine-meyl]] and others derive:
- **Helicoidal waves** — longitudinal waves with helical geometry structurally matching double-helix DNA
- **Scalar longitudinal waves** — pressure-wave-like disturbances in the electromagnetic potential field with unusual propagation and interaction properties
