# Space-Time as Epiphenomenon

[[eric-davis]]'s framing of the physical substrate underlying UAP/NHI behavior, as relayed by [[jacques-vallee]] in [[fyX8V1XXmQM]]:

> *"Those dimensions like space and time — they're arbitrary. There's something deeper that creates the illusion of space and time in humans. We really don't have a good idea of what reality is. It's not even obvious that we can stop it long enough to look at it."*

Davis frames reality as **quantum foam** — not tied to any particular space-time frame, with space and time as emergent epiphenomena of something more fundamental rather than the fundamental substrate itself.

## The NHI Witness Account

The context is a witness account in which an NHI entity contested the witness's sense of both time and location — telling them they were wrong about what time it was and where they were. Davis's interpretation: the entity's challenge is not an impossibility but a **demonstration of perceiving from outside the emergent space-time layer**. An entity operating from the substrate level at which space-time parameters are defined would experience the space-time constraints humans take as fundamental as merely conventional approximations.

This position is directly relevant to [[interdimensional-hypothesis]]: if space-time is a human-constructed epiphenomenon, craft that appear to move impossibly or exist "inside 4D space" are not violating physics — they are operating from the layer at which those physics parameters are defined.

## Flatland Geometry Analogy

Vallée uses the [[flatland-abbott]] analogy alongside Davis's framing: what humans perceive as the craft's exterior is only the cross-section of a higher-dimensional object intersecting the space-time epiphenomenon layer. This is not metaphor; it is the literal geometric implication of Davis's "space-time as emergent" position.

The "inside bigger than outside" phenomenon, reported across multiple close-encounter accounts and referenced by both Vallée and Grusch, is the direct observational signature of a higher-dimensional object whose cross-section we can perceive but whose full extent exists in dimensions we cannot directly access.

## Relationship to Consciousness Research

Davis's epiphenomenon framing complements the [[consciousness-non-local]] thesis: if space-time is emergent from something more fundamental, and if consciousness is the substrate from which it emerges (as simulation theory and panpsychism suggest), then non-local consciousness is not anomalous — it is a feature of the substrate layer at which space-time has not yet been rendered.

The framing also aligns with [[eric-wargo]]'s temporal non-locality: if time is an epiphenomenon rather than a fundamental dimension, retrocausality and precognition become straightforward properties of the substrate rather than violations of physical law.
