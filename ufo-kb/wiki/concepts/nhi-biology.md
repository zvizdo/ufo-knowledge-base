# NHI Biology

The attempt to characterize the biological or physical nature of non-human intelligences based on available testimony, alleged recovered remains, and abduction accounts. Unlike mainstream astrobiology, which models hypothetical life based on chemistry, NHI biology works backward from claimed direct encounters.

Key themes in the KB:

**Morphology**: The most frequently described NHI type ([[grey-aliens]]) is characterized by large cranium relative to body size, vestigial or absent digestive organs, apparent lack of sexual dimorphism, and skin texture described as smooth or waxy. Physiologically, this profile suggests either a highly evolved post-biological form or a purpose-designed biological construct.

**Postbiological possibility**: [[avi-loeb]] and others in the KB argue that any NHI capable of interstellar travel is almost certainly postbiological — the biological phase of intelligence being a brief evolutionary transition to machine or hybrid substrate. If true, "NHI biology" is a category error; the entities may be technological artifacts, not living organisms.

**Genetic manipulation**: Some KB sources (abduction researchers, crash-retrieval testimony) describe NHI as engaged in genetic sampling of humans — a pattern consistent with either observational science or active genetic modification programs. [[john-mack]]'s abduction research documented this systematically.

The [[crash-retrieval-research]] record includes reported recovery of NHI biological material; the physiological analysis of such material, if it occurred, would be the most classified component of the UAP secrecy complex.
