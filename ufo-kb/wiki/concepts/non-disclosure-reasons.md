# Non-Disclosure Reasons

[[carl-nell]]'s six-reason taxonomy for government UAP non-disclosure, offered at Hereticon by someone who served as Army representative to the [[uap-task-force]] and was privy to classified information.

## The Six Reasons

1. **National Security** — You do not want adversaries to know what you know about UAP, NHI capabilities, or recovered materials. Standard classification logic applied to an anomalous domain.

2. **Lack of a Plan** — The phenomenon is so "profound and impactful" (Nell's words) that disclosure without a framework for managing public response could have "significantly detrimental effects." Technology with unknown ramifications cannot be released without protocols for handling the societal consequences.

3. **Potential for Societal Disruption** — The "ontological shock" of realizing that angels-and-aliens are the same phenomenon, and that NHI are real, could destabilize religious, scientific, and cultural institutions simultaneously.

4. **Covert Agreement** — Parties may be benefiting from the status quo of non-disclosure — profiting from reversed-engineered technology, strategic advantage, or access that disclosure would democratize or eliminate.

5. **Cover-Up of Misdeeds** — Personnel involved in managing UAP programs may have done things (legally, ethically, or in terms of treaty violations) that they do not want exposed. Disclosure exposes the program; exposing the program exposes the misdeeds.

6. **Organizational Inertia / Bureaucratic Indifference** — Simple institutional transigence: no internal champion, no prioritization, no mechanism to force the decision to be made. Bureaucracies default to the status quo when there is no mandate for change.

## Historical Confirmation: Robertson Panel and Project Blue Book

Nell presents two cases as empirical support that non-disclosure is deliberate policy, not conspiracy theory:

- **Robertson Panel (1953)**: Declassified CIA documents confirm a panel explicitly decided to **debunk** UFO reports and **infiltrate** civilian UFO organizations to suppress public interest. "This is absolutely established fact, nobody disputes this, and the documents are available." ([[robertson-panel]])
- **Project Blue Book / Condon Study**: The Air Force commissioned the University of Colorado study to close the Blue Book program. Ed Condon (Manhattan Project alumnus) **wrote the negative conclusion before the study was complete** and bragged about it. Many scientists on the study publicly defected. The study's content contradicts its conclusions. The Air Force cancelled Blue Book anyway. ([[project-blue-book]])

## Non-Disclosure as Universal Constant

Nell's first heresy: "Non-disclosure of uncomfortable aspects of reality is a universal constant across cultures and across time." This goes beyond explaining government UAP secrecy — it is a claim about the structural behavior of all institutions when confronted with phenomena that threaten their legitimacy or comprehension.

[[diana-pasulka]] adds a historical-religious dimension: the stigma/stigmata mechanism in medieval Catholic encounters with aerial phenomena was the same non-disclosure operation — institutional marking that enforces silence — centuries before the CIA Robertson Panel.
