# Beta Israel

The **Ethiopian Jewish community** — historically self-designated as Beta Israel ("House of Israel") — concentrated traditionally in the Gondar and Tigray regions of northern Ethiopia. Most of the community emigrated to Israel in the late 20th century via Operations Moses (1984), Joshua (1985), and Solomon (1991).

In UAP-adjacent discourse, Beta Israel features primarily as a **primary-witness lineage** in [[graham-hancock]]'s 1992 book *[[sign-and-the-seal]]*, which traces the Ark of the Covenant's alleged journey from Jerusalem through Egypt to Aksum, Ethiopia, drawing on Beta Israel oral tradition and ritual. The thread connects into the broader [[lost-civilization-hypothesis]] cluster only loosely — its primary KB role is as an ethnographic anchor for Hancock's Ark thesis, which is itself peripheral to but cited in the [[ancient-astronaut-hypothesis]] discussion.
