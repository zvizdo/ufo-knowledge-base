# UFO Legacy Program Pyramid

The **UAP Legacy Program Pyramid** is an analytical framework developed by [[uap-gerb]] to describe the organisational structure of US government programs to retrieve, store, exploit, and derive technologies from non-human-origin craft. It synthesises 80–90 years of program evolution from the Manhattan Project and Atomic Energy Commission through the present.

## Structure

### Apex
An unknown figure — or a figure like the late [[dick-cheney]]. [[walter-kern]] relayed [[david-grusch]]'s claim that Cheney was the apex for "quite a while"; when Cheney left in 2009, the programs lost central leadership. Below the apex are legacy-program **administrators**.

### Intelligence-Agency Administrators (Tier 2)
Directorates within the **six major US intelligence agencies**:
- NSA (National Security Agency)
- DIA (Defense Intelligence Agency)
- CIA — especially **CIA DS&T** (Directorate of Science & Technology), whose OGA (Office of Global Access, founded by [[doug-wolf]]) is specifically named
- NRO (National Reconnaissance Office)
- NGA (National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency)
- [[nuro]] — National Underwater Reconnaissance Office (est. 1969, still undisclosed — the overlooked "sixth" agency)

### FFRDC Intermediaries (Tier 3)
**Federally Funded Research and Development Centers** — government-owned, contractor-operated. This is UAPGerb's most distinctive analytical contribution: FFRDCs sit between intelligence-agency administrators and prime contractors, allowing the government to retain access control and classification authority while outsourcing R&D.

Key FFRDCs named:
- [[mitre-corporation]] — appears alongside Sean Kirkpatrick post-AARO
- [[battelle-memorial-institute]] — Ronald Moltrie's scrubbed connection
- [[sandia-national-laboratories]] — TR3B propulsion reverse-engineering; Roswell retrieval co-location
- [[lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory]] — TR3B propulsion
- Oak Ridge, RAND, Center for Naval Analyses, Aerospace Corporation

### Defense-Industrial Prime Contractors (Tier 4)
[[lockheed-martin]], [[northrop-grumman]], Raytheon, TRW, Teladyne Ryan, BDM. The mortar holding this tier in place is:
- Major Range and Test Facility Bases (**MRTFBs**): Nevada Test and Training Range (Area 51, S4, Tonopah), Utah Test and Training Range / West Desert Test Center (Dugway), [[edwards-air-force-base]] 412th Test Wing, China Lake, Pax River, etc.
- Creative funding: IRAD (independent R&D), carryover funds, overcharging

### Limestone Casing — Program Protection (Tier 5)
The outermost layer protecting the pyramid from external view:
- DARPA Security and Intelligence Directorate (SID)
- DOE Office of Intelligence and Counterintelligence (OICI)
- SAPs, USAPs, CAPs, carve-out contracts
- Disinformation campaigns, psyops
- Personnel rotation (ODNI → MITRE → CIA DS&T → NRO → contractor)
- Bigot lists; dual roles (scientists doing their own accounting)
- 2010s initiative: cut off historical briefings to new program incumbents → compartmentalisation within compartmentalisation

## Key Insight: FFRDCs Prevent Runaway

A common misconception is that legacy programs have "run away" into Lockheed Martin or Northrop Grumman beyond government control. UAPGerb argues the opposite: the FFRDC tier ensures the DoD/IC retains ownership of materials, personnel access, and classification authority. The programs are tightly controlled — just through non-traditional oversight channels.
