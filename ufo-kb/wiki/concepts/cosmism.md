# Cosmism

The observation — articulated by [[diana-pasulka]] and endorsed by [[carl-nell]] — that the founding figures of the Russian and American space programs were not secular materialists but esoteric NHI-believers, and that their belief in contact with non-human intelligence was the **motivating cosmological framework** for their rocketry work.

The name "cosmism" refers to a specific 19th–early 20th-century Russian philosophical tradition (originating with Nikolai Fyodorov and others), but [[diana-pasulka]] uses it more broadly to name the shared epistemological substrate across both programs.

## Russian Cosmism

[[konstantin-tsiolkovsky]], father of Russian rocketry, **explicitly believed in non-human intelligence** and wrote books articulating this belief. His framework had a Slavic-Christian overlay: NHI are real, humanity is destined to expand into the cosmos, and this expansion is part of a cosmic evolutionary or divine order. This is the NHI-cosmism seed of the Soviet/Russian space tradition.

## American Cosmism

[[jack-parsons]], JPL co-founder and father of US solid-fuel rocketry (in Pasulka's framing), worked within Aleister Crowley's Thelema system — not framing his NHI contacts as demonic, but as part of the "common task" of getting humanity to space. [[diana-pasulka]] explicitly says: "He didn't view what he was doing as demonic. He thought he was working for the common task of humans." This is a Greco-Roman framework rather than a Christian one.

Pasulka named her book *[[american-cosmic]]* because this tradition had gone unacknowledged.

## Space Program Patches: The Living Tradition

Beyond the founding generation, cosmism is a **living oral tradition** in the space programs. Insiders showed Pasulka mission patches bearing **Roman gods and first-century Latin** on classified satellite/space missions. At least one person collected 150+ such patches. These are dedications to **"the sponsors"** — the NHI the programs believe they work with.

When Pasulka asked insiders whether the NHI read Latin: **"Absolutely."**

The tradition is transmitted **orally**, not via memo. People discover their mission's true nature by figuring it out themselves — they sometimes don't know they got it right until they receive a patch or award years later. Pasulka compares this to 30,000-year-old Australian Aboriginal oral traditions about NHI: durable, non-documentary, hermetically passed.

## Tim Taylor / "Tyler" and Consciousness Protocols

[[tim-taylor]] (Pasulka's "Tyler") is a NASA Mission Controller who uses **consciousness protocols** as an essential part of his work — not incidental to the engineering. Pasulka witnessed him undergo a religious conversion at the **Vatican Observatory (Castel Gandolfo)** after examining original Copernicus and Kepler documents. European Space Agency scientists recognized Taylor by name with immediate acclaim — implying his work is known to the European aerospace community, even if invisible in public discourse.

Both frameworks posit that getting humanity off the planet is a mission given meaning by NHI relationship, not pure technical ambition.

## Karl Nell's Synthesis

[[carl-nell]] lists cosmism as one of his **four heresies**: "[[cosmism]] is at the root of both the Russian and the US space programs." He argues this is not coincidental — the initiatic traditions (Gnostic, Sufi, Kabbalistic, Advaita Vedanta, Hermetic/Egyptian) all converge on inward-looking contact experience that the space founders were drawing on. The Pyramid Texts (Egypt, ~2500 BC) as initiatory documents connect this to the oldest recorded esoteric traditions.
