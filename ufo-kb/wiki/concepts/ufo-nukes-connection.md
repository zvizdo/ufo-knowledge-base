# UFO-Nuclear Nexus

The UFO-nuclear nexus refers to the documented pattern of UAP activity near nuclear weapons facilities, nuclear testing sites, and strategic missile installations — suggesting either NHI surveillance of nuclear capabilities or a reactive/responsive relationship to nuclear technology.

**Documented incidents**:
- **[[1967-echo-flight]]**: On March 24, 1967, ten Minuteman ICBMs at Malmstrom AFB Echo Flight went into "No-Go" status simultaneously during a reported UAP sighting near the launch control facility. Captain Robert Salas reported the duty officer described a glowing red object. This is the most frequently cited nuclear-UAP incident.
- **[[1980-rendlesham-forest]]**: The RAF Bentwaters/Woodbridge incident occurred at a NATO base widely reported to have stored US nuclear weapons. The multi-night UAP encounter near a nuclear storage site has been noted as potentially non-coincidental.
- **Soviet incidents**: Soviet military witnesses reported parallel UAP activity at Soviet nuclear missile sites, including an incident at a Ukrainian ICBM base where missiles allegedly activated briefly without human command.

**The Salas/Hastings thesis**: Robert Salas (Echo Flight) and researcher Robert Hastings have documented and publicized the nuclear-UAP correlation through congressional briefings and a 2010 Washington press conference with multiple nuclear weapons officers. Their thesis: UAP demonstrates the ability to disable nuclear weapons, possibly as a demonstration of capability or a warning about nuclear proliferation.

**Space Brothers tradition**: The contactee tradition's Space Brothers consistently warned about nuclear weapons — this convergence between contactee messaging and nuclear-site UAP activity is cited in the KB (including [[the-nine]]) as potentially indicating a coherent NHI concern about human nuclear capability.
