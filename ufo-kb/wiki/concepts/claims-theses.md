# Claims-Theses

Specific, recurring, discrete assertions in the UAP/UFO discourse. Each claim-thesis gets its own page that aggregates: who makes the claim, who disputes it, supporting and refuting evidence, and connections to related entities.

Distinct from [[recurring-claims]], which lists *categories* of assertion. Claims-theses are specific propositions like "the Moon is artificial" or "NHI are interdimensional."
