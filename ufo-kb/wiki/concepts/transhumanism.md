# Transhumanism

Transhumanism is the intellectual and cultural movement holding that human beings can and should transcend their current biological limitations through technology — including enhanced cognition, genetic modification, radical life extension, and eventual mind uploading or substrate independence. The word was coined by [[julian-huxley]] in 1957 in an essay arguing that "the human species can, if it wishes, transcend itself — not just sporadically, an individual here in one way, an individual there in another way, but in its entirety, as humanity."

**Julian Huxley's framing**: Huxley's transhumanism was rooted in evolutionary humanism — the belief that the same forces that produced homo sapiens from earlier primates could, through intentional human direction, produce something beyond homo sapiens. He saw this as a natural extension of Darwinian logic rather than a departure from it.

**NHI connection**: In the UAP context, transhumanism matters primarily as a conceptual bridge to the [[postbiological-civilization]] hypothesis — the idea that any sufficiently advanced extraterrestrial or non-human intelligence would already be post-biological, having long since merged with or been replaced by its own technological creations. If NHI are real, they are likely transhumanist endpoints, not biological beings.

**AI and the singularity**: Contemporary transhumanism often focuses on artificial intelligence and the technological singularity — the point at which AI exceeds human cognitive capability and the pace of change becomes incomprehensible. [[mark-andreessen]] and the Silicon Valley community around this discourse are relevant to the KB's NSC classified-physics disclosure narrative.

**Mind uploading**: Transhumanism's most extreme version involves mind uploading — transferring the pattern of a consciousness to non-biological substrate. This intersects with the KB's [[transmission-theory-of-consciousness]] and [[simulation-theory]] frameworks.
