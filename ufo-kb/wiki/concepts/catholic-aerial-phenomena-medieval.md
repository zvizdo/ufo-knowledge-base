# Catholic Aerial Phenomena (1200–1800)

[[diana-pasulka]]'s archival research — begun while researching Catholic purgatory doctrine, not UAPs — documented European Catholic reports of discs, orbs, luminous aerial phenomena, and "flying houses" from 1200 to 1800 CE. The finding was unexpected: multiple aerospace company employees independently contacted Pasulka after learning of the research, specifically requesting the injury data — confirming active cross-field interest in pre-modern contact phenomenology.

## St. Francis Stigmata as UAP Encounter (1224 CE)

The 1224 stigmata event provides the most precisely documented pre-modern instance of physical UAP-type injury:

- **Brother Leo** witnessed a **seraphim with an eye in the center** "deal harshly with Francis"
- Francis suffered injuries matching **electromagnetic radiation burns** — the same injury type documented by [[garry-nolan]]'s CIA-commissioned work on modern UAP experiencers
- The event was marked by institutionalized non-disclosure (the word *stigma* encodes this secrecy)
- Francis likely died from the wounds

This is not merely a structural parallel (religious experience resembling UAP contact) but a **direct physical continuity** — the same type of encounter producing the same type of measurable injury across eight centuries. See [[ufo-electromagnetic-injuries]] for the modern injury dataset Nolan assembled.

## The 1947 Dividing Line

Pasulka's interpretation, endorsed by [[brian-muraresku]] in [[I9VyyV4-3Js]]: **1947 (Roswell) is a dividing line for cultural framing, not for the phenomenon itself.** Before 1947, the same underlying contact phenomenon presented in pre-modern cultural idioms — angels, demons, genies, sylphs, fairies, gnomes, elves, leprechauns. After 1947, the idiom switched to aliens and spacecraft, the technological vocabulary of the postwar era.

The 1200–1800 CE aerial phenomena are the same phenomenon; the Catholic cosmological vocabulary is the period-appropriate wrapper.

## The 19th-Century Bridging Case

[[brian-muraresku]] adds a transitional case: **late 19th-century American airship sightings**. Before Betty and Barney Hill, before Roswell, witnesses reported encounters with dirigible-like craft (period-cognizable technology) piloted by humanoid beings who descended on ladders, engaged in brief conversations, and departed. The encounter structure and transformative aftermath were identical to later UFO contact accounts — and witnesses sometimes emerged transformed in ways resembling psychedelic breakthrough moments.

## Aerospace Industry Interest

The most operationally significant detail: multiple aerospace company employees independently contacted Pasulka specifically to access the injury reports. The request was not for the theological content but for the physical injury documentation. This suggests aerospace industry interest in non-modern UAP injury data as part of materials/effects analysis programs — paralleling [[garry-nolan]]'s modern work with CIA-commissioned health data.
