# UFO Electromagnetic Injuries

The systematic correspondence between **historical descriptions of physical injury from angel-contact events** and **modern CIA-commissioned studies of physical injury from UAP encounters**. ## The Stigmata Case: St. Francis of Assisi (1224 CE)

Pasulka's analysis of the **primary source material** (not later hagiographic interpretations) for Francis's stigmata event:

- **Witness**: Brother Leo, approximately 15 years old, was watching from a distance.
- **What Leo describes**: A "seraphim" descended — described as having an **eye in the center**. Leo reports the being "dealt harshly with Francis." 
- **Physical aftermath**: Francis suffered wounds on his hands (the stigmata — interpreted by tradition as the "wounds of Christ"). Pasulka's reading: these are **radiation-burn-like injuries** consistent with electromagnetic radiation exposure.
- **Non-disclosure**: Francis and Leo told no one initially. The stigma (marking) was kept secret — the word *stigma* carries the connotation of institutional enforced silence. The tradition surrounding this event is marked by deliberate suppression.
- **Death**: Francis died most likely from these wounds.

## The "Stigma" as Non-Disclosure Technology

The word *stigmata* derives from *stigma* — a mark, brand, or disgraceful sign. Pasulka notes this etymology carries a social-control function: the person who has encountered something anomalous is *marked* in a way that enforces silence. The mechanism of institutionalized non-disclosure (shame, disgrace, social marking) appears in both medieval Catholic communities and modern UAP encounter reports.
