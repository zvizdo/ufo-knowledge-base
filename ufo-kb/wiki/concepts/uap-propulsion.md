# UAP Propulsion

The physical mechanisms hypothesized to explain UAP flight characteristics that are inconsistent with known aerodynamic, rocket, or ion propulsion. Witnesses and analysts have documented behaviors — including instantaneous acceleration to hypersonic speeds, right-angle turns at Mach 5+, trans-medium transition (air to water without deceleration), and extended hovering in high-wind conditions — that violate Newton's laws as applied to known vehicle types.

**Observed characteristics** requiring explanation:
- Instantaneous acceleration without observable thrust plume or exhaust
- No sonic boom at transonic/hypersonic speeds (implies no air displacement wave)
- No inertial effects on the vehicle structure (implies either no inertia or inertia cancellation)
- Trans-medium operation (air/water/space transitions) without structural change
- Apparent ability to "cloak" or mask IR/radar signature selectively

**Leading hypotheses** in the KB:
- **Gravity manipulation** (→ [[gravity-manipulation-propulsion]]): Craft generate a localized gravitational field that moves the craft within it, eliminating inertia relative to the craft frame. Derived from [[electrogravitics]] research and consistent with T. Townsend Brown's experimental findings.
- **Inertial reference frame manipulation** (→ Ryan Graves framing): The craft manipulates its local inertial reference frame — equivalent to Alcubierre warp drive at smaller scale.
- **Vacuum energy / Casimir extraction**: Craft extract zero-point energy from the quantum vacuum; no combustion required. Consistent with the apparent absence of fuel exhaustion in long-duration UAP events.
- **Plasma propulsion / magnetohydrodynamics**: High-energy plasma surrounding the craft enables MHD drive — explains the luminosity and IR profile while providing propulsion.

**Evidential grounding**: The [[pais-effect]] patent (US Navy, 2019) explicitly describes inertial mass reduction via high-frequency electromagnetic fields — the same mechanism that would explain inertia-free UAP maneuvering. Whether this represents reverse-engineered understanding or independent research is contested.
