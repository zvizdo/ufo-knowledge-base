# Sacred Geometry

The framework in which specific geometric ratios, proportions, and patterns — the golden ratio, Fibonacci sequence, vesica piscis, Platonic solids — are understood to encode cosmological, physical, and spiritual truths. Sacred geometry holds that these patterns are not merely aesthetic but are structural features of reality that pre-modern cultures discovered empirically and embedded in their monuments, temples, and ritual art.

In the KB's ancient-civilization cluster, sacred geometry is the mechanism through which knowledge survived [[catastrophism]]: after a civilization-ending event, the mathematical encoding in [[ancient-mega-engineering]] sites preserves critical information in a form that survives material and textual destruction. Pyramids at Giza, Teotihuacán, and other sites encode pi, phi, and astronomical constants in their dimensions, alignments, and proportions to a precision that cannot be coincidental.

[[randall-carlson]] and [[graham-hancock]] treat sacred geometry as encoded catastrophism data: the [[great-pyramid]] encodes the Earth's dimensions with sub-kilometer accuracy in its base measurements, implying a degree of geodetic knowledge that mainstream archaeology credits only to 19th-century surveying.

The mystical reading (from [[mystery-schools]] through [[theosophy]] to modern esoteric traditions) holds that sacred geometric forms are not merely repositories of measurement data but structural features of consciousness itself — that geometric meditation produces the same altered states as entheogenic initiation, and that the two traditions were historically integrated.
