# Subterranean NHI Hypothesis

The hypothesis that the most persistent non-human intelligence interacting with humanity is not extraterrestrial but subterranean — resident within or beneath the Earth, in underground oceans, deep geological voids, or artificial cavern complexes. A variant of the broader [[ultraterrestrial-hypothesis]] family, it diverges from the extraterrestrial hypothesis by locating NHI origin on Earth itself.

Primary evidence cited in the KB:
- UAP frequently observed entering or exiting bodies of water ([[transmedium-objects]])
- The [[dulce-underground-base]] reports describe a multi-level underground complex allegedly housing NHI and housing recovered technology
- [[skinwalker-ranch]] UAP often emerge from or return to ground-level points, not from the sky
- Indigenous traditions worldwide describe "people beneath the earth" or subterranean intelligences as antecedents to current human civilization

The hypothesis has implications for disclosure: if NHI is resident on Earth, the geopolitical and territorial framing is entirely different from the extraterrestrial model. It also explains why UAP appear indifferent to national airspace boundaries — they may not recognize the surface-dwelling boundary system as meaningful.

[[archaeoacoustics]] provides a tangential connection: many ancient cultures engineered subterranean ritual spaces, suggesting either contact with or emulation of subterranean entities.
