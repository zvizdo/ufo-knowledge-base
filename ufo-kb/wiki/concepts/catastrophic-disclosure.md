# Catastrophic Disclosure

A term and framing used by [[jaime-maussan]] and [[jesse-michaels]] (and the title of [[michael-mazzola]]'s book) to describe the idea that disclosure of non-human intelligence and NHI physical evidence is already occurring in Latin America — not as a controlled, government-sanctioned drip but as a "catastrophic" (sudden, bottom-up) revelation through physical evidence in the public domain.

## Core Argument

The conventional "disclosure narrative" in the US UAP community centers on waiting for official government acknowledgment — congressional hearings, executive-branch declassification, Pentagon confirmation. Jesse argues this is a misnomer: disclosure has already occurred for those who examine the evidence. The very concept of waiting for official disclosure is an "appeal to authority."

The Nazca mummies are the primary evidence: forensic experts from three countries have independently concluded the M-type bodies are likely real organisms. DICOM files are publicly available. A US congressman has relayed a Pentagon whistleblower's private confirmation. Yet mainstream media's coverage is dominated by the wooden-figurine debunk narrative.

## Distinction from Existing KB Concept

This concept differs from [[disclosure-narratives]] (which covers meta-theories about how truth is/isn't being released) and from [[catastrophic-disclosure-fear]] (which covers institutional fear of destabilizing revelation). This page covers the specific claim that disclosure is *already happening*, bottom-up, through physical evidence.
