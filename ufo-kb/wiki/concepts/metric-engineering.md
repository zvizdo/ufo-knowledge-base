# Metric Engineering

A framework — most strongly associated with [[hal-puthoff]] — that treats the **metric of spacetime as an engineerable property** rather than a fixed background. If physical effects like gravitational acceleration, inertial mass, and the local speed of light are functions of vacuum-state parameters that can in principle be locally modified, then propulsion regimes that are "impossible" under flat-space general relativity become tractable engineering problems.

Concretely, metric engineering encompasses:
1. **Polarizable-vacuum (PV) model** — Puthoff's reformulation of GR in which gravity emerges from a position-dependent dielectric constant of the vacuum, parameterizable via electromagnetic boundary conditions.
2. **Warp-metric solutions** — including [[alcubierre-warp-drive]] and related metrics that, in principle, allow effective superluminal travel without local violations of relativity.
3. **Inertia-modification proposals** — including the **[[aharonov-bohm-effect]]** and Mach-Lorentz-style models invoked in the AATIP-era physics survey work.

The framework is the explicit theoretical bridge between mainstream GR and the propulsion regimes that AAWSAP/AATIP commissioned literature surveys on (the DIRDs).
