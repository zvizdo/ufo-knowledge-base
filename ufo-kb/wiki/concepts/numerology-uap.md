# Numerology in UAP-Adjacent Contexts

A pattern of specific numbers appearing across unconnected witness accounts, prophetic dream sequences, classified program dates, and mystical traditions — raising questions about whether these represent meaningful signal or observer-selection bias.

## The 747 Convergence (Larson)

[[ralph-moat-larson]] received the number **747** in an Armageddon dream at Fort Knox in 1976. The same number subsequently appeared at:

- **Roswell date**: The DOE hazing briefing used July 7, 1947 (7/4/7) as the date of the Roswell crash
- **Vela Incident**: The 1979 South Africa/Israel nuclear test was labeled internally at DOE as **"the 747 Incident"** — see [[1979-vela-incident]]
- **Larson's wife's dream**: Same night as Fort Knox download, Larson's wife independently dreamed number cubes showing 3-6-9-49

The sequence **3, 6, 9, 12, 27, 40, 49** (also received in Fort Knox dream) maps to:
- Tesla's obsession with 3, 6, 9 as fundamental
- Biblical numerology: 12 tribes, 40 days/nights/years, 49 = 7×7
- Religious eschatology numbers (Ezekiel, Daniel, Revelations all reference similar sets)

## Tesla's 3-6-9 System

Nikola Tesla stated he believed the numbers 3, 6, and 9 held the key to the universe. He reportedly structured his daily habits around multiples of three. The same numbers appear in Larson's Fort Knox download and his wife's simultaneous dream.

## Skeptical Frame

Numbers 3, 6, 9, 12, 40, 47, 49 appear extensively in Western religious tradition. The probability that a person immersed in biblical and Western culture would dream of these specific numbers and later identify "matches" is high under a confirmation-bias model. The Larson case is more compelling specifically because:
- Wife dreamed identical numbers independently and simultaneously without prior knowledge
- Historical corroboration of Odius Maximus / St. Maximos was entirely unknown to Larson
- The 747 / Roswell date convergence was not noticed by Larson until pointed out by Jesse
