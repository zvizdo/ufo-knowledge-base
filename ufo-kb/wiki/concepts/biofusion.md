# Biofusion

## What

Biofusion is the program name [[john-norsine]] published in 1999 for a closed-loop brain-machine interface: read EEG → compare to a target brain state ("brain print") → apply light, sound, or electromagnetic pulses to push the brain toward that state → re-measure. Per Norseen and his colleagues:

- Pilots could fly aircraft "by merely thinking" — no joystick latency.
- If the pilot processes a wrong heading, the system catches the divergence and **injects the correct value into the pilot's mind via electromagnetic waves** — Doug Pasternak's description.
- "Brain print" is an energy-dispersion pattern unique to a thought; like a fingerprint, "you can find one thought in a million" — Duncan Laurie relaying Norseen.

## Funding & cover

- Filed under **Navy contract N66001** at the Space and Naval Warfare Systems Center, San Diego (today the [[navy-information-warfare-center]]).
- Publicly labelled as an "aviation weather information systems project."
- Mirrored Lockheed Martin internal documents around 2000 list the same contract number against Norseen's adaptive-cockpit / Biofusion R&D.

## Lineage forward

- 2018-era — [[charles-chase]] and [[gerald-yonas]] (same company, Lockheed Martin) file the **"Cognitive Enhancement using Feedback"** patent describing the same control-loop architecture.
- 2018 — [[darpa-n3|DARPA N3]] openly funds bidirectional brain-machine interfaces for UAV control.
- 2020 — [[battelle-memorial-institute]]'s [[brainstorms-bci|BrainSTORMS]] reaches Phase 2 — nanoscale magneto-electric devices injected into the bloodstream.

## Why it matters for the KB

[[jake-barber]]'s claim that **black-program pilots without psionic ability cannot fly recovered ET craft**, but can do so via a brain-machine interface routed through a psionic asset's mind, has a documented R&D ancestor in this same company. [[OOr98r51Sj0]] argues that the public lineage Biofusion → Chase/Yonas patent → DARPA N3 → BrainSTORMS makes the [[mind-controlled-craft]] hypothesis less exotic than it sounds.

## Norseen's death

Shortly before a planned [[naval-war-college]] lecture in Newport, Norseen emailed colleagues a VIP-pass invitation. He signed off with the semiotic line: *"I RVed the upcoming lecture — John not heard nor seen."* Two weeks later he was found dead in a motel room. ([[duncan-laurie]] and [[doug-pasternak]] as witnesses.)
