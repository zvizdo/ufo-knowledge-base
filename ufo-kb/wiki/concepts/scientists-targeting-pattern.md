# Scientists Targeting Pattern

The recurring observation — documented across multiple eras and national contexts — that scientists and engineers working at the frontier of strategically sensitive domains are disproportionately represented among those who die under ambiguous circumstances, disappear without explanation, or are absorbed into classified programs and vanish from public discourse.

## The Core Claim

The people closest to the edges of what is known — those rewriting the rules of physics, mapping the sky for strategic threats, unlocking exotic materials or propulsion, or producing consciousness research that dissolves the matter-mind boundary — seem to become vulnerable in proportion to their proximity to that edge. Whether by design or structural accident, the most dangerous minds disappear.

> "You can steal documents or hack a server, but if you really want to shortcut the future, you steal the person who already solved the problem." — [[jesse-michaels]] ([[wlsQIbpEKSM]])

## Historical Precedents

**GEC-Marconi Deaths (1982–1990):** 25 British defense engineers and computer scientists working on SDI-related classified programs died under bizarre circumstances over 8 years. Rulings included suicide, accident, "sexual misadventure." No charges were ever filed; files were classified. ([[1982-gec-marconi-deaths]])

**Jacobo Grinberg (1994):** Mexican neurophysiologist disappeared after his most significant consciousness discovery; lab cleared; records erased; note left: *"If you understand the system, you disappear."*

**Itzhak Bentov (1979):** Consciousness researcher and Stargate-era figure died in AA Flight 191; parapsychology community treats his proximity to a complete consciousness theory as significant.

**Star Wars SDI Scientists (1982–1990):** See GEC-Marconi deaths above.

**Ning Li (2014/2021):** Superconductor/gravity researcher; email intercepted by West Point IP; went dark; car accident 2014; died 2021. ([[ning-lee]])

**John Norsine (2007):** Lockheed Martin neuroengineer; neuroweapons; claimed presence at the Allagash abduction site; died Sept 27, 2007. ([[john-norsine]])

**MH370 (2014):** 20 Freescale Semiconductor engineers with defense-relevant microchip expertise were among the MH370 passengers. (Circumstantial — no confirmation of targeted targeting.)

## Explanatory Frameworks

**Terrestrial / nation-state:**
- Foreign adversaries targeting US scientific human capital before major conflict: the transcript notes that Israeli military doctrine targets scientists first ("The first thing you do is kill your scientists")
- Domestic black-budget cleanup crews eliminating potential whistleblowers as classification regimes become stressed
- Commercial interests (energy, defense contractors) protecting proprietary technology

**Institutional / structural:**
- [[physics-lockdown]]: scientists who touch certain domains get classified into secrecy, not eliminated — they simply vanish from public view
- Coincidence: separate, unrelated events with no common thread beyond institutional proximity

**Speculative / NHI:**
- The [[threebody-problem-nhi]] framework: non-human intelligences track and suppress the human scientific frontier, as described by [[harold-malmgren]] and prefigured by Liu Cixin's novel
- [[harold-malmgren]]: NHI "were opposed to anything which threatened their control" and "track the frontier of human innovation"

## Key Structural Observation

Sensitivity domains where the pattern concentrates:
- Nuclear and plasma physics (fusion)
- Anti-gravity and exotic propulsion
- Consciousness research at the border of remote viewing / NHI communication
- Exotic materials science (especially at Wright-Patterson AFRL)
- Astronomical observation systems with UAP detection potential
