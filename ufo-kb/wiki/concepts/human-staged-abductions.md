# Human-Staged Abductions

[[steven-greer]]'s claim, stated as 100% certainty in [[areO7Mej44E]], that so-called "alien abductions" are not NHI encounters but covert human operations — black-program personnel and actors made to look like aliens, using man-made electrogravitic craft to perform staged contact.

## Core Assertion

> *"Alien abductions are covert human programs, by the way, 100%. That's the other big secret nobody knows."*

Greer adds that he has personally met individuals from "abduction squads" — people who participated in these staged operations and "feel terrible later in life."

## Mechanism

By 1961 (the [[1961-betty-barney-hill-abduction]]), Greer claims the US program already had:
- Fully operational man-made electrogravitic craft
- Personnel trained to dress and behave as alien beings ("stagecraft")
- The organizational capacity to select targets and perform abduction scenarios

The Betty and Barney Hill case, Greer argues, is "very likely" a human-staged operation because "the mechanism of how it happened would not be consistent with interstellar transdimensional technologies."

## Channeling Through Researchers

Greer claims that the abduction cases studied by [[john-mack]] and [[bud-hopkins]] were systematically funneled to those researchers — cases selected precisely because they would be interpreted as evidence of genuine NHI abduction. Neither Mack nor Hopkins knew this. In this framing:

- Hopkins and Mack were conducting sincere research on fraudulent data
- Their conclusions reinforced the "alien threat" narrative that served black-program interests
- The academic credibility of Harvard psychiatry ([[john-mack]]) gave the human-staged operations cover

## Relationship to Real NHI Contact

Greer explicitly distinguishes this claim from a blanket denial of NHI contact. He insists his own 1973 experience (see [[steven-greer]]) was genuine NHI contact — "beautiful, benign, closer to an angel than a demon." His position is that real NHI contact exists *and* that staged human abductions exploit the broader cultural framework of NHI belief.

## > ⚠ Conflict:
This claim directly contradicts the mainstream reading of the abduction literature — Hopkins, Mack, and the extensive case records they produced — as reflecting genuine NHI encounters. See [[abduction-phenomena]] for the broader category. The claim is unverified; Greer provides no documentation or named witnesses to the "abduction squads."
