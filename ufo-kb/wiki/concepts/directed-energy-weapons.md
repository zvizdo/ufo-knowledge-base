# Directed-Energy Weapons

Weapon systems delivering operational effect through **electromagnetic or particle-beam energy** rather than kinetic mass. Categories include high-power microwave (HPM), high-energy laser (HEL), particle-beam, and niche pulsed-RF systems.

In UAP discourse, DEW shows up as the primary **mundane attribution candidate** for several otherwise-anomalous classes of report:
1. **[[2016-havana-syndrome]]** — pulsed RF / microwave attribution debate; favored hypothesis of [[eric-haseltine]] and [[chris-gilbert]] for at least a subset of cases.
2. **[[voice-of-god-technology]]** — microwave-hearing / Frey effect as a deniable IC-class capability.
3. **[[microwave-hearing-effect]]** — the underlying physiological mechanism.
4. **Anomalous physiological signatures** of close-encounter UAP witnesses (radiation burns, EM-effect-class skin lesions) — Cash-Landrum-style cases.

The DEW frame is also where **Russian-attribution debates** and **friendly-DEW-deception** scenarios converge.
