# Multiple Alien Groups

The claim that humanity is not dealing with a single alien civilization but with **multiple distinct non-human intelligence groups** simultaneously present. [[lue-elizondo]] advances this in [[1f16VvXaSSE]] as a significant departure from the standard single-species assumption in UAP discourse.

## Evidence Base

The primary empirical basis is **craft morphological diversity**. Reported and documented UAP craft types include:
- [[tic-tac]] — white, oblong, featureless; best-documented type
- Black Cube — distinct geometry suggesting a different design logic
- Jellyfish — organic-appearing, different propulsion signature
- Red triangle — triangular configuration; echoes "Black Triangle" reports elsewhere

If all UAPs were products of a single non-human civilization, one might expect greater design coherence. The radical diversity of observed morphologies — combined with apparent differences in behavior, interaction patterns, and associated phenomena — has led researchers like Elizondo to hypothesize multiple groups.

## Supporting Threads

- [[jacques-vallee]]'s classification system implicitly allows for diverse origin hypotheses; his Vallée classification is one framework for distinguishing types
- Abduction phenomenology (the contactee/experiencer literature) describes radically different entity types — Greys, Nordics, Mantids, Reptilians — which some researchers interpret as evidence of multiple groups rather than a single species
- The diversity of reported effects (technological interference, biological effects, apparent telepathic communication, poltergeist activity) may be more consistent with multiple agents than a single actor
- [[richard-barth]]'s September 1964 Vandenberg CE3 adds a **direct NHI statement** confirming multiple species: the entity explicitly communicated "not every species — there were more than one species — and they weren't all as benevolent as they were." Further: if push came to shove, there could be conflict over who controls the situation once missiles are disabled. This is the only known case in the KB where the multiple-groups thesis is attributed to a direct NHI communication rather than researcher inference.

## Counterclaims

- The morphological diversity may reflect one civilization's different craft for different purposes (like how humans have fighter jets, helicopters, and submarines)
- Diversity in reported experiences may reflect perceptual and psychological variation in human witnesses rather than genuine ontological diversity in the visitors
- "Multiple groups" narratives are particularly resistant to falsification, which is a methodological concern

## Charles Hall's Three-Species Account (QgxjtDS2sIQ)

[[charles-hall]]'s account provides the KB's richest firsthand taxonomy of multiple groups, based on 26 months of direct observation at [[indian-springs]]:

| Group | Arrival | Technology | Earth relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[[small-grays]]** | Before last ice age | Advanced FTL; moon base | Farmed Nevada valleys; crashed at Roswell |
| **[[tall-whites]]** | Ice age | Most advanced FTL | Pit stop; foreign embassy base at Indian Springs |
| **[[nordics-norwegians]]** | End of last ice age | Just barely FTL | Diplomatic; building craft with US government in 1965 |

Key differentiators: the Tall Whites are the most technologically advanced; the Grays the earliest arrivals; the Nordics the least capable technologically. All three are fully biological plant-eaters. Hall observes the Tall Whites use Earth as a "pit stop" in a large void in space — while the Grays (with their closer home planet and moon base) operate more flexibly.

Hall adds a specific craft-physics differentiator: Tall White deep spacecraft require the full-moon orbital window for safe Earth approach; Grays do not because they have a moon base; Nordics cannot reliably navigate and must land "at any price."

## Pais's Multi-Tech-Tree Framework (8TYMQOUDQBo)

[[salvatore-pais]] provides a more nuanced framing than morphological diversity alone. He explicitly states:

> "There's man-made tech and there's ET tech. You can tell them apart."

And goes further: within ET tech, there may be multiple branches — some craft are "approachably nuts-and-bolts" (a few hundred years more advanced than us, using physics recognizable in principle) while others are "far more exotic — when you open them up, you see nothing familiar, nothing recognizable."

This creates a **three-tier model**: (1) classified human craft (man-made using recovered or independently derived physics), (2) ET craft that operates on comprehensible physics but with more advanced engineering, and (3) ET craft whose manufacturing and operation are entirely beyond current human understanding (consistent with Planck-scale spacetime manipulation).

Pais also endorses the good/bad NHI factions thesis, specifically citing [[robert-hastings]]'s nuclear-UAP research: "I pray for good factions — without good factions we have no chance in hell." He supports [[peter-thiel]]'s binary-forcing-function argument as a possible physical basis for why NHI civilizations might self-sort into good/bad categories.

## Steve Coburn's Gray Alliance Account (iPxbILOMQ08)

[[steve-coburn]] provides a rare experiencer account with specific structural detail about the gray NHI collective:

- **Seven distinct species** bound by **treaty**, all originating from planets within approximately **100 light years** of Earth
- Collectively called the "gray alliance" — though individual species differ
- Alliance symbol: **three orange circles arranged in an equilateral triangle** (displayed on uniform patches and craft walls)
- Alternate uniform marking: snake over a triangle
- Primary uniforms: cobalt blue

The beings told Coburn that Earth is one of only **4–5 planets in the galaxy** with such a dense, multi-layered biosphere; they want to preserve it because:
1. They mine Earth and the Moon for resources
2. They collect biological samples
3. There are structures in the "ether" that contribute to life on this planet that could be disrupted by nuclear events

On intra-group politics: Coburn describes them as "not scum-sucking evil either" but "mainly out for themselves." They want humanity to mature and join the Galactic Federation eventually, but in the meantime they don't care about individual suffering.

> ⚠ **Compare with Charles Hall's three-species account**: Hall observes Tall Whites, Small Grays, and Nordics as three distinct civilizations with very different technology levels. Coburn's seven-species treaty alliance among "similar aliens" may overlap with this taxonomy or describe a subset — the relationship is unresolved. If the Tall Whites are excluded from the gray alliance (given their different technology level and apparent non-gray nature), Hall's model and Coburn's could be compatible.
