# UAP Summoning Protocols

## Summary

A cluster of claimed methodologies for **actively attracting UAPs** rather than passively waiting for sightings. The spectrum runs from civilian consciousness-based protocols (CE-5) to operationally framed programs connected to classified intelligence contexts ([[jake-barber]] / [[sky-watchers]]). [[jesse-michaels]] discusses these in [[SZBI85yvV5A]] in the context of upcoming field work.

## Sky Watchers: Dog Whistle Frequency

The **Sky Watchers** program ([[sky-watchers]], founded by [[jake-barber]]) operates a mobile sensor unit that can be deployed anywhere. Their key claimed capability:

- A specific **acoustic/electromagnetic frequency** they call a "**dog whistle**" — because it is outside normal human auditory/sensory range
- This frequency reportedly **attracts UAPs with 100% effectiveness** according to Jesse's characterization
- The sensor array detects anomalous returns when UAPs respond
- Operations have been conducted in **Palmdale, California** and the **Finley Mountain Range in West Texas**
- Critique: the West Texas location is near "sensitive sites," raising the possibility that readings reflect government spoofing or proximity-induced signals from classified test programs

Jesse's summary: "They claim to have this thing called a dog whistle, which is this frequency that attracts UFOs like 100% of the time and they have sensors and they like pick up a lot of weird stuff."

## Neuritators: Psionic Summoning

Sky Watchers also deploys what they call **"neuritators"** — individuals with psionic capabilities (similar to [[p3-psionic-assets]] in the classified program context) who **attempt to summon UAPs through consciousness interface**.

This frames UAP attraction as a consciousness phenomenon rather than a purely physical/technological one — consistent with [[psionics-uap-interface]] and Jake Barber's own firsthand account of locking consciousness onto a craft during retrieval.

## The Egg (9th Class of UAP)

Sky Watchers' primary stated goal is to summon **"the egg"** — the egg-shaped craft that [[jake-barber]] claims to have retrieved in the mid-2000s. Jesse notes:

- Jake Jo (a predecessor Sky Watcher) had established 8 classes of UAP over ~4 years of observation
- Barber joined and they identified **the egg as a 9th class**
- The goal is to summon the egg **publicly and on camera**
- Jesse: "if they do that, that's [one of the most significant things ever]"
- Counterpoint from Barber: the US government may not prioritize the egg because "they might have too many." Jesse: "What a crazy thing to say."

## CE-5 (Civilian Parallel)

[[steven-greer]]'s CE-5 (Close Encounters of the Fifth Kind) initiative represents the civilian consciousness-based parallel: trained meditators attempt to attract UAPs through focused intention and specific protocols. Greer connected Barber and [[michael-herrera]] — establishing a direct institutional bridge between CE-5 civilian practice and the classified-program-defector community.

See also [[p3-psionic-assets]] for the classified program that appears to parallel CE-5 methodology.

## The Spoofing Critique

Jesse notes the key credibility challenge for Sky Watchers' dog-whistle frequency: proximity to classified test sites means anomalous sensor returns could be:
1. Genuine UAP responses
2. **Classified government test programs** deliberately generating returns near active monitoring groups (spoofing)
3. Sensor noise or prosaic explanation misinterpreted

This parallels the general challenge in UAP research of distinguishing genuine NHI phenomena from classified human aerospace tests — see [[uap-camera-evasion-problem]].

## Open Questions

- What is the physical mechanism of the dog-whistle frequency — electromagnetic, acoustic, or something else?
- Has the sensor array at Palmdale or Finley produced reproducible readings under controlled conditions?
- Is the neuritator protocol the same mechanism as CE-5, or are there operational differences that reflect classified vs. civilian training lineages?
- If the government has "too many" egg UAPs, why has none been publicly disclosed or leaked?

## Two Formally Classified Summoning Classes (dnnpyNuPdXs)

[[jake-barber]] confirms in [[dnnpyNuPdXs]] that two classes of summoning are "proven to work" within the program and continue to be used:

1. **Machine-based**: Signal and energy systems tuned to attract/elicit UAP response. These are technological devices, not human operators.
2. **Man-based**: Psionic operators using the consciousness field as the medium of contact.

Barber also describes the **post-program egg demonstration (August)**: after reorganizing under [[arrow-organization|Arrow]], the team was able to "make the egg show up" in broad daylight — it flew past in dead silence at ~10,000 mph. This is the first public claim of a successful, externally witnessed, post-program UAP elicitation demonstration.

The two-class taxonomy aligns with [[sky-watchers]]' public methodology: dog-whistle frequency devices (machine-based) and neuritator operators (man-based). The SZBI85yvV5A source gives the deployment details (Palmdale, West Texas); this source gives the programmatic confirmation that both classes are operationally validated.

## Controlled-Absence Test (y_8IKKcTntQ — Valor)

[[james-valor]] reports the strongest causal evidence for the dog-whistle yet published. In January 2025, the full Skywatcher sensor array was deployed to an operational site *without* activating the dog-whistle frequency. Result: **zero UAP detections** over the entire session. Standard operations with the dog-whistle active routinely produce daily UAP appearances. This is the closest approach to a controlled experiment available in the civilian UAP-summoning literature — same site, same sensors, one variable changed.

Valor also describes the dog-whistle in combined terms with psionic assets: the two systems appear to be synergistic rather than independent. The psionic team (Jordan) was not deployed during the January 2025 control run, so the relative contribution of each cannot be isolated.

## February 2025 Operational Outcomes

The February 2025 "conflict in the sky" event produced the most documented summoning-to-response chain to date:

- Jordan's psionic prediction of "a conflict in the sky with multiple classes" — delivered before event — confirmed exactly
- Three distinct UAP classes appeared simultaneously: Class 7 (jellyfish), Class 2 (Tic Tac), Manta Ray variant
- **Class 7 prior behavior (days before)**: Craft had been systematically approaching and measuring the team's radar beam parameters for 4-5 days — probing at the edge of active telemetry, flying through the radar bubble. Suggests reciprocal awareness: the UAPs were also studying Skywatcher's detection capabilities
- **Helicopter immobilization**: [[jake-barber]] launched to intercept Class 2; helicopter froze in place ~half mile from target — engines running, blades turning, no translational movement in any direction. Duration and mechanism unknown
- **Class 7 EM interference**: Two Class 7s caused continuous radar jamming for 2+ hours simultaneously with the Tic Tac pursuit

This event is the most operationally complex multi-class UAP engagement in the public record. See [[y_8IKKcTntQ]] for full account.

## UAP Responses and Intent-Reading

Valor adds several new data points to the intent-reading hypothesis:

- **Class 2 (Tic Tac)**: Departed the instant Valor *began running* toward the helicopter — before any overt threat action. Suggests detection of intent before behavioral expression
- **Class 7 probing behavior**: Days of systematic radar parameter mapping before the February engagement — active reconnaissance of Skywatcher's capabilities before the main event
- **2003 Class 6 energy weapon incident**: Three separate, unconnected electronic systems disabled simultaneously before Valor's team completed turning an energy-weapon antenna toward the craft. Pre-emptive response to intent, not action

If accurate, these suggest UAP systems are not merely reactive to physical behavior but are monitoring operator intent — consistent with the consciousness-interface model in [[psionics-uap-interface]].
