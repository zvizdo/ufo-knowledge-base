# UAP Testimonial Evidence

The body of firsthand and secondhand testimony from credentialed insiders — military officers, intelligence officials, defense contractors, scientists — asserting direct or derivative knowledge of:
- Crashed non-human craft and material recovery programs
- Non-human intelligences observed or communicated with
- Classified reverse-engineering efforts
- Programs operating outside normal congressional oversight

Distinguished from the general UFO sighting literature by the access level and institutional credentials of the witnesses. The epistemic challenge: insider testimony is difficult to corroborate by definition (the programs being described are compartmented), yet the witnesses are more credible than random observers.

## Key Instances

- David Grusch: 40+ firsthand witnesses with national security credentials
- Karl Nell: uniformed military confirmation of Grusch's core claims
- Lue Elizondo: AATIP director testimony on UAP threat assessment
- Eric Weinstein: conversion from skeptic based on Pentagon report access
