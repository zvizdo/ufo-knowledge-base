# Directed DMT World

[[andrew-gallimore]]'s core model for how DMT entities operate. Standard lay framing of the DMT experience: you "break through" into another world and find beings there. Gallimore's inversion: **you don't break through into the DMT world. The DMT world breaks through into you.**

## The Mechanism

Perception in the normal waking state works not by direct access to an external world but by the brain continuously constructing a **world model** — a dynamic simulation of what the environment is — tested against sensory inputs. This is not controversial neuroscience; it is the predictive processing model.

In the DMT state:
- Recent neuroimaging data (Gallimore cites as "very recent") shows DMT makes the cortex **dramatically more sensitive to perturbation** by external inputs — it becomes "primed" to be commandeered
- External intelligences — discarnate, non-physical, without physical form that could be represented visually in 3D — **gain access to the world-modeling machinery**
- They direct what the world model produces: the user does not see "the DMT world," they see what the entities are **directing them to see** in their own brain

The brain is the stage. The entities are the directors and performers.

## Why This Is Not Standard "Hallucination"

The hallucination model says: the brain generates exotic imagery from its own resources (memory, pattern generation, etc.). Gallimore argues this fails because:

1. The imagery is **impossible** in the specific mathematical sense: tesseracts visible in their true form, higher-dimensional objects with more degrees of freedom than 3D space can contain. The brain cannot construct these because it has no experience of them and no models of them.
2. The experience is **not random exotic imagery** but consistently populated with specific, purposive, structured entities that are clearly doing something — not noise
3. Entities exhibit **superhuman computational capability** (see [[andres-gomez-emilsson]] four-color theorem): the "imagery" is performing mathematical operations beyond what the brain itself could produce

## Extension of the Transmission Model

[[aldous-huxley]]'s reducing-valve model ([[transmission-theory-consciousness]]): the brain filters an impersonal consciousness field; psychedelics reduce the filter. Gallimore's extension: the filter is not just reduced — specific agents *exploit* the vulnerability. The signal is not passive; it is **intentional and directed**.

[[stanislav-grof]] anticipated this: he argued that if non-physical discarnate intelligences exist everywhere, they would interact via the brain, not by materializing. Gallimore explicitly cites Grof as the precedent.

## Implications

- The user has limited control: attempting to control the experience backfires (trying to dictate is "when bad experiences happen")
- Surrender is required — but so is preparation and prior training to make good use of the access
- The proposed DMTX research program (send trained specialists in) is designed precisely around the directed-world model: entities will perform and demonstrate their intelligence spontaneously; observers need to be equipped to recognize and document what they observe
