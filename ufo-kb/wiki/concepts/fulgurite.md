# Fulgurite

Fossilized lightning — glassy mineral structure formed when high-voltage electric discharge passes through silica or iron-bearing minerals; fulgurites found inside sealed iron ore veins beneath Giza are cited as evidence of ancient high-voltage current distribution through the bedrock.

A fulgurite is the natural glass produced when a high-voltage electric discharge (lightning, or in artificial cases industrial current) fuses silica or iron-bearing minerals along its path. [[jeffrey-drum]]'s ore samples from the iron veins beneath the Giza plateau allegedly contain fulgurites within sealed (non-exposed-to-surface) layers — interpreted as evidence that high-voltage current was distributed through the iron-vein network as part of the alleged [[pyramid-purpose-debate|industrial pyramid complex]].
