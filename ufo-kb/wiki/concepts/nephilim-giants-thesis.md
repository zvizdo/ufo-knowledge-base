# Nephilim / Giants Thesis

The claim that the biblical Nephilim — giant humanoid offspring of the Watchers (angelic beings) and human women, described in Genesis 6 and the Book of Enoch — were physically real, possessed advanced technology, left skeletal and architectural remains worldwide, and may have had surviving descendants or present-day analogues.

## Textual Basis

The primary source is **[[book-of-enoch]]** (specifically the *Book of the Watchers*, c. 300 BC compilation, older oral tradition claimed), which expands the cryptic Genesis 6 passage ("sons of God saw the daughters of men were beautiful...") into a full narrative:

- 200 Watchers bound themselves by oath on Mount Hermon before descending to mate with human women
- They taught humans forbidden knowledge (metallurgy, astronomy, sorcery, cosmetics)
- Their offspring — the Nephilim — grew to enormous size, consumed all resources, then devoured mankind
- God dispatched loyal angels to bind the Watchers; incited the giants to fratricidal war; then sent the Flood to cleanse the earth
- The disembodied spirits of the slain giants became the demons of the New Testament

Corroborating texts include the Dead Sea Scrolls (*Book of Giants*, *Book of Jubilees*, *Genesis Apocryphon*) and the Septuagint's rendering of Genesis 6 ("angels of God" rather than "sons of God").

The thesis also draws on ubiquitous cross-cultural traditions of giants: Gilgamesh (described as a giant), Greek Titans and demigods, Egyptian legends, Polynesian accounts, Aztec and Inca records, and North American indigenous traditions of six-fingered red-haired giants.

## Physical Evidence Claims

### Sardinian Nuragic Culture
- [[tim-alberino]] visited Sardinia multiple times and interviewed dozens of elderly witnesses who excavated or witnessed giant skeletal remains (≥9 ft) in the 1930s–1980s
- Sites include the Church of Sant'Anastasia excavation (c. 1978–1981); witnesses described entire skeletons, preserved bodies with sinew, giant goblets and jewelry
- Remains systematically removed by unknown parties (university officials, government representatives) with witnesses instructed not to photograph or speak about them
- The nuragic "tombs of the giants" (Sardinia) are shaped like wombs and were used for **incubation rites** — young initiates sealed inside to commune with spirits of buried giants
- Alberino argues the nuragic people were Canaanites displaced by Joshua's conquest; a Tunisian column inscription allegedly read "We are the Canaanites who fled before the face of Joshua son of Nun"

### Smithsonian Records / North America
- Smithsonian Institution annual reports from the 19th–early 20th century documented unusual large skeletal remains from burial mounds in America
- Abraham Lincoln's Niagara Falls speech referenced "that ancient species of giants whose bones fill the mounds of America"
- Alberino contends these remains were subsequently removed and suppressed; connects this to the same occult elite that suppresses giant finds globally

### Kandahar, Afghanistan
- [[2002-kandahar-giant]] — the most contemporary alleged encounter; a 10–12 ft, ~1,100 lb giant with six fingers/toes and red hair was reportedly killed by US military and airlifted from Bagram (c. 2003 or 2005)
- Two independent military witnesses; reportedly ended up at [[wright-patterson]]

## Suppression Hypothesis

Alberino's explanation for why giant evidence is suppressed above even UFO crash-retrieval secrecy:

The cover-up is driven primarily by **occult interests** rather than scientific or theological concerns. Specifically:
- Dead Nephilim spirits (demons) can reportedly be contacted through necromantic practices
- Ancient Canaanite rites (Baal worship, child sacrifice, giant-cult incubation) were used to harness demonic power or recover antediluvian knowledge from the giants
- A contemporary occult elite (described as "Baal-worshipping" and "Epstein-adjacent") maintains possession of giant remains to continue these practices and extract technological/esoteric knowledge
- This explains why government contacts who will freely discuss UFOs become visibly alarmed when giants are mentioned

## Theological Implications

- Directly corroborates the supernatural interpretation of Genesis 6 (as opposed to the "Sethite theory" adopted by Augustine)
- Confirms the Book of Enoch's historicity, which in turn deepens the biblical case for Jesus's use of "Son of Man" from the *Parables of Enoch*
- [[michael-heiser]]'s scholarly work (*Reversing Hermon*, *The Unseen Realm*) provides academic corroboration from a mainstream evangelical perspective
- For [[tim-alberino]], living giants and their spirits are part of a continuous story culminating in Armageddon
