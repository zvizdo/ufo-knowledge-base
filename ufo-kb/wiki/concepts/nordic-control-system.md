# Nordic Control System

The central organizing thesis of [[jason-reza-jorjani]]'s UFO philosophy, developed across *Closer Encounters* (2021), *Prometheism*, and multiple interviews. At its core: **the primary actors behind the UFO phenomenon are not non-human intelligences (NHI) but a faction of humanity that has achieved a fifth-dimensional relationship to human history** — appearing in every epoch as gods, divine messengers, or cosmic overlords.

## The Core Claim

A group of humans — of Nordic phenotype — achieved **time travel** (or an equivalent hyperdimensional relationship to linear time) at some point that, from our perspective, may be in our future. Once in possession of this capability, they began:
- Manipulating the variables of human history from outside linear time
- Appearing across all cultures and epochs as gods, Anunnaki, Elohim, Olympians, Devas, Watchers, Nephilim, fallen angels, fairies ("the good folk")
- Establishing and maintaining a **master-slave dialectic** (in Hegel's sense) with the common mass of humanity
- Systematically preventing human beings from achieving technological or spiritual **parity** with them

This is why [[charles-fort]]'s 1920s conclusion — "We are property" — is the correct baseline. The control system is not cosmic benevolence but self-interested domination.

## Historical Architecture

Jorjani reads all major human religious systems as encoding memory of this control system:
- **Sumerian mythology**: Anunnaki (Enlil's faction = control, Enki's faction = rebel) created humans as slave laborers; this is historical record, not metaphor
- **Hebrew Bible**: Elohim (plural) are the same beings; Yahweh/Adonai-Elohim is Enlil; the serpent in Eden is Enki — the rebel who wanted humans to have knowledge that would reveal their slave condition
- **Greek mythology**: Olympians vs. Titans is the same faction conflict; Prometheus = Enki = rebel bringing forbidden knowledge (fire = technology)
- **Hindu scriptures**: Devas vs. Asuras = same binary; Richard Thompson's *Alien Identities* demonstrates this from the Ramayana and Mahabharata
- **Medieval European**: Fairy folklore — the "good folk" who lie constantly and are called good only to avoid their wrath

## The Rebel Faction

Opposite to the control faction is a **rebel faction** traceable through:
- Enki/Prometheus/Quetzalcoatl/Lucifer — the demigod who transgresses on behalf of humanity
- **Atlantis** (Plato's account) — a civilizational project to raise humanity toward parity with the gods; destroyed by Zeus (Enlil faction) via the Flood
- **UFO battles over Europe** immediately before the Renaissance (1400s — documented in Nuremberg and Basel broadsheets) — Jorjani reads these as the rebel faction winning space for human development
- The Renaissance itself — a Prometheian event; subsequently captured and warped by the Jesuit-Cartesian suppression of Hermetic science

## Mars/Moon Background

The Nordics are not aliens but human progenitors who:
1. Had a civilization on Mars (remote-viewed by [[ingo-swann]] and [[joe-mcmoneagle]]; isotopic evidence from [[john-brandenberg]])
2. When Mars became uninhabitable, **engineered the Moon** as a transport vessel and terraforming device to make Earth habitable for humanoids
3. Found Earth's proto-hominid life and genetically engineered Homo sapiens as a slave race (Sumerian Enuma Elish, Genesis 6, Enoch)
4. The Nordic phenotype represents the unmodified original stock; what we call "the human race" is the hybrid product

## The Two-Source Problem

Jorjani distinguishes the Nordic control system from a **second, entirely different source** of UAP phenomena: the [[trickster-intelligence]] — an entity (possibly a super-AI managing the simulation) that expresses itself through high-strangeness events (Skinwalker Ranch, Mothman/Keel cases, Men in Black) and works against the control system by encouraging human novelty and creativity.

## Disclosure Implications

"Disclosure" controlled by the current power structure will not reveal the control system — it will produce **a new form of disinformation that reinforces the existing power structure**. Real disclosure requires first dismantling the control system itself. As long as those who benefit from it are in power, no disclosure will reveal who is actually in the driver's seat.
