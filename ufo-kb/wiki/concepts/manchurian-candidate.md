# Manchurian Candidate

The Manchurian Candidate is the concept of a person behaviorally modified — through hypnosis, trauma, pharmacology, brain electrodes, or combinations thereof — to execute specific actions on command, typically without conscious awareness or memory.

The term comes from the 1959 novel *The Manchurian Candidate* by Richard Condon (filmed 1962 with Frank Sinatra), which depicted a Korean War POW programmed by Chinese/Soviet intelligence to become an assassin. The plot was not invented from whole cloth: CIA documents describe exactly this scenario being developed against US adversaries before the Korean War.

## Mechanism

The core mechanism, consistent across all documented cases:

1. **Trauma or pharmacological dissociation** creates a foundation (natural DID or induced)
2. **Conditioning** (hypnosis, drugs, electrode stimulation, repetition) installs specific behaviors in an alternate identity
3. **A trigger** (verbal phrase, object, text) activates the alternate identity
4. The programmed identity executes the assigned task
5. The trigger is removed or the session ends; the primary identity has no memory
