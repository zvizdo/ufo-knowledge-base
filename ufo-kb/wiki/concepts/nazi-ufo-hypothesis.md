# Nazi UFO Hypothesis

The multi-part thesis — advanced most comprehensively by [[jason-reza-jorjani]] in [[ABw7EsWh2Us]] and by [[joseph-farrell]] in his *Saucer Swastikas* series — that:

1. **The 1933 Magenta crash** provided a non-human technological template that Nazi SS scientists reverse-engineered
2. **Die Glocke** (The Bell) was the result: an electrogravidic antigravity device combined with a saucer airframe to produce a working flying saucer
3. **Saucer-shaped craft** were observed launching from Spain toward America in 1946–47 — Nazi test flights
4. **Roswell (1947) was a Nazi psychological operation** — not an alien crash — using this reverse-engineered technology
5. **The CIA was formed** in September 1947 to absorb the Nazi intelligence apparatus ([[odessa]] / [[reinhard-gehlen]]'s network) that had managed these programs
6. **US UFO secrecy** is structurally continuous with Nazi secrecy — the same personnel, networks, and institutional culture, now operating under American cover

## Relationship to the Extraterrestrial Hypothesis

This hypothesis is **partially competing, partially compatible** with the extraterrestrial hypothesis (ETH):

- **Competing version** (Jorjani's Roswell claim): "I'm not suggesting that all these crashes have to do with Nazi psychological operations, but the particular incident at Roswell, I think did." — This makes Roswell human-made, not ET.
- **Compatible version** (Magenta/general): The Magenta crash was *genuinely non-human*; the Nazis *reverse-engineered* non-human technology; subsequent "saucer" observations may be either ET originals or Nazi copies.

The most conservative reading: some UAP events are Nazi/human reverse-engineered technology, others are non-human originals. Attributing all crashes to Nazis is overclaiming; attributing all to ET ignores documented Nazi advanced aviation.
