# Autistic Savant Telepathy

The claim that non-verbal autistic children and adults demonstrate apparent telepathic abilities — access to information held in another person's mind, with no normal sensory pathway — under controlled experimental conditions. The phenomenon appears linked to, but distinct from, the broader savant-abilities profile: while savants are known for prodigious memory and pattern recognition, this claim extends to real-time access to specific, externally generated information (random numbers, images, objects) held in a facilitator's mind.

## Primary Evidence

### Diane Powell's Research
[[diane-powell]], Harvard-trained neuroscientist, conducted the foundational research: structured experiments with non-verbal autistic subjects and their caregivers, with systematic controls for sensory cueing. Documented hit rates of **90%+ across multiple subjects** — a level she describes as statistically decisive. The research was published in scientific literature and preceded the [[telepathy-tapes]] documentary.

### Telepathy Tapes — Ky Dickens' Controlled Testing
[[ky-dickens]] extended Powell's work with independent filming and testing:
- **Mia (Glendale, CA)**: Non-verbal girl from Mexico. Tested in a rented house with mirrors removed, cameras throughout, all materials controlled by Dickens. Physical divider and blindfold separated Mia from her mother. Results: **100% accuracy** across trials involving random number generators, random image generators, flash cards, and colored balls.
- **John Paul**: Non-verbal child who correctly identified items in his mother Libby's mind during flash card and object trials.
- **Houston**: Coined "Talk on the Hill." Demonstrated reception of specific thoughts placed in his head by his wife, and correctly reproduced the content of a text message he had never seen.

### Josiah Case — *[[josiahs-fire]]*
Non-verbal autistic child whose typed communications (via supported typing) contained specific, verifiable, and theologically sophisticated content beyond his documented experiential history. Independent case not controlled by Powell or Dickens, but phenomenologically consistent.

## Mechanism Hypotheses

1. **Non-local consciousness field** — consciousness is not brain-localized; autistic individuals with unusual brain organization may have reduced filtering of the shared field, enabling direct access to others' mental states. See [[consciousness-non-local]].
2. **Neural architecture** — [[garry-nolan]]'s neurodensity research suggests some individuals have unusual neural density in executive-function regions; autistic savants may have a distinct neural architecture that facilitates non-conventional information access. Studies show autistic individuals have measurably different caudate nucleus/putamen structure — the same regions [[diane-powell]] identifies as the "first encoder" of a hypothetical scalar signal.
3. **Reduced "default-mode network" filtering** — speculative: if the default-mode network functions partly to suppress non-local information access, non-verbal autism might involve reduced suppression.
4. **Scalar wave transmission** — [[constantine-meyl]]'s helicoidal wave theory proposes that scalar waves derived from Maxwell's full equations couple with DNA structure. If biologically real, this provides a physical medium for non-local information transfer that would explain the 90%+ hit rates. [[diane-powell]] identifies [[scalar-waves]] as a plausible candidate mechanism in [[0qlppHc3-gg]].
5. **Transmission theory** — [[transmission-theory-consciousness]] (William James / Aldous Huxley): the brain filters rather than generates consciousness. Autistic children with unusual neural architecture may have reduced filtering, accessing the shared field more cleanly. [[kim-peek]]'s case — memorizing 12,000 books without a corpus callosum — is the most extreme evidence that standard localisationism cannot account for this class of ability.

## Historical Precedent

The [[society-of-psychical-research]] (founded 1882 by [[frederick-myers]] and [[william-james]]'s network) conducted the first systematic experiments on telepathy. The methodology Powell uses is continuous with this 150-year tradition. [[john-mack]] and [[ian-stevenson]] represent parallel cases of credentialed researchers documenting anomalous phenomena despite institutional resistance.

[[andrija-puharich]] ran the most direct historical precursor: in the 1960s–70s, he gathered children with high psychic/telepathic abilities in upstate New York for controlled research. The research was partly CIA-funded, which creates uncertainty about its purpose and fate, but phenomenologically it is the immediate precedent for the *Telepathy Tapes* methodology. Puharich also documented bone-induction radio reception — patients receiving radio signals through metallic dental work — which is an analog for the [[transmission-theory-consciousness]] model. Jesse Michaels directly asks Dickens to draw the Puharich parallel in [[0qlppHc3-gg]].

## Relationship to NHI Telepathy

[[nhi-telepathic-communication]] documents a separate but structurally parallel phenomenon: individuals transmitting or receiving information via non-acoustic, non-electromagnetic channels in the context of UAP encounters or materials. Both streams raise the same mechanistic question — what is the channel? — and suggest that human capacity for non-local information access may be more widespread than recognized.
