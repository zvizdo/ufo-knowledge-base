# Gravity–Electromagnetism Unification

The problem of reconciling **General Relativity (GR)** — Einstein's geometric theory of gravity — with **quantum field theory** (of which electromagnetism is one sector) remains the central unsolved problem in theoretical physics. No consistent quantum theory of gravity exists.

## Relevance to UAP

[[jesse-michaels]] frames this explicitly: solving GR + QM incompatibility would unlock the physics underlying exotic propulsion. The argument structure:

1. UAP exhibit propulsion behavior (instantaneous acceleration, no visible thrust, inertia-less turns) that cannot be explained by conventional physics
2. The only candidate physics that could explain such behavior involves gravity manipulation or spacetime metric engineering
3. A unified theory of gravity and electromagnetism would provide the theoretical framework for such manipulation
4. [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s empirical work on the [[biefeld-brown-effect]] — demonstrated in vacuum — is the closest existing empirical anchor

## Historical Attempts

- **Einstein** himself spent the last 30 years of his life attempting a unified field theory — failed
- **[[hal-puthoff]]**'s extended electrodynamics restores scalar fields deleted from Maxwell's equations — possibly relevant
- **[[extended-electrodynamics]]** as the framework Brown's anonymous Navy scientist describes

## Connection to UAP Programs

[[doe-secret-science-nexus]]: the "born secret" provision of the Atomic Energy Act means any privately funded physics that touches nuclear processes is automatically classified — potentially capturing breakthrough gravity-EM work.
