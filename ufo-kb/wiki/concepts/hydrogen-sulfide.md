# Hydrogen Sulfide

H2S — toxic, corrosive gas documented in the Giza subsurface; the primary feedstock in Jeffrey Drum's chemical-manufacturing thesis for the pyramid complex's industrial function.

Hydrogen sulfide rising from the [[karst-cave-system]] beneath Giza is, in [[jeffrey-drum]]'s pyramid-as-chemical-factory thesis, the input molecule that — in the presence of oxygen and iron-oxide catalysts from the hydrothermal iron ore veins — is oxidized to sulfur dioxide, then sulfur trioxide, and finally combined with water vapor to produce sulfuric acid for [[acid-mine-drainage|acid leaching]] of metal ores.
