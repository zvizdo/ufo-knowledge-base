# Consciousness as Fundamental

The ontological claim that consciousness is not an emergent product of material processes but is instead a primitive feature of reality — or the foundation from which material reality emerges. Distinct from dualism; closer to idealism or panpsychism.

## Key Proponents in KB

- [[john-wheeler|John Wheeler]]: "it from bit" — physical reality is information; observer choices determine physical facts
- [[roger-penrose|Roger Penrose]] / [[stuart-hameroff|Stuart Hameroff]]: quantum coherence in microtubules as basis for non-computable consciousness
- [[donald-hoffman|Donald Hoffman]]: perception is a species-specific interface, not veridical reality; consciousness is primary
- Erwin Schrödinger: "consciousness cannot be accounted for in physical terms; it is absolutely fundamental"

## UAP Relevance

If consciousness is fundamental rather than produced, then NHI-consciousness interaction is not anomalous — and UAP phenomena that respond to observer state or intention are expected rather than inexplicable.
