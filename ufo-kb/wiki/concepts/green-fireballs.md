# Green Fireballs

Anomalous luminous objects with a distinctive emerald-green color observed repeatedly in the skies above New Mexico nuclear weapons facilities — primarily [[los-alamos|Los Alamos]] and Kirtland Air Force Base — beginning in late 1948. Unlike conventional meteors, they traveled horizontally, did not fragment, and appeared to maneuver.

## Investigation

Dr. [[lincoln-lapaz|Lincoln LaPaz]] of the University of New Mexico investigated the sightings and concluded they were not meteors. [[project-blue-book|Project [[project]]-blue-book|Blue Book]] head [[edward-ruppelt|Edward Ruppelt]] also studied them and found no conventional explanation. The Air Force convened a secret conference in 1949 at Los Alamos that concluded the fireballs were real but unexplained.
