# Synesthesia–Remote Viewing Correlation

The empirical finding — attributed by [[chris-ramsey]] to [[stargate-program]] data, specifically [[ed-may]]'s compilation — that **all six of the original Stargate program's highest-talent remote viewers independently had synesthesia**: the neurological coupling of senses in which stimulation of one modality triggers an involuntary experience in another (sound→color, smell→sound, etc.).

This was not a selection criterion. It was discovered *after* the six were identified through performance testing — meaning synesthesia may be a prerequisite for exceptional RV ability rather than a trained skill or arbitrary personal trait.

## What Is Synesthesia?

Synesthesia = entanglement of sensory modalities. A synesthete who hears a middle-C tone may simultaneously see a particular shade of blue. Estimates suggest 3–5% of the population has some form, ranging from grapheme-color synesthesia to more exotic couplings. Famous synesthetes include Kanye West and Pharrell Williams (music-color).

## All Babies Are Born Synesthetic

[[ian-whitley]], a space psychologist, has found that:
- **All infants are born with synesthesia**
- At approximately **6 months of age**, the cross-modal coupling is suppressed in most individuals
- Whitley is developing modalities to **help children retain synesthesia longer**, hypothesizing that early loss represents a loss of broader perceptual capacity

If the Stargate correlation is robust, retaining synesthesia past infancy might preserve access to whatever perceptual channel underlies remote viewing.

## Autism-Synesthesia Parallel

Non-verbal autistic children — the population studied in *The Telepathy Tapes* ([[diane-powell]] / [[autistic-savant-telepathy]]) — are disproportionately synesthetic. Ramsay's synthesis: these individuals are "less ported into the physical body" and thus less filtered by the standard sensory hierarchy, giving them greater access to what he calls primordial or non-local information. They need **situating in their bodies** (awareness of their hands, their physical presence) before conventional grounding takes over — in the interim, they are more connected to a pre-embodied perceptual mode.

This maps onto the [[platos-cave-allegory]] framework: synesthetic individuals are partially outside the cave, perceiving the higher-dimensional signal rather than the cave-wall shadow.

## Entropy as Remote Viewing Amplifier

A distinct but related finding: **high-entropy targets produce more accurate remote viewing sessions.** Data points:
- Hostage situations and imminent death = higher RV accuracy
- Nuclear sites = more accurate (entropy = disorder)
- Spraying target sites with **liquid nitrogen** (increases local entropy) improved RV session accuracy
- [[ganzfeld]] experiments work by **eliminating entropy in the viewer's sensory environment** — the brain, detecting no change, stops processing the white noise and red light, clearing the channel for weak signals

Proposed mechanism: the brain is hardwired to detect *change* (entropy). If there is a non-local signal mechanism, it would propagate more clearly against a low-noise background (Ganzfeld) toward a high-entropy target. This might explain:
- Why UFOs cluster around nuclear events ([[ufo-nuclear-nexus]])
- Why shamans were historically associated with life-or-death decisions (high entropy = strong signal)
- Why RV accuracy for stock markets is lower than for life-threatening situations

## Shaman Archetype as RV Practitioner

[[joe-mcmoneagle]] describes the historical shaman as the proto-remote viewer: often physically sickly (less individually fit), but retained by the group because their precognitive downloads — where to find water, which plants to eat, where danger lurked — had group survival value. They were the original "dowsers" of the information field. The group fed and protected them because the social ROI was positive even when the individual was a genetic liability.

Ramsay and Michaels connect this to the present: in a comfortable Western context, there is less *need* for survival-grade psi, so it atrophies. The relaxation of existential pressure may explain both why modern RV results are lower-stakes (less entropy driving accuracy) and why modern consciousness exploration (DMT, meditation, astral projection) is becoming more common as a deliberate substitute for the survival-pressure that once activated these channels.
