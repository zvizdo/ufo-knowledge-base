# Interdimensional Hypothesis

The proposition that UAP phenomena and apparent non-human intelligences (NHI) originate from other dimensions, densities, or layers of reality coexisting with ordinary human perception — rather than from other star systems. Most prominently associated with [[jacques-vallee]], who advanced it as an alternative to the extraterrestrial hypothesis after cataloguing the folkloric and phenomenological continuity between UAP encounters and pre-modern entity encounters (fairies, demons, angels).

## Core Argument

UAP behavior (instantaneous appearance/disappearance, physics violations, folkloric parallels across centuries) is more consistent with interdimensional transit or consciousness-interface than with interstellar travel. The entities may always be present in the environment; the perceptual barrier is on the human side. This connects to [[basal-ganglia-uap-predisposition]]: if the enlarged caudate-putamen slightly widens the perceptual filter, some individuals register what is always there.

## "Bigger Inside Than Outside" (fyX8V1XXmQM)

Vallée recounts a first-person witness account from San Jose: a woman saw a disc the same diameter as her house; inside she was taken up a staircase to an enormous circular room "like a movie house." The *outside* was house-sized; the *inside* was cinema-sized — a geometric impossibility in 3D.

Vallée explains via Edwin Abbott's *Flatland* and Carl Sagan's tesseract explanation: a 4D hypercube intersecting 3D space would present only its 3D cross-section to observers — but its full interior would remain intact in the fourth dimension. The "outside" is just what the 3D slice looks like; the actual object is much larger. This makes craft seemingly violating conservation of volume explicable *without* exotic physics if the craft extends into a 4th spatial dimension.

This connects to Vallée's general thesis: UAP operating from a dimensional substrate that intersects human 3D space only partially would naturally exhibit impossible-geometry properties — size inconsistencies, disappearance mid-flight, materialization from nothing.
