# Gravity Modification Experiments

[[john-brandenberg]]'s experimental program demonstrating local reduction in apparent gravitational force using pulsed electromagnetic fields. Three independent experimental methods; presented at the American Physical Society (APS) meeting; DARPA-funded.

## Experimental Methods

### Method 1 — Mylar Balloon (~$200)
- A mylar balloon with a toy motor armature suspended beneath it
- Apply ~30 watts of AC power at approximately 400 Hz to the armature
- **Result**: the balloon rises — the suspended object becomes measurably lighter
- Cost: approximately $200 in materials

### Method 2 — Chemical Scale
- A coil arrangement measured on a precision chemical scale
- Controlled for the potential that the EM field is interfering with the scale's sensor rather than changing the mass/weight
- Brandenburg claims controls demonstrated the effect is real and not a scale artifact
- **Result**: measurable weight reduction

### Method 3 — Tesla 3-Phase Rotating Power
- Tesla three-phase rotating electromagnetic field applied to a test object
- This configuration is widely used in drone motors (lightweight, compact, powerful)
- Test object: ~100g; **result**: ~0.5g weight reduction (1 part in 1,000)
- Brandenburg: "We didn't have anything flying around the room. It was basically like coils, bites balloons."

## Mechanism (Per GEM Theory)

Per [[gem-theory]]: the electromagnetic field causes lines of gravitational force to migrate away from the interior of the object — concentrating in the surrounding space rather than passing through the center of mass. The object "feels" less gravity because fewer gravitational field lines thread through it, reducing the effective downward force while buoyancy from the atmosphere remains constant.

## DARPA Engagement and Implications

- DARPA funded $200,000 to develop the theory and experiments formally
- Brandenburg handed over the final report; received polite acknowledgment, no scientific verdict
- Interpretation: "I think they found parts of my theory interesting… it's gone black"
- The pattern of classified absorption follows the 1950s anti-gravity research described by [[hal-puthoff]] as having "either not gotten anywhere or gone black"

## APS Presentation

Brandenburg presented gravity modification results at an APS meeting. He reports it was "well received." He is "waiting for somebody to verify it" but hasn't found independent replication.
