# PEAD — Presidential Emergency Action Documents

Presidential Emergency Action Documents (PEADs) are classified executive-branch orders or directives issued directly by the president. They are not subject to standard legislative or judicial oversight and are classified at or above the level of the programs they authorize.

## Mechanism

PEADs represent a presidential prerogative to act outside normal governmental procedures — originally designed for continuity-of-government scenarios and national emergencies. Their classification level means their existence and content are not typically disclosed to Congress or the public.

In the UAP context, [[eric-davis]] describes PEADs as the mechanism through which Eisenhower-era programs created **waived Special Access Programs (SAPs) free from congressional oversight**:

> "Under Eisenhower pads is a presidential emergency action documents were kind of used to create wave special access programs free from congressional oversight."

This connects to the broader argument that the most sensitive UAP programs were established by presidential action in the 1950s-60s and have been sustained outside the normal oversight chain ever since.

## Historical UAP Events and PEADs

Davis also explains why the Pentagon liaison officer who accidentally briefed [[george-hw-bush]] as CIA Director did not then brief him on a PEAD covering the early-1960s UAP event:

> "That historical event might already be covered by a peed and that's probably why that guy that briefing officer the liaison officer from the Pentagon actually slipped up by wrongly assuming that Bush was already briefed. The fact is is yes you can brief the peed within the executive branch but he assumed that bush had been and he hadn't been."

This implies PEADs can cover historical events (not just future emergencies) and that executive branch personnel can be briefed on a PEAD even if the underlying event is decades old — but only those with specific need to know.

## Congressional Visibility

The existence of PEADs is known in general terms; the specific contents are not. Congress has attempted to gain oversight over PEADs with limited success. This is the legal mechanism that allows UAP programs established by presidential directive to remain outside the intelligence oversight committees' purview.
