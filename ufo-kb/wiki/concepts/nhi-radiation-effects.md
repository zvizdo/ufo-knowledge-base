# NHI Radiation Effects

Physical health effects attributed to close proximity to UAP, consistent with ionizing radiation or intense electromagnetic fields. The pattern appears across multiple independently investigated cases and constitutes some of the most objectively verifiable physical evidence of UAP reality.

**Documented case patterns**:
- **[[1967-falcon-lake]]** (Stephan Michalak, Manitoba): Burns in a grid pattern matching the exhaust port arrangement of a hovering craft; acute radiation syndrome symptoms (nausea, vomiting, lymphopenia); blistering consistent with microwave or thermal radiation; documented by physician and pursued by RCMP, Canadian Department of National Defence.
- **[[1980-cash-landrum-incident]]** (Betty Cash, Vickie Landrum, Colby Landrum, Texas): Diamond-shaped craft emitting intense heat and light; acute radiation syndrome; progressive hair loss, cancer-like symptoms; Cash and Landrum hospitalized repeatedly; Cash died in 1998 of radiation-linked illness.
- **[[1980-rendlesham-forest]]** (USAF personnel): Radiation readings elevated at the landing site; some personnel reported health effects in years following the encounter.

**Medical significance**: The radiation effects documented in close-encounter cases — particularly the grid burn pattern in Michalak's case and the acute radiation syndrome in Cash-Landrum — are difficult to explain as psychosomatic or as conventional aircraft proximity effects. They constitute physical evidence of a real, high-energy phenomenon.

**[[ufo-electromagnetic-injuries]]** is the primary KB concept page covering this broader category; NHI radiation effects is a subset focused specifically on ionizing/EM radiation as the injury mechanism.
