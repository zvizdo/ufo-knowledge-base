# Adjustment Bureau (UAP Framework)

The "Adjustment Bureau" is the conceptual framework [[tim-taylor]] used when explaining his role to [[chris-bledsoe]]: *"I work for the Hammer and the Hammer works for God."* Taylor told Bledsoe directly to watch the film *The Adjustment Bureau* (adapted from [[philip-k-dick]]'s 1954 short story *The Adjustment Team*) as the closest analog to his actual function: *"That movie was created for a reason — watch it. It's a lot like that."*

**Philip K. Dick origin**: Dick's *Adjustment Team* — which he claimed was inspired by a domestic experience of finding a light switch mechanism changed without explanation — describes agents who "freeze-frame" segments of reality and make micro-adjustments to nudge timeline trajectories. Tessa Dick told researcher Riz Virk that this story came directly from Dick's personal experience of noticing small discontinuities. See [[simulation-theory]] and [[philip-k-dick]] for Dick's broader framework.

**Tim Taylor's claim**: In the KB's most explicit timeline-steering claim, Taylor described himself as a member of the [[nassau-group]] — a time-travel-adjacent classified program reportedly headed by [[thomas-townsend-brown]] — and used the Adjustment Bureau metaphor to describe the operational function: an operative who influences small variables in the timeline to steer outcomes. His autobiography [[launch-fever]] contains what are interpreted as coded references to this role.

**Connection to psionic assets**: The Adjustment Bureau framing connects to the broader [[p3-psionic-assets]] network — wherein psionic-capable individuals like Bledsoe function as informational or influence channels, and figures like Taylor serve as handlers or coordinators within a classified ecosystem that operates on timeline-scale rather than event-scale objectives.
