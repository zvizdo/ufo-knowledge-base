# UAP Disclosure Act

## What It Is

Proposed US legislation (also related to the Schumer-Rounds amendment context; see [[schumer-uap-amendment]]) designed to:
- Mandate **civilian congressional oversight** over UAP-related programs
- Establish an **elite presidential review board** to advance disclosure
- Ensure that classified UAP programs are subject to democratic accountability mechanisms

## Lobbying Against It

[[sean-kirkpatrick]] — former director of [[aaro]] — lobbied against the UAP Disclosure Act before it went to a vote, per [[jesse-michaels]]. This is significant: the head of the government's official UAP investigation office worked to prevent the civilian oversight mechanism that would have checked AARO's work.

Michaels's framing: "If UFOs are all just some fake multi-decade long hazing ritual, then nobody should have any issue with the impending UAP Disclosure Act — who cares if we have civilian oversight over programs that don't exist." The contradiction between Kirkpatrick's debunking posture and his resistance to oversight implicitly suggests he was protecting something real.

## Connection to the Church Committee Model

Michaels proposes that evidence for witness harassment (hazing of nuclear witnesses, [[bob-jacobs]] mailbox bombing, [[david-grusch]] NGA reprisals) warrants Church Committee–style hearings — the 1975 Senate investigation that exposed CIA/FBI abuses. The UAP Disclosure Act is framed as a prerequisite infrastructure for such hearings.

## Status

As of the episode's recording, the Act had gone up for a vote at least once and not passed; another vote was pending.
