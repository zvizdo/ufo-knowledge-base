# NHI Origin Hypotheses

The competing frameworks for explaining where non-human intelligence comes from and what it fundamentally is. The major categories:

**Extraterrestrial Hypothesis (ETH)**: NHI originate from other star systems and travel to Earth via physical or technologically advanced spacecraft. This is the dominant popular framing and the implicit assumption of most congressional UAP legislation. Critics (notably [[jacques-vallee]]) argue the frequency and behavioral patterns of UAP events are inconsistent with physical interstellar craft. See [[extraterrestrial-hypothesis]].

**Ultraterrestrial / Cryptoterrestrial**: NHI are indigenous to Earth — whether a parallel evolutionary lineage, an ancient civilization that went underground, or a form of intelligence that evolved alongside *Homo sapiens*. [[john-keel]] coined "ultraterrestrial" (1970); [[mac-tonnies]] developed the "cryptoterrestrial" variant posthumously. See [[ultraterrestrial-hypothesis]].

**Interdimensional**: NHI exist in dimensions or branes adjacent to observable spacetime. Their apparent "craft" are dimensional intrusions rather than spacecraft; their ability to appear and disappear reflects dimensional transit. See [[interdimensional-hypothesis]] and [[extradimensional-beings]].

**Temporal (Future Humans)**: NHI are future humans or post-human descendants who have developed time travel or temporal manipulation. Their apparent alienness reflects evolutionary drift over millennia. Their interest in human biology reflects archaeological research into their own origins. See [[future-humans-hypothesis]] and [[time-travel-uap]].

**Paraphysical / Consciousness-Substrate**: NHI are aspects of a consciousness field or informational substrate underlying physical reality — not material beings at all but forms emergent from or navigating a deeper ontological layer. This connects to [[consciousness-non-local]] and [[extradimensional-beings]].

**Von Neumann Probe variant**: Self-replicating autonomous probes launched from other civilizations and operating independently of any biological originator. The original senders may no longer exist; the probes follow programmed objectives. See [[von-neumann-probe]].
