# Healing and UAP

Healing and UAP describes the recurring report among close encounter and contact witnesses of unexpected physical healing following their experiences — including resolution of chronic conditions, cancer remissions, accelerated recovery from injuries, and elimination of longstanding illnesses. The pattern is consistent enough across independent cases to constitute a distinct phenomenological category.

**Chris Bledsoe's Crohn's disease**: The [[2007-bledsoe-cape-fear]] encounter is the most prominent example in the KB. [[chris-bledsoe]] had been severely debilitated by Crohn's disease before the encounter. Following the experience — a missing-time event with orb encounters — his Crohn's disease resolved. He attributes this to the contact experience; the timing is documented in his account.

**NHI radiation effects context**: UAP exposure produces both harmful radiation effects ([[nhi-radiation-effects]]) and, in some cases, healing effects. The same phenomenon has been described as causing cancer in some witnesses and resolving it in others — a paradox that suggests either differential exposure (dose, proximity, duration) or an active modulation of some kind.

**John Mack's research**: [[john-mack]] at Harvard documented physical aftereffects of contact experiences in his sample, including cases where medical conditions improved following encounters. His research was methodologically controversial but collected the largest clinical dataset of post-contact physical effects in the 1990s.

**Garry Nolan's angle**: [[garry-nolan]] has studied the physiological effects of UAP exposure on individuals, including damage patterns in bone marrow stem cells. His research has noted both damaging and anomalous healing-associated patterns, without yet establishing mechanism.

**Patterns across cases**: Common healing reports include:
- Resolution of autoimmune and chronic inflammatory conditions
- Cancer remissions following encounters
- Rapid healing of physical injuries
- Reports of restored vision or hearing
- Elimination of substance addictions

The mechanism — if the reports are accurate — is entirely unknown. Proposed explanations range from radiation hormesis to psychosomatic effects to direct NHI biological intervention.
