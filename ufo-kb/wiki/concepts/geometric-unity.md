# Geometric Unity

**Geometric Unity** is a proposed unified theory of physics developed by [[eric-weinstein]]. The framework is 14-dimensional and attempts to derive both general relativity and the Standard Model from a single geometric structure.

## UAP Relevance

Weinstein argues Geometric Unity is directly relevant to UAP propulsion:

1. **GR as effective theory**: Just as Newtonian mechanics is an effective limit within General Relativity, Weinstein's framework recovers GR as a limit within a broader structure. This creates theoretical space for physics *beyond* Einsteinian constraints — including FTL-adjacent propulsion.

2. **Extra parameters**: The framework's additional parameters could account for the exotic propulsion observables in the [[five-observables]] — instantaneous acceleration, transmedium travel — which General Relativity alone cannot explain.

3. **AATIP alignment**: Weinstein endorses [[hal-puthoff]]'s AATIP framing that GR should be treated like Maxwell's equations — as an effective theory with something deeper beneath it. Geometric Unity is his candidate for what lies beneath.
