# Gateway Documents

Gateway documents are texts that have historically served as the primary entry points by which readers enter the UAP disclosure and related conspiracy research frameworks. They are characterized by: compilation of existing whistleblower testimony, reproduction or description of alleged classified documents, dense cross-referencing between claims, and an epistemological posture of "this is what the government is hiding."

**Behold a Pale Horse (1991)**: Milton "Bill" Cooper's compilation of UFO whistleblower claims, alleged MJ-12 briefings, and anti-New World Order material. Became one of the most widely distributed conspiracy texts of the 1990s — particularly in communities outside mainstream UAP research. The KB's [[behold-a-pale-horse]] page describes it as both genuinely informative and riddled with unverified material.

**The Day After Roswell (1997)**: Philip Corso and William Birnes' account of how Roswell crash materials were reverse-engineered into commercial technologies through Army R&D channels. Contains the [[transistor-roswell-thesis]] and other specific claims. The [[day-after-roswell]] page treats it as high-interest, contested whistleblower testimony.

**Above Top Secret**: Timothy Good's compilations of UAP evidence across military cases; more conventionally sourced than Cooper but similar gateway function.

**The MJ-12 documents**: The alleged briefing document describing Majestic-12, a claimed secret committee managing UAP crashes. Whether authentic or fabricated (possibly by disinformation sources), the MJ-12 documents entered the community through photocopied reproduction and created the organizational vocabulary that subsequent research either affirms or debunks.

**Function in the research community**: Gateway documents serve the same function in UAP research that textbooks serve in academic fields — they establish the vocabulary, the key claims, the reference points, and the organizational framework that all subsequent discussion takes for granted. The difference is that gateway documents contain a mixture of verified history, informed speculation, and fabrication that is rarely cleanly separated.
