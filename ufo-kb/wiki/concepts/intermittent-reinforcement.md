# Intermittent Reinforcement

In operant conditioning (B.F. Skinner's framework), intermittent reinforcement refers to a schedule in which rewards or responses are delivered on unpredictable, variable-ratio schedules rather than after every behavior. Variable-ratio reinforcement produces the most persistent and extinction-resistant behavior patterns of any reinforcement schedule — the basis of slot machine design, social media engagement loops, and other systems engineered for behavioral retention. Fixed-ratio schedules (reward every N responses) are easier to extinguish; variable-ratio schedules produce near-compulsive sustained behavior because the next reward could always be the very next trial.

**Vallée's application**: [[jacques-vallee]] uses intermittent reinforcement as the explanatory mechanism in his [[control-system-hypothesis]]. The UAP phenomenon, he argues, does not produce consistent evidence or consistent contact — it is intermittently real, ambiguous, and always just out of reach of verification. This pattern, which frustrates and seems to undermine investigation, is precisely the optimal conditioning schedule for sustaining attention, generating belief, and shaping behavior without ever providing definitive confirmation. If NHI or some control system is deliberately managing human beliefs and behavior through UAP phenomena, intermittent reinforcement is the psychologically optimal delivery mechanism.

**Trickster parallel**: The same unpredictability is at the core of the [[trickster-phenomenon]]: UAP behave in contradictory, absurd, and paradoxical ways that resist any stable interpretive framework. The trickster's apparent randomness and the intermittent reinforcement schedule produce the same psychological effect — sustained, compulsive engagement with a phenomenon that never fully resolves.

**Abduction pattern**: Generational abduction claims (families reporting experiences across multiple generations, with unpredictable spacing between events) fit the variable-ratio model precisely. The abductee cannot predict when or whether contact will recur, producing sustained hypervigilance and ontological destabilization — the functional equivalent of a human subject in a variable-ratio conditioning experiment.

**Government management as parallel system**: [[ufo-secrecy]] — the pattern of official denial interspersed with occasional selective acknowledgments, FOIA releases, and semi-official leaks — operates as an independent intermittent reinforcement system. Researchers and public audiences receive occasional "rewards" (genuine releases, congressional hearings, official acknowledgments) interspersed with denials and silence, sustaining engagement while preventing resolution.

**Implications**: The intermittent reinforcement model suggests that the confusing, inconsistent, and frustrating nature of UAP evidence may not be accidental or a product of genuine complexity — it may be engineered, whether by non-human intelligences, government programs, or a combination. The model is epistemically uncomfortable because it predicts that dedicated UAP researchers are the most thoroughly conditioned subjects in the system.
