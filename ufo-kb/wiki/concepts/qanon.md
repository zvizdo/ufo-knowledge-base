# QAnon

QAnon is an internet-based conspiracy theory and disinformation movement that emerged in October 2017 when an anonymous poster called "Q" began leaving cryptic messages on 4chan (later 8chan/8kun) claiming to be a high-level government official with Q-level security clearance. The posts — "Q drops" — claimed that a secret deep state cabal of Satanic pedophiles controlled the government and that a covert military operation led by figures including Donald Trump was in the process of exposing and destroying them.

## Key Claims / Framings

- An anonymous government insider ("Q") is leaking classified information about a deep state shadow government in coded messages
- A secret group of elites engaged in child trafficking and Satanic ritual abuse controls major institutions
- A "Great Awakening" is imminent in which the cabal will be exposed and arrested ("the Storm")
- Mainstream media, political opponents, and fact-checkers are all part of the cover-up
- Q's coded posts require community decoding effort, creating an ARG (alternate reality game) structure

**The counter-thesis**: [[alex-jones]] has explicitly stated on record that QAnon is a designed disinformation operation whose purpose was to entrap and discredit populist political commentators — specifically by getting them to amplify false and unprovable claims, thereby destroying their credibility when the predictions failed to materialize. In this reading, QAnon is not a sincere conspiracy theory but a [[flood-the-zone-disinfo]] operation: deliberately seeding the information environment with unfalsifiable noise to obscure genuine signal (including potentially real disclosures about government malfeasance).
