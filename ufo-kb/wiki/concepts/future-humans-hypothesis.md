# Future Humans Hypothesis (NHI = Future Homo sapiens)

The claim that UAP entities — particularly the Gray-type humanoid — are not beings from another planet but are **biologically evolved descendants of modern humans** who have mastered temporal displacement and are visiting the past for research, genetic sampling, and/or timeline management. The most systematic academic treatment is [[michael-p-masters]]' **Extratemporalis model** (see *Identified Flying Objects*, *The Extratemporalis Model*, *Revelation: The Future Human Past*).

## The Core Argument

### Biological Convergence Evidence

If UAP visitors were truly extraterrestrial, evolutionary convergence to human form on an alien planet would be extraordinarily improbable. The hypothesis becomes parsimonious when visitors are hypothesized to be humans:

- **Neoteny**: See [[neoteny-alien-morphology]]. The predicted trajectory of human evolution (larger heads, smaller sensory organs, reduced musculature, child-like proportions) maps onto Gray alien morphology.
- **Reproductive compatibility**: Entities can breathe human air, engage in sexual intercourse, extract viable gametes, and produce viable offspring with humans. This is biologically inexplicable for species from another planetary biosphere.
- **Linguistic compatibility**: Many contactees report entities speaking human languages — impossible for beings who evolved on another planet.
- **PEER study**: Of ~5,000 experiencer accounts, 52% encountered humanoid forms; 85% of those reported friendly or neutral interactions — consistent with ancestral care, not indifferent alien research.

### Physics Framework

[[frank-tipler]]'s rotating cylinder/disc solution to Einstein's field equations (1970s) shows that a finite rapidly-rotating ring or disc creates sufficient **frame-dragging** to produce closed timelike curves — time travel within general relativity. The geometry is exactly the disc/saucer UAP form factor. See [[time-travel-uap]] for the full physics discussion.

Time-dilation effects consistently reported near UAP (missing time, watches showing days elapsed, beard growth, accelerated plant growth) are consistent with proximity to a frame-dragging-intensive craft.

### Textual Corroboration

- **Jim Penniston / Rendlesham 1980**: Contact beings explicitly said "we are you, we come from 8,000 years in the future" and described reproductive problems requiring ancestral gametes — see [[jim-penniston]] and [[1980-rendlesham-forest]].
- **George Hoover** (Navy Commander, top-secret clearance): Stated Roswell beings were time-traveling humans — see [[george-hoover]].
- **Herman Oberth**: UFOs "jump from one time-space coordinate to another" rather than traversing space — see [[herman-oberth]].
- **Tom DeLonge** (Newsweek): "These things aren't coming from other planets; these things might be coming through time" — see [[tom-delonge]].
- **Steven Spielberg** (1993, to Robert Liefeld): "What's easier to believe — that we are being monitored by aliens from another world, or that we are monitoring ourselves and studying us from the future?"

### Proximity Principle

Just as geographic proximity determines who you are most likely to encounter locally, **temporal proximity** determines who you are most likely to encounter in time: beings from the near future are overrepresented; beings from the very distant future are rare (explaining why non-humanoid forms — mantis-like, insectoid — are less common in the contact literature and associated with more negative experiences).

## Variants

1. **Extratemporalis model** (Masters): future Homo sapiens, genetic banking, timeline research
2. **Jorjani / civilizational self-actualization**: a future culture reaching into its own past to ensure its existence via consciousness-mediated influence
3. **Nassau time travel group** (Tim Taylor claim): Thomas Townsend Brown as president of a clandestine group operationalizing gravitational time manipulation
4. **Avatar hypothesis**: Some entities may be biologically engineered avatars created by a future intelligence to be less jarring to contemporary humans — not actual future humans in the flesh (Masters acknowledges in *Revelation*)

## Counter-Arguments / Tensions

- If future humans can visit the past, why has this not created observable paradoxes or been more widely acknowledged historically?
- The Fermi paradox applies temporally as well as spatially: if future humans have had this capability for centuries, why is contact so rare and so carefully managed?
- Some UAP entities are described as non-humanoid (Mantids, Reptilians) — the proximity principle accounts for their rarity, but does not fully explain their presence.
- [[david-grusch]] suggests some biological entities recovered may be bio-engineered by an intelligence behind them — consistent with the avatar variant but complicates the pure future-human reading.

## Open Questions

- Are different entity types (Grays, Nordics, Mantids) representatives of different temporal distances or different future factions?
- If the intertemporal breeding program (gamete collection) was ongoing through the 1990s, has it concluded?
- What distinguishes future-human consciousness (hive mind, no ego) from consciousness as understood today — and when/how does that transition occur?

## Garry Nolan's Endorsement (dzTZbSNsKV8)

[[garry-nolan]] raises the future-humans hypothesis directly in [[dzTZbSNsKV8]]:

> *"Some people think that aliens might be us from the future. And if you think about the way we're evolving, it's probably smaller bodies, bigger heads — sort of what you would see a gray alien looking like."*

Nolan does not commit to the hypothesis but presents it as a credible possibility alongside extraterrestrial and interdimensional framings. His observation that Gray morphology maps onto predicted human neoteny is independent convergence with [[michael-p-masters]]' systematic Extratemporalis model.
