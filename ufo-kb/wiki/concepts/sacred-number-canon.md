# Sacred Number Canon

## Definition

The **sacred number canon** is the term used by [[randall-carlson]] and related researchers for a family of numbers that appear **redundantly** across completely independent domains:

- Everyday time and space measurement
- Angles of the five Platonic solids
- Subdivisions of the precessional Great Year
- Solar system dimensions measured in miles
- Vedic Yuga durations and the Sumerian king list
- Mayan calendar units
- Greek and Hebrew alphabetic numerology ([[gematria]])
- Dimensions of the Great Pyramid and Parthenon
- Passages in the Book of Revelation

The core family is generated by the number **72** multiplied by simple integers, or equivalently by the number **432** (6 × 72) and its multiples. All numbers in the family reduce to the digit **9** under Kabbalistic digit-summing (e.g. 1440 → 1+4+4+0 = 9; 25920 → 2+5+9+2+0 = 18 → 9).

## Why It Matters

The key claim is that these numbers are **not coincidental**. They appear to link:

1. **Earth's rotation** (86,400 seconds/day) to **Platonic solid geometry** (octahedron = 1,440°)
2. **Solar system dimensions** (Sun radius = 432,000 miles) to **Vedic time** (Kali Yuga = 432,000 years)
3. **Precessional astronomy** (Great Year = 25,920 years) to **sacred architecture** (Pyramid scale = 1:43,200)
4. **Ancient language** (gematria values = 432, 864, 1080) to all of the above

If the convergence is real, the sacred number canon represents a **transmission medium** — a system by which pre-catastrophe civilizations encoded cosmological knowledge in forms that would survive cultural destruction: stone dimensions, mythological numbers, alphabetic values.

## Connections to Specific Domains

### Platonic Solids
[[randall-carlson]] maps each of the five Platonic solids to a specific subdivision of the Great Year via their total angular degree counts:

- Tetrahedron (720°) → 1 decan = 720 years
- Octahedron (1,440°) → 2 decans = 1,440 years
- Cube (2,160°) → 1 Platonic month = 2,160 years
- Icosahedron (3,600°) → Saros cycle = 3,600 years
- Dodecahedron (6,480°) → 1 Platonic season = 6,480 years

The dodecahedron, which Plato declared "the shape of the universe," has 6,480° total — and one season of the Great Year is precisely 6,480 years. [[randall-carlson]] considers this non-coincidental.

### Solar System Architecture
The English mile may itself be a geodetic unit encoding planetary relationships. If so, the fact that solar system bodies have dimensions expressible as clean multiples of 432 in miles is evidence that an ancient civilization derived the mile from the same knowledge base.

### Ancient Calendars
Three independent ancient calendar traditions used a **sacred year of 360 days** plus 5 unlucky intercalary days: Egyptian, Greek, and Mayan. The Sumerian belief was that the year was *originally* exactly 360 days and was lengthened by catastrophe. The 360-day sacred year is the foundation of the number system.

## Skeptical Response
The conventional response is selection bias ("you can find any number in enough data") and unit arbitrariness ("miles are a Roman/English unit, not universal"). Carlson addresses the unit objection by arguing the mile, furlong, and foot are geodetically derived and thus encode Earth's actual geometry. The strongest counter-argument: why does the system work in English miles but not in metric units?

## Open Questions
- Why does the sacred number canon appear in miles but not metric? Is this a selection artifact or evidence that the mile is genuinely ancient and geodetic?
- What is the origin transmission chain? How did the same numbers reach Egypt, India, Sumer, Greece, and the Maya independently?
- Is the canon the residue of a single pre-catastrophe civilization, or does it emerge from independent cultures all trying to describe the same astronomical facts?
