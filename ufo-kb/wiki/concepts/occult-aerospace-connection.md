# Occult-Aerospace Connection

A pattern, raised by [[lue-elizondo]] and others, connecting the founding figures and early institutional culture of American aerospace to esoteric and occult traditions. The most documented thread: [[jack-parsons]], co-founder of JPL and pioneering rocket engineer, was a devoted follower of Aleister Crowley's **Thelema** occult system and reportedly recited a Crowley hymn before rocket tests.

## Key Data Points

### Jack Parsons and Thelema
[[jack-parsons]] co-founded JPL, contributed fundamental solid-fuel rocket chemistry, and simultaneously led the Agape Lodge of the Ordo Templi Orientis in Pasadena. He conducted magical workings (including the "Babalon Working" in 1946) and corresponded directly with Crowley. Parsons's esoteric practice was not secret — it was known to colleagues, including early Scientology founder L. Ron Hubbard, who participated in his rituals. Elizondo explicitly cites Parsons reciting a Crowley hymn *before rocket tests* — not in private — as an institutional data point.

### Buzz Aldrin and Freemasonry
[[buzz-aldrin]] is a Shriner Mason who carried a Masonic flag to the Moon during Apollo 11. The flag was consecrated by the Supreme Council of the Scottish Rite in Washington D.C. This is more ambiguous as evidence for this pattern — Masonic membership among senior military/government officials is common — but it has been cited in the same context.

### Wernher von Braun and Mysticism
[[wernher-von-braun]], who came through [[operation-paperclip]], is separately reported to have held views about consciousness and cosmic intelligence that went well beyond mainstream materialism, though the evidence here is thinner.

## Interpretive Range

The range of interpretation for this pattern runs from:

1. **Incidental**: These individuals happened to hold esoteric beliefs alongside their technical work; the two domains were independent. Many historical scientists had unorthodox beliefs.

2. **Cultural**: Early aerospace attracted a particular personality type — iconoclastic, anti-establishment — for whom esoteric traditions were part of the same intellectual counterculture that made them willing to attempt rocketry when the mainstream dismissed it.

3. **Structural**: The esoteric traditions and the aerospace programs shared personnel, funding networks, and knowledge channels — possibly including non-publicly-sourced knowledge about non-human intelligence or technologies. This is the interpretation Elizondo is gesturing toward without stating directly.

4. **Instrumentalized**: Government/intelligence entities deliberately recruited esotericists because their networks and epistemological frameworks were useful for managing UAP-adjacent phenomena (e.g., remote viewing, as in [[stargate-program]]).

### Ben Rich's ESP Statement (G7Ns4Aq1tVc)

[[ben-rich]], director of [[lockheed-skunk-works]] from 1975–1991, gave perhaps the most striking example of aerospace/consciousness intersection when [[nick-cook]] asked him how the technology to "take ET home" would work. Rich answered with a question: "How does ESP work?" Then: "All points in space and time are connected. That's how it works." He then walked away.

This statement — from the director of the most classified aerospace division in the world — collapses the distinction between advanced physics and parapsychological phenomena. Cook notes that [[john-alexander]] (the DOD Colonel who oversaw the [[psionics-uap-interface]] experiments with [[john-hutchinson]]) and the whole remote viewing program represent the same intersection: aerospace/defense entities funding "heretical science" that should have no scientific basis.

### Robert Weiss and Tom DeLonge (G7Ns4Aq1tVc)

[[robert-weiss]] (Rich's successor at Skunk Works) collaborated with [[tom-delonge]] on the *Secret Machines* books. Cook notes that Weiss became interested when DeLonge and [[peter-levenda]] started talking about **Celestial Ascent practices and Kabbalah** — esoteric frameworks. The pattern: a senior Skunk Works director got interested in DeLonge's public UAP disclosure efforts specifically when esoteric/consciousness frameworks entered the conversation.

### George Ellery Hale and the Pre-Parsons Layer (zjpvfDFc4fg)

[[george-ellery-hale]] is the upstream figure who created the institutional environment (at [[caltech]]) from which Parsons emerged. Hale built Mount Wilson Observatory, transformed Caltech, and:
- Claimed creative inspiration from an **"elf" or spirit entity**
- Embedded **Akhenaten / Aten (Egyptian solar monotheism)** into the Hale Solar Laboratory in Pasadena
- His obituaries called him *"a priest of the sun"* and *"a modern Zoroaster"*

This places the occult-science fusion one generation before Parsons and at the level of Caltech's founding institution-builder — not just a single eccentric practitioner.

Both Chase and Ryder, along with [[robert-weiss]] and AFRL Commander Neil McCaslin, formally advised the *[[secret-machines]]* project — and in those conversations, elite aerospace insiders consistently identified **human consciousness** as the key variable in UAP understanding.
