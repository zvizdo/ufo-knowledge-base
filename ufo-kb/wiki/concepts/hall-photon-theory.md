# Hall Photon Theory

Charles Hall's original physics framework, first copyrighted in 1997, developed from his interpretation of [[tall-whites]] propulsion technology observed at [[indian-springs]] (1965–1967). Published in *Beyond Relativity* and accompanied by Rumble video *Mirage Galaxies and Edges in Space*.

## Core Argument

Einstein's Special Relativity is **incomplete**, not wrong in its domain — but it assumes light has only two internal force fields (electric and magnetic). Hall argues there are many more subatomic force fields, some with far lower permittivity of free space than the electromagnetic field. This enables particles (and craft) using those fields to travel at speeds **many orders of magnitude faster than light**.

## Key Claims

### Gravity Is Faster Than Light
Gravity has **no electromagnetic interaction cross-section** — it only interacts with other gravitational fields. Because it never slows via electromagnetic drag, it propagates faster than light. Hall cites this as evidence: gravity organizes galaxies millions of light years across without breakdown — impossible if it propagated only at light speed.

### Neutrinos Are Faster Than Light
Same principle — neutrinos only interact weakly and do not slow to apparent light speed unless forced into electromagnetic interaction. Hence they are extraordinarily difficult to detect; Hall links this to CERN and Antarctic neutrino detector experiments.

### The Michelson-Morley Misinterpretation
The Michelson-Morley experiment is standardly cited to prove there is no luminiferous ether. Hall's counter: the interference pattern bands do not change color (red in → red out). This proves the device is **steering** the photon rather than producing constructive interference — steering requires an additional force field not accounted for in standard SR.

### Craft Must Be Convex Ellipsoids
Any concave surface or sharp edge in a FTL craft causes force-field turbulence → heating approaching infinity → the craft is limited to ~80% of light speed. Only fully convex ellipsoids allow clean passage through and beyond light speed. Hall applies this to:
- Tall White scout craft (smooth ellipsoid = FTL capable)
- Ryan Graves's "sphere inside a cube" (concave corners → capped at ~80% light speed)
- Roswell craft (same smooth ellipsoid type as Nevada sightings)

### Five Propulsion Particle Classes
At least nine subatomic particle types beyond the Standard Model are relevant to ET propulsion:
- **5** used in standard spacecraft design (commercial/civilian Tall White craft)
- **2** more unstable ones for military-only craft (no children/families aboard)
- **2** most unstable for weapons only

Hall speculates this is why the **Eisenhower five-craft demonstration** at Edwards AFB showed five craft — one demonstrating each of the five standard propulsion particle types.

### No Wormholes, No Warping

> "If you were in the way, that craft would run into you. There is no such thing as wormholes or warping space and time."

Hall explicitly refutes the interdimensional and wormhole interpretations popular in current UAP discourse (Vallée, some AARO-adjacent scientists). FTL travel in his model is through physical space, not around it.
