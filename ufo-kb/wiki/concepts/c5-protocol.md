# C5 Protocol

## What

The "C5 protocol" is [[steven-greer]]'s formalized practice for inviting voluntary contact with non-human intelligence — the protocol he organizes the CE-5 (Close Encounter of the Fifth Kind) events around. The signature technique inside the protocol is [[coherent-thought-sequencing]].

## Key elements

- Meditative state induction; group setting amplifies the signal.
- Coherent thought sequencing: mentally zoom from cosmos to one's exact location, broadcasting love and peace as the carrier signal.
- Optional environmental cues: low-power laser pointed safely into the starfield (never at aircraft — federal crime).
- Multiple practitioners coordinating ("synchronized love beam") amplify the invitation.
- Internal-state hygiene matters — "if you're going through a rough time… you will probably get burned."
