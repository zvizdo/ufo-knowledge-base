# Gnosis and UAP

The gnosis-and-UAP framework interprets close encounter and contact experiences not as material events (beings in craft) but as modern instances of the ancient Gnostic concept of gnosis — direct transmission of knowledge or revelation from a non-material intelligence into human consciousness, bypassing normal cognition.

**Philip K. Dick's 2-3-74 experience**: In February–March 1974, [[philip-k-dick]] experienced what he described as a beam of pink light downloading "gnosis" into his mind — comprehensive knowledge including accurate medical information about his son's undetected birth defect. He identified the communicating intelligence as VALIS (Vast Active Living Intelligence System) and spent the remainder of his life in the *Exegesis* trying to determine whether it was God, an alien, a Gnostic deity, or his own unconscious. Dick explicitly framed the experience using Gnostic vocabulary: anamnesis (recovery of suppressed primordial knowledge), the cosmic wound, and the amnesia imposed by material existence.

**Anamnesis structure**: Gnostic anamnesis is the recovery of memory suppressed at birth — the idea that humans carry genuine knowledge of transcendence that material existence buries. In Dick's reading, UAP/VALIS contact events are instances of anamnesis: a break in the amnesia through which the primordial self briefly re-emerges. This framing is endorsed in the KB through Jacques Vallée and [[jesse-michaels]] discussions.

**Vallée's framing**: [[jacques-vallee]] has consistently noted that UAP contact experiences are phenomenologically indistinguishable from mystical and religious encounters across cultures — and has suggested that the most productive framework treats them as information events rather than material ones. The Gnostic tradition's account of non-material intelligences transmitting knowledge to individual humans is structurally equivalent to what contact witnesses describe.

**Cult mimicry caveat**: Both Vallée and Michaels note the critical discernment problem: anamnesis and Gnostic recovery can be mimicked by processes that "steal your brain" — cults, programmed subjects, [[mk-ultra]] manipulation. The subjective phenomenology of genuine gnosis and manufactured mind control experience may be indistinguishable from inside.

**Relationship to DMT entities**: The [[dmt-entities]] category overlaps strongly with Gnostic contact phenomenology — both involve non-material intelligence transmission, entity presence, and information download in a non-ordinary state.
