# Symbols and Glyphs

Recurring visual motifs reported in UFO/NHI contact across cultures and time periods: markings on craft hulls, symbols seen during abductions, crop formations, and glyphs in contactee automatic writing. The recurrence of similar forms across unrelated witnesses is one of the stronger arguments for a coherent NHI origin rather than purely psychological projection.

## Categories

- **Craft hull markings**: Symbols observed on UAP surfaces — reported since the 1940s; appear in Blue Book photos and multiple witness accounts including [[1977-sheehan-blue-book-photos]]
- **Contactee glyphs**: Symbols received via automatic writing or mental download during contact experiences
- **Crop formations**: Geometric patterns in fields; the most publicly visible form
- **Entity writing**: Script or symbols seen in abduction scenarios that do not correspond to known human writing systems
