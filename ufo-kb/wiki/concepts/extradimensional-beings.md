# Extradimensional Beings

The extradimensional beings thesis holds that non-human intelligences (NHI) encountered in UAP contexts are not visitors from another star system but entities from another dimension or plane of existence. In several formulations, this includes the claim that an early, covert precursor to the modern US space program deliberately made contact with these entities — and that subsequent secrecy programs have managed this contact ever since.

## Core Thesis

As relayed by [[jesse-michaels]] in [[JE1oM89dpBc]]:

> "What we're thinking of as aliens. They're extradimensional beings that an earlier precursor of the space program made contact with."

The "earlier precursor of the space program" framing is significant: it places first contact well before NASA's public 1958 founding, potentially in the late 1940s–1950s black program space via figures like [[wernher-von-braun]], or in the post-Roswell period when classified study of recovered materials was allegedly underway. Jesse does not name his source for this claim.

The extradimensional framing has several variants:

1. **Interdimensional travel** — NHI move between dimensions rather than across light-years; their craft are dimensional transition devices, not propulsion vehicles in the conventional sense.
2. **Higher-dimensional reality** — NHI exist in spatial dimensions humans cannot normally perceive; UAP encounters are moments when dimensional boundaries thin or are deliberately crossed.
3. **Consciousness-mediated access** — the boundary between our dimension and theirs is navigable through consciousness practices (CE-5, DMT, meditation), not just technology.

## Relationship to Time Travel Hypothesis

The extradimensional and [[time-travel-uap]] theses are structurally related: in some models, time is a dimension, making time travel a special case of extradimensional travel. [[jason-reza-jorjani]]'s civilizational retroactive causality model — a future culture reaching into its past — is compatible with either framing depending on whether time is treated as a spatial dimension.

## Epistemic Status

The extradimensional claim is unverifiable through standard scientific methodology — it invokes dimensions beyond current experimental access. Its strongest supporting evidence comes from:
1. The apparent violation of known physics in UAP flight (instantaneous acceleration, no propulsion signature) — more consistent with dimensional transit than conventional propulsion
2. Consciousness-correlated encounter reports (CE-5, DMT, NDEs) suggesting non-physical access mechanisms
3. The "interdimensional" interpretation endorsed by several figures with alleged classified access ([[lue-elizondo]], via indirect statements)

## Open Questions

- Who is the original source of the "earlier precursor of the space program made contact" claim? This assertion, if true, would represent the most significant hidden historical event of the 20th century.
- Is "extradimensional" a physical claim (extra spatial dimensions per string theory) or a consciousness claim (non-material planes of existence)?
