# Celestial Ascent

The ancient and cross-cultural practice of guided or induced ascent of consciousness through a structured hierarchy of realms, levels, or "heavens." The structural features are remarkably consistent across traditions:
- **Seven levels**: Sumerian cosmology, Merkavah Judaism, Ptolemaic planetary spheres, Neoplatonism, Shangqing Daoism, and Gnostic systems all describe seven levels of ascent
- **Big Dipper / North Star axis**: multiple traditions use the circumpolar stars as the physical orientation marker for the celestial axis
- **Progressive initiation**: access to each level requires specific knowledge, preparation, or transformation; gatekeepers or guardians appear at level transitions
- **Non-human intelligences at higher levels**: the beings encountered at advanced levels are uniformly described as non-human and significantly more capable than human baseline

[[peter-levenda]]'s *Stairway to Heaven* (in the KB via [[y7pleu5rtv4]]) documents the structural parallels across traditions and provides a practical framework for understanding the practice. Levenda reads celestial ascent not as metaphor but as a repeatable technology for accessing information and beings inaccessible through ordinary consciousness — the same claim made by modern DMT researchers and remote viewing practitioners.

The connection to NHI is direct: if celestial ascent reliably produces encounters with non-human beings across thousands of years and dozens of independent cultural traditions, the beings are either universal features of the human psyche or actual entities accessible through altered states. The [[dmt-nhi-hypothesis]] is the modern experimental version of the same question.
