# Smart Dust

## Definition

"Smart dust" is the public-record program name for **millimeter-scale autonomous sensing and communication nodes** capable of forming distributed sensor swarms. Active DARPA-funded research from the 1990s; field-demonstration by 2001. Smart dust is the visible-tip of the [[programmable-matter]] tech-tree that extends — at least theoretically and possibly in classified hands — into [[utility-fog]] and [[foglets]].

## Public-record milestones

- 1990s — DARPA funds smart-dust R&D in parallel with broader MEMS work.
- 2001 — DARPA demonstrates smart dust in field tests.
- 2004 — MEMS technology matures significantly.
