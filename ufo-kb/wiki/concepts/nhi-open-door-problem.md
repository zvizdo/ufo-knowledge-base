# NHI Open Door Problem

## Thesis

[[whitley-strieber]] attributes to [[john-von-neumann]] (via [[general-exon]], who was at Air Material Command during the 1947 Roswell period) a theoretical paper arguing:

> Non-human entities cannot fully enter the shared human perceptual reality without human consensus belief enabling their manifestation. Their entry requires that they "become as real for us as we are to each other." Once this threshold is crossed — once humans collectively accept NHI into their reality — the entities cannot be removed. The door opens only once and only in one direction, forever.

Strieber's direct formulation:

> "This is a door which opens in the mind and in the perceptual system and can never be closed. So once the door is open — once we have accepted them into our reality — we can never get rid of them. We can never tell them, 'No, it turns out we don't want you here because we don't like you and we don't like what you're doing to us.' The door opens only once and only in one direction and forever."

## Mechanism (per Strieber/Exon)

The physics grounding is Von Neumann's own: the role of consciousness in wave function collapse. If consciousness is required for quantum wave function collapse (Von Neumann's thesis in *The Mathematical Foundations of Quantum Mechanics*), then the presence or absence of NHI in shared reality is not a fixed fact — it is a function of what consciousness collectively accepts as real. NHI exist in a liminal state until human belief/acceptance collapses the wave function in their favor.

## Why This Thesis Explains Disclosure Suppression

If the open door thesis is correct, then disclosure is not merely politically sensitive — it is ontologically irreversible. The standard argument for disclosure suppression (panic, destabilization, economic collapse) doesn't fully explain the intensity and permanence of the suppression. The open door thesis offers a deeper motive: the keepers of the secret believe that **we cannot undo what we collectively agree to**, and therefore disclosure must wait until humanity can genuinely negotiate the terms of contact on its own behalf.

Strieber frames human sovereignty as the key issue: *"It might be that this is a time right now where we have to be very careful about preserving our dominion of our reality."*

## Evidentiary Status

> ⚠ The Von Neumann paper's existence is unverified. [[general-exon]] was not a scientist; he tried to explain a paper he'd heard about from colleagues. No physical copy has surfaced in the literature. The thesis may be Strieber's synthesis from Exon's imprecise verbal account.
