# Mars Ancient Civilisation Thesis

The claim — most fully developed by the late researcher **[[david-flynn]]** in his book *Sidonia* — that Mars was once inhabited by an advanced non-human extraterrestrial civilisation (the biblical "gods"), that this civilisation was destroyed in a pre-Adamic cosmic war, and that the recovery of its remnant knowledge has been the hidden objective of mystery schools from antiquity to the present day.

## Core Claims

### Mars as Origin of the "Gods"
- Ancient peoples across cultures (Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Greek, Roman, Mayan, etc.) believed their gods descended from a celestial body
- [[david-flynn]] traced this belief and argued — through astronomical and textual analysis — that the ancients identified **Mars** as the primary origin point
- The "face on Mars" and pyramid structures in the **Sidonia region** of Mars (photographed by Viking in 1976) were cited as physical remnants of the Martian civilisation
- [[richard-hoagland]] publicised this in the 1990s; he and Flynn gave joint conferences, sometimes alongside scholar [[michael-heiser]]

### The Planet Rahab
- The asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter represents the remnants of a **destroyed planet** — called **Rahab** in the biblical text (cited cryptically in Psalms, Job, and Isaiah)
- God is described as having "pierced the dragon and shattered Rahab like a vessel of clay"
- Flynn and Alberino argue peer-reviewed papers support sufficient debris for a destroyed planet (most debris vaporised or ejected into deep space)
- Rahab's destruction was the pre-Adamic cosmic war between the "king of heaven" and the dragon faction; the shockwave devastated Mars

### Destruction of the Martian Civilisation
- Rahab's explosion sent shards bombarding Mars (and Earth and other planets), destroying whatever civilisation existed there
- Mars was not accidentally barren — it was **eviscerated** by this cosmic event
- Mars's ruined biosphere (water caverns, thin atmosphere remnants) is consistent with a planet that once supported life

### NASA Suppression Claim
- Alberino argues NASA's initial Viking photos showing the Martian "face" were accurate; the later higher-resolution photos released to debunk the feature were deliberately manipulated or selectively framed to suppress public interest
- [[john-brandenberg]] (physicist, Lawrence Livermore/Sandia National Laboratories) argues excess Argon-40 and Xenon-129 in the Martian atmosphere constitute proof of a nuclear cataclysm on Mars; reportedly received a call from Carl Sagan urging him not to publicise these findings

### Mystery Schools and Mars
- One of the primary objectives of mystery schools (Phoenicians → Freemasons → modern elite) is to **go to Mars and recover the lost knowledge of the gods**
- Alberino cites [[david-flynn]] as having demonstrated this through the mystery school tradition
- The colonisation of Mars — including Elon Musk's SpaceX programme — is connected (consciously or not) to this ancient aspiration
- Neil McCasslin (reportedly ran foreign material exploitation lab; Tom DeLonge's source; went missing recently at time of interview) was described as obsessed with Greek mythology, consistent with this occult Mars-connection pattern

### CIA Remote Viewing / Joe McMoneagle (Firsthand — JpLThEF2dTM)

[[joe-mcmoneagle]] provides the most detailed firsthand account of this session (not hearsay via Alberino — directly in [[JpLThEF2dTM]]). DOD assigned him **seven GPS coordinates on Mars** with the tasking "Mars 1 million BC":

- **Pyramid larger than the Great Pyramid** with "monster rooms"
- **Pyramidal structures** interpreted as hibernation/survival chambers
- **12-ft-tall humanoid beings** (described as twice normal human height; Alberino rounds to "8–12 ft")
- People **trapped**, atmosphere deteriorating, population attempting to survive until rescue
- Post-session: JPL negatives provided from the exact GPS coordinates showed structures consistent with descriptions — pyramids, old fort, collapsed walls, "The Shard" (4-sided pyramid thousands of feet tall on crater edge), 30-mile perfectly straight cliff edge, subterranean cavern with apparent "wiring," bone-like surface objects
- 2001 Mars Global Surveyor image (square structure several km per side with right-angle geometry) cited as corroboration
- McMoneagle has approximately 60 photographs of Martian structures he considers "clearly alien"

> ⚠ Note on sizing: Alberino in [[CiQTBOQ1dTg]] quotes "8–12 ft beings" (hearsay); McMoneagle in [[JpLThEF2dTM]] says "12-foot-tall" (firsthand). Likely rounding; treat "~12 ft / twice human height" as the accurate description.

McMoneagle also advances a **Martian origin of humanity** hypothesis: survivors of the Martian catastrophe escaped to Earth; technology was cannibalized from spacecraft, leading to devolution from advanced to primitive. "I'm not sure we aren't aliens."

This thesis overlaps [[john-brandenberg]]'s nuclear war model and [[graham-hancock]]'s eroded-monolith analysis in Cydonia — McMoneagle cites both by name in [[JpLThEF2dTM]].

### Brandenburg's SRI Direct Work (p0S0BfoZy0w — Firsthand)

[[john-brandenberg]] worked directly in the **Independent Mars Investigation** at [[stanford-research-institute]] — "the same place where the remote viewing program was just down the hall." His firsthand contributions:

- **Second face discovery**: Found a second face in the **Utopia region** of Mars — same face design as Sidonia, about two-thirds the size. Published at a conference. NASA subsequently renamed the region to "Galaxias Chaos" (where "chaos" = cliffy ground in geological nomenclature) — Brandenburg interprets this as deliberate suppression
- **Viking targeting suppression**: Cydonia was originally planned as a Viking landing site. Scientists were overruled by an unknown group ("boys downtown say you can't land there") — Brandenburg believes the Face and pyramid had been noticed in Mariner 9 imagery beforehand
- **Paleo-Ocean**: Following the zero-kilometre elevation contour around the full planet revealed a global ocean boundary — smooth basin terrain below (ocean floor), rough terrain above; all river channels feeding into the basin. Announced at a 1986 scientific conference. NASA has not credited Brandenburg; he calls it the "Brandenburg Ocean"
- **Emotional moment**: "When it all hit me — I wept like a child for about four minutes. They were all dead. It looked like a thermonuclear holocaust." After composing himself: "Don't be sad. Be happy. Now you know the universe has no safety net."

Brandenburg's direct quote on JPL scientists: if you walked in and mentioned ancient Mars life, "their eyes would dart around to see if anyone was listening, and then they'd say yes, but don't tell anybody I told you." He attributes this to both institutional politics (robotic vs. human space program funding war) and the chilling effect of the UFO cover-up.

### Brandenburg's Mars Atmosphere Thesis (p0S0BfoZy0w — Firsthand)

The mechanism for Mars's atmospheric loss:
1. Mars's magnetosphere faded naturally (smaller planet, cooled core faster, lost the liquid iron dynamo)
2. But the **atmosphere was not gradually stripped by solar wind** — something "blew most of the atmosphere off into space" in a near-instantaneous event that looks like a thermonuclear detonation
3. Loss of dense atmosphere → loss of greenhouse effect → rapid cooling → accelerated magnetosphere decline (feedback loop)
4. Areas of Mars with residual magnetization still produce aurora
5. Brandenburg: a remnant biosphere likely still exists underground
