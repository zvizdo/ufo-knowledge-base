# Red Light Therapy

Photobiomodulation therapy using red (630–700 nm) and near-infrared (700–1100 nm) wavelengths. The proposed mechanism: red and near-infrared photons are absorbed by cytochrome c oxidase in mitochondria, increasing ATP production, reducing oxidative stress, and triggering cellular repair cascades. Used clinically for wound healing, inflammation reduction, and neurological applications.
