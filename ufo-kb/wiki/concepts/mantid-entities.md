# Mantid Entities

Giant praying-mantis-form non-human intelligences reported across abduction accounts, remote viewing sessions, and contact testimony. Distinct from grays in form but potentially overlapping in origin — [[jason-reza-jorjani]] argues mantids are one of the manifestations of the Type 2 shapeshifting distributed intelligence (vs. Type 1 gray robots built by Nordics).

## Key Cases

- **[[ted-owens]]**: claimed to receive psychokinetic powers from two mantid entities he called "Twitter and Tweeter"; wrote documented letters predicting the Challenger disaster
- **[[david-huggins]]**: artist who documents extensive hybrid encounters in paintings; mantid figures appear in his accounts
- **[[carla-turner]]**: abductee accounts include soul-energy harvesting that Jorjani associates with the mantid intelligence
- **[[terence-mckenna]]**: a girlfriend reportedly had a mantid encounter; McKenna considered it significant data

## Cultural-Theoretical Frame

**[[roger-caillois]]** (surrealist theorist) wrote extensively about the praying mantis as the **trickster/diabolos** — an agent of dialectical growth through apparent cruelty. The mantid's literal behavior (female devouring the male during mating) maps onto the soul-harvesting accounts. Caillois read the mantid as an emblem of the surrealist project: destruction as the engine of creative transformation.

**[[jacques-vallee]]'s Trinity book**: the craft at the 1945 Trinity site had mantis-like creatures; Jorjani speculates this was deliberately "coded" to reference Trinity and the atomic bombings — a trickster message about human nuclear capability.

## Shapeshifting Distributed Intelligence

Jorjani's framework places mantids within the Type 2 gray category: a shapeshifting distributed intelligence that:
- Can manifest as grays, mantids, owls, and ectoplasmic forms
- Uses information physics to materialize hard objects from soft states (Keel: soft UFO → hard UFO)
- Is not robotic; challenges humanity's relationship to Nordic overlords
