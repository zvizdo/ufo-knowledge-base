# Pyramid Texts as Initiatory

A reading of the **Egyptian Pyramid Texts** (Old Kingdom inscriptions found primarily in the pyramids of Unas and his successors at Saqqara) as containing **encoded initiatory instructions** — possibly technical, possibly experiential — rather than only funerary liturgy.

In the KB, this framing is anchored in [[diana-pasulka]]'s discussions of the structural parallel between Egyptian, Eleusinian, and later Catholic mystery traditions: each tradition encodes consciousness-altering and contact-with-the-other-realm protocols in language that is liturgical on the surface and operational underneath. The frame ties into the broader [[mystery-school-consciousness-bridge]] synthesis and to [[randall-carlson]]'s and [[graham-hancock]]'s readings of Egyptian monumental architecture.

The skeptical reading is that "encoded initiatory" claims are unfalsifiable and project modern technical concerns onto religious texts.
