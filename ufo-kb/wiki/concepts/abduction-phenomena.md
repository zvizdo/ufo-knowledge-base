# Abduction Phenomena

The category of contact experiences in which individuals report being involuntarily taken by non-human beings, subjected to medical or biological procedures, and returned to their origin point — typically with partial or total amnesia of the event, often recovered under hypnosis. The phenomenon is cross-cultural and spans multiple decades of continuous reporting.

## Defining Features

- **Missing time**: Gaps in conscious memory, typically 1–3 hours
- **Medical/biological procedures**: Examination, implantation, tissue or genetic sampling
- **Hybrid program indicators**: Some abductees report being shown hybrid children; ongoing contact across generations
- **Telepathic communication**: Entities typically communicate non-verbally
- **Paralysis**: Physical inability to resist or move during the experience

## Class 1 / Class 2 Experiencer Typology (iPxbILOMQ08)

[[steve-coburn]] introduces a structured typology for the depth of NHI program involvement, drawn from personal experience and from roughly 400 scans of experiencers:

| Class | Population (US) | Involvement | Implants |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|---------|
| **Class 1** | ~3–5% (10–15M) | Biological sample collection only | Typically none |
| **Class 2** | ~1 in 1,000 (~350K) | Taken aboard craft; part of an ongoing NHI program | Yes — including brain implant |

**Class 2 standard protocol**:
- Brain implant in the frontal lobes (typically placed in childhood, often the first encounter)
- Additional implants (e.g., above-ear) emitting radio signals
- Assigned a **handler** — a specific NHI individual who manages the experiencer's "case" on board ship; typically the scientist-engineer type of gray (~4.5 ft)
- Monitoring of sensory input and thought in real time; connected to [[gray-hive-mind]]
- Experiences often occur aboard large **mother ships in geocynchronous orbit** (~500 miles up), reached by smaller craft

**Timing**: Abductions typically occur around **2–3 AM**. Being told "they usually come about 2 or 3 in the morning" was unsolicited, corroborating cross-experiencer consistency.

**Behavioral effects**: Coburn noticed a subtle thought-suppression effect ("governor on his thoughts") while the toe implant was present; felt more mentally free after removal. Other experiencers notice gray complexion changes. The interface may include more than passive monitoring.

**Bloodline selection**: Abductions of pregnant women for fetal genetic modification preferentially target Germanic, Celtic, and Native American bloodlines — though exceptions exist. The target genetic combination is a "closely guarded secret."

**Handler relationship**: Coburn has reason to believe his handler — a taller gray scientist-engineer — is monitoring his conversations in real time. When asked about the handler, Coburn said to the camera: "What's up, handler?" The handler concept implies ongoing, personalized NHI management of each class-2 experiencer, not just episodic contact.

- [[1961-betty-barney-hill-abduction]] — 1961; foundational; first major documented case

- [[1964-vandenberg-richard-barth]] — 1964 CE3 at a Minuteman missile site; paralysis, transport, 3–4 hours lost time, NHI telepathic communication; post-encounter ecstatic love; possible nasal implant; notable for nuclear-base context and explicit NHI communication about intent
