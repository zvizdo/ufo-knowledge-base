# Gematria

## Definition

**Gematria** is the system of assigning numerical values to alphabetic characters in ancient languages — particularly Hebrew and Greek — such that words and phrases simultaneously carry linguistic meaning and numerical content.

Prior to the Hindu-Arabic numeral system, the **same symbols** served as both letters and numbers in Semitic and Greek writing. This is the "denery system": letters encode units (1–9), tens (10–90), and hundreds (100–400/900). Words are therefore always also numbers.

Gematria is a subdivision of **Kabbalah** — the mystical interpretive tradition applied to Hebrew scripture. The Greek equivalent applies to New Testament and Pythagorean/Neo-Pythagorean texts.

## The Greek System

Greek uses the same denery structure: alpha=1 through theta=9 (units); iota=10 through koppa=90 (tens); rho=100 through sampi=900 (hundreds). Sampi was not a written letter in most contexts — included purely for the value of 900.

## The 144,000 of Revelation

The 144,000 "redeemed of the earth" in the Book of Revelation (Rev. 14:1–3) is a direct reference to the sacred number 144. The Greek word *agorazzo* (translated as "redeemed") etymologically means "to go to the *agora*" (marketplace = public square) — carrying the geometric concept of the **square** at its root. Carlson interprets this as a veiled reference to the sacred geometry of squaring, with the 144,000 as those who have internalized the sacred number canon.

## Significance

The convergence of gematric values with solar system measures and precessional cycle numbers is [[randall-carlson]]'s strongest argument that ancient texts are not simply religious narrative but **carriers of cosmological data** encoded for an audience capable of reading the numerical layer.

The challenge for translators and interpreters: multiple layers of translation (Hebrew → Greek → Latin → English) by people who may not have understood the numerical encoding can destroy the gematric content entirely. The outer "story" survives translation; the inner mathematical architecture does not, unless you work from original languages.
