# DMT Entities

DMT entities are non-human beings consistently reported by human subjects during high-dose N,N-dimethyltryptamine (DMT) experiences. Unlike the generic visionary content produced by many psychedelics, DMT entities have a distinctive phenomenology: they are typically described as autonomous, seemingly intelligent, often communicative, and independent of the subject's own mental content. They appear to have their own agenda and are frequently experienced as more real than ordinary waking reality.

**Rick Strassman's research**: Psychiatrist Rick Strassman conducted the first US government-approved clinical study of DMT's effects on human subjects at the University of New Mexico (1994, published as *DMT: The Spirit Molecule*). His 60 volunteers reported entity contact at a striking rate — approximately half experienced encounters with distinct non-human beings. Strassman systematized the entity taxonomy: entities described as "beings of light," mechanical or insectoid beings, clown-like figures, and reptilian entities. Strassman proposed the hypothesis that DMT might facilitate contact with genuinely non-human intelligences rather than merely producing hallucinations.

**Jace Callaway connection**: [[jace-callaway]]'s work on the relationship between endogenous DMT synthesis, the INMT enzyme, and REM sleep is relevant to the entity contact question — if DMT is produced endogenously during sleep, the hypothesis that DMT entities encountered during dreaming and those encountered during psychedelic experiences share a common mechanism becomes important. Callaway proposed that REM sleep and DMT facilitate a form of recurring contact with the same entities.

**UAP entity overlap**: UAP researchers have noted parallels between DMT entity descriptions and the entities reported in UAP close encounter cases — particularly insectoid and praying-mantis figures, small grey beings with large eyes, and reptilian beings. This overlap has generated hypotheses that both experiences access the same phenomenon through different means (exogenous psychedelic vs. endogenous release or other triggering mechanism).

**Philosophical status**: The question of whether DMT entities are purely psychological constructs or have some form of objective existence is unresolved. Strassman's data shows that subjects consistently rate entity encounters as more real than ordinary reality — a fact that conventional neuroscience attributes to the overwhelming activation of serotonin receptors, while alternative researchers take more literally.
