# Nuts-and-Bolts Hypothesis

The "nuts-and-bolts" position holds that UAPs are **fully physical vehicles** operated by **biological non-human intelligences** — subject to all the engineering constraints of physical reality: they need engines, bathrooms, food storage, repair access, and navigation windows. This stands in contrast to "extradimensional," "consciousness-based," or "interdimensional" theories that treat UAP phenomena as non-physical or beyond conventional matter-energy constraints.

## Core Claims

- ET beings are biological: they eat, drink, breathe air, reproduce, age, and use bathrooms. All craft have bathrooms.
- Propulsion uses physical force fields (not wormholes or spacetime warping)
- Craft have engineering constraints: concave surfaces limit speed; ceramic fiber optics can melt; approach windows exist due to gravitational physics
- Biological beings experience fatigue, emotion, fear — they are not gods or pure consciousness
- FTL travel is possible but dangerous; even advanced craft have failure modes (hence Roswell crashes)

## Primary Advocate in This KB

[[charles-hall]] is the KB's most explicit and detailed advocate of the nuts-and-bolts position, based on 26 months of direct observation at [[indian-springs]] (1965–67):

> "If you were in the way, that craft would run into you. There is no such thing as wormholes or warping space and time."

Hall's evidence for the fully physical model:
- Observed Tall Whites using human bathrooms on base (selected because they were clean)
- All Tall White and gray craft have bathroom facilities at the rear
- Scout craft design: cockpit/controls front, living quarters middle, bathroom/food storage rear — identical across species
- Craft can be damaged (Roswell crashes, fiber-optic failures)
- Engineering constraints are real: craft shape determines speed limit; the Tall Whites' smooth ellipsoids are FTL-capable precisely because of their geometry

See [[hall-photon-theory]] for Hall's specific physics framework.
