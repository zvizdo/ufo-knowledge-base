# NHI Biologics

NHI biologics refers to alleged physical biological remains — bodies, tissue, skeletal material — attributed to non-human intelligence. If authenticated, this category would represent the most materially falsifiable class of UAP evidence, bypassing witness testimony entirely.

## Cases in This KB

**Nazca Tridactyl Mummies** — The highest-profile active case. Multiple M-type bodies (3-fingered, 4–5 ft) assessed by independent forensic teams from the US, Peru, and Mexico as real organisms. See [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]], [[2015-nazca-mummy-discovery]]. Key sub-threads: [[osmium-cadmium-implants]], [[tridactyl-global-pattern]], GLI3 digit-development mutation in NCBI data. [[david-grusch]] privately told Rep. [[eric-burlison|Eric Burlison (Berles)]] that some mummy physiology is "congruent with what he has seen in classified briefings."

**Kecksburg / Roswell Alleged Biologics** — Multiple whistleblowers allege biological recovery from crash retrievals. See [[reverse-engineered-craft]], [[1947-roswell]], [[crash-retrieval-research]].

**Varginha (Brazil, 1996)** — Multiple beings recovered by Brazilian military (with covert US involvement). One being was treated alive at [[hospital-regional-varginha]] by [[marcos-vinicius]] (surgery) and [[italo-venturelli]] (observed 3–4 minutes face-to-face). Military police officer [[marcos-charez]] who captured a being with bare hands died ~2 weeks later from a virulent, unprecedented bacterial infection; pathologist [[jean-bachista-janini]] suspects alien or genetically engineered bacteria. The bacterial contamination represents a unique sub-category of NHI biologics — living biological agents transmitted from NHI to human. See [[1996-varginha]], [[post-contact-biological-contamination]], [[UkKwa4jU0fc]].

## Authentication Challenges

- **Chain of custody**: bodies that surface via private collectors lack provenance
- **Fabrication baseline**: Peruvian tourist trade produces convincing fake figurines
- **Government interference**: Peruvian Ministry of Culture documented to have conflated tourist figurines with real specimens in press coverage; multiple seizure attempts against [[university-of-ika]]
- **Morphological tests insufficient alone**: must be combined with genetic analysis, isotopic dating, and material composition
