# Memory Metal

A recurring description of alleged UAP crash debris: extremely lightweight metallic foil-like material that, when crumpled or deformed, immediately returns to its original shape. The material resists being permanently deformed — it behaves as if it has a crystalline memory for its original configuration at room temperature.

## Properties Described Across Cases

- **Weight**: Lighter than conventional aluminum foil; "effectively weightless"
- **Texture**: Smooth, metallic, polished — reflects light like aluminum foil
- **Shape memory**: Crumple it in your hand → release → immediately returns to exact original shape
- **Structural integrity**: No evidence of standard manufacturing seams or joints

## Cases in This KB

### Roswell 1947 — Jesse Marcel Sr.
The iconic memory-metal account. [[jesse-marcel]] described holding "thin foil-like stuff" that he could crumple in his hand and then "it would unfold itself and go back to its original shape." Multiple witnesses at [[1947-roswell]] describe variants of this material. See [[arts-parts]] for alleged physical fragments now in private collections.

### Varginha 1996 — Carlos de Souza
[[carlos-de-souza]] picked up a piece of the crash debris from the Varginha site. His description: "aluminum foil that you use to wrap up chicken to roast... it was that kind of weight. It didn't weigh anything at all." When he crumpled it: "this foil or this sheet regained its shape." He was still holding it when an army soldier knocked it from his hand. The material was confiscated; no physical sample exists outside the military chain. See [[1996-varginha]] and [[UkKwa4jU0fc]].

The Varginha description matches the Roswell account precisely — made independently, by a witness with no prior UFO interest, in Brazil in 1996.

## Scientific Context

Earth-manufactured shape-memory alloys (like nitinol — nickel-titanium) exist, but:
- Nitinol's shape memory requires specific temperature triggering (not room temperature crumple-release)
- Nitinol is heavy, not feather-light like aluminum foil
- Industrial shape-memory alloys are not produced in foil-thin configurations with these properties
- The combination of near-zero weight + instant room-temperature shape recovery is not achievable with any currently known material

[[arts-parts]] in the Bigelow/MUFON chain are alleged samples; the chain of custody and material analysis results are disputed.

## Connection to Crash Retrieval

Memory metal is one of the most consistently described elements across multi-decade crash retrieval accounts. Its description across independent witnesses (Roswell military, Varginha pilot) with no contact between them and 49 years between incidents supports either: (a) a real material property that crash debris consistently exhibits, or (b) a cultural meme that spreads through the UFO literature. The Varginha case is more robust on the second possibility — de Souza had no prior UFO interest and describes finding it while thinking he was helping a blimp crash victim.
