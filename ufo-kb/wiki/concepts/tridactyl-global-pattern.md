# Tridactyl Global Pattern

The observation that three-fingered and three-toed beings appear across widely separated cultures, time periods, and media — from ancient Nazca cave art to Chilean petroglyphs, Hopi legends, modern alien abduction cases, and the *Close Encounters of the Third Kind* depictions that Ronald Reagan described as containing "a lot of truth."

## Interpretation Spectrum

- **Cultural transmission**: A widely shared artistic convention or mythological archetype (least unusual explanation; but doesn't explain consistent phenotypic detail).
- **Real extinct/living species**: A global species whose range extended widely; the Nazca bodies are physical remains.
- **Active contact**: The same beings have been visiting and interacting with human cultures across time; consistent depiction reflects consistent experience.
- **NHI engineering/breeding**: The tridactyl form is engineered; beings were created/modified multiple times in multiple places (Thierry Jamin's "laboratory of hybridization" etymology supports this).
