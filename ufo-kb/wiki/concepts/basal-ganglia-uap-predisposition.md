# Basal Ganglia UAP Predisposition

The hypothesis, associated with Stanford microbiologist [[garry-nolan]], that certain individuals are neurologically predisposed to perceiving UAP and paranormal phenomena due to higher neural density in the **caudate-putamen region** (basal ganglia) of the brain.

## The Nolan Thesis

Nolan's research on brain imaging of UAP experiencers found anomalously high neural density in the area between the head of the caudate nucleus and the putamen. The critical distinction from a simpler "exposure creates the change" model is:

- Nolan's view is that people had this higher neural density **before** their encounter — it was a pre-existing structural feature
- This higher density made them **more predisposed to perceiving** the phenomenon
- Encounters may then *further* increase that neural density in some cases

The implication is that UAP encounters are not random — the phenomenon may preferentially interact with individuals who have a specific neurological profile, essentially selecting for perception-capable observers.

## Application at Skinwalker Ranch

In [[7W7iOt57fOo]], [[brandon-fugel]] references Nolan's work in the context of observed patterns at [[skinwalker-ranch]]:
- The most severe biological effects (paralysis, hospitalization) disproportionately affect "alpha male" / aggressive personality types
- Fugel speculates that the Hells Angel "George" who experienced 10-minute paralysis in the [[2016-skinwalker-illness-incident]] may have been specifically targeted due to a predisposition profile
- The phenomenon's apparent discriminative targeting — affecting some visitors severely while others have mild or no effects — is consistent with a selection mechanism based on neurological predisposition

## Relationship to Savant Research

This concept connects to [[garry-nolan]]'s parallel work on autistic savants (referenced in other AA episodes), where high neural density in executive function areas correlates with anomalous cognitive capacities. The same structural feature (unusual neural density in specific brain regions) may underlie both enhanced cognitive processing and enhanced UAP-encounter susceptibility.

The concept also overlaps with [[autistic-savant-telepathy]] — if certain individuals can receive information through non-standard channels due to brain structure, the same architecture might make them visible or accessible to a phenomenon that operates on consciousness directly.

## The Caudate-Putamen as Reward-Prediction System (RofQnByLwOo)

In [[RofQnByLwOo]], Nolan is directly quoted extending the structural finding into a functional claim: the caudate-putamen is not merely structurally anomalous in high-capability individuals — it is the brain's **reward-prediction and anticipation system**:

> *"That system is part of the reward system... it's involved in our registration of rewards, our learning based on rewards, our predictions based on past rewards... I think that system is central in terms of the organism changing its behavior based on rewards and getting excited about possible future rewards and anticipated rewards."*

Nolan notes this in the context of fMRI studies of Go players: the caudate-putamen lights up when players make intuitive moves (locally inexplicable by board state) that later prove correct. The genetic component is confirmed: family members of high-density individuals share the structure.

This functional framing connects directly to [[quantum-brain-precognition]]: if the caudate-putamen is the brain's native system for processing anticipated future outcomes, it is the anatomically plausible site for [[eric-wargo]]'s retrocausal signal to surface into conscious experience. The "gut feeling" that a decision is correct despite insufficient data may be the reward-prediction system receiving a signal from a future knowledge state.

## Genetic Component Confirmed (dzTZbSNsKV8)

In [[dzTZbSNsKV8]], Nolan provides the strongest direct account of the CIA recruitment: the CIA showed him MRIs not just of DoD/intelligence personnel with UAP contact histories but also of their **family members**. The family members showed the same enlarged, denser caudate-putamen structure. Nolan's conclusion: *"That means that structure had a genetic component, whatever it was."*

This rules out the hypothesis that the density increase is caused purely by the encounter (though encounters may further increase it). The predisposition pre-exists exposure and runs in families — it is heritable.

## Japanese Chess (Shogi) Corroboration (dzTZbSNsKV8)

Nolan cites brain imaging studies of Japanese shogi (chess variant) masters. When masters make intuitive moves — unexpected but brilliantly correct decisions not fully explainable from board state — the caudate-putamen "lit up." This provides independent neuroscience validation of the caudate-putamen as the **intuition organ** for decision-making under uncertainty, separate from the UAP context.

## Schizophrenia as Broken Transmitter (dzTZbSNsKV8)

The caudate-putamen area is also damaged in both autism and schizophrenia. Nolan proposes a striking reframing for schizophrenia:

> *"Are they all crazy? Well, that goes into the transmission theory. I think schizophrenics just — it's like a transmitter being broken or oscillating between different [states]. They can't turn it off."*

In this model, schizophrenics are not hallucinating but perceiving real stimuli (the same perceptual channel opened by the enlarged caudate-putamen) that they cannot regulate or contextualize. Nolan is actively researching this using both autistic and schizophrenic populations. This directly connects to [[consciousness-non-local]] and the transmission theory of consciousness.

## Sensory Reduction Theory (dzTZbSNsKV8)

Nolan articulates the theoretical underpinning most clearly in [[dzTZbSNsKV8]]:

> *"Our senses are a filter to stop our brains from being overwhelmed with reality. So what we see is a limited aspect of everything around us. The sensory organs are not necessarily productive. They're reductive."*

The default brain state on this model is near-omniscient; ordinary perception is a reduction of that. The enlarged caudate-putamen slightly widens the filter, exposing more of the underlying reality to conscious experience. This is the empirical neuroscience grounding for the interdimensional-coexistence model ([[interdimensional-hypothesis]]): UAP entities are always present in the environment; only widened-filter individuals can register them.
