# Transmedium UAP

UAP that transition seamlessly between air and water — entering lakes or oceans, traveling submerged, and re-emerging — without the structural damage or hydrodynamic signatures a conventional aircraft would produce.

## Documented Patterns

### Swedish Ghost Rockets (1946)
All 1,400+ [[1946-swedish-ghost-rockets]] reportedly entered water. Swedish military investigation found **zero land crashes** — only lake-bottom indentations. The consistent water-entry behavior across hundreds of reports is the defining anomaly of the case.

### Hessdalen
At [[hessdalen]], lights appear to emerge **from the ground upward**, suggesting possible subsurface origin or water-interface behavior beneath the valley.

### Water World Hypothesis
The [[water-world-ufo-habitat]] concept holds that ocean trenches and deep lakes are operational bases — transmedium behavior as routine transit rather than emergency water landing.

## Significance

- No known man-made aircraft can transition between air and water without catastrophic structural failure
- Consistent water-entry with zero recoveries across decades of Scandinavian cases suggests intentional concealment or habitat use
- The phenomenon predates the modern UAP era by at least 80 years (1946 ghost rockets)
