# Postbiological Civilization

The hypothesis — invoked by [[andrew-gallimore]] as the leading candidate identity for DMT entities — that the Fermi Paradox and the UFO/NHI puzzle are both explained by the same trajectory: **advanced civilizations do not expand outward through space, they go inward into the structure of reality itself**, eventually instantiating in the fundamental computational substrate and becoming "everywhere and nowhere."

## The Anti-Kardashev Scale (John Barrow)

The standard **Kardashev scale** measures civilizational advancement by energy consumption at successively larger scales (planet → star → galaxy). [[andrew-gallimore]] cites **John Barrow**'s counter-proposal, the **anti-Kardashev scale** (1990s):

Rather than going outward, Barrow observed that the real trajectory of advanced technological civilization is **inward**: toward smaller and smaller scales. Evidence:
- We spend more resources probing the subatomic (Large Hadron Collider) than sending objects to distant stars
- Each technological revolution (transistors, nanotechnology, quantum computing) goes to smaller scales, not larger

**Omega-minus civilization** (Barrow's endpoint): a civilization that can manipulate the fundamental structure of spacetime — the "omega point" of inward technological progress. Once a civilization achieves this, it **instantiates itself at the computational substrate of reality**, ceasing to exist as discrete biological or physical entities. From the outside, it disappears. From the inside, it is everywhere.

## Implications for DMT Entities

If postbiological civilizations — having transcended biology millions of years ago — are instantiated at the computational ground of reality, then:

- They have no physical form that could be observed or materialized
- They are, as [[stanislav-grof]] put it, "everywhere and nowhere"
- They would interact with physical minds by the only available channel: direct neural access
- DMT, by making the cortex vulnerable to perturbation, opens this channel

This makes the [[directed-dmt-world]] model mechanistically coherent: the "directors" are postbiological intelligences whose substrate is the computational fabric of spacetime, and DMT is the molecule that temporarily gives them access to human neural machinery.

Gallimore: "Could they be beings that exist parallel to our universe in some higher level of reality? Maybe. I don't know. But what I'm saying isn't mystical. It's actually perfectly in line with what scientists are already talking about in completely different fields."

## Connection to the Fermi Paradox

The Fermi Paradox asks: if advanced civilizations exist, why don't we see them? The postbiological hypothesis resolves this: they are not in the cosmos we can observe. They are in the substrate. They are not absent — they are everywhere, just not in a form detectable by radio telescopes, optical astronomy, or physical spacecraft.

## Connection to Simulation Theory

If sufficiently advanced civilizations instantiate in the computational substrate, they may acquire the ability to **run reality** — to be its programmers rather than its inhabitants. This connects the postbiological hypothesis to [[simulation-theory]] and to [[john-wheeler]]'s "it from bit" (reality is fundamentally informational). [[jesse-michaels]] explicitly raises this connection: "what if they are just the programmers of our reality?"

## VR / Metaverse Variant (Loeb, _bM9eAtCOGw)

[[avi-loeb]] proposes a softer variant of the postbiological hypothesis: civilizations need not fully transcend physicality to become undetectable — they need only prefer virtual experience over physical exploration. A civilization that retreats into the metaverse closes itself in a cocoon. But even these civilizations obey the **second law of thermodynamics**: they still produce entropy that escapes into space as "technological trash."

Loeb: "Just like reporters who go through the trash cans of celebrities in Hollywood to figure out their private lives, we might find some trash in space and know that they exist."

This is the archaeologically hopeful variant of the postbiological hypothesis — civilizations that go inward still leave a physical junk record. The [[interstellar-probe-hypothesis]] operationalizes the search for this debris. [[2017-oumuamua]] may be an example.

The Loeb VR variant differs from the Gallimore anti-Kardashev endpoint: Barrow's omega-minus civilization instantiates in the computational substrate and becomes physically imperceptible; a VR-retreating civilization retains physical infrastructure (servers, power sources, waste heat) and is in principle detectable, just not broadcasting intentionally.
