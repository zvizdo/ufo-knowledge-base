# Zeta Reticuli

Zeta Reticuli (ζ Reticuli) is a binary star system located approximately 39.5 light years from Earth in the southern constellation Reticulum. The two stars (Zeta1 and Zeta2 Reticuli) are solar-type stars separated by approximately 0.06 light years — close enough to be gravitationally bound, far enough that both could potentially host stable planetary systems. At least one exoplanet has been reported in the system.

**Betty Hill's star map**: The Zeta Reticuli system became associated with UAP when Betty Hill, who along with her husband Barney claimed to have been abducted by non-human beings in September 1961 ([[1961-betty-barney-hill-abduction]]), reported under hypnosis that she had been shown a star map by her captors depicting their home star system and its trade routes. Betty Hill reproduced the map from memory.

**Marjorie Fish interpretation**: In 1974, amateur astronomer Marjorie Fish produced a three-dimensional model of nearby star systems and found a match for Betty Hill's map in Zeta Reticuli. Fish's analysis suggested that the two most prominent stars in Hill's map — interpreted as the base stars for the extraterrestrial travelers — corresponded to Zeta1 and Zeta2 Reticuli. The interpretation was published in *Astronomy* magazine and generated significant discussion.

**Controversies**: The Fish interpretation has been criticized on multiple grounds: the pattern-matching methodology is subjective; the original map was reproduced from memory under hypnosis and may not be reliable; and subsequent updates to stellar position catalogs have weakened the match. Nonetheless, the Zeta Reticuli identification became widely cited in UFO literature as one of the few testable claims from abduction accounts.

**Serpo and the Zeta Reticuli connection**: The Project Serpo claims — an alleged secret exchange program between humans and extraterrestrials — also place the home system of the "Ebens" (the alleged exchange partners) in Zeta Reticuli. This parallel reinforces the system's cultural association with UAP claims regardless of its astronomical significance.
