# Five Observables

## Definition

The Pentagon has codified **five observable properties** that are highly consistent across hundreds of UAP detections recorded via optical, radar, and infrared sensors. These properties provide an empirical baseline derived from instrumented military data rather than witness testimony alone — making them the most defensible starting point for scientific investigation of UAP.

The five observables (as understood from DoD public statements and UAP community synthesis; not all five are fully named in the qAou_h1POWs source):

1. **Sudden and instantaneous acceleration** — UAPs accelerate at rates impossible under classical mechanics without enormous energy input and structural failure from g-forces.
2. **Hypersonic velocity with no signatures** — UAPs travel at speeds exceeding Mach 5 without producing sonic booms, plasma sheaths, or infrared heat signatures expected of high-speed classical objects.
3. **Trans-medium travel** — UAPs transition between air, water, and space seamlessly with no apparent physical reconfiguration.
4. **Low observability** — UAPs evade detection across multiple sensor modalities (often appearing on radar but not visually, or vice versa).
5. **Positive lift** — UAPs exhibit apparent levitation or antigravitational behavior without visible propulsion surfaces or exhaust.

Items 1 and 2 are explicitly named in the qAou_h1POWs source. Items 3–5 are established from other DoD-adjacent documents and UAP community consensus; their exact DoD formulation may differ.

## Why This Matters

The five observables are load-bearing for multiple claims-theses in this KB:

- They are the primary evidence base for [[eric-weinstein]]'s "new physics not new engineering" characterization.
- [[deep-prasad]] uses them as the explicit **design target** for [[quantum-generative-materials]]' simulation program — the goal is to produce quantum simulations of metamaterials whose predicted properties match the five observables.
- They are the empirical anchor for [[macroscopic-quantum-uap]] — Prasad argues the observables are consistent with quantum behavior at macroscopic scale.
- They are cited in virtually every serious UAP disclosure hearing and investigation as the minimum fact set that requires explanation.

## Key Example: Tic-Tac

The [[2004-tic-tac-nimitz]] incident is the canonical multi-instrumented demonstration of the five observables. The Tic-Tac is estimated to have reached at least 31× the speed of sound while producing no acoustic signature — instantiating observables 1 and 2 simultaneously with multi-witness and multi-sensor corroboration.

[[deep-prasad]] notes that the Baconian scientific method (requiring repeatability) structurally cannot accommodate the five observables, because UAP events are not produced on demand. A modified scientific epistemology capable of formally integrating non-repeatable instrumented anomalies is needed.
