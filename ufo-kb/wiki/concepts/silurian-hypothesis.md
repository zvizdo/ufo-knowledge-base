# Silurian Hypothesis

## Definition

The hypothesis that Non-Human Intelligence (NHI) may not be extraterrestrial but rather an ancient terrestrial civilization that arose on Earth millions of years before *Homo sapiens*. Named after the Silurian period, the hypothesis was popularized in academic form by Adam Frank and Gavin Schmidt (2019 paper in the *International Journal of Astrobiology*).

Key claim: a sufficiently ancient technological civilization would leave almost no detectable physical trace. Plate tectonics recycle the ocean floor every ~300 million years; geological activity obliterates surface structures; organic materials decompose. An industrial civilization from 100+ million years ago would be largely invisible to present archaeology.

## Implications for UFO/UAP Discourse

If the Silurian hypothesis is correct:
- NHI may have been cohabiting with humans on Earth for millions of years — not visiting from elsewhere
- The "where are they?" question dissolves: they are here, underground, underwater, or in dimensions we cannot perceive
- Archaeological anomalies (unexplained ancient structures, out-of-place artifacts) may be genuine traces
- The lost civilization and forbidden archaeology hypotheses gain a scientific framing

[[eric-haseltine]] uses the hypothesis as the central premise of [[shadow-of-time-book]]: private corporations are competing to excavate anomalous objects in the American Southwest desert that may be ancient NHI artifacts. He describes it as one of the intellectually serious UAP hypotheses alongside extraterrestrial, time travel, and extra-dimensional models.

Haseltine notes: "maybe they've been cohabiting with us on Earth for a while."

## Relationship to Other Hypotheses

- **Distinct from extraterrestrial origin**: no interstellar travel required
- **Distinct from "future humans time travel"**: origin is parallel evolution, not temporal displacement
- **Compatible with underground/oceanic NHI habitation**: NHI surviving in deep ocean trenches or subterranean environments
- **Compatible with [[lost-civilization-hypothesis]]**: overlaps with theories about prehistoric advanced civilizations (Graham Hancock, Randall Carlson territory) but with a much longer timeline

## Open Questions

- Is there any direct physical evidence consistent with the hypothesis? (Anomalous isotope ratios on Mars are interesting but don't directly support Earth Silurian hypothesis.)
- How does the hypothesis intersect with [[forbidden-archaeology]] claims about suppressed pre-human artifacts?
