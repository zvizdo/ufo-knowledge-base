# Phenomenology Categories

**Type**: meta-framework / lens  
**Applied to**: incident pages, craft-phenomena pages, entity-encounter descriptions

Classification systems that structure how UAP/UFO encounters are described and compared. Multiple systems coexist; the KB uses Hynek as primary and Vallée as a secondary overlay.

## Hynek Scale

Developed by astronomer and Project Blue Book consultant [[j-allen-hynek]], later a major proponent of serious investigation.

| Code | Name | Definition |
|------|------|------------|
| NL | Nocturnal Light | Anomalous light at night; no associated craft structure |
| DD | Daylight Disc | Object observed in daytime with discernible shape |
| RV | Radar-Visual | Simultaneous radar and visual confirmation |
| CE1 | Close Encounter 1st Kind | Craft observed within ~500 feet; no physical effects |
| CE2 | Close Encounter 2nd Kind | Physical trace evidence: ground marks, electromagnetic effects, physiological effects on witnesses |
| CE3 | Close Encounter 3rd Kind | Entities or occupants associated with the craft |
| CE4 | Close Encounter 4th Kind | Witness abduction or being taken aboard (added post-Hynek) |
| CE5 | Close Encounter 5th Kind | Bilateral, intentional human-initiated contact (Steven Greer's addition — contested) |

## Vallée Classification

[[jacques-vallee]] proposed a parallel system that decouples *appearance* from *behavior*:

- **AN** (Anomaly): unexplained natural phenomena
- **MA** (Maneuver): vehicle performing controlled maneuvers
- **FB** (Flyby): straight-line trajectory
- **CE** (Close Encounter): broadly matching Hynek's CE categories
- Plus a phenomenological rating (1–5) combined with a "phenomenon type" rating

Vallée's system is important because it captures the *intentional* or *interactive* quality of encounters — some UAP seem to respond to being observed, a pattern that cross-cuts the Hynek scale.

## Abductee / Contactee / Experiencer Typology

A social-framing distinction that matters for credibility analysis:

| Term | Implication |
|------|-------------|
| **Abductee** | Taken against will; often traumatic, involuntary physical examination |
| **Contactee** | Ongoing voluntary communication with non-human entities, often with a mission or message |
| **Experiencer** | Neutral catch-all; someone who has had an anomalous encounter regardless of framing |

[[whitley-strieber]] resists the "abductee" label; the Telepathy Tapes subjects (autistic children) fit neither category. [[andrija-puharich]]'s work with [[uri-geller]] (not in this KB yet) spans contactee and psi domains.

## East Coast UAP Observables (2014–2015) — [[ryan-graves]]

The [[2014-east-coast-uap]] incident cluster adds a distinct set of instrumentally-confirmed observables that challenge conventional classification:

| Observable | Description |
|---|---|
| **Stationary-in-extreme-wind** | Objects held perfect position in ~120-knot winds with zero drift or positional correction |
| **Geometric shape** | Dark gray/black cube inside a clear sphere (~5–15 ft diameter) — see [[cube-in-sphere]] |
| **Multi-object coordinated formation** | Three to seven simultaneous objects; four in line-abreast in the GOFAST video |
| **Over-water exclusivity** | Objects operated exclusively over water; never observed crossing over land |
| **IR masking** | Appeared as undifferentiated energy blur on ATFLIR — uniform surface emission rather than concentrated jet exhaust |
| **Speed range** | 0.0 Mach (stationary) to 1.2 Mach; no exhaust plume at any speed |
| **Triple-sensor corroboration** | APG-79 radar + ATFLIR + AIM-9X seeker all independently confirmed physical objects |

Under Hynek: these encounters are primarily **RV** (Radar-Visual) events, with the near-miss incidents elevating to CE1 (within 500 ft). Under Vallée: **MA-2** (vehicle performing controlled maneuvers with physical effects on witnesses/craft).

Notably, the East Coast objects did **not** display the reactive/interactive behavior Fravor observed in the 2004 Tic-Tac — they appeared indifferent to the aircraft approaching them, continuing their own activity.

## Application in This KB

- Every incident page notes the applicable Hynek classification in its frontmatter or Account section.
- Entity encounter descriptions note whether the beings were described, interacted, or were inferred.
- The contactee/experiencer distinction is applied on person pages to distinguish testimony type.
