# JFK Assassination — UAP Motive Thesis

Source summary: [[09KP8XVf5nY]]

## Core Thesis

President Kennedy's assassination on November 22, 1963 was motivated at least in part — and possibly primarily — by his intent to share UAP/NHI intelligence with the Soviet Union as a unifying geopolitical framework. This would have required dismantling the CIA's secrecy architecture around the UAP issue and would have been perceived by elements within the intelligence community as an existential threat to that infrastructure.

[[harold-malmgren]]'s direct assessment, from his final recorded interviews:

> "Do I think so? Yes. I think it was probably the number one issue."

This is a firsthand opinion from a senior economist and White House insider who had direct access to figures across the Kennedy administration and its aftermath. It is not a documented evidentiary claim; it is an informed judgment from someone positioned to have heard things not in the public record.

## Malmgren's Logic

The argument proceeds as follows:

1. Kennedy was aware of the UAP/NHI phenomenon at a classified level.
2. Kennedy believed the phenomenon represented a shared challenge — or shared reality — that the US and USSR could use as the basis for cooperation rather than confrontation.
3. His framing: *"Let's drop the swords down and let's come up with a way to develop our futures without being mortal enemies."* UAPs as a unifying external referent.
4. Acting on this required sharing intelligence with Moscow — which required overriding or bypassing the CIA's control over UAP-related information.
5. The CIA, and specifically figures like [[allen-dulles]] (whom Kennedy had fired after the Bay of Pigs), had strong institutional and personal reasons to prevent this.
6. The result was assassination.

## Supporting Vectors

### JFK Letter to CIA Director McCone (FOIA-released, ~2005)
The [[jfk-letter-mccone]] document: Kennedy wrote to CIA Director John McCone asking for UAP-related data held by NASA, explicitly framing the request around the need to **"deconflict with the Soviets"** — to ensure that neither side misidentified UAPs as the other's weapons during a period of extreme nuclear tension. The letter reveals Kennedy was actively working the UAP-Soviet interface at an official level, not merely speculating.

### Soviet Ambassador Dobrynin's Approach to Malmgren
At a Washington social gathering, Soviet Ambassador Anatoly Dobrynin approached [[harold-malmgren]] and raised the UAP subject unprompted. Dobrynin described it as something that was **"dividing your country but maybe could unify our two countries."** This suggests the Soviets were aware of the US internal fractures around the UAP secrecy question and were themselves interested in a cooperative framework — mirroring Kennedy's own interest.

### Howard Hunt's Deathbed Statement
CIA officer [[howard-hunt]], on his deathbed, told his lawyer [[douglas-caddy]]: *"It was about the alien presence."* Hunt was involved in the Bay of Pigs operation and had documented connections to CIA circles that overlapped with the Kennedy assassination investigation. His statement is unverified and contested, but it is consistent with the broader thesis.

### Allen Dulles as Motivating Factor
[[allen-dulles]] was fired by Kennedy after the Bay of Pigs disaster — a humiliation for the Agency and a personal one for Dulles. He was subsequently appointed to the Warren Commission investigating Kennedy's assassination, a structural conflict of interest that has drawn sustained scrutiny. Dulles's personal motivation to prevent Kennedy's UAP-cooperation agenda aligns with his institutional role in maintaining CIA secrecy structures.

### James Angleton and James Forrestal
[[james-angleton]], CIA counterintelligence chief, is identified in various accounts as a key figure in the UAP secrecy infrastructure. [[james-forrestal]], the first Secretary of Defense, was reportedly in acute distress over UAP-related secrets before his death in 1949 — an early data point suggesting the UAP issue had been generating internal political casualties for years before Kennedy's presidency.

## Greer's GAPS Claim (areO7Mej44E)

[[steven-greer]] adds a different specification of the motive in [[areO7Mej44E]]:

> *"Jack Kennedy was killed over this issue and the constellation of programs attached to GAPS and particularly the science and technology branch of it."*

"GAPS" is the name Greer uses for the relevant classified program cluster — it is unclear whether this is a specific acronym he has seen in documents or a label he uses descriptively. Greer states he has people with "actionable intelligence" confirming this is the motive.

Greer also claims that Eisenhower's farewell address (January 1961, warning about the military-industrial complex) was a direct expression of the same threat JFK subsequently confronted — that the secrecy structure had decapitated the presidency on Eisenhower's own watch.

Additionally, Greer claims that **9/11 was engineered as a deliberate diversion** when the Disclosure Project's May 9, 2001 National Press Club event (800 million viewers) was about to force the issue into the open. 9/11 occurred approximately four months later. This claim is highly contested and goes far beyond most other sources in this KB, which treat the JFK and 9/11 events as distinct.

> ⚠ Greer's GAPS claim and 9/11-diversion claim are presented without documentary evidence. They are single-sourced to Greer's private intelligence contacts and should be weighted accordingly.

## Epistemic Status

This thesis is **contested**. Malmgren's assessment is firsthand opinion, not documented evidence. The [[jfk-letter-mccone]] is the closest thing to hard documentary support, and it establishes intent without proving motive for assassination. Howard Hunt's statement is unverified. The overall case is circumstantial but sourced from individuals with direct proximity to the events.

The claim should be held as a serious hypothesis with partial evidentiary support, not as established fact.
