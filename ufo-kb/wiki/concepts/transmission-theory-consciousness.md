# Transmission Theory of Consciousness

The hypothesis, associated with [[william-james]] and [[aldous-huxley]], that the brain does not generate consciousness but rather **receives, filters, and transmits** a pre-existing consciousness field. On this model:

- Consciousness is non-local and fundamental (like an electromagnetic field)
- The brain functions as a radio receiver tuned to a specific signal — "playing" conscious experience the way a radio plays music
- Brain damage or lesions disrupt the reception (destroy the radio or specific components) but do not destroy the source signal
- Certain neurological configurations — unusual brain organization, savant profiles, autism, head injury, psychedelics — may reduce filtering, allowing reception of a broader or cleaner signal

## Primary Argument

[[diane-powell]] presents the transmission theory to [[ky-dickens]] in [[0qlppHc3-gg]] using the smartphone / radio analogy:

> "Your smartphone can turn on but it won't come to life without a signal coming from somewhere else."

And the Lucille Ball analogy: Ball temporarily received radio station signals through metallic dental fillings — the dental work acted as an antenna. The *experience* was real; it was transmitted from an external source through an unusual hardware configuration. The brain may work the same way.

## Challenge to the Localisation Model

Standard neuroscience holds that consciousness is produced by neural computation — damage specific areas, lose specific functions. The transmission theory does not deny this. Rather, it argues the function-loss cases are consistent with a receiver metaphor too: destroy part of the radio's circuitry and you lose specific bands or features. You cannot infer the music lives in the transistors.

**Kim Peek** (inspiration for Rain Man) is the key edge case: born without a corpus callosum, with several other major neurological anomalies, yet memorized 12,000 books word-for-word. Standard localisationism cannot explain this. The transmission theory does: Peek's unusual neural architecture may have left the filter unusually open.

## Relationship to Non-Speaking Autistic Telepathy

[[autistic-savant-telepathy]] fits the transmission theory naturally: non-speaking autistic individuals may have reduced default-mode-network filtering of the consciousness field, allowing them to receive information held in other minds. [[diane-powell]]'s 90%+ hit rates become theoretically coherent on this model — these children are not generating information, they are **receiving** it.

The model also explains why the telepathic connection is strongest with loved ones ([[julia-mossbridge]]'s finding): emotional attunement functions as a tuning mechanism — the receiver locks onto a specific signal more cleanly when the relationship is close.

- [[aldous-huxley]] — *The Doors of Perception*; psychedelics as filter-reducers

## Implications for UAP / NHI

If consciousness is transmissive and the brain is a tunable receiver, then [[nhi-telepathic-communication]] — human-NHI information transfer — may use the same channel as autistic-savant telepathy: a shared consciousness field accessible to minds that have reduced conventional sensory filtering. This would also make [[andrija-puharich]]'s work with psychic children intelligible — they were naturally low-filter receivers.

## Open Questions

- What is the physical substrate of the consciousness field? [[scalar-waves]] and the zero-point field are candidate mechanisms.
- Can filter-reduction be trained (meditation, psychedelics) to produce verifiable anomalous cognition?
- Does the transmission model make different predictions from the production model in cases like Kim Peek?

## Default Mode Network Silencing (Psilocybin)

In [[I9VyyV4-3Js]], [[brian-muraresku]] adds a neural correlate: psilocybin silences activity in the **Default Mode Network** (DMN) — specifically the anterior and posterior cingulate cortex, the region most associated with ego, self-referential thinking, future anxiety, and past rumination. This is the closest neural equivalent to "housing the ego in any one brain region."

When the DMN is quieted:
- The ego dissolves or recedes
- The "hard problem" boundary between self and world softens
- Muraresku frames this in terms of the transmission theory: the reducing valve becomes less effective, allowing more of the underlying consciousness field through

This connects Huxley's metaphor (reducing valve) to a specific, measurable neural mechanism. The DMN is not just philosophically interesting — it is the identifiable brain structure that psychedelics are targeting when they produce the effects Huxley described.

**Near-death experiences** show the same pattern: at the edge of death, the metabolic support for high-level cortical processing (including DMN function) collapses — and people report precisely the bliss, ego dissolution, and sense of merger with something larger that psilocybin produces pharmacologically. [[jesse-michaels]]'s age-5 NDE is a spontaneous instance of this.

## Active-Agency Extension (Gallimore)

[[andrew-gallimore]] in [[8XD1ZiuhXoY]] extends the transmission model to a more radical version: the signal is not passive. DMT doesn't merely reduce the filter — it makes the brain susceptible to **commandeering by specific external intelligences** that direct what the world model produces. In Huxley's model, more of a pre-existing consciousness field floods through. In Gallimore's model, specific agents with intentions seize control of the brain's world-modeling machinery.

This is [[directed-dmt-world]]: the distinction between passive reception (Huxley, Grof) and active directed performance (Gallimore).

[[stanislav-grof]] partly anticipated this: he argued NHI would interact via the brain, not by materializing physically. Gallimore takes this from "would interact" to "does interact, via DMT."
