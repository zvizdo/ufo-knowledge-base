# Reverse-Engineered Craft

**Type**: claims-thesis  
**Category**: recurring-claims

The assertion that recovered non-human aerospace vehicles have been studied and reverse-engineered into operational (or proto-operational) craft by human programs — possibly beginning with the [[1933-magenta-crash]] transfer to Nazi Germany, continuing via [[operation-paperclip]] scientists into US programs.

## Kevin Knuth's Confirmation (IWui5cBkwoE)

[[kevin-knuth]] (physicist, UAlbany, ex-NASA) offers the most credentialed public endorsement of the reverse-engineering claim:

> "I think that's probably true. I've heard this from enough people who've done that kind of work for years now that that's probably the case."

He has personally met individuals who claim to have worked on recovered craft or seen them at bases — found them "entirely believable," with no sense of deception. The most credible detail they shared: "We don't know what anything is or how anything works." Knuth: "I was like, alright, that makes sense." This admission of ignorance despite years of work is more convincing than if they claimed to have figured things out.

Knuth was offered clearance at NASA but declined to preserve his ability to work in the open. He believes that whatever understanding has been achieved is "surface level — 'Oh, this is some interesting material. What's it made of?' That level."

The tech-transfer failure — if true — is itself significant: whatever physics the craft represent, it's not yielding to human analysis after decades of study, which is consistent with the performance envelopes Knuth calculates (5,000 G for Nimitz Tic-Tac; 11,000 G for JAL 1628).

## Bob Maguire's Technical Argument for the Pais Patents

[[bob-maguire]] adds the most technically grounded reverse-engineering argument in this KB: the three [[salvatore-pais]] Navy patents are interdependent — none works without the others — and their architecture only makes sense if Pais was working from a functioning system he needed to mathematically describe:

> "I'm going to guess they gave him some crash material and he figured out how to write down the equations for it."

The argument:
1. The patents are connected as a functional circuit: Patent A produces the input that Patent B processes, which drives Patent C to achieve spacetime warping. This is not how lone inventors typically patent ideas — it's how engineers document a working system they're trying to protect.
2. The Navy overrode the patent examiner's rejection — a rare and consequential step — suggesting either national security urgency or a need to establish IP protection for reverse-engineered technology.
3. Pais's choice of Schwinger QED (vs. Feynman's equivalent formulation) to implement the stress-energy tensor manipulation suggests he was fitting mathematics to existing observations rather than deriving from first principles.

This is currently the most technically detailed public argument that the Pais patents are reverse-engineered rather than independently invented.

## Eric Davis's Explicit Classified Refutation — No ARVs (LnAiNChnuEQ)

> ⚠ **Major Counter-Claim**: [[eric-davis]], who received a classified briefing from program insiders during AAWSAP, states definitively that **no ARVs were built and no reverse-engineering progress was made**:

> "I was given a firm hey in 1989 the CIA shut it all down because nobody us and we know the other companies… didn't make any progress after several decades of bestes."

Davis dismisses [[brad-sorensen]], [[mark-mccandish]], and [[bob-lazar]] as "Lazar type" mythology — self-believed accounts without program access. He confirms [[bruce-maccabee]] assessed ARV photographs as "hoaxes."

Davis's refutation distinguishes two separate questions:
- **Material custody** — Yes: companies like [[lockheed-martin]] hold crash retrieval materials since before 1989.
- **ARV construction** — No: no reverse-engineered functional craft were built.

This is currently the most direct, cleared-insider refutation of the ARV claim in the KB. It creates a genuine conflict with Kevin Knuth's confirmation above, Jake Barber's Russia/China RE craft account, and the Pais patent analysis. The KB reflects this conflict without resolving it.

> ⚠ **Conflict**: Davis's classified "no ARVs, no progress" briefing conflicts with Kevin Knuth's "probably true" assessment, Jake Barber's account of Russia/China RE craft, and the Pais patent interdependency argument. Davis has program access; the others have varied degrees of inside knowledge. This is an unresolved conflict.

## Electrogravitic Achievement Date and FFRDC Funding (D2tKCFmJjks)

[[michael-herrera]] was told by an anonymous Greer associate (who reportedly saw documents in a vault) that **electrogravitic control was achieved in 1954** — the earliest specific date claim in this KB for a functional antigravity/electrogravitic propulsion system.

[[uap-gerb]] adds the most detailed financial architecture for how RE programs are funded: through **FFRDCs** (Federally Funded R&D Centers: MITRE, RAND, CNA, IDA, Aerospace Corporation), **URCs** (University Affiliated Research Centers), and **MRTFBs** (Nevada Test Range, Edwards AFB). An anonymous Pentagon witness ("Ed," ~2012–2016 Air Force R&D role) reported $34 billion and $40 billion scrubbed from Air Force budget in separate years as non-transfer-of-authority funds for joint RE programs at Nevada Test Range and Edwards AFB.

This FFRDC conduit architecture is the operational mechanism by which RE programs remain invisible to congressional budget oversight — complementing the classification mechanisms described in [[atomic-energy-act-classification]].

## Russia/China RE Programs (Jake Barber via Herrera; Grusch via Michaels)

[[jake-barber]] told [[michael-herrera]] that **Russia and China both have reverse-engineered NHI craft** and their own retrieval teams. A "gentleman's agreement" exists: whichever team first reaches a downed craft claims it; the others wave off. This internationalizes the RE claims beyond a US-only program.

[[david-grusch]]'s public testimony adds the weapons-race dimension: per [[jesse-michaels]] in [[ePdH01pphbk]], Grusch described "an ongoing Cold War to reverse engineer the non-human technology found" with the US competing against China and Russia to produce the first modern super-weapons. The caveat: "once you use it, you burn it — and now everybody knows." This adds a strategic disclosure-suppression motive on top of the standard secrecy rationale — using the tech reveals the capability.
