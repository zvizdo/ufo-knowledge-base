# Eleusinian Mysteries

The most celebrated religious rites of ancient Greece, held annually at Eleusis (near Athens) for approximately 2,000 years — from ~1500 BCE to 392 CE. Central ritual: drinking the kykeon (grain-based drink likely containing ergot, a natural LSD precursor) followed by a profound transformative experience in a darkened hall.

## Psychedelic Hypothesis

Gordon Wasson, Albert Hofmann (LSD discoverer), and classical scholar [[carl-ruck|Carl Ruck]] argued in *The Road to Eleusis* (1978) that the kykeon contained ergotamine alkaloids from ergot-infected barley. This would make the Eleusinian experience a mass-administered psychedelic initiation — suggesting that ancient consciousness-expansion was institutionalized at the highest levels of Greek society.

## UAP Relevance

Cited in discussions of mystery schools as ancient protocols for accessing non-ordinary states of consciousness — potentially including contact with NHI or interdimensional entities. The convergence of mystery-school architectures across cultures (Eleusis, Merkavah, Daoist ascent) suggests either parallel discovery or a shared source.
