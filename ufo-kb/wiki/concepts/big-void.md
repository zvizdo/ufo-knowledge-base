# Big Void

Large internal cavity above the Grand Gallery detected by the [[scan-pyramids-project|ScanPyramids]] muon tomography project in 2017; Biondi argues it is the Grand Gallery itself misidentified, not a separate undiscovered chamber.

ScanPyramids announced the Big Void in 2017 based on muon tomography data: a roughly 30-meter-long cavity above the Grand Gallery in the Great Pyramid, of unknown function. Biondi disputes the interpretation — based on his SAR reconstruction of the same region, he argues the 'Big Void' detection is in fact the Grand Gallery itself, mis-localized due to the substantial noise in the muon data. The dispute is ongoing as of the episode recording.
