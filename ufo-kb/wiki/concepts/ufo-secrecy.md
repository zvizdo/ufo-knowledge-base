# UFO Secrecy

The systematic, multi-decade suppression and compartmentalization of UAP-related information across government agencies, defense contractors, and private entities. Distinguished from standard national-security classification by several anomalous features: the secrecy predates the National Security Act (1947 UAP wave immediately classified), it crosses presidential administrations without declassification review, and it has been maintained even from elected officials with relevant oversight responsibilities.

David Grusch's congressional testimony (2023) added a new dimension: he alleges the secrecy apparatus migrated out of the Executive Branch and into private contractor networks specifically to evade Congressional oversight and presidential authority. This would make UAP secrecy not merely a classification issue but a constitutional one — a program structure that operates outside the normal checks that govern classified programs.

Key mechanisms in the KB:
- [[majestic-12]] — alleged committee established by Truman to oversee UFO secrecy
- [[compartmentalization]] — the "need to know" structure that prevents any single person from having full knowledge
- [[deep-state-continuity]] — the institutional infrastructure that maintains programs across administrations
- The contractor migration thesis ([[david-grusch]]): moving programs to private entities to use corporate NDAs instead of government classification

The secrecy's existence is now less disputed than its scope. The 2023 UAPDA hearings made clear that even AARO has been denied access to programs that witnesses allege exist.
