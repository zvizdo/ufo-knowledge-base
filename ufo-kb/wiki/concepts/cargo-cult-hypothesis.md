# Cargo Cult Hypothesis

The cargo cult hypothesis holds that human civilization — its foundational achievements of agriculture, writing, astronomy, mathematics, and monumental architecture — arose not from autonomous human development but from a contact event with non-human intelligences (NHI) who transmitted knowledge to early humans. Humanity then built its religious and technological traditions around imitation and worship of what it received, like a cargo cult.

## Primary Source in KB

[[peter-levenda]] and [[tom-delonge]] developed this thesis collaboratively in the *Secret Machines* series. The *Gods* volume explicitly invokes the Prometheus myth as an encrypted account of the contact event.

## The Prometheus Tip

The most operationally significant version of this claim comes from Levenda directly: a senior government/intelligence advisor (name withheld) told Levenda and DeLonge ~2015 to study the Prometheus myth specifically. Levenda's interpretation:

> *"At some point, somebody did something they weren't supposed to do. It might have jumpstarted civilization on this planet."*

The Prometheus myth, decoded: a non-human being (Prometheus) transgressed against the gods (other NHI, or NHI governing council) on behalf of humans — giving them fire (knowledge, technology) — and was punished for it. The myth would thus be an encrypted account of a real event: one NHI faction providing technological gifts to humanity against the wishes of another NHI faction.

If accurate, this implies:
1. NHI factions exist with different orientations toward humanity
2. Some NHI have actively intervened to accelerate human development
3. This intervention was *contested* — it violated rules of an NHI governing structure
4. The CIA/intelligence community at some level *knows this* and has chosen Prometheus as the mythological frame to encode it

## The Oannes Account

Levenda's second major example: the Sumerian account of Oannes — a fish-headed being who emerged from the Euphrates and taught humanity mathematics, writing, agriculture, and astronomy. Levenda notes that the Catholic bishop's mitre (the tall pointed hat) is a direct descendant of the Oannes fish-head hat — the priestly class preserving the memory of the original contact event in its ritual costume.

The Oannes account appears in the writings of Berossus (3rd century BC) quoting much older Babylonian sources. It describes a being who gave humans all the arts of civilization in one transaction — after which nothing new was added that was not already present in seed form. This matches the cargo cult model precisely: a one-time transmission event from which all subsequent civilization unfolds.

## Connection to Ancient Astronaut Hypothesis

The cargo cult hypothesis overlaps with but is distinct from the [[ancient-astronaut-hypothesis]]:
- Ancient astronaut theory: NHI physically visited and were interpreted as gods
- Cargo cult hypothesis: NHI transferred *knowledge* deliberately, and human civilization is the accumulated imitation of that knowledge

The Levenda/DeLonge framing emphasizes the *deliberateness* of the transfer and the *contested nature* of it (Prometheus punished, implying violation of NHI norms).
