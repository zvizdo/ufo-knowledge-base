# Plasma Discharge Theory

## Core Claim

[[robert-schoch]]'s thesis, developed in *Forgotten Civilizations* (2012): during massive solar coronal mass ejection (CME) events, charged particles flood Earth's upper atmosphere and create large-scale **plasma discharge** phenomena — structured, geometric electrical discharges visible across the sky, similar to aurora borealis but vastly more intense and geometrically complex.

These events:
1. Generated extreme heat at ground level → [[vitrification-evidence|vitrification]] of stone surfaces
2. Created dramatic visual patterns in the sky that ancient peoples recorded as sacred imagery

## The Recording Thesis

Schoch argues that several enigmatic ancient traditions are records of plasma discharge sky events:

| Site | Medium | Claim |
|------|--------|-------|
| [[nazca-lines]] (Peru) | Ground geoglyphs | Imitations of plasma sky patterns |
| [[easter-island]] Rongo Rongo | Carved tablets | Plasma designs recorded as "script" |
| [[colossus-of-memnon]] (Egypt) | Burn marks | Directional plasma heat marks |
| [[kepkesi]] (Turkey) | Vitrified basalt | Surface melting from plasma heat |

The Nazca figures and Rongo Rongo glyphs, on this view, are not astronomical calendars or a writing system — they are visual recordings of terrifying plasma events, preserved in the only materials available to the survivors.

## Laboratory Corroboration

Schoch cites laboratory plasma discharge experiments: when electricity is applied to scaled-down atmospheric models, the resulting plasma forms **geometric, fractal, and animal-like patterns** very similar to the shapes found at Nazca. Mars's Valles Marineris gorge (cited by Lroy) shows a feather-like scarring pattern consistent with large-scale plasma discharge on a planetary surface.

## Schoch's Broader Framework

Schoch developed this theory to explain:
- The end of the last Ice Age (~12,000 BP) without a confirmed cometary impact
- The sudden collapse of multiple civilizations at the same period globally
- The apparently inexplicable vitrification of ancient sites on multiple continents

He lays this out in *Forgotten Civilizations* alongside his Sphinx water erosion work.

> ⚠ Note: Schoch's plasma discharge theory is not widely accepted in mainstream geology or solar physics. The "plasma discharge" mechanism he invokes operates differently from modern CME science, which does not predict ground-level heat generation at vitrification temperatures.
