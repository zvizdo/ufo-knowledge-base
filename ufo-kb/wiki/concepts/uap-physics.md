# UAP Physics

UAP physics refers to the theoretical framework — or frameworks — needed to account for the observed capabilities of UAP: extreme acceleration without apparent inertia effects, trans-medium operation (air to water to space), silent supersonic travel, apparent gravity manipulation, and the ability to appear and disappear from radar.

**Observed capabilities requiring explanation**: Based on sensor data and witness testimony across military-grade UAP cases:
- Instantaneous acceleration to hypersonic speeds without sonic boom
- 90-degree turns at speed (incompatible with inertial forces on known aircraft)
- Trans-medium operation without transition signatures
- Apparent ability to manipulate local electromagnetic and gravitational fields
- Reported radiation effects on nearby observers (→ [[ufo-electromagnetic-injuries]])

**Leading theoretical approaches** in the KB:
- **[[electrogravitics]]**: direct coupling of electromagnetic fields to gravitational mass/acceleration
- **[[uap-propulsion]]**: overview of competing drive mechanisms
- **Inertial frame modification** (Haisch/Rueda quantum vacuum approach): reducing effective inertia by manipulating the vacuum quantum field
- **[[gravity-electromagnetism-unification]]**: if gravity and EM are aspects of the same field, EM-driven gravity control follows
- **[[salvatore-pais]] patents**: Navy patents claiming high-frequency EM field effects on gravity and inertia

**Classified physics divergence**: The [[reverse-engineering-hypothesis]] implies that UAP physics has been studied in classified programs since at least the 1950s, and that public theoretical physics diverged from classified understanding. The disappearance of electrogravitics research from public literature circa 1956 is cited as evidence.
