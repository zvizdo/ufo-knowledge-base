# Dissociative Identity Disorder

**Dissociative Identity Disorder (DID)** — formerly called Multiple Personality Disorder (MPD), renamed in 1994 — is a psychiatric condition in which a person has two or more distinct identity states (alters) that recurrently take control of behavior, accompanied by amnesia between states.

- The dissociative barrier between alter states means that an alter programmed to execute an assignment has complete amnesia when the primary identity is active
- Trauma-induced DID provides the foundation; additional alters can be conditioned via hypnosis and pharmacology
- [[gh-esterbrooks]]'s "moon is clear" technique is precisely the engineering of a triggered, task-specific alter
