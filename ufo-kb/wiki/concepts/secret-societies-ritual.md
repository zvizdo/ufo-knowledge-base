# Secret Societies and Ritual

The pattern of elite groups using shared ritual practice — drawn from mystery school, occult, pagan, or initiatory traditions — as a bonding, filtering, and coordinating mechanism. Relevant to UAP-adjacent discourse because the same networks (intelligence community, defense contractors, policy elites) that allegedly control UAP information share membership in secret societies with ritualistic practices.

## The Bohemian Grove Case

The clearest contemporary Western example: the [[bohemian-grove]] Cremation of Care ritual. [[alex-jones]] provides a firsthand account (2000) of approximately 1,000 elite men sincerely performing what he characterizes as a pagan/occult ritual fusing Moloch worship with Faustian and Druidic occultism.

The significance for UAP discourse: if elite power networks are bound by initiatory ritual rather than simply institutional role, their behavior around UAP secrecy may be partially explained by esoteric commitments — not merely bureaucratic or strategic interests.

## Mystery Schools Parallel

Jones (and [[jesse-michaels]]) connect Bohemian Grove ritual to the broader [[mystery-schools]] tradition: initiation rites simulate death/rebirth to collapse the sensory apparatus and produce transmission-mode consciousness. The "outer group" receives a simplified narrative; the inner initiates receive direct experiential access.

This connects to [[transmission-theory-consciousness]]: ritual as technology for temporarily bypassing the reducing-valve brain and accessing broader consciousness.

## Skull & Bones / Other Societies

Not treated in [[KhmUixSXxDo]] but part of the same pattern: Yale's Skull & Bones (Bush family, Kerry, many CIA directors), Freemasons, Rosicrucians, and similar societies as coordination and initiation networks overlapping the intelligence/defense/financial sectors.
