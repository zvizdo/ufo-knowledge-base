# NHI Contact Protocols

NHI contact protocols are proposed frameworks for initiating, establishing, or structuring communication with non-human intelligence. The category spans a wide range of approaches:

**Technical (SETI-adjacent)**: Radio and optical SETI assumes NHI communicates via electromagnetic signals; protocols involve monitoring for patterns inconsistent with natural phenomena. Active SETI (METI — Messaging to Extraterrestrial Intelligence) involves sending deliberate signals toward target star systems.

**Consciousness-based**: [[ce-5-protocols]] developed by [[steven-greer]] use group meditation, intentional focus, and light signaling. The premise is that NHI responds to consciousness rather than to electromagnetic transmission. Remote viewing-adjacent approaches similarly use altered states as the contact medium.

**Structured ritual as template**: In the KB, the [[eleusinian-mysteries]] page proposes that ancient mystery traditions (Eleusinian, Dionysian, shamanistic) functioned as structured protocols for NHI contact — the initiatory experience producing the altered state necessary for contact, with the secrecy requirement protecting the integrity of the contact framework. If ancient traditions successfully established contact by manipulating consciousness state in specific structured contexts, modern protocols that replicate those consciousness states (psychedelics, sensory deprivation, meditation) may be functionally equivalent.

**Diplomatic protocols**: At the government level, if UAP represent NHI with territorial claims or strategic interests, contact protocols would need to include diplomatic frameworks — the framework that allegedly produced the "Eisenhower treaty" era agreements. See [[1954-eisenhower-ufo-meeting]] and [[nhi-government-contact]].
