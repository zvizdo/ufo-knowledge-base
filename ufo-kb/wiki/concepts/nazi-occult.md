# Nazi Occult

The Nazi occult refers to the integration of esoteric, mystical, and pseudo-scientific ideas into the ideology and programs of the Third Reich, particularly within Heinrich Himmler's SS apparatus. While mainstream Nazi ideology was formally materialist and anti-Christian, a significant strand within the leadership — primarily centered on Himmler — drew on German Romantic mysticism, runic traditions, Aryan origin myths, and occult practices.

**Thule and Vril Societies**: The [[thule-society]] was an early post-WWI German occultist/nationalist organization from whose membership the Nazi Party partly emerged. The [[vril-society]] (partly fictional, partly historical) is associated with claims about channeled contact with non-human intelligences and advanced energy technology. These organizations provided part of the ideological matrix for Nazi racial mythology.

**Himmler and the SS-Ahnenerbe**: [[heinrich-himmler]] established the [[ss-ahnenerbe]] (SS Ancestral Heritage Research Institute) in 1935 to conduct pseudo-scientific research supporting the racial ideology and esoteric beliefs of the SS. The Ahnenerbe sponsored expeditions to Tibet (seeking Aryan origins), investigations of runestones and sacred sites, and research into occult healing practices. The Wewelsburg Castle was redesigned as an SS ceremonial center with explicitly pagan and occult symbolism.

**UAP and die Glocke**: The Nazi occult intersects with UAP claims primarily through [[die-glocke]] (The Bell) — an alleged classified Nazi technology project of uncertain nature, sometimes described as an anti-gravity or dimensional device, whose exotic properties are attributed to occult-informed physics research. The V-weapon program and post-war Operation Paperclip also feed into the claim that Nazi advanced research extended beyond known technology.

**Operation Paperclip**: The Nazi occult frame also informs claims that [[operation-paperclip]] scientist transfers to the US and USSR brought not only rocketry knowledge but classified exotic physics data — potentially including anomalous material from alleged crash retrievals during the war.
