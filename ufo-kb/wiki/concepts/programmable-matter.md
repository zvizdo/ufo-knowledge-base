# Programmable Matter

## Definition

Programmable matter is the umbrella concept for materials whose shape, mechanical properties, or optical surface can be **reconfigured on command** — typically through swarms of active micro- or nano-scale units that share state with one another. The civilian R&D lineage runs through MEMS, [[smart-dust]], and [[utility-fog]]; the alleged classified application that motivates this concept-page in the KB is the use of foglet-like swarms to mimic mountain rock as a building skin that opens only to authorized aircraft.

## Public-record milestones

- 1989 — [[j-storrs-hall]] design paper on [[utility-fog]].
- 1993 — Hall's follow-up paper at a NASA conference.
- 2001 — DARPA demonstrates [[smart-dust]] in field tests.
- 2004 — MEMS technology matures significantly.

## Applied hypothesis

In [[OOr98r51Sj0]], programmable matter is the candidate mechanism for the [[holographic-mountain-bases|holographic-mountain]] phenomenon depicted in [[sentinels-of-ether]] — and observationally consistent with the *craft-vanishing-into-mountains* pattern at [[skinwalker-ranch]], [[secret-mountain-sedona]] / [[bradshaw-ranch]], [[mount-hayes]], [[mount-perdido]], [[mount-inyangani]], and [[mount-ziel]].
