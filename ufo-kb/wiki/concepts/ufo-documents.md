# UFO Documents

UAP-related primary source documents constitute the evidentiary foundation for evaluating government knowledge and program history. The category spans a wide credibility range:

**Authenticated government records**:
- The [[halt-memo]] (1981): Lt. Col. Charles Halt's official memorandum to the UK Ministry of Defence documenting the Rendlesham Forest incident
- Project Blue Book files (1947–1969): USAF investigation records; declassified and now publicly accessible
- CIA UFO records (released via FOIA): Partial declassification of CIA assessments from the 1950s–1990s
- DIA [[dia-uap-report]] and Wilson-Davis memo (contested authenticity)
- AATIP/AAWSAP study documents

**Congressional records**:
- David Grusch, David Fravor, Ryan Graves testimony (2023 HPSCI hearing)
- Schumer-Rounds UAPDA amendment text
- AARO historical review (2024)

**Disputed documents**:
- [[majestic-12]] documents: Alleged 1947–1952 executive briefings; authenticity disputed; FBI investigation found probable forgery
- Wilson-Davis memo: Alleged 1997 conversation between Admiral Wilson and Dr. Davis about SAP programs; provenance disputed

**FOIA landscape**: The Freedom of Information Act has been the primary mechanism for obtaining authenticated UAP documents. Key FOIA requesters include the Black Vault (John Greenewald Jr.) and various researchers. Redaction patterns in released documents are themselves analyzed as evidence of what remains classified.
