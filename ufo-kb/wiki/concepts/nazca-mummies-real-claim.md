# Nazca Mummies Are Real Organisms (Claims-Thesis)

The core empirical claim in the Nazca mummy case: that the M-type (hominid) bodies are genuine biological organisms that were once alive — not fabrications, not ancient humans with ritual mutilations, and not modern constructions.

## Who Asserts It

- [[john-mcdow]] — American Forensics Association president; "data suggests perhaps living organisms" (firsthand examination)
- [[jim-caruso]] — Denver chief medical examiner (firsthand examination)
- [[david-ruiz]] — Peruvian Society of Legal Medicine president; asserts definitively non-human (firsthand examination)
- [[jose-zalce]] — Mexican Navy medical division chief; definitively real, possibly extraterrestrial (firsthand examination + DNA review)
- [[jaime-maussan]] — primary promoter; asserts absolute certainty
- [[jesse-michaels]] — probabilistic assessment: "gun to my head, I think the M-types are probably real"

## Supporting Evidence

1. **No mutilation markers**: Three independent forensic teams specifically looked for finger/toe mutilation and skull-binding artifacts; none found any evidence of either.
2. **CT/DICOM anatomical self-consistency**: Image reconstructions from CT scans show internally consistent anatomy. J-type DICOM files show capillaries nourishing eggs.
3. **Pregnant Monzerat**: A fetus inside [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies|Monzerat]] appears to be tridactyl — if confirmed, accusation of fraud requires claiming medieval Peruvians had cloning capabilities exceeding modern US biotech.
4. **GLI3 mutation**: [[elena-hardy]] found a digit-development mutation in NCBI database that is not commonly known even in genetics circles — hard to plant deliberately.
5. **Osseointegrated implants**: [[osmium-cadmium-implants]] have organic tissue fused to them, indicating the beings were alive when implanted — impossible to fabricate in a dead specimen with osseointegration.
6. **David Grusch**: Privately told Rep. [[eric-burlison|Eric Burlison (Berles)]] that some mummy physiology is congruent with what he saw in classified briefings.

## Who Disputes It

- [[flavio-estrada]] / [[peruvian-ministry-of-culture]]: Claims fingers deliberately mutilated and lengthened (disputed by three forensic teams). Also responsible for the wooden-figurine conflation debunk.
- Anonymous proteomics expert: One isolated J-type head returned alpaca bone proteins (applicable to J-types, not M-types per se).
- [[steve-meera]] report: One male + one female finger-bone DNA result from Maria's body — unresolved (contamination vs. composite construction).
