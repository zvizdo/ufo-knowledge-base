# Subrosa Quantum Communication

## Concept

Communication system architecture proposed and partially demonstrated by [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]] in which the transmitter is designed to cancel the electric and magnetic field components of an electromagnetic emission while preserving an underlying quantum signal. The receiver must be a quantum-only device (Josephson-junction-based) operating at cryogenic temperatures because ordinary EM detectors generate currents from incoming E and B fields and therefore see nothing when the E and B components are absent.

The term "subrosa" (under the rose) reflects the architecture's defining property: the signal travels beneath the layer of EM that conventional detection equipment monitors.

## Provenance

[[hal-puthoff]] (Gf_tKn9TaP8) describes the program timeline:
- **1990s:** Puthoff demonstrated proof-of-principle on a classified contract — confirming both that the transmitter could be designed to suppress E/B while preserving the quantum signal and that quantum detection in principle worked.
- **Shelved:** the available quantum-detector hardware (cryogenic circuitry near absolute zero) was not mature enough to package as a deployable system.
- **Present (2025):** the explosion of investment in quantum computing has produced commercial-grade [[josephson-junctions|Josephson junction]] receivers operating at ~3.7 K. Puthoff partnered with [[john-paul-dejoria|John Paul DeJoria]] to take the program off the shelf and build production-grade demonstrators.

## Applications

Per Puthoff:

- **Through-shielding communication.** The application that motivated CIA's initial 1972 concern about Soviet remote viewers reading classified documents from superconducting safes ([[1972-sri-quark-detector-session]]).
- **Submarine communication.** Seawater is highly conductive and blocks low-frequency EM (the reason ELF radio is the only conventional option); the quantum signal is not stopped.
- **Plasma-blackout communication.** Apollo reentry comms were lost during atmospheric ionization; the same EM-blocking that causes blackout would still pass the quantum signal.
- **Consciousness-substrate research.** Puthoff explicitly ties the quantum receiver class to [[microtubule-quantum-consciousness|Penrose-Hameroff microtubule]] biology: if the brain itself contains quantum-signal-sensitive structures, this is the receiver category they live in.

## Theoretical Framing

The architecture sits within Puthoff's broader [[extended-electrodynamics]] / scalar-and-vector-potential research program, in which the 4-potential is treated as fundamental and the E, B fields as derived ([[aharonov-bohm-effect|Aharonov-Bohm]] analogy). Puthoff holds two patents on engineering with vector and scalar potentials only (no E/B fields).

Connection to UAP physics ([[metric-engineering|metric engineering]]): the same shift from field-based to potential-based engineering that drives the polarizable-vacuum model also gives the engineering handle for through-shielding communication. In [[Rfmy5oW_r9c]], Puthoff confirmed to Jesse Michaels that he believes the [[project-preserve-destiny|Project Preserve Destiny]]-style psychic communication channel runs on the same underlying scalar-field substrate.
