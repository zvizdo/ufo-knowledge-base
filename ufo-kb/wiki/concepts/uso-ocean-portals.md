# USO Ocean Portals

Claims-thesis that UAP/USO regularly emerge from specific, identified deep-ocean zones — implying the existence of underwater non-human installations, dimensional portals, or transit points.

## Tim Burchett's Five Ocean Zones

Congressman [[tim-burchett]] publicly stated that the US government has identified **five (or six) specific areas in the world's oceans** where UAP/USO emerge on a regular basis. Per [[joe-rogan]]'s paraphrase:

> "Tim Burchett recently said that there's — they've identified five areas in the oceans of the world where these things are coming out of on a regular basis. We have a higher propensity of sightings around these five or six, I believe, deep-water areas."

The regularity of emergence from fixed geographic points implies:
- The ocean floor locations are not random; they are functionally significant to whatever is emerging
- The entities/craft are based in or transit through the ocean, not just occasionally entering it
- The US government has accumulated enough sighting data to identify the pattern

## USO Phenomenon Background

Unidentified submerged objects have been reported since at least the mid-20th century, often by naval personnel. Key characteristics in the literature:
- Craft observed entering/exiting water at speeds impossible for human submarines
- Simultaneous surface and aerial tracking in some cases
- Emergence patterns near deep-ocean trenches and geological features

## Baltic Sea Anomaly as Fixed-Location Candidate

The [[baltic-sea-anomaly]] (BSA) — a 60-meter circular object at 90 m depth in the Baltic Sea, discovered by [[ocean-x]] in 2011 — represents a potential physical example of a fixed-location seafloor USO installation. It is not a sighting of an emergent craft but a static object with:
- Consistent and reproducible EM interference (GPS loss confirmed by Stockholm University)
- Sub-bottom profiling showing it is separated from the seafloor (not geological)
- Possible internal corridors and energy output (per Finnish ROV operator)
- Biological material anomalies on the surface

The BSA is significant here as it may be a decommissioned or stationary "made to purpose" craft per the framing in [[immaculate-constellation]] — designed for a specific mission at depth. See [[dennis-asberg]] and [[aGqFOxuYRT0]].

## Connection to the 2014 East Coast Encounters

The 2014–2015 East Coast UAP encounters (see [[2014-east-coast-uap]]) were tracked operating over and around the ocean. The possibility that these objects were using the ocean as a base of operations is structurally consistent with Burchett's five-zone claim.

## Interpretive Frameworks

**Underwater installations**: NHI maintain permanent or semi-permanent underwater bases, using deep ocean as cover from satellite observation. The "five zones" are entry/exit points for these installations.

**Dimensional portals**: The ocean zones are stable access points between our physical dimension and wherever NHI originate — the physics of deep water somehow facilitates the transition.

**Transit hubs**: The zones are waypoints in a transit network, not permanent bases — analogous to airports rather than cities.

## Open Questions

- Are the zones correlated with geological features (trenches, hydrothermal vents, magnetic anomalies)?
- Does the Bermuda Triangle's historical pattern of disappearances correlate with any of the five zones?

## Richard Dolan's USO Database (GQlWf54K_7Y)

[[richard-dolan]] has independently compiled ~670 USO cases (see [[usos-volume-1]]) and found systematic patterns. His EM interference finding (see [[uso-em-interference]]) is the most statistically significant: 10% baseline, 20% military, 40% in entity/abduction cases. His interpretation: "There's an intelligence and these guys — they know what they're doing. They know exactly what they're doing."

Key historical USO cases Dolan has documented:
- **[[1825-hms-blonde]]**: Orange spherical USO, South Pacific, 1825 — published diary of ship's naturalist
- **[[1945-uss-delarof]]**: Disc ~200ft, emerged from Pacific near Adak Island, circled transport ship twice
- **[[1971-uss-jfk-orb]]**: Glowing orb, USS Kennedy carrier; communications and weapons offline

Dolan's view on USO retrieval: "I believe that's probably a yes" — citing the [[global-marine-corporation]]/[[glomar-explorer]] as proof of deep-ocean retrieval capability, and an unverified claim of a USS Nautilus side hatch for disc acquisition.
