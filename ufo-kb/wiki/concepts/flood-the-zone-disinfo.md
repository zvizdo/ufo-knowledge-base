# Flood-the-Zone Disinformation

A strategic information management model described by [[richard-dolan]] to explain the apparent shift in US government UAP information strategy from the late 1970s through the 1980s.

## The Model

Earlier US government strategy (1950s–1970s): **pure denial**. The policy was simply "there is nothing to investigate; these are misidentifications or delusions."

The flood-the-zone strategy (1980s onward): **structured partial disclosure embedded with falsifiable elements**.

> "And flood the zone doesn't mean put out BS. It means put out partial truths and put out a lot. That is the way disinformation works."

Dolan's inoculation metaphor:
> "So maybe you put it out, but you sandwich it between things or you stick at least one thing in there that can be factually demonstrated to be a hoax or a lie. And that's an inoculation. It's like you get inoculated against a disease by getting part of a virus, but it's deadened and your body recognizes it and can fight it. So it's like you become inoculated against the truth."

## Mechanism

The technique works by ensuring that any true information released is *surrounded by* or *bundled with* false information. When the false information is later exposed as false, the public/research community discards the entire package — including the true components. This is more effective than pure silence because:

1. Pure silence creates a pressure differential — researchers know something is being hidden, and the mystery grows
2. "Sandwiched" truth can be released and then effectively buried by exposing the wrapper as false
3. It allows plausible deniability: "We gave you information; you couldn't distinguish the real from the fake — that's a you problem"

## Historical Application

The MJ12 documents are the paradigm case Dolan discusses:
- Released through the [[bill-moore]]/[[jamie-shandera]] pipeline in 1984
- Sent from near [[kirtland-afb]] — connected to [[richard-doty]]
- Sophisticated period-accurate content (the potentially true component)
- Debunked by Philip Klass et al. on signature grounds (the potentially planted false element)
- Result: even researchers who believed in UAPs were uncertain about MJ12, and the public was inoculated against taking them seriously

## Paul Bennewitz as Paradigm Case

The [[paul-benowitz]] operation was a more extreme version: pure disinformation without embedding much truth. [[richard-doty]] deliberately fed Bennewitz false information about underground alien bases and alien threats until Bennewitz had a psychological breakdown. [[bill-moore]] participated and later admitted to it. This operation may represent the *failure mode* of flood-the-zone: when the disinformation is too extreme, it creates a visible victim and a discoverable pattern.

New detail from [[WBTqsbwJyqU]] (Greg Bishop interview): the operation involved pre-programming a computer with fabricated alien communications and piping signals to it from operatives stationed across the street (likely NSA). [[j-allen-hynek]] may have physically delivered the computer. Fake video footage (old films of dinosaurs and Roman battles) was piped to Benowitz's CRT display. [[gabe-valdez]] (state police) witnessed the setup firsthand. [[harry-rositzki]] — a retired CIA Soviet specialist known as "Falcon" — was the senior handler who orchestrated the broader operation. The Benowitz affair was a small cog in a much larger Cold War CI wheel; it became famous only because it intersected the UFO community.

## Alex Jones as Target (Non-UAP Instance)

[[alex-jones]] describes an analogous pattern from the perspective of a media target in [[KhmUixSXxDo]]:

> "It was always like, 'Look, the New York Times and Washington Post are all reporting on how 4chan says they're slaves in a pizza place in DC.' So then we go, 'Is it true?' And just look at the anomalies. Then the media goes, 'Jones made this up.' And I'm like, 'Whoa, whoa, whoa. You're the ones put it out.'"

Jones's account suggests the flood-the-zone mechanism operates in reverse for media targets: disinfo is *credited to* a target after the target merely amplified claims already in mainstream media — then the target is scapegoated as the originator. When QAnon emerged, Jones identified the pattern and refused to endorse it, explicitly calling it a disinfo group designed to "set up" populist commentators. See [[scapegoat-mechanism]].
