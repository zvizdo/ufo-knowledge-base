# Three-Body Problem / NHI Science Suppression

The thesis that non-human intelligences (NHI) systematically monitor, interfere with, and sometimes eliminate human scientists who approach key thresholds of scientific knowledge — particularly in nuclear, plasma, and fundamental physics domains.

## The Analogy

Liu Cixin's *The Three-Body Problem* (2008) — written by a Chinese power plant engineer — features Trisolarian extraterrestrials who, upon learning of humanity's existence, deploy **Sophons** (sub-atomic quantum-locked probes) to lock down human fundamental physics. The Sophons create false results in particle accelerator experiments, effectively preventing humans from advancing beyond a certain scientific threshold. Scientists who get too close to understanding the lockdown are targeted directly.

The domains they suppress: nuclear, plasma, and particle accelerator physics — specifically the sciences that govern the rules of reality itself.

## Government Endorsement

- During the **Bush administration**, key players in the federal government were assigned to read *The Three-Body Problem*. This is not fiction: it was policy-relevant reading for national security officials.
- **[[harold-malmgren]]** — presidential adviser to JFK, LBJ, Nixon, and Ford, briefed on UAP — told [[jesse-michaels]] that this is "how the non-human intelligences operate: by tracking the frontier of human innovation."
- **[[pippa-malmgren]]** — Harold's daughter and member of George W. Bush's National Economic Council — was specifically tasked with reading the novel.
- Harold Malmgren stated NHI "were opposed to anything which threatened their control."

## The Apocryphal Obama Account

An unverified account holds that Barack Obama, when pressed by Pearl Jam singer Eddie Vedder around a campfire about the true nature of how the world works at the highest level, reportedly replied: *"Read the Three-Body Problem."* The source explicitly flags this as apocryphal; no primary documentation exists.

## Application to the Missing Scientists Pattern

The episode frames the [[scientists-targeting-pattern]] through two competing lenses:
1. **Terrestrial:** Nation-states, black-budget programs, commercial interests targeting human capital
2. **NHI:** Something operating above the level of human clearance systems — an unseen force "shaping our timeline from somewhere above it"

The Three-Body Problem framework is the most explicit articulation of the NHI interpretation. It requires:
- NHI awareness of human scientific progress
- NHI capability and motivation to interfere with specific human scientists
- A connection between the scientists being targeted and proximity to threshold discoveries in physics, consciousness, or propulsion

## Sensitivity Domains

Consistent with the novel and with Malmgren's testimony, the domains most associated with targeting in UAP discourse are:
- Nuclear and plasma physics
- Particle accelerator physics
- Anti-gravity and electrogravitic propulsion
- Consciousness and remote viewing
- Exotic materials science

These are also the domains covered by [[physics-lockdown]].
