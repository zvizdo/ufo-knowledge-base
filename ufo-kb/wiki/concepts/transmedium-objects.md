# Transmedium Objects

Unidentified Aerial Phenomena observed operating across multiple physical media — typically transitioning between air and water without slowing down, creating wakes, or producing conventional propulsion signatures. The behavior suggests a propulsion mechanism indifferent to medium density.

- USS Omaha (2019): multiple UAPs tracked entering the ocean; no wreckage recovered

- Multiple deep-sea emergence zones identified by UAP researchers
