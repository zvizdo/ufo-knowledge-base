# Non-Lethal Weapons

Non-lethal weapons (NLW) are weapons designed to incapacitate, disorient, or influence targets without killing them — though all such weapons can cause death at sufficient doses or in vulnerable individuals. The category includes:

**Directed energy**:
- Active Denial System (ADS): millimeter-wave microwave that causes intense skin heating sensation and drives people away; deployed by US military
- Laser dazzlers: temporarily blinds targets
- High-intensity acoustic devices (LRAD): directed sound causing pain and disorientation

**ELF/EM systems**: In the KB, the most relevant non-lethal weapons category is [[elf-weapons]] — electromagnetic weapons operating in the extremely low frequency (ELF) range, some claimed to affect nervous system function and potentially induce altered mental states. [[voice-of-god-technology]] is a related claimed capability.

**Havana Syndrome connection**: The injuries suffered by US diplomatic personnel in Cuba and elsewhere ([[2016-havana-syndrome]]) are consistent with a directed microwave or acoustic NLW — causing brain injury, tinnitus, and neurological symptoms consistent with focused RF or ultrasound exposure. This represents possible state-level deployment of NLW against diplomatic personnel.

**UAP proximity**: NLW research and UAP-related injuries (radiation burns, neurological effects from close encounters) occupy overlapping phenomenological territory — some UAP-proximity injuries are consistent with directed energy exposure, raising questions about whether some apparent UAP encounters involve NLW testing rather than or in addition to genuine NHI contact.
