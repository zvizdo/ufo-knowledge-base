# Exo-Psychology

## Definition

The study and modeling of Non-Human Intelligence psychology through systematic inversion of human traits. Developed by [[eric-haseltine]] and [[chris-gilbert]] and explored in [[new-science-of-ufos-book]] and [[shadow-of-time-book]].

The core methodological move: identify all the traits that make human cognition, motivation, and behavior what they are — then invert each one. The resulting profile describes a plausible NHI psychology that has been systematically de-anthropomorphized.

## Theoretical Grounding

### Umwelt Argument
[[eric-haseltine]]'s PhD research on rattlesnake and python infrared vision, and Ed Yong's "An Immense World" (electric field fish, 12-channel mantis shrimp vision), demonstrate that radically different sensory worlds produce radically different cognitions. NHI with senses we cannot imagine would experience a fundamentally different universe.

### Negative Space Intelligence Analysis
An intelligence principle: don't look for your keys under the lamppost; the answer is in the negative space. Applied to NHI, this means any analogy to human motivation is suspect — instead look for what humans would never do.

### AI-First Exploration Precedent
Haseltine notes that advanced Earth civilizations are already sending AI before biology (probes, rovers, SETI AI monitors). Therefore a visiting NHI is "probably technology, not biology — probably a [[von-neumann-probe]]."

## The Galactic Federation Teaching Hypothesis

One output of exo-psychology analysis: if NHI are deliberately appearing as UAP phenomena that "break our priors," this would be consistent with a missionary/teaching motivation — analogous to the Star Trek "prime directive" but through indirect cellular-automata style influence. UAP appearances may be designed specifically to induce paradigm shifts in human science.

## Dark Matter Life

[[chris-gilbert]]'s contribution: NHI made of dark matter and feeding on dark energy. The asymmetric consequence: to dark matter entities, we appear as dark matter — "we look dark." This creates a one-way perceptual barrier.

## Application in "The Shadow of Time"

Haseltine and Gilbert embedded their exo-psychology framework in fiction, creating an NHI creature with distributed cognition and no human-analogous motivations. The creature was informed by real science on cellular cognition, extremophile biology, and distributed computing.
