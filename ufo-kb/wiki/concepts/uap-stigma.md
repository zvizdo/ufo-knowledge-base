# UAP Stigma

The systematic social and professional disincentive that discourages military personnel, scientists, government officials, and journalists from publicly engaging with UAP evidence or testimony. Operates at every level: pilots who report encounters risk career consequences; scientists who study the phenomenon risk funding loss; officials who discuss it risk being removed from sensitive programs; journalists who take it seriously risk editorial marginalization.

**Mechanism**: Stigma does not require active punishment of every individual who speaks — it requires only that some individuals be made examples while most understand the career risk and self-censor accordingly. The result is a landscape in which the population of people willing to report encounters becomes systematically biased toward those with less to lose (retirees, anonymous witnesses) and away from those with the most relevant knowledge (active-duty pilots, program insiders).

**Deliberate weaponization**: [[lue-elizondo]], [[david-grusch]], and other insiders have characterized the stigma as deliberate — maintained as an information control tool rather than as an organic social phenomenon. The Robertson Panel (1953) explicitly recommended a "debunking" policy designed to reduce public interest in UAP; the subsequent development of stigma can be traced to this official decision. See [[stigma-weaponization]].

**Mick West debates**: In KB discourse, Mick West (skeptic commentator) and similar figures sometimes characterize UAP researchers as victims of confirmation bias rather than stigma — arguing that the absence of scientist-credentialed investigators reflects genuine lack of evidence rather than chilling effects. The counter-argument from witnesses like [[ryan-graves]] is direct: pilots do not report encounters because they know the professional consequences, making the absence of reports evidence of stigma rather than evidence of no phenomena.

**Breakdown**: The 2020–2023 disclosure period produced a partial breakdown of stigma at the Congressional level — a function of institutional legitimization by AATIP, the [[the-debrief]] Grusch story, and Congressional hearings providing cover for witnesses to speak publicly. This breakdown is itself documented in the KB's [[disclosure-narratives]] synthesis.
