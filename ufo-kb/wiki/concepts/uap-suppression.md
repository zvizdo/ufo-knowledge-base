# UAP Suppression

UAP suppression refers to the systematic and deliberate concealment of UAP evidence, crash retrieval programs, reverse-engineering activities, and related intelligence from the public, from most members of Congress, and from senior government officials who do not hold the relevant clearances. The claim is not merely that UAP programs are classified (which is uncontroversial) but that active measures have been taken to prevent oversight and disclosure.

**Mechanisms**: UAP suppression is alleged to operate through several overlapping mechanisms:

- **Classification architecture**: Special Access Programs (SAPs) and Unacknowledged SAPs (USAPs) under the SCI system can be withheld from all but a small number of cleared individuals; even senior cabinet officials (including Presidents) may not be read in. See [[compartmentalization]].
- **Contractor capture**: Moving programs into private [[military-industrial-complex]] contractors removes them from the direct oversight available to Congress and Inspector General offices.
- **Witness intimidation**: Multiple UAP whistleblowers and witnesses have reported being told their testimony was classified, threatened with prosecution, or pressured to recant. David Grusch and others have testified to Congress about retaliation.
- **Disinformation campaigns**: The [[robertson-panel]] (1953) explicitly recommended debunking and discrediting UFO reports; [[project-blue-book]] is cited as a public-relations exercise rather than a genuine investigation; the [[condon-committee]] report is widely regarded as politically predetermined.
- **Evidence destruction/removal**: Claims include recovered materials being removed from crash sites by military personnel and physical evidence suppressed before civilian investigation could occur.

**Apollo context**: [[apollo-program]] imagery and lunar surface data are cited in claims about suppressed anomalous photography — [[farooq-el-baz]]'s control over the Apollo image archive is cited as meaningful in this context.

**Legislative response**: The [[uap-disclosure-act]] (2024) represents Congress's attempt to legislatively compel disclosure of classified UAP programs through an independent review board with declassification authority — a direct response to the suppression framework.
