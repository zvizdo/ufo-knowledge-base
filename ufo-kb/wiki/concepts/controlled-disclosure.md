# Controlled Disclosure

## Core Thesis

The public UAP revelations that have accumulated since 2017 are not the product of investigative journalism discovering hidden government programs, nor of genuine whistleblowing that caught the government off guard. They are **authorized, managed releases** — the intelligence community controls what is revealed, when, in what form, and at what resolution.

[[danny-sheehan]] is the primary asserter of this thesis in this KB, though variations appear across the disclosure community.

## Evidence and Examples

### The 2017 NYT Story
- [[lue-elizondo]] and [[chris-mellon]] coordinated the December 17, 2017 New York Times story about AATIP and the three UAP videos (Gimbal, Go Fast, FLIR Nimitz).
- Sheehan's characterization: the three videos were **government pre-cleared** — they were deliberately selected as the lowest-quality, least revealing material from a much larger archive. The story was an authorized disclosure step, not a leak.
- The fact that government clearance was obtained before publication confirms that someone in the chain of command approved the release.

### David Grusch's Congressional Testimony (July 26, 2023)
- Grusch's testimony before Congress was extraordinary in its specific claims (crash retrievals, NHI biologics, government programs concealed from Congress).
- But Grusch received **authorization from the Intelligence Community Inspector General** to make his disclosures publicly. This is not what genuine whistleblowing looks like — it is an authorized channel for information that the IC decided it was acceptable to surface.

### The Schumer Amendment (2024 NDAA)
- The most practically significant recent disclosure step: a requirement that government entities compile UAP-related data and transfer it to Congress/National Archives.
- The key provisions that would have made disclosure genuinely threatening to the control apparatus — **eminent domain** (government reclaims materials from private contractors) and an **independent nine-person review panel** — were stripped out through Lockheed lobbying via [[mike-turner]] (House Intel Chair) to [[mike-johnson]] (House Speaker).
- What remains is a data-compilation requirement without enforcement teeth.

## Who Controls the Pipeline

Sheehan's structural argument:
- The intelligence community — specifically the covert apparatus descended from [[brown-brothers-harriman]] through [[allen-dulles]]'s CIA — retains ultimate control over UAP information.
- The 2017–2024 release sequence is managed to:
  1. Shift public awareness toward acceptance of UAP reality (managing "ontological shock")
  2. Drive congressional appropriations for aerospace/defense programs (the "unfunded opportunities" dynamic [[ben-rich]] described)
  3. Protect private contractor IP and classified reverse-engineering programs from eminent domain or independent oversight
  4. Prevent the most destabilizing information (NHI communication, biological specimens, theological dimensions) from reaching an unprepared public

## What Is Being Withheld

Per Sheehan's account, the genuinely undisclosed material includes:
- Physical specimens (NHI biological material)
- High-quality craft imagery (vs. the deliberately low-resolution videos released)
- Direct communication records with NHI (the Stein/S4 account)
- The full scope of what [[project-blue-book]] classified section documented

## Relationship to Other Frameworks

> ⚠ Tension with [[disclosure-narratives]]: Sheehan's "controlled disclosure" thesis is not the same as slow/gradual disclosure. It implies active management by a capable institutional actor — not the slower institutional process of declassification norms gradually eroding secrecy. The managed-release thesis implies intentionality; the gradual-erosion thesis implies entropy.
