# The Nine

## Description

The Nine (also "Council of Nine") are a group of nine purported non-human entities who first communicated through [[andrija-puharich]]'s research circle at the [[roundtable-foundation]] in Rockland, Maine, on New Year's Eve 1952. They were channeled through an Indian psychic named [[dr-venad]] who fell unexpectedly into trance.

## Self-Description

In recorded sessions, the Nine described themselves as:
> "We are the positive principles of cosmos. We penetrate, visit persons. We work through this study but it is under our control. We nine."

When asked what "the nine" meant, they said: "It's hard to explain. We're not personalities as you think of personality. We're more like laws or principles of the universe."

They are variously associated with:
- Nine Egyptian gods (not specified which nine)
- A council of nine cosmic intelligences with governance-like concern for Earth
- Non-human entities watching nuclear weapons sites and responsible for UAP sightings near nuclear bases

## History of Contact

**1952 (New Year's Eve):** First channeled through [[dr-venad]] at Roundtable Foundation, Maine. Puharich's archive contains tape recordings of this session. Thousands of pages of transcripts were produced.

**1971:** Re-emerged when Puharich hypnotized [[uri-geller]] in Israel. Geller began channeling the Nine — the same entities, ~19 years after Dr. Venad's sessions. At the time, Puharich was still running [[intellect]], his covert thought-transmission research company, leading researcher [[greg-malazzi]] to question whether the Nine communications were genuinely anomalous or were being synthetically transmitted via Puharich's technology.

**Mid-1970s:** Channeled through [[phyllis-schlemmer]] during sessions attended by [[gene-roddenberry]], [[gordon-cooper]], and others at Puharich's Ossining, NY compound. Also emerged through some of the [[space-kids]] when hypnotized by Puharich.

## Nuclear Site Connection

The Nine claimed in channeling sessions to be "kind of behind a lot of the sightings that are occurring on nuclear bases" and described concern about humanity's capacity for self-destruction. This aligns with the broader [[ufo-nukes-connection]] documented since the 1940s.

## The Deep Space Nine Connection

[[gene-roddenberry]] attended multiple Nine channeling sessions and wrote an unproduced 130-page script called "The Nine." Star Trek: Deep Space Nine — which premiered after Roddenberry's death — features nine entities and a character named "Venad," directly mirroring the 1952 channeler.

## Interpretive Debate

Three main interpretations exist:
1. **Genuine NHI contact** — the Nine are real non-human entities of cosmic significance
2. **Manufactured via technology** — Puharich's [[elf-waves]] and thought-transmission devices (TD-100, prosthetic finger implant) may have been used to simulate the Nine to test group psychology or weaponize belief
3. **Disinformation operation** — the CIA funded Puharich and may have orchestrated the Nine narrative as a psychological or social control experiment
