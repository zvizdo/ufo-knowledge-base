# Biefeld-Brown Effect

The Biefeld-Brown effect, described by [[thomas-townsend-brown]] in the 1920s and developed through the 1950s, is the production of thrust from an asymmetric capacitor under high voltage — the positive electrode region experiences apparent attraction toward the negative plate, producing net movement. Brown and his collaborator Paul Biefeld (Denison University Observatory) documented the phenomenon; Brown spent decades arguing it represented a coupling between electromagnetism and gravity.

## Mainstream vs. Alternative Interpretations

Mainstream physics attributes the observed thrust primarily to **ion wind** (charged air molecules accelerating between electrodes), not a gravity-electromagnetism coupling. Brown disagreed, claiming the effect persisted in vacuum conditions.

The controversy remains unresolved because Brown's most striking vacuum experiments are incompletely documented, and the classified context of his later work (see [[thomas-townsend-brown]]) makes independent replication difficult.

The standard ionic wind model predicts the thrust should **vanish in vacuum** — where there are no air molecules to ionize and accelerate. This prediction was long used to dismiss electrogravitic claims. Brown himself observed anomalies that ionic wind cannot explain: thrust correlating with the diurnal cycle (time of day), the sidereal cycle (Earth's position relative to fixed stars), and solar activity — suggesting a gravitational or cosmological coupling.

## Vacuum Survival — Charles Buhler / Exodus Results (mOWwdIuyaQA)

[[charles-buhler]] and [[andrew-arjima]] at [[exodus-propulsion-technologies]] have conducted **over 2,000 distinct test-article variants** since their collaboration began around 2016, establishing the most systematic modern replication of the Biefeld-Brown effect. In [[mOWwdIuyaQA]] (the most detailed public account), Buhler describes:

- **Weight reduction demo**: thruster on a scale loses ~0.1 g (≈1 mN) at −480 V; flipped, it gains weight — direction follows thruster orientation, not lab frame, ruling out scale attraction.
- **Hard vacuum spinner**: Bluetooth-actuated dual thruster in a Faraday cage inside vacuum chamber; 14 mm peg deflection at turn-on = ~2.5 mN. No ion wind possible; Coulomb attraction to walls eliminated by cage grounding.
- **Power-off persistence**: thrust continues after DC power disconnected (charge trapped in dielectric); [[charles-buhler]] compares this explicitly to the Casimir effect — no ongoing energy input required.
- **Fine structure constant α** appears in force magnitude scaling across variants — key indicator of quantum (not classical) mechanism.

These results confirm and extend the [[jacque-corneau]] 1956 Paris vacuum findings while using modern instrumentation and rigorous Faraday cage + reversed-direction controls.

[[charles-buhler]] and colleagues at Exodus Propulsion Technologies (formerly of NASA Kennedy Space Center) published results approximately 2022–2023 reporting that asymmetric capacitor thrust **persists in hard vacuum**. If confirmed under independent replication, this invalidates the ionic wind explanation as sufficient. Competing explanations under discussion:

1. **Residual gas contamination** — skeptics argue that achieving true hard vacuum is difficult and trace atmospheric residue could explain diminished but non-zero thrust
2. **Electrostatic forces on the test apparatus** — the apparatus might be attracted to vacuum chamber walls in ways that mimic thrust
3. **Genuine electrogravitic coupling** — as Brown originally claimed
4. **Vacuum polarization** — at sufficiently high field strengths, the quantum vacuum itself can be polarized, potentially producing force effects not captured by classical ionic wind models

## Key Materials — The Dielectric Connection

The effect is strongest in capacitors using **high-K dielectric materials** — materials with very high permittivity. Empirically effective dielectrics include:

- **Barium titanate** (BaTiO₃)
- **Strontium titanate** (SrTiO₃) and strontium compounds generally
- **Calcium copper titanate** (CCTO, CaCu₃Ti₄O₁₂) — what [[gary-mckinnon]] is building experiments with
- **Molybdenum alloys** — in certain configurations

These are precisely the exotic chemicals found in [[gary-mckinnon]]'s [[non-terrestrial-officers]] spreadsheet: **barium, strontium, and molybdenum** in fleet-to-fleet and ship-to-ship transfer records. ## The 1956 Vacuum Confirmation (RTEWLSTyUic)

[[jacque-corneau]], a French Air Force officer and technical representative for Sud-Ouest aircraft, facilitated Brown's experiments at the Montgolfier facility in Paris in 1956. In a vacuum of at least **10⁻⁶ torr**, the observed residual thrust was **several orders of magnitude larger than residual ambient ionization could account for** — eliminating the ion-wind explanation for at least some of the thrust. Corneau confirmed these findings in a deathbed interview with [[paul-schatzkin]] in 2008 (Corneau was 98 years old).

This is the most rigorous documentation of the effect in vacuum on public record and directly undermines the standard mainstream dismissal.
