# Acid Mine Drainage

Modern industrial chemistry process whereby sulfuric acid (often from oxidized H2S) extracts metals from iron-bearing ores; central to Jeffrey Drum's reconstruction of the pyramid complex as a metal-extraction facility.

Acid mine drainage is the H2SO4-mediated dissolution of metal-bearing ores — the same mechanism modern heap-leach gold and silver mining exploits. Drum identifies this as the exact chemistry required by his pyramid-as-chemical-factory thesis: hydrothermal iron ore veins beneath the Giza plateau, sulfuric acid generated from karst-derived [[hydrogen-sulfide]], and the resulting metal-bearing solution processable by precipitation or electrochemistry to extract gold, silver, electrum, platinum, and rare earths.
