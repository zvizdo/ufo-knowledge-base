# USO (Unidentified Submerged Object)

Unidentified Submerged Objects (USOs) are the underwater counterpart to aerial UAP — objects observed operating in or transitioning between water and air at capabilities that exceed known submarine or autonomous vehicle technology. USO reports include:
- Objects descending from air into water with no splash or cavitation signature
- Underwater motion at speeds (hundreds of knots) impossible for known submarines
- Objects ascending from water into air and continuing as aerial UAP
- Underwater craft detected by military sonar at depths below known submersible limits

**Historical documentation**: [[richard-dolan]] has documented USO reports in US Navy and maritime records dating to the 19th century — predating the modern UAP era and suggesting the phenomenon is not a product of Cold War radar technology. The longevity of underwater anomalous object reports is parallel to the aerial UAP historical record.

**Trans-medium operation**: USOs are the primary evidence class for UAP trans-medium capability — the ability to operate seamlessly in multiple physical media (air, water, space) without transition artifacts. The [[2004-nimitz-tic-tac]] UAP descended into the ocean and was subsequently detected on sonar, establishing trans-medium operation for at least one modern military case.

**Water-world habitat hypothesis**: [[water-world-ufo-habitat]] develops the thesis that some UAP are based in Earth's oceans rather than arriving from outside — using the deep ocean as a concealed operational environment. USOs are the primary evidence for this hypothesis.

**Physics implications**: The sonar-reflective but non-water-displacing properties of some reported USOs (the [[1977-hmnzs-southland-uso]] is cited as "physics-breaking" by Kevin Knuth) suggest either extreme density control or a different relationship to bulk matter than known underwater vehicles.
