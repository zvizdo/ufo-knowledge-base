# Fine Structure Constant (137)

The **fine structure constant** (α) is a dimensionless physical constant approximately equal to 1/137.036. It characterizes the strength of the electromagnetic interaction between elementary charged particles. It determines:
- How strongly electrons and photons interact
- The scale of atomic structure
- The behavior of electrons in magnetic fields

## Why 137?

The number's mystique in physics:

- **Feynman**: Richard Feynman famously described α as "the greatest mystery in physics" — a pure number with no known derivation from deeper principles. He reportedly encouraged physicists to put a plaque bearing 137 on their lab walls and meditate on it daily.
- **Lederman**: Nobel laureate Leon Lederman (1988) reportedly lived in a house numbered 137.
- **Eddington**: Early (wrong) attempt to derive 137 from first principles
- The constant appears wherever light and matter interact — it is, in a sense, the "tuning" of our universe's electromagnetism.

## The Khafre Pyramid Claim

[[armando-mei]] argues in his books that the proportions of the [[khafre-pyramid]] at [[giza-plateau]] encode the value 137 — specifically the ratio that mirrors the inverse fine structure constant. Per [[filippo-biondi]] in [[zrmG2jBckgk]]:

> "[Armando] is convinced that the number 137 is related to — especially to — Khafre pyramid."

The claim is not yet peer-reviewed and Biondi acknowledges he has not fully read Mei's books. It belongs to a broader tradition of mathematical encoding in ancient Egyptian architecture — which includes pi, phi (golden ratio), and the Orion correlation — though the fine structure constant claim would be the most extraordinary if verified.

## Significance if True

If the Khafre pyramid proportions genuinely encode α = 1/137:
- The builders would have required knowledge of quantum electrodynamics millennia before the concept existed in Western science
- This would be either: (a) evidence of a vastly advanced pre-historical civilization, (b) a numerical coincidence, or (c) evidence of mathematical universals being encoded via different conceptual routes
- It would parallel other cases where ancient structures appear to encode constants only "discovered" in modern science (e.g., pi in the Great Pyramid)
