# Schumann Resonance

Natural extremely low frequency (ELF) electromagnetic resonances of the cavity between the Earth's surface and the ionosphere, first proposed by physicist W.O. Schumann in 1952 and measured experimentally in the 1960s. The fundamental mode resonates at approximately 7.83 Hz, with harmonics at ~14.3, ~20.8, ~27.3 Hz, and higher.

**Physics**: The Schumann resonances arise because lightning discharges (approximately 50 per second globally) excite the Earth-ionosphere cavity. The cavity behaves as a waveguide with a resonance frequency determined by the Earth's circumference and the speed of light. The resonances are continuously present in Earth's electromagnetic background.

**Consciousness research connection**: The fundamental Schumann resonance (7.83 Hz) falls within the alpha brainwave range (8–12 Hz) and near the theta range (4–8 Hz). This overlap has generated claims — ranging from credentialed biophysics research to speculative New Age claims — that human consciousness is entrained to or evolved in resonance with Earth's electromagnetic field. [[robert-monroe]]'s [[monroe-institute]] research used binaural beats in this frequency range to induce altered states; some researchers have claimed Schumann resonance changes correlate with human physiological and psychological states.

**ELF weapons adjacency**: In the KB, Schumann resonance appears in [[elf-waves]] as a naturally occurring component of the ELF electromagnetic environment that ELF weapons research must account for. Its frequency overlap with brainwave frequencies is also cited in [[voice-of-god-technology]] and related mind-influence research contexts.
