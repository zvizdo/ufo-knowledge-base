# Holographic Mountain Bases

## What

The "holographic mountain bases" framing is the recurring witness pattern across multiple sites where **what appears to be solid rock opens to allow authorized craft to pass through, then re-seals** as the craft enters or exits. The pattern shows up in several otherwise unconnected reports:

- [[sentinels-of-ether]] — fictional Coyame canyon: a 1,500-ft mountain face "pixelates from 3D to 2D" as helicopters slip through; F-18 chase suffers total electrical failure
- [[skinwalker-ranch]] — Bigelow describes a UFO "shooting straight into the mountain itself, but there's no explosion. It's just gone." Fugal describes a UFO "entering the east field portion of the ranch below a helicopter, then exiting from a mesa"
- [[bradshaw-ranch]] + [[secret-mountain-sedona]] — Coulthart witnesses a golden orb escorted by Blackhawks; GPR confirms a 50-ft reinforced steel-concrete tunnel
- [[mount-hayes]] / [[mount-perdido]] / [[mount-inyangani]] / [[mount-ziel]] — 1973 Pat Price + later Joe McMoneagle remote viewings of mountain underground bases connected as network nodes

## Candidate mechanisms

1. **Holographic projection** — [[william-arkin]]'s 1999 article on a 1994 Air Force concept document for massive 3D-sky projection; supported by [[bob-lazar]]'s S4 holographic-book account (via [[robert-bigelow]]) and [[steven-greer]]'s [[raytheon]]-at-[[fort-irwin]] allegation. Limitation: requires perfect weather; a mountain-sized 3D illusion is an "insane power bill" that breaks on monsoon rain.
2. **Programmable matter / [[utility-fog]] / [[foglets]]** — [[j-storrs-hall]] (1989, 1993 NASA paper). Better fit for the [[sentinels-of-ether]] mountain mechanic: physical pass-through, audio-rectification side effect, asymmetric EM kill, friend-or-foe coding.
3. **Built underground installations with conventional camouflage** — [[joe-mcmoneagle]]'s [[mount-perdido]] reveal (mountain *being built* by humans with helicopters and cargo crates) supports a conventional [[breakaway-civilization]]-built [[deep-underground-military-bases|DUM]] reading for at least that node.

## Why it matters

If mechanism (2) or even (1) is real, the implication is that [[invention-secrecy-act|classified programs]] have operationalized programmable-matter / holographic camouflage decades ahead of the civilian record — and the pattern of "UFOs vanishing into rock" across multiple witness sets becomes a coherent observation rather than independent anomalies.
