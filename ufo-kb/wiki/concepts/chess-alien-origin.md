# Chess — Alien Origin Thesis

**Primary advocate:** [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]] — former FIDE president (23 years), former president of Kalmykia.

The thesis that chess was not invented by any human culture but transmitted to humanity by an extraterrestrial or advanced non-human civilization. Ilyumzhinov holds this as both an intellectual argument and a firsthand-informed belief — the NHI beings he encountered in 1997 did not explicitly state this, but the "Vasily Smyslov" incident (where a translucent entity demonstrated a future chess game) reinforces the connection between NHI and chess.

## Evidence Claimed

### Archaeological Distribution

Chess sets and boards have been found through archaeological excavation on every major continent — in Mongolia, Peru, China, India, Japan, across Latin America and Africa — with the oldest specimens exceeding 5,000 years. At the time these games appeared, there was no long-distance communication or trade capable of distributing an invention globally. Ilyumzhinov: "How did chess get here? It must have been brought here by another civilization."

Archaeological note: The game's origins are historically traced to India (~6th century CE, the game Chaturanga) before spreading to Persia and then globally — the 5,000-year claim is contested by mainstream archaeology, which places chess much more recently. The oldest confirmed physical chess pieces (ivory, from the Gupta Empire period) date to ~6th–7th century CE. The 5,000-year archaeological pieces Ilyumzhinov references likely include game tokens or pieces from different board games misidentified as chess.

### The 64 Squares / 64 DNA Codons Alignment

The chess board has exactly 64 squares. Human DNA uses exactly 64 codons — three-nucleotide sequences that encode amino acids in the genetic code. Ilyumzhinov frames this as a non-coincidental information-transfer code: "This is, you know, like information transfer."

This argument is structurally similar to arguments made in the [[dna-intelligence-encoding]] concept — that DNA encodes information from a designing intelligence.

### Black/White Duality

All chess sets worldwide share the black/white binary. This maps to yin/yang (Taoism), light/dark energy (cosmology), the "law of unity and struggle of opposites." Ilyumzhinov sees this as a universally embedded code representing the fundamental structure of reality.

### The NHI-Chess Encounter (Smyslov / "Vasilei Smith")

A world chess champion privately told [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]] that a translucent alien entity appeared in his hotel room before a match in Germany and demonstrated the entire game on the board. The match played out identically the next day. This is presented as evidence of NHI active interest in chess — not just a historical transmission but ongoing engagement with the game. See [[vasily-smyslov]].

## Purpose Claimed by NHI (Via Ilyumzhinov)

The NHI beings — per Ilyumzhinov's 1997 encounter — told him that humanity needs to raise its civilizational level. Chess, he argues, is a tool they provided precisely for this purpose: it trains both hemispheres, teaches thinking ahead, and develops the cognitive infrastructure required for civilizational advancement. Ilyumzhinov's 23-year mission as FIDE president was to make chess universal — "1 billion chess players can truly change the world."

## Analytical Notes

- The mainstream archaeological case against the 5,000-year claim is significant and should be flagged
- The 64-square/64-codon alignment is arithmetically real but correlation; the two 64s have independent origins in game design and biology
- The "simultaneous global emergence" claim is the strongest anthropological puzzle — but is better explained by Silk Road and trade diffusion than by extraterrestrial transmission
- The NHI-active-chess-engagement (Smyslov incident) is hearsay ×2 and inherently unverifiable
- Despite analytical weaknesses, this is the most specific and memorable UFO-adjacent claim Ilyumzhinov makes — and has been repeated publicly enough to be a known part of his worldview
