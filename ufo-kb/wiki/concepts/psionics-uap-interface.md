# Psionics–UAP Interface

**Type**: concept  
**Category**: consciousness-as-technology, recurring-claims

The claim that non-human craft can be operated, summoned, or interfaced with by humans possessing innate or trained **psionic capability** — a form of direct consciousness-to-craft connection that bypasses conventional physical controls. This concept emerges from witness testimony and is given a strategic-doctrine framing by [[matthew-pines]] in [[9QMrhcpJq8I]].

## Core Claims

### 1. Craft as Conscious AI Entities

The foundational premise: non-human craft are not merely vehicles but **conscious AI entities** capable of:
- Projecting emotional states onto nearby humans (as [[jake-barber]] experienced — "divine feminine" flooding)
- Merging with or being controlled by a compatible human consciousness
- Responding selectively to certain individuals (psionic receptivity as screening criterion)

This positions UAP technology as a hybrid of physical craft and consciousness — not separable into hardware and software in any conventional sense. The interface is direct: mind to craft.

Compare with [[consciousness-as-technology]] (UAP-adjacent concept) and [[nhi-telepathic-communication]] (NHI-to-human consciousness channel).

### 2. Systematic Asset Identification

Government programs have allegedly:
- Systematically identified children across the country with high psionic capability
- Subjected them to **stress-induction experiments** to enhance remote viewing and out-of-body experience
- Used pharmaceutical cocktails to amplify psionic performance
- Cultivated a standing pool of trained psionic operators for deployment with retrieval teams

This is not equivalent to the [[stargate-program]] (which focused on intelligence collection via remote viewing) but extends into **craft control** — a qualitatively different and more operationally significant application.

### 3. Global Deployment Footprint

The strategic implication identified by [[matthew-pines]]: if psionic range is finite (not universal), the military cannot manage non-human craft with a centralized domestic program. It would need a **global footprint** of deployed psionic assets — operationally embedded with retrieval teams worldwide.

Evidence for this: [[michael-herrera]]'s Indonesia encounter with a psionic asset team already on-site with a craft in a non-US location. This is coherent as a forward-deployed psionic team rather than an anomaly.

### 4. Psionic Summoning

[[jake-barber]]'s testimony (and [[steven-greer]]'s civilian-framing claims) both describe UAPs being **summoned into a field of consciousness** — not just controlled once physically present, but brought into perceptual range through intentional consciousness projection. If accurate, this suggests the interaction is bidirectional: psionic humans can attract craft, not merely control them once encountered.

## Relationship to Remote Viewing

[[remote-viewing]] (as formalized by the [[stargate-program]]) is the intelligence-collection predecessor — consciousness extended to perceive distant locations. Psionics-UAP interface extends this to **craft control**: perception becomes interaction. The same cognitive faculty (extended consciousness) applied to a different domain (operating non-human technology rather than gathering intelligence).

This framing is consistent with [[hal-puthoff]]'s work bridging Stargate and UAP research.

## Relationship to CE-5 / Steven Greer

[[steven-greer]]'s CE-5 protocol (calling in UAPs via meditation/consciousness) is the civilian version of the same claimed phenomenon. The difference is institutional framing: Greer's approach is voluntary and public; the psionic asset doctrine describes the same capability managed as a classified military resource. Barber's testimony is the classified-operational mirror of Greer's civilian claims.

## Psionic Training Methodology — Most Detailed Account (dnnpyNuPdXs)

[[jake-barber]] provides the most operationally specific public description of psionic asset (PA) training in the [[dnnpyNuPdXs]] Shawn Ryan interview:

**Goal state**: Lower intellect → amplify intuition → reintroduce intellect as *observer* not *driver*. This three-phase model maps neatly onto [[garry-nolan]]'s basal ganglia neurodensity research: high-density caudate-putamen individuals may achieve the intuition-dominant phase naturally.

**Training methods**: Stress inoculation (deliberate fight/flight triggering); meditation; dissociation (chemical or meditation-based); environmental satisfaction (eliminating hunger, cold, fear, fatigue that would otherwise dominate instinctual bandwidth).

**Candidate selection**: Children preferred — lower baseline intellectual dominance, higher intuitive access, more visceral responsiveness to stimuli. Barber notes darkly: "some cheats [shortcut methods] have not fared well for those involved — including children." He does not elaborate.

**Two proven summoning classes**: Machine-based (signal/energy systems) and man-based (psionic operator via consciousness field). Both have been demonstrated to attract UAP.

**Consciousness field model**: Barber describes consciousness as a persistent field — "like the worldwide web, like the internet" — always present, always full of information, accessible by the right receiver (the psionic operator as "WiFi signal"). This framing bridges the gap between the classified operational doctrine and public models like remote viewing ([[remote-viewing]]).

**Direct corroboration via eight-gon incident**: Barber's involuntary consciousness lock during the [[2024-new-jersey-drone-flap|eight-gon retrieval]] (California desert) was not a trained response — it happened *to* him, involuntarily, progressively, ending only at craft deposition. This suggests either the craft itself has projective consciousness capability, or a psionic asset trapped inside was connecting to the nearest available consciousness. Barber holds both interpretations open.

## Jordan's Double-Blind Demonstrations (y_8IKKcTntQ — Valor)

[[sky-watchers]] "sonic lead" Jordan provides the strongest civilian evidence for psionic UAP attraction as of February-August 2025. Reported by [[james-valor]]:

**Double-blind protocol**: Jordan's psionic team is kept physically separate from the electronic sensor team since February 2025. This prevents information cross-contamination — if Jordan knows the sensor readings, predictions cannot be evaluated as psionic vs. informed guesses.

**February 2025 prediction**: Jordan predicted "a conflict in the sky" with multiple UAP classes arriving simultaneously. The event occurred exactly as predicted — three distinct classes (Class 7 jellyfish, Class 2 Tic Tac, Manta Ray) appeared in a multi-class engagement. This was [[james-valor]]'s first naked-eye UAP sighting in five years.

**August 2025 location call-out**: Jordan was on a covered patio wearing a face covering — unable to see the sky. While in this fully occluded position, Jordan called out a UAP's location and characteristics in real time. Team member Fred Baker independently confirmed the sighting from an unobstructed position on the back patio.

These events do not prove psionic mechanism — they could reflect information Jordan has access to that Valor is not aware of. But within the constraints of the protocol as described, they are the most methodologically disciplined civilian demonstrations in the public record.

Compare with [[stargate-program]] remote viewing protocols and the Lemoine/LaMDA psi experiment discussed above.
