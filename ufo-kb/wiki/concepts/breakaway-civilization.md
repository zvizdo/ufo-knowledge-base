# Breakaway Civilization

Hypothesis, most associated with historian [[richard-dolan]], that a covert group has achieved technological capabilities so far beyond public knowledge — through crash-retrieval reverse engineering, exotic propulsion, life-extension, or other means — that it constitutes a separate civilization operating inside human institutions but no longer subject to them.

The breakaway civilization concept differs from simple "deep state" theories: it posits not just secrecy but a *divergence* in capability — a group with access to physics and technology decades or centuries ahead of the public domain, able to act with effective impunity.

## Origin of the Term

[[richard-dolan]] coined the term "breakaway civilization" approximately 20 years before this interview (so ~2004–2006), inspired by reading historian [[arnold-toynbee]]'s meta-historical framework. Dolan's reasoning: if crash-retrieved technology is classified so deeply that it never re-enters public science, the custodians accumulate private patents and technological knowledge that diverges increasingly from the public domain. The question becomes: will they achieve functional exotic propulsion? Can they go off-world? Can they encounter NHI independently? If so, they are not merely "secret" — they are a *different civilization* that has broken away from the main branch.

[[catherine-austin-fitts]] independently arrived at the same concept through financial forensics — Pentagon accounting "discrepancies" of $2.3 trillion (announced September 10, 2001) and the global financial system operating as an "open window" rather than a closed accounting system. She concluded: some of this money is "going to that" — the breakaway infrastructure. She cited Dolan's work publicly (Tucker Carlson interview).

## Variants

- **Human-origin**: Reverse-engineered alien tech, Nazi advanced physics (Die Glocke, [[kammler-stab]]), or black-budget continuity across administrations.
- **NHI-hybrid**: Humans in cooperation with NHI, with access to NHI technology and knowledge.
- **Walter Bosley / Nenza variant**: A 19th-century American secret society that achieved exotic propulsion before the Wright Brothers and has maintained technological secrecy since ([[walter-bosley]]).
- **Prehistoric refuge variant** (Carlson): A previous technological civilization facing a known Taurid meteor stream cataclysm (~12,900 BP) established the Moon as a refuge. Their descendants represent a breakaway branch — technologically separate from Earth, possibly the NHI observed today. Carlson explicitly frames this as the most parsimonious explanation for *both* the moon anomalies and the UFO phenomenon. [[randall-carlson]] in [[JGE1NIGhBzw]] connects this to world-tree myths where "some humans kept climbing" after the flood rather than returning to repopulate Earth.
