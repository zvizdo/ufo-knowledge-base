# Holographic Projection

## Public-record lineage

- **1994 Air Force concept document** — reported by [[william-arkin]] in 1999. Massive 3D-sky projection holographic projector intended for PSYOPS use.
- **1990s-onwards** — sporadic public coverage of military holographic-warfare R&D; no operational system publicly fielded.

## Alleged classified applications

- **[[raytheon]] triangle at [[fort-irwin]]** — Per [[steven-greer]] (via [[OOr98r51Sj0]]): a whistleblower at Fort Irwin allegedly saw Raytheon projecting a triangular UFO craft as part of a false-alien-invasion scenario. The whistleblower and his wife were subsequently "abducted by this thing… and tortured."
- **S4 holographic book** — Per [[bob-lazar]] (via [[robert-bigelow]]): an alien "Kindle" at S4 — "as you opened up the pages, whatever the stories were became a hologram."
- **[[naval-surface-warfare-crane|Crane NSWC]]** — Per [[randy-anderson]]: a metallic gauntlet with a crystalline screen and holographic hieroglyphic symbols "appearing… almost looked like a mirage type of effect."
- **[[holographic-mountain-bases|Holographic mountain bases]]** — one of two candidate mechanisms (the other is [[utility-fog]] / [[programmable-matter]]) for the recurring "UAP/helicopters vanishing into rock" pattern.

## Limitations

A massive 3D sky-projection is power-hungry, weather-sensitive, and observation-dependent. [[OOr98r51Sj0]] argues that for a permanent mountain-cloaking installation, the energy budget and weather robustness of pure holographic projection are insufficient — favoring a [[utility-fog|programmable-matter]] alternative.
