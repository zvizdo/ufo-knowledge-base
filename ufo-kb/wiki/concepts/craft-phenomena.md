# Craft Phenomena

Craft phenomena is the parent category for UAP entities classified by morphology, behavior, or reported physical characteristics. The KB's `entities/craft-phenomena/` folder contains individual pages for each major documented craft type; this concept page provides the taxonomic overview.

**Major craft types in the KB**:

- **[[disc-shaped-craft]]** — the archetypal "flying saucer" form; dominant in 1947–1960s reports; present in Roswell, contactee accounts, and Nazi advanced-aircraft hypotheses
- **[[black-triangle-uap]] / [[tr3b]]** — large silent triangular craft; Belgium 1989–90 wave; TR-3B hypothesis (Edgar Fouché); possibly both classified human aircraft and genuine unknown phenomena
- **[[tic-tac]]** — elongated white oval; the 2004 Nimitz encounter with Commander Fravor; defined the contemporary military UAP reporting culture
- **[[cube-in-sphere]]** — reported by [[david-fravor]] and others; a cube suspended within a transparent sphere; among the most geometrically anomalous reported forms
- **[[alien-reproduction-vehicles]]** — alleged reverse-engineered disc craft; ARV claims from the 1988 Norton AFB display
- **[[fast-walkers]]** — high-speed objects tracked by satellite sensors crossing Earth's atmosphere

**Phenomenological notes**: Witness descriptions cluster around a small set of morphologies across decades and cultures with minimal cross-contamination — suggesting either genuine recurring phenomena or deep cultural transmission. The disc form dominates pre-1980 reports; the triangle form becomes prominent post-1975; the tic-tac form is primarily post-2000 in public documentation (though likely observed earlier in classified channels).
