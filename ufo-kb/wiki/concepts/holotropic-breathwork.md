# Holotropic Breathwork

Holotropic breathwork is a practice developed in the 1970s by psychiatrist Stanislav Grof and his wife Christina Grof as an alternative to LSD-assisted psychotherapy after LSD became illegal. "Holotropic" means "moving toward wholeness" (Greek *holos* = whole, *trepein* = moving toward). The method uses accelerated circular breathing combined with evocative music and focused bodywork to induce non-ordinary states of consciousness.

**Stanislav Grof's background**: Grof had conducted extensive clinical LSD research at Johns Hopkins and the Maryland Psychiatric Research Center in the 1960s–70s, producing one of the largest datasets of psychedelic-assisted therapy sessions. When LSD became Schedule I, he searched for a non-pharmacological method to produce comparable states and found that certain patterns of accelerated breathing could achieve similar phenomenology.

**Phenomenology**: Holotropic states produced by the practice regularly include experiences categorized by Grof as: biographical (reliving early life memories), perinatal (birth process reliving), and transpersonal (past-life experiences, entity encounters, cosmic consciousness, identification with other organisms). The transpersonal category overlaps significantly with UAP contact phenomenology and DMT entity experiences.

**Wim Hof parallel**: [[wim-hof]] describes his breathwork method as independently developed but parallel to holotropic breathwork — both operate by inducing altered states through controlled hyperventilation patterns. Hof frames his method in terms of vascular system imprinting and trauma release; Grof frames his in terms of accessing unconscious biographical and transpersonal material.

**UAP relevance**: Holotropic breathwork is relevant to UAP research because the entity contact experiences it consistently produces raise the question of whether those entities are endogenous (constructed by the mind under altered states) or represent genuine contact with non-human intelligence accessed through altered neurochemistry — the same question that attaches to DMT entities and NHI contact phenomenology.

**Transpersonal psychology**: Grof co-founded transpersonal psychology with Abraham Maslow and Anthony Sutich — a psychology that takes seriously human experiences beyond the conventional ego, including mystical, psychedelic, and non-ordinary consciousness states.
