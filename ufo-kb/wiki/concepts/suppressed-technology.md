# Suppressed Technology

Suppressed technology refers to technologies that exist or have been developed but are withheld from public markets, academic research, or general knowledge — either through classification, active suppression by commercial interests, or integration into classified programs without disclosure.

**Spectrum of claims**: Claims of suppressed technology range from well-documented to highly contested:

- *Well-documented*: Black program advanced aircraft (U-2, SR-71, B-2, F-117) were operational for years before any public disclosure. The entire [[black-budget]] architecture exists to develop technology outside normal oversight. This is confirmed suppression by definition.

- *Contested*: [[transistor-roswell-thesis]] claims that Bell Labs' transistor technology was derived from recovered Roswell crash materials — that a fundamental commercial technology was itself reverse-engineered from non-human sources. Corso's *Day After Roswell* makes this claim explicitly.

- *Claimed but unverified*: Energy generation technologies (zero-point energy, over-unity devices), propulsion systems based on [[electrogravitics]], and healing devices like the [[scalar-frequency-healing]] described by [[michael-herrera]] — a 886,000–1.2 million frequency scanning device claimed to be kept off-market due to FDA dual-use concerns and pharmaceutical opposition.

**Classification architecture**: The [[black-budget]] and Special Access Program (SAP) architecture provides the institutional mechanism for genuine technology suppression. Technologies developed in SAPs can remain classified indefinitely, with congressional oversight limited by need-to-know restrictions. The [[ffrdc-uap-architecture]] has been proposed as a structure within which UAP-derived technologies might exist.

**Commercial suppression**: Separate from classification, pharmaceutical and energy industries have historically opposed technologies that threatened market positions — the suppression of tetraethyl lead alternatives, the history of electric vehicle development, and the insulin pricing dynamics all demonstrate commercial suppression operating through regulatory capture.

**The reverse-engineering nexus**: If any UAP crash materials are real and being studied in classified programs, the resulting technologies would constitute both suppressed knowledge about UAP origin and suppressed commercial technology — a double suppression problem that could sustain concealment across many decades.
