# Nazi Anti-Gravity Program

A claims-thesis recurring across the disclosure literature: the Nazi SS, primarily through [[kammler-stab]] in occupied Czechoslovakia and adjacent facilities (with [[ss-ahnenerbe]] occult-research overlap), ran an advanced-propulsion / anti-gravity research program from roughly 1942–1945. The program's personnel, prototypes, and theoretical work were then absorbed into the United States after the war via [[operation-paperclip]], providing — per this thesis — the technical and historical backbone for postwar US classified anti-gravity work concentrated at [[wright-patterson]].

This concept sits at the intersection of three larger framings: [[anti-gravity-manhattan-project]] (the broader claim that a Manhattan-Project-scale US anti-gravity effort followed), [[reverse-engineered-craft]] (the cross-cutting category of recovered/non-human craft analysis), and [[nazi-ufo-hypothesis]] (the broader claim that some UFOs are man-made Nazi-derivative craft).

## Core Claims

1. **Centralized SS command structure**: Hans Kammler's [[kammler-stab]] (also called SCOD/Škoda Works in some accounts) operated as the SS's advanced-weapons clearinghouse, with mandate spanning conventional V-weapons, exotic propulsion, and anti-gravity prototypes.
2. **Multiple parallel research lines**:
   - [[victor-schauberger]] — vortex / impeller propulsion (saucer-shaped airframe with rotating water/air vortex engine)
   - [[richard-miethe]] — high-voltage electrogravidic experiments (parallel to [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s work)
   - [[henry-coanda]] — lenticular aerodyne (1936 patent); later consulted on the [[avrocar-project]] at Wright-Patterson
   - [[rudolph-schriever]] — disc-shaped craft prototype tested in Prague (claimed first flight Feb 1945)
   - [[die-glocke]] — "the Bell" device; electromagnetic / time-dilation / nuclear research depending on speaker; produced under Kammler's command
3. **Magenta-craft reverse-engineering** (Jorjani-specific in [[ABw7EsWh2Us]]): the [[1933-magenta-crash]] non-human vehicle was studied at the same facility under joint SS / Italian / Vatican oversight, providing the propulsion knowledge that fed the saucer / Bell programs.
4. **Operation Paperclip transfer**: Schauberger, Miethe, Coanda, and other Kammler-Stab personnel were brought to the US, with [[wright-patterson]] (then Wright Field / Wright-Patterson Air Force Base) as the primary receiving site.
5. **Continuity into postwar US programs**: anti-gravity research at Wright-Patterson's WADC (Wright Air Development Center) during the late 1940s–1950s sits downstream of the Nazi knowledge base. The 1957 UNC Chapel Hill conference, [[townsend-brown-gravitator]] surveillance by Wright Airfield, and the Avrocar program are positioned by this thesis as continuations rather than independent originations.

## Pushback / Open Questions

- **Why didn't Nazis deploy?** If the program produced operational anti-gravity craft, why did it not affect the war's outcome? Asserters typically reply: the program was years from operational deployment when the war ended, and the prototypes were knowledge-stage rather than weapon-stage.
- **Distinguishing knowledge transfer from results transfer**: [[operation-paperclip]] transferred personnel and notes; whether what they had constituted *working* anti-gravity tech vs. *theoretical* programs is the contested point.
- **Nazi vs. non-human reverse engineering**: when alleged Nazi tech intersects alleged crash retrievals (e.g. Magenta + Roswell), which is foundational and which is derivative? Different asserters draw the boundary differently.
