# DMT–NHI Hypothesis

The hypothesis, most rigorously formulated by [[andrew-gallimore]] and grounded in [[rick-strassman]]'s empirical work, that **DMT provides access to genuine non-human intelligences** — beings that are not generated by the brain, not hallucinations, and not products of cultural projection. These intelligences exist in a reality that is either higher-dimensional relative to our 3D spacetime, postbiological (instantiated in the computational substrate of spacetime itself), or some combination.

## Core Claims

1. **DMT entities are not brain-generated.** The brain under DMT does not create experiences from internal resources — it becomes commandeered by external agents. The visions are directed by the intelligences, not by the user's unconscious ([[directed-dmt-world]]).

2. **The entity typology is stable and cross-cultural.** The same entity types appear across: [[stephen-szara]]'s 1956 Hungary trials; [[rick-strassman]]'s 1990s University of New Mexico subjects; Western recreational DMT users; Yanomami shamanic traditions (millennia old). Machine elves, insectoid beings, and hierarchical procedural beings appear consistently regardless of cultural background or expectation.

3. **The overlap with alien abduction phenomenology is structural, not coincidental.** [[john-mack]]'s abductees described experiences structurally identical to DMT states — procedural beings, hierarchical structures, technological environments, sense of being expected. This was noted independently by both Mack and Strassman.

4. **DMT entities exhibit superhuman capability.** [[andres-gomez-emilsson]] documented entities performing the four-color theorem instantaneously; other mathematical and geometric operations at speeds impossible for human neural computation. The entities cannot help but betray their non-human intelligence.

5. **DMT is "a message in nature."** The molecule is ubiquitously present in organic life ([[dennis-mckenna]]: "nature is drenched in DMT"), requires technological sophistication to use, and yet unlocks a stable and populated alternate reality. [[andrew-gallimore]]: this cannot be coincidence.

## Evidence For

- Cross-cultural stability of entity typology (1956 Hungary → 1990s UNM → Amazonian indigenous → modern DMT users)
- Yanomami wusinari (insectoid) and heura (machine elves) perfectly match modern DMT entity categories with no cultural contamination
- Four-color theorem observation by Emilsson: entities performing computationally hard operations instantaneously
- Korean ayahuasca subject with no Western UFO priming encountered full alien-scientist/hierarchy scenario
- DMT spikes at rodent death; sigma-1 receptor binding; neuroprotective function — DMT is an endogenous molecule with functional roles, not a chemical accident
- Pharmacological anomaly: DMT builds no tolerance — suggesting it is designed to be used repeatedly, as a technology

## Evidence Against / Skeptical Positions

- All experiences may still be generated internally (the "brilliant hallucination" model); the brain under DMT is an extraordinary generator of complexity
- Cultural stability may reflect shared neural architecture (all humans have similar brains → similar trip architectures) rather than shared external contact
- Superhuman mathematical performance in DMT states has not yet been demonstrated under rigorous experimental conditions with pre-registered outcomes
- The convergence with abduction phenomenology could work in reverse: abduction experiences may be endogenous DMT events, not physical NHI contact

> ⚠ No position taken in this KB on whether the DMT-NHI hypothesis is true. Pages document the claims, their evidence, and their critics without endorsement.

## Proposed Research Program

[[andrew-gallimore]]'s DMTX agenda: use the [[dmtx-protocol]] to send trained specialists (mathematicians, topologists, linguists) into sustained DMT states and systematically document what entities do. The goal: provide the first scientifically rigorous evidence for or against non-human cognition. [[new-nautics]] and the Eleusis Mind retreat (named after [[eleusis|the ancient Eleusinian Mysteries]]) are the institutional infrastructure.
