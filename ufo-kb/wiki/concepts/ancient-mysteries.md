# Ancient Mysteries

The "ancient mysteries" designates the body of esoteric knowledge — cosmological, astronomical, spiritual, and claimed technological — that mystery school traditions maintained was accessible only through initiatory structures. The concept appears in UAP-adjacent research primarily through two channels: the hypothesis that ancient knowledge of NHI contact was preserved through secret lineages (Freemasonry, Knights Templar, Rosicrucians), and the related argument that ancient architectural monuments encode advanced astronomical and mathematical knowledge that implies a sophisticated earlier civilization.

**Mystery school lineage**: [[freemasonry]] is the modern end of a proposed transmission chain: Egyptian mystery schools → Hermeticism → Pythagoreanism → Neoplatonism → Gnostic traditions → Knights Templar → Rosicrucians → Freemasonry. [[graham-hancock]] and others argue this chain represents not just symbolic continuation but active preservation of specific cosmological and technical knowledge. The astronomical alignments of ancient monuments — Giza, Chaco Canyon ([[chaco-canyon]]), Göbekli Tepe — are presented as evidence that this knowledge was cosmographic and practical, not merely ceremonial.

**NHI contact hypothesis**: In the UAP-adjacent reading, ancient mysteries traditions preserve firsthand or transmitted knowledge of non-human intelligence interaction. The [[merkavah-mysticism]] tradition (Jewish celestial ascent), [[shangqing-daoism]] (Chinese celestial travel), and the [[eleusinian-mysteries]] (Greek psychedelic-sacrament initiation) are all interpreted as encoding actual experiences of contact with non-human intelligences — whether NHI, psychedelic entities, or dimensional travelers.

**George Ellery Hale**: [[george-ellery-hale]], who transformed Throop Polytechnic into [[caltech]], reportedly claimed to receive guidance from spirits and embedded Egyptian symbolism in architectural choices — an example of an early 20th-century scientist in whom the ancient mysteries tradition and modern science overlapped.
