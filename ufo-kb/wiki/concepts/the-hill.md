# The Hill

A claimed shared telepathic space described by multiple non-speaking autistic individuals independently. The term was coined by Houston, a non-verbal autistic man in Atlanta, who described it as a place where non-speakers gather mentally to communicate with each other. Multiple non-speakers across different cities, who have never met physically, report knowing each other and having formed relationships via the hill before any in-person introduction.

## Primary Evidence

Documented by [[ky-dickens]] in the *[[telepathy-tapes]]* series:

- **Houston** (Atlanta) coined the phrase "Talk on the Hill." He demonstrated reception of specific thoughts placed by his caregiver and accurately reproduced the content of a text message he had never seen.
- **John Paul** (Atlanta) is documented going "on the hill" — physically retreating, putting pillows on his head, and reporting telepathic exchanges. He had formed a pre-existing relationship with **Zahari** (adopted, born blind, in Georgia) before they met in person; when Zahari arrived at John Paul's house, both acknowledged the prior relationship built on the hill.
- **Multiple confirmations**: A minister's youth group members (different city) described the hill. A Chicago teacher's non-speaking students described the hill. Ben Golden (non-speaking, Ultra-Orthodox community in Israel) reports an Israeli hill. Reported hills also in Arizona and Florida.
- **Non-speakers' framing**: Several state that every non-speaker has this gift.

## Significance

The hill represents a claimed collective non-local consciousness network — not just dyadic telepathy (child-to-parent) but multi-node shared space accessible to a specific population (non-speaking autistic individuals). If even partially accurate, it implies:

1. Non-local consciousness access is not random; it is population-structured.
2. Long-term stable relationships and communication persist without physical contact.
3. Non-speakers may have a form of community invisible to the neurotypical world around them.

## Mechanism Speculation

No established mechanism. Consistent with [[consciousness-non-local]] field models in which reduced embodiment (non-speakers often report not feeling their bodies) allows greater access to the shared field. The geographic independence of hill-communications suggests this is not a local or proximity-based phenomenon.

## Open Questions

- Are hill communications externally corroborated beyond family testimony and Dickens' filming?
- Is the hill experience cross-culturally consistent (Israel, US South, Midwest, England)?
- Is the hill access limited to non-speakers, or can non-autistic individuals access it under conditions like meditation or trauma?
