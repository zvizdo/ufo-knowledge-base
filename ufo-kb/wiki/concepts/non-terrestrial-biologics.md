# Non-Terrestrial Biologics

**Non-terrestrial biologics** is the term [[michael-aquino]] provided to [[peter-levenda]] at an AFIO Las Vegas event as his interpretation of the "MJ" cryptonym in US Space Command documents. According to Aquino, "MJ" referred not to alien bodies or recovered NHI technology but to **organic compounds derived from meteorites** — a genuine astrobiology program tracking non-Earth biological chemistry.

If accurate, this interpretation would mean the [[majestic-12|MJ-12]] documents' references to "MJ" are about a meteorite-biology program, not alien contact programs — and the decades of speculation about Majestic-12 would be based on a misread acronym.

[[peter-levenda]]'s response in [[Y7PLeu5rTv4]]: he is explicitly "in two minds." Possibilities:
1. Aquino's interpretation is the genuine clarification — MJ really did stand for non-terrestrial biologics; the alien-contact reading was always a misinterpretation
2. The interpretation is a deliberate cover story designed to provide a plausible innocent explanation for the cryptonym after [[stanton-friedman]] and others publicized MJ-12

Aquino's background — founder of Temple of Set, former Army PSYOPS officer, documented intelligence community connections — makes him both a plausible truth-teller and a plausible disinformation source.
