# Holographic Principle and UAP

## The Core Idea

A theoretical framework — advanced primarily by [[david-grusch]] in [[kRO5jOa06Qw]] and independently by [[jacques-vallee]] through his interdimensional hypothesis — holds that some UAPs may not be spacecraft traversing physical space in the conventional sense. Instead, they may be **higher-dimensional objects that intersect with our 3D spacetime**, manifesting to observers only as cross-sections, projections, or "shadows" of their actual form.

The analogy: just as a 3D sphere passing through a 2D plane would appear to Flatland inhabitants as a circle that grows, holds, and shrinks — with no explanation for its origin or destination — a 4D or higher-dimensional object passing through 3D space would appear to human observers as a strange solid that materializes, changes, and dematerializes. See E.A. Abbott's *Flatland* (1884).

[[david-grusch]]'s formulation in [[kRO5jOa06Qw]]: *"If this is a 2D universe and this is a 3D object, if it intersects you're just going to see a sliver of it — so you'll just see a disc."*

## The Holographic Principle Proper

In physics, the [[holographic-principle-uap]] was originally proposed to explain how information is encoded on event horizons in black holes (Bekenstein-Hawking entropy; later the AdS/CFT correspondence). It holds that a 3D volume can be fully encoded on a 2D surface — the higher-dimensional reality is informationally equivalent to its lower-dimensional projection.

Grusch invokes this in the UAP context as an analogy: *"Maybe some of the UAPs are not even from out there — they're here but they're not actually from out there."* The craft exists in a higher-dimensional space that overlaps with ours; we see only the quasi-projection.

## Inside Bigger Than Outside — The Key Empirical Test

The most striking observational implication of the higher-dimensional model is the persistent report of **interior spaces larger than the exterior dimensions of a UAP would allow**. This pattern appears across multiple independent sources:

- [[jacques-vallee]] describes a woman in San Jose who entered a disc-shaped object and found herself in a space she compared to a cinema amphitheater — far larger than the visible exterior. Vallée: *"I said: that's bigger than your house. Why is the inside bigger than the outside?"*
- Multiple abductee accounts report disorienting interior spaciousness inconsistent with the observed exterior hull
- This would make sense if the craft's interior exists in a higher-dimensional extension: the 3D hull we observe is a cross-section; the internal space extends into additional dimensions

## Quasi-Projection and Materialization

The model also provides a mechanism for UAP **sudden appearance and disappearance** without violating conservation of momentum. A higher-dimensional object moving through dimensions *orthogonal* to our spacetime would appear to materialize when its path intersects our 3D slice and dematerialize when the intersection ends — no acceleration required, no FTL travel within 3D space.

Grusch and [[eric-weinstein]] (who proposes multiple temporal dimensions in Geometric Unity — see [[iQOibpIDx-4]]) both gesture toward this framework. Weinstein's multiple-temporal-dimension version: *"you could go back into this extra time dimension and find yourself at an earlier event without ever having to retrace time steps."* This is structurally similar: non-Einsteinian movement not through 3D space but through additional dimensions.

## Vallée's Interdimensional Hypothesis

[[jacques-vallee]] independently developed this framework across decades (most fully in *The Invisible College*, 1975). His key departure from the extraterrestrial hypothesis:

- Bipedal, bilaterally symmetric NHI — as reported at the [[1994-ruwa-zimbabwe]] school incident and many abductions — is statistically implausible as independent convergent evolution on another planet
- But such morphology is consistent with **bioengineered avatars** designed for human-recognition and communication, representing intelligence from a different dimensional origin
- The non-interventionist behavior, inside-bigger-than-outside geometry, and sudden appearance/disappearance all fit the extra-dimensional model better than the interstellar travel model

Grusch endorses this line in [[kRO5jOa06Qw]]: *"Maybe we're seeing higher dimensional stuff casting shadow into our space just like how we cast a shadow on a two-dimensional surface."*

## Relationship to Time-Travel UAP Hypothesis

The extra-dimensional model and the [[time-travel-uap]] hypothesis are not mutually exclusive — [[michael-p-masters]]'s future-humans model also invokes extra-temporal dimensions for travel. In both cases the key conceptual move is the same: movement through dimensions orthogonal to ordinary spacetime, producing effects (materialization, inside-bigger-than-outside, time dilation) that look anomalous from within the 3D/1T framework.

## Open Questions

- Can the AdS/CFT correspondence be applied to generate testable predictions about UAP geometry or electromagnetic signature?
- Is the extra-dimensional model genuinely distinct from the time-travel model, or are they the same physics described in different vocabulary?
