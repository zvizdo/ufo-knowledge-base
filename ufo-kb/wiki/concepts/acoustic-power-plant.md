# Acoustic Power Plant

Christopher Dunn's hypothesis that the Great Pyramid functioned as an acoustic resonance power-generation system, exciting the King's Chamber's Aswan granite at characteristic frequencies; compatible with Drum's chemical-manufacturing reading since both require the same underlying infrastructure.

Christopher Dunn's *The Giza Power Plant* (1998) argues the Great Pyramid was engineered as an acoustic-mechanical power generation system: vibrations from natural sources (wind, geothermal) were channeled and amplified through the corbelled Grand Gallery into the King's Chamber, where Aswan granite's piezoelectric and resonant properties converted acoustic energy into useful work. Biondi accepts this as a valid alternative to Drum's chemical-factory reading, since both require the same underlying infrastructure (deep shafts, granite chambers, ventilation/extraction system).
