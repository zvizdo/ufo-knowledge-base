# Physical UAP Evidence

Physical UAP evidence refers to material and measurable physical traces associated with UAP encounters — as distinct from testimonial or photographic/radar evidence. The category includes recovered metallic fragments, landing trace marks on terrain, biological effects on witnesses, radiation signatures, and electromagnetic instrument anomalies.

**Ubatuba magnesium fragments**: The most scientifically analyzed physical UAP material in the KB. Following the [[1957-ubatuba-orb]] explosion over the São Paulo coast, a fisherman collected fragments. These eventually reached [[jacques-vallee]]'s sample collection and were analyzed by [[garry-nolan]] using Stanford's NanoSIMS instrument. The analysis found that the magnesium isotope ratios deviated from terrestrial norms — specifically, the ratio of magnesium-26 to magnesium-24 was anomalous in a direction inconsistent with known isotopic processing methods. This represents one of the few cases where physical material shows measurable anomalies consistent with non-terrestrial or exotic processing.

**Landing traces**: [[landing-trace-evidence]] is a parallel category — physical soil, vegetation, and material alterations at UAP landing sites. The Trans-en-Provence case (France, 1981) produced the most rigorously analyzed landing trace, with documented soil chemistry changes and plant growth anomalies.

**Witness biological effects**: Physiological changes in witnesses — radiation burns, unusual healing patterns, altered blood work, implants detected by X-ray — constitute a distinct sub-category of physical evidence. [[garry-nolan]]'s bone marrow study of individuals exposed to UAP is the most systematic medical investigation.

**Alleged recovered materials**: The most contested category involves allegedly recovered UAP crash materials held in classified programs. [[david-grusch]]'s Congressional testimony asserts that recovered metamaterials with exotic properties exist in US government possession. [[hal-puthoff]] has described materials with unusual electromagnetic properties from alleged UAP sources. These claims cannot be independently verified.

**Analytical limitations**: Physical UAP evidence faces a custody-chain problem: the most extraordinary samples are those with the most contested provenance, and the samples with solid provenance (Ubatuba) show anomalies but not definitive non-terrestrial origin.
