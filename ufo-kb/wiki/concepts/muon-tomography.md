# Muon Tomography

Cosmic-ray-muon attenuation imaging used to detect voids in dense structures; the technique behind the 1970s Alvarez Khafre scan and the 2017 ScanPyramids Big Void detection — the competing technology to Biondi's [[satellite-sar|SAR phonon tomography]] for non-invasive pyramid investigation.

Muon tomography measures the absorption of cosmic-ray muons by dense materials; voids appear as zones of lower muon attenuation. The method has been used since [[luis-alvarez]]'s 1970s muon scan of Khafre's pyramid (which scanned 19% of volume above the Belzoni chamber and found nothing) through to the [[scan-pyramids-project|ScanPyramids]] 2017 detection of the [[big-void]]. Biondi argues SAR phonon imaging produces substantially higher-quality reconstructions, demonstrated by his crystal-clear [[gran-sasso-laboratory|Gran Sasso]] 1.4 km validation.
