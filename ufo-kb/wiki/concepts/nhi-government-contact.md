# NHI-Government Contact

The class of claims that representatives of the US government (and to a lesser degree other governments) have engaged in direct contact with non-human intelligence — whether as formal meetings, treaty negotiations, ongoing technical liaison, or intelligence operations against NHI.

**Spectrum of claims**:
- **One-time meetings**: The [[1954-eisenhower-ufo-meeting]] is the most widely cited single-meeting claim; other variants place it at Holloman AFB and extend through the 1950s–60s.
- **Treaties**: Multiple sources claim formal agreements between the US government and NHI — covering human abductions, technology transfer, non-interference in each other's operations. [[hal-puthoff]] and [[tim-taylor]] reference the "Eisenhower treaty" era in KB sources.
- **Ongoing programs**: [[dan-sherman]]'s [[project-preserve-destiny]] account describes an ongoing intuitive-communicator program as evidence of sustained NHI-government communication. [[david-grusch]]'s testimony implies ongoing crash retrieval and materials exchange.
- **Intelligence-collection**: Some accounts (see [[operation-laser-strike]]) describe active US operations to track or intercept NHI rather than cooperate with them.

**Epistemics**: No primary documentation confirms formal NHI-government contact. All known accounts rely on secondhand or tertiary testimony, alleged leaked documents with disputed provenance, or interpretation of circumstantial evidence. The KB records these as contested claims with diverse credibility tiers.
