# Time Travel and UAP

Source summary: [[09KP8XVf5nY]]

## Core Thesis

UAP and non-human intelligence phenomena may involve time travel or the deliberate manipulation of human timelines. At minimum, certain individuals whose lives have intersected with the UAP phenomenon believe themselves to be connected to such manipulation. The thesis is not merely speculative: it arises from firsthand testimony by figures with documented proximity to classified UAP programs, and it is structurally consistent with other aspects of the phenomenon — particularly the apparent foreknowledge displayed by NHI entities and their materials.

## Harald Malmgren's Final Testimony

[[harold-malmgren]]'s most direct statement on this thesis came during a phone call made **three days before his death** — one of the last conversations documented in [[09KP8XVf5nY]]. The interviewer asked him directly: *"Do you think your life is connected with time travel?"*

His reply:

> "I've thought about this many many times and the answer is yes. Nothing else explains when I told you [to the MIT professor] — I didn't have the slightest inhibition, I was talking as if I knew something I don't know why I knew it."

The specific incident Malmgren references — speaking to an MIT professor with complete confidence about something he had no conventional basis for knowing — is framed by him as evidence that he possessed information whose origin he could not account for through normal means. His conclusion, after a lifetime of reflection, was that time travel is the most coherent explanation.

### The Silence in the Car

During a car ride to the hospital on his last admission — an occasion when Malmgren was described as extraordinarily forthcoming across a wide range of sensitive topics — the interviewer asked whether there is *"a coordinated group trying to deliberately affect timelines."*

Malmgren fell **conspicuously silent**. It was his only silence in an otherwise remarkably open conversation. He did not deny the premise. He did not redirect. He said nothing.

In the context of an individual who was willing to discuss UAP shootdowns, JFK assassination motives, Roswell survivors, and nuclear weapons programs in his final hours, the choice to remain silent on this specific question is itself significant testimony.

## Thomas Townsend Brown

[[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s obsession with time travel is documented in his 500-page notebooks, which have recently been made publicly available. Brown believed that **gravity and time are linked** — that manipulation of gravitational fields could produce effects on temporal experience, potentially including travel or influence across time. His notebooks include work on anti-gravity propulsion, element transmutation, and alleged collaboration with [[nikola-tesla]].

Brown's professional credibility is not trivial: his electrogravitics work was taken seriously enough by the US Navy to attract classified interest. His time-travel convictions were not the ravings of a crank but the conclusions of a physicist who believed he had identified a gravitational mechanism. The full notebooks are now public, and [[harold-malmgren]] downloaded them shortly before his death with the intention of reviewing them, per [[09KP8XVf5nY]].

## Structural Coherence with Other Concepts

The time-travel thesis, while the most speculative in this knowledge base, has structural coherence with several adjacent concepts:

- [[nhi-telepathic-communication]]: If NHI entities can transmit knowledge to human minds, they could transmit knowledge *from* a future timeline, explaining cases of inexplicable foreknowledge.
- [[physics-lockdown]]: The theoretical physics of time dilation and gravitational time effects — relevant to Brown's mechanism — falls within the domain of classified or suppressed science.
- The host's observation that Malmgren's entire life had a sense of **directed purpose** that Malmgren himself found difficult to explain through conventional biography is consistent with a timeline-management narrative in which certain individuals are positioned at key intersections of events.

## Epistemic Status

This is the most thinly evidenced thesis in this knowledge base. It rests on:
1. Malmgren's own assessment of his life, made in extremis
2. His single silence under questioning
3. Brown's theoretical work (notebooks now public but not yet widely analyzed)
4. The structural coherence argument above

It should be held as a **hypothesis under serious consideration by the primary sources** rather than an evidentiary claim. Its inclusion here reflects that the primary sources — individuals with documented proximity to classified UAP programs — took it seriously enough to raise it explicitly.

## Tim Taylor's Nassau Time Travel Group

[[bob-maguire]] adds a contemporary institutional claim: [[tim-taylor]] (NASA Mission Controller, biotech entrepreneur, Vatican-credentialed figure) told [[diana-pasulka]] and Ryan [[chris-bledsoe]]'s son that he is part of a **secret time travel group headquartered in Nassau, Bahamas** — with [[thomas-townsend-brown]] as its president.

Bob's assessment: Townsend Brown's documented time in the Bahamas + his lifelong gravity-time obsession + general relativity's time-gravity coupling = this claim is internally coherent. Nothing else makes sense.

The "group" would represent exactly what the theoretical physics points toward: a clandestine operational program working on gravitational time effects, using Brown's experimental framework.

## General Relativity and Information Time Travel

[[bob-maguire]] cites physicist **Jim Gates** (string theorist / GR researcher) as having worked out mathematically how **information can be transmitted backward in time** within general relativity. Key points:

- The Hawking chronological protection conjecture limits *physical* time travel (closed timelike curves) under certain conditions.
- Sending only information — not matter — may not violate those restrictions.
- Quantum mechanical approaches (reversing qubit positions, quantum computation) are possibly even better paths for sending information backward in time.
- The traversable wormhole literature (Maldacena, et al.) provides additional GR paths.

The implication: if NHI entities have mastered gravity, they may have mastered information time travel — and UAP encounters may involve NHI acting on knowledge of future events to shape the present.

## Ralph Larson's Time Travel (Mystical / Dream-State Category)

[[ralph-moat-larson]], a former CIA officer and DOE intelligence director, claims a qualitatively different type of time travel experience in [[ATJwqp5twAg]]: a prolonged vivid dream-state at [[mount-athos]] in 1991 in which he lived as a medieval monk for months. The experience was physically indistinguishable from waking reality; he was aware he was dreaming. Unlike the consciousness/information-transfer model above, Larson's account involves subjective full-body presence in a historical time period.

His experience is distinguished from the Malmgren and Brown cases by:
1. **Mystical/religious framing** rather than physics — he interprets it as God-mediated spiritual development, not NHI manipulation
2. **Historical corroboration**: he discovered [[maximos-kafsokalivitis]] in 2005 — a real monk who shared the exact distinctive traits (burning huts, flying) of Larson's dream character, whom Larson had no knowledge of in 1991
3. **Subjective identity continuity**: he retained his 20th-century identity while in the 14th-century timeline

Larson does not frame this as UAP-related. Its inclusion here is as an additional data point for the "time travel as phenomenological category" cluster — particularly the corroborated-detail angle.

## Joe Rogan's Future-Humans Dream

[[joe-rogan]]'s 2025 dream account on American Alchemy ([[C_Na1tI5qpw]]) adds a contemporary data point to the future-humans variant of this thesis:

- Tall, slim, genderless beings — pinkish, large heads, larger eyes, suits matching skin color
- Playfully scared him then revealed it was games — interpreted as acclimatization
- Showed reptilian entities being kept at bay behind flimsy barriers
- Rogan's own interpretation: **"These things seem very slim... genderless... there's no need for muscle, there's no need to physically move things"** — i.e., what humans will become
- Frame: same evolutionary trajectory as ancient hominids → modern humans (bigger brains, smaller bodies), projected forward

Rogan explicitly frames this as the future-humans hypothesis: *"They seemed like what we will be... if you look at ancient hominids versus modern humans, our heads are larger, our bodies are smaller. These things seem very slim... genderless, like where humanity is kind of headed."*

This is notable as a contemporary firsthand dream account from a highly public figure in an era of heightened UAP awareness — consistent with [[jason-reza-jorjani]]'s civilizational self-actualization model (a future culture reaching into its own past) and with [[ralph-moat-larson]]'s mystical time-travel phenomenology.

## Eric Wargo's Endorsement and Quantum Tesseract Model (hXYdkcv5TtY)

[[eric-wargo]] in [[hXYdkcv5TtY]] explicitly endorses [[michael-p-masters]]'s Extratemporalis model as the **strongest available hypothesis for the genuine core of the UAP phenomenon**. Wargo adds the quantum computing dimension:

**Quantum computer as Tesseract**: A quantum server left plugged in for years/decades develops a backward information channel to its start-up moment. [[william-gibson]]'s *The Peripheral* is the best narrative: a future publicist communicates with and directs a present-day drone operator via a quantum server — and sends 3D-printing instructions for future technology. UAP could be craft *built in the present* from future-transmitted designs, operated remotely by future humans.

**Timeline seeding**: The point of time travel is not to change history (block universe prevents paradox) but to *plant seeds in the past for a better tomorrow*. Example: an astronomer in the 22nd century detects an asteroid too late to respond conventionally, but has a quantum server connected to 3D printers — she sends instructions to the past to launch a rocket 30 years earlier. The rocket was "always there." Same logic applies to pandemics, drug development, or any problem that requires more lead time than the present allows.

**Nuclear sites as leverage points**: Jesse's inference that UAP clustering around nuclear sites reflects future humans identifying nuclear weapons as the highest-leverage intervention point — the Archimedes lever for humanity's trajectory. Wargo agrees with the general logic.

**Multiple factions**: Different future factions competing across time mirrors present geopolitical competition. Russian vs. American time travelers, different future ideological factions — this explains the incoherence of UAP behavior and apparent variety of agendas.

**Die Glocke as intermediate step**: Wargo connects [[die-glocke]] to this model — not primarily as antigravity but as a high-voltage inertial reference frame shift, creating time dilation in a chamber. [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s gravity-time obsession is the American parallel. Physical time travel via gravity manipulation is the next step after quantum information time travel.

**Block universe prerequisite**: The whole framework requires [[block-universe]] physics — a fixed future that can transmit signals backward. Jesse articulates this perfectly: "If time travel becomes possible at any point in the future, it's inherently always been possible."

## Michael P. Masters' Extratemporalis Model (Y26iMB0r-f8)

[[michael-p-masters]] provides the most systematically argued biological + physics case for the future-humans variant in the KB. His [[future-humans-hypothesis]] (Extratemporalis model) adds:

**Biological convergence**: The predicted evolutionary trajectory of humans (neoteny — larger heads, smaller sensory organs, reduced musculature) maps precisely onto Gray alien morphology. Reproductive compatibility (intercourse, viable gametes, mixed offspring) is only possible between the same species — impossible for beings from another planetary biosphere. See [[neoteny-alien-morphology]].

**Tipler Disc Physics**: [[frank-tipler]]'s 1970s work shrank infinite-cylinder time-machine solutions to a finite rapidly-rotating disc — exactly the UAP saucer form factor. Frame-dragging from the rotating disc bends light cones backward, enabling closed timelike curves. This provides a physics lineage: Einstein GR (1915) → Lense-Thirring → van Stockum → Gödel → Tipler (1970s).

**Time dilation effects documented near UAP**:
- [[1973-armando-chile]]: Corporal Armando disappeared 15 minutes; wristwatch showed 5 days elapsed; beard grew out — 6 platoon witnesses corroborate
- [[travis-walton]]: consistent beard growth during abduction; subjective days elapsed
- Linda Jones (Manchester): grass growing rapidly under her feet as she fled a UAP
- [[amy-rylance]]: 5–7 days on a craft; transported 790 km; craft reversed time to deposit her closer to point of origin

**Sphere of influence**: [[jim-penniston]]'s description of the Rendlesham field — "like walking through water" — is the subjective experience of a frame-dragging time-dilation gradient. Time runs differently inside the field boundary.

**Electromagnetic anomaly correlation**: Steve Mea (UAP researcher) documented that UAP sightings over 200 years correlate with known electromagnetic/geomagnetic anomalies. Portal-like sites (Skinwalker Ranch, others with high EM/nuclear activity) may be the **temporal transit points** for UAP — not the White House lawn.

**Electromagnetic Fallout**: Creating a traversable wormhole or Tipler-disc time machine would produce massive EM anomalies in the surrounding area — consistent with instrumentation failures and EM drain documented at UAP encounter sites and [[skinwalker-ranch]].

**Corroborating historical statements**:
- [[george-hoover]] (Navy Commander, TS clearance): Roswell beings were time-traveling humans
- [[herman-oberth]] (father of German rocketry): UFOs "jump from one time-space coordinate to another"
- [[tom-delonge]] (Newsweek): "These things might be coming through time"
- Spielberg (1993, to Robert Liefeld): "What's easier to believe — monitoring by aliens or monitoring ourselves from the future?"

**Textual corroboration**: [[jim-penniston]]'s Rendlesham contact message — "We are you. We come from 8,000 years in the future" — is the most specific on-record claim by alleged NHI identifying as future humans.

## Jorjani's Temporal Causality Model (ABw7EsWh2Us)

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] offers a distinctly philosophical framing of temporal causality in [[ABw7EsWh2Us]]: some UFO phenomena represent **"a future state of a certain culture contacting itself in the past, trying to pull the timeline towards itself"** — what he calls "actualizing" or "temporizing" itself. 

Where the Malmgren/Townsend Brown framework involves NHI or individuals experiencing time travel, Jorjani proposes a **civilizational agent**: a culture (possibly a surviving Atlantean remnant, or a future evolved civilization) that is attempting retroactive self-actualization — reaching into its own past to ensure its existence. This is less a technology story and more a philosophical/ontological one.

The mechanism would work through influence, contact, and consciousness-mediated communication rather than physical time machines — consistent with Jorjani's broader thesis that consciousness is the fundamental technology.
