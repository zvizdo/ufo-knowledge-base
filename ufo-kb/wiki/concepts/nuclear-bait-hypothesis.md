# Nuclear Bait Hypothesis

**Type**: Recurring claim / contested hypothesis  
**Asserter**: [[garry-nolan]] (primary public voice); Soviet corroboration through unnamed sources

## Core Claim

Rather than UAPs passively monitoring nuclear activity, some programs *deliberately attracted* UAPs using nuclear material or detonations as a lure — either to study their response, establish contact, or obtain technology. Garry Nolan has stated this publicly, citing sources from within the intelligence community, and notes Soviet programs pursued a parallel strategy.

## Supporting Evidence

- **Pattern reversal**: The [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] documents UAPs appearing at nuclear sites *before construction is complete*, implying intelligence-level awareness that human nuclear programs then tried to exploit.
- **Soviet parallel**: Per Nolan, Soviet researchers independently developed similar bait-and-study methodology.
- **Nuclear Bait as program**: If true, this reframes US nuclear testing (1945–1963) partially as UAP-contact experiments, not merely weapons development.

## Relationship to Other Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Position |
|------------|---------|
| NHI monitoring (passive) | UAPs observe without being invited |
| Nuclear Bait (active) | Humans deliberately invite UAP response |
| Disablement-as-message | UAPs respond to demonstrate capability |
| Breakaway surveillance | Human faction uses NHI-tech to monitor other nations |

These are not mutually exclusive; the bait hypothesis could explain why the disablement pattern is *so consistent* — the UAPs are responding to a known human signal.

## Caveats

- Nolan has not provided documentary evidence for the bait hypothesis — it rests on his claimed intelligence-community sources.
- The hypothesis is unfalsifiable in open-source research: any nuclear test that attracted a UAP could be reframed post-hoc as intentional.
