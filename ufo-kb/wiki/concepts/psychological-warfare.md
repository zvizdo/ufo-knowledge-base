# Psychological Warfare

Psychological warfare (PSYWAR or PSYOP) is the use of propaganda, deception, false information, and information operations to influence the beliefs, emotions, and behavior of adversaries and civilian populations. It is distinct from conventional warfare but is treated as a component of military and intelligence operations.

**Cold War PSYWAR infrastructure**: During the Cold War, the CIA and military developed extensive psychological warfare capabilities, including covert influence operations, Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty, and the use of academic and cultural institutions as influence vectors (later revealed by the [[church-committee]]). C.D. Jackson, special assistant to President Eisenhower for psychological warfare, is a key figure in the KB's intersection of PSYWAR doctrine with UAP policy.

**Robertson Panel and UAP**: The [[robertson-panel]] (1953 CIA study) explicitly recommended a policy of educating the public to debunk and dismiss UAP reports, in part to prevent the public from being susceptible to UAP-based psychological operations by the Soviets. This is the documented entry point of PSYWAR doctrine into official UAP policy — the concern was not the objects themselves but their potential as a channel for enemy psychological manipulation.

**UAP as PSYOP vector**: The PSYWAR framework cuts both ways in UAP discourse:
1. **Government disinformation**: Claims that the government uses UAP reports as cover for classified aircraft tests (e.g., the U-2/SR-71 misidentification hypothesis), or actively plants false UAP narratives to conceal real programs
2. **Adversary exploitation**: The Robertson Panel's actual concern — that the Soviets could use UAP reports to saturate US early warning systems or panic the population

**Disinformation claims**: Some researchers (Richard Dolan, Jacques Vallée) argue that UAP disinformation has been deliberately seeded by intelligence agencies to muddy the waters around both genuine phenomena and classified programs — making it impossible to distinguish real cases from planted ones.
