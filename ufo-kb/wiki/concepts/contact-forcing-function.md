# Contact as Forcing Function

The concept — coined by [[jesse-michaels]] in [[tS_64sTN5AU]] and immediately adopted by [[diana-pasulka]] — that UAP/contact/anomalous experiences function as **forcing functions** for personal transformation: they compel people toward moral seriousness, self-examination, and a "straight and narrow" path, not because the experiencer intellectually chooses this but because the experience makes ordinary human defense mechanisms untenable.

## Origin of the Term

Jesse uses the engineering/systems term "forcing function" (an external input that compels a system to change its behavior) in a discussion about St. Francis of Assisi's UAP-encounter experience (his stigmata as radiation burns). Pasulka responds:

> *"Very good, Jesse. It's a forcing function. I'm going to now use that term. I tried to understand what it was when I told people that these people aren't holy, yes, but they are doing this and they're doing this because they have to do it — and that's not a choice."*

The term captures something that "transformation" or "conversion" doesn't: the **involuntary, mechanistic quality** of the change. Contact does not invite you to grow — it forces the issue.

## Phenomenological Evidence

Pasulka identifies several recurring features across documented forcing-function cases:

1. **St. Francis of Assisi** — started as a popular, social person ("Life of the Party"); had a UAP-encounter experience (seraph/eye, atmospheric sparks, harsh treatment, radiation burns/stigmata); essentially died from the encounter over years; became a figure of radical poverty and compassion — the transformation was total and not chosen
2. **Tim Taylor ("Tyler")** — used consciousness protocols (sleep, hydration, signal-reception state) not as spiritual practice but because he "had to do it"; underwent a religious conversion at the [[vatican-observatory]] that shifted his understanding of his own "downloads"; decided he no longer wanted the Space Program — only to help people
3. **Pasulka herself** — documented her own defense mechanisms being "busted" in *American Cosmic*; stopped drinking and caffeine; became more sensitive to reality; describes this as a natural consequence of proximity to the phenomena, not a deliberate choice
4. **Robert Hastings** — dry, hard-nosed researcher of UFOs and nuclear weapons; privately disclosed to Jesse (first time publicly) that he has had many anomalous experiences since beginning his research into the phenomena
5. **Jesse Michaels** — personally increased sense of connection to the subject; Skinwalker Ranch sulfur experience followed by worst month, then "saved by God"; the subject has tracked him as much as he has tracked it

## The Amplification Model

Pasulka develops a related model in [[tS_64sTN5AU]]: proximity to the phenomena makes a person into a **higher-frequency amplifier or receptor** — more sensitive to everything, good and bad. Bad energies attach more easily; good things are also more available. This is analogous to C.S. Lewis's *Screwtape Letters* (wormwood and screw tape targeting people on the path). The forcing-function quality means the experiencer cannot return to blissful ignorance — they must navigate at higher sensitivity.

## Defense Mechanisms

The concept is grounded in Pasulka's broader thesis about defense mechanisms: human beings have psychological defense mechanisms that prevent them from recognizing the anomalous quality of ordinary life. The UAP comes along and **busts these defense mechanisms** — forcing a confrontation with reality that was always there but inaccessible. This is why Pasulka says:

> *"I think that being alive is an anomalous experience — and we just have defense mechanisms to keep us from recognizing it."*

Contact removes the mechanism. What you do with the resulting clarity is then a question of personal integrity.

## Relationship to [[rene-girard]]

[[rene-girard]]'s account of the **defense mechanism of religion** is the structural parallel: religion institutionalizes and automates the scapegoat mechanism, so that communities enact ritual violence without knowing what they are doing. The UFO as forcing function is the inverse: it breaks through the automated program, forcing genuine self-examination — the one thing the automated program is designed to prevent. In this sense the UAP functions the way Girard thought the Gospels function: as a revelation that exposes and disrupts the program from within.

## Open Questions

- Is the forcing function a byproduct of contact, or is it functionally intended — i.e., does the control system produce forcing functions deliberately?
- Why do some experiencers report trauma and fragmentation rather than transformation? What determines the direction of the forcing?
- Does the forcing function only apply to individuals who interact closely with the phenomena, or are there population-level forcing functions (e.g., the current disclosure moment)?
