# Foglets

## What

"Foglet" is the single-unit name for the ~100-micron robots that make up [[utility-fog]] — invented by [[j-storrs-hall]] in his 1989 design paper and presented at a 1993 NASA conference. Each foglet has rudimentary onboard compute, 12-axis arm-linking, and can share power and data with neighbors.

## How it would behave at scale

- **Solid mode.** Arm-locking produces a programmable solid that can mimic rock, sandstone, or any other surface texture.
- **Pixel mode.** Light-emitting antennae make the outer skin function as a 3D display, blending into the surrounding environment.
- **EW mode.** Coordinated swarms can jam specific RF signals, bleed power from drones, and run friend-or-foe codes — explaining how a foglet "mountain" could disable an F-18 while letting friendly helicopters pass through.

## Why it shows up in this KB

[[OOr98r51Sj0]] explicitly invokes foglets as the most parsimonious public-record explanation for the [[sentinels-of-ether]] mountain-cloak scene: a 1,500-foot mountain face "pixelating from 3D to 2D" as helicopters slip through; an F-18 experiencing total electrical failure while the helicopters are untouched. A simple optical hologram cannot do this; a foglet swarm can.
