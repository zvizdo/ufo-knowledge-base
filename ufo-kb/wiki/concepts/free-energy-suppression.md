# Free Energy Suppression

The claim that fully operational zero-point or electrogravitic energy systems — capable of providing pollution-free, cost-free power to every home, vehicle, and nation — have been developed and then deliberately suppressed by the black-program control structure ([[majestic-12]] / MAGIC) to protect existing energy and financial power structures.

## Greer's Central Argument

[[steven-greer]] frames this as the core reason for UAP secrecy: not national security, not alien threat management, but preservation of a global energy and economic monopoly worth "hundreds of trillions of dollars in assets."

> *"The really high-tech stuff they've slammed into a box. It would liberate every home, every car, every factory, every country — into energy independence, no pollution, and no poverty."*

The suppressed technologies, in Greer's account, trace back through:
- [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s electrogravidic experiments (late 1920s)
- The Nazi electrogravidic disc retrieved by [[paul-mellon]] (1945)
- US mastery of gravity control (October 1954, per [[richard-foch]])
- Ongoing black programs run through [[northrop-grumman]], [[lockheed-skunk-works]], and related defense primes

## Fortune 50 Chairman Corroboration

In [[areO7Mej44E]], Greer describes a 2024-era incident: a retired chairman (not CEO) of a Fortune 50 household-name company approached his team. After contact, the man received wet-works threats against his entire family including grandchildren. His statement:

> *"We could have had free energy for the world for decades."*

Greer declines to name the company except to confirm it is "one of the household names in the United States." The man is approximately 80 years old and wants to come forward but cannot without federal protection.

## Technology Chain

Per Greer's account:

| Date | Development |
|------|-------------|
| Late 1920s | Townsend Brown electrogravidic experiments with high-K dielectrics |
| ~1929 | Reproduced in Germany (Kowarski-Frost experiment) |
| 1945 | Paul Mellon retrieves Nazi electrogravidic disc |
| 1954 (October) | US masters gravity control (per Richard Foch) |
| Late 1950s → present | Man-made electrogravitic craft operational |

## Disclosure Project Archive

Greer claims the full technology history is documented in the Disclosure Project Intelligence Archive (`dpiarchive.com`) — "terabytes of data" released publicly.

## Open Question
The Fortune 50 chairman is unidentified. No documentation of the threat or of the energy systems themselves is publicly available. The technology chain is internally consistent within Greer's narrative but each link (Foch, Mellon, Brown) is unverified beyond secondary accounts.
