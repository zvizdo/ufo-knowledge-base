# Space Kids

## Description

"Space Kids" is the informal term for the group of psychic children and young adults that [[andrija-puharich]] gathered at his research compound in Ossining, New York, in the mid-1970s (approximately 1973–1978). The program emerged from the phenomenon of children spontaneously displaying psychic abilities after watching [[uri-geller]] perform on television.

## Origin

When Geller became internationally famous in the early-to-mid 1970s, parents around the world reported their children unexpectedly exhibiting psychic phenomena — spoon bending, poltergeist-like activity, telepathic flashes — after watching him on television. Puharich investigated these cases and eventually gathered approximately a dozen such children at his Ossining house. Researcher [[greg-malazzi]] interviewed five of them for *Mind Traveler* after approximately two years building trust.

The children came to Puharich voluntarily — many approached him at public lectures, saying they wanted to understand their abilities. They did not seek fame; most did not want to be known publicly.

## Activities at the Ossining Compound

- **Psychic testing** — standard ESP and remote viewing protocols
- **Channeling sessions** — children were hypnotized; several channeled [[the-nine]] despite having no prior knowledge of the Nine narrative
- **ELF frequency experiments** — Puharich used a prosthetic finger containing a chip emitting an ELF frequency to instantly induce hypnotic trance when touching someone's forehead; at least one Space Kid recalls this device being used on them
- **TD-100 device** — Puharich's thought-transmission device was apparently used with the kids in ways he kept secret from colleagues

Puharich also used the Space Kids as demonstrations for visitors including [[gordon-cooper]], physicists, and government officials, showing what the children could do.

## Geographic Scope

Most Space Kids in the documented accounts were from the United States, UK, and China. Puharich also traveled to the UK to help that government identify children with psychic abilities, according to his own statements.

## Disappearances

Two female Space Kids are particularly notable:
- **Chiron** (Sharon with two Rs): A woman who was recognized as a genuinely powerful psychic; Puharich would bring her to show to academics and government officials. She "disappeared" and has no online presence; former Space Kids believe she was recruited into a government program (a "Montauk-style project"). Multiple people who knew her well want her found.
- **Valerie Ransone**: Connected to the Nixon White House and ELF research organizations; in contact with CIA's [[kit-green]]; [[greg-malazzi]] believes she can be located.

## Connections to Wider Programs

- **GATE Program** (Gifted and Talented Education): Malazzi interviewed someone claiming to have been in GATE who recognized strong parallels with the Space Kids program; pattern suggests systematic identification of psychic-ability children through educational programs
- **Montauk**: Space Kids' accounts of their experiences are compared to alleged experiments at [[montauk]]
- **Stranger Things**: Malazzi explicitly compares the Space Kids to this fictional program
- **Atomic Energy Commission**: Gave Puharich over $1 million in funding during the Space Kids era

## Legacy and Significance

The Space Kids program represents the operational arm of Puharich's combined research and intelligence activities: if the [[the-nine]] contact was genuine, the Space Kids were extraordinary human receivers; if it was synthetic (manufactured via ELF devices), they were unwitting experimental subjects in behavioral research.
