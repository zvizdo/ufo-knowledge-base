# UAP Consciousness Link

The UAP consciousness link refers to the pattern of claims and evidence suggesting a direct connection between observer consciousness state and UAP phenomena — that UAP may respond to, be triggered by, or co-create their phenomenology with observer mental states.

**Evidence categories**:
- **CE-5 reports**: [[steven-greer]]'s [[ce-5-protocols]] claim that UAP appear in response to group meditation and intentional signaling — suggesting UAP can detect or respond to human consciousness states
- **Altered state reports**: Many close-encounter witnesses report entering unusual mental states during encounters — time distortion, paralysis, heightened perception, telepathic communication — consistent with a consciousness-interface component
- **Observer variability**: The same UAP may be visible to some witnesses but not others in the same location, suggesting perceptual filtering or consciousness-dependent visibility
- **Precognitive aspects**: Some witnesses report knowing the encounter was coming, or receiving information during contact that was later verified

**Vallée's control system**: [[jacques-vallee]]'s [[control-system-hypothesis]] argues that the UAP phenomenon is not simply physical but operates on human belief and behavior — which would require a consciousness-responsive mechanism. The phenomenon appears engineered to affect psychology, not just to be observed.

**Psionic interface**: The [[psionic-uap-interface]] concept develops the thesis that UAP interface is primarily consciousness-to-consciousness rather than physical craft to human observer.

**Epistemics**: The consciousness-link claim is the hardest class of UAP evidence to evaluate, because it lies at the intersection of subjective experience, reproducibility problems, and a physics that does not yet include consciousness as a causal variable.
