# Human Energy Field

The **human energy field** model holds that every cell and organ in the human body emits measurable electromagnetic signals that encode biological information — health state, organ function, pathology. [[colin-ross]] reframes this as the scientific foundation for what Eastern medicine calls chi/qi/prana and what Western medicine already measures in limited forms (EEG, EKG).

## The Scientific Foundation

Ross's argument, which he presents as "mainstream not flaky":

- Every atom emits electromagnetic fields — basic physics, no controversy
- The brain emits EEG; the heart emits EKG; both are already clinical diagnostics
- **But**: we only measure brain and heart — "the human body only has two parts, the brain and the heart" is the implicit assumption of current medicine
- **The whole body** is emitting electromagnetic information at all times — systematically unmeasured
- **Voltage-gated ion channels** (the mechanism of cell-to-cell communication) are electromagnetic in nature; local EM fields affect these differentials and therefore affect cellular communication — studies going back to the 1970s confirm this (per [[robert-becker]]'s *The Body Electric*)

## Chi as EM Field

Ross's synthesis:
> "I'm going to say that chi energy, the spirit, the whatever and the electromagnetic field of the body are the same thing. Just looked at through different lenses."

If you measure it with Eastern instruments (training, meditation, practiced perception), it appears spiritual/mystical. If you measure it with ELF-sensitive electrodes, it becomes bioelectromagnetics.

## The Tricorder Vision

Ross proposes a **medical tricorder** — an array of high-sensitivity electrodes or antennas that would scan the whole body's emitted electromagnetic field simultaneously:
- No radiation input (unlike X-ray, CT, MRI) — only measuring what naturally exits the body
- Can the scan detect disease at the **electromagnetic level** before it manifests biochemically?
- "Probably. Can you spot disease at the electromagnetic level before it trickles down to the biological level? Mhm. Probably."
- Personalized: everyone may have a **unique electromagnetic signature** as distinct as a fingerprint or iris

## Electromagnetic Healing

The corollary: if disease manifests first at the EM level, **EM treatment** should work upstream of biochemistry. Examples already in medicine:
- **Pacemaker** — electromagnetic device controlling heart rhythm
- **Defibrillator** — electromagnetic restoration of cardiac function
- **[[royal-rife]]'s Rife machine** — frequency-based treatment, reportedly effective for Lyme disease patients
- **AmpCoil** — modern frequency-based healing device using PEMF

## Agricultural Applications

Ross speculates: medicine men in indigenous cultures "chanting and waving feathers" may have been emitting specific focused electromagnetic signals from the solar plexus that enhanced crop germination. Testable hypothesis: measure what the medicine man emits, replicate it with an emitter, compare germination rates.
