# Remote Viewing

**Type**: concept/practice  
**Category**: consciousness-non-local, recurring-claims

The practice of perceiving locations, objects, people, or events that are spatially or temporally inaccessible to normal sensory channels — shielded, distant, or in the past or future. In its formalized government application (Coordinate Remote Viewing / CRV), a viewer works with only a geographical coordinate or an opaque "target number" and produces a verbal or sketch description of the target without knowing what it is.

## Origins at SRI (1972–1985)

**Stanford Research Institute (SRI)** became the institutional home for US government-sponsored remote viewing research in 1972, when physicists [[hal-puthoff]] and [[russell-targ]] began evaluating [[ingo-swann]]'s claimed abilities. The CIA funded this initial phase after a pilot session in which Swann allegedly described a shielded magnet at Stanford's physics building with 97 hits out of 100 measurements.

**[[pat-price]]** became the program's most operationally useful subject, reportedly describing Soviet facilities with enough accuracy to be tasked for intelligence collection.

Targ and Puthoff published results in *Nature* (1974) — a paper subjected to intense peer scrutiny but ultimately published. It remains one of the few psi-positive papers in a top-tier mainstream journal.

## The CRV Protocol

[[ingo-swann]] developed **Coordinate Remote Viewing** with Puthoff: a structured protocol dividing a session into stages (Stage 1: major sensory gestalt; Stage 2: sensory data; Stage 3: dimensional data; Stage 4: analytical overlay; Stage 5: symbolic data; Stage 6: 3D modeling). The protocol was designed to minimize imagination and analytical overlay contaminating raw perception.

This protocol became the foundation for the Defense Intelligence Agency's operational **Stargate Program** ([[stargate-program]]).

## Government Operational Use (1978–1995)

- The DIA took primary operational control after the SRI phase, running remote viewers at Fort Meade, Maryland
- Tasked against: Soviet nuclear sites, hostage locations (Iran 1980), Libyan military targets, North Korean facilities, and more
- The 1995 AIR evaluation (requested by CIA): Jessica Utts (statistician, University of California) concluded the effect was statistically real (replication across meta-analysis); Ray Hyman (skeptic) found methodological issues. The CIA cited Hyman's critique to shut the program down despite Utts's positive conclusion
- Program closed in 1995; personnel debriefed and dispersed

Remote viewing intersects with UAP/NHI in two distinct ways:

## McMoneagle's Protocol Model (JpLThEF2dTM)

[[joe-mcmoneagle]] provides the most detailed practitioner account of how remote viewing works:

- Remote viewing is **innate**, not learned — it's about unlearning bad habits (analytical overlay, ego engagement), not acquiring new skills
- **Intent is the critical variable**: when intent is imperative (life-saving), viewers override blocking conditions; success rate jumps 7× with proper double-blind protocols vs. casual tasking
- The unconscious mind has no language; it borrows images from personal memory to represent perceived data — viewers must "down-analyze" (accept raw images) rather than "up-analyze" (interpret toward conclusions)
- **Separation of transmission from analysis**: the monitor (who asks questions) and analyst are both psychically functioning; only the analyst knows the target
- Failure rate: ~40% (comparable to other intelligence modalities under normal conditions)
- **Corpus callosum hypothesis**: the filter between left/right brain hemispheres may be the psychic filtering mechanism; [[kim-peek]] (no corpus callosum, 12,000 books memorized) and non-verbal autistics exemplify what happens when this filter is absent or reduced

McMoneagle's operational sessions with verified outcomes (TK-089 submarine confirmed 114 days post-session; MX Missile program confirmed over 1 year of testing) constitute the strongest firsthand evidentiary record in this KB.

## Puthoff's Firsthand Accounts (iQOibpIDx-4)

### The Quark Detector Origin (Firsthand)
[[hal-puthoff]] recounts the Stargate trigger directly: he invited [[ingo-swann]] to SRI, placed him in front of a shielded quark detector (quantum chip inside nested mu-metal, steel Dewar, and superconducting shielding — "nothing could affect this thing from the outside"). Swann stopped the oscillation *"unequivocally 100%"*, then when asked how, said he *"looked inside"* and drew an accurate schematic of the device's internal configuration — never published. The CIA came to Puthoff within days: a 20-year, $20 million program was born.

Crucially: Puthoff asked Swann where the physical connection was. The result established for Puthoff that the effect was not mediated by conventional EM. His subsequent work connecting remote viewing to vector/scalar potential physics (extended electrodynamics) traces back to this formative experiment.

### Stock Market Application
Puthoff describes the practical application to financial trading in detail: a viewer generates a description of a target object; that object is associated with "market up" or "market down" the following day; the next day the viewer sees whether the association was correct. At 70/30 accuracy (vs. 50/50 chance), the protocol generated **$260,000** over approximately 30 days. Puthoff stopped not because the result failed but because he was simultaneously training army intelligence officers "24 hours a day" and became exhausted.

> "We ran 7030 instead of 50/50... That's how we got this $260,000. And why did you stop? Well, the reason I stopped was I was already working frankly 24 hours a day on training the army intelligence officers."

This is the most specific financial RV account in this KB — from the program's architect, in firsthand terms.

### Causal Mechanism and Repeatability
Puthoff acknowledges the repeatability problem: "If you don't understand the causal mechanism then you're going to have repeatability issues." There were good data runs and failed runs. The 70/30 is an average; it was not consistent session-to-session. He connects this to the difficulty of scaling: the mechanism is not understood well enough to guarantee performance. His two patents on vector/scalar potential engineering represent one attempt to find the physical substrate.

### Submarine Remote Viewing
Puthoff references (without naming the specific session) that remote viewers placed on submarines at the bottom of the ocean produced "some of our best results" — eliminating EM brain waves as the carrier mechanism since no conventional EM can penetrate that shielding.
