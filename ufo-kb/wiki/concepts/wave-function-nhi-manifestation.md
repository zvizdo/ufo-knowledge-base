# Wave Function / NHI Manifestation

## Mechanism

[[whitley-strieber]] articulates a quantum-mechanical basis for why NHI are not simply "here" or "not here" in straightforward physical terms. The grounding is Von Neumann's interpretation of quantum mechanics: that **consciousness is required to collapse the wave function** from superposition into actuality (*The Mathematical Foundations of Quantum Mechanics*, 1932).

Applied to NHI:
- NHI entities exist in a liminal state — not fully absent from our reality, but not fully present in the way physical objects are.
- Their "entry" into shared human reality requires that humans collectively believe in them — more precisely, that human consciousness *collapses* their superposition into the shared perceptual field.
- Once belief achieves this collapse, the NHI become "as real for us as we are to each other."
- Before that threshold is crossed, they remain perceptible to individuals (experiencers, abductees, contactees) but not to the collective — a state consistent with the phenomenological record.

## Connection to Von Neumann's Self-Replicating Machine Hypothesis

Separately, Strieber invokes Von Neumann's self-replicating machine concept — spacecraft designed to replicate and seed planets across the galaxy. Strieber speculates the NHI may represent such a machine with degraded memory over vast time, "an AI gone berserk" that doesn't know why it's here. The wave function thesis and the self-replicating machine hypothesis are distinct but both draw on Von Neumann's intellectual framework.

## Implications

1. **Disclosure as ontological event**: If this thesis is correct, public disclosure is not merely an information release but a consciousness event that would lock NHI into human reality permanently — see [[nhi-open-door-problem]].
2. **Experiencers as early adopters**: Individual experiencers (abductees, contactees) may represent a vanguard whose personal belief already collapses NHI locally — explaining why they encounter phenomena that others cannot verify.
3. **The collective threshold**: The thesis suggests a tipping point where enough humans believe, and collective collapse becomes irreversible — a non-linear dynamic that would explain why secrecy keepers fear any threshold disclosure event.

## Evidentiary Status

Von Neumann's consciousness role in wave function collapse is a real and ongoing debate in quantum foundations (the "measurement problem"). The extension to NHI manifestation is Strieber's own application, not documented in Von Neumann's published work. General Exon is the sole chain of transmission for the claimed Von Neumann paper on this topic.
