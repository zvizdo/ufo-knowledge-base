# Non-Human Intelligence (NHI)

Formal term used by UAP disclosure advocates and increasingly in congressional language to describe potential intelligence of non-human origin, without specifying whether that origin is extraterrestrial, interdimensional, ultraterrestrial, temporal (future humans), or some other category. The term is deliberately agnostic about the nature of the intelligence to avoid prejudging questions that remain open.

Adopted widely after Lue Elizondo and David Grusch used it in their public testimony and media appearances as a way of signaling that the phenomenon involves intelligence without committing to the alien hypothesis specifically.
