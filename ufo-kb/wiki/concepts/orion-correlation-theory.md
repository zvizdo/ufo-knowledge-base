# Orion Correlation Theory

## Definition

Established by [[robert-bauval]] (developed through the 1980s–90s): the ground plan of the three Giza pyramids — Khufu, Khafre, and Menkaure — mirrors the pattern of the three stars of Orion's Belt (Alnitak, Alnilam, Mintaka), including the slight angular offset and the relative dimness of the smallest pyramid matching the dimmer star Mintaka.

Using [[precession-encoded-monuments|precession]] to backtrack the sky, Bauval found that the Belt's culmination at dawn on the Spring Equinox most precisely matches the Giza ground plan at ~10,500 BC (approximately 12,500 years ago) — the epoch the ancient Egyptians called *Zep Tepi*, "the First Time."

## The Precessional Argument

Precession causes the apparent orientation of stars to slowly shift over a ~25,920-year cycle. The Orion Correlation only clicks into precise alignment in the distant past. At the conventional dating (~2500 BC), the Belt stars do not align as well with the pyramids. This implies either:
- The pyramids were designed to reference the 10,500 BC epoch rather than to match the contemporary sky, or
- The pyramids (or at least their ground platforms) were laid out ~12,500 years ago.

[[graham-hancock]] collaborates with Bauval and supports the second interpretation, arguing that the megalithic ground platforms pre-date the pyramid superstructures the ancient Egyptians built on them.

## The Sphinx Convergence

In the same ~10,500 BC epoch:
- The Orion Belt culminates at dawn on the Spring Equinox (Bauval's correlation)
- The Great Sphinx, facing due east, gazes at the constellation Leo as it rises — Leo being the zodiacal house of the Spring Equinox in that era

[[graham-hancock]] and [[robert-bauval]] worked together on this dual convergence, and Hancock credits Bauval as the foundational author whose work he could not have done without.

## Attacks and Significance

Bauval received what Hancock calls "vicious and vile attacks from Egyptologists" simply for noting the pattern. This is treated in the KB as a paradigmatic case of [[archaeology-mainstream-vs-alternative]] — institutional archaeology attacking outsider researchers who notice anomalies.

The theory is a primary pillar of the [[lost-civilization-hypothesis]]: the sky-ground correspondence implies astronomically sophisticated knowledge of precession (a 26,000-year cycle) millennia before any recognized civilization.

## Open Questions

- Does the correlation hold equally well for all three pyramids, or is the fit imprecise for Menkaure?
- What is the exact mechanism by which the "First Time" epoch was chosen as the reference?
