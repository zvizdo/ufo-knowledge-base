# Wolfram-Gorard Physics

A candidate theory of everything developed by [[jonathan-gorard]] in collaboration with Stephen Wolfram (Wolfram Institute), with major results published in 2020–2021. [[matthew-pines]] presents it in [[LpLFWdsIU7M]] as the most plausible physics framework for understanding NHI capabilities, UAP propulsion, and consciousness-based phenomena.

## Core Architecture

**Starting point**: Abstract hypergraphs — directed-edge graphs of pure abstract nodes — plus rewriting rules that transform subgraph patterns into new configurations.

**Multi-way system**: Applying rules in all possible orderings generates a branching "multi-way system" rather than a single deterministic trajectory.

**Three representations**:
1. **Causal graph** (one causal ordering of rule applications): In the limit, discrete measures on this graph converge to the Einstein field equations (general relativity). Gorard derived a discrete Ricci tensor and Riemann manifold structure purely from combinatorial hypergraph structures — without assuming background spacetime.
2. **Branchial graph** (all possible orderings generating branches): Recovers quantum mechanics. Discrete measures converge to the Feynman path integral; branch space converges to projective Hilbert space.
3. **Rulial space** (space of all possible rules on all possible hypergraphs): The infinite-limit structure is an "infinity groupoid" (Grothendieck's homotopy hypothesis), providing a classifying space from which both GR and QM are functorially inherited.

**Observer**: Mathematically characterized as a "Nupendix completion" — equivalencing multiple branches into one perceived thread of history. Quantum mechanics is what any observer necessarily sees when performing this collapse. The measurement problem is dissolved. We perceive consistent physics because we are all nearby in rulial space, slicing the higher-dimensional structure similarly.

## NHI / UAP Implications

**Branchial non-locality**: Two events far apart in spacetime can be one edge apart in branchial space. This provides a physical basis for non-local phenomena (entanglement, remote viewing, telepathy) without invoking anything supernatural — they are simply close in branchial space.

**NHI origin**: Rather than interstellar travel, NHI may originate from "branch space" — nearby in branchial terms but not in spatial terms. This resolves propulsion plausibility entirely. See [[extradimensional-beings]].

**Consciousness access**: Consciousness may be the mechanism for accessing branchial space. Psychedelics and meditation may "relax constraints" on how observers stitch the underlying structure together, expanding perception of the branchial graph.

**Time travel**: An observer encounters a "future state" observationally indistinguishable from a past state — no causal paradox, just branchial equivalencing. "CIA wizards messing with the timeline" can be cashed out in these terms. See [[time-travel-uap]].

**Negative energy / vacuum engineering**: General relativity does not conserve energy globally. If local "negative causal-edge gradient" states are engineerable (analogous to Casimir effect), warp-drive-style propulsion may be physically realizable within this framework.
