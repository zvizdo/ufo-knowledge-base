# ELF Weapons

The category of weapons systems and influence technologies operating in the **Extremely Low Frequency** band (3–300 Hz). Includes both ionospheric communication systems (HAAR, Project Sanguine) and contested neurological-influence applications.

## Discourse Position

Within the UFO / suppressed-research literature, ELF weapons are repeatedly named as a CIA-developed capability for behavioral modification, induced-trance, and remote affect — adjacent to but distinct from acoustic and microwave weapons. [[bob-beck]] is recurringly described as the CIA liaison who attempted to recruit [[andrija-puharich]] to head an ELF weapons department, which Puharich is said to have refused.

## Mechanism Claims

- Brain-entrainment via near-Schumann frequencies (7–14 Hz)
- Trance induction via [[elf-waves]] delivered through Puharich's prosthetic-finger device used on the [[space-kids]]
- Possible substrate for "manufactured" channeled communication interpretations of [[the-nine]]
