# Nanotechnology

## Definition

Nanotechnology covers engineering and manipulation of materials and devices at the nanometre scale (≤100 nm). The KB uses the term in two adjacent senses:

- **Public-record nanotechnology** — MEMS, [[smart-dust]], biological-nanocarrier systems (e.g., [[brainstorms-bci]]'s magneto-electric injectable particles), DARPA-funded MEMS work that matured significantly by 2004.
- **Speculative / classified molecular nanotechnology** — [[utility-fog]] / [[foglets]] (per [[j-storrs-hall]], 1989/1993), programmable matter, nano-assemblers. Public research is theoretical; claimants ([[ben-rich]], Hall) suggest classified counterparts are decades ahead and buried via the [[invention-secrecy-act]].

## Why it appears in the KB

- The [[holographic-mountain-bases]] hypothesis (per [[OOr98r51Sj0]]) requires either holographic projection or programmable-matter swarms — the latter is a molecular-nanotechnology application.
- The [[mind-controlled-craft]] stack (Biofusion → DARPA N3 → BrainSTORMS) extends nano-scale into the human bloodstream as a guided neural-interface delivery mechanism.
- Hall's stated frustration ("a lot of the work needed for real nanotech got actively suppressed") provides the suppression-vector framing the [[invention-secrecy-act]] makes legally possible.
