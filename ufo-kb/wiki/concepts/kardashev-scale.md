# Kardashev Scale

Proposed by **Nikolai Kardashev** (Soviet/Russian radio astronomer) in **1964**, during the era when optical and radio astronomy were being developed as tools for detecting signs of civilizations. The scale classifies civilizations by energy access:

- **Type I**: Controls the entire energy output of its home planet
- **Type II**: Controls the entire energy output of its home star (e.g., Dyson Sphere — would block visible light but create a large IR signature detectable by astronomers)
- **Type III**: Controls the entire energy output of its home galaxy

Our civilization is currently estimated at approximately 0.7 on a logarithmic extension of this scale.

The scale is popularized by Carl Sagan, Neil deGrasse Tyson, Michio Kaku, and others.

## Karl Nell's Critique

[[carl-nell]] argues at Hereticon that the Kardashev scale is **"not very instructive"** for analyzing UAP and NHI:

> "Total civilization energy usage is like an unknowable thing based on observations of potential craft or potential recovered material or biologics or what have you."

You cannot observe total galactic energy usage from UAP evidence. The scale was designed for radio-telescope detection of civilization-scale electromagnetic signatures, not for characterizing specific NHI contacts.

## Nell's Alternative: Universal Civilization Development Model (UCDM)

Nell proposes a three-axis framework:

### Axis 1: What They Have — Energy Density
Not magnitude of energy but *density* — the technological capability implied per unit:
- Fire → metalworking → chemistry → nuclear → spacetime manipulation → modification of the laws of physics
- Jacques Vallee's "physics of information" — how much about reality's deep structure does the civilization understand?

### Axis 2: What They Know — Information Complexity
- Complexity manageable by the civilization's cognition
- Time constant for thought (faster/slower than human?)
- Number of parallel reasoning paths simultaneously available
- This introduces a "consciousness dimension" — cognitive architecture, not just knowledge inventory

### Axis 3: Who They Are — Ecological-Evolutionary Position
- Chemical basis of life (carbon? other?)
- Position on the evolutionary path (100 years ahead? millions? billions?)
- What does it look like for a civilization to be a *billion* years more evolved?
- Relationship with environment (omnivore-descended, herbivore-descended, etc.)

**Phase space distance** between human civilization and any given NHI — what Nell calls a **Hamming Distance** (by analogy with signal processing) — would predict the likelihood of a competitive, symbiotic, or indifferent relationship between civilizations.
