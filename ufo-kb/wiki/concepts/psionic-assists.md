# Psionic Assists

A classified U.S. program reported by [[danny-sheehan]] that uses technology to amplify individuals' capacity for telepathic communication. Sheehan learned of it under an informal secrecy oath approximately 1.5 years before the March 2025 interview (hence approximately late 2023 or early 2024).

## Description

"Psionic assists" refers to a technological amplification of the human nervous system's latent telepathic capacity. Sheehan describes the technology as:
- Derived from work on the **[[1947-roswell]] helmet** — the alleged telepathic interface device recovered from the 1947 Roswell crash that [[philip-corso]] documented in *The Day After Roswell*
- The helmet originally functioned as a craft-pilot interface: the craft responded to the operator's intention without conventional controls
- The reverse-engineered version applies the same amplification principle to human subjects outside of a craft context
- The goal appears to be creating telepathic communication capacity in human operators — potentially for intelligence, signals, or remote viewing applications

## Reported Dangers

The program is reported to be causing fatal neurological damage:
- The amplification produces effects that "fry the brains" of subjects (Sheehan's phrase)
- **American pilots subjected to the program have been killed**
- The program continues operating despite fatalities

Sheehan characterizes this as consistent with the broader pattern of programs that treat human subjects as expendable — the same pattern seen in MK-Ultra, the Pat Price Canadian mind-control broadcast, and other classified consciousness-research programs.

## Current Legal Status

Sheehan is pursuing an investigation through DOJ contacts:
- He identifies [[kash-patel]] (FBI Director) and [[pam-bondi]] (Attorney General) as relevant current officeholders
- The investigation is complicated by the fact that DOJ Criminal Division staff were asked to sign mass resignation letters in early 2025, disrupting institutional continuity
- No public legal action has been taken as of the March 2025 interview
