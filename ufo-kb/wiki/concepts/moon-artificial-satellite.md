# Moon as Artificial Satellite

The hypothesis that the Moon was **constructed** rather than naturally formed — specifically, according to [[jason-reza-jorjani]]'s framework, by the Nordic precursor civilization as a vessel to transport a population from a dying Mars to Earth's orbit, and as a terraforming device to stabilize and make Earth habitable.

## Physical Anomalies

Jorjani catalogs multiple anomalies that he argues are inconsistent with a natural Moon:

**Crater geometry**: Craters are never more than 5–6 miles deep regardless of their width. Some craters are 186 miles wide but only a few miles deep. The crater bases show a **convex surface** — as if impacts are hitting a hard shell rather than penetrating freely. Natural impact craters (e.g., on Mars) scale proportionally in depth.

**Seismic signature**: When part of an Apollo lander struck the Moon (accidental), a seismograph in place registered a seismic signature lasting minutes. A deliberate second impact (reportedly Apollo 13) caused the Moon to "ring like a bell" for **three hours** — a behavior consistent with a hollow metal object, not solid rock. Earth's seismic signature from similar strikes disperses through solid mass entirely differently.

**Mass anomaly**: The Moon is significantly less dense than expected for its volume. Gordon McDonald — Eisenhower's chief space adviser — reportedly stated that the only way the Moon's dynamics made sense was if it were hollow.

**Orbital mathematics**: The Moon is 1/400th the distance between Earth and the Sun, and 1/400th the diameter of the Sun — producing perfect solar eclipses. This coincidence is statistically extraordinary. Its orbit is also unusually stable compared to other planetary moons.

**Stabilizing effect**: Without the Moon, Earth would spin much faster (~8-hour days) and be prone to axial instability. The Moon acts as a powerful stabilizer — behavior suggesting **intentional placement** for precisely this effect.

## Jorjani's Construction Thesis

In *Closer Encounters*, Jorjani argues the Nordics (Mars civilization) built the Moon for two purposes:
1. **Population transport**: Moving a significant population from dying Mars to Earth's orbital vicinity
2. **Terraforming**: Earth in its pre-Moon state had violent electrical storms, rapid axial spin, and constant volcanic activity — inhospitable. The Moon's gravitational influence stabilized the planet, reduced spin, controlled tides, and made sustained complex life possible.

This explains the simultaneous presence of:
- A lunar city (the ongoing base/administration)
- Nordic slave-labor operations on the surface (human or hominid beings being worked there)
- The megalithic architectural continuity between the Moon city, Mars ruins (Swan and McMoneagle), and Earth's largest megalithic structures

## Mainstream Assessment

The hollow Moon hypothesis is not accepted by mainstream planetary science. The seismic "ringing" data is acknowledged but attributed to the Moon's low internal dissipation (its cold, dry interior doesn't absorb seismic energy the way Earth's does). The crater geometry anomaly (shallow relative to width) is explained by the regolith/bedrock structure. The orbital mathematics are treated as coincidence.

## Carlson / Swann Convergence (JGE1NIGhBzw)

[[randall-carlson]] discusses the hollow-moon / geoengineered-moon hypothesis with [[jesse-michaels]] and explicitly states he is "willing to consider that the moon has been geoengineered." His physical evidence:

- **Apollo seismic ringing**: Apollo 12/13 deliberate crash of a booster onto the Moon caused the Moon to "ring like a bell" for 3+ hours — consistent with a hollow metal object. NASA seismometers placed during Apollo 11 confirmed the long vibrational train (~10× longer than Earth's for equivalent strike).
- **Sediment inversion**: Lower lunar sediment layers are *newer* than upper layers — exact inverse of natural deposition; matches an **excavation site**.
- **Mass distribution inversion**: Denser material is *above* less-dense material — reverse of natural geological formation (cf. Earth, where denser material sinks).
- **High-refractory metals**: Chromium, titanium, and uranium-236 present on the Moon in quantities anomalous for a naturally-formed body.
- **Perfect sphere**: The Moon has sufficient mass that it should have naturally deformed into a sphere — and it did — yet it somehow formed in exactly that shape in the first place.
- **Tidal lock**: The Moon rotates on its axis once per orbit, permanently hiding its far side — extraordinary coincidence if natural.

**[[penetration-ingo-swann]]**: Carlson identifies Swann's book as the trigger for his moon-anomaly inquiry. Swann (CIA Stargate program remote viewer) argues in *Penetration* that the Moon is a hollow alien spaceship, citing the same seismic and geological anomalies.

**Zulu Legend**: Carlson references a Zulu tradition that the Moon was brought to Earth "out of the Younger Dryas" — that it arrived and made the climate stable. The Moon's known climate-stabilizing function (preventing axial instability, enabling the intertidal zone) is consistent with the claim.

**Apollo Program Cancellation**: Carlson notes Nixon cancelled the Apollo program (officially: Vietnam War costs), but speculates something might have been found. He frames the conspiratorial reading more cautiously than the prosaic one.
