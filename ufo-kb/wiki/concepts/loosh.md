# Loosh

A term coined by [[robert-monroe]] (*Far Journeys*, 1985) to describe an energy allegedly produced by living beings — especially through strong emotional states — and harvested by non-human intelligences. Monroe reportedly encountered the concept during out-of-body experiences.

## Monroe's Framework

Monroe described encountering a class of non-human intelligences during OBE states who:
- Viewed Earth as a "loosh farm"
- Had engineered or maintained conditions on Earth that maximize loosh production (suffering, intense emotion, birth, death, sexuality)
- The afterlife "recycling" of souls serves to return humans to the farm for continued production

## Jorjani's Extension

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] in [[ABw7EsWh2Us]] extends Monroe's framework:
- Loosh farming = the Nordic overlords' primary motive for the control system
- Psychotronic manipulation (voice-of-god technology, mind control programs) is continuous with loosh-farming architecture
- The monotheistic God concept was inserted into the Nordic control system — making humans surrender their free will voluntarily yields more loosh
- [[mantid-entities]] and soul-harvesting accounts (Carla Turner, David Huggins) may describe the same process at the individual abduction level

## Relationship to Other Control Mechanisms

- Religious submission → voluntary loosh production at scale
- Warfare and mass death → high-intensity loosh events
- Individual abductions → direct extraction described by abductees
- Afterlife "recycling" → prevents loosh-producers from escaping the farm
