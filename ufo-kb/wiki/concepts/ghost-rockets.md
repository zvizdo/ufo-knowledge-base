# Ghost Rockets

The Ghost Rockets were a wave of unidentified flying object sightings that occurred primarily over Sweden (and to a lesser degree Norway, Finland, and Denmark) during the summer and autumn of 1946. An estimated 2,000 reports were filed with Swedish authorities between May and December 1946. Objects were typically described as fast-moving, rocket- or cigar-shaped, occasionally metallic, and frequently reported flying low over or diving into Scandinavian lakes.

**Swedish military investigation**: The Swedish military and the Swedish Defence Staff (FMV) conducted an official investigation. The leading hypothesis was that the objects were Soviet tests of captured German V-1 or V-2 rocket technology over friendly neighboring airspace as a demonstration of capability. However, the investigation failed to conclusively identify the objects, and no recovered hardware was ever produced despite many lake-dive reports and subsequent lake-bottom searches (including sonar surveys that found anomalous "spindle-shaped" objects in some lakes).

**Lake investigations**: The Kölmjärv lake in northern Sweden received particular attention after multiple witnesses reported an object crashing into it in July 1946. Subsequent searches found unusual material on the lake bottom but nothing definitively identified. Similar lake-dive incidents occurred across Sweden, and the Swedish military formally requested that witnesses not publicize the lake-crash reports.

**Historical significance**: The Ghost Rockets are significant as:
1. One of the first major modern UAP waves, predating the 1947 American flying saucer era by a year
2. An early example of official military acknowledgment that reported objects were unidentified, combined with pressure to suppress public reports
3. A demonstration that the modern UAP phenomenon was not uniquely American

**UFO Sweden connection**: [[ufo-sweden]] (Sveriges UFO-Sällskap), one of Europe's oldest UFO research organizations, traces its founding context to the ghost rocket wave. The organization has conducted its own archival research on the 1946 reports.
