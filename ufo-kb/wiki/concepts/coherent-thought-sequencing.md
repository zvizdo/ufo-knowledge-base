# Coherent Thought Sequencing

## What

Coherent thought sequencing is the core meditative technique inside [[steven-greer]]'s [[c5-protocol|C5 protocol]] for inviting UAP contact. Per [[OOr98r51Sj0]], the technique:

1. Sit, close eyes, breathe.
2. Mentally zoom out: galaxies → the Milky Way → a particular dot in its arm.
3. Push back in: solar system → Earth → continent → US → state → city → roof → exact chair.
4. Broadcast "love and peace" as the carrier signal.
5. Optional: low-power laser tag to the starfield (never at aircraft; federal crime).
6. Multiple practitioners amplify the signal — "synchronized love beam."

## Why it allegedly works

- The model: UFOs are "directly coupled to their observer's consciousness." Coherent thought sequencing sends a precise location pin into the consciousness field that the craft's navigation system can lock onto.
- Greer's parallel: like a radio picking up an electromagnetic signal, the craft picks up a directed coherent thought as a clear addressable carrier.

## Why it shows up in this KB

In [[OOr98r51Sj0]], Michels treats coherent thought sequencing as the **consciousness-based attraction method** that pairs with [[jake-barber]]'s **machine-based attraction method** ("dog whistles"). Both methods sit inside the broader [[psionics-uap-interface]] doctrine and the [[p3-psionic-assets]] program.
