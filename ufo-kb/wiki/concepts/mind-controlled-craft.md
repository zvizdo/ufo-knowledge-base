# Mind-Controlled Craft

## The claim

Recovered NHI craft cannot be controlled by conventional pilots — they require a *consciousness interface*. Two operational variants surface in the discourse:

1. **Direct psionic flight.** A pilot with innate psionic ability connects directly to the craft, which "talks back" and responds telepathically. ([[jake-barber]]'s personal account of being consciousness-overtaken mid-flight by a craft.)
2. **Brain-machine-interface bridge.** A non-psionic pilot inputs commands; a BMI translates them into neural patterns in a connected **psionic asset's** brain; the craft reads the asset's neural patterns. ([[p3-psionic-assets]] / "human joystick" model.)

## Public R&D evidence

- [[ben-rich]] (Lockheed Skunk Works): "Exotic propulsion systems work the same way ESP works. All points in time and space are connected. Mind, body, and craft integrated as one."
- [[don-phillips]] (Lockheed-tied whistleblower): "Humanity has the quickest computer built into them than any man-made computer can ever approach. And that includes the Cray." Compares the architecture to the *Firefox* film's mind-controlled weapons helmet.
- [[philip-corso]] + Philip Corso Jr. (with [[danny-sheehan]]): a UFO-pilot helmet with onboard AI that responds telepathically.
- [[john-norsine]] / [[biofusion]]: Lockheed Martin's 1999 closed-loop EEG → target-state-injection BMI.
- [[charles-chase]] / [[gerald-yonas]]: "Cognitive Enhancement using Feedback" patent — same control-loop architecture, same company.
- [[darpa-n3]] (2018): openly funds bidirectional BMIs for UAV control.
- [[battelle-memorial-institute]]'s [[brainstorms-bci]] (2020 Phase 2): nanoscale magneto-electric devices injected into the bloodstream, magnetically guided into specific brain regions.
- [[salvatore-pais]]' 2016 Navy patent ([[pais-patents]]): exotic propulsion that *requires* real-time field-geometry control "faster than a joystick, faster than a keyboard."

## Why it matters

The R&D stack — Lockheed BMIs, DARPA non-surgical BMIs, Navy exotic-propulsion patents — has all three of the layers Jake Barber's claim requires *publicly visible at the patent and program-brief level*. The remaining piece (psionic-asset bridge) is alleged to live inside [[special-access-programs]] not subject to patent disclosure ([[invention-secrecy-act]]).
