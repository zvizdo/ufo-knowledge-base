# Gantenbrinks Door

Limestone slab with copper fittings discovered in 1993 by [[rudolf-gantenbrink]]'s [[upuaut-ii|Upuaut II]] robot at ~65 m depth in the Queen's Chamber's southern shaft; second door behind it found in 2002 by Pyramid Rover but never penetrated.

Gantenbrink's Door is a limestone slab with two copper fittings (originally described as 'metal') blocking the Queen's Chamber's southern shaft at ~65 m depth, discovered by [[rudolf-gantenbrink]]'s Upuaut II robot in 1993. Egyptian authorities restricted further robotic access immediately. In 2002 the Pyramid Rover drilled a small hole through the door and inserted a fiber-optic camera, finding a 21 cm cavity with what appeared to be ancient red ochre markings and a second door behind it. The second door has never been penetrated; the cavity's significance is unresolved.
