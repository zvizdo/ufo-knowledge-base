# Prophetic Dreams

Precognitive or veridical dreams are dream experiences whose content corresponds to real events the dreamer could not have known through normal means — occurring either simultaneously with the event (as in [[remote-viewing]]-adjacent phenomena) or in advance.

## Documented Cases in the KB

### Ralph Moat Larson

[[ralph-moat-larson]] documents the most extensive first-person series from an intelligence officer context:

- **Sadat Assassination (1981)**: Dreamed of the assassination in real-time at Fort Bliss, Texas while the event occurred in Cairo; details including faces of gunmen (later identified as Zawahiri and Seif al-Adl) matched newspaper accounts
- **Numerological downloads (Fort Knox, 1976)**: Numbers 747 and sequence 3-6-9-12-27-40-49 received in an Armageddon dream; same numbers independently dreamed by his wife the same night
- **Mount Athos time travel (1991)**: Extended month-scale experience in dream-state at 14th-century Mount Athos; historical corroborated by the existence of St. Maximos Kafsokalyvis — flying monk who burned huts — which Larson had no knowledge of when dreaming
- **Star Wars / CIA craft dream (1982)**: Dreamed of a global shield program with American craft; Reagan's Star Wars speech followed two years later

## Physical Mechanism Proposals

Larson proposes (via discussions with DOE physicists): a "universe at rest" outside the big bang universe where time does not exist and all events co-exist eternally; prophetic access = briefly accessing the timeless layer. See [[time-travel-uap]] for related frameworks.

Quantum physics discussion: retrocausality; Cramer's "handshake" interpretation (present = handshake between future and past); Schrödinger's time-indeterminate formulation; spooky action across time.
