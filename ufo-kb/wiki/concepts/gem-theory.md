# GEM Theory (Gravity-Electromagnetism)

John Brandenburg's theoretical framework for unifying gravity and electromagnetism — abbreviated "GEM" (Gravity-Electromagnetism). Developed independently of but convergent with [[hal-puthoff]]'s polarizable vacuum / Sakharov-Puthoff approach.

## Core Mechanism

The key mathematical object is the **Poynting vector**: when electric and magnetic fields are combined (as in a light ray), the Poynting vector points in the direction of photon travel and represents radiation pressure. Brandenburg's central claim: **gravity can be understood as a form of radiation pressure** generated by zero-point vacuum fluctuations.

### Vacuum Bernoulli Picture

A conceptual analogy:
- Two bright objects in a dark box (zero-gravity) repel via mutual radiation pressure.
- Two dark objects in a white-hot box **attract** with a 1/r² force (mutual shadowing of the radiation field) — identical in form to gravity.
- In Brandenburg's full theory, the vacuum's zero-point radiation plays the role of the white-hot box; massive objects "absorb" some zero-point energy (effectively casting a "shadow"), and the mutual shadowing produces the gravitational force.

### Plasma Physics Route

An electromagnetic analog:
- Two oppositely-charged plates with electric field between them and magnetic field running across the faces.
- Every charged particle placed in this configuration, regardless of mass or charge, **drifts in the same direction at the same rate**.
- If the plates are tilted relative to each other, all particles accelerate uniformly — indistinguishable from a gravitational field.
- Brandenburg built GEM theory around this electromagnetic-gravity analogy.

## Key Predictions

- **Predicts Big G** (the gravitational constant) with no free parameters / "diddle factors" — something string theory has been unable to do.
- **Derives the proton-to-electron mass ratio of 1836** from the theory's structure. The square root of 1836 = **42.85** — Brandenburg arrived at this result before encountering *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*.
- "The vacuum is hardwired to make hydrogen" — the universe's initial conditions (pure hydrogen plasma after the Big Bang) follow naturally from the vacuum structure the theory describes.
- **Vacuum mass cancellation**: combining GEM with Einstein's metric-tensor formulation of GR gives that the vacuum weighs nothing — the anti-gravity effect of zero-point fluctuations neutralizes their own mass.
- Requires only **one additional dimension** (the fifth dimension of Kaluza-Klein theory) to derive G — not 11 dimensions as in string theory.

## Experimental Work

See [[gravity-modification-experiments]] for the full account. Briefly:
- Mylar balloon with suspended armature, 30W AC at ~400Hz: balloon rises (object loses weight); cost ~$200.
- Tesla 3-phase rotating electromagnetic power: 100g object loses ~0.5g (1 part in 1,000).
- Mechanism: the EM field moves lines of force to concentrate around the object's edges rather than through its center of mass → reduced effective gravitational force.
