# UAP Donation Hypothesis

## Concept

Hypothesis that some recovered NHI craft were not accidents but deliberate donations — left intact for retrieval and reverse engineering. The hypothesis was introduced to [[joe-rogan]] by [[hal-puthoff]] in [[Gf_tKn9TaP8]] as originating with [[diana-pasulka|Diana Pasulka]]'s sources: "She refers to them as donations. She said that's how they were described to her."

The hypothesis modifies the question "how could craft that capable have crashed?" by partially dissolving the premise: some retrievals may not be crashes.

## Variants

- **Single-power donation.** One NHI faction donates to humanity collectively.
- **Distributed-evaluation donation.** NHI donate craft in multiple countries — "something here, something in China, something in Russia" — to evaluate which civilization makes the fastest progress with the technology. Effectively an NHI ISR (Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance) operation against humanity's engineering capability.
- **Accelerated-evolution donation.** Donations function as instrumental nudges in a longer-running shaping program; resembles broader uplift / panspermia / seeding hypotheses.

Puthoff: "Maybe some of them are donations to help us accelerate our forward motion. Or maybe they donate something here, something in China, something in Russia, and see who is best at moving forward, right? Just as part of their ISR evaluation of us."

## Evidentiary Footprint

The hypothesis is asserted, not yet differentiated empirically. Differentiation would require:
- Comparing recovery sites for crash-physics consistency (debris distribution, impact damage, terrain match) vs. intact-emplacement consistency.
- Comparing reverse-engineering progress curves across US, Russia, and China retrievals.
- Establishing whether retrievals cluster temporally or geographically in a way consistent with deliberate distribution vs. random crash physics.
