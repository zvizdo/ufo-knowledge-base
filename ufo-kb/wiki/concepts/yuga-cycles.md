# Yuga Cycles

## Definition

The Yuga system is the Hindu cosmological framework of cyclical time, divided into four ages of decreasing spiritual quality:

1. **Satya (Krita) Yuga** — Golden Age; highest consciousness; longest duration
2. **Treta Yuga** — Silver Age; some decline
3. **Dvapara Yuga** — Bronze Age; further decline
4. **Kali Yuga** — Iron Age / Dark Age; lowest consciousness; shortest but most intense; said to be the current age

One full Mahayuga cycle spans ~4.32 million years in orthodox calculation. However, some modern interpreters (e.g., Sri Yukteswar Giri in *The Holy Science*, 1894) propose a shorter 24,000-year cycle aligned with the precession of the equinoxes.

## Alignment with Great Year and Binary Star

[[matt-lroy]] and others in the ancient-mysteries field note the 24,000-year Yukteswar interpretation aligns closely with the **[[great-year]]** (~25,000–26,000 years). On this reading:

- Each Yuga transition corresponds to a quarter of the Great Year / precessional cycle
- Transitions are driven by increasing or decreasing "cosmic energy" (solar influence)
- The rise and fall of consciousness / civilization mirrors the approach and retreat of the [[binary-star-companion]]

In Lroy's synthesis: the Yuga framework is ancient India's way of encoding the binary-star perihelion cycle — the same knowledge that the Egyptians encoded in precessional monument alignments ([[precession-encoded-monuments]]) and the Greeks in the myth of the Golden Age.

## Current Position

Per the Yukteswar interpretation, we are ascending out of Kali Yuga — consistent with Lroy's argument that we are approaching the next perihelion (which will be catastrophic) but also the beginning of a new cycle of rising consciousness.

> ⚠ Note: The orthodox Yuga cycle durations (4.32 million years) are entirely incompatible with the Great Year correlation. The 24,000-year version is a modern revisionist reading, not mainstream Hindu orthodoxy.

## Sumerian King List Convergence (R7oyZGW99os)

[[randall-carlson]] adds a crucial cross-cultural data point. The Sumerian **pre-diluvian king list** records 10 kings who reigned before the flood, with individual reigns of 36,000; 43,200; 28,800; 36,000; 64,800; 36,000; 21,600 years (etc.). These numbers belong to the [[sacred-number-canon]] family.

The **total** of the 10 pre-diluvian reigns = **432,000 years** — exactly one Kali Yuga.

The **Maha Yuga** (full Vedic cycle) = **4,320,000 years** = 10 × Kali Yuga = the Sumerian total × 10.

Carlson: "It's almost as if we have two different traditions but one system... The Sumerians preserve knowledge about the *subdivisions* of this 432,000-year span, symbolized by the reigns of the 10 pre-diluvian kings, and then the Vedic cycles kick in."

**The Vedic breakdown (human years)**:
| Yuga | Human years | Divine years (360 each) |
|---|---|---|
| Kali Yuga | 432,000 | 1,200 |
| Dvapara Yuga | 864,000 | 2,400 |
| Treta Yuga | 1,296,000 | 3,600 |
| Satya (Krita) Yuga | 1,728,000 | 4,800 |
| **Maha Yuga** | **4,320,000** | **12,000** |

Each Yuga also has transition periods (Sandhya/Sandhyamsa) equal to 1/10 of the main period, adding another 144,000 years to the Kali Yuga total — the same number as the 144,000 redeemed in Revelation and the Mayan Baktun.
