# "This UFO Shut Down 10 Nukes!" — Robert Salas on American Alchemy

Host [[jesse-michaels]] interviews First Lieutenant (ret.) [[robert-salas]], primary witness to the [[1967-oscar-flight]] incident at [[malmstrom-air-force-base]], Montana, where all 10 Minuteman missiles in Oscar Flight went offline during a UFO encounter on March 24, 1967.

## The March 24, 1967 Oscar Flight Incident

- Salas and crew commander [[fred-maywald]] assigned to Oscar Flight near Roy, Montana (~100 miles east of Great Falls / [[malmstrom-air-force-base|Malmstrom AFB]])
- Underground at the launch control center monitoring 10 Minuteman I missiles
- Around 9pm: topside flight security controller (FSC) called down reporting strange lights
  - Lights flying very fast, stopping in midair, reversing course, making 90-degree turns, no engine noise
  - "These are not airplanes" — Salas replied "You mean like UFOs?" and essentially dismissed the call
  - That same week, the Great Falls Tribune had published reports of farmers and citizens seeing strange lights
- ~5 minutes later: FSC called back, terrified and screaming, barely coherent
  - Salas had to calm him down before he could speak
  - "Sir, we got all the guards out. They've got their weapons pointed at this red-orange pulsating light. It's huge."
  - Estimated ~40 feet long; guard tried to look inside and reported "some kind of oval-shaped solid object"
  - Salas told FSC to keep the facility secure and let no one inside the fence
  - FSC also mentioned a guard had been injured (unrelated to UFO — likely climbing the fence)
- Salas went to wake up [[fred-maywald]] to report the calls
  - Before he could finish the briefing: alarm horn went off — one missile went from green to red (no-go, can't launch)
  - Then all 10 missiles went down sequentially, one at a time
  - All reading: "guidance and control system failure"
  - Salas confirmed: in three years, maybe one missile had gone down from a power glitch — never multiple simultaneous shutdowns
- Incursion lights activated at two launch facilities (LFs) during the shutdown
  - Salas sent guards out to those LFs; they also saw objects hovering just above the facilities
  - Guards lost radio contact on the way back; had to be relieved of duty after returning — "scared to death"
- [[fred-maywald]] called wing command post; ordered Boeing and maintenance crews out to restart and retarget all 10
  - After the call, Maywald told Salas: "The same thing happened at another flight" — referring to the Echo Flight incident 8 days earlier
- Later recorded Maywald's confirmation: posted at spiralgalaxy.org (Salas's website)

## The Belt, Montana Sighting (Same Night, ~7:30–8pm)

- Simultaneously ~35 miles southeast of Great Falls:
  - Truck driver spotted a bright light pacing his vehicle, blinking at him
  - He pulled over, saw it land in a ravine, inspected the site
  - Called the highway patrol; patrolman came and also observed the object
  - Light came back up, flew off at high speed, then returned while the patrolman was present
  - County sheriff was called; inspected area around where object landed — found broken branches
  - All three called [[malmstrom-air-force-base]]
- Lt. Col. [[lewis-chase]] (base operations officer + designated UFO officer for the Condon investigation) responded
  - Couldn't bring helicopter in at night due to lack of night-flight training in that area
  - Decided to wait until morning
  - In the meantime: UFOs seen on radar at Malmstrom; airmen at the base saw bright white lights
  - Chase filed telegrams to USAF HQ and to the [[condon-commission|Condon Commission]]; took truck driver's statement
- [[bob-jameson]] (targeting officer) was called in from home around 10–10:30pm to restart missiles
  - Briefed at hangar: was told a UFO had been tracked going down into a canyon near [[belt-montana|Belt, Montana]]
  - Went to wing command post colonel's briefing room; heard radio contact with field teams
  - Teams reported blue lights, then green lights at the bottom of the canyon; terrain too steep for personnel or helicopter at night
  - At dawn, helicopters sent in — "this UFO just went right through the helicopters and out"
  - Jameson assigned 4 missiles to restart; given briefing: if you see a UFO on the road, call in and go to nearest LCF; if inside an LCF, take your team and targeting tapes into the silo, close it
  - All 4 missile restarts went smoothly; missiles came back up as if nothing had happened

## Echo Flight (8 Days Earlier, March 16, 1967)

- [[walt-feele]] (then lieutenant, later retired colonel) was in charge

## The Boeing Investigation

- SAC HQ commissioned Boeing to do a complete investigation
  - Boeing identified a mechanism: injecting a certain electrical signal (specific frequency, voltage, duration) could cause the logic coupler (onboard guidance computer) to momentarily malfunction
  - Bench test confirmed: could cycle off a single missile's guidance system via signal injection
- Critical unexplained problem: they could not explain how that signal penetrated the system across all 10 simultaneously
  - The cables were ~1 mile long, about 8–9 inches in diameter, and **triply shielded** against electromagnetic interference
  - Boeing admitted in their report: the shutdowns were caused by an "external signal" but they "could not explain how that signal got into the system"
  - The two Boeing engineers named by SAC HQ confirmed to Salas and co-author James Klotz: they couldn't explain it
