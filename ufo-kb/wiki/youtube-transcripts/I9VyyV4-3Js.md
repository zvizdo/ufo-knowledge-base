# "Jesus Was In A Secret Occult Group!" — Top Researcher Brian Muraresku

Filmed at the **Hagia Sofia Greek Orthodox Cathedral** in Washington DC (beside the National Cathedral) — a deliberate choice by Muraresku to frame the conversation in the physical setting of the mystery he investigates: how did Christianity emerge from Greek mystery religion, and where did the psychedelic sacrament go?

## Headline Claims

- The Eleusinian Mysteries were the **spiritual epicenter of the ancient Mediterranean** — Plato, Pythagoras, Sophocles, Pindar, and Cicero all participated; revealing secrets was punishable by death ("the fight club of the ancient world").
- [[carl-ruck]] (Boston University) asked 42 years ago whether **Dionysus was the god of wine or the god of psychedelics** — the kykeon drink consumed at Eleusis contained ergot (grain fungus with LSD-like compounds); Muraresku argues the scientific organic data now supports this.
- Jesus used the Greek word **"musterion"** (religious secret, to be confided only to the initiated) when asked why he spoke in parables — "Christianity by definition is born with secrets."
- John 6 in the Gospel of John parallels Dionysus mystery rites: "whoever eats my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life" (present tense, not future) — the eucharist as potential psychedelic sacrament in early Greek-speaking Christian communities.
- The Roman hierophant **Praetextatus** told Emperor Valentinian in 364 AD that shutting down the Eleusinian Mysteries would put the human species in peril — "life would be a biatos [unlivable]."
- [[terence-mckenna]] framed NHI entities as either "autonomous fragments of psychic energy temporarily escaped from the ego's control, OR things that actually exist in a freestanding autonomous reality." Muraresku places this squarely in the historical mystery continuum.
- [[diana-pasulka]]'s thesis: **1947 (Roswell) was a dividing line** — before it, people saw angels, demons, fairies, leprechauns, genies; after it, people see aliens. The idiom changes; the phenomenon may be continuous.
- [[aldous-huxley]] predicted a new Eleusis — a psychedelic religion — in the 21st century; Muraresku sees state-sanctioned therapeutic psilocybin (Oregon model), underground Dionysian traditions, and First Amendment religious-exercise paths as the three emerging channels.

## Jesse Michaels Near-Death Experience (Firsthand)

Jesse describes a near-death experience at age 5: hit in the head by a golf club, rushed to hospital bleeding, experienced **bliss and comfort with no pain** ("felt like I leave my body" as anesthesia kicked in), had vivid visionary experiences during surgery. He only made sense of it 10–20 years later reading NDE and mystical literature. He now frames it as a personal mystery ritual — "dying before dying."
