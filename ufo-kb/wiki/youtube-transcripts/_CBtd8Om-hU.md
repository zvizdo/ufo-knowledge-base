## Pre-filter Note

## Headline Claims

- **Autonomic nervous system control**: Wim Hof demonstrated willful control of the autonomic nervous system in peer-reviewed lab conditions — first documented case in medical science. Controlled brain scans showed 100% neural activity; previously thought impossible.
- **Corona radiata activation**: Hof claims to activate the corona radiata (nerve endings normally inaccessible) via breathing exercises, calling this "enlightenment" in neurological terms — not metaphysical.
- **DNA cold-shock protein reinstallation**: The breathing + cold method reinstalls cold shock and heat shock proteins around cells, restoring the mRNA filter mechanism that stress-induced comfort-zone living erodes. Elizabeth Blackburn (Nobel Prize, telomeres) and Elissa Epel (UCSF) confirmed the DNA/cellular mechanism in San Francisco research.
- **E. coli injection study**: Hof and subjects he trained were injected with E. coli bacteria; breathwork-activated immune system suppressed symptoms within 15 minutes vs. the normal 6–8 day flu cycle.
- **Flexner Report as suppression origin**: The 1910 Carnegie/Rockefeller-commissioned [[flexner-report]] restructured American medicine, eliminating osteopaths, homeopaths, and holistic practitioners and creating an FDA/AMA model that can only monetize patentable drugs — actively marginalizing open-source techniques that work.
- **Dying every day as mystery ritual**: Jesse explicitly frames Hof's "die once a day" practice as a modern analogue of the [[mystery-schools]] initiation at Eleusis — Plato, Aristotle, and Socrates all went to Eleusis (13 miles from Athens) and came back claiming they witnessed their immortal soul; Hof's method replicates the structure without the institution.
- **Royal Rife parallel**: Jesse cites [[royal-rife]]'s Tesla-coil frequency medicine as the paradigm of open-source healing technology suppressed by the same institutional dynamics now alleged to suppress UAP knowledge.
