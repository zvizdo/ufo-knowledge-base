# "I Located A UFO Base In Arizona!" (Ft. Ross Coulthart)

Extended interview between host [[jesse-michaels]] and Australian investigative journalist [[ross-coulthart]]. The episode covers Coulthart's full origin story as a UAP journalist, the [[nat-kobitz]] disclosure in detail, the [[1978-kaikoura]] incident and the NZ government cover-up, the [[bogota-sphere]], the [[secret-mountain-sedona]] underground base investigation, the [[lockheed-martin]]–[[bigelow-aerospace]] spacecraft transfer attempt, the Five Eyes / [[echelon]] background, [[harry-turner]]'s JIO analysis, the [[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown]] rumors, and the current disclosure landscape.

## Headline Claims

### Sedona Underground Base (Coulthart Firsthand)
1. **Coulthart witnessed a large golden orb escorted by Blackhawk helicopters over [[secret-mountain-sedona|Secret Mountain]] at 1:30 AM** — accompanied by [[melinda-leslie]] and her night-vision goggles; multiple military-grade helicopters present; helicopter fuel bill estimated at $50–100K for a single evening; orb had no visible means of propulsion.
2. **Ground penetrating radar located a 50-foot-wide reinforced steel concrete tunnel just below the road around [[bradshaw-ranch]]** — confirmed by Australian GPR operator Paul (surname withheld); also found two large three-phase power boxes humming underground at the fenced ranch area.
3. **A CCTV camera at the fenced area of [[bradshaw-ranch|Bradshaw Ranch]] points directly at the spot where [[linda-bradshaw]] says she photographed a portal** — not outward toward intruders, as security logic would demand.
4. **Tunnel line extrapolated from tunnel walls runs directly to the Clark Dale Cement Plant** — locals were compensated 20 years ago for property damage from intense underground drilling; timeline consistent with secret tunnel construction.
5. **Armed tier-one soldiers have ordered hikers in public parkland to leave under threat of lethal force** — multiple corroborating accounts; men with automatic weapons in civilian jurisdiction on public land near Secret Mountain.

### Nat Kobitz — Full Disclosure Account
6. **Kobitz confirmed the US has recovered multiple craft** and implied Roswell, Aztec, and Kingman were all real retrieval incidents — Kobitz was former head of Science and Technology for the [[us-navy|US Navy]]; Coulthart reached him via handwritten letter (one of ~160–170 letters sent to avoid electronic surveillance).
7. **Kobitz confirmed he was shown a piece of a UAP bulkhead at Wright-Patterson, underground, under heavy guard** — material appeared almost weightless despite its size; under electron scanning microscope it showed two metals bonded at the atomic level, atom by atom. "I know for a fact that's not a technology I'm aware of."
8. **Kobitz invited Coulthart to ask the question** — after months of conversations about Navy drone tech and the lunar lander, Kobitz signaled "Why don't you ask me the question?"; when asked if he had been read into a UAP/alien technology program, he answered "Yes" before Coulthart finished the sentence.
9. **Kobitz facilitated introductions to additional sources** — these introductions were Coulthart's "big breakthrough" that shifted him from skeptic to convinced.

### Lockheed Martin → Bigelow Spacecraft Transfer
10. **A senior Lockheed Martin executive implicitly confirmed holding alien technology** — executive said "It's not us. It's [[cia|the agency]]" when asked why they don't come out; said "We want to do it."
11. **Lockheed was planning to hand off an alien spacecraft to [[bigelow-aerospace]]** — Coulthart identifies this as the real story behind the "metamaterials" reference in the 2017 New York Times piece; may be the [[1953-kingman-crash]] craft.
12. **[[glenn-gaffney]] (or the [[office-of-global-access|Office of Global Access]]) intervened to stop the Lockheed → Bigelow transfer** — Coulthart expresses uncertainty whether it was Gaffney specifically.

### Bogotá Sphere
13. **[[jaime-maussan]] received a sphere found in Colombia** — multi-minute video shows it flying in air; aluminum alloy reportedly 3× harder than standard aerospace aluminum; symbols around the top.
14. **[[mario-woods]] and [[randy-anderson]] both independently recognized the symbols on the sphere as matching what they saw during their own UAP encounters** — Woods saw symbols inside the craft at Ellsworth 1977; Anderson saw them at [[naval-surface-warfare-crane|NSWC Crane]].
15. **AI analysis of the sphere symbols returned the phrase "the circuit"** — Coulthart acknowledges this could indicate a hoax but treats the convergence of witness recognition as significant.
16. **[[brandon-fugel]] ([[skinwalker-ranch|Skinwalker Ranch]]) has agreed to fly [[jim-maron]]'s sphere in his private jet to Utah for testing in his laboratory** — Coulthart's source confirms this deal is in place.

### 1978 Kaikoura UFO Incident
17. **New Zealand Prime Minister [[robert-muldoon|Robert Muldoon]] implied that the Americans had the Kaikoura investigation shut down** — Coulthart learned this from Muldoon himself as a young journalist; Muldoon said something like "Yeah, the Americans wanted that one shut down pretty quickly."
18. **NZ Air Force officers were pressured by the PM's office to find an explanation as quickly as possible** — archives released ~2011 confirm the squidboat explanation was manufactured under pressure.
19. **Witnesses included the radar operator, two journalist-passengers, and the flight crew** — contemporaneous radar confirmed solid objects moving in the exact directions the visual witnesses described.

### Australia / Five Eyes / Echelon
20. **Australian SAS operators were used in US UAP retrieval operations and paid cash US dollars, required to sign NDAs** — no US personnel involved; Australian government was never briefed; retrieval operations happened on Australian soil with Australian operators.
21. **Canadian scientists are actively involved in the legacy reverse-engineering program** — kept secret from the Canadian government and people; Coulthart learned to ask about this from his [[echelon]] investigation experience.
22. **Russia disclosed to the US during early Glasnost that it had a crashed craft** — a known US astronaut was deployed to assist in the retrieval; this is secondhand from a friend of Coulthart's who speaks with astronauts.

### Consciousness / Psionics
23. **A Georgetown source in 2018 told Coulthart "It's all about consciousness"** — Coulthart says it took him seven years to begin understanding what that meant.
24. **Coulthart confirms an ongoing psionic operation being run in [[pentagon|the Pentagon]]** — consistent with the CIA Gateway/Stargate precedents; nominal 1995 shutdown was only "offensive, no defense" — illogical reason to terminate an offensive-capable program.

### Disclosure Politics
25. **[[david-grusch|David Grusch]] gave Congress chapter-and-verse on where to find specific spacecraft, including codes and titles** — not a single subpoena has been issued as a result. Coulthart views this as congressional failure, not mere slowness.
26. **A congressional staffer working on UAP testimony was intimidated by DoD using Blackhawk helicopters hovering over his home** — Coulthart knows this staffer personally.
27. **Coulthart was detained at CBP after the Grusch story** — a flag had been placed on his travel file at a level too high for the person who inquired to access.
