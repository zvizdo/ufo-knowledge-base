# Peru's Three Fingered "Aliens" (WTF Are These?!)

Jesse Michaels documents his firsthand visit to the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] collection — three-fingered, three-toed humanoid bodies found in 2015 near Nazca, Peru. The episode covers forensic expert opinions, the political battle over the bodies, DNA complexity, rare-metal implants, broader connections to global UFO cases, and the offer from [[colossal-biosciences]] (the firm that de-extincted the direwolf) to run ancient DNA testing.

## Headline Claims

### Forensic experts

- Four credentialed forensic experts independently concluded the M-type hominid bodies are real organisms.
- [[john-mcdow]] — president of American Forensics Association; Grand Wall Award; called the bodies "perhaps living organisms."
- [[jim-caruso]] — Denver chief medical examiner, former Navy medical officer with 300+ autopsies; joined McDow in person.
- [[david-ruiz]] — president of Peruvian Society of Legal Medicine; "we are talking about humanoid species — perhaps a hominid like us, a three-fingered hominid."
- [[jose-zalce]] — chief of medical division, Mexican Navy; states beings are "definitely real organisms" and possibly extraterrestrial given DNA dissimilarity.
- None of the four found stress-fracture lines on the skulls indicative of cranial-deformation rituals.
- None found evidence of finger or toe surgical mutilation.

### Pregnant M-type and the falsification test

- [[monzerat]], an M-type body, has a fetus visible on tomography.
- The mother's body shows "her hands in the air" because she was holding a now-deflated amniotic belly at time of death.
- Per [[david-ruiz]]: a conventional doctor handed only the raw measurement data would say "this baby has three fingers in the hands and feet."
- If the fetus is genuinely tridactyl on independent reanalysis, the surgical-mutilation hypothesis is falsified — no known synthetic-biology team can replicate this fraud.

### Rare-metal implants

- M-type bodies contain implants made of [[osmium-cadmium-implants|osmium and cadmium]].
- Osmium is 1,500× rarer than gold and 2,500× rarer than silver.
- 100 kg–1 ton of osmium is extracted globally per year (100 kg ≈ a newborn elephant; 1 ton ≈ a Honda Civic).
- Both osmium and cadmium were only officially discovered in the 19th century.
- The metals appear in bodies carbon-dated 700 to 18,800 years ago.
- Organic tissue is osseointegrated to the metal — the beings were alive at implantation.
- Osmium has also been detected on meteorites.
- Population geneticist [[alan-earas]] speculates the implants modulate **bioelectricity** to drive gene expression: "the rest of the alteration is at another layer of the phenotype, this bio-electricity layer."

### DNA: the Maria decomposition

- DNA analysis of the M-type [[maria-tridactyl]] returned ~70% identifiable, ~30% "completely different" / unknown.
- The 70% known fraction itself contained an anomalous combination of three lineages: chimpanzee/bonobo, a separate African chimp lineage, and a South Asian human primate.
- Per [[jose-zalce]]: "even in the known DNA, it's a combination that you would never see — it's so exotic."
- Bioinformatics expert [[elena-hardy]] cross-referenced the publicly-released NCBI data against the literature on digit-development genes (~50 in the Sonic Hedgehog pathway).
- She found a mutation in the GLI3 gene of the type associated with polydactyly (extra/fused digits) in human studies — including a documented Chinese family case.
- Hardy: "It doesn't have anything to do with [tridactyly] but it's not a super common mutation. It's enough to make me go *huh*."

### Government suppression

- [[jose-zalce]] was imprisoned for 5 days for his involvement: "they said I was a liar and was putting in risk the honorability of the whole system of the Navy."
- The [[peruvian-ministry-of-culture]] has made *seven* documented attempts to seize the bodies from the [[university-of-ika]].
- VP [[martin-alcron]] reports each seizure attempt arrived "first with police, with a prosecutor, and all that."
- The Ministry raided one of [[jaime-maussan]]'s press conferences mid-event, with federal police, federal prosecutors, and Ministry of Culture personnel taking over the microphone.
- Ministry has falsely claimed scientists like [[roger-zuniga]] are themselves "grave robbers manipulating human remains."
- [[jaime-maussan]] has filed a $300 million defamation lawsuit against the Peruvian government.
- Per Pavle (Activo podcast), the lawsuit forced the Ministry to take the case more seriously and tacitly endorse research at the [[university-of-ika]] from September 2024 onward.

### The 2024 Reuters debunk and the conflation

- In 2023, wooden tourist figurines were confiscated at Lima airport.
- The figurines were made by tourist-souvenir maker Manuel Caceras and glued together.
- Reuters published [[flavio-estrada]]'s claim that these wooden trinkets were the "Nazca mummies" — "dolls made from animal bones joined together with modern synthetic glue."
- Jesse argues this conflation is the sole reason most Westerners assume the case is fake.
- Estrada has since shifted to claiming the M-type fingers were *deliberately mutilated* — a tacit admission the bodies themselves are real.

### Reverse-engineering thesis

- Per [[david-ruiz]]: "If I accept the bodies, I will accept the technology."
- "If I want to use the technology for reverse engineering and have supremacy over planet earth, then I prefer to hide the bodies, do hidden reverse engineering, and use it for war."
- This frames Nazca suppression as continuous with the broader [[reverse-engineering-hypothesis|reverse-engineering thesis]] thread.
- The local Nazca-language place name reportedly translates to "laboratory where cloning and hybridization were done."

### Grusch / Burlison / Congress

- Pentagon UAP whistleblower [[david-grusch]] privately told Rep. [[eric-burlison|Eric Burlison (rendered "Berles" in this audio)]] that the physiology of *some* (not all) Nazca mummies is "congruent with what he has seen in briefings in his former role."
- Grusch qualified: "some are faked, but some of the mummies… based on the physiology, are congruent."
- The qualification's specificity reads as authentic engagement rather than blanket endorsement.
- Burlison initially declined to attend; he reversed after Grusch's recommendation.

### Colossal Biosciences offer

- [[ben-lamb]] of [[colossal-biosciences]] has agreed to run proprietary ancient-DNA tests on the mummies.
- Colossal is advised by Harvard geneticist George Church.
- The firm has already de-extincted the direwolf from ancient DNA.
- Permission from the [[peruvian-ministry-of-culture]] is the only remaining gate.
- Jesse offered to personally fund any qualified geneticist, paleoarchaeologist, or proteomics expert to travel to the [[university-of-ika]] if Colossal is blocked.

## Specific Anecdotes & First-Hand Material

### Leandro's discovery (2015)

- Huaquero (gravedigger) [[leandro-gravedigger]] tripped on a cave wall in the Nazca region.
- The wall fell apart; he saw a chamber "full of datoms" (diatomaceous earth).
- He began digging clumps that turned out to be heads and limbs.
- The cave contained 200+ body parts: isolated heads, appendages, and intact bodies.
- All bodies were desiccated by [[diatomaceous-earth]] — which is why organs are preserved.
- Carbon dates span 700 to 18,800 years ago, mostly overlapping with the Nazca period (100–800 CE).
- Three dominant body types: S-types (small, winged), J-types (2–3 ft "reptilians" with eggs in their bellies, ~25–30 specimens), M-types (4–5 ft hominids with full organs/tendons/cartilage/bones intact).

### The "wrong cave" provenance issue

- Multiple anonymous sources told Jesse that [[leandro-gravedigger]] charges ~$3,000/tour to bring researchers to a cave that physically cannot hold 200+ bodies.
- The actual source is described by sources as a large subterranean tunnel system.
- Sources declined to disclose identities for fear of reprisal from local cartels.
- Cartels have a financial incentive to keep selling either real bodies *or* high-fidelity knockoffs to elite collectors — Jesse heard prices "in the seven figures."

### Spielberg's Reagan anecdote

- Steven Spielberg recounts that [[ronald-reagan]] watched [[close-encounter-cases|Close Encounters of the Third Kind]] in the White House.
- Reagan said afterward: *"I want to thank you for bringing ET to the White House. We really enjoyed your movie. And there are a number of people in this room who know that everything on that screen is absolutely true."*
- Spielberg notes Reagan delivered the line without smiling, then everyone in the room laughed.

### Neil Armstrong's 1976 Tayos Cave trip

- [[neil-armstrong]] led a BBC film crew into the [[tayos-cave|Tayos Cave system]] at the edge of the Ecuadorian Amazon in 1976.
- He was looking for a rumored ancient metallic library made of gold.
- Jesse flags the anomaly: why would the first man on the Moon then go looking for aliens *on Earth*?

### The alpaca-protein debunk on a J-type head

- An anonymous proteomics expert ran peptide-sequence analysis on bone samples from one isolated J-type head.
- Peptide sequences identified *all* the bone (main skull and attached parts) as **alpaca**.
- A published paper independently shows CT-scan morphological similarity between this J-type head and an alpaca brain case.
- This is the strongest specific debunk on the J-types and supports Jesse's working hypothesis: M-types likely real, J-types likely reconstructions / homages.

### The Sri Lanka Maria finger-bone test

- [[steve-meera]] reports two finger bones from Maria sent to a Sri Lanka lab returned conflicting sex chromosomes — one came back male, one female.
- Jesse's reading: human contamination during handling, not evidence of composite construction.
- "Ancient DNA is very tricky."

### David Ranell's hybridization theory

- [[david-ranel]] proposes Maria is a *natural* (not engineered) hybrid.
- ~250,000 years ago, proto-bonobo and proto-troglodyte populations were still active in Africa.
- Some lineage migrated to Asia and back ~1,700–2,500 years ago.
- A parasite found in one body is endemic to Africa — supportive evidence.
- Three fingers + extra phalanx + elongated digits would be an arboreal-life adaptation.
- Sloths (also tridactyl) provide a parallel.

### Skull-elongation distinction

- [[skull-elongation|Cranial deformation]] using boards, cloth bindings, or head frames on infants was common in [[nazca-peru|Nazca]] and Paracas cultures.
- The practice was an *imitation of the gods* — implying the gods looked tridactyl/elongated.
- Deliberate deformation leaves visible stress-fracture lines on the skull where pressure was applied.
- On Maria's tomography, no such lines exist.
- The skulls are also already large at the natural form.

### The Bogotá sphere

- [[jaime-maussan]] showed Jesse a recovered aluminum-alloy [[bogota-sphere]] (Colombia).
- Over 4 minutes of video shows the object dropping below tree canopy and being retrieved on the forest floor.
- Maussan claims the alloy is 3× the hardness of aerospace-grade aluminum.
- Claimed weight changes: 2 kg → 10 kg.
- Claimed electromagnetic anomalies.
- Jesse is more skeptical of this artifact than of the mummies; flags a follow-up episode.

### Russian Kutsk three-strike convergence

- February 2011: mass UFO sighting near Kutsk, Irkutsk Oblast — see [[2011-kutsk-ufo-sighting]].
- Local nuclear assets sit at "Object 644," bunkered ICBM warhead magazines (not silos).
- Home of the 29th Guards Missile Division — a live nuclear-armed mobile ICBM force.
- One month later, two Russian teens uploaded the [[2011-kutsk-snow-alien]] video (12M YouTube views).
- The teens "confessed" the body was hoaxed with breadcrumbs, plasticine, and chicken skin.
- Jesse: the morphology matches the Nazca J-types, and the breadcrumbs cover-story echoes Caceras's tourist-trinket cover-story for Peru.
- The teens (Timor and Kir) were 18 and 19 at the time — within plausible YouTube-prank range, but Jesse questions the official debunk's quality.

### Wagant's Peru crash retrieval

- UFO whistleblower [[jonathan-wagant]] (in [[1997-peru-ufo-crash-retrieval]]) describes a crash-retrieval team in the Peruvian jungle.
- Craft observed embedded at a 45° angle into a rocky cliff face.
- Wagant observed what appeared to be a **four-fingered arm** hanging limply from the open hatch.
- Morphology echoes (but doesn't match) the Nazca tridactyls.
