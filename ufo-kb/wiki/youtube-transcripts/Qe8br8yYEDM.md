# Meet the Nuclear Missile Guard Abducted by Aliens

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] episode interviewing **[[richard-barth]]**, a former USAF air policeman stationed at [[vandenberg-afb]] in 1964 who claims a CE3 encounter — physical approach by a humanoid NHI entity, abduction aboard a craft hovering above the base, and direct telepathic communication about NHI intent to prevent nuclear war. The encounter occurred in **September 1964**, the same month that [[bob-jacobs]] filmed a UAP interacting with an Atlas missile from [[vandenberg-afb]].

## Headline Claims

1. **September 1964 CE3 at Vandenberg**: Barth, on night guard duty at a Minuteman missile site, was approached by a humanoid figure in a trench coat who paralyzed his weapon arm and rendered him unconscious — he awoke aboard a circular craft hovering above the base with four silhouetted NHI beings observing him. (Firsthand)
2. **NHI stated they would prevent nuclear war**: The entity communicated (telepathically, no words) that Earth is important to them, that they were gathering intelligence on the missile electronics, and that they would not allow nuclear war — even if it meant intervention. (Firsthand — Barth)
3. **Multiple NHI species, not all benevolent**: The entity communicated that more than one non-human species is present, and that "not all were as benevolent" as the ones Barth encountered — with implications of conflict between groups over control. (Firsthand — Barth)
4. **NHI accessed missile electronics**: The entity apologized for frightening Barth, explaining they needed "access to the site" and the "data" from the missile's electronics room (the guidance computer at the top of the Minuteman). (Firsthand — Barth)
5. **Possible nasal implant**: Barth found a small chrome-like metallic sphere in his nose years after the encounter; separately, during 2001 nasal surgery a doctor encountered an anomalously hard object in his right nostril that was subsequently suctioned out and discarded. (Firsthand — Barth)
6. **Memory suppression and time lapse**: The full memory surfaced approximately two weeks after the event in a single vivid flash; Barth believes the NHI installed a time-delayed release of the memory. Lost time of approximately 2am–6am (3–4 hours). (Firsthand — Barth)
7. **Vandenberg UAP hotspot continues**: [[jeff-natelli]] and colleagues experienced five UAP incidents at Vandenberg between 2003 and 2005, including the [[2003-vandenberg-red-square]] event (massive glowing red square over two missile defense sites, witnessed by Boeing contractors) and a triangular craft larger than a football field. ([[jeff-natelli|Jeff Natelli]] — firsthand, read into record by Michaels)
8. **May 1964 yellow orb tracking Atlas missile**: A separate earlier incident — Barth witnessed a yellow orb track an Atlas missile through staging on a clear day in approximately May 1964. A sergeant told him to keep his mouth shut. This is distinct from [[bob-jacobs|Bob Jacobs]]' September 1964 incident (different conditions, different behavior). (Firsthand — Barth)
9. **Post-encounter universal love state**: After the entity departed, Barth experienced a profound state of ecstasy and unity with all living things — illustrated by an overwhelming flood of love for a nearby chipmunk. He interprets this as NHI-induced or a residual effect of the head-on-lap experience. (Firsthand — Barth)
10. **NHI-conditioned silence for years**: Barth tried multiple times after the event to tell someone but found himself unable to — "like there was lingering control." The conditioning persisted until approximately 2008 when he called Coast to Coast. (Firsthand — Barth)

## Bob Jacobs Case Cross-Reference (Same Base, Same Month)

Michaels introduces Bob Jacobs' case in detail as context for Barth's encounter — both occurred at Vandenberg, September 1964:

- Mansman showed the film to Jacobs and two CIA men in "gray suits"; told Jacobs "It looks like we got a UFO"; then said "You are never to speak of this again. It never happened." CIA men threatened him with consequences.
- Subsequently: Jacobs' Air Force records were deleted; the launch itself was scrubbed from official records; officials denied anyone named Jacobs was at Vandenberg; the 1369th Photographic Unit was denied

- [[florenz-mansman|Major Mansman]] was tracked down by researchers in the 1990s and confirmed he managed Jacobs — corroborating the incident

Hastings connected Barth and Jacobs; they discussed their cases directly. Barth's May 1964 orb-tracking incident was likely a separate encounter, but both men believe September 1964 Vandenberg activity was coordinated.

**Jacobs' credibility corroboration**: Major Mansman was tracked down by UFO researchers in the 1990s and confirmed he managed Bob Jacobs at Vandenberg — providing independent corroboration for Jacobs' account. Mansman's confirmation anchors the September 1964 Vandenberg activity with a named, confirmed superior officer.

## Background — Richard Barth

Barth joined the Air Force after struggling in college. He was initially enrolled in a navigator training program — his academics were high but he had undiagnosed dyslexia causing him to transpose numbers under pressure (123 → 132), which caused significant navigation errors. He washed out after two failed test missions. With 13 months of selective service remaining, the Air Force assigned him to guard duty: low training requirement, close to his Los Angeles home. He was assigned to the **4392 Combat Defense Squadron** at Vandenberg, wore fatigues (not blue uniforms), carried a .38 pistol, and "basically stood on post all day."

**Missile systems he guarded**: Atlas, Thor, Minuteman, Titan. Vandenberg was a *test* site, not a primary nuclear deployment base — too easy a target (right on the Pacific; tsunami from an ocean detonation would wipe it out). The actual Minuteman deployment bases were far inland (Malmstrom AFB, Montana). Vandenberg's purpose:
- Testing missile fuel integrity after 2+ years of storage
- Training crews on actual launches
- Launching satellites (Thor for low-orbit spy sats; Atlas for heavier payloads; Titan for ICBMs with pre-fueled silo storage)
- Testing guidance systems down the Pacific Test Range

The Minuteman guidance electronics are in a separate "donut" room at the top of the missile — has its own access door into the silo, contains the computers that execute the launch command once given. This is the area Barth believes the NHI accessed.

## The May 1964 UAP Sighting (Earlier, Separate Incident)

In approximately **May 1964** — four months before his September CE3 — Barth witnessed a **yellow orb** tracking an Atlas missile during a daylight launch. Unlike the Jacobs case (which had ground fog), this was a clear cloudless day. The orb didn't circle the missile or interact — it simply stayed with it through staging: "It tracked it. Then it staged and then it continued tracking — which meant it wasn't a fuel leak."

Barth reported this to a sergeant at the launch site the next day. The sergeant told him: "If I was wise, I would keep my mouth shut." Barth did.

When Barth later compared notes with Bob Jacobs, they determined this was likely a *different* incident — different timing (May vs. September), different conditions (clear vs. foggy), different behavior (tracking vs. beam attack). Both agreed Vandenberg UAP activity was coordinated across incidents.

## NHI Craft and Entity Description

**Interior of the craft** (from the inside only — Barth never saw it externally):
- Circular room approximately 15 feet across
- Gray-colored, smooth and seamless — "like one giant piece of gray plastic," appeared molded
- Concave floor — Barth was on his knees; the four entities were on a bench somewhat elevated
- A transparent circular hole in the floor, ~2.5–3 feet across — clear but not glass; no seam; rim only; barrier prevented cold air from entering
- Backlighting from the walls put the entities in silhouette; room was otherwise dark
- No seams, rivets, or visible mechanical features

**The four seated entities** (different from the primary humanoid):
- Sat on a bench that was a continuation of the same wall material
- Silhouetted — large heads, proportionate round necks (not the thin-neck stereotype), very large dark eyes visible even in silhouette
- Round heads, round chins — not the typical pointed chin of pop-culture depictions; Barth compares them to a baby's head in proportions
- Two on each side of a space Barth interpreted as a door; all watching him; not communicating
- Barth felt comfortable in their presence — "everything was okay, I wasn't afraid"

**The primary humanoid entity** (the communicator):
- Approximately 5'10", humanoid build
- Wore what looked like an officer's raincoat (trench coat) and a cap — initially mistaken for a human officer conducting a check
- Face is a complete blank in Barth's memory — "if I try to see this face I can't see it — it's a blank. But whatever it was was terrifying me."
- At ~5'10" in human clothing, this entity does not match the classic "gray alien" morphology

## Post-Encounter Experiences and Conditioning

**The chipmunk moment**: After the entity departed and told Barth to keep his eyes closed for a while, Barth opened them to find a chipmunk nearby. He felt an overwhelming wave of love: "I just felt this connection — here's another living being and I'm part, we're part of the... there was no separation. We were just living beings and there was something good about that." He attributes this to a residual ecstasy-like state that the being had induced.

**Conditioning to stay silent**: Multiple times in the months following the event, Barth tried to tell someone but found himself unable to — "it was like I was told not to and the beings had conditioned me or hypnotized me or something. I would try, but then it was 'don't, you know' — like there was lingering control." He kept silent until ~2008 when he called into Coast to Coast during a Robert Hastings segment.

**The Coast to Coast call**: Barth called into George Noory's show while Hastings was a guest, got through a large portion of the story, and the call dropped at the exact moment Hastings asked him about lost time. Hastings had remembered the call but not the specific content. This call is live and still accessible. Barth also called into [[art-bell|Art Bell]]'s show once and exchanged emails with the person being interviewed, who believed him but couldn't include him without corroboration.

**Post-2000 Fourth of July sighting**: Barth and approximately five others at a park along a wash were watching fireworks when someone noticed a bright light that wasn't Jupiter. Barth viewed it through binoculars — it moved side-to-side, then into a zigzag, then shot off north. He identified it as a UFO; the skeptical friend present confirmed the movement after looking through the binoculars. No direct contact; no interaction.

## Institutional Debunker Pattern (Michaels' Analysis)

Host Jesse Michaels offers a structural analysis of who ends up as UFO debunkers in official capacities:
- **Donald Menzel**: deep state figure, close to [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]], held all clearances, ran Bureau of Statistics — dogmatically opposed
- **[[j-allen-hynek|J. Allen Hynek]]**: initially opposed but "repented" and became a sincere researcher over time
- **[[edward-condon|Edward Condon]]**: wrote the first quantum mechanics textbook in English; appointed to run the supposedly independent Condon Committee review of Blue Book. Had been close friends with Oppenheimer from their studies at Göttingen in the 1920s; wrote the *Los Alamos Primer* (required reading for all Manhattan Project employees); helped select Los Alamos as the site; wrote the McMahon Secrecy Act (1946) — precursor to the Atomic Energy Act (1954) — which Michaels frames as overlaying atomic secrecy architecture with UFO secrecy
- **[[sean-kirkpatrick|Sean Kirkpatrick]]** (AARO director, 2022–2023): ties to DOE, worked at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, received an award from Brookhaven as a teenager, financial incentives to deny — Michaels frames AARO as "probably just a fake hit job on UFOs," similar to Blue Book
- Michaels' thesis: "Nobody who spends more than three days on the UFO topic comes to the conclusion that it's fake other than people associated with our nuclear programs or the DOE or the Atomic Energy Commission."
