# NASA Whistleblower: "We Systematically Suppress UFO Data!"

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] episode. Guest: [[kevin-knuth]], physicist at [[university-at-albany|University at Albany]], formerly at NASA Ames (early 2000s), computational/ML background. Host: [[jesse-michaels]].

## Headline Claims

- **UFOs are local**: "They're not coming in from another star system every other Tuesday. They're present here." — Knuth endorses the underwater/ocean-base hypothesis as the best bet for NHI habitation.
- **US reverse-engineering program probably exists**: "I think that's probably true. I've heard this from enough people who've done that kind of work for years." Knuth has met people who claim to have worked on craft at bases.

[[john-callahan]] secretly preserved 45 minutes of FAA radar data; Reagan's scientific team said "we've never had 45 minutes of radar data from one of these things before."
- **Nimitz Tic-Tac physics**: ~5,000 G acceleration, ~1,100 gigawatts power requirement (more than total US nuclear output). At 1% heat-loss efficiency the craft would melt — implying either >99.99% efficiency or a completely different mechanism (warp, inertia manipulation).
- **Pre-construction nuclear surveillance**: SCU study shows UFOs present at nuclear sites *before construction was complete and before radioactive material was present* — implying precognitive or intelligence capability beyond passive observation.
- **Gemini 11 battery drain + "strange object" lithograph**: Public NASA transcripts record a power anomaly when an object flew over; [[scott-simkinson]]'s archive contains a lithograph labeled "Strange object as seen by Gemini 11 astronauts" — Knuth believes it's a commemorative mock-up, not a real photograph, but it implies institutional NASA awareness of the event.
- **[[alan-bean]] (Apollo 12) told Knuth personally**: During Skylab, astronauts photographed a red flashing light — photos are public but blurry. "Nobody puts lights on satellites."
- **[[donald-hornig]]** — Kevin's father's cousin, presidential science advisor to Eisenhower/Kennedy/Johnson — appears in an official government document on the [[1965-kecksburg]] crash, listed as present.

## Water-World NHI Habitat Hypothesis

- Knuth's thesis: underwater bases are "your best bet" — water provides global mobility, zero radar return, and effectively infinite concealment
- Historical cases stretch back to 1800s: balls of light emerging from ocean, hovering beside ships, then ascending into clouds; [[richard-dolan]] documents these in his underwater UFO book
- Multiple water worlds in Earth's solar system (Europa, Enceladus, Titan subsurface) — not a unique planetary condition, making it plausible life evolved in water elsewhere
- Underwater top speed of UAPs estimated ~3,500 mph (vs. 250,000 mph in open air) — still far beyond any human submarine technology
- UAP heating problem solved by water: waste heat dissipation underwater is orders of magnitude more efficient than in air
- NHI use of water is consistent with historical maritime UAP reports from every maritime civilization; [[richard-dolan]]'s case database extends this centuries back
- Knuth notes astronauts' reports of objects "coming out of the water and taking off into the clouds" fits this hypothesis precisely

## The Shostakus Neutron-Activation Technique

- [[matthew-shostakus]] (UAlbany, Knuth's co-investigator) developed novel neutron-activation isotope analysis specifically for UAP debris
- Method: irradiate material with a neutron source → heavier isotopes created via neutron capture → mass-spectrometry spray identifies created isotopes → original isotope ratios inferred
- Advantage over standard mass spec: reconstructs *original* isotope composition rather than current-state surface chemistry
- Smoking-gun test: if isotope ratios don't occur naturally on Earth AND don't pattern-match asteroid samples, strong evidence for off-world or non-natural manufacture
- [[arts-parts]] — magnesium-bismuth nanolayers purportedly from Roswell; one key piece: bismuth as high-k dielectric connects to Biefeld-Brown electrogravitic effect
- Nano-layer construction: one layer paramagnetic, adjacent layer diamagnetic — structural properties with no known natural Earth analog
- [[garry-nolan]] suggested these materials might function as micro-scale terahertz waveguides — potential propulsion energy-transfer component
- Ukraine and Israel UAP debris samples also in Shostakus program alongside Art's Parts
