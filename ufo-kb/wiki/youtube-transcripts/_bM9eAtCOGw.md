# Harvard is Studying UFOs (Ft. Avi Loeb)

American Alchemy interview with [[avi-loeb]], Harvard astrophysicist and founder of the [[galileo-project]]. Recorded at Loeb's home in Massachusetts during a snowstorm. This is the most substantive public interview Loeb has given on the Galileo Project's technical setup and his ʻOumuamua reasoning. Loeb appears unfazed by both the cold and his colleagues' criticism.

## Headline Claims

- **ʻOumuamua may be an artificial lightsail.** Loeb's canonical argument: all natural-object explanations (frozen nitrogen, hydrogen cloud, dust cloud) have holes. The object's flat pancake shape (length-to-width ratio >3, almost never seen in asteroids), non-gravitational acceleration without a cometary tail, and erratic speed changes are collectively consistent with a thin solar-reflective sail. Nature does not produce such thin solid structures. The lightsail hypothesis is the only one requiring no new physics — only a builder.
- **The Galileo Project has 100+ scientists and is installing a multi-sensor array on Harvard College Observatory.** Sensors: optical, infrared, radar, audio, magnetic. AI algorithms will classify objects as bird, drone, or unidentified. Data will be fully public — distinguishing it from all government programs.
- **Fermi Paradox critique:** Loeb dismisses the passive "where is everybody?" framing. Analogy: someone sitting at home saying "nobody is knocking so I have no neighbors" — the correct move is to look through windows with telescopes, not to conclude absence. Or: use a fishing net with holes smaller than the fish; if you don't deploy one, you catch nothing.
- **VR / Metaverse as Fermi resolution:** When Zuckerberg announced the metaverse, Loeb immediately recognized it as a potential explanation for why advanced civilizations disappear. Civilizations that retreat into virtual realities would still emit detectable waste (second law of thermodynamics — they still produce entropy/trash into space). We could find this "technological trash" as interstellar archaeology.
- **Proxima Centauri b city lights may be detectable by JWST.** Loeb and a Stanford undergraduate wrote a paper arguing the James Webb Space Telescope could detect artificial illumination on the night side of Proxima b (a tidally locked planet with permanent day/night sides). City lights on the night side would be the signature.
- **Anti-gravity is real — proved by cosmic expansion.** Einstein's cosmological constant (negative gravity from vacuum) was confirmed when astronomers found the universe is accelerating its expansion. The question is whether anti-gravity can be harvested on a local scale; Loeb says this is unknown but "definitely possible in principle."
- **Eric Weinstein joined the Galileo Project.** Weinstein and Loeb speculate about UAP physics — Weinstein proposes Geometric Unity-like properties; Loeb's co-speculator suggests macroscopic quantum properties as an emergent phenomenon of underlying quantum mechanics.
- **Instruments, not testimony.** Loeb refuses to write a scientific paper based on eyewitness testimony, including fighter pilot accounts. The quality of government sensor data is unknown because it's classified (adversaries must not know instrument capabilities). The Galileo Project's value is open, instrument-grade data.
- **The "caveman and cellphone" analogy.** If we found technology we couldn't explain, a non-curious caveman would call it "a rock of a type we've never seen before." Loeb's peer review of his colleagues' ʻOumuamua explanations is that they're all doing exactly this.
- **AI astronauts as first contact vector.** Loeb's preferred model: advanced civilizations send technological equipment with AI rather than biological beings (cosmic rays would destroy any living traveler). This is his updated UFO/UAP working hypothesis — craft, not creatures.
