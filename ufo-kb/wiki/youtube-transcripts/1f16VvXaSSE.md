# Multiple Alien Groups May Be Visiting Earth! (Lue Elizondo Documentary)

Documentary-style [[american-alchemy]] episode centered on [[lue-elizondo]], described as "the man behind modern UFO disclosure." [[jesse-michaels]] hosts. The episode was filmed across two locations — Elizondo's home in Wyoming and a Masonic Naval Lodge in Washington DC — and blends a long-form Elizondo interview with a serendipitous first-person witness account of the [[1952-washington-dc-flyover]] from an 82-year-old groundskeeper, Maurice Hill ([[mr-hill-1952]]), who recognized Elizondo at the Lodge door. It ranges from insider government disclosure politics through speculative UAP propulsion physics into Elizondo's personal high-strangeness experiences.

## Headline Claims

- **Multiple alien groups**: Elizondo asserts more than one distinct non-human intelligence (NHI) group may be visiting Earth simultaneously — a notable departure from the singularity assumption ([[multiple-alien-groups]]).
- **[[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]] as classification gatekeeper**: Per a Walter Kirn–[[david-grusch]] conversation Elizondo references, [[dick-cheney]] sat "at the top of the pyramid" controlling access to legacy UAP programs ([[walter-kern]] is the existing slug).
- **Binary reality framing**: Either UAPs are real or there is mass hysteria among admirals, generals, cleared pilots, nuclear technicians, and weapons officers — "those are the only two options" ([[credibility-frameworks]]).
- **UAP propulsion involves gravity manipulation**: Craft don't burn fuel — they warp the space-time metric around themselves, requiring Planck-scale energy. The propulsion bubble is the engine; the visible craft surface is ablative/sacrificial ([[gravity-manipulation-propulsion]]).
- **"Angel hair" as ablative residue**: When the craft is "juiced up," the outer-layer interaction with the energy bubble sloughs off as molten residue ("angel hair") that has been documented at landing sites.
- **Nuclear connection confirmed**: UAPs are specifically drawn to nuclear weapons infrastructure ([[ufo-nuclear-nexus]]). Elizondo confirms [[robert-hastings]]'s reporting ([[ufos-and-nukes-hastings|UFOs and Nukes]]) is "spot on" — and reports the same pattern was internally observed during AATIP.
- **Occult roots in aerospace**: [[jack-parsons]] (JPL co-founder) recited an [[aleister-crowley]] hymn before rocket tests; multiple top US astronauts were Freemasons; the Apollo / Mercury / Saturn / Juno NASA missions are named after deities, suggesting pervasive esoteric imprinting on the American space program ([[occult-aerospace-connection]]).
- **Atomic-classification system overlays UFO secrecy**: Per [[david-grusch]], the [[manhattan-project]] custody chain ([[manhattan-project|Manhattan Project]] → [[atomic-energy-commission]] → [[doe]]) was the template for a parallel "Q-clearance" UFO secrecy system, with documented sightings over [[savannah-river-site]], [[oak-ridge-national-laboratory]], and [[los-alamos]] in the 50s and 60s.
- **Government classification is unnecessary for the basic fact**: NHI existence does not intrinsically require classification — secrecy is political, not national-security-necessary.

## Elizondo's Firsthand Claims

Per Rule 13: each is presented as personal experience or direct handling. Decomposed into concrete-evidence bullets rather than abstracted summary.

### Cuban-Exile family origin

- Elizondo's father (Lou Sr.) fought shoulder-to-shoulder with Fidel Castro in the Cuban Revolution before falling out with the Cuban Communists.
- Lou Sr. came to the US and joined the [[cia]]'s Bay of Pigs invasion force.
- After Bay of Pigs, Lou Sr. joined [[alpha-66]] — the hardcore militant Cuban-Exile group whose explicit mandate was to invade Cuba and topple Castro.
- Elizondo grew up in this environment from infancy, learning to handle every gun "under the sun."
- This is the first time Elizondo has publicly contextualized his career arc with the family-origin militancy.

### Met Edward Teller at age 7

- On an Air Florida flight from Sarasota to Miami, Elizondo sat next to a man eating chicken soup.
- The man was [[edward-teller]] (Manhattan Project; "father of the H-bomb").
- His father had government friends — the seating wasn't accidental.
- Elizondo only learned Teller's significance years later.
- Teller is named in [[harry-turner]]'s 1971 Australian Joint Intelligence Organisation memo as a US researcher associated with anti-gravity work — making the chicken-soup encounter retrospectively load-bearing.

### Astral-projection / remote-viewing of a Guantanamo detainee

- A CIA-connected small group convened in a SCIF over lunch.
- Method: [[hal-puthoff]]-style remote viewing / astral-projection.
- Target: a high-value detainee at Guantanamo Bay (where Elizondo had previously held a leadership posting).
- Goal of the experiment: attempt to mentally "extract" or influence the detainee.
- Days later, a major news article reported the detainee had perceived "five white figures over his bed shaking his bed."
- Elizondo called [[hal-puthoff|Puthoff]] and they discontinued — operational psi-influence at this level was crossing ethical/legal lines.
- Implication: the underlying capability worked; the constraint was institutional, not technical.

### Premonition of a DC Metro fire

- Elizondo told [[hal-puthoff|Puthoff]] he had "a really bad feeling" something would happen on the DC Metro within 90 days.
- He smelled smoke and fire — somatic pre-cognition.
- Puthoff logged the prediction with a date.
- On day 89, a fatal fire occurred on the Red Line of the DC Metro — within Elizondo's predicted window.

### Handled a UAP-encounter implant (VA pathologist case)

- A US veteran reporting close-encounter contact had a foreign body surgically extracted by a VA pathologist.
- The object: roughly a square inch.
- Encapsulation: soft, gelatinous human soft tissue.
- Core: iridescent multicolored metal (purples and silvers shifting in light).
- Extending fibers: multicolored yellows, greens, and blues threading into the surrounding human tissue.
- Per the pathologist's microscope observation, **the central metal piece was moving under its own metabolism**.
- Elizondo cites a parallel case at an [[aerospace-corporation|aerospace corporation]] where an embedded object has migrated through a person's body for years.
- He also references [[whitley-strieber]]'s documented ear implant, which evaded extraction.

### Green orbs at Wyoming home

- Onset coincided with Elizondo's entry into AATIP — the phenomenon followed the program.
- Witnessed by Elizondo, his wife, his children, and his neighbors.
- Witnessed by other AATIP-program colleagues independently — *the same phenomenon followed others into the program too*.
- During filming for this episode, [[jesse-michaels]] and Aaron Candel (of [[yes-theory]]) personally witnessed one of the green orbs on-site at the Wyoming property.
- This converts what would otherwise be an Elizondo-only claim into multi-witness on-camera testimony.

### Sulfur at Skinwalker Ranch (Michaels firsthand)

- Following a prior interview with Elizondo, [[jesse-michaels]] visited [[skinwalker-ranch]] in person.
- Michaels smelled sulfur on the property.
- Michaels reports "a kind of not-the-best couple weeks after that" — physical/psychological aftermath.
- Treated as supporting Elizondo's broader high-strangeness pattern; sulfur is recurrent in occult / paranormal contact reports.

### Worked alongside Grusch in a SCIF

- Elizondo reports professional overlap with [[david-grusch]] inside a SCIF.
- "I am telling you straight up — that guy, he knows a lot more even than he was allowed to say."
- This is the first time Elizondo has publicly described their direct working overlap, not just acknowledged Grusch's existence.
- Implication: Grusch's congressional testimony was deliberately constrained by what he was permitted to disclose, not by what he knows.

### Mattis briefing blocked / NYT release

- In 2017, Elizondo attempted to brief Defense Secretary General [[james-mattis|James Mattis]] on UFOs.
- Intermediary staff blocked the briefing from reaching Mattis — Elizondo did not even get the meeting.
- Elizondo and [[chris-mellon]] then took the three Pentagon UAP videos to *The New York Times*.
- The resulting December 2017 NYT article triggered the modern UAP disclosure wave.
- The lesson Elizondo draws: at the right level of bureaucratic blockage, the press becomes the only remaining channel.
