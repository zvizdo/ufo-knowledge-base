# UFOs Are Monitoring Nuclear Bases Globally (Ft. Robert Hastings)

Deep-dive interview with [[robert-hastings]], author of *[[ufos-and-nukes-hastings|UFOs and Nukes]]* ([[ufos-and-nukes-hastings]]), who has personally interviewed 167 security-cleared (PRP-level) personnel who witnessed UAP activity at nuclear weapons sites. Host: [[jesse-michaels]] ([[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]]). This is the definitive Hastings interview and the most comprehensive primary-source coverage of the nuclear-UAP nexus in the American Alchemy catalog.

## Headline Claims

1. **UAPs have monitored every major class of US nuclear site** — warhead storage areas, ICBM missile fields, nuclear test ranges, plutonium production sites — since at least 1945, with sightings ramping up sharply after Trinity and Japan.
2. **Echo Flight (1967):** All 10 LGM-30 Minuteman ICBMs at [[malmstrom-air-force-base|Malmstrom AFB]] Echo Flight went offline simultaneously while a red glowing object was reported hovering over the front gate — witnessed firsthand by [[robert-salas]] and [[bob-jameson]].
3. **Big Sur filming (1964):** USAF Lt. [[bob-jacobs|Bob Jacobs]] filmed a disc-shaped UAP intercepting an Atlas missile test warhead at ~8,000 mph and firing four beams at it; the CIA confiscated the film; [[lue-elizondo]] saw it in a Pentagon safe.
4. **Minot 1966:** [[david-shindell]] received an actual launch-indicator signal (missile countdown triggered remotely) while a UAP hovered nearby — had to flip an inhibit switch to stop an automatic launch.
5. **Soviet Ukraine:** A Soviet intelligence document (smuggled to [[george-knapp]] by [[vladimir-plantonov]]) describes a huge disc hovering over a Soviet missile base; missiles entered countdown mode for 15 seconds unprompted.
6. **AARO whitewash:** [[sean-kirkpatrick]] told Congress "no evidence of extraterrestrials" after AARO failed to record 11 PRP-veteran testimony sessions; veterans felt "betrayed." [[robert-hastings]] calls AARO "the Condon Committee 2.0."
7. **The Condon Committee was a CIA/Air Force hit job:** [[edward-condon]] wrote his dismissive conclusion *before* the study was complete; was a close associate of [[j-robert-oppenheimer]] and helped design [[los-alamos]].
