# "Tesla's Technology is Still Classified!" (Ft. Senate Staffer Kirk McConnell)

A wide-ranging interview with [[kirk-mcconnell]], retired professional staff member on the **[[senate-armed-services-committee|Senate Armed Services Committee]]** and Senate Intelligence Committee. McConnell is unusual in UAP discourse for combining deep institutional access (decades in Congress, personally knew [[chris-mellon|Chris Mellon]] since the 1980s, invited [[sean-kirkpatrick|Sean Kirkpatrick]] to Senate briefings) with genuine engagement with exotic physics — Tesla's longitudinal waves, extended electrodynamics, and the [[die-glocke|Nazi Bell]] program.

## Headline Claims

- **Tesla's technology is classified**: McConnell believes Tesla discovered what we now call extended electrodynamics (scalar/longitudinal waves), and that his papers — seized by the FBI after his 1943 death — remain classified. He speculates this suppression is driven by the weapons-proliferation risk of easy vacuum-energy extraction.
- **[[robertson-panel|Robertson Panel]] origin in Foo Fighters**: [[hp-robertson]] was assigned to investigate Foo Fighters in World War II, then 7 years later led the CIA panel that created the [[project-blue-book|Blue Book]] debunking mandate; [[luis-alvarez]] was on both as well — a non-coincidental continuity from wartime anomalous-object investigation to peacetime suppression.
- **UAP downed by nuclear test (1962)**: McConnell corroborates [[harold-malmgren]]'s [[bluegill-triple-prime|Bluegill Triple Prime]] account — ship logs from the test recovery fleet describe two anomalous radioactive objects (a black sphere and a green tube) not part of the instrument package; a separate salvage operation log also appears; XW-51 warhead details confirmed.
- **September 19, 1962 AVCO MarkV incident**: A separate test one month before Bluegill shows a luminous object accelerating to catch up with a re-entry vehicle traveling at ~10,000–12,000 mph; the official [[department-of-defense|Defense Department]] report explicitly stated "objects of unknown origin and identity were tracking/pacing" the warhead — a UAP in official documents.
- **[[dick-cheney|Dick Cheney]] as UAP gatekeeper**: McConnell confirms hearing consistent reports that George HW Bush was read into the legacy program as CIA director and briefed his son; that the Bush-Cheney administration took a more hands-on approach; that Cheney's NSC staff were involved. He cannot confirm this but also cannot dismiss it.
- **NSC disclosure study ([[george-w-bush|George W. Bush]] era)**: A study was commissioned by the NSC to assess whether to disclose possession of ~6–12 recovered craft; conclusion was costs outweigh benefits; [[hal-puthoff]] states publicly he consulted for this study at the Office of Net Assessment; Sean Kirkpatrick publicly denied it was anything more than an academic exercise — McConnell assesses Puthoff the more credible account.
- **Telepathy / extended electrodynamics nexus**: McConnell advances the theory that the same physics ([[hal-puthoff]]'s extended EM scalar/vector potentials) may explain both NHI telepathic communication and human-to-human telepathy; links [[telepathy-tapes]] podcast, [[roger-penrose]]'s Orchestrated Objective Reduction, avian cryptochromes, and [[cleve-baxter]]'s plant communication as converging evidence streams.
