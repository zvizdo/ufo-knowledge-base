# Your Brain Is A Quantum Time Machine (ft. Eric Wargo)

Video essay + interview with [[eric-wargo]] on his thesis that the human brain is a quantum time machine — capable of sending information backward in time from a future knowledge state, explaining precognition, intuition, and remote viewing in a materialist framework.

## Headline Claims

- [[eric-wargo]]'s core thesis: the brain is a **hybrid classical/quantum system**; as a quantum system it can exhibit **temporal non-locality** — sending information from a future knowledge state back to an earlier moment in time, producing what we call precognition.
- Remote viewers may not be perceiving the present target — they may be perceiving **future confirmatory feedback** (when they are later told whether they were correct). Hal Puthoff confirmed this "feedback loop" model as consistent with his view. **(firsthand: Jesse Michaels relaying Puthoff confirmation)**
- [[jessica-utts]], as president of the American Statistical Association, reviewed the Stargate Program and broader SRI psi data and concluded: *"using the standards applied to any other area of science, it is concluded that psychic functioning has been well established."* **(firsthand: Utts's published conclusion)**
- [[ray-hyman]] — a committed skeptic — co-reviewed the data and conceded that the experiments were *"free of obvious flaws"* and the data was too consistent to dismiss as statistical flukes. **(firsthand: Hyman's published conclusion)**
- [[garry-nolan]] directly quoted: the caudate nucleus and putamen region of the brain lights up when Go players make intuition-based moves that later prove correct; the same region shows higher neural density in anomalously capable individuals; this region is central to the **reward and prediction system** — learning based on anticipated future rewards. **(firsthand: Nolan, this transcript)**
- [[roger-penrose]] hypothesized the brain is a room-temperature quantum system, with [[stuart-hameroff]]'s microtubules as the candidate structure. [[efim-liberman]] (Soviet consciousness researcher, 1970s–80s) independently conjectured Quantum effects in cytoskeletal membranes encode semantic information. **(hearsay: Wargo/Michaels presenting Penrose/Hameroff/Liberman)**
- Quantum biology is now an established field documenting warm-biological quantum effects: **enzyme tunneling**, **cryptochrome electron-spin navigation** in migratory birds, and possibly **quantum entanglement in plant photosynthesis**. **(scientific consensus per transcript)**

## Quantum Biology Evidence Chain

1. **Enzyme tunneling**: quantum tunneling enables enzymes to catalyze reactions that would be thermally impossible classically.
2. **Cryptochrome (Cry4 protein) in robin eyes**: electron spin used to sense Earth's magnetic field — a room-temperature quantum sensor in a warm, wet biological system.
3. **Plant photosynthesis**: may depend on quantum entanglement to route energy with near-100% efficiency.
4. **Microtubules**: [[stuart-hameroff]]'s candidate for quantum processing in neurons — turning them off with anesthesia eliminates consciousness while all other brain functions remain normal.
5. **[[efim-liberman]] goldfish experiment**: freezing goldfish cytoskeleton while spinning them destroyed navigation ability; other fish recovered; implicates cytoskeletal microtubules in spatial cognition.
