# I Surprised Bob Lazar With NASA's Lead Scientist

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]]'s most-anticipated episode, built around [[bob-lazar]] — the only public whistleblower claiming direct hands-on work reverse-engineering a nonhuman craft. [[jesse-michaels]] structures the interview in two phases: a deep technical and biographical interrogation of Lazar co-facilitated by Canadian filmmaker [[luigi-vendetti]] (who spent four years producing *[[s4-the-bob-lazar-story|S4: The Bob Lazar Story]]*), followed by a surprise introduction of [[charles-buhler]], NASA [[kennedy-space-center|Kennedy Space Center]]'s lead electrostatics scientist. The episode also features a brief [[logan-paul|Logan Paul]] cameo presenting UFO footage from ~1995 near [[area-51|Area 51]] for Lazar's reaction.

## Headline Claims

1. **The [[s4-sport-model|sport model]] craft has a structurally impossible 53-ft floor with no central support column.** [[luigi-vendetti|Luigi Vendetti]], who built a hyperrealistic CGI reconstruction, states there is no material on Earth that could achieve this span — making the craft definitively non-human in origin. Lazar confirms: "That's 100% true."
2. **Element 115 was identified at S4 in 1987–88, sixteen years before its synthesis.** Using atomic absorption spectroscopy and X-ray diffraction at S4, Lazar and lab partner [[barry-castillo]] characterized the element. Lazar knew the specific stable isotope at the time but has since forgotten it. Moscovium (Group 15, five valence electrons, same group as bismuth) is the closest public analog.
3. **The [[s4-reactor|S4 reactor]] produces effects that Lazar increasingly believes are a previously unknown fifth or sixth force** — not gravity. A candle flame froze at the focal point of the emitter. A small black ball with no light escape appeared when the emitter was rotated. A mechanical watch stopped at the focal point. The repulsion is elastic and logarithmic, without polarity.
4. **The USGS map of [[papoose-lake|Papoose Lake]] was modified exactly 8 days after Lazar's first anonymous broadcast (May 23, 1989)**, with only the road to S4 removed. The road to the western edge of the lake was left intact — a surgical edit that reveals government awareness.
5. **The [[s4-sport-model|sport model]] was retrieved by the Navy from an underwater archaeological dig** (per [[barry-castillo|Barry Castillo]]; not documented in briefing materials). Lazar connects this to the Navy's institutional control over the program; Congressman [[tim-burchett|Tim Burchett]] independently cites five UAP ocean hotspots with submarine deployment.
6. **Nine structurally distinct craft at S4, all with identical reactors.** This implies a manufacturing origin rather than nine independent crash events. "You don't find nine."
7. **NASA scientist Dr. [[charles-buhler|Charles Buhler]] has achieved measurable thrust in hard vacuum at only 400 V** in 2,000+ experiments since 2016, validating [[thomas-townsend-brown|Thomas Townsend Brown]]'s electrostatic propulsion principles and connecting to the craft's hull electret behavior.

## Lazar Background and Path to S4

Lazar grew up on Long Island with a reputation for tinkering — he built a jet car as a teenager. He worked at [[los-alamos|Los Alamos National Laboratory]] on a particle accelerator project, which is where he encountered [[edward-teller]] and allegedly caught his attention. Lazar's wife at the time was also employed in [[los-alamos|Los Alamos]] circles.

The path to S4 ran through [[egng]] (EG&G). During the initial interview, the first question EG&G interviewers asked Lazar was about his relationship with [[john-lear]], who had already been vocal about UFOs. Lazar said: "He's a crazy friend." EG&G was offering two positions; Lazar was switched to the S4 role. He flew in on Janet Airlines and arrived at the facility with no prior indication of the subject matter.

MIT came up repeatedly in the interview. Lazar insists he was "sent there on a specific kind of program, an isolated program" and did "a lot of auditing" — at both MIT and Caltech. He acknowledges his records are difficult to find but says [[george-knapp|George Knapp]] located people who knew him there.

## S4 Facility Description

On the first day, Lazar was escorted through a corridor of nine hangars accessed via hand scanner. The first craft he saw was the sport model — a 9-meter diameter disc with a 53-foot interior floor span. He was surprised to discover the facility's physical impossibility before even learning what he was working on.

The facility is built into a hillside at [[papoose-lake]], south of [[area-51]]. Nine craft of structurally distinct designs all housed identical reactor units. Lazar interprets the identical reactors as evidence of extraction from the same manufacturing source, not nine separate crashes.

The hangars were accessed from a long corridor. The walls, floor, and ceiling were described as smooth, uniform material. The hangar entry systems were pressure-sensitive hand-contact scanners, not fingerprint. [[barry-castillo|Barry Castillo]] was Lazar's assigned lab partner.

## Project Briefings

Lazar received three formal briefings at S4:

1. **Project Galileo** — the primary directive: duplicate the propulsion system at any cost, and as a secondary objective, develop the ability to disable the system at a distance.
2. **[[project-looking-glass]]** — time. Lazar read only a brief overview; this project used the craft's technology to observe temporal information.
3. **[[project-sidekick]]** — weaponize the craft's emitters as a directed-energy weapon. The same focused gravity field that froze the candle flame was the mechanism; Sidekick was its offensive application.

Before any technical work, Lazar was tested with an allergen injected by a nurse — a routine procedure apparently necessitated by staff reactions to touching the craft's hull material.

## Craft Physical Description

- **Shape/Color**: Saucer disc; Lazar confirmed [[billy-meier|Billy Meier]]'s original sport model photographs are genuine and depict the same craft.
- **Hull material**: Lazar believes the hull is an "electric" — a material that permanently stores an electric field, the electrical analog of a permanent magnet. This is consistent with bismuth titanate, a high-K multiferroic.
- **Seating**: Three small seats inside, positioned near the emitter bases. Lazar interprets them as amplifier positions rather than conventional cockpit seating.
- **Archway**: At least one internal archway becomes fully transparent when [[s4-reactor|the reactor]] is active, possibly serving as navigation display.
- **Symbols**: Korean-like script on certain surfaces; Lazar has no interpretation.
- **Damage**: One craft on the wall of a hangar had a single hole in its brim, bent outward from below — consistent with a kinetic penetrator shot from underneath. This is not depicted accurately in Vendetti's documentary (which shows two holes).

## Reactor Physics

[[s4-reactor|The reactor]] is a golf-ball-sized unit. Lazar describes it as generating a field that repels all matter with a logarithmic force profile: easy to push against at distance, impossible to compress past a few inches. There is no polarity — repulsion is uniform regardless of orientation.

Experiments conducted at S4:
- **Candle flame**: flame froze in mid-flicker at the focal point of the emitter. "Freezing movement, but not affecting anything else." No mechanism in conventional physics stops kinetic energy selectively.
- **Black ball**: emitter produced a small black sphere with no light escaping — a localized region from which no photons emerged. Jesse theorizes this may be a micro-gravity well.
- **Mechanical watch**: a running mechanical watch stopped at the focal point. Not included in the documentary to shorten running time but corroborates the candle result.

[[barry-castillo]] cut a running reactor once during experiments, which killed three people. Lazar treats this as confirming the reactor was operating at an extreme energy density.

Lazar now believes the force is **not gravity** but an undiscovered fifth or sixth force — attractive and repulsive, capable of affecting the flow of time the way gravity does but distinct. "I think it had root access to reality itself."

## Propulsion Configurations

Two flight modes described from briefing documents and test observations:

- **Omicron configuration**: craft operates with an unstable wobble. This is the configuration Lazar observed during Wednesday-night test flights. The orange-corona wobbling disc captured by [[logan-paul|Logan Paul]] (~1995) and in other [[area-51|Area 51]] footage matches this profile precisely.
- **Delta configuration**: craft locks in on a point in spacetime and "jumps" — no transit, no traversal. Lazar describes this as the craft bending spacetime and the destination carrying the vehicle to it.

The three cylindrical emitters at the base generate the directed field. In normal flight they produce the gravity-like attraction vector; in delta mode all three are aligned for spacetime distortion.

Briefing documents included notes from [[barry-castillo]] (possibly derived from earlier program materials) proposing two distinct gravitational forces:

- **Gravity A**: operates at the micro/subatomic scale; corresponds to what mainstream physics labels the strong nuclear force. This is the wave that must be accessed and amplified for spacetime distortion.
- **Gravity B**: operates at cosmological scale; conventional gravity as understood by general relativity.

Jesse introduces the **Extended Heim Theory** of Burkhard Heim — a German physicist who became deaf and blind at 19 from an explosion and later worked for Lockheed Martin in the 1950s as a recognized genius. Heim's model proposes six fundamental forces: standard gravity (graviton), dark-energy gravity (gravitophoton), and a vacuum repulsive force (quintessence), in addition to the standard four. This is structurally similar to the Gravity A/B model Lazar was briefed on.

**Amy Eskridge**, an anti-gravity researcher who died under mysterious circumstances, repeatedly discussed a "sixth force" near the end of her life. Lazar responds: "My group has a mathematical equation to physically describe [it]. We have sixth force on lockdown."

## Bismuth and Element 115 Connection

Jesse presents a detailed technical argument running through all 8 sessions of the interview:

- [[element-115]] (Moscovium) and bismuth share **five valence electrons** and are both in **Group 15** of the periodic table (pnictogens). They have the same bonding geometry and crystal family.
- Bismuth is the most **diamagnetic** stable element on Earth — its electrons move at relativistic speeds, generating a self-opposing magnetic field through spin-orbit coupling that makes the material actively repel external fields.
- Bismuth is a natural **topological insulator dopant**: when introduced into certain crystalline materials it creates topologically protected quantum states — electron configurations locked into the structure of the material itself, immune to disorder.
- Bismuth is simultaneously: high-K dielectric, ferroelectric, topological insulator, multiferroic (electric and magnetic properties linked).
- **Bismuth titanate** and **bismuth ferrite** represent both hull-material candidates (permanent electric field storage) and active material in the emitter array.
- Lazar predicted element 115 in 1989. When synthesized in 2003, it turned out to be a pnictogen — exactly the same chemical family as bismuth. Jesse: "That's either the most chemically literate lucky guess in history, or it isn't a guess at all."
- [[garry-nolan]] at Stanford currently has a **magnesium bismuth** piece from a UAP sample for analysis.
- The **Ubatuba, Brazil** crash material (1950s/60s) exhibited magnesium isotope ratios impossible to produce via known Earth processes.
- [[lewis-whitten|Lewis Whitten]] at RIAS ([[martin-corporation|Martin Corporation]]'s anti-gravity research arm) described in an American Institute of Physics interview a person named "Townzend" who claimed to have an isotope of bismuth that **repelled instead of attracted**.

## Dr. Charles Buhler — The NASA Surprise

Jesse arranges a live video introduction between Lazar and Dr. [[charles-buhler|Charles Buhler]] without advance notice to Lazar. Buhler is:
- Lead electrostatics scientist at NASA [[kennedy-space-center|Kennedy Space Center]]
- Incoming president of the American Electrostatics Society
- Author of two principles now accepted as fundamental to the field
- Lead on 2,000+ experiments replicating and extending [[t-townsend-brown|Townsend Brown]]'s asymmetric capacitor thrust

Key findings from Buhler's experiments:
- Achieves measurable thrust at only **400 V** — Brown used 150,000 V. The lower voltage eliminates corona wind and ionization artifacts entirely.
- All experiments conducted at **hard vacuum** (10⁻⁶ torr or better), eliminating ion wind as a confound.
- Thrust in vacuum goes in the **opposite direction** from ion thrusters — consistent with a true electrostatic force rather than an ionic momentum artifact.
- In some configurations, **trapping charge is sufficient** — the thrust persists after the power source is disconnected.
- Current thrust range: hundreds of micronewtons to ~50 millinewtons (stacked). Area and volume are optimizable; a walk-in vacuum chamber ("Drew's garage") coming online soon.
- Buhler: "It's shocking... 1.5 millinewtons at 400 volts."

Lazar's reaction: "I can't believe you're getting these results... and I can't get past the 400 volts either." He notes that DC voltage on the craft's hull skin — high-voltage DC — matches Townsend Brown's operating parameters. "We're making progress live here."

After the call, Lazar acknowledges: "I wonder if that [Townsend Brown mechanism] really applies to the craft more than I was giving it credit for."

- **The response pattern**: every time Lazar goes public — 1989 radio, [[bob-lazar|Bob Lazar]] documentary, [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]], this episode — escalating health events follow. Five heart attacks after the Rogan episode. A shingles outbreak across his face that nearly blinded him. ## Nine Craft: Structural Diversity and Manufacturing Hypothesis

[[the-nine|The nine]] structurally distinct craft at S4 represent one of the most important and underappreciated details in Lazar's account:

- **No duplication**: all nine craft had different structural designs — different shapes, sizes, apparent age, and surface conditions
  - One was an open-pan style, different from the sport model disc; others varied significantly in profile
  - At least one had what appeared to be ancient hieroglyphic inscriptions, distinct from the Korean-like script on the sport model
- **Identical reactors**: despite the structural diversity, all nine craft housed the same reactor unit — a golf-ball-sized device with the same field characteristics
  - This strongly implies all craft came from the same manufacturing civilization, not nine separate crashes from unrelated origins
  - "You don't find nine" — the phrase Lazar uses to indicate why the identical-reactor finding is so significant
- **Storage condition differences**: some craft showed obvious aging and maintenance, others appeared pristine — suggesting different retrieval dates spanning potentially decades
- **The damage detail**: one craft had a hole in its brim approximately one inch wide, bent outward from the inside-out — consistent with a kinetic penetrator (bullet, flechette) hitting it from below
  - [[luigi-vendetti|Luigi Vendetti]]'s CGI reconstruction shows two holes; Lazar explicitly corrected this: "It was one, just one hole"
  - The outward-bent damage profile is a very specific forensic detail — rules out air-burst, consistent with ground-fire engagement
- **Manufacturing implication**: Lazar believes the program was trying to duplicate not just the propulsion system but the specific stable isotope of element 115 that could not yet be synthesized on Earth — a closed loop: you need element 115 to power the reactor, but you can't make element 115 without the technology element 115 enables
