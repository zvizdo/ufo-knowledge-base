# Exposing the CIA's Darkest Mind Control Secrets

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] episode. Host [[jesse-michaels]] interviews psychiatrist [[colin-ross]], author of *CIA Doctors*, on the documented history of CIA-sponsored psychiatric experimentation, mind-control programs, and—in the second half—Ross's own research into the human energy field and extramission.

## Headline Claims

- **Bluebird/Artichoke predated the Korean War**: The CIA's Director signed [[bluebird]] into operation in April 1952—three months *before* the Korean War started in June. The disinformation cover story ("we were just reacting to what the Chinese were doing") was built after the fact.
- **[[gh-esterbrooks|G.H. Esterbrooks]] created a documented "super spy" with a hypnotic code word**: "The moon is clear" was the verbal trigger that switched a courier between his normal personality and a secret-messenger alter. Published in open literature and in the [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] archive documents.
- **[[frank-olson|Frank Olson]] was murdered**: [[frank-olson]], working on biological warfare at [[fort-detrick|Fort Detrick]] as part of [[mk-naomi]], was dosed with LSD by [[sidney-gottlieb]] at a party, then pushed from a 10th-floor hotel window by [[pierre-lafitte]]. His son [[eric-olson]] eventually proved the window was too small to jump through; the family received a $700,000 White House compensation check.
- **[[jolly-west|Jolly West]] told Congress [[jack-ruby|Jack Ruby]] shot Oswald during an epileptic seizure**: This was the stated explanation for why Ruby did not remember killing Oswald. Per Ross, Ruby "probably was an MK Ultra patient" before the shooting.
- **C.D. Jackson—chief of U.S. psychological warfare in the 1950s and responsible for the missing Zapruder film frames—met with Betty and [[barney-hill|Barney Hill]] within weeks of their 1961 abduction**: A striking connection between the UFO abduction landmark event and the intelligence community's psychological warfare apparatus.
- **[[les-wexner|Les Wexner]] wrote in his diary about being haunted by a dybbuk**: Epstein's primary financial backer, the founder of Victoria's Secret, recorded what he described as being tormented by a demon—taking the Epstein network into supernatural territory.
- **[[colin-ross|Colin Ross]] has experimental evidence for human ocular extramission**: Using a floating electrode in front of (not touching) his eye, he captured a measurable ELF signal indistinguishable from the brain's frontal-lobe EEG. Published in a mainstream electrical engineering journal.
