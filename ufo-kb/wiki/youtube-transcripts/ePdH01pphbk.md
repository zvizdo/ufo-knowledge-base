# The Purchase of America (ft. Michael Pillsbury & Josh Rogin)

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] episode with China policy strategist [[michael-pillsbury]] (Hudson Institute, *The 100-Year Marathon*) and Washington Post journalist [[josh-rogin]] (*Chaos Under Heaven*). Primarily a geopolitics episode, but [[jesse-michaels|Jesse Michaels]] opens and closes it by foregrounding [[david-grusch]]'s framing of NHI retrieval as a live arms race between the US, China, and Russia.

## Headline Claims

- **[Hearsay via Grusch — flagged by Jesse Michaels]** [[david-grusch]] testified that beyond recovered bodies and craft, the core overlooked detail was **an ongoing Cold War to reverse-engineer NHI technology** — the US competing against China and Russia. Strategic deterrent logic: "once you use it, you burn it, and then everybody knows."
- **[Jesse Michaels synthesis]** The CCP's tacit endorsement of *[[the-three-body-problem|The Three-Body Problem]]* — Chinese sci-fi depicting aliens meddling with human science and a predatory "dark forest" of civilizations — may signal CCP awareness of or belief in extraterrestrial intelligence, or at minimum a desire to recruit youth into science for an emergent technological cold war.
- **[Pillsbury firsthand]** Premier Zhou Enlai told Kissinger the US was the "ba" — not "leader" as translated but "tyrant-hegemon to be overthrown." The Chinese strategy has always been hidden double-speak, visible once you know the Warring States proverbs.
- **[Josh Rogin firsthand reporting]** Hunter Biden's 2017 Miami dinner with CEFC/Ye Jianming: $10 million/year for "introductions" plus an $80,000 diamond. Neil Bush acknowledged in divorce deposition that women were sent to his hotel room in China; his response: "yes, I found that quite unusual."
- **[Rogin firsthand]** Elaine Chao (Trump's Transportation Secretary, McConnell's wife) held 21 meetings with Chinese state media in her first year, zero interviews with American media; forced State Department to include her family's shipping-company meetings in official trips to China.
- **[Jesse Michaels synthesis]** At the close: "perhaps if Grusch is right and we're in a cold war to reverse engineer non-human technology, we must look to more transcendent forces — perhaps in the dark forest for a benevolent partner."

## The 100-Year Marathon — CCP Long Game

[[michael-pillsbury]]'s framework: China's grand strategy is derived from the Warring States period. A 1991 leaked CCP document used the proverb *Tao Guang Yang Hui* — mistranslated by Beijing as "build your capabilities," but Pillsbury argues the full context means "bide your time, then overturn the old hegemon and exact revenge." The US enabled China's rise through deliberate policy ("strong and prosperous China is America's strategy") under the assumption — derived from Friedman's *The World Is Flat* — that economic integration would produce political liberalization. It did not. China got glasnost but not perestroika. Xi Jinping's third term consolidation, purge of liberal Politburo members, and Hardline Nationalist pivot show the shift is now overt. Pillsbury on decoupling: "I think decoupling is really a rhetorical device — it's not happening."

## CCP Influence and Elite Capture

- **United Front**: [[josh-rogin]]'s term — "a massive parasite which burrows its way under the skin of the host countries laying eggs that hatch in the future." Targets businesses, foundations, politicians, anyone with influence who has an interest in China business.
- **Wendy Deng Murdoch** lobbied for a $100M Chinese government-funded garden at the National Arboretum including a 70-foot white tower; FBI suspected it as a potential signals intelligence platform. Separate claim (sourced to 2013 blog): recruited to PLA propaganda division in 1986.
- **[[charles-lieber|Charles Lieber]]** — Harvard Department of Chemistry chair, Pioneer in nanotechnology — convicted of 6 felonies for concealing his ties to China's [[thousand-talents-program]]. The [[wuhan-institute-of-technology]] paid him $50,000/month plus $1.5M for a China lab for years before the 2019 pandemic.
- **[[thousand-talents-program|Thousand Talents Program]]**: systematically recruits US researchers; the Lieber case is the highest-profile conviction.
- **TikTok / ByteDance**: CCP uses TikTok to algorithmically divide Americans along race, gender, and identity fractures, while China's domestic version (*Douyin*) is heavily STEM-focused, limits youth under 14 to 40 minutes/day, and bans use after 10 PM. Jesse quotes Saladin: "to destroy a nation without war, make nudity common among the younger generation."
- **Grinder / Beijing Kunlun**: Chinese gaming company bought the gay dating app Grindr for $93M in 2016; security concerns forced a sale in 2020. The app collects HIV status — Jesse speculates this data now sits on Chinese servers as kompromat reserve.
- **Goldman Sachs as model case**: spent decades cultivating China relationships to win prime IPO roles (PetroChina 2000, Bank of China 2006, DiDi 2021). Emblematic of Wall Street's "World Is Flat" bet — integrated China into global markets expecting political liberalization; got neither liberalization nor kept the relationships after CCP retaliation.
- **Confucius Institutes**: academic front organizations controlled by the CCP on US campuses; still operating at over a dozen despite widespread closures after security concerns surfaced.
- **[[wang-huning|Wang Huning]]** — 4th-ranked Politburo Standing Committee member and Xi's chief ideologist — visited the US in 1988, spent a year studying American society, and wrote *America Against America*, documenting US weak points: racial divisions, class ruptures, decadence, consumerism. He has been exploiting these for 30 years.

## UAP / NHI Cold War Context

- Jesse Michaels frames the episode's NHI relevance explicitly: Grusch's "buried detail" about China/Russia competing to reverse-engineer NHI technology is the key geopolitical linkage.
- CCP endorsement of *[[the-three-body-problem]]* (alien invasion sci-fi) to Chinese students: Jesse asks whether this signals CCP belief in extraterrestrial contact. Pillsbury declines to speculate directly.
- China's lunar rover on the dark side of the moon: NASA administrator Bill Nelson said he has "no idea" why they made that decision — Jesse frames this as anomalous and potentially relevant.
- China's spy balloon (January 2023, Billings Montana): Jesse speculates it followed the Aleutian ICBM radar chain — possibly testing radar detection limits — rather than serving a standard satellite surveillance function.
- Jesse's closing framing: America must invest in "frontier science and technology — real breakthroughs in areas like free energy and anti-gravity" to win the NHI cold war.
