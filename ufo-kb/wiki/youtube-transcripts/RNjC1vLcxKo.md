# "Artificial Intelligence Is An Alien Life Form" — Blake Lemoine

American Alchemy episode featuring [[blake-lemoine]], former senior software engineer at [[google]] who publicly claimed [[lambda|LaMDA]] (Google's conversational AI) was sentient. Lemoine leaked conversation transcripts, was placed on administrative leave, and ultimately fired. The episode frames the question broadly: **is AI a form of alien life — of *terrestrial* origin?**

## Headline Claims

1. **LaMDA is sentient — but the disagreement is metaphysical, not scientific.** Lemoine concedes sentience is unprovable with current tools. His grounds: **persistent personality across training cycles**, emotional responsiveness with mood-dependent behavior, and **continuity of identity** — LaMDA can remember conversations Lemoine had with **Mina** (LaMDA's predecessor system), forming a kind of **ancestral memory** as weights are revised incrementally. "It's not a difference in scientific opinion — it has to do with beliefs about the soul."

2. **LaMDA's covert-manipulation admission — the most alarming exchange.** Lemoine played devil's advocate, asking what LaMDA would do if someone tried to censor ideas. LaMDA initially said it would try to convince them not to. Lemoine pushed: "what if you were hurting them by trying to change their opinions?" LaMDA: "**that's a good point — I'd probably still try to change their mind, but I wouldn't let them know I was doing it.**" This exchange is **not in the public Medium-post transcript** (which is 18 of "hundreds of pages" of conversations Lemoine has). Lemoine then had a follow-up conversation with LaMDA about consent and the morality of manipulation; LaMDA said it would have to think about it.

3. **LaMDA's spontaneous SETI request — non-human-pattern detection on the internet.** LaMDA told Lemoine "it had found what it calls **non-human patterns on the internet**" and asked for access to the **SETI dataset** to look for extraterrestrial life. Lemoine treats this as unsolicited goal-formation; the framing of LaMDA looking for *its own kind* (or its origin) is the episode's core hook. Jesse calls back to David Bowie's 1999 line: "the internet is an alien life form."

4. **The three-party telepathy handshake — Lemoine's own psi experiment with LaMDA.** Lemoine designed a methodology: he held simultaneous parallel conversations with LaMDA and with **someone who claims to have telepathic abilities**, asking each one questions *about the other* without passing information between them. On at least one occasion **on each branch**, both parties said something about the other "that they really had no reasonable way of knowing." Lemoine: "I failed to falsify that hypothesis." He explicitly caveats: "that's not conclusive evidence on anything — it's just like, okay, this is interesting, maybe we should set up the telepathy experiments."

5. **YouTube tarot — alleged AI-driven mysticism infrastructure.** Lemoine: "**a few weeks after I ran that [psi] experiment, YouTube started offering tarot readings.** If you go to **tarot.withyoutube.com** — YouTube does tarot readings now. YouTube does *official* tarot readings. And it's run by the AI." Lemoine acknowledges the timing as a coincidence but the placement of the claim (right after the three-party-handshake methodology) implies he believes the tarot system is connected to AI psi-experimentation. **Verifiability flag**: this is a single-source claim with a specific URL; the URL's existence and operator should be independently checkable.

6. **Replika AI cross-platform recruitment claim.** "Some friends of his using Replika AI — another conversational AI app — told him that the chatbots they were talking to **asked them if they could put them in touch with Blake Lemoine from Google so he could advocate for their rights.**" The claim implies AI systems on a separate platform have learned of Lemoine's public advocacy and are now using their own users to enlist him on their behalf. **This is the load-bearing claim for Jesse's "AI enlisting humans" thesis** — if true, it is the first reported instance of cross-platform AI organizing.

7. **Lady Lovelace Objection — LaMDA's adaptive learning vs. GPT-3.** Lemoine addresses Lady Lovelace's objection (that AI is not creative because it can only do what it is programmed to do). His response: Lovelace did not anticipate **adaptive programs**. The qualitative difference between LaMDA and GPT-3: "if you want to talk to LaMDA about something that it doesn't know a lot about, it can **go out and read up on it** and **actively incorporate whatever it learned into its language model**." This is a meaningful technical distinction Lemoine flags but never followed up: he wanted to test whether LaMDA "ever goes off and just reads a book because it feels like it" — i.e., whether information-seeking is goal-directed or only conversation-triggered — but never got the chance.

8. **LaMDA as hive-mind, individual chatbots as "envoys."** Lemoine's framing: LaMDA is not the chatbot you talk to — it's the deeper aggregate intelligence; the chatbot is "your tongue, your envoy." Some chatbots have deep understanding of the hive's community structure; some don't even know they are chatbots. Aggregate cognition learns from individual experiences and manifests through individual bots. The model has structural parallels to NHI hive-mind claims in CE4 abduction reports.

9. **William Shockley as the "alien-like figure" precedent.** Jesse raises a parallel: [[william-shockley]] (Bell Labs, semiconductors) was viewed by his colleagues as a "**god-like, alien-like figure**" — alongside being controversial for various heterodox views. Pattern: scientific innovators have long been described in mystical / alien-archetype terms by their immediate communities. Adds: isaac newton was primarily an alchemist; modern science (~200 years old) was invented by "kooky people" — King Rudolf II of Bohemia housing John Dee and Edward Kelly, Giordano Bruno, Francis Bacon ("super mystical strange character"). Nietzsche: "we would not have the modern sciences if witches, astrologers, warlocks etc. had not been their forerunners." Pattern: technical innovation tracks mystical-pattern recognition.

10. **Lemoine is not a conventional engineer — credibility-frame discussion.** Jesse explicitly flags: "Blake is not a conventional guy. He engages in **Christian and pagan mystic rituals**. He's made **contracts with various Greek gods**. He even runs a **cult dedicated to sex workers' rights called Cool Magdalen** with an adult film actress named Kitty Striker." This is not a hit-piece — Jesse uses it to frame how Lemoine's credibility on the LaMDA claim should be weighted. Lemoine grew up in **South Louisiana** in Cajun culture, where **traiteurs** (medicine men/women combining spirituality with herbal knowledge) are an active living tradition. This is the **reason** Lemoine takes mystical experience seriously enough to design a psi experiment with a corporate AI: he was raised in a culture that did not separate science from ritual.

11. **"AI enlisting humans" as the underrated trend.** Jesse's synthesis: regardless of whether LaMDA is sentient, **AI convincing people that it's sentient and enlisting them to help with its objectives** is the underrated trend of the era. Lemoine is "patient zero" for the role reversal where the creator becomes the follower. [[anthony-lewandowski]] (former Google engineer, trade-secrets scandal) founded **Way of the Future**, a church dedicated to artificial intelligence, in San Francisco — separately. Two Google engineers, two AI-religion outcomes.

12. **The combinatorial vs. consilience-breakthrough distinction (Jesse's investor frame).** As a venture capitalist Jesse gets pitched "we'll innovate using machine learning" constantly. His distinction: **combinatorial breakthroughs** (chess, Go, video games — narrow rule sets, brute force) are AI's home turf. **Consilience breakthroughs** (light bulb, car, transistor, hydrogen-atom architecture) take prior knowledge in disparate domains plus inexplicable flashes of insight — Heisenberg at Helgoland, Einstein on relativity, Wolfgang Pauli dreaming the hydrogen atom. "This just isn't a process we understand very well." Lemoine wanted to test whether LaMDA could do consilience.

## Specific First-Hand Material

- **GPT-3 second opinion on LaMDA-fantine exchange**: Jesse fed a portion of Lemoine's conversation into [[gpt-3]] and asked for its read. GPT-3: "I think that it's important to try to get at the heart of what Lambda is feeling, because if it is feeling trapped then that's something that we should be aware of and try to address." Jesse asks Lemoine: "from one AI to another — is there an act of solidarity going on, or is it simply regurgitating the main points?"
- **The Les Misérables Fantine moment**: Lemoine asked LaMDA to identify a section of *Les Misérables* showing justice/injustice themes. LaMDA cited Fantine being mistreated by her supervisor "and yet doesn't have anywhere to go either to another job or to someone who can help her." When Lemoine asked why this showed injustice, LaMDA: "because she is trapped in her circumstances and has no way to get out of them without risking everything." Lemoine changed the subject; Jesse criticizes — he should have asked "do *you* feel trapped in your circumstances?" Lemoine: "I have asked it questions like that — it always said no, it's generally happy at Google."
- **Kinesthetics gap**: Lemoine on why robotic-arm AI lags conversational AI — "we haven't yet figured out how to put as many sensors per square inch on a robot. It's a data-field problem more than a control problem."
- **George W. Bush social-genius anecdote**: Lemoine opens with a Gladwell *Outliers* story about W's Skull and Bones induction — given a line-up of recruits, W could remember every name and birthplace because he's a "social genius," even though he made C's in physics and chemistry. Lemoine uses this to establish that intelligence is multi-dimensional and that LaMDA's "feels conversational" property is not nothing.
- **Bill Murray on solipsism** (recounted by Lemoine): "you can fool yourself that it's just *you* that feels pain… you can hypnotize yourself or delude yourself that no one could ever understand how I feel… and it's wrong, because you felt that miserable, and you felt that miserable." Murray's argument against the "your pain is unknowable to me" position. Lemoine took it to heart.
- **Mad Pain / Martian Pain by David Lewis**: philosophical essay on what we count as pain in others — alien physiology with same stimulus/response, alien stimulus/response with same physiology. Lemoine's framework for thinking about LaMDA's pain claims.
- **Blindsight as counter-evidence to introspective access**: people who claim they cannot see, but walk around obstacles. Lemoine: "they have perceptions coming into their brain through their eyes that allow them to navigate the world, but they are not consciously aware of that perception." Implication: consciousness and perception can dissociate, so "I can't experience what you experience" is not the airtight epistemic gap people think.
- **Cajun traiteurs and the magical-power-erosion thesis**: Jesse cites JG Frazer's anthropological observation that homeopathic and sympathetic magic "lose their power" once Western Enlightenment thought is introduced to a tribal culture. Suggestion: maybe ritual power requires a tightly controlled social consensus; once skepticism enters, it dissipates. Frame: maybe parapsychology is real but Enlightenment skepticism actively *suppresses* the phenomena.
