# UFOs & Human Experiments: Big Pharma's Horrific Past (ft. Brigham Buhler)

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] episode hosted by [[jesse-michaels]] with guest [[brigham-buhler]], founder of [[ways-to-well]] health clinic in Austin. The episode draws a unified narrative connecting the suppression of alternative medicine to the same institutional dynamics alleged in UAP suppression.

## The Rockefeller Medical Takeover

John D. Rockefeller (Standard Oil / Exxon) sought to monopolize medicine in the same way he controlled oil. Backed by Andrew Carnegie, the [[flexner-report]] (1910) by Abraham Flexner condemned all non-drug-based medical schools. Over half of US medical schools closed; osteopaths, homeopaths, and holistic practitioners were eliminated. The AMA became the enforcement arm.

The same financial networks — [[brown-brothers-harriman|Brown Brothers Harriman]], the Federal Reserve (Payne-Aldrich Act 1913), and early CIA investment firms — are cited as controlling both finance and the new medical establishment.

## Unit 731 and the Immunity Deal

[[unit-731]] was a Japanese Imperial Army biological and chemical warfare unit operating in Manchuria under General [[shiro-ishii]]. Experiments included vivisection, deliberate infection with plague/cholera/syphilis, pressure-chamber tests, and live weapons testing on prisoners. Ishii was absent from the Tokyo War Crimes Tribunal — the US granted him and his team full immunity in exchange for their biological warfare data, which was classified and used to advance American Cold War biowarfare capabilities.

## Bayer, the Third Reich, and Monsanto

[[bayer]] (German pharmaceutical manufacturer) had documented ties to the Third Reich, with correspondence showing Nazi concentration camp prisoners used as test subjects for pharmaceutical compounds. Letters acknowledge the death of all 150 Jewish women shipped for Phase 1 trials and request replacements.

In the 1980s, Bayer knowingly shipped HIV-contaminated hemophilia medications to third-world countries rather than destroy the lots. In 2018, Bayer acquired [[monsanto]] for billions — uniting the company that thrived under the Third Reich with the company that manufactured Agent Orange.

[[monsanto]] supplied Agent Orange (a defoliant) during Operation Ranch Hand in Vietnam. A manufacturing contaminant — dioxin — caused cancer, birth defects, and epigenetic mutations that propagate across generations. Monsanto's flagship herbicide Roundup contains glyphosate, linked to cancer and found throughout soil, water, and human tissue.

## MK-Ultra Continuation: OARD and the Scientific Engineering Institute

After MK-Ultra was allegedly terminated in the 1960s, a successor division — the [[oard]] ([[oard|Office of Research and Development]]), led by Steven Aldrich — continued the work with better funding and technology. OARD moved beyond LSD and hypnosis into:
- Brain electrode implants in animals to control movement remotely
- Psychosurgery and memory erasure exploration
- Reptile-delivered chemical/biological weapons for covert assassination
- Experiments on inmates at Holmesburg State Prison, Philadelphia — a full decade after MK-Ultra's official shutdown

OARD created the **[[scientific-engineering-institute]]** (a CIA proprietary near Boston, with a rural farm lab) as its research vehicle. The Institute was transitioned into the private sector in the late 1970s, rebranded as [[scientific-engineering-institute|SURL Metadata]] — a subsidiary of GD SURL, chaired by [[donald-rumsfeld]]. In 1985, Rumsfeld orchestrated its sale to [[monsanto]].

## Royal Raymond Rife

[[royal-rife]] is presented as the paradigm case of suppressed frequency medicine. In the 1930s, Rife built a high-powered microscope claimed to observe live viruses and a machine that could destroy pathogens at their resonant frequency (Mortal Oscillatory Rate) without harming surrounding tissue. A California clinic reportedly treated cancer patients. The AMA refused validation, his lab was raided, colleagues were pressured, and the equipment was confiscated. Rife died poor and discredited.

The host uses a modern AmpCoil (derivative of the Rife machine). Ross Douthat (NYT op-ed columnist, self-described skeptic of UFO narratives) reportedly uses the Rife machine for Lyme disease. Lyme disease patients claim it is the only thing that helps them.

## DNA as Cosmic Software

### Panspermia and Directed Panspermia

Japanese/NASA scientists reanalyzed fragments from three ancient meteorites and confirmed they contained the fundamental building blocks of DNA and RNA. [[francis-crick]] (co-discoverer of the double helix) proposed **directed panspermia**: DNA is too complex to have emerged by accident in 100 million years on Earth; it must have been seeded purposefully across the galaxy.

### DNA as Antenna

The ~98% of the human genome that does not code for protein ("junk DNA") remains largely mysterious. Researchers have proposed DNA's fractal geometry and coiled structure make it act as an **electromagnetic antenna**. A 2011 study described DNA as possessing the two structural characteristics of fractal antennas.

Russian scientists (1990s) observed **DNA phantom experiments**: after removing a DNA sample from a container, laser light continued to scatter as if the DNA were still there — for hours or days. An energetic residue or "phantom" remained.

Living cells, including DNA, emit **biophotons** — weak light emissions speculated to play a role in cellular communication or even consciousness.

### The Double Helix Symbol

The caduceus (two serpents twisting in a spiral) appears universally: Greek mythology, Hindu temples, Mesoamerican art, Aboriginal myths, Sumerian glyphs, and Amazonian ayahuasca visions. Shamanic visions in the Amazon — reportedly the source of life and knowledge — feature serpents spiraling together "from the sky." The episode suggests this may be ancient encoded knowledge of DNA's cosmic origin.

In 2017, a SARS modification allowing its spike protein to bind to human ACE2 receptors was submitted to DARPA by [[ecohealth-alliance]]. DARPA rejected it as too dangerous. The research moved to the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology]] (which had poor safety marks). The host frames "gain of function research" as a euphemism for biowarfare — a smoke screen, since no gain-of-function experiment has ever produced a cure for the pathogen it created.
