# My Joe Rogan Appearance: What I Learned (ft. Young Jamie & Viktor Hovland)

## Episode Summary

Jesse Michaels frames the episode around his appearance on the Joe Rogan Experience (episode live at air time). Before releasing that episode, he shares a casual golf outing at Barton Pitch and Putt in Austin with [[jamie-vernon]] ("Young Jamie," Joe Rogan's longtime producer and a deep UFO rabbit-holer) and Norwegian golfer [[viktor-hovland]] (top-10 PGA Tour, UAP enthusiast who DM'd Jesse after discovering American Alchemy). The conversation covers UAPs, exotic propulsion, ancient mysteries, the DOE as a secret-science hub, [[sky-watchers]], and the [[thomas-townsend-brown]] / *Back to the Future* synchronicity.

## Headline Claims

- **GR + QM incompatibility = exotic propulsion key**: Jesse argues that reconciling Einstein's general relativity with quantum field theory would unlock exotic propulsion — the very physics underlying UAPs. [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s EM-gravity manipulation is the empirical starting point. → [[gravity-electromagnetism-unification]]
- **DOE as locus of secret science**: Under the Atomic Energy Act of 1954, anything emitting alpha/beta/gamma radiation is "born secret" (restricted data upon discovery), giving the Department of Energy an umbrella under which exotic physics research is quietly contracted to Lockheed, Northrop, etc. [[battelle-memorial-institute]] manages all US nuclear sites and sits adjacent to [[wright-patterson]]. → [[doe-secret-science-nexus]]
- **Back to the Future / Townsend Brown synchronicity**: "Doc Brown" = Thomas Townsend Brown (EMTT = Emmett, Towns, and Brown); Pasadena setting = where Brown did his experiments; "flux capacitor" = Brown's capacitor device; the film frames a time machine as a mistaken UAP ("no seams, no rivets — must be alien"). Tom DeLonge says the Hollywood encoding is deliberate; Jesse leans toward wild synchronicity. → [[back-to-the-future-townsend-brown]]
- **[[harold-malmgren]] Wikipedia edit**: Immediately after Jesse's Malmgren interview, Wikipedia demoted Malmgren from "top presidential aide (JFK/LBJ/Nixon/Ford)" to "American writer and lobbyist" and added damaging claims (Japanese whaling lobby). Jesse and Jamie treat this as deliberate discrediting, consistent with the pattern of suppressing credible UAP witnesses.
- **[[jake-barber]]'s "The Egg"**: Sky Watchers operates a mobile sensor unit; their "dog whistle" frequency reportedly attracts UAPs 100% of the time with sensors detecting anomalous returns. Jake Barber describes an egg-shaped UAP (the 9th class of UAP) that he retrieved in the mid-2000s — which Sky Watchers is now attempting to summon publicly. The government may not prioritize the egg because they have too many. → [[uap-summoning-protocols]]
- **[[haim-eshed]] galactic-federation claim**: Former head of Israel's space program publicly stated there is a galactic federation and that humans are working with aliens on Mars. Jesse connects this to Eshed's intelligence background — the implication being he knows it from classified briefings.
- **[[richard-bissell]] corroborated 1933 Magenta to Malmgren**: Already in [[harold-malmgren]] page, but reinforced here as cross-corroboration when Viktor notes Bissell's mention of 1933 predates Grusch's 2023 disclosure by decades.
- **Spielberg insider knowledge**: *Close Encounters of the Third Kind* features "two crates" that Jesse identifies as the two most advanced aerospace R&D programs at Lockheed and Northrop respectively. Jesse questions how Spielberg knew about this in the late 1980s — suggesting he was "fed some stuff."
