# "There's A UFO On The Seafloor!" — Treasure Hunter Dennis Asberg

[[dennis-asberg]], co-founder of [[ocean-x]], describes the discovery and ongoing investigation of the [[baltic-sea-anomaly]] — a ~60-meter-diameter anomalous object sitting 90 m below the Baltic Sea. Host [[jesse-michaels]] interviews Dennis aboard a vessel directly above the anomaly site.

## Headline Claims

- **The BSA is separated from the seafloor**: sub-bottom profiling data from the latest expedition shows the object is not a geological formation attached to the seabed — it appears to sit on or above the bottom layer. (Dennis firsthand)
- **Consistent EM interference**: every expedition to the site — including Stockholm University's own vessel with independent GPS equipment — experiences GPS loss and ROV/drone failures when close to the object. The interference is reproducible and scales with proximity. (Dennis firsthand; Stockholm University captain corroboration)
- **90° corridors and flat walls**: the object has right-angle corners, flat walls, and a 2-meter-diameter hole that appears to "breathe" (sand/material flows in and out rhythmically). The surface is dark grey-black and too hard to sample. (Dennis + [[stefan-hogborn|Stefan Hogborn]] firsthand)
- **Organic burned material on the object**: loose samples taken from the object's surface tested positive for biological material, described as organic charred residue at high-temperature basalt — anomalous because no biological material should exist in that dead-sea zone of the Baltic. (Dennis firsthand; tested in Israel)
- **Finnish colleague's ROV assessment**: independent Finnish divers brought their own ROV and ROV operator immediately assessed the object as "man-made / constructed," and detected something resembling "a power line" or "high voltage" energy output. (Firsthand corroboration)
- **NATO-level surveillance**: 6–7 ships from NATO member nations (French, German, American, British, plus a Swedish corvette) have been filmed by [[ocean-x|Ocean X]] encircling the site during dives. Swedish military headquarters denied involvement when confronted but could not explain the footage. (Dennis firsthand)
- **Matthew Brown's "[[immaculate-constellation|Immaculate Constellation]]"**: [[jesse-michaels]] references [[matthew-brown-whistleblower]] as having encountered a government document ([[immaculate-constellation]]) describing trans-medium objects — objects designed to operate in water — as "made to purpose" (built for specific functional use cases). Potentially relevant to BSA morphology.

## Dennis Asberg — Background and Discovery

- Career treasure hunter and salvage diver before BSA; Ocean X had previously located a 1904 Russian Tsar-era submarine (found 2015) and 1600s-era wine and cognac from a shipwreck (no buyers found for the cognac).
- The 2011 discovery was accidental: Ocean X was searching for a lost cognac wreck in a different area when the sidescan sonar sweep showed the BSA profile; [[peter-lindberg|Peter Lindberg]] joked, "Dennis, UFO." Both took it seriously immediately.
- Discovery happened at 1–2 AM aboard the ship; crew immediately understood this was not a known geological feature.
- Dennis took the sidescan image to the newspaper Expressen; worldwide media followed; no expert he contacted could identify it.
- After 15 years of failed scientific outreach, scientist [[beatrice-varel|Beatrice Varel]] opened doors; scientific collaborators now say they have "never seen anything like this."
- Dennis remains independent of government and military funding by choice; current expedition uses a Swedish government-owned ship but without NATO surveillance — he attributes this to using a different vessel class.
- Dennis has received threatening emails naming the specific kindergartens his young children attended; he kept the emails; also received messages claiming he was "opening a gateway to hell"; his wife and family struggled to accept the public exposure.

## BSA Morphology — Extended

- Circumference approximately 180 m (diameter ~60 m); object sits at ~90 m depth in the Finnish economic zone of the Baltic Sea.
- Stefan Hogborn (expert diver, ~6,000 lifetime dives) brushed sediment off the surface, revealing a dark grey-black color; described the material as too hard to take samples from directly.
- Corridors on the object have flat walls meeting at 90° angles — right-angle geometry that Dennis describes as unlike any natural formation he has encountered in salvage work.
- A 2-meter-diameter hole is present; material (sand/sediment) flows rhythmically in and out — Dennis calls this "breathing"; the hole appears framed, not broken.
- A small triangle feature (~15–20 cm) with a perfectly round hole was photographed; Dennis describes the round hole as looking "laser-cut."
- Temperature near the object is close to zero °C; ambient Baltic temperature at that depth is normally 4–5 °C — a localized thermal anomaly.
- Sub-bottom profiling data from the 2025 expedition shows visible layering between the object and the natural seabed sediment, consistent with the object sitting above rather than embedded in the seafloor.
- Basalt material was found associated with the site — anomalous because there are no volcanoes in the Baltic basin; loose stones tested in Israel; organic burned biological material (described as charred residue at high temperature) also found on the surface.

## Electromagnetic Interference — Full Detail

- GPS loss is reproducible every expedition; confirmed independently by the captain of a Stockholm University research vessel (identified as captain Thomas) who had no prior connection to Ocean X.
- Cameras and lighting equipment stop functioning when the ROV approaches the object; restart when pulled back.
- Radar shows phantom objects approximately 150 m away from the object during proximity dives — false returns not explained by sea conditions.
- Smoke and fumes have been filmed rising from the water at the site.
- Thunder and lightning conducted to the object during one expedition was filmed; crew interpreted this as unusual current behavior near the structure.
- Finnish ROV operator assessed in real time: "looks like a power line — something putting out high voltage" (on tape, quoted by Dennis).
- Interference scales with proximity — the closer the equipment, the more severe the failure — consistent with an active field source rather than passive interference.

## NATO Surveillance and Institutional Response

- Six to seven ships from NATO member nations (French, German, American, British) plus a Swedish corvette have been filmed by Ocean X encircling the BSA site during dives; Dennis says this is documented on video.
- Dennis brought the footage to Swedish military headquarters; officials denied involvement and claimed no knowledge of the ships; Dennis confronted them directly with the footage — they could not explain it.
- Swedish SÄPO (security police) contacted Dennis in connection with a Russian submarine found in 2015 — separate salvage find by Ocean X.
- Current 2025 expedition is using a Swedish government-owned research ship; no NATO surveillance has appeared this time, which Dennis attributes to the vessel type being less conspicuous.
- Scientific community response shifted after Beatrice Varel's involvement — previously, Dennis's outreach to researchers for 15 years produced no engagement; now scientists are collaborating and expressing that they "have never seen anything like this."

## Dennis's 1996 Sighting — Extended

- Dennis woke at approximately 3:30 AM for no understood reason — unusual behavior; he was young and normally slept as long as possible.
- He lay in bed for about 10 minutes before getting up for water; then saw the object through the window.
- Object was cigar/hotdog-shaped; located approximately 1–1.5 km away over a Swedish military installation that housed plutonium.
- As he watched, the object rotated to reveal a round profile; the sighting lasted approximately 14–15 seconds before it disappeared.
- Dennis contacted [[ufo-sweden|UFO Sweden]] — received no reply.
- He told no one publicly for years; the first public disclosure was on a ship cruise, when a young man asked him about UFOs and Dennis decided to speak.
- Dennis retrospectively believes he was "meant" to see the object — that being woken at that moment was deliberate; Jesse connects this to [[carl-jung|Carl Jung]]'s synchronicity concept and the idea of closed timelike curves (two points connected in time that couldn't exist without each other).
