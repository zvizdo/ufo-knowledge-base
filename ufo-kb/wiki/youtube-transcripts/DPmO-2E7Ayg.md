# Breaking UFO Story: 'Immaculate Constellation' UFO Program (Ft. Michael Shellenberger)

Filmed at [[rayburn-house-office-building]] immediately before/after the Congressional UAP oversight hearing (November 2024). [[michael-shellenberger]] had just published the [[immaculate-constellation]] report — a whistleblower document describing a large US government UAP strategic intelligence program — and was appearing before the [[2024-congressional-uap-hearing]]. Host [[jesse-michaels]] conducted the interview on-site.

## Headline Claims

- **Immaculate Constellation** is a USAP (unacknowledged special access program) — initially described to Shellenberger as a DoD program, then later clarified by a second source as "managed by the DoD but controlled by the White House." Neither characterization could be multiply sourced before publication.
- The program functions as a **strategic intelligence project** combining SIGINT, HUMINT, still images, video, and human-report databases to classify and evaluate UAPs; the report includes a section on different UAP types with documented psychological and biological effects on witnesses.
- [[lue-elizondo]] responded to the report with "I can neither confirm nor deny" — Shellenberger notes this is conspicuous phrasing, since a false claim should earn a flat denial.
- [[john-greenwald]] of the [[black-vault]] has established through FOIA that **UAP secrecy is growing, not shrinking** — directly contradicting the mainstream "gradual disclosure" narrative.
- Shellenberger reported a single-source claim of a **high-definition helicopter video** of an orb emerging from the ocean ~20 miles off the coast of Kuwait and joining a second orb — see [[2024-kuwait-orb-sighting]].
- [[garrett-graff]] — co-author of a Hunter Biden laptop pre-bunking operation at the Aspen Institute — later wrote a UAP book framed as a debunk; Shellenberger flags the overlap as suspicious, terming Graff "a suspicious character."
- [[john-greenwald]] documented that the Pentagon invoked a **law enforcement** (rather than national security) justification for UAP secrecy in at least one FOIA denial — an anomalous legal maneuver that implies a classified law enforcement investigation.
