## Summary

[[steve-coburn|Steve Coburn]] is a scientist with a background in analytical chemistry and materials science who worked alongside [[roger-leir]] — the "alien implant doctor" — attending three of Leir's removal surgeries and independently analyzing recovered objects. Coburn is himself a class 2 experiencer: he has a documented alien-implanted device removed from his toe, two implants still present in his skull, and memories of abduction recovered under regressive hypnosis.

The episode is one of the densest single-source deposits in this KB: it covers implant structure and isotopic analysis, NHI propulsion physics, the class 1/2 experiencer typology, the gray alliance, scalar waves, Apollo 13, the moon, and Bigelow's alien encounter.

## Key Segments

### The Roger Leir Origin Story

[[roger-leir]] was a podiatrist in Thousand Oaks, CA with a longstanding UFO interest. He attended a conference and met [[derrel-sims]] — the original implant researcher. Initially dismissive, Leir was persuaded by a mutual friend to re-examine the case. He invited patients, X-rayed them, and performed his first removals. Over ~20 years he removed objects from 17 different patients.

Coburn was working a few miles from Leir in Camarillo, CA (Camo). He showed up at Leir's office claiming a possible implant in his toe after waking one morning with severe stinging pain. Leir prescribed an X-ray; what showed on film looked like "a bent piece of wire." [[jaime-maussan]] funded the removal. Coburn then analyzed the object using lab equipment at his workplace.

### Coburn's Implant Analysis

The toe implant structure (layers from outside in):
1. **Gray membrane** — hard to cut, no inflammatory response despite being a foreign body
2. **Bone-like layer** — similar to bone or mother of pearl (biological hard part)
3. **Metallic core** — meteoric iron with carbon nanotubes inside the metal
4. **Nerve cell connections** — nerve cells growing into/onto the device

**Isotopic anomalies**: Elements including boron and copper showed isotopic ratios skewed by up to **30%** relative to terrestrial norms — far beyond the 1% variability expected for Earth-origin material. The heavier isotopes were over-represented, consistent with formation in a high-neutron environment (e.g., near the galactic core where supernovae are more frequent) or artificial isotope separation (estimated cost: millions of dollars).

Coburn concluded the material likely originated from outside our solar system, possibly from another part of the galaxy.

He attempted to publish these findings with Leir in the **Journal of Scientific Exploration**. The journal initially expressed enthusiasm, then reversed — Coburn believes under outside pressure. They were not even claiming alien origin; they stated they had found "an unknown object recovered from someone's leg."

**Protocol for detecting implants in living subjects** (Leir/Coburn):
1. Stud finder / small metal detector: scan for conductive objects in the skin
2. G-meter (sensitive magnetometer): scan areas that had stud-finder hits
3. Both instruments registering at the same site = almost certainly an implant (implants almost always have magnetic fields)

Coburn has conducted approximately **400 scans** on experiencers; roughly **half produced hits** (pre-selected audience).

### Class 1 and Class 2 Experiencers

Coburn introduces a typology:

- **Class 1**: Aliens take biological samples only. ~3–5% of American population (~10–15 million people). Do not generally receive implants.
- **Class 2**: "Next level" of alien program involvement — taken aboard craft, given implants, assigned a handler. ~1 in 1,000 Americans (~**350,000 people**). Standard procedure: aliens implant a **brain implant** enabling them to access sensory information (sight, hearing, thought) in real time. This connects the experiencer to the [[gray-hive-mind]].

Class 2 experiencers typically remember seeing UFOs, being taken aboard craft, and often have an assigned **handler** — an NHI contact responsible for their "case" on board. Coburn believes his handler is a taller gray (~4.5 ft, scientist-engineer type) and that the handler is monitoring conversations in real time through the brain implant.

### Coburn's Personal Abduction

First experience: age 5 in Oxnard, CA. Woke before dawn, saw a bright yellow disc-shaped UFO over his parents' house, blacked out, woke at 8 AM with blood down the front of him and a vague memory of something being put up his nose — probable placement of a brain implant.

Toe abduction (adult, Fillmore, CA, ~3 AM): Two small gray beings entered his room, woke him, asked him to come outside. A UFO hovered over his avocado tree at low altitude. He was taken up via a **tractor beam** (blue-green gravity beam; "Star Trek got it right"). The craft was ~50 ft in diameter, similar to Lazar's [[s4-sport-model|sport model]]. Center of craft: airlock with human and alien space suits; four doors to four quadrants; habitation ring; thought-controlled pilot station with hieroglyphic-style labeling.

A taller gray laid him on a couch that slid from the wall and inserted the device with what looked like a black plastic handle with stainless steel tubing. Fiber optics in the tubing activated the device with UV light (which also accounts for the red marks/mini-sunburns seen on experiencers).

Coburn was aboard approximately one hour. Communication was purely **telepathic**.

### NHI Propulsion (Communicated Directly)

The beings described three propulsion methods:

1. **Homopolar generator**: A metallic disc rotating in a perpendicular magnetic field creates voltage between center and outer edge. Aliens use a version where molten metal (iron-silicon mixture — silicon lowers melting point) circulates around the craft perimeter. Combined with a strong magnetic field between top and bottom of the craft. This is also why electrical systems fail near craft: the extreme magnetic field.

2. **Biefeld-Brown effect**: Rapidly charging a capacitor produces thrust toward the positive pole. The entire craft functions as a capacitor, top and bottom plates. Consistent with [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s work. Corroborated by [[jacque-corneau]]'s 1956 vacuum test at Montgolfier facility in Paris (positive result).

3. **Element 115** (per [[bob-lazar]]): Used to amplify gravitational effect (not directly told to Coburn but consistent with his account).

For faster-than-light travel: anti-gravity drive **opens a wormhole**; craft transits a few million kilometers before wormhole collapses. Same mechanism used for time travel — opening the wormhole at a different time. Risks: wormholes are unstable; imprecise temporal arrival; risk of crossing into a branching timeline and being stranded.

### Quantum Mechanics / Time Travel Physics

Aliens communicated a nuanced many-worlds model: most quantum branching events "collapse on themselves" and don't generate separate timelines; only some persist and become real parallel realities. Time travel is possible but avoided for long jumps due to risk of ending up in the wrong timeline. [[travis-walton|Travis Walton]]'s case: [[roger-leir]] theorized Walton was killed by the electric field around the craft and was resurrected by the aliens.

### The Gray Alliance

Seven distinct species of similar NHI bound by **treaty**, all originating within ~100 light years of Earth. Symbol of the alliance: **three orange circles arranged in an equilateral triangle**. Individual species wear uniforms that include either a snake over a triangle, or the three-circles symbol.

Aliens told Coburn: Earth is one of only **4–5 planets in the galaxy** teeming with life (life-bearing planets are common, but dense biospheres are rare). They want to preserve Earth's biosphere and are concerned humanity will destroy it. Their primary activities: mining Earth and Moon; collecting biological samples; monitoring human developments.

They do not care about individual suffering — they are a collective, everything for the group. But they want humanity to eventually join the "Galactic Federation."

### Genetic Program / Bloodlines

NHI abduct pregnant women from specific bloodlines and **genetically modify the fetus**. Bloodlines of particular interest (more abundant of some target genetic combination): Germanic, Celtic, Native American peoples. Implanting is done selectively — only "bloodlines they're interested in." Medical monitoring devices track blood sugar, body temperature, etc.

### The Moon

- Grays "own the moon"
- [[neil-armstrong|Neil Armstrong]] was overheard at a Washington DC party saying something to the effect: "The grays said don't come back until we give you permission"
- NASA whistleblowers reported that a second craft landed on the moon near Apollo 11 shortly after and observed the astronauts
- [[clyde-tombaugh|Clyde Tombaugh]] recruited to investigate geocynchronous objects in orbit (~500 miles up); Coburn believes these are NHI mother ships that launch smaller craft for abductions

### Apollo 13 Nuclear Warhead Hypothesis

Coburn's hypothesis (speculative, acknowledged as such): The Apollo seismic program was escalating — Apollo 12's crash of the ascent stage made the moon "ring like a bell for hours"; Apollo 13's arrival would have added a third seismograph to enable 3D mapping of the moon's interior. The "next logical step" would have been a remote-detonated nuclear device. Coburn suspects Apollo 13 may have carried a nuclear warhead and that the mission failure was not mechanical but an alien intervention. Evidence: the Atomic Energy Commission was "going ape" demanding the returning lunar module be crashed into a deep ocean trench, with a level of urgency far beyond what an RTG would warrant. Compare: **Project A119** (documented Air Force plan to nuke the moon as a Cold War demonstration, c. late 1950s).

### Scalar Waves

Coburn defines scalar waves (per [[tom-bearden]]'s framework and his own synthesis):
- E and B fields are **90° out of phase** (in contrast to conventional transverse EM waves where E and B are in phase)
- Magnetic field curves concentrically; electric field radiates as if from a point charge
- "Like a sound wave in the ether"
- Travel **faster than light** (not constrained to light speed)
- Interact with **atomic nuclei** rather than electrons (unlike transverse EM waves)
- Can affect radioactive **decay rates** — governments have known this for decades
- Government keeps this knowledge classified

Generating scalar waves: Coburn believes standard radio equipment with a **dome-shaped capacitive antenna** (plastic dome with metal on both sides, oscillator connected to each metal face) would work. Inspired by [[t-townsend-brown|Townsend Brown]]'s asymmetric capacitor work and the principle that large electric field differentials harness quantum vacuum fluctuations.

### Robert Bigelow

[[roger-leir]] told Coburn that [[robert-bigelow]] founded his aerospace company because aliens encountered him in the desert and told him to **"meet them in space."** The [[bigelow-aerospace|Bigelow Aerospace]] website apparently displayed a small alien head as a kind of announcement. Bigelow was going to hire Coburn at one point but it fell through — Coburn believes government pressure was involved.

### San Augustine Crash Sphere

Coburn analyzed a sphere (~hand-sized) allegedly from the [[1945-san-antonio-trinity-crash]] (San Augustine, NM) that Maussan purchased from a Mexican farmer near Brownsville, TX. The sphere had vaporized holes at north and south poles (massive heat damage on edges), consistent with collapse of a strong magnetic field upon crash. Material: titanium alloy with **carbon nanotubes built into the metal** and small (~0.5 mm) voids to reduce density (same density as aluminum, but titanium-strength). Isotopic ratios were not remarkably anomalous on this piece — Coburn suspects NHI may have manufacturing facilities on Earth.

### Scalar Ether Physics

Extended discussion of the ether hypothesis: Coburn argues the Michelson-Morley experiment did not disprove the ether — the apparatus may itself have dragged the ether. Robert Millikan (Caltech; mentor to Townsend Brown) reportedly detected Earth's motion through the ether via an experiment on Mt. Wilson. Einstein in later lectures acknowledged that an ether compatible with relativity was possible, provided it was multi-dimensional with unusual properties.

Coburn's gravity model: gravity is a **pushing** force, not pulling — space/ether accelerates toward a gravitating body (zero-point energy converted to real energy in planetary/stellar cores creates a partial vacuum pulling the ether inward).
