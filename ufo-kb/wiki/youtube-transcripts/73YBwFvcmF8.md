# Meet The Nuclear Base Employee Abducted By Aliens (Ft. Mario Woods)

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] full interview. [[jesse-michaels]] sits down with [[mario-woods]], age 69 at time of recording, a former USAF Security Police officer who held Q clearance and guarded Minuteman II ICBMs at [[ellsworth-air-force-base]], South Dakota. In November 1977, at age 23, he experienced an incident that resulted in ~5 hours of missing time, physical burns, unexplained marks, and a catatonic partner.

## Headline Claims

- A massive sphere UAP — "the size of a Walmart building" — was hovering 10 ft above the blast door of [[november-5-silo]] when Woods and partner Michael Johnson arrived to investigate a site alarm.
- After pulling up 60 ft from the object, Woods experienced a blinding light, loss of breath, a black probe ball circling the truck, and a telepathic message: **"Do not fear"** (repeated, wave-like, through his body).
- He witnessed four beings — three [[small-grays|small Grays]] and one taller Gray with a chest device — gliding alongside the truck before losing consciousness.
- ~5 hours of missing time elapsed; the truck was found ~8.5 miles away at [[new-lake-reservoir-dam]], in mud despite sub-freezing temperatures, parked perfectly at the only exit point of a narrow road.
- Partner Michael Johnson remained catatonic (hands locked on steering wheel, eyes open, non-responsive) from the moment they approached the site until after rescue by Sgt. Garza.
- The OSI debrief included [[rick-doty]] — who would later conduct the Benowitz psyop — even though Doty was a brand-new OSI agent at the time.
- Post-incident flight surgeon examination included unexplained inspection of Woods's teeth; [[jesse-michaels|Jesse Michaels]] connects this to [[andrija-puharich]]'s bone-induction / tooth-implant research.
- Hypnotic regressions by Bob Upton and Yavon Smith revealed: inside a large sphere craft, beehive-like multi-level structure, [[tall-gray|tall Gray]] in charge, force-field gurney, a "museum room" of antique Earth objects (Singer sewing machine, old lamps, tube radio, steam iron), and a round window Mario was too afraid to look through.
- [[robert-hastings]] placed Woods in the context of 167 Q-cleared nuclear-site witnesses, noting ~7–8% had CE3 encounters with beings — and that number is almost certainly an undercount given nuclear-site cultures of secrecy.

## Pre-Incident Light-Signaling Sequence

- At approximately 9:15 PM (before the alarm), Mario was outside smoking and noticed a light to the East at roughly 30° elevation — stationary, the size of his outstretched palm at arm's length, larger than his fist; he initially assumed B-52 landing lights.
- Mario returned inside to the flight security controller office and asked Bill Hollman (phone with his wife) if he could flash the facility lights at the object; Hollman waved assent without looking up.
- Mario flashed the lights three times (off-on-off-on-off-on); approximately two minutes later the object responded with the same three-pulse pattern; he repeated the signaling four times over roughly 25 minutes; each time the object replied.
- Michael Johnson came outside to observe on Mario's invitation; he acknowledged seeing the object but was not excited by it — he said "whatever, man" and went back inside.
- On the final sequence the object briefly moved — shifting from a 3-o'clock to a 1-o'clock position in Mario's frame of reference — before going dark; Mario went to bed; the alarm sounded at 12:25 AM.
- The object that responded to light-signaling at 9:15 PM and the sphere on top of November 5 at 1 AM are almost certainly the same craft; the pre-positioning suggests deliberate observation of the site before the alarm was triggered.

## Post-Incident Debrief and Separation

- After return to November 1 control, Woods was interrogated by Master Sergeant Joel Gray, Tech Sergeant Hawkins, and two OSI officers led by Captain Jack Reed; he was questioned "up one side and down the other" for approximately two hours.
- A drug-sniffing dog team arrived from the base (55 miles away) to sweep all belongings — their arrival coincided with Mario's out-of-body experience in the restroom stall, including lime-green visual field and a "clear channel" through his feet.
- Mario omitted only the beings sighting from his written report; he disclosed everything else — the sphere, the light, the black probe ball, the smell, the catatonic partner, the missing time; he withheld the beings description to preserve his PRP clearance and career.
- The written report was classified; Mario was not allowed to retain a copy; an NDA-type agreement was signed — the incident was declared classified; Mario was told not to speak to media or even his wife.
- His wife noticed he returned early and had Band-Aids on his face and hand; he could not explain.
- Mario was permanently reassigned from November 1 to Kilo control, under direct supervision of flight chief and assistant flight chief, and the OIC; he was effectively monitored for nine months until his Korea assignment.
- He was never allowed back to November control; Michael Johnson was taken separately by Tech Sergeant Hawkins and was not seen by Mario again for approximately 18 days.
- Captain Jack Reed handed Colonel Spraker a second document (the classification/NDA form) while Mario was still in the room; Rick Doty's stated role was to collect the medical records of both Woods and Johnson from the flight surgeon's office.

## Michael Johnson's Return Visit — Matched Drawings

- Approximately 18 days after the incident, Michael Johnson knocked on Mario's apartment door in Hanes Way, Rapid City; Mario says he knew instinctively it was Johnson before opening the door.
- In the apartment, Mario sat at the coffee table and Johnson at the kitchen table; both independently drew what they had witnessed — the sphere, the black probe ball, the beings.
- Johnson confirmed he had experienced the same sensory events as Mario: the blinding light, the smell, the "do not fear" transmission, the beings at the truck window — but he was physically unable to respond to any of it; he was aware throughout but locked in a catatonic state.
- Their drawings were "exactly the same."
- Johnson stayed about an hour and a half; they parted on good terms saying they would meet again; that was the last time Mario ever saw him.
- No one has located Michael Johnson since — not Mario, not the Air Force, not AARO; he has not been found in hospitals, military records, or government databases.

- Mario followed a small Gray (in a spandex-like suit) through a short corridor into a fogged room; this is where his memory ends for the abduction sequence.

## 1961 Port Arthur Childhood Sighting

- Mario (age 5–6) was in the front passenger seat of his mother's car in a school drop-off line at De Queen Elementary, Port Arthur, Texas; his sister was in the back seat.
- His mother braked suddenly and said: "Look, Anthony — look at those flying saucers."
- Three silver/white saucer-shaped craft with dome tops and dome bottoms hovered 25–50 ft above a church directly adjacent to the school; lights around the outer rim blinked in synchronized patterns.
- Every child on the adjacent playground was pointing; teachers and students at every floor of the school building (kindergarten through grade 12) were leaning out of open windows pointing.
- Mario's mother opened her car door and stood outside with Mario and his sister; when his sister said she was scared, the mother replied: "It'll be okay, honey — they're not here to hurt you."
- The three craft departed simultaneously, straight up, accelerating from full size to a point to invisible in an instant; no one on the playground spoke of it again for the rest of the day.
- Mario's mother later revealed she had seen similar things growing up in Rocky Mount, North Carolina.

## Personal Background and Family Legacy of Sightings

- Mario's father was a Honduran merchant seaman who sailed with Texaco; he recounted numerous crew sightings of USOs exiting the ocean and ascending into the atmosphere; his entire crew (29–30 men) witnessed one such event.
- Father died in a car accident in 1973 when Mario was 17; this closed off Mario's plan to attend a maritime institute and become a captain.
- Mario was colorblind and failed the wiring-color test required to work on rocket engines; a recruiting sergeant redirected him to Security Police after he saw a training video showing troops rappelling from helicopters in berets.
- Before the 1977 incident he had no interest in pyramids; afterward he felt the interest was "installed" or "downloaded" — a post-abduction cognitive redirection he could not explain and still cannot.
- Mario saw *Close Encounters of the Third Kind* in Rapid City shortly after the incident; during the scene where Roy Neary is under craft light at a railroad crossing, Mario walked out and sat on the curb — the scene too closely mirrored what he had experienced.
- The phenomenon of sightings running in families is noted explicitly: mother, father, and now Mario; post-documentary, many contactees have told him the same.
- Mario served as security inspector at major DOE nuclear facilities post-Air Force: Los Alamos, Sandia National Laboratory, [[oak-ridge-national-laboratory|Oak Ridge National Laboratory]], Y-12 facility, Oak Ridge Gaseous Diffusion facility, and General Electric Neutron Devices (Largo, Florida, now decommissioned) — where triggering mechanisms for nuclear weapons were manufactured; his clearance and exposure to nuclear facilities spanned decades.
- Mario grew up in South Florida and Long Beach; regularly attended [[cape-canaveral|Cape Canaveral]] rocket launches as a youth; aspired to become a naval captain like his father before colorblindness redirected him into Air Force security.
- Mario wrote a book about his experiences and participated in a documentary that included a return visit to the November 5 site in 2021; he felt strongly affected upon approaching the site on foot — nearly went to his knees; the fencing and rock base of the former silo are still present, used by ranchers for hay storage.

## 2017 December Anomalous Household Event

- December 17, 2017, approximately 4:11 AM: Mario awoke standing at the foot of his bed, gripping the bed frame, with no memory of rising — he has no history of sleepwalking.
- A circular hole approximately 4.5–5 ft in diameter appeared in the north wall of his bedroom; two dressers partially intersected the circle; the hole vanished almost immediately with sparks flying from both sides.
- All lights in the house began flickering at candle-bright levels; every low-voltage appliance alarm (stove, microwave, refrigerator, doorbell system) triggered simultaneously.
- Mario's son Jake heard the alarms, came in, and they both witnessed the effects; Mario cleared the house with a pistol.
- The next morning, Georgia Power crews arrived without being called — his house alone had "lit up their board downtown like the Fourth of July." He had lost a leg of power; his house was the only one affected on the street.
- A neighbor told Mario he had seen the backyard lit up "like a Hollywood set" with lights hovering in the air at 4 AM that same night (the neighbor was leaving for work).
- Mario woke abnormally at clock times — 1:11, 3:33, 4:11 AM — with regularity in the years following the 1977 incident; he attributes no direct explanation to this pattern but notes it.

## Ellsworth Broader UFO Pattern and Nuclear Nexus

- A prior Ellsworth incident (pre-1975, location near the Badlands, 66th area) involved two security policemen found outside their vehicle on open prairie: one fully unclothed, one approximately 90% unclothed; vehicle found at a distance; this was an open rumor among personnel.

- Mario notes a pattern: both he and Lovelace had light-skinned Black partners who disappeared or were unreachable afterward; Close Encounters of the Third Kind came out that same year (1977); Mario saw it in a Rapid City theater and walked out during the Roy Neary railroad-crossing scene because it mirrored his experience.
- Mario attempted to contact [[linda-moulton-howe|Linda Moulton Howe]] about cattle mutilations in the region (eventually reached her in 2014 via email); she was investigating that area of South Dakota in 1977 — the same time frame as the November 5 incident.
- The only Minuteman II missile that ever launched from a silo during the entire program did so from November 2 (near Mario's flight area) during testing in the 1960s; the detonation was a dummy warhead launched for 5 seconds to confirm ejection; context for why alarm response to November 5 was taken extremely seriously.
- Mario has been told by an unnamed Congressional-adjacent contact that after scientists, pilots, and credentialed witnesses are finished testifying, Congress will eventually need someone who had "up-close and personal time with these things" — he has said he would appear before Congress.
