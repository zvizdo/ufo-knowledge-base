## 1996 Varginha: Flight Path and Crash

A cylindrical, cigar-shaped craft approximately the size of a school bus was observed by multiple witnesses on or around January 13, 1996. It was in visible distress — struggling to stay airborne, emitting white vapor from a tear in the back. A new crash witness in the updated film:

- **[[fabio-furtado|Fabio Furtado]]** (farmer) — heard the craft make a "W"-like sound of distress over his farm before it went down. This is the first testimony establishing the final segment of the flight path, completing the map Fox assembled from multiple sightings.

- **[[carlos-de-souza|Carlos de Souza]]** — ultralight pilot and geography professor; saw the cigar-shaped craft struggling, followed it to the Molini Farm, saw it hit the ground. Debris field larger than a football field. Observed two types of debris: [[memory-metal]] fragments (crumpled to nothing but regained original shape when released — "same thing [[jesse-marcel|Jesse Marcel]] said about Roswell") and a liquid that burned the grass. The site still shows differential ground appearance visible from drone photography.

De Souza was threatened at gunpoint by arriving military from the Eza base, then stopped at a gas station miles away where Men in Black arrived in an unmarked, plateless vehicle and recited his wife's name, daughter's name, and address — in pre-internet 1996 Brazil. Message: "You didn't see anything. Things will get very weird for you."

## 1996 Varginha: The Beings

Three beings were reported to have emerged from the craft. The case has the best-corroborated two:

**Being 1 (Fire Department capture, morning):** A civilian (Kuber Lada) saw two military trucks entering town days after the crash. At the blockade point, eyewitnesses reported this being was captured with a net "like a wild animal," screaming like a child. Apparently died in captivity.

**Being 2 (Military officers, ~6pm):** Military police officer **Marco Sharice** and his partner [[eric-lopes|Eric Lopes]] encountered the being crossing the road near the Marakon gymnasium — two blocks from where the three girls had seen a creature two hours earlier. Sharice grabbed it with bare hands, placed it on his lap in the vehicle, and transported it first to a small clinic (whose doctor refused it) and then to [[hospital-regional-varginha|Hospital Regional Varginha]].

**The three girls' encounter:** Sisters Valkyria (14) and Lilian (16), plus Katya (21), came within approximately 3 meters of one of the beings in the afternoon. Description: brown oily skin, large head with horn-like ridges, wide red eyes, huge feet with what appeared to be three toes. The being was suffering from the intense heat, exuding a sulfur-like smell (rotten eggs / ammonia). Katya locked eyes with the creature, which telepathically conveyed: "I need help." Lilian ran back and pulled Katya away. Fox argues aerial photography of the neighborhood confirms this was the same being that ended up at [[hospital-regional-varginha|Hospital Regional Varginha]].

**Physical evidence of the encounter:** The smell lasted weeks in the sinuses of multiple witnesses; the hospital wing where one being was brought for X-rays had to be closed for days and repeatedly disinfected before the odor cleared.

## 1996 Varginha: Dr. Italo Venturelli

The centerpiece of the updated film. [[italo-venturelli]] — head neurosurgeon at Hospital Regional Varginha since 1983, concert pianist — came forward after a heart attack and a cancer diagnosis while in the ICU. His framing:

> "If I get through this, I can't take this to the grave."

Venturelli spent 3–4 minutes alone with the being in the ICU. Physical description:

- Skin **more white than brown** (contrasting with the outdoor witnesses who described it as dark-brown/oily — possibly because it had been cleaned up, or because sulfur emission varies with emotional state and temperature)
- Large head, almond-shaped eyes, **red eyes** with ridges on the forehead matching the girls' description
- Same spindly arm structure and overall body plan

Venturelli on the interaction:

> "When he looked into the eyes of this creature, despite the fact that it was in captivity, it had control of the whole room."

> "There's so much to learn from the eyes of that being."

He describes the communication as telepathic — without speech:

> "As if to say: I'm perfectly fine. I've been well looked after. A communication as someone would say: yes, I am okay, I've received care."

He called the being an **"angel"** — the word he reached for to describe an intelligence "way more advanced than anything on Earth." Compare to the Zimbabwe school children and Humanitas Hospital message (see below).

The military arrived and took the being from the hospital. Hospital staff were threatened to remain silent. Venturelli was ejected from the building and may have lost his job.

Fox: "I'm told it's the first time in history we have this — a named, credentialed witness on video who was face-to-face with a live alien being."

The entire regional medical community is now backing him. Multiple other Humanitas Hospital doctors have not yet come forward but are considering it.
