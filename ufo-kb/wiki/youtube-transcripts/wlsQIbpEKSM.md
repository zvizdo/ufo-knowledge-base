# Why America's Top Scientists Are Going Missing

## Headline Claims

- Retired Air Force General [[neil-mccasland]] — who commanded the [[afrl]] at [[wright-patterson]] and oversaw [[pentagon|the Pentagon]]'s special access programs — disappeared February 27, 2026, eight days after Trump announced UAP file releases; no confirmed sightings despite FBI, Air Force OSI, helicopters, and drones.
- NASA materials scientist [[monica-resza]], co-inventor of the [[mandeloy-alloy]] (a nickel-based superalloy developed for oxygen-rich rocket engines under contracts with the same AFRL McCasland later commanded), disappeared June 22, 2025, on a Mount Waterman hike; no body found after 150+ days of searching.
- MIT plasma physicist [[nuno-lorero]] — deputy director of MIT's Plasma Science and Fusion Center and a leading expert on magnetic reconnection in nuclear fusion — was shot and killed December 15–16, 2025, at his Brookline, MA home, two days before a competitor fusion company announced a $6B merger with Trump Media.
- Caltech/JPL astronomer [[carl-gilmore]] — who had recently begun working on the [[vera-rubin-observatory]] and whose techniques for detecting exoplanet atmospheres are now central to the biosignature search — was shot and killed February 16, 2026, by a neighbor who had previously been arrested on his property and released.
- [[melissa-casillas]], an administrative assistant at [[los-alamos]], disappeared June 26, 2025; evidence suggests a voluntary departure under financial stress, but the factory-reset phone and badge confusion remain unexplained.
- The pattern echoes the 1982–1990 [[1982-gec-marconi-deaths|GEC-Marconi scientists deaths]] in Britain, where 25 defense engineers died under bizarre circumstances; no one was ever charged.
- [[harold-malmgren]] told Michaels that non-human intelligences operate by "tracking the frontier of human innovation" — a direct structural parallel to the *Three-Body Problem* premise, which senior Bush administration officials were assigned to read.
