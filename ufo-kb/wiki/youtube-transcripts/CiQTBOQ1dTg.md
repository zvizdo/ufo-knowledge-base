## Peru Face-Peelers Investigation

- **Setup**: Summer 2023, San Antonio de Pintoyaku (Kichwa people, Alto Naney/Naney River region, Peruvian Amazon). Multiple villages reporting attacks. Authorities concluded "jetpack miners trying to drive villagers off land" — a narrative Alberino calls absurd, since river miners don't have money let alone jetpacks. The incursions also occurred in Iquitos (36,000 people, Navy presence, hardened target), debunking the land-grabbing motive.
- **Alberino's preparation**: Made contact via WhatsApp with a school teacher named Christian; played his recorded message to the chief (APU) who granted official invitation. Brought: night vision goggles (recording-capable), thermal binoculars, high-powered flashlights, radios, food/medical supplies, and two active-duty Peruvian Navy jungle commandos.
- **Village state on arrival**: Men sleeping during the day, patrolling armed at night. First time Alberino was greeted by a "small army of men with loaded shotguns." Severe PTSD among witnesses.
- **Assailant description**: ~6.5–8 ft tall (villagers gestured heights up to ~7.5–8 ft; conservative estimate ~6.5–7 ft given possible exaggeration). Dressed head-to-foot in black armored body suits with helmets; almond-shaped tinted eye lenses (some said yellow, some green — possibly night vision). Hoverboard technology: circular discs, sometimes single occupant, sometimes two standing together "surfing." Boot-mounted disc technology that allowed floating ~1 meter off ground. Impervious to 16-gauge birdshot at point-blank range ("BBs dinking off the armor"). One villager described a silver-suited assailant.
- **Craft description**: Multiple witnesses independently (some drew it in the dirt, one on an acrylic chalkboard) described an acorn/almond-shaped craft, matte black, hovering silently at 30–40 ft, no propellers or audible propulsion. One witness pulling nets saw a translucent mesh version (like "through a screen door") with two figures visible inside (one at front, one at belly), stubby retractable protrusions with blinking lights, and a low-frequency hum when it departed.
- **Talia incident**: A 15-year-old girl, Talia, was the subject of an attempted abduction. She was reaching for dropped fruit after sundown when two armored assailants on hoverboards seized her — one grabbing from behind (hand over mouth), one grabbing her feet — and carried her hovering behind a thatch chicken coop. They injected something up her nose (causing disorientation), applied a numbing cream to her face, and began making an incision with what Alberino believes was a laser scalpel. One assailant said to the other: "Be careful. Don't put too much on her face — it'll ruin the flesh" (referring to difficulty separating skin from underlying tissue). Talia pushed up the rear assailant's helmet; he let go to pull it down; she screamed; armed villagers arrived within seconds. Her scar was visible when Alberino interviewed her. She associated Alberino with the assailants on first sight (trembling, hiding face in hair).
- **One assailant spoke like a "gringo"**: Talia said one of the two assailants sounded like Alberino (American-accented Spanish); the other sounded Peruvian.
- **Operation Resolute Sentinel**: A joint military training operation was simultaneously occurring in Peru involving every US military branch (Marines, Air Force, Navy, [[space-force|Space Force]], Coast Guard) plus Peru, Uruguay, Ecuador, Brazil, and the UK. Alberino finds the coincidence significant — either a cover operation to investigate/capture the assailants, or something more troubling.
- **[[michael-herrera|Michael Herrera]] connection**: Alberino connects the Peru events to [[michael-herrera]]'s Indonesia encounter: paramilitary actors with exotic aerospace technology (octagonal craft "blacker than black") apparently escorting "psionic assets" — which in the Peru case may mean individuals being harvested for psychic ability, face tissue, or other biological material. Alberino frames it as: nefarious actors with advanced aerospace tech using it for "the most abominable things imaginable, including human trafficking."
- **Face-peeler legend origin**: The "face-peeler" (pelacara) mythology in the region goes back only to the 1980s; villagers describe gringos with advanced technology showing up occasionally; bodies found later with faces removed or organs harvested. This suggests the face-peeler phenomenon is decades old in the region.
