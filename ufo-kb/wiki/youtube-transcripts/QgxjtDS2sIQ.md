# "I Spent 3 Years With Tall White Aliens At Area 51" — Charles Hall

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] episode featuring [[charles-hall]], a USAF weather observer (March 1965 – May 1967) at [[nellis-air-force-base|Nellis AFB]]'s [[indian-springs|Indian Springs Gunnery Ranges]], Nevada. Hall claims sustained, daily interaction with an alien race he calls the [[tall-whites]] over roughly two years. His wife [[marie-theres-hall]] appears briefly at the end confirming details about [[tim-taylor]]'s visits. Six-volume account published as [[millennial-hospitality]].

## Hall's Background and Selection

- Hall enlisted in the USAF at 19 (1964); trained as weather observer at Sheppard AFB, Illinois — scoring 499/500 on the Air Force math ability test.
- He holds a Master's degree in nuclear physics; he classifies himself as certain that Einstein's relativity is incomplete based on what he observed.
- His initial orders were secretly routed to Nellis/[[indian-springs|Indian Springs]] via cover orders; a clerical error accidentally sent him to weather school first, delaying his assignment by six weeks while the FBI searched for him.
- He was told by a CIA psychiatrist (one of the top three at the CIA, nine PhDs, in his 30s) why he was chosen:
  - There are 105 psychological defense mechanisms humans can use.
  - Most people use 1–2; the CIA tries to train generals to use 3–5.
  - In the first 15 minutes of meeting Hall, the psychiatrist observed him deploy 70 of the 105.
  - "If there ever was a person to send, it would be you. They'll never lay a glove on you."
- 41 men were sent before Hall; all came back psychologically "compromised" — some with mental breakdowns, some with severe radiation injuries.
- The problem: earlier men recognized they were dealing with something threatening *before* they had decided how to react — so their defenses collapsed in the first encounter.
- Hall's method: always maintain a mundane rationalization (sleepwalking, vitamin deficiency, hallucination) until emotionally ready to acknowledge reality.

## Three Alien Species: Hierarchy and Timeline

### Tall Whites

- Arrived on Earth by at least the middle of the last ice age.
- Home planet likely near Arcturus (36 light years) or a nearby star — they were gone ~2 months and returned, implying FTL travel of at least 44×c.
- Best technology of the three groups; craft can exceed the speed of light.
- Live 600–800 years (approximately 10× human lifespan); elders, adults, and children present at Indian Springs.
- Fully biological: eat only plants, drink water, breathe air, use bathrooms; all craft have restrooms in the rear.
- Carry no visible handheld device — thought-reading and thought-insertion electronics are built into their suits/helmets.
- Radioactive suits emit visible alpha and beta particles in darkness (Hall recognized them from childhood radium experiments); tracks extend much further than radium paint — indicating extreme power.

### Small Grays ("Roswell Grays")

- Arrived before the [[tall-whites]]; got here first; base on the Moon.
- From a planet closer to Earth than the [[tall-whites|tall whites]]' home world.
- Much more numerous: Hall estimates ~90% of craft sightings are gray, ~10% tall white.
- Technology clearly advanced but below tall white standards.
- They are also planteaters and biological.
- Grays farmed the Nevada/Utah deserts during the ice age — Hall describes an L-shaped valley with mountains on both sides and 13 tunnels carved as granaries; the first tunnel (largest) could accommodate their deep spacecraft for grain loading.

### Norwegians with 24 Teeth (Nordics)

- Arrived toward the end of the last ice age.
- Least technologically advanced of the three — barely capable of interstellar travel; must land at any price even if the Earth "runs into them."
- More patient, diplomatic, and sometimes benevolent compared to the tall whites.
- Some can pass among humans in public without attracting attention.
- In 1965, they primarily wanted to be able to work with the American government and build craft on Earth.

## Area 51 / Indian Springs Geography

- [[area-51|Area 51]] = [[area-51|Groom Lake]] — just the entry area, not "Dreamland."
- **Dreamland = Areas 53 and 54** — the northern half of Indian Springs Valley (plus [[indian-springs|Dog Bone Valley]] to the east and part of French Peak area to the west).
- Areas were numbered up to 60; Hall had been in all of them.
- Tall White base at the north end of Indian Springs Valley; underground hangars.
- Dog Bone Lake serves as their primary landing field; they prefer to come in near the full moon (when Earth and Moon form a line, giving a uniform gravitational field behind the incoming craft as a safety buffer).
- The grays come and go at any time from their Moon base.
- "Permanent guard" tall whites were always stationed at the base; new arrivals on a schedule — some on two-month cycles, some on five-year cycles (the five-year ships came directly from their home planet).

## FDR's Desert Game Range — Three-Strike Argument

Hall presents this as his strongest institutional-level evidence that the tall white presence predated official US military knowledge:

- **Strike 1 (1935–1937):** FDR, after consulting with a single Army Air Force colonel, issued an executive order creating the Desert Game Range to protect the desert bighorn sheep. He positioned a battalion of anti-aircraft guns with live ammunition to guard the sheep preserve — pre-WWII, before any military range existed in the area.
- **Strike 2:** Sheep Peak (the range's namesake) was first summit-climbed in 1970, despite being described as an easy climb. The area had an implicit prohibition.
- **Strike 3:** The Desert Game Range predates [[area-51|Area 51]], the Nellis Gunnery Range, and Camp Mercury — it is the first layer of federal land protection over the tall white territory.
- Hall also notes that Park Rangers were apparently instructed to tell visitors: if you see something that looks like a human looking at you from over the sagebrush, "don't look back — especially if it looks like a woman with children."
- Marine Corps logic applied: if it happened once, it's random; twice, a coincidence; three times, somebody planned it.

## The "Legend of Range 4 Harry"

- The legend among USAF rangemen: a giant white radioactive horse that floated around the ranges at night, fluorescent and terrifying, supposedly a horse that had gotten too close to nuclear blasts at Yucca Flats.
- Reality: the tall whites, when wearing their protective suits, could form a "high-speed horse formation" — two women in front bending at the waist with arms out, three children floating at waist height in the middle, two men with larger suits bending over the children from behind.
- Force fields from adjacent suits merged into one combined field; from 1/4 mile away in the dark, this looked exactly like a giant white floating horse.
- In this formation they could sustain ~100 mph over the desert, which made them appear to "gallop."
- An actual tall white male was named "Range 4 Harry" — one of Hall's friendliest contacts, who he describes as "like a brother."

## Physical Encounter Details

### Protective Suits and Force Fields

- Suits enable floating 9–10 inches off the ground (higher if needed); children's suits allow floating to waist height.
- When fully powered, force fields make all materials repel each other — every lock pops open (they used the Nellis base operations building's restrooms on Sunday nights because the rangemen were supposed to be sleeping).
- Standing within range when suits are powered is like walking into the back of a jet engine — Hall repeatedly warns about "getting your ass burned."
- At 2.5–3 miles Hall could safely watch their craft operations; closer was dangerous.

### One Radiation Injury (Not Hall's)

- The most serious case before Hall: a rangeman out at Range 4 saw the tall whites in the horse formation approaching across the dry lake bed; ran toward them thinking they were horses; his shoulder nearly touched one of the suits.
- Result: radiation burns equivalent to Hiroshima victims — the meat was cooked off the bone. Hall saw the x-rays at the Nellis Space Hospital.
- This man barely made it back to base alive. The suits operate at extreme power.

### Hall's First Encounter

- His OJT instructor had already been traumatized and was being discharged.
- Walking back at midnight from the weather station, Hall saw a 12-year-old-equivalent tall white girl following him.
- Backing them up 1/4 mile away: the teacher (an adult female tall white who he would come to regard as a primary contact) and Range 4 Harry.
- Hall's defense: decided he must be sleepwalking. Then the girl hovered her feet off the ground, floated over the grass, and bent to pick something up — replicating exactly the position Hall had read in a Titanic book two nights before (an account of an "angel" in a lifeboat).
- He concluded he must be mentally ill and went back to his barracks. The encounters continued.

### Flight Inside the Craft

- Hall rode in the cargo hold of a tall white scout craft at least twice.
- First time: he thought he had broken his kneecap; the tall whites came to transport him to their base (Areas 53/54) for a medical evaluation. American generals and medical personnel were already present at the base.
- The craft didn't fully power up for the short trip — just lifted off gently and flew 100 feet off the ground up the valley.
- Hall remembers everything because the electronic hypnosis didn't work — altered brain blood flow from his injury/pain bypassed their equipment. "It's almost like anesthesia that doesn't work during surgery — still awake."

## Why Hall Was Never Debriefed

- The tall whites (especially the teacher) could read the thoughts of anyone in Hall's vicinity.
- If a debriefing officer thought something critical about the teacher's mothering or competence, the teacher would know instantly — and would demand that officer be brought to her in the desert.
- Hall's orders were structured so he could never be formally briefed or debriefed; no one could inspect him; anyone wanting to speak with him had to come to the Nellis chow hall parking lot between 12:00 and 12:30.
- Even then, they had to check in with the Nellis base commander before and after.
- Hall: "Nothing I would do would ever be classified — they physically couldn't come in and inspect my barracks."

## Pamela's Trust Test

- "Pamela" was a young tall white woman who needed to pass a test to head up a technology-transfer team that would interact with human scientists.
- The test: with all electronics/force fields turned off, walk alone down a pathway toward Hall while he was tracking weather balloons and stand behind him with her back turned — demonstrating complete trust.
- On the night of the test, 70+ tall whites gathered on the east side of Indian Springs Valley to watch — plus some American generals and other officials. Hall stopped counting at 35 scout craft but estimated 70+.
- First attempt: Pamela panicked ~1/4 mile short and fled, convinced Hall was "an ugly monster she couldn't trust."
- Eventually she passed the test. The male companion disguised as "Charlie Hall" and Pamela as his wife were then supposed to visit Livermore Labs to interact with human scientists — Hall flags this as speculation from what the tall whites told him.

## Hall Photon Theory

- Hall copyrighted "Hall Photon Theory" in 1997; also published *Beyond Relativity*.
- Core argument: Einstein's special relativity is wrong because the photon contains more force fields than the two Maxwell assumed (electric + magnetic).
- Evidence from Michelson-Morley (1887): the experiment's failure means photons must have additional internal structure (at least one more force field for "steering" in the interferometer); splitting one photon gives the same results — quantum entanglement, which they dismissed as "spooky action at a distance" instead of accepting more structure.
- Gravity as FTL proof: the Andromeda galaxy is organized (rotation curves intact) across 2.7 million light years — impossible if gravity travels at c. Hall: "If it was going to interact along the way and stop, it would never get there. Instead it goes into high gear."
- The key physics: different force fields have different "permitivity of free space" values — some (like the ones surrounding neutrinos) are so low that the corresponding "speed of light" for those fields is a trillion times faster.
- Five core subatomic particle types used in craft design (ceramic fiber optic coils) — Hall believes this is why Eisenhower was shown exactly five craft: one demonstrating each particle type's purpose.
- Craft propulsion: produce these particles in an "engine," wind them into ceramic fiber optic coils (49.6% aluminum, 50.2% silicon dioxide — melts between 1,800–2,200°F); power up or down different coil sets to control direction and speed.

## Roswell Debris — Hall's Explanation

- Two gray scout craft crashed at Roswell, not one.
- The "melted rocks" = melted ceramic fiber optics (which look exactly like lava hardened on impact).
- The "memory metal" foil = trivial protective sheathing over the fiber optic coils; not the actual craft material.
- The debris salvagers "passed over the stuff that matters" — the melted fiber optics — while collecting the foil wrapper.
- The crashed craft in one case had three Chinese children (orphans the grays were trying to return to human society) and one young gray teenager as backup pilot — all inside when Hall personally examined the same model.
- The restroom was in the rear: the alien "survivor" (Kingman crash) stayed in the back of the craft because it was the bathroom/food storage area, not a shrine.

## Craft Classification

- **Deep spacecraft**: house-sized, double hull, fiber optic windings between hulls, all repair accessible from inside, living quarters + cockpit + propulsion; built for 100+ light-year transit; emergency beacons use FTL force fields.
- **Scout craft (ellipsoidal/tic-tac)**: can exceed light speed but Range 4 Harry said "nobody in their right mind would take it that fast" — at 1% of c, if anything went wrong, "your friends couldn't find you."
- **Children's play craft**: for travel within Earth (~Bishop, CA to Roswell in 30 min); not designed to exceed c.
- **Military craft**: use 2 additional unstable particle types; never used on family/child-carrying vessels.
- Normal scout craft performance Hall witnessed: 8,000–12,000 mph in a straight 7-mile run, stop, power down — experiencing 15,000+ G-forces (for comparison, 10,000 G liquefies steel). "Not even in low gear."
- When powered up, the craft appears fuzzy white — the force field interacts with light, making actual metal invisible.
