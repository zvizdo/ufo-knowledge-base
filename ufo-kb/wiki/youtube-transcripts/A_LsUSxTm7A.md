## The Older Dryas and Younger Dryas

- Two catastrophic events bracketing the end of the Ice Age:
  - **Older Dryas (~14,500 years ago)**: most underappreciated; marked the start of the catastrophic cycle
  - **Younger Dryas (~12,000 years ago)**: the more famous event; marked the end of the cycle
- Antarctic ice cores go back 450,000 years (oldest ice on Earth); Greenland ice cores only 20,000 years
  - Lroy: Antarctica conspiracy theories wrong — ice cores confirm ice cap age; no ancient civilization there
- At ~14,500 BP: carbon dioxide spiked astronomically — dwarfs modern industrial carbon levels
  - Temperature went from coldest point of Ice Age → warmer than now → instantly back to colder than ice age
  - All within ~100 years — impossible without massive external forcing event
- Lroy argues for CME (Coronal Mass Ejection) over cometary impact (contra Hancock/Carlson/Taurid theory)
  - No confirmed impact craters from ~12,500 BP (Greenland crater is >1 million years old, under 20,000-year ice cap)
  - Airburst from Taurid meteor stream remains possible but Lroy finds plasma discharge evidence more compelling
  - CME → weakened magnetosphere → holes open → plasma discharge events globally → vitrification
  - CME → massive glacial melt → freshwater floods oceans → Gulf Stream shuts down → rapid cooling event explains temperature plunge
- The Laurentide ice sheet (North America): 2–3 miles deep, covered all of Canada and northern US
  - This mass caused the Earth's axis to wobble; when it melted, the pole shifted
  - Lroy believes the North Pole was previously over Greenland, not the current Arctic position
- Mount Nemruth (near [[lake-van|Lake Van]], different from the summit-statue mountain): exploded at end of Younger/Older Dryas
  - Giant crater; volcanic explosion could have produced sufficient heat for vitrification in surrounding region

## Pioneer 10/11 and the Binary Dead Star

- Pioneer 10 and 11 launched in the early 1970s — first deep-space probes, predating Voyager
  - Sent in completely different directions to create a 3D gravitational model of the outer solar system
  - Original mission: study gravitational influences causing tilt of Uranus and Neptune
- Early 1980s: both probes exited the inner solar system, passed the Kuiper Belt
  - [[thomas-van-flandern|Thomas Van Flandern]] (NASA astronomer) called [[robert-harrington|Robert Harrington]] (U.S. Navy chief astronomer): "check this data"
  - They found a large planet (now known as Planet 9/Planet 10) beyond the Kuiper Belt
    - Four to five times the mass of Earth; does not orbit the Sun normally
    - Caltech now places 99.9% confidence in its existence based on Kuiper Belt perturbations
  - NASA publicly announced the planet in the 1990s; then went completely silent
    - Miles Standish published mathematical "corrections" disproving Van Flandern and Harrington; story vanished
- **The binary dead star**: what Pioneer data actually revealed beyond Planet 9
  - Pioneer 10 and 11, traveling in opposite directions, both detected an **equal gravitational pole** on the entire solar system
  - Something of enormous mass is exerting a consistent pull on our entire solar system from the same direction regardless of probe position
  - Identified as a dead star (not a brown dwarf; underwent supernova or similar); no longer fusing = invisible (no light)
  - Distance from Pioneer probes at time of detection: ~50 billion miles (1983–84 data)
  - This finding appeared in a 1987 Science Encyclopedia diagram (page ~2,488) with no accompanying article or explanation
    - Lroy found a PDF, uploaded it to his website to preserve it; original largely scrubbed from internet
  - 80% of known stars are binary or ternary; single-star systems like ours assumed exceptional, but may not be
- **Both investigators died of throat cancer**:
  - [[thomas-van-flandern|Thomas Van Flandern]]: non-smoker; died in the 1990s shortly after investigation
  - [[robert-harrington|Robert Harrington]]: non-smoker; died in the 1990s shortly after investigation
  - Lroy does not explicitly claim murder but calls it "very mysterious"
- Binary companion model explains the Great Year:
  - Dead star orbits our sun on a ~25,000-year elliptical cycle
  - **Perihelion** (closest approach) every ~12,500 years: gravitational influence forces the Sun to expel excess energy as CME
  - This aligns with Younger Dryas, Older Dryas, and all prior civilizational reset events
  - The Great Year documentary (recommended by Lroy) correctly identified the binary dance but wrongly attributed it to Sirius; Sirius is far too distant to cause such precise ~25,000-year cycles
- Yuga Cycles (Hindu) track the same pattern:
  - Rise → golden age → CME reset → iron/bronze age → silver → gold → reset
  - Multiple factors: binary companion, galactic center alignments, planetary alignments
- The Great Year documentary: available on YouTube; narrator recently deceased; Lroy recommends it

## Underground Cities of Turkey

- Derinkuyu: largest subterranean city in the world; discovered when a man knocked down an apartment wall
  - His chickens kept wandering in and disappearing; he investigated and found the entrance
  - 30 interconnected levels; air shafts; multi-ton rolling stone doors to seal entrances
  - Rooms for animals, schools, sleeping, food storage; could house 20,000 people simultaneously
  - Tourists can visit today but only see a couple of levels
- Kaymakli: sister underground city, 5.5 miles away from Derinkuyu
  - A 5.5-mile underground tunnel connects the two — built so inhabitants could travel without going above ground
  - This means going above ground was unsafe — nuclear winter-type conditions, not just army avoidance
- Why these cities exist where they do:
  - Area has uniquely soft volcanic porous stone — the only place in the ancient world easy enough to carve
  - No excavated material found at the sites: the removed stone simply does not exist aboveground
  - Lroy: built before the Younger Dryas events; reused later by Hittites and others (who did NOT build them)
  - Archaeologists dated them to Hittites and similar cultures, but that's just the most recent occupation layer
  - Parallel: someone carving their name on an ancient monument doesn't mean they built it
- The concentrated network of underground cities in Eastern Turkey = center of the ancient world
  - Lroy interprets them as planned refuges, built in response to knowledge of a previous catastrophe
  - The star-calendar function of [[gobekli-tepe|Göbekli Tepe]] may have been to track Great Year cycles and predict the next reset

## Eastern Turkey Megalithic Sites

- Recently discovered sites (2001–2017), largely unknown to general public:
  - **[[kepkesi|Kef Kesi]]**: giant square basalt blocks, machine-like precision cuts; vitrified surfaces
  - **Iyanis / Ianos**: andesite foundation blocks (one of hardest stones); mud brick built on top by Urartu/Urans
  - **Shavo Tepe**: T-pillars in basalt; same T-pillar design as [[gobekli-tepe|Göbekli Tepe]] and Pumapunku Bolivia
  - **Alton Tepe**: 425 miles from the other sites; same symbols and building style — shows a migration route toward Mediterranean/Black Sea
  - **Coru, Asver Ate**: additional sites in same Lake Van region
- Materials used: basalt, andesite, golden kysite alabaster — none of them local to the immediate area
  - Basalt possibly from 350 miles away; andesite from even farther
  - Same pattern as Egypt (pink granite from Aswan); energetically specific stone chosen regardless of distance
- Technical impossibilities for the credited Urartu culture (Iron Age, ~2000 BC):
  - Multi-ton stones transported hundreds of miles → impossible with Iron Age tools
  - Cuts so precise you can still cut your finger on them → impossible by hand
  - Symbols spanning multiple blocks seamlessly → required machine-level precision
  - Urartu built crude mud brick ON TOP of the earlier megalithic foundations — clearly two different civilizations
  - Urartu incorporated some symbols as "war gods" and turned doorways into "false windows" — misunderstanding the original meaning
- Symbols found across these sites and globally:
  - **T-pillar**: also at Göbekli Tepe, Minorca Spain, Pumapunku Bolivia — seeds of knowledge (not spearheads)
  - **Sun Cross**: became [[knights-templar|Knights Templar]] cross; also on Pope's vestments; pine cone in front of Vatican
  - **Pine cone**: passing of knowledge/spiritual doctrine — found in Assyrian art, Vatican, these sites
  - **Handbag**: also seen in Mesopotamian and Mesoamerican art
  - **Tree of Life**: same concept across all these sites
  - **Griffin**: guardian of time (Hourglass); same guardian concept as [[great-sphinx|the Sphinx]]
  - **Three-step pyramid / Trinity**: Father/Son/Holy Spirit → mind/body/spirit → Shiva/Vishnu/Brahma
  - **Viracocha holding condors** (Bolivia) = same concept as Lake Van bas-relief holding seeds
- Lroy's argument: all symbols = conserved hermetic knowledge passed through secret societies (Templars etc.)
  - Manly P. Hall's thesis: symbolism was the language of the ancients deliberately opaque to non-initiates
  - Same reason the [[great-pyramid|Great Pyramid]] and Pumapunku have no inscriptions — language was architecture + symbolism
- Sun-aligned Susi temples (openings facing rising/setting sun) are now offset from east-west
  - This proves the Earth's axis has shifted since they were built
  - When the temples faced True East/West = when the axis was in a different position = date of construction
  - Similar reasoning: sites aligned to Greenland as True North → built when pole was over Greenland (>12,000 years ago)
- Lake Van as the ideal post-flood settlement:
  - 4th deepest lake on Earth (1,500 ft); surrounded by active volcanoes → fertile volcanic soil
  - Perfect conditions for creating new civilization and agriculture after a catastrophe
  - Lroy's thesis: survivors of the flood (the Noah figure's descendants) founded civilization here, then spread globally

## Sumerian Tablets and the Flood Hero

- Cuneiform: a wedge-shaped writing style (not a language); emerged from Mesopotamian region
  - Sumerian is a language isolate — almost nothing in common with any other known language
  - George Smith (British Museum, 1875): cracked the code by back-tracing through Assyrian and Akkadian cuneiform
  - Smith first cracked the Epic of Gilgamesh tablet, then worked backward; described as weeping when he first read it
- The Royal Ashurbanipal Library (Nineveh, modern Mosul, Iraq):
  - Found by Austin Henry Layard, 1848–49; 40,000 cuneiform tablets
  - Only ~150 have been translated out of 40,000 — vast knowledge locked away
  - King Ashurbanipal (Assyrian) gathered tablets from all of Mesopotamia into one collection
  - Contains: Epic of Gilgamesh, Sumerian King List, Atrahasis, Eridu Genesis, Enuma Elish, Legend of Atana, Legend of Etana, Death of Bilgames
- Sumerians invented: astronomy, animal husbandry, agriculture, mathematics, trigonometry, understanding of the solar system
  - First currency: the shekel = value of one bushel of wheat (agricultural civilization)
  - Lroy believes Sumerians are older than the conventional ~4,000–6,000 years: geodesy alignments suggest >12,000 years; possibly 20,000 years old
  - Evidence: geodesy alignments to True North over Greenland (when pole was in different position)
  - Ocean shells found on the ziggurat summit at Eridu — Persian Gulf flood, not just Euphrates river flooding
- Eridu: the first city on Earth (Sumerian records); found in late 1800s in southern Iraq
  - Eridu ziggurat never excavated; currently being pillaged by locals selling tablets on black market
  - Lroy ran a "Save Aridu" campaign on his website
  - Visited Penn Museum to see original Eridu artifacts
- Shupak (Shuruppak): last of the original five cities; found in 1931 at Tell Fara, Iraq
  - 16 feet below surface, below Uruk civilization layer, below a 4-foot alluvial flood layer
  - British and German team: 80% of members left when they found no human activity between layers
  - Remaining team dug to 22 feet; found destroyed, emptied chambers; diorite hand with six fingers (diorite = extremely hard stone); beautiful pottery
  - The Penn Museum excavation report explicitly notes similarities between findings and the Utnapishtim/Zas-Sudra flood story
- The Noah figure: in different texts called Zas-Sudra (Sumerian), Utnapishtim (Akkadian/Babylonian), Atrahasis (Babylonian)
  - Son of Ubara-Tutu (last king of Shupak before the flood)
  - Was a high priest and sage — "Atrahasis" literally means "very wise"
  - Warned of the coming flood; given specific instructions (length of cedar planks, bitumen sealing)
  - Landed in the Ararat Mountains region after the flood (modern Eastern Turkey/Armenia)
  - Given immortality — "Death of Bilgames" tablet explicitly states no human after him will receive immortality
- Epic of Gilgamesh: Gilgamesh (king of Uruk/Erech, ~3,500–4,000 years ago) seeks Utnapishtim to find secret of immortality
  - Enkidu's death triggers his fear of mortality
  - Travels to the "Underworld" via the ferryman (origin of Greek Charon/River Styx myth)
  - Finds Utnapishtim on an island; sets him two tasks (dive for a plant of immortality, stay awake 7 days)
  - Gilgamesh fails both tasks; returns home empty-handed
  - Tablet 10 of the Epic contains "there was once a city called Shupak, far older than your time" — Gilgamesh acknowledges extreme antiquity
  - "The gods were once in it" = anomalous quote Lroy finds significant
- Lroy: the Ararat landing and Eastern Turkey settlement connects Mesopotamian origin stories to the Lake Van sites
  - The Noah figure's descendants = the builders of the Lake Van megalithic civilization
  - The same tradition says three sons of Noah (Japeth, Shem, Ham) repopulated and traveled around the world
  - Lroy traces the symbols globally as evidence of this diaspora
