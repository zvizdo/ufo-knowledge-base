## Summary

[[gary-mckinnon]], a Scottish IT contractor and UFO enthusiast, describes in forensic detail his 2001–2002 unauthorized access to 97 US military and government computer networks — hacking the [[us-army|US Army]], Navy, Air Force, NSA, DoD, DISA, and [[johnson-space-center|NASA Johnson Space Center]] using blank/default passwords and off-the-shelf tools. His stated motive was to find evidence of free energy technology and UAP propulsion — not espionage or malice.

## Background and Motivation

McKinnon grew up in Falkirk, near **Bonnybridge, Scotland** — a Scottish UFO hotspot where UFO tourism is a real phenomenon. His stepfather had multiple sightings; his step-uncle also reported them. McKinnon's own sighting at age 12: a reddish-orange glowing light moving in a brownian-motion arc from horizon to horizon, not a straight line. He became a member of **BUFORA** (British UFO Research Association).

Career path: Atari games console 1978–79 → Atari computer → BASIC then assembler → network installation and Windows configuration → failed computer science degree (spent too much time in the student bar) → banking IT contractor (high-level but routine networking).

First internet connection: 1995. First searches were for UFOs. Around 2000–2001, he read [[steven-greer|Steven Greer]]'s **Disclosure Project** book, which listed specific installation locations and personnel accounts. McKinnon thought: "I want this from the horse's mouth. I didn't want to just believe. I wanted to know."

## Hacking Methodology

Tools used: all commercially available, off-the-shelf.

**Phase 1 — Wide-Net Scan**: McKinnon wrote a **Pearl script** that scanned thousands of IP addresses per minute, testing for blank passwords or passwords set to "password," "admin," or similar. Tested first on UK sites (Cambridge FTP server, Oxford, polytechnics) to verify the technique worked.

**Phase 2 — Target Identification**: Used **NIHEI's IP Index** — a site where someone had compiled which subnets were owned by which US government departments. McKinnon could grab a subnet block and scan 250,000 computers in 8 minutes. Typically 5% would respond; of those, 5% had blank passwords.

**Phase 3 — Escalation**: Once on a machine with a blank password, he'd attack the domain server using a dictionary attack tool called **LoftCrack** to become domain administrator (controlling the entire network).

**Phase 4 — Search**: Used a tool called **LanSearch** to type search terms across all local PCs under his control at once — could search 5,000 machines simultaneously. Search terms included: UFOs, ET, terrestrial, spacecraft, secret, top secret, PDF. Importantly, unredacted PDFs from that era could be downloaded and the redaction removed locally.

**Result**: 97 sites accessed total, including: NSA, Fort Meade, DISA (Defense Information Systems Agency), DoD networks, Army networks, Navy networks, US Air Force networks, and [[johnson-space-center]].

McKinnon's approach was via **RemotelyAnywhere** (like PCAnywhere) — he would see the desktop of the target machine on his own screen. He accessed everything from his girlfriend's aunt's house in London on a **56k dialup** connection, in his dressing gown, smoking weed and drinking beer at 4am, at age 36.

## Building 8, Johnson Space Center — UAP Image

[[donna-hare]], a NASA launch photographic specialist, testified in the Disclosure Project that colleagues in Building 8 of [[johnson-space-center]] routinely airbrushed UAP out of satellite photography before public sale. McKinnon was already in JSC systems when he read her account and decided to look.

**Finding Building 8**: Used Windows network audit commands that listed each machine with its building number. Stripped out Building 8 machines — about a dozen. About half had blank passwords.

**The desktops**: Unlike normal user desktops covered with shortcuts and notes, these machines had bare desktops with only two folders: **"raw"** and **"processed"** — plus a couple of shortcuts. "Obviously images. Obviously where she said it was."

**The image**: Double-clicking a file in the raw folder opened a proprietary NASA image format — only viewable with NASA's own software on that machine. McKinnon used RemotelyAnywhere at 2-bit color (basically 4–5 colors total) to reduce bandwidth from the 56k connection. The image loaded line by line:

1. Blackness, then slowly — a hemisphere. "That's a planet. What the hell."
2. Blue and white hemisphere: Earth.
3. A silvery straight line coming down: a **smooth, cigar/tic-tac-shaped craft** in **lateral orbit** (horizontal to the Earth, 90 degrees — not end-on, not pointing at Earth).
4. The craft: **no rivets, no seams, no bolts, no antenna, no telemetry instruments**. Very smooth exterior. White/silvery even at 2-bit color.
5. **Geodesic domes** at each end — "man-made looking, but not man-made looking."
6. The craft appeared to be well beyond low Earth orbit.

Then: McKinnon saw the mouse cursor move. Someone else was at the computer. They right-clicked the LAN icon on the Windows taskbar, selected **disconnect**, and cut him off. The NASA employee disconnected him remotely mid-image-load. McKinnon never got to see the full craft or the surrounding context.

Jesse's observation: The 2004 USS Nimitz tic tac uap incident occurred just three years later — Commander [[david-fravor]] described the same smooth, featureless cigar/tic-tac shape. McKinnon's Building 8 image independently pre-corroborates the craft description.

[[donna-hare]] and McKinnon later corresponded (emails and a ~3-hour phone call). She has since passed away. McKinnon's Building 8 access confirms her account that this was still the active UAP image processing facility ~10–20 years after her time there.

## Non-Terrestrial Officers Spreadsheet

On a **[[us-navy|US Navy]] system**, McKinnon found a **Microsoft Excel spreadsheet** titled **"Non-Terrestrial Officers"** — explicitly meaning not Earth-based (not extraterrestrial, but non-terrestrial).

**Contents** (as best McKinnon remembers at ~800×600 monitor resolution, approximately 30–40 rows visible):

- **Tab 1 — Officers**: ~30–40 names in initial+surname format (no full first names remembered). Approximately 1.5–2 Excel pages worth.
- **Tab 2 — Ship Names**: Names not matching any [[us-navy|US Navy]] registry. McKinnon searched AltaVista (the dominant search engine pre-Google) and found no matches for any vessel. Not USS Lincoln, not any known seagoing ship.
- **Tab 3 — Materials / Transfers**: Fleet-to-fleet and ship-to-ship transfers of exotic chemicals including:
  - **Barium** (specifically barium titanate-adjacent compounds) — a high-K dielectric; [[thomas-townsend-brown|Thomas Townsend Brown]] used barium titanite in his experiments
  - **Strontium** — also a high-K dielectric; later connected to [[garry-nolan]]'s crash material analysis at Stanford
  - **Molybdenum** — primarily a strengthening alloy in metallurgy; possible radiation shielding properties

McKinnon downloaded the spreadsheet to his hard drive. When arrested, all his data was seized and taken to the **Office of [[naval-intelligence|Naval Intelligence]]** (ONI). He has tried to retrieve his hard drives for years; ONI's response: "It's still an ongoing investigation."

## Space-Based Manufacturing Hypothesis

Jesse and McKinnon develop a joint interpretation during the interview, which McKinnon calls "fresh and unique — I've never seen it connected in this way before":

**Core hypothesis**: The non-terrestrial officers are humans managing a **secret space-based materials manufacturing operation**. The exotic chemicals (barium, strontium, molybdenum) are not for propulsion directly — they are raw materials for **zero-gravity atomic layer deposition** manufacturing of exotic metamaterials that cannot be produced on Earth.

**Why zero-gravity manufacturing matters**:
- No convection, no settling, no buoyancy effects tearing materials apart
- Perfect liquid mixing; extraordinary crystal purity
- Optical fibers with almost no internal flaws
- CCTO (calcium copper titanate) and barium titanate — **high-K dielectrics** that store and discharge electromagnetic energy readily — can be grown to unprecedented purity in zero-g
- These are exactly the high-K dielectrics relevant to the [[biefeld-brown-effect]]: thrust in the B-B effect is proportional to the dielectric constant of the insulating material

**The self-reinforcing logic**: A secret space program manufacturing better high-K dielectrics in orbit would be improving the very propulsion technology it uses to maintain the program. "They're making their own program more powerful."

**Solar Warden disclaimer**: McKinnon explicitly states he never found anything to do with Solar Warden and considers it disinformation — an anonymous [[above-top-secret]] forum post used to "flood the zone" with adjacent-but-ridiculous claims that pre-immunize the public against the real story.

Historical context: The **[[manned-orbiting-laboratory|Manned Orbiting Laboratory]]** (US Air Force, 1960s) was a real, declassified secret space program — 17 military astronauts selected, modified Gemini capsule, Titan 3 rockets, polar orbit from Vandenberg, cancelled June 1969 (one month before Apollo 11). "Cancellation doesn't mean disappearance — programs are restructured, personnel are reassigned, infrastructure is absorbed into deeper compartments."

## Bifield-Brown Experiments and Thane Heins

McKinnon has been researching the **Bifield-Brown effect** since 2007 and is conducting his own experiments:

**Current setup** (planned completion by April of the recording year):
- **Material**: CCTO (calcium copper titanate) — 40mm discs, heated to 1,000°C in a gas furnace then electric furnace
- **Press**: 10-ton hydraulic press to form the green body
- **Plates**: High-quality silver paste
- **Problem**: At 30kV+, arcing/sparking between plates; McKinnon researching solutions (smaller discs on top to reduce arcing; may need oil submersion)
- **Vacuum chamber**: Plans to test in vacuum to exclude ion-flow objections

**Army Research Laboratory** found the thrust in B-B lifters was at least 10–20% above what can be explained by ion flow — exactly what T.T. Brown claimed. The DIY "lifter" (balsa wood + aluminum foil) thrust cannot be aerodynamically explained by ionized air flow.

**Gravitech** (Henrik, different name now): Has conducted vacuum chamber experiments (proper vacuum, minus hundreds of torr) confirming the effect. Now works on private satellites.

**Jean-Louis Naudin** (French experimenter): Has done B-B experiments in vacuum tubes with isolated electrodes.

**[[paul-laviolette|Paul LaViolette]]**: Wrote a history of anti-gravity; proposed the B2 stealth bomber uses B-B effect on leading/trailing edges (charged to millions of volts positive → negative). McKinnon's interpretation: this probably just manipulates airflow (electrohydrodynamics), not full electrogravitic. However, UK aerospace journalist **William Gunston** wrote he would "refrain from talking about leading edges charged to millions of volts positive followed by trailing edges charged to millions of volts negative because I don't want to end up in the Tower of London" — and then added caveats.

**[[thane-heins|Thane Heins]]** (Canadian physicist): Found a way to reverse Lenz's law drag in electric motors by increasing coil inductance to delay the counter-EMF — ultimately so delayed that the incoming and outgoing magnet is both accelerated rather than opposed. McKinnon replicated a simple version: when his coil powers a load, rotation speed **increases** and input current goes **down** — the opposite of standard motor-generator theory. McKinnon says: "The one and only thing I've seen and been able to replicate that works in terms of free energy." Heins has contracts with Siemens/Philips; was in contact with China.

## Arrest, Extradition Battle, and Legal Aftermath

**Arrest (March 2002)**: NHTCU officers came at 8am while McKinnon was asleep after an all-night gaming session. Warrant was for the whole house; McKinnon, his girlfriend, and his girlfriend's aunt's 12-year-old daughter were all detained. Initial UK advice: 6 months community service under the Computer Misuse Act.

**Tone shift**: NHTCU officers visited ONI and other top brass in Washington. When they returned, the approach changed entirely. US charges: 7 counts × 10 years = **70 years**. DOJ sought extradition under **Military Order #1** — Guantanamo-equivalent conditions: no media coverage, no family visits, total secrecy.

**Ed Gibson** (US embassy attaché in London) told McKinnon's lawyer Karen: **"We want to see him fry."** (Electric chair reference.)

**The extradition treaty**: McKinnon's lawyers obtained a draft of the new 2003 UK-US extradition treaty, and it was written in **American English** (unusual for a British legal document) with language almost verbatim from McKinnon's specific charges. McKinnon believes he was used as a pretext to create a broader extradition power.

**2008**: After years of lost appeals, McKinnon purchased **potassium chloride** — one of the three chemicals in lethal injection — and calculated the dose per kilogram of body weight. He intended to swallow it to cause a heart attack rather than face extradition.

**2012**: Home Secretary **[[theresa-may|Theresa May]]** blocked extradition: "Mr. McKinnon's extradition would give rise to such a high risk of him ending his life that a decision to extradite would be incompatible with Mr. McKinnon's human rights."

McKinnon remains on the **Interpol red list** and cannot enter the United States.

His Asperger's diagnosis (2008–2009) gave authorities a "mentally ill" category to file him in, McKinnon believes. He was embraced by UK public figures: recorded in the same studio as **Chrissie Hynde** and **Bob Geldof**; **David Gilmore** and **Crosby Stills & Nash** mentioned him publicly.

## Verichip Implant Incident

In **2006**: McKinnon and his girlfriend were asleep in their first-floor bedsit. He woke suddenly from deep sleep at 2–3am with a sharp pain in his left heel — then immediately fell back asleep. In the morning, he found:

- **Two perfectly circular holes**, ~5mm each, in his left heel
- One still had a **skin flap** hanging from it, like a hole punch
- The holes were on the first floor — no rodent access
- No conventional explanation possible

Years later: McKinnon discovered **VeriChip** (not VeriSign — he initially misremembers the name) and its **double-injection implant process** — two simultaneous injections that would produce exactly this wound pattern. 

Corporate lineage: VeriChip → Applied Digital Solutions → **Raytheon subsidiary**. The chip itself: a grain-of-rice-sized RFID device that transmits a unique ID when scanned, linking to a database. By the late 2000s, VeriChip had secured rights to technology allowing chips to detect viruses from inside the body, including H1N1 strain — monitoring physiology, not just identity.

Today: McKinnon has **two bumps** in his left heel that have since migrated toward the arch of his foot. He has done RF and EM scanning but found nothing definitive. He wants to do updated scanning before considering surgical removal. Laments [[roger-leir]] (implant researcher) has died and is unavailable.

McKinnon's theory: **government tracking** during his extradition battle — not aliens.
