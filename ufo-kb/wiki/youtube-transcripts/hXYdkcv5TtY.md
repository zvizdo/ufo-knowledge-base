# UFOs, Synchronicities & Prophetic Dreams (Ft. Eric Wargo)

In-person [[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] interview recorded in Washington DC. [[jesse-michaels]] hosts [[eric-wargo]], author of *Time Loops*, *Precognitive Dreamwork and the Long Self*, and *From Nowhere*. The conversation covers Wargo's physics-grounded model of precognition, its implications for art and creativity, the [[stargate-program|Stargate program]], UFOs as time-traveling humans, and tangential topics from the Penrose-Hameroff microtubule model to Philip K. Dick's convergence with [[jacques-vallee|Jacques Vallée]].

## Headline Claims

- **Self-fulfilling prophecy is physically necessary**: in a block universe where future events are fixed, any backward flow of information cannot change history — precognition can only produce time loops in which awareness of the prophecy causes the actions that fulfill it (Oedipus, Jung's scarab beetle).
- **The Freudian unconscious is a ready-made precognition model**: flip the iceberg sideways — the "submerged" part is not repressed past but unknown future, manifesting obliquely through dreams, slips, symptoms, and creative work.
- **Remote viewing is precognition, not clairvoyance**: viewers are perceiving the future confirmation moment; feedback is not merely motivational but constitutive of the effect — [[hal-puthoff]] found this consistent with his understanding of [[stargate-program]] mechanics.
- **[[ed-may|Ed May]]'s decision augmentation theory**: micro-PK effects may be explained not by mind-over-matter but by researchers' unconscious precognition guiding them to start trials at moments when random outcomes will confirm the hypothesis — potentially implying massive confounds in all experimental parapsychology and possibly beyond.
- **Quantum computers as temporal communicators**: a quantum server left plugged in for years/decades could theoretically send information backward to its own start-up moment; this is the "Tesseract" — the intermediate technology toward physical time travel and the key link to the UFO phenomenon.
- **UFOs as time-traveling humans is strongest hypothesis**: Wargo endorses [[michael-p-masters]]'s Extratemporalis model as the most plausible core explanation for genuine UAP.
- **Philip K. Dick and Jacques Vallée independently precognized each other's work in 1974–75**, producing structurally identical manuscripts (*Radio Free Albemuth* / *The Invisible College*) without access to each other's drafts.

## Open Questions

- Can the confirmatory feedback model of remote viewing be experimentally distinguished from clairvoyance using double-blind protocols with permanently withheld feedback?
- Does decision augmentation (researcher precognition guiding trial start-times) partially explain the replication crisis in psychology and health sciences?
- Are the microtubule suprradiance results (Hameroff's team) sufficient to confirm quantum coherence at the scale required for temporal non-locality?
- Is the helicoidal EM wave resonance with DNA ([[dave-rossi|Dave Rossi]] / [[constantine-meyl|Constantine Meyl]]'s work) a genuine information-transfer mechanism or artifact?
- What is the relationship between Phil Dick's *VALIS* "teaching satellite" concept and the UAP control-system hypothesis?
- Could Philip K. Dick's Metz speech claim — that *Man in the High Castle* depicted a real timeline that was "rewound" by simulators — be interpreted as genuine precognitive knowledge of the branching-timeline structure rather than psychosis?
- Morgan Robertson wrote *Futility* about the SS Titan 14 years before Titanic. Did Robertson show any subsequent behavioral changes or statements suggesting conscious awareness of his own precognitive authorship?
- Has Ed May's decision augmentation theory been applied retrospectively to any specific anomalous experimental result in biology or health science to quantify the potential confound?
- Wargo claims Dick and Vallée independently wrote structurally identical manuscripts in 1974–75 without seeing each other's work. Are there dated drafts or correspondence confirming zero cross-contact between them?
- Jesse's Paris/Selene story involves apparently precognizing a conversation days before it occurred. Does Jesse have contemporaneous written records (diary, texts) predating the event that could document the precognition?
- Wargo's "Tesseract" model predicts quantum computers will exhibit anomalous backward-temporal communication. Have any documented anomalies from early NISQ-era quantum computing (IBM, Google) been assessed against this prediction?
- Joe McMoneagle's Legion of Merit cites 200+ intelligence contributions from remote viewing. Are any of these specific predictions or retrievals now declassified and independently verifiable?
- The Die Glocke's reported association with Walter Gerlach — does any archival record (correspondence, SS project files, Kammler papers) place Gerlach at the sites described in Die Glocke accounts?
- [[rupert-sheldrake|Rupert Sheldrake]]'s pre-cognitive body-response experiments (earthquakes, approaching parents) — have these been independently replicated in peer-reviewed settings with adequate blinding?
- Wargo describes the "long self" as a single temporal entity extending through an artist's entire creative output. Has any systematic analysis been done on a known precognitive artist (Morrison, Lennon, Hendrix) to map specific works against later life events?
