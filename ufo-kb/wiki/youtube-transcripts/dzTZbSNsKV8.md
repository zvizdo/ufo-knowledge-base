# "These Are Parts Of an Alien UFO!" — Stanford Scientist Garry Nolan

[[garry-nolan]] in conversation with [[jesse-michaels]] at the Nolan Research Lab, Stanford. One of Nolan's earliest American Alchemy appearances and the only one that ends with physical UAP material samples on camera. Two-part structure: first the wide-ranging conversation, then a follow-up visit where Nolan produces the physical samples.

## Headline Claims

- **Anomalous isotope ratios**: The Ubatuba, Brazil magnesium fragments have magnesium isotope ratios that are "not even close to natural" — a finding from [[nanosims]] mass spectrometry analysis. The ratios cannot be produced without expensive centrifuge isotope separation; no known motivation for a hoaxer.
- **Inhomogeneous bismuth-magnesium piece**: A second sample (allegedly from the same [[1957-ubatuba-orb]] event) has correct Earth isotope ratios — but the material itself is an inhomogeneous bismuth-magnesium mixture with "no good reason" to exist in known metallurgy.
- **Arts Parts terahertz**: [[hal-puthoff]] confirms the bismuth-magnesium layered material (allegedly Roswell, sent by an anonymous army officer claiming to have found it in grandfather's diary) reduces terahertz microwave guide size by ~1/30th of the wavelength, enabling 30 waveguides in the volume of one.
- **CIA knocked on Nolan's door** to study people who claimed UAP contact; they showed him MRIs of DoD/intelligence personnel with UAP interaction histories. He found enlarged/denser caudate-putamen region in all of them — and in their family members, confirming a genetic component.
- **Sensory organs are reductive, not productive**: Nolan's framing — the brain is normally in a state of near-omniscience; senses are filters. The enlarged caudate-putamen may widen the filter slightly, allowing perception of phenomena others cannot register.
- **Schizophrenia as broken transmitter**: The caudate-putamen area is damaged in both autism and schizophrenia; schizophrenics may not be hallucinating but perceiving real phenomena they cannot switch off. Nolan is researching both groups.
- **"Angry monkeys" theory**: From NHI perspective (if they are ~1 million years ahead), humans are a few hundred years from spreading in our galactic arm. NHI would rationally want to monitor a nuclear-capable species before they become a galactic neighbor.
