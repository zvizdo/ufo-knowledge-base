# He's Seen More UFO Evidence Than Anyone Alive (Ft. Jacques Vallée)

Long-form interview between [[jesse-michaels]] (host, *American Alchemy*) and [[jacques-vallee]] — French-American computer scientist, UFO researcher, primary articulator of the [[interdimensional-hypothesis]] and the [[control-system-hypothesis]]. One of the most substantive Vallée interviews in the podcast corpus. He discusses his new book [[trinity-best-kept-secret]], the Lazar/MK-Ultra hint, the science-spirit Enlightenment schism, the [[invisible-college]], intermittent reinforcement as a control mechanism, hyperdimensionality, McCarthy's Bureau of Simulation parable, and the nature of reality. Spielberg's UFO scientist character in *Close Encounters* (played by François Truffaut) was based on Vallée.

## Headline Claims

1. **Trinity crash, 1945 — pre-dates Roswell by two years and pre-dates the term "flying saucer."** A metallic avocado-shaped craft crashed in San Antonio, New Mexico, **20 days after the first atomic test** and **two days after Japan's surrender**. One panel was missing; the body was otherwise intact and structurally rigid. Two child witnesses — [[remy-baca]] and [[jose-padilla]], ages 7 and 9, sons of local cattle ranchers — saw three humanoid creatures inside through the missing panel. The creatures were ~3 ft tall, had two eyes / two arms / three fingers / two legs, and "moved without moving their legs" (translation: glided). **Remy was crying and refused to enter** — "I no way you know I'm going in into that thing." Jose did go in; the floor was flat, suggesting an engine compartment underneath. The craft was "another cattle" (i.e. similar to a Roswell-style oval). Kenneth Arnold did not coin "flying saucer" until **1947**, two years after Trinity — meaning the public lexicon for "saucer" did not yet exist when this happened. This is one of [[trinity-best-kept-secret]]'s structural arguments: the UFO phenomenon precedes the modern public discourse, and Roswell is a sequel rather than the origin.

2. **Bob Lazar as a probable MK-Ultra subject — the "peculiar liquid" detail.** In Vallée's *[[revelations-alien-contact]]* (page 9), Vallée writes: "Robert Lazar also told me of his strange memory lapses, of the **peculiar liquid he was made to drink**." Vallée frames this disclosure — made privately to him — as evidence that Lazar's S4-saucer memories may be the product of [[mk-ultra]]-style induction rather than genuine reverse-engineering employment. The "peculiar liquid" detail is the operative anchor: induced amnesia + chemical administration is a documented MK-Ultra protocol; spontaneous recovered "S4" memories are consistent with that protocol's outcomes. This puts Vallée at odds with the bulk of the Lazar-believing community and is one of his most controversial heterodox claims.

3. **The Wilson–Davis memo — black-budget Aerospace Corp reverse-engineering UFOs without official oversight.** Vallée invokes the leaked [[wilson-davis-memo]], which describes a meeting between Director of Intelligence J2 **Admiral [[thomas-wilson]]** and physicist / propulsion expert **[[eric-davis]]**. Wilson expresses "complete exasperation" upon discovering a **black-budget private aerospace corporation** attempting to reverse-engineer crashed UFOs without government oversight. The custodians believe the material is non-terrestrial; "the process of doing anything productive with the material has been slow and cumbersome with little to no success since Roswell." Vallée corroborates the memo's compartmentalization picture: "no one has the whole puzzle or an image of the whole puzzle, but they have pieces — they have corners of the puzzle. The whole thing was set up to be that way on purpose." This dovetails with [[chris-mellon]]'s reported attempt to meet a UFO-program insider at "one of these aerospace companies" who **died of a heart attack two weeks before the meeting**.

4. **DOE/AEC custodianship — a separate clearance track that even top-secret-cleared officials don't see.** When Vallée and [[paola-harris]] inquired about the Trinity crash, **no one with above-top-secret clearances had heard of it**. Their conjecture: Trinity, occurring during the [[manhattan-project]], would have been logged with the project, then transitioned to the [[atomic-energy-commission]], then to the **Department of Energy**, which "has its own line of clearances." This is the structural reason Pentagon top-secret holders are blind: they are not **briefed on atomic secrets**, so they don't get the clearances needed to access UFO material that lives in the atomic-clearance lineage. Vallée: "Would Oppenheimer have been told?" — open question.

5. **Philip Corso's reverse-engineering tech list.** Vallée discusses [[philip-corso]] — staff of Eisenhower's National Security Council, later chief of the Pentagon's foreign technology desk in Army research and development — and *[[day-after-roswell]]*. Corso claims a covert group under Admiral [[roscoe-hillenkoetter]] (first director of the CIA) collected all off-planet technology and Roswell debris, and that reverse-engineering of Roswell parts indirectly led to: **fiber optics, lasers, integrated circuits, and Kevlar**. Corso also claims he was personally part of the Roswell cleanup crew. Vallée doesn't fully endorse the specific causal chain but treats Corso as a high-credibility witness: "he was definitely in high government circles, as corroborated by many colleagues."

6. **Vallée's France transistor-circuit anecdote — UFO debris as an artifact "from the Devil."** Vallée recounts working with the first IBM computer in France: when a card failed, the repair tech would identify the bad card and discard it intact — "we had a trash can full of these wonderful little complete cards with complete circuits." Vallée took one to lunch with a priest friend (Father Michel): "I told him: you are my priest, I respect you very highly, and I have found something in my field that I cannot recognize. Can you tell me what I should do with it?" The priest looked at it and replied: "**My son, burn it. It is from the Devil.**" Vallée's analogy: this is what an ordinary observer would do when confronted with UFO material — refuse to engage with what cannot be classified within an existing worldview. The anecdote reframes the "Devil" response as a class of cognitive defense, not as a religious claim.

7. **McCarthy's [[bureau-of-simulation|Bureau of Simulation]] parable.** Vallée recounts a story from **[[john-mccarthy-ai]]** (Stanford AI lab founder; one of AI's namers): a man receives a letter from "the Bureau of Simulation" stating that in the last six months, his behavior "has not done anything that was not predicted by the model." If he doesn't deviate within six months, **"you will be replaced by a simulated human."** The man panics, drops his job, climbs the Himalayas, meditates. Six months later: a second letter — "you still haven't done anything that wasn't predicted by the simulation model — and you have been replaced." He's still going to work, driving his car, but he doesn't exist anymore — he's an NPC. Vallée connects this to UFO behavior: NHI act in deliberately absurd ways (11 F-18s chasing one craft, contradicting witnesses' time and location) to break the predictive model — to introduce signal that *can't* be simulated.

8. **Intermittent reinforcement as the control-system mechanism (BF Skinner).** Consistent reinforcement produces fast learning that quickly extinguishes when the reward stops. **Intermittent reinforcement** produces slower but more durable behavior change. Applied at civilizational scale, sporadic and absurd UFO encounters may be the optimal signal pattern to co-direct human development. "If you do it every time you get fast learning, but then they go back to the way they were before." NHI don't land on the White House lawn because consistent disclosure would extinguish behavior change; intermittent absurdity sustains it.

9. **Hyperdimensional "bigger inside than outside."** Vallée recounts a San Jose witness whose disc appeared "the same size as her house" — but inside, the staircase led up the side of a "big round room" the size of a movie theater. Vallée invokes [[edwin-abbott|Abbott]]'s *[[flatland-abbott|Flatland]]* (1884): a 3D object intersecting a 2D plane appears as a sliver. A 4D hypercube intersecting our 3D space would appear much larger inside than its visible cross-section. He cites Carl Sagan's tesseract demonstration. The model: UFOs are intersections of higher-dimensional objects with our 3D plane.

10. **Noble myth / cultural filter — Betty and Barney Hill's Outer Limits aliens.** [[betty-hill]] and [[barney-hill]]'s 1961 abduction account included aliens with **"eyes around the rim of their heads"** — exactly matching the alien design from the CBS show *Outer Limits*, which premiered **11 days before their abduction**. Vallée's reading: not that they fabricated the story, but that they encountered something incomprehensible and unconsciously cast it through the closest available cultural meme. Medieval witnesses produced demons and fairies; biblical witnesses produced angels; post-1947 witnesses produce space aliens. The substrate is the same; the costume is the era's.

11. **Project Rulebook — an alleged classified Air Force project.** Vallée cryptically: "Project rule book was the best I could do at the time because I felt they had a real project going on that was secret." He identified it from open sources but provides no further detail. Listed as an open question.

12. **The science-spirit schism as Vallée's actual stake.** Vallée's [[invisible-college]] (named after Robert Boyle's 17th-century Rosicrucian-influenced natural philosophers, who became the Royal Society) is an attempt to re-merge science and spirit, bifurcated since the Enlightenment. UFO research, because it forces empirical inquiry into metaphysical territory, is uniquely positioned to bridge the divide. Vallée explicitly resists "UFOs as religion" framing — "you have the science approach where you try to find equations… but there is another level which is when you go out at night in the desert and see the Galaxy spread in front of you, you don't think of equations."

## Specific Anecdotes & First-Hand Material

- **Vallée's age-15 sighting**: an object in the field outside the room over the yard. His response: "I would be ashamed of Being Human, with the equipment that I'm given… if I didn't try to find out — at the minimum acknowledge that it was there." This is his framing for why he became a UFO researcher.
- **Mr. PK Tetto / biological UFO sensors**: Jesse Michaels mentions [[jeffrey-mishlove]]'s recent interview with a "Mr. PK Tetto and Tedo" who claims to be a biological sensor of UFOs — and [[chris-bledsoe]] in North Carolina. Vallée raises [[j-allen-hynek]]'s concern about repeat-witness phenomena: people who keep seeing UFOs the way some people get hit by lightning twice or win the lottery three times. Vallée: "we have people like that on the [[skinwalker-ranch|ranch]]" — trained Army officers who went to the ranch a few days, went home, "and boom — there was some phenomenon following them."
- **Diana Pasulka's hierophanies**: positive religious-conversion experiences vs. demonic-trickster encounters with false information. [[diana-pasulka]]'s framework. Vallée: "from what I've read, people perceive it as bad, they want to be rid of it… they are bothered by things in their home, including things that cause trouble."
- **Eric Davis on space-time as illusion**: Vallée relays Davis's physics position — "those dimensions like space and time, they're arbitrary. There's something deeper that creates the illusion of space and time in humans." Reality is "really quantum foam" and "not even obvious that we can stop it long enough to look at it."
- **Witness account: NHI contradicts time and location**: A forester encountered a disc; the entity asked the time, was given the time, replied "you're wrong, it's 2:30." Then asked: "Am I in Italy or Germany?" Witness: "you're in Alsace, in France." The entity went back inside and the craft took off. Vallée's reading: at one level, the witness was dreaming. At another: "you're wrong about time, you're wrong about space — you don't know where you are, you don't know what time it is. In fact, you don't know what time *is*."
- **Templar time-travel theory and Future-Humans hypothesis**: Vallée is asked about [[michael-p-masters]]'s thesis (UFOs = humans from the future). Vallée: "the idea that they are from the future is one idea. There's an idea that's more radical: this is all a simulation. It doesn't matter when, if we are in a simulation."

## Open Questions Raised

- What is "Project Rulebook"? Vallée implies he identified it from open sources but provides no detail.
- Does Oppenheimer have documentary knowledge of the Trinity crash? Vallée and Harris speculate yes, given the Manhattan Project → AEC → DOE custody chain — but no document confirms it.
- Is the "peculiar liquid" Lazar described to Vallée corroborated anywhere else in Lazar's public record? Vallée treats this as a private disclosure.
- Does the separate DOE/AEC clearance chain for Trinity connect to the same lineage that produced the Wilson–Davis compartmentalization? Both Mellon and Vallée point to DOE custody but neither has the full picture.
- Who was the "aerospace company UFO insider" Mellon was about to meet when he died of a heart attack? Identified only as "a fellow… in one of these aerospace companies."
- Does Corso's specific tech-list claim (fiber optics → lasers → ICs → Kevlar all from Roswell) have any documentary backing beyond *Day After Roswell*?
