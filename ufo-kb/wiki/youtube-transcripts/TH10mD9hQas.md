# How I Know David Grusch Is Not Lying…

Solo video essay by [[jesse-michaels]] presenting six interlocking arguments for why [[david-grusch]]'s UAP whistleblower testimony is credible, framed as a rebuttal to mainstream media dismissal (notably the *Washington Post*). No interview guest — Jesse presents his own analysis.

## Headline Claims

1. **UFOs systematically appear at nuclear sites** — [[robert-hastings]]'s *[[ufos-and-nukes]]* documents 167 Q-cleared military witnesses; missileers are the most rigorously vetted personnel class in the US military (PRP-certified: must report taking ibuprofen), making their testimony uniquely hard to dismiss.
2. **Atomic secrecy was deliberately written as a trojan horse for UFO secrecy** — The [[atomic-energy-act-classification]] (1954) automatically classifies anything with radiological properties; Grusch identifies the same mechanism; [[david-grusch]] states in a car ride that "the Manhattan Project was basically the original UFO program."
3. **Past public-facing UFO debunkers were atomic insiders who likely knew the secret** — [[edward-condon]] wrote the **McMahon Secrecy Act of 1946** (the AEC's legal precursor), helped site [[los-alamos]], and wrote the **Los Alamos Primer**; FBI agent [[guy-hottel]] was simultaneously building a blackmail dossier on Condon while himself writing the [[hottel-memo]] (1952) describing a crashed UFO with alien crew — the most-read document on the FBI website.
4. **Townsend Brown's trajectory is inexplicable without classified program involvement** — Navy fired [[thomas-townsend-brown]] in 1942 without cause; he reappears at Martin Vega Corporation the year before [[lockheed-skunk-works]] is formed; Lockheed's response to Grusch's testimony was notably non-denial: "questions about UAPs are best answered by the federal government."
5. **Wright-Patterson as anti-gravity research center** — [[wright-patterson]] becomes a classified anti-gravity hub in the 1950s; politicians who inquired were shut down hard (Barry Goldwater / Curtis LeMay exchange).
6. **JFK's assassination may connect to his UFO inquiry** — [[jfk-letter-mccone]] is dated November 12, 1963 (10 days before the assassination), asking for access to "unknowns in space"; [[e-howard-hunt]] claimed posthumously that JFK's death was partly tied to his UAP inquiries.
