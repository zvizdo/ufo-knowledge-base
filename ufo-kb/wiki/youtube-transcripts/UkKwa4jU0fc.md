# "My Hospital Operated On An Alien!" — Top Surgeon [Exclusive Interview]

This [[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] episode is the most detailed first-person witness testimony on the [[1996-varginha]] incident assembled in one interview. [[jesse-michaels|Jesse Michaels]] and guest co-host [[michael-shellenberger]] interview three witnesses that [[james-fox]] brought to the United States: crash eyewitness [[carlos-de-souza]], neurosurgeon [[italo-venturelli]], and forensic physician [[armando-fortunato]]. [[hal-puthoff]] is also present and engages with Dr. Fortunato about bacteria analysis and the crash site.

## Segment 1 — Carlos de Souza: The Crash Witness

[[carlos-de-souza]] was driving toward Varginha for an ultralight pilot course graduation on a morning in January 1996 when he spotted a cylindrical, metallic, light-reflecting object losing altitude rapidly:
- Tic-tac shape; described as the size of a school bus / mini bus
- Initially assumed it was a blimp or aircraft in distress and followed it
- The craft was smoking, reflecting light, and clearly losing control

### The Crash Site

The object came down and crashed into a tree on the outskirts of Varginha:
- The rear section was destroyed, with debris scattered around
- The front section slid forward and struck a tree; the craft did not explode
- Smell was ammonia and rotten eggs — emphatically not aviation or rocket fuel
- The exterior was metallic; de Souza observed a bluish interior through the damaged hull
- The road near the crash site leads directly to EZA (Escola de Sargentos das Armas, the Brazilian Army Sergeant School)

A military convoy arrived within approximately two minutes:
- Components: jeep, ambulance, two trucks with green tarps, soldiers
- Soldiers immediately deployed in a V-formation perimeter around the crash
- A soldier held a gun to de Souza's head and ordered him to leave
- The speed of arrival strongly implies they were already tracking or following the craft

De Souza picked up a piece of debris before being ordered away:
- Described as metallic foil, far lighter than kitchen aluminum foil
- When crumpled in his hand, it returned to its original shape immediately (see [[memory-metal]])
- The soldier knocked it from his hand; de Souza did not retain the sample

### Post-Crash Intimidation (Men in Black)

Ten to fifteen minutes after leaving the crash site, at a roadside café, two tall men in black suits approached de Souza:
- They arrived in a black car with no license plate
- They recited his wife's full name, home address, and business name without being asked
- They stated: "You didn't see anything. Keep quiet or you'll have serious problems for the rest of your life"
- This occurred before any networked database lookup was plausible in rural Brazil in 1996 — no cell phones, no internet
- See [[men-in-black]]: de Souza's encounter is among the most temporally precise on record

Subsequent surveillance and harassment over following years:
- Phone line tapped (audible clicks and interference)
- Business subjected to repeated extended tax and regulatory audits
- Unmarked cars stationed outside his company for months at a time

### UFO Researcher Contact and Physical Evidence

Eight or nine months later, when Varginha creature sightings entered Brazilian media, de Souza contacted researcher Claudio Voto (also referred to as Claudia Kovu; now deceased):
- Voto visited the crash site with de Souza, filmed it on Super 8 camera, collected soil and tree samples
- Lab analysis of the samples: unknown chemical products caused the burning of the tree and soil
- Voto's widow and daughter (a psychologist) hold portions of the archive
- Voto later leaked the interview footage to TV Globo's Fantástico program, which broadcast it with a mocking editorial framing

Physical evidence at the crash site persisted for decades:
- The burnt tree remained unchanged for 26 years
- An anomalous vegetation circle (nothing growing inside a perimeter ring) lasted 26 years
- Drone footage shot by [[james-fox|James Fox]] with de Souza showed distinctly different soil coloration still visible
- In 2022–2023, shortly after Fox's Moment of Contact documentary aired, 7–8 meters of earth was bulldozed at the crash site — nominally to "plant soybeans" — at no rational agricultural gain given the site dimensions

### Hypnotic Regression Session

De Souza later underwent hypnotic regression with therapist "Saga" (a specialist), which was recorded:
- He had no conscious memory of the session content
- On the recording, he was apparently in mental contact with a being from the craft
- The beings communicated: "Calm down. We're fine. Thank you for your concern. We want to thank you for worrying. You can go. Stay calm."
- The beings stated they would ensure de Souza would not retain memory of this contact
- During the recorded session, de Souza became extremely distressed
- He credits the recording — not personal memory — as the source of this information
- See [[nhi-telepathic-communication]]

## Segment 2 — Dr. Italo Venturelli: The Neurosurgeon

[[italo-venturelli]] is the head neurosurgeon at [[hospital-regional-varginha]] and has practiced in Varginha since 1983. He came forward publicly only in 2023, when he was hospitalized with a pulmonary thrombosis and heart attack during an interview with [[james-fox|James Fox]] — believing he might die, he disclosed that he had seen the being directly, not only the video.

### How He Came to See the Being

On the night of January 20, 1996, after finishing surgery on a child with Crouzon syndrome, Venturelli returned to the hospital:
- The entrance was cordoned off; army trucks with green tarps were parked at the hospital door
- He entered on foot through the pedestrian area; had no car that night
- A TV Globo reporter (female, brunette, medium stature, brown dress) approached him with a microphone: "Did you operate on the ET?"
- He did not understand the question — he hadn't yet seen the being — and told her that was not how to speak about patients
- His younger colleague [[marcos-vinicius]] had called him in earlier to check his work (standard practice)

Dr. Venturelli saw the video before he saw the being directly:
- Marcos showed him a black-and-white video on a camera with a small screen, at the hospital
- The video: ~1.5–2 minutes, being with a larger teardrop skull lying on a hospital bed, calm, awake, no sign of suffering
- Surgical material visible on a tray: needle holder and forceps (indicating minor suturing, not major surgery)
- Venturelli initially said: "What is this? Could it be another Crouzon?" — then walked in to see the being in person

### Physical Description of the Being

Venturelli was approximately one meter from the being for a total of 3–4 minutes:
- Size and build of a 7-year-old child
- Very pale / white skin; no hair, no fur, no gender markers, no earrings or distinguishing ornaments
- Teardrop-shaped, elongated skull (slightly enlarged relative to the body)
- Lilac-colored, teardrop-shaped eyes; in the black-and-white video he could not see pupils
- Small mouth, very small neck
- Long arms; visible arm, forearm, hand, three fingers plus an opposable thumb
- No foul smell — only the hospital's antiseptic environment (in contrast to crash-site ammonia smell)
- Conscious and awake; not intubated; no IV drip; not restrained
- Positioned semi-upright in a standard neurological-assessment bed position
- One hand under the surgical drape, one hand outside

### The Being's Behavior and Gaze

The being's demeanor was the most emotionally impactful aspect for Venturelli:
- Appeared to have full awareness of its surroundings — "total control of the room"
- Analogy he uses: like an adult watching children play — calm, aware, not afraid
- Made deliberate eye contact with Venturelli: "like an angel looking at you as if I've known you for a long time"
- Looked up toward a window occasionally (appeared to be orienting itself spatially)
- The gaze communicated, to Venturelli: "I know what you're doing, but it's all right. Whatever you did to me, it's over now."
- He describes the look as "maternal" and "forgiveness-like" — non-judgmental, moving, freely given
- Time appeared to slow down for the entire encounter: "4 minutes that felt like an eternity"
- Venturelli becomes visibly emotional recounting this; cries on camera
- He states: "To this day, I still want to go back there to see if he's left yet" — the same attachment a doctor forms to a critical patient

### Marcos Vinicius's Procedure

Dr. [[marcos-vinicius]] performed a minor procedure on the being:
- Not major neurosurgery — the tray contained only basic suturing instruments
- Assessment: he placed a few stitches in the being's head, likely a simple laceration closure
- The setup was improvised; it was not a proper surgical theater
- Venturelli was called in only to assess whether the patient was stable, not to consult on the technique
- Marcos told him: "Look what I did. Come see if it's okay." — treated as a sign-off, not a detailed consultation
- Venturelli did not witness the procedure; the video showed the post-operative state

### The Missing Video

[[marcos-vinicius|Marcos Vinicius]] regularly showed the video to family, friends, and colleagues at his home for years after 1996:
- He stored it on a CD and displayed it on his home computer; people would come over and he would show watches, gadgets, and the alien video together
- His family saw it; fellow doctors saw it; it was considered an open secret in Varginha medical circles
- James Fox searched Marcos's house after his 2018 death and took his computer to a warehouse in São Paulo — the video was not found
- Venturelli believes it may still be in Marcos's house, in a personal cabinet where he kept his watch collection (Breitling and Omega watches, souvenirs, CDs)
- The house was robbed after Marcos's death; some watches were stolen; the thief may not have known what else they took
- James Fox offered $1 million to Marcos's widow Rosaline for the tape; she has not produced it

### 2023 Inconsistency Explained

In a 2023 interview with [[marco-leal|Marco Leal]], Venturelli told the interviewer he had only seen the video, not the being directly:
- He explains: he feared professional censure from the regional medical council (CRM), feared losing his private practice clientele, feared being labeled mentally ill
- Brazilian culture around medical discretion: doctors do not discuss patients publicly, like an HIV diagnosis
- He was hospitalized with pulmonary thrombosis and a heart attack when he finally told James Fox the full story
- Facing possible death, he decided disclosure was more important than professional protection
- He is explicit that both statements about the video and the being in person are true; the sequence was: video first, then being directly

### Aftermath and Ongoing Impact

- [[marcos-vinicius|Marcos Vinicius]] started chain-smoking after the experience, cigarette after cigarette; Venturelli argues with him repeatedly; Marcos dies in 2018 of cardiac complications (had multiple stents placed)
- Nurses at the hospital privately know the three girls who saw the being in town — those girls had to change schools due to bullying and social harassment; they never made money from the story
- The hospital has a commemorative plaque: "The path of the ET came here to this hospital, January 20th, 1996"
- Venturelli wrote a short story called "the universal chess game" as a way to explore how he might have examined the being had he had proper diagnostic tools — in the story, the being already knows all the chess moves

## Segment 3 — Dr. Armando Fortunato: The Autopsy

[[armando-fortunato]] is a forensic physician, public health physician, occupational physician, and lawyer in Varginha. He performed the official autopsy on [[marcos-charez]] — the military police soldier who captured one of the Varginha beings and died approximately two weeks later.

### Circumstances of the Autopsy

Fortunato received the body with no information about alien contact — the context given was routine malpractice investigation:
- The military police colonel requested the autopsy to investigate whether the battalion doctor had committed professional error
- A drainage procedure had been performed on Charez's left-armpit skin lesion (a boil / hydrodenitis) at the battalion's outpatient clinic
- Fortunato found only the drainage incision — no other lesions, no signs of external trauma
- Blood samples, liver tissue, stomach tissue, and lung tissue were collected and sent to pathologist Dr. [[jean-bachista-janini]]

### The Bacteria

Janini's laboratory findings, which became publicly significant only years later:
- Cause of death: pulmonary infection leading to septicemia (generalized infection)
- The responsible bacteria: an extremely rare staphylococcal strain that does exist on Earth
- Its virulence and lethal progression were unlike anything Janini had seen in 45–50 years of pathology work
- Charez died in approximately 12–14 days from symptom onset despite receiving antibiotics and anti-inflammatory treatment
- He visited 3–4 doctors complaining of lumbar/sciatic pain as the infection spread
- Dr. Cesario (cardiologist): "I threw the kitchen sink of antibiotics at it — nothing worked; I've never seen anything like it before or since"
- Janini describes it as an "evolution of a bacteria that he'd never seen on Earth" — same strain, unprecedented behavior

Janini's current assessment (he is nearly 90 years old):
- Submitted an authenticated signed document to this episode's recording that he does not rule out alien bacteria
- States: the bacteria may be of alien origin, or may represent "genetic engineering" — an artificially modified or highly evolved strain
- Has formally requested court authorization for exhumation of Charez's remains for DNA testing of the bacteria
- He was not able to attend the US interview due to health, but is still actively corresponding with Fox's team

### Charez's Contamination and Testimony to Family

Charez's sister (and reportedly Charez himself per Dr. Cesario) believed the infection came from alien contact:
- Charez held the being with bare hands during the capture
- The being was reportedly covered in a greasy, oily substance on its skin
- Charez began telling family members he believed he was dying because of this contact
- He was worried about what might happen from touching that material
- The military police doctor who performed the drainage was investigated for malpractice but fully acquitted — the infection's progression was aberrant, not caused by improper technique

### Varginha Zoo Deaths

A second contamination hypothesis involves the [[varginha-zoo|Varginha zoo]]:
- That same week, multiple animals at the [[varginha-zoo|Varginha zoo]] died of completely unknown causes
- James Fox: he interviewed the zoo director (described as a zoologist-level scientist, not just a keeper) on camera
- The director had never seen anything like the cause of death before or since
- Working theory: the being may have contacted zoo animals → picked up Earth bacteria → the bacteria then underwent rapid evolutionary alteration in the being's biology → transferred to Charez on capture
- Alternatively: the bacteria is genuinely alien and not derived from zoo animal contact at all
- [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]] expressed interest in collaborating on bacterial analysis; Fortunato extended a formal invitation to Puthoff and the American research community to assist with exhumation and analysis

### Dr. Jean Bachista Janini Profile

Janini is the senior pathologist to whom Fortunato sent Charez's samples and who produced the original autopsy report:
- Arrived in Varginha from Rio de Janeiro approximately 45–50 years ago
- University professor, forensic doctor, and diagnostic pathologist
- Founded the Propedéutic Institute diagnostic laboratory, which serves 90% of the region through the public health system (SUS) and over 100 cities
- Described by Fortunato as the "godfather" of medical science in the regional area — the reference authority for all regional doctors when facing difficult cases
- Not commercially motivated; Fortunato emphasizes his career is built on scientific exactitude
- Participated in James Fox's Moment of Contact documentary; James describes Janini's on-camera statement as powerful

## Brazilian Military Response and Suppression Architecture

The speed and sophistication of the Brazilian military response implies the craft was being actively tracked before it came down — and that an organized suppression program was already operational.

- **Two-minute response**: the military convoy (jeep, ambulance, two trucks, soldiers) arrived within approximately two minutes of the craft's impact; no emergency services response occurs this fast without prior tracking
- **V-formation perimeter**: soldiers immediately deployed in a V-formation perimeter around the crash site — a standard military security formation, not an emergency response improvisation
- **Gun to de Souza's head**: the immediate use of lethal-force threat against a civilian crash witness signals a pre-established protocol for witness suppression, not an improvised on-site decision
- **The EZA proximity**: the crash road leads directly to the Escola de Sargentos das Armas (Brazilian Army Sergeant School); the Army had institutional proximity to the site
- **The MIB timeline**: Men in Black appeared within 10–15 minutes of the crash and already had de Souza's wife's name, home address, and business name — impossible in 1996 rural Brazil without prior surveillance or a pre-established intelligence network
- **Years of harassment**: phone tapping (audible clicks), prolonged tax audits, unmarked cars stationed outside de Souza's business for months — a sustained suppression campaign, not a one-time intimidation
- **Joint US/Brazilian operation hypothesis**: the speed and coordination imply US intelligence involvement; [[james-fox|James Fox]] estimates a US/Brazilian military joint recovery operation based on the documentary evidence
- **The 2022–23 bulldozing**: the crash site was bulldozed (7–8 meters of earth removed) in the period shortly after Fox's Moment of Contact documentary aired — nominally to "plant soybeans" at a site with no rational agricultural use given the dimensions; timed suppression of physical evidence
- **The three town girls**: saw the being on the street; were forced to change schools due to bullying and social harassment sustained over years; never made money from the story; consistent testimony across 30 years — their treatment is consistent with institutionalized witness pressure, not organic social reaction
- **Fernando Jane** (now deceased, 2015): saw only the foot of the creature at the hospital emergency entrance before military ordered him away; the scope of the witness list implies a wider suppression operation than individual MIB encounters
- **The TV Globo mocking editorial**: Claudio Voto leaked de Souza's interview footage to Fantástico; the program aired it with a mocking editorial framing — consistent with a coordinated media-management response
- **Pattern conclusion**: The Varginha case exhibits every characteristic of a coordinated multi-layer suppression operation: pre-tracking, fast military response, civilian intimidation, ongoing surveillance, media management, and belated physical evidence destruction
