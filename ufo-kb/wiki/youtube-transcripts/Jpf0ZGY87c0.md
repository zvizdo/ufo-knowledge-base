# Did Henry Kissinger Oversee A UFO Crash Retrieval Program?

## Summary

This episode builds a circumstantial but detailed case that [[henry-kissinger]] occupied a central node in the US government's covert UAP management structure — possibly from the early 1950s through his final departure from official government in 1977, and arguably beyond. The argument proceeds through five overlapping threads.

---

## Thread 1: The Kingman Crash (1953) and Dr. Eric Wang

On 19 May 1953, during Operation Upshot-Knothole at the Nevada Test Site, a nuclear device nicknamed "Harry" detonated with unusual force. Shortly after, engineer [[arthur-stansel]] — a decorated WWII veteran and blast effects consultant — was covertly transported with 15 specialists by blacked-out bus to a site near [[kingman-arizona]]. He observed a 30-ft dull-metal craft (two fused saucers, no landing gear, no visible damage) buried 20 inches in the sand, with a small open hatch; inside a nearby tent lay several 4-ft humanoid figures.

Former intelligence official [[chris-mellon]] later confirmed the Kingman craft was recovered and studied over subsequent decades.

Stansel's superior at [[wright-patterson]] was **Dr. [[eric-wang]]**, head of the Special Studies Department — a Vienna-born engineer who left virtually no public record despite his name endowing a facility at the University of New Mexico. UFO researcher [[william-steinman]] eventually located Wang's widow, **Maria Wang**, who confirmed:
- Her husband worked on classified projects involving non-human technology.
- He reported directly to [[henry-kissinger]].
- Kissinger was deeply involved in the flying saucer program.
- Kissinger had visited their home on occasion.

In 1956, Wang's department was relocated from Wright-Patterson to [[sandia-national-laboratories|Sandia Laboratories]] at [[kirtland-air-force-base]]. A partially redacted Sandia history notes that **Don Carter** (later Nixon administration AEC adviser) organized interdisciplinary seminars at Kirtland around 1961–62 — one participant was [[henry-kissinger]].

## Thread 2: Kissinger in the Counter Intelligence Corps (CIC) at Oberammergau

Kissinger entered the US Army in WWII, was recruited into the [[counter-intelligence-corps]] (CIC), and distinguished himself managing denazification in Bensheim. In 1945 he was assigned to Oberammergau, Bavaria — precisely the location of a secret Messerschmidt weapons facility (jet planes, radio-controlled missiles) and the area where Werner von Braun and ~400 scientists had been ordered by SS General Hans Kammler to relocate.

[[victor-schauberger]] — alleged developer of anti-gravity propulsion — was interrogated by CIC agents for nine months in this same field of operations. Per Nick Cook's *The Hunt for Zero Point*, those agents considered his discoveries more important than Nazi nuclear research. CIC agent Neil Gershimer subsequently tricked Schauberger into signing over all his work to the US; it was deposited at [[brookhaven-national-lab]].

Jesse's argument: Kissinger, as a CIC officer in that exact region, would almost certainly have been peripherally aware of the Schauberger-type exotic propulsion intelligence being gathered.

## Thread 3: Harvard, the Psychological Strategy Board, and Gordon Gray

At Harvard, Kissinger moved in circles deeply tied to the intelligence community and the emerging UFO classification structure:

- **James Conant** (Harvard president 1933–55): Manhattan Project figure, central to atomic policy, close ally of Oppenheimer.
- **Donald Menzel**: Harvard's top astronomer, public UFO debunker, named in MJ12 documents as a Truman-era panelist; allegedly ordered destruction of Harvard observatory plates taken during the 1952 Washington DC UFO flap.
- **Lewis Branscombe**: PhD Harvard 1949; allegedly offered UFO committee chair Edward Condon a deal to bury the UFO question in exchange for security clearance reinstatement.

From 1951–55, Kissinger consulted directly for the [[psychological-strategy-board]] (PSB) and its successor, the Operations Coordinating Board, under **[[gordon-gray]]** — Eisenhower's National Security Adviser, University of North Carolina president, and a figure connected to both MJ12 documents (as named adviser) and to gravity research (the 1956 Chapel Hill Conference on gravity, sponsored by Wright Field, was sanctioned by Gray at UNC). The Chapel Hill Institute of Field Physics also funded [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s gravity manipulation experiments.

[[philip-corso]] was another PSB member — the same Corso who later claimed to have distributed alien technology to US industry.

## Thread 4: The Council on Foreign Relations, Nelson Rockefeller, and Edward Teller

In 1955 Kissinger directed a **[[council-on-foreign-relations]]** study group on nuclear strategy, briefed by [[j-robert-oppenheimer]], producing *Nuclear Weapons and Foreign Policy*. The study group's membership overlapped heavily with MJ12-named figures. **Richard Bissell** — CIA Deputy Director, Area 51 co-founder, and the man who privately told [[harold-malmgren]] he had been "briefed on otherworld technologies" — was a CFR study group colleague.

**[[nelson-rockefeller]]** recruited Kissinger to his Eisenhower advisory panel (first at Quantico) and made him director of the **Rockefeller Brothers Fund Special Studies Project**. That project included [[edward-teller]], who is named in an Australian intelligence document by Harry Turner as working on gravity control objectives. The classified output of the Rockefeller project remains locked in Rockefeller's archive center.

Detlev Bronk — president of the Rockefeller Foundation — is named in MJ12 documents as a Majestic 12 member.

## Thread 5: The Nixon White House, the 303 Committee, and Operation Moondust

During the Nixon administration (National Security Adviser 1969–75; Secretary of State 1973–77), Kissinger:
- Ran the NSC through unprecedented personal compartmentalization ("keep key decisions out of reach even of those charged with carrying them out" — *his own 1968 writing*).
- Oversaw the **[[303-committee]]**, the secret inter-agency body approving all CIA covert operations, which some researchers identify as interchangeable with MJ12-successor structures.
- Had [[harold-malmgren]] installed alongside him as a check — Malmgren, who had already seen recovered UAP material at [[los-alamos]] and been briefed by [[richard-bissell]] about the [[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown]].

The episode also surfaces **Operation Moondust** — the USAF Cold War program ostensibly for recovering foreign space debris, whose declassified documents reveal it tracked UAP recoveries including: spherical objects on New Zealand farmland; a Bolivian crash with anomalous aircraft; and a 1957 UAP event in Afghanistan sent to Wright-Patterson. In **January 1977**, just before leaving office, Kissinger sent a telegram to the US Embassy in Ottawa requesting a report on "possible returned space objects" — subject line: MOON DUST.

The Jackie Gleason story (Nixon shows him alien bodies at a classified facility in the 1970s, according to Gleason's ex-wife) is presented as circumstantial color.

## Thread 6: MJ12 Document Corroboration

A 1995 MJ12 leak describes a June 28, 1961, JFK request to CIA director [[allen-dulles]] for a review of MJ12 intelligence operations relative to psychological warfare — a date that the 2022 JFK files confirmed was an actual JFK-Dulles meeting day (impossible for a forger in 1995 to have known). Jesse uses this as evidence that the MJ12 document corpus, while mixed, contains authentic material. He frames Kissinger's career trajectory as placing him squarely within the network described by the verified portions.

**PI-40** is mentioned as a possible alternate name for MJ12's evolved successor structure, through which Kissinger allegedly maintained advisory involvement.
