# NASA & UFOs: Pagan Rituals, Secret Science & Time Travel

A solo deep-dive by [[jesse-michaels]] tracing the esoteric undercurrent of American and Soviet space programs from their founding to the present. The central argument: the history of rocket science is inseparable from mystical traditions, occult belief, and possible contact with non-human intelligence. Parsons, Von Braun, Tsiolkovsky, and [[tim-taylor|Tim Taylor]] form the four pillars.

## Headline Claims

- **[[jack-parsons|Jack Parsons]] as the occult founder of American rocketry**: Parsons co-founded JPL, developed the first JATO solid-fuel boosters, and simultaneously conducted elaborate sex-magic rituals — including the **Babalon Working** (1946) with [[l-ron-hubbard]] — explicitly aimed at contacting non-human intelligence. He believed he was guided by NHI and recited Crowley's Hymn to Pan before rocket tests.
- **NASA's power structure was Freemasons + ex-SS + occultists**: Multiple Apollo astronauts and senior NASA/NRO officials were 33rd-degree Scottish Rite Freemasons: [[buzz-aldrin]], [[john-glenn|John Glenn]], James Webb, Kenneth Kleinknecht, [[gus-grissom|Virgil Grissom]], [[edgar-mitchell|Edgar Mitchell]], and others. [[wernher-von-braun]] and many colleagues came through [[operation-paperclip]] with scrubbed Nazi/SS records.
- **Mission patches, launch timing, and launch pad numbering embed occult symbolism**: Apollo's Orion's-belt alignment, runway 33 at KSC, launch pad 33 at [[white-sands-missile-range|White Sands]], NROL-16 launched at 12:50 AM on Walpurgisnacht 2005 with a payload named Prometheus fired northeast toward the Big Dipper — matching a Necronomicon ritual template almost exactly.
- **Tim Taylor ([[tim-taylor|Tyler D]]) receives engineering "downloads" from NHI and claims involvement in a secret time travel program**: Tim Taylor is a real NASA Mission Controller documented by [[diana-pasulka]] in *American Cosmic*. He holds patents derived from these downloads and allegedly told Christopher Bledsoe's son he was part of a secret time travel program in [[nassau-bahamas|Nassau, Bahamas]] — linked to [[thomas-townsend-brown]].
- **[[george-ellery-hale|George Ellery Hale]] received creative inspiration from an "elf" or spirit and embedded Egyptian sun-worship into his Pasadena observatory**: Hale, builder of Mount Wilson Observatory, claimed mystical guidance. His Solar Laboratory in Pasadena includes a tribute to Akhenaten (the monotheist pharaoh who worshipped the sun) over its entrance. His obituaries called him "a priest of the sun."
- **Russian cosmism explicitly linked rocket science to NHI transmission**: [[konstantin-tsiolkovsky]] wrote that geniuses receive messages from "ethereal beings… existing beyond our dimensions" and that his rocket equation was received through cosmic transmission. [[nikolai-fyodorov]] (his teacher) believed humanity's task was to resurrect all the dead and colonize space.
- **[[herman-oberth|Herman Oberth]] publicly stated humanity has been helped by "the people of other worlds"**: In a 1954 lecture, Oberth said "We have been helped" — when asked by whom, replied "The people of the other worlds." He believed UFOs had monitored Earth's nuclear sites since at least the 1400s.
