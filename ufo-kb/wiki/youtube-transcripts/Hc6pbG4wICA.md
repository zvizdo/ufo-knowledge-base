# Joe Rogan Experience #1510 — George Knapp & Jeremy Corbell

Three-hour conversation with investigative journalist [[george-knapp]] and documentary filmmaker [[jeremy-corbell]], recorded July 17, 2020. Recorded shortly after Corbell's [[bob-lazar|Bob Lazar]] documentary amplified mainstream interest and one year after the 2019 Naval UAP video releases. First in-person meeting between Knapp and Rogan.

## Headline Claims

1. **Soviet 1978–1988 Military UFO Study** — [[colonel-borosokolov]], interviewed by Knapp in Russia, revealed the largest UAP study in history: every Soviet military unit worldwide was required to file UFO reports for 10 years. All reports funneled to one desk at the Ministry of Defense (held by Colonel Sokolop).
2. **Ukraine ICBM incident via Colonel Sokolop** — The Ministry of Defense desk officer (Colonel Sokolop) disclosed to Knapp that a giant UAP appeared over a Soviet ICBM base in Ukraine; missiles trained on US targets entered launch mode unprompted.
3. **[[1975-northern-tier-case|Northern Tier case 1975]]** — A string of US nuclear missile bases from Maine to Montana was visited by UAPs in 1974–75; in "a couple of cases they disabled the missiles"; launch control codes may have been interfered with. Scrambled fighter jets "left in the dust."
4. **AAWSAP shutdown — fundamentalist Christians** — DIA officials pulled AAWSAP funding not for scientific reasons but because they believed the phenomena were "demonic" — "they thought we might be invoking Satan in here." These were fundamentalist Christians in the Pentagon.
5. **AAWSAP DURD Papers — 2 unpublished** — AAWSAP commissioned 38 Defense Intelligence Reference Documents (DURDs) in 2009 establishing a baseline of human technology. 6 had leaked; Knapp brought 2 more unpublished papers to the JRE studio — Rogan was "the first person outside the program" other than Knapp to see them.
6. **Holmes & Narver stenographer threatened** — A defense contractor employee (Holmes & Narver) sat in on meetings between contractors and Air Force officials discussing crashed saucers; after speaking to Knapp, she was visited by two men who said: "We know you travel to LA to see your daughter, and we know she comes here. It's a big desert out there. It'd be terrible if something happened to either one of you." Six of Knapp's sources for Lazar background were visited by similar men, one after another.
7. **[[james-lacatski]] Skinwalker SCIF pipeline** — The DIA scientist who would co-found [[aawsap]] visited [[skinwalker-ranch]] with Bigelow; within 15–20 minutes, something appeared inside a house visible only to him; he returned to DC and briefed [[harry-reid]] and two senators in a SCIF, triggering the Congressional briefing series.
8. **GIMBAL — Boeing contractor confirmation** — Corbell spoke to a Boeing employee who serviced the ATFLIR pods; confirmed the craft is self-rotating "mechanically against the wind" — not a plane banking. "They all know it has to be a gravity-propelled program."
9. **Bob Lazar cloud chamber footage** — Corbell found the only known video of Lazar's gravity-wave experiment sandwiched between two Golden Girls episodes on VHS; Knapp witnessed the experiment live (light bending in the chamber). Lazar called the recovered clip "just the cool-down period."
10. **[[harry-reid|Harry Reid]] — Knapp told him in 1996** — Knapp was the first person to tell Reid about [[bob-lazar]] outside the KLAS-TV newsroom; knew Reid since he ran for Congress in 1982; Reid sat in on NIDS meetings in 1996 and "was kind of hooked." Reid did not want his interest publicized — "that does not help your re-election chances."
11. **Virginia coast 2015–2016 — 70–80 witnesses** — UAPs described as "beach balls with cubes inside of them" were seen for hours off the Virginia coast during 2015–2016; 70–80 pilot witnesses; appeared to "want to be seen" over training exercise areas.
12. **Lazar hypnosis and green liquid at S4** — Lazar admitted during interviews he "underwent some kind of hypnosis" at S4 and was made to drink "some weird green liquid" — Knapp suggests "probably LSD"; Lazar said he glimpsed something "that size" (alien?) but "I don't believe it was an alien, just a glimpse — maybe they're messing with my head."
13. **[[michio-kaku|Michio Kaku]] burden-of-evidence shift** — [[michio-kaku]] stated publicly the burden of evidence that UAP craft are NOT extraterrestrial is now on the scientific community.
14. **[[ariel-school|Ariel School]] 1994 — Corbell first-person** — Corbell personally met two witnesses: Salma and Liesel; 80 kids saw craft land; [[john-mack|John Mack]] brought in by BBC the next day.

## George Knapp's Origin Story (1987)

Knapp had no particular interest in UFOs before 1987:

- In 1987, [[john-lear]] (son of Learjet inventor [[bill-lear|Bill Lear]]; had helped Knapp's station KLAS-TV break the [[f-117-nighthawk|F-117]] stealth fighter story at Tonopah/[[area-51|Area 51]]) walked into the newsroom with "a stack of what turned out to be UFO documents."
- Knapp's boss Ned Day dismissed it: "If it was true, I'd already know about it." Knapp eavesdropped and took the material.
- Lear had run for the Nevada state senate and had credibility from the F-117 tip: "He had helped us break a really big story."
- Knapp: "It's always out there somewhere — 'hey, I wonder what the deal is on that' — but I had not really dwelled on it."

## Bob Lazar — Cloud Chamber, Footage Recovery, and the JRE #1315 Aftermath

**Cloud chamber experiment (Knapp's firsthand):**
- Lazar ran a cloud chamber experiment demonstrating the gravity-wave effect of the [[s4-reactor]] — the only time he removed the reactor from its housing.
- Knapp arrived as the experiment ended: "There was a laser that bent. It shouldn't be able to do that." The chamber bent light.
- Lazar built a big UV scanner in his garage for the demonstration. A friend brought Corbell a laser gun as a gift at the same event.
- The footage: Lazar's friends filmed the test so "his friends didn't think he was fucking crazy." It was never intended for public release.

**Footage recovery:**
- The footage disappeared into Knapp's archive. Corbell spent months searching Knapp's entire house.
- Found it sandwiched between two Golden Girls episodes on an old VHS tape. Recovered ~17–30 seconds of the cool-down phase.
- Lazar was unimpressed: "Jeremy, that doesn't show the cool part of the test — it's just the cool-down period." The clip is in Corbell's documentary.
- On-camera validation: Lazar filmed the cloud chamber test in the early 1990s with witnesses present; the tape itself is evidence "he believed he had element 115" at that time.

**JRE #1315 aftermath:**
- After the Lazar/Corbell episode (JRE #1315, June 2019), skeptical friends called Corbell: "Hey man, I'm starting to believe this is real."
- Corbell on Lazar's truthfulness: "I have been with Bob in his life, met everybody, his family. The closer you get to his inner circle, the more you understand that he's telling the truth. Never once have I found something where I'm like, you're a fucking liar."
- Lazar's migraine during JRE #1315: Corbell gave him McDonald's ("his wife was furious at me"). The stress was real — Lazar was "afraid he was going to be killed" at an earlier point; Knapp recorded a long interview with Lazar just to have it on record in case Lazar "suddenly disappeared."
- Corbell pre-screened Andrew Schulz (comedian friend) to watch Lazar at dinner the night before; Schulz's verdict: "I don't know — that guy's telling the truth."

**Lazar's S4 experience — hypnosis and green liquid:**
- Lazar admitted: "They underwent some kind of hypnosis." They made him drink "some weird green liquid — probably LSD" (Knapp's interpolation).
- Lazar: "I saw something that size [gestures]. I don't believe it was an alien. It was just a glimpse — maybe they're messing with my head. He also admits that they did mess with his head."
- Lazar's credibility argument: "This is not a guy who profited off of this story. He told this story for 31 years and didn't profit off of it."

**Element 115 validation:**
- Corbell: "In 1989 he was talking about element 115 — it wasn't even scientifically proven to exist until 2013. In 2013 they actually were able to create, for a brief moment, element 115. An insanely dense element that they believe is perhaps the propulsion system for this craft. Every time history writes itself right — Bob is [vindicated]."

## GIMBAL Video — Boeing Contractor and Gravity Propulsion

- Corbell spoke to a Boeing employee who serviced the ATFLIR pods that recorded the [[2015-gimbal|GIMBAL]] footage.
- The contractor's assessment: "That thing is self-rotating. It is a saucer-shaped thing that is rotating mechanically against the wind."
- Rogan observation: "I've talked with a dude at Boeing now — they all know this has to be a gravity-propelled program. We understand the science it takes to do it. We can't replicate it — with our material sciences, our best information at this time, we can't replicate this technology. But we do understand how it works, fundamentally."
- "Lazar is parallel with what we're seeing today" — the meta-materials discussion in 2020 mirrors what Lazar described in 1989.
- Of the three Pentagon UAP videos: Corbell considers GIMBAL and [[2004-nimitz-tic-tac|Tic-Tac]] the most compelling. GoFast: "gives us very little information."
- Knapp on the credibility argument: "Our government does not tell members of Congress — the Pentagon doesn't go to the [[senate-intelligence-committee|Senate Intelligence Committee]], show them those videos and say 'yeah, it's unidentified' if it isn't."

## Harry Reid — Knapp's 30-Year Relationship and the 1996 Skinwalker Briefing

- Knapp told Reid about [[bob-lazar]] before the 1989 stories aired — Reid was "the first person I told about Bob Lazar outside of our newsroom."
- Knapp had known Reid since Reid first ran for Congress in 1982; Reid later became Senate Majority Leader.
- In 1996, Knapp told Reid about [[skinwalker-ranch]] and NIDS meetings: "He said, 'Can I go?' So he showed up and sat in on the meetings — and he was kind of hooked."
- Reid "always had an interest" but "didn't want it to be known publicly — that does not help your re-election chances."
- In 2005, Knapp and [[colm-kelleher]] wrote [[hunt-for-the-skinwalker]]; NIDS had been on the property for 7–8 years by that point with hundreds of incidents documented.
- After the December 2017 NYT story: Reid's "phone had been ringing off the hook from other members of congress who are now suddenly interested" — this started the Capitol Hill briefing series.
- [[lue-elizondo]] and [[chris-mellon]] appeared on Tucker Carlson monthly. Knapp and Corbell believe Trump was briefed more than once because he watches Tucker Carlson.
- Trump on TV (interview with his son): "We're going to open Roswell." Knapp: "He's barely paying attention."

## Robert Bigelow — First Contact After 1989 Lazar Broadcast

- After the 1989 KLAS-TV Lazar broadcasts, "one of the first people to call me was a guy I'd never heard of before — named Robert Bigelow. A billionaire."
- Bigelow: "I was really impressed." He initially dismissed some high-strangeness cases (bigfoot, ghosts) as "too incredible" — discarded them.
- Bigelow then reached out to Lazar directly and met with him, "pumping him for information" — "that really amplified Bigelow's lifelong interest."
- Bigelow created [[nids]] (National Institute for Discovery Science): investigated all high-strangeness UFO cases. After the Skinwalker story emerged, he bought the ranch.
- Corbell's film *Hunt for the Skinwalker* was based on Knapp's book of the same name.

## Skinwalker Ranch — Scientific Investigation and AAWSAP Pipeline

**NIDS investigation (7–8 years):**
- NIDS approached the investigation scientifically: "They looked for prosaic explanations — gravity anomalies, psychoactive plants, could there be hallucinations, are the witnesses lying? They investigated the background of the family that had lived there and they found out it could not be explained."
- The [[uinta-basin]] (northeastern Utah) has always been a UAP hotspot — balls of light, orbs, structured craft day and night.
- A local science teacher named **Junior Hicks** collected hundreds of cases from locals for decades. In 1976, Dr. Frank Salisbury wrote a book about it: *The Utah UFO Display*.
- NIDS documented hundreds of incidents. "They didn't want to tell anybody about it — they shared the information with me, but you can't publish it. There's no real physical proof."
- Camera failures at the moment of activity were common — "that's the thing: they have cameras, and they fail when some giant disk shows up. Their car batteries would die in the same spot."

**Lacatski SCIF pipeline:**
- "This scientist [Lacatski] who sees it, he goes to Bigelow, they visit the property."
- Within 15–20 minutes: "Something appears inside a house that only he can see. He doesn't say anything about it."
- Returns to Washington; meets with [[harry-reid]] and two other US senators in a SCIF at the Capitol.
- Presents "a proposal for a study of UFOs and related phenomena — not just like ATIP looking at flying saucers that encounter US military, but something more expansive."
- Contract put out; Bigelow bids, wins; creates **[[bass|BASS]]** (Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies) with 50–60 employees; goes around the world, interacts with other governments, sends teams to hotspots.

## AAWSAP Shutdown — Fundamentalist Christians in the Pentagon

- After a couple of years, "somebody yanks the funding."
- The reason: "The DIA suddenly became worried about all this weirdness that was being reported at Skinwalker and elsewhere because they thought it was demonic. And they thought: if this comes out that we're studying this weird phenomena — a) it's going to end up on the front page of the New York Times (which it did), and b) we might be invoking Satan in here. These were fundamentalist Christians in the Pentagon who pulled the money because they thought it was the devil."
- After the shutdown: "The program — it now continues. They keep reporting that it's stopped. It hasn't stopped."
- AATIP (Elizondo's program): The Pentagon says it was ended; it was not — "it continued for a couple of years" after the NYT story.
- Post-NYT: AATIP got funding restored and a "structured organization"; Congress informed; they want to expand.

## AAWSAP 38 DURD Papers — Two Unpublished

- AAWSAP commissioned **38 Defense Intelligence Reference Documents (DURDs)** in 2009 establishing a baseline of human technological knowledge across disciplines.
- Published on an internal DoD website; never made public.
- As of July 2020: 6 had leaked publicly. Knapp brought **2 more unpublished papers** to the JRE studio.
- Rogan: "the first person outside the program" other than Knapp to see them.
- One paper covers **meta-materials** — how to engineer existing Earth materials to "duplicate what you're seeing up there."
- One paper covers **physical effects on humans** from close proximity to UAP craft — radiation/microwave-like effects; "hundreds of cases investigated"; "known effects to the human body that are horrible."
- Another paper covers how to track a **hypersonic vehicle** traveling through space — "doesn't say UFOs, but looking at things that travel very fast coming from space into our atmosphere."
- Only two of the 38 papers actually mention aliens or UFOs — the rest are strictly human technology baselines.

## OSAP/AATIP Crash Retrieval Wall

- Critical disclosure point: AAWSAP ("OSAP" in transcript) and AATIP ("ATIP") "didn't get special access permission — that's a special type of program. They knew about crash retrieval programs." Meaning: both programs had awareness that crash retrieval programs existed but were structurally blocked from accessing them.
- NYT reportedly working on a follow-up story specifically investigating crash retrievals, reverse engineering, and meta-materials — "bits and pieces, grand pods stashed in a barn somewhere, whether it's Roswell or other alleged crashes."

## George Knapp's Russia Trips — Origin and Key Sources

**How the Russia trip was arranged:**
- Knapp met a Russian physicist — "Nikolai" — at a US national lab disarmament conference. Nikolai had been [[boris-yeltsin|Boris Yeltsin]]'s national security adviser, national security adviser to the Russian parliament, and had "taught Russian cosmonauts how to spot American nuclear submarines from space."
- Knapp met Nikolai "at the suggestion of a US Congressman." Over a beer, Nikolai recalled a KGB contact who "had looked into [UFOs] for a while."
- Knapp sent Nikolai back to Moscow with a stipend and instructions: "Find people who were in a position to know — high up in the military or the political government — who had never talked about this before."
- Eight months later, Nikolai reported back. Knapp traveled to Russia to conduct interviews.

**[[colonel-borosokolov|Colonel Borosokolov]]:**
- One of the former military officers Knapp interviewed.
- "A gold mine of information."
- Revealed the **1978–1988 Soviet Military UFO Study**: "For 10 years from '78 to '88, the Russian military empire — every military unit in Russia, in their whole military globally — if they saw any ball of light, anomalous aircraft, UFO, anything weird like that, they had to report it to the KGB."
- All reports went to a single desk at the Ministry of Defense.
- Knapp: "Probably the biggest UFO study in the history of the world."

**Colonel Sokolop (Ministry of Defense desk officer):**
- The officer who held the central reports desk at the Ministry of Defense.
- Had "thousands of reports that had never been made public."
- Told Knapp about "45 different incidents" (transcript truncated at this point).
- Separately disclosed the Ukraine ICBM incident: "A giant UFO appeared over an ICBM base in Ukraine. These are missiles trained to go to [US targets]. The missiles entered launch mode unprompted."
- Colonel Sokolop's account corroborates what [[vladimir-plantonov]] later provided in written document form (the [[1982-soviet-ukraine-missiles]] incident).

**Significance of Russia trip:**
- "It tells us basically a lot of what we now know about our own government — that they have always taken this seriously."
- Knapp made the trip twice.

## 1975 Northern Tier Case — US ICBM Bases

- In 1974–75 — "they called it the Northern Tier case" — a string of US nuclear missile facilities along the Canadian border (Maine to Montana) was visited by UAPs:
  - Cold War ICBMs "aimed at Moscow and other places."
  - UAPs appeared "right over the missiles."
  - "In a couple of cases they disabled the missiles. If we had gone to war, we wouldn't have had those missiles being able to fire."
  - "There are some indications that the launch control codes were interfered with."
  - Scrambled fighter jets: "The things go away — we can't catch them."
- Official record: Washington Post broke the story in the 1970s via FOIA documents. AAWSAP also studied the Northern Tier cases; re-interviewed nuclear missile officers.
- [[robert-hastings]] covered these cases exhaustively in his book *[[ufos-and-nukes-hastings|UFOs and Nukes]]*.
- Knapp on the stakes: "That clearly is a threat to national security."

## Holmes & Narver Stenographer — Crashed Saucer Meetings

- A cop gave Knapp a tip about a woman who had worked at **Holmes & Narver** — a defense contractor — as a stenographer.
- She sat in on meetings between government contractors and Air Force officials where crashed saucers were openly discussed.
- After each meeting: "They took the tape out of the typewriter and destroyed it. Took all the notes."
- She agreed to tell Knapp her story over the phone — "just a tiny piece."
- Next day: two men in suits visited her. "They said: 'You are still subject to your security clearance. We know you travel to LA to see your daughter. And we know she comes here. It's a big desert out there — it'd be terrible if something happened to either one of you.'"
- "This lady is scared shitless. She didn't make that stuff up."
- Six of Knapp's sources for Bob Lazar background were visited by similar men "one right after another" — all went silent.
- Context: Knapp was trying to fill in Lazar's background at Los Alamos and S4; these were people who knew Lazar or the program.

## Cattle Mutilations — Kelleher Prion Theory

- [[colm-kelleher]] (NIDS biochemist) developed a theory explaining the cattle mutilation pattern:
  - Someone is tracking the spread of **mad cow prions** through the food supply, using UFO hysteria as cover "so no one would take it seriously."
  - Kelleher: prions are indestructible. Ground beef from infected cattle → pigs → chickens → deer → humans. Related to chronic wasting disease and possibly Alzheimer's and Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease.
  - Method: surgically precise mutilation in isolated ranch land where "the guy would shoot you in the face if you go near one of his cows."
  - "They're doing it in helicopters" — Knapp.
- Mutilated cattle: "Their face is completely irradiated and they died" — suggesting high-energy exposure.
- NIDS documented cases for years at the ranch: no humans ever found doing it. "No one's ever been caught — never. That's what's weird."

## Uinta Basin as Historical UAP Hotspot

- In 1976, Dr. Frank Salisbury wrote *The Utah UFO Display* about this region.
- A local science teacher named **Junior Hicks** collected hundreds of cases from locals for decades because "he had taught generations of kids science in that area."
- NIDS chose the ranch partly because it was embedded in this historically active corridor.

## Lazar Background Investigation — Kirk Meyer Cover-Up

Knapp's investigation into Lazar's background at Los Alamos became "what got me hooked in the story":

- Knapp knew Lazar had worked there; interviewed multiple people who "remembered him from being there."
- But the lab insisted: "No record of him. He's never here."
- Knapp found a Los Alamos newspaper: "A small town paper — front page story: 'Bob Lazar, physicist, Maison facility, Los Alamos Lab.' A story about his jet car." Still, the lab denied records.
- Found Lazar's name in the lab phone book from the era — confirmed he was listed.
- Discovered Lazar had been hired through **Kirk Meyer**, a headhunter firm that fills positions at Los Alamos and similar facilities.
- Kirk Meyer said: "Yeah, we hired him. We've got records. Can I get his employment records?" — Two and a half years of letters later, Kirk Meyer reversed: "He was never here. We don't have any records."
- "That pissed me off. It made me mad. I was being jacked around by government and national facilities like that."
- Knapp also confirmed: Lazar's MIT and Cal Tech attendance during the same period he was reportedly at Los Alamos — an apparent scheduling impossibility unless classified programs have different timelines.

## NHI "Deliberate Display" Theory

Corbell and Rogan discuss whether UAPs choose to be observed:

- "If they didn't want us to see them, we wouldn't. They have stealth capability. We can see them right there and not see them on radar. We can see them on radar but they're invisible. You can only see them sometimes through thermal."
- Corbell's analogy: "Throw the cell phone in the monkey cage. 'Here, help yourself — knock yourself out.' We see these displays."
- The displays appear deliberate: "As if they wanted to be seen" — referencing the Virginia coast UAPs over Navy training areas.
- Some craft display thermal signatures, some don't; "they display different forms of propulsion" — "anything you can imagine and more."
- A cop called Corbell "just a month ago" who "had an encounter — not a UFO guy at all. He saw something so extraordinarily strange — non-aerodynamic, something impossible."
