# The CIA Scientist Who Built UFOs Before Bob Lazar (Townsend Brown Documentary)

A feature-length [[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] documentary on [[thomas-townsend-brown]] — the "Godfather of American dark science" and "original UFO architect." [[jesse-michaels|Jesse Michaels]] interviews [[paul-schatzkin]] (Brown's newly published biographer), [[jan-lundquist]] (independent researcher), [[nick-cook]] (Jane's aviation journalist, author of *[[hunt-for-zero-point|The Hunt for Zero Point]]*), [[linda-brown|Linda Brown]] ([[t-townsend-brown|Townsend Brown]]'s daughter), and an anonymous Navy scientist who describes the theoretical implications of Brown's work but fears reprisals.

## Headline Claims

- The [[biefeld-brown-effect]] was confirmed to work in a vacuum of at least 10⁻⁶ torr by French Air Force officer [[jacque-corneau]] at the Montgolfier facility in Paris in 1956 — eliminating the ion-wind explanation for at least some of the observed thrust.
- [[edward-teller]] (father of the H-bomb) witnessed Brown's demo and stated "I don't understand how it works" — his wife adding "you don't know how nice it is to hear him say that."
- Brown deliberately ran a self-discrediting "wounded prairie chicken" disinformation routine after 1950, showing fake fluid-dielectric experiments to deflect attention from his real solid-dielectric work.
- The [[b2-stealth-bomber|B-2]] stealth bomber almost certainly incorporates aspects of Brown's electrogravitics: the 1968 Northrop electro-aerodynamics paper appeared just 3 months after Brown's [[guidance-technologies]] shut down, and investor [[floyd-odlum]] was majority owner of Northrop.
- [[john-lear]] (CIA pilot, son of [[bill-lear]]) almost certainly acted as a deliberate leak conduit for CIA when he introduced [[bob-lazar]] to journalist [[george-knapp]] — framing Lazar as a disinformation wrapper around the real, terrestrial exotic-propulsion secrets.
- Brown's 1953 [[winter-haven-proposal]] is a near-exact blueprint for how insiders describe America's secret UAP program.
- The [[caroline-group]] (private intelligence consortium operating from elites on the yacht *Caroline*) appears to have been a key behind-the-scenes actor in steering Brown's IP from civilian hands into black aerospace.
- An anonymous Navy scientist confirms [[extended-electrodynamics]] — restoring the original Maxwell equations dropped by Oliver Heaviside — produces scalar, longitudinal, and helicoidal waves that may couple with gravity more tightly than conventional Hertzian waves, potentially explaining the Biefeld-Brown effect without invoking new physics.
- Brown was parachuted into Nazi Germany in 1945 under a mission coordinated by MI6 spy [[william-stevenson]] ("Intrepid") to capture exotic propulsion technology; his mission apparently targeted German high-voltage physicist [[richard-maita]].
