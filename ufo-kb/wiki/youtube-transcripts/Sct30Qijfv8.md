# "I Saw Alien Technology At A Military Base!" (ft. Randy Anderson)

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] interview with [[randy-anderson]], a [[us-army|US Army]] Green Beret and 18 Bravo (Special Forces Weapons Sergeant), who describes being taken underground at [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]] in March 2014 and shown a room labeled "Off-World Technology Division" containing two anomalous objects: a levitating metallic sphere recovered from a crashed craft and a gauntlet-like arm device displaying shifting hieroglyphic symbols.

## Headline Claims

- In March 2014, [[randy-anderson]] and one other Green Beret were taken underground at [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]] (Crane, Indiana) after excelling at an advanced weapons training course
- A room in the deep-underground section was marked **"Off-World Technology Division"** — the sign appeared as a placard, possibly permanent, possibly temporary
- The first object: a metallic basketball-sized sphere **levitating above a podium** with no visible support — instructors said it was **recovered from a crashed craft** and served as a **control mechanism** interacting with consciousness
- The second object: a metallic gauntlet-like arm device with a crystalline display screen showing **shifting hieroglyphic symbols** — someone was reportedly harmed or killed during its retrieval due to an energy discharge
- The symbols match those described by [[danny-sheehan]] from the 1977 [[project-blue-book|Blue Book]] crash photos and those reported by DMT researchers using laser diffraction experiments — suggesting a common informational substrate
- [[randy-anderson]] also reports a **green orb** flying over during training exercises at [[area-51]], with cadre acknowledging it with a "you didn't see that" response
- [[lue-elizondo]] and [[jesse-michaels]] together witnessed a green fireball at Elizondo's Wyoming home
- Jesse theorizes [[bob-lazar]] was a "limited hangout" — a deliberate filter used to screen for people with sufficient technical acuity to reverse-engineer his gravity A/B disclosures
- [[harold-malmgren]] is cited as having been briefed by [[richard-bissell]] on "other world technologies" — the same phrasing that appears at Crane as "off-world technology"
- [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]] is described by Jesse's contacts as the **Navy's center for off-world reverse engineering**, paralleling [[wright-patterson]] for the Air Force
