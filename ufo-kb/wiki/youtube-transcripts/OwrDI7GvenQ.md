# Skip Atwater — Shawn Ryan Show #154 (2025-01-02)

157-minute interview with [[skip-atwater]], operations and training officer for the Army INSCOM remote viewing program ([[stargate-program]]) at [[fort-meade|Fort Meade]], later president of the [[monroe-institute]]. - After college (Spanish speaker, counterintelligence background), drafted 1A and unable to find civilian employment. Walked into an Army recruiting office and chose Military Intelligence after seeing a blue trifold brochure.
- Assigned to Alabama; Vietnam ended six months later. Transferred to teaching counterintelligence at **Fort Huachuca, Arizona**.
- At Fort Huachuca, Atwater and friend **Rob Coward** found [[hal-puthoff]] and [[russell-targ]]'s book *Mind Reach* — the SRI remote viewing research documentation.
- As counterintelligence specialists, they immediately recognized a threat gap: nobody was checking whether adversaries were remote viewing [[us-army|US Army]] units.
- Atwater went to the colonel: "I think this is very dangerous and a threat to the counter intelligence work we do." The colonel agreed and arranged a Pentagon assignment.
- The Pentagon assignment was cancelled at the last minute and replaced with orders to **Fort Meade** — redirected by an INSCOM assignments officer. Atwater describes this as arriving "behind the Green Door."
- His mother, when he told her he was moving east, said: "You should look up Bob Monroe" — [[robert-monroe]], who Atwater did not recognize at the time. He later became president of Monroe's institute.

## Recruiting Remote Viewers

- Atwater and Major Keenan surveyed approximately **200 Army personnel and civilians** in the greater Washington DC area.
- Screening method: newspaper clippings about people who had near-death experiences ([[near-death-experience]]) or reported psychic abilities. They would show candidates these clippings, ask leading questions, and gauge reactions.
- Career-driven military personnel seeking promotion were screened out — they needed people who wouldn't prioritize career over the unusual assignment.
- Narrowed to 6–8 candidates. [[hal-puthoff]] and [[russell-targ]] came to Fort Meade, interviewed all six, and decided over lunch to take all six for the price of three.
- Selected candidates signed secrecy agreements; supervisors were told to send them to Fort Meade without explanation.
- [[joe-mcmoneagle]] was working in the DC area and came to Fort Meade on Tuesdays and Fridays, his boss having been instructed to send him without being told why.

## SRI Evaluation Protocol

- SRI evaluation method: an "outbounder" drives to a random location; viewer at SRI describes where they went; a blind judge matches viewer description to a set of six candidate locations.
- Atwater's team achieved approximately **80% correct match rate** across the six candidates.
- Variables ruled out by SRI: underwater barriers, distance, electromagnetic shielding — none stopped [[remote-viewing]]. The phenomenon appeared distance- and barrier-independent.
- SRI refused the word "train": [[hal-puthoff]] told Atwater not to use it — "We don't train people, we evaluate people." The implication: ability is innate.
- Fort Meade practice sessions ("outbounder" exercises): **Scotty Watt** would drive to a location; Atwater would interview candidates in the operations building. Watt was surprised when a candidate accurately described the tower he was standing on: "I wasn't so sure this stuff worked, but I was the one that went out there and they described the tower I was on."

## Project 8200 — Validating Price's Bases

The mountain is hollow. It is a large cave or crater. This has huge rooms and walls, curved, high ceilings. There is an opening into the caves... There are silver metallic ships which are very quick. They are durable and are large and small. They are used to transport people and equipment. There are some people here. They are thin, unemotional, and have programmed feelings. They are strange and have a pre-designed mission... The site has a research feeling, like security. The people are benign and serious and are interrelated."
- [[joe-mcmoneagle]]'s preface to his own Mount Hayes session: "You have to bear with my descriptions... I've never seen anything that remotely resembles this part of the target... It's brand new, it's never existed before, this is a prototype of something — a completely new concept. It's like asking an Aborigine who has never seen an automobile to crawl inside and then describe in his terms what's an automobile."
- Viewers reported the beings use **programmed telepathy** to conceal themselves — they can direct a person to turn left instead of right, preventing them from finding cave entrances. Atwater's wife suggested the beings may be able to walk through solid matter, so the entrance question is moot.
- The beings reportedly interfere with aircraft or satellites flying over Mount Hayes: "that satellite would have some problems because they didn't want anything to be photographed."
- Atwater's overall assessment: viewers' results "matched pretty interestingly" to what Price had reported a decade earlier.
