## Hypnotic Regression Science

Blitch devotes significant time defending hypnotic regression against troll criticism (specifically: "this guy claims to be a psychologist and doesn't even know what sleep paralysis is"):

- **What it is**: A period of "relaxed focus" — verified by EEG as a distinct neurophysiological state; allows the hippocampus to stitch back together fragmented memories
- **The FAT analogy**: Like a hard drive's file allocation table — memories are saved to non-contiguous sectors; hypnosis allows the hippocampus to retrieve from the table of contents rather than scanning sequentially; anxiety, fear, and anger interrupt this consolidation process
- **Legal validity**: Two people are currently on death row in Texas where hypnotic regression evidence was part of the case; a majority of U.S. counties allow hypnotically retrieved testimony as admissible
- **Limitation**: Highly hypnotizable subjects can confabulate (make connections between unrelated things); [[leo-sprinkle|Leo Sprinkle]]'s axiom: "All hypnosis is self-hypnosis" — you cannot hypnotize someone who doesn't want to be
- **Application to Blitch**: The mountain bike and mantis events were both in conscious memory before any regression; regression only sharpened details (the red uniform was on the mantis's back, covering wings; the encounter with the mantis happened at the fuel truck that same night, not days later)

## Blitch's Behavioral Tradecraft Assessment of UAP Witnesses

Blitch applied military behavioral tradecraft — developed in Delta Force and refined with a cognitive psychology doctorate — to evaluate UAP witnesses; his methodology goes beyond interview techniques to simultaneous multi-subject cross-observation.

- **Three-sigma assignment**: 99% confidence is the highest Blitch gives in this space; reserved for Barber's story as told across the team context

- **The reciprocal check**: [[ross-coulthart]] interviewed separately to prevent coordination-as-validation; Blitch's conversation was intentionally siloed so he couldn't be accused of borrowing Coulthart's assessment

## The Fort Collins 2013 Incident and the Irrational Ambivalence Pattern

The 2013 Fort Collins incident differs from the 1995 Daniels Park missing-time event: it happened at home, with conscious memory of the pre-encounter phenomenon and physical evidence upon waking.

- Blitch was standing on his back deck observing an anomalous small localized "miniature hurricane" storm cloud hovering directly over the space between his and neighbor Andy's lots — not moving, completely stationary and localized

- Both then, without discussing it, went inside — what Blitch calls the **"irrational ambivalence" pattern**: two people independently making the irrational choice to stop observing an anomalous phenomenon without coordinating
- This coordination-without-discussion is itself anomalous and suggests external influence on decision-making
- Blitch felt resigned certainty the visitors were coming; made a deliberate psychological choice to submit and let them "get it over with"
- Woke with three bruises on the underside of his left bicep and one on his tricep; no medical explanation; no physical activity that could account for them
- The video camera behavior: before fully waking, retrieved a video camera unused for five years and immediately taped his arm — as if a post-hypnotic directive compelled documentation before conscious memory could construct an alternative explanation
- Andy later confirmed he also had an unexplained experience that same night; independently validated the storm cloud without being prompted
- The Terry Lovela parallel: Lovela described being on a table while "Dr. Bug" said "stop screaming, we're not going to hurt you" — same entity category (insectoid), same medical-examination context; the cross-witness coherence without apparent coordination is significant
- Blitch's compassionate framing of NHI memory removal: "veterinarians remove a dog's memory of the vet — if I were abducting something, I'd do it in a heartbeat"; frames the post-abduction amnesia not as suppression but as mercy
- The irrational ambivalence pattern is the most universal pre-abduction marker Blitch identifies across the Mack/Hopkins/Freeman experiencer literature; it suggests environmental influence on normal decision processes rather than internal choice
