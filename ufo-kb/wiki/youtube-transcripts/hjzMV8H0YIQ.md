## McMoneagle's Model of How Remote Viewing Works — Full Account

The complete theory as stated in this interview:

**Mechanism:** Unknown. "We don't know why we get the information. We've tried everything under the sun to pin that down. You can't pin it down." Candidate models:
1. **Retroactive self-knowledge**: "Maybe when we know ourself in the past" — the viewer exists along a time continuum beginning at birth and ending at death. Information known anywhere on that continuum is in principle accessible at any point.
2. **Non-local information field**: information exists outside time and space and is accessible through intent.
3. **Entropic coupling**: targets with high energy/entropy output more accessible information regardless of mechanism.

**What it's not:** Not like a language. Not learned. Not talent-based in the typical sense. McMoneagle: "More like breathing. Like any normal thing you would do as a hunter." The training is *un-learning*, not learning. Destroying the habituated perceptual models is the work.

**Conscious vs. unconscious use:** Once habituated, the "engine just comes on when you know you're going to get a viewing." The problem for scientists: the viewer often collects the data before the formal session starts; by session time, it's memory retrieval not live functioning.

**The unconscious mind has no language:** It borrows images from memory to approximate data. "The monitor and the analyst are both functioning psychically" — the monitor shapes the session output through their own unconscious, even when double-blind. "Separation of transmission from analysis is critical."

**The filter model:** The corpus callosum is the information-suppression mechanism in neurotypical people. Those with reduced corpus callosum function ([[kim-peek|Kim Peek]], non-verbal autistics) may have less filtering and more spontaneous psychic access. Remote viewing training effectively teaches viewers to bypass this filter deliberately.
