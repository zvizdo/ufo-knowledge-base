# I Searched for UFOs on Skinwalker Ranch…

American Alchemy episode where Jesse Michaels visits [[skinwalker-ranch]] in person and interviews current owner [[brandon-fugel]] and surveillance director [[eric-barr]]. The episode covers both historical context and recent field discoveries under Fugel's stewardship.

## Headline Claims

- **AATIP data is FOIA-exempt**: Because AATIP ran "under the auspices of [[bigelow-aerospace]]" — a private company — all data gathered 2007–2012 is both classified and shielded from the Freedom of Information Act through proprietary-label protections. The system was "set up to be that way on purpose."
- **400-foot manufactured metallic object buried under the Mesa**: Ground-penetrating radar and soil resistivity surveys confirmed a large metallic object; drilling hit a hard object 400 ft wide. Metallurgist [[ravi-chandran]] identified the samples as fused metal containing **europium** and **tellurium** — rare earth elements indicating manufactured, not natural, origin.
- **Rocket deflection and simultaneous UAP appearance**: A 15-foot instrumented rocket launched into "the Triangle" area made an abrupt westward deflection with no wind to account for it. Cameras simultaneously captured a UAP entering and exiting the sky at the same location. High gamma radiation was detected by EM sensors on the rocket.
- **2016 illness incident**: On October 14, 2016, a group of 11 people including a prominent doctor's security detail experienced vertigo, nausea, and in one case ("George," a 6'4" former Hells Angel) complete paralysis and loss of consciousness lasting ~10 minutes, followed by hospitalization for 3–4 weeks with no biomarkers explaining the incapacitation.
- **Basal ganglia predisposition**: [[garry-nolan]]'s research is cited: individuals with higher neural density in the caudate-putamen region (basal ganglia) may be *predisposed* to UAP perception, not made susceptible by it after the fact. The encounter may then further increase that neural density.
- **Trickster / conscious entity hypothesis**: The phenomenon appears to show itself selectively — responding to direct invitations (Eric Barr's screen-grab challenge), reacting to rocket launches, and causing biological harm disproportionately to "alpha male" aggressive personality types. The group interprets this as evidence of a reactive, intentional non-human presence.
- **Hitchhiker effect confirmed anecdotally**: Jesse Michaels' own editor, working remotely on the East Coast, experienced the same EM anomalies documented at the ranch while editing this episode.
