# David Grusch Breaks Silence: Inside Secret UFO Programs

## Overview

Filmed in the weeks surrounding Grusch's July 26 2023 Congressional testimony, this episode combines pre- and post-hearing access by [[jesse-michaels]] and [[yes-theory|Yes Theory]] (co-host [[omar-candal]]). The team hiked Colorado mountains with Grusch, rode ATVs, and held the first public hearing with Grusch and 20 Yes Theory fans in Washington DC. The long-form conversation covers Grusch's personal history, the mechanics of his disclosure process, the nuclear-UFO pattern, anti-gravity history, NHI origin theories, and his evolving worldview.

## Headline Claims

- **Biologics confirmed (firsthand, pre-approved):** Grusch confirmed: *"Biologics came with some of these recoveries — non-human."* When asked alive or dead: "I don't want to get into specifics." He was cleared by DoD's pre-publication review (DOTSR) to say this; anything further would require re-submission.
- **[[manhattan-project|Manhattan Project]] as first [[project-blue-book|Blue Book]]:** Grusch states directly: *"The Manhattan Project — they were kind of the first Blue Book. They were getting UFO reports back in the day."* He adds that colleagues' grandparents were the UFO report handlers on the Manhattan Project.
- **SKIFF denial:** Despite Grusch's request for a SCIF to share classified information during the Congressional hearing, *"we were denied access to the skiff"* — attributed vaguely to security reasons.
- **DOTSR catch-22:** If any agency tried to redact Grusch's pre-approved testimony, they would have to self-identify and cite the specific security classification guide — allowing Grusch to litigate the redaction and publish the redacting agency's identity. This is why they allowed him to mention biologics.
- **Atomic Energy Act classification mechanism:** The 1954 Act defines "special nuclear material" broadly to cover any crash-retrieved material that emits atomic energy — the same secrecy framework used for nuclear weapons was applied to UAP programs, via Oppenheimer and Sarbacher's architecture.
- **Reverse engineering progress:** Grusch believes progress has been made but doubts the program can "scratch" the surface unaided: *"We would own the skies if they had it."* He frames sharing the talent pool as an argument for limited disclosure.
- **Autism spectrum diagnosis:** Grusch did not know he was autistic until his early 30s. It aided his intelligence career (hyper-focused deep-dive analysis) but complicated interpersonal and emotional communication.
