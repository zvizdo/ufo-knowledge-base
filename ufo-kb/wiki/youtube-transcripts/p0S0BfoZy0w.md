# "Life on Mars Destroyed by Nuclear War!" (Ft. Dr. John Brandenburg)

**[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] | YouTube ID: p0S0BfoZy0w**
Host: [[jesse-michaels]] | Guest: [[john-brandenberg]]

## Overview

Brandenburg is a plasma physicist with credentials at [[sandia-national-laboratories]], Lawrence Livermore, and mission research corporation. He argues that isotopic anomalies (Xenon-129, Argon-40) in the Martian atmosphere constitute proof of an ancient thermonuclear holocaust on Mars; that Cydonia contains genuine archaeological remnants; and that he personally identified the Martian paleo-ocean at the [[stanford-research-institute]]'s Independent Mars Investigation. He also describes firsthand encounters with the UFO cover-up, DARPA-funded gravity modification experiments, an original unification theory (GEM theory), and a patented water-based space thruster that has flown in orbit.

## Key Claims and Evidence

### Brandenburg's Career and UFO Cover-Up Confirmation
- Grew up in Oregon; physics+math at Southern Oregon University; uncle worked on [[manhattan-project]]
- Graduate work at [[lawrence-livermore-national-lab]] (fusion research; [[edward-teller]] was mentor and an "inspirational" figure Brandenburg had "several pleasant conversations" with)
- Then [[sandia-national-laboratories]] (directed energy weapons, 3 years)

- Moved to Washington DC; worked at Mission Research Corporation consulting for Pentagon and intel agencies
- Confirmed UFO cover-up from **two** sources: (1) a colonel on the Joint Chiefs staff who said "Yes. That's all I can tell you. The rest you can figure out for yourself." (firsthand, direct conversation); (2) a staffer at Space Command who said "let me connect you with somebody" and never called back

### Mars: Isotopic Evidence
- [[viking-landers]] 1976 results: mixed life signals (now reinterpretable as positive) plus the key finding — anomalous isotopic abundances:
  - **Xenon-129** super-abundance: product of supernova explosions and hydrogen bomb explosions; no supernova on Mars possible; no mainstream explanation exists
  - **Argon-40** super-abundance: product of potassium irradiated by neutrons — the standard byproduct of any nuclear explosion (neutron source)
- Brandenburg states: "The consensus among the real experts on isotopes… is that there is no explanation for the xenon-129 super-abundance or the argon-40 super-abundance."
- ALH84001 (Martian meteorite): veins of carbonate in lava with organic matter; looked like bacterial fossils; Brandenburg found the evidence "rather convincing" though "viciously attacked"

### Mars: Cydonia and the Paleo-Ocean
- Brandenburg was part of the **Independent Mars Investigation** at [[stanford-research-institute]] (same building as the [[stargate-program]] remote viewing lab, he notes with significance)
- His role: gathering images of the [[cydonia|Face on Mars]] and D&M Pyramid at different lighting angles
- Found **second face on Mars** in the Utopia region — NASA renamed the region to "Galaxias Chaos" in response; Brandenburg believes this was deliberate suppression
- Independently identified the **Martian paleo-ocean**: followed the zero-kilometre elevation contour around the entire planet; below it was smooth (old ocean floor) with all river channels feeding into the basin; above it was rough terrain
- Called the feature the "Paleo-Ocean of Mars" at a 1986 scientific conference; NASA has never credited him
- Sidonia was chosen as a landing site target for Viking but scientists were overruled by an unnamed group ("boys downtown say you can't land there") — Brandenburg believes the Face and Pyramid had already been noticed in Mariner 9 imagery

### Carl Sagan Firsthand Account
- [[carl-sagan]] called Brandenburg twice (firsthand):
  1. First call (hostile): "Why are you causing this trouble?" — implied Sagan knew more than he let on
  2. Second call (after second face found): Sagan apologized for being short; asked for photos; pictures mailed to Cornell never arrived
  - Faxed copies; Sagan said second face was "very interesting" (about two-thirds size of Sidonia face) — then silence

- Became dedicated to ensuring humans go to Mars and "find out what happened to make sure it doesn't happen here"

### Mars: Atmosphere and Magnetosphere
- Mars magnetosphere died because the planet (half Earth's size, one-eighth the mass) ran out of molten core faster → no liquid iron dynamo
- Something then blew most of the atmosphere off — "what looks like a thermonuclear holocaust" — leaving only thin remnant; greenhouse collapse accelerated cooling
- Still-magnetized areas of Mars produce aurora
- Remnant biosphere still present underground (based on separate evidence not detailed in this episode)
- Haim Eshed (Israeli [[space-force|Space Force]] general): claims galactic federation, US astronauts working underground on Mars — Brandenburg finds this "amazing" but not implausible given the framing

### GEM Theory (Gravity-Electromagnetism)
- Brandenburg developed a unification theory linking gravity and electromagnetism via the **Pointing vector** (direction of radiation pressure in combined E+M fields)
- Gravity = radiation pressure from zero-point vacuum fluctuations; the vacuum is "hardwired to make hydrogen"
- Predicts **Big G** (gravitational constant) with no "diddle factors"
- Derives the electron/proton mass ratio **1836**; its square root = **42.85** ("the key number to the universe" — not aware of *Hitchhiker's Guide* when he derived it)
- Presented at APS meeting and a string theory conference; got mad at string theorists who "couldn't derive the gravitational constant from string theory"
- [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]] arrived at similar conclusions independently; Andrei [[andrei-sakharov]] also had similar work (the vacuum Bernoulli / Sakharov-Puthoff approach)
- Vacuum Bernoulli equation: two dark objects in a white-hot box attract with 1/r² force (mutual shadowing of zero-point radiation) = conceptual model of gravity
- **DARPA-funded**: $200k grant; handed them final report; received payment and was thanked politely with no verdict — "I think they found parts of my theory interesting" and it "went black"

### Gravity Modification Experiments
- Three experimental methods showing local gravity reduction:
  1. Mylar balloon with toy motor armature hung beneath; 30W AC at ~400Hz → balloon rises (object gets lighter); cost ~$200
  2. Object on chemical scale (controlled for EM interference)
  3. Tesla 3-phase rotating power on 100g object → ~0.5g weight reduction (1 part per 1,000)
- Effect: EM field concentrates lines of force around the object, reducing them through the center of mass → less effective gravitational pull
- Presented at APS meeting; "well received"; waiting for independent verification
- Implies work went black: "if you have anti-gravity technology, it would have great military importance"

### Roswell (Firsthand Accounts from Veterans)
- Attended "Reminiscences of Roswell" conference; sat at banquet next to firsthand witnesses (survivor officers, daughters of sheriff)
- 509th bomb group at Roswell stored **all nuclear weapons on Earth** (five bombs)
- Black Widow (P-61) night fighter squadrons relocated from Europe/Japan to protect the nuclear stockpile; likely patrolling when Roswell events occurred
- **Two separate crash events** circa July 4, 1947:
  1. **Field of wreckage near Roswell**: explosion in midair; Mac Brazel found scattered material; taken to base; Col. Blanchard called the newspaper → retracted and berated by Fort Worth
  2. **Plains of San Agustín** (~100 miles away): morning of July 4; entire saucer with big hole in the side; dead alien bodies (coyotes and vultures had reached some); **one live alien recovered**; archaeologists and campers present; a red-haired colonel threatened to kill them and their families
- Same **red-haired colonel** appeared at both sites, screaming orders; took over Roswell Army Air Field for ~a week with deuce-and-a-half trucks; gathered all wreckage; I-beams with hieroglyphics and unusual plates described by witnesses
- Col. Blanchard quote at subsequent Monday meeting: "Boy did we screw up"
- Brandenburg's interpretation: crafts were monitoring the nuclear weapons stockpile; crashed during severe lightning storm that caused electromagnetic interference with sensitive equipment; Black Widows (vacuum tube technology, EM-tolerant) may have been involved in the encounter
