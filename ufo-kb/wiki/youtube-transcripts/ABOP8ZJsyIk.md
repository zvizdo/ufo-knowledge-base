# ABOP8ZJsyIk — "Aliens Live Among Us…I've Met Them!" — Whitley Strieber Tells All

[[american-alchemy]] episode in which host [[jesse-michaels]] and co-host [[omar-candal]] ([[yes-theory|Yes Theory]] founder) interview author and long-term experiencer [[whitley-strieber]] at length on NHI motivations, the family intelligence connection, the 1952 Skinner box program, the 1989 ear implant, the blue dwarf phenomenon, the [[collins-elite|Collins Elite]] persecution, and the spiritual philosophy Strieber has developed across 40 years of contact. Recorded in late 2024 in Austin, Texas; Strieber and Candal had met at [[garry-nolan|Gary Nolan]]'s Soul Conference at Stanford.

## Headline Claims

1. **The 1989 implant** — Two humanoid figures entered Strieber's locked, alarmed cabin in May 1989; an implant was placed in his ear, later confirmed by [[southwest-research|Southwest Research]] lab analysis to have metallic structure with cilia-like features and an active radio signal. The signal was acquired by a classified signals lab and described as "the strangest signal we've ever received — still under study" over 20 years later.
2. **Von Neumann's "open door" paper** — [[general-exon|General Exon]] (Air Material Command, Roswell era) tried to explain a paper Strieber believes [[john-von-neumann]] wrote: non-human entities cannot fully enter shared reality without human consensus belief; once accepted, they cannot be removed. The door opens in one direction only, forever — which Strieber argues is why disclosure is deliberately suppressed.
3. **Grays have lost free will** — Strieber's core claim about Gray motivation: they see too far into past and future to have genuine choice. Communion with time-bound humans is precious to them because it gives them back novelty and surprise. They don't want slaves or resources; they want friends and equals.
4. **Collins Elite actively persecuted Strieber** — A military-intelligence group (evangelical Christian, Air Force, not CIA) believed Strieber was "in league with demons" and orchestrated a social engineering campaign through old media connections that destroyed his career, culminating in his bankruptcy and the loss of his cabin.
5. **[[robert-sarbacher|Robert Sarbacher]] died the night Strieber mailed him *Communion*** — Sarbacher had requested Strieber's written account before the book was published; the manuscript was sent by UPS overnight; the next morning the UPS driver called to say Sarbacher had died that night. Sarbacher's last disclosure: he told Strieber about a "molecular grid" inside crashed-craft materials studied at Battel Memorial Institute — designed structure giving exceptional strength, reverse-engineered into military/civilian technology.
6. **[[anne-strieber|Anne Strieber]] activated the implant from beyond death** — The implant was dormant from 1989 until 2015; Strieber believes Anne activated it after her death so contact could continue. It now "turns on" at 3 AM communion sessions (Brahma Muhurta hour) and guides him via synchronicity and an eye-slit vision channel.
7. **Hybrid beings are suffering in plain sight** — Strieber encountered a non-verbal hybrid woman at a grocery store; she communicated telepathically, felt threatened, and attacked him. Hybrids are described as severely autistic, telepathic, non-verbal, chain-smoking, non-functional in human society — intentional but tragic products of NHI genetic engineering.
8. **General Exon held a Roswell body and described it "like a big insect"** — Strieber's uncle Mickey worked at Air Material Command with General Exon in 1947 when Roswell debris and bodies arrived. Exon privately told a family member he had held one of the bodies; his description: "like a big insect." Strieber's own first impression during his 1985 abduction was identical — "my god, bugs. Huge bugs."
9. **Von Neumann machine / broken probe hypothesis** — Strieber's alternative NHI model: von Neumann postulated a self-replicating machine that copies itself and seeds planets. Strieber proposes the visitors may be exactly this, but after vast periods the probe's "memory" has degraded — it arrives, finds humanity, and does not know what to do, producing the bizarre, inconsistent behavior observed. "It could be an AI that has gone berserk."
10. **The 1952 Skinner box program — Nazi-derived conditioning** — Age 7, Strieber was placed in a dark box with "horrible noise" by Air Force personnel who told his parents it was an accelerated learning program for children with very high IQs; a childhood friend (now senior government official) witnessed the recruiters. Strieber's immune system collapsed by October; he required isolated hospital treatment with gamma-globulin shots. Strieber believes the program was derived from [[operation-paperclip|Operation Paperclip]] scientists (who lived in his neighborhood), and connects it to the sound he heard to recordings of screaming in Nazi gas chambers — speculating that children who disappeared there were induced into an altered state, and the program sought to replicate it.
11. **Blue dwarf phenomenon — a distinct NHI class** — Strieber and Anne witnessed blue dwarf beings on West 55th Street, New York: a man in a brown suit was being dragged behind a curtain by small blue figures while trying to escape. Separately, Strieber's secretary Lori Barnes was visited in 1954 while pregnant: a blue dwarf stood at her bedside, said "we're not here for you — we're interested in the girl child you're carrying," and when she recoiled at its appearance said: "One day, my dear, you will look just like us." The girl was born; Strieber knows her as a "very good, sweet person" with no unusual traits. Blue figures had also appeared in Strieber's Texas childhood.
12. **The implant's eye-slit vision channel** — The implant opens a slit in Strieber's eye through which words race too fast to read; occasionally one becomes legible. A human insider explained to Strieber that this mechanism draws up unconscious material relevant to his current writing — not random, but a targeted retrieval system. Strieber credits it for his ability to recall extraordinary detail across six post-2015 books.
13. **Breakaway civilization manages the implant** — Two men whose names Strieber declines to share knocked on his door and explained how the implant works, including identifying its inventor as [[constantine-raudive]]. Strieber is convinced these men are "breakaway civilization" in [[richard-dolan|Richard Dolan]]'s sense — they appear fully human, move in his life periodically, and have known about his situation for decades. Sarbacher also knew; Dr. Webb was supportive before *Communion* was published.
14. **Anne's discovery: the dead and the visitors are connected** — Reading tens of thousands of reader letters after *Communion*, Anne Strieber found the letters again and again connected dead relatives to alien contact. She was also the one who selected cabin guests who would then cause the visitors to appear. She named the book *Communion*. Strieber: "Ann is a mystery wrapped in an enigma."

## The 1952 Skinner Box Program

Strieber's account, corroborated by a childhood friend now senior in government:
- Air Force personnel visited families of high-IQ children in San Antonio in August 1952, describing an accelerated learning program; Strieber's friend's parents were told it involved a "Skinner box"
- Strieber has no memory of the program itself; only recalls "a dark place I couldn't get out of and noise, horrible noise"
- By October, Strieber had no immune system — required isolation, gamma-globulin shots at Brook General Hospital; missed months of school; his sister later destroyed the report card proving the absences
- Operation Paperclip scientists lived on his block; Strieber suspects the program was Nazi-derived
- Strieber's darkest speculation: Nazi records showed children sometimes disappeared from gas chambers rather than dying normally; the Skinner box noise matches recordings of screaming from inside those chambers — as if the program sought to induce the same extreme state that caused children to "step out of reality"
- His father Carl — seemingly involved at some level — claimed to have spent WWII picking up cigarette butts; Strieber: "I have a feeling that's not true"

**The glowing car / back pasture (age ~9)** — Father drove Strieber to the country house; a car appeared with "glowing lights that didn't shine," stopped abruptly (not a normal car), then an object tumbled across the sky and landed in the back pasture glowing. **The blue dwarfs in Texas** — Blue figures connected to Strieber's childhood; linked by Strieber to the West 55th Street blue dwarfs he and Anne would encounter decades later.

## The Implant at 3 AM

Strieber's current communion ritual, structured around the implant:
- Wakes at the Brahma Muhurta hour (1–1.5 hours before dawn; also the sacred Islamic pre-dawn prayer time) — now self-directed rather than physically triggered by the visitors
- Enters a semi-meditative state and "interrogates" the implant — asks about whatever he is currently writing; it does not answer in words but in synchronicities: he is guided to where the needed information already exists
- The eye-slit opens during these sessions; words race through; material from the unconscious is drawn up selectively and relevant to his current project
- The implant also heats and cools during significant conversations — during this interview it was hot and cold "all the way through"
- When the implant first activated post-1989, it produced a "horrible noise" in his head and turned his ear bright red — he did not know it was trying to communicate; this is what the signals lab captured
- Strieber: "It's the best thing I have in my life right now" — Anne was right to prevent the removal

## Von Neumann's Two Contributions

Strieber draws on von Neumann in two distinct ways:

**1. The consciousness/wave-function paper** — attributed via General Exon: NHI cannot enter our shared reality without penetrating to the level of consciousness where the wave function collapses. At the level of ordinary discourse, we can reject them. But at the level of deep perceptual assumption — below rational deliberation — if we accept them, the collapse of the wave function is altered permanently and they become part of our experienced reality. This is why "we're ready" is not a sufficient answer to disclosure readiness; we don't know what we're accepting or what it will do once accepted. The paper reportedly was only a few pages.

**2. The self-replicating probe** — von Neumann formally postulated a machine that copies itself, dispatches copies to seed planets, and searches for other civilizations. Strieber's amendment: this machine, across geological timescales, would inevitably suffer memory degradation. When it finds us — a civilization it was perhaps not designed to find, because its makers gave up searching — it doesn't know what to do. Result: not the sophisticated, consistent presence that a purposeful civilization would send, but something erratic, inconsistent, and "a little bit like an AI that has gone berserk." Both hypotheses — the wave-function paper and the broken probe — remain unverified and rest on fragmentary evidence.
