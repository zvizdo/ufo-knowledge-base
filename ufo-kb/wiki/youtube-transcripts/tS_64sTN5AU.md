# UFOs & Religion: Vatican Reveals Hidden Link (Ft. Diana Pasulka)

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] interview recorded in Palo Alto around the launch of Pasulka's book *[[encounters-pasulka|Encounters]]*, just before [[garry-nolan|Gary Nolan]]'s "Soul Conference" at Stanford. The conversation covers the full arc of Pasulka's intellectual journey from skeptical religious-studies scholar to UAP researcher — and introduces a cluster of new material not fully covered in prior KB imports.

## Headline Claims

- **UFOs constitute a new form of religion** — not just a UFO cult but a structural religiosity emerging through media screens, with the contemporary mythology defining how experiencers recall and interpret their encounters. Pasulka calls this prediction validated: she said it before it happened.
- **St. Francis of Assisi's stigmata = UAP radiation injury** — first-source translations (not official Church versions) describe atmospheric sparks, a spinning object with an eye, harsh treatment, and burns consistent with electromagnetic radiation damage matching [[garry-nolan]]'s documented UAP-injury cases.
- **Tyler ([[tim-taylor|Tim Taylor]]) received a religious conversion at the [[vatican-observatory|Vatican Observatory]]** — after accompanying terminally-ill patients with Father Kay and meeting young ESA scientists who recognized him, he decided he no longer needed the Space Program; his concept of the "signals" he receives shifted to align with what the saints described.
- **The Muse is real** — Pasulka gave a talk titled "The Muse Is Real" tracing muses from ancient Greece through Benjamin Franklin's Masonic Muse Lodge to modern mission patches in the US space program; she argues this is an operational oral tradition, not allegory.
- **René Girard's scapegoating theory applied to the Eucharist and UFOs** — the Mass is a recurring ritual sacrifice of a perfect victim that has become so automatic people no longer know what they are doing; UFO phenomena may be a "[[contact-forcing-function|forcing function]]" that breaks through programmatic behavior.
- **Disclosure as a colonial concept** — framing the current moment as "disclosure" assumes the US government is the first legitimate authority to have had this information; it erases Indigenous, Aboriginal, and non-Western traditions that have maintained continuous contact frameworks for millennia.
- **The fourth research tradition** — Pasulka identifies four UAP research traditions: intelligence-community/classified; public hobbyist ufology; academic scholarship ([[john-mack|John Mack]] etc.); and an emerging fourth: accidental collaborations between secret-tradition insiders (Tyler) and civilian scholars (Pasulka) producing emergent transparency never seen before.
