## DOPSR Red-Teaming Strategy

- **Mechanism**: DoD pre-publication security review (DOPSR) — required for anyone who held a clearance; must submit anything spoken, written, or recorded publicly

  - Key insight: "What's NOT redacted passes through as open source; by redacting, they inadvertently validate the information is classified and real"

- **Barber's interrogation of DOPSR**: Hired ghostwriter and wrote 120,000-word historical fiction manuscript (faction: fact + fiction)
  - Submitted first 20 pages (12,000 words) with four categories:
    1. **Known classified** — should get flagged and redacted by any competent reviewer
    2. **Operators-only classified** — existed only in memories of field operators; too granular for admin-level SMEs; precise bait for who cares about specific operations
    3. **Open-source classified** — technically classified but already leaked; testing whether context triggers review
    4. **Fictional speculation** — connected known facts to each other via inference; going fishing
  - Result: 7 pages of redactions from all four categories across just 20 pages → "Game on"
  - When DOPSR caught on and demanded full manuscript: Barber said "This is Episode 1 of a series — do you want us to release without review? Then review it one episode at a time"
  - DOPSR's bind: can't redact without validating; can't ignore without risking exposure
  - Counterplay identified: intentionally allow fiction to pass, then retroactively claim it was fact — "best place to hide truth is in a pile of horseshit"
