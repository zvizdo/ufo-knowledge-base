# "These Children Can Read Your Mind!" — Ky Dickens / Telepathy Tapes

[[jesse-michaels]] interviews [[ky-dickens]], documentary filmmaker behind the *[[telepathy-tapes|Telepathy Tapes]]* podcast/documentary, and [[diane-powell]], Harvard-trained neuroscientist documenting autistic non-speaking children with statistically irrefutable telepathic abilities. The episode covers the controlled test protocols, specific subject cases, a claimed cross-geography telepathic network called "the hill," institutional suppression of anomalous researchers, and the physics of consciousness as a non-local field.

## Ky Dickens Background

- Documentary filmmaker with five feature films; career was focused on mainstream subjects before the telepathy tapes project
  - Father's warning when she started: "Your hard-earned career and reputation are going to be scattered to the wind"
  - Began working with [[diane-powell]] after hearing her on a podcast about autistic savant telepathy research
  - First convincing moment: Powell showed Dickens her inbox — filled with independent parents from unconnected families all describing the same phenomenon
  - "What I found really early on were two families... when they had to spend tons of time with their child all of a sudden their child started spelling... and she started having a diary that said 'I can read my parents' minds'"
- Skeptical DP (cinematographer) Michael brought in as outside observer; came in highly skeptical; conclusion: "I can't not believe this stuff"
  - Michael tested the blindfold himself and confirmed he could not see through it — dismissing Morse-code-via-touch hypotheses

## Dr. Diane Powell's Research History

- Harvard-trained neuroscientist and psychiatrist; trained at Johns Hopkins where she was taught to look for biological, psychosocial, and spiritual causes of syndromes
  - Met [[oliver-sacks]] in 1986 — the encounter sparked her interest in savant abilities; Sacks had documented institutionalized autistic twins who played a game of exchanging consecutive six-digit prime numbers with no algorithm
- Became interested in autistic telepathy after reading prior literature suggesting ESP-like abilities in the severely autistic non-speaking population ("Bernie" first proposed autistic ESP as a savant skill)
- Wrote *[[esp-enigma]]*; Oregon medical board subsequently revoked her license — she had to undergo psychological testing to get it reinstated
  - When board members actually read the book they found it to be sound science; license restored in Oregon and California
  - Powell acknowledges the experience as traumatic; Dickens draws direct parallel to [[john-mack]] (Harvard Psychiatry Department head) who faced a Harvard tribunal for his abduction research
- Now stewards the [[peer-institute]] ([[peer-institute|Program for Extraordinary Experience Research]]), [[john-mack|John Mack]]'s institute, which she took over after his death (hit by a car in London); her Oregon house serendipitously shared a birthday date with Mack

## Controlled Testing — Mia (Glendale Experiment)

- Dickens rented a house in Glendale, California; took down all mirrors, TVs, and reflective surfaces; installed cameras throughout
  - Procured a brand-new iPad, downloaded random number generators and word generators on-site (eliminating any possibility of pre-loaded content)
  - Brought Mia, a Mexican girl from Monterey — most of the family did not speak English; family had never been tested by Powell before
- Test protocol: Mia was blindfolded; a physical divider separated her from her mother; mother was shown random images, numbers, and words on the iPad
  - Tests progressed from 3-digit numbers → 4-digit → 5-digit; Mia spelled each correctly every time without exception
  - Colored ball tests (color hidden in a can), flash card tests, popsicle stick tests (popsicle sticks randomly handed, Mia identifies quantity/color blind)
  - Touch: Mia required one slight touch on shoulder or head from her mother to communicate; Dickens' argument against Morse code hypothesis — "this is a child who doesn't feel where her body is; the idea that abstract semantic information is being transmitted via shoulder-touch is impossible"
- Mia's family reaction: deeply Catholic, worried "is this evil?" — every non-speaker Dickens interviewed describes these abilities as a spiritual gift from God
- Powell's scientific verdict: Mia's tests couldn't count for formal science because she was being touched; prompted Powell to begin designing tighter protocols for the future film

## Facilitated Communication Controversy

- Non-speaking autistic children communicate via "spelling" — pointing to letters on a board or iPad; techniques include facilitated communication (FC), Rapid Prompting Method (RPM), and Spelling to Communicate
- Asha (American Speech Language Hearing Association) classifies RPM and spelling as "pseudoscience," refuses to recognize them as valid communication
  - Historical parallel drawn by Dickens: Louis Braille invented Braille at the first French School for the Blind as a young pre-teen; new headmaster locked away all the braille books and called it pseudoscience; when students first demonstrated the system, adult witnesses said: "It's a magic trick — they obviously practiced this in advance"
  - Sign language: not accepted or encouraged in schools until the 1970s — over 150 years after its development
  - "I can't think of a bigger human rights violation than denying a non-speaker access to communication"
- The touch controversy: Asha argues that facilitator touch creates opportunities for unconscious cueing; Dickens acknowledges this and designed her tests to control for it
  - Counterargument: a child who cannot feel her own body cannot receive abstract semantic information via shoulder touch; Morse code hypothesis requires high-throughput semantic encoding impossible through touch
- Spelling community is divided on whether to advocate for telepathy research alongside spelling access — some fear association will delegitimize spelling advocacy

## Transmission Theory of Consciousness

- [[diane-powell]]'s analogy: brain = smartphone; consciousness comes from a signal outside the hardware
  - "If your smartphone could turn on but wouldn't animate without a signal from somewhere else — that's what consciousness is like"
  - Radio analogy: if you damage a radio's capacitor or antenna, it stops playing music; you could wrongly conclude those parts *produce* the music, when they actually *receive* it
  - Piano analogy: piano needs a piano player; brain needs a consciousness-field to animate it
- [[kim-peek|Kim Peek]] (Rain Man inspiration): born with hydrocephalus; larger-than-normal head; no corpus callosum (bundle connecting brain hemispheres); slightly separated skull bones; could recite 12,000 books word-for-word, backwards and forwards; "where does that information reside if not in the hardware?"
  - [[oliver-sacks]] documented Peek; the case demolishes any hardware-only model of memory and information storage
- Lucille Ball dental filling story: World War II-era temporary metal fillings received AM radio signals as bone induction; Ball heard music from her teeth, turned off the radio — the radio was already off
  - [[diane-powell]]: "I had a patient almost 30 years ago with exactly that — getting radio signals through her filling; when it was removed, it stopped. I'd already read the case reports so I didn't dismiss her."
  - [[andrija-puharich]] documented a similar case with a metalworker who had metal ore lodged in his teeth — not psychosis, literal bone induction
  - [[jesse-michaels|Jesse Michaels]]: "That dovetails with abduction phenomena — [[mario-woods|Mario Woods]] at [[ellsworth-air-force-base]], Q clearance nuclear guard, was abducted and then had his teeth checked by a medical doctor afterward"
- [[william-james]] and [[aldous-huxley|Aldous Huxley]]: mind as a radio rather than a local generator of consciousness — the brain *filters* pre-existing consciousness rather than producing it

## Caudate Nucleus & Putamen — Potential Neurological Substrate

- [[garry-nolan]] (Stanford microbiologist): higher neuronal density in the caudate nucleus and putamen correlates with higher "executive function" — which on close examination looks like what people call psychic intuition
  - fMRI study: Go game players make "brilliant" moves (optimal for an end state the player shouldn't yet know about) → caudate nucleus lights up
  - Ibogaine (African tree bark psychedelic used for thousands of years in visionary ceremonies) → same region activates
- [[diane-powell]]'s theory (first articulated on this episode): caudate and putamen are physically adjacent to the brain's ventricles, which are lined with cilia-containing cells
  - The ventricles = the receptors; the caudate/putamen = the first encoder/processor of that sensory input
  - "Nobody else has said that. You heard it here first."
- Known finding: autistic people show measurable differences in caudate nucleus structure; schizophrenia research shows similar findings
  - Implication: what gets labeled schizophrenia may in some cases be an over-active version of the same faculty; DSM categories are symptomatic descriptions not causal diagnoses

## Scalar Waves & Extended Electrodynamics

- Jesse Michaels raises the physics of how telepathy might propagate:
  - Maxwell's original 21 equations include scalar and vector potentials; Oliver Heaviside's condensed vector calculus form set these to zero for mathematical convenience
  - [[constantine-meyl]] (German physicist, self-described "modern Tesla"): re-derives the full Maxwell equations; finds helicoidal/longitudinal "scalar waves" — and notes that helicoidal waves match the geometry of DNA's double helix
  - The Biefeld-Brown effect: electric potential present without magnetic field — "explained" by the electromagnetic four-potential in a very unsatisfying way
- [[thomas-townsend-brown]] and [[nikola-tesla|Nikola Tesla]] cited alongside Meyl as discoverers of related effects; Jesse: "Deep government people often say there's a there there when it comes to extended electrodynamics"
  - Lee Healey publishes on it; an anonymous Navy scientist appeared in one of Jesse's prior documentaries describing extended electrodynamics
- Michelson-Morley re-analysis:
  - [[beverly-rubik]] (biophysicist) and Harry Jabs published a paper arguing the 1887 Michelson-Morley experiment *did* detect a small effect; the expected magnitude was miscalibrated
  - "Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence — even if there was no effect, you can't definitively disprove ether"
  - Einstein's 1921/22 paper: general relativity is "totally compatible with an ether"
  - Quantum electrodynamics (Feynman era): vacuum energy is everywhere — "the ether is back; it sure is"
- [[andrija-puharich]] used scalar physics in his research at his upstate New York compound; Jesse describes his "psychic children" program channeling "[[the-nine|the Nine]]" and the cosmological information they provided; Puharich was partly CIA-funded
