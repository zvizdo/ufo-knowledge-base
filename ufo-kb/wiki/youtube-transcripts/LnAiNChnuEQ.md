# Eric Davis: "A US President Was Briefed on UFOs!"

Interview on American Alchemy with [[eric-davis]], theoretical physicist and former AAWSAP researcher, accompanied by technical co-interviewer "Jack" (pseudonym "missileman"), a defense/aerospace professional with 15 years of experience. The interview was co-organized with several UAP researchers including THV (Catastrophic Disclosure), Katarina (biomedical PhD), James Faulk (Neon Galactic), Rob Jones (nuclear engineer), and UAP Gerb.

## Headline Claims

1. **Davis was the primary data source for David Grusch.** [[jay-stratton]] introduced Davis to [[david-grusch]] (who was then NRO liaison officer to the UAP Task Force). Davis gave Grusch all his accumulated data and briefing materials from NIDS, BASS, AAWSAP/AATIP, and CIA interactions. Grusch then independently found 40 witnesses.

2. **George H.W. Bush was accidentally briefed as CIA Director about an early-1960s UAP event.** A Pentagon liaison officer wrongly assumed Bush had already been briefed; Bush's eyes "lit up" when told about it. He was then denied access to the evidence (primarily film) because it was DoD intelligence and he had "no need to know."

3. **No ARVs (Alien Reproduction Vehicles) have been built; no reverse-engineering progress was made.** Davis states this as a firm classified briefing received from program insiders: "Nobody… made any progress after several decades of bestes [best efforts]." He attributes ARV claims to Bob Lazar-type mythology and disputes claims by [[brad-sorensen]] and [[mark-mccandish]].

4. **Russia has had at least one crash retrieval**, evidenced by classified documents Davis reviewed through an AAWSAP-era CIA asset (late 1980s era). Davis assessed the material as "legitimate."

5. **China has at least one UAP** — sourced from [[hal-puthoff]]'s DIA work.

6. **[[pead]] documents (Presidential Emergency Action Documents) are used to create waived SAPs free from congressional oversight** — a rarely discussed classification mechanism that explains why certain programs predate and survive standard oversight structures.

7. **Hidden SCIs and SAPs are functionally identical** — Davis and [[david-grusch]] both concluded this after working through SAP security training. The distinction is argued about within the IC/DoD but both sides treat them as equivalent.

8. **Black program contracting uses sole-source contracts and pass-through companies** to prevent competitive bidding that would compromise security. Money flows through front companies; the actual contractor and government customer communicate directly on program management.
