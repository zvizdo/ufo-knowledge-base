# "I Was Taken By Aliens In 1997!" — Kirsan Ilyumzhinov / American Alchemy

Filmed in Tirana, Albania. [[jesse-michaels]] interviews [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]] — former president of the [[kalmykia|Republic of Kalmykia]] and 23-year president of the [[fide|FIDE World Chess Federation]]. Ilyumzhinov is, to public knowledge, the highest-ranking sitting head of government to claim an alien encounter and go public with it.

## Headline Claims

1. **September 17, 1997 encounter (firsthand):** Ilyumzhinov was transported from his Moscow penthouse apartment via a "translucent glowing pipe" onto a very large spacecraft, in the presence of tall humanoid beings in yellow garments. His chief of staff vasili chief of staff and two others searched the apartment for over an hour, confirmed he was missing, and watched him reappear from a room they had already thoroughly checked.

2. **Nixon–Brezhnev UFO folder / moon mission stop (hearsay × 2):** [[yuri-andropov]] (KGB chairman) told [[mikhail-gorbachev]] about a private meeting between [[leonid-brezhnev]] and President Nixon where Nixon opened a small folder and showed Brezhnev its contents. Immediately after, all Soviet and US funding for lunar exploration halted and was never resumed. Gorbachev relayed this story to Ilyumzhinov.

3. **Kissinger UFO knowledge (firsthand observation):** In a three-way meeting (Ilyumzhinov, Kissinger, Gorbachev), Kissinger asked "how are they — how are they feeling, what were they talking about?" and Gorbachev pushed Kissinger to tell Ilyumzhinov "everything." Kissinger smiled and fell silent. In a later meeting Kissinger spoke "in a professional way" confirming deep familiarity with UAP documents at NSC/CIA/NASA.

4. **Anonymous G7 president contactee (hearsay):** A sitting G7 head of state, visiting Ilyumzhinov at dinner, had a sighting with his wife while fishing at the coast. The president refused to go public due to upcoming elections.

5. **Chess transmitted by ETs (firsthand belief + argument):** Ilyumzhinov argues chess was delivered to humanity by extraterrestrials because (a) archaeological sets appear independently on every continent 5,000+ years ago with no communication between cultures, (b) the 64-square board matches the 64 DNA codons exactly, and (c) the black/white duality maps to the universal yin-yang principle.

6. **[[vasily-smyslov|Vasily Smyslov]] / "Vasilei Smith" alien encounter (hearsay):** A world chess champion privately confided to Ilyumzhinov that in the 1970s, before a match in Germany against Hübner, a translucent entity appeared in his hotel room and moved chess pieces, demonstrating the entire game — which then played out exactly the next day. His wife forbade him from speaking publicly.

7. **[[baba-vanga|Baba Vanga]] confirmed NHI presence everywhere (firsthand observation):** During walks together, Baba Vanga pointed and said "they are everywhere — on the ground, underground, in the sky" and would converse with invisible entities she described as "souls, maybe extraterrestrials, materialized souls from past lives."

## Spacecraft Interior Details

- Before entering the tube, Ilyumzhinov paused to consider — "I thought about whether to go or not, like any person would because it was kind of in plain sight" — then decided because "everything unknown is interesting to me"
- Translucent glowing pipe or corridor; one of the beings indicated pressing a button on the chest area; after pressing, breathing became easy and the air smelled like sea or forest
- Interior: a very large space, "like several football fields"; no one paid attention to Ilyumzhinov — beings were tall, wearing yellow garments, busy with their own tasks; something was flickering like screens in different colors
- Ilyumzhinov proposed the NHI address Earth via CNN, Russia Today, or Al Jazeera — they declined, saying it was "still early" and humans "have not yet reached the level in civilization to communicate with us"
- One being led him to a large porthole window; Ilyumzhinov saw something like "a fiery well, like a planet giving off heat"; beings were taking out cylinders or boxes and loading them — possibly collecting samples from another planet
- The NHI presented "formulas in space" — not on a blackboard, but conjured in the air, one after another, with symbols Ilyumzhinov could not recall fully after returning
- Time-dilation concern: Ilyumzhinov worried an hour on the ship might equal centuries on Earth; the being said, "It's fine. We'll return you to where we took you from"
- Beings communicated telepathically, not verbally — similar to how Baba Vanga answered questions before the translator finished, and similar to Atlanteans communicating through thought

## Chief of Staff Vasili Testimony

- Vasili described 40 years working with Ilyumzhinov; characterized him as "a very honest man"
- On September 17, 1997: Vasili and a driver/friend arrived at the Moscow apartment at approximately 11:00 am; called on the phone — no answer; used his own keys and entered
- Found Ilyumzhinov's clothes hanging, two mobile phones on the nightstand, slippers in place — no sign of the president; searched every room, bathroom, kitchen; balcony door was open but no sign he had left
- Vasili called up his friend: "We have a situation. His things are here, but he's nowhere to be found"
- Called police; checked building security cameras — no one arrived or left; security guard confirmed he did not see Ilyumzhinov leave
- After approximately one hour, Ilyumzhinov appeared from the bedroom — a room they had already entered and exited 10 times; Vasili says: "I was so surprised — you weren't there before"
- Alternative explanation considered by Vasili: teleportation; would never characterize Ilyumzhinov as dishonest
- Vasili offered his testimony publicly in this interview for the first time; Jesse describes it as the first time the chief of staff has gone on record
- Vasili's description of entering and exiting the bedroom ~10 times before Ilyumzhinov appeared is a key physical corroboration eliminating simple "missed him in the apartment" explanations

## Baba Vanga — Extended Details

- French Academy of Sciences and Belgian Academy of Sciences officially recognized Baba Vanga as "humanity's number one phenomenon" in the 1970s; also studied by Bulgarian Academy of Sciences
- Ilyumzhinov met with her 20–40 times over the years; not always for personal predictions — often just to observe how she knew things; people booked appointments a year in advance (English bankers, Arab sheikhs, prime ministers)
- **Election telegram prediction**: the day before Kalmykia's 1993 presidential election results were announced, a telegram arrived from Bulgaria addressed "to President Kersen" — Baba Vanga already knew the outcome before the ballots were counted
- **"Two chairs" prediction (April 1995)**: Baba Vanga said "he's sitting on two chairs" — Ilyumzhinov had only the Kalmykia presidency and protested there was "so much work"; she said he would lead "the organization of the smartest people"; six months later (November 24, 1995) he was elected FIDE president in Paris
- Overall accuracy rate: 8/10 major predictions came true; overall approximately 90–95%
- Baba Vanga described as living in multiple timelines simultaneously: "she talked to people who had already died and she talked to those who hadn't been born yet"; sometimes she "couldn't make contact" for days and would receive no one
- During garden walks, she would stop at flowers and greet invisible entities; when Ilyumzhinov asked who she was speaking with, she said she spoke with the souls of the dead; characterized extraterrestrials as "materialized souls from past lives"
- Ilyumzhinov also mentions Sai Baba alongside Baba Vanga and the Dalai Lama as the four transformative spiritual encounters of his life
- Baba Vanga's channel description: "something just opens up like a channel, a kind of corridor" — she accessed what Ilyumzhinov calls a "cosmic computer" or vast database where all past and future information is recorded
- Baba Vanga's condition was described as a torment: she was "constantly among them" and had to continuously fight to return to ordinary reality

## Kalmyk Cultural Context

- **Tangrianism**: the indigenous Kalmyk spiritual tradition; "Tangri" translated from Kalmyk and Mongolian means "sky"; Kalmyks have always "drawn energy from the sky, from space, from the stars"; Ilyumzhinov frames this as the cultural foundation for his openness to extraterrestrials
- Genghis Khan is a Kalmyk/Mongolian ancestor; Kalmyk nomadic cavalry entered Paris in 1814 riding camels; French writer Honoré de Balzac witnessed it; Alexander Dumas visited the Kalmyk steppes and wrote about the people
- Vladimir Lenin's grandmother was Kalmyk; his grandfather Ulan ("ulan" = Kalmyk word); Ilyumzhinov claims Lenin as a Kalmyk countryman who "influenced 16th of the land on earth" (22.4 million km²)
- **Jungari epic**: ancient Kalmyk oral epic (millennia old, transmitted by song, not writing) that describes underworld beings — how they live, communicate, and sometimes emerge to the surface; Ilyumzhinov cites this as evidence of long-standing subterranean civilization awareness
- **Kalmyk Constitution Article 10 (1994)**: Ilyumzhinov abolished the Soviet-era constitution and wrote in an article declaring all Kalmyk citizens responsible for everything happening on Earth — including hunger in Africa, ozone holes, incoming asteroids; framed as "ethnoplanetary thinking"
- Schools in Kalmykia began receiving lectures on ethnoplanetary thinking — developing a planetary rather than national consciousness in children
- Kalmyk felt chess sets: Kalmyk chess pieces are made from felt (nomadic tradition) with figures carved from animal bones; Ilyumzhinov presented such a set on camera during the interview
- Ilyumzhinov's 1 billion chess players thesis: "1 billion intelligent chess players can truly change the world"; chess trains both brain hemispheres simultaneously and develops forward planning — "if people learn to look ahead, there will be peace"

## Subterranean Civilizations

- Baba Vanga confirmed beings underground: she pointed down and said "they live there" — Ilyumzhinov asked "who?"; she was not referring to the buried dead; she said "they live there"
- KGB had a dedicated department for researching unidentified flying objects AND underwater objects; large unidentified underwater craft were recorded in the Pacific Ocean (US thought it was Soviet; Soviets thought it was American), and also in the Baltic Sea, North Sea, and Atlantic Ocean
- US and Soviet intelligence and defense ministries subsequently began coordinating on USO data to prevent misidentification triggering armed conflict; exchange was limited and discreet but documented
- Jesse mentions: Peru mummies (three fingers, three toes, emerging from underground tunnels) and a Siberian "snow alien" discovered after a UFO event near Kutsk, a small village; the snow alien resembles the Peru beings, suggesting possibly a global underground civilization
- Living microorganisms were recently found inside volcanic lava flows — evidence that life can survive in extreme conditions extending the range of plausible subterranean habitats
- Ilyumzhinov frames Earth's crust as "like the peel of an apple" — with 40,000 km to the core housing unknown civilizations; notes humanity can reach ~11 km underwater but sends probes to Mars
- Ilyumzhinov: "In some books and studies it is written that deep down all the way to the core of the earth there are civilizations there as well. They live there maybe in some different form."
- Ilyumzhinov argues it is "quite remarkable" that humanity sends probes to distant planets but cannot build a device to descend below ~8 km underwater; suspects deeper civilizations are deliberately obscured by our current technology ceiling
- The underground NHI also "fight each other" and "set rules sometimes coming to the surface," per Ilyumzhinov — suggesting active, not dormant, civilizational dynamics below the crust

- **Haim Eshed** (head of Israeli Space Force): Jesse mentions Eshed's public statements about a Galactic Federation and a secret space program involving humans on Mars — Ilyumzhinov responds that "there are different programs" and "certain knowledge that some countries have"
- **Medvedev April Fool's remark**: Jesse mentions a Medvedev TV interview in which — ostensibly as a joke — he said "UFOs are locked up with the nuclear codes"; Ilyumzhinov reads it as more serious than presented

- Jesse also mentions his prior interview with [[harold-malmgren]], who described Anatoli Dobrynin (Soviet Ambassador to the US) as personally very interested in UFOs; Ilyumzhinov does not comment specifically but this corroborates the Soviet-US parallel interest
- **Ilyumzhinov on disclosure preparedness**: "humanity needs to be prepared gradually"; "such broadcasts and interviews help reduce cosmic illiteracy"; argues full disclosure at once could cause people to "lose their mind"

## Saddam Hussein and Gaddafi

- **Saddam Hussein**: Ilyumzhinov had a Yeltsin-granted mandate for diplomatic missions to Iraq from 1993; held an Arab chess championship in Baghdad; visited Saddam many times
- In Saddam's final period, Ilyumzhinov flew to Baghdad to warn him the bombing was imminent; proposed an audacious chess-player move: take journalists to all supposedly secret warehouses on camera to prove there were no WMDs; also proposed playing chess on camera at the Al-Rashid Hotel to prove mental competence
- Saddam said "this is very unexpected" and asked for time; his son Kusay later returned and said "my father refuses"; Ilyumzhinov flew out; hours later, Iraq was bombed
- **Gaddafi**: Ilyumzhinov was secretly summoned by Gaddafi's eldest son Muhammad in June (Russia Day) when the world believed Gaddafi was dead or hidden; found him alive at the Olympic Committee building
- Persuaded Gaddafi to play chess on camera; the game ended in a draw; broadcast worldwide, confirming Gaddafi was alive and mentally present
- Gaddafi refused to flee: "my adopted daughter is buried here. My 10-month-old granddaughter was killed by their air bombs. My ancestors are buried in this soil. I know they will kill me and it will be a very difficult death. But I will remain on my own land with my people."
