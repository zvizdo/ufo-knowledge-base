## Overview

The most operationally detailed public account of [[sky-watchers]]' five-year data-collection program. [[james-valor]] — a 23-year Army Special Operations Sergeant Major — describes how a government war game exercise in 2021 led to the accidental discovery of a UAP phenomenon, the subsequent development of a multi-spectrum detection platform, a nine-class UAP taxonomy built from direct observation, psionic asset integration, and a February 2025 "conflict in the sky" involving multiple UAP classes simultaneously. This is the first video to publicly describe Valor as a named co-lead of [[sky-watchers|Sky Watchers]] alongside [[jake-barber]].

## Skywatcher Technology Platform

A mobile, multi-spectrum detection system deployed at multiple sites over five years:

- **Radar** — Primary detection layer; Class 7 jellyfish caused continuous radar jamming for 2 hours
- **Electro-optical (EO)** — Cannot detect UAPs that are visible only in infrared bands (they emit in "a little bit extra" beyond EO range)
- **Shortwave infrared (SWIR)**
- **Medium-wave infrared (MWIR)**
- **RF signal detection + direction-finding** — geolocate UAP RF signatures in real time
- **Helicopter interception platform** — Flown by [[jake-barber]]; used for pursuit attempts

Key operational difference from fixed observatories (e.g., [[skinwalker-ranch]]): Skywatcher is **nomadic** — goes where UAPs are detected or predicted.

## The Dog Whistle

An electromagnetic/RF signal developed by Mr. K that reportedly attracts UAPs to the operational area with high consistency. Evidence for causal attribution:
- January 2025 controlled absence: Equipment deployed *without* dog whistle → zero detections
- Standard operations *with* dog whistle → consistent daily UAP appearances
- The frequency and mechanism cannot be publicly detailed

See [[uap-summoning-protocols]] for full context.

## Origin Story: 2021 War Game Discovery

During a government-contracted war game exercise (blue vs. red team air defense/counter-drone), Valor's team on a rooftop with interns spotted a Class 1 formation of seven objects at 7 km, 2-3,000 ft above — detected by camera (Mr. Ko) but invisible to human senses. Initial assumption: classified US technology. Maintained secrecy for two weeks before broadening investigation. Foundation of the Skywatcher technology program.

## February 2025 "Conflict in the Sky"

The most significant multi-class engagement reported:

- **Jordan's prediction**: Predicted "a conflict in the sky" with multiple UAP classes — confirmed exactly
- **First naked-eye sighting in 5 years**: Valor saw a Class 7 (gold chrome mirror) hovering ~800m overhead for ~5 minutes with naked eye
- **Class 7 prior behavior**: Over 4-5 preceding days, Class 7 had been measuring their radar beam parameters — approaching the edge of active telemetry, flying through radar bubble, probing capabilities
- **Class 2 (Tic Tac) behavior**: Used hills to hide from sensors; entered through the only unmonitored avenue; intent-reading evasion triggered the moment Valor began running to helicopter
- **Two Class 7s (jellyfish)**: Offset exactly 6 km north and south of team position — symmetric flanking geometry
- **Helicopter immobilization**: Aircraft launched to intercept Tic Tac; engines running, blades turning, but frozen in place ~half mile from target; no forward/up/lateral movement possible
- **EM interference**: Class 7s caused 2+ hours of continuous interference to primary sensors; radar jammed during Tic Tac pursuit
- **Manta Ray sighting**: Tumbling amorphous object observed for 20-30 min

## Soft Disclosure Thesis

Valor's central argument about government behavior:

> Government knows it has advanced secret physics (since WWII minimum). It cannot release the science without revealing classified aerospace technology. Solution: allow private companies to independently observe, analyze, and publicly explain the phenomena. The civilian company achieves "soft disclosure" by reverse-engineering observable science. The government maintains plausible deniability while the knowledge enters the public domain.

Supporting evidence he cites:
- Government has contracted Skywatcher for war games without issuing NDAs or demanding operational silence
- Congressional staff privately ask for UAP data while publicly denying interest
- No government entity has told Skywatcher to stop

See [[soft-disclosure-civilian-observation]].

## The Three-Category Problem

Valor explicitly distinguishes three possible phenomena occurring simultaneously:
1. Genuine NHI
2. Chinese/Russian adversary technology
3. US classified programs

His assessment: "It could be both adversary and NHI." The Jersey drone incursion (17+ days, 2024-2025) he attributes primarily to China but acknowledges the mixed-scenario possibility.

## Alleged Crash Events

Four observed events (2023-2024) where UAPs descended below ridgelines and did not resurface:
- Three Class 1s during the 200-UAP sortie event (2023): massive weather event followed
- One Class 8 (January 2024): descended from 3,500m to terrain level
- Searches (hours, rugged terrain): zero physical wreckage recovered
- Valor does not resolve whether non-recovery indicates non-physical craft, self-disintegration, or search failure

## Class 7 Jellyfish — Operational Profile

The Class 7 is Skywatcher's most behaviorally complex documented type:

- **Appearance**: chrome mirror / gold reflective surface; visible to the naked eye in direct bright sunlight — the only UAP class in Skywatcher's taxonomy that can be visually tracked without instruments in daylight
- **February 2025 approach sequence**: over 4–5 preceding days, the Class 7 systematically measured Skywatcher's radar beam parameters — flew to the edge of active telemetry, passed through the radar bubble, probed detection limits; consistent with reconnaissance before an engagement
- **Symmetric flanking**: two Class 7s positioned exactly 6 km north and south of team position during the conflict event — symmetric geometric spacing; suggests coordinated operation not coincidence
- **Sustained EM warfare**: emitted 2+ hours of continuous microwave EM interference to primary sensors; sustained radar jamming throughout helicopter pursuit of Tic Tac
- **Hover duration**: Class 7 hovered at ~800m above Valor's position for approximately 5 minutes in the February event — the longest naked-eye observation in 5 years of operations
- **Class 6 energy weapon precedent**: a Class 6 disabled three unconnected systems simultaneously in 2003 — intent-reading before the weapon deployed; suggests threat detection and response capability

## Jake Barber and the Egg-Class Priority Mission

The egg class (Class 9) is Skywatcher's primary operational target — the object [[jake-barber]] personally retrieved in the mid-2000s:

- **Barber's background**: helicopter pilot; personally retrieved an egg-shaped NHI craft approximately 20 ft in size in mid-2000s California; this retrieval experience forms the basis of his operational partnership with Valor
- **Class 9 target rationale**: because Barber has prior direct contact with this class, Skywatcher's summoning effort is specifically aimed at establishing a second landing event — a "peaceful landing" with witnesses and sensors present
- **H1 2025 confidence claim**: Barber stated confidence in achieving a peaceful egg-class landing within H1 2025 (i.e., by June 2025) — the strongest public timeline commitment in civilian UAP operations
- **Helicopter pursuit as Barber's specific role**: all aerial interception attempts are piloted by Barber; he functions as both operator and bait — the asset with the prior contact history
- **Intent-reading evasion pattern**: during the February 2025 event, the Class 2 Tic Tac detected the exact moment Valor began running toward the helicopter — it immediately entered through the only sensor-unmonitored avenue; this implies real-time awareness of human intention, not just sensor coverage
- **Helicopter immobilization mechanism**: the helicopter was frozen in place with engines running and blades turning — no forward, upward, or lateral movement possible; distance from Tic Tac was ~half a mile; no known EM mechanism fully explains this
- **[[garry-nolan|Garry Nolan]] engagement**: Nolan met with Valor early in the program and has been analyzing Skywatcher data; the hackathon setting (USS Hornet) brought together the civilian science and operations communities
