# The 26 Year Old Prodigy Reverse Engineering UFOs (Ft. Deep Prasad)

## Summary

[[jesse-michaels]] interviews [[deep-prasad]], a 26-year-old quantum computing entrepreneur and founder of [[quantum-generative-materials]], who has raised $15 million to reverse-engineer UAP materials using quantum computers. Prasad's core thesis: the DoD's [[five-observables]] cannot be explained by classical physics; they are emergent properties of underlying quantum mechanics at macroscopic scale — a phenomenon with precedent in superconductivity. His company uses the many-body Schrödinger equation (solvable only on quantum hardware) to simulate exotic metamaterials that could produce the observed UAP behaviors.

## Headline Claims

- UAP five observables (instantaneous acceleration, hypersonic travel with no sonic signatures, etc.) indicate **macroscopic quantum behavior** — the same class of phenomenon that produces superconductivity, but operating at craft scale. *(Firsthand — Prasad's analysis)*
- The classical many-body Schrödinger equation cannot be solved for more than ~3 atoms on classical computers; quantum computers can scale this to simulate any material. A caffeine molecule's 97 electrons require more classical bits to represent than there are atoms on Earth's surface. *(Firsthand — Prasad)*
- **Lockheed** successfully replicated the robin bird's quantum magnetic navigation (cry4 cryptochrome mechanism) ~6 years prior, producing the **[[dark-ice-magnetometer]]** — a quantum sensor providing GPS-grade positioning anywhere, including underwater, with no satellite dependency. *(Hearsay — Prasad citing Lockheed)*
- **[[hal-puthoff]]** is described as "the guy that briefs all of the presidents on UFOs"; Jesse and [[eric-weinstein]] have had a "trippy conversation" with him (cross-reference: [[iQOibpIDx-4]]). *(Hearsay — Prasad/Jesse confirming Puthoff's insider role)*
- Deep Prasad predicts that within 10 years, enough scientific evidence and post-scientific methodology will yield consensus that Earth is not the only home of intelligent/superintelligent life in the solar system. *(Firsthand — Prasad prediction)*
- Quantum computers will render RSA encryption obsolete within ~15 years — breaking the backbone of all modern financial and communications security. *(Firsthand — Prasad prediction)*
- Scientific method needs modification: Sir Francis Bacon's repeatability assumption excludes genuine anomalies (like UAP sightings) by design. A modified epistemology must account for non-repeatable real events. *(Firsthand — Prasad's essay "The Evolution of Science and the Interactive Universe")*
