# Meet the Scientist Who Scanned Beneath the Pyramids

Interview with [[filippo-biondi]], the Italian radar engineer who developed SAR Doppler tomography (the [[beyond-protocol]]) and applied it to the [[giza-plateau]], hosted by [[jesse-michaels]] on [[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]], filmed in Perugia, Italy.

## Headline Claims

- **Eight hollow cylindrical structures** detected below [[khafre-pyramid]]: four-plus-four tubes spiraling downward to approximately 1 km depth, each with a core column and spiral coils around it — confirmed by **four independent satellites** ([[capella-space|Capella Space]], Umbra, ICEye, [[cosmos-skymed|COSMO-SkyMed]]).
- The structures are **definitively man-made** per Biondi: nothing analogous appears in any other SAR Doppler tomography scan he has performed.
- Similar structures found at smaller scale beneath [[menkaure-pyramid]] (2+2 tubes) and **one cube structure** descending beneath the [[great-sphinx]].
- The [[giza-plateau]] substructure is a **network of interconnected tunnels** — the three pyramids and Sphinx are "the tip of the iceberg" atop a vast underground system.
- Technique validated at the [[gran-sasso-laboratory]] (Gran Sasso d'Italia physics lab, ~1.4 km underground): structures matched reality, no AI used — only measurement → model → pseudo-inversion.
- **Water is the principal actor** of Giza: Biondi's hypothesis is that water flowing through the spiral structures excited **piezoelectric material**, generating electricity. The pyramid may have been an information/energy system.
- Biondi's colleague [[armando-mei]] connects the [[khafre-pyramid]] proportions to the **fine structure constant (α ≈ 1/137)**, a fundamental dimensionless constant in quantum physics emphasized by Feynman and Lederman.
- A **scientific paper is under peer review** at time of filming. The research group (Biondi + [[corrado-malanga]] + [[armando-mei|Armando Mei]]) is preparing a project proposal to the Egyptian authorities for in-situ shaft exploration.
- [[christopher-dunn]]'s *Giza Power Plant* theory (1998) endorsed the research; Biondi endorses Dunn's electron-generator hypothesis as potentially correct.
- [[guglielmo-marconi]]'s grandson confirmed to [[jesse-michaels|Jesse Michaels]] (a separate episode) that Marconi headed [[rs-33]], a secret Italian government committee studying a 1953 UFO case in Varese — and was president of the committee, with [[enrico-fermi|Enrico Fermi]] as vice president.
