# Did Oppenheimer Work on UFOs?

Solo video essay by [[jesse-michaels]] (American Alchemy). No guest. Originally based on a private presentation given to a group of scientists in Italy, followed by a debate with skeptic [[michael-shermer]].

## Headline Claims

- The 1971 Australian JIO memo ([[1971-australian-jio-memo]]) names [[j-robert-oppenheimer]], [[edward-teller]], and [[freeman-dyson]] as having been deeply involved in US anti-gravity research — and asserts that [[project-blue-book]] was a front to conceal far deeper programs.
- [[david-grusch]] states that Oppenheimer and [[robert-sarbacher]] designed the classification architecture overlaying UFO secrecy with nuclear secrecy (per the [[atomic-energy-act-classification]] / [[atomic-energy-act-classification]]).
- [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s gravitator experiments — asymmetric high-voltage capacitors producing anomalous thrust — were witnessed by [[edward-teller]], [[curtis-lemay]], [[bill-lear]], and a [[wright-patterson]] technical rep; Teller admitted he didn't understand how they worked.
- The postscript of the [[twining-memo]] (1947) explicitly calls for a "Manhattan Project–style" secret effort to build UAP-equivalent craft — insulated from civilian bureaucracy.
- [[hp-robertson]]'s 1952 panel and [[edward-condon]]'s 1966–69 committee were both deliberately rigged to debunk UFOs, with Condon coordinating directly with Air Force colonels including [[robert-hiper]]. Condon also wrote the McMahon Secrecy Act (1946) — the precursor to the Atomic Energy Act (1954).
- [[robert-hastings]] documented 167 UFO sighting cases at every US nuclear base, including incidents where UAPs rendered ICBM silos inoperable.
- The [[avrocar-project]] and Operation Paperclip brought Nazi anti-gravity researchers ([[victor-schauberger]], [[richard-miethe]], [[henry-coanda]]) into the Wright-Patterson ecosystem.
- [[bill-gunston]] (British aerospace journalist) asserted that Townsend Brown's electrogravitics work was incorporated into the B2 stealth bomber.
- Kissinger's SALT II transcripts document him seriously discussing Soviet microwave beam weapons targeting US embassy staff — a Havana Syndrome precursor treated as established fact in 1971.
