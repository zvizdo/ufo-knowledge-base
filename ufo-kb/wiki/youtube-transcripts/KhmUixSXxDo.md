# Alex Jones Getting Drunk & Painting Rocks [INTERVIEW]

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] episode with [[alex-jones]] (host of [[infowars]]), recorded at [[jesse-michaels|Jesse Michaels]]' home in LA between drinks of tequila and art projects. Shot during or just before the release of director [[alex-lee-moyer]]'s documentary *Alex's War*. Wide-ranging: [[bohemian-grove|Bohemian Grove]] and Bilderberg infiltrations, the July 2001 9/11 on-air prediction, [[joe-rogan]] relationship, QAnon disinfo analysis, consciousness-as-antenna endorsement, quantum physics, simulation theory, and the speciation of mankind under technological pressure.

## Headline Claims

- **[Jones firsthand]** Jones snuck into [[bohemian-grove]] in 2000 and witnessed the **Cremation of Care** — a pagan midsummer ritual performed by approximately 1,000 men around a pond. He describes it as "a fusion of Moloch worship... Faustian occultism and Druidic occultism." The men were "taking this extremely seriously" — Jones: "I would call it satanic."
- **[Jones firsthand]** Jones infiltrated the [[bilderberg-group]] on multiple occasions and was removed at gunpoint. He describes Bilderberg as where "the final deals and battle planning happen" and half the steering committee as "James Bond villains." He identifies [[world-economic-forum]] ([[klaus-schwab|Klaus Schwab]]) as Bilderberg's public mouthpiece arm, activated in the 1990s.
- **[Jones firsthand claim — disputed]** In July 2001, Jones said on air that the US government would orchestrate an attack on the World Trade Center and blame it on Osama bin Laden. He attributes the prediction partly to recurring intense dreams: "I would wake up sweating and usually throw up and it's like you've gone to the future."
- **[Jones firsthand]** When QAnon emerged, Jones immediately identified it as a deliberate disinfo operation designed to co-opt and discredit populist dissent. Many of his listeners turned against him for this. He contrasts this with Pizzagate — claims he questioned mainstream media coverage, didn't originate the claims himself.
- **[Jones synthesis]** The nervous system is not a generator of consciousness but a receiver — electrochemically transmitting a pre-existing consciousness field (endorsing [[aldous-huxley]]'s "transmissive function" via [[william-james]]). The quantum observer effect confirms the mind has a "disproportionate impact on material reality."
- **[Jones synthesis]** Humanity is bifurcating into two groups: those absorbed into the Borg-metaverse-nutrient-soup (information slaves), and those with expanded parasychological minds — potentially a full **speciation event**.
- **[Jones synthesis]** [[julian-huxley|Julian Huxley]]'s 1961 Berkeley speech revealed that *Brave New World* (1931) was not a warning but a blueprint: "we wrote this in 1931 because this is what we were planning." The future is not Orwellian surveillance — it is Huxleyan biological control and genetic engineering. The elite families who projected this 90 years ago are the same ones now building the metaverse and brain-computer interfaces.
- **[Jones synthesis]** Human-animal transgenic cloning has been documented in mainstream medical literature for at least 25 years. Jones frames this not as fringe speculation but as extension of the same elite biotechnological agenda that Julian Huxley made explicit.
- **[Michaels synthesis]** Jones was "a philosopher who got forced into politics" — his original platform was populist anti-elitism and human empowerment (Richard Linklater's *[[waking-life|Waking Life]]* speech). He was co-opted into the role of "tinfoil-hat villain" as a scapegoat mechanism per [[rene-girard-scapegoating]].

## Alex Jones — Background

Jones grew up with an unusual family background: father had connections to the John Birch Society, mother was interested in psychedelic research in San Francisco countercultural circles, uncle conducted clandestine activities in South America. He began reading ancient Roman and WWI/WWII history from age 6–7, ordering scholarly history books as a child. He started on Access TV in Austin circa 1995, then moved to radio. His first major film appearance was in Richard Linklater's *Waking Life* (2001), delivering a speech on human liberty and populist anti-corporatism — Jones and Michaels both describe this as his authentic core identity before political co-optation.

## Bohemian Grove — Firsthand (2000)

Jones successfully infiltrated the [[bohemian-grove]] retreat in Northern California in summer 2000. The members present: approximately 1,000 men from business elite and policy decision-making circles. He witnessed the **Cremation of Care** — a midsummer pagan ritual performed around a pond/lake at night.

His description: "a composite pagan ritual — a fusion of Moloch worship, the bull out of the Middle East, Tyre and places — and Faustian occultism and Druidic occultism." He emphasizes the men were fully serious: "I would call it satanic... they were taking this extremely seriously." He states it evoked ritual societies that historically attacked civilization's future ("in six societies influenced by these dark forces, there was the practice of attacking our future and attacking our civilization and attacking our children").

This footage was captured on video and is a centerpiece of the documentary *Alex's War*. Connected to [[secret-societies-ritual]] and [[elite-global-governance]].

## Bilderberg Group — Infiltrations

Jones has infiltrated the [[bilderberg-group]] on multiple occasions, the most dramatic of which ended with removal at gunpoint. He characterizes the [[bilderberg-group|Bilderberg Group]] as where "final deals and battle planning" happen — distinct from Davos/WEF, which he frames as Bilderberg's public operational arm. Klaus Schwab: "the real deal," not a joke — "head gopher who organized it and ran it," with the group going operational publicly in the 1990s. Jones notes "not everybody who goes to Bohemian Grove or Bilderberg is bad," but describes the steering committee as effectively a shadow power hub.

## 9/11 Prediction — July 2001 On-Air

Jones claims he said on air in July 2001: "I know they're going to blow up the World Trade Center and blame it on Bin Laden." A call with [[joe-rogan]] is referenced where this was discussed ("I've been talking about this for the last six months, Joe"). Jones attributes the prediction to: (a) analysis of the Joint Chiefs' [[operation-northwoods|Operation Northwoods]] precedent — "we know the government's planning terrorism, we know Oklahoma City was terrorism" — and (b) intense recurring dreams: "you've gone to the future... you fold space or something."

He distinguishes this from casual prediction — he was specific, on-air, with a named target and named party to blame, two months before the event. Jones references this prediction as evidence that belief, consciousness, and precognition may be more real than official epistemology allows. See [[quantum-brain-precognition]] and [[RofQnByLwOo]].

## Consciousness and Parapsychology

Jones and Michaels engage in extended discussion of quantum mechanics and consciousness:

- **Double-slit experiment**: without observation, a wave interference pattern; with a human observer, it collapses into one slit — [[roger-penrose]]'s interpretation: a quantum sensor exists in the human brain, converting probabilistic quantum reality into classical reality.
- **Observer effect universality**: "the experimenter has an impact on the data from any experiment" — belief is a confounding variable in every scientific trial, making true objective science impossible.
- **Prayer and placebo**: Jones cites studies showing prayer is "a lot better than drugs" for certain outcomes. Placebo works. The fact that belief affects material outcomes is suppressed because "if parapsychology is true, then belief in the positive or negative sense will actually affect the results."
- **Consciousness openness**: "we all have connections that we can open up to larger more complex systems — but it's hard to experience for most people."
- **Parasychology political threat**: elites suppress parapsychological knowledge because "if we actually believed in the power of psychology and the power of belief, it would mean we were unrulable. You can't rule great people if they have all their skills."

## Aldous Huxley, William James, and Transmission Theory

[[aldous-huxley]]'s *The Perennial Philosophy* and *Brave New World* (co-authored with help from his brother [[julian-huxley]]) are framed as blueprints for the current world order. Julian Huxley's 1961 Berkeley speech: "we wrote this book in 1931 because this is what we were planning... a chemical system of biological control and genetic engineering." Jones presents this as elite families projecting modernity 70 years in advance.

[[aldous-huxley]] died with a massive LSD dose and was deeply interested in [[william-james]]'s **transmissive theory of consciousness**: the brain as "reducing valve" that filters a pre-existing consciousness field into material experience — rather than generating consciousness. Jones endorses this framework fully: "electrochemically the DNA is doing that now." The [[transmission-theory-consciousness]] connects to epigenetics: death rituals of tribal leaders are described as a consciousness download mechanism — the dying chief "transmits the knowledge" through energy and ritual, not just words.

## Simulation, Dark Matter, and Speciation

Jones and Michaels discuss the cosmological picture:

- **Dark matter/energy**: 95% of the universe is dark matter and dark energy — "proven in the last 30 years." Every ancient culture — Egyptians, Mayans, Vedics — posited an invisible force "holding everything together." Einstein and Max Planck's equations pointed to this. Jones: "is that simulation or is that our group collective manifesting all of this as a giant art project?"
- **Simulation hypothesis**: "maybe we're like another species having a hallucination, or something else, and it's manifesting all this." Michaels and Jones treat simulation theory not as merely academic but as a live cosmological possibility adjacent to NHI contact.
- **Speciation**: humanity is bifurcating into: (a) those who retreat into the Borg/metaverse/nutrient-soup, receiving state propaganda downloads, losing embodied reality; and (b) those with expanded parasychological minds who retain sovereignty over consciousness. Jones calls this "scary" and "already happening."
- **Human-animal cloning**: Jones asserts transgenic human-animal hybrids have been in mainstream medical literature for 25 years. Frames it as a dimension of the same elite biotechnology agenda documented by [[julian-huxley]] in 1931.

## Alchemy, Francis Bacon, and the Death of Natural Philosophy

Jones and Michaels argue that the scientific method killed a richer mode of inquiry. Before the 17th century, natural philosophers — Newton, [[francis-bacon|Francis Bacon]], Copernicus — were polymaths simultaneously doing hard science, alchemy, and esoteric exploration. Francis Bacon wrote *New Atlantis* (a utopian sci-fi about a society run by scientist-priests) while also being deeply alchemical; Newton was an active alchemist who spent more time on alchemy than on the *Principia*. Jones: "we need epistemological anarchism — science was anarchy until the 20th century."

The modern bifurcation: professional "middlewit" scientists claiming settled truth on one side, and "woowoo" manifestation practitioners on the other. Jones and Michaels argue the loss of polymathic integration — someone who can simultaneously peer-review and practice ritual — is what prevents progress on consciousness, NHI contact, and advanced physics. Michaels cites Umberto Eco's *Travels in Hyperreality* (medieval historian and semiotician) as evidence that modernity structurally resembles the medieval period — centralized institutional authority suppressing heterodox knowledge systems. The alchemy analogy runs through the episode's title frame: "the real alchemy — separation of good metal from garbage, wheat from chaff."

## QAnon and Planted Disinfo

Jones explicitly identifies QAnon as deliberate disinfo — "BS" — designed to co-opt and discredit genuine populist dissent, similar to how he was "set up" with Pizzagate. He says he was set up repeatedly: third parties (media) would circulate claims, he'd question anomalies, media would then attribute the original claims to Jones. QAnon was more sophisticated: "Trump's invincible, he's secretly telling me what to do" — designed to hook his listener base and make them passive. Jones refused to endorse it; lost followers. See [[flood-the-zone-disinfo]].

## Joe Rogan Relationship

Jones has known [[joe-rogan]] for 23–24 years. Describes him as "the smartest person I know" and a "wizard" who "thinks 20 steps ahead." On Rogan's Spotify controversy (Neil Young, Joni Mitchell, N-word compilation): Jones advised Rogan NOT to apologize; Rogan did anyway, but Jones says Rogan's framing was actually nuanced — he apologized to "people who genuinely felt hurt by out-of-context footage" rather than to the system. Rogan's decision to go to Spotify: Jones frames it as preemptive judo against YouTube censorship. Jones speculates Rogan is building infrastructure for an independent platform and others like Jones would join.
