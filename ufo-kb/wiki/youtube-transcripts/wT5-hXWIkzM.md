## Consciousness: Materialism vs. Idealism vs. Panpsychism

- Sheldrake positions himself as **neither materialist nor idealist**:
  - Materialism: everything is unconscious matter; consciousness is just matter behaving mechanically
  - Idealism: everything is consciousness; matter is "dumbed down mind"
  - Both have fatal flaws: materialism can't explain consciousness, idealism can't explain matter
- The **hard problem of consciousness**: mechanistic materialism has no explanation for why brains produce subjective experience
  - Attempts to dismiss it: Sam Harris and others deny the self; epiphenomenalism (consciousness does nothing → why did it evolve?); "consciousness is illusion" — but illusion presupposes consciousness
  - Philosophers "go round and round in circles" for entire careers
  - The eye-sees-the-eye problem: the aperture through which we understand anything is the mind itself
- **Panpsychism**: consciousness throughout nature; difference of degree not kind between electrons and human brains
  - Now taught in mainstream universities (was called "animism" and dismissed as primitive superstition when Sheldrake studied)
  - Problem: panpsychist philosophers stop at the brain and don't extend the logic further
  - Sheldrake extends it: if brains are conscious via EM fields, why not the Sun? Stars? Galaxies?
- Descartes split world into Consciousness (God, angels, minds) and Matter (nature, mechanism)
  - 19th century materialism and atheism eliminated God and angels → left only human mind → forced mind into matter → hard problem

## Vision, Extramission, and the Projected Mind

- **Official model**: Light from object → retina → optic nerve → visual cortex → brain produces "3D virtual reality display inside the skull"
  - Sheldrake: no one has ever seen such a display inside a head; theory has zero direct evidence
  - The implication: the image of the person you're looking at is "inside your head" — "there's a little Jesse wearing a red t-shirt somewhere inside my brain"
- **Sheldrake's extramission model**: images are *projected outward* from the brain; your image of a person is exactly where they seem to be
  - "My mind is extended beyond my brain, filling the whole world around me"
  - This is what 90%+ of the world's people (and all children under 10) believe experientially
  - Hidden evidence it's correct: **mirror virtual images** — physics textbooks explain virtual images *by using* the outward-projection model, then add "but of course these lines of force are imaginary"
- **Transactional interpretation of QM** as proposed mechanism (John Cramer, developed by Ruth Kastner):
  - When your eye absorbs a photon, the absorbing cone cell sends a "reverse anti-photon" from the future toward the past
  - The forward photon (object→eye) and reverse photon (eye→past) form a **standing wave** linking observer to observed
  - This standing wave is a physical connection through the light itself
  - When you stare at someone from behind: your projections overlap with their back; the light rays physically connect you via this bidirectional mechanism
  - Alfred North Whitehead: all mental causation is retrocausal — mind works future→past while physics works past→future; they overlap in the present
- Anil Seth's "controlled hallucination" framing: perception is a hallucination constrained by sensory input
  - Sheldrake is "perfectly happy" with this phrasing; it doesn't contradict extramission

## Scopaesthesia (The Sense of Being Stared At)

- **Scopaesthesia** (from Greek *skopein* = to see, *aesthesia* = feeling): the ability to detect when someone is looking at you
  - ~90% of the general population reports experiencing this
  - Standard scientific position: impossible (mind is in brain → can't affect someone 100 yards away) → doesn't exist → not a valid research topic
  - Sheldrake is one of approximately **three researchers globally** currently investigating it
- **Directional response** finding from ~1,000 case histories:
  - When stared at through an upstairs window, people don't just vaguely sense being watched — they turn and look **directly at** the person staring
  - Works with animals responding to human stares, and humans responding to animal stares
  - Sheldrake argues this is very deep in animal nature, evolved via Predator-prey relations
  - A prey animal that can detect a hidden predator watching it has a survival advantage → natural selection favors this ability
- Test results: average subject scores ~**55% correct** (vs 50% chance); some subjects reach 60–80%
  - University professors and academics are the **worst subjects** — excessive left-brain rational thinking suppresses the instinctive response
  - Most people "have" the ability but it's suppressed by materialist cultural conditioning
- **Staring App**: Sheldrake is launching a citizen-science mobile phone app (available on his website)
  - Two users link phones via QR code; one sits with back to other, optionally blindfolded
  - Trier either stares or looks away/thinks of something else; subject says "yes" or "no" to being looked at
  - App records and tracks accuracy improvement over time
  - Martial arts tradition: various programs already train people to detect being looked at from behind (useful for self-defense)
  - Sheldrake wants users to experiment with: meditation, cannabis, MDMA, circadian timing — things that would be impossible in university ethics committees

## Retrocausality and Precognitive Dreams

- **Retrocausality**: future influencing the present/past
  - Quantum computation evidence: reversible qubit positions; temporal non-locality; double-slit measurement affects past state
  - Whitehead: consciousness is primarily about *possibility* — possible actions in mind before one is chosen
  - When you form an intention and act on it, the future goal "worked back" on your past to draw you toward it
  - This is virtual future acting on past — not metaphor, a structural feature of consciousness
- **Precognitive dreams**: Sheldrake reports them as "much more common than most people think"
  - Once you start writing dreams down, precognitive aspects appear regularly
  - Mechanism proposed: dreaming in advance one's *own future experience of seeing something on TV* — a "pre-memory" rather than direct future knowledge
  - Sheldrake collected **75+ precognitive 9/11 dreams** within 3 days of the attacks:
    - Method: posters in Washington Square; ads in the Village Voice ("Did you dream of the World Trade Center?")
    - Three categories emerged: (1) planes flying into skyscrapers (not necessarily WTC); (2) trapped in blazing buildings; (3) walking city streets with eerie doom, vast dust clouds rolling down
    - All three things literally happened and were widely broadcast on TV
  - Washington Post article titled "Lucky to Be Alive" documented people who didn't go to work on 9/11 due to premonitions
  - Pre-Titanic novel (~10 years before): described large ship "Titan" with wealthy elites, hits iceberg, sinks — a near-literal prediction

## Morphic Fields and Morphic Resonance

- **Morphic fields**: all self-organizing systems organized by a field that shapes their form
  - Morphic = relating to form (Greek: *morphe*)
  - At every level of organization, the whole is more than the sum of the parts
  - Levels: subatomic particles → atoms → molecules → cells �� organs → organisms → societies
  - Each level has its own field; the field organizes the parts into a coherent whole
  - DNA codes for amino acid sequences in proteins — it cannot code for the shape of a wing, the instincts of a giraffe, or the structure of a termite colony
  - These complex patterns of organization can't be inherited via genes; they require a different mechanism
- **Morphic resonance**: transmission of form/memory across time and space via similarity
  - Resonance analogy: sustaining pedal on piano → your voice resonates strings of matching pitch
  - Radio: aerial resonates with broadcast frequency; whole room filled with invisible transmissions
  - Morphic resonance: similar systems in the present resonate with similar systems in the past
  - Doesn't require physical contact or signaling — it works across time
- **Key experimental examples**:
  - **Rat experiment**: train rats in London to learn a new trick → rats in New York, San Francisco, Melbourne, Beijing all learn the same trick faster, with no normal communication
    - Evidence exists from long series of rat experiments; effect is real but contested
  - **Crystallization**: first-time synthesis of a new chemical compound → very difficult to get first crystal (sometimes years); thereafter it gets easier worldwide
    - Well-known phenomenon in chemistry; conventional explanation is "seed crystals travel on researchers' beards and clothes"; Sheldrake calls this inadequate
  - **Athletic barriers**: once a feat is accomplished (4-minute mile etc.) it falls more easily worldwide
    - Skeptics: larger pool of athletes; Sheldrake: morphic resonance is at least a contributing factor
- **Language acquisition** and morphic resonance:
  - Chomsky's innate grammar cannot be genetic — Chinese baby adopted by Europeans learns English perfectly
  - Sheldrake: children learn language via morphic resonance from the millions who have learned that language before
  - This is a kind of resonance with past speakers, not a hard-coded genetic module
- René Thom's mathematical models: topology/differential topology; attractors drawing developing systems toward goals
  - Sheldrake has received multiple mathematical models from followers; cannot evaluate them himself (not a mathematician)
  - Argues most biologists don't use math anyway — taxonomy, morphology, behavior can't be reduced to equations

## Epigenetic Inheritance and the Mouse Fear Experiment

- **Epigenetic inheritance**: offspring inherit characteristics acquired by parents, not explained by DNA sequence changes
  - Darwin was Lamarckian (admitted inheritance of acquired characters); Neo-Darwinists like Dawkins reject this
  - Since ~2000, Lamarckian inheritance is back as "epigenetics" — accepted by mainstream when explained via molecular changes (methylation, histones, small RNA)
  - Sheldrake: the molecular basis is often assumed, not proven; morphic resonance is an equally valid explanation
- **Mouse acetophenone fear experiment** (published in *Nature*):
  - Researchers conditioned male mice to fear acetophenone (cherry-like smell) via electric shocks
  - Mice bred with unexposed mothers; offspring tested → offspring were terrified of acetophenone
  - Inherited in a **single generation** — impossible to attribute to cultural transmission (father had no contact with offspring)
  - Conventional framing: epigenetic molecular changes in sperm cells
  - Sheldrake's framing: morphic resonance between father mice and offspring
- **Heart transplant recipients** and donor memory transfer:
  - Recipients without knowing anything about donors take up donor's behavioral traits, appetites, skills, memories
  - Conventional explanation: "cellular memory" — memories stored in heart cells
  - Sheldrake's explanation: transplanted heart continues to resonate morphically with the donor
  - The memories are not in the cells; they flow through morphic resonance with the donor's pattern
- **[[michael-levin|Michael Levin]]'s flatworm experiment**:
  - Flatworms trained to respond to specific stimuli
  - Heads removed; flatworms regenerated new heads (flatworms regenerate easily)
  - Flatworms with regenerated heads **still retained the learned behavior**
  - Conventional explanation: memory distributed throughout body cells
  - Sheldrake's explanation: morphic resonance with the flatworm's own past intact form

## The Sun as Conscious

- Paper: "Is the Sun Conscious?" published in *Journal of Consciousness Studies* — a fairly mainstream journal
- Motivation: medieval traditions worldwide assumed the sun was conscious (Japanese: sun goddess; Greek: sun god; medieval Christian: guiding intelligence of each planet)
  - Plato called planets "visible gods"
  - Mainstream science assumes the sun is an unconscious nuclear fusion reactor — Sheldrake calls this assumption arbitrary
- **Interface model**: in humans, the EM field (brain waves: alpha, theta, beta, gamma) is the interface between consciousness and the physical brain
  - Nobody knows exactly how this works; it's correlated with consciousness and assumed connected
  - The Sun has a **vastly more complex EM field** spanning the entire solar system — the heliosphere
  - Carrington event 1859 demonstrated solar EM activity can knock out electrical infrastructure on Earth
  - The Aurora Borealis (currently visible further south than normal — seen as far south as London) = solar EM discharge reaching Earth
- **What could the Sun "think about"?**: Sheldrake speculates the Sun could choose the **direction of solar flares and CME events**
  - If directed at Earth they can take out the power grid; if directed away from Earth they're harmless
  - "Is the Sun modulating activity in the whole solar system through its electromagnetic field?"
- **Cosmic extension**: if stars are conscious → why not galaxies?
  - The Cosmic Web (plasma threads millions of light-years long, electric currents, magnetic field lines connecting all galaxies) looks exactly like a neural network
  - Could be a "Cosmic Mind" — the universe itself may be conscious with EM phenomena as the interface
- Connection to [[john-joe-mcfadden|John Joe McFadden]]'s theory: the EM field of the brain IS consciousness (experienced from within)
  - Sheldrake agrees EM is the **interface** but not consciousness itself — morphic fields are the underlying mind; EM is the bridge

## Institutional Science and Hidden Agreement

- **The "closet" phenomenon**: Sheldrake describes a consistent pattern at scientific conferences
  - Scientists approach him privately (at drinks receptions, when "no one else is listening") to express agreement
  - They say: "I'm really interested in your work, I've had these telepathy experiences, but I can't talk about it here"
  - He tells them: "You're not alone — several people in your own department have said the same thing to me"
  - They: "How do you know?" He: "They just told me — him, her, him"
  - The "silence" is each scientist assuming they are uniquely heretical, when in fact there is a hidden majority
- **Funding problem**: Bial Foundation (Portugal) = largest parapsychology funder; ~$1M/year
  - NIH = $35 billion/year; NSF comparable; virtually no state funding for parapsychology

## Faraday, Light, and Bidirectional Electromagnetism

- Sheldrake reading Michael Faraday to understand the deep nature of electromagnetism and its relation to consciousness
- **Faraday's position on lines of force**: believed magnetic field lines are **physically real**, not imaginary
  - Modern textbooks: describe and explain everything using lines of force, then add "but these are of course only imaginary, a convenience for thinking"
  - Faraday considered this self-contradictory: if they do all the work, why are they imaginary?
  - Faraday and Maxwell both thought lines of force were tubes through which a fluid (ether) flowed with spiraling vortex activity
- **Bidirectional light in Maxwell's equations**: Maxwell's equations work equally well whether light moves object→eye or eye→object
  - [[richard-feynman|Richard Feynman]] and [[john-wheeler|John Wheeler]] (1945 paper): bidirectional basis of the transactional interpretation
  - Feynman diagrams: lines can go backward in time; a positron = an electron going backwards in time
  - Sheldrake: "the bit that goes from future to past is related to consciousness" — the reverse-causal flow of mind
- **Speed of gravity paradox**: Van Flandern argues speed of gravity ≥ 2 billion times the speed of light
  - All orbital mechanics calculations only work if gravity is assumed to be **instantaneous**
  - If you assume Sun's gravity takes 8 minutes to reach Earth (like light), calculations for probes don't work
  - Physicists assume instantaneous gravity but rarely acknowledge the contradiction with special relativity
- **The Ether question**: Michelson-Morley 1890s experiment disproved a *uniform stationary ether*; did not disprove all ether theories
  - Einstein's general relativity is compatible with some ether concepts (Einstein wrote a 1923 paper acknowledging this)
  - Quantum vacuum / zero-point field: space "full of energy and virtual particles popping in and out"
  - Different ether theories (Faraday's fluid, Maxwell's vortex, Quantum vacuum, Higgs field) are distinct and not all refuted

## AI and AlphaFold

- Sheldrake's assessment of AI: **pattern-matching, not intelligence or consciousness**
  - AlphaFold doesn't predict protein structure from first principles (tried for years; multiple-minimum problem makes it impossible)
  - AlphaFold works like facial recognition: pattern-match against thousands of known structures to find the closest match
  - "It's not a fundamental understanding — it doesn't know what it's doing"
- **Turing Test** is "silly" and obsolete:
  - Proposed in the 1950s when computers could barely match a simple calculator
  - Passing a behavioral test does not prove consciousness — it proves sophisticated pattern mimicry
  - Feigning agreement with the test often reflects social pressure: if Turing were alive today he'd have moved on
- Jesse's framing: "the last safe hold for mankind is self-identification — figuring out who we are"
  - Sheldrake agrees the self-understanding question is what AI cannot address
  - AI does not "understand" in any deep sense — no theory of mind, no conscious experience of its own outputs

## Extraocular Vision and Related Phenomena

- **Extra-ocular vision**: blindfolded subjects can recognize objects, navigate terrain, or "see" without using eyes
  - Most successful with 7–8 year old children as subjects
  - Active training programs in Mexico and Spain; Sheldrake's colleague in Spain working on it
  - Texas school (mentioned by Jesse) teaching people to develop these senses
  - Distinct from "blindsight" (lesion-based unconscious visual processing in monkeys/humans)
- **Blind sight** vs **extra-ocular vision**:
  - Blindsight = damage to visual cortex → behaviorally responds to objects without conscious awareness
  - Extra-ocular vision = perceiving objects without eyes at all; not explained by any known visual pathway
- **Brain injury and distributed cognition**:
  - Cases where people function normally with only 44% of their brain intact
  - Semantic information does not appear to be locally stored in specific brain regions
  - Sheldrake interprets these via morphic fields; the mind is not in the brain
