## Wolfram/Gorard Physics Framework

Pines presents [[jonathan-gorard|Jonathan Gorard]]'s work as the strongest candidate for a theory of everything with direct implications for NHI capabilities and UAP propulsion.

**Foundation**: Start with abstract hypergraphs (directed-edge graphs of pure abstract nodes) and define rewriting rules. Applying rules in all possible orderings generates a branching "multi-way system."

**Three graph representations**:
1. **Causal graph** (one causal ordering of rule applications): In the limit, discrete measures on this graph converge to the Einstein field equations and general relativity. Gorard showed the discrete Ricci tensor and Riemann manifold structure emerge naturally.
2. **Branchial graph** (all possible orderings, generating branches): Recovers quantum mechanics. Discrete measures converge to the Feynman path integral; branch space with suitable assumptions converges to projective Hilbert space.
3. **[[wolfram-gorard-physics|Rulial space]]** (all possible rules on all possible hypergraphs): The infinite-limit structure is an "infinity groupoid" (Grothendieck's homotopy hypothesis). General relativity sits at the top of the homotopy tower; discrete quantum mechanics at the bottom. Quantum gravity = filling the tower in between.

**Observer**: An observer is a "Nupendix completion" procedure — equivalencing multiple branches into one perceived state. This dissolves the measurement problem: measurement IS the completion procedure applied by an observer who cannot resolve microstate branchings.

**Why we see consistent physics**: We are all observers in very similar positions in "[[wolfram-gorard-physics|rulial space]]" — slicing the higher-dimensional structure in nearly identical ways. Different physics would be seen by observers at radically different positions in [[wolfram-gorard-physics|rulial space]].
