## Remote Viewing Theory and Methodology

- **Evolved human ability, not supernatural**: McMoneagle argues remote viewing is rooted in evolutionary history
  - 400,000 years ago, before language, tribal groups communicated telepathically across great distances
  - The corpus callosum now acts as a filter, suppressing this capacity in modern humans
  - [[julian-jaynes|Julian Jaynes]]'s bicameral mind concept: voices directing ancient behavior were a different brain mode
  - "[[telepathy-tapes|Telepathy tapes]]" study: non-verbal autistic children who cannot communicate verbally show high clairvoyance
- **Perception is radically subjective**: Our visual cortex refreshes at ~13-14 times per second; reality is a constructed model, not a live feed
  - Blind people who have never seen are better at remote viewing because they spend their whole lives imagining what the world looks like
  - Losing one sense strengthens others — and if the mind is a sense, this applies directly
- **Core protocol — down-analysis, not up-analysis**:
  - The subconscious has no language of its own; it borrows from memory to represent targets
  - A sewage plant may appear as an Olympic swimming pool — the job is to tear apart that image, not accept it
  - Ask behavioral proxy questions: "Would I swim in it? No. Why not? Taste it — horrible smell, something spinning, fluid with material floating."
  - Never name the target; only describe base elements; let the analyst make the final identification
- **Sketch first**: Sketches come from a different brain area than verbal description
  - A bridge appears as two anchor shapes with repeating X-patterns in between; the viewer doesn't know it's a bridge but draws the structure
  - Sketch, then "retaste" the target, then put down analyzed elements — never commit to a conclusion
- **Monitor as external left brain**:
  - Remote viewer stays in intuitive/right-brain mode; monitor handles logical structure and asks clarifying questions
  - The monitor often functions psychically without knowing it — asking the exact right question to elicit the key answer
  - Both participants contribute to successful sessions, not just the viewer
- **Intent overrides blocking conditions**:
  - Certain conditions reduce psychic function — e.g., sun above the horizon and viewing toward it causes fourfold reduction; geolocation and time of day also matter
  - However: if intent is imperative (e.g., someone being tortured right now), the viewer will violate all blocking conditions and succeed anyway
  - All five or six people in an experiment must share intent; a disinterested monitor or indifferent observer degrades results
- **Ego removal**: "I tell my ego to run away — if I can't get my ego out of it, I'll put my notebook in my pocket and go cut grass."

## Near-Death Experience (1970)

- **Poisoned in Passau, Germany**: McMoneagle was delivered DOA to a clinic with no detectable heartbeat for approximately 40 minutes
  - Was at a guest house in Austria with his wife when he began to feel ill during pre-dinner drinks; rushed outside
  - Immediately found himself standing on a cobblestone road in soft rain, rain passing through his palms — first sign he was out of body
  - Watched his friend perform improvised chest compressions (CPR didn't exist yet) — each punch brought him back into his body in pain, then back out again; cycle repeated 4-5 times
  - Friend and wife drove him fireman's carry through two Austria-Germany checkpoints, running both; arrived at Passau clinic at 8 PM (locked; friend kicked the glass door open)
  - Clinic doctor was partially retired, arrived in a wheelchair — "getting better every minute," McMoneagle thought while watching from outside his body
- **The white light**: While watching his own resuscitation under bright surgical lights, McMoneagle felt heat at the back of his neck
  - Turned around and was enveloped in "extremely intense white light — perfect"; no fear, no pain, profound desire to stay
  - Voice in his head: "You can't stay, you have to go back." He looked for something to hold onto; second snap of fingers; sat upright on the table
  - German patient in the room ran from the room; later said McMoneagle's eyes had turned crystal blue with fire coming out of them
- **Post-NDE transformation and crisis**:
  - Stopped carrying a gun; no one would work with him (they feared he couldn't defend them); ended up working alone for years — didn't care because "dying is so what"
  - Six months of severe depression: contemplated 15 different methods of suicide; existence felt like being stuck in a "primitive backwoods back-alley regressive place where nobody understands"
  - Entity intervention: woken in the middle of the night; entity "snatched him out of his body and speed-slapped him" verbally — "What are you, that stupid? You know what's going to happen when you die. Why aren't you teaching people in this place?"
  - Next morning: completely transformed; "I don't care anymore" — did his job well regardless of posting or assignment

## Stargate Program Origins and Operations

- **1972 origins**: [[russell-targ]] and [[hal-puthoff]] at [[stanford-research-institute]] tested [[pat-price]], a retired police commissioner who claimed psychic abilities
  - Test target: an old cabin in the woods belonging to a CIA man who was not present during the viewing
  - Price described a clandestine intelligence site near the cabin — not the cabin itself
  - Described the interior: five-door safes; named classified project names inside the top safe door; all confirmed true
- **Blue Book of successes**: Two binders containing 160 Stargate operational successes; McMoneagle's specialty was ICBM sites — large areas where rockets were underground with surface shielding visible in overhead imagery
  - Remote viewing value: overhead photos show surface; matching the surface to the drawing meant 80% ground-truth of underground structure, enabling targeted follow-up with other sensors
- **[[rosemary-smith|Rosemary Smith]] / Tu-22 Bear Bomber**:
  - Soviet Tu-22 strategic bomber downed over Africa; entire continent given as search area to a California psychic named Rosemary Smith
  - Three psychics in three different cities independently drew circles on maps; when brought together, all three circles interlocked
  - [[jimmy-carter]] held the green-stripe "[[stargate-program|Grill Flame]]" briefing file while announcing the plane had been found in Zaire; credited psychics on national television, blowing the program's cover name
  - [[harley-trent]] described the plane as "parked on a road" — analysts didn't realize he meant rivers (roads in central Africa are rivers); the plane was on a river
  - The bomber was carrying two city-busting nuclear weapons; every terrorist organization in the world was looking for it
- **TK-089 Submarine (1979)** — most significant operational success:
  - McMoneagle and [[harley-trent]] both tasked independently; described an unprecedented submarine design
  - Two submarines merged together; high-intensity laser welding; 20 slanted ICBM tubes (first sub capable of launching while moving); each missile carried 5 independently guidable warheads, 50 megatons each
  - Submarine housed in a large inland building with no visible water connection; railroad tracks bringing in heavy materials; building large enough to contain the Mall of America without touching the walls
  - McMoneagle estimated the submarine was approximately 30 feet shorter than a Soviet aircraft carrier (~850 feet); final measurement confirmed 33 feet off
  - Robert Gates (then CIA head analyst, later CIA Director and Defense Secretary) dismissed it as "total fantasy" and sent back the report
  - McMoneagle added a note: "will be launched in 112 days" — Admiral Jake Stewart arranged satellite photography 114 days later
  - Photos confirmed: side of the building removed; trench cut to harbor; submarine tied to an aircraft carrier to block harbor entrance view; two icebreakers had cleared the frozen Arctic harbor
  - Intelligence collected in 4 days on that one submarine exceeded all prior Soviet submarine intelligence history
  - Sub disappeared, never seen again; eight more were built; the US defeated the weapons system because they could now identify and track it
- **MX Missile Program**: White paper sent to White House Situation Room stating "won't work — we can tell you where it is every time"
  - Program involved 30,000 miles of railroad tracks, hundreds of identical cargo cars, one in 20 containing an ICBM launcher shifted weekly — a shell game with hydrogen bombs
  - Stargate proved they could locate the missile every time it moved; tested for a year with a live nuclear weapon; US government cancelled the $100 billion program
  - Senator William Cohen sent a thank-you letter for saving the $100 billion; Air Force was furious
- **No defense against remote viewing**: Program brought in October 1978; by July 1979, determined there was no defense against remote viewing
  - Spent 20+ years and millions of dollars trying to develop a countermeasure; none found
  - Program failed 40% of the time — but all intelligence modalities fail; it was not inferior, and was better than other methods proportionally

## Soviet / Russian Remote Viewing Program

- **Russian mouse experiment flaw** (McMoneagle's analysis presented to pharmaceutical companies):
  - 24 mice randomly numbered by a man using a random number generator; some designated "target" (anxiety to be induced) and some "control"
  - Psychics in Siberia tasked to identify which mice were which; mice shipped to another lab, killed, brain chemistry analyzed for anxiety markers
  - Top 12 (highest anxiety) matched target mice exactly; bottom 12 were controls; done three times, nearly perfect
  - **The flaw**: The only person functioning psychically was the man who sorted the mice — he was the only one who knew which were target and control; he sorted them psychically, giving the "psychics" their answer at the end
  - The trained psychics were not doing anything; the selection process itself was the psychic act
  - McMoneagle uses this to explain why pharmaceutical drug trials fail: somewhere in the selection process there is a psychic giving researchers exactly what they want to see, creating false positives
- **Russian psychics trained to target US officials**: Using the flawed methodology, they attempted to influence US Senators, Cabinet members, and the President; it did not work because the methodology was broken from the start
- **Materials obtained via perestroika**: US researchers met Russian counterparts, obtained a full box of Russian psychic program documentation; analysis revealed the structural flaw

  - Inhabitants were 12-foot-tall humanoid beings
  - An atmosphere-destroying catastrophe (possibly a large impactor) forced an exodus

  - This may explain the Martian genetic contribution to human ancestry — "I'm not sure we aren't aliens"
- **Physical evidence he cites**:

  - Structures include: the shard on the crater rim; buildings in rows; the subterranean cavity with apparent infrastructure

## Himiko Japan Remote Viewing

  - Ruled for 76 years in total peace
  - Converted Japan from hunter-gatherer to rice culture, locking warlords to the land and ending territorial warfare
  - Was a shaman who could apparently read minds; warlords who planned to act against her found she would arrive before they could execute their plans
- **Remote viewing tasking**: Hired by the Noai Group (Okinawa-based organization that attempts to demythologize recorded Japanese mythology through physical evidence)

  - Results: found what they believe is her Summer Palace, Winter Palace, Temple, mother's Temple, and burial mountain
  - The remote viewing sessions "de-mythed" the legend by identifying physical locations corresponding to his descriptions
- **Significance**: One of McMoneagle's most detailed historical remote viewing targets; spans Japan's most pivotal cultural transformation
