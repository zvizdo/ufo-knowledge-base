# "Aliens Are Humans From The Future!" (Ft. Mike Masters & David Grusch)

American Alchemy episode featuring [[michael-p-masters]] (biological anthropologist, Montana Tech) presenting the **Extratemporalis model** — the theoretical framework that NHI/UAP phenomena are explained by humans from the future who have mastered time travel. [[david-grusch]] contributes via inserted clips with material-science and hardware claims.

## Headline Claims

1. **Future humans hypothesis**: UAP visitors are biologically modern humans who evolved further over thousands of years and mastered time travel; convergent biological evidence (neoteny, reproductive compatibility, breath-ability of air, linguistic capacity) strongly disfavors an extraterrestrial origin.
2. **PEER / Edgar Mitchell study**: Of ~5,000 contactee/abductee experiencer respondents, 52% encountered humanoid beings; of those, 85% had friendly or neutral experiences — the human-majority-friendly pattern fits the future-human model more than an alien one.
3. **Intertemporal breeding program**: Reproductive gamete extraction is the most ubiquitous theme in abduction literature; future humans may face a ~60% reduction in male sperm count (mirroring present 50–60% decline in 40 years) and return to the past to obtain viable genetic material.
4. **Tipler cylinder/disc physics**: Frank Tipler's 1970s work shrank the time-machine concept to a finite rapidly-rotating disc — exactly the form factor of disc/saucer UAP. Frame-dragging (general relativity) allows closed timelike curves, consistent with UAP appearance, rotation, and time-dilation effects reported near craft.
5. **Jim Penniston / Rendlesham 1980**: Penniston's contact experience included a message that the visitors "are you, we come from 8,000 years in the future" — primary textual confirmation of the future-human claim within a well-documented military UAP incident.
6. **George Hoover & Herman Oberth quotations**: Hoover (Navy Commander, top-secret clearance) admitted Roswell beings were time-traveling humans; Oberth said UFOs "jump from one time-space coordinate to another" rather than traversing space.
7. **Masters' personal contact**: Masters describes a firsthand encounter at a Phoenix UFO conference (approximately one year before taping) where two beings confirmed he was a future human, knew his unexpressed thought about quitting, and telepathically loaded three pieces of partitioned information into his brain — which he cannot access consciously.
8. **Electromagnetic / EM anomalies**: UAP correlate with documented electromagnetic/geomagnetic anomalies across 200 years; Skinwalker Ranch shows persistent EM activity; portal-like locations may serve as departure/arrival points for time machines.
9. **Material analysis (Grusch)**: Recovered craft materials include heavy elements europium and californium in unusual atomic arrangements within a ceramic-metal hull structure — understood via X-ray diffraction shadow imaging; emergent metamaterial properties not yet understood.
10. **Timeline wars hypothesis**: Multiple future-human factions or civilizations may be competing for control of the timeline, with different intents — some benevolent (the Extratemporalis model's "guides"), some extractive or adversarial.

## Open Questions Raised

- If future humans are telepathically connected (hive mind), does that imply a transition away from individualism — and when/how does it happen?
- Are some UAP entities bio-engineered avatars rather than actual future humans? (Masters: his Revelation book explores "robotic Droid avatars" grown via advanced genetic manipulation.)
- What is the relationship between the different future-human factions? Are some benevolent guides and others adversarial timeline-managers?
- Why is gamete extraction less commonly reported in modern abduction cases than in 1970s–90s cases?
- Do electromagnetic portal locations (Skinwalker Ranch, etc.) correlate with known tectonic or geological features?
- Is the "dye in the pipe" / confirmatory feedback model (Wargo) compatible with Masters' claims of deliberate information partitioning?
- Can the Tipler disc time machine be physically scaled down to UAP size within materials we have now, or does it require exotic matter?
