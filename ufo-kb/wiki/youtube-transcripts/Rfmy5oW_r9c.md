# "The NSA Hired Me To Speak To Aliens!" — Sergeant Dan Sherman

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] long-form interview with retired USAF Technical Sergeant [[dan-sherman]], author of *[[above-black]]*. Sherman claims he served as an NSA "intuitive communicator" (IC) under [[project-preserve-destiny]] — a classified program in which he telepathically received and relayed transmissions from alien beings to his handler chain. Host: [[jesse-michaels]]. This is notable as the first podcast-format, unedited, informal conversation Sherman has done (all prior interviews were heavily edited Q&A).

## Headline Claims

1. **Project Preserve Destiny existed.** An NSA program, nested inside a conventional ELINT black project, recruited "intuitive communicators" — humans genetically pre-conditioned by aliens — to receive and relay alien transmissions. Sherman's IC code was `Staunch 118`.
2. **Genetic pre-conditioning by NHI.** Sherman was told that aliens abducted his pregnant mother in the early 1960s and altered his fetal genetics specifically to enable the telepathic faculty the NSA would later exploit. This positions him as an [[nhi-genetic-modification]] subject by proxy.
3. **Two alien contacts, named "Spock" and "Bones".** Sherman received transmissions from two distinct entities (distinguishable by "tapestry" or "flavor" of communication). Each could shut down communication when a disallowed subject was raised.
4. **Abduction monitoring role.** Toward the end of his service, Sherman began receiving reports that he interpreted as abduction activity across the US — location potentials, pain levels, and recall indicators — making him feel like an "accessory to a crime."
5. **Multiple alien races, human-alien interbreeding.** Aliens confirmed (via Bones) that: (a) there are many alien races; (b) they have experimented with human-alien interbreeding; (c) they distinguish intelligent from non-intelligent creation; (d) they refer to humans as "water beings/vessels."
6. **Propulsion via electromagnetic field manipulation akin to Alcubierre warp.** Aliens described travel using EM energy manipulation — creating a void ahead of the craft and falling into it — consistent with [[gravity-manipulation-propulsion]] / Alcubierre drive.
7. **NSA FOIA response is "fairly telling".** Independent researcher Noah Hradek submitted FOIA requests for PPD files. Air Force redirected the request to NSA; NSA invoked Executive Order 13526 (classified in the interest of national defense) — precisely the order that should not apply to a non-existent program.

## Sherman Background

Sherman grew up in extreme poverty, attending 27 schools between kindergarten and 12th grade as his welder father moved frequently. At age ~10, he met an SR-71 pilot who boarded a horse at the stable they managed; the pilot's conversations about Air Force service left a permanent impression. No military family background.

He joined the Air Force as a security policeman (any-available-job entry), then cross-trained into ELINT after 4-5 years. Total service: 12 years. Stations: Korea, Italy, Colorado, North Dakota, Nebraska, Texas; TDY at NSA headquarters (Maryland), Philippines, Japan. He has a DD214 verifiable service record and NSA completion certificate for the EA280 Intermediate Electronics Intelligence course.

His mother had been told she would never carry a baby to term after a childhood accident. Sherman was her only child who survived — a detail he later correlates with the PPD briefing.

## NSA Briefing — How Sherman Was Read In

Sherman traveled to the NSA complex in Laurel, Maryland for the EA280 continuation course. A captain called him directly — unusual for enlisted — and told him to come to the NSA main building. The captain escorted him through security (Sherman's clearances hadn't transferred yet; captain signed him in) to a private office.

The captain described Project Preserve Destiny in flat, businesslike terms:
- Program originated in 1947 (first alien contact) and began operational fetal modification work around 1960
- Aliens were given access (or took it) to genetically modify selected fetuses to grow with heightened IC ability
- Sherman's mother was abducted twice: first in the early 1960s for testing/selection; again in 1963 during her pregnancy for the actual genetic modification
- Underprivileged demographics were selected specifically because their offspring were statistically more likely to enter the military, enabling controlled program management
- Sherman's IC ability would make him useful in a "future event" requiring ground-level telepathic communication

Sherman's immediate reaction was numbness, then the realization that the NSA headquarters was not where hazing occurs: "This is not possible to happen. And then it did happen."

## IC Training — Underground School

Sherman was transported daily after ELINT school in a blue van with blacked-out windows. He could not see the route. The van parked in what appeared to be an interior garage or building annex; he entered through a door with palm scanner and possibly retina scanner, then descended by elevator (one button) to a room with two computer consoles.

A second student sat at the adjacent console. They were never to speak — not even a grunt. Sherman took two pills (later reduced to one) before each session, given no explanation.

**Training protocol:**
- 10 sine-wave boxes on the screen, each corresponding to a distinct tone
- Sherman mentally hummed each tone while staring at the corresponding box until the sine wave "clicked" — described as a camera suddenly focusing, or a sudden mental-visual integration
- Once clicked, the box was "unlocked" and could be influenced by mental attention alone
- All 10 boxes were unlocked over several days of ~3-4 hour sessions with no bathroom breaks
- After unlocking, the instructors fed information (numbers, descriptions, images) through the headphones and measured the corresponding response in the boxes

The "clicking" sensation was the discovery that mind-matter separation was not absolute. Sherman had suspected an electromagnetic connection between the wired headphones and the screen; this was disproved later when he operated without headphones.

**Pills:** At school, two pills (no taste; no explanation given). Later, one pill at his operational post. Sherman sometimes forgot to take the pill and the communications continued regardless — suggesting experimentation rather than a hard biological dependency. His interpretation: they were trying different dosing configurations in parallel with the IC training.

**Typing format:** When a communication arrived, Sherman opened a dialogue box labeled "Staunch 118" followed by a 5-6 digit code he treated as a zip code. Each discrete concept received in the tapestry was typed, then separated by a slash before the next concept. Communications could carry: numbers, imagery, embedded emotional color, smell, and any other sensory mode that Sherman could translate into text. His role was transcription; he did not know what the NSA was doing with the output.

Sherman's self-model: humans are "carrier waves" and IC is a different encoding method on that carrier — the way ELINT stations encode intelligence on radio carrier waves. The IC channel runs on a different modality than thought or speech, accessed via the "mental humming" process that unlocked each sine-wave box. Once unlocked, that faculty operated automatically and needed no tonal stimulus to maintain.

Jesse connects this architecture to [[robert-sarbacher|Robert Sarbacher]] — [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]]'s heir who ran national labs R&D and wrote *Ultra High Frequency Engineering* before coming out to UFO researchers in 1987 saying "the UFO rumors are substantially true." Jesse speculates Sarbacher's vector into UFOs may have been EM communication modalities with NHI rather than propulsion.

The company "Noise Cancellation Technologies" (NCT) in Linthicum, Maryland appeared to be connected: Sherman once followed the second (white) van from the school and watched it enter NCT's facility. NCT had multiple DARPA contracts (~$210K, June 1991, exactly when Sherman trained), conducted NASA and Army acoustics research, and described its work as "active wave management applications for sound signal wave manipulation." Linthicum is ~15 minutes from Offutt Air Force Base and near Fort Meade.
