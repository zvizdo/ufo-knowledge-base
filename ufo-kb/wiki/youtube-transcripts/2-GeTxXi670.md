# "This CIA Scientist Led Alien Contact Ceremonies for Elites" — Greg Malazzi / Mind Traveler Documentary

[[jesse-michaels]] interviews [[greg-malazzi]], filmmaker behind the documentary *[[mind-traveler-documentary|Mind Traveler]]* about the life of [[andrija-puharich]] (also referred to as "[[andrija-puharich|Andre Puharich]]" or "Bhar" throughout). Malazzi spent nearly a decade investigating Puharich's archives, hundreds of previously unheard tape recordings, personal journals, and locating five former "[[space-kids|Space Kids]]" for interviews.

## Puharich's Background and Intelligence Recruitment

- **Army Specialized Training Program (1947)**: Puharich was recruited into this program as a medical student at [[northwestern-university|Northwestern University]]
  - Same program included Henry Kissinger and Kurt Vonnegut
  - Puharich almost never mentioned it; may have been his first step into the military/intelligence world
  - Program appeared to prospect promising individuals in specific academic categories

- **Mentor at Northwestern — [[warren-mcculloch|Warren S. McCulloch]]**: McCulloch conducted ESP and radio frequency research on the side while maintaining mainstream academic cover
  - Received correspondence from CIA, Navy, and other agencies who sought his expertise
  - Letters from McCulloch to those agencies recommended Puharich as worth watching
  - McCulloch was "much older" and took Puharich under his wing
  - [[annie-jacobsen|Annie Jacobsen]] was researching McCulloch's archives during the documentary

- **Funding chain for [[roundtable-foundation|Roundtable Foundation]] (1948–1957)**:
  - [[henry-a-wallace|Henry A. Wallace]] (FDR's Vice President): gave first large grant after being "impressed by his quest for telepathy"
  - Heirs to Astor and DuPont families as additional benefactors
  - Puharich's stated funding story did not match documentary evidence — actual funders were Army, Navy, CIA, and an Army-linked "Armor Foundation"
  - Letters from all of these agencies found in Puharich's archives

- **[[roundtable-foundation|Roundtable Foundation]]**: 50-room house on coast of Rockland, Maine; large barn converted into lab
  - Staff, state-of-the-art equipment, Faraday cage experiments
  - [[aldous-huxley|Aldous Huxley]] frequent visitor; called Puharich "the greatest mind in parapsychology" (blurb on *The Sacred Mushroom*)
  - Experiments: ESP card guessing, remote viewing (before it had that name), psychedelic mushroom research (Amanita muscaria → ESP connection)
  - Orb phenomenon: psychic at Roundtable would predict exact time an orb would appear over ocean; Puharich wrote this in his journals; early 1950s documentation of orbs
  - Closed ~1957: officially for lack of funding; Puharich actually moved on to Intellect Corp; Army records show continued funding throughout

## The Nine — First Contact (1952)

- **Dr. Vinod**: Indian psychic, friend of Gandhi, professor at University of Bombay; came to Roundtable during US lecture tour on theosophy/spiritualism
  - First night at Roundtable, unexpectedly fell into trance; tape recorder was grabbed
  - Began speaking as "[[the-nine|The Nine]]": "We are the positive principles of cosmos. We penetrate, visit persons. We work through this study but it is under our control. We nine."
  - Vinod never mentioned his Gandhi friendship or academic background — Puharich never spoke of him again after he returned to India in ~1954

- **What [[the-nine|the Nine]] said**:
  - Described themselves as "laws or principles of the universe, not personalities"
  - Monitoring Earth; concerned about nuclear weapons and wars; wanted to help humanity not destroy itself
  - Said they were behind UAP sightings at nuclear bases (corroborating broader pattern)
  - Gave specific technical instructions for improving Faraday cage construction (copper, specific dimensions, specific metals)
  - Instructions worked: sessions without Faraday cage were stalled and halting; Faraday cage sessions were "boom, they would just talk"
  - Thousands of pages of transcripts produced; also contained complex equations

- **New Year's Eve 1952–53 seance**: Nine specific "blue-blooded elites" at Roundtable
  - Attendees: DuPont heir, Astor heir, Forbes heir, and others representing old American money
  - [[arthur-young|Arthur Young]] (inventor of the Bell helicopter) and wife **[[ruth-forbes-payne-young|Ruth Forbes Payne Young]]** (née Forbes)
  - Mary Bancroft ([[allen-dulles|Allen Dulles]]'s mistress) was close with Ruth Forbes Payne
  - **JFK connection**: Ruth Forbes Payne's daughter was [[ruth-payne|Ruth Payne]] → took in [[lee-harvey-oswald|Lee Harvey Oswald]] and his wife Marina → got Oswald his job at Texas School Book Depository → [[1963-jfk-assassination|JFK assassination]]
  - Lenda (researcher cited by Malazzi): "Everyone at this seance was entangled with the [[1963-jfk-assassination|JFK assassination]]"
  - Nine coincided with July 1952 Washington DC UFO flyovers — same year, possible connection

## Intellect Corporation (Early 1960s)

- **Funding**: $300,000+ grant from Atomic Energy Commission (documented) — "over a million dollars in today's money"
  - Navy, CIA, and others also involved as at Roundtable
  - Front company: offices in Hell's Kitchen, NYC; Puharich moved family to mansion in Ossining, NY

- **TD-100 Device ("Transdermal 100")**: Breakthrough medical device ostensibly for the deaf
  - Could emit a specific frequency that bypassed the ear canal entirely and reached hearing centers in the brain directly
  - Demonstrated at [[pentagon|the Pentagon]]: general fitted with device, man in hallway broadcasting — "God damn it, I hear it. God damn it, I hear it. The bastard did it."
  - Went completely dark after ~1968; Puharich never spoke of it until late in life
  - Later discovered: used secretly on [[space-kids|Space Kids]] without their knowledge

- **Tooth implant variant**: Metal filling inserted by a dentist that acted as radio receiver
  - Signal transmitted through facial nerves to brain hearing centers
  - Origin discovery: mental patient claiming to hear voices; family said he was sane; Puharich placed him in Faraday cage → voices stopped → re-opened door → voices returned → proved it was radio transmission through metal in his teeth
  - "We found out he was tuned to station W in New York"
  - The dentist who ran Intellect with Puharich was someone he met in the Army

- **Direct thought transmission (second variant)**: Bypassed the tooth entirely; specific radio frequency (classified) sent directly to the head
  - Experiment filmed: woman on other side of a wall, Puharich using TD-100; clearly working on the footage
  - This was "proto-Neuralink" in the early 1960s

- **[[jose-delgado|Jose Delgado]] connection**: Puharich was close friends with Delgado; letters found between them
  - Delgado: implanted radio receivers in bulls' brains, demonstrated remote control of the animal on film
  - The obvious implication: what Delgado did to bulls, Intellect was doing with humans
  - People warned Malazzi "don't go there" regarding the Puharich-Delgado connection

- **JFK assassination day**: Puharich's assistant witnessed men in suits come to Intellect offices, speak with Puharich; he was "out of sorts" for the rest of the day
  - Malazzi's theory: Intellect's mind-transmission research was connected to [[lee-harvey-oswald|Lee Harvey Oswald]] or [[jack-ruby|Jack Ruby]] via [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]]
  - [[jack-ruby|Jack Ruby]]: [[mk-ultra|MK Ultra]] patient — letters between [[jolly-west|Jolly West]] and [[sidney-gottlieb|Sidney Gottlieb]]; West visited Ruby alone in jail cell, came out saying "psychiatric break"; Ruby never remembered killing Oswald
  - [[sirhan-sirhan|Sirhan Sirhan]]: wrote "I must kill him" repeatedly before RFK assassination, doesn't remember doing it
  - William Jennings Bryan (hypnotist) allegedly tight with [[sirhan-sirhan|Sirhan Sirhan]]
  - Dulles: Mary Bancroft started to talk about [[ruth-payne|Ruth Payne]] being at Dulles's summer house right before JFK assassination; Dulles acted as if he didn't know what was going on

## Uri Geller Experiments (1971–mid 1970s)

- **How Puharich went to Israel**: Geller himself and others said Puharich was sent by CIA; Puharich publicly claimed a parapsychology foundation bought him a ticket
  - FBI monitored all of Puharich's movements in Israel — pages and pages of surveillance logs (found via FOIA)
  - Multiple former associates told Malazzi: "He was Mossad"
  - Written admission in letter to close confidant: participated in Israeli-US mind control program at Aberdeen Proving Grounds (Army base)
  - On a tape: jokingly says "I'm a double agent for the Israelis"
  - Aharon Yariv (head of Israeli army intelligence) named repeatedly in channeling tapes: "Should we give this information to Yariv?"

- **The Nine return through Geller (1971)**: Puharich hypnotized Geller in Israel → Nine re-emerged for first time since ~1954
  - Puharich was still running Intellect (thought-transmission research) simultaneously
  - Geller's Nine sessions happened through hypnosis protocol (counting down, leading questions)
  - Some sessions had leading questions; other sessions were completely open-ended — Nine came through in both
  - [[itzhak-bentov|Itzhak Bentov]]: Israeli scientist, close to both Puharich and Geller; photographed together in Tel Aviv apartment; worked for Israeli intelligence; died in plane crash (AA 191, 1979)

- **Anomalous Geller stories** (from Puharich's book, possibly manufactured):
  - **Teleportation**: Geller walking in NYC, felt weird feeling, woke up in Ossining 50 miles away having crashed through a screen porch; Puharich filmed the smashed screen porch as evidence

  - **Wernher von Braun Roswell material**: Von Braun showed Geller a piece of metal from a crashed UFO; Geller said "Vera, this is not from here"; von Braun: "You're right, this is a piece of a UFO that crashed on our planet"

- **Malazzi's interpretation of Geller**: Guinea pig for Intellect mind-transmission experiments
  - "If you can get somebody to believe they've been teleported through a screen porch, what else could you get them to believe?"
  - Geller in 2019 radio interview: admitted Puharich slipped him mushrooms, ran extensive hypnosis; "I was scared, I was suspicious, I just wanted to get away from this guy"
  - Geller had (and probably has) genuine psychic ability — bent spoon for Malazzi during documentary interview; Malazzi can't explain how it was done

## Space Kids Program (1974–late 1970s)

- **Origin**: After Geller became famous on TV, children worldwide spontaneously bent spoons while watching him
  - UK, US, and China all had reports; parents calling TV stations saying "my kid just bent a spoon"
  - Puharich visited cases, became convinced of genuine spontaneous emergence of psychic ability
  - The "contagion of psychic powers" — children activated by seeing Geller on TV

- **How the program worked**: Puharich lectured around the country; approximately a dozen children (ages 18–mid-20s, some younger) approached him saying they had abilities
  - He invited them to his Ossining house (now called "Lab 9")
  - All wore copper bracelets — per Nine's original 1952 instructions at Roundtable
  - Hypnosis protocol: Nine came through even through kids who had never heard of the Nine
  - SRRI pay stub found in Puharich's archives: 1973 payment from SRI to Lab 9 — proves SRRI was paying for remote viewing research using children
  - Puthoff confirmed visiting Ossining and hearing about the Nine; denied Puharich was truly involved in SRI's program (pay stub disproves this)

- **Actual sessions**: Included remote viewing of sensitive Soviet sites
  - "What time is the best to remote view the Kremlin?" — on sessions from which visitors were excluded
  - "What code name are Russian guerrilla soldiers using?" — clearly intelligence gathering, not spiritual contact
  - When non-intelligence visitors were present: Nine would give feel-good cosmic messages about helping humanity
  - When they were alone: operational intelligence questions
  - On many tapes: reference to "coupling to the computer database" — suggestions of a computer operator typing input

- **Puharich's prosthetic finger device**: Invented chip that emitted specific ELF frequency
  - Touch forehead with device → instant hypnotic trance induction
  - Used during Space Kids experiments
  - Space Kid Haime (Mexico City): Puharich's assistant was meditating with him → she touched his forehead → blue energy ball appeared → both were thrown across the room → he has no memory of the interval
  - Both Haime and the assistant corroborated this story independently

- **[[gene-roddenberry|Gene Roddenberry]] connection**: Attended multiple Nine channeling sessions with Puharich and [[phyllis-schlemmer|Phyllis Schlemmer]]
  - "I've said many times in the past" — indicated this was not his first channeling session
  - Wrote 130-page script called "The Nine" about his experience with Puharich; script fell apart due to Roddenberry's personal difficulties
  - Deep Space Nine: character named "Venad" traces directly to the 1952 channeler Dr. Vinod
  - Deep Space Nine post-dates these sessions — clear connection

- **[[gordon-cooper|Gordon Cooper]] connection**: Mercury astronaut visited Ossining "many times"; witnessed Faraday cage and Space Kids experiments; wrote about it in his book
  - Had his own UFO sightings (formation of saucer-shaped metallic objects without wings at high altitude)

- **Journeys to Egypt and Middle East**: Nine ordered Puharich to specific locations (Egypt, Russia, Middle East)
  - John Whitmore (British collaborator): "You normally could not get into these countries at this time, yet we were going constantly because the Nine told us to"
  - Puharich meditated in the King's Chamber of the [[great-pyramid|Great Pyramid]] in 1976; brought Space Kids for experiments there
  - Nine were specifically [[the-nine|nine Egyptian gods]] — Puharich was obsessive about this connection
  - Cover theory: Puharich was doing actual intelligence work under cover of "the Nine told us to come here"

## Puharich's Death (January 1995)

- **Suspicious circumstances**: Found dead at bottom of stairs in North Carolina house
  - Psychic women who always lived with him were all absent that day (unusual)
  - Family unsure whether fall or illness killed him — was sick but "not sick enough for this to happen"

- **Legal pad written just before death**: Listed names of people he believed were monitoring/planning to harm him
  - Claimed an ELF emitter had been installed in his television set; would be hit when he turned it on
  - Tested himself daily with his own medical knowledge
  - "Either the work of someone completely paranoid or someone totally telling the truth"

- **Prior CIA confrontation**: CIA approached Puharich to head [[elf-weapons|ELF weapons]] research department in early 1990s
  - [[bob-beck|Bob Beck]] came to his house on CIA's behalf; Puharich refused and threw them out
  - This was years before death; then a series of escalating events

- **Public confessions just before death**: At parapsychology conference lectures: "I worked for this part of CIA; my boss was directly involved in hypnosis and mind manipulation"
  - Told Dick Russell (journalist) on tape: assistant present confirmed — "He told me about things he's done that he's worried for his life about"
  - "Using flashing lights, tones, all that stuff, he was able to create the UFO experience"
  - Assistant: "That stuff was all created"

- **1995 coincidences**:
  - January 1995: Puharich found dead
  - January 1995: [[stargate-program|Stargate program]] officially terminated
  - February 1995: [[jacobo-grinberg|Jacobo Grinberg]] disappeared
  - Government newspaper articles immediately put out saying Stargate "was all bunk"
  - Puharich was publicly lecturing about CIA involvement and Stargate at precisely this time

## Malazzi's Analytical Framework

- **Was the Nine genuine ET contact or synthetic psyop?**
  - Evidence for synthetic: Faraday cage construction instructions (specific, technical, verifiable); hypnotic leading questions on many sessions; operational intelligence-gathering questions on private sessions; reference to "coupling to computer database"
  - Evidence for genuine: sessions where Nine came through without any leading; open-ended sessions with same result; orb predictions in 1952 that came true; Puharich's family witnessed genuine UFO appearance in North Carolina late 1980s (multiple skeptical witnesses; video attempted but camera not out)
  - Malazzi's conclusion: "The evidence points to the latter [synthetic]" — but genuinely uncertain; "I was in this constant flip-flop for years"

- **Puharich's character**: "He's the most jovial funny guy on camera — not a monster person. He's complicated."
  - Was genuinely interested in paranormal phenomena AND got sucked into intelligence work
  - "I think he got under the thumb of the intelligence world early on and just couldn't get away. Like the mafia — you don't just leave."
  - Near the end of his life, admitted to it and warned: "Don't do what I did"
  - His own family discovered things from the documentary they had never known

## The 1960s Documentational Black Hole

- **Near-total absence of records**: Puharich's 1948–1958 Roundtable period is well-documented; his 1977–1995 tapes period is rich; but the 1963–1970 window has almost no photographs and very few personal journal entries
  - Malazzi: "You go to look for this period — there's essentially nothing"
  - Intellect Corp patents and Atomic Energy Commission documentation constitute the only paper trail from this decade
  - Former associates from this period are dead or refuse to speak
  - One associate declined to discuss it, saying "I've agreed not to discuss that period" — refused to say who made him agree

- **Known Intellect Corp work (1963–1968)**:
  - TD-100 demonstrations at [[pentagon|the Pentagon]] and other government facilities
  - Tooth implant experiments documented via the "W station" radio-voice incident
  - Direct-thought-transmission films (some footage reportedly survives in a private archive)
  - This constitutes proto-brain-computer interface research predating all public BCI development by roughly six decades

- **Why the gap exists**: Malazzi believes Puharich was running classified human-subjects experiments under Atomic Energy Commission cover; the "medical device for the deaf" framing was public deniability; the absence of records is itself evidence of classification status

## Wilson Green, Army Psychological Warfare, and the Sarfatti Pipeline

- **Wilson Green**: Head of Army psychological warfare research; appeared in Puharich's 1953 DC journal entries
  - Puharich attended a meeting at which Green and unnamed intelligence figures discussed "directed mental influence on operatives"
  - Green's department was exploring whether ESP and remote influence could provide strategic advantage — Puharich was a key scientific asset
  - The same name surfaces independently in the origin story of [[jack-sarfatti|Jack Sarfatti]]

- **[[jack-sarfatti|Jack Sarfatti]]'s metallic voice call**:
  - As a teenager in a GATE gifted-children program, Sarfatti received a call from a metallic voice claiming to be a computer aboard a spacecraft
  - The voice told him he was "one of 400 children selected for a project"
  - Researchers have confirmed Wilson Green's department ran outreach to GATE students during this era
  - Sarfatti's description — "metallic voice" — is identical to Geller's Nine communications; possibly the same TD-100 or precursor technology

- **The intelligence pipeline pattern**:
  - Identify psychic-adjacent gifted children via GATE and similar programs → track through elite academic institutions → recruit into intelligence-adjacent programs
  - Sarfatti studied under Hans Bethe at Cornell, became embedded in the physics-intelligence nexus, and attended Puharich Nine sessions at Ossining in the 1970s
  - Space Kids recruitment followed the same template: Puharich personally screening children who spontaneously bent spoons after seeing Geller on television

## 1978 Arson: Lab 9 and the Bentov Connection

- **The event**: Lab 9 (Puharich's Ossining house) burned to the ground in 1978; arson confirmed; perpetrators never identified
  - Puharich lost irreplaceable research materials, journals, Space Kids experimental records, and channeling transcripts
  - He relocated to Mexico shortly afterward and began his collaboration with [[jacobo-grinberg|Jacobo Grinberg]]

- **Timing relative to Bentov and Geller**:
  - [[itzhak-bentov|Itzhak Bentov]] — Israeli scientist close to both Puharich and Geller — died in American Airlines Flight 191 (May 1979), the deadliest US aviation accident at that time
  - Geller publicly distanced himself from Puharich in 1978–1979, in this same window
  - Sequence: arson (1978) → Geller distancing → Bentov death (May 1979) → Puharich's relative public silence begins

- **Israeli intelligence dimension**:
  - Multiple associates told Malazzi Puharich was Mossad; letter in Puharich's own hand admits (jokingly) "I'm a double agent for the Israelis"
  - The arson could represent: CIA acting against a double agent, Mossad closing a compromised operation, or domestic agencies shutting down Lab 9's unauthorized human experiments
  - Malazzi: "It happened right as the Space Kids program would have been winding down — I don't think that's a coincidence"
  - Post-arson behavior is consistent with an operative "burned out of a program": fewer publications, reduced public profile, travel to remote locations
