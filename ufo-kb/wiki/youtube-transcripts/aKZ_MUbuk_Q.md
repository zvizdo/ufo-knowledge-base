# "Aliens Are Simulating Our Reality" — Riz Virk

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] episode hosted by [[jesse-michaels]] featuring [[riz-virk]], MIT- and Stanford-trained computer scientist and video-game entrepreneur, as the primary architect of a life-affirming, RPG-modelled simulation hypothesis.

## Headline Claims

1. **RPG model over NPC model**: Reality is best understood as a massively multiplayer online RPG in which souls voluntarily port into low-resolution physical avatars; the alternative NPC interpretation (everyone is just code) is dismissed as nihilistic and unsupported.
2. **Quantum indeterminacy = lazy evaluation**: Superposition of particles until observed maps directly onto standard video-game rendering — render only what the avatar can see. This is not merely metaphor; Virk argues it is the *best available explanation* for why the wave function collapses on observation.
3. **Reality's code is visible**: Fibonacci sequence in DNA and biology, golden ratio in spiral galaxies, electron/positron binary pairs, DNA base pairs (A–T, C–G) as bits — all point to a computational substrate using repeating optimised code.
4. **PEAR random-event-generator (REG) results are real**: [[bob-jahn]]'s Princeton lab produced 28 years of statistically robust data showing human intention skews quantum-random outputs. Jesse's anonymous hedge-fund friend ran that lab.
5. **Nuclear detonations may "break" the simulation**: High-energy events rip through abstraction layers toward the raw information substrate; [[green-fireballs]] appearing around atmospheric nuclear tests (documented by [[robert-hastings]]) and ufo clustering near CERN and Brookhaven may be caused by — or attracted to — simulation ruptures.
6. **UFOs as holographic projections that phase into physicality**: [[jacques-vallee]]'s redwood-tree case (UFO cut through 40-foot trees at 45° then became physical on landing) suggests objects that are rendered into the world rather than physically present throughout.
7. **Life review as simulation proof**: Near-death experiencers replay life from every perspective in holographic 3-D — if it can be replayed, it must be recorded. This is a computational necessity.
8. **Timeline optimization**: The universe (or its simulators) runs the simulation multiple times, trims suboptimal branches, and advances the best timeline — explaining deja vu, [[mandela-effect|Mandela Effect]], and why [[philip-k-dick]] believed simulators "chose" the World War II outcome timeline over the German-victory timeline he had written.
9. **Consciousness is not locally produced**: Evidence from morphic resonance ([[rupert-sheldrake]]), PEAR REGs, and extended electrodynamics suggests a client-server model of mind — individual brains are clients downloading from (and uploading to) a central consciousness server.
10. **Ancient mystical traditions encode the same architecture**: Seven heavens / seven palaces / seven veils across Egyptian, Jewish, Islamic, Hindu, and Buddhist traditions; identical breathing/visualization/password protocols; suggests common experiential access to the simulation's layer structure.

## Open Questions Raised

- Is the "optimization function" the simulators are running toward knowable? What are they optimizing for — highest consciousness, longest survival, most love?
- If UFOs are holographic projections that become physical on landing, who or what is doing the rendering?
- Can mind-matter interaction (PEAR REG data) be turned into usable technology? If not, why not?
- Are deja vu experiences evidence of timeline reruns, or is that unfalsifiable?
- What triggered the decision (if any) to "choose" the current World War II outcome timeline over the German-victory one?
- Is the Mandela Effect a genuine simulation-variable-change, or explainable by normal memory distortion?
- Do the seven-palace/seven-heaven architectures across unconnected cultures point to a genuine shared experiential access to simulation layers?
- Is [[nick-bostrom]]'s simulation argument actually evidence that we are in one, or does the Fermi paradox suggest civilizations self-destruct before reaching the ancestor-simulation stage?
- If Virk's client-server consciousness model is correct, what happens to identity when the "server" goes offline? Does it support or contradict traditional afterlife claims?
- Can the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] clustering observation be explained purely by increased surveillance near nuclear sites, or is the correlation robust after controlling for observer density?
- Has [[astrid-stuckelberger]]'s claim that CERN physicists told her entities come through portals been investigated or corroborated by any named physicist?
- [[bob-jahn]]'s PEAR lab reported 28 years of statistically robust REG data — how has this held up in independent replication attempts?
- Does timeline optimization require a designer-entity evaluating branch quality, or could the simulation prune timelines through a purely mechanical process such as minimum entropy?
