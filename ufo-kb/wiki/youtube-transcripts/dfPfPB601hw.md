# Graham Hancock: Aliens, Atlantis & the Apocalypse

Long-form interview on [[jesse-michaels]]'s [[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] podcast. Unusually wide-ranging for a Hancock appearance — covers not just the archaeological thesis but the Ark of Covenant Ethiopia investigation in depth, Hancock's Gnostic self-identification, his Ayahuasca-sourced novel, personal SSRI experience, directed panspermia, and the full Templar/Lalibela speculation.

## Headline Claims

- The date Plato gives for Atlantis's destruction (9,600 BC) aligns within ~20 years with **meltwater pulse 1B** — one of the most massive sea-level-rise events at the end of the last Ice Age. Hancock states this is not coincidence.
- Archaeology is only ~200 years old; every decade pushes civilization's roots further back. [[gobekli-tepe|Göbekli Tepe]] (~9,000 BC) required assembly of 50-ton blocks — not possible for casual hunter-gatherers.
- The Taurid meteor stream is becoming **more dangerous in the next 30 years** (per Dr. Alan West, Comet Research Group) — we are entering a denser, lumpier zone.
- The [[ark-of-the-covenant|Ark of the Covenant]] is plausibly in **Axum, Ethiopia** — guardians develop cataracts and die within ~3 years ("it is a thing of fire"); the Beta Israel (Ethiopian Jews) trace an unbroken chain from the Elephantine Island Jewish community (~650 BC) to [[lake-tana|Lake Tana]] to Axum.
- **[[james-bruce|James Bruce of Kinnaird]]** confirmed as a **Freemason** (Kill Winning Lodge No. 2, Edinburgh) — his journey to Ethiopia was likely motivated by Masonic interest in the Ark. Bruce brought back the **[[book-of-enoch|Book of Enoch]]**.
- **Lalibela** churches: an Ethiopian prince-in-exile in Crusader Jerusalem tipped the Templars off about the Ark; Templars installed him on the throne of Ethiopia; one Lalibela church has a **Templar cross on its ceiling and an image of Al-Aqsa Mosque with a cross on top** (only visible while Templars occupied it).
- Hancock identifies as **Gnostic** and believes the Gnostic transmission survived underground through Templars, Rosicrucians, Freemasons, and now diffuse carriers who no longer know what they're transmitting.
- In 2006, Hancock received the **entire plot of his novel *Entangled*** in five Ayahuasca sessions in Brazil — a direct creative gift from what he calls Mother Ayahuasca.
- [[pablo-amaringo]]'s "flying saucers" are "vehicles for entering and leaving the spirit world" — the most illuminating UFO framing available.
- [[francis-crick]] (in *[[life-itself]]*, 1989) concluded DNA could not have evolved on Earth in 100 million years and proposed **directed panspermia**: bacteria engineered by a dying civilization and launched in cryogenic spacecraft.
- [[chris-timmermann]] at [[imperial-college-london|Imperial College]] is now running **1-hour steady-state DMT sessions** in MRI scanners; entity encounters are phenomenologically consistent across hundreds of subjects — "normally when large numbers of people agree on the same experience, we define that as real."
- [[michael-shermer]] emailed Hancock after *[[ancient-apocalypse|Ancient Apocalypse]]* to say he had made a good case for "missing an episode of the human story."

## Michael Shermer, Archaeology Critics, and the Netflix Effect

- Shermer's personal anomalous experience: on his (then-fiancée Jennifer's) wedding night, a mechanical heirloom clock belonging to her late father — which had been silent and broken for ~20 years — spontaneously began playing; her father was supposed to walk her down the aisle; Shermer later wrote about this experience, stating that sometimes you have to allow anomalous mystical events to modify a skeptic's worldview.
- Post-*Ancient Apocalypse*, Shermer emailed Hancock saying he had "made a good case for missing an episode of the human story"; Hancock views this as evidence of Shermer's intellectual integrity — his ability to update when evidence demands it.
- Mainstream archaeology criticism: Hancock's primary critique is that conventional archaeologists respond to heterodox evidence not with "let's examine this" but with name-calling, ridicule, and deliberate exclusion; he argues they should compete "speculation versus speculation" rather than presenting their own unproven model as settled fact.
- Moore's Law for archaeology: every decade of excavation pushes the origin of civilization further back; taking the current consensus as final is therefore logically premature; the Sahara (4 million square miles), the Amazon (size of the Indian subcontinent), and deep ocean continental shelves remain largely unexamined.

## Personal SSRI Experience and Consciousness Thesis

- Hancock suffered depression and was prescribed SSRIs (Seroxat/Prozac); he describes them as "absolutely useless" in his personal experience; weaned himself off over six months by shaving pills with a razor blade, reducing the dose incrementally.
- He cites scientific studies showing SSRIs are no more effective than placebos; the serotonin-deficiency model of depression was recently publicly debunked after 50 years of clinical application.
- Core consciousness thesis: Hancock believes consciousness is a fundamental force in the universe, comparable to gravity — not a byproduct of brain activity; the brain is a "reducing valve" or filter on a default state of greater knowledge and wider reality.
- Physical life provides a "theater of experience" in which consciousness can make choices and grow — but consciousness is not reducible to its physical substrate.
- Psychedelics temporarily collapse the reducing-valve function, allowing access to the wider reality that consciousness normally filters; this is functionally equivalent to what the Eleusinian kykeon, Ayahuasca, and other ancient mystery-school substances accomplished.

## Vallee's Fairy-Alien Thesis and Hancock's Own DMT Encounter

- Hancock credits Jacques Vallee's *[[passport-to-magonia|Passport to Magonia]]* as the breakthrough text recognizing that medieval fairy/elf encounters and modern alien encounters are phenomenologically identical: both involve surgery on humans, abductions, sexual contact, hybrid offspring, and access to a non-material realm.
- Shamanic traditions document the same phenomenology going back tens of thousands of years in cave art: shamans routinely have sex with spirits, produce spirit-world children; Hancock knows an Amazon shaman whose wife left him because of his spirit-world wife and children.
- [[pablo-amaringo|Pablo Amaringo]]'s framing — "vehicles for entering and leaving the spirit world" — is Hancock's preferred UFO definition; it places the phenomenon in a parallel-dimension context rather than a materialist interstellar-travel context.
- Hancock had his own DMT entity encounter on an early Ayahuasca session in the Amazon: saw multiple flying saucers and a classic heart-faced large-dark-eyed alien materialized above him; received a message that they were going to take him; opened his eyes and said "no" out of fear; he regrets backing out and says he should have said yes.
- The shamanic world's consistency with cave art: tens of thousands of years of cave paintings document shamans having sex with spirits, producing spirit-world offspring, and navigating a consistent non-material realm — phenomenologically identical to modern abduction accounts; Hancock argues this is conclusive evidence these are encounters with the same phenomenon through different cultural lenses.
- Hancock explicitly frames the DMT realm as analogous to Quantum physicists' concept of a parallel dimension; he does not think the UFO phenomenon is primarily about extraterrestrial spacecraft (materialist/tech view) — he thinks it is about navigation of adjacent realities.
- The Imperial College findings are central: when large numbers of independently studied subjects describe the same entities in the same realm, the standard epistemic rule is to treat that as real; only civilization's anti-psychedelic prejudice prevents applying that rule to DMT encounters.

## Great Sphinx as Originally a Lion — Robert Schoch's Evidence

- [[robert-schoch|Robert Schoch]]'s geological analysis (brought to Giza by [[john-anthony-west|John Anthony West]]) concluded the erosion patterns on the [[great-sphinx|Great Sphinx]] and its surrounding trench are caused by precipitation-induced weathering from heavy rainfall — not wind or flood.
- This dates [[great-sphinx|the Sphinx]]'s construction to the period of heavy Saharan rainfall, which ended with the Younger Dryas climate shift (12,800–11,600 years ago) — making the Sphinx over 12,000 years old, not 4,500 years as Egyptology claims.
- Hancock's position: the Sphinx was originally a full lion — lion head and lion body — built during the First Time (Zep Tepe); the human pharaoh head is a later remodeling, which explains why the head is disproportionately small.
- "Zep Tepe" (the First Time) is the ancient Egyptian name for the period in which all their knowledge originated — Hancock explicitly maps this to the Younger Dryas period; the priests of Sais told Solon about the same era.
- Khafra may have remodeled the lion head into his own likeness — but this pharaonic addition does not affect the precipitation-weathering evidence that the underlying monument is prehistoric.

## Orion Correlation Extended — The Stellar Landscape

- [[robert-bauval|Robert Bauval]] identified that the three Giza pyramids mirror the belt stars of Orion (Osiris in Egyptian tradition); Hancock notes the Duat (afterlife realm) that souls traverse is organized around the Orion constellation — a worldwide tradition shared with Mesoamerican mound-builder cultures.
- Extended analysis shows the Dashur pyramids (Red Pyramid, Bent Pyramid) also map portions of the Taurus constellation; the full pattern suggests an entire stellar landscape was mapped onto the ground north of Giza.
- Hancock notes the Sumerian representation of a heliocentric solar system thousands of years before modern astronomy; this pre-Sumerian knowledge system did not begin with the Sumerians but is a legacy of the lost prehistoric civilization.

## Consciousness, Evolution, and the Reducing Valve

- Hancock rejects pure Darwinian materialism while accepting that evolution is real; his position is that consciousness is not an "accidental byproduct" of survival of the fittest — it is what evolution is fundamentally about.
- The "alert problem-solving state of consciousness" is the state society privileges; legal drugs like alcohol only provide a break from it; psychedelics offer access to a fundamentally different state.
- Depression, in Hancock's framing, is a locked, narrowed state of mind; psychedelics break that lock by fundamentally reorganizing neural patterns — unlike SSRIs which only modulate a single neurotransmitter without shifting the underlying state.
- [[rupert-sheldrake|Rupert Sheldrake]]'s morphic field / telephone telepathy research is cited as evidence that innate human abilities beyond the materialist framework exist and are scientifically measurable; Hancock endorses Sheldrake's protocols as rigorous.
- Hancock suggests ancient Egyptians may have used acoustic levitation or some innate telekinetic ability to move 200-ton stone blocks 350 feet into the air — ancient texts describe priests levitating blocks using chanting and music; he frames this as "speculation vs. speculation," not established fact, contrasting it with Egyptology's equally unproven ramp-and-sled hypothesis for precision placement at height.
- [[terence-mckenna|Terence McKenna]]'s *[[food-of-the-gods|Food of the Gods]]* is credited as the first major work proposing that the Quantum Leap in human behavior and consciousness was caused by psychedelic experimentation; [[brian-muraresku|Brian Muraresku]]'s *The Immortality Key* extends this into early Christian ritual; Hancock positions his own *Supernatural* (2005/reissued as *Visionary*) in this lineage.
- [[santha-faiia|Santha Faiia]] (Hancock's wife and photographer) is present during the Earl of Elgin interview; she is the person who accompanied Hancock to Broomhall estate; she is also his research partner on the [[great-pyramid|Great Pyramid]] climbs.
- Hancock is open to but not committed to civilizational cycle theories (Lemurians, Hyperboreans, Atlanteans as separate root races); his current evidentiary focus is one lost Ice Age civilization, not a succession of pre-human civilizations, which he considers "a bridge too far."
- *Entangled* (Hancock's 2010 Ayahuasca-sourced novel) features a young woman 24,000 years ago and a young woman today entangled across time fighting a demonic force that travels through time distorting humanity's true mission — themes that directly parallel his Gnostic worldview about dark forces opposing human spiritual evolution.

## Acacia, DMT, and the Ark of the Covenant

- The Ark of the Covenant was made of acacia wood, which contains DMT; the Eleusinian kykeon, early Christian rituals, and Ayahuasca all point toward the same cross-cultural use of psychedelic substances in sacred contexts.
- The Acacia leaf features prominently in Freemasonry as a symbol; Hancock connects the Masonic acacia symbol, the DMT-containing acacia of which the Ark was built, and the psychedelic root of mystery-school traditions.
- The Ethiopian Beta Israel (Falasha) connection to acacia and ritual dates back to the Elephantine Island community (~650 BC), consistent with a lineage carrying both the Ark and associated ritual knowledge southward through the Nile River system to Lake Tana and eventually Axum.
- The Ark's guardians developing cataracts and dying within ~3 years is consistent with ionizing radiation exposure; Hancock compares this to radiation-burn effects without claiming a specific mechanism.

## Additional Secondary Claims and Context

- **Hancock's grandfather**: Philip Hancock climbed the Great Pyramid on 5 April 1916 (confirmed from his own diary entry, which Hancock found while searching for the name "P. Hancock 5th of April 1916" scratched on the south face, one step below the summit); two of Hancock's five pyramid climbs were legal.
- **[[erich-von-daniken|Erich von Daniken]]**: Hancock explicitly rejects the Ancient Aliens hypothesis as unnecessary — he knows Von Daniken personally and credits him for alerting people to ancient mysteries, but argues the lost human civilization thesis is more elegant and requires no extraterrestrial technology.
- **Indus Valley Civilization**: discovered only in the 1920s/30s accidentally (railway workers); its language still unread; used as evidence that entire civilizations can vanish from historical memory and be rediscovered.
- **Amazon pyramid**: Hancock's archaeologist friend at the University of Exeter has used lidar in Colombia to identify an enormous pyramid in the Amazon rainforest; ground-truth team needs to be deployed; Hancock says he will go if invited.
- **Civilizational cycles**: Hancock does not go as far as root-race theories (Lemurians, Hyperboreans, Atlanteans); he focuses on arguing for one lost Ice Age civilization without seeking to multiply prior civilizations, calling multiple-root-race theories "a bridge too far" for his current evidential position.
