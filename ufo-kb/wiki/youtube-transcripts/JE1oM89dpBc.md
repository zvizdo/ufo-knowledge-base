# Legendary Hip Hop Artist Reveals UFO Obsession (Ft. Flying Lotus)

[[jesse-michaels]] interviews [[flying-lotus]] ([[flying-lotus|Steven Ellison]]) — hip-hop producer, filmmaker, and great-nephew of [[alice-coltrane]]. A wide-ranging three-hour conversation spanning personal UAP sightings, the Thomas [[t-townsend-brown|Townsend Brown]] / Back to the Future theory, Spielberg's alleged insider knowledge, Ross Cohart's buried UAP claim, Matthew Brown's prison planet thesis, and the fractiousness of the UFO community.

## Flying Lotus Background

- Real name: [[flying-lotus|Steven Ellison]]; great-nephew of [[alice-coltrane]], whose music he calls "tapped in to the source"; grandmother marilyn mcleod wrote "Love Hangover" (Diana Ross), sampled by Bone Thugs, Will Smith, Styles P ("I Get High")
- Runs **Brain Feeder Records** (founded as passion project; discovered Thundercat — Thundercat told him "I want to be your artist" at Ellison's Northridge apartment surrounded by "medicinal marijuana jars")
- Films: *Kuso* (2017), *VHS*, *Ash* (featuring Aaron Paul; AI/parasite themes); was interning at Stones Throw Records when Jay Dilla was finishing *Donuts*; delivered a check to Dilla's house near the end of his life — "the force was still strong even then"
- **DMT**: [[joe-rogan|Joe Rogan]] introduced him to it in early MySpace days via [[rick-strassman|Rick Strassman]]'s book; got his supply from random Australians in Perth on New Year's Day, smuggled it back through an entire tour; used it after his mother passed — "it was a way for me to connect to the other side and still be here"
- Became aware of UAP through Aaron Paul introducing him to Grusch's Ross Cohart interview while filming *Ash*; went down the rabbit hole from there

## Flying Lotus's Personal Sighting

- During the episode [[flying-lotus|Flying Lotus]] says: *"I might have. I might have. I saw a thing with three fingers and three toes."*
  - Minimal detail provided; unclear when, where, under what circumstances
  - Morphologically matches the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] from the Nazca period — tridactyl beings with fused digit structure
  - Attended the tridactyl mummy **unveiling presentation** (down the street from his LA house): only photos and X-rays were shown, not the physical beings; took everything with a grain of salt
  - Notes that at the presentation [[jaime-maussan|Jaime Maussan]] was involved, whom others consider a "known fraud"; Ellison says he loves Maussan despite the controversy
  - Rogan's reaction echoes his: Rogan also said the CT scan showing ligaments/bones/tendons was the most convincing physical evidence he'd ever seen

## Thomas Townsend Brown / Back to the Future

Jesse makes a detailed case that *Back to the Future* (Spielberg executive produced) encodes Thomas [[t-townsend-brown|Townsend Brown]]'s anti-gravity research:
- **"EMTT" = E.M.T.T. Brown** — the initials match
- **Pasadena setting** for the flux capacitor experiment — Brown lived in Pasadena
- **1985** = the year Brown died; the film is set in that year
- **1957** = the year Brown proved his Biefeld-Brown effect experiments; the film's past is set in 1957
- **Mr. Fusion** = the car's power source in the film → Brown wanted his anti-gravity experiments powered by nuclear reactions
- **Flux capacitor = capacitor experiment** — Brown's primary research tool was the high-voltage capacitor
- **Time and gravity linked in general relativity**: Brown's whole life was obsessed with both; if anti-gravity can bend gravity, time travel follows from Einstein
- The Delorean comes back and a **kid looks at it and says**: *"It has no seams. It has no rivets. It must be a [[disc-shaped-craft|flying saucer]]"* → the film is showing us how future humans' craft get mistaken for alien spacecraft
- Marty's father won't act unless "an alien from another galaxy showed up in my bedroom" → the film models our cognitive block to accepting time-travel = UFO
- Jesse: "If some of this stuff is time travel, that movie is fascinating because it points to us mistaking the UFO thing for aliens when actually it's time travel"

## Spielberg's Alleged Insider UAP Knowledge

Multiple evidence points discussed that Spielberg may have had classified briefings or insider access:

**Close Encounters of the Third Kind** (1977):
- **Crates labeled TRW and Skunk Works** in the film — organizations nobody in the public knew about in the late 1970s
- **Orbs trailing the main UFO** — small spheroid craft following the mother ship matches modern UAP witness accounts
- **Electromagnetic sunburn**: the protagonist receives radiation-type burns when the craft passes over — identical to the UAP biological effects being studied by [[garry-nolan]] at Stanford and discussed by CIA physician [[kit-green]]

**ET the Extra-Terrestrial** (1982):
- **ET and Elliott locked in biorhythm**: both characters share heartbeats and bodily sensations — parallel to [[jake-barber]]'s description of mentally "locking on" to UAP craft
- Jesse only watched *ET* for the first time as an adult: "I couldn't stop crying for the last half hour"

**Reagan White House screening quote**:
- Spielberg brought *ET* to the White House screening room for Reagan and first lady
- Reagan stood up to thank Spielberg and then: *"looked around the room almost like he was doing a headcount and said, 'And there are a number of people in this room who know that everything on that screen is absolutely true.' He said it without smiling."*
- The whole room laughed "because he presented it like a joke, but he wasn't smiling as he said it"
- Jesse interprets this as Reagan signaling to intelligence community insiders in the room

**Spielberg's public comments**:
- Asked about his view on where UFOs come from, Spielberg has said: *"is it a distant galaxy... or is it us thousands of years in the future, and they're keeping track and trying to adjust timelines?"*
- Said it to both Conan O'Brien and the Deadpool creator
- Praised Denis Villeneuve's *Arrival* as "the best UFO movie since Close Encounters" — which Ellison and Jesse find amusingly self-referential
- Wrote an endorsement for [[james-fox|James Fox]]'s first documentary out of the blue — shows he has followed UAP research for decades

## Art Bell Caller — Extradimensional Beings Claim

Jesse plays a clip from [[art-bell|Art Bell]]'s [[coast-to-coast-am|Coast to Coast AM]] (date unspecified):
- An anonymous caller (apparently being pursued by authorities) calls in:
  - *"What we're thinking of as aliens — they're extradimensional beings that an earlier precursor of the space program made contact with"*
  - *"They are not what they claim to be"*
  - *"They have infiltrated aspects of the military establishment, particularly [[area-51|Area 51]]"*
  - Claims the government knows about coming "disasters" and has identified safe areas but is not moving the population there — intending to let major population centers be wiped out so survivors are "more easily controllable"
- Transmission then cuts out entirely; [[art-bell|Art Bell]]'s satellite system went down
- Bell returns on a backup system: *"I can't help but wonder if somebody zapped us in some way"*
- Jesse uses this as a recurring frame: whoever this caller was, they were claiming NHI are *here* now (extradimensional, not interstellar), embedded in the military-industrial apparatus

## Ross Cohart and the Buried UAP

Jesse interviewed Ross Cohart and pushed him directly:
- Cohart has publicly maintained: *"there is a buried or at least built-upon UFO that was too big to move"* — and did not resile from that claim in Jesse's interview
- Cohart added privately: *"It's an extremely sensitive site. Revealing the full extent of what I know would compromise the lives of young men and women doing good things for America — probably oblivious to what's sitting downstairs"*
- Jesse interprets "young men and women on the ground" as suggesting a **current active war zone** — a site where US military operations are ongoing on top of a UAP site
- Both Jesse and [[flying-lotus|Flying Lotus]] note that Cohart could leak the information anonymously if he "really has the goods"
- Flying Lotus on Cohart's source credibility: Cohart's sources said Chinese drones first, then changed to American military exercises — shows his sources don't always hold up

## Matthew Brown and Immaculate Constellation

- matthew brown figured out **[[immaculate-constellation|Immaculate Constellation]]** — the classified UAP/intelligence program
- [[lue-elizondo]]'s name was tied to [[immaculate-constellation|Immaculate Constellation]]; Elizondo testified alongside [[michael-shellenberger|Michael Shellenberger]] saying he "can't confirm or deny" being read into it — which Jesse reads as confirmation
- **White House "crystal ball" AI**: Brown tweeted about a White House proprietary AI system that **predicts future timelines** — Jesse: "that's fascinating like if I were trying to be inspired for a movie"
- **Prison planet thesis**: Brown said there is a God, but "we're in kind of like a prison planet and these beings — we're kind of like captured" / "we're a resource, that's what it said"
  - Jesse and Flying Lotus discuss this: "I hope there's some more meaningful stuff going on. We're not just getting mined."
  - Flying Lotus: "I wouldn't be surprised though"

## Randy Anderson — Deep Underground Military Base (DUMBS)

[[randy-anderson|Randy Anderson]] (Naval Surface Warfare Center, Crane, Indiana, 2014):
- Taken down to a **deep underground military base** via elevator — multiple floors below surface
- Walked through a tunnel past multiple **SCIFs** into an area labeled "**off-world**" or "**off-planet technology**"
- Inside he saw "**a gauntlet thing that emitted hieroglyphics**"
- Had been speaking with both UAP Gerb and [[steven-greer|Steven Greer]] before Jesse connected with him
- Jesse spent 6 months building trust with Anderson; met him for dinner the night before his public appearance
- Critics immediately attacked Anderson's military background, not his testimony; Jesse: "When they then go after his background as a war hero, it's like come on"

## Jake Barber and the Mental Connection

Flying Lotus and Jesse discuss [[jake-barber]]:
- Barber's Ross Cohart interview: Flying Lotus and a friend took a shot every time Barber blinked — "he didn't blink at all. He was sober like a [expletive]"
- Barber's *Skywatcher* program involves mentally "locking on" to craft — a psychic/mental interface
- Flying Lotus thinks Barber's mental connection claim is *why* he believes him rather than why he's skeptical: "the reason a lot of people don't believe Jake's story is the reason I do believe it because it involves this mental connection"
- Jesse concedes Barber "dropped a lot" in his first interview and may have lost some audiences by going into psionic territory too fast: "you should have stayed on the first half, shown the egg, and let it go"

## Eric Hecker — Neutrino Device at South Pole

[[eric-hecker]] (Raytheon employee):
- Claims he installed a **neutrino emitter/detector at the South Pole** (IceCube Neutrino Observatory)
- Claims this device was linked to or caused the **2010 Christchurch, New Zealand earthquake**
- Flying Lotus: *"I don't know if it's true but I love that. It's wild."*
- Jesse: *"He must have been there — there are photos of him there"*
- Caveat: much of Hecker's testimony was described as "overheard water cooler talk," not direct observation
- Flying Lotus met him at the tridactyl mummy presentation and found him credible as a person

## UAP Community Dynamics

Both Jesse and Flying Lotus discuss the toxicity and fragmentation of the UFO online community:
- **[[skinny-bob|Skinny Bob video]]**: Flying Lotus publicly called it "clearly CGI — it's clearly like a [expletive] dude"; he was attacked by UFO accounts as a "disinfo agent" with a "blue check nobody had heard of"
- Jesse: "20% of Reddit thinks I'm like a CIA agent or something"
- The community functions as a religion: if the topic is real, it "has to incorporate into some larger metaphysical worldview" — which makes people extremely defensive of their specific beliefs
- Both want a round table including skeptic [[mick-west]], Lou Elizondo, and Kirk Patrick — "why can't we get them all in a room?"
- **[[jeremy-corbell|Jeremy Corbell]] criticism**: Corbell made a public stink about his name being removed from an official government document; Matthew Brown had met Shellenberger *before* Corbell, and Brown wasn't attaching promotional material to the document — Jesse found Corbell's reaction self-serving
- **Black abductees**: Flying Lotus notes that documented abductees skew heavily white — [[barney-hill|Barney Hill]] is "the OG"; Ariel School children mentioned; Flying Lotus wants demographic statistics on this; Jesse says it may be under-reported rather than absent

## Alice Coltrane and Music as Cosmic Connection

- **[[alice-coltrane|Alice Coltrane]]** (Flying Lotus's great-aunt): Flying Lotus spent his childhood at her LA home; never once saw her practice — "and when she played, she was Yoda: tiny, gentle, and then everything was busting out all crazy"
- Listening to her music makes Flying Lotus "recall Egypt, ancient Egypt — it makes me seem to remember that I have a past or history there somewhere"
- Jesse: "Maybe we were closer to God, closer to the source, closer [[to-the-stars-academy|to the stars]]... connected to it bit more earlier in our development as human beings"
- Music-as-cosmic-connection parallels the morphic resonance / Sheldrake thesis discussed in other episodes: art as downloading from a universal repository
