## The 2009 Indonesia Encounter

### Mission Context and Anomalies

[[michael-herrera]] was a member of the 31st Marine Expeditionary Unit (31st MEU) aboard the **USS Denver**:
- Rerouted from original Philippines humanitarian assignment (Operation Katsana, post-Sumatra earthquake/tsunami) to western Sumatra, Indonesia
- Mission briefing mentioned a SEAL team present to extract Obama family members from the disaster zone
- Herrera's team received an anomalous "working party" type assignment — his first mission of this kind in service
- Fire team plus two = 6 Marines total; condition three (no rounds chambered); no communication equipment
- No civilians present at the clearing, which struck Herrera as operationally unusual

### The Craft (see [[2009-indonesia-ufo]])

From elevated ground overlooking the clearing, the Marines observed an approximately 300-foot octagonal craft:
- **Shape:** Three-tiered concentric octagonal platforms; accentuated dome on top, spread wider at base (stacked-ring relief structure)
- **Surface:** Matte black transitioning to matte grey; specific panels described as Vantablack-like — absorbing light like a black hole
- **Sound:** Low-frequency bass hum; no conventional propulsion signatures
- **Rotation:** Clockwise rotation revealing red, yellow, green, and blue corner lights
- **Lower platform:** Extended below craft body; cargo trucks (Ford F-350s / Tundras) with reinforced HVAC shipping containers drove up the ramp onto it
- **What was in the containers:** [[jake-barber]] later told Herrera these were [[p3-psionic-assets]] — humans with psionic ability used to attract NHI craft to the location
- **Departure:** Shot off at several thousand miles per hour — a "black blur" — without disturbing surrounding vegetation; no sonic boom, no rotor wash

### The Paramilitary Team

Before the Marines could approach the clearing, an 8-man armed force had them in a pincer formation (4+4):
- Spoke American-accented English; no unit insignia or identifying markings
- Openly discussed whether to "smoke" the Marines — audibly evaluated lethal options
- Disarmed the Marines: weapons taken, magazines removed, chambers checked
- Processed each Marine using a **BAT-like biometric scanner** and a thin **CavCom smartphone-like device**
- Herrera: former CIA contacts later identified this equipment as legacy program issue
- Fluid and professional movement; safeties audibly flipped during the encounter
- Did not execute the Marines — described as a deliberate restraint given what they had witnessed

### Camera Sabotage and NDA

**Camera sabotage at Subic Bay:**
- Herrera had a Panasonic camera in his dump pouch; not discovered during the disarmament search
- Returned to Subic Bay Philippines liberty 4–5 days later
- Locked locker accessed without visible forced entry; camera placed on his rack
- Battery and memory card removed; camera inoperable with replacement battery
- Fellow Marines also reported missing phones and personal items from the same liberty period

**NDA at Camp Hansen, Okinawa:**
- An Air Force Lt. Colonel in dress blues appeared at an empty command post
- Silver oak leaf, jump wings, heavy ribbons; notably no pilot badge
- Had Herrera sign an NDA referencing only: "TSSCI," "Indonesia," and his Social Security Number
- Threatened Herrera with prison or execution
- The same NDA procedure was performed on each of the 6 Marines separately

### Corroboration from Fellow Marines

- Two of the five other Marines have subsequently made contact with Herrera
- Neither has denied the event:
  - One said: "keep the faith, there are no borders they won't cross"
  - One said: "getting the word out; leave me out, not worth my life"
  - Three remain completely silent
- The fire team leader who publicly contradicted Herrera was **not on the mission**
- [[uap-gerb]] and intermediary "Joey" have seen unredacted names of all those on the mission
- Text messages from corroborating Marines provided by Herrera to the **Senate Select Committee on Intelligence**
