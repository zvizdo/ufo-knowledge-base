# "The Man Sitting On More UFO Evidence Than Anyone Alive" — Claus Svahn / American Alchemy

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] visit to the **[[archives-for-the-unexplained]] (AFU)** in Sweden with founder and archivist [[claus-svahn]]. Host [[jesse-michaels]] is joined by amar kandiel ([[yes-theory|Yes Theory]]). The AFU is a 16-room archive started in 1973 housing 22,000+ Swedish case files, original first-generation documents, photographs, radar data, and audio recordings — widely regarded as the world's largest UFO archive.

## Headline Evidence and Claims

### The 1957 Väddö Island Tungsten Object
Two carpenters driving late at night on Väddö Island (northeast of Stockholm) saw a craft make a U-turn and hover in front of their car, killing the engine. After it departed they found a metallic piece on the road — still hot. Analysis: **tungsten carbide (wolframite)** with impurity profile consistent with 1957-era tungsten production (earthly origin), but the object had been super-heated to a degree the car could not account for. Svahn holds the physical sample. See [[1957-vaddo-island-incident]].

### 1946 Swedish Ghost Rockets — New Details
[[claus-svahn]] provides additional detail beyond prior KB sources: at least **1,400 documented** rocket/cigar-shaped objects descended on Norway and Sweden; **every single one entered water** — not one crashed on land. Swedish military formed a specialized investigative unit. They searched multiple lakes and found only **indentations at the bottom** — no debris. Svahn interprets the exclusively aquatic landing behavior as intentional and not explained by conventional crash-retrieval models. See [[1946-swedish-ghost-rockets]].

### Betty Hill's Unreported UFO Crash
[[claus-svahn]] spent extensive time with [[betty-hill]] and discovered that **outside of the famous 1961 abduction**, Betty witnessed a **UFO crash near her home** and recovered debris from it. She buried the material in her backyard, where it may still be to this day. This aspect of [[betty-hill|Betty Hill]]'s experience is almost unknown in the public UFO discourse. See [[betty-hill]].

### 2005 Northern Sweden Radar Case
Svahn personally witnessed a military radar return of an object circling a lake in northern Sweden. A photograph was taken. Two witnesses total. Svahn describes this as possibly **one of the best-documented UFO cases in existence** — multi-sensor (radar + photo) with independent corroboration. He intends to investigate the area further.

### Hessdalen Phenomenon
[[hessdalen|Hessdalen valley]] in Norway is the premier European UAP hotspot. In the early 1980s AFU participated in expeditions there and captured photos and a radar return. Key characteristic: lights emerge from the **ground upward** — never entering from above. People in the area have high witness rates, and the community normalizes sightings. See [[hessdalen]].

### Photo Analysis Philosophy
Svahn has analyzed every known Swedish UFO photo from the 1970s and personally tracked down each photographer. Result: every single 1970s Swedish photo was a hoax by young men — with one unresolved exception (Christer Sunström). His verdict on **McMinnville**: an analyst at a French conference showed a visible string in data-enhanced images. On evaluating photos in general: **the story matters more than the image** — the narrative under interview either stands or falls.

### Billy Meier — Additional Critique
Beyond prior KB entries, Svahn adds direct physical analysis of Meier's photographs: a gun in one image is clearly a toy-store prop; a "dinosaur photo from time-travel" is lifted directly from a printed book (Svahn owns the book and compares them side by side). Svahn considers Meier a deliberate self-promoter aiming for "new Jesus" status. He's unaware of the Jorjani MI6 theory. See [[billy-meier]].

### George Adamski — Unresolved
Svahn considers the Adamski case "complicated" — photos not properly analyzed (one analyst won't share methodology); [[glen-steckling]] has read Adamski's private diaries and hasn't shared them. Svahn keeps "a slightly open door." See [[george-adamski]].

### Eric Walker, Kit Green, and the "Sixth Sense" Gate
Svahn is familiar with [[eric-walker]]. The Walker cluster involves: (1) [[grant-cameron]] published a transcript of a conversation with Walker; (2) [[william-steinman]] visited Penn State to meet Walker, who called him "Don Quixote" and referenced a "sixth sense" / psi prerequisite for access; (3) [[kit-green]] visited Walker wanting UAP secrets — Walker reportedly kicked him out. See [[eric-walker]], [[kit-green]].

### Henry Azadehdel — Disinformation Suspect
A British-Armenian physicist known by multiple aliases including "Henry Aadero" — published a fraudulent "silver diamonds South Africa" claim. Svahn does not trust him. Jorjani has a transcript of a Walker-Azadehdel conversation but Svahn won't vouch for Azadehdel's credibility. Flagged for review: henry azadehdel (not yet in KB).

### The Trickster / Absurdity Element
Both Svahn and Michaels converge on the [[trickster-phenomenon]] framing (cf. [[jacques-vallee]], [[john-keel]]): high-strangeness cases are not characterized by archetypal grays but by absurd, context-defying detail — men in silver suits on a scrap-metal railcar, glowing plus signs rolling on a road. This **pre-stigmatizes** the case, making it self-discrediting.

## New Evidence and Cases

### 1975 November 7th — Military Helicopter Ghost Rocket Encounter

- Newly declassified from Swedish DoD archives covering a 1970s customs/police/military interdiction operation (full release restricted until 2040; Svahn received special clearance just days before this interview)
- A helicopter pilot was on standby intercepting unlit trespassers flying from Norway into Sweden at night; ordered to pursue an object; flying 20m over treetops in moonlit, snow-covered terrain with excellent visibility
- An elongated rocket-like object with no wings and no lights passed UNDER the helicopter in that 20m gap, then flew straight away at much higher speed than the helicopter; the pilot "lifted his feet" instinctively
- Radar plot in the declassified files shows the intersection of the helicopter's path and the object; military security personnel debriefed the crew on landing; Svahn spoke to the pilot just days before the interview
- Co-pilot also witnessed it; co-pilot now lives in Australia; Svahn plans to contact him for an independent account
- Shape: "a typical ghost rocket" — elongated, no wings, no lights; consistent with all Scandinavian ghost-rocket descriptions since 1946

### 1973–74 — Radar Station Cigar Sighting

- Six radar operators at a classified installation inside a mountain in northern Sweden, winter 1973–74; during a lunch break on the surface, they saw a cigar-shaped object above the treeline
- They ran back inside to the radar screens and tracked the object executing 90° right-angle turns; it then flew out over Norway and straight up vertically
- All six operators, located independently over several years by Svahn, tell the same story with consistent details; commander ordered photographs of the radar screen but Svahn has not been able to locate those images
- Svahn has taped interviews with all six witnesses; written about in his books; tapes not published

### 1980 Damaya Lake — Object Still in the Mud

- Northern Sweden national park lake; two witnesses watched a craft flying below 100m altitude pass over them, execute a 180° turn, return toward them, descend and land with a splash and bubbles, then sink — mid-day, good visibility
- Witnesses returned to Stockholm and contacted the military; the Swedish military's dedicated UFO investigator at the time could find no explanation; a helicopter was dispatched, found dead fish near shore (later confirmed to be a common parasite, not radioactivity)
- Svahn's team conducted sonar survey; object detected in the mud at approximately 2m depth (not at the lake bottom); it is a protected national park — nothing can be removed without special permits
- Next expedition planned with 3D sonar; in 2D survey the object's shape could not be determined; see [[transmedium-uap]]
- The case joins the broader Scandinavian pattern: in Svahn's data, not a single UAP in Sweden or Norway has ever crashed on land — all end in water

### KGB Files Recovery

- Svahn traveled to Russia specifically to attempt to save KGB UFO files; arrived during a period of street fighting with soldiers in the streets
- Located an individual who had copies of some KGB files; these are among the international government UFO-related documents now held at AFU

## Claus Svahn's Personal Sightings and Career

- **Age-16 Jupiter debunk**: first major investigation; hundreds of people including police and journalists watched an object over a Swedish mountain for hours; Svahn observed the same object 100km away, identified it as Jupiter, wrote to newspapers, and was published; an Adamski believer in the crowd had suggested "windows" — really Jupiter's moons — and the crowd saw what he suggested; Svahn's first lesson in crowd observation dynamics
- **1995 November 5th sighting** (with wife Annalie): midnight, driving home from a birthday party; saw two men at a bus stop looking up; went inside and then stepped out to look; three illuminated plus signs appeared out of the black sky in Gemini, between Castor and Pollux (not from the horizon — they simply materialized); flew over in rigid formation at sub-satellite, sub-aircraft altitude; Svahn and Annalie ran after them and watched them pass over neighboring rooftops
- **Double-blind protocol**: immediately after the sighting Svahn told Annalie to sit in the kitchen while he sat separately; both made independent drawings before comparing notes; the drawings were not identical but described the same objects — maintained scientific discipline even in a personal sighting
- **1930s corroboration**: six months after Svahn described the sighting on radio, an elderly woman called — she had been a young girl in the 1930s; on a sunny daytime road in Sweden, three illuminated plus signs appeared from nowhere, rolled along the road in front of her, then vanished; same shape, very different behavior (rolling vs. floating); Svahn had not published the plus-sign detail before the radio appearance
- **Educator of the Year**: named educator of the year in Sweden; represents his commitment to public science communication about anomalous phenomena
- **Career motivation**: no personal anomalous experience at the time he started the AFU; driven by genuine curiosity; knocks doors, interviews witnesses, makes independent drawings; his approach is explicitly: "I want to know. I'm not a believer. I have no axe to grind."
- **Approach to missed interviews**: his single biggest regret is a northern Swedish policeman who had extensively investigated ghost rocket witnesses in the 1980s and passed away before Svahn could properly interview him
- **Research philosophy on the field**: "You need to look at this very broadly — most ufologists focus on their small part and never get ready with what they're doing because it's so much more complex than you thought from the beginning"

## Photo Analysis Methodology

- **Story over image**: Svahn's central principle — "it's not the picture, it's the story"; under careful interview, the narrative around a photograph either stands or falls; photo analysis alone is insufficient
- **Swedish 1970s sweep**: Svahn personally tracked down every photographer of a Swedish UFO photograph from the 1970s; every single one was a hoax by young men; not one woman; when he called them 10–15 years later they all admitted it — except Christer Sunström, who still denies it
- **Svahn faked his own**: acknowledges he has personally faked a UFO photograph, demonstrating that technique is accessible; uses this as calibration for evaluating others' claims
- **McMinnville photo**: Svahn's previous opinion was that it was among the best UFO photographs; at a conference in France an analyst showed via data-enhancement that a string is visible; Svahn is "no longer sure" it's genuine
- **Gold standard**: the 2005 northern Sweden case — radar return Svahn saw himself on military radar + mobile phone photograph + two independent witnesses; the only photograph Svahn is "100% confident" corresponds to a real event because he personally verified the radar
- **The [[hannah-mcroberts|Hannah McRoberts]] photo (1981, Vancouver Island)**: Svahn spoke with McRoberts by telephone; she accidentally photographed a domed saucer she did not see with the naked eye; he considers her credible and not grifting; compares it favorably to the "Elizabeth photo" (mysterious figure behind a child, single photographer, open horizon)
