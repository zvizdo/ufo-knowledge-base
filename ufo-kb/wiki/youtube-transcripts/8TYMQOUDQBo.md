# Meet the Navy Scientist With UFO Patents (Ft. Salvatore Pais)

First in-person interview with [[salvatore-pais]], [[us-navy|US Navy]] physicist and author of five exotic-propulsion patents (three granted). Co-hosted by [[jesse-michaels]] and a pseudonymous physicist referred to only as "[[jack-pseudonym]]" who has a background in condensed matter / topological materials physics and works in "sensitive areas." The conversation ranges from Pais's personal biography to detailed patent physics, room-temperature superconductivity, NHI threat framing, and the MH370 connection.

## Headline Claims

- **The Pais effect** — Pais defines it as "controlled motion of electrically charged objects (solid to plasma) under accelerated vibration and/or accelerated spin, giving rise to extremely high energy densities and hence electromagnetic energy fluxes." Pais considers this purely original; [[victor-lachinov|Victor Lachinov]] endorsed it in a published paper as a viable mechanism for room-temperature superconductivity.
- **Physics pathway: Heaviside → harmonic oscillator → Schwinger limit** — Pais coupled Oliver Heaviside's four-equation version of Maxwell's equations with the harmonic oscillator (Hook's law as differential equation) and showed that angular frequency squared drives nonlinear field amplification. The scaling analysis yields electric fields on the order of 10¹⁸ V/m and magnetic fields on the order of 10⁹ T — consistent with Maxwell's E_max / B_max = c check. At sufficient density this breaks the Schwinger limit (10³³ W/m²), tearing apart the quantum vacuum and — Pais believes — opening a black-hole / white-hole wormhole pair usable for propulsion.
- **Room-temperature superconductor via Pais effect** — Pais selected lead zirconate titanate (PZT) — all three components are superconducting at very low temperatures — and argues the Pais effect can drive a Bose-Einstein condensate in these materials at room temperature via a bipolar mechanism, consistent with [[victor-lachinov]]'s analysis. Two of the five patents were denied; the room-temperature superconductor was one of the two denied, despite Lachinov's published support.
- **Three patents granted, two denied** — The high-frequency gravitational wave generator, high-energy electromagnetic field generator, and inertial mass reduction device all became patents. The room-temperature superconductor and one other were denied. Pais explicitly states the HFGWG and HEEMFG patents are interdependent with the high-temperature superconductor (as the resonator's high Q factor requires it).
- **Man-made tech and ET tech are both real** — Pais explicitly affirms both branches: some UAP is man-made (possibly derived from gifts, reverse engineering, or independent physics discovery); some is non-human in origin. They can be distinguished. He does not believe only NHI can achieve this technology level.
- **[[bob-lazar|Bob Lazar]] validated** — Pais endorses Lazar's claim that the sports model was "3D printed out of the space-time fabric itself." He connects this to the super-force equation (C⁴/G acting as a linear operator on the quantum vacuum), which implies manipulation at the Planck scale — the same scale Lazar described implicitly.
- **Schwinger-limit black holes as propulsion** — Pais explicitly states that breaking the Schwinger limit opens a black hole, and every black hole has a corresponding white hole connected by a wormhole, enabling faster-than-conventional transport. He stops short of calling it FTL: "once you break the Schwinger limit, Einstein physics no longer truly applies."
- **Teleherence / MH370** — When shown the MH370 orb videos by [[ashton-forbes|Ashton Forbes]], Pais recognized the Pais effect at work immediately. The fourth orb hypothesis ([[dave-rossi|Dave Rossi]]'s insight: three orbs define origin, a fourth must define destination) is consistent with Pais's three-axis + wormhole model.
- **We are property** — Endorses [[charles-fort|Charles Fort]]'s "Book of the Damned" framing. Pais believes NHI have been in contact with humanity for thousands of years in a "weakly entangled, ephemeral way." He prays for good and bad factions to exist (citing [[robert-hastings|Robert Hastings]]' work on nuclear missiles), as without good factions "we have no chance."
- **Physics stagnation as national security risk** — Pais directly states that keeping STEM students in "wrong frameworks" (not exposed to heterodox physics) is a national security risk as serious as any adversary program. String theory is named as a broken paradigm. China graduates more STEM professionals annually than exist in all of the US.
