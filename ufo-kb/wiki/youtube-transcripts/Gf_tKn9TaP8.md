## Stargate Origin Story

### The Cleve Backster Chain

While writing the textbook *Fundamentals of Quantum Electronics* (English/French/Russian/Chinese editions) at [[stanford-university|Stanford]] during his PhD, Puthoff noticed an unstudied gap: consciousness, living things, the animate/inanimate boundary. He came across published work by [[cleve-baxter|Cleve Backster]], a polygraph expert who taught polygraph technique to the [[cia|CIA]] and [[fbi|FBI]]. Backster had wired plants to a polygraph "on a lark," seen signals analogous to those of humans, then threatened the plant verbally and "got a big response." Wiring two plants in parallel showed coupling: affecting one elicited a response from the other.

Puthoff designed a follow-up — a split algae culture, one half at a laser-link site five miles away, the local half zapped, propagation velocity measured. He never ran it. Backster mentioned the proposal to [[ingo-swann|Ingo Swann]] at a New York cocktail party. Swann wrote to Puthoff: "If you're interested in the borderline between animate and inanimate physics, why deal with algae culture? They can't tell you anything. You should be dealing with somebody like me." Swann attached a City College of New York report on his thermistor-temperature experiments.

### The 1972 Quark Detector Session at SRI

By this time Puthoff had moved to [[stanford-research-institute|SRI]] for laser work. He invited Swann for a weekend. The available instrument: an ultra-shielded quantum chip — magnetic shielding, electrical shielding, superconducting shielding, acoustic isolation — being used to search for quarks. The chip ran a slow ~30-second oscillation. Swann affected it: the oscillation stopped. Asked to do something else, he made it oscillate fast. Then Swann drew an accurate internal diagram of the device — never published.

Within weeks the CIA arrived at Puthoff's SRI office: "Oh, have we been looking for you?" Their concern was concrete — if Swann could read through that level of shielding, Soviet psychics might be reading classified documents out of superconducting safes. A small ~$50–60K initial contract launched what became Stargate, running >20 years at the highly classified level. Backstory pulled in by the CIA: Puthoff had been a Naval Intelligence officer at NSA between his master's and PhD, holding high-level clearances already.

### Why Puthoff Resisted Throughout

As a quantum physicist who had written the equations, Puthoff says he resisted every step. The phrase he uses repeatedly: "I had no clue how this could possibly be." His admission today: "I still don't really have a clue about exactly what's going on, other than consciousness seems to be expandable out into the environment in a way that we don't usually consider could possibly be the case."

## The Remote-Viewing Program

### Pat Price and Sugar Grove

After local press coverage, walk-ins began arriving. Among them: ex-Burbank police commissioner [[pat-price|Pat Price]], who reported gut-image hits while solving crimes. Right at the moment of his arrival, [[cia|CIA]] was demanding that the team prove the program wasn't experimenter–subject collusion. The CIA gave Price coordinates of what turned out to be next to [[sugar-grove-naval-station|Sugar Grove Naval Station]] — a classified NSA Soviet-satellite-intercept facility. Price merged his mind with a safe at the site and produced a long list of project codename words. "The entire law enforcement apparatus of the country landed on us": classified project titles, demands to know the source. A CIA paper published ~20 years later confirms the hit.

### Semipalatinsk and the Giant Crane

Deputy director [[john-mcmahon|John McMahon]] then said: "Let's not waste it on our science for God's sakes. Do a Soviet site." They gave Price coordinates for an R&D facility at [[semipalatinsk|Semipalatinsk]]. Price described a giant crane that rolled over the top of a building — sounded like science fiction. Satellite imagery later confirmed the drawings. That's when "okay, this stuff is real" got declared internally and the program was funded as an espionage tool.

### Jupiter Ring (Ingo Swann)

Before the [[nasa|NASA]] Jupiter flyby, Puthoff tasked Swann with viewing the planet. Swann reported the standard features (red spot etc.) plus "a thin ring around Jupiter — I wonder if I went to Saturn by mistake, but I really see a ring around Jupiter." No one knew of any Jupiter ring. [[carl-sagan|Carl Sagan]] came by the lab; Puthoff showed him the result. Sagan: "Ring around Jupiter? That's nonsense." The NASA flyby confirmed the ring. Puthoff and team published the result before mainstream science knew.

### Underwater Test Rules Out Brain Waves

The Russians had proposed that brain-wave-frequency long-wavelength EM signals carried the effect. Puthoff piggybacked on [[stephan-schwartz|Stephan Schwartz]]'s archaeological-wreck remote-viewing experiments, putting his viewers on a submarine and into the depths. Seawater conductivity blocks all EM at brain-wave frequencies. Pristine results were obtained underwater. Conclusion: not ordinary electromagnetism. "We could strike one thing off the list."

### The IEEE Proceedings Paper (1976) and PEAR

Puthoff insisted on declassifying enough to publish: "The only secret about this project is that there's a secret project." Russell Targ and Puthoff submitted to the [[ieee|IEEE Proceedings]]. The editor (head of Bell Labs communications) received the now-famous one-sentence review: "This is the kind of thing I wouldn't believe in even if it were true." Puthoff demanded a presentation to [[bell-labs|Bell Labs]] engineers; the engineers got excited; the editor still balked. Trump card: a full blind replication run in-house at Bell Labs, by Bell engineers and target list-makers. Bell Labs got the same kind of results. Published 1976 in the *Proceedings of the IEEE*. [[bob-jahn|Robert Jahn]] (Princeton engineering dean) heard the briefing and set up a ~20-year replication program at [[princeton-parapsychology-lab|PEAR]] with [[brenda-dunne|Brenda Dunne]] — replicated SRI remote viewing and added the random-number-generator (quantum-driven) anomaly work.

### The Bill Casey Briefing

Puthoff hated briefing the very top of the food chain — one skeptical sentence from a director could end the program. With Reagan's CIA director [[bill-casey|Bill Casey]], they had 45 minutes scheduled; Casey was so absorbed he cleared his afternoon calendar and ran the briefing for five hours. Puthoff developed a working hypothesis: people who reach the top of the food chain are people who routinely make right decisions on insufficient information — there may be a sub-conscious-level psi component at play. The hypothesis was tested directly: a parapsychologist had 67 CEOs of major corporations guess random numbers being generated the next day. The CEOs who scored significantly above chance were the ones running the businesses that were actually doing well; the chance-or-worse scorers ran failing firms. Interview consensus: "When I trust my gut instinct, I'm usually right."

### The Bell Curve and Hella Hammid

A full medical workup on the program's top performers (seven-layer brain scans, neurology, psychology) found "these are just normal people." Puthoff then brought in [[hella-hammid|Hella Hammid]], an SRI laboratory employee with explicit "I don't believe in that stuff" priors. First trial: a chain-link overpass on a freeway. She drew the trough-with-holes structure ("couldn't carry water"), described something going by really fast, and nailed the site. The discovery: psi ability is a bell curve, like athletic or musical ability — superstars and duds at the extremes, but everyone can do it to some degree.

### The Soviet Plane in Africa (Stansfield Turner)

Carter's CIA director [[stansfield-turner|Stansfield Turner]], aware of the program, challenged it: a Soviet plane had gone down somewhere in Africa, hundreds of thousands of square miles, satellites couldn't penetrate the vegetation — find it. Two remote viewers (one at SRI, one at [[wright-patterson-air-force-base|Wright-Patterson]] Foreign Technology Division) put an X on a map 3 miles from where the plane actually was. The Tu-22 bomber wreckage was recovered before the Soviets found it. Carter later publicly broke the security: in a Georgia speech he said "we had some remote viewers so-called and they pinpointed where it was." Rogan: "So that was a breach of confidence?" Puthoff: "That was a breach of security. But a president can. They're allowed to do that." Rogan (laughs): "Don't tell Trump."

### Joe McMoneagle and the Typhoon-Class Submarine

[[joe-mcmoneagle|Joe McMoneagle]] — Army intelligence officer trained at SRI — described an enormous titanium submarine being built at a Soviet site, larger than any submarine known, with missile silos on the top rather than along the sides. The National Security Council reviewed it as nonsense. About a month later the Typhoon-class submarine — the largest submarine ever made — rolled out. McMoneagle eventually received a National Merit Award for >200 sessions performed for [[cia|CIA]], [[national-security-council|NSC]], and [[fbi|FBI]].

### The Skeptic Who Became a Viewer

A CIA investigator arrived "to find out what the fraud is." Day 1: Puthoff went out, viewer described the location well. The investigator: "You told him where you were going." Day 2: investigator chose the site himself, drove his own car (against a hypothesized car-transmitter). Same result. Day 3: Puthoff and Targ had the investigator be the viewer himself. Outbound team went to a playground with a merry-go-round; the investigator drew a playground with a merry-go-round. He went back to CIA and reported: this works. He later became one of their star remote viewers.

### Training Protocol — Visceral, Not Imagistic

Counter to intuition: don't have the viewer imagine the site, because imagination kicks in and produces noise. Instead get the visceral response. If the site has water, viewers might draw waves; a mountain peak, they "just feel like drawing something like that." If they get an image, put it down the right-hand side of the page because it's probably wrong. Multi-stage training: feelings, colors, flashes of things — "you see a flash of piece of metal, don't try to turn it into a car or a bicycle." Door analogy: drill one hole, then another, then another, "and then finally the door crumbles."

### Time-Decoupled Remote Viewing at Princeton

[[bob-jahn|Robert Jahn]] at Princeton, a quantum-theory veteran, ran time-decoupled variants — send someone to a site a week later but have a viewer describe the site today; or have the viewer describe a site that someone went to a week ago. Same hit rates. Implication: temporal locality doesn't bind the effect. Jahn published in *IEEE Proceedings* a couple years after the Puthoff/Targ paper (1978).

### Why Aren't Remote Viewers Rich?

Stress-test from skeptics: if these people are so psychic, why aren't they at Las Vegas? Puthoff's wife Adrienne Kennedy was on the board of a Bay Area grammar school short $25K. A wealthy dentist Puthoff knew of made an offer: do remote viewing on silver futures, follow your viewers' calls, split 10% with the school if it works. Puthoff used the associative-remote-viewing protocol (don't ask the next day's direction — pick two objects, label one "market up" and the other "market down," ask viewers today to describe tomorrow's object). One viewer reported: "I've got something all squirreled up in a can, all wound around, and I hear the words one, two, three" — said rhythmically. The mark was a tape measure. Over 30 days the investor made $260K. The school got its $26K. They stopped because Puthoff was training Army Intelligence viewers 24 hours a day in parallel and "it was almost a 24-hour-a-day job to do this."

### The Pat Price Oval Office Prediction

One morning Price came into the office and said: "I got bored last night, started looking around, decided to look at the Oval Office. There's something in the Oval Office that will harm him, and he will not get through his second term." Puthoff reported it to his CIA contract monitor; a team swept for hidden microwaves and toxics; nothing. The Nixon tape recorder later "did him in." Price additionally said Vice President [[spiro-agnew|Spiro Agnew]] would not take over because he would go first — Agnew resigned over the Maryland money-laundering scheme. Puthoff's framing: the statistical odds of independently predicting both an aborted second term *and* the VP going first are essentially zero.

We learned in the LSD days that if the experimenters get involved in the subject they're researching, they lose their objectivity. And don't think you can sneak away and get away with it because we'll get you on the polygraph." Rogan: "What a bizarre thing to tell someone."

### Mount Ziel Australia and UFO Bases on Earth

A SRI remote viewer (Puthoff strongly implies [[pat-price|Pat Price]]) came in and said he had found UFO bases on Earth. One of the locations: [[mount-ziel|Mount Ziel]] in Australia. Puthoff's CIA contract monitor — without revealing the question — called the CIA station keeper in Australia and asked about the Mount Ziel area. Puthoff's warning to those viewers: don't advertise the dope work — "you don't want cartels nearby putting a check on your back."

## UAP, Crash Retrievals, and the Disclosure Track

### Entry Point — 1990s Bigelow Aerospace

[[hal-puthoff]] was already doing work for [[robert-bigelow|Robert Bigelow]] (Bigelow Aerospace) in the 1990s — Bigelow was running orbital modules, including the inflatable module that attached to the International Space Station, plus a deep UFO interest. That relationship would become the route by which Puthoff ended up officially inside the UAP question.

### The W. Bush–Era Disclosure Workshop (~2004)

A think-tank head in Washington — name withheld — summoned Puthoff: "Come, it'll be the most important meeting you've ever had." Attendees included Puthoff's ex-CIA contract monitor, [[dia|DIA]] people, and military. The framing: "Let's just say that the United States, Russia, and China have obtained ET craft that have crashed, and we have proof of that. Bodies that aren't human." Question: can this be released to the public, what would the effect be? They built a ~60-item impact list (stock market, religion, government, policies, politics) and each group scored each item from −9 to +9.

Puthoff's group reached negative net numbers on a deep-weeds scenario: crash material given to corporation A, not to corporation B; A becomes wildly profitable years later; B goes bankrupt and learns it was excluded; B sues A and the government. Every breakout group returned negative net. The exercise's takeout: forget disclosure, at least for now. Puthoff: "Was this during George Herbert Walker Bush's? No, it was during Bush 2."

Puthoff did not know during the exercise whether the framing was hypothetical or briefed truth. He still doesn't.

### Why He Reversed — The Edward Teller Argument

The argument that flipped Puthoff toward favoring more disclosure came from [[edward-teller|Edward Teller]]: in the Manhattan-project nuclear domain we kept the secret tightly, but the US and Russia advanced in lockstep step-by-step. In electronics — circuit boards, semiconductors — we never classified the field, and we "took off like a rocket and left Russia in the dust." Teller's conclusion (also Puthoff's): more openness in even national-security areas wins more than it loses.

### Harry Reid, 2008, and the Bigelow Contract

In 2008 [[harry-reid|Harry Reid]] (Senate Majority Leader), [[daniel-inouye|Daniel Inouye]] (Hawaii), and [[ted-stevens|Ted Stevens]] (Alaska) — Gang of Eight members — directed [[dia|DIA]] to issue an RFP. The DIA's top propulsion physicist [[james-lacatski|Jim Lacatski]] wrote the RFP. [[robert-bigelow|Bigelow]] won it. Bigelow brought Puthoff onto the resulting program — what later became known publicly as [[aawsap|AAWSAP]]/[[bass|BAASS]]. That was 2008–2012; [[aatip|ATIP]] (later under [[lue-elizondo|Elizondo]]) picked up the ball. When the funding dried up, the cover labels ("advanced propulsion") meant another group took the money for actual propulsion work — and challenging it would have exposed what the program was really about.

### Kona Blue and DHS

Post-AATIP, the same network went to the [[department-of-homeland-security|Department of Homeland Security]] to set up a new SAP under the name [[kona-blue|Kona Blue]] — a name now declassified because [[aaro|AARO]] disclosed it. Puthoff says they "built up a stack of documents to the ceiling here about what needed to be done." Kona Blue did not get approved.

### Materials Negotiations With Aerospace Primes

Puthoff says his AAWSAP task was: look at all the physics and engineering that might be behind UAP. The other side of the deal: "we will arrange for you to get access to some materials." A long back-and-forth with aerospace executives ensued. The final position: "if that were the case, it would be too compartmentalized. We can't share this even though you have an official program. You've got Top Secret SCI Gamma HCS — but if we had materials, it'd be too highly classified to share." Rogan: "If there was nothing to negotiate about, you'd say, 'We don't have materials.' You wouldn't say, 'You don't have enough clearance for us to even discuss this.' Exactly. And so already they're tipping their hat." Puthoff confirms. The fallback plan — survey subject-matter experts on where their fields will be in 2050 under a [[bigelow-aerospace|Bigelow Aerospace]] cover story — produced the [[dird-papers|38 DIRD papers]].

### The Jaywick Server and the 38 Papers

The 38 papers — titles include "Anti-neutronic Fusion Propulsion," "Superconductors and Gravity Research," "Positron Aerospace Propulsion," "Warp Drive, Dark Energy, Extra Dimensions," "Advanced Nuclear Propulsion," "Spacetime Metric Engineering," "Traversable Wormholes," "Stargates," and "Brain-Machine Interfaces" — went up on the [[jaywick-server|Jaywick]] classified server (Pentagon/IC/aerospace-contractor access only). They were so popular among readers that every attempted takedown was protested, so they stayed up indefinitely. Most have since been released via FOIA. None of the authors were told the survey was for UAP work.

### Art Bell's Bismuth-Magnesium Sample — Oak Ridge Analysis

An Army-officer source said his grandfather was involved in picking up debris from the [[1947-roswell|Roswell]] crash and sent the material to [[art-bell|Art Bell]]; Art Bell handed it to [[linda-moulton-howe|Linda Moulton Howe]] (referred to as "Linda how" in transcript). Results: (i) isotope ratios are terrestrial — no proof it's from outside our solar system. (ii) The layered bismuth-magnesium structure — some layers human-hair-thick — has no precedent in materials-development history, and "they can't even imagine anybody want to make it." An aerospace contractor was asked: can you bond bismuth and magnesium in layers like this? Two layers achieved; cost over $1M; broke their instruments. The sample (~4 inches, ~18 layers, ~quarter-inch thick) remains officially "an anomaly." See [[arts-parts]] for the full provenance and follow-on analysis.

Puthoff cites [[diana-pasulka|Diana Pasulka]]: "She refers to them as donations. She said that's how they were described to her." ### Battelle Memory Metal — Nitinol Trail

Puthoff confirms the public-domain story that [[battelle-memorial-institute|Battelle Institute]] received Roswell foil material that "you could crumple up and then let go and it just flattens out again." Battelle worked on it for years; the publicly-known outcome was [[memory-metal|Nitinol]] — heat-and-reform metal that doesn't fully reproduce the original effect but is partial. Senior material engineers on the project told their relatives on their deathbeds that they had been working on Roswell pieces.

## Physics of the Phenomenon

### "It's Not Beyond Our Physics — Beyond Our Engineering"

Puthoff's standard frame for UAP performance: not magic, not new physics; existing physics whose engineering is intractable for us. His worksheet method: list all the weird claims (right-angle Mach-10 turns, size changes with proximity, color changes with proximity, time dilation reports, visible-spectrum heat signatures) on the left. List Einstein's general-relativistic effects on the right. Look for hand-in-glove matches. He finds them. The missing piece: the engineering energy density.

### The Alcubierre Energy Problem

[[miguel-alcubierre|Miguel Alcubierre]] — Star Trek fan who turned his interest into a peer-reviewed [[alcubierre-warp-drive|warp-drive]] paper from Einstein's equations — produced solutions that work *in the equations*. But the required energy density is "hundreds of times more than the energy of the sun." Puthoff: "Until we have a new energy source or until there's some backdoor that we haven't sewered in on, it's just really outside of our expertise."

### Vacuum Energy and the 120-Order-of-Magnitude Problem

The candidate backdoor: [[zero-point-energy|vacuum fluctuations]] / [[zero-point-energy|zero-point energy]]. Quantum oscillators don't go to zero at the ground state — the swing keeps swinging. "Empty space" is full of quantum fluctuations. The conundrum of modern physics: when standard QFT is used to calculate the local vacuum-fluctuation energy density, the result is ~120 orders of magnitude greater than other theories permit — it would collapse gravity and everything else. The fluctuations evidently cancel out, all randomized. The engineering question: cohere them, make them non-random, and you have access to (potentially) the engineering energy density required.

### Joseph of Cupertino — Vacuum-Energy Levitation Hypothesis

Puthoff frames the 1628 Italian Franciscan [[joseph-of-cupertino|Joseph of Cupertino]] levitation events as a clue. The Spanish ambassador and his wife visited him; Joseph saw an elevated statue of Mary 10 ft above the ground, flew over the crowd to the statue, prayed, flew back to the door, and went home. The church took depositions from the witnesses; their accounts were consistent. The Catholic Church, contra a "magical-saint" reading, sequestered known levitators in monasteries — Puthoff says the records of the Inquisition support the genuineness of the phenomenon precisely because the Church was uncomfortable with it. Puthoff's physics read: "The only thing I can think of in terms of the physics we know today would be that somehow the vacuum energy, which can be very high, if you cohered it and if you made it non-random — maybe maybe that could do it." Mechanism: ecstatic-state consciousness accessing what we don't yet know how to access scientifically.

### Sakharov and Inertia-as-Vacuum

Puthoff places his vacuum-energy program in the lineage of [[andrei-sakharov|Andrei Sakharov]]: "I don't think gravity is its own thing — I think really it's a manifestation of the underlying quantum fluctuations." Puthoff and Lockheed Martin colleagues followed the inertia-from-vacuum idea: when you accelerate in a car, you're pressed back, and the windshield is not the cause; one model says it's because acceleration through vacuum fluctuations is what pushes back on you. If inertia and gravity are connected (equivalence principle), and inertia is vacuum-fluctuation-mediated, then a route to gravity control runs through vacuum-fluctuation control.

### Visible-Spectrum Heat and X-Ray Burns

A specific consequence of [[metric-engineering|metric engineering]] gravity control: under those conditions, frequencies get shifted upward. Ordinary infrared heat of a craft gets shifted into the visible — explaining why powered-up craft are so bright. Same shift continues upward: get too close to a landing powered-up craft and ordinary visible-spectrum becomes UV / soft X-ray. Result: sunburn, possible radiation poisoning. Both are reported close-encounter phenomena.

### Quantum Communication With John Paul DeJoria

Puthoff is now actively in partnership with [[john-paul-dejoria|John Paul DeJoria]] on a [[subrosa-quantum-communication|quantum-communication program]] — a proof-of-principle Puthoff demonstrated on a classified contract in the 1990s and shelved while quantum-detector hardware caught up. The 1990s blocker — cryogenic detectors — has now been broken by quantum-computing investment: [[josephson-junctions|Josephson junctions]] operating near absolute zero (Puthoff's bench: ~3.7 K) are now mature. The communication architecture: explicitly designed antenna geometries that prevent E and B field components from being transmitted while preserving the quantum signal underlying ordinary EM. Detection is the trade secret: ordinary EM detectors generate currents from incoming fields, but the quantum signal doesn't carry E/B; only quantum devices can detect it.

Applications:

## The Schumer–Rounds Push and the State of Disclosure

### The 2023 UAP Disclosure Act

[[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] and [[mike-rounds|Mike Rounds]] (with [[kirsten-gillibrand|Gillibrand]] and [[marco-rubio|Rubio]]) drafted a multi-page amendment to the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) called the [[uap-disclosure-act|UAP Disclosure Act of 2023]]. Per Puthoff: an official government document that mentions "non-human intelligence" >20 times. Mechanism: a presidentially-appointed nine-person panel; [[eminent-domain|eminent domain]] over corporate-held materials; a [[national-archives|National Archives]] track for public release. The Senate passed it; the House killed it. Schumer and Rounds returned to the Senate floor: "We're not giving up. We're going to get it in there next year." 2024: most was killed again, but the National Archives provision survived and "has started to do that." Practical limit: corporations holding genuinely consequential material aren't going to volunteer it.

### Recent Senator Rounds Briefing

Puthoff was asked to brief [[mike-rounds|Senator Rounds]] in person, who said: "We always get the pushback that, well, we're not going to make any headway. The pilots say this is way beyond our physics, but I understand that you and your colleagues have worked on this. Give me what you've got on the physics, because I need to push back on the pushback." Puthoff delivered the metric-engineering / polarizable-vacuum lecture he has also given to the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence (SSCI), the Senate Armed Services Committee (SASC), and [[aaro|AARO]].

### The Anna Paulina Luna Hearing

Puthoff notes the recording occurs the day before a Congressional hearing organized (per his recollection) by [[anna-paulina-luna|Representative Luna]] — UFO-files release on the official list alongside JFK/RFK/MLK/Epstein. (The JFK/RFK/MLK/Epstein/UFO release-list framing comes from a recent administration document.)

### The Age of Disclosure Documentary

Puthoff is one of 38 interviewees in [[age-of-disclosure|The Age of Disclosure]], directed/produced by [[dan-farah|Dan Farah]] (who collaborated with Spielberg on *Ready Player One*). Farah's strategy: get 38 maximum insiders — including [[marco-rubio|Senator Rubio]] (Secretary of State), former DNI [[james-clapper|Clapper]], and [[jay-stratton|Jay Stratton]] (former UAP Task Force head) — to coordinate on what each is willing to say and release it as one bundled disclosure event. South by Southwest premiere; Netflix or major distribution sought. "Disclosure on steroids."

### How To Do It Right

Puthoff's prescription mirrors the Schumer-Rounds language: a high-classification all-on-the-table review of what can be released without jeopardizing national-security advantage; a nine-person panel deciding what comes out; reconciliation/no-jail amnesty for those who covered up under instruction; structured lanes for whistleblower information. Driver: compartmentalization is hurting US competitiveness more than openness would, and China can mobilize unified state labs while US contractors silo. Puthoff: "even though generally I'm of the feeling that there should be more disclosure, I'm also very tight on anything that could be potentially helpful to an adversary in this area."

### "Within a Decade" Forecast

Closing prediction: "I would find it difficult to believe that within a decade we're not going to figure out how to do this and there will be what you and I would call disclosure, but in a responsible way."
