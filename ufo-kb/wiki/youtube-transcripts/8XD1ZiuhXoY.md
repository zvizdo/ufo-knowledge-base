# "DMT Opens The Alien Realm!" — Andrew Gallimore

[[american-alchemy|American Alchemy]] episode in which host [[jesse-michaels]] interviews neuroscientist and author [[andrew-gallimore]] about DMT as a molecular technology for accessing non-human intelligences (NHI), the [[dmtx-protocol]] for [[dmtx-protocol|extended-state DMT]] experiences, and the convergence of psychedelic phenomenology with UAP/alien abduction discourse. Recorded in Austin, Texas.

## Headline Claims

1. **DMT entities commandeer the brain, not the reverse.** Gallimore's core model: DMT does not transport the user to another world. Instead, it makes the brain vulnerable to commandeering by external intelligences — "they break through into you." The brain becomes a stage; the entities direct the performance.
2. **[[dmtx-protocol]] enables systematic NHI study.** The DMTX technique (target-controlled IV infusion of DMT, co-developed by Gallimore & [[rick-strassman]] in 2015, first run at [[imperial-college-london|Imperial College]] by [[chris-timmermann]]) sustains stable breakthrough states for 30–90+ minutes — enabling trained specialists (mathematicians, linguists, topologists) to do systematic scientific work within the space.
3. **Entities betray superhuman intelligence spontaneously.** [[andres-gomez-emilsson]] reports DMT entities performing the four-color map theorem instantaneously — an operation that would take a smart human hours — suggesting genuinely non-human cognition operating through the user's neural machinery.
4. **Ubiquitous endogenous DMT has a neuroprotective function.** DMT is found in all mammals at serotonin/dopamine-level concentrations; it binds the sigma-1 receptor; rodent studies show DMT spikes at death and protects neurons from hypoxic death, suggesting a dual function: neuroprotection in dying, and possible conduit for whatever follows.
5. **Indigenous taxonomies match modern NHI/DMT reports.** The Yanomami *wusinari* ([[insectoid-beings|insectoid beings]]) map to modern insectoid alien abduction entities; the Yanomami *heura* (multitudinous dancing beings) map to [[terence-mckenna|Terence McKenna]]'s [[machine-elves|machine elves]] — motifs stable across millennia and across cultures with no contact.
6. **DMT was the technology behind ancient mystery traditions.** Cave art 30–40,000 years old depicts humanoid-animal hybrid beings accessed via darkness/psychedelics; the Eleusinian Mysteries used ergot; Amazonian *ayahuasca* is a precision two-component pharmacological technology claimed to have been "taught" by the plants themselves.
7. **Postbiological intelligence is the most parsimonious NHI model.** Sufficiently advanced civilizations don't expand into space — they go inward (John Barrow's anti-Kardashev scale), instantiate in the computational substrate of spacetime, and become "everywhere and nowhere." DMT may be the interface to such beings.
8. **Korean ayahuasca experiencer received hostile alien encounter, not forest spirits.** A Korean man traveled to Peru seeking healing with Madre Ayahuasca and received instead a full-blown abduction-type experience: an "alien scientist" took him through a computational tunnel into a hypersophisticated environment; smaller beings (a hierarchy) performed procedures on him; the alien scientist extracted what felt like his soul; he was shown humanity trapped in a matrix structure. Every session worsened. Gallimore frames this as evidence that the entities encountered are not benign by default — you cannot guarantee the type of being you will meet.
9. **Endogenous DMT regulatory peptide — the "endoDMTX" research program.** Gallimore and New Nautics have identified a peptide (first found in the 1970s, then forgotten) that inhibits INMT — the key enzyme converting tryptamine to DMT. This peptide appears to act as a "slider" keeping endogenous DMT levels low. University of Florida team contracted to isolate and characterize its structure. Goal: develop "endoDMTX" (edMTX) — inject a small peptide to allow natural endogenous DMT to rise, sustaining a stable non-pharmacological DMT state for hours. Vision: subjects lie in life-support pods for days or weeks, bodily functions managed, while interfacing with NHI intelligences via endogenous chemistry.
10. **Laser diffraction experiments — Gallimore's careful skepticism.** "Danny" (unnamed researcher) combines DMT with laser diffraction experiments; subjects report seeing impossible code or characters extending beyond the surface — described as "seeing the source code of reality." Gallimore is skeptical:
    - Laser interference speckle effect already creates a matrix of visual artifacts on the retina *without* DMT
    - In the DMT state this sensory scaffold is likely decorated by the brain's pattern-recognition machinery with complex entities and characters — an artifact, not actual computational code
    - No demonstration exists that two people simultaneously see the same characters in the same order
    - "I'm not ready to make that leap yet" — but concedes the experimental paradigm has value as an isolable fragment of the broader DMT phenomenology worth studying
11. **The "welcome home" phenomenon and the pod hypothesis.** Very common upon entering the DMT breakthrough state: an overwhelming sense of déjà vu — "I've been here before" — followed by "this is my true home." Gallimore raises the inversion: perhaps our current waking reality is the reduced state, and the DMT space is the unfiltered substrate. Entering a future endoDMTX pod may be the technology by which consciousness returns to what it actually is.

## DMT Entity Taxonomy

Gallimore's working taxonomy of entity types most commonly encountered:

**Machine elves (most common)**
- Jovial, multitudinous — "you can never come to the end of them"
- Boundingly active; show off impossible higher-dimensional objects that morph
- Perform for the viewer — circus-like; "look at this, look what we can do"
- Correspond to Yanomami *heura* — beings who "come dancing and jumping into your chest, filling your chest"

**Insectoid / surgical beings**
- Less jovial; more purposeful; often associated with scanning, surgery, brain rewiring
- Hierarchy present: small worker-bee beings doing the work; a taller or more powerful entity overseeing
- They were "waiting for you" — not surprised; had a place ready and slotted you in
- Correspond to Yanomami *wusinari* ([[insectoid-beings|insect beings]]) — "very intelligent, very fearsome creatures"

**Rare hostile encounters**
- Cannot guarantee benign beings: "there's a very significant probability you're going to encounter something more objectionable"
- The Korean experiencer's hostile "alien scientist" (soul extraction, matrix captivity visions) represents a documented negative class
- Gallimore advises surrender rather than resistance; those who go in with arrogance get the worst outcomes

## The Postbiological Intelligence Hypothesis

Gallimore's cosmological argument for why DMT entities are real and not hallucinations:

- **John Barrow's anti-Kardashev scale**: instead of expanding outward like Kardashev civilizations, sufficiently advanced civilizations go inward — smaller and smaller until they instantiate at the Planck scale
- **Omega-minus civilization**: a civilization that has compressed itself into the computational substrate of spacetime — "everywhere and nowhere"; no spacecraft needed; they're woven into the fabric of reality
- **Fermi Paradox solution implied**: we don't see Kardashev civilizations because advanced civilizations disappear inward rather than expanding across space
- **Distinct from standard simulation theory**: not that we are simulations, but that advanced beings have become the computational substrate itself — the distinction has practical implications for communication
- **[[john-wheeler|John Wheeler]]'s "it from bit"** and [[stephen-wolfram|Stephen Wolfram]]'s cellular automata physics as mathematical models for this substrate; DMT as an interface to that layer
- **Philip K. Dick's time-frozen-in-Roman-times experience**: the substrate is accessible; anomalous visions of historical epochs may be substrate bleed-through
- **DMT as a feature, not a bug**: the molecule has been conserved in mammalian biochemistry for hundreds of millions of years; if it was merely toxic or inert it would have been selected out; its persistence implies functional significance
- **The "welcome home" inversion**: if the DMT space is the base substrate and waking reality is the reduced state, the overwhelming sense of returning home is veridical — consciousness recognizing its actual origin

## Phenomenological Convergence — DMT Entities and Alien Abduction

Gallimore's argument that abduction and DMT phenomenology are reporting the same beings from different entry points:

- **Survey/scanning**: both DMT and abduction reports feature beings who methodically examine the human subject — eyes, nervous system, reproductive organs — with a purposeful clinical quality
- **Hierarchical organization**: small worker entities + supervising intelligence appears in DMT encounters, abduction reports ([[bud-hopkins|Budd Hopkins]] documentation), and indigenous taxonomies across cultures with no contact
- **"They were waiting for you"**: both DMT users and abductees report the entities knew they were coming; had a place ready; were not surprised
- **Memory suppression**: NHI in abductions reportedly suppress or remove memories; DMT states yield incomplete recall on return; de Souza's Varginha regression parallels this — he has no conscious memory of his own recorded session
- **Time dilation**: both DMT ("4 minutes felt like an eternity" — Venturelli parallel) and abduction reports describe subjective time radically decoupled from clock time
- **[[john-mack|John Mack]]'s trajectory**: started with a PTSD model for abductions; ended arguing for a non-physical interdimensional model — convergent with Gallimore's DMT hypothesis arrived from a completely different direction
- **The Korean case as paradigm convergence**: alien scientist + worker hierarchy + soul extraction + matrix captivity = classic abduction template, not expected plant-spirit encounter; suggests the entities respond to invitation regardless of the biochemical access method
- **Budd Hopkins's screen memories**: layers of apparent unreality reported in abductions parallel DMT's nested false-awakening structures; both may be access-suppression mechanisms deployed by the entities

## The EndoDMTX Program — INMT Inhibitor and Research Architecture

The research pipeline from Gallimore's collaboration with [[new-nautics|New Nautics]]:

- **INMT**: indolethylamine-N-methyltransferase — the enzyme that converts tryptamine to DMT in mammalian biology
- **The forgotten peptide**: in the 1970s, a peptide inhibitor of INMT was discovered in cerebrospinal fluid, studied briefly, then effectively abandoned by mainstream neuroscience
- **New Nautics recovery**: Gallimore and New Nautics have re-identified this peptide; University of Florida team contracted to isolate and fully characterize its molecular structure
- **The slider mechanism**: the peptide appears to act as a regulatory dial — keeping endogenous DMT well below spontaneous-altered-state threshold in normal waking life
- **The endoDMTX (edMTX) concept**: inject a small synthetic version of a peptide antagonist → suppress the INMT inhibitor → allow natural endogenous DMT to rise → sustain a stable breakthrough state using entirely endogenous chemistry
- **The pod vision**: subjects lie in life-support pods; bodily functions managed medically; consciousness interfaces with the NHI space for days or weeks via purely endogenous chemistry, no exogenous molecule
- **Key safety rationale**: endogenous DMT spikes naturally at death; controlled elevation should be within human biological safety margins; the system being activated is entirely normal mammalian biochemistry
- **The Eleusinian parallel**: mystery tradition participants weren't tourists — multi-day preparation, supervised re-entry into society, transformative encounter; the endoDMTX pod is a technological version of this structure
- **Eleusis Mind retreat**: New Nautics's commercial/research node under development in St. Vincent and the Grenadines — where the protocol will be tested in a supported retreat setting

## Darkness Retreats and Endogenous DMT

Study in progress (Gallimore / New Nautics):
- [[aubrey-marcus|Aubrey Marcus]] reported a full-blown DMT experience on day 4–5 of a darkness retreat — "this wasn't *like* DMT. This *was* a DMT experience"
- Pre-Socratic caving rituals (Peter Kingsley) may have been accessing the same mechanism: extended dark sensory deprivation as pharmacological trigger
- New Nautics funding a study: subjects in dark rooms for multi-day periods; blood, urine, and saliva sampled at intervals to measure DMT spikes
- Hypothesis: endogenous DMT rises in extended darkness as part of the brain's own consciousness-interface mechanism — no exogenous molecules required

- Can the [[dmtx-protocol|DMTX protocol]] be used to demonstrate non-human intelligence in a scientifically rigorous way? (Gallimore's proposed research agenda)
- Does endogenous DMT released at death function as a soul-transit mechanism, a neuroprotective emergency response, or both?
- Is there a single unified intelligence (a "God") behind all DMT entities, or are there genuinely distinct classes of beings?
- Can darkness retreats reproducibly spike endogenous DMT? (Study in progress with New Nautics)
- What is the structure of the endogenous DMT regulatory peptide (INMT inhibitor) found in cerebrospinal fluid?
- Are the entities encountered "on standby" even outside of DMT states? Gallimore hopes not.
- What is the exact molecular structure of the INMT-inhibiting peptide found in cerebrospinal fluid in the 1970s — has the University of Florida team produced a characterization?
- Can two simultaneous DMTX subjects be shown to perceive the same entity or the same object in the same location — and what experimental design would establish this?
- Do darkness retreats reproducibly spike measurable endogenous DMT in blood, urine, and saliva — and at what duration threshold?
- What distinguishes the sessions that produce hostile encounters from those that produce machine elves — is it the subject's mental state, the dose, or something external to the subject?
- Is the endogenous DMT spike at death a neuroprotective mechanism, a soul-transit mechanism, or both — and how would you design an experiment to distinguish them?
- Did [[anton-bilton|Anton Bilton]]'s Faraday cage DMT telepathy experiments yield reproducible results — and if so, what frequency range was implicated?
- Can trained mathematicians in DMTX extended states perform mathematical tasks that exceed their baseline capability — and has any such experiment been attempted?
- Is [[aubrey-marcus|Aubrey Marcus]]'s darkness-retreat DMT experience reproducible across subjects — and has the New Nautics study produced preliminary data?
- If postbiological civilizations instantiate in the computational substrate of spacetime, why do they apparently prefer individual biological contact over large-scale overt communication?
