# Insectoid Beings

A recurring NHI entity archetype encountered in both DMT states and alien abduction accounts — distinct from [[machine-elves]] in their affect and behavior. Characterized by:

- **Insect or mantid morphology**: large compound eyes, elongated limbs, exoskeletal appearance; often described as praying-mantis-like
- **Serious, purposeful affect**: less jovial than machine elves; more procedural, clinically focused
- **Medical/surgical agenda**: the dominant reported interaction involves scanning, examination, brain surgery, body modification — the abductee or DMT subject is worked on
- **Hierarchical context**: insectoid beings are often "worker" entities under a supervisory being (tall grey, or a more powerful alien scientist-type)
- **Fearsome or neutral**: encounters range from neutral clinical procedures to deeply distressing; unlike machine elves, they rarely "perform" for entertainment

## Convergence: DMT, Abduction, and Indigenous Reports

[[andrew-gallimore]] documents a three-way convergence:

1. **[[rick-strassman]]'s 1990s DMT subjects** spontaneously reported insectoid beings performing scanning and surgery — structurally identical to alien abduction reports, with no priming from abduction literature
2. **[[john-mack]]'s abductees** — the classic alien abduction typology includes small worker beings performing examinations under a supervisory taller being; [[bud-hopkins]]'s documentation also centers on this pattern
3. **Yanomami people (*wusinari*)**: the Yanomami describe insectoid beings who are "not human, not animal" — "very intelligent, very fearsome." This motif has no apparent cultural contamination from modern UFO discourse; it is indigenous. [[andrew-gallimore]] specifically names wusinari as the Yanomami insectoid being type.

This three-way convergence — modern DMT trials, alien abduction research, and pre-contact indigenous mythology — is Gallimore's strongest argument that insectoid beings represent a real, stable, externally-sourced entity archetype rather than a cultural artifact.

## Korean Ayahuasca Report

[[andrew-gallimore]] describes receiving an email from a Korean man who traveled to Peru for healing and instead had a full alien-science encounter: a hierarchical structure with smaller insectoid worker beings and an "alien scientist" figure who subjected him to soul-level torture across multiple sessions. He had no prior priming from Western UFO culture. The experience was entirely at odds with his expectations (he expected forest spirits); the beings matched the insectoid alien typology precisely.

## John Blitch Terror Encounter

[[john-blitch]] — Lt. Colonel (ret.), Delta Force veteran, DARPA program manager — describes a non-clinical insectoid encounter that diverges from the typical examination pattern:

> "This big bug came like through the screen door on our deck and he's standing over me, his little triangle face opens up and these jaws open up and he starts tugging chunks of flesh off of me — from my cheeks, my shoulders — and he's flinging it... He wanted me to know that they could rip me to shreds if they wanted to, but they couldn't get to my soul."

Blitch woke with bruises. He interprets the violence as **intentional communication calibrated to his psychology**: as a combat-trained warrior for whom only extreme force could penetrate resistance, the entity used graphic violence to deliver a specific message — *the soul is beyond their reach*. The interaction is thus a form of **hostile demonstration followed by reassurance**, rather than the examination/procedure pattern typical of abduction accounts.

This adds a distinct sub-category to insectoid encounter typology: **terror as message delivery**, where the entity deliberately demonstrates destructive capability before establishing its limit.

## Connection to Abduction Hierarchy

The typical hierarchical structure in abduction accounts:
- **Small grey worker beings** ([[small-grays]]): perform the procedural work
- **Tall grey or supervisory entity** ([[tall-gray]]): oversees; stronger presence; not physically working
This matches both Strassman's DMT subjects and the Yanomami wusinari hierarchy (smaller workers + one powerful supervisory being).
