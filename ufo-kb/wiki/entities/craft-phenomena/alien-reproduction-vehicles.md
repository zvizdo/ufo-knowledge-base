# Alien Reproduction Vehicles (ARVs)

Alien Reproduction Vehicles (ARVs) are alleged human-manufactured craft built by reverse-engineering recovered non-human technology. The term distinguishes domestically-produced copies from the original non-human craft — the ARV hypothesis claims the US (and possibly other states) has successfully replicated some functional principles of recovered UAPs.

## The 1988 Norton AFB Account

The primary witness is [[brad-sorensen]], an aerospace professional who attended the 1988 USAF Museum Gala at [[norton-air-force-base]]. Sorensen was reportedly taken to a secure hangar and shown three craft:

1. **Craft #1**: A small "saucer" described as roughly 10-12 feet in diameter
2. **Craft #2**: The "Pulsar" — a larger craft attributed to [[lockheed-skunk-works]]; reportedly used materials recovered from the [[1947-roswell]] crash
3. **The "Flux Liner"** — described by [[mark-mccandish]] in his 2001 [[2001-disclosure-project]] testimony as a craft using a central rotating mass and ring of capacitors to generate an electrogravitic field (essentially a scaled-up [[biefeld-brown-effect]] device)

McCandish produced detailed technical drawings of the Flux Liner based on Sorensen's description. [[eric-davis]] has disputed the account from a classified briefing context.

## Technical Claims

ARVs reportedly employ:
- **Asymmetric capacitor arrays** (Biefeld-Brown propulsion principle) at high voltages
- **Rotating magnetic/gravitational fields** — sometimes described as a "rotating mercury plasma" core
- **Electrostatic lift** rather than conventional thrust — no exhaust, near-silent operation

These features overlap with independent claims about the B-2 stealth bomber's undisclosed charge system (see [[anti-gravity-manhattan-project]], [[thomas-townsend-brown]]).
