# Monzerat

One of the named M-type hominid bodies in the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] collection (4–5 ft tall, three fingers, three toes, organs intact). Monzerat was pregnant at the time of death and the fetus is visible on tomography.

**Why Monzerat is load-bearing**: per [[david-ruiz]] in [[HxQN2tkQHs8|the American Alchemy episode]], a conventional doctor handed only the raw measurement data — no images, no 3D reconstructions — would identify "this baby has three fingers in the hands and feet." If the fetus is genuinely tridactyl on independent reanalysis, the surgical-mutilation hypothesis is **falsified at the level of any known synthetic-biology capability** — no contemporary lab can fake a fetus inside a mummified body that matches the mother's morphology. This makes Monzerat the highest-leverage specimen in the collection for ending the debunk debate.

**Pose at time of death**: Monzerat's hands appear in the air on tomography. Per [[david-ruiz]], this is because the amniotic fluid (water) was lost post-mortem — she was holding her belly when she died, and the deflation of the amniotic sac made her arms appear elevated.

**Status**: as of episode recording, Monzerat is among the bodies held at the [[university-of-ika]] under VP [[martin-alcron]]'s administration, which has fought 7 attempts by the [[peruvian-ministry-of-culture]] to seize the collection. The forthcoming [[colossal-biosciences]] / George Church proprietary ancient-DNA testing — if granted by the Peruvian government — would reanalyze Monzerat among others.
