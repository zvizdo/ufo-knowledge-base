# J-Rod

**J-Rod** is the name given to an alleged living recovered non-human entity said to have been housed in a containment sphere located 7–6 levels underground at the [[s4]] facility near Area 51. The claim originates primarily with [[dan-burish]], who describes himself as a microbiologist assigned to extract blood and tissue samples from J-Rod during multiple contact sessions inside the sphere (~2003–2004 interviews).

[[bill-uhouse]] also references a J-Rod figure and an ET vehicle simulator at Area 51 auxiliary sites, providing an independent second claim.

## Description

Burish describes J-Rod as a grey-type biological with neurological differences from humans. The "J-Rod" designation is reportedly a phonetic transliteration of the entity's name.
