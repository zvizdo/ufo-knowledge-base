# Mobile Construction Unit (MCU)

Term used by the [[4chan-whistleblower-2023]] to describe what appears to be a class of large, semi-permanent underwater craft operating as **autonomous manufacturing facilities** for smaller UAPs.

## Description

Per the anonymous whistleblower account (as summarized by [[chris-ramsey]]):

- **Shape**: Large, burger-like — wide, flat, layered structure
- **Location**: Ocean floor; primary reported location near the Bahamas / Bermuda Triangle area; whistleblower believed multiple MCUs exist globally based on simultaneous sightings in geographically separate locations
- **Mobile**: Can relocate; the "mobile" designation distinguishes it from a fixed installation
- **Size**: Sufficient to house multiple smaller craft inside, with crew/operational space

## Function: Manufacturing Platform

The MCU's primary apparent function is **manufacturing smaller UAPs to exact specification**:
- Interior walls have precisely fitted instrument/tool mounts — every item placed in a dedicated recess
- Smaller craft (including unmanned spherical drones) are assembled inside and deployed to destinations unknown
- Deployed craft always return to the MCU after their missions
- Manufacturing appears to follow a consistent template: the whistleblower's second team stripped internal components post-crash and confirmed the "built-to-spec" consistency across multiple vehicles

## Craft Types Deployed

- **Spherical drones**: Unmanned; used for scanning; emit orange light when scanning for minerals or biologics; if an orange light approaches a human, abduction likelihood increases
- **Larger crewed vehicles**: Earlier generation; post-body-recovery switch to unmanned fleet

## Behavioral Profile

- **Non-aggressive by default**: If approached non-threateningly, evades and disappears
- **Defensive if cornered with hostile intent**: Destroys nearby electronics; any weapons platforms (submarines, jets, boats) that engaged "never came back"
- **Underwater speed**: Consistent with [[lue-elizondo]]'s public report of a USO traveling at 500+ knots underwater
