# Nordics / Norwegians

Nordic-type humanoid aliens — called "**Norwegians with 24 teeth**" by [[charles-hall]] — are the third and least technologically advanced of the three ET groups Hall observed using Earth during his 1965–67 posting at [[indian-springs]]. In the broader UAP discourse they appear in the contactee literature as "Pleiadians," "Space Brothers," or simply "Nordics."

Hall's account treats them as fully biological and physically distinct from humans in a specific dental/biological way (24 teeth vs. 32 in humans), though he notes some can pass as humans in public.

## Characteristics (per Hall)

**Technology:**
- The least capable of the three groups — "just barely able to travel in space and make it here"
- Cannot make in-flight repairs to their craft; must wait until they arrive to repair
- Cannot navigate to the "back" (gravitationally clear) side of Earth the way Tall Whites can; they must land "at any price" wherever they can, including the riskier approach side
- As of 1965, were negotiating with the American government to **build new craft on Earth** — implying their existing fleet was inadequate or in poor repair

**Biology:**
- 24 teeth (Hall's identifying biological marker vs. human 32)
- Some can pass among humans in public — close enough in appearance to blend into crowds in appropriate contexts

**Disposition:**
- More patient and diplomatic than [[tall-whites]], even more so than Grays
- Described by Hall as genuinely benevolent in disposition
- Late arrivals relative to the other two groups (arrived toward the end of the last ice age)

## Earth Relationship

- Arrived at the end of the last ice age — later than both Grays (pre-ice-age) and Tall Whites (mid-ice-age)
- Their relationship to Earth is less established than the other two groups; they lack the Grays' moon base and the Tall Whites' well-appointed embassy-style base
- In 1965, were actively trying to establish a more permanent presence through cooperation with the US government

## Cross-Account Patterns

The "Nordic" phenotype is one of the most consistently reported ET entity types in the contactee literature (1950s–1980s):
- George Adamski's "Orthon" (1952) — blond, beautiful humanoid from Venus
- Howard Menger's contacts — Nordic-type humanoids
- Billy Meier's "Pleiadians" — blonde, humanoid, philosophical
- Multiple Cold War contactee accounts describe Nordics emphasizing human brotherhood and peace

Hall's account is unusual in framing them as the *least technologically capable* group rather than the most advanced/spiritual — inverting the typical contactee portrayal.

See also [[contactee-nordic-fascist-nexus]] for the dark political thread: some Nordic-type contact claims have been associated with far-right or fascist political movements.
