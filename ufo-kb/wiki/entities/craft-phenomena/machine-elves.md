# Machine Elves

The most commonly encountered and reported NHI type in DMT states. Named "self-transforming machine elves" or "machine elves" by [[terence-mckenna]], who described them as the dominant entities in the DMT breakthrough experience. Characterized across thousands of independent reports by:

- **Multitudinous presence**: they appear in large numbers, never one alone
- **Jovial, performative affect**: they are energetic, lively, and appear delighted to be encountered — "circus-like" in Gallimore's framing
- **Impossible higher-dimensional objects**: their signature behavior is displaying or performing geometric objects that should not be physically possible in 3D space — hyperdimensional objects, morphing constructions, tesseracts visible in their true form
- **Trickster quality**: they seem to enjoy the impossibility — "look at this, look what we can do"
- **Spontaneous appearance**: they do not wait to be invited; they seem prepared, as if expecting the arrival

[[andres-gomez-emilsson]]'s observation — entities performing the four-color theorem instantaneously — is the most rigorous evidence of superhuman capability from this entity class.

## Cross-Cultural Antiquity

[[andrew-gallimore]] emphasizes that machine elves are not a cultural artifact of Terence McKenna's influence. The motif appears:

- **1956 Hungary**: [[stephen-szara]]'s very first DMT trials produced reports of "little diminutive creatures, multitudinous, lively beings" — before McKenna was a public figure
- **Yanomami people (heura)**: multitudinous beings that "come dancing and singing and playing flutes, bounding around and jumping into your chest" — a precise match for the machine-elf typology. The Yanomami do not call them spirits; they call them "other" — not human, not animal
- **30,000-year cave art**: [[graham-hancock]]'s *Supernatural* argues that humanoid-animal hybrid beings in cave art (accessed via darkness and possibly psychedelics) represent the same ontological layer now accessed via DMT

This cross-cultural, cross-time stability is central to the argument that machine elves are not brain-generated but represent a consistent external intelligence.

## Relationship to Alien Abduction Entities

The **jovial, performative** machine elf type contrasts with the more serious insectoid/grey abduction entities in alien abduction accounts — though both appear in the DMT space. Rick Strassman's 1990s subjects reported the full gamut of abduction phenomenology: medical examinations, hierarchical beings, surgical procedures. The machine elves are more the "show" phase; the [[insectoid-beings]] are more the "work" phase.

[[john-mack]] noted directly that many of his abductees' experiences — including the sense of beings waiting for them, the technological environment, the hierarchy — matched what Strassman's DMT subjects reported.
