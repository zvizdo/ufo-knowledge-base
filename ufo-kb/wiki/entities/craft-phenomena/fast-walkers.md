# Fast Walkers

A classified UAP category tracked by **NORAD** (North American Aerospace Defense Command) via the **Defense Support Program (DSP)** — a network of infrared satellites monitoring Earth's outer defense layer, operated by the US Air Force. Fast Walkers are objects detected **transiting the DSP sensor layer at anomalous velocities** — moving through the outer edge of Earth's detection envelope in ways that do not match any known satellite, rocket, or aircraft.

## USAF Fast Walker Program — Official UAP Mission (9gLPtRwXgCM)

[[lue-elizondo]] states in JRE #2194 that the USAF **Fast Walker Program** had detecting UFOs as part of its **official mission** — *"that's a fact, that was part of their mission."* He also references a classified **Navy analog program** for tracking transmedium/USO objects, which he cannot discuss publicly without approval. This confirms the Fast Walker category has institutional acknowledgment at the program level, not just via the Podesta email reference.

## Classification Status

[[john-greenwald]] submitted a FOIA request to NORAD for documents on Fast Walkers. NORAD denied the request, classifying the subject as "very sensitive material" — one of the strongest FOIA rejections available, suggesting active operational classification rather than merely historical sensitivity.

## Podesta/Bob Fish Email (Wikileaks)

The term appears in the **Wikileaks archive** in an email exchange between [[john-podesta]] and [[bob-fish]], an aerospace contractor. Podesta's email references Fast Walkers as "vehicles moving through our outer layer of defense" — using the DSP/NORAD framing directly. This is notable because:
1. It confirms the term is used at the political/contractor nexus, not only among classified military operators
2. Podesta (later Hillary Clinton's 2016 campaign chair, and a longtime UAP disclosure advocate) was apparently briefed on or discussing the phenomenon at some level

## Significance

The Fast Walker category represents detections at the **outermost surveillance boundary** — space-to-Earth or Earth-to-space transits — rather than in-atmosphere sightings. This is qualitatively different from low-altitude UAP reports. If anomalous, it implies craft capable of entering or exiting Earth's atmosphere at speeds and trajectories detectable only by the most sensitive satellite sensors.

Shellenberger and [[jesse-michaels]] discuss the Podesta/Fish email in the context of a "cargo cult" structure — UAP programs set up from the 1950s whose oversight lines are deliberately obscured.
