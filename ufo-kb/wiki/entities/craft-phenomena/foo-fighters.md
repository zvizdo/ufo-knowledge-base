# Foo Fighters

"Foo fighters" was the Allied nickname for unexplained luminous objects encountered by aircraft crews over Europe and the Pacific during WWII. They were described as glowing spheres or discs, typically 1–3 feet in diameter, that would pace aircraft at speed, change direction instantly, and disappear without explanation. They never appeared hostile but could not be outmaneuvered or engaged.

Both Allied and Axis pilots reported the same phenomena — each initially assuming the objects were the other side's secret weapon. Post-war investigation revealed neither side had produced them. The US military conducted an investigation but reached no conclusion.

Foo fighters are significant as the earliest well-documented multi-witness military UAP encounters of the modern era, predating Roswell by 2–3 years and establishing the pattern of luminous, maneuverable objects behaving outside known physics.

## Reported Encounters

- European Theater (1944–45): USAAF 415th Night Fighter Squadron reported repeated encounters over the Rhine valley
- Pacific Theater: B-29 crews reported similar objects
- German pilots: Documented the same phenomena from the other side
