# Nazca Tridactyl Mummies

A collection of over 200 body parts, isolated heads, limbs, appendages, and intact bodies discovered in 2015 by huaquero [[leandro-gravedigger]] in a cave near the [[nazca-peru]] region of Peru. All specimens were covered in diatomaceous earth, a desiccant that dried and preserved them, keeping organs, tendons, cartilage, and bones intact. Carbon dating ranges from ~700 years ago to ~18,800 years ago, with most overlapping with the Nazca period (100–800 CE).

## Description

Three distinct body type categories:

**M-types (hominids):** 4–5 feet tall, most human-looking. Organs fully intact. Three fingers and three toes. Some have metal implants ([[osmium-cadmium-implants]]). Named specimens include Maria, Monzerat, Sebastian, and Santiago. Monzerat appears to have a three-fingered fetus inside, suggesting phenotypic inheritance and a living population. Skulls show no pressure-line artifacts of cranial binding. Fingers/toes show no mutilation evidence on CT scans.

**J-types (reptilians):** 2–3 feet tall, skinny, geometrically boxy bodies. Faces resemble beings from *Close Encounters of the Third Kind*. Some have eggs inside their bellies with visible capillaries nourishing the eggs on DICOM scans. 25–30 specimens. One isolated J-type head has alpaca proteins — potentially constructed from alpaca bone.

**S-types (winged):** Small winged creatures; less studied.

## Reported Encounters

Discovery in 2015 by Leandro in a cave (possibly the wrong cave — the real source may be a larger subterranean tunnel system). Over 200 body parts. Named M-types seen by Jesse Michaels firsthand: Monzerat, Sebastian, Santiago. Maria studied in most detail by DNA researchers. Louisa: J-type with eggs.

## Evidence

- CT scans and DICOM files publicly available at tridactyls.org ([[tridactyls-org]])
- Three independent forensic teams (McDow/Caruso, Ruiz, Zalce) found no mutilation or artificial deformation
- GLI3 genetic mutation found in NCBI data by [[elena-hardy]]
- DNA of Maria: ~70% known (anomalous combination), ~30% unknown — per [[jose-zalce]] and [[david-ranel]]
- [[osmium-cadmium-implants]] with organic osseointegration
- Fetus inside Monzerat: if three-fingered, strongest evidence for anatomical self-consistency
- J-type isolated head: alpaca proteins (proteomics analysis, anonymous expert) — potential fabrication indicator

## Controversies / Contradictions

> ⚠ Conflict: [[flavio-estrada]] (Peruvian Ministry of Culture) claims M-type fingers were mutilated and lengthened with cotton joints and synthetic adhesives — directly contradicted by three independent forensic teams finding no mutilation evidence.

> ⚠ Conflict: One isolated J-type head tested positive for alpaca bone proteins (anonymous proteomics expert) — potentially constructed; other J-type bodies show DICOM-visible capillaries and eggs.

> ⚠ Open: Maria's DNA reportedly produced one male and one female result from two different finger bones (per [[steve-meera]]'s report from Sri Lanka lab) — suggesting either contamination or composite construction.
