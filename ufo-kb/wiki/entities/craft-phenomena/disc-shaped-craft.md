# Disc-Shaped Craft

> *Stub page — expand with primary source import.*

The disc or flying-saucer form is the archetypal UAP shape, entering public consciousness after Kenneth Arnold's June 24, 1947 sighting near Mount Rainier. Arnold described nine objects with a motion "like a saucer skipping over water" — the press coined "flying saucer" from this description, even though Arnold's objects were crescent-shaped. Subsequent witness reports of *actual* disc shapes proliferated after the term entered the culture.

## Key Appearances

- **[[1947-roswell]]** — recovered craft described as disc-shaped in early Army press release (later retracted)
- **Contactee accounts** — [[udo-wartena]] and others describe disc-shaped craft in close-approach encounters
- **Nazi hypothesis** — [[horten-brothers]] flying wing designs and German disc projects are sometimes cited as terrestrial precursors
- **Frank Tipler** — references disc propulsion in theoretical physics speculation ([[frank-tipler]])

## Phenomenological Notes

The disc shape is aerodynamically stable for certain propulsion hypotheses (magnetohydrodynamic, electrogravitic) and appears across cultures in UAP reports with minimal prior exposure to Western UFO culture.
