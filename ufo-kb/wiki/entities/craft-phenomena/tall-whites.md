# Tall Whites

The Tall Whites are a non-human species described in detail by [[charles-hall]], who interacted with them for approximately 26 months (1965–1967) while stationed as a USAF weather observer at the Indian Springs Gunnery Ranges (Areas 53–54, "Dreamland"), Nevada. Hall's account is the longest sustained firsthand contact record in the knowledge base and provides the most detailed biological, technological, and sociological description of a specific NHI group.

## Appearance

- Tall, pale humanoids; white/fluorescent in appearance, particularly at night
- Wear protective suits that generate force fields; suits allow floating 9–10 inches off the ground (higher when powered up further)
- Suits emit visible radioactive decay-chain particles in darkness — tracks far longer than radium paint, indicating enormous power output
- When fully powered, suit+wearer appears as a fuzzy white glow; multiple Tall Whites powering up simultaneously merge their fields into a single larger force-field bubble
- Children's suits are slightly different; children float at ~waist height
- Can form a **"high-speed horse formation"**: 2 women bent forward (the "head"), 3 children at waist level (middle), 2 men at the rear with larger packs — visually indistinguishable at 1/4 mile from a giant fluorescent floating horse; capable of ~100 mph in this formation

Getting too close to a powered suit causes radiation burns equivalent to Hiroshima blast-victim injuries — Hall saw x-rays of a prior weather observer injured this way.

## Biology and Lifespan

- **Fully biological** — eat, drink, breathe air, reproduce, use bathrooms; Hall is emphatic that this eliminates "angel" or spiritual interpretations
- **Lifespan**: 600–800 years (~10× human); Hall's primary contact, "the Teacher," was born approximately when George Washington was born (~1730s)
- **Diet**: strict plant-eaters; no meat (same reported for [[small-grays]])
- **Reproduction**: travel as families; children present; teacher's daughter "the Butterfly" observed directly
- **Electronics**: wearable thought-reading/insertion devices (helmet-mounted); electronic, not psychic; fails on sick, very angry, or injured individuals — this is why Hall retained memories of his craft ride (he was injured at the time)

## Communication

- Communicate with each other in sounds above human hearing range (like horse winnies or bird calls)
- Electronic thought-reading and insertion technology reads human thoughts and inserts thoughts into the conscious mind — not spiritual telepathy, hardware only
- After Hall requested it, they agreed to speak English when around him
- The Teacher could read Hall's thoughts without him speaking; this asymmetry was why formal military debriefing of Hall was deemed impossible — any thought a debriefer had about Tall White children would be immediately read, potentially triggering lethal response

## Behavior and Disposition

- **Always heavily armed** even when friendly; Hall notes this as a constant
- Very goal-oriented even with humans they like
- Initially **terrified** of humans as much as humans are of them; the trust-building process was mutual and graduated
- Sociable: play on airstrips pretending to be airplanes; bring children to see interesting humans; have family outings; go to clean human bathrooms on base
- Highly protective of children — extremely dangerous if any threat to children is perceived
- Emotional: capable of laughter, nostalgia; the Teacher was observed being nostalgic about a Native American childhood friend from the ice age
- Relate to humans as humans might relate to a beloved aging dog — the brevity of human lives makes deep friendship bittersweet

Named individuals known to Hall:
- **The Teacher** — primary contact; female; ~230 years old in 1965; born ~1730s
- **Range for Harry** — permanent male guard at Indian Springs; Hall's closest relationship ("like a brother")
- **The Butterfly** — Teacher's young daughter
- **Pamela** — a younger female who initially failed, eventually passed, a human-trust test

## Technology

### Scout Craft
- Ellipsoidal/egg-shaped — smooth exterior, no protrusions, no sharp edges, no concavities
- Accelerate to 8,000–12,000 mph; sustain >15,000 G-forces (10,000 G's liquefies steel)
- Appear as white fuzzy "tic-tacs" when force fields fully powered
- **Cannot safely exceed ~80% of light speed** — this is not a ceiling of their FTL capability but a safety limit; craft with concave surfaces (like Ryan Graves's "sphere inside a cube") are inherently capped at ~80% light speed by force-field turbulence
- Can blink out laterally in an instant

### Deep (Interstellar) Spacecraft
- Roughly house-sized (longer and wider than a house, ~1.5× as tall)
- Fully smooth ellipsoidal exterior, double hull; ceramic fiber-optic coil windings between hulls
- Living quarters (cabins), cockpit on top, propulsion at rear; all internal components accessible for in-flight repair
- Capable of **faster-than-light travel** to distant star systems
- Scheduled arrivals/departures tied to the **full-moon window** — the gravitational field behind Earth in its orbit is uniform and receding, providing a safety margin if anything goes wrong; [[small-grays]] (with their moon base) can approach at any time; [[nordics-norwegians]] lack the navigation precision for this

### Propulsion
- Five classes of subatomic particles (including two unknown beyond Standard Model's six quarks) used in ceramic fiber-optic coils wound around the hull
- Powering different coil sets controls direction, acceleration, and force-field generation
- Ceramic fiber optics melt at 1,800–2,200°F — Hall argues this explains the Roswell crash debris (melted fiber optics misidentified as "melted rocks")
- **Not wormholes or space-time warping** — Hall explicitly rejects these; at FTL speeds, craft would still collide with physical obstacles

### Force Fields
- Surround craft; merge between multiple craft
- Dangerous to humans within ~2.5–3 miles (burns like a microwave)
- Can pop every lock open (electromagnetic repulsion)
- Explain the "fuzzy white glow" appearance of both suits and craft in witness accounts

## Their Relationship to Earth and Humanity

- Present on Earth **since at least the ice age** (Teacher was nostalgic about a Native American friend from "the middle of the last ice age")
- Use Earth as a **pit stop** — the solar system sits in a large void in space (~8–10 light years across); a refueling/repair/resupply stop mid-void is strategically essential for interstellar commerce
- The Tall White base at the north end of Indian Springs Valley is treated as a **foreign embassy** — American generals visited and interacted with them there
- A "tiny handful" of Pentagon personnel knew of and interacted with them; Hall was never formally briefed/debriefed
- Proposed to work with the American government to **build craft on Earth** — a technology transfer arrangement in progress in 1965
- Hall speculates (explicitly flagged as guesswork) that "Pamela" and a male disguised as "Charlie Hall" were attempting to pass as humans at Livermore National Laboratory as part of this transfer program

## Relationship to Other ET Groups

Per Hall's account, three groups use Earth; the Tall Whites are the most technologically advanced:

## Open Questions

- Is the Tall White base at Creech AFB (formerly Indian Springs Air Base) still operational?
- What is the Tall Whites' home star? Hall guesses Arcturus (~36 light years) based on approach timing, but is not certain.
- Did the technology transfer program (government collaboration to build craft on Earth) succeed?
- Can humans be trained to fly Tall White craft?
