# Tall Gray

A taller variant of the [[small-grays]] entity type, reported as occupying a supervisory or commanding role in encounters that include both small and tall Grays. Morphologically similar to small Grays but notably larger — in the Woods account, "a chest and a head taller" than the small Grays.

## Mario Woods Account (1977)

[[mario-woods]] observed one tall Gray walking behind the group of three small Grays during the [[1977-ellsworth-woods-abduction]]:
- A chest and a head taller than the uniform small Grays
- Had a device on its chest that **pulsated the same color** as the yellow-tipped wand carried by one of the small Grays
- This chromatic link between the chest device and the wand was what drew Woods's attention — and triggered his fear response
- The tall Gray appeared to be in command of the small Grays in the encounter

Under hypnotic regression, the tall Gray was the entity who performed the wrist procedure on Woods, telling him "we don't hurt you" when Woods communicated his pain.

## Pattern Across Accounts

Tall Grays appearing as supervisors or coordinators alongside smaller worker-type Grays is a documented recurring pattern across CE3/abduction accounts. The distinction parallels a caste or role structure rather than merely a size variation.
