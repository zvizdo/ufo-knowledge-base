# Maria (M-type)

One of the named M-type hominid bodies in the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] collection. Maria is the M-type body with the most-cited DNA work, and the subject of three of the most contested findings in the case.

**The 70/30 DNA decomposition** — sequencing returned ~70% identifiable + ~30% "completely different" / unknown. The 70% identifiable fraction itself contained an anomalous combination of three lineages: chimpanzee/bonobo, a separate African chimp lineage, and a South Asian human primate. Per [[jose-zalce]]: "even in the known DNA, it's a combination that you would never see — it's so exotic." This is the strongest single-specimen DNA argument in the collection.

**The GLI3 / Sonic Hedgehog finding** — bioinformatics expert [[elena-hardy]] cross-referenced the publicly-released NCBI data against the literature on digit-development genes (~50 in the Sonic Hedgehog pathway) and found a mutation in the GLI3 gene of the type associated with polydactyly (extra/fused digits) in human studies — including a documented Chinese family case. Hardy framed it as anomalous but not directly causally linked to tridactyly.

**The Sri Lanka contamination puzzle** — per [[steve-meera]], two finger bones from Maria sent to a Sri Lanka lab returned conflicting sex chromosomes — one came back male, one female. Jesse's reading: human contamination during handling, not evidence of composite construction. The episode flags "ancient DNA is very tricky."

**The Ranell hybridization theory** — [[david-ranel]] proposes Maria is a *natural* (not engineered) hybrid: ~250,000 years ago proto-bonobo and proto-troglodyte populations were active in Africa; some lineage migrated to Asia and back ~1,700–2,500 years ago. He cites a parasite endemic to Africa found in one body as supportive. The three fingers + extra phalanx + elongated digits would be an arboreal-life adaptation, paralleling sloths.

**Skull morphology** — Maria's tomography shows no stress-fracture lines indicative of cranial-deformation rituals (which were practiced in Nazca/Paracas culture per [[skull-elongation]]). The skull is also already large at the natural form.
