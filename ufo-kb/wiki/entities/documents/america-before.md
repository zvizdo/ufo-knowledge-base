# America Before

Graham Hancock's 2019 book extending the lost-civilization thesis specifically to the Americas. Uses lidar imagery of the Amazon rainforest — which has revealed massive geometric earthworks, urban-scale infrastructure, and road systems under the forest canopy — as evidence that a previously unknown chapter of human history existed in South America.

## Core Arguments

- The Amazon Basin contains lidar-confirmed evidence of large-scale pre-Columbian settlement and geometric earthwork construction invisible to conventional archaeology due to forest cover
- These settlements predate the accepted timeline for complex civilization in the Americas
- The hypothesis that the Americas were home to a branch of the same pre-Ice Age civilization posited in *[[fingerprints-of-gods]]*

## Amazon Lidar Pyramid

In [[dfPfPB601hw]], Hancock mentions receiving information from a friend — an archaeologist teaching at the University of Exeter doing lidar work in Colombia — who has identified what "they are certain is an enormous pyramid in the middle of the Amazon rainforest." The next step is ground-truth fieldwork. Hancock plans to join the team if possible.
