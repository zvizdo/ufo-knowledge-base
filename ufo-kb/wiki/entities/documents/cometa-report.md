# COMETA Report

The COMETA Report (*UFOs and Defense: What Must We Be Prepared For?*, 1999) was produced by COMETA — an informal group of senior French military officers, intelligence officials, and scientists — and released publicly. At approximately 100 pages, it remains one of the most substantive official-adjacent UAP analyses ever made public.

## Key Conclusions

1. **The extraterrestrial hypothesis is plausible** — after reviewing French and foreign cases, COMETA concluded that a non-trivial fraction of UAP reports cannot be explained by known phenomena, and that the extraterrestrial hypothesis deserves serious scientific consideration.
2. **The United States is withholding information** — COMETA explicitly concluded that the US government possesses information about UAP that it has not shared with allies.
3. **France should develop independent investigation capacity** — rather than relying on US-filtered information.
