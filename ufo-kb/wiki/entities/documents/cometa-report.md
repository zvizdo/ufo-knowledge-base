# COMETA Report

The COMETA Report (*UFOs and Defense: What Should We Prepare For?*, 1999) was produced by COMETA (Comité pour des Études Approfondies — Committee for In-Depth Studies), an informal group of ~15 senior French military officers, intelligence officials, aeronautics engineers, and scientists, most of them former auditors of [[ihedn|IHEDN]]. Chaired by General (ret.) [[denis-letty]]. The document was formally presented to the French President and Prime Minister and first published in the magazine *VSD* in July 1999.

## Scope and Structure

Approximately 90 pages organized in 3 parts (14 chapters) plus 7 appendices. Part 1 documents French and international UAP cases. Part 2 analyzes physical hypotheses (including MHD propulsion). Part 3 examines strategic, aeronautical, scientific, political, religious, and media implications of the ETH.

## Key Cases Documented

- **[[1994-air-france-3532]]** — Capt. [[jean-charles-duboc]], Air France Flight 3532 (28 Jan 1994): 250m craft, radar + visual, 50 seconds; Cinq-Mars-la-Pile CDC primary radar confirmation
- **[[1977-giraud-mirage-uap]]** — Col. Giraud, Mirage IV (7 Mar 1977, Dijon): 3–4g then 6.5g turns, no radar, no sonic boom at supersonic speed
- **[[1956-lakenheath-uap]]** — RAF/USAF, 13–14 Aug 1956: RAF Venom pursuit; multi-station radar; DIA: "classic case"
- **[[1957-rb47-uap]]** — USAF RB-47, 17 Jul 1957: 6 officers, microwave emissions, radar/visual/electronic tri-sensor
- **[[1976-tehran-uap]]** — Iran, 18–19 Sep 1976: 2 F-4s, electronics disabled, sub-object launched; DIA "outstanding report"
- **Russia/Maltsev 1990** — 100+ military witnesses, radar/visual; Gen. Maltsev's signed account published in *Rabochaya Tribuna*

## Key Conclusions

1. **ETH is "by far the best scientific hypothesis"** — after systematic review, no conventional explanation fits the totality of UAP-D evidence; ETH is not proven but has "strong presumptions in its favor"
2. **US is withholding information** — COMETA explicitly states the US likely holds crash-retrieval evidence and has practiced secrecy + disinformation since 1947 (Robertson Committee, JANAP 146, Bolender letter)
3. **MHD propulsion is credible** — Jean-Pierre Petit's magnetohydrodynamic framework (CNRS/ONERA) accounts for observed performance: silent supersonic flight, EM effects, plasma sheaths
4. **France must develop independent capacity** — SEPRA expansion + high-level inter-ministerial unit + EU diplomatic coordination

## Six Recommendations

1. Inform decision-makers and train aeronautics personnel
2. Expand [[geipan|SEPRA]] resources
3. Make UFO detection an explicit objective of space surveillance systems
4. Create a high-level unit to develop prospective hypotheses
5. **EU diplomatic demarches to the US** to end unilateral secrecy
6. Prepare contingency plans for overt contact scenarios
