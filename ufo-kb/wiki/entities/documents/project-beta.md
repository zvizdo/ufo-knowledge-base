# Project Beta

Book by [[greg-bishop]] (2005) documenting the Air Force OSI disinformation operation run by [[rick-doty]] against civilian UFO researcher [[paul-benowitz]]. Described by Jesse Michaels as "a great book" and cited as the source through which [[mario-woods]] became aware of Rick Doty before recognizing him on *Ancient Aliens*.

## Summary

Benowitz, a businessman in Albuquerque, had been observing and filming exotic craft near [[kirtland-afb]]. He began intercepting what he believed were alien signals on his computer. Rather than debunking or ignoring him, OSI Counter Intelligence agent Rick Doty infiltrated his circle, confirmed his beliefs, and fed him an elaborate false narrative involving underground alien bases, human-NHI government treaties, and direct alien communications. The escalating disinformation destroyed Benowitz's mental health, leading to psychiatric hospitalization.

*Project Beta* traces this operation in documentary detail, using interviews with Doty (who eventually cooperated), Benowitz (before his death), and other figures in the Albuquerque/Kirtland UFO research community of the 1980s.

## Key Claims

- The operation was deliberate, organized, and sustained over years — not a spontaneous one-off deception
- Multiple OSI personnel were aware and involved
- The operation achieved its goal: Benowitz was neutralized as a researcher
- The "disclosure" of what he saw (real craft at Kirtland) was managed by overwhelming it with fabricated material

## Provenance

Bishop conducted original interviews with Rick Doty, who ultimately confirmed the operation (though the extent of his current candor remains contested). Published 2005; remains the primary published source on this case.
