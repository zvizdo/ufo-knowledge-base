# USAF Modeling of Unlikely Space-Booster Failures (1996)

RTI Report RTI/5180/77-43F. Final report prepared for [[vandenberg-afb]] 30th Space Wing and [[patrick-air-force-base]] 45th Space Wing Safety Offices under Contract FO4703-91-C-0112. Authors James A. Ward Jr. and Robert M. Montgomery of Research Triangle Institute and ACTA Inc.

Appendix D contains the most comprehensive declassified US military launch failure database: 1,186 flights (Atlas 532, Delta 232, Titan 337, Eastern-Range Thor 85) covering 1946–August 1996, with classified failure-response mode assignments for each anomalous flight.

Overall failure probabilities: Atlas 2.2% (phases 0–1), Delta 1.0%, Titan 4.0%. Mode-5 (anomalous deviation) probability: Atlas 0.00223 (~1 in 449), Titan 0.00405 (~1 in 247). Mode 4 (near-flight-line impact) accounts for ~86% of all failures; only ~8% produce significant off-nominal deviations (Mode 5) — the primary benchmark for ruling out launch-vehicle sources for UAP reports observed far from flight paths.

Distribution restricted to US Government agencies and contractors when published (1996); released in PURSUE Release 01 UAP batch.
