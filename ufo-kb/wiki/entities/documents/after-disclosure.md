# After Disclosure

*After Disclosure: The People's Manual for the Postdisclosure World* was published approximately 2010 by [[richard-dolan]] and [[bryce-zabel]]. It is one of the few serious book-length treatments of the *consequences* of disclosure rather than the evidence for UAP reality.

## Core Premise

The book takes UAP reality as established and asks: **what happens the day after official disclosure?** Rather than advocating for disclosure or cataloguing evidence, it models:

- How existing political institutions respond (Congress, intelligence agencies, military)
- How religion processes the news
- How financial markets react
- How individuals psychologically accommodate reality revision
- What new social and political coalitions form
- How science reorganizes around new questions

Dolan and Zabel treat disclosure not as a single event but as a process — and the book explores what the immediate aftermath looks like at individual, institutional, and civilizational scales.

## Context
