# UFOs and Nukes

Book by [[robert-hastings]] documenting 167 security-cleared USAF witnesses to UAP activity at nuclear weapons facilities. Described by [[jesse-michaels]] as "probably the authoritative chronicle" of UFO-nuclear incidents. Salas's [[1967-oscar-flight]] case is among the documented incidents.

## Summary

The book aggregates witness testimony, FOIA-obtained documents, and investigative research to establish that UFO activity at nuclear weapon storage and launch sites is a repeating, well-documented pattern — not isolated anomalies. Hastings conducted the interviews himself over decades.

## Key Claims

- 167 Q-cleared USAF witnesses; many signed NDAs immediately after their sightings
- Pattern extends across multiple bases (Malmstrom, Minot, and others)
- UFOs have both disabled and (in some reports) briefly activated nuclear missile systems
- The pattern suggests deliberate interest in human nuclear weapons programs by non-human intelligence

## Provenance

Hastings is the **son of a special weapons officer** at [[malmstrom-air-force-base]], Montana, which placed him inside the nuclear-security establishment from childhood. In the 1960s, working as a janitor at the base, an **FAA supervisor** casually mentioned he was tracking five unknowns on his scope and called Hastings over — a chance encounter that launched the life's work ([[TH10mD9hQas]]). He spent decades accumulating witness contacts.

## PRP Witness Quality

[[jesse-michaels]] highlights ([[TH10mD9hQas]]) that the 167 witnesses come overwhelmingly from the most rigorously vetted personnel class in the US military: ICBM missileers on the **Personal Reliability Program (PRP)**, who must report taking ibuprofen and are the literal "key turners for nuclear weapons." Nobody applies for that role to become famous. The PRP standard means credibility dismissals that might apply to ordinary witnesses do not apply here.
