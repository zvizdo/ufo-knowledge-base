# Mind Traveler

Documentary film by [[greg-malazzi]] investigating the life and covert work of [[andrija-puharich]]. Distributed via Age of Disclosure.

## Summary

The film compiles archival material on Puharich's [[roundtable-foundation]] (Rockland, Maine), the founding 1952 [[the-nine]] channeling, the [[space-kids]] hypnosis experiments, and Puharich's covert company [[intellect]] — including the Berkeley [[beardsley-graham]] archive that provides the only external trace of Intellect's existence. [[bob-beck]] appears in the film discussing the CIA's [[elf-weapons]] recruitment of Puharich.

## Key Claims

- Puharich's funding was substantially government (Army/Navy/CIA/AR Foundation), not the wealthy-patron story Puharich himself told
- Intellect was a thought-transmission and ELF research company that has been almost completely scrubbed from the public record
- The Nine communications cannot be cleanly separated from Puharich's own ELF technology
