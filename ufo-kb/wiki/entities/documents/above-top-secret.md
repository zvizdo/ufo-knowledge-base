# Above Top Secret

*Above Top Secret: The Worldwide UFO Cover-Up* (1987) by [[timothy-good]] is a survey of government UFO secrecy across multiple nations, drawing heavily on declassified documents, government admissions, and multiple independent sources. It is widely regarded as a canonical work in UAP research.

## Significance

The book occupies a unique position in the literature: it arrived from a British Ministry of Defence-adjacent vantage point (rather than the American military-industrial insider network), giving it breadth and an external perspective on the US cover-up that American researchers could not provide.

## Gateway Text for Richard Dolan

In ~1994, [[richard-dolan]] — then an ABD historian studying US diplomatic policy at the University of Rochester — found the book in a Syracuse bookstore. The subtitle triggered immediate recognition: he knew the agencies, era, and names from his own historical research. His question was not "Are UFOs real?" but rather "Even if this was a *mistake* — if they were *mistakenly* interested in flying saucers — how come I never read about it in any academic history book?"

That question — the question of the silence, not the question of UFO reality — drove his subsequent five-year deep dive and ultimately the two-volume *[[ufos-national-security-state]]*.

> "You got to pay respect. You got to know that these are canonical works." — Dolan on Good
