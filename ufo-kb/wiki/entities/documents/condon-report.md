# Condon Report

> *Stub page — expand with primary source import.*

The *Scientific Study of Unidentified Flying Objects* (1969) — universally called the **Condon Report** — was a $500,000 Air Force-funded study conducted at the University of Colorado, led by physicist Edward U. Condon. Its conclusion: further scientific study of UFOs was not warranted.

## Significance

- Used by the Air Force to justify closing **Project Blue Book** in 1969
- Condon's summary conclusion contradicted the body of the report: roughly 30% of 59 analyzed cases remained unexplained
- Philip Klass, Donald Menzel, and other debunkers cited it as authoritative; UFO researchers including [[j-allen-hynek]] cited the unexplained-case percentage as evidence the conclusion was predetermined

## The O'Brien Committee Critique

An internal memo by project coordinator Robert Low — leaked mid-project — suggested the study was designed to produce a negative conclusion before the research began. The American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA) issued a report disagreeing with Condon's conclusion.
