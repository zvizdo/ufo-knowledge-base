# Communion (1987)

Memoir by [[whitley-strieber]] documenting his NHI contact experiences beginning Christmas Eve 1985 at his [[cabin-upstate-new-york]]. Published 1987; became a major bestseller, selling millions of copies and bringing the alien abduction phenomenon to mainstream cultural awareness.

The book was groundbreaking in its framing: Strieber refused to confirm or deny that his experiences represented literal alien abduction, instead presenting them as real anomalous events whose nature remained genuinely uncertain. This epistemic honesty made the book more credible to skeptics than previous abduction accounts.

## Key Contributions

- Established the emotional and psychological texture of abduction experiences in public discourse
- Strieber's refusal to over-interpret opened space for serious research
- Drew [[john-mack]] and others into taking abduction accounts seriously
- The book's alien face cover image became one of the most iconic images in modern UFO culture
