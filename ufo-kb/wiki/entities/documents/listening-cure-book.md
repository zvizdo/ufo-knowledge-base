# The Listening Cure

Book co-authored by [[chris-gilbert]] and [[eric-haseltine]]. Gilbert's primary clinical synthesis: a framework for medicine grounded in the idea that listening — to the body, to the patient, to subtle signals — is itself a healing modality, not merely a diagnostic step.

## Relevance to KB

The book establishes Gilbert's medical credibility and her interest in subtle sensory phenomena, both of which inform her and Haseltine's exo-psychology methodology ([[exo-psychology]]) and their approach to [[2016-havana-syndrome]] investigation. Gilbert's clinical training in body-mind medicine is the empirical substrate for the exo-psychology framework's inversion methodology (taking every human cognitive trait and examining its absence in NHI).
