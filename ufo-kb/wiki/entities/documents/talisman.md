# Talisman: Sacred Cities, Secret Faith

Co-authored by [[graham-hancock]] and [[robert-bauval]]. Argues that secret societies — identified as the inheritors of ancient Egyptian mystery traditions — deliberately preserved and transmitted esoteric knowledge across millennia, from the "First Time" civilization through to the emergence of Freemasonry and other post-medieval secret societies.

## Core Argument

The knowledge encoded in the Giza monuments (precessional astronomy, soul-ascension cosmology) did not disappear when the civilization that created it was destroyed. Instead, it was entrusted to initiated custodians — the "Followers of Horus," the "Souls of Pe and Nekhen" in Egyptian tradition — who passed it down through structured secrecy.

Hancock in [[9PJJiut0Iuw]]:
> "These were secret societies which were entrusted with preserving information from what they call the first time and passing it down to Future Generations... that information was preserved for thousands and thousands of years and constantly passed down."

## Connection to "Switching On" Civilization

Hancock argues that around 3,000 BC, the preserved knowledge was deliberately activated — "switched on" — producing the rapid emergence of Egyptian civilization fully formed. This explains why Egyptian civilization appears at its apex at the beginning (Old Kingdom) rather than developing gradually. Talisman extends this argument forward into medieval secret societies.
