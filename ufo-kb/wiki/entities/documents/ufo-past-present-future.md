# UFOs: Past, Present and Future

Documentary film by [[bob-emenegger]] and [[allan-sandler]], produced in the early 1970s. Notable for its unprecedented cooperation with active US Air Force bases and active/retired military personnel — an access level [[james-fox]] describes as signaling that something significant was happening with the production.

## The Holloman Reference

The film included a reference to a landing case at [[holloman-air-force-base]], phrased as an event that "may have already happened or may happen" — deliberate plausible deniability. Emenegger later confirmed to Fox that actual footage of the event was obtained, and that at the last minute "they pulled it." He did not specify who "they" were.

Fox's interpretation: the vague phrasing was the filmmakers covering themselves while still wanting to acknowledge the event's reality. The extraordinary military cooperation throughout the rest of the film implies high-level support that stopped just short of allowing the most sensitive material to air.

## Allan Sandler Saw the Footage

[[allan-sandler]] personally viewed the [[1964-holloman-afb-landing]] footage at [[norton-air-force-base]], shown to him by [[paul-charlton]], before it was confiscated. Sandler never told Emenegger this until Fox put them on the phone together years later.
