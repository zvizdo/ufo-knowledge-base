# DIRD Papers (AAWSAP 38-Paper Survey)

## Summary

Set of approximately 38 Defense Intelligence Reference Documents commissioned by [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]] under the [[aawsap|AAWSAP]] / [[bass|BAASS]] program (2008–2012). Subject-matter experts in propulsion physics, materials, and aerospace engineering were each asked to write a forward-looking technical paper on "where your field will be in 2050," ostensibly for [[bigelow-aerospace|Bigelow Aerospace]]. None of the authors were told the actual purpose was UAP-engineering research.

## Key Claims

The papers cover an explicit roadmap of UAP-relevant exotic physics. Puthoff (Gf_tKn9TaP8) names titles including:

- **Anti-Neutronic Fusion Propulsion** (Anti-neutronic-fusion neutronic-fusion-propulsion)
- **Superconductors and Gravity Research**
- **Positron Aerospace Propulsion**
- **Warp Drive, Dark Energy, Extra Dimensions** ([[alcubierre-warp-drive|Alcubierre]] line)
- **Advanced Nuclear Propulsion**
- **Spacetime Metric Engineering** ([[metric-engineering]])
- **Traversable Wormholes**
- **Stargates**
- **Brain-Machine Interfaces**

Puthoff's framing in the interview: "We weren't kidding around." The collection's existence is direct evidence that the AAWSAP program was a UAP-physics research effort, despite its public framing as advanced aerospace propulsion review.

## Provenance

- Commissioned 2008–2012 under the AAWSAP/BAASS contract
- Cover story given to authors: "Bigelow Aerospace wants to know where your field will be in the year 2050"
- Uploaded to the [[jaywick-server|Jaywick]] classified server — Pentagon, intelligence-community, and aerospace-contractor access only
- Normally such collections are taken down after a short period; the DIRD set was so popular among Jaywick readers that every takedown attempt drew protests, so they stayed up indefinitely
- Most have since been FOIA'd into the public domain
- Puthoff says he was personally concerned the authors would complain when they discovered the actual sponsor — none did
