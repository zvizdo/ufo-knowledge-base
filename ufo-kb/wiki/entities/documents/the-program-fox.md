# The Program

Documentary film by [[james-fox]], **released December 23, 2024** on Amazon Prime. Debuted as the **#1 documentary on Amazon Prime** in its opening period. Fox had described it as his "most explicit" and most consequential film, and its reception confirmed that positioning. Harry Reid, in a statement quoted in the film, described the phenomenon as "the greatest national security issue of our time."

## Stated Purpose

Fox chose to produce the film on a faster timeline than [[the-phenomenon-fox]], sacrificing some production quality for relevance. He describes the deliberate timeliness: "Things are moving fast and the witnesses coming forward want momentum maintained." The film is explicitly intended to force institutional acknowledgment rather than merely to document testimony.

## Fox's Statement

"I would go to jail to say things that I've been exposed to in the program." — Fox's strongest public statement about the content of the film and his personal conviction about UAP reality.

## Key Subjects

### [[jason-sanss]] — Nellis 1994

USAF Master Sergeant with the 67th Intelligence Group; describes a face-to-face encounter at the Nellis testing range, August 1994 with a figure that "did not appear to be human" in military-style clothing. New details from 3yrKVu35uSE: the entity departed **wearing Sanss's jacket**; the encounter induced what Sanss describes as a **"K state"** (a specific altered cognitive state); the metal from the object was given a classified name ("tenellum" or "tillum" per the transcript — classified word confirmed by the Doser pre-publication review process); Sanss's credentials were verified by Congressional committees before being included.

### [[craig-lindsay]] — 1990 Calvine Photograph

RAF Press Officer stationed in Scotland in 1990 who received one print of the [[1990-calvine-photograph]] from the local newspaper. Kept it hidden for approximately 34 years. Lindsay gave his first on-camera interview ever to Fox, who traveled to Scotland specifically to film it. [[david-clarke-ufologist]] identified Lindsay through a declassified MOD document and introduced him to Fox.

### [[kirk-mcconnell]] — Aegis Radar Transmedium Tracking

Retired Senate Armed Services Committee and Intelligence Committee professional staff member who describes Aegis radar data showing transmedium UAP tracked routinely — characterized the frequency as **"all day long."**

### [[jeff-natelli]] / Jeff Nelli — Vandenberg (2003–2005)

USAF security officer who witnessed and responded to multiple UAP incidents at [[vandenberg-afb]], including the [[2003-vandenberg-red-square]]. Fox read Natelli's written statement on-air during JRE #2246. Also reported an orb on the flight line and at least four other incidents during the 2003–2005 period.

### [[ricky-sorrels]] — Stephenville 2008

Texas hunter who observed the [[2008-stephenville-ufo]] at close range; examined the craft's underside through a rifle scope — **no rivets, seams, or panel lines** visible. Subsequently subjected to military helicopter surveillance. Fox expressed concern about Sorrels' long public silence as of December 2024.

### [[stacy-rhodes]] — Phoenix Lights 1997

Witnessed the [[1997-phoenix-lights]] from I-10; described the craft as **gunmetal black**; held a newspaper up against it and could not see past the edges of the object.

### [[hal-puthoff]] — Star of the Documentary

Fox screened a cut with Puthoff the night before the [[bVhSPH2A5Vw]] interview. Puthoff is described as a "star of the documentary."

### [[gary-mckinnon]] — Non-Terrestrial Officers

Interviewed about finding the "non-terrestrial officers" list with ranks and names and "material transfer between ships" in US military databases. Fox draws a speculative connection between this list and the Nellis entity Sanss encountered.

### [[david-grusch]] — Whistleblower

Featured in the documentary; Fox worked hard to portray him fairly and states he believes him completely.

### Navy X — Anonymous Mosaic Intelligence Source

An anonymous active-duty US Navy source who provided Fox with information about UAP storage locations on the **SIPRNet and JWICS** classified networks. The source knows the specific building locations on specific bases. Fox could not identify or verify the source's identity independently but describes the information as highly specific and consistent with other credible reporting.

### [[brian-benner]] — Politico Journalist

Provides media/political perspective; on-camera statement: **"Congress can't keep a secret."**

## Doser Process

Fox describes the US government's **Doser pre-publication review** process — a classification-enforcement mechanism that whistleblowers and witnesses who have held clearances must navigate before publishing. Jason Sanss's account went through Doser review, which resulted in the metal name being classified (redacted from what could be publicly stated).

## Thematic Architecture

Fox connects: (1) Sanss's face-to-face encounter at Nellis 1994 with an entity in military clothing; (2) McKinnon's "non-terrestrial officers" list; (3) McConnell's Aegis data showing routine transmedium UAP; (4) the Calvine photograph and MOD suppression; — as converging evidence that the phenomenon is real, ongoing, and systematically hidden.
