# Halt Memo

A one-page memorandum from Lt. Col. [[charles-halt]], Deputy Base Commander at [[raf-bentwaters]], to the UK Ministry of Defence, dated January 13, 1981. Addressed to "RAF/CC" and titled "Unexplained Lights." Released under FOIA pressure; declassified and published in 1983.

## Summary

Key claims in the memo (verbatim paraphrase):

1. A "triangular-shaped craft" was observed in Rendlesham Forest, December 26–27, 1980. When [[jim-penniston]] and [[john-burroughs]] approached, it appeared to move through the trees; at one point it illuminated the entire forest.
2. The next morning, three depressions were found in a triangular pattern at the landing site; radiation levels measured by a Geiger counter were 0.1 milliroentgens at the center.
3. On December 29, Halt personally observed two star-like objects in the sky that moved rapidly and silently; beams of light descended from one into the Woodbridge weapons storage area (WSA).
4. The objects were seen for approximately two to three hours.

## Significance

The Halt Memo is the primary institutional document confirming the [[1980-rendlesham-forest]] incident occurred. Unlike most UFO reports that rely on civilian or junior-rank witnesses, this is a signed memo from a senior USAF officer to the UK Ministry of Defence, using official letterhead. Its existence prevents deniability of the events.

A companion document is the Halt audio tape — a recording Halt made in the field during the third night's observation. Together they constitute the most thoroughly documented multi-night military UAP incident in European records.

## Provenance

Obtained via FOIA request, first published in 1983 by researcher Brenda Butler. The original document is held at the UK National Archives (TNA AIR 2/19617).
