# Project Gravitar

Upcoming documentary about [[bob-lazar]] in production as of 2025. [[joe-rogan]] mentions it on American Alchemy as something a friend of his is producing; Rogan expects it to represent a significant new corroboration step.

## Anticipated Content

Per Rogan's preview:
- Will present **satellite imagery** of S4, including the hangers — establishing that a real physical facility exists at the Papoose Lake escarpment south of Area 51
- Will present more "confirmation and data points" corroborating Lazar's story
- Rogan: "I think we're going to find out that S4 is very real too, and that's been denied up until today"

## Context

The naming — "Project Gravitar" — invokes Lazar's propulsion model, in which [[element-115]] (moscovium) generates an anti-gravity wave focused by "gravity amplifiers." The title suggests the documentary will engage directly with the physics claim, not just the biographical/credential story.

Lazar's S4 claims have been denied by the government since 1989. Official satellite imagery of the Papoose Lake area exists; some imagery reportedly shows the terrain consistent with Lazar's described facility layout, though with visible blur-outs that Rogan attributes to deliberate obscuring (analogous to the Point Doom underwater blur discussed in the same episode).
