# FBI Case File 62-HQ-83894

The FBI's 21-year investigative case file on UFOs/flying discs, spanning from [[kenneth-arnold]]'s 1947 sighting through routine reporting handoffs to [[project-blue-book]] by 1968. Released in PURSUE Release 01 as a more complete version than the FBI Vault edition.

**Key primary sources within the file:**
- Bureau Bulletin No. 42 (July 30, 1947) — formal FBI-AAF cooperation agreement; contains [[j-edgar-hoover]]'s handwritten demand for access to recovered discs
- Gen. [[george-schulgen]]'s July 9, 1947 letter to Hoover — confirms no US programs behind the discs
- [[lincoln-lapaz]] briefing materials — documents UAP concentration over nuclear installations (Sandia/Los Alamos/Kirtland)
- Gen. [[cp-cabell]]'s September 8, 1950 no-publicity directive — sent simultaneously to FBI and CIA directors
- Newton/Koehler Mojave occupant claim (January 1950) — 18 three-foot bodies; directly antecedent to the [[hottel-memo]]
- Section 10 AFSCA surveillance (1966) — primary-source contactee organizational records
