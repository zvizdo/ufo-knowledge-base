# Sinister Forces

*Sinister Forces: A Grimoire of American Political Witchcraft* is a three-volume investigative history by [[peter-levenda]] tracing the intersections of the occult, American intelligence agencies, and political assassinations from WWII through the modern era.

**Volume I** covers Nazi occult programs and their importation to the US through Operation Paperclip. **Volume II** examines the JFK assassination network and the American Orthodox Catholic Church as an intelligence front. **Volume III** follows the thread into Cold War-era intelligence operations.

The book was not initially a UFO title — it contains one chapter on the JFK/UFO connection and a later chapter on Levenda's correspondence with [[whitley-strieber]]. However, a presentation by Levenda based on its research went viral on YouTube (hundreds of thousands of views), connecting JFK, Operation Paperclip, and UFO phenomena for a popular audience — and triggering Tom DeLonge's outreach that led to TTSA.
