# UFOs and the National Security State

*UFOs and the National Security State* is a two-volume work by [[richard-dolan]] that applies the methods of academic history — primary-source documents, FOIA releases, cross-referenced testimony, and analytical framework — to the history of the UAP phenomenon from 1941 through the modern era.

## Significance

This work transformed Dolan from an unrecognized graduate student dropout into the most academically credible historian in the UAP research field. It was the first full-scale attempt to treat UAP secrecy as a structural feature of the national security state rather than as a collection of isolated incidents.

[[bob-maguire]] (former intelligence officer) described his approach: "The person that I decided could teach me about all these UFO nuts was Richard Dolan who wrote two volumes on UFOs and the National Security State. I am a denizen of the National Security State, so I figured, okay, I'll go to this guy and figure stuff out." The book works for intelligence professionals precisely because it uses the same analytical vocabulary they use.

## Origin

Dolan began the research after encountering [[above-top-secret]] by [[timothy-good]] in 1994 in a Syracuse bookstore. The cognitive dissonance of recognizing Truman-era departments and names from his own historical research, combined with the complete absence of any academic treatment of the UFO issue in the historical literature, drove him to spend two or three months resolving the question for himself — and then never left.
