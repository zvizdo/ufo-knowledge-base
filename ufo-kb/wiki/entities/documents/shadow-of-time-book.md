# The Shadow of Time

Co-authored novel by [[eric-haseltine]] and [[chris-gilbert]]. Described as fiction that embeds the science from [[new-science-of-ufos-book]] in narrative form, making it accessible to a wider audience. Haseltine notes that the intelligence tradecraft in the book is authentic ("the tradecraft is real").

## Plot Summary

- Protagonist: a paleontologist who has been fired from his job at UCLA
- He discovers an anomalous artifact in his father's Native American art collection in Trona, California (Haseltine's birthplace)
- Private corporations are competing to systematically excavate anomalous objects from the desert
- The conspiracy is high-stakes: features an FBI director character; espionage elements
- A genius African gray parrot named Walter (a real science-backed cognitive claim — African gray parrots are among the most cognitively complex animals) appears
- Features an NHI creature with distributed cognition (designed using exo-psychology methodology)
- Tic Tac appearances are included with a narrative explanation
- Explores the [[silurian-hypothesis]] as the central mystery

## Scientific Elements Embedded

- [[alcubierre-warp-drive]] — theoretical FTL travel mechanism
- [[voice-of-god-technology]] — used for cross-species communication between humans and NHI creature
- [[laser-propulsion-disc]] — exotic propulsion
- [[silurian-hypothesis]] — the central archaeological mystery
- [[exo-psychology]] — NHI character design
- Intelligence tradecraft: Haseltine's real methodology (competing hypotheses, source evaluation)

## Significance

The "Shadow of Time" title implies the thesis: NHI or ancient civilizations have been casting a shadow over human history that we can barely detect. The forbidden archaeology / private corporation excavation element reflects a real concern Haseltine has about how anomalous objects are handled outside government oversight.
