# Birthright (Alberino)

*Birthright* is a book by [[tim-alberino]] that presents his Christocentric cosmological framework — a synthesis of biblical theology, ancient alien narratives, claims about Martian civilization archaeology, and transhumanist themes. The book is the primary written statement of Alberino's worldview.

**Core thesis**: Alberino argues that humanity has a "birthright" — a legitimate inheritance of spiritual and physical lineage — that is being contested by fallen angelic entities who have worked throughout history to corrupt human genetics and spiritual identity. This framework integrates:
- [[nephilim-giants-thesis]] — the argument that fallen angels interbreeding with humans produced giant offspring (Nephilim/Rephaim) documented in Genesis and other ancient texts
- Ancient alien traditions — the hypothesis that "gods" in ancient mythology were actual beings, interpreted by Alberino through a biblical fallen-angel lens
- Mars archaeology — Alberino has discussed alleged evidence of past Martian civilization, situating it within his broader argument about the origins of non-human intelligences
- Transhumanism — presented as the modern manifestation of the ancient fallen-angel agenda: genetic manipulation of human biology

**Field research context**: Alberino spent approximately 10 years living in Peru, conducting field research into giant skeleton claims, ancient ruins, and related phenomena. *Birthright* synthesizes this research with his theological framework and with the [[2002-kandahar-giant]] and related giant-encounter claims he has documented.
