# Necronomicon (Simon, 1977)

The **Necronomicon** (published 1977 under the pseudonym "Simon") is a modern occult grimoire styled as an ancient Sumerian text. Despite its fictional/synthetic origins, it has been widely used as a practical magic text and became a bestseller at Fort Benning for a period.

## Ceremony of Walking

The text describes a ritual called the **"ceremony of walking"** — a spiritual journey to the stars. Key parameters:
- Best performed **around midnight**
- Practitioner faces **north**, with specific emphasis on **northeast**
- The Great Bear (**Big Dipper**) must be rising; performed "when the Great Bear hangs by its tail in the sky"
- The **number 5 is sacred** to the ceremony

These parameters match the [[2005-nrol-16-launch]] almost exactly: midnight, northeast heading toward the Big Dipper, five pentagrams on the patch, 5th year of the millennium.

## Military Feedback

[[peter-levenda]] has stated that he received letters from inside the US military praising the Necronomicon as **"spot on"** — including letters from officers in **Strategic Air Command**. For a period, the book was reportedly a **bestseller at Fort Benning**.

This suggests that whatever one makes of the book's origins or validity, elements within the US military found it operationally relevant.
