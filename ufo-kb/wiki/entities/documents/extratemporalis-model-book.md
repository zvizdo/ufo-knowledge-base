# The Extratemporalis Model

Book by anthropologist **[[michael-p-masters]]** advancing a formal taxonomic name — *Homo sapiens extratemporalis* — for the hypothesized **future-human** species behind a substantial fraction of UAP-class encounters. Sequel/expansion of Masters's earlier *Identified Flying Objects*.

## Summary

The argument: morphological and behavioral features of reported NHI ("greys" especially) — bipedal hominid skeleton, larger neurocrania, gracile limbs, reduced sexual dimorphism, reduced sensory acuity — are the directional extrapolation of present-Homo-sapiens trends. Masters reads this as positive evidence for [[future-humans-hypothesis]] over either generic ETH or interdimensional framings.

## Key Claims

- Morphology is the load-bearing argument: greys *look* like extrapolated future hominids
- Time-travel mechanism is treated as a tractable engineering problem given GR's permissive structure (see [[closed-timelike-curves]], [[tipler-cylinder]])
- Reproduction-program elements of the abduction literature read coherently if the visitors are demographically narrow descendant-population members trying to recover lost genetic diversity

## Provenance

Civilian publication; Masters is a tenured anthropologist at Montana Tech.
