# Vandenberg AFB Launch Summary 1958–2000

30th Space Wing History Office registry of all major launches from [[vandenberg-afb|Vandenberg AFB, CA]], prepared as of 3 February 2000 by Jeffrey Geiger. Documents 1,790 launches across 42 years, beginning with the first Vandenberg launch on 16 December 1958 (Thor IRBM TUNE UP) through 26 January 2000 (JAWSAT/MINOTAUR). Companion document to [[dow-uap-d48-modeling-space-booster-failures-1996-09]], which provides failure-mode probability classifications for the same vehicle types.

The chronological launch log is the primary dataset: 1,790 entries with date, facility designation, vehicle type, program category (DASO, OT, R&D, SPACE, CTL, etc.), and commanding agency. Peak launch activity: 1963–1967 (~100–120 launches/year, roughly one launch every three days). Major programs: SAC ICBM operational testing (633 launches total); AFSC research and development (795 total); RAF IWST Thor IRBM training (21 launches, 1959–1962); AEC sounding-rocket space probes (16 launches, 1960–1963).

Key milestones noted in the facility guide: first polar-orbiting satellite (SLC-1W, 28 Feb 1959); first ICBM launch at Vandenberg (Atlas D, 9 Sep 1959); OSTF Titan silo destroyed by in-silo explosion 3 Dec 1960 (no launches completed); first Minuteman (LF-04, 28 Sep 1962).
