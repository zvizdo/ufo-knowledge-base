# Supernatural (reissued as Visionary)

Graham Hancock's 2005 book examining upper Paleolithic cave art (30,000–40,000 years ago) through the lens of altered states and NHI entity encounters. Reissued under the title *Visionary*.

## Core Argument

Paleolithic cave art — produced in pitch-black caves, often deep underground — depicts clearly nonhuman beings: humanoid-animal hybrids, insectoid figures, beings that match the phenomenology of modern DMT entity encounters. Hancock argues:

1. These artists used darkness and psychedelics to access the same "NHI layer" that modern DMT users encounter
2. The entity types are cross-culturally stable across 30,000+ years — not cultural invention but stable phenomenological features of a contact experience
3. Shamanic traditions worldwide encode the same being-types and contact protocols; this is not coincidence

## Eleusinian Mysteries Connection

Hancock extends the analysis to [[eleusis]] and the [[mystery-schools]] tradition: the kykeon drink given to initiates at Eleusis was almost certainly psychedelic (ergotized barley — the same ergot-on-barley pathway [[brian-muraresku]] documents in [[the-immortality-key]]). The initiates' "loss of fear of death" is the same outcome as deep psychedelic contact with the NHI layer.

## UFO/UAP Connection

As Hancock explains in [[dfPfPB601hw]], *Supernatural* provides historical depth — 30,000 years of documented record — to the claim that NHI entities are consistently encountered in altered states. This undermines the "cultural construction" dismissal: the motifs predate any known human civilization.

[[andrew-gallimore]] cites *Supernatural* in [[8XD1ZiuhXoY]] as foundational evidence for the antiquity and cross-cultural stability of NHI entity motifs.
