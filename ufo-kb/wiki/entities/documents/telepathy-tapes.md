# Telepathy Tapes

Feature-length documentary directed and produced by [[ky-dickens]] investigating apparent telepathic abilities in non-verbal autistic children. The documentary combines [[diane-powell]]'s published research with new controlled field experiments Dickens conducted herself — including the Glendale, California testing sessions with primary subject Mia (from Mexico), and documentation of the "Talk on the Hill" phenomenon through subject Houston.

## Summary

The documentary proceeds from Dickens' initial skepticism (concern about professional reputational risk) through escalating evidence to a position of strong conviction. Key evidentiary elements:

- **Mia (Glendale, CA)**: Young, non-verbal Mexican girl tested in a rented house with mirrors removed, cameras installed, and all test materials controlled by Dickens. Blindfold and physical divider separated Mia from her mother. Mia identified random numbers, images, flash cards, and colored balls held in her mother's mind — 100% accuracy.
- **John Paul**: Non-verbal autistic child who demonstrated telepathic reception of objects and images during controlled testing; placed pillows on his head during sessions.
- **Houston and "Talk on the Hill"**: Houston coined the phrase describing a telepathic "chat room" used by non-speaking autistic individuals. He demonstrated the phenomenon by accurately reproducing the content of a text message he had never seen, and by spelling out thoughts his wife placed in his mind.
- **Josiah**: Subject of the book *[[josiahs-fire]]*; case study incorporated into the documentary.

## Key Claims

- Non-verbal autistic children achieve statistically significant hit rates (typically 90%+, 100% in best cases) in controlled telepathy experiments.
- The "Talk on the Hill" is an active, informal telepathic communication network among non-speaking individuals.
- The phenomenon is consistent with [[consciousness-non-local]] models; conventional explanations (sensory cueing, fraud) are methodologically ruled out.
- Parallels to historical [[society-of-psychical-research]] research and to the work of [[john-mack]] and [[ian-stevenson]].

## Provenance

Created by [[ky-dickens]] after she encountered [[diane-powell]]'s research via a podcast. Powell's controlled experiments (pre-documentary) documented 90%+ hit rates; Dickens extended this with independent filming and testing.
