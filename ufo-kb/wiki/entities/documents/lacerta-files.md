# The Lacerta Files

A 1999 document circulated in Swedish UFO circles, attributed to an anonymous author ("Ole K.") who claims to have conducted interviews with a reptilian female entity calling herself "Lacerta" (Anglicized from "Lerta" in some translations). The document purports to be a transcript of these meetings and describes Lacerta's account of reptilian origins, history, and relationship to humanity.

## Claimed Content

Per the Lacerta entity's stated claims in the document:
- **Native origin**: Reptilians are not visitors from space — they are native to Earth, predating humanity by hundreds of millions of years; they evolved from a dinosaurian ancestor while mammalian primates were still emerging
- **Underground habitation**: They live primarily underground and in other concealed environments, deliberately hidden from human civilization
- **Knowledge of human history**: They have observed human civilization from the beginning; they are aware of human governments, religions, and technology
- **Distinction from space visitors**: They distinguish themselves clearly from the Zeta Reticuli entities (who are spacefaring visitors) — two separate NHI types that superficially appear related but are not
- **Relationship to power structures**: They have limited interactions with certain human authorities but are not in alliance with them; they regard human development with a mixture of concern and detachment

## Sheehan's Engagement

[[chris-ramsey]] references the Lacerta Files during the interview; [[danny-sheehan]] engages with the content seriously rather than dismissively. Sheehan's synthesis:
- If multiple NHI types are real (which his 50 years of sources suggest he believes), the Lacerta description of native Earth reptilians is **not inconsistent** with the Scientology cosmology's Zeta Reticuli visitors
- The two categories (native Earth reptilians vs. spacefaring Zeta Reticuli reptilians) could coexist
- The Pleiadian humanoid entities described in the Scientology Guardians Office files could be distinct from both
- Sheehan's concern is not which specific account is accurate but whether the meta-claim — multiple NHI types with overlapping territorial claims on Earth — is true

## Provenance and Credibility Assessment

The Lacerta Files are unverified in any conventional sense. The claimed author and circumstances of the interviews cannot be confirmed. The document is widely circulated in the European UFO community and is treated by some as genuine testimony and by others as creative fiction. Sheehan does not endorse the document as verified; he treats it as consistent with the broader multi-faction NHI framework he has assembled from other sources.
