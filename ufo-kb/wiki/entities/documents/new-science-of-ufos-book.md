# The New Science of UFOs

Co-authored non-fiction book by [[eric-haseltine]] and [[chris-gilbert]]. The primary stated goal is to bring rigorous scientific and intelligence-analyst methodology to UAP analysis — specifically to attract serious mainstream scientists who have avoided the field due to career risk.

## Methodology

The book applies the intelligence community's **method of competing hypotheses** to UAP: enumerate all plausible hypotheses, identify the evidence for and against each, weight them, and assign explicit confidence levels. This is the methodology Haseltine used throughout his intelligence career.

## Hypothesis Matrix Covered

Per Haseltine's description in [[jMCavr3dVP0]]:
1. **Human perceptual limitations / observer error**
2. **Atmospheric and plasma effects** (natural glowing orbs, ball lightning)
3. **Spoofing / drones** (human-made deception including [[maskirovka]])
4. **Classified human craft** (advanced programs that look like UAP to uncleared observers)
5. **Non-human terrestrial origin** ([[silurian-hypothesis]])
6. **NHI extraterrestrial** (visiting alien technology, likely [[von-neumann-probe]])
7. **Time travel** ([[tipler-cylinder]], [[alcubierre-warp-drive]], [[closed-timelike-curves]])
8. **Parallel dimensions / extra-dimensional physics**

## Key Arguments

- UAP are multiple distinct phenomena — treating them as one is methodologically wrong
- [[exo-psychology]]: systematically invert human traits to model NHI
- Human perceptual architecture (sensory limitations, cognitive biases) is the first thing to account for
- The absence of UAP evidence in intelligence channels is consistent with ultra-compartmentalization, not absence of phenomenon

## Significance

Unique in the UAP literature for being authored by a former intelligence official with real access (Haseltine) applying the actual methodology used inside the intelligence community. The book provides an establishment-credentialed framework for taking UAP seriously.
