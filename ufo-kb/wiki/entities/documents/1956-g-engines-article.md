# "The G-Engines Are Coming"

1956 article published in *Young Men's* magazine (an aviation and hobbyist publication) that included extraordinary public statements from the heads of the top US aerospace firms. The article corroborates the claims in the [[1971-australian-jio-memo]] by showing that aerospace executives were openly (if briefly) discussing anti-gravity programs.

## Key Quotes

**[[lawrence-bell]]** (President, [[bell-aircraft]]):
> "We are working with nuclear fuels and equipment to cancel out gravity."

**[[george-trimble]]** (VP, [[martin-corporation]]):
> "Beating gravity could be done in about the time it took to build the first atom bomb."

These are contemporaneous public statements from credentialed, senior aerospace executives — not retroactive whistleblowing or anonymous sources.

## Significance

[[jesse-michaels]] uses the article to validate the Australian memo claims on independent grounds: if these executives were publicly saying they were working on anti-gravity in 1956, the Australian intelligence assessment that they were doing so is independently corroborated.

The article presents the expected "industrialist optimism" caveat — aerospace executives have historically been bullish on timelines — but the *nuclear fuels* claim from Bell specifically is precise enough to suggest real, classified program activity rather than mere boosterism.
