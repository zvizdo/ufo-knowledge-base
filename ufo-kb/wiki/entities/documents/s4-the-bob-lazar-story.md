# S4: The Bob Lazar Story

Documentary film directed by [[luigi-vendetti]] about [[bob-lazar]]'s claimed work at [[s4]]. Produced over approximately four years with close access to Lazar; nearly bankrupted Vendetti's production company. The film was "ferociously attacked" for 18+ months during production — including institutional debanking and court demands for all communications between Vendetti's team and Lazar.

## Key Contents

### Structural Impossibility Analysis
Vendetti built a hyperrealistic CGI model of the [[s4-sport-model]] to Lazar's specifications. The engineering analysis revealed that the craft's floor — 52–53 feet in diameter at the stated thickness — has no central support column and cannot be supported by any known Earth material. This is a physics constraint, not a witness claim. Lazar confirmed the finding.

### Satellite and Aerial Imagery Corroboration
- **Google Earth (2022)**: Pre-blur satellite imagery of the [[papoose-lake]] area showing vehicle tracks consistent with sustained operational activity
- **Aerial photograph**: Contrast-enhanced aerial photo taken ~17 miles from Papoose Lake showing slanted rectangular hangar doors matching Lazar's description

### Edward Teller Tape
Vendetti's researcher Vanessa located an on-camera interview with [[edward-teller]] in which Teller, when asked whether he knows Bob Lazar, refuses to answer — threatening to sit silent for the entire interview. This mirrors Teller's identical refusal on nuclear propulsion beyond fission/fusion.

### CGI Appearance Effect
In building the CGI model, Vendetti's team accidentally reproduced the "cartoonish" optical effect Lazar described seeing at distance — the craft looks like an animation or optical illusion from far away. Lazar confirmed the accidental reproduction was accurate.

### Film Corrections
The film contains at least one factual error: it shows two holes in the "top hat" craft (one of the nine at S4). Lazar clarifies in [[d9tdJ2SkBKQ]] that there was only one hole, appearing to be from a single projectile fired from below.
