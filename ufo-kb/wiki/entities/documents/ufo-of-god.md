# UFO of God

Memoir by [[chris-bledsoe]] documenting his transformation from a financially ruined, Crohn's-diseased North Carolina homebuilder to a nationally recognized UAP contactee — beginning with a 4-hour missing-time event on January 8, 2007 on the Cape Fear River.

## Key Claims

- **First encounter**: Two large balls of fire on the Cape Fear River; a third descends; 4-hour unexplained gap; Crohn's disease gone afterward.
- **Five years of darkness (2007–2012)**: Community persecution in Fayetteville, NC (fundamentalist Christianity dominant); social services visit; son Ryan affected and forced from home; zero data collected despite nightly camera attempts.
- **2012 Easter encounter**: The Lady appears after Bledsoe declares he will stop speaking publicly. She identifies herself as having many names across cultures. She promises he will now be able to film her presence if he continues.
- **Prophetic visions**: 2012 prophecy about missiles, Regulus star appearing red, and unveiling of truth for humanity.
- The book originally contained 140,000 words; published version is ~85,000 by editorial recommendation.

## Notable Details

- Bledsoe describes being shown Egypt from above — pyramids, Sphinx, Hathor's temple — during memory-recovery sessions with [[michael-oconnell]].
- The Camp David presidential-seal napkin from [[tim-taylor]] is covered.
- The burning tree incident involving a silver snake (Choctaw grandmother omen) with Taylor present.
- Bledsoe predicts the book mentions a future incident involving Iran/Israel missile exchange and orbs.
