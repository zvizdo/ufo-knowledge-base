# Penetration

Ingo Swann's 1998 book, published late in his life, recounting what he describes as his most secret remote-viewing experiences — sessions taken outside the official [[stargate-program]] by an unnamed black-program unit. The latter half is specifically dedicated to his analysis of the Moon and his argument that it is a hollow alien spaceship.

## Summary

### Key Claims

- **Moon as hollow spaceship**: Swann concludes the Moon is not a natural satellite but an engineered artificial object — a spacecraft or hollow construct housing NHI activity.
- **Swann's Moon remote-viewing session**: He was transported blindfolded via helicopter to an undisclosed facility and tasked to remote-view the Moon. He perceived (per [[jason-reza-jorjani]]'s account): a city on the dark side, tall Nordic people overseeing slave labor under a transparent dome. He received a hostile telepathic warning to leave.
- **Anomalous physical properties**: Swann catalogs the physical anomalies that convinced him — seismic ringing, mass distribution inversion, high-refractory metals — as consistent with an engineered hollow object.

### Provenance

[[randall-carlson]] mentions *Penetration* as the book that triggered his own interest in moon anomalies, describing Swann as "part of the CIA psychic spy program called Stargate." Carlson's framing: Swann's experience in classified remote-viewing lends unusual credibility to the hypothesis compared to purely speculative sources.

## Key Evidence / Arguments

- Apollo seismometers: Moon rang for 3+ hours after deliberate crash of booster — behavior inconsistent with solid rock
- Sediment inversion: lower layers newer than upper layers — excavation-site pattern
- Mass concentration anomaly: denser material above less-dense material (reverse of natural formation)
- High-refractory metals (chromium, titanium, uranium-236) present in anomalous quantities
