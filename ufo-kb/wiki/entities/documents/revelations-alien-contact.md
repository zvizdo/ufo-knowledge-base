# Revelations: Alien Contact and Human Deception

1991 book by [[jacques-vallee]] arguing that the UFO field is structured around three nested layers of deception: the government deceives the public about what it knows, witnesses do not fully represent their experiences (consciously or otherwise), and the NHI themselves are intrinsically deceptive. This framework (referenced in [[DPmO-2E7Ayg]]) is Vallée's most systematic treatment of the intelligence/disinformation dimension of ufology.

## The Lazar Passage (p. 9)

On page 9, Vallée notes that [[bob-lazar]] "also told me of his strange memory lapses, of the peculiar liquid he was made to drink." Vallée reads this as consistent with **MKULTRA-style memory manipulation**: an induced amnesia protocol rather than evidence of genuine employment at S4. This would make Lazar not a deliberate liar but a *programmed subject* — a brainwashed individual implanted with a cover story sophisticated enough to include technically accurate nuclear physics.

> ⚠ This is Vallée's interpretation, not a proven fact. Lazar's memory lapses and the "liquid" are documented only through Vallée's relayed account. See [[bob-lazar]] and [[mk-ultra]] for the broader context.

## Three Levels of Deception Framework

Vallée articulates the deception structure in three tiers:
1. **Government → public** — withholding real data, manufacturing cover stories, seeding disinformation
2. **Witnesses → audience** — witnesses alter or omit details, consciously or not, based on social pressure, shame, or incomplete memory
3. **NHI → humanity** — the phenomenon itself presents misleadingly; it tailors its presentation to the receiver's cultural frame (see [[control-system-hypothesis]] and [[noble-myth-cultural-filter]])
