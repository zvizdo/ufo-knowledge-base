# Unidentified Submerged Objects (Volume 1)

Richard Dolan's *Unidentified Submerged Objects* (Volume 1) is the first of a planned three-volume series documenting USO cases from historical antiquity to the modern era. Volume 1 covers the earliest cases through approximately the 1970s; volumes 2 and 3 will cover the 1970s–1980s and 1990s–present respectively (per Dolan's description in the source).

## Methodology

Dolan assembled a database of approximately **670 USO cases** from disparate sources — largely forgotten cases that had never been aggregated. His wife Tracy suggested applying categorical analysis to the data. The resulting spreadsheet encoded 15 fields per case including:
- Time of day
- Shape of object
- Special effects
- Whether EM interference occurred
- Whether entity sighting was reported
- Whether missing time was reported

## Key Statistical Findings

After coding all cases, Dolan identified patterns:
- **~10%** of all USO cases involve EM interference
- **~20%** of *military* USO cases involve EM interference (2x baseline)
- **~40%** of cases involving **missing time, entity sighting, or being-linked USO** involve EM interference (4x baseline)

Dolan's interpretation: **EM effects are a tool or weapon, not an accident.** The scaling with encounter intimacy suggests deliberate deployment.
