# Book of Enoch (1 Enoch)

Ancient Jewish pseudepigraphal compilation attributed to the patriarch Enoch (great-grandfather of Noah). Contains the foundational narrative of the Watchers' descent, the Nephilim, and the antediluvian world's destruction. Preserved exclusively by the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church and the Beta Israel Jewish community; rediscovered for Western scholarship in the 18th century (brought to Europe by James Bruce, 1773).

## Structure

1 Enoch is a composite document with multiple authors and periods of composition:

- **Book of the Watchers** (chapters 1–36): the descent of 200 Watchers, the Nephilim, the Flood; earliest section, compiled c. 300 BC at the latest; oral tradition likely much older
- **Book of the Parables / Similitudes** (chapters 37–71): oracles of Enoch; messianic prophecies about the "Son of Man" figure; disputed dating (possibly pre-Christian, possibly 1st century AD)
- **Astronomical Book** (chapters 72–82): solar/lunar calendrics
- **Book of Dream Visions** (chapters 83–90): allegorical history of Israel
- **Epistle of Enoch** (chapters 91–108): moral instruction and eschatology

## Biblical and Second-Temple Attestation

The Book of Enoch is attested across canonical scripture:

- **Genesis 6:1–4**: the cryptic "sons of God / daughters of men" passage is the abbreviated version of the Watchers narrative; the Septuagint renders it "angels of God"
- **Jude 14–15**: directly quotes 1 Enoch verbatim ("Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied...")
- **2 Peter 2:4**: "angels who sinned...cast into Tartarus, bound in chains of gloomy darkness" — directly from the Book of the Watchers
- **Jesus's title "Son of Man"**: [[tim-alberino]] and biblical scholars note this is a proper messianic title found **only** in the Parables of Enoch within the Old Testament context, not in the Hebrew OT; implies Jesus's audiences and Jesus himself were conversant with Enochian cosmology
- **The Gadarene Demoniac** (Mark 5; Luke 8): demons cry "Why have you come to torment us before the appointed time?" — the "appointed time" of judgment for Nephilim spirits comes from the Book of Enoch's curse on the giants' spirits

## Suppression and Preservation

- Excluded from the Western biblical canon by the 4th century, partly due to St. Augustine's influence in favour of the non-supernatural (Sethite) interpretation of Genesis 6
- Some early Church Fathers cited portions of Enoch as scripture; Origen, Tertullian, Irenaeus were conversant with it
- The Ethiopian Tewahedo Orthodox Church and Beta Israel community incorporated 1 Enoch into their canon c. 4th century AD — ensuring its preservation
- Dead Sea Scrolls (Qumran) yielded Aramaic fragments of the Book of the Watchers, *Book of Giants*, *Book of Jubilees*, and *Genesis Apocryphon* — all attesting to the Nephilim narrative and confirming it was mainstream Second Temple theology

## The "Son of Man" Connection

For Alberino, the strongest evidence that the Parables of Enoch predate Christ and were genuine prophecy:

- The title "Son of Man" as a proper messianic designation appears **only** in the Parables of Enoch in any pre-Christian Jewish text
- Jesus uses it as his most frequent self-designation
- The New Testament's eschatological content (judgment, Gehenna, the beast, return of the judge) draws heavily from Enochian imagery not found in the Hebrew OT
