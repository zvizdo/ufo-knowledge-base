# Mexico's Roswell — The Coyame Incident

## Summary

Book-length investigation by researchers **Noi Torres** and **Ruben Uriarte** documenting decades of UAP activity around [[coyame-mexico]] — especially the [[1974-coyame-ufo-crash|August 25, 1974 midair collision]] between a small Mexican plane and an unknown craft, and the subsequent US-led recovery operation. Title coined the "Mexico's Roswell" framing that has since been reused across UFO journalism.

## Key Claims

- The 1974 incident involved a Mexican aircraft and a UFO colliding in mid-air over Chihuahua state.
- A Mexican military recovery team was dispatched to the site and all members of the team died at the scene.
- US forces airlifted the object out of Mexican territory in the operational vacuum that followed.
- The relevant leaked memo (claimed source: an internal Mexican military report) surfaced publicly in the 1990s.
- Coyame, population 1,230, is described as a clandestine-ops habitat — isolated, with weird geology, and locals who "don't talk about the lights anymore" after persistent intimidation.

## Provenance

- Authors are independent civilian researchers; Torres and Uriarte each have additional UAP titles in their bibliographies.
- Document chain: leaked memo → Spanish-language press in the 1990s → Torres/Uriarte assembled the broader location-based narrative.
