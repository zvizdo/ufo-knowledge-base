# Faded Giant

Co-authored by [[robert-salas]] and researcher James Klotz. Documents the 1967 UFO/nuclear incidents at [[malmstrom-air-force-base]] — specifically [[1967-oscar-flight]] and [[1967-echo-flight]] — with primary source documents obtained through FOIA requests and witness testimony.

## Key Claims

- Reproduces the USAF SAC telegram expressing "grave concern" about the Oscar Flight shutdown
- Includes testimony from multiple witnesses beyond Salas himself
- Documents the NDA (non-disclosure agreement) administration by [[afosi]] immediately after the incidents
- Provides the documentary backbone for Salas's public presentations and congressional testimony

## Significance

*Faded Giant* is the primary sourced document for the Oscar/Echo Flight claims. It established a paper trail — using the government's own FOIA-released records — that separates Salas's account from pure testimony. The title refers to "Operation Faded Giant," the USAF operational term for UFO-related nuclear incidents.
