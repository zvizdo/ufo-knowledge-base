# Alien Information Theory

Book by [[andrew-gallimore]] (2015) presenting a metaphysical model of DMT and higher-dimensional reality. Gallimore stresses it is **a work of metaphysical speculation, not a statement of belief** — an attempt to show that reality could well be, and probably is, fundamentally different from what mainstream physics and astronomy present.

## Core Thesis

Our three-dimensional plus one (3D+time) reality is a "thin slice" within a much larger higher-dimensional structure. We are embedded in this structure but normally have no access to it. For reasons not fully understood, DMT temporarily grants access: the brain "unfolds" into the higher-dimensional structure, constructing a world model that is no longer limited to 3D inputs. This is why DMT worlds are described as **impossible** — they could not exist within our 3D reality, but our reality could exist within them.

The information flow model: information from the deeper levels of reality (quantum → subquantum → fundamental substrate) normally wells up through the brain's hierarchy but is gated. DMT opens the gate, allowing information from higher-dimensional structure to flood the world model.

## John Wheeler Connection

The book explicitly connects to [[john-wheeler]]'s "it from bit" — the idea that reality is fundamentally informational rather than material. If reality is information-theoretic at its base, then accessing deeper levels is accessing higher-information-density layers of the same computational structure. This also connects to the [[simulation-theory]] thread: DMT entities as "programmers" who operate at the code level.

## Relationship to Later Work

*Alien Information Theory* is the theoretical foundation; *[[death-by-astonishment]]* is the more empirically-grounded account of science's encounter with DMT. Gallimore's later work (directed-world model, DMTX proposal) builds on but modifies the framework — specifically moving from a passive "information from higher dimensions flows in" model to a more active "intelligences direct the experience" model ([[directed-dmt-world]]).
