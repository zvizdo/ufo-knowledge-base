# Twining Memo

Internal Air Force memorandum from General [[nathan-twining]], Commanding General of Air Materiel Command, dated September 23, 1947. Written in response to a request from Brigadier General George Schulgen, it was the first formal military assessment that UAP were real phenomena warranting investigation.

## Key Claims

### UAP Are Real
Twining concluded that "the phenomenon reported is something real and not visionary or fictitious." This was a direct rejection of the "mass hysteria" or "misidentification" dismissals circulating at the time.

### Observed Characteristics Listed
- Metallic or light-reflecting construction
- No trail except in certain conditions (vapor or smoke)
- Circular or elliptical shape, flat on bottom, domed on top
- High stability, probably approaching the size of a man-made fighter aircraft
- High maneuverability and ability to form loose formations
- **Evasiveness when approached** — objects consistently evaded contact by aircraft

### Nick Cook's Reading: The Performance Observables

[[nick-cook]] in [[G7Ns4Aq1tVc]] highlights Twining's explicit listing of performance characteristics as equally significant as the postscript: "extreme rates of climb, maneuverability, no vapor trails — which would imply a novel form of propulsion." Cook also notes that Twining's postscript range specification — at subsonic speed — exceeded what the U-2 spy plane (which wasn't revealed until ~7 years later) could achieve on its best day. This makes the postscript's confidence in "present US knowledge" even more remarkable: Twining was describing capabilities beyond contemporary public knowledge.

### Evasiveness as a Systemic Pattern
The evasiveness finding is cited by [[marik-von-rennenkampff]] in [[1iaH1a3A4Lk]] as a 1947 precursor to the modern "crappy video problem" argument. Twining documented evasion in 1947; [[mick-west]] argues in 2024 that UAPs "only appear when there are no quality cameras." Both observations describe the same pattern — but reach opposite conclusions about its cause.

### The Overlooked Postscript: Manhattan Project–Scale Secret Program

[[jesse-michaels]] highlights what he calls the most important and most ignored section of the memo. In the postscript, Twining writes:

> "It is possible within present US knowledge provided extensive detail development is undertaken to construct a piloted aircraft which has the general description of the object above."

He is stating that US knowledge *already* (in 1947) could produce craft with the observed characteristics — craft that break known aviation physics. He then calls for this to be pursued as a secret, fully insulated, Manhattan Project–style effort:

> "Any developments in this country along the lines indicated would be extremely expensive, time-consuming, and at the considerable expense of other projects and therefore should be set up independently of existing projects."

This is the direct institutional origin point for the claim that UAP programs were structured like the Manhattan Project — compartmentalized, insulated from civilian bureaucracy, and operated on a need-to-know basis. It prefigures [[david-grusch]]'s 2023 description of "deep black compartments" precisely.

## Provenance

Declassified and released through FOIA. It predates [[project-blue-book]] and the [[condon-commission]] and represents the closest thing to an unfiltered military assessment before the institutional response was systematized. The same year as [[1947-roswell]].
