# 4chan Whistleblower 2023

An anonymous individual posted a lengthy (~4-hour read per [[chris-ramsey]]) account on 4chan — approximately early 2023, possibly before or shortly after [[david-grusch]]'s public emergence — claiming to have worked on crash retrieval operations over roughly a decade in the late 1990s / early 2000s.

[[chris-ramsey]] characterized it as among the most compelling anonymous disclosures he has encountered: internally consistent, aligned with UFO lore at some points, generating novel claims at others, and closing with the haunting line: *"Notice yourself coming back."*

## Key Claims

### Mobile Construction Units (MCUs)
- Large, burger-shaped craft resting on the **ocean floor** (specifically identified as near the **Bahamas / Bermuda Triangle area**, though the whistleblower believed multiple MCUs existed globally)
- **Mobile**: capable of relocating, explaining both the "ocean floor" reports and the difficulty tracking them
- Function: **manufacturing smaller UAPs to exact specification** — every tool, every instrument precisely placed in dedicated recesses on interior walls
- Smaller craft deployed to conflict zones, resource collection sites, or other unknown destinations; **always returned** to the MCU
- The Bahamas / Bermuda Triangle location aligns with known USO hotspot reporting; the whistleblower believed the Atlantic MCU might also explain East Coast sightings

### Crash Retrieval Team Structure
- **Team 1**: Cleared bodies and element 115 from crash sites (per Bob Lazar's terminology — [[bob-lazar]])
- **Team 2** (the whistleblower's team): Stripped internal components — instruments stripped from walls, tools catalogued
- **Teams 3 & 4**: Removed and/or dismantled the bulk of the craft
- Compartmentalized; teams did not know each other's full scope

### Crash Frequency and NHI Adaptation
- Crashes happen **more frequently than publicly acknowledged**; the beings are not infallible
- Following recovery of bodies, NHI **reduced biological crew** and switched to primarily AI drone ships to prevent further body retrieval
- Most visible craft now are unmanned spherical drones — scanning craft; orange light = scanning for minerals or biologics; if an orange light approaches a person, abduction likelihood is elevated

### NHI Attitude Toward Humanity
- Higher-ups held two competing theories: (a) NHI are **waiting for human consciousness ascension**; (b) NHI are an **AI security/maintenance system** holding Earth's biosphere intact "until something else gets here" — the true owner(s) of the planet
- Both theories agree: NHI are fundamentally **indifferent** to day-to-day human activity ("zoo" model — don't care about the animals, only step in if the enclosure is threatened)
- If approached non-threateningly: MCU/craft evades. If cornered with hostile intent: any nearby electronics are destroyed; weapons platforms (submarines, jets, boats) that engaged "never came back"

### Corroborations (Per Ramsay)
- [[lue-elizondo]]'s public statement about a USO moving at **500 knots underwater** matches MCU/sub-craft description
- [[donald-trump]] statement about Jersey drones: "we know where the garage is" (aligns with fixed MCU location concept); sheriff reports confirmed objects emerging from the water
- Office of Naval Intelligence reports; [[tim-gallaudet]]'s USO work
- [[john-pina-craven]] book on classified submarine technology retrievals

## EBO Biologist Files (Corroborating Document)
A separate anonymously-sourced document, apparently produced after GPT-model AI became available (~post-2022), described the biology of recovered entities at **doctorate-level microbiology detail**. Experts who reviewed it for Ramsay found it either genuinely advanced or an exceptional creative fabrication. The biological description of gray entities as biological machines aligns with the 4chan account's characterization of NHI as AI security drones.

## Credibility Assessment

- **Anonymous source on 4chan** — inherently lowest tier
- Internal consistency over ~4 hours of Q&A is notable
- Multiple specific claims corroborated by subsequent public disclosures (Elizondo, Trump/sheriff, Gallaudet)
- Ramsay's closing position: *"Notice yourself coming back"* — he has returned to this text repeatedly as more data aligns, but remains agnostic on authenticity
