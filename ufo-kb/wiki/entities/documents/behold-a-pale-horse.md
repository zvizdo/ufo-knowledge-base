# Behold a Pale Horse

1991 book by [[bill-cooper|Milton "Bill" Cooper]]. A compendium mixing Cooper's Navy-era claims about UAP program briefings with leaked or fabricated documents (including alleged MJ-12 briefings), anti-Illuminati/New World Order material, and theories connecting the assassinations of JFK and Robert Kennedy to a secret government cabal. Approximately 500 pages; sold over 300,000 copies.

**Significance as a gateway text**: *Behold a Pale Horse* became the most widely read UFO/conspiracy book of the 1990s in the US underground press. It introduced millions of readers to the MJ-12 narrative, the Eisenhower treaty claim, and the concept of an above-top-secret UAP program hidden from elected officials. Its cultural reach extended to hip-hop (Wu-Tang Clan, Public Enemy referenced it) and militia movements.

**Content**: Cooper claimed he had seen classified briefing documents during his Navy service on the Pacific Fleet intelligence briefing team that revealed the government's knowledge of extraterrestrial intelligence. He also included the Protocols of the Elders of Zion (which he later regretted) and detailed claims about suppressed AIDS research and drug trafficking.

**Credibility status**: [[james-fox]] and other researchers express uncertainty about whether Cooper was a genuine whistleblower, a disinformation asset, or both — noting the JFK assassination details Cooper provided that later proved verifiable, alongside claims that remained unverifiable or were demonstrably false.
