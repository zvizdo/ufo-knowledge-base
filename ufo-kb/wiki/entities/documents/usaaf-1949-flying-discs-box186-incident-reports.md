# USAAF Flying-Disc Incident Reports Box 186

Declassified USAAF records collection (Box 186, Record Group 341.319.1) containing approximately 30 incident reports and administrative correspondence generated under the [[cp-cabell|C.P. Cabell]] Feb 6, 1948 flying-disc reporting directive and Flight Service Regulation FSR 200-4 (2 November 1948). All reports route to Air Materiel Command, [[wright-patterson]] ATTN: MCIAXO-3.

## Key Documents in Collection

- Feb 6, 1948 Cabell letter (reproduced): establishing direct routing to AMC, bypassing ordinary channels
- Oct 26, 1948 HQ USAF TWX (Maj Gen Kuter): alerting all AACS units for a new interval of sightings; scope expanded to include reports outside US/Alaska
- ~30 incident reports spanning Nov 1948–Jan 1950 at SECRET/CONFIDENTIAL/RESTRICTED levels
- Fairfield-Suisun AFB Project Sign EEI form (Dec 3, 1948)
- Fort Chimo SAC message explicitly naming "PROJECT SIGN" (Mar 18, 1949)
- Japan 6-station simultaneous weather sighting report (Jan 25, 1949)
