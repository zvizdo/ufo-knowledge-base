# Millennial Hospitality

Six-volume book series by [[charles-hall]] documenting his firsthand interactions with the [[tall-whites]] at [[indian-springs]] (Areas 53–54, Dreamland), Nevada, from March 1965 to May 1967. Hall describes the series as intended for his grandchildren — to explain what life was like at Indian Springs and to distinguish between *guardian angels* (spiritual beings) and *extraterrestrials* (fully biological beings in engineered machines).

## Contents (Volumes)

- **Vol. 1–3** — Main account of sustained Tall White contact; Hall's gradual trust-building with the Teacher, Range for Harry, Pamela, and the Butterfly; his craft flights; his observations of Tall White society
- **Vol. 4** — Contains a reproduced xerox of a **written thank-you note from Pope Benedict** after Hall personally presented Vols. 1–3 in Rome (~2005–2006)
- **Vol. 5–6** — Continuation of the account; one volume specifically focused on the gray craft encounter and Roswell interpretation (subtitle includes "Valleys of the Greys" in Hall's references)

## The Pope Benedict Presentation

Hall and his wife [[marie-theres-hall]] visited Rome and personally presented the first three volumes to **Pope Benedict**. Hall received a written thank-you note in return, reproduced in Vol. 4. [[tim-taylor]] also underwent a religious conversion at the Vatican archives — both Hall and Taylor connect through Vatican contact, though separately and for different reasons.
