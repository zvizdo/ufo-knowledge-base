# Hunt for the Skinwalker

*Hunt for the Skinwalker: Science Confronts the Unexplained at a Remote Ranch in Utah* (2005) — the primary public account of paranormal and UAP phenomena at [[skinwalker-ranch]]. Co-authored by investigative journalist [[george-knapp]] and [[nids]] biochemist [[colm-kelleher]].

## Summary

Documents the Sherman family's experiences in the mid-1990s (cattle mutilations, UAP sightings, poltergeist activity, encounters with large predatory animals impervious to bullets) and the subsequent NIDS scientific investigation funded by [[robert-bigelow]] after his 1996 purchase of the property. The book is the foundational public text on the multi-phenomenon nature of the ranch's anomalous activity.

## Key Claims

- The Sherman family experienced a sustained multi-phenomenon assault across multiple years, beginning shortly after taking ownership
- An oversized bipedal wolf-like creature was shot at close range with no apparent effect
- Cattle were mutilated under conditions with no tracks, no predator signs, and unexplained surgical precision
- The NIDS scientific team documented orbs, UAP sightings, and instrument anomalies despite attempts at controlled investigation
- The phenomena appeared responsive and intelligence-driven rather than random

## Provenance

Based on NIDS investigation records to which Kelleher had direct access, and on George Knapp's journalism including interviews with the Sherman family. First public disclosure of the NIDS investigation's findings.
