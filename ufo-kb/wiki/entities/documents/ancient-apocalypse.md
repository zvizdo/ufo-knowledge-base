# Ancient Apocalypse

Netflix docu-series (8 episodes, 2022) by [[graham-hancock]] presenting the case for a sophisticated pre-Ice Age civilization destroyed in a global catastrophe. A sequel season was later produced.

## Context

The series attracted unusual mainstream controversy for a documentary. A *Guardian* journalist described it as "the most dangerous show on Netflix" — a characterization [[jesse-michaels]] uses to introduce Hancock in [[dfPfPB601hw]].

The characterization appears to stem from mainstream archaeologists' concern that the series undermines confidence in established archaeological timelines without meeting peer-review standards. The Society for American Archaeology wrote to Netflix asking for a disclaimer.

## Key Sites Covered

The series visits multiple megalithic sites globally — similar to Hancock's book *[[fingerprints-of-gods]]* but with visual on-site investigation. At least one episode focuses on Göbekli Tepe.

## Michael Shermer Response

[[michael-shermer]] — longtime skeptic and previous Hancock debate opponent — emailed Hancock after watching the series to say that Hancock had made a "good case for my basic proposition that we're missing an episode of the human story." Hancock cites this in [[dfPfPB601hw]] as evidence of Shermer's intellectual integrity.
