# The Phenomenon (Fox Documentary)

2020 documentary film by [[james-fox]], widely regarded as one of the most comprehensive mainstream UFO documentaries. Fox describes it as his most thoroughly produced work, taking longer to complete than its follow-up [[the-program-fox]].

## Key Claims / Content

- Features the H.C. Cross letter (Director of Exotic Metallurgy at [[battelle-memorial-institute]]) discovered by [[jacques-vallee]] in [[j-allen-hynek]]'s files — confirming a classified parallel program running alongside [[project-blue-book]]
- Includes [[fife-symington]]'s firsthand testimony about the [[1997-phoenix-lights]]
- Features the 1994 Zimbabwe school encounter (62–100 children reporting craft and telepathic entity contact) — [[harry-reid]] urged Fox to cut it; Fox kept it in
- Featured in a Larry King Live discussion with Symington and [[buzz-aldrin]]
