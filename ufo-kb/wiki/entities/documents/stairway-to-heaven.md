# Stairway to Heaven

*Stairway to Heaven* is a scholarly book by [[peter-levenda]] that examines the seven-level celestial ascent tradition across Jewish Merkavah mysticism, Shangqing Daoism, and Sumerian cosmology. Its central thesis is that all three traditions encode the same contact-event-derived knowledge, using the Big Dipper (Ursa Major) as the cosmic navigation axis for the ascent path.

In [[Y7PLeu5rTv4]], Levenda refers to the book when asked about how to begin practice. He describes it not as a step-by-step instruction manual but as an overview of the first step — the state where "what you think is real and what is really real meet in between." Once that first step is achieved, subsequent levels become self-directing.
