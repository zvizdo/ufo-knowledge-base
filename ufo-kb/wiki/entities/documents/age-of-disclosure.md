# The Age of Disclosure

Documentary film produced by [[dan-farah]] (2025). Described by [[jesse-michaels]] as "one of the very best documentaries on the whole UAP/UFO phenomenon" — distinguished from others by the seniority and clearance level of its interview subjects rather than reliance on footage.

## Summary

Most UAP documentaries rely heavily on footage (gimbal, go-fast, tic-tac) and whistleblower testimony. *The Age of Disclosure* is different: its core claim is made through the words of former DNI-level intelligence officials and senior senators, who either explicitly confirm or strongly imply that UAP crash retrieval and back-engineering programs are real.

## Key Interview Subjects

- **[[james-woolsey]]** — former Director of Central Intelligence
- **[[james-clapper]]** — former Director of National Intelligence
- **[[john-brennan]]** — former CIA Director
- **[[marco-rubio]]** — Senator (former acting DNI); urgency around China race
- **[[chuck-schumer]]** — Senate Majority Leader; co-sponsored UAP disclosure legislation

## Dan Farah's Core Thesis

Per [[joe-rogan]]'s summary:
1. Both the US and China have retrieved crashed non-human vehicles
2. Both are running parallel back-engineering programs
3. The race is the new Manhattan Project — but for propulsion/spacetime manipulation, not nuclear weapons
4. Rubio's urgency is specifically: if China cracks back-engineered propulsion first, the US loses a generational military advantage
5. **The only path forward requires an amnesty bill**: decades of lying to Congress, misappropriated funds, potentially illegal technology allocation to specific defense contractors — all must be legally protected before gatekeepers will speak

## Significance

*The Age of Disclosure* represents the highest-level official endorsement cluster in contemporary UAP documentary form. Where prior documentaries relied on former military witnesses (Nimitz pilots, etc.) or mid-tier whistleblowers, this film's interview subjects held the top positions in the US intelligence apparatus. Their willingness to speak on camera — even in carefully worded terms — is itself a disclosure event.
