# *Space Relations* (1973)

Science-fiction novel written by [[donald-barr]] and published in 1973. Its plot involves a man who travels to another planet, where he is exploited by a powerful woman in a system based on sexual access to minors as political leverage and control.

## Significance: The Epstein Template

[[donald-barr]] was the headmaster of the Dalton School who hired a manifestly unqualified 20-year-old [[jeffrey-epstein]] as a math teacher — a hire with no legitimate institutional justification. Epstein subsequently rose from that position to control over $300 million and ran what appears to have been a sexual exploitation and blackmail operation targeting the world's most powerful figures.

The structural parallel between the novel's plot — underage sexual access as political compromise and control — and Epstein's operational model is the core of the claim made in American Alchemy's analysis: Epstein may have been programmed with *Space Relations* as a life template, whether consciously or via [[mk-ultra]]-adjacent conditioning.

The host described the woman in *Space Relations* as being "almost like Ghislaine or like one of his handlers."

## Donald Barr's Background

Barr was a highly cleared Navy intelligence officer before becoming a school administrator. He is also the father of [[william-barr]], who served as US Attorney General twice. His intelligence background, combined with his hiring of Epstein and the thematic content of his novel, creates a nexus that researchers find difficult to dismiss as coincidence.
