# Chariots of the Gods

*Chariots of the Gods? Unsolved Mysteries of the Past* (1968) by Erich von Däniken is one of the most widely read books in the ancient astronaut genre. Von Däniken argued that ancient monuments — the Nazca lines, Egyptian pyramids, megalithic structures worldwide — were built with extraterrestrial assistance or serve as signals and landing strips for alien craft.

## Significance and Criticism

The book sold over 65 million copies worldwide and popularized the [[ancient-astronaut-hypothesis]] for a mass audience. It is also extensively criticized by archaeologists for misrepresenting the capabilities of ancient civilizations and cherry-picking evidence.

In the UAP research context, von Däniken's work functions as a **cultural gateway text** — a book that led many researchers to investigate UAP and ancient mysteries — while simultaneously being a credibility liability due to methodological looseness.

## Nazca Lines Connection

The book popularized the theory that the Nazca lines in Peru were landing strips for alien aircraft. This framing is significant context for the modern [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] controversy: the Peruvian Ministry of Culture has historically lumped von Däniken-style ancient astronaut claims with the modern forensic mummy claims, using the former's poor reputation to discredit the latter.
