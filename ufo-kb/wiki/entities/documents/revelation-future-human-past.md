# Revelation, the Future Human Past

Book by anthropologist **[[michael-p-masters]]** extending the future-human argument into the religion-and-mythology corpus. Companion to [[extratemporalis-model-book]] and *Identified Flying Objects*.

## Summary

The argument: religious revelation traditions — Hebrew prophets, Marian apparitions, contactee accounts — admit a structural reading in which future humans contacting their own past produce phenomenology indistinguishable from the religious-revelation category. The book is a deliberately ambitious cross-domain synthesis treating myth and contact as commensurable data.

## Key Claims

- Religious revelation phenomenology and abduction phenomenology share structural features that future-human contact would naturally produce
- The argument runs in both directions: it reframes religious revelation in light of UAP data, and reframes UAP encounters in light of revelation traditions
- Bridges into [[ufo-religion-convergence]] and [[diana-pasulka]]'s adjacent work

## Provenance

Civilian publication; Masters is a tenured anthropologist at Montana Tech.
