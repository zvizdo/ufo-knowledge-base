# Trinity: The Best Kept Secret

Book by [[jacques-vallee]] and [[paola-harris]] (2021) documenting the alleged [[1945-san-antonio-trinity-crash]] near San Antonio, New Mexico. The crash occurred approximately 20 days after the first atomic test ([[1945-trinity-test]]) and two days after Japan's formal surrender — making it arguably the earliest documented US crash retrieval, predating [[1947-roswell]] by two years.

## Core Argument

The book argues three things:

1. The crash predates the coinage of "flying saucer" (Kenneth Arnold, 1947), demonstrating that the UAP story is not a product of postwar atomic-age culture.
2. The phenomenon has been happening continuously for millennia — Roswell is not the beginning but a famous mid-chapter.
3. The case resides in a **separate classification architecture** (DOE/AEC lineage) invisible to standard Pentagon SAP holders, explaining why it remained unknown even to high-clearance defense officials.

## Witness Testimony

Primary witnesses [[remy-baca]] and [[jose-padilla]] (ages 7 and 9 at the time) were interviewed by Harris on audio tape before Vallée came aboard. Their accounts remained consistent over decades. Vallée credits Harris with bringing the case to his attention through Italian UFO research networks.
