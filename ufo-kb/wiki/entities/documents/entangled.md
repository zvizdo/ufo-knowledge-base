# Entangled

Graham Hancock's debut novel. Unusually for a work of fiction, Hancock describes its origin as a direct creative transmission during five Ayahuasca sessions in Brazil in 2006 — the first time he had drunk Ayahuasca.

## Origin Story

As Hancock explains in [[dfPfPB601hw]]:

> "I was given literally the entire plot of a novel — I'd never written a novel before. And at the end of those sessions I got a very clear message from the entity that I call Mother Ayahuasca: write it. Did you? And I did."

The novel features two young women — one living 24,000 years ago, one living today — who are **entangled across time** in a shared battle against a **demonic force traveling through history**, twisting humanity away from its true mission. Both women must stop this force, simultaneously in the Stone Age and the present.

## Significance for Hancock's Worldview

The *Entangled* origin story is Hancock's most direct claim of an NHI-like creative transmission — not a philosophical conclusion but a lived experience. He treats Mother Ayahuasca as a real entity, regardless of scientific dismissal.

> "If you're going to roll your eyes at the notion of Mother Ayahuasca, go drink some Ayahuasca and then see if you're going to roll your eyes."

The demonic-force-through-time narrative aligns structurally with the broader discourse around negative non-human intelligences and temporal manipulation.
