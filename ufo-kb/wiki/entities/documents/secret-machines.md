# Secret Machines

*Secret Machines: Work in Progress* is a book series by [[tom-delonge]] and [[peter-levenda]], with a core thesis that **UAP are mystical machines** — that is, craft or phenomena that operate at the intersection of advanced technology and consciousness.

## Advisory Structure

Notably, the project was formally advised by senior figures in classified aerospace and defense:
- **[[charles-chase]]** — Director of Lockheed's Revolutionary Projects Organization (Skunk Works)
- **[[robert-weiss]]** — Senior Skunk Works figure; Rich's successor
- **[[james-ryder]]** — Former VP of Lockheed Martin, Skunk Works director
- **Neil McCaslin** — Air Force Research Lab Commander
- Additional senior aerospace insiders

According to DeLonge and Levenda, **these elite scientists consistently returned to human consciousness as the key variable** in their conversations about UAP. The "mystical machines" thesis is not DeLonge's or Levenda's speculation alone — it reflects the private views of some of the most capable individuals in the US defense sector.

## Key Claim

> UAP are mystical machines — phenomena that operate at the intersection of advanced technology and human consciousness.

This connects to the broader [[occult-aerospace-connection]] and [[consciousness-as-technology]] patterns documented in the KB.

## Necronomicon Connection

[[peter-levenda]] has noted that he received letters from inside the US military praising the Necronomicon as "spot on" — including from Strategic Air Command officers. Levenda has also suggested that figures close to these launches encouraged people like Tom DeLonge to study Greek mythology (especially Prometheus) to understand UAP. The NROL-16 payload was named Prometheus.
