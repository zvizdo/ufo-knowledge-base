# Wilson-Davis Memo

Fifteen pages of handwritten notes allegedly taken by physicist [[eric-davis]] immediately after a private conversation with Vice Admiral Thomas R. Wilson at a parking lot in Las Vegas on October 16, 2002. Wilson had recently retired as Director of the Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA).

## Alleged Contents

According to the notes:
- Wilson confirmed that in 1997, as J-2 (Director of Intelligence on the Joint Chiefs), he attempted to gain access to a Special Access Program involving crashed UAP hardware and reverse engineering.
- He was denied access by program gatekeepers from a private defense contractor, who told him the program existed but that he had no "need to know."
- The contractor's lawyers threatened consequences if Wilson pursued the matter.
- Wilson confirmed that "they" (the program managers) had crash-retrieved hardware and biologics.

## Provenance

The document appeared online in June 2019, leaked from the estate of astronaut [[edgar-mitchell]]. Both Wilson and Davis have publicly denied or declined to confirm the document's authenticity. The document's internal details have been extensively analyzed by UAP researchers and journalists.
