# Science in Default

Lecture/paper by atmospheric physicist [[james-mcdonald]], delivered to the American Association for the Advancement of Science (AAAS) in December 1969. Its full title is "Science in Default: Twenty-Two Years of Inadequate UFO Investigations." It is the most rigorous scientific critique of the [[condon-commission]] report and a foundational text for the ETH position.

## Key Claims

### The Condon Committee Failed Its Mandate
McDonald argued that Edward Condon's committee systematically neglected its best cases, that the report's conclusions were predetermined before the investigation was complete, and that the scientific community was abandoning a genuine anomaly due to social and institutional pressure rather than evidence.

### ETH as Least Unsatisfactory
McDonald's canonical formulation: given the accumulated record of hundreds of multi-witness, close-range sightings by credible observers (police, pilots, engineers, military personnel), no conventional explanation — atmospheric, psychological, or classified aircraft — adequately covers the full dataset. The extraterrestrial hypothesis is not proven; it is simply the hypothesis that fails least often. This formulation was deliberately probabilistic.

### Twenty-Two Years of Inadequate Investigation
By 1969, UAP had been officially reported for 22 years (since 1947). McDonald argued the institutional response had been structurally incapable of serious investigation — a critique that [[marik-von-rennenkampff]] implicitly applies to [[aaro]] in 2024.

## Historical Significance

McDonald died by suicide in 1971. His work was largely suppressed in the UFO community's mainstream through the stigma era (1970s–2010s). [[marik-von-rennenkampff]] specifically recommends *Science in Default* as the entry point for serious UAP research, calling McDonald's body of work "remarkable."
