# Pentacle Memo

Internal memorandum written by Howard Cross of [[battelle-memorial-institute]] (Battelle), found by [[jacques-vallee]] in [[project-blue-book]] files. The memo describes a program where Battelle was conducting a surgical, real UFO investigation focused on known hotspots — particularly around sensitive sites including nuclear installations — while Blue Book served as a public-facing PR operation.

## Summary

Cross's memo establishes that there were known UFO hotspots around sensitive US installations and that Battelle was conducting the actual investigation of these. The memo could not be raised at the [[robertson-panel]] (the 1952 CIA UFO review) — it was suppressed from that discussion.

[[jacques-vallee]] discovered the memo in Blue Book files and brought it to [[j-allen-hynek]], asking whether Hynek knew about it and whether he was complicit in concealing the real investigation. Hynek reportedly "played dumb."

## Key Claims

- Existence of a parallel, real UFO investigation beneath Blue Book's public operation
- Known UFO hotspots existed around nuclear and sensitive installations
- The Robertson Panel was deliberately kept from seeing this memo
