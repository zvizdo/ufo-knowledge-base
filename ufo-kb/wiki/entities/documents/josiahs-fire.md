# Josiah's Fire

Book documenting the case of Josiah, a non-verbal autistic child whose mother (Tahni Cullen) began receiving typed communications from him that included apparent precognitive statements and theologically sophisticated content Josiah could not have acquired through normal means. Co-authored by Josiah's mother and writer Max Davis.

Referenced in [[0qlppHc3-gg]] as a case study incorporated into [[ky-dickens]]'s *[[telepathy-tapes]]* documentary. Josiah's case is one of multiple documented instances of non-verbal autistic individuals demonstrating anomalous cognitive or communicative abilities beyond standard sensory channels.

## Key Claims

- Josiah, non-verbal from an early age, began communicating through Facilitated Communication / Supported Typing.
- The content of his communications included specific, verifiable statements and spiritually sophisticated ideas that his documented learning history could not explain.
- Josiah's case is framed within a Christian theological context (unlike Powell's secular research), but the phenomenological core — a non-verbal autistic child accessing information beyond normal sensory channels — is the same.
