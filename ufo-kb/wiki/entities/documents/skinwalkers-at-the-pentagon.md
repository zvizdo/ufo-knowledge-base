# Skinwalkers at the Pentagon

*Skinwalkers at the Pentagon: An Insider Account of the Secret Government UFO Program* (2021) — co-authored by [[colm-kelleher]], [[george-knapp]], and DIA official [[james-lacatski]]. The book is the most authoritative public account of [[aawsap]]'s investigation of [[skinwalker-ranch]], based on partially declassified program materials and direct insider access.

## Summary

Documents the [[aawsap]] program's findings at the ranch, with particular focus on:
- The **hitchhiker effect**: the disturbing finding that phenomena associated with the ranch followed investigators home after visits. Scientists working at the property reported paranormal activity at their own homes — poltergeist-like events, apparitions, and anomalous phenomena appearing in their personal lives weeks or months after leaving the ranch. This is one of the most significant and least-explained findings of the entire investigation.
- **Biological effects**: detailed documentation of physical and neurological effects experienced by personnel at the ranch, including effects that required medical attention.
- The breadth of the AAWSAP mandate, which went beyond aerial phenomena to encompass the full multi-phenomenon environment.

## Key Claims

- AAWSAP scientists experienced paranormal activity in their homes after working at the ranch — the hitchhiker effect is documented with multiple case examples
- Biological effects on ranch visitors and investigators were severe and medically documented
- The program operated under [[bigelow-aerospace]] as a private contractor, making its data classified and FOIA-exempt
- The scale and consistency of phenomena at the ranch convinced senior program officials of a genuine anomalous reality

## Provenance

Based on Lacatski's direct insider position as a DIA official who co-founded AAWSAP, combined with Kelleher's investigative records from NIDS and AAWSAP, and Knapp's journalism. The title references Lacatski physically bringing the material to congressional attention.
