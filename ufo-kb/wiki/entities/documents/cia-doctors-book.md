# The CIA Doctors

2006 book by psychiatrist **[[colin-ross]]** documenting the network of academic and military psychiatrists who participated in CIA-funded mind-control research from the 1950s through the 1970s. Ross compiled FOIA-released contracts, declassified meeting minutes, and named-personnel records into a single reference that quantifies the scope of MK-Ultra-and-successor-program contracting through the medical-academic establishment.

In UFO-adjacent discourse, the book is a primary anchor for the [[mk-ultra-mind-control-ecosystem]] synthesis: it documents the institutional infrastructure that the [[the-controllers-martin-cannon]] essay argued overlapped with the abduction-phenomena literature.

## Summary

Ross's central claim is that the public has been told the MK-Ultra program ended with the 1973 destruction of files, when in fact only one program-name ended; the underlying contracting infrastructure continued under successor program names through the 1980s and beyond.

## Key Claims

- Documented contractor-network spanning 80+ universities and research institutes
- Specific named psychiatrists with multi-program contract histories
- Continuity of contracting through 1980s
- Hospital-based experimentation on uninformed civilian populations
