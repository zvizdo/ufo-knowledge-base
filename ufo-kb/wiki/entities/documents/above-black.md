# Above Black

Self-published memoir by [[dan-sherman]], a retired USAF Technical Sergeant, documenting his claimed service as an "intuitive communicator" (IC) under [[project-preserve-destiny]] — an alleged NSA classified program in which humans with alien-modified genetics telepathically received and relayed NHI transmissions.

## Summary

The book covers:
- Sherman's ELINT career background and the conditions under which he was recruited into PPD
- The biofeedback training protocol used to activate and calibrate the IC faculty
- His overseas IC deployments and the character of his alien contacts ("Spock" and "Bones")
- The alien-communicated content: multiple races, human-NHI interbreeding, travel via EM void, humans as "water beings"
- His increasing discomfort when transmissions shifted to appear to describe real-time abduction monitoring
- His eventual departure from the program and the Air Force

## Provenance

Self-published by Sherman; no mainstream publisher. The book predates the internet's broad availability, making it a primary document of the pre-disclosure era. It is cited by [[jesse-michaels]] as having deeply influenced his thinking on UAP legacy programs.

## Key Claims

1. The classification architecture of PPD: "gray project" concealed inside a "black project" — a nesting structure providing an additional layer of concealment beyond standard black program protocols
2. IC code: Staunch 118
3. Training: sine wave biofeedback; tone humming; no alien contact during training
4. Two alien contacts distinguishable by "tapestry" of communication
5. Abduction monitoring role toward end of service
