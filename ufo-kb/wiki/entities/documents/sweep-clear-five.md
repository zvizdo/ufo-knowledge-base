# Sweep Clear Five

Book (full title: *Sweep Clear Five: NATO's Underwater UFO Encounter*). Documents two related underwater UAP events:

1. **Early 1960s NATO mine warfare exercise**: During a NATO exercise near Canada, UFOs were observed entering the water. This appears to be an event that preceded and is related to Shag Harbor.

2. **[[1967-shag-harbor-uso]]**: The main subject of the book. Canadian Navy divers were dispatched to investigate after multiple civilians witnessed a UAP descend into Shag Harbor, Nova Scotia. The divers reported observing two disc-shaped craft underwater, with what appeared to be occupants outside the craft apparently making repairs to one of the discs. Video footage was allegedly captured during the diver investigation; the tapes' location is unknown.

The book was written by a researcher who interviewed the Canadian Navy divers directly. [[tim-gallaudet]] cites it as one of the most compelling pieces of USO evidence in the public record.
