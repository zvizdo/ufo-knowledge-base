# National Security Act of 1947

Signed by President [[harry-truman]] on July 26, 1947 — six weeks after Roswell. The legislation created:
- The **Department of Defense** (unifying the Army, Navy, and newly independent Air Force under a civilian Secretary)
- The **Central Intelligence Agency** (giving the CIA broad authorities for foreign intelligence and covert action)
- The **National Security Council** (advising the President on national security)
- The **Joint Chiefs of Staff** (military advisory body to the President)

**KB relevance**: The NSA 1947 was signed in the same month as the 1947 Roswell crash recovery. The CIA it created was one of the first agencies with both the legal authorities and institutional culture to run deeply compartmented programs. The legislation's broad "other functions" clause for the CIA has been interpreted by legal scholars as providing authority for covert programs not specifically enumerated — which UAP researchers cite as the legal foundation for secret UAP programs operating outside normal congressional oversight.

**Timing significance**: The near-simultaneous creation of both Roswell (July 1947) and the NSA 1947 (July 1947) is noted in the KB's [[ufo-secrecy]] and [[history-of-uap]] pages as either coincidence or evidence that the legislation was shaped by awareness of the UAP reality.
