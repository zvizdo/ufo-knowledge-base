# UFOs and Nukes (Hastings)

[[robert-hastings]]'s approximately 600-page compilation of firsthand accounts from 167 security-cleared (PRP-level) US military personnel who witnessed UAP activity at nuclear weapons sites. Published 2008; first edition followed by updated editions. Jesse Michaels describes it as "literally the world's best database on this phenomena."

## Key Cases Covered

- [[1967-echo-flight]] and [[1967-oscar-flight]] (Malmstrom AFB; [[robert-salas]], [[bob-jameson]], [[walt-feele]])
- [[1964-vandenberg-bob-jacobs]] (Big Sur telescope filming)
- [[1977-ellsworth-woods-abduction]] ([[mario-woods]] CE3)
- [[1966-minot-ufo-missiles]] ([[david-shindell]] launch indicator)
- [[2010-fe-warren-outage]] (Tic-Tac/mothership; back-channel [[john-mills]])
- Dozens of global cases across UK (Rendlesham), Soviet Union, and other nuclear states

## Significance

- Senator Harry Reid read the book (via [[hal-puthoff]]) and it was a direct trigger for his funding push for AATIP/AAWSAP
- [[jake-barber]] cited it by name in his 2024 Sean Ryan interview when contextualizing the NJ drone flap / Salem nuclear plant incidents
- Hastings's 167-witness corpus is the largest curated database of nuclear-site UAP testimony in existence
- [[marik-von-rennenkampff]] cites the corpus as the most under-counted credibility anchor in UAP discourse

## Companion Work

Hastings published a second book, *Confession*, a personal memoir disclosing his own UAP experiences including a nasal implant — and his broader claims about the nature of the phenomenon.
