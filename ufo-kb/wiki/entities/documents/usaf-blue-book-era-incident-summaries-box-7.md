# USAF Blue Book Era Incident Summaries — Box 7

233 standardized incident report forms from [[project-sign]], USAF's first formal UFO investigation program (1947–1949). Released in PURSUE Release 01. The forms use an A–D × 1–5 intelligence reliability rating system throughout.

**Key incidents:**
- Incidents 1–4: Muroc AFB (Edwards) military cluster, July 7–8, 1947
- Incidents 33–33g: [[1948-mantell-incident]] — first UAP-pursuit aviation fatality
- Incident 144: [[1948-chiles-whitted]] — windowed cigar-shaped craft; drove Estimate of the Situation
- Incident 172: [[1948-gorman-dogfight]] — 27-minute F-51 engagement
- Incident 183: [[1948-fukuoka-f61-intercept]] — F-61 radar evasion; investigator hypothesizes radar-detection equipment
- Incidents 223–231: New Mexico green fireball wave; [[lincoln-lapaz]] 9-point non-meteoric analysis
