# UFO (Graff 2024)

*UFO: The Inside Story of the US Government's Search for Alien Life Here—and Out There* (2024) is a book by journalist Garrett Graff providing a historical overview of US government UFO/UAP investigation programs. The book is positioned as a mainstream, authoritative history.

**Shellenberger's analysis**: [[michael-shellenberger]] flags Graff's book as a sophisticated debunking operation rather than a neutral history. Shellenberger's argument focuses on Graff's prior activities: Graff co-orchestrated a Hunter Biden laptop pre-bunking operation at the [[aspen-institute]] alongside [[vivian-schiller]] — a coordinated effort to manage a sensitive political story before its full emergence. Shellenberger argues that Graff's simultaneous involvement in two major information-management operations (Hunter Biden laptop and UAP history) is suspicious and suggests he is operating in a pattern of narrative control around government-adjacent sensitive topics.

**Garrett Graff context**: Graff is a prominent journalist and author (known for *Watergate*, *The Only Plane in the Sky*, and other books). He has written for major media outlets and is a fellow at the Aspen Institute. His credentials make him an effective voice for a mainstream narrative — which, from Shellenberger's perspective, is precisely what makes his UAP book concerning as a potential institutional debunking vehicle.

**Credibility**: The debunking-operation characterization is Shellenberger's interpretation. Graff's book receives mainstream positive reviews. The KB documents Shellenberger's analysis as a contested interpretation, not an established fact.
