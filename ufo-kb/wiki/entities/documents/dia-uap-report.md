# DIA UAP Report

A set of Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) documents related to advanced aerospace threats and UAP phenomena. The most significant are the AAWSAP-era reports.

**AAWSAP technical reports**: Between 2008 and 2010, DIA funded the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program (AAWSAP), contracted to Robert Bigelow's Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies (BAASS). This produced 38 technical reports on advanced aerospace topics — ranging from conventional aerospace threats to biological effects of UAP encounters, warp drives, and traversable wormholes. A set of these were released via FOIA, providing documentation that DIA-funded research explicitly addressed UAP-adjacent physics including Alcubierre drives, Casimir effect energy, and wormhole propulsion.

**Relationship to AATIP**: AAWSAP and [[aatip]] (Advanced Aerial Threat Identification Program) were overlapping programs; Lue Elizondo ran AATIP, which drew on the AAWSAP research. The DIA's role in funding this research is documented.

**Wilson-Davis context**: [[wilson-davis-memo]] references DIA in the context of alleged Admiral Wilson inquiries about crash retrieval programs — placing DIA in the institutional chain of alleged UAP program oversight. The memo's authenticity is contested.

**KB relevance**: DIA documents constitute part of the authenticated government UAP record — alongside the [[halt-memo]] and Project Blue Book files. The AAWSAP technical reports in particular provide evidence that serious physics research on UAP-relevant topics was DIA-funded, even if the program was never publicly acknowledged.
