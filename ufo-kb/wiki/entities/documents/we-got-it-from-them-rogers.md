# *We Got It From Them* (2024)

Book written by [[greg-rogers]] — retired US Air Force flight surgeon — published in 2024 following his first public disclosure of the [[1992-cape-canaveral-saucer]] incident via UK Daily Mail journalist [[josh-boswell]] in May 2024.

## Title Origin

The title quotes a senior officer who, when Rogers asked about the disc-shaped craft he had witnessed, replied: "We got it from them." The phrase implies direct acknowledgment that recovered craft of non-human origin are in US possession — and that this was known to some within the military at the time.

## Content and Structure

The book alternates between:
- Rogers's UFO testimony about the 1992 incident
- Military career vignettes designed to give civilian readers a feel for military culture and the challenges of service
- A dedicated section on PTSD advocacy — Rogers has used his public platform to support veterans dealing with trauma

This structure was apparently deliberate: interleaving the UAP content with relatable military stories to make the testimony accessible to civilian readers who might otherwise discount a straightforward UFO account.
