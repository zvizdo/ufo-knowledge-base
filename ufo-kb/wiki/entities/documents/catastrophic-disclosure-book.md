# Catastrophic Disclosure

Book by **[[michael-mazzola]]** compiling witness narratives and disclosure-era analysis. Companion to Mazzola's documentary [[this-is-not-a-hoax-documentary]].

## Summary

A long-form treatment of the 2023–2024 disclosure window — the period bracketed by [[david-grusch]]'s Congressional testimony and the parallel waves of civilian and military witnesses surfacing during the same months. The book argues that this surge constitutes a distinct phase shift in the disclosure timeline, irrespective of whether any single claim is sustained.
