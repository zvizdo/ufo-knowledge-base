# American Cosmic

2019 book by [[diana-pasulka]], professor of Religious Studies at the University of North Carolina Wilmington. The book examines the convergence between UAP belief and religious experience, with access to the **Vatican archives** and to anonymous high-level UAP researchers.

## Core Thesis: 1947 as Dividing Line

The central argument cited by [[brian-muraresku]] and [[jesse-michaels]] in [[I9VyyV4-3Js]]:

> **1947 (Roswell) is a paradigm shift in how the ancient contact phenomenon presents.** Before 1947, people encountered the same phenomenon framed in the idiom of their cultural moment: angels, demons, genies, sylphs, fairies, gnomes, elves, leprechauns. After 1947, the same phenomenon arrives in the technological idiom of the modern era: aliens, spacecraft, ETs.

The phenomenon may be continuous; the interpretation is culturally mediated.

This thesis is endorsed by [[terence-mckenna]] (who used similar framing) and connects directly to [[ufo-religion-convergence]].

## Late 19th Century Airships

Muraresku adds a bridging case in [[I9VyyV4-3Js]]: **late 19th century airship sightings** in the American Midwest. Before Betty and Barney Hill, before Roswell — people reported seeing strange craft piloted by humanoid beings who would descend on ladders, have conversations with farmers, and fly away. The technology was "cognizable at the time" (airship-era); the encounter structure was identical to later ET contact. These encounters sometimes left witnesses transformed positively — similar to clinical psychedelic and mystery-ritual outcomes.

## Vatican Access

Pasulka obtained unusual access to the Vatican Observatory and Vatican archives in connection with her research. [[bob-maguire]] (in [[2Xxmguz0GEQ]]) notes she was escorted by **Tim Taylor**, who was admitted to the Vatican archives without her, flashing multiple three-letter-agency credentials.

## Why the Title "American Cosmic" (aa9Xx5wI8Rw)

Pasulka explains at Hereticon: she named the book *American Cosmic* because the [[cosmism]] tradition underpinning the US space program "we hadn't talked about." The book is about the American branch of the tradition that places NHI-belief at the epistemological foundation of rocketry and astronautics — with [[jack-parsons]] as the American anchor and [[konstantin-tsiolkovsky]] as the Russian parallel.

The book documents the existence of the **oral tradition** through which this cosmism is transmitted (not via memos but hermetically, person-to-person) and the **mission patches** with Roman gods and first-century Latin that are its living institutional expression.

## "Tyler D" — Tim Taylor in American Cosmic

The book features a high-level UAP researcher under the pseudonym **"Tyler D"** — identified in the broader research community as [[tim-taylor]] (NASA Mission Controller). Key events Pasulka documents involving Tyler D:
- Escorted her to the Vatican Observatory and Vatican archives; was admitted to the archives alone, without her, flashing multiple three-letter-agency credentials on demand
- Led Pasulka and [[garry-nolan]] to a **UAP crash site in New Mexico** (location not specified)
- Underwent a religious conversion experience in the Vatican archives
- Claims to receive "downloads" from non-human intelligences

Taylor's connection extends further: per [[QgxjtDS2sIQ]], Taylor also visited [[charles-hall]] (the Tall White witness) for 15 years before going silent — making him a recurring validator across independent contact/experiencer clusters.
