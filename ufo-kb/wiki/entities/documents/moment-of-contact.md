# Moment of Contact

Documentary series directed by [[james-fox]] and co-produced with Brazilian UFO researcher [[marco-leal]]. The first film was released in 2022. A second film followed. Together they constitute the most thorough journalistic investigation of the [[1996-varginha]] incident.

## Summary

The films document the full arc of the Varginha case:
- The crash and its eyewitnesses (including [[carlos-de-souza]])
- The alien beings and the young women who saw them
- The hospital treatment (featuring testimony building toward [[italo-venturelli]]'s account)
- Military involvement and the chain of custody for the beings
- The death of [[marcos-charez]] and the bacterial contamination thread
- The transfer of beings to US custody (alleged)
- Anonymous military witness "Military X" who confirms events started at his farm

Fox made multiple trips to Brazil per year over 15 years. Marco Leal found and earned the trust of witnesses who had been silent since 1996.

## Key Contributions

- First public account of Carlos de Souza's crash testimony on film
- Drone footage of crash site showing anomalous soil coloration
- Animal deaths at Varginha Zoo documented through interview with zoo director
- "Military X" testimony (anonymized) placing the origin of events at a specific farm
- Documentation of crash site vegetation anomaly that persisted 26 years
- Recording of [[armando-fortunato]] and [[jean-bachista-janini]] discussing the bacterial findings
- Hospital visit showing the plaque acknowledging the ET's treatment

## After the Release

The crash site was bulldozed in 2022–2023 — shortly after the first film's release. This timing is considered suspicious by Fox and de Souza.

## Origin of Fox's Involvement

[[tim-coleman]], Fox's co-developer on "Out of the Blue" (a former BBC correspondent), suggested investigating Varginha in the late 1990s. Fox dismissed it for approximately 10 years. When Moment of Contact was released, Fox called Coleman to apologize; Coleman received a special thanks credit. Additionally, [[chase-brandon]]'s book "Crypto Conundrum" documents that Roswell beings emitted a sulfuric odor — a detail that Fox noted matches Varginha witness reports, providing unexpected cross-case corroboration.
