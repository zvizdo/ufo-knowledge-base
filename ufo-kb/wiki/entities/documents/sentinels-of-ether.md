# Sentinels of Ether

## Summary

*Sentinels of Ether* is a novel written by [[jake-barber]] under the pen name **Alva Douglas** ([[alva-douglas]] is a ghost writer collaborating with Barber). Published as ~120,000 words of fiction, the manuscript was submitted in chunks to [[dopsr]] (the Pentagon's Defense Office of Pre-publication and Security Review). Per the [[OOr98r51Sj0|Jesse Michels episode]] decoding the book, DOPSR took **six months to process just 20 pages** (a 10% sample), escalated to subject-matter experts in classified Special Access Programs, and returned **seven pages of redactions**. Barber inverted DOPSR's normal preventive use: by drip-feeding chapters and watching which lines triggered redactions, he used the system as a real-time classification map and a Trojan-horse delivery mechanism for legacy-program facts hidden in plausibly-deniable prose.

## Key Claims (per Michels' decoding)

- The central operational mechanism of the alleged UFO legacy program: recruitment of [[p3-psionic-assets|psionic assets]] from third-world refugee / disaster-displacement flows, drugged and microchipped, deployed as biological interfaces required to fly recovered NHI craft.
- A fictionalized 2004 incident outside [[coyame-mexico]]: tier-one US operator Alex Mchuan executed by a paramilitary using US-authentic verbal IFF codes (challenge: "Matchbox"); refugees loaded into a shipping container with mini-split HVAC unit "like a coffin on a hook."
- A holographic / programmable-matter mountain face that pixelates from 3D to 2D as helicopters slip through ([[utility-fog]] / [[foglets]] / [[holographic-mountain-bases]]).
- An EMP-driven aerial weapon that surgically downs drones and F-18s while leaving friend-coded helicopters untouched.
- A `JSOC`-vs-`Secretary of Defense` exchange that frames the political nightmare as **"black on black"** — domestic covert operators killing US tier-one operators with no normal chain of command.
