# Hottel Memo

## Summary

An internal FBI memo dated March 22, 1950, authored by Special Agent [[guy-hottel]] and addressed to Director J. Edgar Hoover. It forwards an account received from an Air Force investigator describing three "flying saucers" recovered in New Mexico. Each disc was approximately 50 feet in diameter; each contained a crew of three "human-like" beings approximately three feet tall, wearing metallic cloth suits. The Air Force investigator attributed the recoveries to high-powered government radar interfering with the craft's control systems.

The memo is the single most-read document in the FBI's online public Vault (the FOIA reading room), having been accessed millions of times since being released publicly.

## Provenance

The memo's provenance is indirect — Hottel relays an account from "an investigator for the Air Force," not firsthand observation. Hottel's credibility as a senior FBI field agent gives the document institutional weight even though the original source is unnamed.

[[jesse-michaels]] highlights the memo's significance in [[TH10mD9hQas]] not primarily as crash-retrieval evidence, but as evidence that [[guy-hottel]] — an FBI agent who definitively knew about UFOs at the level of crash-with-crew — was simultaneously building a dossier on [[edward-condon]]. This makes the blackmail-coercion hypothesis for Condon's later debunker role structurally coherent.

## Key Claims

- Three disc-shaped craft recovered in New Mexico (no date specified for the recovery in the memo)
- Discs approximately 50 feet in diameter
- Three humanoid crew members per craft; roughly 3 feet tall; wearing metallic cloth suits
- Suggested cause: high-powered government radar interference with control mechanism
- Forwarded to Hoover by Hottel but (per available records) not acted upon or investigated further

## Open Questions

- What was the actual incident (or incidents) this memo describes? Most researchers associate it with Roswell-era recoveries, but the memo itself is vague on timing.
- Why did J. Edgar Hoover not follow up, given the source's institutional credibility?
