# NHI Hieroglyphic Symbols

Recurring visual motifs — geometric, hieroglyphic-style symbols — reported across multiple independent UAP/NHI contexts spanning decades. Their consistency across unconnected witnesses and even across states of altered consciousness is one of the most intriguing cross-reference patterns in the UAP discourse.

## Where Observed

### 1. Crashed Craft — Blue Book Photos (1977, via Danny Sheehan)

[[danny-sheehan]] was granted access in January 1977 to classified USAF microfiche files (Blue Book materials) in the Madison Building, National Archives, at the behest of incoming Carter Administration officials. He identified a photograph of a crashed disc-shaped object embedded in a snow-covered hillside, with Air Force personnel present. Around the **rim/dome** of the craft were symbols he described as hieroglyphic in character.

Using his helipad as a transparent overlay placed under the microfiche viewer, Sheehan **traced the symbols verbatim** — approximately half the ring visible in the frame. These tracings represent the earliest documented drawing of UAP craft symbols in a verified government-document context.

### 2. NSWC Crane Gauntlet Device (March 2014, Randy Anderson)

[[randy-anderson]] observed shifting hieroglyphic symbols appearing on the crystalline display screen of the [[nswc-crane-gauntlet-device]] while looking at it in the Off-World Technology Division at [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]]. The symbols were not static — they **moved and cycled**, comparable to a countdown display. When shown documentary footage depicting symbols from the DMT diffraction experiments and Sheehan's tracings, Anderson confirmed they "look a lot like what I saw."

### 3. DMT Laser Diffraction Experiments

Researchers conducting controlled DMT sessions incorporated a laser diffraction experiment (modeled on the double-slit setup). Subjects under DMT reported visually encountering a set of hieroglyphic symbols in their visual field that corresponded to the diffraction pattern projected in the room. The documentary coverage (referenced by Jesse Michaels as "a YouTube documentary on DMT") showed these symbols — and Anderson confirmed visual similarity to the Crane device symbols. Jesse's interpretation: these symbols represent a "source code" or informational substrate underlying perceived reality — consistent with John Wheeler's "it from bit" / information-theoretic universe framework.

## Interpretive Frameworks

**Source code of reality:** If the universe is information-theoretic at its core (Wheeler, Shannon), then NHI technology may operate in the substrate layer — and certain altered states (DMT) or unusual device interfaces may surface that substrate as perceivable symbols.

**Enochian / angelic communication parallel:** Jesse draws the historical line from John Dee (16th-century spy-magician, Queen Elizabeth's advisor) who claimed communication with angels via an "Enochian language," to Aleister Crowley's similar claims, to the possibility that NHI or intra-dimensional intelligences have been communicating via a consistent symbolic system for centuries.

**Cryptographic recruitment filter:** Jesse's broader theory (applied to Bob Lazar's disclosures as well): symbols and technical puzzles are deliberate filters — those who can decode or recognize them may be selected for deeper engagement with the program.
