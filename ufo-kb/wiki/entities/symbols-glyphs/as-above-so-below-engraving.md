# As Above So Below Engraving (Skinwalker Ranch)

An old engraving bearing the Hermetic phrase **"as above so below"** found on the [[skinwalker-ranch]] property. Origin unknown; nobody knows who carved it or when.

## Context

The Hermetic maxim "as above so below" derives from the Emerald Tablet — a foundational text of Western alchemy and esoteric tradition — and means that the earthly and celestial realms mirror each other. In the ranch episode, the inscription is framed as potentially meaningful given the Mesa's properties: a buried manufactured metallic object (the "below") and recurring UAP activity directly above in "the Triangle" (the "above").

[[brandon-fugel]]'s team speculates that [[augustus-wally]] — a Buffalo Soldier decorated with the Medal of Honor who operated in the Utah region in the 19th century — may have carved the inscription. The Buffalo Soldiers were speculated by some to have had Masonic affiliations, which would make the Hermetic inscription symbolically consistent.

## Interpretations

- **Freemasonic marker**: If carved by a Masonic-affiliated Buffalo Soldier, it could be a ritual or commemorative inscription with no anomalous significance
- **Knowledge marker**: Someone aware of what was buried under the Mesa used the inscription as a deliberate indicator — "what is below is mirrored by what is above"
- **Coincidental Hermeticism**: The inscription predates modern awareness of the ranch's anomalous properties and has no intended connection to them
- **Platonic metaphysics**: As framed by Jesse Michaels — the inscription may indicate that the earth and its materials are a "low-level instantiation or reflection of a more platonic realm"
