# Bryce DeWitt

Theoretical physicist best known for the Wheeler-DeWitt equation (quantum gravity / wave function of the universe). A key participant in the 1957 gravity research conference at [[unc-chapel-hill]] organized by [[agnew-bahnson]].

## Connection to Anti-Gravity Research

The 1957 conference was not a purely academic gathering: it was sponsored by Wright Air Development Center (see [[wright-patterson]]) and attended by [[lewis-whitten]], the [[martin-corporation]] representative from their anti-gravity division ([[rias]]). DeWitt himself nearly joined Martin Corporation's anti-gravity division — a fact that illustrates how porous the boundary was between academic gravity research and aerospace anti-gravity programs in the late 1950s.

[[jesse-michaels]] presents DeWitt's near-hire at RIAS as evidence of the deliberate industrial-academic nexus around anti-gravity: the same aerospace firms building saucer-shaped aircraft ([[avrocar-project]]) were recruiting the physicists trying to understand gravity theoretically.
