# Barry Michaels

Therapist, co-developer (with [[phil-stutz]]) of *The Tools* — a Jungian-archetypal visualization framework for everyday psychological challenges — and Jesse Michaels's father. Co-authored the NYT bestselling book *The Tools* and follow-up *Coming Alive*. Has worked with CEOs, actors, and directors, some described as "on the verge of self-destruction" before his involvement. Referenced in a *New Yorker* profile ("Hollywood Shadows").

## Connection to Domain

Michaels has no direct UAP/UFO research involvement. He is relevant to this KB as Jesse's primary intellectual influence and as someone who explicitly endorses [[graham-hancock]]'s alternative-history framework:

When Jesse asks about Hancock's thesis that humanity goes back much further than mainstream archaeology accepts, Barry replies: "I was just a blind follower of Rudolph Steiner — that's what he says." He notes archaeology consistently underestimates the age of civilization: "every 30 years they say the Earth is older than what they thought. They never say 'hey, isn't this weird, we underestimate it every fucking time.'"

This connects the Steiner / Hancock / ancient-history thread into Jesse's family background.
