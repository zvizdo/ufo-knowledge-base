# Albert Stein

> ⚠ **Name conflict**: In episode [[C4rSj5Aum7w]], [[danny-sheehan]] tells the same story (Army clerk typist, hospice in northern Minnesota, Project Blue Book, S4 visit, ET communication through two-way mirror, ET's "God but very different" statement) but names the witness **"Oscar Wolf"** (with brief uncertainty: "Oscar Wolf or Oscar Lane"). All identifying details match. A separate page exists at [[oscar-wolf]]. The discrepancy has not been reconciled. [[jeremy-corbell]] and [[richard-dolan]] reportedly have independent interviews with this person that may clarify the name.

U.S. Army clerk typist assigned to [[project-blue-book]] who gave [[danny-sheehan]] a deathbed account of being taken to [[s4]] (a facility approximately 11 miles from [[area-51]]) and witnessing his commanding officer conduct a telepathic communication session with an extraterrestrial being.

## Background

Very little biographical detail is available. Stein held a clerical role in Project Blue Book, which gave him access to UFO-related files. Sheehan flew to a hospice in northern Minnesota to hear his account. No recording was made.

## Notable Claims

Stein's deathbed account (relayed by Sheehan — **hearsay**):

- He was transported to S4 at an unspecified date during his Blue Book assignment.
- His commanding officer conducted what appeared to be a telepathic two-way communication with an entity described as extraterrestrial.
- The entity said it was part of a **multi-star-system monitoring team** — not a single-planet delegation — tasked with observing civilizational development across multiple star systems.
- When asked about the coordinating principle of this galactic effort, the entity said: *"You people would refer to it as God, but it is very different from what you think."*
- The entity reportedly indicated that humanity's current developmental trajectory was being monitored as part of this broader interstellar survey.

## Epistemic Status

**Hearsay / deathbed account.** Sheehan explicitly acknowledges no recording exists and the claim cannot be independently verified. Stein is now deceased. The account is consistent with other S4 accounts (notably [[bob-lazar]]) but adds a theological dimension not present in Lazar's technical testimony.
