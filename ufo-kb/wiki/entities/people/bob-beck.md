# Bob Beck

## Background

Bob Beck was a CIA-connected researcher who, in the late 1970s, came to [[andrija-puharich]]'s house on behalf of the CIA to recruit Puharich to head a new ELF (Extremely Low Frequency) weapons research department. Puharich refused, an argument ensued, and he told Beck to leave.

[[greg-malazzi]] describes Beck as "another Puharich type where he got roped into a lot of things I think he wasn't necessarily wanting to do" — suggesting Beck himself may have been an unwilling participant in intelligence operations rather than a true believer in the CIA's agenda.

Beck appears in the *Mind Traveler* documentary.

## Significance

Beck's visit to Puharich represents the CIA's attempt to formally bring Puharich into ELF weapons research at the very end of his active career. Puharich's refusal — shortly before his death in 1995 — may have made him a liability to the program.
