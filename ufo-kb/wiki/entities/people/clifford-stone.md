# Clifford Stone

Clifford Stone is a retired US Army Sergeant who served in the military from 1969 to 1989 and has testified publicly, including at the [[2001-disclosure-project]] National Press Club event, that he was part of a classified military unit tasked with responding to UAP crash sites to recover materials and, on some occasions, non-human biological entities.

**57 species claim**: Stone's most frequently cited claim is that the US government has catalogued 57 different extraterrestrial species with which it has had contact. He claims he served as a "human interface" — an individual whose unusual psychological profile allowed him to interact with non-human beings without extreme trauma responses.

**Crash retrieval testimony**: Stone has described arriving at crash sites where non-human beings were present — some deceased, some alive. He has spoken about the ethical dimensions of these encounters, including that he attempted to help surviving beings and that this created conflicts with his military superiors.

**Roswell connection**: Stone lived in Roswell, New Mexico, and conducted his own research into the 1947 Roswell incident, including interviewing local witnesses before many had spoken publicly.

**Credibility assessment**: Stone is one of the more colorful and contested witnesses in the disclosure community. His claims about specific encounter details — the number of species, the nature of his "human interface" role — go beyond what most other whistleblowers have claimed and cannot be verified through documentary evidence in the KB. His military service is documented; the specific classified unit activities he describes are not.

**KB context**: Stone appears in the KB primarily through the [[2001-disclosure-project]] as one of Steven Greer's witnesses — his testimony is part of the broader disclosure movement's evidentiary base, which ranges from credible (AATIP-adjacent) to contested (species-count claims).
