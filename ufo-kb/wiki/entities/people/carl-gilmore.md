# Carl Gilmore

Canadian-born astrophysicist at Caltech and JPL whose career spanned galactic structure mapping, dark matter research, biosignature detection in exoplanet atmospheres, and planetary defense. He was building his own private observatory in the remote Analopee Valley, east of Palmdale, California, and had recently joined the [[vera-rubin-observatory]] project. He was shot and killed at his property on February 16, 2026.

## Background

Born 1959 in Calgary, Alberta. A polymath within astrophysics — research interests spanned every scale from solar system bodies to galaxy clusters:

**Stellar streams and dark matter:**
- Discovered the **Leahy Stream** — a vast river of stars pulled into the Milky Way from nearby globular clusters
- Used the Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS) to track subtle disturbances in stellar stream paths caused by dark matter gravitational tugging
- Key insights into the **dark matter halo** enveloping the Milky Way — dark matter that constitutes most of the universe's mass (ordinary matter is only ~5%)

**Exoplanet atmosphere detection:**
- Using the Spitzer Space Telescope, pioneered techniques for detecting atmospheric molecular fingerprints in exoplanet transits
- **2007** — part of the team that detected the **first water vapor ever found in an exoplanet's atmosphere**
- His techniques are now deployed to probe habitable exoplanet atmospheres for **biosignatures** — chemical footprints of living ecologies

**Planetary defense:**
- Worked with NEOWISE — the planetary defense instrument scanning for near-Earth objects; literal first-line defense against extinction-scale asteroid impacts

**NASA honors:** NASA Exceptional Scientific Achievement Medal, 2011

**Final project:** Working on the [[vera-rubin-observatory]], specifically testing new instrumentation at Caltech's Palomar Observatory to monitor for meteor impacts on the Moon's surface during a lunar eclipse.

## His Private Observatory

Gilmore built a personal observatory on a remote, sprawling lot in **Lano** (unincorporated area near the Los Angeles/San Bernardino County line, ~20 miles east of Palmdale). He chose the Analopee Valley specifically for its dark skies and low light pollution. The remoteness of the property would become relevant to the circumstances of his death.

## Murder

**Date:** February 16, 2026  
**Location:** His private property in Lano, California

**Background to the killing:**
- December 20, 2025 — Gilmore called police to report a trespasser: 29-year-old Lano resident **Freddy Snyder**, found wandering his property with a loaded, unregistered rifle. Snyder claimed he was headed to the post office; the post office was in the opposite direction. He was arrested on a felony weapons charge but released on his own recognizance after a judge cited his clean record.
- February 16, 2026 — At 6:10 AM, deputies responded to a 911 call for assault with a deadly weapon. Gilmore was found shot on his porch. Paramedics pronounced him dead.
- A simultaneous carjacking call came in from nearby. Snyder was arrested for the carjacking and subsequently charged with Gilmore's murder. Bail: ~$3 million.

**Arraignment** was postponed from March 26 to April 29, 2026. No confirmed motive. No evidence that Snyder and Gilmore knew each other.

**Open structural questions:**
- Why did a man with no criminal record suddenly conduct surveillance, get arrested and released, then return to kill the scientist within two months?
- Was Snyder acting independently, or is there a third-party connection investigators haven't disclosed?
- Gilmore had been working with Vera Rubin Observatory imagery, which is Pentagon-filtered before reaching scientists. Did he see something in that data he wasn't supposed to?

## The Vera Rubin Observatory Connection

The [[vera-rubin-observatory]] is one of the largest all-sky surveys ever built. It produces 1,000+ images per night covering the entire southern hemisphere. Every image is reviewed by intelligence agencies before the scientific community receives it — to filter spy satellites and other classified orbital assets. (The same redaction practice occurred with PanSTARRS, the smaller all-sky survey that discovered 'Oumuamua.)

[[beatriz-villarroel]] found tens of thousands of transient mirror-like objects in pre-Sputnik astronomical plates from Palomar Observatory — the same observatory where Gilmore worked on his final project. If the Vera Rubin Observatory were to detect similar anomalous objects, those images would pass through the Pentagon filter before any scientist could act on them.

The question the source poses: did Gilmore see something in early Vera Rubin data that was classified — and could that be a motive for his silencing? This is framed explicitly as speculation, with no direct evidence.
