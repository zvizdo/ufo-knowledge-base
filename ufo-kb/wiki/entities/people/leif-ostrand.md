# Leif Östrand

Volunteer digitizer at the **[[archives-for-the-unexplained]] (AFU)** in Norrköping, Sweden — described by AFU founder [[claus-svahn]] as "a real hero." Östrand is, as of the [[Rjr3Yq-dMUE]] episode, the **sole individual** responsible for digitizing the AFU's holdings, which Svahn characterizes as having him "sitting under an avalanche."

**The digitization task**: The AFU occupies a 16-room building and holds over 22,000 formal Swedish UFO case files, alongside photographs, audio recordings, radar data, video footage, and original documents that exist nowhere else. Many of these first-generation materials — original witness statements, handwritten reports, physical film negatives — have no duplicate. Without digitization, they are at risk of physical deterioration and institutional loss. Östrand is performing this preservation work without institutional salary; the AFU is seeking external funding to hire additional help.

**Significance for UAP research**: The AFU's holdings include records of the [[1946-swedish-ghost-rockets]] flap, decades of Hessdalen research, materials from multiple national archives donated over 50+ years, and the library of the [[borderland-science-research-association]] (reputedly the world's oldest UFO organization). Digitizing this corpus would make it searchable and cross-referenceable against the global UFO research record for the first time. Östrand's single-volunteer effort is the bottleneck between this primary-source archive and modern research accessibility.
