# Jake Sullivan

US National Security Advisor (2021–2025) under President Biden. Sullivan previously served as national security advisor to Vice President Biden (2013–2014) and as Director of Policy Planning at the State Department. A senior foreign policy strategist focused primarily on Ukraine, China, and Iran.

**UAP briefing**: [[ross-coulthart]] reports (via [[LpLFWdsIU7M]]) that Sullivan attended at least one Senate UAP briefing in which firsthand witness testimony was presented. According to this account, Sullivan's response was effectively to treat UAP as a lower-priority concern relative to Ukraine, China, and the Biden administration's capacity constraints — effectively shelving any executive action on UAP disclosure for the next administration.

**Significance**: If accurate, the claim that a sitting National Security Advisor attended UAP briefings with firsthand witnesses and chose not to act represents the highest-level documented instance of executive branch awareness of the UAP testimony corpus. It would imply the disclosure decision is not merely a matter of classified programs being unknown to executive leadership but a deliberate choice to defer.

**Epistemics**: The account comes from Coulthart's reporting based on sources; it is not confirmed by official records. Sullivan did not publicly comment on UAP matters during his tenure.
