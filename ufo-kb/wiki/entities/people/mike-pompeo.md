# Mike Pompeo

**Mike Pompeo** served as CIA Director (2017–2018) and US Secretary of State (2018–2021) under President Trump. He is a West Point graduate and former US Representative.

## UAP Statements

Pompeo has claimed to have "seen the UAP files" during his time as CIA Director — a remark cited in discussions of official awareness of UAP evidence at senior levels of the intelligence community. He is also mentioned in the context of JFK files release discussions.
