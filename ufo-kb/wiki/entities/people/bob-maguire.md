# Bob Maguire

## Background

Applied mathematician and engineer with roughly 30 years of work for NSA, CIA, NRO, and affiliated agencies (Institute for Defense Analysis). His specialty was nonlinear filtering (PhD, Applied Mathematics, Brown University under Leon Cooper), signals processing, and embedded systems — capabilities the intelligence community routinely needed for novel collection problems. He describes his role as developing something an agency needed, implementing it in the field, then moving on to the next problem.

He interacted directly with John Wheeler at a Princeton retirement village, where Wheeler's wife was a nurse; Bob became a regular visitor and occasional chauffeur to IAS talks. Freeman Dyson sat on the IDA Advisory Board during Bob's tenure there. He attended church with Dyson. Carl Sagan held a contractor green badge and appeared in a classified NSA meeting Bob attended.

After leaving daily intelligence work, Bob co-founded [[hawkeye-360]] (satellite RF geolocation) and [[federated-wireless]], and became a founding member of the [[hume-center]] for National Security and Technology at Virginia Tech — funded initially by CIA science/tech officer Ted Hugh.

He attended the SCUAP 2022 Huntsville meeting, where Space Force and three-letter agency personnel asked him about applying Hawkeye's capabilities to detecting UAP RF emissions on the ground (he no longer held clearance so directed them to Hawkeye staff). His working hypothesis for UAP detection frequency: **1.6 GHz**, which he is "certain" Hawkeye has searched.

## Missing Time Event

Before his government career: as a college student, driving his girlfriend home, Bob and she experienced approximately 1.5 hours of missing time — a classic absence of memory during a car journey. He had no context for what happened; the event changed him, causing him to leave pre-med and become a mathematician. He has undergone hypnotic regression but not specifically for that episode.
