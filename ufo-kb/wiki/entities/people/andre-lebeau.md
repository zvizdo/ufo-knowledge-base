# André Lebeau

Former President of [[cnes|CNES]] (Centre National d'Études Spatiales), the French national space agency. Lebeau wrote the foreword to the 1999 [[cometa-report|COMETA Report]], situating the UAP question within the broader context of humanity's expanding presence in space and the scientific credibility of the extraterrestrial hypothesis.

Author of *L'espace en héritage* (Odile Jacob, 1986), cited in COMETA's space-colonization appendix alongside Jean-Claude Ribes and Guy Monnet's framework for interstellar migration using O'Neill-type asteroid habitats and antimatter propulsion.
