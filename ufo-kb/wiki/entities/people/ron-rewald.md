# Ron Rewald

CIA front operative who ran a cover investment firm in Honolulu, Hawaii designed to provide financial and operational cover for CIA activities across Asia. His case gave [[danny-sheehan]] an unusual window into classified CIA operations — including a judge-granted Q clearance on the spot during testimony proceedings.

## The Hawaii Investment Firm

Rewald operated a Honolulu-based investment firm guaranteeing extraordinary returns as a cover for CIA operations across the Pacific and Asia. His only six investors were: the admiral of the Sixth Fleet, the CIA station chief in Hawaii, a major general, an FBI officer, and two other senior officials. Their participation constituted both the firm's operational legitimacy and CIA cover for their activities.

Rewald placed a newspaper advertisement guaranteeing 100% annual returns — an obvious fraud signal that triggered arrest by the bunko (fraud) squad. The CIA immediately disavowed him entirely, leaving him exposed.

## Sheehan's Case

Sheehan had Rewald transferred to the **Eastern District of Virginia** — the court used for CIA-adjacent cases — to testify in a separate case (the Mormon priest-penitent privilege violation case). The Department of Justice opposed Rewald's testimony via national security claims. The judge granted Sheehan a **Q clearance on the spot** to allow him to review classified documents in closed session to evaluate their testimony relevance.

Documents uncovered in the process included:
- **Japanese bullet train blueprints** — suggesting CIA industrial espionage operations
- Records of CIA **POW-search operations in Laos and Cambodia** — connected to [[bo-gritz]]
