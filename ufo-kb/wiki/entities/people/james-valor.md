# James Valor

## Background

James Valor is a retired U.S. Army Sergeant Major with 23 years in Special Operations — airborne, military freefall, global deployments. He retired in 2020. In 2021 he was running a government-contracted war game exercise (blue vs. red team air defense/counter-drone) when a camera operator on his team spotted an anomalous Class 1 formation of seven objects at 7 km altitude. That accidental discovery became the founding event of [[sky-watchers]] (publicly branded as "Skywatcher").

He serves as Lead Operations Officer, handling the technical detection platform and operational planning. [[jake-barber]] serves as helicopter pilot and co-founder/partner, bringing the psionic and retrieval background; Valor brings the military operations and sensor systems expertise.

## The 2021 Discovery

Two weeks into a government war game exercise, Valor's team was scanning from a rooftop when camera operator Mr. Ko spotted the Class 1 formation — seven objects in tight formation maneuvering 2-3,000 feet overhead at 7 km distance. No human sense had detected them; only camera captured them.

Initial assumption: classified U.S. government technology. Valor maintained operational security for two additional weeks before concluding the phenomenon was not from a known domestic program. This event set the trajectory of the next five years.

## Technology Platform Built

From first principles, Valor assembled a multi-spectrum detection system:

- **Radar** — primary detection; discovered Class 7 jellyfish causes sustained jamming
- **Electro-optical (EO)** — standard cameras; cannot detect UAPs emitting only in infrared
- **Shortwave infrared (SWIR)** — captures UAP emissions beyond visible spectrum
- **Medium-wave infrared (MWIR)** — second IR band
- **RF signal detection + direction-finding** — geolocate UAP signals in real time
- **Helicopter interception** — [[jake-barber]] pilots the pursuit aircraft

The dog-whistle signal (developed by anonymous team member Mr. K) generates the electromagnetic frequency that attracts UAPs to the operational area. A January 2025 control test (equipment deployed *without* dog whistle) returned zero detections, supporting the causal claim.

## UAP Classification System

Valor describes nine classes observed over five years. Key classes:

- **Class 1**: Formation fliers; most numerous; ingress from vertical (space) direction
- **Class 2 (Tic Tac)**: Classic oblong; uses terrain to hide; intent-reads pursuit attempts
- **Class 6**: Bright star; demonstrated pre-emptive energy weapon use in 2003
- **Class 7 (Jellyfish)**: Gold chrome mirror surface; generates sustained microwave EM interference; visible to naked eye
- **Class 8 (Hornet)**: Large; four descent/crash events 2023-2024; no wreckage recovered
- **Class 9 (Egg)**: [[jake-barber]]'s primary target; retrieved in mid-2000s; goal is peaceful public landing

## Notable Operational Events

### 2003 Energy Weapon Incident
During a government event, Valor's team aimed a microwave energy weapon antenna at an approaching Class 6. Before completing the antenna turn, the Class 6 activated what appeared to be a directed energy weapon — simultaneously disabling three separate, unconnected computer systems. Interpretation: the craft detected the *intent* to target it before execution. Never repeated; Valor notes it is now commercially illegal.

### 2023 200-UAP Sortie Event
During a government special event, approximately 200 UAPs (primarily Class 1) were observed in a 4-hour window ingressing from directly overhead — vertical descent from space — and egressing the same way. Three Class 1s deviated and descended below ridgelines and did not resurface. Searches one week later found nothing. A large rainstorm followed the event and continued for two days.

### February 2025 "Conflict in the Sky"
Jordan (psionic lead) predicted the event; it arrived exactly as predicted. Multi-class engagement:
- First naked-eye UAP sighting in five years for Valor: Class 7 hovering ~800m overhead for ~5 min
- Class 7s had been probing radar parameters for 4-5 preceding days — systematic capability mapping
- Class 2 (Tic Tac) entered through only unmonitored corridor; departed the instant Valor began running to helicopter
- Two Class 7s flanked team symmetrically at 6 km north and south
- Helicopter immobilized by unknown field — engines and rotors active, zero translational movement
- Class 7s jammed radar for 2+ hours during the engagement

## Soft Disclosure Thesis

Valor's governing thesis about government behavior: the US government has held advanced physics knowledge since at least WWII but cannot publicly release it without revealing classified aerospace programs. The resolution is to allow private civilian companies to independently observe and analyze UAP phenomena, achieving "soft disclosure" — the science enters the public domain through civilian channels while the government maintains plausible deniability. Supporting evidence: Skywatcher has operated under government contracts without receiving any NDAs or cessation orders despite operating in sensitive airspace.
