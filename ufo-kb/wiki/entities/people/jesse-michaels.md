# Jesse Michaels

Host of *American Alchemy*, a YouTube channel and podcast focused on UFO/UAP research with particular interest in nuclear connections, disclosure politics, and deep-state secrecy. Background includes investment/venture work. He has been covering [[robert-salas]]'s Oscar Flight case for "two or three years" before this in-person interview.

## Background

Michaels appears to have transitioned from venture investing to full-time UFO media work. He references having spoken with a former US presidential cabinet member who submitted (and had rejected) an op-ed to the *Wall Street Journal* about lack of civilian oversight of UAP programs. He attended the San Marino UFO conference in Italy.

## Near-Death Experience (Firsthand)

In [[I9VyyV4-3Js]], Jesse discloses for the first time a near-death experience at age 5: his older brother accidentally hit him in the head with a golf club. He was rushed to hospital, bleeding, yet felt **no pain — only bliss and enormous comfort** even as his mother wept. During emergency surgery as anesthesia began, he felt he "left his body" and had vivid visionary encounters where he "met people and discussed things and was told things." He woke with the imagery intact but no framework for understanding it. Only 10–20 years later, reading NDE and mystical literature, did he recognize what had happened. See [[near-death-experience]].

Muraresku responds: "Maybe you had your own mystery ritual." Jesse: "Maybe so."

His openness to fringe topics traces directly to his upbringing: his godfather is psychotherapist [[phil-stutz]] (subject of Jonah Hill's Netflix documentary *Stutz*), a devotee of [[rudolph-steiner]]'s esoteric anthroposophy; his father is [[barry-michaels]], co-developer with Stutz of *The Tools* (Jungian shadow-work framework). Both Barry and Phil encouraged rigorous self-examination rooted in archetypal and esoteric traditions. Jesse credits them explicitly as the childhood influences who "opened me up to the prospect that life is a little more trippy than we realize." He also considered working on a "more official UFO disclosure documentary project" before choosing to continue American Alchemy independently.

Jesse models reality via retrocausality and quantum indeterminacy: the present is a "collapsing function" between probable futures and the past; sub-atomic time-breakdown enables entanglement via retrocausality. He cites [[john-archibald-wheeler]]'s delayed-choice experiment as the anchor for this view. See [[AVJEXCTAJUc]] for the full discussion.

## Notable Claims

No independent firsthand UFO claims in this episode. His commentary frames the UFO-nuclear pattern as "empirically grounded" relative to other areas of UFO discourse, and he articulates the hypothesis that explicit disclosure might occur during a nuclear brinkmanship crisis.
