# Mike Rounds

US Senator from South Dakota (R); former Governor of South Dakota (2003–2011); member of the Senate Armed Services Committee and Senate Banking Committee. In UAP discourse, Rounds is the most significant Republican Senate legislator on the disclosure question — his co-authorship of the UAP Disclosure Act with Democratic Senate Majority Leader [[chuck-schumer]] made it the most high-profile bipartisan legislative push for transparency.

**UAP Disclosure Act of 2023**: Rounds and Schumer co-authored the Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Disclosure Act of 2023, modeled on the JFK Records Act and designed to require mandatory declassification and public release of all government UAP records. The bill would have created an independent review board with authority to compel declassification, similar to the Assassination Records Review Board that processed JFK files. The Schumer-Rounds bill passed the Senate in the NDAA but was substantially weakened in conference committee — the mandatory review board was stripped, and the declassification provisions were made advisory rather than binding. Rounds and Schumer publicly expressed disappointment with the weakened version.

**Bipartisan significance**: Rounds's co-sponsorship with Schumer — a conservative Republican and the most senior Democrat in the Senate — was significant as a signal that UAP transparency had moved beyond the defense-nerd/conspiracy fringe to mainstream legislative priority. When the most powerful Democrat in Congress and a conservative Republican Senate Armed Services Committee member jointly push legislation, it indicates genuine institutional momentum. [[danny-sheehan]] cites Rounds specifically as evidence that UAP transparency enjoys authentic cross-party Senate support.

**Conference committee defeat**: The bill's weakening in conference committee is attributed to resistance from the DoD and intelligence community — a pattern the KB documents under [[ufo-secrecy]]: the executive branch using conference negotiation to gut congressional oversight mechanisms before they become law.
