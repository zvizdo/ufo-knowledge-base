# Howard Menzel

Harvard astrophysicist (1901–1976) and director of the Harvard College Observatory. Publicly one of the most prominent and aggressive UFO debunkers of the 1950s–1970s. Paradoxically, he appears on several alleged Majestic-12 documents as a member or associate of the MJ-12 committee — which, if authentic, would make him a coordinated debunker playing a deliberate public role while secretly inside the program.

## Public Role

Menzel wrote three major books debunking UFOs: *Flying Saucers* (1953), *The World of Flying Saucers* (1963, with Lyle Boyd), and *The UFO Enigma* (1977, with Ernest Taves). His scientific authority as a Harvard astronomer gave his skepticism particular cultural weight during the peak flying saucer era.

## MJ-12 Alleged Membership

The SOM1-01 and other alleged Majestic-12 documents list Menzel as a member. His Naval Intelligence background (wartime cryptography work) provides a credible mechanism for IC ties. If accurate, his public debunking was an information operation — a coordinated effort to suppress UAP belief while remaining inside the actual investigation.
