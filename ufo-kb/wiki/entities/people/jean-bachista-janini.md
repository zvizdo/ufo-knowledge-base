# Jean Bachista Janini

Pathologist and forensic doctor; arrived in Varginha from Rio de Janeiro approximately 45–50 years ago. He established a diagnostic and laboratory service (proprietary institute) that serves approximately 90% of public health patients across more than 100 cities in the Varginha region. A university professor and a reference in Brazilian forensic medicine, regarded by colleagues (including [[armando-fortunato]]) as a trusted scientific authority. Now nearly 90 years old.

## Role in the 1996 Case

Janini's laboratory received the tissue samples (blood, liver, stomach, lung fragments) from [[armando-fortunato]]'s autopsy of [[marcos-charez]]. He issued a standard report: death by pulmonary infection leading to generalized septicemia from a highly virulent bacterium.

At the time, Janini had no knowledge of the alien contact context — the autopsy request framed the case as a possible malpractice investigation.

## 2024–2025 Reassessment

After the Varginha case became more publicly documented (via James Fox's documentaries and Marco Leal's investigations), Janini reassessed his position in light of:
1. Charez's sister's testimony that he had handled the alien being
2. The [[varginha-zoo]] animal deaths the same week
3. The unprecedented virulence of the bacteria in an otherwise healthy young soldier

Janini now states — in a signed, authenticated document submitted to this interview ([[UkKwa4jU0fc]]) — that he does not rule out the bacteria being alien or genetically engineered. He describes the bacterial behavior as something he had never seen before or since in decades of practice.

He is actively requesting court authorization for exhumation of Charez's remains and new DNA/bacterial analysis.

## The Bacteria in Detail

- Staphylococcal strain: exists on Earth but extremely rare
- Virulence: exceptionally high; death in 12–14 days despite aggressive antibiotic treatment
- Progression: atypical — the military police doctor who performed the original drainage was fully acquitted of malpractice; the disease course was determined to be outside normal clinical parameters
- Janini's framing: an "evolution of a bacteria on Earth that he'd never seen before" — suggesting possible modification or an extra-terrestrial variant

[[hal-puthoff]] was present at the recording and engaged with [[armando-fortunato]] about the possibility of American scientific institutions supporting the investigation.
