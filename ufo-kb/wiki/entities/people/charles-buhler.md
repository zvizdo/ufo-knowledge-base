# Charles Buhler

NASA physicist specializing in electrostatics, granular physics, and surface charging. Buhler spent years at NASA Kennedy Space Center working on electrostatic dust mitigation (relevant to lunar/Mars surface operations) before co-founding Exodus Propulsion Technologies, a company focused on electrostatic propulsion systems.

## Background

Buhler's mainstream NASA work covers electrostatic behavior in dusty plasma environments — how charged particles behave in space and planetary surface conditions. This work gave him a deep technical foundation in high-voltage electrostatics and dielectric materials, which he later applied to questions about asymmetric capacitor thrust.

## Vacuum Thrust Research

The most significant result attributed to Buhler in the UAP/electrogravitics community concerns an experimental finding (c. 2022–2023, published through Exodus Propulsion Technologies and cited in preprints) that asymmetric capacitor systems — the type studied by [[thomas-townsend-brown]] in the 1920s–1950s — produce measurable thrust even when placed in a hard vacuum, where no air is present to create ionic wind.

This is directly significant because the dominant mainstream explanation for the [[biefeld-brown-effect]] has always been **ionic wind**: high-voltage electrodes strip electrons from air molecules, creating a directional ion flow that produces apparent thrust. If the thrust persists in vacuum, the ionic wind explanation is insufficient, and a genuine electrostatic or electrogravitic coupling must be considered.

[[gary-mckinnon]] cites Buhler as one of the researchers who has validated the [[biefeld-brown-effect]] in conditions that rule out the ionic wind dismissal. McKinnon is specifically building his own CCTO (calcium copper titanate) capacitor apparatus to attempt replication.

## Significance for UAP Research

If Buhler's vacuum thrust finding holds under independent replication, it represents the most important experimental development in electrogravitic research since Brown's original work. It would:

1. Confirm that the phenomenon Brown observed is not an atmospheric artifact
2. Open the question of mechanism — if not ionic wind, then what?
3. Make the exotic dielectric materials (barium, strontium) relevant to the [[non-terrestrial-officers]] spreadsheet — those same materials optimize the [[biefeld-brown-effect]]

The connective chain that [[gary-mckinnon]] and [[jesse-michaels]] construct in [[2ttdlCa5ZCI]] runs directly through Buhler's vacuum finding.

## Detailed Experimental Results (d9tdJ2SkBKQ)

Buhler appeared as a surprise guest on American Alchemy to confront [[bob-lazar]], testing whether Lazar's S4 propulsion descriptions are physically coherent against current experimental electrostatics research.

Apparatus: 6×6-inch asymmetric capacitor plates at ~400 V inside a vacuum chamber at 10⁻⁶ torr. Key findings:
- **~1.5 millinewtons** thrust — small but repeatable and instrument-confirmed. Ion-wind explanation ruled out at this vacuum level.
- **Direction paradox**: Thrust acts opposite to the direction of ionic wind. Unexplained by conventional electrostatics.
- **Charge persistence**: Thrust continues after power is disconnected when charge is trapped. The field, not current, produces the effect.
- **2,000+ experiments since 2016** with consistent results; contributed two new foundational principles to electrostatics.

**Connection to Lazar/S4**: Lazar and Vendetti believe the sport model hull is an electret (permanent electric field). Buhler's stored-charge thrust experiments confirm that permanently charged dielectric materials can produce measurable thrust — the same mechanism.

## Electrostatics Discoveries

Buhler has contributed two foundational principles to electrostatics beyond the anomalous force work:

1. **No brush discharges in high vacuum**: It was not generally accepted that insulator charge-stripping could not produce brush discharges under vacuum; Buhler was first to demonstrate this in the literature — discharges in air are a gas-breakdown effect, not present in true vacuum.
2. **Particle dynamics, not size, determine charge polarity**: In a vat of same-size particles, those that "roll" (partake in more collisions) become positive; those that remain stationary become negative. This contradicts the prevailing size-based model for tribocharging. Practical implications: electrostatic beneficiation (separating plastics by charge), understanding lightning and volcanic lightning, and Moon/Mars tribochemistry programs.

## Career Background

PhD in theoretical condensed matter physics (Florida State University / National High Magnetic Field Laboratory). Joined NASA Kennedy Space Center as a postdoc under Dr. Carlos Kay (KSC electrostatics lab founder) after graduate school; became lead scientist of the electrostatics and surface physics laboratory (Swamp Works, KSC). The lab was founded ~26 years prior to this interview, motivated by electrostatic accidents including the 1960s spin-test-facility incident (11 people trapped, solid rocket accidentally fired, casualties) and the Apollo fire.

NASA KSC is the only dedicated electrostatics laboratory in all of NASA; Buhler is its lead scientist — making him NASA's de facto lead electrostatics expert. Important disclaimer: **Exodus Propulsion Technologies work is entirely separate from NASA; NASA has not endorsed or replicated the Exodus force experiments.**

## First Observation of the Force (2010)

Buhler's first observation of anomalous thrust was in 2010, in air, in his laboratory — not in vacuum. He set up an experiment and had his then-future brother-in-law Nathan run it. A laser displacement monitor showed unexpected movement. His skeptical colleague and mentor **Dr. Sid Clemens** — a world-class electrostatics expert — witnessed the result and immediately abandoned his other work to examine the experiment. Clemens was converted from skeptic to "there's something there" by direct observation.

## Origin (Childhood Sighting)

Buhler's career direction was shaped by witnessing the **[[1985-brewster-ny-ufo-flap]]** at age 11–12. Six silent white lights in formation, hovering at treetop level, returning for several nights, covered in local newspapers. He was working a Halloween haunted house in Brewster, NY when the lights appeared. The experience motivated his scientific interest in the unexplained and eventually led him toward exotic propulsion.

A second, closer sighting occurred approximately 2013 near [[patrick-air-force-base]] (Cocoa Beach, FL): a red beacon light ~3 miles out in the ocean grew extremely bright (blinding, lit up the whole beach), was investigated by an Air Force helicopter that returned without action, then split into 6–7 rotating orange-pink lights that went in and out of the water and followed Buhler and wife Janessa to within ~50 yards before going dark. Buhler notes [[steven-greer]] brings groups to the same beach for sightings; similar events reported by others.

Buhler and wife Janessa (also at NASA, Launch Services Program, strong math background) were approached by NASA's second UAP investigation for participation. Buhler accepted but found **no other physicists** in the group — only instruments specialists. He was "shocked" and declined to join. He sees this as a potential data point that the UAP physics is either already classified or considered too "consciousness-based" for conventional physics to address.
