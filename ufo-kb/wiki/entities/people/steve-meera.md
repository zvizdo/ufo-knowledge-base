# Steve Meera

UFO researcher connected to the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] investigation. Meera is cited for commissioning or reporting on a DNA analysis of Maria's finger bones conducted at a **Sri Lanka laboratory** — the result of which complicates the Nazca mummy evidentiary picture.

**The anomalous DNA result**: Meera's Sri Lanka lab report returned **one male DNA result and one female DNA result from two different finger bones** of the same "Maria" specimen. This is problematic: if Maria is a single organism, she should produce consistent sex-chromosome results from all her tissues. The discrepancy has two interpretations:
1. **Contamination**: Handling, storage, or lab processing introduced foreign DNA from a male individual into one of the samples.
2. **Composite construction**: The "Maria" specimen is not a single organism but was assembled from parts of different individuals — possibly a deliberate construction rather than a natural being.

**Evidential significance**: This result is listed as an open question in the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] and [[nazca-mummies-real-claim]] pages. It does not by itself resolve the question of the mummies' nature — contamination is common in forensic DNA work on decomposed specimens — but it adds a data point that advocates of the "constructed specimen" hypothesis cite. [[david-ranel]]'s DNA analysis (showing 70% known, 30% unknown) came from a different lab and predates or parallels Meera's work.
