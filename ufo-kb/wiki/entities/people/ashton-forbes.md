# Ashton Forbes

Independent UAP researcher who rose to prominence through his investigation of the 2014 disappearance of Malaysian Airlines Flight MH370. Forbes is notable for advancing the hypothesis that the plane was teleported by UAP orbs — based on what he describes as video evidence — and subsequently developed a broader interest in exotic physics and free energy research.

## MH370 Investigation

MH370 disappeared on March 8, 2014. The plane went missing after deviating from its flight path; despite a large international search, no definitive wreckage was found for years, and questions remain about the cause. Forbes's hypothesis:

- Video footage shows orbs surrounding and apparently teleporting the aircraft
- CNN and mainstream media covered the disappearance intensively for approximately three months
- Forbes interprets the event as evidence of UAP technology capable of spatial teleportation

**Pais endorsement**: In [[8TYMQOUDQBo]], [[salvatore-pais]] says that when Forbes first showed him the MH370 orb videos (at Forbes's "Hard Truths Podcast"), Pais immediately recognized the [[pais-effect]] in action: "I saw the Pais effect, man. This is the Pais effect. I'm gone." [[dave-rossi]] added the fourth orb hypothesis — three orbs define the three axes of the origin coordinate; a fourth orb must define the destination — which Pais considers correct. Forbes's current working name is "Four Orbs" reflecting this insight.

This is a significant credibility boost for Forbes's thesis: the author of the only US Navy exotic-propulsion patents publicly confirms he recognized his own theoretical mechanism in the videos.

This claim puts Forbes in the domain of extreme-end UAP research and is a significant credibility discussion point.

## Exotic Physics and Disclosure Debate

[[dave-rossi]] and Jesse Michaels describe Forbes as a friend who has moved from MH370 research into exotic physics and free energy. Forbes's position as described: **the exotic physics must come out** even if it could be used as a catastrophic weapon.

Rossi and Michaels push back on this position, arguing:
- Neither Forbes nor anyone outside classified programs has sufficient information to be high-conviction about disclosure safety
- Putting out exotic physics "willy-nilly" risks enabling a Klaus Fuchs-type leak to adversaries

The Forbes episode illustrates the broader **weaponization dilemma**: free energy / exotic propulsion physics and reality-bending weapons may share the same theoretical substrate.
