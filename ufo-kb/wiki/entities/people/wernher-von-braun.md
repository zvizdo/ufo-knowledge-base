# Wernher von Braun

German-American rocket scientist. In Nazi Germany, was a leading figure in the V-2 rocket program under mentor [[herman-oberth]]. Brought to the US after World War II as part of [[operation-paperclip]] and became the central figure in NASA's rocket development, leading the Saturn V program that put Apollo astronauts on the Moon.

Per [[robert-salas]] citing [[gordon-cooper]]'s book: von Braun reportedly told Cooper (during the social gatherings von Braun hosted for astronauts) that his team in Nazi Germany had actually worked on and flown a craft operating on similar principles to a UFO. This claim, if accurate, would connect [[operation-paperclip]] to [[reverse-engineered-craft]] and possibly to the [[1933-magenta-crash]] (if the Magenta craft was transferred to Germany before the war).

Also: [[edgar-mitchell]] reportedly claimed von Braun came to him and shared knowledge about UFO/ET matters. Other Operation Paperclip scientists allegedly corroborated the UFO-related claims in conversations with astronauts at von Braun's weekend gatherings.

## Role in American Space Program (zjpvfDFc4fg)

Von Braun was the **architect of the V2 missile program**, a major in the SS, and one of Hitler's top weapons scientists. He used slave labor from concentration camps. After the war, the US swept him into [[operation-paperclip]]; his record was scrubbed and he was given a new job. He became the **public face of NASA**, collaborating with **Walt Disney** on educational space films in the 1950s and directing the **Saturn V** program.

He was reportedly **deeply supportive** of [[edgar-mitchell]]'s post-Apollo consciousness research, even encouraging Mitchell to find a site within NASA to study psychic phenomena, and attended one of Mitchell's early fundraising dinners for the [[institute-of-noetic-sciences]].

FBI documents show von Braun was caught sending classified documents to Nazi counterparts in the USSR; apparently no disciplinary action was taken.

The [[occult-aerospace-connection]] argument: von Braun, his team, and the broader Nazi rocket-science milieu passed through institutions (SS, [[thule-society]] / [[vril-society]] adjacent) steeped in occult tradition, bringing that cultural substrate into NASA alongside the technical knowledge.
