# Jan Lundquist

An independent researcher described by Jesse Michaels as "an absolute research prodigy with an eidetic memory." She is one of the key interview subjects in [[RTEWLSTyUic]], where she offers detailed contextual analysis of [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s classified career, including his Germany mission, the origins of his "wounded prairie chicken" disinformation routine, and the true function of [[nicap]].

## Key Claims

**The Pearl Harbor incident (1950)**: After a Soviet spy infiltrated one of Brown's demos as a janitor, Brown began deliberately showing the wrong version of his experiments — the fluid dielectric "lifter" system — instead of the real solid dielectric gravitator. This is the origin of the disinformation that made his work look like an eighth-grade science fair project.

**NICAP as Counter Intelligence**: When Brown returned from the International Geophysical Year in 1957, he founded [[nicap]] — officially a civilian UFO investigation committee. Lundquist believes this was primarily CIA-affiliated Counter Intelligence: a honey trap for civilians to provide intelligence data to the agency, and a mechanism for spreading controlled disinformation. Brown reportedly told his daughter Linda to "filter out" sightings where people describe motion "like a fluttering leaf" — those were "ours" (man-made anti-gravity craft that wobbled, consistent with the Avrocar's stabilization problems).

**International Geophysical Year as cover**: Brown's participation in the 1957 global scientific survey was, per Lundquist, primarily a National Security operation — allowing Brown to travel worldwide and set up listening posts for U-2 overflights of Soviet Russia.

**The Germany mission**: Lundquist frames the Taom/Germany mission as Brown functioning as "a rogue operative who went and found one piece, brought it behind the curtain, went and found another piece, and brought it behind the curtain" — suggesting a deliberate mosaic-intelligence approach rather than a single retrieval.
