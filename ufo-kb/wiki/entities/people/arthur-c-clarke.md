# Arthur C. Clarke

**Arthur C. Clarke** was a British science fiction author and futurist best known for *2001: A Space Odyssey* and *Childhood's End*. He lived in Sri Lanka for the last decades of his life.

## UAP Relevance

[[whitley-strieber]] attended the premiere of *2001* and later described a private conversation in which Clarke told him about a "portal" on a mountain in Sri Lanka. [[jesse-michaels]] notes that Clarke "probably" had MI6 connections — framing him as an intelligence-adjacent figure whose public science fiction served as a cultural conditioning vector for contact/portal concepts.
