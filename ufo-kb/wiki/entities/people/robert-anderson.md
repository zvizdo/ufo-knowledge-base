# Robert Anderson

Deputy Secretary of War under President Truman and a senior partner at the investment bank Brown Brothers Harriman. Anderson is a key node in accounts of the postwar secret financial infrastructure: the [[anderson-trust-gold]] — the alleged covert trust established to manage looted Axis gold (particularly [[yamashita-gold]]) — bears his name.

## Background

Anderson moved between high government office and private finance in the pattern typical of the postwar American establishment. His Brown Brothers Harriman affiliation placed him within the same network as [[robert-lovett]] and [[john-j-mccloy]], who were also brought into the trust structure by [[henry-stimson]].

## Notable Claims / Role

According to sources cited in [[C4rSj5Aum7w]], Stimson assembled Anderson, Lovett, and McCloy into a small group tasked with managing the disposition of Yamashita gold discovered in the Philippines. The trust formed by this group took Anderson's name. [[clark-clifford]] reportedly briefed Truman on the discovery after being informed by [[charles-willoughby]] and Edward Lansdale.
