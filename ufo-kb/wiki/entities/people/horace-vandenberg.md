# Horace Vandenberg

General Hoyt Sanford Vandenberg served as the second Chief of Staff of the United States Air Force (1948–1953). In the context of UFO history, he is best known for rejecting [[project-sign]]'s classified [[estimate-of-the-situation]] in 1948 and ordering all copies destroyed.

## Background

Vandenberg had a distinguished WWII career and was deeply involved in establishing the independent USAF (1947). As Chief of Staff, he held ultimate authority over early Air Force UFO investigations.

## Role in UFO Suppression

When [[project-sign]] analysts forwarded the Estimate of the Situation — their conclusion that UFOs were most plausibly extraterrestrial — Vandenberg rejected the document outright, judging the evidence insufficient to support such a conclusion. He ordered the report destroyed. [[edward-ruppelt]] later described this decision as the moment that killed any institutional willingness to seriously investigate the ET hypothesis.

This is the earliest documented high-level suppression of a positive UFO assessment within U.S. government channels.
