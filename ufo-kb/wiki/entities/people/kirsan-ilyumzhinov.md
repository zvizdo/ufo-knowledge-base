# Kirsan Ilyumzhinov

Youngest president ever elected in the post-Soviet space (age 30 in 1993; recorded in the Guinness Book of Records), simultaneously serving as president of Kalmykia and FIDE World Chess Federation — the only person in recorded history to hold two elected presidencies concurrently (second Guinness record). Member of the Russian Federation State Council, Senate, and an advisor to the presidents of South Korea and Mongolia. He entered Yeltsin's and then Putin's inner circle during the St. Petersburg / Anatoli Sobchak years. Traveled to 108 countries in a single year. Had business connections to the Rockefeller, Kissinger, Oppenheimer, and Rothschild families.

## The 1997 Encounter

See full incident page: [[1997-ilyumzhinov-encounter]].

On the night of September 17, 1997, while in his Moscow penthouse apartment on a business trip, Ilyumzhinov was awakened around 10 PM by a sound or call. He approached the window — his apartment on the top floor, only roof above, open window — and found a translucent glowing pipe or corridor of unknown matter/energy. He entered it and found himself aboard a very large craft.

**Interior of the craft:** Several football fields in size. Beings resembling humans but taller, dressed in yellow garments. Screens and panels flickering with images. No one paid attention to him. One being led him to a porthole through which he saw what appeared to be another planet. He watched beings loading cylindrical containers from this planet into the craft, possibly collecting samples. He believes they may have traveled through dimensions while he was aboard — possibly fourth-dimensional movement.

**Communication:** The NHI communicated purely via thought-transmission. No spoken language. Ilyumzhinov compared this to Baba Vanga (who similarly answered before translators finished) and to legends of Atlantis where people communicated telepathically.

**What NHI told him:**
- Humanity is at an "embryonic level" and cannot yet be communicated with directly, like humans cannot converse with ants
- NHI created humanity and all life on Earth
- Humans destroy their environment, wage 117+ armed conflicts simultaneously, and direct all science toward weapons
- Einstein quote: "I don't know what weapons WWIII will use, but WWIV will use sticks and clubs"
- Direct communication will begin when humanity reaches a civilizational development threshold

**Return:** He reappeared from his bedroom — which his chief of staff, driver, and friend had checked at least ten times — as if materializing. Police were called; security cameras showed no one leaving or entering the building. His witnesses were so shaken they "started touching me like, are you alive or not?"

## Political Calculus Around Disclosure

Ilyumzhinov made a two-sheet cost-benefit analysis before going public. The cons list had 10–20 items: loss of business credibility, voter trust, ridicule. Pros: nearly none tangible. He eventually went public when, as if in a dream, a voice asked "What are you afraid of? You met with us, didn't you?" He concluded someone with institutional weight had to speak first — a farmer or doctor would be dismissed as intoxicated; a double-president might be taken more seriously. He was right that the backlash was severe: [[garry-kasparov]] immediately called for his removal from FIDE leadership, and political opponents demanded he be institutionalized.

## Kissinger Encounter

In two separate private meetings at Moscow that included [[mikhail-gorbachev]], Ilyumzhinov observed [[henry-kissinger]]'s UFO knowledge firsthand:

1. First meeting: Kissinger, unprompted, asked "how are they — how are they feeling, what were they talking about?" Gorbachev pushed: "Come on, Henry, tell him." Kissinger smiled but said nothing.
2. Second meeting, years later: Kissinger was "more talkative" and spoke "in a professional way" about UAP materials and documents held at NSC, CIA, and NASA. He was "very familiar with the materials, very deep in the subject." He also referenced the Andropov–Gorbachev story about the Nixon-Brezhnev UFO folder. See [[jfk-assassination-ufo]] and the moon program stoppage.

## Baba Vanga Relationship

Ilyumzhinov met [[baba-vanga]] 20–40 times over many years. She correctly predicted both of his presidential positions before they occurred:
- Predicted his presidency of Kalmykia months before the 1993 election (sent a telegram the night before results, addressed to "President Kiren")
- In April 1995 predicted he would "lead a very large global organization of the smartest people — chess players." On November 24, 1995, he was elected FIDE president.

He observed her conversing with invisible entities during walks. When he asked who she was speaking with, she named specific deceased individuals. She pointed downward when he asked about underground beings and said "they live there." She told him "they are everywhere — on the ground, underground, in the sky." She described the experience as a torment — constantly existing simultaneously in multiple time frames, conversing with the dead and the unborn.

## Chess and Extraterrestrials

Ilyumzhinov's most controversial intellectual claim: chess was invented and transmitted to humanity by extraterrestrials. Evidence he cites:
- Archaeological chess sets found independently in Mongolia, Peru, China, India, Japan — 5,000+ years old — with no inter-cultural communication possible
- All sets share identical 64-square black-and-white grid
- Human DNA is encoded by exactly 64 codons — the same number as squares on a chess board
- Black/white duality = yin/yang = universal law of unity of opposites

He served as FIDE president for 23 years with the goal of raising 1 billion chess players to "change the world within our own consciousness." He introduced chess as a compulsory school subject in Kalmykia and spent years lobbying the UN to redirect weapons budgets to brain research, cancer cures, and space exploration.

## Soviet-US UFO Coordination

Ilyumzhinov served in Soviet Army special forces communications troops in 1981. During exercises on the Black Sea, "several dozen unidentified craft flew in directly." Soldiers photographed them with personal cameras. A commission arrived from Moscow central headquarters and confiscated and destroyed all cameras. Everyone was required to sign a secrecy agreement. Ilyumzhinov signed one. He says such agreements still exist today.

He also confirms that US and Soviet ministries of defense exchanged UFO/USO information specifically to prevent misidentification of each other's weapons — particularly after massive unidentified underwater craft were detected in the Pacific Ocean, Baltic Sea, North Sea, and Atlantic Ocean. See [[1981-black-sea-ufo-sighting]].

## The Smyslov Incident (Hearsay)

At an opening ceremony banquet for a world chess championship in the Kremlin, a tall (approximately 2m) world chess champion privately told Ilyumzhinov: in the 1970s, before a match in Germany against Hübner, a translucent alien entity appeared in his hotel room and moved the chess pieces, demonstrating the entire game. The next day, the match played out move-for-move identically. He documented it in his records. His wife forbade him from speaking publicly. Only when he heard Ilyumzhinov had gone public did he approach him asking "did you actually meet them?" and confirming "yes, now we can publish." Identity transcribed as "Vasilei Smith" — likely [[vasily-smyslov]] or another Soviet-era world champion.
