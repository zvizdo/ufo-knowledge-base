# Thierry Jamin

French explorer known for searching for El Dorado and other Incan lost cities. Founded and leads the [[enari-institute]]. Jesse Michaels describes him as "a real life Indiana Jones" from another era. Met Leandro the huaquero in 2016, one year after the initial discovery, and has led the systematic research effort on the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] since then.

## Background

A serious Amazonian/Andean explorer who happened upon the tridactyl case through Leandro. Has organized the collection, arranged CT scans, published DICOM files publicly (via [[tridactyls-org]]), and invited international forensic and genetic experts to examine the bodies.

## Notable Claims

- Provides linguistic analysis: the regional place name in the Nazca language translates to "laboratory where cloning and hybridization were done."
- Argues the bodies represent a civilization more advanced than ours that mixed genetic material approximately 1,800 years ago.
- Speculates that creators "are probably from elsewhere."
