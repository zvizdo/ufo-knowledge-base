# Bernard Norlain

Retired French Air Force General and former head of [[ihedn|IHEDN]] (Institut des Hautes Études de Défense Nationale), France's elite national defense studies institute. Norlain wrote the preface to the 1999 [[cometa-report|COMETA Report]], providing the report with institutional endorsement from France's senior security-studies body.

In the preface, Norlain frames the report as a serious exercise in strategic anticipation: given the accumulation of credible UAP evidence and the possibility that some phenomena are of non-terrestrial origin, France's defense establishment has an obligation to reason through the implications — not to validate extraordinary claims but to avoid being caught unprepared.
