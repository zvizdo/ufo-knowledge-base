# Akinashev

> ⚠ Note: This name is approximate. [[kevin-knuth]] recalled from memory, noting it "starts with an A" and is a long Russian name. The specific spelling and identity require verification.

Soviet cosmonaut. During one of the early Soviet space station missions, a UAP pulled up alongside his spacecraft and paced it for a portion of the orbit before departing.

## Notable Claims

- The craft pulled up alongside the spacecraft in orbit
- The craft had **windows** (an unusual design detail)
- It paced the spacecraft for part of the orbit then departed
- The crew **photographed the craft**; Soviet authorities confiscated the photographs
- Akinashev **drew a picture** of the craft from memory — this drawing became public

## Significance

Knuth cites this as a space-based encounter corroborating the pattern of NHI interest in human space activity. Combined with the Skylab incident ([[alan-bean]]'s red light), the Gemini 11 battery drain ([[1966-gemini-11-ufo]]), and the Soviet Phobos probe disabling, it forms a consistent pattern of anomalous craft observing (and occasionally affecting) human spacecraft.

The confiscation of photographs by the Soviet government is a parallel to Western suppression patterns — suggesting both superpowers actively managed UAP encounter records.
