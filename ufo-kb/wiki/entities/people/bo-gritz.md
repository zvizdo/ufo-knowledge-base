# Bo Gritz

Retired US Army Special Forces officer known for leading private POW/MIA rescue missions in Southeast Asia. Referenced by [[danny-sheehan]] in the context of the [[ron-rewald]] case: CIA front-company documents obtained during the case included records of POW-search operations in Laos and Cambodia connected to Gritz. The connection is incidental to the UAP KB's primary focus but documents the scope of CIA covert operations documented in Sheehan's legal files.

## Background and UAP/Conspiracy Context

James "Bo" Gritz (b. 1939) is a retired US Army Special Forces lieutenant colonel; the most decorated Green Beret of the Vietnam War. Post-military career: independent POW/MIA-rescue activist (1980s Laos missions), 1992 Populist Party presidential candidate, "Spike training" survival-skills instructor, and prominent figure in patriot-movement / militia-adjacent circles.

Gritz's UAP-adjacent role is via his 1990s public statements connecting black-budget special-access programs, alleged underground bases, and government UFO concealment. He claimed insider sources for accounts of underground military-NHI joint facilities. His credibility on UAP topics is contested: military-special-operations background lends weight, but his post-1990 affiliations with conspiracy-movement organizations have made mainstream UAP researchers cautious about citing him as a primary source.
