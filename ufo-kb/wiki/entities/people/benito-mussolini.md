# Benito Mussolini

Benito Mussolini (1883–1945) was the founder of Italian Fascism and dictator of Italy from 1922 until his removal in 1943 and death in 1945. In the UAP/KB context, Mussolini is significant as the head of state who responded to the [[1933-magenta-crash]] — the alleged recovery of a non-human craft near Magenta, Lombardy in June 1933 — and who personally shaped the first known government-level UAP suppression structure.

**Gabinetto RS/33**: Following the Magenta recovery, Mussolini created [[gabinetto-rs33]] (Cabinet RS/33), a special state investigation body tasked with studying the recovered craft. RS/33 reported directly to the highest levels of the fascist government and operated under total secrecy enforced through the Stefani state news agency — which Mussolini personally directed to suppress all reporting on the incident.

**Political vs. investigative contradiction**: Mussolini maintained the public political position that the recovered craft was a foreign experimental aircraft of human manufacture. RS/33's own investigators reached the opposite conclusion: the craft was not of known human origin. This internal contradiction — state investigators acknowledging non-human origin while the political leadership publicly denied it — prefigures the post-Roswell structure of US UAP secrecy. Mussolini's insistence on the terrestrial narrative appears to have been politically motivated (acknowledging a non-human craft would undermine fascist narratives of human, specifically Italian, supremacy).

**1943–1945**: After Mussolini's fall in 1943, the Magenta craft had been stored at [[sia-marchetti]]'s aerospace hangars near Vergiate for approximately a decade. The OSS under [[allen-dulles]] conducted [[project-mcgregor]] to retrieve the craft before German forces could extract it — making the stored craft a target of the final major OSS operation of WWII.
