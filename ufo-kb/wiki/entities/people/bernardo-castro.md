# Bernardo Castro

Author of an early 2024 essay in *The Debrief* titled (approximately) on the most statistically likely origin of UAP. Castro's argument:
- The most likely source of UAP is not interstellar visitors but rather an **ancient pre-human advanced technology civilization** that arose on Earth
- A **750-million-year gap** in the fossil record leaves room for a prior advanced civilization to have developed and left
- The **Younger Dryas** event (~12,000 years ago) — a sharp global temperature drop — could explain why evidence of a prior technological civilization was obliterated
- Earth's geologic cycle (continental recycling, volcanic activity) would erase surface evidence of a civilization over millions of years

[[tim-gallaudet]] references this article as representative of scientific thinking about UAP origins that engages seriously with the data rather than dismissing it. The article's framing resonates with Gallaudet's view that UAP may be ocean-based and long-present on Earth.
