# Stanislav Grof

Czech-born psychiatrist and pioneering researcher of non-ordinary states of consciousness. Developed **holotropic breathwork** — a technique using sustained hyperventilation and music to induce altered states, hypothesized to trigger endogenous DMT or similar effects. A major influence on [[john-mack]], who incorporated holotropic breathwork into his clinical practice with abductees to help them access and integrate their experiences.

## Key Argument (as cited by Gallimore)

Grof argued in one of his books: if advanced intelligent beings are non-physical discarnate entities — "everywhere and nowhere" — then the way they would appear to humans would inevitably be dismissed as hallucination. They would not materialize in physical form. Instead, they would interact directly with the brain. In Grof's formulation: this is "clearly what's going on" — they interact with us via our neural machinery. [[andrew-gallimore]] directly adopts this model as the framework for his directed-DMT-world thesis.

## Connection to John Mack

Mack was inspired by Grof's work and used holotropic breathwork in his practice with abductees to help them access recall of anomalous experiences. This connection links abduction phenomenology to the transpersonal / non-ordinary-states tradition.
