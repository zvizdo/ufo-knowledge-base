# George Garrett

Lieutenant Colonel, USAF; Acting Chief, Collection Branch, Air Intelligence Requirements Division, Directorate of Intelligence, Headquarters USAF, Washington DC. Garrett was the primary administrative officer responsible for routing flying disc field reports from Air Force installations to AMC Wright-Patterson between 1947 and 1948.

## Key Actions

**Aug–Sep 1947**: Sent the 22 August 1947 routing form to Army R&D requesting confirmation that no US program matches the characteristics described in disc sighting reports. The response — signed by [[curtis-lemay]] on 29 August 1947 — confirmed "The Army Air Forces has no research project with the characteristics described." This was a crucial early ruling-out of domestic explanations.

**1948**: Garrett's name appears on dozens of routing memos forwarding sighting reports to AMC. He routed under Colonel Robert Taylor III as chief. He coordinated with [[cp-cabell]] and drafted the early intelligence machinery that became [[project-sign]]'s collection apparatus.
