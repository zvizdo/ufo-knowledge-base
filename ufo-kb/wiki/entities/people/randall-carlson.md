# Randall Carlson

## Background

Independent researcher, Sacred Geometry International podcast host, and catastrophism advocate. Argues that a cometary or asteroid airburst event approximately 12,800 years ago at the Younger Dryas boundary caused massive flooding, fires, and a near-extinction event that reset human civilization — explaining the apparent sudden emergence of complex civilizations around 10,000–8,000 BCE and the lack of evidence for pre-reset technological societies.

## Younger Dryas Impact

Carlson's core thesis aligns with the peer-reviewed Younger Dryas Impact Hypothesis (Firestone et al., 2007), which proposes a cosmic impact based on a platinum-iridium anomaly layer and spherule distribution across multiple continents. Carlson extends this into a broader "civilization with amnesia" narrative popularized alongside [[graham-hancock]].

In the [[8TYMQOUDQBo]] interview, [[salvatore-pais]] cites both Hancock and Carlson in a discussion of whether NHI have caused "reset events" in human history. Pais and co-host Jack suggest the 12,000-year reset is likely a natural cosmic event rather than NHI intervention, contrasting it with earlier, more ambiguous bottleneck events (e.g., the Toba volcanic bottleneck ~70,000 years ago).

## Moon Anomalies and *Penetration*

In [[JGE1NIGhBzw]], Carlson discloses that Ingo Swann's book *[[penetration-ingo-swann]]* was his entry point into moon anomalies. He describes the Apollo seismic "ringing" event (module crash → 3+ hour seismic train), the inverted sediment stratigraphy (lower layers newer than upper — excavation-site pattern), mass distribution inversion (denser material above lighter), and the presence of high-refractory metals (chromium, titanium, uranium-236). He is "willing to consider" the moon was **geoengineered**.

## Taurid Meteor Stream

Carlson provides the most detailed account in this KB of the [[taurid-meteor-stream]] orbital mechanics: twice-yearly Earth crossings, the June/summer and October/Halloween torids, clumps within the stream, and the Tunguska event (June 30, 1908) as a near-perfect modern demonstration of a Taurid hit. He credits [[bill-napier]] for linking the Taurid stream to the Younger Dryas event.

## Younger Dryas (Pre-2007)

Carlson gave five lectures at Warren Wilson College in **1995** arguing the Younger Dryas mass extinctions and rapid climate change were almost certainly caused by cosmic impacts — predating the Firestone et al. 2007 paper by 12 years. He notes that "eight or ten independent teams" have now documented cosmic signatures at the lower Younger Dryas boundary.

## Sphinx Erosion Field Work

Carlson's limestone erosion research for the Sphinx:
- Studied gravestone erosion rates (dated stones = known exposure time)
- Observed surf-zone limestone cliff erosion via metal tenon measurements
- Studied a Smithsonian Institution limestone cube exposed on a rooftop for decades
- Argues the Sphinx *ditch* walls (pristine, no restoration) show 2+ feet of limestone recession — incompatible with desert climate alone
- Calls for **luminescence dating** of the exposed Sphinx enclosure walls — not yet done

The astronomical argument (vernal equinox in Leo ~10,500 BC; Bval and Hancock's calculation) and Schwaller de Lubicz → [[john-anthony-west]] → [[robert-schoch]] intellectual lineage both inform Carlson's position.

Carlson has studied the Grail romances deeply (Chrétien de Troyes, Robert de Boron, Wolfram von Eschenbach). ## Sacred Number Canon (R7oyZGW99os)

Carlson's most systematic presentation of the [[sacred-number-canon]] — the family of numbers (72, 144, 432, 720, 1440, 2160, 25920, 86400, etc.) that appear across time measures, Platonic solid geometry, precessional cycles, solar system dimensions, ancient calendars, and sacred language.

**Platonic solids → time cycles**: The total angular degrees of each Platonic solid exactly matches a subdivision of the 25,920-year Great Year. Tetrahedron (720°) = 1 decan; Cube (2,160°) = 1 Platonic month; Dodecahedron (6,480°) = 1 Platonic season. Carlson treats this as the strongest evidence the number system is not accidental.

**Solar system in sacred numbers**: Sun radius = 432,000 miles; Moon diameter = 2,160 miles; Earth diameter = 7,920 miles. Sun-Earth distance ÷ Sun diameter = Moon-Earth distance ÷ Moon diameter = **108** — the number of the Moon, the atomic weight of silver, and the angle of a regular pentagon.

**Sumerian–Vedic convergence**: The Sumerian pre-diluvian king list totals **432,000 years** — exactly one Kali Yuga. The Vedic Maha Yuga = 4,320,000 years. Carlson argues these are two traditions preserving the same numerical system.

**Mayan calendar**: The Baktun = 144,000 days; the Tun = 360 days — same number family, smaller cycles.

## Great Pyramid as Geodetic Earth Model (R7oyZGW99os)

Carlson's most detailed presentation of the pyramid-as-earth-model thesis, based on J.H. Cole's 1925 survey:

- Pyramid height with sockle: **482.757 feet**
- 482.757 × 43,200 = **3,949.83 miles** — within 313 feet of the satellite-measured polar radius (WGS72: 3,949.89 miles)
- The two base measures (with/without sockle) encode the east-west vs. north-south difference at one square minute of latitude at the equator
- 43,200 is one of the core sacred numbers (= seconds in a half-day; appears in the Vedas and Sumerian king list)

"Someone somewhere thousands of years ago was able to measure the earth with remarkable accuracy" — William R. Fix, cited by Carlson.

Carlson also notes: in **2 seconds** of Earth rotation, a point on the equator travels a distance exactly equal to the perimeter of the pyramid's base (with sockle). The pyramid thus encodes both the polar radius and the rotational speed.

## Parthenon and Gothic Cathedrals as Geodetic Monuments (R7oyZGW99os)

Carlson extends the geodetic argument beyond Egypt:

- **Parthenon** (Athens, 37°58' N): Width = 100 Greek feet = 1 second of meridian arc at that latitude. The Greek foot is geodetically defined by Earth's curvature at Athens. Total difference from the theoretical value: 0.05 inch (effectively zero when the latitude is corrected from 38° to 37°58').
- **Gothic cathedrals**: Each was built using a **local cubit** derived from a multiple or subdivision of the meridian arc at that cathedral's latitude. The geodetic tradition from Giza ran through Greece to medieval Christendom.

The implication: someone was able to measure Earth's shape with satellite-grade accuracy at least 4,200 years ago, and this knowledge persisted as a living tradition for thousands of years.

## Gematria and Sacred Texts (R7oyZGW99os)

Carlson demonstrates that Greek and Hebrew alphabets encode the [[sacred-number-canon]] through the denery system (letters = numbers). Key gematric values:

- *Aletheia* (truth) = 72; *Katabola* (conception) = 432; *Ierousalem* = 864; *Pneuma hagion* (Holy Spirit) = 1,080.
- Book of Revelation's "New Jerusalem" encodes: 144-cubit wall = Earth's circumference in Egyptian royal cubits; 12,000-furlong perimeter = 7,920,000 feet = 1,500 miles; ratio of Earth diameter to New Jerusalem diameter = 5.28 = ratio of human pace to human foot.

The "144,000 redeemed of the earth" are those who have internalized the sacred number system — with his name "written in their foreheads" = the number canon as internalized consciousness.

## Catastrophism Timeline (R7oyZGW99os)

Carlson presents a graph of **16+ civilization-ending events** over 150,000 years of Earth history, using the criterion: events that would "end civilization as we know it" if they occurred today. Green dots from the Great Year model (6,480-year node intervals) show high correlation with independently dated catastrophes.

Key events:
- **~26,000 BP**: Onset of Late Wisconsin Ice Age (≈ 1 full Great Year)
- **~12,900 BP**: Onset of Younger Dryas / first megafauna extinction wave (≈ half Great Year)

The correlation implies ancient cultures encoded knowledge of this cosmic timetable in the sacred number system — the numbers are warnings as much as cosmological descriptions.

## Sphinx Age — Discussion with Schoch (R7oyZGW99os)

Carlson states he personally discussed limestone weathering rates with [[robert-schoch]] and found Schoch "hasn't looked in depth" at the comparative literature on limestone weathering rates. After spending ~2 months studying limestone weathering across multiple sites, Carlson puts the **minimum age of the Sphinx at 20,000–30,000 years** (with rainfall averaging 45 inches/year, comparable to recent Atlanta weather). Schoch publicly uses 7,000–9,000 years as a conservative academic position but "is perfectly willing to believe it could be much older."
