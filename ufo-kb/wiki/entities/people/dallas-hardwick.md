# Dallas Hardwick

Materials scientist who co-invented the [[mandeloy-alloy]] with [[monica-resza]] at the Rockwell Science Center in the 1990s, solving the long-standing problem of oxygen-rich rocket engine combustion compatibility. She subsequently joined Wright-Patterson's AFRL Materials Directorate, where she was embedded from 2000 through her retirement in 2012 — the period during which Mandeloy 200 was co-developed and [[neil-mccasland]] arrived to command AFRL.

The alloy's name fuses the first three letters of both co-inventors' names: **Man**daloy = **Dal**las + **Mor**nica (or variant fusion).
