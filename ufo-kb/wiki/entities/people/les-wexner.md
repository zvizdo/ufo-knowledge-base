# Les Wexner

Leslie "Les" Wexner (born 1937) is the founder and long-time CEO of L Brands, the parent company of Victoria's Secret and other retail chains. He was Jeffrey Epstein's most significant financial backer and closest elite associate — providing Epstein with a house in Ohio and what [[jesse-michaels]] describes as "piles of money."

## Relationship with Epstein

The Wexner-Epstein relationship is one of the most anomalous in the Epstein financial story: Epstein had no credible legitimate investment track record, yet Wexner — who had succeeded independently building a multi-billion dollar retail empire — gave him control of substantial assets and access to his social network. The mechanism by which Epstein convinced Wexner to trust him has never been explained publicly.

[[jesse-michaels]] notes the Wexner-Epstein relationship as the most glaring unresolved financial mystery in the Epstein case.

## The Dybbuk Diary Entry

In the 1980s or 1990s, Wexner wrote in his diary about being "haunted by a dybbuk." A **dybbuk** is a concept from Jewish mysticism/folklore — a malevolent spirit of a dead person that possesses the living.

[[jesse-michaels]] raises this in the context of the broader question: were Epstein's elite associates doing the bidding of "some higher, even more evil thing" rather than acting purely from personal sexual desire or financial greed? Wexner's diary entry is the only direct documentary evidence (in the KB) of an Epstein-adjacent figure experiencing or reporting supernatural/demonic presence.

[[colin-ross]]'s response: "Now we're definitely outside regular psychiatry." This is not a dismissal — Ross acknowledges this takes the Epstein story into the territory of "supernatural multi-dimensional entities, which then takes us into aliens."
