# Grant Cameron

## Background

Grant Cameron is a well-known UFO researcher and author with many books on the experiencer phenomenon. He visited [[chris-bledsoe]] and posted a photo on his Facebook page showing an envelope addressed to Bledsoe alongside a napkin partially obscuring the address.

## The Napkin Photo

Grant apparently missed the significance of the napkin. [[bob-maguire]], looking at the same photo, immediately identified:
1. The address as Bledsoe's.
2. The napkin as bearing the **Camp David presidential seal** — which is a federal crime to reproduce; the only authentic napkins exist at Camp David.

Bob's conclusion: the only person who could have sent a Camp David napkin in an envelope addressed to Bledsoe is someone with White House access — and given [[tim-taylor]]'s role as the conduit between Bledsoe and Washington, the most likely sender is Taylor, delivering Bledsoe's prophecies to **Barack Obama**.

Grant's posting of the photo (without recognizing the seal's significance) inadvertently made the evidence public.

## Eric Walker Transcript

In a separate work, Grant Cameron published a book that includes a **full transcript of a conversation with [[eric-walker]]** (former Penn State president and alleged Majestic-12 adjacent figure). Walker was evasive during the conversation but acknowledged awareness of UAP-related information without confirming specifics. The transcript is cited by [[jesse-michaels]] and [[claus-svahn]] in the context of the broader Walker cluster (which also includes [[william-steinman]]'s visits and [[kit-green]]'s meeting with Walker).
