# Danny Sheehan

## Background

Daniel P. Sheehan is a Harvard-trained constitutional attorney and former counsel to the Jesuit National Headquarters. He served as counsel to the U.S. Senate Intelligence Committee. He founded the [[christic-institute]] (1980s–1991) in Washington D.C. and later the New Paradigm Institute / Ubiquity University. His career intersects UAP research, JFK assassination investigation, Watergate, Iran-Contra, and consciousness research — with firsthand exposure to classified materials across multiple cases.

## Firsthand UAP Claims

### January 1977 — Blue Book Classified Photos
Working as special legal counsel to the [[congressional-research-service]] (Science and Technology Division, Director [[marsha-smith]]) on a UAP study commissioned by President [[jimmy-carter]], Sheehan gained access to classified [[project-blue-book]] microfiche files in the **basement of the Madison Building**, National Archives, Washington D.C. He viewed photos not included in the publicly released Blue Book materials:
- A **saucer ~40 feet wide** crashed into a snow-covered embankment
- **U.S. Air Force personnel** surrounding the craft — confirming a U.S. military recovery operation
- A **dome on the upper surface** with unknown **symbols** around its base
- Sheehan traced the symbols on the back of a legal pad; the tracing has never been published

Full documentation: [[1977-sheehan-blue-book-photos]]

### Scientology Files — Remote Viewing Intelligence
While approached to represent L. Ron Hubbard, Sheehan reviewed Scientology's most sensitive internal files at their Florida HQ. He saw:
- **[[ingo-swann]]'s internal reports** to Scientology's UK Guardian's Office, documenting the ONI remote-viewing program's activities — confirming Swann was spying on the program for Scientology
- **[[pat-price]]'s capability files** — including the Soviet submarine location session, a UAP hovering over the submarine, a coordinate-based document retrieval from a top-secret CIA facility, and an 85%-compliance mind-control broadcast from a Canadian ONI facility

### Deathbed Blue Book Witness — Name Conflict
Flew to a hospice in northern Minnesota to hear a deathbed account from a U.S. Army clerk typist assigned to Blue Book who claimed to have witnessed telepathic communication between a U.S. commander and an ET at [[s4]]. The ET said: *"You people would refer to it as God, but it is very different from what you think."* No recording was made.

> ⚠ **Name discrepancy**: In episode [[2SQXAPCdmPE]], Sheehan names this witness as **"Albert Stein."** In episode [[C4rSj5Aum7w]], he names him as **"Oscar Wolf"** (with momentary uncertainty: "Oscar Wolf or Oscar Lane?"). All other details match — same story, same location, same S4 encounter. [[jeremy-corbell]] and [[richard-dolan]] reportedly interviewed him independently; the name has not been reconciled across accounts. See [[albert-stein]] and [[oscar-wolf]].

And I happen to know who they are. And what they've done is they've formed an association and they're working to try to drag the program back into the government."
