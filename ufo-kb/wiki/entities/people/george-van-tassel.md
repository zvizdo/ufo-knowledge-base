# George Van Tassel

California engineer who worked for Lockheed Aircraft and later Howard Hughes. From 1947 he lived with his family in an underground space beneath **Giant Rock** in the Mojave Desert — a location previously owned by **Frank Critzer**, a Nazi operative who died when FBI agents closed in (an explosion, either accidental or deliberate, killed him in the stand-off). Van Tassel took over the property knowing Critzer's history. Howard Hughes would fly in to the private airstrip and eat at the Van Tassels'.

## Nordic Contact Claims

Van Tassel claimed contact with Nordic beings he initially identified as "Venusians." Their message:
- Humanity came from space; **original humans are the Nordic phenotype**
- When the first expedition arrived on Earth (all-male vanguard), they found hominid life
- Subsequent expeditions found the male vanguard had **interbred with hominid females**, producing hybrid "lower races"
- The "master race" are direct-lineage space humans; hybrid populations are lower

## Significance: Contactee-Fascist Nexus

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] identifies this narrative as **identical** to the race doctrine promulgated by the Nazis and notes Van Tassel's Lockheed / Howard Hughes / Nazi-property connections. Along with [[george-hunt-williamson]], [[george-adamski]], and [[billy-meier]], Van Tassel is part of what Jorjani calls the [[contactee-nordic-fascist-nexus]] — a pattern of 1950s contactees all transmitting the same racialist hierarchy disguised as cosmic revelation.

Van Tassel also built the **Integratron** at Giant Rock — a domed structure he claimed was designed by Venusians for rejuvenation and time travel.
