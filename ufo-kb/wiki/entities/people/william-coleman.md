# William Coleman

United States Air Force Colonel who had a documented close encounter with a UAP while piloting a B-55 over Alabama — and subsequently served as the public spokesman for [[project-blue-book]], the USAF's official UAP investigation program.

## Background

Coleman's dramatic personal UAP encounter is referenced by [[james-fox]] in the context of the broader investigation connecting [[1964-socorro]] to the [[1964-holloman-afb-landing]]. The encounter — a pilot who personally witnessed something unexplained then became the official face of the government's UAP-dismissal program — is emblematic of the credibility tension at the heart of [[project-blue-book]].

## Role in Holloman Investigation

Fox encountered Coleman while probing how [[bob-emenegger]] and [[allan-sandler]]'s 1974 film *UFOs: Past, Present and Future* came to include veiled references to a landing at [[holloman-air-force-base]]. Coleman was a Pentagon liaison during the film's production — his involvement in facilitating the filmmakers' access suggests official awareness of the Holloman footage.
