# Bill Cooper

Milton William Cooper (1943–2001), known as Bill Cooper. US Navy veteran and UFO/conspiracy author whose 1991 book *[[behold-a-pale-horse]]* became one of the most widely read conspiracy texts of the 1990s. Killed in a November 2001 shootout with Apache County sheriff's deputies in Eagar, Arizona after they attempted to arrest him on assault charges.

**Claims and credibility**: Cooper claimed to have viewed classified UFO documents while serving on the briefing team for the Commander-in-Chief of the Pacific Fleet (1970–1973) — specifically a document titled "Project Grudge / Blue Book Report 13" that allegedly described agreements between the US government and NHI. He also claimed to have seen film footage of alien entities. *Behold a Pale Horse* compiled MJ-12 material ([[majestic-12]]), UFO-related documents, and a broad conspiratorial framework encompassing the Illuminati, drug trafficking, and shadow governance. [[john-lear]] co-authored anonymous sections of the book with Cooper.

**Disinformation concerns**: [[james-fox]] suspects both [[john-lear]] and Cooper were disinformation assets — "agent or useful idiot" — and frames Cooper's most sensational claims as deliberately adjacent to real events (the standard disinformation technique: accurate core, false framing). The maximalist-conspiracy presentation in *Behold a Pale Horse* is often cited as an example of the [[flood-the-zone-disinfo]] pattern: so much unverifiable material mixed with verifiable documents that the result is total signal-noise confusion. The [[credibility-frameworks]] assessment is: Cooper's Navy service is documented; his claimed document access is unverified; his analytical framework is discredited in most serious UAP research.

**Cultural impact**: *Behold a Pale Horse* reached large audiences before the internet and introduced millions of readers to MJ-12 documents, the Bilderberg Group, and UFO crash retrieval as connected topics. This makes Cooper historically significant as a gateway text regardless of his accuracy.
