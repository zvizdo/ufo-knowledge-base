# Noi Torres

## Background

Noi Torres is a civilian UFO researcher who, with co-author [[ruben-uriarte]], wrote *[[mexicos-roswell]]* — the canonical compilation of witness accounts, leaked memo provenance, and historical mapping for the [[1974-coyame-ufo-crash]] incident and the broader [[coyame-mexico]] UAP corridor.

## Notable Claims

- Documented the August 1974 Coyame midair collision; reconstructed the timeline from the leaked Mexican military memo that surfaced in the 1990s.
- Compiled multi-decade witness reports of orbs, mountain-light displays, and intimidation patterns in Coyame.
