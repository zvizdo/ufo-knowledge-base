# Gavin de Becker

Elite personal security consultant who protects "incredibly important people" — heads of state, CEOs, high-value individuals in high-threat environments. Appeared on American Alchemy ([[jesse-michaels]]'s podcast) in an episode described by [[joe-rogan]] as "crazy" and recommended viewing for its discussion of CIA conspiracies and governmental deception.

## Background

De Becker runs Gavin de Becker & Associates, one of the top private security firms in the world. His clients occupy the highest levels of government, business, and entertainment. This access gives him a vantage point on how power actually operates that most public commentators lack.

## Claims on American Alchemy

Per Rogan's summary, de Becker's appearance on American Alchemy covered:
- **CIA conspiracies** broadly
- **Fauci/AIDS parallel to COVID**: de Becker drew an analogy between Anthony Fauci's role in the AIDS epidemic and his role around COVID — presenting similar patterns of narrative management and information suppression
- **Who runs the government**: de Becker holds a view that elected officials are not the actual power, consistent with the deep-state/money-runs-everything framework Rogan endorses

Rogan notably wore a Betty and Barney Hill t-shirt on the de Becker episode, which Jesse Michaels references. De Becker reportedly did not know what the shirt signified.
