# Harold Dahl

Harbor patrolman Harold Dahl was the primary witness in the [[1947-maury-island]] incident. On June 21, 1947 — three days before [[kenneth-arnold]]'s landmark Mount Rainier sighting — Dahl claimed to have observed six doughnut-shaped craft near Maury Island in Puget Sound. One craft appeared to malfunction and ejected slag-like debris that injured Dahl's son and killed his dog.

## Account

Dahl provided a sample of the alleged disc debris through his supervisor [[fred-crisman]]. The material was ultimately transferred to Army Air Forces Intelligence officers Capt. Davidson and Lt. Brown on July 31, 1947. Neither Dahl nor Crisman recanted their account during the FBI and AAF investigation, despite the Bureau's eventual conclusion that the case was a hoax intended to promote gravel pit slag sales.
