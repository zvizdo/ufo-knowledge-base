# Aleister Crowley

British occultist, ceremonial magician, and writer (1875–1947). The 20th century's most influential figure in Western esotericism — founder of Thelema, leader of the [[oto]] from 1925 to his death, and author of the foundational ritual literature (*The Book of the Law*, *Magick in Theory and Practice*, the *Hymn to Pan*) that shaped most subsequent ceremonial-magic traditions. The press called him "the wickedest man in the world."

In the UFO discourse, his significance is structural rather than direct: through the [[oto]] Agape Lodge in Pasadena, Crowley was [[jack-parsons]]'s magical mentor during the precise years (1939–1947) when Parsons was co-founding [[jpl]] and developing the solid-rocket fuel chemistry that became the basis of the American space program. Per [[lue-elizondo]] in [[1f16VvXaSSE]], Parsons recited Crowley's "Hymn to Pan" before rocket tests at JPL — making Crowley the upstream esoteric source for the [[occult-aerospace-connection]] thesis.

## Background

- b. 1875 Leamington, Warwickshire; d. 1947 Hastings.
- Joined the Hermetic Order of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn (1898); broke with Mathers; founded the A∴A∴ (Argenteum Astrum) in 1907 with George Cecil Jones.
- Received what he claimed was a dictated revelation in Cairo (April 8–10, 1904), producing *Liber AL vel Legis* — *The Book of the Law* — declaring the Aeon of Horus and the central Thelemic axiom: "Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law."
- Assumed leadership of the [[oto]] in 1925 from Theodor Reuss; rewrote its ritual structure to incorporate Thelema and codified the higher-degree sex-magic teachings (the IX° and XI°).
- Authorized [[jack-parsons]] to lead the OTO Agape Lodge in Pasadena (1942–1946); their correspondence is preserved.
- Connection to [[l-ron-hubbard]]: Hubbard performed the [[1946-babalon-working]] with Parsons in 1946 — a Crowleyan invocation Parsons believed manifested an entity ("Babalon") shortly before his accidental death in 1952.

## Notable Claims

- **Foundation of Thelema** as a coherent religious-magical system.
- **OTO modernization**: reframed the OTO as a Thelemic order, introducing the central sex-magic curriculum.
- **Indirect founding influence on JPL**: Parsons's adoption of the "Hymn to Pan" as a pre-test ritual; Parsons's integration of Crowley's magical philosophy into his engineering practice (per [[diana-pasulka]] and Elizondo in [[1f16VvXaSSE]]).
- **Cairo Working (1904)**: the founding revelation of *The Book of the Law*; cited in subsequent occult-contact literature as a possible early instance of trans-human contact, antedating the modern UFO era.
