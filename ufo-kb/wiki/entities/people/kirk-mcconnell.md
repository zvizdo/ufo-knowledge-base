# Kirk McConnell

## Background

Kirk McConnell spent decades as a professional staff member on the **Senate Armed Services Committee** and **Senate Intelligence Committee**, retiring approximately one year before this interview (~2024). He entered the UAP issue in late 2017 following the Leslie Kean / Ralph Blumenthal New York Times article — the same trigger for many congressional staffers. His path was accelerated by his pre-existing relationship with [[chris-mellon]], whom he had known since the late 1980s when both worked in Senate staff roles.

Over the subsequent years, McConnell personally:
- Interviewed Navy pilots [[david-fravor]] and [[alex-dietrich]] about the 2004 Nimitz incident
- Met [[hal-puthoff]], [[eric-davis]], and staff working on AAWSAP / Skinwalker Ranch
- Received [[harold-malmgren]]'s firsthand Bluegill Triple Prime account
- Invited [[sean-kirkpatrick]] to sit in on Senate briefings before Kirkpatrick became AARO director (a relationship that later became strained when Kirkpatrick took a "traditional DoD line")

McConnell is unusual in UAP-adjacent congressional circles for combining institutional access with genuine engagement with exotic physics: extended electrodynamics, Tesla's scalar waves, and the Nazi Bell program.

## Notable Claims and Assessments

### Reverse-Engineered Craft (Hearsay — Multiple Sources)

McConnell confirms that a handful of individuals — not publicly known whistleblowers — came to Capitol Hill and made claims of having seen recovered or reverse-engineered craft with UAP-like performance characteristics. He describes one instance where a source was **visibly shaking** during the briefing, indicating genuine fear rather than performance. McConnell explicitly distinguishes these from Grusch (who is hearsay) — these sources claim more direct access, but are unwilling to go public.

### Bluegill Triple Prime — Ship Log Details

McConnell personally received [[harold-malmgren]]'s account and adds corroborating detail: the Navy ship logs from the recovery fleet (the vessels deployed near Johnston Atoll to collect instrument packages from the Bluegill Triple Prime test) describe two anomalous radioactive objects recovered from the water — a **black sphere** and a **green tube** — that were not part of the expected instrument package. A separate entry in the logs documents a Navy salvage operation. These log details are declassified and corroborate Malmgren's account of a Navy recovery.

### September 19, 1962 AVCO MarkV Incident

One month before Bluegill Triple Prime, a separate nuclear re-entry vehicle test (AVCO MarkV on a Cape Canaveral launch, September 19, 1962) was tracked by a luminous UAP that **accelerated and caught up with the warhead traveling at ~10,000–12,000 mph**. The official Defense Department report explicitly stated that *"objects of unknown origin and identity were tracking/pacing"* the re-entry vehicle. This represents an official government document acknowledging UAP.

### Robertson Panel / Foo Fighter Continuity

McConnell surfaced a non-obvious link: [[hp-robertson]] was the U.S. scientist assigned to investigate **Foo Fighters** during WWII — and then 7 years later was chosen to chair the CIA's Robertson Panel that created the Project Blue Book debunking mandate. [[luis-alvarez]] was on both. This "not a coincidence" continuity suggests the Foo Fighter investigation produced classified findings that informed later suppression policy.

### Dick Cheney as UAP Gatekeeper

McConnell reports hearing consistent accounts that: (1) George HW Bush was read into the legacy UAP program as CIA Director and briefed his son; (2) the Bush-43 administration took a more hands-on approach; (3) Dick Cheney's NSC staff were involved as custodians of the program. McConnell cannot confirm this but explicitly does not dismiss it either.

### NSC Disclosure Study

McConnell credits [[hal-puthoff]]'s account over [[sean-kirkpatrick]]'s denial: the NSC under George W. Bush commissioned a study (through the Office of Net Assessment, contract placed through Oakridge National Laboratory) assessing whether to disclose possession of ~6–12 recovered craft. Conclusion: costs outweigh benefits. McConnell's logical argument: "No serious organization simulates scenarios they're completely convinced are fictional."

### Sean Kirkpatrick — Conflict of Interest Assessment

McConnell knew Kirkpatrick for ~20 years, from when Kirkpatrick was a program manager at the National Reconnaissance Office. He believes Kirkpatrick may have been read into the legacy program and told to hold the party line — but cannot prove it. He explicitly flags that the AARO advisory contract was run through **Oakridge National Laboratory** (an AEC successor lab), and that Kirkpatrick now works for Oakridge after leaving government — a potential conflict of interest if UAP secrecy is structurally tied to atomic-energy secrecy, as McConnell believes it is.

### Tesla and Classified Science

McConnell believes [[nikola-tesla]] discovered what we now call extended electrodynamics (scalar/longitudinal waves), that his FBI-seized papers remain classified, and that the suppression is driven by fear: zero-point energy extraction may be easy to weaponize, and knowledge of it should not proliferate to adversarial state or non-state actors. He presents this as a potentially benevolent reason for UAP-era secrecy, while also insisting Congressional oversight cannot be bypassed regardless of the motivation.
