# Kurt Russell

American actor and licensed pilot who witnessed the [[1997-phoenix-lights]] while flying his personal aircraft into the Phoenix area with his son. His account is notable because he was an experienced pilot giving a professional aviator's assessment of scale.

## The Encounter

Russell was flying into Phoenix with his son when the object appeared. He described it as so large that he felt he "could land his airplane on it." Jesse Michaels also notes the Governor of Arizona (Fife Symington) "has now admitted it is real."

## Significance

Russell's status as a rated pilot makes his scale estimate credible. The Phoenix Lights case benefits from a broad witness base — truckers on I-10, ground observers, elected officials — and the addition of an airborne pilot's perspective corroborates the craft's extraordinary size.
