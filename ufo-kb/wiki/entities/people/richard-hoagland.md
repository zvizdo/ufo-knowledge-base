# Richard Hoagland

Science journalist and independent researcher best known for his 1990s work publicising the hypothesis that the Cydonia/Sidonia region of Mars contains artificial structures — specifically the "Face on Mars" and the D&M Pyramid — as remnants of an ancient extraterrestrial civilisation.

Hoagland worked with [[david-flynn]] during the 1990s–2000s, giving joint conferences sometimes accompanied by [[michael-heiser]]. Their joint work publicised the idea that ruins of an advanced ancient civilisation exist on Mars, building on Flynn's theological/astronomical framework in *Sidonia*.

Alberino describes Hoagland as "instrumental" in bringing the Mars civilisation hypothesis to public attention in the 1990s. He cites the "D&M Pyramid" in Sidonia as a massive structure that Hoagland analysed.

Alberino's personal view: NASA's initial Viking photos of the Face on Mars were accurate; the later higher-resolution images released by NASA were a deliberate suppression exercise. This position implicitly aligns with Hoagland's longstanding critique of NASA's handling of Cydonia data.
