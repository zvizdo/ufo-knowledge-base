## Research Focus

UAPGerb's primary analytical contribution is the **financial architecture of black programs** — tracing how legacy UAP-related R&D is funded off-book through:

- **FFRDCs** (Federally Funded Research and Development Centers): MITRE, RAND, Center for Naval Analyses (CNA), Institute for Defense Analyses (IDA), Aerospace Corporation (Crystal City, Arlington)
- **URCs** (University Affiliated Research Centers)
- **MRTFBs** (Major Range and Test Facility Bases): Nevada Test Range, Edwards AFB

These conduits make legacy programs effectively invisible to congressional budget oversight. His research cites an anonymous Pentagon witness ("Ed", ~2012–2016 Air Force R&D role) who reported $34 billion and $40 billion scrubbed from Air Force books in separate years as non-transfer-of-authority funds for joint reverse engineering at Nevada Test Range and Edwards AFB.
