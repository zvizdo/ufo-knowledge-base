# Dan Sherman

Retired USAF Technical Sergeant and ELINT (electronics intelligence) specialist who claims that while serving in the Air Force in the early 1990s he was recruited into **[[project-preserve-destiny]]** — a classified NSA program, concealed inside a conventional black project, in which genetically pre-conditioned humans acted as "intuitive communicators" (ICs) to receive and relay telepathic transmissions from alien beings.

His account is documented in the book **[[above-black]]** (self-published) and detailed in a long-form interview with [[jesse-michaels]] on American Alchemy ([[Rfmy5oW_r9c]]).

## Background

Sherman grew up in transient poverty — 27 schools between kindergarten and 12th grade — with no military family background. His interest in the Air Force was sparked in fourth grade by a Major and SR-71 pilot who boarded horses at a stable Sherman's family managed in Iba City, California. He enlisted at 18, initially as a security policeman, then cross-trained into ELINT after 4–5 years.

**Service profile:**
- 12 years total Air Force service
- Bases: Korea, Italy, Colorado, North Dakota, Nebraska, Texas
- TDY: Philippines, Japan, NSA headquarters (Laurel, Maryland), Fort Meade area
- First ELINT posting: Offutt Air Force Base, Omaha, Nebraska
- ELINT school: San Angelo, Texas (continuation course EA280 sent him to NSA complex)

Sherman has indicated his DD214 and service records are available — his ELINT service is not in dispute.

## Recruitment into Project Preserve Destiny

At NSA headquarters in Laurel, Maryland, during continuation course EA280 (~1991–92), Sherman was called to the NSA complex by a captain and informed he was to be enrolled in an additional classified program. He underwent training in a converted biofeedback framework:

- Seated in a dark van with headphones; given a tone to mentally hum
- A sine wave on a monitor would visually shift when mental contact was established
- The sine wave moving was the confirmation that the IC faculty was active: *"I went, 'Oh, okay. Well, there's a mental disconnect there — that's not supposed to be happening. This is not possible.'"*
- Training lasted ~3 months alongside his conventional EA280 coursework

Sherman was told to keep the IC program entirely separate from his conventional ELINT work; his colleague in the same van knew only the conventional side.

## IC Operations at Overseas Bases

At his first PPD overseas posting, Sherman was assigned to a detachment with its own security perimeter inside a larger base. He worked a "right seat" (IC) alongside a "left seat" (conventional ELINT). His unit commander — who knew only the administrative shell of PPD, not its NHI content — introduced him to the system.

His PPD handler chain ran through Captain White (at NSA) and the local unit captain. Contact officers knew little beyond logistics. Sherman's IC code: **Staunch 118**.

He received transmissions from two distinguishable alien entities:
- **Spock** — first contact; logical, consistent with the Star Trek character; communication shut down abruptly when disallowed topics were raised
- **Bones** — second contact; different "tapestry" or flavor; Sherman became more adept at informal communication with Bones and used a "closer plane" of communication for personal questions

Key alien communications (via Bones unless noted):
- Humans are "water beings" or "water vessels" — the water component is likely salient because the aliens themselves contain far less water
- NHI have been on Earth "for a very long time" and have intersected with humanity at different times; sustained presence became harder to conceal as human civilizations started communicating with each other
- There are many alien races ("Of course — it's the entire universe")
- Human-alien interbreeding was experimentally conducted in the past; unclear if ongoing
- Alien travel involves EM energy manipulation — creating a void ahead of the craft and falling into it (consistent with Alcubierre drive geometry)
- "No problem" with Sherman communicating as he was; implied the aliens themselves had limited knowledge of PPD's human-side protocols
- On religion: two classes of creation — intelligent and non-intelligent; plants are non-intelligent; humans and aliens are both intelligent; intelligent beings have some form of interior agency (Sherman interpreted this as "soul")

Toward the end of his overseas posting Sherman began receiving transmissions that appeared to describe **abduction events in progress**: location potentials, pain levels, recall indicators. This caused him profound discomfort — he felt like "an accessory to the crime" — and contributed to his decision to leave the program. He eventually resigned from the Air Force.

## Genetic Pre-Conditioning Claim

Sherman was briefed by Captain White that the alien beings themselves selected him before birth: they abducted his pregnant mother in the early 1960s and modified his fetal genetics to produce the IC faculty. This narrative is structurally parallel to hybrid program claims documented by other researchers (see [[abduction-phenomena]] and [[nhi-genetic-modification]]).

Circumstantial detail Sherman notes: his mother was told by doctors she would never carry a baby to term (due to a childhood injury) yet Sherman survived — he frames this as possible evidence of alien protective intervention around the genetic modification event.

The NSA tracked him through childhood and military service before reading him in, according to what he was told.

## FOIA and Documentation

An independent researcher submitted FOIA requests to both the Air Force and NSA for all documents related to Project Preserve Destiny. Per Michaels's account, the responses from both agencies were "fairly telling" — neither a clear denial nor a document dump. The specific language of these responses is flagged by Michaels as significant.

Sherman's own service record is verifiable; his ELINT career is documented and not disputed.

## Credibility Assessment

Per [[credibility-frameworks]]:
- **Verifiable**: Military service (12 years, ELINT), DD214 available, ELINT postings independently traceable
- **Unverifiable**: The PPD program itself; the NHI communication content; the genetic modification of his mother
- **Consistency**: Sherman's account is internally coherent; answers from different handlers aligned; narrative has been stable across book publication and interview
- **Absence of credential dispute**: Unlike Bob Lazar, Sherman makes no academic claims that can be checked and falsified
- **Anomalous detail**: His "nonchalant" affect (Michaels's word) and technical ELINT background distinguish his account from typical contactee narratives

> ⚠ **Open question**: The identity and whereabouts of the overseas bases where PPD operations were conducted remain partially concealed. Michaels's researchers identified at least one candidate base, which prompted a visible reaction from Sherman when named at the end of the interview.

## NSA Director of Research Awareness

In [[jMCavr3dVP0]], [[eric-haseltine]] — former NSA Director of Research — was asked about Dan Sherman. His response: "I heard about something like that." He offered no confirmation of specific program details but his use of "guilty knowledge" framing and his carefully hedged language is the highest-credentialed partial corroboration in the public record. An NSA senior official neither confirming nor fully denying Sherman's account raises its epistemic weight significantly.
