# Harry Reid

United States Senator from Nevada (D), Senate Majority Leader (2007–2015). The primary congressional architect of AATIP funding. Nevada's location near [[area-51]] and the Nevada Test Site gave Reid both political constituency interest and direct exposure to the defense programs in his state.

## AATIP Funding

Reid worked with fellow senators **Ted Stevens** (Alaska, R) and **Dan Inouye** (Hawaii, D) — both powerful appropriations figures — to secure the black-budget line item that funded [[aatip]] beginning ~2007. The $22 million program was embedded in the defense appropriations bill without public disclosure. Died 2021.

## SAP Elevation Failure — Wrong Channel (Davis Account — LnAiNChnuEQ)

[[eric-davis]] provides detailed context for why Reid's attempt to elevate AAWSAP to SAP status failed:

Reid wrote a letter to [[william-lynn]] (Deputy Secretary of Defense under Obama). Davis's assessment:

> "Harry was using political muscle and you can't do that. It has to go through the security channels and the program management to create a justification and a need."

The executive branch is trained to recognize that **Capitol Hill is not the Taskmaster** on SAP creation — the president is. Reid had a Senate Majority Leader's political weight, but that doesn't substitute for the proper SAP creation process:
1. Security channels and program management build a formal justification
2. The need to elevate a TSSI program to SAP is documented
3. Presidential authorization (or NSA clearing through [[steven-hadley]]) is the correct authority

The correct path, per Davis: Reid should have contacted President [[george-w-bush]] directly ("met him face to face which would have been far more effective"), or worked through Hadley's NSA office. Had Bush personally requested the SAP, the DoD would have complied.

Davis does not question Reid's sincerity — he understands Reid genuinely wanted access to the material for legitimate oversight reasons. The failure was procedural, not motivational.
