# Luis Felipe San Salvador

## Background

Ecuadorian filmmaker (has directed films under the "Jamaica No Problem" brand). Comes from a line of 16th-century conquistadors from Ecuador — he lives on a large compound and has personal documents tracing his family back to the era of Atahuallpa's ransom and El Dorado legends.

[[jesse-michaels]] met him ~6–7 years ago and became friends after verifying his conquistador lineage was genuine.

## Claims

San Salvador conducts annual expeditions into the Amazon with a team of ~10 people searching for:
1. **El Dorado** — the legendary city of gold; he says he has inherited family maps pointing to its location
2. **The Tayos Cave / Tayos Library** — a cave system allegedly containing metallic artifacts of apparently advanced/anachronistic manufacture; also called the Tayos Library

His claim about [[neil-armstrong]]: Armstrong came to Ecuador after the moon landing and went searching for the Tayos Cave with a BBC film crew — but San Salvador says his *father* intentionally sent Armstrong in the wrong direction, and that the family knows the real location.
