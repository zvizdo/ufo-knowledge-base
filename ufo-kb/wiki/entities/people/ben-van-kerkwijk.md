# Ben Van Kerkwijk

## Background

YouTube-based researcher working alongside [[randall-carlson]]. Appeared on a podcast called "Lost Ancient Technology" (or similar) recorded in front of the pyramids, where he and others discussed [[graham-hancock]]'s updated position that the Great Pyramid was built during the 4th dynasty — a significant convergence from earlier claims of much greater age.

He also appeared on Joe Rogan's podcast to discuss the **[[hawara-labyrinth]]** — the vast underground complex near Hawara described by Herodotus, Pliny the Elder, Strabo, and Diodorus Siculus as containing 3,000 rooms (1,500 underground). His appearance brought the classical historical accounts of this structure to wide public attention.
