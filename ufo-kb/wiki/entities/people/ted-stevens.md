# Ted Stevens

US Senator from Alaska (R), serving 1968–2009. World War II US Army Air Forces pilot in the China-Burma-India Theater, where — per [[lue-elizondo]] in [[1f16VvXaSSE]] — he personally encountered [[foo-fighters]] as a wartime pilot. Cited by Elizondo as one of the three Senators who founded and funded [[aawsap]]: Stevens, [[harry-reid]] (Nevada), and [[daniel-inouye]] (Hawaii). His Foo Fighter witness experience is presented as the firsthand grounding for his interest in the program.

## Background

- US Senator (Alaska, 1968–2009); Senate President pro tempore 2003–2007.
- WWII pilot; flew transport missions over Japanese-occupied China; cited by Elizondo as a witness to UAP encounters during wartime aviation.
- Together with Reid and Inouye, provided the bipartisan Senate appropriations cover for [[aawsap]]'s ~$22M [[bigelow-aerospace]] contract studying UAP and paranormal phenomena at [[skinwalker-ranch]].

## Notable Claims

- **Firsthand WWII Foo Fighter encounter**: per Elizondo's recounting on [[1f16VvXaSSE]], Stevens openly acknowledged seeing Foo Fighters as a fighter pilot — placing him among the most senior US officials with documented firsthand UAP experience.
- The triple-Senator AAWSAP funding (Reid + Inouye + Stevens) is cited as the seed-condition for the program.
