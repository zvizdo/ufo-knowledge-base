# Richard Feynman

Nobel Prize–winning physicist (1965, for quantum electrodynamics). Best known for path integrals and his popular science communication. Feynman contributed to the [[manhattan-project]] at [[los-alamos]] and later became one of the most consequential theorists of the 20th century.

## Appearance in UAP Discourse

Feynman appears in [[5udx_SDdL3Y]] in two roles:

**1. 1957 UNC Chapel Hill Conference**
He attended the gravity research conference at [[unc-chapel-hill]] organized by [[agnew-bahnson]] and sponsored by Wright Air Development Center. Other attendees included [[john-wheeler]], [[bryce-dewitt]], and [[peter-bergmann]]. The conference's sponsorship by the Air Force's premier flight research center places Feynman, unknowingly or not, in the military-academic anti-gravity nexus of the late 1950s.

**2. Falsifiability Standard**
[[jesse-michaels]] invokes Feynman's standard — "not even wrong" — to critique UFO theorizing that cannot be falsified. He cites Feynman as one of his favorite physicists and uses this as motivation for focusing on the historical documentary record rather than unfalsifiable claims about NHI intent.
