# Jay Stratton

Defense intelligence official who ran (or led operations within) the UAP Task Force (UAPTF). Stratton served as the critical institutional link between the AAWSAP-era research network built by [[eric-davis]] and [[hal-puthoff]] and the modern disclosure effort led by [[david-grusch]].

## Role as Connector

[[eric-davis]] states in [[LnAiNChnuEQ]] that **Jay Stratton introduced him to David Grusch** and Grusch's government customer (a GS-15 who later promoted to SES-1, unnamed). Grusch at the time was the NRO liaison officer to the UAP Task Force and worked under this anonymous GS-15.

Davis flew to Colorado Springs to brief Stratton, Grusch, the GS-15, and Aerospace Corporation personnel who were supporting the NRO office co-located on the Aerospace campus. The briefing covered Davis's full dataset — accumulated through NIDS, BASS, AAWSAP/AATIP, and CIA interactions.

> "Jay Stratton introduced me to Dave he was the nro liaison an officer to the uaf so anyway I met both of them and I gave Dave all my data all my briefing information." — Eric Davis

## Significance

Stratton's introduction of Davis to Grusch is the operational link that explains how Grusch was able to identify 40 witnesses and provide detailed programmatic knowledge during his 2023 congressional testimony. Without Stratton's introduction, Davis's classified dataset would not have flowed to Grusch's investigation.
