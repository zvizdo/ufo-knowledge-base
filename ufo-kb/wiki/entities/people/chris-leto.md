# Chris Leto

UFO researcher and journalist; founder and host of the "Letto Files" YouTube channel and media project focused on UAP witness interviews, disclosure-movement coverage, and investigative UAP journalism. Operates in the independent-media tier of the UAP disclosure ecosystem.

**Greg Rogers coverage**: Leto was among the first journalists to conduct a substantive interview with [[greg-rogers]] following Rogers's initial public disclosure through [[josh-boswell]]'s UK Daily Mail article in May 2024. His interview with Rogers for the Letto Files provided additional detail on the [[1992-cape-canaveral-saucer]] incident beyond what appeared in the initial Daily Mail piece — including Rogers's account of the event timeline, his medical-officer credentialing context, and his reasons for coming forward after 30+ years of silence. The Leto interview contributed to the amplification sequence: Boswell (Daily Mail) → Leto (Letto Files) → [[jesse-michaels]] (American Alchemy) → broader disclosure ecosystem.

**Independent media role**: Leto represents the independent YouTube journalism tier of UAP coverage — channels that lack mainstream-media institutional backing but have sufficient audience and production quality to contribute meaningfully to the dissemination of witness testimony. This tier is faster and less constrained than legacy media but also carries less editorial vetting. The combination of initial mainstream break (Daily Mail) followed by independent amplification (Letto Files, American Alchemy) is the standard pattern for how credible UAP witness accounts reach broad audiences.
