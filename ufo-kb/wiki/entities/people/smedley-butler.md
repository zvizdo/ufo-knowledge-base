# Smedley Butler

Major General and Commandant of the United States Marine Corps; decorated WWI veteran; famous for his later book *War Is a Racket* (1935).

## The 1934 Business Plot

[[danny-sheehan]] cites Butler as a direct data point for [[brown-brothers-harriman]]'s willingness to use armed force domestically. In 1934, BBH-aligned Gilded Age interests — Carnegies, Rockefellers, and allied industrial families — recruited Butler to lead a major insurrection of World War I veterans against Franklin Roosevelt's government. The goal: oust FDR and install a fascist government that would "place all instrumentalities of the government at the disposal of the interests of the private corporations."

Butler exposed the plot to the McCormack-Dickstein Committee (a House special committee). His testimony was corroborated by other witnesses. The industrialists involved were never prosecuted.

## Significance in Sheehan's Framework

Sheehan uses this episode to demonstrate that the same network that financed Hitler (through BBH's Union Bank and Fritz Thyssen) was simultaneously attempting to install fascism domestically. The same network's postwar iteration created the CIA through Robert Lovett's NSC 5412 memo. Butler's exposure of the plot is the clearest public evidence that these families viewed government itself as a tool to be captured.
