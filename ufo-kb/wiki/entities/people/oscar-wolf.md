# Oscar Wolf

U.S. Army clerk typist assigned to [[project-blue-book]]'s special unit investigating the ~700 most credible and unexplained UAP cases. Gave [[danny-sheehan]] a deathbed account of witnessing his commanding officer conduct a telepathic communication session with an extraterrestrial being at [[s4]].

> ⚠ **Name conflict**: In episode [[2SQXAPCdmPE]], Sheehan names this same individual as "Albert Stein." In [[C4rSj5Aum7w]], he says "Oscar Wolf" — and himself adds "(or Oscar Lane?)" before settling on Oscar Wolf. Both accounts share identical details: Army clerk typist, Project Blue Book, hospice in northern Minnesota, S4 visit with his commanding officer, live ET seen through two-way mirror. [[jeremy-corbell]] and [[richard-dolan]] reportedly also interviewed him independently. The two names have not been reconciled; both pages exist pending clarification. See also: [[albert-stein]].

## Background

Wolf served as a clerk typist in a specialized unit of Project Blue Book focused on the subset of 700+ cases that resisted conventional explanation. He remained in this position for years as other staff cycled through. A shared membership in a fundamentalist Christian sect (Worldwide Church of God) with his commanding officer apparently created the trust relationship that led to the S4 visit.

## S4 Visit

Wolf accompanied his commanding officer to [[s4]] (distinct from [[area-51]]; accessed via elevator underground):
- Saw **three or four UAP craft** hovering in individual underground garage bays — consistent with [[bob-lazar]]'s account
- His commanding officer was brought in to "interview" a live ET
- Wolf **refused to enter the room** because he thought the being was demonic
- He observed through a **two-way mirror** while his commander and the ET engaged in apparent telepathic communication (no audible speech)

## ET Description

- Approximately **5.5 feet tall** — taller than typical "Grey" descriptions
- Large head, large eyes
- Wearing a jumpsuit provided by the military
- Described as cooperative and non-threatening (though Wolf found it spiritually alarming)
- Communication was entirely **telepathic**

## ET's Statements (from index cards Wolf was shown)

Military personnel showed Wolf index cards with recorded Q&A from prior ET interviews:
- "We are beings from **different star systems** in this galaxy. Our job is to monitor where life has started on planets inside the galaxy."
- When asked who assigned this mission: *"What you people would refer to as God, but it is very different from what you think."*
- Wolf personally rejected this framing as demonic and never reported the encounter until his deathbed.

## Independent Corroboration

Sheehan states that [[jeremy-corbell]] and [[richard-dolan]] separately interviewed Wolf at a later date, though he believes they "buried the lead" by not pressing on this specific incident as deeply as Sheehan did.
