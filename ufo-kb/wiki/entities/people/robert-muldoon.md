# Robert Muldoon

New Zealand Prime Minister at the time of the [[1978-kaikoura]] UFO incident. Knighted; later gave Coulthart an informal Christmas-season interview as part of a routine reporter duty at the NZ Herald.

## Key Statement

When a young [[ross-coulthart]] asked Muldoon about the Kaikoura incident, Muldoon chuckled and let slip something like: **"Yeah, the Americans wanted that one shut down pretty quickly."** He did not elaborate further, but the implication was that American pressure had influenced how quickly the NZ government moved to produce and publicize the official "squidboat lights" explanation.

## Significance

Muldoon's remark is the only known statement from a head of government suggesting direct American intervention in the suppression of a foreign ally's UAP investigation. It aligns with the broader Five Eyes pattern Coulthart documented — plausibly deniable favors and cross-service suppression.
