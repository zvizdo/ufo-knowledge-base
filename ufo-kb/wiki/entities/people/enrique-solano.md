# Enrique Solano

Astronomer at the Spanish Virtual Observatory, Spain. Colleague of [[beatriz-villarroel]] on the [[vasco]] project. Discovered one of the two most significant multi-transient examples in the Palomar archive: **three super-bright objects appearing simultaneously on July 19, 1952** — described by Villarroel as "the most beautiful example of all we have."

## Significance

July 19, 1952 is the first weekend of the [[1952-washington-dc-flyover]] — when unknown objects appeared on radar over the US capital. The coincidence of this date with one of the strongest Palomar plate events was pointed out by [[vasco]] team member Dave Alman, who also manages the project's media.
