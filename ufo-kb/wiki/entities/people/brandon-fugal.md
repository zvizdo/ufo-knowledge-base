# Brandon Fugal

Utah-based commercial real estate developer who purchased Skinwalker Ranch from Robert Bigelow's National Institute for Discovery Science in 2016 for approximately $4 million. Fugal has since opened the ranch to investigation teams and filming, producing the History Channel series *Secret of Skinwalker Ranch*.

## UAP and Anomalous Phenomena

Fugal was initially skeptical but describes experiencing anomalous phenomena personally on the property. He has described the ranch as a "living laboratory" and has worked with scientists and researchers to document the activity.

## AATIP Connection

[[aatip]] ran investigations at Skinwalker Ranch during the Bigelow era; the FOIA exemption from Bigelow's private ownership continued under Fugal's ownership, meaning some data remains non-public.
