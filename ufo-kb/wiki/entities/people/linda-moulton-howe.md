# Linda Moulton Howe

American journalist, documentary filmmaker, and UAP researcher best known for her investigations into cattle mutilations and their potential connection to UAP phenomena. She was working in Denver, Colorado at a TV station and actively investigating cattle mutilations throughout the Northwest / South Dakota / High Plains region in 1977 — the same year and geographic area as the [[1977-ellsworth-woods-abduction]].

## Cattle Mutilation Investigations

Howe was a pioneering investigator of cattle mutilations in the American West during the late 1970s and 1980s. The same terrain in which [[mario-woods]] was stationed — the South Dakota High Plains surrounding Ellsworth AFB, the Minuteman II fields, and the Sturgis corridor — was an active zone for mutilation reports during 1977.

Woods noted he tried to contact Howe around 2014 and she responded (got his email), suggesting a willingness to engage with his account. Whether they connected further is not stated.

## Significance

The geographic and temporal overlap between the 1977 cattle mutilation wave Howe investigated and the [[1977-ellsworth-woods-abduction]] is a data point worth tracking. Both phenomena involve:
- High Plains South Dakota / Northern Great Plains terrain
- The same year (1977)
- Possible NHI activity in proximity to nuclear weapons storage sites

The connection between cattle mutilations and UAP/NHI phenomena is documented across multiple researchers and remains contested but persistent.

## Doty Deception (1982)

In 1982, [[rick-doty]] contacted Howe and showed her fabricated documents including a presidential UFO briefing document and material related to "Project Garnett," allegedly involving accelerated human evolution via alien intervention. Howe was "over the moon" immediately afterward, telling cattle mutilation researcher Tom Adams that she had seen "all this great stuff — so important."

Greg Bishop describes this as Howe "taking the bait" — she was later shown to have been deceived, and in a subsequent documentary interview acknowledged she was tricked in this specific instance while insisting the broader UFO phenomenon is real. This episode is one of several documented instances of AFOSI/Doty using fabricated documents to manage UFO researchers during the same period as the Benowitz operation. (Greg Bishop, firsthand research, [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])
