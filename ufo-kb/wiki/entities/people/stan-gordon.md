# Stan Gordon

Stan Gordon is a Pennsylvania-based UFO and paranormal researcher who has spent decades investigating local incidents, most notably the [[1965-kecksburg]] crash. He is widely regarded as the most thorough researcher of the Kecksburg event.

## Kecksburg Investigation

Gordon's work on Kecksburg involves extensive local witness interviews, document gathering, and physical site investigation. [[richard-dolan]] says Gordon "is still around" and "I love Stan. He's really dove into this and I think he's got the goods on it." Dolan's assessment is that Kecksburg involved something genuinely anomalous — "I think it was important. It wasn't just a satellite and maybe it was something more. Maybe it was a craft."

Gordon's investigation also documented the story of local radio journalist [[frank-murphy]], whose career was destroyed after attempting to cover the Kecksburg story — a pattern of suppression consistent with other crash cases.

## Broader Research

Beyond Kecksburg, Gordon has investigated numerous paranormal incidents in Pennsylvania, including cases of UAP and Bigfoot activity — sometimes in proximity — in western Pennsylvania, particularly in areas near the Chestnut Ridge.
