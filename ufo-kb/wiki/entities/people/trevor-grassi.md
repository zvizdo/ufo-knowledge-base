# Trevor Grassi

Independent researcher studying the [[giza-plateau]] underground. Per [[filippo-biondi]] in [[zrmG2jBckgk]], Grassi was the first to direct Biondi's attention to the specific blocked shafts between the [[khafre-pyramid]] and the [[great-sphinx]] as potential entrance points to the main underground facility.

## Key Contributions

- **Shaft tip**: Grassi identified surface shafts on the Giza plateau causeway as secondary entrances to the underground system — these are currently blocked by debris.
- **Independent corridor discovery**: Conducting his own parallel research approximately 100 m from the same shafts, Grassi identified what he calls a "corridor of rooms" that descends from the surface and connects at the bottom to a network of tunnels.
- **Personal conviction**: Biondi describes Grassi as "100% convinced" and eager to physically enter the underground tunnels.

## Significance

Grassi's independent corroboration of Biondi's SAR findings — from a different methodology and research angle — represents a second data point supporting the existence of underground interconnected structures at Giza. The convergence of independent researchers pointing to the same shaft locations adds weight to the [[khafre-research-project]]'s focus area.
