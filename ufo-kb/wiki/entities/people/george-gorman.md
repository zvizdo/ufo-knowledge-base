# George F. Gorman

North Dakota Air National Guard F-51 Mustang pilot; civilian occupation: construction manager. Pilot of the landmark "Gorman Dogfight" (October 1, 1948, Fargo, ND), a 27-minute aerial engagement with a luminous orb that consistently outmaneuvered his fighter at full throttle.

Gorman's dual background (rated military pilot with professional civilian employment) was noted by investigators as elevating his credibility rating above typical military-only observers. The engagement was corroborated by Hector Airport tower controllers and ground witnesses.
