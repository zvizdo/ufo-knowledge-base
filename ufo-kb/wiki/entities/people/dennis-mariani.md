# Dennis Mariani

[[bob-lazar]]'s official supervisor and escort at [[s4]] during Lazar's assignment (~1987–1989). All information about Dennis comes from Lazar's account.

## Role at S4

Dennis escorted Lazar in and out of S4 at every visit. He was described as "militant" and "robotic" — the polar opposite of the casual, enthusiastic [[barry-castillo]]. He enforced strict compartmentalization: Lazar was his responsibility, and Dennis tracked his access with visible precision via the hand-scanner entry system.

Dennis also arranged for Lazar to observe a craft test flight — an unusual privilege that Lazar believes was either a reward or part of a deliberate indoctrination. The test flight involved a craft lifting silently from outside a hangar, emitting corona discharge, hovering, and becoming optically invisible as Lazar walked beneath it.

## Post-Disclosure Casino Incident

After Lazar's public disclosure through [[george-knapp]] in 1989, Dennis reached out and arranged to meet Lazar at a Las Vegas casino. At the meeting, Dennis refused to make eye contact with Lazar — standing nearby while [[gene-huff]] observed him, then disappearing.

When Lazar returned home after the casino meeting, items had been removed from his house. The casino meeting appears to have been a diversion, with Dennis either acting voluntarily or being used as a lure to draw Lazar out while others searched his home.

Lazar does not know what was taken. He confirmed the theft was "consequential" but will not say what it was on camera.

## Status

Lazar believes Dennis died not long before the episode was filmed ("I think he's dead now"). The basis for this belief is not elaborated. If correct, Dennis cannot corroborate or deny the S4 account.
