# Edwin Abbott

English schoolmaster, theologian, and author (1838–1926). Headmaster of the City of London School. Best known to the KB for *[[flatland-abbott|Flatland: A Romance of Many Dimensions]]* (1884), a satirical novella narrated by "A. Square" — a two-dimensional being living in Flatland — describing the visit of a three-dimensional Sphere who tries to explain the existence of higher dimensions to a populace who can only perceive their own plane. The book became the foundational popularization of higher-dimensional geometry and remains a standard reference in physics and mathematics teaching.

**KB relevance — the dimensional-intersection analogy**: [[jacques-vallee]] in [[fyX8V1XXmQM|the American Alchemy interview]] uses the *Flatland* model to explain hyperdimensional UFO encounters: a 3D object intersecting a 2D plane appears only as a sliver (a line segment, the edge of the object); a 4D hypercube intersecting our 3D space would appear much smaller on the outside than on the inside, because we only see its 3D cross-section. This is the framework Vallée uses to interpret a San Jose witness whose disc was "the same size as her house" externally but contained a "big round room" the size of a movie theater internally. The analogy generalizes Carl Sagan's tesseract demonstration (a tesseract's 3D shadow appears as two nested cubes connected by edges, but the actual 4D hypercube would have all edges of equal length).

**Why Abbott matters specifically vs. the abstract math**: The popular accessibility of *Flatland* gave the field a shared vocabulary. Without Abbott's narrative framing, talking about the 4D-3D intersection as a UFO phenomenology would require non-trivial mathematical setup. With it, the analogy is teachable in two sentences. This is why Vallée invokes Abbott directly — the cognitive lift to "hyperdimensional UFO" is much shorter once the listener has the *Flatland* model.

**Theological dimension**: Abbott himself wrote substantial theological works alongside *Flatland*; the novella's higher-dimensional being who speaks "in code" to Flatlanders has been read as a meditation on the limits of theological knowledge — knowing God being structurally analogous to a 2D being knowing a 3D one. This connects to the [[ufo-religion-convergence]] thread: hyperdimensional NHI may be theologically-significant entities whose interactions with 3D humans necessarily appear partial / cryptic / coded.
