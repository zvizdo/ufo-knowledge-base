# Uri Geller

Uri Geller rose to international prominence in the 1970s claiming psychokinetic abilities (bending spoons, stopping watches). More significantly for UAP research, he was tested at Stanford Research Institute (SRI) by [[hal-puthoff]] and Russell Targ under a CIA contract — the same program that produced the [[stargate-program]] remote viewing apparatus. The published SRI results in *Nature* (1974) reported positive findings; subsequent investigators disputed the methodology and security.

[[andrija-puharich]], who first brought Geller to the US and wrote a book about his abilities, claimed Geller had a UAP contact experience in Israel. Geller himself has asserted contact with non-human intelligence that allegedly guides his abilities.

## The Nine — 1971

In 1970–71, Puharich traveled to Israel after hearing about Geller. He hypnotized Geller there, and the same Nine entities that had been channeled through [[dr-venad]] in 1952 re-emerged through Geller. Puharich was apparently shocked. This re-emergence happened while Puharich was still actively running [[intellect]], his covert thought-transmission research company. [[greg-malazzi]] believes Geller may have been a willing or unwilling experimental subject for the TD-100 device — that what Geller experienced as "the Nine" may have been transmitted thoughts.

## Geller's Own Account

In a 2019 radio interview (possibly since removed), Geller was open about being "scared" during the Puharich period. He credited Puharich with helping him reach America and get his career started. But he consistently used the phrase "Puharich made me believe this" about the Nine, and mentioned suspecting Puharich had slipped him mushrooms. Despite this, Malazzi — after extensive research — believes Geller genuinely had some anomalous ability, estimating he could perform psychically "maybe 60% of the time."

## Trigger Effect

Geller's TV appearances in the early-to-mid 1970s apparently triggered spontaneous psychic phenomena in children around the world (spoon-bending, poltergeist activity), which led to [[andrija-puharich]]'s [[space-kids]] program.
