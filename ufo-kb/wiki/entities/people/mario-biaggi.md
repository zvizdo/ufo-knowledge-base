# Mario Biaggi

Mario Biaggi (1917–2015) served as US Representative from New York's 10th congressional district from 1969 to 1988. Before his congressional career, he was a decorated NYPD officer — holder of more citations for bravery than any other officer in NYPD history at the time of his retirement. He served on House committees with jurisdiction relevant to defense and public safety matters.

**Congressional UAP context**: Biaggi is cited alongside [[jay-rockefeller]], [[richard-shelby]], and [[ron-wyden]] as a congressional figure who sought information about UAP programs through oversight positions. His tenure (1969–1988) covers the period of the [[condon-report]] (1969), [[project-blue-book]] closure (1969), and the early decades of post-Blue Book civilian UAP research — a period when several congressmembers attempted to investigate what the Air Force had been withholding.

**Resignation**: Biaggi resigned from Congress in 1988 following a conviction on federal extortion charges related to the Wedtech scandal.
