# Larry O'Brien

Chairman of the Democratic National Committee (DNC) when the Watergate break-in occurred in June 1972. His DNC office was the primary target. Prior to this role, O'Brien had spent approximately **20 years as [[howard-hughes]]'s chief lobbyist in Washington** — making him a critical node in [[danny-sheehan]]'s explanation of Watergate's real motive.

## The Watergate Motive (per Sheehan)

The conventional question: why did Nixon order a break-in when he was far ahead in the polls? Sheehan's answer:

- O'Brien, as former Hughes lobbyist, knew the funding chain through which Nixon had used [[howard-hughes]] to set up the [[s-force]] (the assassination team that killed JFK)
- Nixon feared O'Brien would reveal that Nixon had organized the team responsible for the Kennedy assassination — even though the S-Force was originally commissioned for Castro
- No politician can survive that revelation in an election year
- Nixon ordered the break-in to determine whether O'Brien was planning to use this information against him
- [[james-mccord]] — Dulles's favorite CIA operative, on retainer (index 4) — led the burglary team; he thought he was simply running a routine CIA domestic covert operation

Sheehan's legal work on McCord's defense during Watergate gave him insight into McCord's mindset and motivations.
