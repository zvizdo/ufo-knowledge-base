# Wein Brener

US Army colonel reportedly connected to the **Foreign Technology Division**. Two separate threads link him to UAP material:

## Deathbed Statement — Utah Facility

[[grant-cameron]] and Anthony Bragalia researched Brener's alleged deathbed disclosure. Before his death, Brener reportedly stated that **dead extraterrestrials are housed at a Utah facility** — a statement that supports the hypothesis that [[dougway-proving-grounds]] (or a nearby Utah installation) functions as the primary biological storage site for non-human materials, displacing or supplementing [[wright-patterson]] and [[area-51]].

This aligns with [[steven-greer]]'s claim that 35–40% of Area 51 programs relocated to Utah, and with [[uap-gerb]]'s research into Dougway as the "new Area 51."

## Connection to the 1971 Holloman AFB Filming Project

Brener is described in [[nTiFs8LudUo]] as having approached filmmaker **Allan Sandler** ([[allan-sandler]]) and Robert Eminger ([[bob-emenegger]]) about documenting the alleged [[1964-holloman-afb-landing]] footage — a film showing landed craft and humanoid occupants at [[holloman-air-force-base]] in 1971. The documentary project was never publicly completed, though Sandler reportedly viewed the footage.
