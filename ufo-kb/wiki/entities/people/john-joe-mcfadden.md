# John Joe McFadden

Quantum biologist and author at the University of Surrey, UK. Most known for the **CEMI theory** (Conscious Electromagnetic Information field theory): the proposition that consciousness is not *generated* by the brain's neural activity but *is* the brain's electromagnetic field, experienced from the inside.

## CEMI Theory

McFadden argues:

1. The brain generates a complex, integrated electromagnetic field via its electrical neural activity.
2. This EM field is not a byproduct — it feeds back onto neurons, influencing which fire and which do not.
3. Conscious experience is what the integrated EM field "feels like" from the inside.

This is distinct from mere correlation between brain activity and consciousness: McFadden's claim is ontological identity — the field *is* the experience.

## Distinction from Sheldrake's Position

[[rupert-sheldrake]] explicitly disagrees with McFadden on one key point: Sheldrake holds that the electromagnetic field is the *interface* or *bridge* between morphic fields (the fields of mind) and physical matter — not that EM is consciousness itself. The distinction: McFadden collapses consciousness into electromagnetism; Sheldrake keeps them as separate ontological categories linked by EM.

Despite this distinction, the two converge on the practical implication: **the electromagnetic field of any sufficiently organized system (brain, sun, galaxy) could be the substrate or interface of that system's consciousness.**

## Implications for Sun Consciousness

[[rupert-sheldrake]] notes that on McFadden's CEMI principle, the Sun's vast electromagnetic field — spanning the entire solar system through the solar wind and heliosphere — could *be* the Sun's consciousness, experienced from within. Sheldrake uses this as supporting framing for his "Is the Sun Conscious?" paper, even while disagreeing about whether EM *is* consciousness or merely *interfaces with* it.
