# Charlie Foltz

One of the four witnesses in the [[1976-allagash-abduction]]. Neighbor of the Weiner twins. At the time of the 1976 event, Charlie was studying ceramics at the Program in Artisanry (co-run by Boston University and the Benjamin Franklin Institute) under master potters Bill Saxs (West Hadley, Massachusetts) and Richard Hirsch. US Navy veteran with nuclear submarine background. Martial artist — first-degree black belt in aikido as primary discipline, with training also in Shotokan karate, goju-ryu, Isshin-ryu, and Pukalan; had previously defended himself against seven armed attackers in Boston. Primary speaker in [[QkuDvTFN7ms]] alongside [[jim-weiner]].

## Prior UAP Encounter — USS Holland (May 1970)

Three years before Allagash, while serving in the US Navy aboard USS Holland off the coast of Spain:
- Approximately 24 witnesses observed an unidentified aerial object
- The on-duty lieutenant immediately ordered all witnesses to say nothing and file no report: "None of that happened"
- Charlie's nuclear submarine work gave him a technical reference frame for recognizing that the Allagash craft's propulsion was operating on principles outside the US military's technological arsenal

## The Allagash Encounter

August 1976 on Allagash Lake: Charlie used a large camping flashlight to signal SOS (three short, three long, three short) at the luminous sphere. The craft responded instantaneously with a neon blue hollow conical beam that extended downward toward the canoes. [[chuck-rak]] became catatonic. Charlie had no conscious memory of the next ~3 hours.

**On-craft account (recovered under hypnotic regression):**
- First awareness: looking at his feet and realizing all his clothing was gone; "Where the hell am I?"
- Cold, lying on a sterile surface ("very sterile, like an emergency room"); four beings present
- A panel descended from above his head covering his full torso; a focused blue light descended from the panel — not diffuse, with "a definite dimensional quality" — conducting a systematic scan
- Told not to resist, not to fight, that he was not in danger; accepted the communication
- Found his previous self-defense experience useless: "I'm as helpless as an infant. I couldn't do diddly to protect myself."
- Mentally screamed at Jack, Jim, and Chuck (seated passively on a bench nearby) for help but could not articulate words or move

**Propulsion explanation:**
- While being ushered toward the exit, Charlie asked the beings about their propulsion system
- They explained via a submarine analogy: a submarine manages depth via the air ratio between inner and outer hull; the craft modulates gravitational fields analogously — matching fields to transition from denser to less dense gravity, "like two magnets of the same polarity repelling each other"
- Charlie interprets the 2004 Nimitz encounter (dropping from ~81,000 feet to 24 inches above water in under one second) as consistent with this propulsion model
- The craft departed at Mach 1+ speed without any sonic boom

## Post-Event Career Shift

Charlie shifted from a planned career in art education to medical art — working with UV light sources, UV film, infrared film, and infrared imaging of the human body. He explicitly connects these UV/IR scanning techniques to the scanning technology he experienced aboard the craft. He frames this trajectory as influenced by the encounter, not coincidental.

## Gordon Cooper Connection

At a 1995/96 Portsmouth, Maine conference where Charlie and [[jim-weiner]] also spoke, Mercury 7 astronaut [[gordon-cooper]] shared his 1957 military base encounter (tic-tac craft on tripod legs, films confiscated) and Mercury orbit green-object encounter. Charlie spent the weekend with Cooper.

## John Norsine Connection

[[john-norsine]], an engineer with a locked patent for biofeedback-based craft piloting (and the concept of "brain prints"), personally told Charlie that he had been at the Allagash in August 1976 conducting a TTR (tag-trace-retrieve) exercise testing subcutaneous bio-telemetry implants. Norsine claimed to have read their ranger incident report on the same day it was filed. Norsine later died under suspicious circumstances after sending Charlie a coded email predicting his own death.
