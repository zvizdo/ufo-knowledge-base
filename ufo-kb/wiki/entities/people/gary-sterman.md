# Gary Sterman

Bible teacher (in his 80s at the time of the Alberino interview) and personal friend of [[tim-alberino]]. Comes from a family with aerospace industry connections — family members reportedly worked with Lockheed Martin and "have been in the programs." Alberino describes him as "a phenomenal Bible teacher and a really good friend of mine."

## UFO Encounter

At some point in his earlier life, Sterman was delivering a prop aircraft (a Cessna) on a routine business flight — possibly from Lubbock, Texas to Dallas (Alberino was uncertain of details). The aircraft's electrical components failed mid-flight, leaving him in a potentially fatal situation.

Key events:
1. He noticed a shiny object approximately a mile away that he initially thought was another aircraft
2. He heard a **voice** that he described as "tour guide" in tone, saying "If you look to your left, you'll see a UFO"
3. Looking again, the craft was now directly off his left wing — a **walnut-shaped** silver object with no seams, no rivets, no markings
4. He could not see the beings inside but felt an intense **"fraternal affection"** — as though the beings were familiar with him and there to protect him
5. The craft flew alongside him and **guided him** for the remainder of the flight until he landed safely at the destination airport
6. He arrived substantially late and with **insufficient fuel for the time spent in the air** — approximately 3 hours of anomalous time
7. The aircraft mechanic the next day, upon examining the fault, told him: "I don't know how you landed this. You don't land a plane like this." — he should by rights have died

Upon landing, Sterman's life trajectory had completely changed: he had intended to be a pilot but now knew unequivocally he was supposed to be a Bible teacher. He has devoted his life to biblical study and teaching since.

When Alberino asked what he thought was in the craft, Sterman's answer was: **"Angels."**

## Significance

Alberino uses this account as a counterpoint to darker UFO narratives — evidence that not all encounters are threatening, and that the NHI landscape includes what the Bible describes as "the loyal sons of God" who can act in ways that protect and redirect human lives. This aligns with his Christocentric taxonomy in which the "good angels" use technology and interact physically with humans.
