# Jason Sands

## Background

Jason Sands is a whistleblower who appeared in [[james-fox]]'s documentary [[the-program-fox]] and subsequently on the [[joe-rogan]] podcast. He claims to have worked as perimeter security for a classified UAP program.

## Key Claims

**Blue alien at Nellis**: Sands describes seeing a blue alien being at [[nellis-test-range]], Nevada, while on perimeter security duty for the program.

**NHI retrieval threat**: Sands stated that the non-human intelligences "want their stuff back, and they're going to kill everybody involved in the program if they don't get it." [[matthew-pines]] interprets this as evidence that the program acquired craft without NHI consent and that NHI operates a regulatory or punitive framework regarding their technology.

## Significance

His testimony, combined with [[jake-barber]]'s retrieval accounts, supports the hypothesis that the legacy UAP program operates in a contested relationship with the entities whose craft it has acquired — not a cooperative exchange but a unilateral seizure. The "kill" threat implies NHI have both the capability and willingness to act against program participants.
