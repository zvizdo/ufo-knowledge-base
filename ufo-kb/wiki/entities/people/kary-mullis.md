# Kary Mullis

Nobel Prize-winning biochemist (Nobel Prize in Chemistry, 1993) who invented the **polymerase chain reaction (PCR)** — the technique that enabled modern molecular biology, genetic testing, and forensic DNA analysis.

## Alien Encounter Claim

Mullis reported a personal encounter with what he described as an alien entity — often characterized as a glowing or fluorescent raccoon-like being that he encountered during a night walk at his rural California cabin. The encounter involved missing time; he wrote about it in his memoir *Dancing Naked in the Mind Field* (1998).

## Significance in This KB

[[jesse-michaels]] mentions Mullis in [[9PJJiut0Iuw]] alongside [[francis-crick]]'s reported visionary/drug-assisted insight during the discovery of DNA's double-helix structure. [[graham-hancock]] uses both examples to argue that:
1. Altered or anomalous states of consciousness are not antithetical to scientific discovery — they have contributed to it
2. Science should stop "drug-shaming" researchers who report psychedelic or visionary experiences
3. The academic establishment's treatment of consciousness experiences as incompatible with credibility is inconsistent with the history of science

Mullis himself was outspoken about his LSD use and his skepticism of mainstream scientific consensus on topics from AIDS causation to global warming — figures that made him either a freethinker or a crank depending on the observer.

## Additional Appearance (q7Czo77qhHA)

[[jesse-michaels]] mentions Mullis in [[q7Czo77qhHA]] in connection with the PCR technique and adds new details about his alien encounter: Mullis reportedly **woke up in a shed** and **spoke with a being** — a characterization slightly different from (but compatible with) the fluorescent raccoon encounter described in earlier accounts. Jesse notes Mullis was a surfer, interested in edge-of-science topics, and skeptical of the HIV narrative. The HIV skepticism is notable: Montagnier (HIV genome discoverer, Nobel laureate) separately expressed skepticism about COVID vaccine safety — suggesting the pattern of eminent virologists questioning the HIV/COVID official narratives recurs.

The Mullis mention in this episode is in the context of connecting the PCR technique — which became central to the COVID testing debate — to fringe science and unconventional consciousness experiences.
