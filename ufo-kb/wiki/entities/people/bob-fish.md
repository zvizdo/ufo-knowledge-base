# Bob Fish

Aerospace contractor who appears in the Wikileaks email archive in an exchange with [[john-podesta]], Clinton chief of staff and longtime Washington Democratic Party operative. The email addresses [[fast-walkers]] — objects detected by NORAD's DSP (Defense Support Program) satellite network transiting through Earth's outer orbital detection layer at velocities and trajectories inconsistent with known space debris, satellites, or re-entry vehicles.

**Fast-walkers significance**: The "fast-walkers" category is a classified NORAD designation for objects detected traversing the cislunar or near-Earth environment at unusual velocities. DSP satellites, originally deployed to detect Soviet ICBM launches by their infrared signature, have also recorded objects without corresponding missile-launch profiles — objects moving from space toward Earth (or in other unusual trajectories) without being catalogued satellites or known re-entries. This category of anomalous detection — at orbital and near-orbital altitudes rather than in the atmosphere — is distinct from atmospheric UAP and implies objects entering (or exiting) Earth's gravitational well.

**Podesta connection**: The exchange between Fish and Podesta indicates that awareness of the fast-walkers category was present in the contractor/political advisor nexus — a circle that was simultaneously engaged in the [[1995-rockefeller-ufo-initiative]] disclosure push during the Clinton era. Podesta's awareness of this classified UAP category, combined with his later role as Clinton campaign chair and his public statements about UAP, places him in a consistent pattern of informed engagement with the UAP question across multiple decades.

**Evidentiary significance**: The email's appearance in the Wikileaks archive provides a documented instance of a classified UAP category being discussed by an aerospace insider and a senior political figure in private correspondence. The existence of the email does not prove fast-walkers are NHI; it confirms that individuals with relevant access were aware of the category and treating it as a legitimate subject of discussion.
