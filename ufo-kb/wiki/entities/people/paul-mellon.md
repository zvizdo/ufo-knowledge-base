# Paul Mellon

One of the founders of the CIA (previously associated with OSS). At the end of World War II, Mellon was part of a top-secret trip to Czechoslovakia alongside his colleague [[allen-dulles]] (who went on to become the third director of the CIA).

## The German Flying Disc Account

According to his grandson [[john-warner-iv]], who relayed the story in [[G7Ns4Aq1tVc]]:

> "My grandfather said: 'Look, we were in a facility — a hangar — and we saw a German flying disc.'"

When Warner asked whether it was the cobbled-together disc with six BMW jet engines (a common reference to the Schriever/Miethe designs), Mellon "laughed and said no."

This implies the craft was more advanced than the known German saucer prototypes. The location — a Czechoslovak hangar at war's end — is consistent with the [[kammler-stab]] and [[kammler-stab]] complex where German secret weapons programs operated under [[hans-kammler]].

## Fascist Sympathizer Network (Greer, areO7Mej44E)

In [[areO7Mej44E]], [[steven-greer]] adds a more explicit characterization of Mellon's political context. Per Greer (relaying what [[john-warner-iv]] told him):

When Greer first walked into John Warner IV's apartment at the Watergate, Warner said: *"My whole family are fascists."* Warner then explained that his grandfather Paul Mellon was a major supporter of Adolf Hitler and fascism — alongside Henry Ford, IBM's Watson, and Prescott Bush. The network included **Union Bank**, **Brown Brothers Harriman**, and **Chase Manhattan** (the latter later chaired by [[david-rockefeller]]).

Greer frames the post-WWII intelligence secrecy apparatus as a direct continuation of this fascist-sympathizer billionaire network: after the war, "those people sort of covertly surfaced again." The MAGIC/[[majestic-12]] control structure in Greer's account traces directly to this network.

## The Disc Retrieval — Electrogravidic, Not Ramjet

Greer adds additional specificity in [[areO7Mej44E]]: the disc Mellon retrieved was **electrogravidic**, not the known ramjet/six-BMW-engine prototype. Greer explicitly distinguishes the two when discussing this with Warner:

> *"Oh, is that the one cobbled together with six BMW jet engines?" [laughs] "No."*

This is consistent with [[john-warner-iv]]'s account in [[G7Ns4Aq1tVc]]. The craft was an advanced German electrogravidic prototype. It was not stable — it took the US program from 1945 to October 1954 to master gravity control (per [[richard-foch]]).

## Significance

Mellon's account, transmitted via family oral history, adds a senior intelligence-community figure as a firsthand witness to German flying disc hardware — distinct from the theoretical or experimental accounts (Schauberger's diary, Schriever's claims). As a CIA co-founder, Mellon would have been part of the intelligence community's post-war technology assessment missions.
