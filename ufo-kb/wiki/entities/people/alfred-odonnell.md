# Alfred O'Donnell

Alfred O'Donnell was a **senior manager at [[egng]]** who, in 2001, disclosed to journalist [[george-knapp]] that EG&G had recovered a flying saucer in New Mexico and had a live being in their possession with humanlike features.

## Disclosure

The disclosure was reported by [[jesse-michaels]] in [[TNtlzEnl8rA]] as part of a broader examination of EG&G's role in the UFO secrecy architecture. O'Donnell was described as a senior EG&G manager (not a field technician), making his account organizationally credible if accurate.

Key claims:
- EG&G recovered a **flying saucer in New Mexico**
- EG&G held a **live being** in possession that had humanlike features

If accurate, this represents a direct EG&G crash-retrieval-and-containment operation — consistent with EG&G's broader management role at Area 51, Sandia Labs, and the Nevada Test Site, all of which are cited as probable custody sites in the wider UAP discourse.

## Context

O'Donnell's disclosure fits a pattern: EG&G's operational reach covered the facilities most plausibly hosting recovered non-human technology. A senior manager with operational knowledge of special projects would have access to this category of information as a matter of daily function rather than a sensational leak.
