# Jean-Pierre Petit

French physicist affiliated with CNRS (Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique) and ONERA (Office National d'Études et de Recherches Aérospatiales). Developed a magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) propulsion framework that became the central physical hypothesis in the [[cometa-report|COMETA Report]] (Chapter 8).

## MHD Propulsion Model

Petit's MHD model proposes that a UAP generates a high-voltage plasma sheath around its hull and uses magnetohydrodynamic forces to accelerate the plasma boundary layer, effectively eliminating aerodynamic drag and sonic-boom generation. COMETA's Part 2 analyzes this framework at length and concludes it is consistent with:
- Observed silent supersonic flight
- Electromagnetic interference with nearby vehicles and electronic systems
- Absence of a sonic boom at supersonic speeds
- Plasma-like visual appearance (luminous objects) during high-speed maneuvers

Petit published early work on MHD propulsion in *Le mur du silence* (Belin, 1983) and in *Techniques avancées* (ENSTA, 1988), both cited in the COMETA bibliography.
