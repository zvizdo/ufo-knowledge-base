# Robert Bigelow

Nevada-based aerospace entrepreneur; founder of Bigelow Aerospace and the **National Institute for Discovery Science (NIDS)**. A significant amount of [[aatip]] and [[aawsap]] investigative activity occurred at Bigelow facilities — most notably [[skinwalker-ranch]], which Bigelow purchased in 1996 to conduct controlled paranormal/UAP research.

## Background

Made his fortune in real estate (Budget Suites of America). Funded aerospace research through Bigelow Aerospace (inflatable space station modules). Has spent tens of millions of dollars on UAP and paranormal research privately.

## NIDS and AATIP

- NIDS (later dissolved) was the institutional vehicle for Bigelow's private UAP research, staffed by figures including [[john-alexander]] (ret. Army Colonel).
- The AAWSAP contract (the program that preceded/surrounded AATIP) was reportedly awarded to Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies — making Bigelow a government contractor for UAP research.
- Bigelow has a political relationship with [[harry-reid]], which facilitated the AATIP funding through Nevada congressional channels.

## FOIA Exemption by Design

[[brandon-fugel]] (current Skinwalker Ranch owner) confirmed in [[7W7iOt57fOo]] that the decision to run AATIP under Bigelow Aerospace was deliberately structured to make all resulting data both classified and FOIA-exempt through proprietary protections. The system was "set up to be that way on purpose." All AATIP investigation data from 2007–2012 remains inaccessible under both classification rules and FOIA exemptions. See [[uap-private-sector-foia-exemption]].

## Alien Contact and Aerospace Motivation (iPxbILOMQ08)

[[roger-leir]] told [[steve-coburn]] that Bigelow founded his aerospace company because **aliens encountered him in the desert** and told him to **"meet them in space."** Coburn relays this as secondhand from Leir, so it's hearsay-of-hearsay, but notable given the source.

Additional detail: the Bigelow Aerospace website reportedly displayed a **small alien head** — Coburn and the host interpret this as Bigelow subtly announcing his NHI orientation without explicit statement.

Bigelow was also at one point going to **hire Steve Coburn** for his company. That arrangement fell through. Coburn believes government pressure was the cause — a pattern he says occurred multiple times when wealthy individuals expressed interest in supporting his implant research.
