# Donald Hornig

Presidential science advisor to three consecutive US presidents: Eisenhower, Kennedy, and Johnson. Later served as president of Brown University.

## Background

A chemist and physicist who worked on the Manhattan Project. Notable roles:

- Designed the **electronic triggers** for the plutonium implosion bomb (the "Fat Man" design, used at Nagasaki and tested at Trinity).
- The night before the **Trinity test** (July 16, 1945): J. Robert Oppenheimer gave Hornig a sidearm and had him stand guard over the assembled bomb in the tower during a lightning storm. He was babysitting the world's first nuclear device alone at 60 feet during a thunderstorm.
- His wife **[[lilli-hornig]]** appears in the *Oppenheimer* film (2023); the line "They don't teach typing at Harvard" — her response when being interviewed at Los Alamos and asked how fast she typed — was used verbatim. Kevin Knuth confirms this from a family dinner visit in Rhode Island before her death.

## Connection to Kecksburg

[[kevin-knuth]] (his father's cousin's relation) found an **official government document** about the [[1965-kecksburg]] crash listing Donald Hornig as **present on site**. As presidential science advisor to President Johnson, who was in office in December 1965, it would be procedurally consistent for Hornig to be dispatched to or briefed at the scene of an anomalous event of potential national security import. [[eric-walker]] (president of Penn State, alleged MJ-12 adjacent figure) was also reportedly present.

Knuth never met Hornig; asked Lilli about Kecksburg directly but she knew nothing.
