# Chris Langan

American autodidact (b. 1952), widely known for a claimed exceptionally high IQ. Developed the **Cognitive-Theoretic Model of the Universe (CTMU)** — a self-referential framework positing that the universe is a self-configuring, self-processing language (a "conspansive" system) in which mind and reality are isomorphic. The CTMU attempts to derive the universe's structure from purely logical/syntactic constraints without physical axioms.

**Malmstrom UFO sighting**: In the [[IWui5cBkwoE]] episode, Langan mentions that he personally witnessed UAP near [[malmstrom-air-force-base]] while working for the Forest Service in the area. This places him in the category of incidental civilian corroborators of the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] — independent civilian observers who saw anomalous craft near nuclear missile sites without prior UAP interest or agenda.

**CTMU and UAP consciousness frameworks**: Langan's CTMU is referenced in the context of [[kevin-knuth]]'s academic UAP research because both sit at the intersection of consciousness and physics. [[knuth-academic-uap-science-cluster]] includes consciousness-as-fundamental frameworks as part of the theoretical landscape for understanding UAP propulsion and NHI cognition. Langan is not a physicist and the CTMU has not been published in peer-reviewed physics venues; its KB role is as a maximally ambitious amateur formulation of the consciousness-is-primary thesis.

**Credibility tier**: Langan is an autodidact whose work circulates in alternative intellectual spaces. The IQ claims (frequently reported as exceptionally high) are based on tests administered outside standard psychometric contexts and are not peer-verified. The CTMU itself has not been formally refuted but also has not received serious engagement from professional physicists or mathematicians. In the KB, he is documented rather than endorsed.
