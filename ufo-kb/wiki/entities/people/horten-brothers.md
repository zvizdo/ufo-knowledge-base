# Horten Brothers

> *Stub page — expand with primary source import.*

Reimar (1915–1994) and Walter (1913–1998) Horten were German aircraft designers who pioneered tailless flying-wing designs for the Luftwaffe during WWII. Their most advanced design, the **Horten Ho 229** (Ho IX), was a twin-jet flying-wing fighter-bomber that achieved significant stealth properties through its wood-charcoal laminate construction.

## The Ho 229

- First flight: 1944
- Twin Junkers Jumo 004 turbojets
- Low radar cross-section due to charcoal-impregnated wood laminate — Northrop Grumman's 2008 analysis confirmed significant radar absorption
- The swept-wing, tailless planform superficially resembles disc or delta-wing UAP shapes in early sighting reports
