# Neil deGrasse Tyson

**Neil deGrasse Tyson** is an astrophysicist and director of the Hayden Planetarium at the American Museum of Natural History. He is one of the most prominent US science communicators.

## UAP Relevance

Tyson is consistently cited in UAP discourse as a representative institutional skeptic who dismisses UAP witness accounts and resists taking the evidence seriously. [[greg-rogers]] explicitly criticizes Tyson in his interview as exemplifying the credentialed-scientist dismissal pattern.

This contrasts with [[avi-loeb]] (Harvard astrophysicist) who represents an alternative academic approach — taking UAP evidence seriously as a scientific question while maintaining rigor.
