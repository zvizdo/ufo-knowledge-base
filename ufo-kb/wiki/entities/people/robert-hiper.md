# Robert Hiper

USAF officer (possibly Robert Hippler of the Air Force Scientific Advisory Board) who reportedly communicated with [[edward-condon]] and/or [[robert-low]] during the [[condon-commission]] investigation (1966–1968), signaling the Air Force's preferred conclusion in advance of the study's completion.

## Significance

The Hiper intervention is cited — alongside the [[robert-low]] memo — as evidence that the Condon Commission was not an independent scientific inquiry but a pre-determined whitewash. The sequence:

1. The Air Force engaged Condon's team at the University of Colorado to provide independent scientific cover for closing [[project-blue-book]].
2. Hiper's communications outlined what conclusion the USAF expected.
3. Robert Low's internal memo (the "Trick Memo") described how to conduct the study while appearing objective even if the outcome was predetermined.
4. The final Condon Report concluded that further study was unwarranted — enabling Project Blue Book's termination in 1969.
