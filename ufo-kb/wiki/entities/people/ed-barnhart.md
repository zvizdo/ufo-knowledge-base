# Ed Barnhart

## Background

Archaeologist and mentor to [[luke-caverns]]. Appeared alongside Luke on a podcast where he stated that global flood myths across unconnected civilizations may encode a genuine ancestral memory of a catastrophic prehistoric event — a position further left than mainstream archaeology but endorsed cautiously even by him.

## Notable Claims

Flood myths are not easily explained away by "people live near water": too many distant, uncorrelated civilizations share structurally identical narratives. Barnhart is open to this being leftover memory of the [[younger-dryas-impact]] or a similar catastrophe.
