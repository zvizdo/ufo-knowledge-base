# Marcos Charez

State military police officer in Minas Gerais, Brazil. Approximately 3 hours after the three Varginha girls first encountered the alien being, Charez and his partner [[eric-lopes]] located it. According to witness accounts (primarily Charez's sister and documents in James Fox's films), Charez captured the being with his bare hands easily.

He died approximately 2 weeks later from a highly virulent bacterial infection. His partner [[eric-lopes]] went into hiding and has been inaccessible since.

## Capture and Medical Deterioration

Charez held the being, which reportedly had an oily, greasy substance on its skin. He subsequently developed a skin lesion (hydrodenitis) in his left armpit area. A battalion military police doctor performed a drainage procedure on the lesion at the military police clinic on an outpatient basis.

The infection then progressed with extreme speed and virulence: the bacteria spread to his lungs and caused generalized septicemia. He visited 3–4 doctors complaining of lumbar pain (misdiagnosed as back pain) before the severity of the systemic infection became apparent. Despite aggressive antibiotic treatment (Dr. Cesario stated he "threw the kitchen sink"), the infection was completely unresponsive. Charez died within 12–14 days of symptom onset.

Throughout his illness, Charez told family members (primarily his sister) and reportedly Dr. Cesario that he believed the infection came from contact with the being. As his condition worsened, he was increasingly emphatic about this.

## Autopsy

[[armando-fortunato]] performed the official autopsy at the EML (Forensic Medical Institute) in Varginha. The request came from the military police colonel as an investigation into possible malpractice by the battalion doctor. Fortunato found only one wound — the drainage incision — and sent samples to pathologist [[jean-bachista-janini]].

Janini's report: death from pulmonary infection leading to generalized septicemia from a rare but Earth-existing staphylococcal strain, whose virulence and lethality were unlike anything Janini had seen in 45–50 years. In 2024–2025, Janini stated he now suspects the bacteria may be alien or genetically engineered.

The military police doctor who performed the drainage procedure was investigated for malpractice, then fully acquitted — the infection's progression was determined to be atypical and not due to negligence.

## Significance

Charez is the central physical-evidence anchor for the [[post-contact-biological-contamination]] hypothesis. His death creates a chain: alien being → physical contact → unprecedented bacterial infection → death. The chain is supported by multiple lines of evidence:

1. His own statements to family and doctors
2. Janini's bacteria assessment (unprecedented virulence)
3. The [[varginha-zoo]] animal deaths the same week (possible shared biological exposure)
4. [[jean-bachista-janini]]'s request for exhumation/DNA analysis (pending court authorization)
