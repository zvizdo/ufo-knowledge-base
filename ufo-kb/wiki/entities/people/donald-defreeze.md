# Donald DeFreeze

Donald DeFreeze (1943–1974) was a petty criminal who became the leader of the Symbionese Liberation Army (SLA) under the revolutionary code name "**CINQUE**" (Cinque Mtume), leading the organization that kidnapped [[patty-hearst]] in 1974. [[colin-ross]] documents his transformation as a case study parallel to [[charles-manson]]'s — a petty criminal converted into a charismatic violent extremist through documented contact with a military psychological warfare specialist.

## The Transformation (per Ross)

1. DeFreeze was incarcerated — a petty criminal, could not read
2. He was **transferred within the prison system** to a new facility
3. He **walked out** — not officially released; walked out of the prison
4. A **Vietnam War psychological warfare veteran** arrived, operating under cover of the **Black Cultural Association**
5. This individual met with DeFreeze **repeatedly**
6. DeFreeze was given the revolutionary identity "CINQUE" and organized into what became the SLA

The parallel to Manson is exact: petty criminal → contact with intelligence-adjacent psychological specialist → new charismatic violent identity → high-profile acts that served specific political purposes.

## Significance

The SLA kidnapping of Patty Hearst (granddaughter of a media baron) had maximum psychological impact — combining terrorism, class anxiety, and the spectacle of a famous victim transformed into a revolutionary. This impact pattern is consistent with [[operation-chaos]]-style objectives of destabilizing political movements through fear and confusion.
