# John McCone

## Background

John McCone served as Director of the CIA from 1961 to 1965 under Presidents Kennedy and Johnson. Before that, he chaired the Atomic Energy Commission (AEC) from 1958 to 1961 — giving him dual command over both the nation's nuclear program and its primary foreign intelligence service.

## UAP Connection

The [[jfk-letter-mccone]] — a letter from President Kennedy to CIA Director McCone — is referenced in the [[09KP8XVf5nY]] episode as part of the documentary record connecting JFK's intent to access or share UAP information. Kennedy's reported push to obtain UAP briefings shortly before his assassination would have required coordination with McCone as CIA director.

McCone's prior AEC chairmanship is significant: the AEC held the primary institutional responsibility for UAP material under nuclear law, creating an impenetrable classification layer that Malmgren describes as operating above presidential access. McCone moved from AEC to CIA, placing him at the junction of both layers.
