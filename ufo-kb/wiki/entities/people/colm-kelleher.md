# Colm Kelleher

Biochemist who served as a primary investigator at [[skinwalker-ranch]] under [[nids]] (National Institute for Discovery Science), [[robert-bigelow]]'s private UAP research organization, from the mid-1990s through the AAWSAP period. Co-authored both major public books on the ranch's anomalous phenomena.

## Background

Scientific background in biochemistry. Worked as a senior researcher at NIDS during the peak years of investigation at Skinwalker Ranch. Had direct access to the classified investigation records that form the basis of the published books.

## Publications

- ***The Hunt for the Skinwalker*** (2005, with [[george-knapp]]) — public account of the Sherman family history, NIDS investigations, and the multi-phenomenon nature of the ranch's anomalous activity.
- ***Skinwalkers at the Pentagon*** (2021, with [[george-knapp]] and [[james-lacatski]]) — partially declassified account of the AAWSAP program's investigation of the ranch; documents the **hitchhiker effect** (the finding that phenomena followed investigators home from the property), biological effects on personnel, and broader AAWSAP findings. Named after the fact that Lacatski — the program's founder — brought the book's contents to congressional attention.
