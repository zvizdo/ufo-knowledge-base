# Raymond Lavars

Raymond Lavars is a Canadian mathematician who reportedly worked on aerodynamic calculations for the [[b2-stealth-bomber]], the most advanced classified bomber aircraft in the US inventory.

**Esoteric mathematics claim**: In accounts related to the B-2 design — as cited in the KB's [[b2-stealth-bomber]] page — Lavars allegedly confirmed, when pressed, that esoteric or unconventional mathematical principles were incorporated into the aircraft's design calculations. The specific nature of these mathematical principles is described in terms that parallel the sacred geometry and anti-gravity research traditions associated with [[thomas-townsend-brown]] and [[electrogravitics]] researchers.

**Context**: The B-2 Spirit stealth bomber incorporates aerodynamic innovations that remain partially classified and that some researchers have argued go beyond conventional aerodynamics — particularly its unusual flying-wing configuration, the absence of vertical stabilizers, and flight control characteristics that require continuous computer adjustment. Whether the mathematical underpinnings reflect classified propulsion research or conventional advanced aerodynamics is unknown.

**KB significance**: Lavars appears in the KB as a node connecting mainstream classified aerospace (B-2) to the mathematical traditions that UAP and anti-gravity researchers claim are operative in advanced propulsion systems. His claim, if accurate, would represent a documented instance of the "suppressed physics" framework being applied in a confirmed classified aircraft program.

**Credibility status**: The claim is cited in the KB without independent corroboration. Lavars' existence and his work on the B-2 appear to be documented; the esoteric mathematics claim requires additional sourcing.
