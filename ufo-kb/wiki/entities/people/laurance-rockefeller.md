# Laurance Rockefeller

Financier and conservationist (1910–2004); fourth son of John D. Rockefeller Jr. Founded multiple environmental and biomedical foundations; long-time patron of consciousness research and (in his final decade) **UFO-disclosure efforts**.

In the late 1980s and through the 1990s Rockefeller funded the **[[1995-rockefeller-ufo-initiative]]** — the most well-resourced civilian disclosure-pressure campaign of the period. The initiative engaged the Clinton White House (Hillary Clinton attended a 1995 [[jy-ranch]] meeting), tasked Marie Galbraith and others with assembling the *Best Available Evidence* briefing, and underwrote multiple academic-research conferences and consciousness-research projects. The Rockefeller initiative is the single best-documented case of elite-financier-driven UAP-disclosure work in the modern record.
