# Pat Price

Among the most operationally capable remote viewers in the U.S. government's consciousness-as-intelligence program, and — like [[ingo-swann]] — a high-level clear within the [[church-of-scientology]]. [[danny-sheehan]] encountered Price's file while reviewing Scientology's most sensitive documents in their Florida HQ.

## Background

Price participated in the [[stargate-program]] experiments facilitated by [[hal-puthoff]] and [[russell-targ]]. Died under unclear circumstances during the program.

## Demonstrated Capabilities (per Sheehan's reading of the Scientology file)

All accounts below are sourced from documents Sheehan reports having seen — **secondhand** from those documents, not Sheehan's direct observation:

1. **Coordinate-based document retrieval**: Given only the geographic coordinates of a top-secret CIA facility, Price was able to identify the name of a specific classified document held inside that facility. This was verified against actual holdings.

2. **Soviet submarine tracking**: Given a photograph, Price was able to provide the exact latitude and longitude of a Soviet nuclear submarine. The same session file contained imagery of a UAP hovering directly over the submarine, apparently tracking it independently.

3. **Canadian mind-control broadcast**: An ONI facility in southern Canada used a helmet and large-antenna system to telepathically broadcast a behavioral suggestion — a change in tax-filing behavior — to a test population. The file reported ~85% compliance.

## UAP Connection

The Soviet submarine session file is particularly significant: a UAP was captured in the same observation, hovering over and apparently monitoring the submarine. This suggests NHI entities may have been conducting their own surveillance of nuclear assets simultaneously with Price's remote viewing — a convergence of human psychic intelligence-gathering and NHI activity at a nuclear-relevant target.
