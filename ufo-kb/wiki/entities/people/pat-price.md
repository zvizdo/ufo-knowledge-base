# Pat Price

Among the most operationally capable remote viewers in the U.S. government's consciousness-as-intelligence program, and — like [[ingo-swann]] — a high-level clear within the [[church-of-scientology]]. [[danny-sheehan]] encountered Price's file while reviewing Scientology's most sensitive documents in their Florida HQ.

## Background

Price participated in the [[stargate-program]] experiments facilitated by [[hal-puthoff]] and [[russell-targ]]. Died under unclear circumstances during the program.

## Demonstrated Capabilities (per Sheehan's reading of the Scientology file)

All accounts below are sourced from documents Sheehan reports having seen — **secondhand** from those documents, not Sheehan's direct observation:

1. **Coordinate-based document retrieval**: Given only the geographic coordinates of a top-secret CIA facility, Price was able to identify the name of a specific classified document held inside that facility. This was verified against actual holdings.

2. **Soviet submarine tracking**: Given a photograph, Price was able to provide the exact latitude and longitude of a Soviet nuclear submarine. The same session file contained imagery of a UAP hovering directly over the submarine, apparently tracking it independently.

3. **Canadian mind-control broadcast**: An ONI facility in southern Canada used a helmet and large-antenna system to telepathically broadcast a behavioral suggestion — a change in tax-filing behavior — to a test population. The file reported ~85% compliance.

## UAP Connection

The Soviet submarine session file is particularly significant: a UAP was captured in the same observation, hovering over and apparently monitoring the submarine. This suggests NHI entities may have been conducting their own surveillance of nuclear assets simultaneously with Price's remote viewing — a convergence of human psychic intelligence-gathering and NHI activity at a nuclear-relevant target.

## The Sugar Grove Incident

Price's most consequential spontaneous session: given coordinates for a CIA colleague's private mountain cabin as a test target, Price instead described the **classified US Navy [[sugar-grove-naval-station]] approximately 100 yards away**, used to monitor Soviet satellites. He read top-secret codewords from the station's programs — **Q-ball, nine-ball, eight-ball** (billiard-themed) — without prior exposure.

The test, designed by [[kit-green]] to validate Price's abilities to CIA skeptics, backfired catastrophically: instead of convincing skeptics, it triggered a security investigation against Green's own CIA office. "Every law enforcement person in California showed up at SRI." The Navy was furious about classified details appearing in civilian transcripts.

[[skip-atwater]] describes this as the "Awakening" for SRI scientists — the realization that something was operating beyond their controlled protocol. Price had not stayed on target; he had been drawn to something more significant nearby. See [[sugar-grove-naval-station]].

## UFO Bases — Four Underground Mountains

Price reported to [[stanford-research-institute]] researchers one day that he had identified **four UFO bases around the globe** — specifically inside mountains. He gave coordinates for four locations:
1. **Mount Hayes, Alaska**
2. **Mount Inyangani, Zimbabwe/Africa**
3. **Mount Perdido area, Spain-France border** (called "Mount Puro" or "Pito")
4. **Mount Zeil, Australia**

Price described underground spaces with beings engaged in different activities, internal machinery and craft, and a research-like atmosphere inside hollow mountains. These accounts were not operationally validated during his lifetime.

Approximately 10 years later, [[skip-atwater]] ran **Project 8200** — a self-initiated challenge-target program — with six Stargate viewers tasked blind against Price's four coordinates. Results from [[joe-mcmoneagle]] and Bill Ray's sessions on Mount Hayes matched Price's original descriptions: hollow mountains, silver metallic craft, thin unemotional beings with "programmed feelings," and concealment via programmed telepathy.

A secondhand CIA station chief report from Australia (relayed through [[kit-green]] to [[hal-puthoff]]): "It's all those UFOs up north" — a casual confirmation that Australian intelligence was aware of the phenomenon.

## Pat Price's Death — Four Conflicting Accounts

Price died in 1975 under disputed circumstances while working for the CIA. [[skip-atwater]] describes four incompatible accounts:

1. **Restaurant collapse/unmarked grave**: Price collapsed in a restaurant; was taken to a hospital; two unidentified men removed him to a funeral home; he was cremated and buried in an unmarked grave (marker: "155"). [[russell-targ]] investigated, found the grave, and Price's wife confirmed this version.
2. **Las Vegas hospital**: [[hal-puthoff]] and Price's wife flew to the Las Vegas area, went to a hospital and funeral home, found a body in a casket that "looked like him but it's not so hard to do that to a body."
3. **Fabricated death narrative**: CIA may have fabricated the entire death scenario — including estate gifts to wife and son — specifically to make the death look authentic so investigators would find the planted stories and stop searching.
4. **Underground continuation**: CIA may have taken Price alive and continued to run him in a classified underground location.

Account 4 is supported by [[joe-mcmoneagle]]'s blind RV of Price's photograph: McMoneagle's first response was "this guy does something like what I do" (suggesting psychic recognition) and his location assessment was "He's in an underground office in Virginia somewhere." [[skip-atwater]] stopped the inquiry deliberately: "I just kept my mouth shut."

## Retroactive Validation

[[russell-targ]] discovered that in a Menlo Park [[stanford-research-institute]] remote viewing session, Price had described towers he identified as a swimming pool. A local government book published **75 years later** revealed the site was a sewage treatment plant — the towers Price had seen were real; the function was misidentified by CIA debriefers at the time. Confirmed by [[russell-targ]].
