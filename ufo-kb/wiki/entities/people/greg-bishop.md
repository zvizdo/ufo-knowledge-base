# Greg Bishop

UAP researcher, author, and radio host. Best known for *[[project-beta]]* (2005), a detailed investigation of the [[rick-doty]] / [[paul-benowitz]] affair — the Air Force OSI disinformation operation that drove Benowitz to psychiatric hospitalization after he filmed craft near [[kirtland-afb]].

## Project Beta

*Project Beta* is the primary published source on the Doty-Benowitz psyop. Jesse Michaels describes it as "a great book" that introduced him to Rick Doty. [[mario-woods]] independently knew the book, which was how he recognized Doty when he saw him on *Ancient Aliens*: "I know that guy — he was at my debrief."

The book's significance extends beyond the Benowitz case: it establishes a documented, named disinformation operation against a civilian UAP researcher, creating a precedent and template for evaluating other cases where researcher credibility or mental health was called into question.

## Key Analytical Claims

From the [[WBTqsbwJyqU]] episode with Jesse Michaels:

- "UFOs are a Swiss Army knife for intelligence — they can use them for anything." This is Bishop's central analytical insight about the relationship between UFO research and intelligence operations.
- The Benowitz operation was "a tiny piece in a big wheel" of Cold War CI — significant to UFO researchers precisely because it intersected the UFO community, not because it was centrally about UFOs.
- Benowitz "was already kind of unstable to begin with" — AFOSI amplified pre-existing instability rather than manufacturing it from scratch. Bishop considers this important context, not an excuse.
- The prosaic-vs-genuine dichotomy is a false choice: "The idea that UFOs are a cover for just prosaic technology is totally wrong. In fact, it's the opposite — prosaic technology is often used as a cover for UFO." And both are sometimes simultaneously true.
- His operational principle for working with intelligence-connected sources (title of first chapter of *It Defies Language*): "You play the game or you get nothing."
