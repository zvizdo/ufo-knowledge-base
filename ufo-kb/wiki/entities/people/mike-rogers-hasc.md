# Mike Rogers (HASC)

Republican Congressman from Alabama serving as chair of the House Armed Services Committee. In the FY2024 NDAA conference committee, Rogers was the primary House-side opponent of the [[schumer-uap-amendment]], leading the effort to strip the amendment's eminent domain authority and mandatory declassification enforcement provisions. [[david-grusch]] documents Rogers's PAC funding from Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, and Boeing as the structural explanation for his opposition.

Note: This page covers the Alabama HASC chair. The page [[mike-rogers-nsa]] covers a different Mike Rogers — the former NSA Director and CYBERCOM commander.
