# Ingo Swann

One of the most influential figures in the development of controlled remote viewing as a repeatable protocol, and — according to [[danny-sheehan]]'s account — simultaneously a covert intelligence asset for the [[church-of-scientology]] against the [[naval-intelligence]] remote-viewing program.

## Background

Classified as a "high level clear" within Scientology, Swann was among the first remote viewers recruited into the Office of Naval Intelligence's early consciousness-as-intelligence-tool program, which later became the [[stargate-program]] under DIA/CIA management. He worked alongside [[hal-puthoff]] and [[russell-targ]] at Stanford Research Institute.

Swann had been experiencing **out-of-body experiences since childhood**. It was the disciplined honing of this ability — developing it into a rigorous reproducible protocol — that became the basis for what Puthoff would later call coordinate remote viewing.

## Quark Detector Session (1972) — Stargate Trigger (Puthoff's Firsthand Account)

In [[iQOibpIDx-4]], [[hal-puthoff]] himself recounts this session directly for the first time in this KB's sources:

> "We had the quark detector... basically a quantum chip inside of mu metal magnetic shielding inside of a steel Dewar for electrical shielding inside of superconducting shielding. The requirement being that nothing could affect this thing from the outside. So we want to see if you can affect it. He affected it unequivocally 100%."

Swann **stopped the slow oscillation** of the detector — something that should have been physically impossible given the shielding. Then, when asked how he did it, Swann said "all I did was look inside" and drew a schematic of the device's internal configuration that had never been published. The drawing was accurate.

> "When we asked him, you know, how do you know what to do? And he said, well, all I did was look inside. And then he drew out how things were related inside that never been published. And it turned out that they were accurate."

Puthoff circulated the results, and the CIA came "knocking at my doorstep" — explicitly initiating the Stargate program in response.

## Quark Detector Session (1972) — Stargate Trigger

In 1972, laser physicist [[hal-puthoff]] was conducting experiments with Swann at SRI. Swann accurately **remote-viewed the internal readouts of a highly shielded quark detector** — a piece of sensitive equipment designed to be impervious to external influence. The postdoctoral researcher running the machine reportedly said: "What are you doing to my machine?" This accuracy "freaked out" the postdoc.

The result got the attention of high-level officials in the US defense department and directly **prompted the CIA to launch the [[stargate-program]]**. Per [[paul-smith]] in [[eQIMantuasQ]], this session is what turned remote viewing from a scientific curiosity into a classified government intelligence program.

## Sheehan's Disclosure (Firsthand)

Sheehan saw Swann's internal reports while reviewing Scientology's most sensitive files at their Florida headquarters as a condition of potentially representing [[l-ron-hubbard]]:

- Swann was filing internal reports to **Scientology's UK Guardian's Office** describing the operational details of the ONI remote-viewing program — what targets were being assigned, what methodologies were being used, what capabilities were being demonstrated.
- These reports were treated as intelligence product within Scientology's internal security apparatus.
- Sheehan characterizes this as Swann being a **spy** inside the program on behalf of Scientology — a church that was, at the same time, deeply interested in consciousness technology and in monitoring government programs studying it.

> ⚠ This account is sourced entirely from Sheehan's recollection of documents seen decades prior. Swann is deceased and has not been confronted with this characterization in a public forum. It should be treated as a serious allegation requiring further corroboration.

## Remote Viewing Capabilities

Despite the espionage angle, the remote-viewing program files (as Sheehan reports them) documented extraordinary capabilities:
- [[pat-price]] could locate a specific named document inside a top-secret CIA facility given only geographic coordinates.
- An ONI experiment broadcast a telepathic suggestion via a helmet-and-antenna system at a Canadian facility, reportedly achieving 85% compliance in a tax-filing behavior change.
- A file showed a UAP hovering directly over a Soviet nuclear submarine that Price had been asked to locate and track.

## Moon Remote Viewing (EzPmG_7WhXc)

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] reports that Swann was taken from the SRI program into an unnamed black unit (a man calling himself "Axel Rod" transported him blindfolded by helicopter to an undisclosed facility). He was tasked to remote view the Moon — blind to the target. What he saw:
- A **city on the dark side of the Moon** — the same location photographed in mosaic by [[carl-wolf]] at an NSA facility in the early 1960s. The facility apparently had access to those photos and wanted Swann to "get a ground's eye view."
- **Tall, statuesque Nordic people** who gave him a hostile telepathic message ("you don't belong here, get out")
- **Slave labor** — armies of enslaved beings (apparently human or humanoid) living in ramshackle shelters under appalling conditions — possibly under a transparent dome

Swann also remote-viewed **Mars** (independently of [[joe-mcmoneagle]]'s 1984 CIA session) and reported the same Nordic architectural style in ruins, with evidence of internal explosions consistent with a battle.
