# Denis Letty

Retired French Air Force General; chair of [[cometa-report|COMETA]] (Committee for In-Depth Studies). Letty coordinated the work of ~15 senior military officers, scientists, and intelligence officials to produce the 1999 COMETA Report (*UFOs and Defense: What Should We Prepare For?*), presented formally to the French President and Prime Minister.

## Role in COMETA

As chair, Letty was responsible for the report's scope, structure, and conclusions. The report's six formal recommendations — including expanding [[geipan|SEPRA]], creating a high-level government unit, and initiating EU diplomatic demarches to the US — reflect a strategic framing consistent with a senior military officer's concern about defense preparedness rather than speculative enthusiasm.
