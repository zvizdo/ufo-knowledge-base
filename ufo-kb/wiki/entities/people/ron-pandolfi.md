# Ron Pandolfi

Ron Pandolfi is a CIA scientist described by former CIA officer [[john-ramirez]] as a long-running figure in the intelligence community's engagement with exotic physics and UAP-adjacent phenomena. His aviary code name was reportedly **"Pelican"** — which [[john-ramirez]] identified on the [[2005-nrol-16-launch]] mission patch as a signal of Pandolfi's direct involvement in that launch.

## Background

According to Ramirez and other sources, Pandolfi:
- Worked on **exotic physics** within the CIA
- Was connected to the **[[jason-group]]** (Defense Science Board-adjacent elite science advisory body) and **MITRE Corporation**
- Operated within the **CIA's Office of Scientific and Weapons Research**
- Has been described informally as running the CIA's **"weird desk"** — meaning he was the institutional interface for anomalous/UAP-adjacent science questions

## NROL-16 Connection

[[john-ramirez]] claims Pandolfi was **directly involved** in the 2005 NROL-16 classified satellite launch, which Ramirez identifies as a deliberately structured occult ritual keyed to Walpurgisnacht. The pelican on the mission patch was Pandolfi's aviary identifier.

If true, this would make Pandolfi the most concrete named link between CIA institutional activity and intentional occult ritual timing in the US space/intelligence complex.
