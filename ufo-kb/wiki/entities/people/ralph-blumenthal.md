# Ralph Blumenthal

New York Times investigative journalist who co-authored the landmark December 2017 article "Glowing Auras and 'Black Money': The Pentagon's Mysterious U.F.O. Program" with [[leslie-kean]] and Helene Cooper. The article disclosed the existence of the [[aatip]] program, released the [[2015-gimbal]] footage, and ended the mainstream media embargo on serious UAP reporting.

[[marik-von-rennenkampff]] notes that he missed this 2017 story when it came out — a testament (in his words) to how little he was tracking the topic at the time.
