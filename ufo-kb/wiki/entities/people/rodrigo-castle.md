# Rodrigo Castle

US Marine Corps AV8B Harrier aviation technician. One of three independent military witnesses identified by [[uap-gerb]] who all received unprompted anthrax boosters after encountering crash retrieval operations — the others being [[jonathan-wagant]] (1997 Peru) and [[michael-herrera]] (USS Denver, Sumatra).

## Firsthand Account

During the 1997 Hunter Warrior advanced war-fighting experiment, Castle was ordered to investigate what was reported as unauthorized flare activity. He encountered:

- A massive rotating triangle approximately 300 feet across with characteristics consistent with the TR-3B described by [[edgar-fouche]].
- An accompanying team of operators in all black, no insignia, no name tags.
- He was held at gunpoint.

After the encounter, Castle received an additional anthrax booster — despite having already received the anthrax vaccine and a standard booster. The third injection is what UAPGerb identified as anomalous and cross-confirmed with the Wagant and Herrera cases.

## Anthrax Booster Pattern

[[uap-gerb]] describes this as a cross-case silencing mechanism: all three witnesses (Castle, Wagant, Herrera) had the same thing happen to them independently. Castle's anomalous third injection is the clearest evidence of intentional post-encounter chemical intervention, since the baseline immunization series provides no rationale for a third dose.
