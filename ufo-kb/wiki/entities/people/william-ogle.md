# William Ogle

## Background

William Ogle was a Los Alamos nuclear weapons scientist who served as Scientific Director of the Joint Task Force 8, the organization responsible for conducting the 1962 nuclear test series in the [[marshall-islands]] — specifically [[operation-dominic]] and its constituent tests including [[bluegill-triple-prime]].

## UAP Connection

Ogle's direct oversight of the Bluegill Triple Prime test series places him as a senior scientific witness to the events surrounding the alleged UAP shootdown during that test. If the [[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown]] account given by [[richard-bissell]] to [[harold-malmgren]] is accurate, Ogle would have been among those with operational knowledge of what happened.
