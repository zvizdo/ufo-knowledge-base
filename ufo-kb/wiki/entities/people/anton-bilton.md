# Anton Bilton

Researcher acquainted with both [[jesse-michaels]] and [[andrew-gallimore]]; exploring a proposed experimental design: place two DMT subjects in **adjacent Faraday cages** and see whether they can telepathically share or transmit images between them. The Faraday cage design eliminates conventional electromagnetic signal transmission as an explanation for any information sharing observed — if subjects in Faraday isolation still share imagery, the effect would be classically non-local.

## Experimental Significance

This experiment addresses a fundamental question about DMT-state communication: Is it brain-to-brain via physical signals (potentially mediated by electromagnetic fields)? Or is it genuinely non-local — requiring a non-electromagnetic mechanism? The Faraday cage design is methodologically clean for this purpose.

Jesse Michaels mentions this as an example of grounded experimental work that could move DMT research from speculation toward scientific mainstream. Gallimore is enthusiastic about this direction.
