# Richard Miethe

German aerospace engineer associated with the Nazi [[kammler-stab]] weapons facility in Czechoslovakia/modern-day Poland. Miethe conducted high-voltage experiments that were, according to [[jesse-michaels]], "very similar" in approach to American scientist [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s electrogravitics work. The convergence of approaches from two independent national programs suggests that high-voltage electrogravitic effects were being independently discovered in the 1940s.

Miethe was transferred to the United States via [[operation-paperclip]], with [[wright-patterson]] as a key receiving destination.
