# Matthew Brown (Whistleblower)

Recent US government UAP whistleblower. Referenced by [[jesse-michaels]] as having discovered the [[immaculate-constellation]] document within government channels and disclosed its contents in an interview with [[jeremy-corbell]] and [[george-knapp]].

## Key Disclosures

1. **Immaculate Constellation**: A classified (or formerly classified) government document describing UAP capable of trans-medium operation — objects that transit between aerial and underwater environments.
2. **Made to purpose**: According to Brown, some of these objects are "built to purpose" — designed for specific mission parameters. If a craft is only meant to operate at depth, it will be optimized for that; if only for air, for that. Specialized rather than general-purpose.

Jesse Michaels raises this in the context of the [[baltic-sea-anomaly]] — speculating that an object with corridors, 90° angles, and a "breathing" hole may have been "made to purpose" for a specific seafloor function, then decommissioned or left behind.
