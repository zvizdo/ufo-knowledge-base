## Background

Thomas Townsend Brown was an American physicist and inventor best known for his discovery of the Biefeld–Brown effect — an observed relationship between high-voltage electricity and gravitational-like force. He spent decades investigating electrogravitics, the study of using electrical fields to influence gravity, work that placed him at the fringe of accepted physics while attracting serious interest from military and intelligence circles.

Brown was obsessed with time travel, believing that gravity was fundamentally linked to the nature of time and that manipulating gravitational fields could therefore allow travel through time. This conviction shaped much of his later research and theoretical writing.

His personal workbooks — reportedly around 500 pages — were recently posted publicly online. [[harold-malmgren]] downloaded them and planned to review their contents, suggesting the material is considered significant by those tracking advanced physics research with possible UAP relevance.

## Notable Claims

- Brown slept next to a device he described as a "shortwave radio" and claimed to use it to communicate with entities he called "space brothers" — an early and recurring motif in mid-20th-century contactee accounts that intersects with both radio-wave experimentation and claims of [[nhi-telepathic-communication]].
- Foreign intelligence sources told Harald Malmgren that Brown and [[nikola-tesla]] collaborated directly — specifically on anti-gravity research and possibly on the transmutation of elements. If accurate, this places Brown inside a suppressed scientific network that operated in parallel to mainstream physics.
- Malmgren's framing positions Brown and Tesla as the more radical alternative to Einstein: "Einstein put a governor on our physical progress in reality" — implying that the dominant Einsteinian paradigm actively constrained the direction of physics, while Tesla and Brown were pursuing results that have since been absorbed into [[physics-lockdown]].

As a **teenager**, Brown was at Catalina Island. A **glowing orb** (ball of light) approached him — physically, directly. Michaels states: *"I know the exact spot he was standing. I used to ride my horse up that ridge."* The orb came up to Brown.

Immediately after, he returned to his private laboratory in Pasadena (funded by his parents) and began working immediately. This account is structurally identical to [[charles-buhler]]'s description of his own childhood UFO sightings as career-shaping — and both researchers converged on the same anomalous electrostatic force. The parallel is explicit in [[mOWwdIuyaQA]]: Michaels notes this mirroring directly.

## The RTEWLSTyUic Documentary: New Details (2024)

[[paul-schatzkin]]'s biography *[[the-man-who-mastered-gravity]]* and the [[RTEWLSTyUic]] documentary add the most complete account of Brown's career assembled to date:

### The Biefeld-Brown Vacuum Confirmation
[[jacque-corneau]], a French Air Force officer and technical rep for Sud-Ouest aircraft, facilitated Brown's 1956 experiments at the Montgolfier facility in Paris. In a vacuum of at least 10⁻⁶ torr, the observed thrust was **several orders of magnitude larger than residual ambient ionization could account for** — definitively eliminating the ion-wind explanation for the core effect. Corneau confirmed this at age 98 shortly before his death (2008).

### The "Wounded Prairie Chicken" Disinformation
After a Soviet spy infiltrated a 1950 Pearl Harbor demo (disguised as a janitor), Brown began deliberately showing the **wrong version** of his work — the fluid-dielectric "lifter" system rather than his real solid-dielectric gravitator. This self-discrediting routine ("wounded prairie chickens limp away from their nest to protect it") explains why Brown's public reputation was that of a minor quack while his actual work was attracting the attention of Edward Teller and Curtis LeMay.

### The Germany Mission (1945)
Brown was recruited by MI6's top spy [[william-stevenson]] ("Intrepid") and OSS director [[bill-donovan]] for the **Taom mission** — parachuted from low altitude behind Nazi lines in East Germany to capture exotic propulsion technology. The specific target appears to have been [[richard-maita]], a German high-voltage physicist whose work paralleled Brown's own. Brown was captured by the Nazis and wounded before being rescued. A telegram to his wife — from Stevenson's Rockefeller Plaza HQ — confirmed he was "recuperating fine in an English hospital."

### NICAP as Counter Intelligence
Brown founded the National Investigations Committee on Aerial Phenomena ([[nicap]]) in 1956 after the International Geophysical Year. He told [[linda-brown]] to filter out sightings describing motion "like a fluttering leaf" — "those are ours." [[jan-lundquist]] argues NICAP was CIA-affiliated counter-intelligence: a honey trap for civilian UFO data and a mechanism for spreading controlled disinformation. Brown's involvement in the 1957 International Geophysical Year was also likely a cover for setting up signals-intelligence listening posts for U-2 overflights of Soviet Russia.

### The 1960 Connecticut Crash Retrieval
Brown personally attended the site of a 1960 Connecticut fireball crash. A chemical analysis showed the debris was **aluminum and barium** — precisely the high-K dielectric materials Brown specified in his [[winter-haven-proposal]] for his gravitator motors. Brown showed his credentials to Project Blue Book operatives and took the material, who noted Blue Book had no authority over it — the classification ran through the Manhattan Project/Atomic Energy Commission line of authority.

### Sidereal Radiation
Brown's lifelong observation that his gravitator's output varied with the sun and moon's positions led him to develop specialized **electrometers** measuring [[sidereal-radiation]]. He pursued this until his death on [[catalina-island]]. An anonymous Harvard physicist in [[RTEWLSTyUic]] confirmed active interest in sidereal radiation as an unsolved thread.

### Extended Electrodynamics Explanation
An anonymous Navy scientist interviewed in [[RTEWLSTyUic]] offers the most technically precise theoretical framework for Brown's work: restoring the **scalar field** that Oliver Heaviside dropped when condensing Maxwell's 20 original equations to 4. With the scalar field restored ([[extended-electrodynamics]]), three new wave types become possible — scalar, longitudinal, helicoidal — that may couple with gravity more tightly than conventional Hertzian waves. The Lorentz gauge condition that classical electrodynamics imposes artificially constrains the theory; removing it reopens the coupling between electromagnetism and gravity that Brown may have empirically discovered.

### Caroline Group and IP Transfer
The [[caroline-group]] — a private intelligence/finance consortium operating from the yacht *Caroline* (Eldridge Reeves Johnson's vessel) — appears to have been the mechanism by which Brown's IP was transferred from his hands into black aerospace programs without public paper trail. Brown was described as "basically CIA research" who was "pulled away for espionage work" — and certain inventions were "handed over to other entities" by "this Consortium." Nassau, Bahamas served as the coordination hub for these meetings between Brown and [[robert-sarbacher]].
