# Brad Wenstrup

Republican congressman who became chairman of the **House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence** in January 2025, replacing [[michael-turner]]. [[danny-sheehan]] identifies Wenstrup as a more tractable figure for UAP disclosure legislation — specifically for bringing the remaining 41 pages of the [[controlled-disclosure-campaign-act]] to a House floor vote.

Turner had blocked the CDCA from the House floor by talking Speaker [[mike-johnson]] out of allowing a vote. Turner's obstruction was connected by Sheehan to Turner's district (Ohio's 10th Congressional District) which contains [[wright-patterson-air-force-base]]. Wenstrup's district relationship to Wright-Patterson is different, reducing the personal political incentive for obstruction.

> ⚠ **Discrepancy**: In an earlier Sheehan interview ([[C4rSj5Aum7w]]), he named [[dan-crenshaw]] as the incoming House Intelligence Committee chair. This March 2025 interview names Brad Wenstrup. Wenstrup did in fact become HPSCI chair in January 2025, replacing Turner. The earlier episode may have recorded Sheehan's expectations before the final appointment was confirmed.
