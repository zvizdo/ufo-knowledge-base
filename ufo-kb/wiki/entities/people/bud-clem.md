# Bud Clem

WWII-era pilot. Documented by [[robert-hastings]] in an interview recording, and cited by [[kevin-knuth]] in Knuth's academic UAP study paper.

## Notable Claims

In 1945, while flying over the [[hanford-nuclear-reservation]] (the Hanford Ordinance Works, the plutonium production facility for the Manhattan Project), Clem observed UFOs and reported the sighting. He states he was directed to have a Lieutenant Commander Brown take off and challenge the bogey over the Hanford site.

## Significance

The crucial detail noted by Kevin Knuth: the Hanford site was not yet **fully operational** in 1945 — radioactive material was not yet fully installed and functioning. This means UAPs were present at a nuclear facility **before it became radioactive**.

This pre-construction presence is the most troubling detail in the nuclear-UAP nexus: it suggests NHI did not simply react to nuclear radiation or track our nuclear activity after it started. They were present before the technology was operational — implying either:
1. Knowledge of what was being built at the site (intelligence ahead of our own disclosure)
2. Interest in the facility itself rather than (or in addition to) the radioactive material

Robert Hastings preserved Clem's audio interview. Kevin Knuth includes him in a published academic paper on UAP study history.
