# Phyllis Schlemmer

## Background

Phyllis Schlemmer was a channeler who worked closely with [[andrija-puharich]] in the mid-1970s at his Ossining, NY research compound. Her role in Puharich's [[space-kids]] and [[the-nine]] channeling program was as the primary channel — Puharich would hypnotically regress her, she would enter trance, and [[the-nine]] entities would speak through her.

## The Roddenberry Sessions

According to tape recordings in Puharich's archive, [[gene-roddenberry]] attended one or more channeling sessions featuring Schlemmer as the channel. These sessions lasted one to two hours. Roddenberry's participation in these sessions directly inspired his unproduced "The Nine" script and ultimately influenced the creation of Star Trek: Deep Space Nine.
