# Ralph DeBose

## Background

Ralph DeBose is a retired US Air Force general officer who appears in the modern UAP-disclosure record as the co-witness to [[arthur-exon|Brig. Gen. Arthur Exon]] in 1990s video testimony about the [[1947-roswell|Roswell]] event. His name appears in different renderings across the source record — "DeBose" in [[R8TqBrrqL4U|David Grusch's 2023 JRE testimony]], "Deose" and "Del Roy" in [[Gf_tKn9TaP8|Hal Puthoff's 2025 JRE account]]. These are most likely the same person; the variant spellings reflect spoken-rather-than-written sourcing through Puthoff's relay of [[eric-davis|Eric Davis]]'s direct interview.

## Notable Claims

### 1990s On-Camera Roswell Testimony (with Exon)

DeBose and [[arthur-exon|Exon]] gave video testimony in the 1990s confirming that material from the Roswell crash was transported to [[wright-patterson|Wright-Patterson AFB]] for analysis. [[david-grusch|David Grusch]] in [[R8TqBrrqL4U]] cites this pair as multi-source corroboration that the post-Roswell program was institutionalized at Wright-Patterson — two on-camera retired general officers, same testimony, independent of each other.

### Eric Davis Interview (Routed Through Puthoff)

In [[Gf_tKn9TaP8]], Puthoff names a second source alongside Exon in [[eric-davis|Eric Davis]]'s direct interview record. Both told Davis the materials taken to Wright-Patterson were "the real deal — unidentifiable, off-planet provenance." Puthoff's rendering of the name is ambiguous ("Deose" / "Del Roy"); the most plausible reading is that this is DeBose again, given the Exon pairing.
