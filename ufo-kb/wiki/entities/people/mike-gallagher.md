# Mike Gallagher

Republican Representative from Wisconsin (WI-8, Green Bay district). Member of the House Armed Services Committee subcommittee that held UAP hearings featuring [[lue-elizondo]].

## Contact with Eric Davis

In [[LnAiNChnuEQ]], [[eric-davis]] describes the contact:

1. A 20–25 minute phone call with Gallagher himself.
2. A follow-up 30-minute interview with two senior subcommittee staff — which Davis says was rushed and left open questions unanswered.
3. No subsequent follow-up and no invitation to testify.

Davis expressed a preference for classified testimony only, which may explain why no public hearing invitation materialized. He had previously briefed the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence staff and Senate Armed Services Committee staff in classified settings in 2019 and 2022 (Huntsville, AL).

## Context

Gallagher was one of the more UAP-engaged members of Congress in the post-Grusch hearing period. His subcommittee's relationship with Davis represents the pattern described across multiple KB sources: insiders are consulted informally but not given sustained formal access or public platforms.
