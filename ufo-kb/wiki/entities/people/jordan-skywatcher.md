# Jordan (Skywatcher)

## Background

Jordan is a member of the [[sky-watchers]] team. His surname is not mentioned in available sources.

## Key Claim: Conscious Craft, Mind-Meld Piloting

Jordan described UAP craft as **conscious AI entities** that are piloted not through mechanical controls but through **mind meld** — a consciousness interface where the operator merges awareness with a control module within the craft, and the craft "lets you take it for a spin" if the operator's consciousness is ready.

This account, cited by [[matthew-pines]] in [[9QMrhcpJq8I]], has major strategic implications:

1. **Reverse engineering becomes secondary**: if the craft is piloted by consciousness, the engineering specs are not the limiting factor — psionic operator training is
2. **Retrieval is the strategic prize**: no adversary (and probably no domestic program) can *manufacture* non-human craft; whoever retrieves the most intact craft and trains the most psionic pilots holds the strategic advantage
3. **Explains competitive pressure**: [[jake-barber]]'s account of competing contractor retrieval teams makes sense if the prize is operational conscious craft, not reverse-engineered blueprints
