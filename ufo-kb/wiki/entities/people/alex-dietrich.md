# Alex Dietrich

US Navy Lieutenant Commander; pilot with VFA-41 Black Aces. Served as wingman to Commander [[david-fravor]] during the 2004 Nimitz Tic-Tac encounter. One of the two primary eyewitnesses to the November 14, 2004 visual UAP encounter off the coast of San Diego.

Dietrich's account corroborates Fravor's description of a white, oblong, ~40-foot object performing impossible maneuvers — no wings, no exhaust, instant acceleration. She has given interviews publicly describing the encounter and the psychological impact it had on her.
