# George Schulgen

General George F. Schulgen was chief of the Air Intelligence Requirements Division at Army Air Forces (AAF) Headquarters. On July 9, 1947 — two weeks after Kenneth Arnold's sighting triggered the national flying saucer wave — Schulgen wrote to FBI Director J. Edgar Hoover requesting the Bureau's assistance in investigating flying disc reports.

## Key contribution to the documentary record

Schulgen's July 9, 1947 letter contains the most consequential official denial on record: the War Department confirmed it had "no research projects in the War Department or Navy that were tied up with the flying disks." This official AAF denial of domestic explanations was issued at the highest military intelligence level within the first month of the modern UFO era.

If taken at face value, Schulgen's statement means that by July 9, 1947, both the Army and Navy had confirmed to AAF Intelligence that no classified US programs were responsible for the national disc sightings — leaving foreign origin or unknown technology as the remaining explanations.

Schulgen is also the likely author of the classified 1947 "Schulgen Memo" (separate from the FBI letter) that requested technical analysis of observed disc characteristics from AAF engineering staff.
