# Michael Aquino

Michael Aquino (1946–2019) founded the Temple of Set after leaving the Church of Satan, where he had been a high priest under Anton LaVey. In parallel, he served as a US Army officer specializing in psychological operations, including deployment in the Phoenix Program in Vietnam, and later became a theorist of non-lethal warfare and consciousness manipulation.

## Background

Aquino held a PhD in political science and rose to the rank of Lieutenant Colonel. His dual career as a military PSYOPS specialist and occult organization leader made him a uniquely positioned figure at the intersection of consciousness research, psychological warfare, and fringe intelligence culture.

He conducted a satanic ceremony at [[wewelsburg-castle]] — Himmler's SS castle — an act he considered a ritual reclamation of the site's "dark energy." [[peter-levenda]] visited Wewelsburg for a TV show and noted Aquino's documented presence.

## Notable Claims

### "MJ" Designation in Space Command

At an AFIO (Association of Former Intelligence Officers) gathering in Las Vegas, Aquino told Levenda that the "MJ" designation used in US Space Command stood for *"non-terrestrial biologics"* — meaning organic material originating from meteors or asteroids, not alien bodies. This directly addresses the [[majestic-12]] documents' "MJ" label.

Levenda is explicit that he is *"in two minds"* about this explanation — it could be the genuine administrative meaning or a sophisticated deflection. Aquino's intelligence background makes either interpretation plausible.

### Phoenix Program and Consciousness Warfare

Aquino was a PSYOPS officer in the Phoenix Program in Vietnam. The Phoenix Program used belief in local supernatural entities — vampires, ghosts — to terrorize Vietnamese villages. Levenda draws a direct line between these Phoenix Program tactics and the documented use of UFO phenomena for domestic psychological manipulation by US intelligence agencies.

### Non-Lethal Warfare Theory

Aquino was a theorist of non-lethal warfare through consciousness manipulation — the idea that populations and individuals could be psychologically destabilized without physical violence. This connects directly to the [[mk-ultra]] and [[operation-often]] lineage and to the documented AFOSI use of UFO mythology against researchers like [[paul-benowitz]].
