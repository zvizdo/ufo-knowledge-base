# John Trump

## Background

John George Trump was an MIT electrical engineer and physicist who worked on radar and high-voltage systems during World War II. He is notable as the uncle of Donald Trump and, crucially, as the scientist tasked by the FBI to review [[nikola-tesla]]'s papers immediately after Tesla's death in January 1943.

## UAP Connection

Trump's review of Tesla's papers is a significant node in the [[tesla-papers]] chain. Tesla had claimed, in his later years, to have received communications from "space brothers" and worked on advanced propulsion and energy transmission concepts. The FBI's decision to dispatch Trump — a senior MIT physicist with wartime clearances — to assess the papers immediately upon Tesla's death reflects institutional concern about what those papers contained.

Trump's official assessment was that the papers contained "nothing of consequence to the war effort." Whether this assessment was accurate or a cover for classified retention of the most sensitive material is a question the [[09KP8XVf5nY]] episode raises explicitly. [[harold-malmgren]] discusses the Tesla papers as likely still held by intelligence agencies — suggesting Trump's review was the beginning of a classification process, not an end to it.

## Additional Context from q7Czo77qhHA

[[jesse-michaels]] references John Trump in [[q7Czo77qhHA]] in the context of Tesla's papers and suppressed healing technology. The key point added: Trump was an MIT professor reviewing the Tesla files to assess **tactical advantage to the United States** — not for healing applications. Jesse speculates that Trump may not have been looking for, or may not have disclosed, Tesla's work on electromagnetic healing or life-extension principles.

The episode raises the hypothesis that Tesla's papers (seized by the FBI immediately after his death, assessed by Trump, then classified) contained ideas about **electromagnetic effects on biology** — ideas that would have threatened the pharmaceutical model by offering non-patentable treatments based on frequency medicine. This connects John Trump to the [[open-source-healing-suppressed]] cluster and the [[flexner-report]] suppression pattern.
