# Pope John XXIII

261st Pope (1958–1963); born Angelo Giuseppe Roncalli. Known as "Il Papa Buono" (the Good Pope). Convened the Second Vatican Council (Vatican II, 1962–65), the most significant reform of the Catholic Church in centuries. Vatican II opened the Church to ecumenical dialogue, liberalized the liturgy (Mass in vernacular languages), and redefined the Church's relationship to the modern world and other religions. John XXIII is widely considered one of the most beloved and transformative popes of the 20th century.

**UAP encounter claim**: In UFO discourse, John XXIII is referenced for an alleged 1961 encounter at Castel Gandolfo (the papal summer residence in the Alban Hills outside Rome). In the claim — which circulates in multiple versions — the pope witnessed a luminous craft and had some form of communication with an entity or entities. Some versions specify that the encounter was with a humanoid figure on a terrace or garden path at Castel Gandolfo; other versions describe an airborne craft.

**Evidence quality**: The claim is uncorroborated by any Vatican document, papal diary, or firsthand testimony with a named attributable source. It is hearsay-tier: a claim that someone reported that the pope reported something. This is not to say it did not happen — Vatican archives are extremely selective in what they acknowledge — but the KB treats it at the evidence tier it occupies, which is low. Its recurrence in Catholic-UAP discourse (cited in [[diana-pasulka]]'s framework and related discussions) gives it cultural significance disproportionate to its evidentiary weight.

**Institutional significance if true**: A papal encounter with non-human entities during the period John XXIII convened Vatican II would be significant not because it changes the event's theology but because it would connect the Church's most significant 20th-century reform moment to a direct encounter experience at the Vatican's senior institutional level — potentially informing Vatican II's expansion of the Church's openness to non-Christian revelation.
