# Howard Cross

Battelle Memorial Institute scientist and author of the [[pentacle-memo]] — a January 9, 1953 classified letter sent to [[project-blue-book]] Lieutenant H.C. Griffing. The memo proposed controlling UFO investigation procedures to ensure scientifically defensible but predetermined conclusions.

## The Pentacle Memo

The memo outlined a plan to:
1. Screen UFO reports and select which cases would be shown to the [[robertson-panel]] (controlling what the panel evaluated)
2. Establish "controlled field experiments" that would appear rigorous but filter out the most anomalous cases
3. Frame investigations to produce statistically supportable null results

Cross wrote the memo before the Robertson Panel convened (January 14–17, 1953). The timing suggests coordination between Battelle, the USAF, and the CIA in shaping the panel's inputs and conclusions.

## Significance

The Pentacle Memo is one of the clearest documentary examples of institutional manipulation of a UFO investigation. It shows planning before the panel met to ensure the desired outcome. [[j-allen-hynek]] and later researchers pointed to this document as proof that the Robertson Panel was not independent.
