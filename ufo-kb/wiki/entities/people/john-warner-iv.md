# John Warner IV

Grandson of [[paul-mellon]], co-founder of the CIA and one of the most influential philanthropists of the 20th century. Warner appears in [[G7Ns4Aq1tVc]] to relay his grandfather's firsthand account of witnessing a German flying disc in a hangar in Czechoslovakia at the end of World War II — an account transmitted as family oral history and not previously documented in public sources.

**The Paul Mellon account**: According to Warner, his grandfather Paul Mellon made a top-secret trip to Czechoslovakia in the final days of WWII or its immediate aftermath, in the company of [[allen-dulles]] — then the OSS's chief of intelligence for Europe. During this trip, Mellon witnessed a German flying disc in a hangar. Warner presents this as his grandfather's direct recollection, relayed to him personally.

**Craft description detail**: When Warner asked his grandfather whether the disc was the "cobbled-together" six-BMW-engine saucer design attributed to German engineers in conventional accounts, Mellon laughed it off — implying the craft was significantly more sophisticated than the improvised prototypes in the historical record. This detail is significant: it suggests Mellon's recollection was of something beyond the known German experimental disc prototypes ([[nazi-ufo-hypothesis]]), potentially pointing toward either more advanced German development than publicly acknowledged or recovered non-human technology that had been incorporated into a German program.

**Chain of custody**: The account's provenance is family oral history: Paul Mellon → John Warner IV → public record. Mellon himself did not write about this publicly. The transmission chain is two generations long, which introduces confabulation risks that the KB acknowledges. However, Paul Mellon's institutional position — CIA co-founder, present in the same circles as [[allen-dulles]] at war's end — makes his access to such a sighting entirely plausible.

**Dulles connection**: The presence of [[allen-dulles]] on the same trip situates the sighting in the exact network — OSS/CIA covert-operations leadership surveying captured German technology — that the KB identifies as the likely origin point of post-WWII UAP secrecy infrastructure.
