# Fife Symington

Republican Governor of Arizona from 1991 to 1997. Symington is the highest-profile official witness to the [[1997-phoenix-lights]] event — a massive boomerang-shaped craft that flew silently over populated Arizona on March 13, 1997.

## Phoenix Lights Witness

Symington was Governor at the time. He initially held a press conference where he made light of the sightings (bringing out an aide dressed in an alien costume). Years later he publicly reversed, confirming he had personally witnessed the craft and described it as unlike anything explainable by conventional aircraft. The reversal is cited as a significant credibility data point for the Phoenix Lights case.

## Appearances with James Fox

Symington appeared on Larry King Live alongside [[james-fox]] and [[buzz-aldrin]] in the context of UAP disclosure. Fox quotes Symington urging restraint after Fox tried to call out Aldrin for lying on air about his UAP knowledge. Symington also appeared in Fox's documentary [[the-phenomenon-fox]].

Fox describes him as the "iconic figure" whose public reversal on Phoenix Lights is a key credibility anchor for the case.
