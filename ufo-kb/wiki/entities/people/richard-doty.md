# Richard Doty

Richard "Rick" Doty was an agent with the Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI) who became one of the most consequential figures in the disinformation layer of UFO history. His operations targeted civilian UFO researchers and continue to cast a shadow over the credibility of 1980s-era UAP evidence.

## Background

Doty's own father was reportedly deeply involved with Area 51 and UAP programs; Dolan notes that Doty "was brought in young." He served at [[kirtland-afb]] in New Mexico — the same base from which the MJ12 documents were mailed in 1984.

## The Bennewitz Operation

Doty's most documented operation targeted [[paul-benowitz]], a civilian UFO researcher who had independently detected and recorded electronic signals from craft near Kirtland AFB. Rather than confront the legitimate surveillance question, AFOSI fed Benowitz increasingly extreme disinformation about underground alien bases, alien threats, and human-alien treaties until Benowitz experienced a complete psychological breakdown and was hospitalized. [[bill-moore]] participated in this operation and later publicly admitted his role.

The Bennewitz operation is the clearest documented case of the US government deliberately driving a civilian researcher to psychiatric hospitalization using false UAP information. See [[flood-the-zone-disinfo]].

## MJ12 Documents

The [[majestic-12]] documents — which surfaced in 1984 mailed from near Kirtland AFB to UFO filmmaker [[jamie-shandera]], friend of [[bill-moore]] — are connected to Doty's network. Whether the documents are genuine, disinformation, or a mix remains contested. See [[majestic-12]].

## Credibility Assessment

[[richard-dolan]] met Doty once and found him "genuinely charming and likable — that was his job." Dolan acknowledges that Doty later disclosed information that proved accurate, complicating a simple "disinformation agent" label. The Ellsworth AFB [[mario-woods]] case (1977) shows Doty's involvement in UAP debriefs as early as two years before the Bennewitz operation — suggesting a pattern of involvement with real UAP events followed by disinformation cover.
