# Carl Nell

US Army Colonel. Established **Army Futures Command** — the Army's modernization and innovation organization. Also served as the **Army representative for the UAP Task Force** — the DoD body directed to investigate UAP/UFO existence and reality — placing him in the same institutional circle as [[david-grusch]]. Earlier career included aerospace companies ([[lockheed-martin]], [[northrop-grumman]]); commanded every Army level up to Brigade. Privy to classified information; speaks publicly only about non-classified material.

## Three Lines of Evidence for UAP Reality

- Life evolved on Earth at the **earliest possible moment** (fossils at 3.5 Gya; scholarship suggests even 4.1 Gya — before the crust had fully cooled). Microbes viable in space; 2-billion-year-old terrestrial microbes revived from stasis. Life is extremely prolific, not rare.
- The Sun is a population-1 star; there are **20 billion** such stars in the galaxy; some are **5 billion years older** than the Sun — an enormous evolutionary head start.
- **Alcubierre drive** ([[miguel-alcubierre]], 1994): solved Einstein's equations for effective FTL; NASA verifies the solution is valid. Requires **negative energy**. The **Casimir effect** demonstrates negative energy exists. Accelerating cosmic expansion = dark energy = negative energy. FTL is theoretically not ruled out.

### Testimonial
Politicians (Obama, Trump), officials (Lord Hill-Norton, [[haim-eshed]], Paul Hellyer, [[chris-mellon]]), and whistleblowers ([[david-grusch]], [[lue-elizondo]]) have all publicly affirmed UAP reality. Grusch testified under oath before House Oversight (2023).

### Data

## The Four Heresies (Hereticon)

At Hereticon, Nell frames four propositions as the conference's genuine heresies:

1. **Non-disclosure as universal constant** — across cultures and time; six structural reasons in government context (see [[non-disclosure-reasons]]).
2. **Hierarchy of being** — NHI included; man is not apex consciousness. Consistent with every major esoteric tradition.
3. **[[cosmism]]** — the Russian and US space programs were both rooted in esoteric NHI-belief traditions.
4. **Consciousness as physics** — not only metaphysics; technologies based on consciousness are achievable (advanced NHI probably already have them).

## Universal Civilization Development Model (UCDM)

Proposes the UCDM as superior to the [[kardashev-scale]]:

- **Axis 1 — What they have**: energy *density* (fire → metal → nuclear → spacetime manipulation → physics modification); includes Jacques Vallee's "physics of information"
- **Axis 2 — What they know**: information-processing complexity; thought-process time constant; parallel-path capacity; consciousness as a dimension
- **Axis 3 — Who they are**: chemical basis; evolutionary position (100 years ahead? millions? billions?); ecological relationship

Phase-space distance between human and NHI civilization ("Hamming Distance" by analogy) would predict competitive vs. symbiotic vs. indifferent relationship dynamics.

## Esoteric Traditions as NHI Taxonomy

Notes that the Gnostic, Sufi, Kabbalistic, and Advaita Vedanta traditions all describe an inward initiatory experience of transcending this reality and connecting with a deeper one. The **Pyramid Texts** (Egypt, ~2500 BC) — oldest large corpus of religious texts in the world — may be initiatory rather than funerary. This creates direct connective tissue from the oldest recorded esoteric tradition through Hermeticism and Gnosticism into early Christianity.

## Suppressed Wall Street Journal Op-Ed (h0hAit-KH9A)

Nell co-authored an op-ed intended for the Wall Street Journal's opinion section, written alongside an unnamed **former cabinet-level official** (a presidential cabinet member). The piece expressed deep frustration at the lack of transparency and civilian oversight with UAP-related programs in government.

The WSJ opinion section rejected the op-ed "for no apparent reason." The rejection is documented by: [[jesse-michaels]] (who met personally with WSJ opinion editor [[lena-bell]] to advocate for it), [[kirk-mcconnell]] (Senate Intelligence Committee staffer), and email exchanges between Nell and the WSJ.

Simultaneously, the WSJ published [[joel-shectman]]'s article claiming UFO mythology is explained by EMP tests and hazing rituals — sourced from [[sean-kirkpatrick]] and an undisclosed DoD psyops consultant. The asymmetry — cabinet-level UAP transparency op-ed rejected; pro-debunking government-sourced article accepted — is characterized by Michaels as evidence of institutional gatekeeping.
