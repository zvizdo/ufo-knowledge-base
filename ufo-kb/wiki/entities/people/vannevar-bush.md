# Vannevar Bush

## Background

Vannevar Bush was the chief science administrator of the US war effort in World War II, directing the Office of Scientific Research and Development (OSRD) which oversaw the Manhattan Project, radar development, and many other programs. He was arguably the most powerful American scientist in government during the 1940s and shaped the postwar science-government relationship through the National Science Foundation and DARPA predecessors.

## UAP Allegations

Bush is one of the most frequently named individuals in leaked MJ-12 documents and is alleged by [[stanton-friedman]] and others to have chaired the [[majestic-12]] committee following the [[1947-roswell]] crash. His position gave him both the authority and the infrastructure to oversee classified recovery and analysis programs. [[robert-sarbacher]], in correspondence with Friedman, confirmed that elite scientists were working on recovered craft and implied Bush's involvement at the highest level.

In the [[09KP8XVf5nY]] episode, Bush is referenced in the network of MIT/scientific administrator connections that place figures like [[carl-compton]] in the same institutional circle — suggesting a WWII-era scientific elite with shared access to UAP knowledge.
