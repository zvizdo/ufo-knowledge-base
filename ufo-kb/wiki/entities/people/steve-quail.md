# Steve Quail

Author and researcher based in Montana whose primary subject matter covers biblical giants, the Nephilim, prophetic topics, and survivalism. Co-founded **[[gen-6-productions]]** with [[tim-alberino]], producing the *True Legends* documentary series which documented giant-related evidence in Sardinia and elsewhere.

## Role in Kandahar Giant Story

Quail was the first point of contact for the [[2002-kandahar-giant]] account. An elderly woman contacted Quail saying her son — a C-130 cargo pilot still on active duty — had a story that needed to be heard. She convinced her son to come forward, and the pilot first told the story publicly on **Coast to Coast AM with George Noory**. Subsequently, the pilot was flown to Bozeman, Montana, where Quail and Alberino interviewed him on camera (in silhouette) for the *True Legends* series.

After Ellie Marculli published an independent corroborating account, the pilot re-contacted Quail to confirm Marculli's source was telling the truth (verified using details the pilot had deliberately withheld from his public account).
