# Antonio Villas Boas

Brazilian farmer from São Francisco de Sales, Minas Gerais, whose abduction on the night of 15–16 October 1957 is the **earliest documented sexual contact claim in UAP/contactee literature**. His case was investigated by Dr. Olavo Fontes (APRO) and journalist Joao Martins; medical examination documented physical after-effects consistent with radiation exposure.

## The 1957 Abduction

While plowing his family's fields at night (to avoid summer heat), Villas Boas reported:
1. A red star-like object descended and landed nearby
2. Four-foot-tall beings in tight gray suits and helmets captured him and dragged him aboard the craft
3. He was stripped, covered in a gel-like substance, and had blood samples taken
4. A female NHI entered the room — human in form but with slightly different features (higher cheekbones, pointier chin, wide-spaced large blue eyes, platinum blonde hair)
5. Sexual intercourse occurred twice; she appeared to deliberately concentrate on the act as reproduction rather than pleasure
6. Before leaving, the female pointed to her belly and then skyward — interpreted as a gestural claim that she was taking something for offspring
7. He was returned to his field approximately four hours after abduction

He reported persistent symptoms afterward: nausea, fatigue, skin sores, and lesion-like marks on his legs — consistent with radiation exposure.

## Significance in the Future Humans Framework

[[michael-p-masters]] uses the Villas Boas case as a pillar of the **reproductive compatibility argument**:

> "Would you get those [reproductive capabilities] with a species that evolved on a different planet? Would they even have anything close to DNA?"

The fact that sexual intercourse was possible, apparently intended to be reproductively productive, and that the female appeared human in every anatomical respect is consistent with the beings being future Homo sapiens rather than an alien species.

Masters: "Antonio Villas Boas had physical intercourse with this woman who he describes as a woman... she looks a little bit different than women that he knew — slightly higher cheekbones, pointier chin — but all of the same parts."
