# Jeffrey Mishlove

American parapsychologist and author (born 1946). Holds the only doctoral degree in parapsychology awarded by UC Berkeley (1980). Author of *The Roots of Consciousness* and host of the long-running interview series **New Thinking Allowed Foundation** — a platform for academics and researchers working in consciousness, parapsychology, and frontier science.

## Relevance to UAP Discourse

In [[fyX8V1XXmQM]], [[jesse-michaels]] references Mishlove as someone who had recently interviewed [[jacques-vallee]], introducing the question about **UAP perception sensitivity** and biosensors.

Michaels references a figure named "Mr. PK Teto" or similar — described as someone who claimed to be a kind of biological sensor for UAPs — that Mishlove had written about. Vallée follows this into a discussion of whether certain individuals have electromagnetic or consciousness-related properties that make them selectively perceive UAP phenomena, and whether humans may be "swimming in" UAP phenomena that most people simply do not register.
