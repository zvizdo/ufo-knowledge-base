# Linda Brown

Daughter of [[thomas-townsend-brown]]. Appears in [[RTEWLSTyUic]] (the Townsend Brown documentary) as one of the few people with firsthand access to Brown's private life and motivations.

## Key Testimony

- Relayed the NICAP "fluttering leaf" directive: her father told her to filter civilian sighting reports for motion resembling a fluttering leaf — "those are ours" — implying NICAP was partly a screening tool for man-made anti-gravity craft.
- Recalls Bill Lear (of [[bill-lear]]) lunching daily with her father in Santa Monica; their labs were across the street from each other.

## Open Questions

## NHI Kitchen Visits — Reddit AMA Confirmation

In a Reddit AMA, Linda Brown confirmed that **pale-skinned beings** (described as looking like they came from another environment — large eyes, alien appearance) visited their kitchen, spoke with her father all night. She initially thought it was a dream; at dinner two nights later she mentioned it, and her father told her: *"That wasn't a dream."* This corroborates [[tim-taylor]]'s account to [[chris-bledsoe]] of NHI entities visiting and informing Townsend Brown directly.

## Tim Taylor Collaboration

Tim Taylor was actively helping Linda Brown write her **biography of her father** — [[chris-bledsoe]] has the partial manuscript saved on his phone. An Amazon review of Taylor's *Launch Fever* biography by Linda Brown (from the early 2010s, before any public awareness of Taylor) independently confirmed their connection. Taylor told Bledsoe he was working with her on the book.
