# Boyd Bushman

Senior scientist at Lockheed Martin's Fort Worth division. According to [[nick-cook]] in [[G7Ns4Aq1tVc]], Bushman was the first person in the aerospace industry to openly discuss anti-gravity with Cook in a matter-of-fact way — "it's not crazy" — and then actively directed Cook's research by pointing him toward several leads.

## Cook's Interview

Cook sat down with Bushman for a documentary he was making at the time (titled *Beyond Secret*). In that interview, Bushman:
- Confirmed that anti-gravity research was a real and serious pursuit within aerospace
- Provided specific research leads, including the work of [[john-hutchinson]] in Vancouver

## Deathbed Video (Context)

Bushman became more widely known in the UFO community after a video filmed shortly before his death (circa 2014) in which he showed photographs allegedly of alien beings and craft, and claimed Lockheed had a reverse engineering program. Cook does not directly address this later testimony in [[G7Ns4Aq1tVc]], and the video is considered controversial — the alien photographs were disputed as model toys. His earlier, less sensational cooperation with Cook on anti-gravity leads is the more institutionally credible thread.
