# Rupert Sheldrake

British biologist and author best known for his hypothesis of **morphic resonance** — the idea that patterns of behavior and structure are transmitted across generations via a memory-like field, not via DNA or conventional causation. Has documented statistically significant cases of animals and humans appearing to sense distant events, including a study on children knowing when their parents are near before any conventional signal is possible. Appears in the *[[telepathy-tapes]]* documentary as a supporting voice and is described by [[ky-dickens]] as a personal friend.

## Background

PhD in biochemistry, Cambridge. Academic career in plant physiology before pivoting to consciousness and anomalous phenomena research. Author of numerous books on parapsychology and the extension of mind beyond the brain. Has been a persistent target of criticism from the scientific mainstream, including having a *Nature* editor call for his book to be burned — a canonical example of [[physics-lockdown]] and materialist-paradigm suppression.

## Notable Claims

- Children and animals demonstrate statistically significant awareness of distant caregivers' presence or intentions — documented in controlled studies.
- Scientists who ask unorthodox questions deserve the same freedom of inquiry as journalists and creators; censure and credential revocation for pursuing anomalous research are markers of insecurity in the dominant paradigm, not of scientific rigor.
- The "extended mind" model: consciousness and awareness are not confined to the skull but extend into the environment and social relations.
- **"Is the Sun Conscious?"** — Sheldrake's paper arguing that if organizational principle (structured energy/matter coordination) is the correlate of consciousness, then the sun — which exhibits extraordinary organization — may be conscious. Extended to: any sufficiently organized system may be conscious.
- **Banister Effect / 4-Minute Mile:** Once Roger Bannister broke the 4-minute mile barrier, many subsequent runners quickly achieved the same — faster than prior improvement trajectories predicted. Sheldrake interprets this through morphic resonance: the first accomplishment creates a field that makes the same achievement easier for others, independent of direct communication or training transmission. [[jesse-michaels]] cites this in [[KRDE1i3LDuU]] as empirical evidence for morphic field non-local transmission.

## Scopaesthesia Research and the Staring App

Sheldrake's most empirically specific program: he has collected ~1,000 case histories of people responding directionally to being stared at — when looked at from an upstairs window, for instance, subjects turn and look directly at the observer, not randomly. This directional response is found in animals (prey detecting hidden predators) and he treats it as evolutionarily ancient. He has published on "directional scopaesthesia" and is launching a **citizen-science mobile app ("Staring App")** to help people train the ability and record results without university ethics board delays.

His theoretical mechanism for scopaesthesia: the **transactional interpretation of quantum mechanics** (John Cramer; further developed by Ruth Kastner). When a photon is absorbed by a cone cell, the absorbing atom emits a reverse "anti-photon" moving from the future toward the past. The two photons (one from past to future, one from future to past) form a standing wave — physically linking the seer with the seen. If the seer is looking at someone from behind, their projections overlap with the target via this bidirectional light connection. This is the closest he comes to a physical mechanism for extramission.

Compare [[john-joe-mcfadden]]'s CEMI model (EM field IS consciousness) with Sheldrake's own position (EM is the *interface* between morphic fields and matter): both agree the electromagnetic field is central, but disagree on whether EM is consciousness or merely interfaces with it.

## Terence McKenna Connection

[[terence-mckenna]] was a close personal friend who stayed frequently at Sheldrake's Hampstead, London home when visiting. Sheldrake's son [[merlin-sheldrake]] knew McKenna personally as a child and was shaped by his ideas. The Sheldrake home was a gathering point for the McKenna-adjacent intellectual network of consciousness and consciousness researchers.

## Physics of Angels

Sheldrake co-authored *The Physics of Angels* (with [[matthew-fox]], a theologian) by selecting the most resonant passages on angels from Dionysius the Areopagite, Thomas Aquinas, and Hildegard of Bingen, then conducting a dialogue on each. The book asks: if medieval cosmology treated angels as the intelligences guiding the heavenly bodies (stars, planets), and if panpsychism is now academically respectable, what is the difference between an "angel" and the consciousness of a star? Jesse notes that specific attributes assigned to angels in these medieval texts (irradiated light, EM-like lingering effects) map onto modern UAP phenomenology. See [[ufo-religion-convergence]].
