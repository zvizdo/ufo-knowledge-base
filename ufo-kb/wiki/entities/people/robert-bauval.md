# Robert Bauval

Belgian-Egyptian engineer and Egyptologist who established the [[orion-correlation-theory]] — the observation that the three pyramids of Giza align with the three stars of Orion's Belt, and that when precession is used to backtrack this alignment it resolves precisely to ~10,500 BC (12,500 years ago), the date the ancient Egyptians called *Zep Tepi* ("the First Time").

Bauval has been seriously ill for several years as of ~2025, and [[graham-hancock]] pays tribute to him specifically in [[9PJJiut0Iuw]] as the foundational author of the Orion Correlation whose work Hancock credits extensively.

## Core Contribution: Orion Correlation Theory

Bauval's key finding: from directly below, the relative positions and brightnesses of the three Giza pyramids (Khufu, Khafre, Menkaure) reproduce the pattern of Alnitak, Alnilam, and Mintaka in Orion's Belt, including the slight offset of the smallest pyramid — which corresponds to the dimmer star Mintaka. Using precession, the Belt's culmination at dawn on the Spring Equinox matches the ground configuration most precisely at ~10,500 BC.

Hancock collaborated with Bauval on this research and co-authored *Talisman* with him, which discusses the secret societies that Hancock believes preserved this knowledge through the millennia.

## Attacks and Vindication

Bauval received "vicious and vile attacks from Egyptologists" for publishing the Orion Correlation Theory, simply for noting the pattern and its precessional date. Hancock explicitly names this as an example of institutional archaeology's pattern of destroying outsider ideas rather than engaging them.
