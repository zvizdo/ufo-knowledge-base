# Tyler Rogoway

Aviation and defense journalist affiliated with The War Zone / The Drive. Known for investigative reporting on classified aircraft programs and military technology.

## Biometric Scanner Verification

[[bob-lazar]] described a hand biometric scanner at [[s4]] — a device that shone light through bones in the fingers, reading unique bone-length patterns to produce identity badges — and was widely dismissed as fabricating science fiction.

Rogoway's investigative reporting found sources inside **[[tonopah-test-range]]** (Area 52) and possibly adjacent classified facilities who confirmed that **similar biometric bone-scanning hand scanners were used in classified aircraft programs during the same late-1980s period**. One source was a technician who worked on these scanners and described them as unreliable and frequently malfunctioning — consistent with early-generation technology.

This was presented by [[jeremy-corbell]] in his 2018 Bob Lazar documentary as one of the cleanest independent corroborations of Lazar's account: a specific, peculiar technical detail dismissed as impossible when Lazar described it, later confirmed by independent sources in the same geographic/programmatic milieu.
