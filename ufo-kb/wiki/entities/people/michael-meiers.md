# Michael Meiers

Michael Meiers is a researcher and author known primarily for his 1989 book *Was Jonestown a CIA Medical Experiment? A Review of the Evidence*, which presents the argument that the November 1978 [[peoples-temple]] mass murder-suicide at Jonestown, Guyana was not a spontaneous religious cult collapse but rather a CIA-sponsored behavioral control experiment.

**Jonestown-CIA thesis**: Meiers argues that the Jonestown compound functioned as a controlled research environment for mass behavioral manipulation — consistent with the goals and methodologies of [[mk-ultra]] and related CIA behavioral research programs. Key elements of his argument include: the systematic nature of the Jonestown operations (sleep deprivation, public confessionals, pharmaceutical administration, punishment structures), the presence of Leo Ryan (the only US Congressman assassinated in the line of duty) at the site, and the intelligence-community-adjacent backgrounds of several figures involved with the Temple's inner circle.

**Research context**: Meiers's work appeared during a period of increased public awareness of MK-Ultra (the Church Committee hearings were still recent memory in 1989) and alongside other researchers examining the Jonestown event for intelligence community fingerprints. The thesis has not achieved mainstream scholarly acceptance but remains influential in UAP-adjacent and deep-state research communities where Jonestown is treated as a data point alongside [[operation-gladio]] and related programs.

**Credibility**: Meiers's thesis is contested. The mainstream historical consensus attributes Jonestown primarily to Jim Jones's escalating paranoia and authoritarian control. The CIA-experiment thesis requires accepting institutional involvement that has not been documentarily confirmed.
