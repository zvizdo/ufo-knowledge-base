# Randy Anderson

## Background

Randy Anderson (Randy M. Anderson per his DD214 / NSWC Crane training record) is a US Army Special Forces veteran born in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and raised in Las Vegas, Nevada from age 10–11. He initially aspired to be a Navy SEAL but chose the Green Berets after a friend in law enforcement recommended the path suited his personality. He qualified through the famously brutal SFAS/Q-Course — including Team Week with 175–180 lb loads over soft sand, and six months of language training (Indonesian / Malay, unusual for a Tier-1 qualification language).

His MOS was **18 Bravo** — Special Forces Weapons Sergeant — responsible for proficiency on over 100 weapon systems (handguns through recoiless rifles, mortars, rocket launchers) and armorer-level maintenance for the ODA. He has served in combat deployments and continues to work at the Nevada Test Range / Area 51 complex as opposition-force (OPFOR) cadre for units training for overseas operations.

He runs **Bravo One Defense** in Las Vegas, a self-defense and weapons training company.

## The March 2014 NSWC Crane Experience

### Context

In March 2014 Anderson attended an advanced weapons training course at [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]] (Crane, Indiana) focused on the **Mark 44 minigun** and the latest SCAR iteration, with armorer qualification components. He and one other Green Beret performed exceptionally well and were pulled aside at the end of a training day by their cadre.

### Underground Access

They were escorted to a different building, entered an elevator, and descended "significantly further than a few floors" underground. The underground level was markedly more modern than the surface facility — better lighting, streamlined layout, hospital-style directional lines on walls.

After passing a standard security checkpoint, they examined **captured foreign (Russian/Chinese) weapon systems** — unconventional but not exotic — in a first room. A second conversation between the cadre appeared to be a decision point: they then proceeded further.

### The SCIF-Level Checkpoint

A second checkpoint, equivalent to **SCIF entry** (watch removed, no electronics, full scan), led to a hallway bearing the placard:

> **"Off-World Technology Division"**

Anderson and the other Green Beret exchanged looks; the cadre proceeded as if this were routine.

### First Artifact: The Levitating Orb

A metallic basketball-sized sphere was **levitating above a podium** with visible gap between sphere and surface. The metal had an unnatural appearance — unlike any known material Anderson could compare it to. The room's energy felt "ominous" and "significant." Cadre:
- Said the object was **recovered from a crashed craft**
- Described it as a **control mechanism** for the craft
- Indicated it **interacted with consciousness** (Anderson wanted to ask how they knew this but held back)

See [[nswc-crane-levitating-orb]].

### Second Artifact: The Gauntlet Device

A second metallic object — described as arm-mounted, possibly a gauntlet or shield — sat on a table (not levitating). It had a rectangular crystalline display screen. While Anderson looked at it, **shifting hieroglyphic symbols** appeared on the display, moving and cycling through sequences. The cadre mentioned that **someone was possibly harmed or killed during retrieval** of this item due to an energy discharge.

See [[nswc-crane-gauntlet-device]] and [[nhi-hieroglyphic-symbols]].

### Aftermath

The pair was escorted back up. They were told not to discuss the experience outside the program. No additional NDAs were signed beyond the standard training paperwork. Anderson never saw the cadre again and never re-contacted the other Green Beret (different team/group).

The experience was **ontologically transformative**: Anderson could not sleep that night, processing implications. It drove him into meditation, consciousness research, and eventually public disclosure — citing [[david-grusch]] and his friend [[michael-herrera]] as precedents.

## Area 51 / Green Orb

During OPFOR work at Area 51 (the broader Nevada Test Range), Anderson observed a **green orb fly over** while he and a unit cadre were evaluating a training exercise. The cadre responded to the soldier who flagged it: "If you saw it, we've got a lot of paperwork to do" — then: "That's better" when the soldier stood down. This matches the [[lincoln-lapaz]] / [[project-twinkle]] pattern of green fireballs at nuclear-adjacent sites.

## Motivations for Coming Forward

Anderson cites [[david-grusch]] and [[michael-herrera]] as inspirations — framing disclosure as a moral duty ("who am I to protect my little story") and hoping others with similar experiences can provide him "closure" about what he witnessed.

## Bogotá Sphere Symbol Recognition (V00WcEiKRAY)

[[ross-coulthart]] showed Anderson photographs of the symbols on the [[bogota-sphere]] (a Colombian anomalous sphere given to [[jaime-maussan]]). Anderson **"freaked out"** — recognizing the symbols as matching those he saw on the gauntlet device at [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]] (the hieroglyphic display on the crystalline screen). This is independent cross-domain corroboration: symbols observed on recovered off-world technology at NSWC Crane matching symbols on a sphere found in Colombia. Additionally, [[mario-woods]] independently recognized the same symbols as matching those inside the 1977 Ellsworth craft — a third, geographically and temporally separate encounter.
