# Fred Whipple

American astronomer (1906–2004) at Harvard Observatory and the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory. Known for his "dirty snowball" model of comets. PhD student of [[donald-menzel]].

## Operation Moonwatch

Whipple created [[operation-moonwatch]] — a program of 12 citizen science stations distributed around the Earth, staffed by amateur astronomers tracking satellites across the sky. [[j-allen-hynek]] helped Whipple run the program.

From the Operation Moonwatch dataset, [[beatriz-villarroel]] and a collaborator named Jacqueline (identity uncertain — possibly Jackie McKittrick or Jacqueline Durant) found at least **36 UFO reports** — indicating that amateur astronomers systematically encountered anomalous phenomena while participating in what was ostensibly a satellite tracking exercise. These reports have been largely ignored by the mainstream and are now being reviewed by Villarroel's team.

## Connection to the Menzel Network

The Whipple lineage extends the [[debunkers-as-atomic-insiders]] framework:
- **[[donald-menzel]]** → PhD advisor → **Fred Whipple** → hired **[[j-allen-hynek]]** for Operation Moonwatch

Hynek, who started as Blue Book's sceptical astronomer and later became a genuine believer, was embedded in Menzel's academic network from this early period — raising questions about how much of his later trajectory was influenced by what he encountered in that context.
