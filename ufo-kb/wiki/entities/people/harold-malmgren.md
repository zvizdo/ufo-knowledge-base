# Harold Malmgren

## Background

Harold (Harald) Malmgren was a senior presidential adviser who served under four consecutive US presidents: John F. Kennedy, Lyndon B. Johnson, Richard Nixon, and Gerald Ford. An economist and international trade negotiator, he held a Q clearance through the Atomic Energy Commission (AEC) and was a member of the Council on Foreign Relations.

At age 27, Malmgren was reportedly the youngest person present in the Cuban Missile Crisis situation room in October 1962. He is credited by his daughter [[pippa-malmgren]] with having slowed General Curtis LeMay and helping to prevent a nuclear launch during the crisis's most dangerous hours.

His extraordinary proximity to power across multiple administrations placed him at the intersection of nuclear policy, intelligence operations, and — by his own account — the inner circle of UAP knowledge management within the US government.

## Notable Claims

### Physical Handling of UAP Material at Los Alamos

Malmgren claims he was brought to [[los-alamos]] and that Lawrence Geist, an AEC official based in Albuquerque, placed a piece of recovered debris on a desk in front of him. He described the material as feeling "weird because it didn't feel anything" — apparently lacking normal thermal or tactile properties. While holding it, he states he heard words telepathically, though he forgot them afterward. This event is framed in the source as a deliberate test of his reaction, a detail relayed by [[pippa-malmgren]].

This claim directly connects to [[nhi-telepathic-communication]] and raises questions addressed in [[reverse-engineered-craft]] about the nature of recovered materials.

### Bluegill Triple Prime and UAP Shootdown

[[richard-bissell]] — CIA Deputy Director of Plans and founder of Area 51 — privately told Malmgren that the 1962 Bluegill Triple Prime high-altitude nuclear test was used deliberately to down a UAP "tag-along" that had been closely monitoring the detonation. Bissell stated the craft tumbled into the ocean and was recovered by the US Navy. This account is central to [[directed-energy-uap-weapon]] and [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]], and speaks to the deliberate use of nuclear events as both bait and weapon against UAP.

### 1933 Magenta Crash — Pre-Grusch Disclosure

Bissell also privately named the 1933 Magenta, Italy crash to Malmgren — decades before David Grusch made it public in 2023. According to Malmgren: "He said this did not begin last week. He mentioned 1933 Magenta." This constitutes a significant independent corroboration within the [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] chain of custody narrative and predates all public discussion of the Magenta incident.

### JFK Assassination and UFO Knowledge

Malmgren believed that JFK's intent to share UFO knowledge with the Soviets was central to his assassination. He described it as "probably the number one issue." This connects directly to [[jfk-assassination-ufo]] and to the broader fear of disclosure addressed in [[catastrophic-disclosure-fear]]. For context on JFK's relationship to UFO briefings, see [[james-forrestal]], who reportedly briefed JFK personally.

### Entry into "The Important Books"

Malmgren was told by a senior official that on the day he met the president of MIT, "your name went in all the important books... not only CIA but... all the majestic, all these guardians." This is one of the rare firsthand descriptions of the compartmentalized custodian structure — pointing toward the [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] as a real organizational entity with membership records.

### Final Phone Call and Time Travel

Three days before his death, in what his daughter describes as his final lucid phone call, Malmgren replied "yes" when asked whether his life was connected to [[time-travel-uap]]. He fell silent when pressed about whether a coordinated group was attempting to influence timelines. This disclosure — given its timing and his history — is treated in the source as among the most significant and verifiable of his claims.

### The Three-Event JFK Briefing Sequence

Pines reconstructs a compressed timeline around the Cuban Missile Crisis (October 1962):
1. Malmgren, age 27, is present in the crisis situation room — his most proximate moment to presidential power.
2. Malmgren is debriefed by Geist, who places UAP material on his desk and apparently tests his psionic receptivity.
3. JFK makes his **first-ever presidential visit to Los Alamos** approximately one month after Malmgren's debriefing — strongly suggesting Kennedy had just received a UAP briefing that required a Los Alamos visit.

This sequence tightens the inferential chain: Malmgren → AEC material access → JFK briefing → JFK assassination motive. Malmgren assessed UAPs as "probably the number one issue" behind the assassination.

4. **Now publicly speaking**: Pines states Malmgren is "now public" saying NHI are real and the US has their technology.
