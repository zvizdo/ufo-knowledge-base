# George Joannides

George Joannides (1922–1990) was a CIA psychological warfare specialist based at the JM/WAVE station in Miami — the CIA's massive anti-Castro operations base in southern Florida. He is one of the most significant documented examples of CIA deception of congressional oversight bodies in the context of the Kennedy assassination investigation.

## Background

Joannides ran the CIA's psychological warfare program targeting the Cuban exile community, including funding and directing the DRE (Directorio Revolucionario Estudiantil — Cuban Student Directorate). The DRE had direct documented interactions with Lee Harvey Oswald in the months before the Kennedy assassination, including distributing material about Oswald's "pro-Castro" activities.

## Notable Claims / Role

When the House Select Committee on Assassinations (HSCA) reopened the Kennedy investigation in the late 1970s, the CIA assigned Joannides as the agency's liaison to the committee — without disclosing that Joannides had personally run the DRE program that had contact with Oswald. He actively misled the committee about the nature and extent of his prior role. This deception was not discovered until decades later through FOIA litigation by journalist Jefferson Morley. Joannides died in 1990 without ever being prosecuted, despite the documented congressional deception. His case is a key exhibit in the argument that CIA obstruction of Kennedy assassination investigations was systematic and institutional.
