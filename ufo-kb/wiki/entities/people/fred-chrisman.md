# Fred Chrisman

Frederick Lee Crisman (1919–1975) was an OSS veteran who became the central figure in the [[1947-maury-island]] UFO incident and later surfaced in [[jim-garrison]]'s investigation of the Kennedy assassination through his documented connection to [[clay-shaw]].

## The Maury Island Incident (1947)

Chrisman supervised the Maury Island incident — where doughnut-shaped craft reportedly dropped slag over Puget Sound. He was present when civilian pilot Kenneth Arnold and other investigators examined the material. The two US Air Force intelligence officers who were killed when their plane exploded while carrying the slag samples back to their base represent, in Levenda's framing, the first deaths in the modern UFO era.

## JFK Network

Chrisman's known relationship with [[clay-shaw]] — the CIA contract agent whom Garrison brought to trial for conspiracy in the Kennedy assassination — drew Garrison's attention to Chrisman. Levenda notes that Chrisman is possibly one of the "hobos" arrested at Dealey Plaza on November 22, 1963 — an identification made by some researchers but not confirmed.

Chrisman was also associated with [[guy-banister]]'s Camp Street network in New Orleans, which included [[david-ferrie]] and connected to the broader anti-Castro Cuban exile infrastructure.

## The UFO-JFK Through-Line

Chrisman's presence in both the 1947 UFO flap (Maury Island) and the 1963 JFK assassination network is one of the cleaner examples in the KB of a figure who physically bridges these two domains — not through theory but through documented relationships.
