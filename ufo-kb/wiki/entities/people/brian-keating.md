# Brian Keating

Astrophysicist and cosmologist at the University of California, San Diego. Known primarily for leading the BICEP (Background Imaging of Cosmic Extragalactic Polarization) telescope project at the South Pole, which sought to detect primordial gravitational waves through CMB B-mode polarization.

## Podcast and Public Role

Host of the *Into the Impossible* podcast, which interviews scientists and researchers including figures at the fringe of mainstream science. This has brought him into contact with — and made him a reference point in — discussions touching [[archaeology-mainstream-vs-alternative]], pyramid research, and consciousness studies.
