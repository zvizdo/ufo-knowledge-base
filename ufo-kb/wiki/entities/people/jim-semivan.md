# Jim Semivan

## Background

Jim Semivan is a retired CIA senior officer who has spoken publicly about anomalous personal experiences. His willingness to discuss these experiences openly is unusual for someone of his institutional background and speaks to a shift in how intelligence community veterans are engaging with UAP/NHI disclosure.

## Personal Entity Experiences

Per [[bob-maguire]]'s account: Semivan and his wife were visited by **entities in their room** — an experience Bob characterizes as "not pleasant." This traumatized Semivan and his wife and drove him to seek answers. He turned to traditional religious texts (Catholic/Greek/Roman sources), which he found resonant with the encounter descriptions.

Discovering [[chris-bledsoe]]'s accounts — which feature an entity Bledsoe describes in feminine/Marian terms — Semivan recognized structural parallels to his own experience and went to visit Bledsoe personally.

## Role as Gatekeeper

Semivan's visit to Bledsoe preceded [[bob-maguire]]'s visit. When Bob reached out to Bledsoe directly (offering scientific sensor assistance), Bledsoe was silent for three weeks. Semivan communicated to Bledsoe that Bob should be allowed in: "Jim Semivan said 'oh yeah, you should let Bob in, this should be interesting.'" Bledsoe is the next-to-last incident in Bledsoe's book *UFO of God* — Semivan opened that door.

This makes Semivan a **node** connecting CIA institutional knowledge, personal anomalous experience, and the Bledsoe experiencer network.
