# Neil McCasland

Retired USAF Major General whose career placed him at the intersection of black-world defense science and early UAP disclosure efforts. He disappeared from his Albuquerque, New Mexico neighborhood on February 27, 2026 — eight days after President Trump publicly announced a UAP file declassification effort — and has not been found despite one of the most intensive missing-person searches in New Mexico history.

## Background

McCasland graduated from the US Air Force Academy, earned a **PhD in astronomical engineering from MIT** on a Hertz Foundation fellowship, and later studied at Harvard's Kennedy School. His documented career arc spans directed energy, space weapons, nuclear oversight, black satellite programs, and special access program oversight.

Key positions:
- **Space-based laser project office** (leadership role)
- **Vice commander, Space and Missile Systems Center**
- **Materials wing director, AFRL Space Vehicles Directorate**, Kirtland AFB
- **National Reconnaissance Office** — career period in black-satellite world
- **Director of Special Programs, Pentagon (OSD AT&L)**, June 2009–May 2011 — oversees ~75–80% of all SAPs in the DoD; per his Wikipedia page, he was executive secretary of [[sapoc]] (Special Access Program Oversight Committee), the body that approves every SAP in the Pentagon
- **Commander, Air Force Research Laboratory**, Wright-Patterson AFB, 2011–2013 — $2.2B science and technology portfolio; advanced materials, future weapons, exotic propulsion; alleged Roswell debris storage site

His post-retirement work was not a quiet retreat:
- **DBE Consulting** — New Mexico national security consulting firm; co-founded with James Technelia, former deputy director of [[darpa]] and director of [[dtra]]
- **Board of Trustees, Riverside Research** — nonprofit with hundreds of millions in defense/intelligence contracts (joined 2019)
- **Director of Technology, Applied Technology Associates (ATA)** — aerospace firm working in space systems and directed energy

## UAP Disclosure Involvement

In 2016, Wikileaks published hacked emails of [[john-podesta]]. A key email from [[tom-delonge]] to Podesta stated that McCasland had "been working with him for 4 months," had received "a 4-hour presentation on the entire project," and that he "was the man in charge of all of this stuff" at Wright-Patterson — specifically the laboratory where Roswell material was allegedly shipped and studied. DeLonge described McCasland as "not a skeptic" despite publicly saying so, and noted he "helped assemble my advisory team."

McCasland's wife [[susan-mccasland]] (a PhD astrophysicist, Air Force Reserve Colonel, NASA astronaut semi-finalist, and former Boeing/Raytheon employee) later acknowledged that McCasland had "less contact with Tom after the emails were released" — confirming prior contact. A Google Calendar notification in the same leaked batch showed Susan McCasland accepting an invite for a "DeLonge-Podesta meeting."

Susan's post-disappearance Facebook statement reads: "Neil does not have any special knowledge about the ET bodies and debris from the Roswell crash stored at Wright-Pat." Notably, she does not deny the Roswell material exists there — only that Neil lacks special knowledge of it.

## Disappearance

**Date:** February 27, 2026  
**Location:** Quail Run Court, Albuquerque, NM (near Sandia Mountains / Cibola National Forest)

Timeline:
- ~10:00 AM — repairman sees McCasland at home
- ~11:10 AM — wife Susan leaves for medical appointment
- ~12:04 PM — Susan returns; McCasland is gone
- **Left behind:** prescription glasses, phone (switched off), smartwatch (all trackable devices)
- **Missing:** red backpack, wallet, one .38 caliber revolver with holster
- 3:07 PM — Susan files missing person report

Despite 700 homes canvased, FBI involvement, Air Force OSI, New Mexico State Search and Rescue, horseback teams, three search-dog types, drones, and helicopters, no confirmed sightings or scent trail emerged. The only possible evidence: a gray Air Force sweatshirt found a mile east of his house; DNA testing inconclusive.

**Context:** McCasland was physically active (cycled 60 miles the week before; regularly hiked the Sandia foothills). His wife noted he had health anxiety but described him as mentally sharp. The Silver Alert issued was standard procedure, not a dementia diagnosis.

**Timing:** Trump's Truth Social UAP announcement was February 19, 2026 — eight days before McCasland vanished.

**Unanswered question:** If McCasland was one of [[danny-sheehan]]'s "24 retired officials" (The Association) quietly pushing UAP programs toward government disclosure, his disappearance at the moment of a potential UAP file release would make him either a liability, a target, or a man acting on his own initiative to avoid what was coming.
