# Ed Rhodes

American citizen; Chief Pilot for Tajik Air (based in Dushanbe, Tajikistan); veteran of Pan American World Airways with extensive long-haul commercial flight experience. Rhodes is identified in US Embassy Dushanbe cable 94 DUSHANBE 259 as the primary reporting pilot for the January 27, 1994 UFO encounter over Kazakhstan.

**January 27, 1994 encounter**: While flying a Boeing 747SP at 41,000 feet over Kazakhstan (approximately 45°N 55°E), Rhodes and two American colleagues observed a bright light at much higher altitude approaching rapidly from the east. They observed the object for approximately 40 minutes performing circles, corkscrews, and 90-degree turns "under very high G's." Rhodes took photographs with a pocket Olympus camera and relayed the report to the US Embassy.

After the sighting, as the sun was rising approximately 45 minutes later, the aircraft flew under contrails left by the object at an estimated altitude of 100,000 feet — the contrail patterns mirrored the observed maneuvers. Rhodes noted the impossibility of conventional aircraft producing contrails at that altitude.

When the Embassy suggested a meteor explanation, Rhodes — drawing on his Pan Am experience — was "adamant" the object was nothing like atmospheric re-entry phenomena and expressed the view that the object was "extraterrestrial and under intelligent control."
