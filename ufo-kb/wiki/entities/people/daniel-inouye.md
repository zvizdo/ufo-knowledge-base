# Daniel Inouye

US Senator from Hawaii (D), serving 1963–2012 — the second-longest-serving senator in US history. Senate President pro tempore from 2010 until his death in 2012. WWII Medal of Honor recipient (442nd Regimental Combat Team).

Cited by [[lue-elizondo]] in [[1f16VvXaSSE]] as one of the three Senate-side architects of [[aawsap]] — alongside [[harry-reid]] (Nevada, Senate Majority Leader) and [[ted-stevens]] (Alaska, WWII pilot and Foo Fighter witness). The Inouye-Reid-Stevens triad provided the bipartisan appropriations cover that funded the ~$22M [[bigelow-aerospace]] contract to investigate UAP and paranormal phenomena at [[skinwalker-ranch]].

## Background

- US Senator (Hawaii, 1963–2012); Chairman of the Senate Appropriations Committee 2009–2012.
- Long-time member of the Senate Defense Appropriations Subcommittee — the authorizing channel for AAWSAP.
- Together with Reid and Stevens, signed the joint letter authorizing the AAWSAP appropriation as a special access black-budget line item.
