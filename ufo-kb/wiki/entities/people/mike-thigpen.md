# Mike Thigpen

Security clearance officer who processed personnel for the S4/Area 51 facility complex in 1989. [[bob-lazar]] named Thigpen publicly as the person who handled his clearance — a very specific and verifiable claim. For 30 years Thigpen had no public presence and ignored [[george-knapp]]'s attempts to contact him (Knapp named him on a news broadcast, causing Thigpen to reportedly "drop his fork" at dinner).

## Corbell's 30-Year Find

[[jeremy-corbell]] traced Thigpen via Facebook and Google image matching through his adult children. Corbell spoke with Thigpen in three phone calls. Thigpen confirmed:
- He performed security clearances for the base in 1989
- He remembers Bob Lazar

Thigpen came close to going on camera but ultimately declined. A handwritten note bearing Thigpen's signature was left at a friend of Lazar's house during the original clearance investigation — a physical artifact Corbell has located.
