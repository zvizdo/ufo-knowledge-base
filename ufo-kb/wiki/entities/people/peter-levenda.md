# Peter Levenda

Author and independent researcher (age 75 at recording) whose work focuses on the intersections of occultism, Nazi ideology, and American intelligence community history. His major works include the *Sinister Forces* trilogy (three volumes exploring American history through the lens of occult networks and covert operations), *The Unholy Alliance* (Nazi occultism), and the *Secret Machines* non-fiction series co-authored with [[tom-delonge]] (the cargo cult thesis for human civilization). He is a member of the TTSA inner circle.

## Background

Levenda grew up in New York and joined the [[american-orthodox-catholic-church]] as a teenager around age 17–18 to obtain a draft deferment from the Vietnam War. He later discovered this church's stationery in [[jim-garrison]]'s released JFK investigation files — creating a direct personal loop between his own history and the Kennedy assassination network. He was involved in the 1977 publication of the *Necronomicon* — a manuscript he helped find in the possession of monks running a book theft operation above a strip club in Jamaica, Queens.

His research methodology treats occult movements and intelligence operations as overlapping phenomena rather than separate domains. His collaboration with [[tom-delonge]] and the TTSA circle brought his work to a significantly wider audience after ~2015.

## Notable Claims

### TTSA Inner Circle Access

Levenda participated in an all-day brainstorming session in Austin, Texas with DeLonge, [[hal-puthoff]], [[chris-mellon]], and [[jacques-vallee]]. When Skinwalker Ranch was specifically brought up, Puthoff, Mellon, and Vallee all went completely silent. Puthoff said only: *"We need a SCIF for this one."* Puthoff reportedly told Joe Rogan: *"We have 15 crash craft in our possession."* Steve Justice (TTSA) told Levenda he did not know if they were in possession of craft.

### The Prometheus Insider Tip

A senior government/intelligence advisor (name withheld) told Levenda and DeLonge ~2015 to study the Prometheus myth — implying insider belief that a non-human being deliberately jump-started human civilization against the wishes of other NHI. See [[cargo-cult-hypothesis]] for the full thesis.

### JFK Network Research

From the Warren Commission transcripts, Levenda reconstructed a striking network: [[ruth-payne]] (who housed Oswald and got him his Book Depository job) was the daughter-in-law of [[arthur-young]] (Bell helicopter inventor who attended the [[1952-nine-seance]] with [[andrija-puharich]]). Arthur's wife [[ruth-forbes-payne-young]] was best friends with Allen Dulles's mistress. [[allen-dulles]] on the Warren Commission personally cut off Ruth Payne's testimony the moment she was about to describe visiting Arthur Young's household.

### Nazi Research

His documented research in [[ss-ahnenerbe]] files (US National Archives), his connections to Paperclip history (including [[walter-dornberger]]'s Wright Patterson posting when Roswell debris arrived), and his reconstruction of the [[kammler-stab]] secret weapons programs under [[hans-kammler]] constitute the core of the *Unholy Alliance* and [[occult-aerospace-connection]] scholarship.

### Men in Black (Firsthand)

In ~1990 in Rhode Island, Levenda had a firsthand MIB encounter — two men in a black Cadillac using the name "Devilbiss," conducting surveillance of his home. He was never able to determine what they wanted. One of the Women in Black from the encounter reappeared years later in Singapore's Changi Airport — tapped him on the shoulder, waved, and vanished into the crowd.

### Personal NHI Experiences

- Table levitation at age 15 at a séance
- 2am electrical buzzing + interior knocking at his Florida home; Whitley Strieber told him this was "visitors saying hello"
- Black triangle UAP sighting in Florida, December 2019: absolutely silent, teleported position — his first clear UAP sighting

## Credibility Assessment

See [[credibility-frameworks]]. Levenda is a professional researcher with documented access to primary sources (National Archives, Warren Commission transcripts, Garrison files). His TTSA membership gave him direct access to figures like Puthoff, Mellon, and Vallee. His personal NHI experiences (MIB, 2am buzzing, triangle UAP) are firsthand but unverifiable. His Warren Commission research is from publicly available transcripts. His interpretation of the network connections is research-based but disputed.
