# Bob Brier

## Background

Egyptologist known for work in forensic mummification and Egyptian history. Conducted an investigation of [[tutankhamun-pharaoh]]'s mummified body (likely in the 1990s) and analyzed the blood pooling patterns in the skull.

## Findings

The blood had pooled at the back of the skull — indicating the king lay on his back for an extended period after suffering what appears to be a blunt-force head trauma. Brier's conclusion: Tutankhamun was likely murdered, possibly struck from behind.

This finding aligns with the political context: Tutankhamun reversed his father [[akhenaten-pharaoh]]'s religious revolution under pressure from his advisor Ay and the generals, and his death conveniently opened the door for Ay to take the throne.
