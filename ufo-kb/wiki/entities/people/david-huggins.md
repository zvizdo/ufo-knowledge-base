# David Huggins

American contactee and self-taught painter known for hundreds of detailed oil paintings depicting his alleged lifelong encounters with alien beings. His experiences were documented in the 2017 documentary *Love and Saucers*.

## Contact Account

Huggins claims encounters beginning in childhood in Georgia. His primary recurring contact is with a grey-type female entity he calls Crescent, with whom he describes an ongoing relationship over decades.

## Roger Caillois Connection

Cited by [[jason-reza-jorjani|Jason Reza Jorjani]] in the context of [[roger-caillois|Roger Caillois]]'s work on mimicry: alien beings appear to establish social/emotional bonds that mirror human attachment, suggesting either empathy programming or strategic manipulation of human emotional systems.
