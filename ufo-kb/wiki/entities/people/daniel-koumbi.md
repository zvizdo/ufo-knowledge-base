# Daniel Koumbi

Physicist affiliated with the Niels Bohr Institute (Copenhagen). Performed the most rigorous quantitative analysis of the [[1986-jal-1628]] encounter by obtaining and analyzing the actual military height-finding radar data preserved by [[john-callahan]].

## Notable Claims / Analysis

[[kevin-knuth]] had done an initial performance estimate from witness descriptions when he published the JAL 1628 analysis. Koumbi separately obtained the radar data and applied a more precise methodology:

- Analyzed discrete position jumps between radar sweeps (~12-second intervals)
- Found **three separate acceleration events each exceeding 9,000 Gs**; one reaching approximately **11,000 Gs**
- Estimated top speed: **~250,000 mph** (moon transit in ~54 minutes)
- The object was approximately aircraft-carrier-sized throughout

For context: F-35 wings fail at 13 G; missile frames survive to ~60 G. No human-built craft approaches these figures.
