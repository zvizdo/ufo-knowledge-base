# Timothy Good

British UFO researcher and author, best known for *[[above-top-secret]]* (1987), a meticulously sourced survey of the global government UFO cover-up that drew heavily on declassified documents and reached across US, UK, and international sources. The book is considered a canonical reference in the field.

## Background

Good comes from a British Ministry of Defence background rather than American intelligence circles, giving his work a different vantage point than most American researchers. His research approach emphasized documentary evidence and governmental documentation rather than witness testimony alone.

## Significance to Richard Dolan

[[richard-dolan]] credits *Above Top Secret* as the catalyst for his entire UFO research career. In ~1994, at age 32 in a Syracuse bookstore, Dolan saw the book's subtitle — "The Worldwide UFO Cover-Up" — and recognized the agencies and era from his own PhD-level historical research. His immediate response was cognitive dissonance: "I know that name. I read his diaries. I know that department. Wait, what? UFOs?" Dolan describes Good's research as "at least halfway decent" — high praise from a trained historian — and credits it as the entry point into a rabbit hole he never exited.

> "You got to pay respect. You got to know that the these are canonical works." — Dolan

## Later Life

Dolan reports that as of 2026, Good "is not well these days" and appears to have retired. He estimates Good is "a little over 80." Dolan describes him as "a real gentleman and a smart guy."
