# John McDow

Former president of the American Forensics Association and recipient of its highest honor, the Grand Wall Award. One of three sets of independent forensic experts who examined the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] in person. Joined by Denver chief medical examiner [[jim-caruso]].

## Background

Highest credentialed American forensic expert to publicly engage with the Nazca mummies. Saw the bodies in person. Specifically looked for evidence of deliberate finger/toe mutilation and skull-binding deformation — found none.

## Notable Claims

- The entities "can be interpreted to perhaps be living organisms" — deliberately careful phrasing suggesting genuine uncertainty rather than debunking.
- No evidence of manual mutilation of the fingers or toes.

## Credibility Assessment

Top US forensic credential; personally examined the bodies; cautious, qualified language. Among the most credibility-weighted voices in this case. See [[credibility-frameworks]].
