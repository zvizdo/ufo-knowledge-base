# James Inhofe

Republican Senator from Oklahoma who served multiple terms as chair of the Senate Armed Services Committee. Inhofe co-signed the SAP request letter authored by [[harry-reid]] that authorized AAWSAP (Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Application Program) in 2008. [[david-grusch]] cites Inhofe's signature as evidence that Reid's UAP investigation had bipartisan senior Senate support from the start — Inhofe and Reid were ideological opposites on virtually every other issue, making their cooperation on this letter notable.

## UAP-Adjacent Senate Activity

Inhofe (R-OK) served in the US Senate 1994-2023, including stints as Chairman of the Senate Armed Services Committee. He was a co-sponsor of the 2020 Intelligence Authorization Act UAP-disclosure rider that originated in HPSCI. Inhofe's UAP posture was driven by his Armed Services portfolio (concern about military aviator UAP encounters as a flight-safety / national-security issue) rather than by NHI-existence advocacy.

Inhofe retired from the Senate in January 2023; his UAP-disclosure legacy is the 2020 IAA rider that created the framework AARO inherited. He did not live to see the 2024 Schumer-Rounds UAP Disclosure Act floor debate; he died July 9, 2024.
