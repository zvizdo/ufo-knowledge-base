# Charlie Duke

Apollo 16 lunar module pilot; one of twelve humans who walked on the Moon. Served as CAPCOM (capsule communicator) for Apollo 11, making him the voice that told Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin "You're Go for Landing" — one of the most famous moments in space history.

## Danny Jones Debate

At approximately 90 years old, Duke appeared on the [[danny-jones]] podcast for a debate with [[bart-sibrel]], the most prominent moon-landing skeptic. The episode was referenced by [[joe-rogan]], who said Danny Jones texted him clips and afterward told Rogan he was "maybe convinced we didn't go to the moon."

Duke's primary defense:
- "I walked on the moon. I don't know about anybody else, but I walked on the moon."
- The approximately 200 lb of moon rocks returned across Apollo missions — "Where did 200 pounds of moon rocks come from? I just showed you."

Sibrel's counter (per Rogan): Werner von Braun was photographed collecting lunar meteorites in Antarctica before the first Apollo mission. The rocks may be Antarctic meteorites, not samples collected on the Moon.

Rogan's observation on Duke's testimony: *"Imagine what his memories are like. I can barely remember a fight from 1987 that's on YouTube. He's recounting something from 1969 and he's Biden's age."* The reliability of a 90-year-old man's memories of events 55+ years ago is noted as a legitimate epistemic concern without dismissing his account.
