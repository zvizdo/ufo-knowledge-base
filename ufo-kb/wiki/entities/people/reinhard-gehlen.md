# Reinhard Gehlen

German military intelligence officer (1902–1979). Head of *Fremde Heere Ost* (Foreign Armies East) — the Wehrmacht's intelligence directorate covering the Soviet Union — during World War II. After Germany's defeat, Gehlen negotiated directly with the US Army and CIA, offering his entire intelligence network (agents, files, infrastructure) in exchange for protection from war crimes prosecution and operational continuity. The resulting "Gehlen Organization" was funded and operated by the CIA from 1946 until it was officially transferred to the West German government as the BND (Bundesnachrichtendienst) in 1956.

This meant the CIA absorbed an intact Nazi intelligence apparatus — with the same personnel, the same informants (many ex-SS), and the same operational methods — at its founding moment. [[jason-reza-jorjani]] uses this fact as evidence that the CIA was structurally continuous with the Nazi intelligence apparatus from inception: "World War II is not what we think it is."

## Role in Magenta Craft Exfiltration

[[harold-malmgren]] testified (per Jorjani in [[ABw7EsWh2Us]]) that Gehlen organized the exfiltration of the 1933 Magenta, Italy crash craft via Nazi rat lines — the underground escape and logistics networks originally used to move SS officers out of Europe after the war. This is consistent with Gehlen's documented operational role in postwar intelligence logistics and with his [[knights-of-malta]] membership in the Monte Carlo faction.

Jorjani notes that the Knights of Malta Monte Carlo group included both Nazi officers like Gehlen *and* Jewish figures like [[henry-kissinger]] — suggesting a transnational elite faction operating above the ideological categories of WWII.

> ⚠ **Conflict with existing Magenta account**: The existing [[1933-magenta-crash]] page (sourced from [[harold-malmgren]] and [[richard-bissell]]) describes the craft as transferred to US custody via OSS [[project-mcgregor]] coordinated by [[allen-dulles]]. Jorjani's account adds Gehlen's rat lines as the specific logistics mechanism — these may be compatible (Gehlen's network providing the operational means for the Dulles-directed transfer) or represent different intelligence chains.

## ODESSA Connection

Gehlen is closely associated with [[odessa]] — the informal network (sometimes called "The Spider") of former SS officers who evaded prosecution and maintained underground continuity. His organization operationalized the rat lines that [[odessa]] used, and James Bond mythology (Ian Fleming) is traced by Jorjani to ODESSA's dramatic underground network.

## Knights of Malta — Transnational Faction
