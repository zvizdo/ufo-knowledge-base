# Dick Cheney

Richard "Dick" Cheney served as Secretary of Defense under George H.W. Bush (1989–1993) and as 46th Vice President under George W. Bush (2001–2009). In the UAP disclosure context he appears as an alleged gatekeeper at the apex of the legacy UAP classification structure.

## Role in UAP Discourse

[[lue-elizondo]] implies that Cheney sat "at the top of the pyramid of classified information" regarding legacy UAP programs — the highest civilian official with acknowledged access. Elizondo's framing is notably ambiguous: he raises the possibility that even Cheney was not read into the full picture, suggesting the classification architecture was designed to compartmentalize even its own apex custodians.

This claim is significant because it:
1. Places UAP secrecy within the Executive Branch's national-security apparatus at the highest levels, not merely in military or contractor structures.
2. Implies the program may be so tightly compartmentalized that no single official, even the Vice President, had complete visibility.
3. Connects to the broader [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] concept — a custodian structure that may operate outside normal chains of command.

## Grusch/Kern Corroboration (2025)

In [[u7g5Sn1DJF4]], [[uap-gerb]] cites [[walter-kern]] — a journalist who knows [[david-grusch]] personally — as relaying Grusch's characterisation of Cheney:

> "Dick Cheney was the head of the UFO pyramid for quite a while... when he left in 2009, that was the last time that these activities really had central leadership."

UAPGerb finds this "highly likely, probably true." This convergence of sources (Elizondo + Grusch via Kern) strengthens the Cheney-as-apex claim, though neither source provides documentary evidence. Cheney died in 2022, leaving no living apex figure identified.
