# Leonid Brezhnev

General Secretary of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union (1964–1982) and head of state of the USSR for 18 years — the longest-serving Soviet leader after Stalin. In the UAP discourse he is the Soviet recipient of whatever information President [[richard-nixon]] showed in a classified folder during their first summit meeting.

## UAP Incident

According to [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]] (relaying [[mikhail-gorbachev]] relaying [[yuri-andropov]]): at the first major meeting between Brezhnev and Nixon, after the formal session ended and only a few people remained, Nixon opened a small folder and showed Brezhnev its contents. Andropov, invited as KGB chairman, was present and witnessed this exchange.

Immediately after the meeting: **all Soviet and US lunar exploration was completely and permanently halted.** "All funding for lunar exploration was entirely halted, both from the Soviet Union and from the US. And what it all came down to, just as Gorbachev himself told me, was this very folder that the US president showed to our leader."

Brezhnev's reaction to the folder's contents is not described; only the outcome is: the moon race stopped.

## Context

The Brezhnev era is when the KGB under [[yuri-andropov]] ran its dedicated UFO and USO research department. Massive unidentified underwater craft were documented in multiple oceans; the US and Soviet militaries were exchanging UFO data bilaterally specifically to prevent misidentification. This policy-level cooperation was occurring at the same time that Brezhnev was being shown whatever Nixon had in that folder.
