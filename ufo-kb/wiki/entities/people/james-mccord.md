# James McCord

CIA security officer and one of the five Watergate burglars arrested on June 17, 1972 at the Democratic National Committee headquarters in the Watergate complex. McCord was security coordinator for the Committee to Re-Elect the President (CRP/CREEP) at the time of the break-in.

[[danny-sheehan]] identifies McCord as part of the same [[s-force]] / [[operation-40]] network that he alleges carried out the JFK assassination — illustrating the thesis that the same covert CIA infrastructure was reused across multiple political operations spanning decades.
