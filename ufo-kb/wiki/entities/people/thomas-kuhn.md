# Thomas Kuhn

Philosopher and historian of science; author of *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions* (1962), which introduced the concept of **paradigm shifts** — the idea that scientific progress happens not linearly but through crises and revolutionary reorganizations in which entire conceptual frameworks are replaced.

Kuhn's central thesis: almost every major scientific revolution was "fought tooth and nail" by the more conservative skeptics before being accepted. The conservative establishment's resistance to paradigm change is normal and predictable, not a sign of the revolutionaries' error.

## Connection to UAP Discourse

Cited frequently by researchers in the UAP field as the intellectual framework for why UAP science faces institutional resistance: the evidence threatens the existing paradigm, so the paradigm defends itself rather than updating to the evidence.

**Personal friendship with John Mack**: [[garry-nolan]] reports in [[dzTZbSNsKV8]] that Kuhn was a personal friend of [[john-mack]] and that Kuhn encouraged Mack to pursue his alien abduction research. This is significant — the paradigm-shift philosopher actively supporting research that challenged the dominant materialist paradigm he had described as resistant to challenge.
