# Alice Coltrane

Alice Coltrane (1937–2007), born Alice McLeod, was a jazz harpist, pianist, organist, and composer — widow of John Coltrane and one of the most important spiritual music figures of the 20th century. In later life she became a devotee of Sai Baba and founded the Shanti Ananda Mission ashram in California, taking the name Swamini Turiyasangitananda. Her music integrates jazz improvisation with Hindu devotional traditions.

She is the great-aunt of [[flying-lotus]] (Steven Ellison), who credits her as the single largest influence on his spiritual receptivity and creative sensibility.
