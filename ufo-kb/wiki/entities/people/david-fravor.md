# David Fravor

US Navy Commander who served as Commanding Officer of VFA-41 Black Aces, a storied West Coast strike fighter squadron. In November 2004, during the USS Nimitz Carrier Strike Group's operational deployment off the coast of Southern California, Fravor was vectored by the USS Princeton to investigate an anomalous contact that had been tracked by ship radar for over two weeks. What he observed became the most widely cited pilot UAP report in modern history.

## The 2004 Encounter

Fravor and his weapons system officer, along with a second F/A-18F crew, were sent to investigate. At the reported location they observed a white, Tic-Tac shaped object approximately 40 feet long with no visible wings, propulsion, or exhaust. The object hovered over a disturbed patch of ocean and reacted to Fravor's intercept attempt by mirroring his maneuver before accelerating away at speeds inconsistent with any known propulsion system. The encounter was also recorded on the [[2004-nimitz-tic-tac]] FLIR footage.

Three additional aviators corroborated the sighting, making this a multi-witness instrumented event.

## Cultural Impact

Fravor's 2019 appearance on Joe Rogan's podcast introduced his account to a broad civilian audience. [[marik-von-rennenkampff]] cites this interview as the event that pulled him out of skepticism and into serious UAP research — specifically noting Fravor's profile ("skipper of a storied squadron") and the presence of three other corroborating witnesses. As Merrick put it: "This guy's the real deal."

## 2023 Congressional Testimony

Fravor testified alongside [[ryan-graves]] and [[david-grusch]] at the 2023 House Oversight Subcommittee UAP hearing. Graves describes meeting Fravor for the first time there; both Fravor and Grusch pushed Graves to the front of the lineup. Their joint appearance made it the most-watched congressional UAP hearing in history. Fravor's role was to anchor the testimony with firsthand military witness credibility that is difficult to impugn.

Graves also relayed in [[6WC4o2yY9Ws]] a detail from Fravor's account: the Tic-Tac appeared to detect Fravor, reacting when he attempted to intercept it — a reactive behavior not observed by Graves in the 2014–2015 East Coast incidents, suggesting either different craft types or different behavioral modes.

## "Conscious Craft" Description (dzTZbSNsKV8)

[[garry-nolan]] relays Fravor's description of the Nimitz Tic-Tac in [[dzTZbSNsKV8]]:

> *"Looking at the UFO and it's almost as if the thing sees that he's looking at it and is conscious and almost breathing. They're all going against the wind. The wind's 120 knots west. But if there's a thing, it's rotating."*

This is a second-hand quote (Nolan relaying Fravor) but consistent with the reactive behavior Fravor described in direct testimony. The description adds detail not typically emphasized: the craft rotating against a 120-knot wind while appearing to sense being observed. Nolan uses this description as evidence that some UAP may be biological rather than mechanical.
