# John Peterson

John Peterson is the founder of the [[arlington-institute]], a Washington DC–area think tank operating at the intersection of national security policy and unconventional futures research. He appeared on "many many short lists for DoD Secretary of Defense position for years," per [[richard-dolan]] — establishing him as a genuine insider figure, not a fringe researcher.

## The Extraterrestrial Simulation (c. 1998)

Around 1998, Peterson convened a small gathering at the Arlington Institute specifically to simulate and explore "what it would be like in a world where extraterrestrials are openly acknowledged as living among us." That a figure of his DoD proximity organized such a simulation suggests either genuine private concern about UAP reality or awareness of classified programs that made the scenario worth rehearsing.

[[richard-dolan]] cites this event as one of the signals that serious insiders were privately treating the extraterrestrial hypothesis as operationally plausible long before public acknowledgment.

## Connection to Catherine Austin Fitts

The Arlington Institute is connected to [[catherine-austin-fitts]]'s work on unaccounted Pentagon funds and the breakaway civilization thesis. Peterson and Fitts represent adjacent insiders who arrived at similar concerns from different angles (defense policy vs. financial forensics). See [[breakaway-civilization]].
