# Craig Lindsay

RAF Press Officer stationed in Scotland in 1990 at the time of the [[1990-calvine-photograph]] incident. Lindsay is the key witness who preserved physical evidence of the Calvine case — keeping one print of the photograph for approximately 34 years against the MOD's efforts to suppress all documentation.

## Background

As an RAF Press Officer, Lindsay's role put him in contact with the local newspaper that had received the photograph from the two witnesses. The MOD immediately took over the investigation when they learned of the photograph, collecting all prints from the newspaper and from the witnesses directly.

## The Surviving Print

Lindsay retained one print after the MOD takeover — whether this was through deliberate preservation or oversight is not entirely clear. He kept this print undisclosed for approximately 34 years, until researcher [[david-clarke-ufologist]] found his name in a declassified but redacted MOD document and located him.

## On-Camera Interview

[[james-fox]] traveled to England (meeting with David Clarke), then to Scotland, specifically to film Lindsay's first on-camera interview. Lindsay had never spoken publicly about the Calvine case before. The interview is included in Fox's documentary *The Program* (released December 23, 2024).

Lindsay described the object as taking off "straight vertical, fast, faster, faster" until it was gone — consistent with the instant vertical acceleration described in many high-credibility UAP cases.
