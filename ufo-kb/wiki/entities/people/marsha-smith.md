# Marsha Smith

Science policy official who served in the Carter administration and at the Congressional Research Service. Cited in UAP disclosure accounts in the context of President Jimmy Carter's UAP briefing history.

## Carter UAP Connection

Jimmy Carter famously reported his own 1969 UAP sighting and campaigned on a promise to declassify UAP files. After his election, Carter reportedly requested UAP intelligence briefings. Multiple accounts — including from [[danny-sheehan]] — claim that Carter was denied full access to classified UAP material by CIA Director George H.W. Bush ([[george-hw-bush]]), who reportedly told the President he did not have a "need to know."

Marsha Smith is cited in some accounts as the administration official tasked with compiling Carter's UAP briefing request and as a conduit between the White House and intelligence agencies on this matter. The specifics of her role in this episode are not fully documented in primary sources accessible to this KB.

## Congressional Research Service Work

Smith worked at the [[congressional-research-service]] on science and space policy issues, producing reports on satellite programs and related topics. This institutional base gave her access to classified space program information that intersects with UAP tracking concerns.
