# Derrel Sims

UFO researcher and hypnotherapist; described as "the original guy on implants" by [[steve-coburn]]. Sims had been collecting accounts from people claiming alien-implanted foreign objects prior to [[roger-leir]]'s involvement. He co-founded **A&S Research** with Leir as a vehicle for their joint investigation.

## Role in the Leir Origin Story

Sims met Leir at a **UFO Congress** event. He attempted to persuade Leir to take the implant phenomenon seriously. Leir's initial response: dismissal ("you're full of it"). A mutual friend of Leir's later convinced him to reconsider. Leir then approached Sims with an offer: bring patients to Leir's office, Leir would X-ray them and surgically remove any objects found, and they would determine what the objects actually were. This arrangement produced Leir's first removals and the beginning of a ~20-year research program.

The Sims–Leir partnership did not persist indefinitely — [[roger-leir]] later operated more independently, though he remained associated with A&S Research in various capacities.
