# Tim Burchett

Republican congressman from Tennessee's 2nd district; one of the most outspoken congressional advocates for UAP transparency. Member of the House Oversight Committee; participated in the 2023 congressional UAP hearing.

## Five Ocean Emergence Zones

[[joe-rogan]] cites Burchett as having stated that the US government has identified **five (or six) deep-ocean areas** where UAP/USO emerge on a regular basis:

> "Tim Burchett recently said that there's — they've identified five areas in the oceans of the world where these things are coming out of on a regular basis. We have a higher propensity of sightings around these five or six, I believe, deep-water areas."

This is significant because it implies:
1. The government has tracked USO patterns sufficiently to identify repeating emergence points
2. The emergence zones suggest underwater installations, bases, or dimensional/portal phenomena
3. The regularity of emergence implies ongoing, non-random activity

See [[uso-ocean-portals]] for the broader concept.

## Congressional Role

Burchett has been particularly aggressive on UAP transparency, publicly pressing military and intelligence officials during hearings and co-sponsoring legislation. He has publicly stated his belief that the US government has crash-retrieved vehicles.
