# Tim Burchett

Republican congressman from Tennessee's 2nd district; one of the most outspoken congressional advocates for UAP transparency. Member of the House Oversight Committee; participated in the 2023 congressional UAP hearing. Background: 16 years in Tennessee state legislature, 8 years as Knox County mayor, ~8 years in Congress. Childhood UFO believer from East Tennessee; got congressional inside information ~5–6 years ago, during Trump's first term, after a TMZ interview brought UAP witnesses to his attention.

## Five Ocean Emergence Zones

[[joe-rogan]] cites Burchett as having stated that the US government has identified **five (or six) deep-ocean areas** where UAP/USO emerge on a regular basis:

> "Tim Burchett recently said that there's — they've identified five areas in the oceans of the world where these things are coming out of on a regular basis. We have a higher propensity of sightings around these five or six, I believe, deep-water areas."

An unnamed admiral separately confirmed to Burchett that USO sightings concentrate in approximately four of the five deep-water zones — but the Navy "doesn't have capabilities to get down that deep" to verify physical bases. See [[uso-ocean-portals]] for the broader concept.

## "The President Is on a Need-to-Know Basis"

In one congressional briefing on UAP, Burchett asked a federal employee: "What about the president on this?" The employee replied: **"The president's on a need-to-know basis."**

Burchett: "Now that's a federal employee saying that he or she has information that the president is not allowed to see. I just was a gasp, man."

He does not believe Trump has full UAP access: "I don't think Trump knows. These people have kept this stuff secret."

## Florida Pilot Briefing Confrontation

Burchett, [[anna-paulina-luna]], and [[matt-gaetz]] flew to Florida after Gaetz was told that pilots had UAP photographs. Military substituted a Chinese-infiltration briefing. Gaetz confronted them; Burchett threatened subpoenas. Burchett spotted CIA/military intelligence "suits" getting nervous in the corner. Within an hour, the military reversed: "You're going to get your briefing. They're bringing the pilots in." Pilots arrived in flight gear, having just come from their planes. They described craft hovering for extended periods and shooting straight up "at incredible speeds that we don't have capabilities of doing." Burchett: "These were top military people."

## Navy 1950s Sighting — UAP-Nuclear Connection

A Navy veteran relayed to Burchett a 1950s sighting: aboard a ship, while arming a submarine with nuclear weapons, everyone on deck watched a "humongous saucer" — estimated at two blocks long — hover silently over them. Classic saucer shape with lights from portals around its circumference. No noise. It just sat there and hovered. This account corroborates the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] pattern.

## Congressional Role and Bipartisan Core

Burchett's consistent legislative partners: himself, [[anna-paulina-luna]], [[matt-gaetz]], and [[jared-moskowitz]] (Democrat). Others "come in and say they want to be read in" but lack sustained engagement. He told UAP hearing witnesses: "I just want y'all to know that people do believe you. This is not some crazy far-out thing."

Burchett's disclosure bills: 2-page bill didn't advance; [[chuck-schumer]]'s 60-page bill (modeled on JFK records act) also stalled. He supports [[eric-burlison]]'s whistleblower protection bill, which keeps being killed in the Senate.
