# Flying Lotus

Steven Ellison, known professionally as Flying Lotus, is a Los Angeles-based hip-hop producer, composer, and filmmaker. He is the great-nephew of jazz and spiritual music icon [[alice-coltrane]], a lineage he credits as opening him to the possibility that human consciousness connects to something cosmic — "closer to God, closer to the source, closer to the stars." He is one of the most prominent artistic figures in contemporary hip-hop production (credits include projects for Kendrick Lamar, Erykah Badu, and his own avant-garde releases) and has also directed the horror film *Kuso* (2017).

## Background

Ellison connects his musical and spiritual sensibility to his family heritage. He describes Alice Coltrane as a "Yoda type person" — profoundly tapped into a universal source. Listening to her music evokes in him a visceral sense of ancient Egypt, as if recalling a past-life memory. This spiritual receptivity extends to his openness toward UAP phenomena.

His interest in UFOs predates his American Alchemy appearance; he discovered [[jesse-michaels]]'s work through social media and the two connected over shared UAP interest. Jesse describes Flying Lotus as one of his "production heroes" who has been engaged with the UAP material Jesse publishes for over 15 years.

## Firsthand Sighting Claim

In [[JE1oM89dpBc]], Flying Lotus discloses (hesitantly) that he may have seen a non-human entity:

> "I might have. I might have. I saw a thing with three fingers and three toes."

He implies this may have been a preserved or "frozen" specimen rather than a live encounter. No further detail is given — location, context, and circumstances are not disclosed. The three-finger/three-toe morphology is notable for its match with the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] (M-type hominid bodies, ~4–5 ft, tridactyl anatomy) and the broader [[tridactyl-global-pattern]] being documented across independent sources. Whether this is coincidence, cultural cross-contamination, or genuine independent observation is unresolved.

## UAP Perspective

Flying Lotus's approach to UAP is experiential and intuitive rather than evidence-driven. He treats [[bob-lazar]]'s Area 51 claims as foundational ("gateway drug"). He engages the [[extradimensional-beings]] thesis enthusiastically — the idea that NHI are not from space but from another dimension accessed by an earlier precursor of the space program. He is skeptical of the [[skinny-bob]] video (host dismisses it; he agrees).

He expresses frustration with the UFO community's internal dynamics: echo chambers, hostility toward skeptics, and reluctance to allow debaters like [[lue-elizondo]] and critics to engage publicly. He values [[mick-west]] as a good-faith skeptic rather than a bad actor.
