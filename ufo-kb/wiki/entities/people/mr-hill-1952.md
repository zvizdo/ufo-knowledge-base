# Mr. Hill (1952 DC Witness)

A witness identified only as "Mr. Hill" who, as a child, was sitting on a porch with his father during the mass UFO sightings over Washington D.C. in July 1952. At the time of filming of [[1f16VvXaSSE]], he was one of the last known living eyewitnesses to this event and was interviewed directly in the documentary.

His account is significant as a rare first-person, living-witness account of what is arguably the most politically significant UAP incident in US history — a mass sighting over the nation's capital that triggered presidential attention (Truman reportedly called the Air Force) and multiple newspaper front pages.
