# Beatrice Varel

> ✅ **Identity confirmed**: "Beatrice Varel" / "Beatatrice Vioale" / "Beatrice Voriel" are all phonetic transcription artifacts for **[[beatriz-villarroel]]**. Confirmed via [[lbGE3EC6StE]] — a direct long-form interview where Jesse Michaels explicitly interviews her as "Dr. Vioale," and the content (VASCO, Stockholm University, Palomar Observatory, Baltic Sea Anomaly) matches perfectly. This page should be treated as an alias stub only; all content belongs on [[beatriz-villarroel]].

Swedish scientist and UAP/UAP-adjacent researcher. [[dennis-asberg]] describes meeting her approximately two years before the interview (c. 2023). She became his key institutional ally for the [[baltic-sea-anomaly]] investigation — the person who "had the keys" and opened academic and scientific doors that 15 years of Asberg's solo effort had failed to unlock.

Varel shares Asberg's interest in understanding anomalous phenomena and brought credentialed scientists — including Stockholm University researchers — into the BSA investigation. Those scientists have now personally visited the site and confirmed they have "never seen anything like it." Their interest has sustained continued expeditions.

During the 2025 expedition, Jesse Michaels also spoke with Varel separately (she is mentioned as having done her own amazing things independently). She emphasized the importance of having a working hypothesis before investigating — rather than just cataloguing observations — as essential for productive scientific testing.

## Palomar Observatory Geocynchronous Objects (iPxbILOMQ08)

[[jesse-michaels]] (the host) describes her as "a friend of mine" and a PhD astronomer at Stockholm University who observed **light-reflecting mirror-like objects in orbit** from the **Palomar Observatory**. These geocynchronous objects match the broader pattern that [[clyde-tombaugh]] was recruited to investigate in the mid-20th century. In [[iPxbILOMQ08]], this detail is raised in the context of [[steve-coburn]]'s claim that NHI mother ships orbit at ~500 miles altitude and launch smaller craft for abductions.
