# Kip Thorne

Kip Thorne (born 1940) is an American theoretical physicist at Caltech and co-recipient of the 2017 Nobel Prize in Physics for his contribution to the LIGO detection of gravitational waves. His research focuses on general relativity, gravitational waves, black holes, wormholes, and time travel — making him one of the world's foremost experts on the physics of spacetime manipulation.

Per [[51N8OxqZIWY]], Thorne visited [[epstein-island]] (Little St. James). [[jay-anderson]] presents this as significant given that Epstein's primary scientific obsession was gravity and propulsion — precisely Thorne's domain. The visit is stated as publicly documented ("You can Google it").
