# Paul Helliwell

Paul Lionel Edward Helliwell (1915–1976). US Army colonel (OSS) during WWII, serving under William Donovan in Southeast Asia and China. Postwar: Miami attorney and CIA officer under legal cover; key architect of the CIA's covert financial infrastructure.

**Sea Supply Corporation / Golden Triangle**: Helliwell established Sea Supply Corporation, a CIA front that channeled heroin profits from Kuomintang (KMT) operations in the Golden Triangle to fund anti-Castro paramilitary activities in Florida. This is the documented precursor to the BCCI and Air America drug-funding patterns. The KMT units, nominally fighting communist China from Burma/Thailand, financed themselves through opium cultivation; Helliwell's Sea Supply provided the conduit from that revenue into CIA covert operations budgets.

**Castle Bank & Trust**: Helliwell established Castle Bank & Trust in the Bahamas — simultaneously a CIA funding conduit for off-books operations and a money-laundering vehicle for organized crime principals including [[santos-trafficante]] and Meyer Lansky. The IRS investigated the bank in the 1970s and was making significant progress when CIA intervention shut the investigation down. This is one of the clearest documented cases of the CIA using legal authority to protect its own covert financial infrastructure from US law enforcement.

**Cuba / Trafficante connection**: Helliwell's CIA financial network intersected directly with [[santos-trafficante]]'s Cuba casino operations under Batista. When Castro ended the Cuba gambling revenue, both Helliwell's infrastructure and Trafficante's organization pivoted to the anti-Castro operations that included the assassination-attempt pipelines. [[theodore-shackley]] operated in the same network during the Cuba and Southeast Asia periods.

**UAP-relevant structural argument**: Helliwell's covert finance architecture is cited in UAP-adjacent research as proof of concept: the CIA possessed the tools to fund large, long-running programs entirely outside congressional appropriations, using organized crime financial networks, shell companies, and foreign banking systems. A comparable structure could sustain a UAP reverse-engineering program for decades without appearing in any budget Congress would ever see.
