# Martin Alcron

Vice president of the [[university-of-ika]] in Peru and a central institutional figure in the defense of the Nazca mummy research. Alcron has documented at least seven separate attempts by Peruvian government authorities to seize the mummy specimens, framing these as a systematic suppression campaign against a legitimate scientific investigation.

## Background

In his institutional role at the University of Ika, Alcron has been positioned at the intersection of academic research and political pressure. The university has served as one of the few institutional anchors willing to publicly support and house the Nazca mummy investigation.

## Notable Claims / Role

Alcron's documentation of repeated government seizure attempts is significant because it transforms the mummy controversy from a question of scientific authenticity alone into one of institutional conflict and potential suppression. His count of seven attempts provides a concrete record of the political dimensions surrounding the research.
