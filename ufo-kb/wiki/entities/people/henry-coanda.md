# Henry Coanda

Romanian engineer and aviation pioneer (best known for the Coandă effect in fluid dynamics). In 1936, Coanda filed a patent for a lenticular aerodyne — a saucer-shaped aircraft design. This patent predates the modern UFO era by over a decade, establishing him as an early designer of disc-shaped craft.

Following World War II, Coanda came to the United States through [[operation-paperclip]] and consulted on the [[avrocar-project]] at Wright Air Development Center (see [[wright-patterson]]). The Avrocar — a saucer-shaped experimental craft using a radial gas turbine — is the known, declassified saucer program; Coanda's involvement links back to his pre-war lenticular aerodyne design work.
