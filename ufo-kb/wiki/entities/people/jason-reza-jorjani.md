# Jason Reza Jorjani

Iranian-American philosopher and author. Holds a doctorate in philosophy (SUNY Stony Brook). Writes on the intersection of continental philosophy, consciousness research, Indo-European traditions, UFO/UAP phenomena, and political theory. Among the few thinkers in the UFO discourse attempting a systematic philosophical framework that connects disparate threads into a coherent worldview — a comparison the host Jesse Michaels draws to Jacques Vallée.

## Background

Jorjani has published several books including *Closer Encounters* (on the close encounter phenomenon) and *From Atheist to Atlantis* (a philosophical-biographical synthesis). He has been associated with Iranian studies, contributing to scholarship on Iranian intellectual history and philosophy. He describes his philosophical method as requiring synthesis across all domains — ontology, epistemology, ethics, politics, aesthetics — without unquestioned presuppositions in any area.

His philosophical touchstones include Nietzsche (aesthetics as ethics), Kant (aesthetic third critique connecting the occult to universal ethics), Hegel (aesthetic styles as evolution of consciousness), and the phenomenological tradition. He is explicitly critical of religious belief as incompatible with genuine philosophy — he argues this disqualifies St. Thomas Aquinas and the entire medieval European philosophical tradition.

## Core Philosophical Framework

Jorjani argues that the decline of philosophy into academic scholasticism has prevented serious engagement with UFO and paranormal phenomena. A true philosopher must develop concepts cutting across all major domains without unquestioned presuppositions — a stance he traces to Socrates. This framework is a prerequisite for understanding:

1. The phenomenological data of close encounters
2. Parapsychological data on telepathy, precognition, and clairvoyance
3. The political and institutional suppression of both

## The Spectral Revolution

Jorjani builds on [[william-james]]'s and [[henry-bergson]]'s founding of psychical research to argue that James predicted parasychology would be "the next great scientific revolution." This [[spectral-revolution]] never occurred due to deliberate institutional suppression — the "extremely serious psychological and sociological implications" of mainstream acceptance of telepathy and precognition were too threatening to existing power structures.

## Temporal Causality Theory

For certain UFO phenomena — particularly those involving apparent foreknowledge and civilizational continuity — Jorjani proposes a temporal causality model: "A future state of a certain culture is contacting itself in the past, trying to pull the timeline towards itself." He connects this to hypothesized "Atlantean surviving remnant groups" attempting trans-temporal actualization. This directly extends the thesis in [[time-travel-uap]].

## Theater of Cruelty Framework

Jorjani developed a concept he calls the "magic theater of cruelty" — hybridizing Antonin Artaud's *Theater of Cruelty* — as a framework for making sense of the absurd and terrifying elements of close encounter phenomena. The framework emphasizes the theatrical, purposefully disorienting character of many encounters as a form of intentional communication from NHI.
