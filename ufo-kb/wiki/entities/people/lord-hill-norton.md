# Lord Hill-Norton

Admiral of the Fleet Peter Hill-Norton, Baron Hill-Norton. Served as **Chief of the Defence Staff** (the UK's highest military position, equivalent to the US Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff) and as NATO's Military Committee Chairman. After retirement, he became one of the most senior establishment voices calling for official transparency on the UFO/UAP phenomenon.

## UFO Advocacy

Hill-Norton used his parliamentary position (House of Lords) to raise questions about government UFO policy and push for disclosure — a remarkable posture given his former rank. He was a credible, high-status figure whose questions could not be easily dismissed.

## Influence of Paul Inglesby

[[nick-pope]] notes a surprising influence: **[[paul-inglesby]]**, a "maverick priest," exerted strong influence on Hill-Norton's approach to the UFO field. Inglesby held that UAP are demonic — not extraterrestrial or technological. This theological framework shaped how Hill-Norton understood and engaged with the phenomenon, even as his public arguments were framed in security and defense terms.

This is a significant and under-documented thread: the UK's most senior retired military official engaging with UAP was partly motivated by a religious/demonic interpretive framework, mediated through a priest's influence.
