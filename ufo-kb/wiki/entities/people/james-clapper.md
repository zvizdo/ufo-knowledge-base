# James Clapper

Director of National Intelligence (DNI) from 2010 to 2017. Previously served as Director of the Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) and Director of the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA). One of the most senior intelligence officials of the Obama era; cited repeatedly in UAP disclosure discussions as a figure who would have had access to or been briefed on classified UAP programs.

Clapper is mentioned in the context of the [[age-of-disclosure]] documentary and related disclosure discussions alongside former CIA Directors [[james-woolsey]] and [[john-brennan]] — all three are cited as high-level endorsers or proximate figures to the official acknowledgment of UAP reality.

## Firsthand Interactions (per Eric Haseltine)

[[eric-haseltine]] (NSA Director of Research, later ODNI CTO) provides two firsthand data points on Clapper and UAP:

1. **Intelligence Distinguished Service Medal**: Clapper presented Haseltine with his medal when Haseltine left his ODNI role. Haseltine's description of the relationship implies direct access and mutual respect.

2. **NSA UFO File Request**: In a public interview (referenced in [[jMCavr3dVP0]]), Clapper described calling NSA Director [[mike-rogers-nsa]] and saying: *"Mike, I need you to go through all of it. I need a team to go through all of NSA's holdings…"* — a direct request for all NSA UFO data. This implies Clapper took UAP seriously enough to commission a classified review from the top of the NSA.

3. **Area 51 Sensor Program**: Per the documentary [[age-of-disclosure]], Clapper publicly referenced a sensor program at Area 51 that was specifically looking for UFOs.

These data points establish Clapper as not merely a bystander in UAP discourse but an active requester of UAP intelligence at the highest level.
