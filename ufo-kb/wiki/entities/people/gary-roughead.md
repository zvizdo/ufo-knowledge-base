# Gary Roughead

US Navy four-star Admiral; former Chief of Naval Operations (CNO, 2007–2011). Was [[tim-gallaudet]]'s boss's boss when Gallaudet was a captain in the Pentagon. Later chaired the Naval Studies Board — the National Academies of Sciences body that provides scientific studies to the Navy.

Gallaudet briefed Roughead about his UAP underwater research and proposed that the Naval Studies Board commission a study on underwater UAP anomalies. Roughead was supportive, citing two reasons: (1) UAP operating in US waterspace represent a genuine national security concern, and (2) strategic competition with China in the ocean domain makes comprehensive ocean knowledge critical. This is notable as one of the most senior US military leaders to have received a formal UAP briefing from a peer and responded positively.
