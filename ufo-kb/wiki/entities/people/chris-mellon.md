# Chris Mellon

Former Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence, serving under both Bill Clinton and George W. Bush. Co-architect, with [[lue-elizondo]], of the **December 17, 2017 New York Times story** that publicly revealed the existence of AATIP and released three UAP videos. He is a central figure in what [[danny-sheehan]] characterizes as the **authorized, managed disclosure pipeline**.

## Background

Comes from the prominent Mellon banking family. Career in defense intelligence policy. After government service, became involved with [[tom-delonge]]'s To The Stars Academy alongside Elizondo.

## The 2017 NYT Story

Sheehan's characterization: the three UAP videos released with the NYT story were government pre-cleared — they were deliberately chosen as the lowest-quality, least revealing material available. The story itself, while publicly appearing as a journalistic revelation, was in Sheehan's view an **authorized release** — a controlled step in a managed disclosure process.

## Schumer Amendment Role

Mellon was involved in supporting the legislative push that produced the [[schumer-uap-amendment]] in the 2024 NDAA — though the key eminent-domain and independent review panel provisions were ultimately stripped by [[mike-turner]] under Lockheed lobbying.

## Near-Miss Source: Aerospace Program Insider (fyX8V1XXmQM)

In [[fyX8V1XXmQM]], Mellon is quoted as saying he was **two weeks away from meeting a source** who was directly involved in a crash retrieval / reverse-engineering program housed inside a commercial aerospace company — a program with its own separate clearance track. The source **died of a heart attack** before the meeting could occur.

This is significant for two reasons: (1) it corroborates Vallée's thesis that UAP programs reside in private aerospace under a DOE/AEC-lineage clearance rather than standard Pentagon SAPs; (2) it represents Mellon's closest firsthand contact with a claimed insider, and the contact was severed before any information transfer.

> ⚠ The source's identity is not disclosed. The account is Mellon's firsthand testimony about an intended meeting.
