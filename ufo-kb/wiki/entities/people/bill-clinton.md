# Bill Clinton

42nd President of the United States (1993–2001). One of the few sitting/former U.S. presidents to publicly express personal curiosity about UFO/UAP holdings — most famously in the 2014 Jimmy Kimmel interview ("If we were visited some day, I wouldn't be surprised") and earlier remarks tasking [[webster-hubbell]] to look into UFOs and JFK assassination records.

**Webster Hubbell tasking**: Clinton personally directed [[webster-hubbell]], his Associate Attorney General, to find out what the government knew about two things: UFOs and who killed JFK. Hubbell reported finding nothing — stating he was unable to get answers through official channels. This is a significant data point: the third-ranking official at the DOJ, acting on explicit presidential instruction, could not access UAP files. If access existed at this level, it would have been found.

**Rockefeller Initiative**: The Clinton White House is the primary executive-branch target of the [[1995-rockefeller-ufo-initiative]]. Laurance Rockefeller privately lobbied both the administration and [[hillary-clinton]] specifically, providing her with Paul Davies's book *Are We Alone* and engaging administration science advisors. The JY Ranch meetings in Wyoming produced a classified briefing document that Rockefeller hoped would prompt executive action. No disclosure resulted, but the initiative demonstrates that a sitting president's administration was being actively lobbied for UAP disclosure from a credible private actor.

**Executive branch access limit**: The Hubbell negative result combined with the Rockefeller-initiative outcome is one of the cleaner proofs in the KB that UAP secrecy operates above the normal presidential authority level — not because presidents are uninformed, but because the programs are structured to be inaccessible even to a president who is looking.
