# Peter Thiel

## Background

Co-founder of PayPal, first outside investor in Facebook, founder of Palantir Technologies. Known for heterodox views on technology, progress, and civilization. Has invested in defense, biotech, and companies aligned with what he calls "definite optimism." Has spoken at length about the stagnation of technological progress and the risks of social conformity in innovation.

## Binary NHI Forcing-Function Argument

In an interview referenced in [[8TYMQOUDQBo]], Thiel argued that **faster-than-light or warp-drive technology acts as a civilizational forcing function**:

- If a civilization possesses FTL capability, attacks become essentially undefendable against — you cannot prepare for what you cannot detect approaching
- Therefore, any civilization that develops FTL *and* survives must have resolved the question of large-scale violence either by:
  1. **Totalitarian control** — enforcing absolute compliance to prevent misuse, or
  2. **Complete altruism** — eliminating the motivation for weaponization entirely

If there are selective pressures favoring one or the other at civilizational scale, the result may be a **binary NHI landscape**: one class of NHI emerged through altruistic evolution and poses no threat; another class emerged through totalitarian control and is potentially adversarial.

[[salvatore-pais]] finds this argument "very compelling" though "controversial." [[jesse-michaels]] notes it maps onto biblical "children of light / children of darkness" framing and describes the current moment as "feeling apocalyptic."

The argument provides a potential physical/game-theoretic basis for [[multiple-alien-groups]] — the NHI factions hypothesis — that goes beyond simple moral categorization.

## Hereticon: Antichrist Talk and Pasulka Citation (aa9Xx5wI8Rw)

At Hereticon (the same conference where [[diana-pasulka]] and [[carl-nell]] gave their panel), Thiel gave a talk on the **Antichrist** — framing it as the most effective concealment of evil:

> The best way to conceal being the Antichrist is to talk about it a lot and push the idea that there is an apocalypse and we need to save ourselves. Everyone agrees with that. That's a great veneer.

He cites **Thessalonians**: "**peace and safety**" is the Antichrist's slogan. His argument: much of today's evil — "on both sides" of the political aisle — is couched in utilitarian effective-altruism language ("I'm rearranging the world for good on a global scale"). This outward-looking world-saving posture never turns inward ("am I harming people?") — which is precisely the Antichrist structure.

Thiel also **cited Pasulka's work on Joe Rogan** (approximately one month before the Hereticon panel), endorsing her thesis and extending it: aliens are not only probably angels and demons, "**they must be** angels and demons" — because of the dual-use forcing function of advanced technology (warp drive as weapons delivery = undefendable). He finds the St. Francis seraphim case significant and notes the Greek letter Tau (τ) — meaning "conversion" — was the last thing Francis wrote in his diary.

Pasulka notes that both she and Thiel studied under [[rene-girard]] (she attended his Berkeley/Stanford colloquiums; he was his student at Stanford), creating a shared intellectual framework for the Antichrist-as-scapegoat analysis.
