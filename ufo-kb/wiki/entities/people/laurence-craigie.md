# Laurence Craigie

General, USAF. On 30 December 1947, Craigie signed the founding directive establishing [[project-sign]] — the first official US Air Force program to collect, collate, and evaluate atmospheric phenomena reports. The directive:

- Designated AMC Wright-Patterson as the responsible agency
- Assigned priority 2A
- Applied security classification: Restricted
- Assigned code name: SIGN
- Directed AMC to collect, collate, evaluate, and distribute information "concerning sightings and phenomena in the atmosphere which can be construed to be of concern to the national security"

The Craigie letter is the original founding document for SIGN, which preceded [[project-grudge]] and [[project-blue-book]] as the first in the series of formal USAF UFO investigation programs.
