# Remy Baca

One of two primary witnesses to the [[1945-san-antonio-trinity-crash]]. Son of a local New Mexico cattle rancher. At the time of the incident (approximately August 1945, ~20 days after the [[1945-trinity-test]]) he was approximately seven years old.

## Account

Remy and his companion [[jose-padilla]] (age ~9) observed the crashed avocado-shaped metallic object in the distance. Through the gap created by a missing panel, they saw three humanoid creatures approximately three feet tall — two eyes, two arms, three fingers per hand, two legs — moving by sliding rather than conventional walking. Remy was frightened and wept; he refused to approach the craft. He told Padilla: "You go there if you want to — no way am I going into that thing."

His testimony was recorded on audio tape by [[paola-harris]] in her investigative work preceding the co-authored book *[[trinity-best-kept-secret]]* with [[jacques-vallee]]. By the time of the interviews, both Baca and Padilla were elderly men.
