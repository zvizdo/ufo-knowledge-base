# Mike McConnell

Admiral J. Michael McConnell served as Director of the NSA. In the UAP discourse he appears in two related but distinct claim clusters:

## The Dan Burish Connection

Researcher Brian Jackson (Night Shift channel) argues that McConnell was effectively [[dan-burish]]'s **godfather**, placing him on special access programs at [[s4]]. Jackson's framing: McConnell's son died in a "freak accident," and McConnell subsequently treated Burish as a quasi-son and granted him extraordinary access — including the J-Rod interface program. This remains an unverified claim.

## The "Mike Mlelen" Question

[[john-lear]] stated in an interview that a **Majestic 12 member named Mike Mlelen** instructed him to help bring [[bob-lazar]] to [[s4]], as part of a limited-disclosure plan. [[uap-gerb]] speculates that "Mlelen" may be a phonetic approximation of **McConnell** — which would link the same senior intelligence official to both Burish's S4 access and Lazar's S4 access. This is speculative but would make McConnell a significant node in the S4 human access architecture.
