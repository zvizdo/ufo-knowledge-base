# Michael S. Rogers

US Navy admiral (b. 1959); **17th Director of the National Security Agency (NSA)** and Commander of US Cyber Command, 2014–2018. One of the most powerful IC figures of the Obama/early-Trump era, with access to the full range of NSA signals intelligence collection.

**UAP holdings request**: In the KB's most specific Rogers-related claim, [[james-clapper]] (Director of National Intelligence 2010–2017) describes calling Rogers and telling him: *"Mike, I need you to go through all of it. I need a team to go through all of NSA's holdings…"* — a direct request for a comprehensive review of all NSA data related to UAP. This exchange, referenced in [[jMCavr3dVP0]], implies that Clapper treated UAP seriously enough at the DNI level to commission a classified review from the head of the most capable SIGINT agency in the world. Whether Rogers's team found anything, and what they reported back to Clapper, is not in the public record.

**Significance**: Rogers was DIRNSA during the same period that [[aatip]] was active under Lue Elizondo at the Pentagon. If NSA had relevant UAP signals intelligence holdings, Rogers would have been the person tasked to assess them at Clapper's request. His subsequent silence on UAP (he has not spoken publicly on the topic) is consistent with either finding nothing or finding something that remains classified.

**Post-government**: After retiring in 2018, Rogers joined the private sector including cybersecurity advisory roles. He has not disclosed UAP-relevant findings.
