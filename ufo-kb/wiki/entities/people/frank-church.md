# Frank Church

Democratic U.S. Senator from Idaho (1957–1981). Known primarily for chairing the **Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities** (1975–1976) — universally known as the **Church Committee**.

## Church Committee

Convened in the wake of Watergate and investigative journalism exposing CIA/NSA/FBI abuses. The Church Committee's most significant revelations include:

- **MK-Ultra** — CIA's mind-control research program; documented LSD experiments on unwitting civilians
- **COINTELPRO** — FBI's domestic surveillance and sabotage of political movements
- **Assassination plots** — CIA operations against foreign leaders (Castro, Lumumba, Allende)
- **NSA warrantless surveillance** — mass collection without legal authority
- **Warren Commission skepticism** — Church Committee surfaced serious doubts about the Warren Commission's completeness

Church was famous for warning that the CIA's surveillance capability could be turned on Americans: *"The United States must not adopt the tactics of the enemy... We must not become what we most oppose."*
