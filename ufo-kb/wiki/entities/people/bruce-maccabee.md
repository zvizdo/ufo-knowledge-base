# Bruce Maccabee

US Navy physicist (retired) who produced numerous analytical reports on UAP photographs and film. Maccabee is regarded within the research community as one of the more technically rigorous photographic analysts of historical UAP evidence.

## Assessment of ARV Photographs

[[eric-davis]] mentions Maccabee in [[LnAiNChnuEQ]] in the context of ARV photographs circulating in the UAP community:

> "I know Bruce mcabee had only said they definitely looked like hoaxes to him but I don't think he ever issued a report on them."

Maccabee's assessment — that the ARV photographs were hoaxes — aligns with Davis's overall position that no ARVs have been built. However, Davis notes Maccabee did not produce a formal analytical report on these specific images, unlike the multiple reports he issued on other UAP photographic evidence.
