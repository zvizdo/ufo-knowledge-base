# Cleve Baxter

CIA polygraph expert who pivoted to plant consciousness research in the 1960s. Known for a 1966 observation: a plant hooked to a polygraph responded with a stress signal when he merely *thought* about burning one of its leaves — before any action was taken. He subsequently documented what he called **primary perception** in plants: apparently real-time electrodermal responses to events happening to nearby organisms, and to human intentions even at distance.

His work is widely dismissed by mainstream biology. It is periodically cited in consciousness-extension research as evidence that information transmission occurs via a mechanism orthogonal to known sensory channels.
