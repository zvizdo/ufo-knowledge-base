# Boris Yeltsin

First President of the Russian Federation (1991–1999). Former Communist Party official who became the leader of Russia's democratic transition. His administration oversaw the chaotic privatizations of the 1990s. In UFO discourse he appears briefly but significantly as the first senior Russian official to be debriefed about the [[1997-ilyumzhinov-encounter]] and to respond with apparent prior knowledge.

## UAP Interaction

[[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]] was a governor-level official (president of Kalmykia) who attended regular State Council meetings chaired by Yeltsin. After one such meeting, Yeltsin asked Ilyumzhinov to stay behind. The two moved to a private area of the office with armchairs. Yeltsin said: **"It's been reported to me that you've been up there."** He chose his words carefully — "up there, on a spacecraft" — delivering it with a tone that was serious but also slightly joking.

Ilyumzhinov confirmed the encounter briefly. Yeltsin responded: **"Yes, I know. I heard about it. Is everything okay?"** — effectively asking whether Ilyumzhinov's mental state was intact. After Ilyumzhinov confirmed he was fine, Yeltsin stood up, gave him a pat, said "All right, then carry on with work," and the meeting ended.

**Analytical note:** Yeltsin's statement "I know, I heard about it" implies that either (a) the KGB or FSB had already reported the incident to him, or (b) Ilyumzhinov's public statements had already reached Yeltsin through normal intelligence channels. The brevity and normality of the response — no investigation, no psychiatric referral, just a pat and "carry on" — suggests either incuriosity or, more intriguingly, that it was not the first such report he had received.
