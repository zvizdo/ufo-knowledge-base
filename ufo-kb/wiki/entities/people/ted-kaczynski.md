# Ted Kaczynski

Theodore "Ted" Kaczynski entered Harvard at 16 and became a mathematics prodigy. Between 1959 and 1962, he participated in psychological experiments run by Harvard professor Henry A. Murray — later revealed to be CIA-funded MK-Ultra research. The experiments subjected participants to sustained psychological attacks, humiliation, and attempts to break down their sense of identity.

Kaczynski's biographers and researchers have argued that these experiments played a role in his alienation and eventual radicalization. He later became the "Unabomber," conducting a 17-year mail-bombing campaign against technologists and academics.

The Kaczynski case appears in [[mk-ultra]] discourse as a paradigm case of the program's long-term harm: not the acute trauma of LSD experiments, but the sustained psychological damage of identity-deconstruction research conducted on a brilliant teenager without genuine informed consent.
