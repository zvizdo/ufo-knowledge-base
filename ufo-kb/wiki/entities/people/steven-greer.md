# Steven Greer

Emergency physician who became one of the most prominent — and controversial — figures in UAP disclosure advocacy. Best known for founding CSETI and developing the CE-5 (Close Encounters of the Fifth Kind) protocol, which claims to enable human-initiated contact with non-human intelligences through group meditation and coherent consciousness practices.

## Background

Trained as an emergency physician. Founded CSETI (Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence) and later the Disclosure Project, which organized a major 2001 National Press Club event featuring ~20 former military/government witnesses. Has been [[danny-sheehan]]'s client.

## CE-5 Contact Protocol

Greer's central claim is that NHI can be summoned via coherent human consciousness — meditation, intentional focus, and bioluminescent signals. He claims numerous successful encounters. [[albert-stubblebine]] (ret. General, Army Intelligence) reportedly showed direct interest in Greer's contact abilities.

## Position Within Disclosure Community

Greer occupies a contested position:
- He is trusted by figures like Sheehan as having genuine contact experiences.
- He is deeply distrusted — reportedly hated — by [[lue-elizondo]], who represents the more establishment-adjacent disclosure faction.
- Sheehan calls the Elizondo-Greer antagonism "unfortunate" but offers no explanation of its root cause in this episode.
- [[jeremy-corbell]] and [[george-knapp]] are critical of Greer for accepting [[bob-lazar]]'s S4 claims uncritically (per Sheehan).

## Origin Story & Sightings

Greer grew up in Charlotte, North Carolina in poverty (no heat, no AC; washed dishes at Red Lobster to get through high school). He had a **childhood sighting around 1963** (age 8–9): a seamless disc appeared in broad daylight with neighborhood kids and his twin sister. His parents dismissed it.

At age 17, a serious illness produced a **near-death experience** that introduced him to the science of consciousness and the idea of an afterlife. He learned transcendental meditation in 1973.

In **October 1973**, at age 18, Greer climbed a mountain in the Blue Ridge Mountains near Boone, NC (above 5,000 ft elevation) to meditate at sunset. A seamless disc appeared, then vanished. He sat down to meditate; when he came out of it, four hours had passed (he thought 40 minutes). During that period, an NHI entity approximately 5 ft tall with "deer-like eyes" touched him on the shoulder. He describes co-creating the CE-5 contact protocols in a collaborative state with NHI he characterizes as "thousands of years in the future" — a consciousness-mediated download of the protocol framework. This experience is the origin point of CE-5.

## Control Structure (MAGIC / MJ-12)

Greer names the current control structure as **MAGIC** — Majority Joint Intelligence Committee — citing a 1990s NRO document as the last known name. He traces it directly to the OSS/WWII network of [[allen-dulles]] and [[paul-mellon]]. Built from fascist-sympathizer billionaires (Hitler supporters: Paul Mellon, Henry Ford, IBM's Watson, Prescott Bush; network: Union Bank, Brown Brothers Harriman, Chase Manhattan). It is transnational, supersedes national governments, has its own surveillance technologies operating beyond electromagnetic encryption.

Greer claims to have personally met members of this structure. General Stubblebine was one contact point: in May 1992, Stubblebine offered Greer **$2 billion** to join "his little cabal of goose-stepping fools." Greer refused. Stubblebine then went to Greer's wife separately to try to convince her. Greer says Stubblebine's career dealing with UFOs goes back to the 1960s.

## Disclosure Project History

The private code name for the disclosure effort in the 1990s was **Project Starlight**. It went public in 2001 with the National Press Club event, which reached ~800 million people. According to Greer, 9/11 occurred "a few months later as a direct consequence" — a manufactured diversion to displace the UFO disclosure momentum.

In December 1993, Greer briefed Clinton's CIA Director James Woolsey at CIA. He has dealt with the Clinton, Obama, and Trump administrations. He characterizes the current (Trump 2025) administration as "the most open since Bill Clinton tried to fix this problem in his first term." He has spoken with "very senior people relevant to this issue and national security" in Trump's circle.

He met David Grusch while Grusch was still an undercover operative; Senate Intelligence Committee officials authorized Grusch's investigation, and one of Greer's Senate Intel contacts set up the meeting.

## September 1993 Rockefeller Meeting

Greer visited [[jy-ranch]] (Lawrence Rockefeller's Teton ranch) in September 1993. At a late-night session on the deck, Rockefeller told Greer his brother [[david-rockefeller]] and nephew Senator [[jay-rockefeller]] were "jumping up and down on my nuts for even having this meeting with you." Early Rockefeller funding was later intercepted by CIA NOC operatives placed on Rockefeller's team.
