# David Spergel

Astrophysicist; former chair of the Department of Astrophysical Sciences at Princeton; president of the Simons Foundation (the philanthropy arm of [[jim-simons]]'s activities). Named as the main author and chair of the NASA Independent Study Team on UAP (the NASA UAP review panel, 2023).

## Background

Spergel is a mainstream academic astrophysicist, not previously associated with UAP research. His selection as main author of the NASA UAP review panel surprised observers in the UFO research community.

## Connection to UAP

[[jesse-michaels]] notes that [[eric-weinstein]] had previously (and speculatively) suggested that Jim Simons might be channeling wealth into UFO research — an idea Michaels had dismissed as conspiratorial. When the NASA UAP review panel was announced in 2022 and Spergel — Simons's top science advisor — was named its lead author, this created an unexpected apparent connection.

The NASA UAP review panel report was criticized for being brief (~10 pages) compared to France's [[cometa-report]] (100 pages) and for not meaningfully engaging the existing astronaut encounter record.

[[kevin-knuth]] does not confirm or deny the Simons-Spergel-UFO hypothesis.

## Wall Street Journal De Facto UFO Gatekeeper (h0hAit-KH9A)

WSJ opinion section managing editor [[lena-bell]] disclosed to [[jesse-michaels]] that Spergel is the WSJ's "de facto adviser on UFOs." His stated position — UFOs are "space trash, light reflections, and temperature inversions" — is functionally identical to [[donald-menzel]]'s 1950s debunking formula, which Michaels treats as evidence of a recurring institutional template rather than independent scientific judgment.

The Spergel-as-WSJ-gatekeeper detail has a nested irony: [[eric-weinstein]] speculated that [[jim-simons]] might be channeling wealth into exotic UAP physics research; Simons's own head of science (Spergel) was simultaneously appointed chair of the NASA UAP review panel and de facto UFO adviser to the newspaper of record — a configuration that could serve gatekeeping or enabling functions, and possibly both.

## Weinstein's "Very Interesting Overlap" (xnxasfyHtfo)

In [[xnxasfyHtfo]], [[eric-weinstein]] names the Spergel / [[jim-simons]] / NASA UAP panel configuration as "a very interesting overlap":
- Simons founded Renaissance Technologies (where Weinstein himself attempted a UAP physics project)
- Spergel is the head of the Simons Foundation
- The NASA 2022 UAP review panel was led by Spergel

Weinstein does not assert a causal claim but flags the overlap as worth noting. The 16-researcher NASA UFO panel that Spergel oversaw was described as inadequate by Weinstein given the scope of the problem.
