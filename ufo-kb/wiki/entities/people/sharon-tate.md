# Sharon Tate

Sharon Tate (1943–1969) was an American actress married to director Roman Polanski. She was eight-and-a-half months pregnant when she was murdered on August 8–9, 1969, at 10050 Cielo Drive, Los Angeles, along with four others: Abigail Folger, Jay Sebring, Voytek Frykowski, and Steven Parent. Roman Polanski was in Europe at the time.

## Symbolic Significance

The Tate house was not chosen randomly under the "Helter Skelter" theory — Manson had a prior connection to music producer Terry Melcher, who had previously occupied the house. But [[tom-oneill]] and [[operation-chaos]] analysis suggest a deeper symbolic dimension: the house was a nexus of liberal Hollywood celebrity support for the Black Panthers and counterculture movements. Jane Fonda, Jean Seberg, Warren Beatty, Marlon Brando, and Donald Sutherland had been meeting to support the Panthers financially.

J. Edgar Hoover wrote a memo in November 1968 warning that white Hollywood liberals supporting the Panthers would be "lined up against the wall and slaughtered" when revolution came. Eight months later, five white people were slaughtered in the house that embodied that support.

## The Brutality as Message

The four victims stabbed were stabbed more than 200 times. Blood writing covered the walls. The crime was designed to be maximally shocking — and it was. Tate's pregnancy intensified the horror and news coverage. The result was the rapid destruction of public sympathy for the hippie movement.
