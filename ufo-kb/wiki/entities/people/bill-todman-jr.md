# Bill Todman Jr.

Warner Brothers executive; son of TV producers Mark Goodson and Bill Todman Sr. (producers of *I Love Lucy*, *The $64,000 Question*, *Name That Tune*, etc.). Had a direct UAP encounter in the Grand Tetons during college, which he eventually disclosed to [[danny-sheehan]] via an intermediary, and told to novelist [[sydney-sheldon]], who fictionalized it.

## The Tetons Encounter

While on college summer break, Todman was hiking/climbing in the Grand Tetons near Boulder when he came into a meadow and found a UAP craft hovering silently above the ground. He walked all the way over to it and placed his hand on the hull:
- **Physical description**: Craft was hovering; felt "alive" to the touch
- **Sheehan's quote**: *"This is alive, this thing"*
- After Todman stepped back, the craft shuddered and shot away

He never publicly disclosed the encounter because he feared it would damage his professional reputation in the entertainment industry.

## Sydney Sheldon Connection

Todman told the story to his father's best friend, romance novelist [[sydney-sheldon]]. Sheldon subsequently wrote *The Doomsday Conspiracy* — a novel about a military intelligence officer tasked with finding witnesses to a UAP crash recovery, all of whom start getting killed. Sheldon never publicly acknowledged the real-life basis. Sheehan uses this as an example of witnesses finding indirect, deniable ways to share their experiences.

## Sheehan's Access

Sheehan was introduced to Todman through a Warner Brothers executive contact (Bob Remy, then president of the Academy of Arts and Sciences) while in LA to discuss an Iran-Contra film project. Todman specifically wanted to tell Sheehan — not to go public, but to ensure someone in the disclosure network knew.
