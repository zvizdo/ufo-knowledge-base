# Richard Thompson

Richard Thompson (1947–2008; Hare Krishna name: Sadaputa Dasa) — mathematician by training (Ph.D. in mathematics from Cornell), researcher at the Bhaktivedanta Institute, author in the fields of Vedic cosmology, anomalous archaeology, and ancient contact studies. Co-authored *Forbidden Archaeology* (1993) with Michael Cremo — the 914-page compendium arguing that modern archaeology systematically suppresses evidence of extremely ancient human presence.

**Alien Identities**: Thompson's *Alien Identities* (1993) applies a methodological approach designed to separate his Hare Krishna theological commitments from the empirical text analysis. Working through the Ramayana and Mahabharata, he argues that the **Davas** (the divine beings worshipped by approximately one billion Hindus) are the same class of entities as the **Anunnaki** of Sumerian religion and the **Elohim** of the Hebrew Bible — entities that intervened in human affairs, possessed advanced technology (flying craft called *vimanas*), engaged in conflict with humans and with rival factions of their own kind, and were not supernatural but physical.

**Jason Jorjani's use of the analysis**: In [[EzPmG_7WhXc]], [[jason-reza-jorjani]] cites Thompson's *Alien Identities* as independent empirical corroboration for the cross-cultural-NHI-control-system thesis: if a mathematician with no interest in defending UAP reality reaches the same conclusion as [[paul-wallis]] (Christian texts), [[zecharia-sitchin]] (Sumerian), and others, the cross-cultural convergence becomes more significant than any single tradition.

**Credibility note**: Thompson is unusual in the ancient-contact literature for his formal scientific training (Cornell mathematics Ph.D.) and his attempt to bracket his religious commitments in the analytical sections. This distinguishes his work from purely devotional Vedic scholarship.
