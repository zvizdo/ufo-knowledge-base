# Jesse Marcel

US Army Air Forces intelligence officer stationed at Roswell Army Air Field (RAAF), 509th Bomb Group. As base intelligence officer, he was dispatched to the Mac Brazel ranch to recover debris in early July 1947. His subsequent public statements in the late 1970s — after years of silence — are the foundation of the most detailed firsthand Roswell testimony.

## The Roswell Recovery

Marcel recovered debris from the field that he described as unlike any conventional material he had encountered:
- **"Malleable memory metal"** — thin foil-like material that could be crumpled but returned to its original shape
- **Disc-shaped** configuration of the debris field
- **Hieroglyphic-like markings or inscriptions** on some materials

## The Forced Photo Op

Marcel claims he was coerced into posing for a photograph holding weather balloon debris — a staged image that became the Air Force's official explanation for the Roswell incident. He stated this publicly:

> "I was forced to hold weather balloon debris for the photo."

The implication is that the actual recovered materials were transported separately — with [[wright-patterson]] identified in the broader Roswell testimony chain as the destination.
