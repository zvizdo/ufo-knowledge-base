# Scott Ramsay

Independent UFO researcher who, with his partner [[suzanne-ramsay]], conducted what is considered the most comprehensive modern investigation of the [[1948-aztec-crash]] — the alleged March 1948 UFO recovery near Aztec, New Mexico, which preceded the more famous [[1947-roswell]] case in print publication but was historically buried by a FBI-connected debunking campaign.

**Witness interview methodology**: The Ramsays' approach was to locate and systematically interview surviving witnesses and secondary sources who had contact with the Aztec event or its principals. This ground-up witness-interview methodology — working from surviving human contacts rather than documents — parallels the approach used to rehabilitate other crash-retrieval cases. [[jay-anderson]] presents the Ramsays as credible researchers who built a substantial evidentiary case.

**Historical rehabilitation**: The Aztec case had been dismissed since JP Cahn's 1950 debunking in *True* magazine. [[richard-dolan]] endorses the Ramsays' work as rehabilitating the case: Cahn's debunking, Dolan argues, was agenda-driven and connected to his family's relationship with J. Edgar Hoover rather than dispassionate skepticism. The Ramsays' witness-based reinvestigation is treated as evidence that the "hoax" label was itself a suppression artifact.

**Significance in crash-retrieval research**: Because Aztec was the first crash case to appear in print — Frank Scully's *Behind the Flying Saucers* (1950) preceded any Roswell account — the Ramsays' work on it is foundational to the argument that a systematic suppression pattern was applied to crash-retrieval claims from the very beginning of public UAP discourse.
