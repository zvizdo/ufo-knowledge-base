# Robert McNamara

## Background

Robert McNamara served as Secretary of Defense from 1961 to 1968 under Presidents Kennedy and Johnson. He is one of the central figures of Cold War nuclear policy and was a primary civilian counterweight to military hawks like [[curtis-lemay]] during the Cuban Missile Crisis.

## UAP Connection

In [[09KP8XVf5nY]], McNamara is referenced as a fellow occupant of the Cuban Missile Crisis situation room alongside [[harold-malmgren]]. [[harold-malmgren]]'s account of the Crisis presents McNamara as part of the rational civilian faction that ultimately prevailed over LeMay's push for a preemptive nuclear strike.

As Secretary of Defense, McNamara would have nominally held authority over the military's UAP-related activities — though Malmgren's account makes clear that nuclear/UAP classification through the AEC created layers operating above SecDef-level access.
