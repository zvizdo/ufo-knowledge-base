# Robert Maheu

Former FBI agent who ran a private intelligence and public relations firm (Robert A. Maheu Associates) with documented CIA subcontracts. In 1956, [[howard-hughes]] hired him as his Las Vegas representative; Maheu eventually became Hughes's alter ego and day-to-day proxy for the entire Hughes empire — handling negotiations, intelligence operations, and sensitive relationships Hughes could not manage as a recluse.

## CIA Connector Role

Maheu's career illustrates the revolving door between federal intelligence and private sector. His firm's CIA subcontract work included psychological operations and covert action. In 1960, CIA case officer Sheffield Edwards (→ see [[santos-trafficante]] context) approached Maheu to enlist organized crime in the Castro assassination plots. Maheu recruited [[johnny-roselli]], who brought in [[sam-giancana]] and [[santos-trafficante]]. This is documented in the CIA IG report (1967) and the Church Committee.

## Hughes-Nixon Money Flow

Maheu handled the delivery of a $100,000 cash payment from Hughes to [[richard-nixon]]'s brother Donald Nixon in 1956 (ostensibly a "loan"). This payment became a political liability for Nixon and was a documented motivation for the Watergate burglars to seek documents in Larry O'Brien's office — O'Brien (a Hughes employee) reportedly had damaging information about the payment.

## Firing and Legal Battles

In 1970 Hughes abruptly fired Maheu without meeting him in person — the two had never met face to face in their entire working relationship. Maheu and Hughes fought extended legal battles over the termination; Maheu won a defamation judgment after Hughes called him a "thief" on a press call.
