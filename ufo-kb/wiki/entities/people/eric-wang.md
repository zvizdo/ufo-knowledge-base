# Eric Wang

## Background

Dr. Eric H. Wang (1906–c.1960) was born in Vienna and graduated from the **Vienna Technical Institute** — the same institution attended by [[victor-schauberger]] (rumored developer of anti-gravity propulsion for the Nazis), raising the possibility of a direct or indirect professional connection. He immigrated to the United States before WWII, lectured at the University of Cincinnati, and eventually became **head of the Department of Special Studies at [[wright-patterson]] Air Force Base**.

Despite having his name attached to a civil engineering research facility at the University of New Mexico, Wang left almost no public biographical trace: no employment records, no published research, no standard obituary accessible through conventional archival search. UFO researcher [[william-steinman]] eventually located a brief obituary in an old issue of a mechanical engineering magazine — the only public footprint found after months of searching.

Wang's near-total erasure from the record is itself treated by researchers as significant, consistent with deep classification of his activities.

## Role in UAP Programs

According to **Maria Wang** (his widow), traced by [[william-steinman]]:
- Eric worked on classified projects at [[wright-patterson]] involving technology not of this world.
- He reported **directly to [[henry-kissinger]]**.
- Kissinger was deeply involved in the flying saucer program and had visited their home.

His subordinate [[arthur-stansel]] was the engineer transported to the [[1953-kingman-crash]] site, where he observed a 30-ft disc and humanoid figures. Wang's role was apparently the institutional superior overseeing the exploitation and analysis of such recovered material.

In **1956**, Wang's Department of Special Studies was relocated from Wright-Patterson to [[sandia-national-laboratories]] at [[kirtland-air-force-base]], New Mexico — a move consistent with centralizing exotic-technology work within the nuclear-complex infrastructure. Wang died c.1960, before the full story became public.

## Schauberger Parallel

Wang's Vienna Technical Institute background places him as a possible contemporary of [[victor-schauberger]]. Schauberger's anti-gravity propulsion work was forcibly transferred to the US by CIC agents after WWII (deposited at [[brookhaven-national-lab]]); the conceptual lineage of that research would have been within Wang's reach both institutionally (Wright-Patterson Special Studies) and possibly educationally.

> ⚠ No direct evidence of a personal Wang-Schauberger relationship has been confirmed. The Vienna contemporaneity is suggestive but unverified.
