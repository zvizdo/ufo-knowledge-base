# Eric Barr

Surveillance and monitoring director for [[brandon-fugel]]'s team at [[skinwalker-ranch]]. One of the first infrastructure projects Barr undertook after Fugel acquired the ranch was upgrading the camera system — maintaining many of [[robert-bigelow]]'s original vantage points but adding a control-room monitoring camera to track equipment anomalies from the source.

## The Screen-Grab Encounter

The most discussed incident involving Barr: the control room's motion-detection system kept triggering pings with no visible cause. Frustrated, Barr issued a verbal challenge to the empty room: *"If you've got something you want to show me, show me. If you've got something you want to tell me, tell me."*

The immediate next screen grab from the control room produced a striking artifact. The timestamp on the security-camera label read "LIVING" — with the word visible to Barr in what appeared to be a direct, literal response to his invitation to communicate. Barr initially rationalized it as a hardware bug, but the specificity and immediacy of the response against a baseline of weeks of random pings led him and the team to treat it as anomalous.

This incident is cited by the team as evidence for the [[trickster-intelligence]] hypothesis: that the ranch entity shows itself selectively and responds to direct engagement.
