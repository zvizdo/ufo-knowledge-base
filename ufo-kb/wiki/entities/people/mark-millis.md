# Mark Millis

## Background

NASA physicist who founded and directed the **Breakthrough Propulsion Physics (BPP)** program at Glenn Research Center (1996–2002), a NASA initiative to rigorously evaluate speculative propulsion concepts — faster-than-light travel, vacuum energy extraction, inertial modification — and determine which deserved further research. The program was terminated for budget reasons, not because the concepts were refuted.

## Frontiers of Propulsion Science

Co-edited with [[eric-davis]], *Frontiers of Propulsion Science* (AIAA, 2009) is a 739-page technical anthology covering:
- Warp drives and metric engineering (including Alcubierre's math)
- Vacuum energy and zero-point field propulsion
- Laser-based propulsion
- Nuclear propulsion variants
- Antigravity assessments

[[salvatore-pais]] brought his personal copy to the [[8TYMQOUDQBo]] interview and says: "my hat's off to Eric Davis just for that book alone," adding Millis equally. Pais considers it a landmark reference that validated much of his own theoretical direction.
