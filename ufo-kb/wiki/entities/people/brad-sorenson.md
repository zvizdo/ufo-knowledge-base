# Brad Sorenson

Aerospace industry figure. Contact of aviation illustrator [[mark-mccandish]].

## ARV Palmdale Showing

At a California air show, Sorenson was taken to Palmdale by a "prominent Italian defense financier" who told him they were going to be shown "the real stuff." Three bell/acorn-shaped craft were presented, described as alien reproduction vehicles (ARVs) or "flux liners." The craft showed visible wear and tear, indicating they had been flown operationally.

[[matthew-pines]]'s researcher contacts believe the Italian defense financier was [[frank-carlucci]] (CIA Deputy Director → SecDef → Carlyle Group). No direct confirmation.
