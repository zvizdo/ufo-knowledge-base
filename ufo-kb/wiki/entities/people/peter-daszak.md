# Peter Daszak

British zoologist who has served as president of the EcoHealth Alliance, a New York-based nonprofit that studies emerging infectious diseases. In the KB, Daszak appears in the context of the COVID-19 lab-leak / gain-of-function research cluster.

EcoHealth Alliance submitted a DARPA PREEMPT grant proposal in 2017 (DEFUSE) proposing to modify SARS-like coronaviruses by inserting furin cleavage sites — a modification that would enhance transmissibility in human airway cells. DARPA rejected the proposal, flagging it as too dangerous. The Wuhan Institute of Virology was a primary research partner. Internal DARPA review documents, leaked in 2021, confirmed the proposal and the rejection.

In the broader KB framing (see [[q7czo77qhha]]), Daszak is presented as part of a gain-of-function research approval chain that operated with minimal Congressional or public oversight, funded through mechanisms (NIH sub-grants to nonprofit intermediaries) that obscured direct government sponsorship of dual-use biological research. The KB's framing treats this as structurally analogous to other unacknowledged programs: controversial research conducted through institutional cutouts to insulate government from accountability.
