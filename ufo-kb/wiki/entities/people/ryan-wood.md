# Ryan Wood

Ryan Wood is a UFO researcher who, together with his late father [[bob-wood]] (Dr. Robert Wood), curated and forensically analyzed the Majestic Documents — a substantial collection of alleged classified documents related to the US government's UFO program, distinct from but related to the original MJ12 documents.

## Majestic Documents

The Wood collection goes beyond the original 1984 MJ12 document leak. Their documents are not merely photographic reproductions — they are actual physical papers that can be studied forensically (paper composition, typeface, ink aging, formatting conventions). [[richard-dolan]] describes having printed them all out, put them in a coil binding, and read them page after page:

> "They are highly sophisticated, extremely sophisticated, frankly. And so if you're thinking if this is a hoax, you have to ask yourself, who's creating this as a hoax? All right, you have documents that have through the decades, the 40s and in the 50s and in the 60s all in a very like seems exactly accurate format."

Dolan's assessment: the period-accurate sophistication across multiple decades rules out a lone hoaxer and suggests either authentic leaks or an intelligence operation with significant institutional resources.

## Bob Wood

Dr. Bob Wood (Ryan's late father) was the primary forensic analyst in the father-son team. He had an aerospace/defense background that gave him technical credibility in assessing document authenticity. The collection is now curated by Ryan.
