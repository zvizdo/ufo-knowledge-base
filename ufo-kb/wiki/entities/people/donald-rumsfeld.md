# Donald Rumsfeld

Two-time US Secretary of Defense (under Gerald Ford, 1975–1977, and George W. Bush, 2001–2006). Also served as White House Chief of Staff under Ford and as a congressman from Illinois. In his private-sector years between government appointments, Rumsfeld chaired pharmaceutical companies, most notably GD SURL and G.D. Searle & Company — where his role created the documented connection between CIA behavioral research and the pharmaceutical-agrochemical industry.

## The GD SURL / Scientific Engineering Institute Connection

In the late 1970s, the CIA's [[oard]] (Office of Research and Development — the post-MK-Ultra continuation of behavioral control research) transitioned its primary research vehicle — the [[scientific-engineering-institute]] (a CIA proprietary near Boston) — into the private sector. It was rebranded as SURL Metadata, a subsidiary of GD SURL.

**GD SURL was chaired by Donald Rumsfeld.**

In 1985, Rumsfeld orchestrated the sale of SURL/Scientific Engineering Institute to [[monsanto]]. This transfer moved:
- A covert behavioral research program with CIA origins
- Into the corporate structure of the world's most powerful agrochemical company
- With no public accountability or congressional oversight

This connection makes Rumsfeld a documented nexus point between the post-MK-Ultra intelligence community, the pharmaceutical industry, and the company that would later become the primary manufacturer of glyphosate-based herbicides.

## G.D. Searle and Aspartame

Rumsfeld's role at G.D. Searle & Company (he was CEO 1977–1985) is separately notorious for his role in obtaining FDA approval for aspartame — an approval that had previously been blocked due to safety concerns. Rumsfeld reportedly leveraged political connections (particularly following Reagan's election in 1980) to install FDA commissioners favorable to approval.

## Secretary of Defense and UAP-Adjacent Programs

As a two-time SecDef, Rumsfeld had access to and oversight of the most sensitive compartmented programs in the US government.
