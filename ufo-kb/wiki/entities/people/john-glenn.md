# John Glenn

US Marine Corps fighter pilot, NASA Mercury astronaut (Mercury Atlas 6, February 20, 1962 — first American to orbit Earth), and US Senator from Ohio (1974–1999). 33rd-degree Scottish Rite [[freemasonry|Freemason]] — the highest rank attainable in the Scottish Rite — and a member of the Concord Lodge No. 688 in New Concord, Ohio.

Per [[lue-elizondo]] in [[1f16VvXaSSE]], Glenn was the senior-statesman-and-astronaut who urged [[harry-reid]] to investigate UFOs as part of [[aawsap]] — a piece of program-genesis context not widely documented. The combination of his astronaut credibility, Senate position, and high Masonic rank places him centrally in the [[occult-aerospace-connection]] cluster.

## Background

- Marine Corps F-86 Sabre pilot in Korea; later a test pilot.
- NASA Mercury Seven astronaut; orbited Earth three times in *Friendship 7* (Feb 1962).
- US Senator (Ohio, 1974–1999).
- Returned to space at age 77 aboard *Discovery* (STS-95, October 1998) — the oldest person to fly in space.
- 33rd-degree Scottish Rite Freemason; member of Concord Lodge No. 688.
