# Philip K. Dick

American science fiction author (1928–1982). Wrote over 44 novels and 121 short stories, many exploring themes of reality, identity, and artificial intelligence. In the UAP/consciousness discourse he is cited primarily for his 1974 visionary experiences and the "Exegesis" — an 8,000-page private journal where he attempted to understand what had happened to him.

## The 2-3-74 Experience

In February–March 1974 Dick experienced a prolonged visionary episode: a beam of pink light downloaded "gnosis" into his mind, he saw an "ancient Rome" superimposed on 1970s California, and an entity he called VALIS (Vast Active Living Intelligence System) communicated via dreams and "hypnagogic" imagery. He later accurately diagnosed his son's undetected inguinal hernia through information received in this state — which was subsequently confirmed medically.

Dick spent the rest of his life trying to understand whether VALIS was God, an extraterrestrial intelligence, a Gnostic deity, or his own unconscious.

## UAP/Simulation Theory Relevance

Dick's simulation-reality framework anticipates modern simulation theory. [[andrew-gallimore]] cites him as a thinker who independently arrived at the "postbiological programmers" model of NHI. His VALIS = a vast computational intelligence operating "orthogonally" to spacetime — a model that maps onto the [[postbiological-civilization]] hypothesis.

## The Metz Speech and Timeline Rerun Hypothesis

At the 1977 Metz, France science-fiction conference Dick delivered a speech arguing: "We are living in a computer-programmed reality, and the only clue we have to it is when some variable is changed." His formulation: if a variable in the timeline is altered and the simulation rerun forward, inhabitants would experience *deja vu* — a sense that events are repeating — as a residual echo of the prior run.

Dick came to believe that his novel *The Man in the High Castle* (Germany and Japan win WWII) was not fiction but a description of a *real* prior timeline. He believed the simulators decided to rerun the timeline and selected the Allied-victory outcome as preferable — a thesis that maps exactly onto [[riz-virk]]'s timeline-optimization / save-state model in [[simulated-multiverse-virk]].

## The Adjustment Team (Light Switch Incident)

Dick's wife **Tessa Dick** told Riz Virk that the short story *The Adjustment Team* (later adapted as the film *The Adjustment Bureau* with Matt Damon and Emily Blunt) originated from a domestic experience: Dick turned on a bathroom light that he had operated many times, and found that the mechanism had changed (chain vs. light switch, or vice versa) without his knowledge. He began to wonder: "Did somebody change this while I wasn't here?" That question became the story of agents who "freeze-frame" reality and make micro-adjustments to maintain timeline trajectories. See [[simulation-theory]] and [[time-travel-uap]].

## VALIS and Anamnesis: Vallée's Reading (fyX8V1XXmQM)

[[jacques-vallee]] explicitly names Dick as a personal intellectual touchstone in [[fyX8V1XXmQM]], specifically citing *VALIS* and its alter-ego character **Horselover Fat** (a punning translation of Dick's first and last name into Greek and German).

Vallée and [[jesse-michaels]] discuss Dick's use of the Gnostic concept of **anamnesis** — the recovery of a primordial memory suppressed by birth and the material world. In *VALIS* and the Exegesis, Dick frames the human condition as:

1. **Amnesia at birth** — the soul forgets its prior existence and true nature upon entering the material body; the condition is Cosmic Wound, not Original Sin
2. **Propelled forward by the wound** — life is driven by the compulsion to find what was lost, even if the seeker doesn't know what they're looking for
3. **Anamnesis as recovery** — moments of genuine gnosis (like the 2-3-74 pink-light download) are instances of the primordial self re-emerging through the amnesia

Michaels reads Horselover Fat as Dick's alter-ego through whom he processes this Gnostic-wound recovery narrative — externalizing the self-examination into fiction because direct autobiography was insufficient to contain the metaphysical weight.

Vallée endorses this framing directly: "At one level the entire world *is* mystical. The inquiry can occur outside a religious context — but it cannot avoid metaphysical depth." He adds the caveat that anamnesis and Gnostic recovery can be mimicked by processes that "steal your brain" (cults, programmed subjects), making discernment essential.

## Dick as Conscious Precognitive Artist (hXYdkcv5TtY)

[[eric-wargo]] in [[hXYdkcv5TtY]] identifies Dick as the **only artist he has studied who consciously knew and accepted that he was precognitive and that his work was influenced by his future**. This distinguishes Dick from cases like [[michael-rolando-richards]] (where the precognitive content was unconscious) or [[morgan-robertson]] (who apparently did not theorize his own gift).

### Radio Free Albemuth / VALIS and the Vallée Convergence

In 1974–75 Dick was writing what would be the first draft of *VALIS* — later published posthumously as *Radio Free Albemuth*. At the same time, [[jacques-vallee]] was writing *The Invisible College* (1975). Neither could have read the other's manuscript at that point. Yet both featured:
- A **teaching satellite** / vast intelligence transmitting signals
- **Awakened human recipients** chosen to receive the signals
- The purpose of saving humanity from a **coming peril**

Wargo interprets this as mutual precognitive convergence: each was absorbing future information from the discourse environment they would both inhabit — their published works would cross-fertilize and neither could have consciously orchestrated the structural overlap.

Jesse Michaels encountered Vallée in person; Vallée showed him VALIS as his favorite book — independent corroboration of the Vallée-Dick connection.

### Dick's Close Encounters Precognition Dream

In ~1974, Dick had a dream involving:
- Small diminutive alien beings emerging from a spaceship
- One of them giving him a piece of technology
- A **brownie scored around its edges by a fork** — a relief map — described as belonging to "Betty Field" (Dick's conscious association: UFO abductee Betty Hill; he transposed the surname)

Three years later, *Close Encounters of the Third Kind* (1977) was released. A key scene features a character **fork-scoring mashed potatoes** to form a relief map that turns out to represent **Devil's Tower** — the site of the alien encounter. The movie also features small beings from a spacecraft.

Dick consciously noted the UFO/Betty Hill association but did not recognize the dream as a precognition of the Spielberg film. Wargo's interpretation: Dick was precognizing the film, not an actual UFO event. The associations (Betty Hill → Betty Field; fork-scored food → fork-scored model) show how precognitive dreams work — through associative displacement rather than literal representation.
