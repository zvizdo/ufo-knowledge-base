# Tim Alberino

Christian researcher, author, filmmaker, and field investigator. Grew up in a pastor's household in Brook Park/Cleveland, Ohio; mother died when he was 14. Lived in Peru for approximately 10 years before returning to the US; now based in Montana. Co-founded **Gen 6 Productions** with [[steve-quail]], producing the *True Legends* documentary series on biblical giants. Author of the book *Birthright*, which lays out his Christocentric cosmological synthesis of angelic theology, ancient alien traditions, Mars civilisation archaeology, and transhumanism.

## Background

Alberino was introduced to the Book of Enoch at age 16 when he drove to a Borders bookstore and purchased the *Pseudepigrapha*. This encounter formed the foundation of his lifelong interest in the antediluvian world and Nephilim theology. His father was a pastor, and he describes himself as coming from a firmly Christian household. He has a close relationship with Steve Quail (his former research partner) and with Ellie Marculli, a colleague who also researched the Kandahar Giant story independently.

## Core Theological Framework

Alberino advocates a **Christocentric** cosmology (as opposed to the traditional **anthropocentric** church view): Christ, not mankind, is the center of creation; humanity is one of many created intelligences in the divine family. This allows him to accommodate extraterrestrial intelligences within a biblical worldview without contradiction.

He distinguishes four categories of NHI relevant to the modern UFO phenomenon:

1. **Loyal angelic sons of God** ("good angels") — pre-existing humans, look human, use advanced aerospace technology; some of what we call UFOs may be their craft
2. **Fallen angelic sons of God** ("fallen angels" / "the dragon's faction") — have been on Earth since before Adam; use same technology as loyal angels; in opposition to the Kingdom of Heaven
3. **The Watchers** (a sub-group of angelic beings) — descended in the days of Jared; mated with human women; created the Nephilim; currently imprisoned in Tartarus until the Day of Judgment (per Book of Enoch, Peter, Jude)
4. **Gray aliens** — a wholly separate faction; not angelic; possibly arrived in the mid-to-late 1800s; running a covert hybridisation / planetary-acquisition program via alien abduction

He explicitly rejects conflating grey aliens with fallen angels.

## Notable Claims

### Book of Enoch / Nephilim (hearsay — ancient texts, personal interpretation)
- The Book of Enoch provides the cosmological substrate for Hebrew understanding of the antediluvian world and is attested in both Old and New Testament passages
- The title "Son of Man" used by Jesus comes exclusively from the *Parables of Enoch*, not the Old Testament — implying Jesus implicitly endorsed Enochian cosmology
- The Nephilim (offspring of Watchers and human women) were intelligent, technologically advanced beings; their disembodied spirits became what the New Testament calls demons
- The Watchers descended to Mount Hermon using advanced aerospace vehicles (i.e., UFOs); their descent initiated the antediluvian empire of the gods

### Mars / Sidonia / Rahab (hearsay — citing David Flynn, Richard Hoagland, Joe McMoneagle)
- The ancients believed the gods descended from Mars and from a destroyed planet called "Rahab" (named cryptically in the Old Testament — Psalms, Job)
- Rahab's destruction formed the asteroid belt; its shockwave devastated the Martian surface and biosphere
- [[david-flynn]]'s book *Sidonia* is described by Alberino as "the most consequential book I have ever read" — it argues the ancient mystery schools have recovering Martian knowledge as a primary objective
- [[richard-hoagland]] and [[david-flynn]] collaborated in 1990s–2000s conferences on Martian ruins, sometimes joined by [[michael-heiser]]
- [[joe-mcmoneagle]] was reportedly tasked by the CIA to remote-view Mars one million years ago and described 8–12 ft beings and pyramid structures
- [[john-brandenberg]] (PhD physics, Lawrence Livermore / Sandia) argues Argon-40 and Xenon-129 excesses on Mars prove a nuclear cataclysm
- NASA's second, higher-resolution photos of the Sidonia face/pyramid were likely released to suppress the discovery — Alberino's personal conjecture

### Mystery Schools / Occult (hearsay — David Flynn, personal research)
- The primary objective of mystery schools (going back to the Phoenicians) is to recover lost antediluvian knowledge and "resurrect" the empire of the gods — specifically by going to Mars
- The Osiris myth encodes the mystery school agenda: Isis reassembling Osiris's scattered body = adepts recovering scattered antediluvian knowledge from Egypt, Phoenicia, and Mars
- The cover-up of giant remains is driven primarily by occult interests (Baal-worshipping elite à la Epstein-adjacent circles), not merely by scientific embarrassment or biblical corroboration concerns
- Elon Musk's drive to colonise Mars is connected, consciously or not, to this ancient mystery-school aspiration

### Kandahar Giant (hearsay — multiple source layers)
- A C-130 cargo pilot (active duty at time of interview, interviewed in silhouette in Bozeman, Montana) described loading a 9–12 ft, ~1,100 lb giant onto his aircraft at Bagram Airfield, Afghanistan, reportedly in 2005
- Physical description: red hair, pale skin, six fingers per hand, six toes per foot, feet wrapped in primitive canvas, solid/muscular build
- The giant had reportedly killed an entire US military reconnaissance team; a second team killed it
- Body was transferred to a base in Qatar; pilot heard (unconfirmed) it ultimately ended up at [[wright-patterson]]
- Alberino's colleague [[ellie-marculli]] received an independent account (possibly 2003, special ops soldier) with nearly identical details — a different year but same cave encounter, same spear weapon
- Alberino has had informal corroboration from several soldiers and officers; one government contact reportedly warned a mutual friend: "What the hell are you getting yourself into? You need to be careful."
- The Pentagon reportedly issued a statement denying the Kandahar Giant story
- Giants in Afghanistan classified above UFO/crash retrieval secrecy in Alberino's experience

### Sardinian Giants (hearsay — multiple elderly eyewitnesses, on-camera interviews)
- Visited Sardinia at least three times; produced documentary content within the *True Legends* series
- Interviewed dozens of elderly Sardinians who physically excavated or encountered giant skeletal remains in the 1930s–1980s
- The site of the Church of Sant'Anastasia (Sardinia) yielded entire giant skeletons (≥9 ft tall), gold jewelry, goblets, and plates during excavations c. 1978–1981; two separate excavators confirmed identical accounts independently
- One 101-year-old witness described a graveyard of dozens of 9–12 ft giants arranged in cross-pattern pairs; officials arrived in vans, photographed everything, loaded it all up, and left
- A hermetically sealed Nuragi tower chamber yielded a seated giant at a table with oysters still on the plate — the body crumbled upon exposure to oxygen
- Alberino links the Sardinian nuragic people to Canaanites driven out by Joshua's conquest; a Greek/Tunisian column inscription reportedly read "We are the Canaanites who fled before the face of Joshua son of Nun"
- The tombs of the giants (shaped like wombs/bull's heads) were used for **incubation rites** — young initiates were sealed inside to commune with the spirit/power of dead Nephilim

### UFO Sighting — Brook Park, Ohio (firsthand)
- ~14 years before the interview (~c. 2010–2012), Alberino and his brother-in-law Tony observed a large diamond-shaped silent craft hover ~30–40 ft above their car in a strip-mall parking lot near the John Glenn NASA Research Center
- The craft had triangular white lights, green/blue perimeter lights, stubby protrusions instead of wings, no visible engines, no sound, and was completely unaffected by strong winds; no insignia
- Tony dropped his phone trying to film it; by the time he retrieved it, the craft had moved away toward the NASA center
- Alberino intuitively concluded it was an ARV (alien reproduction vehicle), though acknowledges this is subjective

### Peruvian Face Peeler Incidents, 2023 (firsthand investigation)
- Conducted a field investigation in San Antonio de Pintoyaku (Alto Nanai region, Peruvian Amazon), summer 2023
- Villagers described assailants ~6.5–8 ft tall in black full-body armour suits with almond-shaped tinted eye lenses ("like Green Goblin"), hovering on circular hoverboard discs
- 16-gauge birdshot had no effect on the armour; one assailant knocked back by a shotgun blast simply popped up and zoomed away
- Interviewed Talia (15-year-old girl): assailants captured her, applied numbing cream, attempted to surgically remove her face with a laser scalpel before she freed herself when one assailant let go to fix his helmet
- An acorn/almond-shaped translucent craft was seen 30–40 ft above a fisherman; he could see two beings inside through a mesh exterior
- Incursions also occurred in the city of Nauta (pop. 36,000), debunking the "jetpack river miners" narrative
- Operation Resolute Sentinel (joint US-Peruvian-UK military exercise) was running simultaneously — Alberino speculates possible connection
- Links face-peeler events to [[michael-herrera]]'s Indonesia craft encounter — both involve paramilitary actors with exotic technology operating independently of national militaries
- Brought night-vision goggles, thermal binoculars, and active-duty Peruvian Navy commandos on the investigation; brought medical/food supplies for villagers

- This scenario = the Antichrist deception described in Revelation: the "man of sin" presenting as saviour while leading humanity into posthuman transformation and ultimately war with the returning Christ (Armageddon)
