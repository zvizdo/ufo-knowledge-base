# Whitley Strieber

Author whose 1987 book *Communion* brought the alien abduction phenomenon to mainstream attention and sold millions of copies. Strieber recounts a December 1985 encounter at his rural cabin in upstate New York with entities he describes as non-human "visitors." The book is notable for its literary quality and for Strieber's sustained ambiguity about the ontological nature of the experience — he does not definitively claim the entities are extraterrestrial.

Over 40 years of contact events, Strieber has developed a coherent theoretical framework: the Grays have lost free will through extreme cognitive evolution; NHI require human belief/consciousness to manifest fully; and disclosure is suppressed because acceptance is irreversible (the "open door" problem attributed to [[john-von-neumann]]).

## Notable Claims

### 1952 Childhood Conditioning (Firsthand, partial memory)
At age 7, Strieber was enrolled in an "accelerated learning program for high-IQ children" near [[randolph-air-force-base]] in San Antonio. He remembers only confinement in a dark place with horrible noise (a Skinner box). Afterward he contracted immune system failure, received gamaglobulin injections at Brook General Hospital, and was isolated until Christmas. A childhood friend who was also in the program now works at a high level in government. Strieber connects this program to Nazi post-[[operation-paperclip]] conditioning research — the dark/noise environment mimicking conditions designed to "crack the cosmic egg" (induce transcendence) in high-IQ children.

### 1985 Abduction (Firsthand)
December 1985 at his cabin in upstate New York — the experience documented in *Communion*. Foundational event in his contact history.

### 1989 Implant Incident (Firsthand, documented)
May 1989, ~11 PM, cabin in upstate New York. Two humanoid figures entered his locked, alarmed room with no detectable point of entry. A male voice said "Condition red" from the backyard; a woman's voice spoke soothingly. A powerful hand pushed his head into the pillow; a flash of light in the woods; complete paralysis. In the morning: the alarm system was destroyed, a magnetic field strong enough to destroy electronics was measured by an alarm technician with a gas meter (needle disappeared off scale). Days later, ear pain indicated an implant had been placed.

**Southwest Research analysis**: Bill Malow, head of material science, analyzed the implant — metallic base with what appeared to be moving cilia or cellular structures. The implant emitted a radio signal. When Strieber was in a classified signals acquisition lab with Malow, the implant turned on; the lab technicians said the signal was "the strangest we've ever acquired" and classified. Over 20 years later, at a San Antonio book signing, a signals technician approached Strieber and confirmed it was "still under study."

**Attempted removal (1994-96)**: A doctor attempted surgical removal; the implant, visible as a white disc, moved down into the earlobe and disappeared under skin. Surgery was abandoned. The ear burned afterward.

**Activation (2015)**: The implant was dormant from 1989 until 2015. Strieber believes [[anne-strieber]] activated it from beyond death after her passing so that communication could continue. It now turns on during 3 AM communion sessions and guides him through synchronicity toward needed information.

**Inventor**: Mid-2000s, two visitors explained that the implant was invented by [[constantine-raudive]], the Latvian EVP researcher who died in the early 1970s — predating the 1989 implantation.

### Von Neumann "Open Door" Thesis (Hearsay via General Exon)
[[general-exon]], who worked at Air Material Command during the Roswell period, tried to explain to Strieber a paper he believed [[john-von-neumann]] wrote: non-human entities require human consensus belief to enter shared reality (via consciousness/wave function collapse); once accepted, they cannot be removed — the door opens in one direction only, forever. Strieber argues this explains the intensity of disclosure suppression: it's not just about panic — it's about irreversible ontological commitment. See [[nhi-open-door-problem]] and [[wave-function-nhi-manifestation]].

### Grays' Lost Free Will (Theoretical)
Strieber's core claim about Gray motivation: they evolved beyond free will — they see too clearly into past and future to make genuine choices. Communion with time-bound humans is precious because it restores novelty. They don't want slaves or resources; they want friends and equals — and to warn humanity not to follow their evolutionary dead end. See [[grays-lost-free-will]].

### Robert Sarbacher — Personal Contact and Death
[[stanton-friedman]] introduced Strieber to [[robert-sarbacher]] before *Communion* was published. Sarbacher asked Strieber to mail him a written account of his experiences. Strieber sent the manuscript overnight via UPS. The next morning the UPS driver called: Sarbacher had died the previous night. Sarbacher's last disclosure to Strieber: materials from crashed craft were studied at [[battelle-memorial-institute]]; when electron microscopes became operational, they discovered a "molecular grid" — a designed structure inside thin material giving it exceptional strength, since reverse-engineered into military and civilian technology.

### Collins Elite Persecution (Firsthand)
[[collins-elite]] — an evangelical Christian military-intelligence group (reportedly Air Force) that believed Strieber was "in league with demons" — orchestrated a social engineering campaign against him, including media attacks and a *South Park* satirizing episode. The campaign destroyed his career, forced bankruptcy, and led to loss of the cabin. Defense Department figures "do not like me and do not want my work out there."

### 3 AM Communion (Ongoing, Firsthand)
Since 2015, Strieber engages in nightly contact sessions at 3 AM (Brahma Maherta time — 1.5 hours before dawn, sacred across multiple traditions). From 2015–2020 he was physically woken each night; from 2020 onward he must self-wake. Sessions involve the implant turning on, an intense communion feeling, and messages conveyed not in words but through synchronicity and direct knowing. He describes [[anne-strieber]] and "the aliens" communing together.

### Blue Dwarfs
Strieber and Anne witnessed, on West 55th Street in Manhattan, a man in a brown suit being pulled back by "blue dwarfs" behind a green curtain at a horror storefront. They did not call police (it wouldn't be believed). His secretary Lori Barnes experienced a 1954 encounter in which blue dwarfs appeared in her bedroom and said "we're interested in the girl child you're carrying" and "one day you will look just like us." These blue entities appear distinct from the classic small Grays.

## Family and Government Connections

- Uncle Mickey and General Exon worked at Air Material Command in 1947 (Roswell era). Exon told a family member he'd held one of the Roswell bodies and said "it was like a big insect."
- Father Carl Strieber was evasive about wartime work; Strieber suspects involvement in classified programs but has no proof.
- The Strieber family had a multigenerational friendship with another high-level government family going back before the Civil War.

## Career Destruction

Strieber was bankrupted by the Collins Elite social engineering campaign. He lost his cabin and went through a "long, agonizing decline." He now lives simply in California, distrustful of the publishing industry's relationship with the defense department. His new book (being circulated to close friends including [[hal-puthoff]] and others as of 2024) addresses NHI genetics, human empowerment, and the resurrection mechanism in the Shroud of Turin.

## MK-Ultra and Intelligence Background (EzPmG_7WhXc / Jorjani)

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] adds significant new detail in [[EzPmG_7WhXc]] based on Strieber's public statements across 20+ years of interviews:

- The Strieber family are **Texas Germans** — a demographic Jorjani connects to pro-Nazi sympathies in the 1930s–40s US German community
- Strieber's father was involved with **military intelligence** (independently known — Strieber has discussed this publicly)
- His parents enrolled him at a **"summer camp" at Randolph Air Force Base** (near their San Antonio home) as a young child
- At Randolph AFB, Strieber alleges he was subjected to **physical torture and mind-control experiments**; the experiences made him repeatedly ill and kept him absent from school for a couple of years around age 7–8
- He was taken from Randolph AFB to a **"school for special children" in Baja California** — a mansion he tried to locate as an adult
- At the Baja facility, children were shown horrific things in dungeon-like conditions — given chainsaws and implements of violence, made to believe they had committed atrocities displayed to them; intense sounds in darkness. Jorjani characterizes this as a **precursor to MK-Ultra involving children** — the aim: fracture the psyche and create a compartmentalized, controllable sub-personality for intelligence use
- Strieber's 1985 Catskills cabin encounter: while being "handled" by a gray entity in a way he found extremely stimulating, he looked around the room and saw **military and intelligence personnel standing there watching** — including a man he recognized from school who had later joined the CIA

Jorjani's interpretation: some of Strieber's experiences are products of a **very advanced mind-control program** producing or managing the perception of the UFO phenomenon.

> ⚠ Conflict: Strieber's own interpretation of his experiences emphasizes genuine NHI contact with grays (gray motivation = lost free will, they want communion). Jorjani's read emphasizes intelligence manipulation of perception using dissociated mental states. The Catskills CIA-personnel-in-the-room claim is Strieber's own, but he reportedly does not often discuss it publicly.
