# Ben Lamb

Scientist at [[colossal-biosciences]], the de-extinction company founded by Ben Novak and George Church, known for its projects to revive extinct species — most prominently the direwolf (announced 2025), and ongoing projects for the woolly mammoth and thylacine.

**Nazca mummies DNA connection**: Lamb is connected to the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] DNA testing pipeline. Colossal Biosciences agreed in principle to conduct genetic analysis of the Nazca tridactyl mummies — the alleged three-fingered humanoid remains recovered from Peru that have been presented as potentially non-human. The testing agreement was conditional on receiving permission from the Peruvian government, which has maintained ownership rights over archaeological finds from Peruvian territory.

**Significance of Colossal involvement**: Colossal Biosciences' involvement — if it proceeds — would provide institutional scientific credibility to the DNA analysis that independent or non-credentialed labs cannot supply. Colossal has Harvard-affiliated scientists, advanced ancient DNA recovery capabilities (from its extinct-species work), and an established publication pipeline. If the Nazca mummy DNA shows anomalous sequences, a Colossal-conducted analysis would be substantially harder to dismiss than prior analyses from lesser-credentialed laboratories.

**Status**: The DNA analysis was pending Peruvian government authorization as of the KB's most recent source documents ([[HxQN2tkQHs8]]). Whether authorization was granted or the analysis conducted is not confirmed in the KB.
