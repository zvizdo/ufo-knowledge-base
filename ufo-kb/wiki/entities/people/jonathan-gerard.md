# Jonathan Gerard

This name is an alternate spelling of **[[jonathan-gorard]]** — Jonathan Gorard, the Wolfram Institute physicist. Links to `[[jonathan-gerard]]` in older transcript pages should be understood as pointing to the same person as `[[jonathan-gorard]]`.

**Who this person is**: [[jonathan-gorard]] (the primary entry) is a physicist at the Wolfram Institute who, with Stephen Wolfram, developed the hypergraph rewriting framework (the **Wolfram Model**) that in 2020–2021 showed: (1) causal graphs of hypergraph rewriting rules converge in the limit to the Einstein field equations (deriving general relativity from combinatorial/discrete rules); and (2) the branchial graph of the same system yields the Feynman path integral and projective Hilbert space (deriving quantum mechanics from the same rules). This is a candidate unified theory of physics from first principles.

**UAP relevance**: [[matthew-pines]] cites [[jonathan-gorard]]'s work in [[9QMrhcpJq8I]] as the most compelling candidate theoretical framework for understanding NHI propulsion and non-local phenomena — specifically through the concept of **branchial space** (the space of quantum branches), which Pines argues UAP may traverse rather than moving through normal 3D space. If UAP navigate branchial space, the physics of their propulsion would look nothing like conventional aerospace.
