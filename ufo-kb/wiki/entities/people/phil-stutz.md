# Phil Stutz

Psychotherapist and co-developer (with [[barry-michaels]]) of *The Tools* — a framework of mental visualizations incorporating Jungian shadow work and archetypal imagery for navigating everyday psychological adversity. Subject of Jonah Hill's 2022 Netflix documentary *Stutz*. Jesse Michaels's godfather and one of the primary intellectual influences behind his openness to fringe topics.

## Background

- Former Rikers Island prison psychiatrist for 5 years — evaluating and helping people was, from the start, literally a matter of life or death.
- In his early 30s was afflicted with chronic fatigue syndrome; this period of breakdown led him to [[rudolph-steiner]] and shaped his worldview.
- Attracted high-profile patients (CEOs, actors, directors); paid homage to in several films and shows (not publicly identified due to patient confidentiality).

## Intellectual Influences

Stutz is a devoted follower of [[rudolph-steiner]] — the turn-of-century Austrian esoteric philosopher and founder of anthroposophy. He cites Steiner's predictions about narcissism and leadership failure as having been vindicated ~100 years later. He also invokes Steiner's cosmology to interpret Karl Marx (Steiner believed Marx was "dictated to by the devil" while writing at the British Museum).

His therapeutic framework incorporates **Jungian shadow work** — establishing a relationship with the repressed, inferior side of one's personality — as the single most powerful tool he's developed.

## Connection to Domain

Stutz himself has no UFO/UAP research involvement. His relevance to this KB is as the **key figure who shaped [[jesse-michaels]]'s orientation toward esoteric, fringe, and consciousness-based thinking** — the intellectual soil from which the American Alchemy editorial voice grew.
