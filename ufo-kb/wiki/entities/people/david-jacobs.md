# David Jacobs

Tenured history professor (emeritus) at Temple University. The most systematic and academically credentialed researcher of the grey alien abduction phenomenon. Used "relaxation technique" regression (not standard hypnosis) to interview thousands of abductees. Closely associated with [[bud-hopkins]], with whom he collaborated extensively.

Reportedly has dementia as of the time of the American Alchemy interview.

## Core Conclusions

### The Hybridisation Program
Jacobs concluded that the grey alien abduction program is a long-running, systematic programme to create human-alien hybrids. The evidence trail — compiled from thousands of abductee accounts — describes a progressive refinement:

- **Early phase** (mid-20th century): Hybrids were visibly imperfect — oversized heads, scraggly hair, eyes too large, stature too small
- **Progressive refinement**: Over decades, hybrids became increasingly indistinguishable from humans
- **Late phase (described in *Walking Among Us*)**: "Personal project hybrids" or "HuBrids" — human-appearing hybrids retaining grey telepathic capabilities; assigned to female abductees as tutees

### Personal Project Hybrids
The most alarming finding from Jacobs' later research:

- Advanced hybrids are assigned to female abductees, who must teach them basic human life skills (grocery shopping, driving, using appliances, behaving in public)
- These hybrids are described as **psychopathic**: verbally and physically abusive, sexually aggressive, highly manipulative
- They are loyal to the grays, not to humans
- They are, for practical purposes, indistinguishable from humans to casual observation

### Planetary Acquisition by Stealth
Jacobs' conclusion about the endgame: the grey alien program constitutes **"planetary acquisition by stealth"** — gradual integration of human-appearing hybrids into human society to covertly take over civilisation from within. The inverse of the Borg assimilation model; the grays absorb themselves into us.

### Timeline: Abduction Began Mid-to-Late 1800s
Jacobs and Hopkins independently concluded — and Alberino independently reached the same conclusion — that the grey alien abduction phenomenon began in the **mid-to-late 1800s**, roughly contemporaneous with the rise of Spiritualism and the mystery airship sightings.

### Hereditary Nature
Alien abduction is hereditary: if one parent was an abductee, the children will be. [[tim-alberino]] says he has found no exception to this rule.

## Epistemological Framework
Jacobs, like Turner, argued that the abduction program is fundamentally deceptive. He opposed Vallée's conflation of abduction with folklore/trickster phenomena, arguing the grey alien abduction is a physically real, biologically-focused program distinct from other paranormal traditions.
