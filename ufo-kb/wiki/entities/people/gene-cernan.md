# Gene Cernan

NASA astronaut; commander of Apollo 17 (December 1972), the final crewed lunar landing mission. Last human to walk on the Moon. Naval aviator and test pilot before joining NASA. Also flew Gemini 9A and Apollo 10.

## Apollo 17 Anomalous Observations

During the outbound transit to the Moon, Cernan observed a distant bright object he initially could not identify:

> "It is a bright object, and it's obviously rotating because it's flashing... It's apparently rotating in a very rhythmic fashion because the flashes come around almost — almost on time."

He described two distinct flash intensities per rotation and noted the object was clearly outside the particle debris field from S-IVB separation. Mission Control requested he provide NOUN 20 attitude references to try to locate it optically. LMP [[jack-schmitt]] later identified it as the probable S-IVB stage based on the two-flash pattern matching the S-IVB's side and engine bell.

Cernan also reported cosmic ray phosphene flashes during sleep — standard deep-space radiation effect — and noted this was the first time he had observed them despite prior spaceflight experience.
