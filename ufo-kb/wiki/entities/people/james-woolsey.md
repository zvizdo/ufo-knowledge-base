# James Woolsey

CIA Director from January 1993 to January 1995 under President Clinton. Prior career included defense and intelligence roles: Under Secretary of the Navy, Director of the Strategic Arms Reduction Talks, and Director of the CTBT negotiations. Post-CIA career as an attorney and advisor includes neoconservative foreign policy advocacy and positions at defense-connected think tanks.

**UAP statements**: Woolsey has expressed public openness to the UAP phenomenon, stating he believes some reports involve real and unexplained phenomena. His statements place him alongside [[john-brennan]] and [[james-clapper]] as former intelligence directors who declined to categorically dismiss UAP during the 2020–2023 disclosure wave. The significance: CIA directors are read into the most sensitive programs in the intelligence community. When three consecutive CIA directors (Woolsey 1993–95, Brennan 2013–17, and implicitly others) express openness to UAP, it either reflects genuine uncertainty at the director level — consistent with [[ufo-secrecy]] operating above CIA — or constitutes a managed disclosure signal.

**Clinton era connection**: Woolsey's tenure overlapped with the [[1995-rockefeller-ufo-initiative]] and the period in which [[bill-clinton]] tasked [[webster-hubbell]] to find UAP files. If the most senior CIA official of the era genuinely could not confirm UAP program existence to the president's designated investigator, the compartmentalization was operating above director level — a critical data point.

Woolsey is cited in the [[age-of-disclosure]] documentary as part of the broader pattern of establishment figures lending credibility to the UAP phenomenon.
