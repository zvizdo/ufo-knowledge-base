# Brian Jackson

UFO researcher associated with the **Night Shift** YouTube channel (co-hosted with Clint and Xander Jones). Jackson specializes in deep investigative research on UAP whistleblower cases, with particular focus on [[dan-burish]] and the J-Rod contact case.

**Dan Burish research**: Jackson's most cited contribution is a detailed presentation on the [[dan-burish]] case — a complex and heavily disputed account involving Burish's claimed work as a microbiologist at the S-4 facility, his alleged contact with an alien entity designated [[j-rod]], and his subsequent role in what he claimed was a time-loop/treaty scenario. [[uap-gerb]] and [[jesse-michaels]] both credit Jackson with doing "great work" on Burish's story. Specifically, Jackson surfaced evidence for the alleged [[mike-mcconnell]]/Burish godfather relationship — a claimed personal connection between Dan Burish and the former Director of National Intelligence that would give Burish's claims institutional grounding if verified.

**Research methodology**: The Night Shift / Jackson approach involves deep-dive analysis of primary source material — original video testimony from witnesses, document analysis, network mapping of individuals and organizations. Chris Ramsey shared with Jesse Michaels some of Burish's raw personal video content (including what appeared to be agency keyword drops from Burish's wife), suggesting that researchers in this cluster share primary-source material and apply analytical tools beyond surface-level reading.

**Burish case significance**: The Dan Burish case is one of the most elaborate and contested in UAP whistleblower history. Jackson's contribution is in taking the case seriously enough to investigate its evidentiary structure rather than dismissing it as obvious fabrication or accepting it uncritically.
