# Lawrence Preston Gise

Head of the Atomic Energy Commission (AEC) Western Division, based in Albuquerque, New Mexico. Maternal grandfather of Jeff Bezos.

## 1963 UAP Disclosure to Harold Malmgren

In 1963, [[harold-malmgren]] was sent to Los Alamos / Albuquerque to manage the design, cost, and build of the anti-ballistic missile system. During that visit, Gise met with him and stated: *"We have been recovering and trying to reverse engineer UFO objects."* This constitutes an official AEC/DOE-lineage disclosure that the US crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering program was ongoing by 1963, having begun after 1947. Malmgren is now publicly vouching for this account.

## Significance

Gise's institutional position — head of the AEC Western Division, which managed national labs including Sandia and Los Alamos — is directly consistent with the thesis advanced by [[jay-andrew-kissner]] and others that the DOE national-lab complex is the primary hosting environment for crash-retrieved objects. His willingness to disclose to Malmgren suggests this was a deliberate read-in rather than an accidental disclosure.

The Bezos connection is notable: the most powerful private cloud computing and aerospace entrepreneur (Blue Origin) has the director of the UAP hosting institution as his maternal grandfather.
