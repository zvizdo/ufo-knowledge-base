# Owen Garriott

NASA physicist-astronaut; science pilot on Skylab 3 (July–September 1973) and later Space Shuttle mission STS-9 (1983). Died 2023. Son Richard Garriott (game developer) briefly flew to ISS in 2008 as a private astronaut.

## UAP Account

Garriott delivered the primary verbatim account of the Skylab 3 unidentified object in the official SL-3 crew debrief (October 4, 1973). From [[nasa-uap-d7-skylab-technical-crew-debriefing-1973]]:

About one week before SL-3 splashdown, [[jack-lousma]] first noticed "a bright reddish object" in the wardroom window. Garriott examined it closely:

- **Brightness**: "Much brighter than Jupiter or any of the other planets"
- **Color**: reddish even when well above the horizon, before atmospheric coloration would apply
- **Duration**: observed for approximately 10 minutes prior to sunset
- **Rotation**: brightness varied with "a period of almost exactly 10 seconds"
- **Orbital proximity**: entered darkness 5 to 7 seconds after Skylab; Garriott deduced "not more than 30 to 50 nautical miles from our location"
- **Relative stability**: drifted only 10–20 degrees in the wardroom window over 10 minutes (indicating very similar orbit)
- **Re-observation**: "We never saw it on any earlier or succeeding orbits"
- **Identification requested**: "We'd be quite interested in having its identification established. It's all debriefed in terms of time on channel A."

No identification was ever publicly provided.
