# Stacy Rhodes

Witness to the [[1997-phoenix-lights]] event who observed the craft from the I-10 highway. Rhodes' account provides a ground-level physical description of the craft's size and material appearance that differs from the more common "V of lights" characterization dominating media coverage of the event.

## The Sighting

Rhodes was on the I-10 when she observed the craft overhead. Key details from her account:

- **Color**: Gunmetal black — a solid material-surface observation, not just a light pattern
- **Size**: Extraordinary. Rhodes held up a newspaper to compare — she could not see beyond the edges of the object. The craft blocked the newspaper from her perspective entirely, indicating a wingspan or diameter comparable to a city block or larger when accounting for altitude

This is consistent with other Phoenix Lights witnesses who described the object as a solid craft rather than a formation of lights.

## In *The Program*

Rhodes' account is included in [[the-program-fox]] (released December 23, 2024). Her testimony adds to the material-description evidence base for the Phoenix Lights case, which James Fox has continued investigating since his earlier documentaries.
