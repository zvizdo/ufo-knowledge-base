# Konstantin Tsiolkovsky

Russian scientist (1857–1935); widely regarded as the **father of theoretical rocketry and astronautics**. His rocket equation and multi-stage rocket concepts are foundational to all modern space launch. He is the foundational figure of the Russian space program.

## Explicit NHI Transmission Writing (zjpvfDFc4fg)

[[jesse-michaels]] quotes Tsiolkovsky directly in [[zjpvfDFc4fg]]:

> *"We are made as the ethereal beings existing beyond our dimensions of recognized reality. These higher beings are in communication with us, reading our thoughts and sending us messages through celestial symbols which most of us do not even perceive, much less understand. A genius is one who comprehends and channels these messages from higher beings into technologies, products, and even art."*

This is the clearest written statement in the KB of the **[[scientists-as-receivers]]** thesis: Tsiolkovsky believed his rocket equation was **received** from higher NHI, not invented. He saw himself as a medium for trans-dimensional intelligence.

He was inspired by his teacher [[nikolai-fyodorov]], whose cosmism held that humanity must defeat death through technology and colonize the cosmos as a Christian duty.

## NHI Belief and Cosmism

[[diana-pasulka]] identifies Tsiolkovsky as the cosmism anchor for the Russian space tradition: he **explicitly believed in non-human intelligence** and wrote books articulating this belief. His cosmism had a Christian overlay — not a Western occult framework like [[jack-parsons]]'s, but a Slavic-Christian vision of cosmic intelligence and humanity's eventual expansion into the cosmos as part of a divine evolutionary process.

This NHI belief was not incidental to his technical work — it was the motivating cosmological framework for why getting humanity off the planet mattered. The same structure appears in the American space program via Parsons, with a different theological veneer.

[[diana-pasulka]] frames both Tsiolkovsky (Russia) and Parsons (America) as evidence that the **[[cosmism]] tradition** — belief that the space programs were operating in relationship with NHI — is baked into the founding epistemology of the space age, not grafted onto it.
