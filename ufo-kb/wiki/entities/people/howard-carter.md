# Howard Carter

## Background

British Egyptologist who, after years of searching in the Valley of the Kings, discovered the nearly intact tomb of [[tutankhamun-pharaoh]] in 1922. Upon breaking through the sealed entrance and shining a light inside, he said "Yes, wonderful things" — one of the most famous quotes in the history of archaeology.

## The Tutankhamun Curse

The tomb's entrance bore a curse inscription. A remarkable series of deaths and misfortunes befell members of the excavation team and those connected to the discovery — some from toxic air inside the chamber (carcinogenic paint residue accumulated over 3,000 years), others from seemingly unrelated tragedies (house fires, disease, scorpion bites). Carter himself survived longer than the others, dying last.

[[luke-caverns]] notes that Carter may have survived because of an exceptionally strong personal will, while still acknowledging the poisonous air as a material explanation for at least some deaths.
