# Ron Wyden

Ron Wyden (b. 1949) has served as US Senator from Oregon since 1996, making him one of the Senate's longest-serving members on intelligence oversight. He serves as a senior member of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence and has a documented track record of aggressive oversight of classified programs — most notably his years-long effort to expose the NSA's bulk surveillance programs (ultimately confirmed by the Snowden disclosures).

**Intelligence oversight pattern**: Wyden's aggressive approach to intelligence oversight — including public warnings about classified programs he could not describe in detail without violating classification obligations — is relevant to UAP discourse as a model for how a senator *with knowledge* of a classified program can signal its existence without being able to describe it. His pre-Snowden warnings about NSA programs are cited as a precedent for reading congressional statements about UAP programs.

**UAP oversight context**: Wyden is cited alongside [[jay-rockefeller]], [[richard-shelby]], and [[mario-biaggi]] as congressional figures who used their intelligence committee positions to seek information about UAP programs. Given his documented approach to NSA surveillance oversight, his institutional position made him one of the senators most capable of pursuing classified UAP program information through formal oversight channels.
