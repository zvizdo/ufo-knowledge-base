# Kit Green

Christopher "Kit" Green is a physician and former CIA analyst identified in UAP circles as a government-connected figure who maintained covert contact with civilian researchers and witnesses. He is associated with the broader [[hal-puthoff]] / [[bigelow-aerospace]] / [[aawsap]] network.

## UAP Connections

- [[charles-hall]] and [[tim-taylor]] episodes: Taylor brought an unidentified doctor to Hall's home during his multi-year visits; [[jesse-michaels]] speculates this was Kit Green.
- Green is named by researchers as a key figure in the AAWSAP contractor network, which investigated UAP biological effects on witnesses and experiencers.
- Involved in reviewing medical records of individuals claiming UAP contact; some claim he maintained a covert database of experiencer medical data.

## The Eric Walker Meeting

[[claus-svahn]] (via [[jesse-michaels]]) reports that Green met with [[eric-walker]] at Penn State, wanting to learn about UAP secrets. **Walker reportedly kicked him out of the office** and refused to say anything — despite Green's CIA credentials and involvement in UAP-adjacent programs. This suggests Walker maintained a separate classification tier above the AAWSAP/DIA/CIA UAP network that Green operated within.

This is consistent with a pattern: Walker refused multiple credentialed researchers (Green, [[william-steinman]], [[grant-cameron]]) while hinting that "psi" / "sixth sense" access was a prerequisite for insider UAP knowledge.
