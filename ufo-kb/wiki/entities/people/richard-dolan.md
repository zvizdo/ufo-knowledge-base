# Richard Dolan

## Background

Richard Dolan is a UFO historian best known for the two-volume *[[ufos-national-security-state]]* — a meticulously sourced history of UAP encounters and government responses from 1941 through the modern era. He also coined the term "[[breakaway-civilization]]" and is currently publishing a multi-volume USO series (see [[usos-volume-1]]).

He holds a partial PhD history from the University of Rochester (two unfinished dissertations — German political history, then US diplomatic policy under Truman). He left academia in ~1994 after finding [[timothy-good]]'s *[[above-top-secret]]* in a Syracuse bookstore and spending two years unable to sleep, running through the implications of what US history actually looked like once UAP secrecy was factored in.

His public channel is Richard Dolan Intelligent Disclosure (YouTube).

## Origin Story

Dolan was studying Harry Truman's presidency and 1950 national security strategy when he stumbled on Good's book. The cognitive dissonance: he recognized the agencies, names, and departments from his own research. His question was not "Are UFOs real?" but "Even if they were mistakenly interested — how come I never read about it in any academic history book?" That question drove everything subsequent.

## Breakaway Civilization (Coined by Dolan)

Dolan coined the term ~20 years before this interview, inspired by [[arnold-toynbee]]'s meta-historical framework. His reasoning: if crash-retrieved technology is classified deep enough that it never re-enters public science, the custodians diverge in capability until they constitute a *separate civilization* — one with potentially functional exotic propulsion, off-world capability, and effective immunity to normal accountability structures. [[catherine-austin-fitts]] independently converged on the same concept through Pentagon accounting anomalies.

## B2 Electrogravitics Encounter

In 2008 at a Las Vegas UFO conference, Dolan sat with a retired Air Force colonel (PhD, B2 stealth bomber project) and mentioned [[paul-laviolette]]'s electrogravitics paper. The colonel — without saying a word — put up a hand, looked left and right, leaned in, and whispered: "That's bullshit." Then went silent. Dolan: "And as soon as he said that, I thought, 'All right, that's got to be true.'"

When [[bob-maguire]] left daily intelligence work, he chose Dolan as his UAP education: "I am a denizen of the National Security State, so I figured, okay, I'll go to this guy." Dolan also conducted a four-part interview with [[chris-bledsoe]] that convinced Bob to seek a personal visit.

## MIB as NHI Counterintelligence

Dolan's theory, discussed by [[james-fox]] in [[hBrfe4hFuM0]], holds that the [[men-in-black]] operate as counter-intelligence *on behalf of the NHI* (or at minimum the OSI). His reasoning centers on the implausible speed of response and depth of personal intelligence in pre-internet contexts — particularly the de Souza encounter in 1996 rural Brazil, where MIB arrived within 10–15 minutes with precise personal data. Dolan argues that no conventional intelligence rapid-response unit could achieve this without pre-positioning that itself implies advance knowledge of the craft's movements. He has attributed the MIB to OSI at minimum, and the NHI counterintel frame at maximum.
