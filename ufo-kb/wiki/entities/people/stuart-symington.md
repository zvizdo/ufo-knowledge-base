# Stuart Symington

Stuart Symington (1901–1988) was an American politician who served as the first Secretary of the Air Force (1947–1950) and later as a US Senator from Missouri. In the UAP context he is documented as one of [[james-forrestal]]'s primary political enemies — and the last person known to have spoken to Forrestal in private before Forrestal's mental collapse accelerated.

## The Limo Ride

After Forrestal's farewell ceremony as Secretary of Defense, Symington was waiting in Forrestal's limousine — despite being his enemy. What passed between them remains unknown. When Forrestal emerged from the car, he entered his office, closed the door, stared at a blank wall for hours, and repeated only: **"You are a loyal fellow."** [[richard-dolan]] notes this as a pivotal moment in the Forrestal story; the limo encounter is the hinge between the Forrestal who resigned and the Forrestal who was hospitalized weeks later.

## Navy-Air Force Rivalry

As Secretary of the Air Force, Symington was a central actor in the institutional Navy-Air Force rivalry that [[richard-dolan]] identifies as a structural driver of early UFO disclosure politics. The Air Force sought to control UFO investigation, locking out Navy brass who had different institutional interests. See [[navy-ufo-rivalry]].
