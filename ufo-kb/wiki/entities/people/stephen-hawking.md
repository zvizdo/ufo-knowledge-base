# Stephen Hawking

Stephen Hawking (1942–2018) was a theoretical physicist and cosmologist at Cambridge University, best known for his work on black hole thermodynamics (Hawking radiation), the singularity theorems (with Roger Penrose), and his popular science book *A Brief History of Time*. He is widely regarded as the most famous scientist of the late twentieth century.
