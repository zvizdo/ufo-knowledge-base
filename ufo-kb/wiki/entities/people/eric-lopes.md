# Eric Lopes

Brazilian state military police officer stationed in Varginha, Minas Gerais; patrol partner of [[marcos-charez]]. Central witness in the second phase of the [[1996-varginha]] incident — the military capture of the entity approximately three hours after the three girls (Catia, Liliane, and Valquiria Xavier) first encountered it on a dirt road.

**Incident role**: On January 20, 1996, Lopes and Charez were patrolling when the being ran across their path. Charez physically captured the entity with his bare hands; Lopes was present and witnessed the capture but did not have direct physical contact with the being. The entity was subsequently taken to the Humanitas Hospital in Varginha and later transferred to a US Air Force facility, according to the account presented in [[UkKwa4jU0fc]] and documented by [[james-fox]].

**Health outcomes**: [[marcos-charez]], who handled the being directly, died several months after the incident under circumstances that his family believes were related to the contact. Charez reportedly developed unusual symptoms after the capture. Eric Lopes did not die and appears to have not suffered comparable health consequences — a differential outcome that, if accurate, suggests whatever biological or chemical effect caused Charez's illness was contact-specific (transmitted by physical handling) rather than proximity-based.

**Disappearance**: Since the incident, Lopes has been in hiding and has been inaccessible to researchers. His continued absence from public life decades after the event — in contrast to some other Varginha witnesses who eventually spoke — suggests either ongoing fear of official retaliation, instruction from authorities to remain silent, or personal unwillingness to enter the public record. The inaccessibility itself is cited as a data point in the [[1996-varginha]] case's credibility assessment.
