# Jaime Maussan

Mexican journalist turned UFO/UAP researcher, independently wealthy, based near Mexico City adjacent to a national park. Self-described bold explorer willing to present controversial evidence publicly and accept the reputational risk. Known in Western UFO circles as a polarizing figure: enthusiastic promoters call him a genuine courageous researcher; critics, including many US ufologists, label him a known hoaxer.

## Background

Became central to the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] investigation after meeting huaquero Leandro through explorer [[thierry-jamin]] circa 2016. Has hosted the mummies and associated scientific analyses at his Mexico City location. Invited credentialed researchers globally and gave open access to DICOM files, tomography passwords, and raw data.

## Notable Claims

- Asserts the bodies are definitively real organisms, not hoaxes.
- Filed a $300 million defamation lawsuit against the [[peruvian-ministry-of-culture]] for labeling him a hoaxer.
- Presented the [[bogota-sphere]] — aluminum object recovered in Colombia — claiming anomalous EM properties and super-hardness.
- States the beings have been visiting Earth for a long time.

## Credibility Assessment

Jesse Michaels describes him as "independently very wealthy," "boldly stated," "extremely generous," "not territorial," and genuinely wanting truth out — while noting he "speaks in absolutes" and "isn't always as nuanced" as Jesse would be. Critics note his previous presentations of material later proven fraudulent. The wooden-figurine / real-body conflation was not his error — that was Flavio Estrada's — but his past controversies created a credibility overhang.
