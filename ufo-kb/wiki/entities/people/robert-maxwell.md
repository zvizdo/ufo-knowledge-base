# Robert Maxwell

Czech-born British media mogul, Labour MP, and intelligence operative. He built a publishing empire centered on **Pergamon Press** — a major scientific journal publisher — before his mysterious death by drowning from his yacht *Lady Ghislaine* in 1991. His daughter is [[ghislaine-maxwell]].

## Triple Agent

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] states Maxwell was a **triple agent** working simultaneously for MI6 (British intelligence), KGB (Soviet intelligence), and Mossad (Israeli intelligence). This characterization is corroborated by multiple independent sources and is relatively well-established in the intelligence literature.

## Pergamon Press and Science Suppression

Pergamon Press was one of the world's major scientific publishers. Jorjani argues in [[EzPmG_7WhXc]] that Maxwell used Pergamon to **monopolize and suppress scientific discoveries** — controlling which papers were published, which were buried, and which were locked in proprietary databases. Jorjani specifically suggests this would have included **propulsion breakthroughs** and **electrogravidic research** — connecting Pergamon's gatekeeping role to the broader suppression of [[gravity-manipulation-propulsion]] research.

The name **Pergamon** is itself noted as significant: Pergamon was believed in antiquity to have been the site of Satan's throne (referenced in Revelation 2:13 — "where Satan's throne is"). Jorjani argues this name choice was not incidental.
