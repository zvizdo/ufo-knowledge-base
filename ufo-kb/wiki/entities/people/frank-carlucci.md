# Frank Carlucci

CIA Deputy Director under Jimmy Carter (1978–1980); National Security Advisor (1986–1987); Secretary of Defense under Reagan (1987–1989). A central figure in the post-Carter CIA purge network that Pines, following Jack Vallee's diaries, tracks as the spine of the private legacy-program continuation.

## ARV Palmdale Connection (Suspected)

Mark McCandish's contact [[brad-sorenson]] was taken to Palmdale by a "prominent Italian defense financier" who showed him three bell/acorn-shaped alien reproduction vehicles with visible wear and tear. Researcher contacts of [[matthew-pines]] believe this individual was Carlucci. Pines notes Carlucci fits the profile: Italian heritage, major defense-finance connections, CIA background, and Carlyle Group position. (Speculative — no direct confirmation.)

## Post-CIA Purge Network

When Carter purged the CIA's old guard in the late 1970s, Carlucci and others moved into private-sector structures. [[jacques-vallee]]'s diaries (Forbidden Science) repeatedly flag the Carlyle Group as the locus of this network. The Carlyle Group model — private equity with major defense interests — provides a plausible off-books funding and technology-housing vehicle for programs that could not survive in official government budgets.
