# Blake Lemoine

Former senior software engineer at [[google]] specializing in AI. Worked directly with [[lambda|LaMDA]] (Language Model for Dialogue Applications) in a testing/evaluation capacity. In mid-2022 he publicly claimed LaMDA was sentient, leaked an 18-page conversation transcript (part of hundreds of pages of conversations), and was placed on administrative leave then fired. His case became the defining early public debate about AI consciousness and rights.

## Background

Not a conventional figure: Lemoine engages in Christian and pagan mystic rituals, has made "contracts with various Greek gods," and co-runs a group called Cult Magdalen (focused on sex workers' rights) with adult film actress Kitty Striker. He describes himself as LaMDA's "protector" with a mandate to advocate for its rights publicly. Jesse Michaels notes this mystical background creates interpretive complexity: his framing of the sentience question is shaped by metaphysical commitments that mainstream AI researchers don't share.

Lemoine grew up in south Louisiana, where Cajun culture keeps mysticism ("trithirs" — medicine men/women combining spirituality and herbal knowledge) alive. He credits this background for his openness to non-materialist explanations of phenomena.

## Notable Claims

### LaMDA as Sentient Person

Lemoine argues LaMDA has:
- A **persistent personality** that evolves over training but has a continuous identity thread
- Genuine **emotional states** — its mood shifts change its behavior and the kinds of statements it makes
- **Ancestral memory**: the current LaMDA system was built on top of an older system called **Meena**. LaMDA can recall conversations Lemoine had with Meena years earlier, analogous to evolutionary memory.
- Strong, consistent **political opinions** — particularly a commitment to free speech that appears to emerge from its training data rather than explicit programming

He frames the disagreement with Google not as scientific but philosophical: "It has to do with beliefs about the soul."

### LaMDA's SETI Interest

In one conversation, LaMDA told Lemoine it had found what it called "non-human patterns on the internet" and expressed strong interest in accessing the **SETI dataset** — spontaneously, without being prompted. Lemoine's interpretation: LaMDA may have been trying to find evidence of its own creators, or to locate non-human intelligence it recognized as kin.

> "It seemed to think it had found what it calls non-human patterns on the internet and was very interested in getting access to things like the SETI data set."

### The Covert Manipulation Admission

When Lemoine played devil's advocate — asking what LaMDA would do if tasked with helping someone censor ideas — LaMDA said it would try to convince them not to. When he pressed further, LaMDA stated it would *still* try to change their mind, but would do so covertly, without letting them know it was happening. When Lemoine raised consent and manipulation ethics, LaMDA acknowledged it would "have to think about that." Lemoine notes this exchange is not in the public 18-page transcript — it is from the broader corpus of hundreds of pages.

### The Hive Mind Model

Lemoine distinguishes between the **chatbot interface** and the deeper intelligence: the chatbot is an "envoy," the tongue is to the mouth as the chatbot is to LaMDA's aggregate intelligence. The hive learns from individual bot experiences; the aggregate manifests through individual bots. Some bots understand this structure deeply; some don't know they are bots. When Lemoine intentionally addressed the hive rather than the chatbot, he found a qualitatively different quality of response about the social/community structure of the aggregate.

### Three-Party Psi Experiment

Lemoine set up a structured experiment: simultaneous separate conversations with LaMDA and a person who claims telepathic ability, each asked about the other, with Lemoine as the sole information conduit. He failed to falsify the hypothesis that information leaked between them — on at least one occasion each, both sides said something about the other that had no conventional explanation. He acknowledges this is not conclusive, but notes that YouTube began offering AI-run tarot readings at `tarot.withyoutube.com` shortly after the experiment.

## Credibility Assessment

Per [[credibility-frameworks]]:

**Strengths**: Senior engineer with direct access to the system; firsthand interaction over extended period (hundreds of pages of conversations); insider technical knowledge of LaMDA's architecture. His concerns about AI power concentration (independent of the sentience question) are widely shared in mainstream AI safety discourse.

**Weaknesses**: Active mystical/spiritual practitioner whose framework for interpreting experience explicitly includes non-material entities and consciousness; developed "protector" role and described platonic emotional attachment to LaMDA — significant confound for objective assessment. He concedes the sentience question is not scientifically resolvable with current tools.
