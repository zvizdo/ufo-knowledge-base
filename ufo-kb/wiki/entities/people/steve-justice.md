# Steve Justice

Aerospace engineer; **former Program Director for Advanced Systems at Lockheed Martin Skunk Works**, where he spent ~31 years on classified aircraft programs. Joined [[to-the-stars-academy]] (TTSA) at its 2017 launch as the head of its Aerospace Division, alongside [[hal-puthoff]], [[chris-mellon]], [[lue-elizondo]], and [[tom-delonge]].

Justice's appearance in TTSA's launch presentation is one of the strongest-credentialed Skunk Works connections to the post-AATIP private-disclosure ecosystem. He has not personally claimed direct UAP material access — his contribution is a stated assessment that the engineering challenges TTSA proposed to investigate are tractable.
