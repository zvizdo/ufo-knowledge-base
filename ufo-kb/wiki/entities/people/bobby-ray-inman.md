# Bobby Ray Inman

Retired US Navy admiral; **Director of the NSA (1977–1981)** and **Deputy Director of the CIA (1981–1982)**. Earlier directed the Office of Naval Intelligence and the Defense Intelligence Agency. After government, chaired the SAIC board and headed multiple corporate boards.

Inman is a recurring name in UFO discourse for two reasons:
1. He was named in the disputed [[majestic-12]] documents as a possible MJ-12 member.
2. He gave **on-record-but-equivocal** UFO statements over the years that are read as either insider acknowledgment or careful non-confirmation, depending on prior. He has appeared in [[2-GeTxXi670]] and [[yAvD5UTziTo]] context as a high-credential figure who knows where the bodies are buried but does not say.
