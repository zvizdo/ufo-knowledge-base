# Candy Jones

Candy Jones was a prominent WWII pin-up girl who entertained US troops in the Pacific theater. After returning to civilian life, she became the subject of one of the most detailed personal accounts of CIA Manchurian Candidate-style programming published.

## The Account

Per her biographer (the book *The Control of Candy Jones*):

- After the war, she was living a relatively quiet life
- A **military intelligence figure** living in the same building began interacting with her
- She ended up being **worked on by a psychiatrist** who created an alternate identity
- Her alternate identity ran **courier assignments**: documented destinations include the Philippines, and possibly Taiwan and other East Asian locations
- Her primary personality had no memory of these assignments

[[colin-ross]] presents her case as one of the clearest examples of an operational [[manchurian-candidate]] courier in the literature — because unlike many claimed cases, Candy Jones's account was detailed enough to be published as a biographical record rather than remaining in classified files.

## Significance

The Jones case bridges the theoretical framework ([[gh-esterbrooks]]'s "moon is clear" technique) and the operational reality. A woman with a split identity was running intelligence courier missions with complete amnesia in the normal personality — exactly what the Esterbrooks papers described as the goal.
