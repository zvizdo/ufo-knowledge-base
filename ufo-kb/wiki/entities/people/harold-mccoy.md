# Harold McCoy

Colonel, US Air Force; Deputy Commanding General for Intelligence (T-2), Air Materiel Command (AMC), Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Dayton, Ohio. McCoy was AMC's primary intelligence officer overseeing the flying disc collection and analysis effort in 1947–1948.

## Loedding Drawing and Horten Report (24 Sep 1947)

In response to a telephone conversation with Major General George McDonald (AC/AS-2), McCoy transmitted to USAF Washington:
1. A copy of the [[alfred-loedding|Loedding LD-2]] flying disc patent drawing
2. Royal Aircraft Establishment Technical Note AERO 1703 on [[horten-brothers|Horten tailless aircraft]]
3. T-2 report "German Flying Wings Designed by Horten Brothers" (No. F-SU-1110-ND)

McCoy's cover letter cited Soviet intelligence: a 9 June 1947 US Military Attaché Moscow report (V-32291-S) indicated 1,800 aircraft based on the Horten VIII design — jet-propelled, 131-ft wingspan, 33,000-lb gross weight — were being built for Soviet bomber squadron use.

## Fighter Alert Proposal (Feb 1948)

McCoy proposed maintaining a continuous fighter/night-interceptor alert at all USAF bases within CONUS to investigate and photograph disc sightings. The proposal was rejected by both the Director of Plans and Operations and the Air Intelligence Requirements Division as unfeasible — too costly, impossible without complete radar coverage, and impractical given that disc reports came primarily from civilian sources.
