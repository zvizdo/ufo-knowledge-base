# Augustus Wally

19th-century US Army Buffalo Soldier, recipient of the Medal of Honor. Mentioned in the [[7W7iOt57fOo]] Skinwalker Ranch episode as one speculative candidate for the origin of the [[as-above-so-below-engraving]] discovered on the ranch property.

**The engraving hypothesis**: The Hermetic inscription "as above so below" (from the Emerald Tablet, the foundational text of Western alchemy) was found engraved on the [[skinwalker-ranch]] property. Its origin is unknown. [[brandon-fugel]]'s team raises Wally as a possible carver because: the Buffalo Soldiers (Black cavalry units of the US Army) operated throughout the Utah/Southwest region in the 19th century; some Buffalo Soldier units were speculated to include Masonic-affiliated members; and Freemasonry incorporates Hermetic symbolism including the "as above so below" maxim. An individual with Masonic affiliations passing through the area could have left the inscription as a ritual or commemorative marking.

**Evidence status**: No direct evidence links Wally specifically to the [[skinwalker-ranch]] location or confirms Masonic affiliation. The hypothesis is speculative and relies on proximity (Buffalo Soldiers were in the region), background (some had Masonic ties), and symbolic consistency (the inscription fits Masonic iconography). The KB treats this as one of several interpretive possibilities for the engraving, not as established fact.

**Structural significance in the ranch context**: The engraving is notable because it was found in proximity to the Mesa — the area over which UAP activity concentrates in the Triangle and under which a buried manufactured metallic object has been detected. Whether the inscription ("what is above mirrors what is below") was placed by someone aware of these properties, or is coincidental, remains open.
