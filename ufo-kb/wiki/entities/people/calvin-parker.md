# Calvin Parker

Calvin Parker was 19 years old when he and [[charles-hixon]] encountered a dome-shaped craft near Pascagoula, Mississippi in October 1973. For many years, he was treated primarily as a secondary witness — the Pascagoula case was publicly associated mainly with Hixon. Parker subsequently "kind of disappeared for a while" and gave few interviews.

## Late Disclosure

Years later — apparently toward the end of his life — Parker re-emerged and independently confirmed that he too had been abducted during the incident, not merely present alongside Hixon. This late confirmation adds to the case's evidential weight: Parker had no obvious incentive to come forward after so many years, and his account was not shaped by decades of public Pascagoula lore.

## Hidden Recording Evidence

The police hidden recording captures Parker in evident distress — "freaking out," as Dolan describes it — which was a significant factor in convincing local law enforcement that both witnesses were sincere. The recording preserves a moment of private, unguarded reaction rather than a public performance.
