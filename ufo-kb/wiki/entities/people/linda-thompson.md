# Linda Thompson

Independent UAP researcher cited in multiple American Alchemy / Jesse Michaels episodes for identifying [[battelle-memorial-institute]] as an under-examined node in the UAP secrecy custody chain.

**Battelle / DOE thesis**: Thompson and "Bob" (a separate researcher she works with) argued that the [[atomic-energy-act-classification]] framework — which places nuclear-adjacent materials and research under Department of Energy control rather than DoD — makes Battelle Memorial Institute (one of the largest DOE contractors, managing multiple national labs) a likely custodian of UAP-related materials. The argument is that UAP reverse-engineering programs classified under the Atomic Energy Act would be held by DOE contractors, not military branches, which is why DoD oversight inquiries repeatedly hit walls. The [[2Xxmguz0GEQ]] transcript flags this thesis as under-examined and notes Thompson as a researcher whose work on the Battelle connection deserves deeper analysis.

**Peterson / Fitts claim**: [[richard-dolan]] separately cites Thompson for the claim that John Peterson ([[arlington-institute]]) allegedly offered to show [[catherine-austin-fitts]] "a UFO" if she would stop her financial investigations of the US government — implying Peterson had verifiable UAP access and was willing to weaponize it as a bargaining chip.

**KB position**: Thompson is a secondary source in the KB — her claims are cited through other researchers ([[richard-dolan]], [[jesse-michaels]]) rather than appearing directly. Her analysis of the Battelle/DOE custody chain is not independently confirmed in the KB but is treated as a live research hypothesis.
