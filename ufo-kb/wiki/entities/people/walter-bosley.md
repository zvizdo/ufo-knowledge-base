# Walter Bosley

## Background

Former counter-intelligence officer who has worked as an independent researcher into what he calls the **Nenza** phenomenon — a hypothesized secret society or breakaway technological group that may explain the 1890s American airship wave. The Nenza hypothesis is a variant of the [[secret-space-program]] / [[breakaway-civilization]] family of claims, proposing that human-engineered exotic craft predated the Wright Brothers by decades.

## Nenza Hypothesis

The 1896–97 American airship wave produced thousands of witness reports of structured, powered craft with lights across California, the Midwest, and Texas — occurring before documented human aviation. Bosley argues, alongside [[joseph-farrell]], that these craft were built by a secretive organization ("Nenza") leveraging Oliver Heaviside's electromagnetic gravity theory and possibly other heterodox physics available in the 1890s.

In the [[8TYMQOUDQBo]] interview, [[salvatore-pais]] invokes Bosley's work when speculating about whether "certain powerful families" who were "privy to Heaviside's insights" in the 1890s could have financed and built exotic craft. Pais says "who the [expletive] were these guys?" and treats the 1890s airship mystery as genuinely anomalous, though he notes keeping such technology secret for 130+ years would be "crazy."
