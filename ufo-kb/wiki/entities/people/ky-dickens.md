# Ky Dickens

Documentary filmmaker who directed *[[telepathy-tapes]]*, a feature-length documentary investigating controlled cases of non-verbal autistic children demonstrating apparent telepathic abilities. Has directed five feature films. Came to the subject as a skeptic, concerned about professional reputational risk ("woo-woo" territory), and moved toward conviction after witnessing controlled experiments firsthand.

## Background

Primarily a documentary filmmaker. Appears to have no prior background in neuroscience or consciousness research. The documentary was sparked by her encounter with [[diane-powell]]'s work — she heard Powell on a podcast and subsequently initiated collaboration and testing.

## Notable Claims

All claims relayed from subjects and Powell's research; Dickens' own contribution is methodological.

- Designed and ran controlled testing with primary subject Mia in a rented Glendale, California house: mirrors and TVs removed, cameras installed throughout, all test materials sourced and controlled by Dickens to prevent family from pre-loading cues.
- Mia achieved 100% accuracy on trials involving random number generators, random image generators, flash cards, and colored balls — with a blindfold and physical divider between mother and child.
- Documented the "Talk on the Hill" phenomenon through subject Houston: Houston accurately reproduced the content of a text message he had never seen, and correctly spelled out thoughts his wife placed in his head.
- [[john-mack]]'s trajectory — Harvard-trained psychiatrist who moved into anomalous research and faced institutional consequences — is explicitly cited by Dickens as a parallel to [[diane-powell]]'s situation.
