# Stephen Wolfram

British-American physicist and mathematician who founded Wolfram Research and created Mathematica (1988) and Wolfram Alpha. His major theoretical contribution, the **Wolfram Physics Project** (formalized in his 2020 book *A Project to Find the Fundamental Theory of Physics*), proposes that the universe is generated by the repeated application of a simple computational rule to a hypergraph — a structure whose nodes and edges represent the fabric of spacetime.

## Wolfram Physics and UAP Discourse

The Wolfram Physics framework appears in this KB at several junctions:

1. **[[bob-maguire]]** references Wolfram's hypergraph model as a possible mathematical language for describing how UAP propulsion might work — specifically, if UAP craft manipulate the underlying computational structure of spacetime rather than moving through it conventionally.

2. **[[donald-hoffman]]** and **[[john-wheeler]]** are cited alongside Wolfram in the context of "it from bit" / computational ontology — the idea that information processing (not matter) is the fundamental substrate of reality. This connects to [[consciousness-as-technology]] and the NHI-consciousness intersection.

3. **[[consciousness-non-local]]**: Wolfram's model implies that consciousness could be a specific pattern in the hypergraph that is not locally bound — potentially relevant to remote viewing and NHI telepathic contact claims.

## Relation to Mainstream Physics

Wolfram's project is controversial within mainstream physics — critics argue he has not demonstrated that his hypergraph rules can reproduce general relativity or the standard model quantitatively. This places him in a similar position to other heterodox physicists cited in this KB ([[freeman-dyson]]'s skepticism of string theory; [[jack-sarfatti]]'s post-quantum mechanics work).
