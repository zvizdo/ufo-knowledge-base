# Jimo Borjigin

Neuroscientist at the University of Michigan. Works at the intersection of two phenomena that [[jesse-michaels]] and [[andrew-gallimore]] suggest may not be coincidental:

1. **Endogenous DMT receptors and production in the brain** — including work on the sigma-1 receptor as a DMT binding site and related neurological processes
2. **Terminal lucidity** — the phenomenon where individuals who have been unconscious, comatose, or severely demented abruptly become lucid, communicative, and recognizably themselves in the hours before death, then die shortly after

## Terminal Lucidity

A medically documented but poorly understood phenomenon: people with severe Alzheimer's, or those who have been in prolonged comas, suddenly "wake up" with full cognitive function shortly before dying. Borjigin studies this alongside DMT research. Jesse Michaels floats the hypothesis that the two phenomena are related — perhaps a DMT surge during the dying process triggers both terminal lucidity and whatever experience follows death. Gallimore says this is plausible but notes DMT is likely only one component of a broader multi-neurotransmitter cascade during dying.
