# Rick Doty

Air Force Office of Special Investigations (OSI) Counter Intelligence officer active roughly 1978–1988. Best known for running a deliberate disinformation campaign against civilian UFO researcher [[paul-benowitz]], a case documented in *[[project-beta]]* by [[greg-bishop]]. His presence at the [[1977-ellsworth-woods-abduction]] debrief — as a brand-new agent — predates his known disinformation work by approximately a year and raises questions about how OSI handled CE3 nuclear-site cases.

## Background

Doty entered OSI around 1978 (possibly late 1977). By the time he appeared in public view, he was stationed at [[kirtland-afb]], New Mexico, where he encountered [[paul-benowitz]] — a researcher who had observed and filmed exotic craft near the base and was intercepting what he believed were alien signals on his computer.

Doty's operation: he convinced Benowitz that the signals were real alien communications and fed him an elaborate false narrative about underground alien bases, secret government cooperation with extraterrestrials, and human-NHI agreements. The disinformation gradually destroyed Benowitz's mental health and led to his psychiatric hospitalization. The operation is described by Jesse Michaels as paradigmatic of the intersection between genuine UAP phenomena and deliberate intelligence suppression.

## Presence at the 1977 Ellsworth Debrief

[[mario-woods]] first encountered Doty (then unknown to him) at the post-incident debrief at Ellsworth. Woods saw him on the TV show *Ancient Aliens* years later and recognized him immediately: "I know that guy — he was at my debrief." After approximately 5 years of unanswered emails, Doty confirmed his attendance and stated his role was to retrieve medical records for both Woods and his partner Michael Johnson.

The timing matters: Doty was, in Woods's words, "brand new" to OSI at the time. Jesse Michaels frames this as significant: Doty may not yet have become the disinformation operator he later was at Kirtland, but his presence suggests the Air Force had a standing protocol for routing CE3/abduction cases through OSI Counter Intelligence from the outset.

## Reform Claim

Doty has publicly stated he regrets his role in the Benowitz affair and apologizes for what he describes as "malpractice." Jesse Michaels notes: "a lot of people are suspicious of that reform" — his behavior of generating extremely speculative alien-race claims now may represent continued information flooding ("flooding the zone"). Woods is more measured, noting Doty responds to emails and that "those were the days" when he probably hadn't changed yet.
