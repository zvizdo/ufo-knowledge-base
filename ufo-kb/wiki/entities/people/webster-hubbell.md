# Webster Hubbell

Associate Attorney General of the United States under President Bill Clinton from 1993 until his resignation in 1994 amid financial scandal (he later pleaded guilty to tax evasion and mail fraud). Before his resignation, [[bill-clinton]] personally tasked Hubbell with two inquiries: find out what the government knew about UFOs, and find out who killed JFK.

In his memoir, Hubbell confirmed receiving this assignment and stated he found nothing — reporting that he was unable to get answers. The significance in the KB is not the negative result but the confirmation that a sitting president with every reason to have access could not obtain answers about UAP through normal executive-branch channels. This is a concrete data point in the UAP secrecy argument: if the Associate Attorney General acting on direct presidential instruction cannot access UAP files, the secrecy exists above the level of the Justice Department.

[[lawrence-rockefeller]] was simultaneously attempting to use his access to [[hillary-clinton]] to advance UAP disclosure through the [[1995-rockefeller-ufo-initiative]] — the Hubbell tasking is the internal-executive-branch parallel to the external-philanthropic pressure campaign.
