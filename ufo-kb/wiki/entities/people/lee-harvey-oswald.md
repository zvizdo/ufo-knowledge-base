# Lee Harvey Oswald

Lee Harvey Oswald was a former US Marine who defected to the Soviet Union in 1959, returned to the United States in 1962, and was charged with assassinating President John F. Kennedy in Dallas on November 22, 1963. He was killed two days later by Jack Ruby before he could be tried. The Warren Commission concluded Oswald acted alone; this conclusion has been contested by the House Select Committee on Assassinations (1979), which found evidence of a probable conspiracy, and by a large body of subsequent researchers.

Oswald's connections to UAP secrecy theory are indirect but structurally significant within [[danny-sheehan]]'s framework and related [[deep-state-continuity]] analyses. The argument runs as follows: Oswald had demonstrable contacts with figures in the CIA's covert operations world (notably through his Fair Play for Cuba Committee activities and his New Orleans network, which overlapped with assets connected to [[theodore-shackley]] and the anti-Castro operation infrastructure). If Kennedy was killed partly because he intended to share UFO information with the Soviets or dismantle the shadow state's UAP programs — a claim made by some researchers, citing a purported Kennedy-CIA memo — then Oswald's network is relevant to understanding the institutional actors who would have had motive. These connections remain speculative and contested; Oswald himself made no UAP-related statements.

## Additional Connections (per [[colin-ross]])

New details from the Ross interview ([[UrHLTFvdEZk]]):

- **Pierre Lafitte / Hydell alias**: [[pierre-lafitte]] — a man with ~20 documented aliases — used the alias "Hydell." Oswald's alias was **"Alex Hydell"** — identical last name. Both Lafitte and Oswald worked at the **Reilly Coffee Company** in New Orleans during the same period (1960–63). This is not a casual connection — it places Lafitte as likely Oswald's handler or a peer in the same intelligence network.
- **Oswald's mother's letter**: Before 1963, a document in the Bluebird archive describes Lee Harvey Oswald's mother contacting a CIA officer because she was concerned someone was **impersonating Oswald in Europe**. This indicates Oswald had a doppelganger or was being impersonated — consistent with a deep-cover intelligence management operation.
- **544 Camp Street / Guy Banister**: Oswald worked at 544 Camp Street in New Orleans; the building was owned by **Guy Banister**, an FBI investigator. A Marine defector to the Soviet Union, allowed back into the US without surveillance, working out of an FBI-connected office.
- **Ruth Paine / Allen Dulles**: Oswald's landlady **Ruth Paine** was close with **Mary Bancroft**, who was CIA Director **Allen Dulles**'s mistress. Dulles was later on the Warren Commission — investigating his own network.
- **Harold Byrd / Texas Book Depository**: The owner of the Texas School Book Depository was **Harold Byrd**, connected to the Byrd family (Richard Byrd of Operation High Jump) and to LBJ.
- **MK Ultra patient probability**: Per Ross, Oswald "very easily could have been" an MK Ultra patient — not proven, but the network circumstances make it realistic.
