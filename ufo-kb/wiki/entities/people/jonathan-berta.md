# Jonathan Berta

European technologist and entrepreneur; recently added to the [[soul-foundation]] board at time of this interview (May 2024). His aerial drone company was **contracted by France** to fly drones near French nuclear sites to observe and document UAP/UAP activity in the vicinity of those sites. This makes him an unusual figure — a drone operator with a government contract specifically to study UAP near nuclear installations, reinforcing the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] pattern.

## Significance

The French government contract is notable because it represents **state-level, institutionalized UAP monitoring near nuclear infrastructure** — not a fringe research effort but a formal procurement that required a government to acknowledge the phenomenon was worth observing. France has historically been more openly engaged with UAP research than the United States; the GEIPAN program (run through CNES, the French space agency) has publicly catalogued UAP reports for decades.

Berta's transition from commercial drone operator to SOL Foundation board member — an organization that includes figures such as [[tim-gallaudet]] (former NOAA head) and [[garry-nolan]] (Stanford professor) — suggests a bridging role between European drone-observation praxis and the US-centered UAP research network.
