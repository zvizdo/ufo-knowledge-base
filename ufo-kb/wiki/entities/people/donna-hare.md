# Donna Hare

NASA Johnson Space Center photographic specialist (held secret clearance; worked closely with launch photography and satellite imagery). Prior affiliation with the US Air Force. Deceased by the time of the [[2ttdlCa5ZCI]] interview.

## Disclosure

Hare was a witness in [[steven-greer]]'s Disclosure Project. Her account: while working in Building 8 of [[johnson-space-center]], a colleague across the corridor called her over and showed her satellite photography contact sheets being developed under red light. One image showed a large white circular disc above pine trees, with a round shadow on the ground below it at the correct angle for the sun position — ruling out photographic artifact (which would not cast a directional shadow). When she pressed the colleague, he confirmed the object could not be explained as a film defect, and that it was their job to airbrush such objects out before the imagery was sold commercially to universities, colleges, and the public.

Her colleague's response to "what are you going to do with this?" — "we always have to airbrush them out before we sell them to the public" — implies a systematic, ongoing practice rather than a one-off event.

## McKinnon Corroboration

[[gary-mckinnon]] read Hare's testimony in the Disclosure Project materials and used it to target Building 8 specifically. He independently accessed Building 8 machines (identified by Windows network audit commands) and observed a satellite image loading showing a smooth cigar-shaped craft in lateral orbit above Earth — matching the class of imagery Hare described. The corroboration is not of the same image (different shapes: she described a disc; he saw a cigar) but of the same institutional practice at the same facility.

McKinnon had email correspondence and a ~3-hour phone call with Hare. She told him that the airbrushing was done for reasons of control — and speculated that even those with highest authority may not fully understand what they are suppressing.
