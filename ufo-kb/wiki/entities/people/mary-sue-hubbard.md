# Mary Sue Hubbard

Wife of [[l-ron-hubbard]] and a senior figure in the [[church-of-scientology]]. When the Church sought legal representation after FBI raids, they offered [[danny-sheehan]] the role of personal attorney to both L. Ron Hubbard and his wife. Sheehan refers to her as "Susan" in the transcript — almost certainly an error for Mary Sue Hubbard.

Sheehan accepted the condition of accessing every Scientology office's significant documents before deciding whether to take the case. It was during this access period that he encountered the intelligence files documenting [[ingo-swann]]'s espionage role and the [[pat-price]] remote-viewing experiments.
