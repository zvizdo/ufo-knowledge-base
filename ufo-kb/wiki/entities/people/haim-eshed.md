# Haim Eshed

Retired Israeli general who served as the head of Israel's Defense Ministry Space Directorate from 1981 to 2010 — a 29-year tenure that oversaw the development of Israel's military satellite program, including the Ofek reconnaissance satellite series. He holds a PhD and is a recipient of three Israeli Security Prizes.

## Background

Eshed's professional career was spent entirely within the Israeli defense establishment. His tenure at the Space Directorate gave him oversight of classified military space programs and, presumably, liaison relationships with allied space intelligence programs — particularly the US. His scientific and administrative credentials are uncontested and were never in dispute before his 2020 statements.

He wrote a book titled **"The Universe Beyond the Horizon"** (Hebrew: *היקום מעבר לאופק*), published in 2020 with Israeli academic publisher Yedioth Sefarim, in which he elaborated on his claims.

## Notable Claims

### December 2020 Interview

In December 2020, Eshed gave an extensive interview to Israeli newspaper **Yediot Aharonot** (and the associated English-language outlet Ynet). The interview was translated and circulated globally. His principal claims:

1. **Extraterrestrials exist** and have been in ongoing contact with the governments of the United States and Israel.
2. A **"Galactic Federation"** of alien species exists — a multi-civilization body with which contact has been established.
3. A **joint US-alien underground base exists on Mars**, where American astronauts and alien representatives cooperate.
4. **Donald Trump was aware** of the extraterrestrial contact and was asked by the Federation not to disclose it yet, on the grounds that humanity was not ready and that disclosure would cause panic.
5. The secrecy is maintained by mutual agreement — both human governments and the Galactic Federation want to prevent premature revelation.

Eshed stated he was disclosing this now because "the UFO subject has gained lots of interest in the world" and that he felt the time was approaching when more could be said openly.

### Israeli Official Response

Israeli officials and government spokespeople did not publicly denounce or dismiss Eshed's statements in the way they might be expected to if a retired general made equivalent claims about nuclear programs or intelligence operations. The absence of an official rebuttal was noted by UAP researchers. However, no Israeli official publicly confirmed his claims either.

## Analytical Framing

Eshed's disclosure sits in an unusual category. Unlike most UAP claimants, he has verifiable, senior-level institutional credentials and a decades-long career that conferred genuine access to classified space programs. His claims are nevertheless extreme in content — a named intergovernmental federation, a Mars base, named world leaders involved. These specifics go substantially beyond what any other named official has claimed publicly and have no corroborating independent evidence.

Within [[disclosure-narratives]], Eshed represents the "high-credentialed, extreme-claim" cluster — a pattern where the credibility of the source is used to provide cover for claims that would otherwise be dismissed. This is analyzed in [[credibility-frameworks]].

His Mars base claim is contradicted by the absence of any physical evidence and by the capabilities of known US space programs. His Galactic Federation framing resembles contactee traditions rather than the intelligence-community framing used by, e.g., [[david-grusch]] or [[lue-elizondo]].
