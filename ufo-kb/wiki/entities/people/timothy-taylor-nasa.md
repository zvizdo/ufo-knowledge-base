# Timothy Taylor

NASA mission controller. Known primarily through his interactions with researcher [[diana-pasulka]].

## Anomalous Hierarchy Claim

Taylor told Pulka that his operational hierarchy has four tiers:
1. Normal humans
2. Elements of the intelligence services (a separately designated ontological tier)
3. Aliens
4. God

[[matthew-pines]] identifies two readings of the middle tier:
- **Ego interpretation**: intelligence officials who think they have a specially elevated status (common in the community)
- **NHI-embedded interpretation**: the middle tier refers to non-human entities operating within human bureaucracies

## Other Claims

- Claims to be part of a secret space program whose superiors don't even know he's enrolled
- Receives "downloads" about what to do (following higher, non-standard marching orders)
- Allegedly showed [[diana-pasulka]] multiple three-letter-agency credentials despite being a standard NASA mission controller — but back-channel intel sources told Pines he is just a normal mission controller

## Hermetic Network Pattern

Pines observes a pattern: Taylor shows up in Pulka's life when she transitions from Catholic purgatory research to UAP/UFO encounters; [[chris-bledsoe]] also shows up when UFOs begin visiting near his house. The pattern suggests either a real hermetic intelligence network directing contacts, or a susceptibility to UFO cult belief structures ("my leader is a hybrid sent to guide the flock").
