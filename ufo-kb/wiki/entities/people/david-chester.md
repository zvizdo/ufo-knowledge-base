# David Chester

Theoretical physicist with undergraduate training at MIT and a PhD from UCLA, both in physics. Chester's primary specializations are quantum field theory (QED), general relativity, and their intersection. He is a personal friend of [[jesse-michaels]] and participates in discussions on APE (the exotic propulsion podcast hosted by Tim Ventura), where he has had ongoing communication with [[andrew-arjima]] of [[exodus-propulsion-technologies]].

## Role in Exodus Propulsion Evaluation

Chester was invited by Michaels to join the [[mOWwdIuyaQA]] American Alchemy episode as an independent stress-tester for [[charles-buhler]]'s quantum-electrodynamics theory of the [[exodus-force]]. His role: assess whether Buhler's third-order QED perturbation theory framework is physically coherent.

Chester's assessment (from [[mOWwdIuyaQA]]):

**On the experimental results**: Highly encouraging. Chester praised the iteration rate and systematic controls. He stated he **cannot think of a prosaic explanation** for the results, particularly given the vacuum tests and power-off thrust persistence.

**On the QED theory**: More cautious. Specific objections raised:
1. Momentum is conserved in QED via Noether's theorem; claiming a net momentum kick in the center-of-mass frame requires something carrying the missing momentum — which Buhler has not yet identified.
2. The "scalar virtual photon" that Buhler invokes as the asymmetric momentum carrier is a mathematical construct inside Feynman diagram internal lines; it cannot be "radiated" as an external line. The distinction between virtual (internal) and real (external) particles in Feynman diagrams is fundamental.
3. Buhler's invocation of **time-independent perturbation theory** (for DC electrostatics) differs from the standard QFT perturbation theory (loop-counting in powers of ℏ) Chester had assumed — Chester acknowledged this is a potentially valid simplification given DC conditions, and suspended judgment pending more review.
4. Renormalization and self-energy terms are likely important and under-explored in Buhler's current theoretical sketch.

Chester's constructive suggestion: Buhler should track **both** time-independent and quantum-loop corrections simultaneously, examine whether the Dirac spinor equations of motion at classical level (which still contain ℏ and α) can account for the force, and look into whether the fine structure constant α appearing in experimental scaling is consistent with a loop-level QED correction.

Chester noted the neutrino analogy: the neutrino was discovered precisely because momentum bookkeeping in beta decay didn't add up — an apparent violation that pointed to something new. If Exodus's results are real, they imply an additional carrier of momentum not yet identified.

## Falcon Space Assessment

Chester assessed the Falcon Space vacuum Biefeld-Brown spinner experiment as "suggestive but not conclusive." Interesting observations: the spinner reversed direction at lower pressure (consistent with ion wind dominating at higher pressure, a different mechanism at low pressure). Problems: magnetic levitation supports introduce error; wire coil geometry was potentially responsible for the observed behavior; field emission under high vacuum is a concern Buhler also raised. More iterations with better shielding needed before a definitive conclusion.
