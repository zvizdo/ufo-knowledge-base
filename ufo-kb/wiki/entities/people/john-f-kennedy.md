# John F. Kennedy

35th President of the United States (January 1961 – November 22, 1963). US Navy veteran and Senator from Massachusetts. Assassinated in Dallas, Texas.

**UAP-related documents**: The [[jfk-mccone-letter]] is an alleged memorandum from Kennedy to CIA Director John McCone requesting data on "unknowns" to coordinate with the Soviet Union and prevent misinterpretation of unknown objects as US incursions. The letter's authenticity is contested; if genuine, it would indicate Kennedy was aware of UAP as a real phenomenon at the presidential level.

**Assassination and UAP connection**: [[jfk-assassination-ufo]] is the KB's primary concept page for claims that Kennedy's assassination was connected to UAP program secrecy — specifically theories involving CIA programs, [[allen-dulles]] (whom Kennedy fired after the Bay of Pigs, then saw sit on the Warren Commission), and the possibility that Kennedy sought disclosure of UAP programs. Peter Levenda's network reconstruction, Harald Malmgren's claims, and Greer's GAPS account all converge on this thesis.

**JKARA precedent**: The [[uap-disclosure-act]] (UAPDA, 2023) was explicitly modeled on the JFK Assassination Records Review Board and the JFK Records Collection Act of 1992 — both created after Kennedy's assassination to force declassification of withheld government records. The model used for UAP disclosure is thus directly derived from Kennedy's assassination's aftermath.
