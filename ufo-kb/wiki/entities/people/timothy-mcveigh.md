# Timothy McVeigh

Timothy McVeigh (1968–2001) was a US Army veteran convicted of detonating a truck bomb outside the Alfred P. Murrah Federal Building in Oklahoma City on April 19, 1995, killing 168 people — the deadliest domestic terrorist attack in US history prior to 9/11. He was executed by lethal injection in June 2001.
