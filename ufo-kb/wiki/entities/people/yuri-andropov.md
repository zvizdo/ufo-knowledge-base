# Yuri Andropov

Chairman of the KGB (1967–1982) and General Secretary of the Soviet Union (1982–1984). Architect of the Soviet intelligence apparatus during the Cold War. Close personal friend of [[mikhail-gorbachev]], through whom his account of the Nixon-Brezhnev UFO encounter reached [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]].

## UAP Role

Andropov was present at the first major private meeting between [[leonid-brezhnev]] and President [[richard-nixon]]. After the formal session concluded and only a small group remained, Nixon opened a small folder and showed its contents to Brezhnev. Andropov was invited to this private session in his capacity as KGB chairman. The contents of the folder were not specified in Ilyumzhinov's account.

The immediate aftermath: **all US and Soviet funding for lunar exploration was completely halted.** Both sides simultaneously abandoned advanced Moon missions that were already in preparation. No official explanation was ever given that satisfied researchers.

Andropov relayed this account to Gorbachev. Gorbachev later relayed it to Ilyumzhinov. The chain is: Andropov (eyewitness) → Gorbachev → Ilyumzhinov.

The KGB under Andropov also ran a special department dedicated to researching unidentified flying and underwater objects. Andropov's tenure (1967–1982) overlaps with the period of most intensive Soviet UFO/USO documentation — including the Black Sea sightings [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]] witnessed in 1981.
