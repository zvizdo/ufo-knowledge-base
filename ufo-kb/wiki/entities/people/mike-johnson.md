# Mike Johnson

US Representative from Louisiana (R-LA-4); 56th Speaker of the House (elected October 2023). Johnson was previously Chair of the House Republican Conference. As Speaker, he controls the House legislative agenda and is the highest-ranking House Republican.

**KB connection — UAP Disclosure Act gutting**: In the [[controlled-disclosure]] concept, Johnson is cited as the congressional endpoint through which Lockheed Martin lobbying (via [[mike-turner]], House Intelligence Committee Chair) succeeded in stripping the most consequential provisions from the [[uap-disclosure-act]]. Specifically:

- **Eminent domain provision**: Would have required private contractors holding alleged retrieved UAP materials to relinquish them to the government. This provision was the Act's primary mechanism for compelling disclosure from the private sector — without it, contractors with alleged UAP materials face no legal obligation to turn them over.
- **Independent nine-person review panel**: Would have provided an independent oversight body to evaluate UAP material claims outside the normal classification structure. Its removal left UAP oversight within existing (and conflicted) intelligence oversight frameworks.

**Role of Speaker's office**: As Speaker, Johnson's office controls which bills receive floor votes and in what form. The stripping of these provisions before final passage required the Speaker's acquiescence or active management. The KB documents this as a case study in how defense contractor lobbying operates through congressional leadership — not through direct bribery but through controlling what reaches the floor.

**Significance**: The removal of these two provisions transformed the UAP Disclosure Act from potential compelled-disclosure legislation into a transparency-adjacent gesture. The KB treats this as the primary legislative example of [[controlled-disclosure]] — how disclosure can be simultaneously advanced (the law passed) and prevented (the enforcement mechanisms were removed).
