# Carla Turner (deceased)

Abduction researcher who was herself an abductee, along with her family. A student of [[bud-hopkins]] from afar and a collaborator with abduction researcher Barbara Bartholic. Died of aggressive cancer; Hopkins also reportedly died of cancer; [[john-mack]] was hit by a car. Alberino notes the pattern of abduction researchers dying under unusual circumstances.

## Key Contributions

### MILABS Documentation
Turner was the first to systematically document **military abductions** (MILABS) — incidents in which abductees, after being taken by grey aliens, were subsequently abducted by elements of the US military:

- Military personnel would arrive at abductees' homes after grey alien abductions
- Abductees were taken to Air Force bases or underground facilities and interrogated about what the grays were doing
- The military's goal was apparently to extract intelligence about the grey alien abduction program
- Turner's own husband was reportedly taken by military personnel

This suggests elements of the US military were aware of the abduction program and were attempting to spy on the grays through their human subjects.

### Virtual Reality Scenarios (VRS)
Turner emphasised that grey aliens possess technology capable of generating completely convincing virtual reality environments — a fundamental epistemological warning for abduction research:

- The entire perceived environment during an abduction can be fabricated by the grays
- Other people present may not see the same thing as the abductee
- The grays can also implant false/screen memories to conceal the real events
- Conclusion: **nothing the grays show or tell an abductee can be trusted**; even the abductee's own memories may be screens

### The Deception Baseline
Turner's foundational principle: the abduction program's starting point is **deception**. The grays do not explain what they are doing. They use VRS, screen memories, and apparently benevolent scenarios to manipulate abductees. [[tim-alberino]] calls this "a very healthy starting point" and fully endorses it.

### Core Physical Facts (what can be established despite deception)
- Abductees are taken physically against their will
- Sperm and ova are extracted
- Women are implanted with and carry hybrid foetuses for ~3 months before foetal extraction to gestation tanks
- The program is covert and the beings never explain it
