# Federico Faggin

Italian-American physicist and entrepreneur; widely recognized as the father of the integrated circuit and architect of the Intel 4004 microprocessor. Founded or co-founded multiple publicly traded Silicon Valley companies.

## Integrated Circuit and Out-of-Body Experience

For decades, Faggin was celebrated solely for his technical accomplishments. What he did not disclose publicly for years was that he had seen the integrated circuit design **outside of his body** in a vision:

> "He had seen the design outside of his body one night — and had had multiple experiences outside of his body."

This is reported by [[jacques-vallee]], who knows Faggin personally as "one of my mentors in venture capital and high tech." Faggin has since become publicly active on consciousness research, writing books on the relationship between consciousness and the physical world.

## Significance

Faggin's case is notable as evidence that spontaneous out-of-body and visionary states occur among mainstream scientists and engineers — not only among self-described psychics or experiencers. Vallée cites him alongside General Patton's battlefield intuition and Ingo Swann's remote viewing as evidence of a spectrum of anomalous cognitive faculty.
