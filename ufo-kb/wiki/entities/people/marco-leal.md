# Marco Leal

Brazilian UFO researcher based in the Varginha region. Co-producer of [[james-fox]]'s "Moment of Contact" documentary series. Leal spent years tracking down and winning the trust of the key Varginha witnesses, most of whom had been silent for 25+ years due to ridicule and intimidation.

## Role in the Varginha Investigation

Leal was instrumental in locating [[carlos-de-souza]] and persuading him to testify. When he called de Souza (who had changed his number and moved house to erase his trail), de Souza agreed partly because of the humiliation at a family birthday party the week before, and partly because Leal's measured approach inspired trust.

Leal verified de Souza's consistency: his 2021–2022 recorded account was "identical, not a single detail changed" from his 1996 account to researcher Claudia Kovu — a powerful credibility indicator.

Leal also brought [[italo-venturelli]] and other hospital witnesses toward disclosure over the years. He connected [[james-fox]] with the Varginha witness network when Fox came to Brazil, hosting Fox (de Souza's brother, who lives in the US, also participated in the editing process).

He also located the military mechanic who worked on the army trucks from the Varginha recovery convoy — who confirmed the trucks required extensive suspension repairs at the time, consistent with racing down pothole dirt roads at speed to reach the crash site.
