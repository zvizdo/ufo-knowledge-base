# Michael Turner

Republican congressman who served as chairman of the **House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence** until January 2025, when he was replaced by [[brad-wenstrup]].

[[danny-sheehan]] identifies Turner as the primary obstacle to House passage of the full [[controlled-disclosure-campaign-act]]. Turner reportedly talked Speaker [[mike-johnson]] out of allowing a floor vote on the CDCA, despite the bill passing the Senate 94-6. Sheehan connects Turner's obstruction to his district: Ohio's 10th Congressional District encompasses [[wright-patterson-air-force-base]], giving Turner personal political incentive to block disclosure legislation that might expose programs associated with that facility.
