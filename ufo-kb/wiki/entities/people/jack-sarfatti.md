# Jack Sarfatti

## Background

Jack Sarfatti is a theoretical physicist with a Cornell PhD who has spent approximately 50 years working on a low-energy warp drive theory — motivated from the outset, he claims, by a firsthand NHI or alien contact experience. He was part of the California counterculture physics scene in the 1970s — a group Bob Maguire describes as "the hippies that saved physics."

His career is decidedly non-traditional: not embedded in mainstream academia, his work has been published but he exists at the margin of professional physics. He is nonetheless regarded by [[bob-maguire]] as "capable and erasable" (hard to understand but demonstrably intelligent and substantive).

## Low-Energy Warp Drive Theory

Sarfatti's thesis differs fundamentally from the [[gravity-manipulation-propulsion]] mainstream:
- **Alcubierre's warp drive** requires enormous negative energy — far beyond any achievable technology.
- Sarfatti's approach: warp drive is achievable with **low energy** via a **scalar field modification** to Einstein's field equations — an additional field term that changes the geometry of the solution space.
- Bob Maguire describes this as Sarfatti's consistent insistence over 50 years: "there is a low-power version of manipulating the stress-energy tensor that will give you the necessary things."

The specific mechanism involves manipulating the **speed of light locally** — making it much slower in a localized region — which makes the energy threshold much more attainable. This is the "governor removal" Bob refers to: taking Einstein's equations and allowing the scalar field to modify them in ways that open up solution space previously assumed inaccessible.

## The Hippie Physics Connection

In 1970s California, Sarfatti was part of a circle of young physicists (including Fritjof Capra and others) who engaged seriously with quantum mechanics, consciousness, and alternative physical frameworks — work that mainstream physics largely ignored but that produced ideas now being reconsidered. Bob views this group as having preserved important theoretical threads during the period when the glass wall (see [[physics-lockdown]]) was being erected.

## Private Email Exchange

[[bob-maguire]] participates in Sarfatti's private email list, where Sarfatti shares and discusses daily work. Bob characterizes the exchange as technically dense and productive, though Sarfatti is difficult to follow. Their shared interest is in the low-energy warp drive path.

## Contrast with Pais

Bob frames the UAP propulsion landscape as having two poles:
- **Pais** — high-energy, quantum vacuum manipulation, Schwinger QED formalism, Navy funding
- **Sarfatti** — low-energy, scalar field modification, scalar-wave adjacent, no institutional backing

Both are trying to manipulate the stress-energy tensor; they differ on the energy regime and the mathematical path. The existence of both approaches (and their convergence on the same physical object) is itself suggestive that there is something real to find.

## Snob Problem

Jesse Michaels notes Sarfatti won't engage with him directly ("you're not a physicist"). Bob: "He's a snob. I'm not trying to — Jack is a hard-nosed scientific snob." But Bob takes his physics seriously regardless.

## Connection to Andrija Puharich

Sarfatti visited [[andrija-puharich]]'s Ossining, NY compound. He references a figure named "Wilson Green" who worked in Army psychological warfare — and Wilson Green appears in Puharich's 1953 DC journals. Sarfatti also claims to have received a call from a "metallic voice" as a teenager — identical to the phenomenon [[uri-geller]] and others report in connection with the Nine entities. Whether this metallic voice was the same phenomenon or a synthetic transmission via Puharich's thought-transmission technology (TD-100) is unknown.
