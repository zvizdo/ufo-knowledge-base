# Ruth Payne

Ruth Payne (Ruth Hyde Paine) was a Quaker woman living in Texas who was the daughter-in-law of [[arthur-young]] — the Bell helicopter co-inventor who attended the 1952 Nine seance with [[andrija-puharich]]. She housed Lee Harvey Oswald, his wife Marina, and their children in the period before the Kennedy assassination, and obtained Oswald his job at the Texas School Book Depository.

## The Warren Commission Cutoff

When Ruth Payne testified before the Warren Commission, [[allen-dulles]] — CIA director fired by JFK, now sitting as a Warren Commission member — personally cut off her testimony at the moment she was about to describe her recent visit to Arthur Young's household.

[[peter-levenda]] found this in the Warren Commission transcripts: the interruption is documented. Whether it reflects Dulles's deliberate suppression of the Young/seance/Nine connection or a coincidence of questioning sequence is a matter of interpretation — but the structural fact (Dulles cutting off testimony about Young) is from the public record.
