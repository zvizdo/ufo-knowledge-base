# Pierre Lafitte

## The Oswald Connection

Both Lafitte (as Hydell) and Oswald worked at the **Reilly Coffee Company** in New Orleans during the **same period, 1960–1963**. This is not a tenuous connection — it is a documented shared workplace with a documented shared alias.

The shared alias may suggest:
- Lafitte and Oswald were managed by the same intelligence handler who assigned them a common cover identity
- Or Oswald adopted the "Hydell" alias from Lafitte (making Lafitte a handler or mentor figure in the network)

The window's physical dimensions (too small to jump through, per Eric Olson's investigation) confirm Olson was pushed rather than jumped.
