# Martin Fleischmann

British electrochemist and Fellow of the Royal Society. One of the most distinguished electrochemists of his generation before the cold fusion controversy. Co-announced low-energy nuclear reactions (cold fusion) with [[stanley-pons]] at University of Utah in March 1989.

## Career Before Cold Fusion

Fleischmann was a highly respected figure in electrochemistry at the University of Southampton. His reputation lent initial credibility to the cold fusion announcement that Pons alone could not have commanded.

## Post-1989 Research

After mainstream rejection, Fleischmann and Pons continued work at IMRA (Institut Minoru de Recherche Avancée) in Nice, France, funded by Toyota. Fleischmann maintained that the excess-heat effect was real until his death in August 2012.

## Role in Suppressed-Science Narrative

[[giuliano-preparata]] and [[emilio-del-giudice]] developed QFT models attempting to provide theoretical grounding for the anomalous effect Fleischmann observed. Within the [[free-energy-suppression]] narrative, Fleischmann's case is particularly potent because of his prior mainstream stature.
