# Bob Jameson

USAF targeting officer at [[malmstrom-air-force-base]] who was called in from home late on the night of March 24, 1967 to assist with restarts of the Oscar Flight Minuteman missiles. He independently confirms the mass shutdown ("all 10 missiles were off alert — all red") and provides a unique second-hand account: he was brought to the wing command post that night where personnel were in radio contact with observers at [[belt-montana]], watching a UFO apparently trapped in a canyon.

## Background

Jameson found out about the shutdown before arriving at the base — a colleague in the hangar told him about both the shutdown and the UFO report over Belt, Montana. He was then sent to the colonel's briefing room (command post) where he observed the unfolding radio communications about the canyon UFO. He was assigned four missiles to restart; all restarted smoothly. He received a briefing that he "wasn't supposed to talk about" but is now willing to do so.

## Notable Claims

- At the command post: "I was looking at the UFO down at the bottom of the canyon. The canyon is too steep to send men down in the night."
- At daylight, helicopters were sent down; "this UFO just went right through the helicopters and out."
- Confirms the entire flight being off alert was unprecedented: "it doesn't happen."
