# Clint Murchison Jr.

Texas oilman (1923–1987) and founding owner of the Dallas Cowboys NFL franchise, which he established in 1960. Son of Clint Murchison Sr., one of the wealthiest Texas oil families of the mid-20th century with deep ties to the Democratic Party establishment despite their conservative politics. The Murchison family's Dallas mansion was the alleged site of a pre-assassination meeting the night before Kennedy was killed.

**The Murchison mansion meeting**: In JFK conspiracy literature — most prominently in accounts from [[madeleine-brown]], a woman who claimed to have been a long-term mistress of Lyndon Johnson — a gathering occurred at the Murchison mansion in Dallas on the evening of November 21, 1963. The meeting allegedly included [[j-edgar-hoover]], [[richard-nixon]], [[h-l-hunt]], Lyndon Johnson, and others from the Texas oil-political establishment. Brown claims Johnson arrived late and told her: "After tomorrow those goddamn Kennedys will never embarrass me again." This account has not been independently corroborated.

**Clint Murchison Jr. vs. Sr.**: The mansion meeting is attributed to Clint Murchison Sr.'s home. Murchison Jr. (the Dallas Cowboys owner) is a separate figure. The KB pages for both are grounded in the son's name because that is the form appearing in many sources; the senior/junior distinction is noted here to prevent confusion.

**Texas oil establishment context**: The Murchison family represents the Texas oil establishment's political and financial power in mid-century America — the same network as [[h-l-hunt]], whose oil money funded far-right political operations and who is named in multiple JFK-conspiracy accounts. This network is relevant to the KB's [[jfk-assassination-ufo]] synthesis because the same Texas oil interests had financial stakes in military-industrial contracts that JFK's policies threatened.

**KB significance**: The Murchison family is a named node in JFK conspiracy accounts; Murchison Jr. himself was primarily a sports businessman. The page anchors family-name and Dallas-establishment wikilinks in the [[jfk-assassination-ufo]] and [[cia-mob-jfk-uap-same-infrastructure]] syntheses.
