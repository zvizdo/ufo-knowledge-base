# Lyn Buchanan

Army remote viewer associated with the [[stargate-program]] at Fort Meade. Afterwards offered remote viewing training to civilians and wrote on the subject. He is notable for claiming that the public Stargate program was a vestibule concealing a deeper, darker black program.

## Claimed Recruitment into Black PK Program

Buchanan told [[jason-reza-jorjani]] (in Jorjani's living room, per Jorjani's account in [[EzPmG_7WhXc]]) that people from a deeper black unit — with no identified name — attempted to recruit him. This unit was using **psychokinesis against human targets**, not just remote viewing. [[pat-price]] is named by Jorjani as someone who may have been successfully recruited into this program and subsequently killed over moral objections.

Micheal Jorjani does not reveal Buchanan's name as the source in the previous interview but does so in this episode, noting Buchanan is now old enough that it doesn't pose a risk.

## Testimony on UFO Pilot Training

In Jorjani's account, Buchanan was taken — blindfolded by helicopter — to a facility accessed through a roadside shack whose floor was an elevator descending hundreds of feet through solid rock. Inside, researchers had reconstructed a **UFO/aircraft collision** FAA-style, laying out wreckage from both craft. The purpose: understanding how the UFO's guidance system worked (Buchanan was asked to remote view the pilots' interaction with it).

Key revelation from the facility staff: **US pilots were being trained to fly UFOs** (recovered or reverse-engineered). The primary challenge was **time displacement** — the craft warped spacetime during flight, disorienting pilots. Mathematical models for predicting time displacement from the electrogravitic drive were being taught to pilots.

## Significance

This testimony is Jorjani's primary personal firsthand source for the [[time-travel-uap]] thesis — specifically that US black programs are already aware that electrogravitic propulsion produces time distortion and are training to manage it.

The description of the roadside-shack/deep-elevator entry point parallels accounts of compartmented black-site architecture at [[area-51]] and S4.
