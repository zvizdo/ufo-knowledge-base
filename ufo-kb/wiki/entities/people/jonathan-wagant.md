# Jonathan Wagant

UFO whistleblower documented by researcher UAP Gerbant. Claims to have participated in a crash-retrieval operation in Peru, where his team located a large craft embedded in a cliff face and he observed a four-fingered arm extending from an open hatch.

## Background

In **1997**, Wagant was a USMC Lance Corporal deployed on [[operation-laser-strike]], a US counter-narcotics operation shooting down narco planes over Peru. His 8–10 Marine team was told there was a "downed friendly or foreign plane" and diverted to investigate.

After a combination of driving and trekking through jungle terrain, they reached a clearing at a cliff face. What they found was not a plane:
- An enormous **egg-shaped / teardrop-shaped craft** embedded at ~45° in the cliff face
- **Extensive ballistic damage** on the rear — Wagant believes consistent with a **Peruvian Hawkman-23 missile** strike
- A swirling **oil-on-water / mother-of-pearl electromagnetic field** around the craft
- A **purplish-green ring of light** along the craft's circumference, slowing down
- A **four-fingered arm** hanging from an open hatch, dripping purplish-green syrup-like liquid

Wagant felt a being communicating telepathically, saying *"they were not going to harm me... everything is going to be all right. Just help us get out."*

**Post-encounter**: All three Marines were intercepted by **8–12 operators in all-black, no insignia, no name tags**, held at gunpoint. They were then given what they were told were **anthrax vaccine boosters** — which [[uap-gerb]] identifies as a standard crash-retrieval silencing mechanism across multiple independent cases. Wagant's fellow Marines, Sgt. Atkins and Sgt. Allen, subsequently "threw him under the bus."

## Credibility Assessment

[[uap-gerb]] describes Wagant as "one of the greatest guys I know" — suggesting direct personal contact and high subjective trust. The anthrax-booster silencing mechanism is a cross-case pattern UAPGerb finds across multiple independent crash-retrieval witnesses.
