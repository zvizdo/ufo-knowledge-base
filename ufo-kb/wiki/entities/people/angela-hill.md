# Angela Hill

UFC strawweight fighter. Granddaughter of [[barney-hill]], one of the first and most famous documented alien abductees (the [[1961-betty-barney-hill-abduction|Betty and Barney Hill abduction]], 1961).

## Connection to the Hill Abduction Case

[[jesse-michaels]] has known Angela Hill personally. She appeared on his podcast but did not disclose that [[barney-hill]] was her grandfather until after the recording was finished — a detail [[joe-rogan]] describes as wild and a missed opportunity:

> "She didn't tell me until after the podcast was over. I was like, 'No, that's mad. That's so crazy.' Looking back, that was a mistake. I should have done it then."

Rogan adds: Angela Hill is [[barney-hill]]'s granddaughter, and has appeared on Jesse's podcast. She wore a Barney Hill-themed shirt on Rogan's episode.

## Significance

Angela Hill is the only known public figure with a direct biological connection to the Betty and Barney Hill abduction case — the foundational case in American abduction phenomenology. Her existence as a UFC fighter (public figure) provides a contemporary human-interest thread connecting the 1961 case to the present.
