# Paul Mueller

Director at the Paris Observatory in 1961. Described by [[jacques-vallee]] as behaving "like a petty army officer." When Vallée and colleagues observed an unidentified object in retrograde orbit on July 11, 1961, Mueller confiscated the tracking tape the following morning and destroyed it.

## The 1961 Incident

On July 11, 1961, Vallée and his colleagues at the Paris Observatory noticed an unidentified object in retrograde orbit — traveling in the *opposite direction* to Earth's rotation. The object was **as bright as Sirius** (the brightest star in the night sky). Multiple observatories observed it; American tracking stations photographed it and could not identify it.

Mueller confiscated the tracking data and destroyed it, preventing any systematic follow-up. Vallée later recounted that this experience taught him scientists destroy data that does not fit their preconceptions — an incident that redirected him toward becoming the foremost independent UFO researcher of his era.

## Significance

Mueller's action is the Paris Observatory parallel to [[donald-menzel]]'s Harvard plate destruction. Both occurred in the same ~10-year window (early–mid 1950s to 1961) when pre-Sputnik and early-post-Sputnik anomalous orbital data was being destroyed by institutional authority figures.
