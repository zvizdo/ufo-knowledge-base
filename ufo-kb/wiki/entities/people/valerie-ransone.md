# Valerie Ransone

## Background

Valerie Ransone is a highly mysterious figure connected to both [[andrija-puharich]]'s circle and the Nixon White House. Filmmaker [[greg-malazzi]] attempted to locate and interview her over several years as part of making *Mind Traveler*, describing her as someone "everyone who knew her then was like yeah I knew her well and I I wish we could find her."

## Connections to Puharich and ELF Research

According to Malazzi's research, Ransone:
- Was connected to figures in the Nixon White House
- Claimed to channel aliens
- Sat on the boards of organizations researching exotic ELF (Extremely Low Frequency) technology
- Was in contact with CIA figure [[kit-green]] in the late 1970s to early 1980s

Malazzi characterizes her as "a woman in the Nixon White House who claims she's channeling aliens and also happens to be on the board of all these sort of spooky science outfits that seem to stem from exotic Tesla, you know, extra low frequency technology."

## Attempted Contact

Malazzi spent considerable time trying to reach Ransone. A man reached out to him claiming to have her contact information and forwarded what was supposedly her email address, then vanished entirely — Malazzi describes the man's Facebook profile as looking like a fake account. Years later the same man reappeared asking about the documentary, but Malazzi never responded.

Malazzi believes he has now located Ransone and intends to seek an interview. When he mentions her name to people who knew her in that era, they consistently do not want to discuss her — which he interprets as significant.
