# C.D. Jackson

Charles Douglas Jackson (1902–1964) was a senior Time-Life executive who served as the head of the US **Psychological Strategy Board** and ran psychological warfare operations for the United States in the 1950s. He is connected to both major assassination-era cover-up events and the landmark UFO abduction case.

## The Zapruder Film

Jackson is documented as responsible for the **missing frames** in the Zapruder film of the JFK assassination. The Zapruder film — the most analyzed video of the 20th century — has frames missing from the original, particularly around the fatal shot sequence. Jackson's role in this is documented in the context of [[jesse-michaels]]'s prior KB entries on the JFK assassination.

## Meeting with Betty and Barney Hill

Within **weeks** of the [[1961-betty-barney-hill-abduction]] (the encounter occurred September 19–20, 1961), C.D. Jackson — the head of US psychological warfare — **personally met with Betty and Barney Hill**.

This is the single most direct documented intersection of the intelligence community's psychological warfare apparatus with an individual UFO abduction case. The meeting's purpose has never been explained.

[[colin-ross]]'s interpretation: consistent with the intelligence community managing the narrative of high-profile anomalous events — consistent with the CIA's treatment of the Zapruder film frames, the CIA's interest in UFO phenomena documented by [[jacques-vallee]] in *Forbidden Science*, and the broader pattern of psychological warfare involvement in UFO discourse.
