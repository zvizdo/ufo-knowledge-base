# James Comer

Republican congressman serving as chairman of the **House Oversight Committee**. [[danny-sheehan]] identifies Comer as sympathetic to UAP disclosure and willing to issue congressional subpoenas even without the judicial mechanism provided by the full [[controlled-disclosure-campaign-act]]. Congressional subpoenas carry political weight even if they can be challenged and delayed; Comer's willingness to use them represents an alternative pressure channel for document disclosure.

## House Oversight Committee Chair and UAP Hearings

Comer (R-KY) has chaired the House Oversight and Accountability Committee since January 2023. Under his chairmanship the committee held the landmark **July 26, 2023 public UAP hearing** that featured David Grusch (whistleblower), David Fravor (Nimitz Tic-Tac), and Ryan Graves (East Coast UAP cluster). The hearing was broadcast on C-SPAN and is the most-watched congressional UAP hearing of the modern era.

Comer's UAP posture is investigative-oversight rather than ideological: he frames UAP disclosure as a transparency and accountability issue (executive-branch withholding of information from Congress) rather than as a science or national-security question. His committee's UAP work has produced follow-up correspondence with DOD, DOE, and the intelligence community throughout 2024-2025 but no further public hearings as of early 2025.
