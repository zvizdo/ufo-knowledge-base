# James Comer

Republican congressman serving as chairman of the **House Oversight Committee**. [[danny-sheehan]] identifies Comer as sympathetic to UAP disclosure and willing to issue congressional subpoenas even without the judicial mechanism provided by the full [[controlled-disclosure-campaign-act]]. Congressional subpoenas carry political weight even if they can be challenged and delayed; Comer's willingness to use them represents an alternative pressure channel for document disclosure.
