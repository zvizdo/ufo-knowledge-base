# Joey Is Not My Name

## Background

"Joey is not my name" is the public-facing pseudonym of an independent researcher who in 2023 received a leak — relayed through [[michael-herrera]] — about the [[p3-psionic-assets|P3 / pink-asset]] program inside the alleged UAP legacy programs. Per [[OOr98r51Sj0]], the insider source is now safely identifiable as [[jake-barber]] in his pre-defection / pre-public phase.

## Notable Claims (relayed leak content)

- Psionic assets are chipped with tracking devices and given heavy drug cocktails to maintain the calm, positive mental state required for the work.
- "After several years of this experimentation and drug use, a lot of these people die or slip into a coma."
- Black-program pilots without psionic ability cannot fly recovered ET craft directly; the craft "won't talk to him" — requiring a brain-machine interface routed through a psionic asset.
