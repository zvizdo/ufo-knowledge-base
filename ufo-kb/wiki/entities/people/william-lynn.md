# William Lynn

William J. Lynn III served as **Deputy Secretary of Defense** (2009–2011) under Secretary Robert Gates in the Obama administration. He appears in the UAP knowledge base through [[harry-reid]]'s failed attempt to elevate [[aawsap]] to SAP (Special Access Program) status.

## AAWSAP SAP Elevation Denial

[[eric-davis]] explains in [[LnAiNChnuEQ]]:

Reid wrote Lynn a "very nice letter" (reportedly posted online) requesting SAP elevation for AAWSAP. Lynn denied it. Davis explains why:

> "Harry was using political muscle and you can't do that. It has to go through the security channels and the program management to create a justification and a need."

The executive branch is trained not to respond to Capitol Hill direction on SAP creation — the president is the correct authority, not Congress. Reid should have approached President [[george-w-bush]] directly or worked through National Security Advisor [[steven-hadley]], not the Deputy Secretary.

Lynn's decision was technically correct under the SAP creation process: AAWSAP already held TSSI clearance; elevating it to a SAP requires a formal justification, program management review, and presidential-level authorization — not a congressional letter.
