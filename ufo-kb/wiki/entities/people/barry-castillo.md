# Barry Castillo

[[bob-lazar]]'s lab partner and informal guide at [[s4]] during Lazar's assignment (~1987–1989). Barry was the person who first showed Lazar the [[s4-sport-model]] and [[s4-reactor]], and who conducted the demonstration experiments that Lazar describes as his most vivid memories of S4.

All information about Barry is hearsay from Lazar; Barry has not come forward publicly. Lazar cannot find him and does not know his current status.

## Role at S4

Barry was described as enthusiastic and casual around the craft — the opposite of the rigid, robotic supervisor [[dennis-mariani]]. He handled the craft and reactor equipment like a mechanic working on a car, suggesting he had been at S4 significantly longer than Lazar.

Barry had previously worked with someone who was killed (context not elaborated by Lazar) — suggesting he had been involved in the program through dangerous incidents.

## Demonstration Experiments (Hearsay via Lazar)

1. **Golf ball incident**: Barry threw a golf ball at the active reactor to demonstrate the gravitational/force field. The ball bounced off the field, dislodged a ceiling tile, and they had to clean up before [[dennis-mariani]] returned.
2. **Candle experiment**: Placed a lit candle at the focal point of the active emitter; the flame stopped flickering — froze in place — while remaining lit and visible.
3. **Black ball experiment**: Rotated the emitter; a small black ball appeared in the air at the focal point — no light escaping — resembling a miniature black hole.
4. **Port holes**: Told Lazar that the apparent windows/portholes of the craft were actually solid material. Lazar did not independently verify this.

## Theoretical Framework

Barry's lab notes contained the **Gravity A / Gravity B** framework — the theoretical architecture the S4 team used to categorize the force(s) produced by the reactor. Barry communicated this to Lazar orally. Lazar is explicit: "I'm just repeating stuff I was told. It's not like I conducted an experiment to verify that."

## Craft Retrieval Claim

Barry told Lazar the sport model was recovered by the U.S. Navy from an underwater "archaeological dig." This claim is not in the briefing documents Lazar read — it was Barry's personal account. If accurate, it explains the Navy's institutional control over the S4 program.

## Current Status

Lazar cannot locate Barry Castillo. His disappearance is an open question: he could be alive under a gag order, deceased, or operating under a new identity. He represents the single person who could most directly corroborate the core S4 technical claims.
