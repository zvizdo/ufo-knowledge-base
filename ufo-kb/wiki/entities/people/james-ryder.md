# James Ryder

James Ryder was a **former vice president of Lockheed Martin** and a **director at Skunk Works** — one of the highest-ranking executives in the most classified aerospace division of the world's largest defense contractor.

## "The Garment of God" Talk (2018)

In 2018, Ryder gave a public talk titled **"The Garment of God"** in which he explicitly emphasized the relationship between mysticism, consciousness, and technological progress. This is remarkable coming from a senior defense executive at Skunk Works, where the norm is extreme technical conservatism and secrecy.

## UAP and Ritual Altered States

In a **separate lecture focused entirely on UAPs**, Ryder spoke openly about:
- **Ritual altered states** — altered states of consciousness induced through ritual practice
- The **overlap between ancient spiritual entities and modern contact experiences** — suggesting the entities described in UAP contact reports are continuous with entities from ancient religious/spiritual traditions

This positions Ryder within the emerging consensus at the elite aerospace level (alongside [[charles-chase]], [[robert-weiss]], [[jeffrey-kripal]], and [[james-lacatski]]'s AAWSAP findings) that UAP contact and consciousness are fundamentally linked.

## Secret Machines Advisory Role

Ryder advised [[tom-delonge]] and [[peter-levenda]]'s *[[secret-machines]]* project alongside [[charles-chase]], [[robert-weiss]], and Air Force Research Lab Commander Neil McCaslin.
