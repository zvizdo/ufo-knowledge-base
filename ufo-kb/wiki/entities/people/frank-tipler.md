# Frank Tipler

Mathematical physicist best known for the **Tipler cylinder** (or Tipler disc) — a solution to Einstein's field equations showing that a finite, rapidly-rotating body can create closed timelike curves (CTCs), enabling time travel within established general relativity. His 1970s work is the theoretical bedrock of [[michael-p-masters]]' argument that disc/saucer-shaped UAP are physically consistent with time machines.

## The Tipler Disc

Building on earlier infinite-cylinder solutions (van Stockum, 1936; Gödel universe solutions, 1949) and the Lense-Thirring frame-dragging effect, Tipler showed in the 1970s that:

1. Rotating massive objects drag spacetime around them (frame-dragging, confirmed experimentally — even Earth creates a measurable frame-dragging effect)
2. A sufficiently dense, sufficiently fast rotating cylinder **tips light cones over** — making "forward in time" point toward the past, allowing travel backward in time
3. Critically: Tipler showed this could be done with a **finite** rotating ring/sphere/disc — not an infinitely long cylinder

The minimum geometry: a rapidly rotating disc. The exact shape universally associated with disc/saucer UAP.
