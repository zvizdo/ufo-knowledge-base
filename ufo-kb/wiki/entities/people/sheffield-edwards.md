# Sheffield Edwards

Colonel Sheffield Edwards served as the CIA's Director (Chief) of Security under Director Allen Dulles. He is the documented CIA official who authorized and operationalized the agency's use of organized crime figures in assassination plots against Fidel Castro — the program that forms the operational core of what researchers call the [[s-force]] infrastructure.

## Background

As Director of Security, Edwards was responsible for the CIA's internal security apparatus and had the authority to greenlight sensitive operational programs. His role in the Castro assassination program made him the institutional bridge between CIA leadership and the American Mafia, a connection that has bearing on subsequent events including the [[1963-jfk-assassination]].

## Notable Claims / Role

Edwards met personally with [[santos-trafficante]] at the Fontainebleau Hotel in Miami to formalize the operational relationship between the CIA and organized crime for the Castro assassination program. The intermediary was [[bob-maheu]], a former FBI agent and Howard Hughes associate who served as the cutout between the agency and the mob. Edwards's direct role demonstrates that the CIA-Mafia assassination program was not a rogue operation but was formally sanctioned at the security directorate level.
