# Matt Lroy

Independent researcher and creator of *Stage of Time* (YouTube, Instagram, stageoftime.com). Lroy spent approximately 10 years researching climatic events through Antarctic ice cores, ancient megalithic sites, and astronomical cycles. His primary thesis links a pre-Flood advanced civilization to a recurring catastrophic solar event driven by a binary dead-star companion orbiting our sun.

## Core Thesis

Lroy synthesizes catastrophism, archaeoastronomy, and plasma physics into a unified model:

1. **Binary Dead Star**: Our sun has a dead stellar companion orbiting on a ~25,000–26,000 year cycle. Perihelion (~12,500-year intervals) stresses the sun, triggering mass coronal ejection events. This explains both the Younger Dryas (~12,000 BP) and Older Dryas (~14,500 BP) catastrophes.

2. **Lost Civilization**: An advanced pre-Flood civilization mastered [[geodesy-sacred-geometry|geodesy]] — positioning stone monuments at coordinates resonant with Earth-Sun-Moon ratios and geomagnetic poles. Only stone survives millennia; their legacy is the megalithic record.

3. **Plasma Discharge Evidence**: [[robert-schoch]]'s plasma discharge theory explains vitrification at [[lake-van]], [[nazca-lines]], and Rongo Rongo script at [[easter-island]] — ancients recorded the terrifying geometric plasma patterns they witnessed in the sky during CME events.

4. **Suppression**: [[nasa]] scrubbed Pioneer 10/11 data confirming the binary companion. Investigators [[thomas-van-flandern]] and [[robert-harrington]] died of throat cancer (non-smokers) shortly after the discovery.

## Lake Van Research

Lroy's most distinctive original contribution: years of fieldwork in the [[lake-van]] region of Turkey. Key findings:

- **[[kepkesi]]**: Giant square basalt blocks (hardness 7/10) with machine-cut surfaces and vitrified faces — [[robert-schoch]] confirmed vitrification on a joint site visit.
- **[[mount-nemrut]]**: Volcanic explosion during the Older/Younger Dryas period; could have contributed to atmospheric heat events and regional catastrophe.
- Mud-brick structures built atop earlier buried basalt megaliths — Lroy interprets as deliberate concealment by later cultures.

The Lake Van region is Lroy's core claim to original discovery, complementing rather than duplicating the Giza/Turkey work of [[graham-hancock]] and [[randall-carlson]].

## Relationship to Graham Hancock

Jesse frames Lroy as completing where Hancock "falls short" — Hancock proved a catastrophe occurred and pre-Flood civilization existed; Lroy characterizes *who they were* and *what mechanisms reset them*. Lroy is broadly aligned with Hancock but differs:
- Prefers CME over Taurid comet impact for the Younger Dryas mechanism
- Places more emphasis on the binary star companion as proximate cause
- Distances himself from [[zecharia-sitchin]]'s Nibiru framing

## Current Warning

Lroy argues we are currently in the perihelion window — binary companion at closest approach. Evidence: aurora borealis observed further south than ever recorded; solar activity ramping up (2024–2026). He frames this as urgent but stops short of predicting a specific catastrophe date.

## Open Questions

- Lroy's pyramids-at-10,500-BC claim conflicts with his own description of that period as "extremely volatile." He does not resolve this tension.
- His conflation of Caltech's Planet 9 with his dead-star binary companion is epistemically sloppy; they are distinct claims.
