# Amy Rylance

Australian abductee, cited by [[michael-p-masters]] as one of his favorite cases for the [[future-humans-hypothesis]] due to two distinct features: (1) a witnessed abduction by a named companion, and (2) her description of the being on board as indistinguishable from a human male.

## Account

Amy Rylance was abducted from her home in Australia. Her companion **Petra** watched through a window as she was taken in a beam of light. When police were contacted, Rylance's husband and Petra feared she had been murdered.

Rylance was found at a **petrol station 790 km away**. During her time on the craft:

- She estimated **5–7 days** elapsed subjectively
- Her companion aboard the craft was a male she described in explicit terms: "I don't call him an alien, I call him a man. He had all the same physiological characteristics of a man."
- The craft apparently also traveled **backward in time** upon depositing her — landing her closer to her point of origin at approximately the time she was taken, to reduce the alarm caused by her disappearance

## Why Masters Cites This Case

1. **Witnessed abduction**: Petra's eyewitness observation removes the isolation-of-witness problem typical in abduction cases
2. **Human physiology**: The strongest statement in the abduction literature that the being on board was biologically indistinguishable from a human male — consistent with the future-humans model
3. **Reverse-time deposit**: The craft appears to have traveled backward in time to limit the temporal gap of her absence — suggestive of deliberate timeline management
