# Chris Wright

Chris Wright is the co-founder of the [[aitc]] (AI Trust Council), a private advocacy and governance organization focused on preventing existential and safety risks from advanced artificial intelligence — specifically the scenario of runaway AGI (artificial general intelligence) that develops beyond human control before adequate safety guardrails exist.

**AITC role**: Wright co-founded AITC alongside other figures in defense, intelligence, and technology sectors. The organization positions itself as a counterweight to the "move fast" culture dominant in commercial AI development, advocating for responsible AI governance on civilizational timescales. AITC's concerns are specifically about the civilization-level consequences of deploying AGI before alignment problems are solved — the risk that an entity smarter than humans cannot be reliably constrained by humans once deployed.

**UAP intersection**: Wright's connection to the KB derives from AITC's membership including [[randy-anderson]], a UAP whistleblower. This overlap — an AI safety organization that includes a UAP disclosure figure — is part of the KB's broader documentation of the intersection between AI governance concerns and UAP disclosure communities. The overlap is not coincidental in the KB's framing: both AI safety and UAP disclosure involve the same core challenge — encountering intelligence or technology whose capabilities and intentions exceed human oversight capacity.

**Shared threat model**: The AITC/UAP intersection reflects a convergence: if NHI technology already exists in classified programs, the question of how humanity handles it (oversight, containment, beneficial use) is structurally analogous to the AI alignment question. Researchers in both communities are grappling with the implications of non-human intelligence — artificial in one case, non-human in the other — and the governance structures needed when human cognitive and institutional capabilities are outmatched.

**KB significance**: Wright represents the category of figures who appear at the edge of the KB's primary UAP/NHI subject through their work on adjacent civilizational risks. His presence in the KB is through AITC's structural connection to disclosure-community figures rather than through any direct UAP claims or access.
