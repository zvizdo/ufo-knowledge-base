# David Norquist

33rd United States Deputy Secretary of Defense, serving under President Trump from 2019 to January 2021. According to [[david-grusch]], Norquist was present alongside Secretary of Defense [[mark-esper]] at a Fall 2019 briefing on an active UAP reverse-engineering program, arranged by a PhD-level program insider. The briefing establishes that program knowledge reached the Deputy SecDef level during the Trump administration.

## DOD Deputy Secretary and UAP Briefings

David Norquist served as Deputy Secretary of Defense from July 2019 through January 2021 under Secretary Esper. He had earlier served as DOD Comptroller (2017-2019) and acting Deputy Secretary in late 2019.

Per David Grusch's June 2023 whistleblower disclosure and subsequent testimony, Norquist (alongside Esper) received a Fall 2019 briefing on alleged UAP retrieval and reverse-engineering programs. Norquist has not publicly confirmed or denied the briefing. His post-DOD career has been in defense-industry consulting; he has made no significant UAP-related public statements. Norquist's relevance to the UAP-disclosure debate is structural: as the second-most-senior civilian DOD official during the 2019-2020 UAP-Task-Force standup, his knowledge state is materially relevant to the question of how much DOD leadership knew about alleged crash-retrieval programs.
