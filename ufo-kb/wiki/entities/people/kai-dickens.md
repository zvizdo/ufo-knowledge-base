# Kai Dickens

## Background

Kai Dickens is a researcher associated with the **Telepathy Tapes** project, which investigates apparent psionic and telepathic abilities in non-verbal autistic children. The project documents cases where these children demonstrate what appear to be genuine mind-reading and remote-perception capacities.

## Key Reference in UAP Discourse

In [[9QMrhcpJq8I]], [[matthew-pines]] and [[jesse-michaels]] discuss the Telepathy Tapes as one of the most significant pieces of evidence for real psionic phenomena. Specifically, one child's statement — **"Mom, you don't get my powers if you lie"** — is cited as profound evidence that:

1. Psionic capability is a real phenomenon, not artifactual
2. The capability has an intrinsic **ethical precondition** — it cannot be faked or weaponized without undermining its own function
3. The government programs attempting to train and deploy psionic operators may be systematically undermining themselves through their adversarial, mercenary orientation

## Connection to UAP Program Critique

Pines uses the Telepathy Tapes finding to argue that government attempts to operationalize psionics for national security purposes ("be a billionaire", weaponize NHI craft control) are structurally doomed — the random event generator (REG) literature shows that heart-centered, internally aligned operators produce measurable effects, but you cannot fake the alignment. The child's statement suggests the capacity is aware of intention.
