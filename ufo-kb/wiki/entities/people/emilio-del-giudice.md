# Emilio Del Giudice

Italian theoretical physicist at the University of Milan. With [[giuliano-preparata]], he co-developed the **quantum field theory of coherent domains** in liquid water — a model proposing that large ensembles of water molecules can synchronize their quantum oscillations, forming stable coherent structures that concentrate and store energy.

## Core Theoretical Contribution

Del Giudice and Preparata's model, sometimes called **QED coherence theory**, proposes:

1. In bulk water, domains of ~0.1 micron diameter spontaneously form in which molecules oscillate in phase.
2. These coherent domains can trap electromagnetic energy, acting as quantum energy reservoirs.
3. When water is confined — in tubes, biological membranes, or geological formations — the coherent domains may sustain and channel vibrations in ways not predicted by classical fluid mechanics.

This framework underpins the hypothesis that water flowing through structured geological or man-made channels (like those theorized beneath [[giza-plateau]]) could interact with piezoelectric material to generate and transmit information or energy.

## Relevance to Pyramid Hypothesis

[[filippo-biondi]] invokes Del Giudice and Preparata's work to bridge the physics of liquid water coherence to the observed spiral structures below [[khafre-pyramid]]. The theoretical chain: water → coherent domain oscillations → piezoelectric excitation → electricity/information generation.

## Fate

Died approximately 2014–2015. Biondi describes him as "wonderful" and regrets that the research could not be continued.
