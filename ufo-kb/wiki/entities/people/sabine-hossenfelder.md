# Sabine Hossenfelder

German theoretical physicist and prominent science communicator. Known for her YouTube channel and Substack newsletter where she explains and critiques physics and related scientific claims. Frequently plays a skeptical role.

## Position on Biondi's Giza Findings

In a video responding to [[filippo-biondi]]'s viral Giza substructure claims, Hossenfelder:

1. **Challenged the visualization**: She argued the AI-generated 3D reconstruction of the underground structures "takes liberties" with the raw tomography data — a valid methodological concern about the gap between raw data and polished image.
2. **Was described as "nice" and "open-minded"** by Biondi himself, who noted she was not purely dismissive.
3. **Conclusion**: Per Jesse Michaels's paraphrase in [[zrmG2jBckgk]], "I don't know what to say. This is interesting."

[[brian-keating]] (physicist who normally plays the skeptical role per Jesse) also publicly found the findings interesting and called for corroboration.

## Context

Hossenfelder's partial endorsement is significant: she is neither a true believer nor a pure debunker, and her admission of genuine uncertainty about the Giza substructure findings represents a higher-than-expected openness from a mainstream physics communicator.
