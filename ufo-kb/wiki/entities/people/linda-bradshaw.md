# Linda Bradshaw

Former owner of [[bradshaw-ranch]], a property near Sedona, Arizona that has become associated with repeated UAP, interdimensional, and anomalous phenomena. Bradshaw is the primary human witness to paranormal activity at the ranch, and specifically the individual who photographed what she describes as a portal opening.

## Portal Photographs

Bradshaw photographed what appeared to be a luminous opening or vortex in a specific patch of ground on the ranch property. The exact location is now fenced off and under camera surveillance — though [[ross-coulthart]] notes that the camera faces *inward* toward that patch rather than outward toward the perimeter, suggesting the fence is monitoring the anomalous location rather than providing perimeter security.

## Ranch Sale and Aftermath

After the ranch changed hands, it was nominally classified as an academic research station. The current owners have restricted access. The surveillance setup — facing the portal area rather than the boundary — is cited as an indicator that official parties are aware of the phenomenon and actively monitoring it.
