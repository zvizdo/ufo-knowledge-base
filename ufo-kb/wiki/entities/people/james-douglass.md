# James Douglass

American Catholic theologian and peace activist who spent years synthesizing the JFK assassination evidence into a unified thesis. His 2008 book *JFK and the Unspeakable* argues that Kennedy was killed by the national security state — primarily the CIA, military-industrial complex, and elements within the Pentagon — because he was moving toward ending the Cold War: the Limited Test Ban Treaty, the American University speech, back-channel communications with Khrushchev, and early signals of Vietnam withdrawal combined to make him an existential threat to the Cold War institutional infrastructure.

The book's title references Thomas Merton's concept of "the unspeakable" — the impersonal, bureaucratic evil that operates within institutions while remaining invisible to those who serve them. Douglass argues the assassination was not a conspiracy in the thriller sense but an institutional act by a security apparatus that had developed an autonomous logic.

In the KB, Douglass is a foundational source for the [[jfk-assassination-ufo]] synthesis: if the CIA killed Kennedy for national security reasons, the same institutional capacity explains UAP secrecy maintenance. [[oliver-stone]]'s 1991 *JFK* introduced Garrison's investigation to mass audiences; Douglass's work provides the more rigorous historical-research foundation that [[danny-sheehan]] and others build on.
