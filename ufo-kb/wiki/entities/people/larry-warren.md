# Larry Warren

US Air Force security policeman stationed at RAF Bentwaters/Woodbridge during the [[1980-rendlesham-forest|1980 Rendlesham Forest UFO incident]]; one of the enlisted witnesses to the event. Warren subsequently became a researcher and co-author on the Rendlesham case.

## Gleason Confidence

[[jackie-gleason]] confided to Warren that President Richard Nixon had driven him personally to a heavily guarded building at the "very far end" of Homestead Air Force Base, where security personnel stood aside as they passed. After proceeding through several labs, Gleason was shown what Nixon described as wreckage from a flying saucer, and an inner chamber containing "six or eight glass-topped Coke freezer" cases with what appeared to be mangled bodies — described as looking like children initially but on closer examination appearing very old and badly damaged.

Warren's account of Gleason's confidence was published in the book *UFOs Among the Stars* by Timothy Green Beckley. [[beverly-gleason|Beverly Gleason]] (Jackie's wife) independently corroborated the Homestead story to Esquire magazine in 1974.
