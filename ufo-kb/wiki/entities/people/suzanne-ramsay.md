# Suzanne Ramsay

UFO researcher and co-author with [[scott-ramsay]] of the most comprehensive modern investigation into the [[1948-aztec-crash]] — the alleged March 1948 UAP recovery near Aztec, New Mexico, approximately nine months after the [[1947-roswell]] incident and the case that actually *preceded* Roswell into print via Frank Scully's 1950 book *Behind the Flying Saucers*.

**Research approach**: The Ramsays' methodology was ground-up witness interviewing — locating and interviewing surviving witnesses and secondary sources who had direct contact with the Aztec event or its principals. They spent years conducting this systematic primary research, which [[richard-dolan]] endorses as the rehabilitation of a case that had been deliberately and unjustly dismissed since JP Cahn's 1950 "debunking" in *True* magazine.

**Historical rehabilitation**: The Aztec case had been labeled a hoax since Cahn's takedown. [[richard-dolan]] argues that Cahn's debunking was agenda-driven rather than dispassionate — Cahn's family had a personal relationship with J. Edgar Hoover, raising questions about the neutrality of his investigation. The Ramsays' witness-based reinvestigation provided a new evidentiary baseline for evaluating the Aztec case independently of Cahn's account.

**Significance in the crash-retrieval corpus**: Because Aztec was the *first* crash case to appear in print, the Ramsays' rehabilitation of it is foundational to the argument that systematic suppression of crash-retrieval accounts began immediately with public UFO disclosure, not years later. [[nat-kobitz]], former Navy Director of Science and Technology, implicitly confirmed Aztec as a real retrieval to [[ross-coulthart]] when he acknowledged multiple craft recoveries.

**KB role**: Suzanne's page parallels [[scott-ramsay]]'s — both are credited equally as co-investigators and co-authors of the rehabilitated Aztec case. Individual claims are attributed to the pair rather than one or the other.
