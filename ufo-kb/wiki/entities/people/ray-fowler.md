# Ray Fowler

Manager working under [[sylvania-corporation]], which held the electrical systems contract at [[malmstrom-air-force-base]] in 1967. His contractor employees witnessed the UFO activity and its connection to the missile shutdowns and reported to him. Fowler compiled a detailed account (with names, dates, places, and phone numbers) and passed it to Roy Craig, the [[condon-commission]]'s chief investigator.

Craig then visited Malmstrom and interviewed [[lewis-chase]], who denied any knowledge of the missile-UFO connection — directly contradicting Fowler's detailed, corroborated report.
