# Richard Sauder

Richard Sauder is an independent researcher best known for his investigation into the existence and extent of deep underground military bases (DUMBs) in the United States. His work focuses on declassified construction contracts, tunnel-boring technology, and engineering documents rather than whistleblower testimony.

## Underground Bases Research

[[richard-dolan]] describes working "kind of closely" with Sauder and says Sauder "went through the documentation" to make "a very very strong case that even back in the 40s and 50s we were looking — the military was looking strong about how to go deep."

Dolan's summary of Sauder's finding: the military's interest in deep underground construction is partly a legitimate civil defense concern (hardening command-and-control against nuclear exchange) and partly a platform for activities that go beyond simple hardening. The documented history of deep underground construction provides a real infrastructure layer beneath the claims about exotic technology programs.
