# Merrick von Rennenkampff

Journalist; described by [[jesse-michaels]] as a friend and colleague who covers UAP topics. Michaels characterizes him as "eloquently" dismantling the Wall Street Journal's EMP claim via tweet: "The Wall Street Journal is suggesting that technicians somehow moved a 60-foot science experiment up to the front gate of an alert missile facility undetected and then they fried the active nukes. This is a joke, right?"

## Significance

His tweet captures the core physical absurdity of the [[emp-misattribution]] claim in its 2024 deployment: the EMP test stand required to generate sufficient voltage was, by the WSJ's own diagram, approximately 60 feet tall — requiring days of construction, visible to all base security, and incompatible with an "undetected secret test" narrative.

[[jesse-michaels]] mentions him in the context of a proposed co-authored Wall Street Journal rebuttal op-ed, alongside [[robert-hastings]], [[carl-nell]], [[david-grusch]], and [[michael-shellenberger]].
