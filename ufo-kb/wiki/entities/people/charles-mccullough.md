# Charles McCullough

I. Charles McCullough III served as Inspector General of the Intelligence Community (ICIG) from 2011 to 2017 — the senior oversight official responsible for receiving and adjudicating whistleblower complaints across the US intelligence community. After leaving office, McCullough became a partner at a private law firm specializing in national security matters.

McCullough became [[david-grusch]]'s personal attorney for Grusch's UAP whistleblower disclosure. His significance is not merely legal: McCullough personally processed whistleblower complaints as ICIG and understands the system from the inside. When the ICIG under his successor found Grusch's complaint "credible and urgent," McCullough's professional judgment about the quality of Grusch's underlying claims carries specific weight.

At the July 2023 Congressional UAP hearing, McCullough was **seated directly behind Grusch** — a deliberate, visible positioning. Corbell and Knapp interpret this as a signal: the former Inspector General of the Intelligence Community was publicly aligning his professional reputation with Grusch's credibility, before a Congressional audience, without filing a separate statement.

McCullough's selection as attorney also reflects a strategic legal choice. Under the Intelligence Community Whistleblower Protection Act, a former ICIG as defense counsel knows exactly what protections apply, what channels were correctly used, and what the government's procedural exposure would be if it pursued aggressive action against Grusch.
