# Aldous Huxley

British novelist (*Brave New World*, *Point Counter Point*) and philosophical essayist. His 1954 essay *The Doors of Perception* — describing his mescaline experience — introduced the **reducing valve** metaphor: the brain does not generate consciousness but filters it. The totality of consciousness is vast; ordinary perception is a narrow survival-directed reduction of it; psychedelics reduce the filter and allow more of the underlying reality through.

This is one of the foundational statements of [[transmission-theory-consciousness]], alongside [[william-james]]'s earlier formulation.

## The Reducing Valve

Huxley's model: "To make biological survival possible, Mind at Large has to be funneled through the reducing valve of the brain and nervous system. What comes out at the other end is a measly trickle of the kind of consciousness which will help us to stay alive on the surface of this particular planet."

Psychedelics — mescaline, LSD, psilocybin — work by reducing the valve's effectiveness, allowing more of the underlying Mind at Large to flood through. The drug does not add anything; it removes a constraint.

[[andrew-gallimore]] extends this: DMT doesn't merely open a filter — it allows external intelligences to commandeer the machinery entirely. Huxley's passive model (reduced filtering of an impersonal field) versus Gallimore's active model (directed access by specific agents) is a key conceptual distinction in the field.

## Death and LSD

Huxley, dying of cancer in November 1963, asked his wife Laura Archera Huxley to administer a large intramuscular dose of LSD. He died under its influence. Jesse Michaels and Gallimore discuss this as Huxley walking his talk — using the molecule as a death-transition technology consistent with his own framework.

## Prediction of Psychedelic Religion / New Eleusis

Huxley predicted that in the 21st century, mass psychedelic use would give rise to a new religion — not doctrinal, but based on individual direct gnostic experience of ultimate reality. Specifically, he envisioned a **"new Eleusis"** — a modern revival of the ancient mystery tradition. [[andrew-gallimore]] is ambivalent: he fears the word "religion" (doctrines, followers, hierarchies of belief) but acknowledges that widespread acceptance of the non-material reality DMT reveals seems inevitable.

In [[I9VyyV4-3Js]], [[brian-muraresku]] directly engages Huxley's prediction and argues it is materializing through three channels: FDA-sanctioned therapeutic (Oregon model), underground Dionysian (Burning Man lineage), and First Amendment religious exercise. See [[psychedelic-religion-future]].

## Julian Huxley and Brave New World as Plan

[[alex-jones]] (in [[KhmUixSXxDo]]) relays a claim attributed to Aldous's brother [[julian-huxley]]: that at a ~1962 Berkeley speech during the *Brave New World Revisited* tour, Julian stated the 1931 novel represented the actual planning of elite families (Huxleys, Wedgwoods, Galtons) for chemical/biological/genetic social control — not speculative fiction. If accurate, this would frame *Brave New World* as programmatic rather than cautionary.

> ⚠ This is Jones's secondhand account. The original Berkeley 1962 Huxley lecture is documented in KPFA archives, but whether Julian made this specific claim there is unverified.
