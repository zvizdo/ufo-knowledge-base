# Robert Low

Deputy director of the [[condon-commission]] (University of Colorado UFO study, 1966–1968). Before the study began, Low wrote an internal memo — subsequently obtained and publicized — stating the study "would appear to be a scientific investigation" while in fact reaching a predetermined null conclusion. This memo became the primary documentary evidence for the "Condon whitewash" thesis.

Low also received communication from Condon investigator Roy Craig suggesting they request the Air Force's report on the missile shutdown investigation. Low reportedly replied that he had already spoken to Colonel [[robert-hiper]] (USAF), who told him the matter was "probably too highly classified" for the Commission to access — and that the Air Force attributed the shutdowns to EMP from nuclear testing. Low wrote this in a handwritten note, which [[robert-salas]] claims to possess.
