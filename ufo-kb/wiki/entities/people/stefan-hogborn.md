# Stefan Hogborn

Swedish expert diver, well-known in Swedish diving circles. Approximately 60 years old at the time of the interview; has been diving since youth, accumulating approximately 6,000 lifetime dives.

## Baltic Sea Anomaly Dive (2012)

Hogborn was the first diver to physically reach and touch the [[baltic-sea-anomaly]]. Key observations:

- **Temperature**: The water near the object was close to freezing (near 0°C). At 90 m in the Baltic, normal temperature is 4–5°C. This anomaly alarmed his equipment.
- **Surface**: Dark grey-black after brushing away sediment. Could not obtain a core sample — material was too hard.
- **Corridors and walls**: 90° right-angle corners; completely flat walls.
- **Hole**: A hole with rhythmic sand movement — material flowing in and out, described by Asberg as "almost like breathing."
- **Visibility**: Essentially zero optical visibility at 90 m depth; had to rely on sonar and tactile exploration.

His reaction upon surfacing — visible confusion on his face — was described by [[dennis-asberg]] as among the most striking moments of the whole investigation. Hogborn said: *"Dennis, I have dived a lot, and I have never ever seen anything like this. That was probably the most important dive I've ever done in my life."*
