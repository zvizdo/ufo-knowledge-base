# Don Phillips

## Background

Don Phillips is a UFO whistleblower with claimed association to [[lockheed-skunk-works]]; appears in Disclosure-Project-era testimony and in subsequent compilations. In [[OOr98r51Sj0]] he is used as a corroborator of the [[mind-controlled-craft]] hypothesis through his on-camera statements about human cognitive capacity exceeding any man-made computer.

## Notable Claims

- UFOs are interfaced through "a full-body connection."
- "Humanity has the quickest computer built into them than any man-made computer can ever approach. And that includes the Cray computer."
- Specifically compared UFO control to the *Firefox* film's mind-controlled helmet ("steer with your eyes, fire with pure thought").
