# Lawrence Krauss

Lawrence Krauss is a theoretical physicist and science communicator, formerly at Arizona State University (director of the Origins Project), known publicly for books such as *A Universe from Nothing* and advocacy for science education. He is one of several physicists linked to [[jeffrey-epstein]] via direct financial support.

## Epstein Connection

Per [[51N8OxqZIWY]], Epstein funded Krauss approximately $250,000 — a figure [[jay-anderson]] states is publicly verifiable ("You can Google it"). Krauss reportedly made a public statement expressing deep discomfort with this connection: *"I desperately want to know why Jeffrey Epstein knew so much about my work. And I want to know why he was connected to my graduate program."*

This quote is presented in the episode as evidence that even the recipients of Epstein's physics funding found the relationship inexplicable — consistent with the thesis that Epstein was acting as an agent of a deeper program with specific scientific interests, rather than a philanthropist.

> ⚠ Attribution note: The Krauss quote is relayed second-hand by [[jay-anderson]]; this is hearsay.

## Physics Foundations Failure (xnxasfyHtfo)

Krauss is mentioned alongside [[leonard-susskind]] in [[xnxasfyHtfo]] as representing the generation of physicists confronting string theory's failure. [[eric-weinstein]] notes Susskind's admission on Kurt Jaimungal's *Theories of Everything* channel ("we have to go back to the foundations") and places this in the broader physics stagnation context. Krauss is cited as a parallel figure navigating the same crisis.
