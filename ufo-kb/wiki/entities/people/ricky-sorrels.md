# Ricky Sorrels

Hunter from Stephenville, Texas who observed the 2008 UFO at closer range than most other witnesses. Sorrels' account is notable for its technical craft description and for the subsequent intimidation campaign directed at him.

## The Sighting

Sorrels was hunting in the Stephenville area in January 2008 when he observed the craft at close range. His observation differed from the media consensus — he reported the object as **smaller** than the widely cited enormous dimensions. He aimed his rifle scope at the underside of the craft and examined it at magnification.

What he observed: **no rivets, seams, or panel lines** — a completely seamless surface. This detail echoes accounts from other close-encounter witnesses of highly advanced craft and is consistent with materials described in reverse-engineering testimony.

After Sorrels directed his scope at the craft, it departed with extraordinary speed.

## Intimidation

Following his public report, Sorrels was subjected to repeated low-level helicopter surveillance over his property — a pattern consistent with other Stephenville witnesses and with the broader witness intimidation pattern documented in multiple Fox cases. As of the time of the Fox/Rogan interview (December 2024), Sorrels had not spoken publicly in years.

[[james-fox]] expressed concern about Sorrels' current situation and noted his silence.
