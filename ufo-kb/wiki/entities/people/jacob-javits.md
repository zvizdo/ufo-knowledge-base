# Jacob Javits

U.S. Senator from New York (1957–1981). Liberal Republican; known for civil rights advocacy and support for the United Nations. In the UAP/deep-state discourse, Javits is most specifically cited for his role as the **Army lawyer who covered up the only successfully litigated MK-Ultra death** — and who later rose to become one of New York's most powerful senators.

**The tennis pro case**: [[danny-sheehan]]'s [[christic-institute]] won the only successful civil lawsuit against [[mk-ultra]] through this specific case: a Long Island tennis pro was admitted to a New York City hospital (ostensibly for counseling after his divorce) and dosed twice with massive amounts of LSD without consent. He died of a heart attack. At the time, Jacob Javits was serving as the Army's lawyer and covered up the death. Warren Burger — serving as intelligence-community counsel and later appointed Chief Justice of the Supreme Court by Nixon — was also involved in the cover-up. When Sheehan's investigation exposed Burger's role, the judge ordered the case settled rather than allow Burger to be named in open court proceedings.

**Structural significance**: The Javits-Burger combination in the MK-Ultra cover-up illustrates the rotational personnel pattern [[danny-sheehan]] describes in deep-state-continuity analysis: the same individuals who laundered or covered up classified program abuses went on to occupy the most senior positions in US law, government, and intelligence. A senator and a Chief Justice were both implicated in a single unauthorized human experiment death — neither faced public accountability.

**Post-senator career**: After leaving the Senate in 1981 (due to a neurological illness), Javits became a Washington attorney. His specific role as Army lawyer in the MK-Ultra cover-up predates his Senate career; the KB uses his page primarily to anchor the Sheehan/Christic MK-Ultra litigation thread.
