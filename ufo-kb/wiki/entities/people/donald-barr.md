# Donald Barr

Donald Barr (1921–2004) was headmaster of the Dalton School, an elite New York City prep school, when he hired [[jeffrey-epstein]] as a math teacher despite Epstein being unqualified for the position. Barr was a former **Navy intelligence officer with a high security clearance**.

## *Space Relations* (1973)

Barr wrote a science-fiction novel titled *Space Relations* — published 1973 — about a man who travels to another planet and is groomed/exploited by a powerful woman. Its central theme is the **use of underage sexual access as political compromise and control**.

[[jesse-michaels]] and [[colin-ross]] raise the parallel:
- Manson (supposedly illiterate) structured his life around *Stranger in a Strange Land* — possibly read to him while under pharmacological influence
- Epstein (a "street kid" with no qualifications) was given a prestigious position, rose to $300 million in wealth, and ran a sexual exploitation/blackmail operation — possibly having been programmed with *Space Relations* as his life template in the same way

The woman in *Space Relations* "almost could be like Ghislaine or like one of his handlers," per the host.

## Giving Epstein the Dalton Job

Epstein had no credentials to teach at Dalton. Barr, a former Navy intelligence officer, hired him. This is the gateway through which Epstein entered elite New York society and began building his network.

## Family Connection to the Cover-Up

Donald Barr's son is [[william-barr]], who became Attorney General under both President Bush 41 and President Trump. Under Trump, when Epstein was arrested, William Barr — as AG — personally visited Epstein's jail cell. Shortly thereafter, Epstein's cellmate **Alfredo "Frain" Stone Reyes** died mysteriously. Epstein himself died officially by suicide.
