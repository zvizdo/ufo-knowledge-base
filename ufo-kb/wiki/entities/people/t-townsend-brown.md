# T. Townsend Brown

American physicist who claimed to discover a connection between strong electric fields and gravitational effects, now called the Biefeld-Brown effect. A contested figure: mainstream physics does not accept his antigravity claims, but he appears repeatedly in UAP-adjacent research histories.

## Philadelphia Experiment Connection

Brown is associated with the [[1943-philadelphia-experiment]] context — the alleged 1943 USS Eldridge test involving radar invisibility or worse. Reports of temporal disorientation among Philadelphia Experiment crew parallel the operator experiences with [[die-glocke]].

## Martin Corporation / Skunk Works Parallel

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] notes that Brown was working at **Martin Corporation** in the same period Lockheed's Skunk Works was founded (early 1950s) — both programs operating in parallel on advanced aerospace research, with Brown's electrogravitic work potentially relevant to the classified track.

## Die Glocke Parallels

The Die Glocke's reported temporal disorientation effects mirror what Brown's research context described: both involve electromagnetic field effects on spacetime or perception, both were pursued under unusual classification.
