# Oliver Stone

American filmmaker (b. 1946) and Vietnam combat veteran. Director of *Platoon* (1986, Academy Award Best Picture), *JFK* (1991), *Nixon* (1995), *W.* (2008), and *Snowden* (2016). Stone's body of work consistently examines the gap between official narratives and the deeper political forces operating beneath them — making him a recurring cultural figure in KB discourse on how suppressed truths reach mass audiences.

**JFK (1991)**: Stone's film dramatized the [[jim-garrison]] New Orleans investigation, arguing that the Kennedy assassination was a conspiracy involving CIA, Pentagon hardliners, and organized crime opposed to Kennedy's withdrawal from Vietnam. The film drew on multiple threads of JFK research including the work of [[james-douglass]] and [[danny-sheehan]]. Despite significant mainstream criticism, *JFK* was a cultural phenomenon: it generated enough public pressure that Congress passed the **JFK Records Act** (1992), establishing the **Assassination Records Review Board** and mandating declassification of Kennedy assassination documents. Stone's film directly forced the release of previously classified materials — a case study in pop-cultural pressure as a disclosure mechanism.

**JFK Records Act consequence**: The Records Act forced the declassification of millions of pages of documents. The 2017–2018 and subsequent partial releases under this act have continued to provide researchers with material supporting a conspiracy interpretation. Stone returned to the subject with the documentary *JFK Revisited: Through the Looking Glass* (2021), reviewing the newly released documents.

**KB significance**: Stone himself is not a UAP claimant. His significance is structural: he represents the mechanism by which investigative-tier findings ([[jim-garrison]]'s case, [[james-douglass]]'s research) reach mass audiences and generate political pressure sufficient to force official action. The same pop-cultural disclosure dynamic appears in the KB's analysis of how UAP information moves from the research-and-witness tier to congressional hearings under [[disclosure-narratives]].
