# Dean Radin

## Background

Dean Radin is a parapsychology researcher who has worked at Princeton's PEAR Lab (Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research) and other institutions. He is known for a body of approximately **100 peer-reviewed papers** on mind-matter interaction — specifically, demonstrating that human consciousness can influence the output of Random Event Generators (REGs, also called Random Number Generators) in statistically significant ways that cannot be explained by chance.

## The Mind-Matter Interaction Evidence

Radin's research addresses the skeptical objections systematically:
- **File drawer problem** — the concern that negative results are unpublished; Radin's meta-analyses account for publication bias
- **Survivorship bias** — controlled for in his meta-analyses
- **Replication** — the effect has been replicated across multiple independent laboratories globally

[[bob-maguire]] endorses Radin's conclusions as compelling:
> "People that have that knee-jerk reaction that that can't happen need to go read the peer-reviewed 100 or so papers written by Dean Radin, demonstrating conclusively these effects for real."

Jesse Michaels notes that one of his close mentors (also very credible professionally) was involved in the Princeton PEAR Lab random event generator experiments, has heard every standard skeptical objection, and has convincing answers to all of them.

## Relevance to UAP

If consciousness reliably influences quantum-mechanical random processes (radioactive decay, electronic noise), the implications for UAP are:
1. Consciousness-mediated communication (NHI-to-human, human-to-UAP) has a physical mechanism that is not mere mysticism.
2. The Stargate / remote viewing program's decades of CIA/DIA funding was not irrational — these agencies were funding research that Radin's later work helps validate.
3. [[donald-hoffman]]'s model (consciousness as the underlying substrate of physical reality) has empirical support in Radin's demonstrations that consciousness can affect the physical layer.

The key observation: in all the REG experiments, **time and distance don't matter** — exactly the non-locality required for quantum-consciousness models and consistent with what UAP propulsion would need.
