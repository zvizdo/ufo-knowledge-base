# Ross Coulthart

Australian investigative journalist with decades of experience in television journalism. Author of *In Plain Sight* (2021), a comprehensive survey of UAP evidence and the disclosure landscape. In the early 2020s, Coulthart became one of the primary conduits for UAP disclosure stories, conducting the first major on-camera interviews with [[david-grusch]] and breaking multiple stories about congressional UAP investigations.

## Origin Story: From Skeptic to Convinced

Coulthart set out to write a **debunking book** — assuming UFOs were a combination of black-world US aerospace and media beat-ups. His early touchstone was the [[1978-kaikoura]] incident: as a teenager, he accepted the official squidboat explanation, but as a young journalist at the NZ Herald he interviewed former PM [[robert-muldoon]], who let slip: *"Yeah, the Americans wanted that one shut down pretty quickly."*

His subsequent UAP research during 60 Minutes downtime in Washington and New York involved visiting national archives and conducting an unusual outreach strategy: **~160–170 handwritten letters** to potential sources, using no phones or email — a tradecraft approach informed by his decade-long [[echelon]] investigation, which taught him about metadata trails and Five Eyes surveillance architecture.

## The Nat Kobitz Breakthrough

The most pivotal source was [[nat-kobitz]], former Navy Director of Science and Technology. Coulthart found him through legal discovery research and letter-writing. They spoke for months — Kobitz was dying of cancer at his daughter's home and enjoyed the conversations. Then one day:

> "Why don't you ask me the question?" — Kobitz initiated. When asked if he had been read into a UAP/alien technology program, Kobitz answered **"Yes"** before Coulthart finished the sentence.

Kobitz confirmed:
- The US has recovered **multiple craft** (Roswell, Aztec, Kingman all implied as real)
- He was flown to [[wright-patterson]], went underground, was shown under heavy guard a piece of **UAP bulkhead material** that was nearly weightless despite its apparent size
- Under electron scanning microscope, the material showed **two metals bonded at the atomic level, atom by atom** — Kobitz was a world expert in electron beam welding (he did the nose wheel of the C-47) and had never seen anything like it
- Kobitz subsequently facilitated introductions to further sources, which became Coulthart's cascade of confirmations

Coulthart has an authorized recording of this conversation; he withholds it out of respect for Kobitz's family.

## Sedona Investigation (Firsthand)

Coulthart is "in no doubt whatsoever" that there is a deep underground military bunker built in the 1950s beneath [[secret-mountain-sedona]]. Evidence gathered on-site with local researchers [[jeremiah-hostman]] and [[melinda-leslie]]:
- Observed a **large golden pulsing plasma orb** escorted by Blackhawk helicopters rise from behind Secret Mountain at 1:30 AM
- Australian GPR operator Paul confirmed a **50-foot-wide reinforced steel-concrete tunnel** just below the road at [[bradshaw-ranch]]
- Two three-phase power supply boxes humming and feeding underground at the fenced Bradshaw Ranch perimeter
- CCTV camera at the site points inward at the portal location (not outward as security logic demands)
- Tunnel trajectory extrapolated to Clark Dale Cement Plant as egress
- Multiple accounts of tier-one armed soldiers threatening lethal force against civilians on public parkland

## Lockheed Martin Source

A very senior Lockheed Martin executive called Coulthart out of the blue after hearing him accurately characterize a situation. The executive implicitly confirmed holding alien technology: **"It's not us. It's the agency. We want to do it."** This is when Coulthart learned about the attempt to transfer a spacecraft (possibly the Kingman craft) from Lockheed to [[bigelow-aerospace]] — blocked by [[glenn-gaffney]] or the Office of Global Access.

## Five Eyes / Australian SAS Revelations

From Australian special forces contacts (retired), Coulthart learned that Australian SAS operators were used in US UAP retrieval operations on Australian soil — paid cash USD, required to sign NDAs, with no US personnel present and the Australian government never briefed. He connects this pattern to his prior Echelon and Five Eyes investigation experience.

## CBP Detention

After the Grusch story aired, Coulthart was detained by US CBP. A mutual friend inquired and confirmed a flag on his file at a classification level too high to access. He believes the CIA flagged his travel record. He threatened to go live on national TV if not released; he was released.
