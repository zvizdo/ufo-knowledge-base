# Hans-Adam II

Reigning Prince of Liechtenstein and a figure in the elite social networks that intersected with early private UFO disclosure efforts. Hans-Adam II is identified as a close personal friend of [[lawrence-rockefeller]] and as the individual who, at Rockefeller's request, convened a gathering of [[1947-roswell]] witnesses in the 1970s and 1980s. Rockefeller subsequently cited Hans-Adam's efforts and the testimony gathered as the primary reason he became personally convinced that the UAP phenomenon was genuine.

## Background

As the sovereign of Liechtenstein, Hans-Adam II moves in the highest levels of European and transatlantic elite society. His personal friendship with Rockefeller placed him at the center of an informal private disclosure network operating well before the public lobbying efforts Rockefeller is better known for (such as the Rockefeller Initiative of the early 1990s, which involved [[john-mack]] and efforts to pressure the Clinton administration).

## Notable Claims / Role

The witness-gathering exercise Hans-Adam facilitated represents one of the earliest documented private efforts by elite figures to aggregate Roswell testimony outside of government channels. The fact that it persuaded Rockefeller — a highly networked and skeptically-minded figure — carries weight in discussions of elite belief formation around UAPs.
