# Rosemary Chalk

Executive secretary of the **National Academy of Science** and a personal friend of [[marsha-smith]] (head of the Congressional Research Service Science and Technology Division). Chalk is the social network link that connected [[danny-sheehan]] to Smith, which in turn led to Sheehan's access to the classified Project Blue Book crash-retrieval photographs.

## The Connection Chain

Chalk met Sheehan at mass where Father Dan Berrigan was celebrating — a connection through the Catholic peace activist network in Washington DC. She and Smith were personal friends through professional circles. When President Carter commissioned the CRS UAP study and Smith needed legal counsel, Chalk introduced the two. Sheehan describes this introduction as the pivotal moment that gave him access to classified UAP materials.

The full chain: Carter → CRS UAP study → Smith → Chalk introduction → Sheehan as special legal counsel → Madison Building access → crash-retrieval photo viewing → symbol tracing.
