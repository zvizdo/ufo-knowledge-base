# John Ramirez

John Ramirez is a former **CIA** officer who has spoken publicly about the intersection of US intelligence community programs with UAP and occult phenomena. His most notable claim concerns the **2005 NROL-16 launch**.

## The NROL-16 / Walpurgisnacht Claim

In a presentation (reported by [[jesse-michaels]]), Ramirez described the **[[2005-nrol-16-launch]]** in detail:

- The mission patch showed a pelican, a sun, pentagrams, a Bigfoot silhouette, and palm trees
- The **pelican** matched the aviary code name of CIA scientist **[[ron-pandolfi]]**, who Ramirez says was **directly involved** in the launch
- The launch occurred at **12:50 AM on April 30, 2005** — which is **Walpurgisnacht**, the traditional pagan holiday associated with gates opening between worlds
- The payload was named **Prometheus**, launched on a Titan 4 rocket, heading **northeast toward the Big Dipper**
- These parameters match — almost exactly — a ritual described in the **[[necronomicon]]** (1977 edition): "the ceremony of walking" performed best at midnight, facing north/northeast, when the Great Bear hangs by its tail; the number five is sacred; the launch occurred at 0:50 on the 5th year of the millennium with five pentagrams on the patch

Ramirez has also connected [[ron-pandolfi]] to exotic physics research, the JASON advisory group, MITRE Corporation, and the CIA's Office of Scientific and Weapons Research — what some call the CIA's "weird desk."

## Credibility Context

Ramirez is a named former CIA officer making specific, verifiable claims about named individuals and launch parameters. The launch date and direction claims are factually checkable. Whether the alignment is intentional or coincidental is unresolved.
