# Chris Sharp

UK investigative journalist and founder of [[liberation-times]]. Sharp focuses on UAP disclosure politics and crash retrieval program history, occupying a similar beat to [[michael-shellenberger]] and [[ross-coulthart]] but from a British editorial perspective.

## Notable Claims

### Glenn Gaffney as Crash Retrieval Gatekeeper
Sharp's most cited piece described [[glenn-gaffney]], a CIA official, as the gatekeeper who allegedly **blocked the transfer** of a craft held by Lockheed (allegedly collected in the 1950s) to a UAP research program connected to AATIP/Bigelow Aerospace. Shellenberger cites this reporting in [[DPmO-2E7Ayg]] as "completely reasonable" given what's known about crash retrieval program fragmentation. Congressman [[eric-burlison]] had asked at the 2024 hearing who the "gatekeepers" of the program were — a question Sharp's reporting addresses directly.
