# Herman Oberth

German-Romanian physicist and rocket pioneer; mentor to [[wernher-von-braun]]. Came to the US under [[operation-paperclip]] and worked for Convair. Notable in UFO discourse for being on video openly and seriously speculating about UFOs — saying their existence should make anyone question reality — and discussing time manipulation as potentially involved in UFO travel. This public openness about UFOs from a Paperclip scientist reinforces the hypothesis that Paperclip scientists had direct knowledge of non-human technology.

## 1954 Lecture (IWui5cBkwoE)

[[kevin-knuth]] has Oberth's 1954 lecture notes and discusses them in detail. Oberth's 1954 claims:

- "After so many witnesses have seen the so-called flying saucers the existence cannot longer be denied."
- "I believe that these flying objects come from another solar system."
- He possessed **radar measurements** showing UFO speeds up to **19 km/s (~40,000 mph)**.
- The German rocket program was **"aided by non-human beings."**

This is a foundational claim: the father of German rocketry — von Braun's mentor — stating in 1954, with radar data, that UFOs exist, are extraterrestrial, and contributed to the rocket program. As Knuth notes: "We already knew that in 1954. And and why are we playing catch-up just now?"

## Time-Space Coordinate Statement (Y26iMB0r-f8)

[[michael-p-masters]] quotes Oberth (paraphrase) as having stated that UFOs **"likely don't traverse large distances in space — they jump from one time-space coordinate to another."** This is among the most direct early endorsements of the time-travel hypothesis from a credentialed aerospace insider. Masters pairs this with [[george-hoover]]'s identification of Roswell beings as time-traveling humans as the two strongest pre-Masters corroborators of the [[future-humans-hypothesis]].
