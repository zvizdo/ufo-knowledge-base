# Ravi Chandran

Metallurgist who analyzed the metal shards collected during [[aaron-blunt]]'s drilling operation into the Mesa at [[skinwalker-ranch]]. His analysis is the primary physical-evidence anchor for the buried anomalous object hypothesis.

## Analysis Findings

Chandran analyzed hundreds of metal shards recovered from the drilling operation. Key finding: the samples were **fused metal** containing **europium** and **tellurium** — both rare earth elements. His conclusion, stated explicitly in the episode, was that the material was manufactured rather than naturally occurring: *"This material was manufactured as opposed to a natural occurrence."* He indicated he was "inclined to think that way" when asked by the team.

Europium and tellurium are not found in natural geological formations in fused combination — their co-occurrence in a single fused metal object implies industrial or advanced manufacturing process. This parallels claims about alleged UAP crash-retrieval materials documented elsewhere in the discourse.
