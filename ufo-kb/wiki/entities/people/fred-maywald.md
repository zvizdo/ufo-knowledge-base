# Fred Maywald

USAF Lieutenant (later retired as full Colonel) who served as crew commander at Oscar Flight, [[malmstrom-air-force-base]] on March 24, 1967. Was on a rest break when the missile shutdowns began and was woken by [[robert-salas]] just as the alarm sounded. He later had a "distinguished career in Strategic Air Command headquarters."

## Background

Salas did not reach Maywald until around 1996, when Maywald clarified that their assignment had been Oscar Flight — not Echo Flight — resolving years of Salas's misidentification. Maywald corroborated the essentials: missiles lost during UFO sightings. His recorded statement is on Salas's website (spiralgalaxy.org).

## Notable Claims

- Recalled guards returning "scared to death" from a launch facility; stated they were relieved of duty and he believed they never returned to guard duty.
- Confirmed he reported the incident to wing command post and requested maintenance crews.
- Was told by wing command post that "the same thing happened at another flight" — referring to [[1967-echo-flight]] eight days earlier.
