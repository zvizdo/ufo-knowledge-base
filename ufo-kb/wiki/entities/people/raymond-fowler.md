# Raymond Fowler

UFO researcher and author who conducted the primary investigation of the [[1976-allagash-abduction]]. His book "The Allagash Abductions" (full verbatim regression transcripts included) is the primary published record of the case.

**Note:** Raymond Fowler (abduction researcher) is a different person from Ray Fowler (Sylvania Corporation / Malmstrom AFB manager who appears in other KB sources on the [[1967-echo-flight]] incident).

## Background and Investigation

Fowler was investigating and writing about UFO cases when he met [[jim-weiner]] at a UFO conference at a Marriott hotel near Jim's home in Massachusetts. Dr. Jonathan Silverman (neurologist, Beth Israel Hospital / Harvard Medical School) had referred Jim to Fowler after observing Jim's extreme involuntary reaction to the Whitley Strieber Communion cover during an EEG study.

Fowler appeared uninterested in the Allagash story until Jim mentioned his identical twin brother was one of the witnesses — at which point Fowler stopped him: "What did you just say? I want you to start all over again." The twin detail offered a control-population dimension (two genetically identical people claiming the same experience) that Fowler recognized as unusually rigorous evidence.

## Investigation Methodology

- Conducted independent hypnotic regression sessions with all four witnesses separately over approximately one year
- Strict no-collusion protocol: witnesses prohibited from discussing sessions with each other, reading UFO books, or talking about the content with others during the investigation
- Sessions were audio-recorded; full verbatim transcripts appear in "The Allagash Abductions"
- Had to repeatedly bring [[jim-weiner]] out of hypnosis to prevent seizure due to Jim's extreme panic states
- In Jim's first session, the beings' faces could not be accessed; they appeared in the second session

**Campfire analysis:** Fowler's critical contribution was connecting the burned-out campfire to the missing time duration — establishing that such a fire would require approximately 3 hours to burn to ash under normal conditions, providing an objective physical indicator of the elapsed time.

## Witness Interviews Beyond the Four

Fowler interviewed the Weiner twins' mother separately. She disclosed to him — but had never told her sons — that she had seen a "hooded being" standing in her bedroom one night. This maternal disclosure added a pre-existing anomalous history dimension to the Allagash case.
