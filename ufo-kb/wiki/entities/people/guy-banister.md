# Guy Banister

William Guy Banister (1901–1964) was a former FBI Special Agent-in-Charge who operated a private detective agency and anti-communist operation out of 544 Camp Street in New Orleans — the same address where Lee Harvey Oswald distributed Fair Play for Cuba leaflets in the period before the Kennedy assassination. His significance to UAP research is twofold: his documented 1947 role in UFO reporting, and his position in the assassination network Jim Garrison investigated.

## The "Mission X" UFO Reports (1947)

In 1947, while heading the FBI's Pacific Northwest bureau, Banister was stationed in the region at the time of the first major modern UFO flap — including [[kenneth-arnold]]'s famous sighting and the [[1947-maury-island]] incident. He reported UFO sightings to [[j-edgar-hoover]] via telegrams using the designation "Mission X" — which [[peter-levenda]] calls the original "X-Files" designation in the US government.

This establishes Banister at the geographic and temporal center of the modern UFO era's inception, in an official reporting capacity.

## The JFK Network (1963)

Banister ran his 544 Camp Street office alongside [[david-ferrie]] and [[jack-martin]], both of whom were investigated by [[jim-garrison]] for connections to the Kennedy assassination. The building at 544 Camp Street simultaneously housed anti-communist Cuban exile groups — the same networks connected to the CIA-backed Bay of Pigs operation and its aftermath.

Oswald's Fair Play for Cuba leaflet distribution from this address is documented. Whether this represents Oswald being used as an intelligence asset (whose pro-Castro activity was a cover) or genuine left-wing activity is a central question in assassination research.

## Connection to Fred Chrisman

Banister's Camp Street network also had connections to [[fred-chrisman]] — the OSS veteran who supervised the [[1947-maury-island]] UFO incident and later appeared in Garrison's investigation through his known relationship with [[clay-shaw]]. This creates a through-line from the 1947 UFO flap to the 1963 assassination network.
