# Whitey Bulger

James "Whitey" Bulger was the head of the Winter Hill Gang in Boston and a long-running FBI informant whose handler was corrupt agent John Connolly. He was also an MK-Ultra test subject: in 1957, while imprisoned at Atlanta Federal Penitentiary, Bulger volunteered for what he was told were experiments to find a cure for schizophrenia. He was administered LSD repeatedly over an extended period under [[mk-ultra]] protocols.

Bulger later described the experiments as deeply traumatizing. His participation — never disclosed to him as CIA research — illustrates how [[mk-ultra]] used prisoners, psychiatric patients, and other captive populations without informed consent. The CIA-mob nexus that [[tom-oneill]] investigates in the Manson context (→ [[75Je_0hZovQ]]) runs through figures like Bulger: organized crime and the CIA's behavioral research program occupied overlapping institutional space.
