# Charles Hixon

Charles Hixon (also spelled Hickson) was the primary witness of the [[1973-pascagoula-abduction]], one of the most well-documented abduction cases in UFO history. He was in his 40s at the time of the encounter, fishing at night near Pascagoula, Mississippi with 19-year-old [[calvin-parker]].

## The Encounter

Hixon and Parker saw a dome- or cone-shaped craft hover above the water near their fishing spot. They were taken aboard and encountered entities that Hixon described as bizarre-looking — with "carrot ears" and unusual features quite unlike the standard "grey alien" template. [[richard-dolan]] notes the bizarreness of the description as one reason it's taken seriously: it doesn't fit the popular cultural template of what an alien "should" look like.

## Hidden Recording Test

When the two witnesses reported the incident to police, the deputy sheriff was skeptical. He placed them in a room alone with a hidden recorder running. Neither Hixon nor Parker knew they were being recorded. Their conversation — privately processing a traumatic, terrifying experience — convinced law enforcement they were "totally sincere." This organic credibility test is one of the most unusual pieces of corroborating evidence in abduction case history.

## Subsequent History

Hixon gave multiple interviews over the years and consistently maintained his account. Dolan: "I've watched the guy. I've listened and read his interview transcripts. He's — I think he was telling the truth."

## Geographic Context

Pascagoula, Mississippi was at the time adjacent to the **largest nuclear submarine production facility in the United States**. A week or two after the Hixon/Parker encounter, a genuine USO incident was reported in the same area — a connection [[richard-dolan]] plans to develop further in volume 2 of his USO book.
