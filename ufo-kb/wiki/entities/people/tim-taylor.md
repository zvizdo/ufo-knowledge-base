# Tim Taylor

## Who Is He?

Tim Taylor is a NASA Mission Controller and reportedly a successful biotech entrepreneur (made ~$100 million on orthopedic instruments). He keeps a very low public profile and has effectively gone dark to most researchers. [[jesse-michaels]] identifies him as the single person he most wants to interview.

His documented roles span incompatible worlds:
- NASA Mission Controller (credentialed aerospace professional)
- Biotech entrepreneur (hard-science, commercial success)
- Vatican insider (escorted [[diana-pasulka]] to Vatican archives without an escort; flashed multiple three-letter-agency credentials on demand)
- Roswell material handler (brought a piece of recovered crash material and placed it in [[chris-bledsoe]]'s hand)
- Time travel group member (per Ryan Bledsoe, Taylor claimed membership in a secret group in Nassau, Bahamas, of which [[thomas-townsend-brown]] was the president)
- "The Hammer" operative (Taylor told Bledsoe he works for an entity called "the Hammer" — unknown whether human, institutional, or NHI)

## The Obama Camp David Napkin

[[grant-cameron]] posted a photo on Facebook showing an envelope addressed to [[chris-bledsoe]] and a napkin partially obscuring the address. [[bob-maguire]] identified:

1. **The address** — Chris Bledsoe's address.
2. **The napkin** — bore the **presidential seal of the United States** from Camp David. Copying the presidential seal is a federal crime; such napkins exist only at Camp David and can only be sent by someone with access to the White House.

Bob's conclusion: the only person who could have both the napkin and knowledge of Bledsoe's story is Tim Taylor, who had the access and was the conduit between Bledsoe and the president. The letter therefore constitutes evidence that Bledsoe's prophecies were transmitted all the way to **Barack Obama**.

## Roswell Material Encounter

Taylor brought a piece of the 1947 Roswell crash to the Bledsoe family and placed it in Chris's hand. Bledsoe reacted unexpectedly strongly. Taylor's response — "Why you?" — was curious and not negative, suggesting Taylor expected something to happen but was surprised by the intensity. Ryan Bledsoe also handled the material. Multiple Bledsoe family members reportedly have unusual abilities; Taylor may have been testing for a specific sensitivity.

## Vatican Access

[[diana-pasulka]] documents that Taylor showed her Vatican connections — escorted her to the Vatican Observatory and Vatican archives, admitted without an escort and without her presence (she was not allowed to accompany him inside). He flashed multiple sets of intelligence credentials. This places Taylor inside Vatican classified archival access — an extraordinary level of institutional reach for a NASA Mission Controller.

## Time Travel Group in Nassau

Ryan Bledsoe relayed that Taylor claimed membership in a **secret time travel group** headquartered in Nassau, Bahamas, and that [[thomas-townsend-brown]] was the president of that group. Townsend Brown is documented to have spent significant time in the Bahamas and to have been privately obsessed with gravitational time travel. Bob Maguire's observation: gravity and time are coupled in general relativity; Brown's anti-gravity work and his time travel interest are coherent as a single research program.

Bob cannot confirm the group's existence but notes that "nothing else makes sense" given the convergence of documentation.

## "The Hammer"

Taylor told Bledsoe he works for something or someone called "the Hammer." No one in Bob's network knows who or what the Hammer is. The nature is explicitly open: Bob says he doesn't know "if it's human, NHI, or what." This remains the deepest unresolved mystery around Taylor.

## The Charles Hall Connection

[[charles-hall]] and his wife [[marie-theres-hall]] knew Taylor for approximately **15 years**. Taylor is the only significant third-party corroborating presence in Hall's account — a pattern consistent with Taylor's role as a "validator" across multiple experiencer cases.

Key details from [[QgxjtDS2sIQ]]:
- Taylor visited the Halls multiple times; brought others including "David King" and a doctor (possibly [[kit-green]])
- Taylor flew the Halls to Atlanta, presumably to a gathering at "Green's house"
- Taylor attended a **ribbon-cutting ceremony at Creech Air Force Base** (formerly Indian Springs Air Base — the exact location of Hall's Tall White contact). Taylor was reportedly **warned not to drive up the road** toward the former Tall White base area
- After ~15 years of sustained relationship, Taylor went silent without explanation. Both Hall and his wife note this abrupt disappearance. Host [[jesse-michaels]] speculates Taylor "went off with them somewhere"

The Creech detail is striking: Taylor's specific interest in Indian Springs and subsequent unexplained silence parallels his pattern with Bledsoe and Pasulka — deep engagement, then abrupt withdrawal.

Taylor also appears in *American Cosmic* under the pseudonym "Tyler D," where Diana Pasulka identifies him as having led her and [[garry-nolan]] to a UAP crash site in New Mexico.

## Dragon Control — Cape Canaveral

[[chris-bledsoe]] (firsthand) reveals that Taylor's NASA station is called **Dragon Control**. Every launch from Cape Canaveral is covered by a surveillance chain:
1. Ground-based articulating cameras to ~50,000 ft
2. **WB-57** (NRO-affiliated) intercepts at 50,000 ft with 7 cameras; covers to ~100,000 ft
3. **F-104 Starfighter** (7–8 cameras) intercepts and covers to optical limit
4. **NRO satellites** take over

At orbit insertion Taylor enters a **private room** with access to all satellite feeds. Only he has eyes on what approaches the rocket. The NRO connection is primary — Bledsoe says the NRO (not CIA or Air Force) holds the keys to UAP classification.

Bledsoe was required to get a **dragon tattoo** to access the base. Taylor also worked at [[aerospace-corporation]], and his name came up in connection with [[elon-musk]] (who runs Dragon capsule launches from the same facility).

Per Taylor: *"For your rocket company to succeed, you have to know what they want"* — the "sponsors" (NHI) are an active variable in launch outcomes.

## "I Work for the Hammer"

Bledsoe heard this directly from Taylor:
> *"I work for the Hammer and the Hammer works for God."*

He pointed to *The Adjustment Bureau* (Philip K. Dick story) as the best analog: the Hammer is an archangel-level figure who handles what lesser beings cannot. The Adjustment Bureau's premise — agents modifying small variables to nudge timelines — is how Taylor understood his own role in reality.

## Vivex Biotechnologies

Taylor founded [[vivex-biotechnologies]], a biotech company sold for ~$88M. The company's viograph scaffold technology reportedly derived from properties of recovered UAP material. [[chris-bledsoe]] served as an intuitive download source — a carpenter spending years reading scientific papers and drawing cellular diagrams weekly. The first human bone-cancer operation using the viograph was performed on a Spanish woman in the US, with complete bone regeneration in ~60 days.

## Linda Brown's Biography

Taylor was actively **helping [[linda-brown]] write her biography of her father [[thomas-townsend-brown]]** — Bledsoe has the partial manuscript saved on his phone. A 2010s Amazon review of Taylor's *[[launch-fever|Launch Fever]]* autobiography by Linda Brown confirmed their connection before any public awareness of Taylor. Taylor confirmed NHI had visited Townsend Brown directly (*"things landing in his yard and getting out and giving him information"*).

## The Zanesville Connection

Taylor escorted Bledsoe to **Zanesville, Ohio** (Townsend Brown's birthplace, 1905) to recover a glowing red metal from a local source. Taylor kept a piece; Bledsoe later brought [[hal-puthoff]], [[tom-delonge]], and [[lue-elizondo]] to the same source for additional pieces. This physically connects Taylor to Brown's birthplace and to the UAP material chain.

## Camp David — Bledsoe's Firsthand Account

Bledsoe confirmed in [[bM18PJY6_Zc]] that Taylor told him **directly**: *"I was at the dinner table with the president"* — and handed him the napkin at Christmas, just back from Camp David. This removes any doubt about the napkin chain. [[bob-maguire]]'s independent identification of the Camp David presidential seal remains the corroborating forensic detail.

## Consciousness Protocols (tS_64sTN5AU)

Pasulka gives more detail in [[tS_64sTN5AU]] about Tyler's information-reception protocol:

- **Sleep**: prioritized heavy sleep to maximize bio-readiness
- **Hydration**: ensured he was well-hydrated; rationale was that the body is ~80% water and water conducts electricity — if there is a bio-electric signal component to the downloads, hydration matters
- **Receptive state**: upon waking, he would enter a specific state of receptivity — what Pasulka describes as feeling "like a signal coming in" — from which he would extract information used to found biotech companies and develop innovations
- Pasulka began practicing these same protocols and found them effective for managing her own cognitive load

The protocols are described in the context of [[john-mack]]'s abductee research and the broader question of whether contact experiences are more parapsychological than "nuts and bolts."

## Vatican Conversion (tS_64sTN5AU)

Pasulka describes in [[tS_64sTN5AU]] the specific sequence leading to Tyler's conversion at the [[vatican-observatory]]:

1. "Father Kay" took the group on hospital rounds — Tyler encountered a terminally ill young man who had dedicated his life to others; he began to internalize and become reflective
2. At the Observatory, ~30 young ESA scientists were present unexpectedly; they recognized Tyler and the room responded warmly
3. Tyler examined Copernicus's first-edition books
4. He "got overcome with the epic nature of what was happening" and decided he no longer cared if he returned to the Space Program — his only desire was to help people
5. His interpretation of the "signals" he received shifted: he came to believe they were **more in line with what the saints experienced** than with his prior secular-technological framing
