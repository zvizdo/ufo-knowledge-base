# Tim Taylor

A NASA Mission Controller and biotech entrepreneur (made ~$100M on orthopedic instruments) who keeps a very low public profile. [[jesse-michaels]] identifies him as the single person he most wants to interview. Taylor sits at the intersection of multiple operationally incompatible domains — credentialed aerospace, Vatican archival access, classified-program custody of Roswell material, time-travel-adjacent program membership, and a self-described role under an unknown entity called "**the Hammer**."

Appears in [[diana-pasulka]]'s *American Cosmic* under the pseudonym **"Tyler D"** — the figure who led Pasulka and [[garry-nolan]] to a UAP crash site in New Mexico.

## Documented Roles

- **NASA Mission Controller** — runs the [[dragon-control-program|Dragon Control]] console at [[cape-canaveral]], an alleged classified surveillance chain monitoring NHI interaction with every cape launch.
- **[[aerospace-corporation]] employee** — FFRDC tied to [[nro]] satellite acquisition; co-affiliation that places Taylor at the institutional locus of US space-based UAP custody.
- **Biotech entrepreneur** — founder of [[vivex-biotechnologies]], the bone-scaffold company sold for ~$88M whose viograph technology reportedly derived from recovered UAP material (with [[chris-bledsoe]] as intuitive input channel).
- **Vatican insider** — escorted [[diana-pasulka]] to the Vatican Observatory and Vatican archives; admitted without an escort (she could not accompany him inside); flashed multiple sets of three-letter-agency credentials on demand.
- **Roswell material handler** — brought a piece of recovered crash material from the [[1947-roswell|1947 Roswell crash]] to Chris Bledsoe's North Carolina home and placed it in his hand.
- **[[nassau-group]] member** — Taylor told Ryan Bledsoe he is a member of the secret time-travel group headquartered in Nassau, Bahamas, of which [[thomas-townsend-brown]] was the president.
- **"Hammer" operative** — see below.

## "I Work for the Hammer"

Taylor told [[chris-bledsoe]] directly:

> *"I work for the Hammer and the Hammer works for God."*

Taylor pointed to *The Adjustment Bureau* (the 2011 film adapted from [[philip-k-dick]]'s 1954 short story *The Adjustment Team*) as the closest analog to his actual function: an agent who makes deliberate micro-adjustments to timeline variables on behalf of a higher authority. He told Bledsoe: *"That movie was created for a reason — watch it. It's a lot like that."* See [[adjustment-bureau]] for the full framing.

No one in the research network around Bledsoe and Bob Maguire knows who or what the Hammer is. The nature is explicitly open: human institution, NHI entity, or other. This remains the deepest unresolved mystery around Taylor.

## Vatican Conversion

[[diana-pasulka]] describes in [[tS_64sTN5AU]] a specific sequence in which Tyler underwent a personal religious shift at the [[vatican-observatory]]:

1. "Father Kay" led the group on hospital rounds — Tyler encountered a terminally ill young man dedicated to others; he began to internalize the encounter.
2. At the Observatory, ~30 young ESA scientists were unexpectedly present; they recognized Tyler and the room responded warmly.
3. Tyler examined Copernicus's first-edition books.
4. He became *"overcome with the epic nature of what was happening"* and decided he no longer cared whether he returned to the Space Program — his only desire was to help people.
5. His interpretation of the "signals" he received shifted: he came to believe they were **more in line with what the saints experienced** than with his prior secular-technological framing.

The personal protocol Pasulka describes — heavy sleep, deliberate hydration, a specific receptive waking state in which information arrives "like a signal coming in" — is documented at [[consciousness-as-technology]] as the most granular first-person account of an individual's NHI-contact protocol in this KB.

## Roswell Material Encounter

Taylor brought a piece of the [[1947-roswell|1947 Roswell crash]] to the Bledsoe family and placed it in Chris's hand. Bledsoe reacted unexpectedly strongly. Taylor's curious response — *"Why you?"* — suggests he expected something to happen but was surprised by the intensity. Ryan Bledsoe also handled the material. The episode is consistent with Taylor's broader pattern of testing experiencers for specific sensitivities to recovered material.

## The Camp David Napkin

Taylor allegedly mailed [[chris-bledsoe]] an envelope containing a Camp David presidential-seal napkin after dining with President Barack Obama. Bob Maguire identified the seal; Bledsoe later confirmed that Taylor told him directly *"I was at the dinner table with the president"* and handed him the napkin at Christmas. See [[2014-obama-camp-david-napkin-incident]] for the full incident record.

## Pattern of Engagement and Withdrawal

Across multiple experiencer cases, Taylor follows a consistent pattern: deep engagement (often 10–15 years), then abrupt unexplained silence:

- **[[charles-hall]] and [[marie-theres-hall]]**: 15-year relationship; Taylor brought visitors including "David King" and a doctor (possibly [[kit-green]]); flew the Halls to Atlanta; attended a ribbon-cutting at Creech AFB (formerly Indian Springs Air Base — the exact site of Hall's Tall White contact) and was warned not to drive up the road toward the old Tall White base area. After ~15 years, Taylor went silent without explanation. [[jesse-michaels]] speculates Taylor *"went off with them somewhere."* See [[charles-hall]] for the full account.
- **[[diana-pasulka]]**: Sustained engagement during *American Cosmic* fieldwork, then reduced contact post-Vatican conversion.
- **[[chris-bledsoe]]**: Sustained relationship across Dragon Control access, Roswell material, Camp David napkin, and Zanesville pilgrimage; Taylor's contact has reportedly tapered.

The Creech AFB visit is structurally important: Taylor's specific interest in Indian Springs and the subsequent silence parallels his exit pattern with the Halls.

## Brown Biography Collaboration

Taylor was actively helping [[linda-brown]] write her biography of her father [[thomas-townsend-brown]]; Bledsoe has the partial manuscript saved on his phone. A 2010s Amazon review of Taylor's *[[launch-fever|Launch Fever]]* autobiography by Linda Brown independently confirmed their connection before any public awareness of Taylor. Taylor confirmed to Bledsoe that NHI had visited Brown directly — *"things landing in his yard and getting out and giving him information."* Taylor also escorted Bledsoe to [[zanesville-ohio|Zanesville, Ohio]] (Brown's birthplace) to recover a glowing red metal from a local source; pieces went to [[hal-puthoff]], [[tom-delonge]], and [[lue-elizondo]] via Bledsoe's facilitation.
