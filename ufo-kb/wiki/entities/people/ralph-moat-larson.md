# Ralph Moat Larson

Former CIA case officer and intelligence executive with one of the most unusual profiles in the knowledge base: blue-chip mainstream credentials combined with explicit claims of time travel, prophetic dreams, and Marian apparitions. Author of *A State of Mind: Faith in the CIA*.

## Background

Larson attended West Point and entered the US Army as an armor officer, spending time on the Czech border during the late Cold War. He joined the CIA in 1983, becoming a European case officer specializing in Soviet affairs. He completed two tours in Moscow — one in the Soviet era and one as **Moscow Station Chief** after the USSR's collapse.

His career spans three phases:
1. **1983–2001** — Classic espionage; running assets in Europe and the Soviet Union; arms control intelligence
2. **2001–2005** — Post-9/11 WMD counterterrorism: Tenet appointed him head of the **WMD department in the Counterterrorism Center**; directly briefed Presidents Bush and Clinton, VP Cheney, NSA Hadley, FBI Director Mueller, NSA Director Hayden. Task: prevent al-Qaeda from acquiring biological or nuclear weapons
3. **2005–2016** — **DOE Director of Intelligence and Counterintelligence 2005–2008**; then returned to Middle East work 2011–2016

His wife also served as a CIA officer throughout most of this period; she wrote *Story of a CIA Wife*.

## Notable Claims

### UAP / DOE Non-Awareness
Larson was head of intelligence at the Department of Energy during 2005–2008 — a period during which UAP incursions at nuclear sites were being documented by [[robert-hastings]] and others. His testimony:

- "I never was briefed on any of that" (referring to UAP incursions at nuclear sites)
- Offers two possible explanations: handled in a separate compartment that he wasn't individually read into, or less to it than claimed
- Confirms DOE's extreme compartmentalization: "someone will tap you on the shoulder" for the most sensitive programs; individual read-ins, not role-based access
- "If I knew that we had evidence of extraterrestrial visits to Earth, I'd go to the press" — frames this as a moral certainty, implicitly validating it as a real question
- "I swear to God I know nothing about UFOs" — said explicitly in the interview

On structure: he provides a clear breakdown of DOE as three pillars: (1) **Energy** (renewables, nuclear power, advanced energy); (2) **Pure Science** (national labs, chaos theory, quantum, exotic physics); (3) **NNSA** (semi-autonomous nuclear weapons complex). Intelligence service feeds all three but is not the same as program access.

### Prophetic Dreams
Larson has a documented pattern of dreams that he argues are predictively accurate:

- **Sadat assassination (1981):** Dreamed the event in real-time; details and timing matched news exactly. Claims to have seen faces of Ayman al-Zawahiri and Seif al-Adl — both later implicated and went on to lead al-Qaeda
- **Star Wars dream (1982):** Dreamed CIA craft controlled via a quantum supercomputer in CIA's underground; a planetary shield dividing protected from unprotected populations, partly dealing with UFOs. Reagan gave his SDI speech two years later
- **Numbers download (1976):** Received 747 and cubes of 3/6/9/12/27/40/49; his German girlfriend independently had the same cube-numbers in a dream the same night without having been told

### Time Travel at Mount Athos (1991)
During a CIA field operation at [[mount-athos]] (investigating a monk-cover for a Russian intelligence officer), Larson fell asleep in a stone courtyard and entered a prolonged vivid dream.

The dream sequence: 1800s Paris → met a woman suffering in a brothel (retroactively identified as Virgin Mary); met Harry Hiller (Hermann Hesse's *Steppenwolf* character); time decelerated backward to the 14th–15th century as he "traveled" to Mount Athos; lived as a medieval monk under the identity "Odius Maximus"; became a recluse; learned to fly; burned his hut; experienced three dreams-within-the-dream (numbered tickets → seven-door judgment room → river crossing/faith test).

He says the experience was physically indistinguishable from waking reality, even within the dream he knew it was a dream. He says he "felt trapped" and wasn't sure he could wake up.

Corroborating discovery (2005): He found the ski *Kafsokalivion* at Mount Athos — named for **[[maximos-kafsokalivitis]]**, a real historical monk known for burning his huts and reported levitation. Larson had no prior knowledge of this figure when he had the dream.

### Virgin Mary Apparitions
- **1991** — Mount Athos dream: old woman in brothel; later red light moving through the church seen after waking — confirmed by monk Timotheos as "the light of the Virgin Mary"
- **~1995** — Portugal: mental contact, no visual; wrote "keep all that in mind as you go forth"
- **1998** — Paris CIA work trip: dream of old woman by a river → woke to see brilliant light resolving into full Virgin Mary image in hotel room; received message "write a letter to the pope"; entered physical ecstasy state; later sent the letter and received acknowledgment from the Vatican

### Intelligence Career Highlights
- 1993: Proposed joint CIA-SVR secret operation to [[yevgeny-primakov]]: CIA keeps Rabin alive, SVR keeps Arafat alive — route to Middle East peace. Never implemented
- 2003: Briefed President Bush on Abu Musab al-Zarqawi's camp location, 3 weeks before the Iraq invasion; Bush asked his personal recommendation; Larson said "bomb it" (against official guidance); camp was not bombed
- Camp David 2000: Observed Clinton tell Arafat "take the deal, Yaser — it's your last best chance for peace"; felt instinctively it wouldn't work
- Assessed Putin as anomalously self-assured at Camp David 2000; predicted he would be more consequential than anticipated

## Open Questions

- The compartmentalization model Larson describes (individual read-ins for the most sensitive programs) is consistent with the scenario where UAP programs exist within DOE without the intelligence director being aware — but it is also consistent with no such program. His non-awareness neither confirms nor refutes.
- The synchronized number download (1976, wife independently) is the single strongest external corroboration of his mystical-experience pattern.
- Was his 1982 "CIA craft + quantum computer + shield" dream a prophetic preview of the Strategic Defense Initiative, or a post-hoc reconstruction? The two-year gap is notable.

> ⚠ **Conflict**: Mowatt-Larssen's categorical denial ("a thousand percent publicize") conflicts directly with [[jim-semivan]]'s and [[chris-mellon]]'s accounts of active crash retrieval and reverse engineering programs in "The Age of Disclosure." Both sides have significant IC credentials. The resolution space is: (a) programs are compartmented beyond even very senior officials, (b) Mowatt-Larssen genuinely has no access and is truthful, (c) Semivan/Mellon are exaggerating or operating on hearsay. This is unresolved.

## UAP Denial (jMCavr3dVP0 Interview)

In a later interview (host Jesse Michaels, [[jMCavr3dVP0]]), Mowatt-Larssen stated categorically that he has no knowledge of any UAP crash retrieval or reverse engineering programs, and that if he did, he would "a thousand percent publicize it." This parallels [[eric-haseltine]]'s "Clockwork Orange" exposure position — both are senior IC officials who claim the most sensitive UAP programs are compartmented beyond their reach. The contrast with [[jim-semivan]] and [[chris-mellon]]'s active-reverse-engineering claims creates a significant unresolved conflict.
