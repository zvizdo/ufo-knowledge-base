# Nelson Dellis

Six-time US Memory Champion with a university background in physics and computer science. Described by [[chris-ramsey]] as "left-brained" — a highly analytical, genuinely curious individual, not a typical psi enthusiast.

## Bridge Figure: Memory → Remote Viewing

Dellis became aware of [[remote-viewing]] through a Facebook memory-enthusiast group, where a hedge fund reached out seeking people to train in **Associative Remote Viewing (ARV)** for stock market prediction. The mechanism: a viewer is not asked to remote view "up" or "down" but instead to describe an unknown target from a binary pair, one of which has been assigned the market outcome. This removes the viewer's knowledge of what they are predicting, eliminating feedback bias.

He was trained by [[brett-stewart]] — a professional remote viewer retained by the hedge fund — one hour per day for one month. He subsequently introduced the practice to Chris Ramsay, who documented the sessions for his YouTube channel. The results were sufficiently anomalous — correct gestalt descriptions appearing repeatedly — that Ramsay started a new channel ([[area-52]]) and entered the UAP research space.

## Significance

Dellis is a data point for the thesis that psi ability is not confined to self-selected "believers." His analytical background and documented skeptical baseline make his strong ARV results harder to dismiss than those of a motivated True Believer. His pathway — elite cognitive performance → ARV → anomalous results — mirrors the government Stargate Program's interest in testing exceptional cognitive performers rather than self-identified psychics.
