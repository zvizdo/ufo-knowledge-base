# Ted Owens

Self-described psychic known as the "PK Man." Claimed to receive anomalous powers from two mantid non-human intelligences he called **"Twitter and Tweeter."**

## Challenger Prediction

The most verifiable element of Owens' claims: he wrote letters to official parties predicting the **Space Shuttle Challenger disaster** before it occurred on January 28, 1986. The letters are documented. [[jason-reza-jorjani]] treats this as genuine anomalous data requiring explanation rather than dismissal.

## Airline Deaths Claim

Owens claimed he caused deaths aboard specific airline flights — a claim Jorjani notes as part of a pattern of claimed psychokinetic harm that is difficult to verify but difficult to categorically dismiss given the Challenger documentation.

## Mantid Connection

Owens' case is central to Jorjani's analysis of mantid entities:
- The mantid form consistently appears in accounts of anomalous power transmission to humans
