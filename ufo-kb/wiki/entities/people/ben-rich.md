# Ben Rich

President of Lockheed Skunk Works (the classified advanced-aerospace division of Lockheed); successor to Kelly Johnson. Rich is a secondary figure in UFO/UAP discourse primarily because of his cryptic public statements about UAP technology and his institutional position at the apex of classified aerospace development.

## Notable Quote

> "UFOs are unfunded opportunities."

— Rich's characterization of UAP, as reported by [[danny-sheehan]]. Sheehan interprets this as reflecting Lockheed's institutional position: the existence of UAP/alien technology is an opportunity for reverse-engineering contracts, but only if the technology remains in private hands.

## Background: Stealth Development

Rich was hired by Lockheed as a thermodynamicist, then moved to Skunk Works. In 1975 he succeeded [[kelly-johnson]] as director. His key contribution: resuscitating the work of an obscure Russian mathematician from the 1960s named Ufimtsev, who developed equations for predicting the reflection of electromagnetic waves — the mathematical foundation for stealth aircraft design. This led to the F-117 stealth aircraft.

Rich described to [[nick-cook]] how Skunk Works had "contemplated burying" the stealth program entirely — treating stealth capability the way nuclear physics was suppressed after the Manhattan Project. Rich: "We would have boxed this thing up... slid it into a warehouse that would have resembled the one at the end of Raiders of the Lost Ark." The idea was abandoned, and stealth proceeded.

Rich's predecessor [[kelly-johnson]] was reportedly anti-stealth at the end of his career, putting him at odds with Rich on this question.

## The ESP Answer

At a UCLA speech in the 1990s, toward the end of his life, Rich made enigmatic statements about Lockheed Skunk Works having the technology to "take ET home" and travel across star systems. [[nick-cook]], who attended and interviewed Rich multiple times, asked how such technology would work.

Rich's response: **"Let me ask you a question: how does ESP work?"** Cook: "I don't know." Rich: **"All points in space and time are connected. That's how it works."** Then he turned around and walked away.

Cook's assessment: "He talked in parables. He talked in analogies. He made some very enigmatic statements."

## Deathbed Statement

Rich's deathbed statement to **John Andrews** (Testors Motor Corporation):

> "We already have the means to travel among the stars, but these technologies are locked up in black projects, and it would take an act of God to ever get them out to benefit humanity."

## Rob Weiss Connection

Rich's successor at Skunk Works, **[[robert-weiss]]**, collaborated with [[tom-delonge]] on the *Secret Machines* books and got deeply involved in To the Stars Academy — particularly when DeLonge and Peter Levenda started talking about Celestial Ascent practices and Kabbalah. Cook notes this as part of the broader pattern of Aerospace interest in consciousness and parapsychological phenomena.

## Sheehan's Interpretation

Sheehan uses Rich as an emblem of the defense-contractor position on UAP disclosure:
- Contractors *want* broad public awareness that UAP (and potentially reverse-engineered technology) exists — because congressional awareness drives appropriations.
- Contractors *oppose* government eminent domain provisions (like those in the Schumer Amendment) that would allow the government to reclaim recovered materials from private custody.
- The fight over the [[schumer-uap-amendment]] — specifically the stripping of the eminent domain clause by [[mike-turner]] (House Intel Chair, Lockheed's top donor) — is the direct political expression of this position.
