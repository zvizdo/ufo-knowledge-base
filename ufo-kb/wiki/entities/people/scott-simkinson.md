# Scott Simkinson

One of the first engineers hired by NASA. Known internally by the nickname "Scotty" — [[kevin-knuth]] speculates (tongue in cheek) that this may have influenced the name of the Scotty character in *Star Trek*, which was created in the same era. Simkinson worked on spacecraft design and maintained a personal archive of NASA files with his secretary Emily Erdle.

## Connection to UAP

After Simkinson and Erdle died, their personal NASA archive was made public and went to auction. **Ed Wilson** purchased the collection and discovered among the files a **lithograph** — a printed photograph — labeled on its face: *"Strange object as seen by Gemini 11 astronauts."*

[[kevin-knuth]] analyzed the lithograph:
- The image shows Earth with a specific cloud pattern in the background and a structured UFO object
- Knuth found the original photograph in University of Arizona archives — same cloud pattern, **no UFO object**
- His conclusion: the lithograph is likely a **mock-up or artist's rendition** of what the astronauts reported seeing, not an actual photograph; NASA transcripts say the strange object encounter occurred ~one orbit after the tether experiment depicted in the background
- The presence of such a lithograph in Simkinson's personal files implies **institutional NASA-level awareness** that the Gemini 11 astronauts had a genuine encounter — even if it was commemorated rather than photographed

Knuth: "If there's a painting of a UFO in that photo and it's found in his records, that implies there's almost institutional NASA knowledge of the fact that the Gemini 11 astronauts did see a UFO."
