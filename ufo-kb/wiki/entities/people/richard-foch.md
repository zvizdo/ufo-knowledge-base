# Richard Foch

Described by [[steven-greer]] as "the top scientist at the Naval Research Labs" who, while alive, privately disclosed to Greer that he had been inside the classified vault and seen the documents recording the date the US program mastered gravity control: **October 1954**.

Greer identifies him as "Rick Foch (FOC)" and states he has since died, which is why Greer feels able to name him in [[areO7Mej44E]].

## Claim

Foch's single attributed disclosure is the date: **October 1954** as the moment the US mastered electrogravitic / gravity-control propulsion. This date is significant because:
- It postdates the 1945 retrieval of the Nazi electrogravidic disc brought back by [[paul-mellon]]
- It predates the wave of triangular and advanced-craft sightings from the late 1950s forward
- Greer uses this date to argue that the majority of post-1955 UFO sightings involve man-made craft, not extraterrestrial vehicles

## Corroboration Status

Hearsay from Greer's private contact with Foch. No independent corroboration. The [[naval-research-labs]] connection is plausible but unverified. Greer did not provide documentation.
