# Marco Rubio

US Senator from Florida, former Acting Chairman of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence. Rubio was the key congressional architect of the first legislative UAP mandate: in June 2020 he inserted a provision into Senate Intelligence Committee report language requiring the IC and DoD to produce a UAP assessment — the directive that resulted in the June 2021 Preliminary Assessment and set the institutional chain leading to [[aaro]].

[[mick-west]] publicly predicted in June 2020 that "years will pass, it will recede in memory, little will come of it." [[marik-von-rennenkampff]] now cites this prediction as a benchmark for how rapidly the institutional landscape shifted.

## Key Actions

- **June 2020**: Embedded UAP reporting provision in Senate Intelligence Authorization Act language
- **Repeated public statements**: Characterized firsthand witnesses to alleged UAP programs as "credible people with high clearances in high positions" who "fear for their lives"
- **Ongoing**: Cited as one of several congressional figures — alongside [[kirk-mcconnell]] and others — who are convinced of programmatic reality
