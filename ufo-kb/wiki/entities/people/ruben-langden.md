# Ruben Langden

Organizer of the **[[citizen-hearing-on-disclosure]]** (2013), described by [[jeremy-corbell]] as "the unsung hero of the citizen hearing." Langden created the first large-scale simulation of congressional UAP testimony — a mock hearing at the National Press Club in Washington D.C. featuring former members of Congress and UAP witnesses — before the format became routine.

## Citizen Hearing and Bankruptcy

The 2013 Citizen Hearing went significantly over budget. Corbell describes Langden as subsequently going bankrupt due to "misappropriation of funds and everything that was going on." The financial collapse left Langden in personal crisis.

## Oscar Wolf Interview Chain

After the bankruptcy, Langden approached Corbell: "I need help. I need a brother." Out of this desperation came an act of significant evidentiary value: Langden connected Corbell to [[oscar-wolf]] through his personal network, and arranged the deathbed interview with [[richard-dolan]] conducting it. Without Langden's connections and willingness to act despite his circumstances, the Wolf testimony would likely not have been recorded.

Corbell characterizes this as the paradigm case of how important UAP evidence surfaces through informal networks of people under stress rather than institutional channels.
