# Coral Lorenzen

UFO journalist and researcher active in the early 1960s, considered by the UFO research community of her era to be highly credible: "if she put something out there, it was thoroughly researched and there was substance to it."

## Holloman Reporting

Lorenzen wrote articles for the UFO Bulletin about a landing at [[holloman-air-force-base]] around the time of the [[1964-socorro]] case. Her sources included anonymous military officials and a pilot — two separate articles about the same event.

[[james-fox]] describes encountering her reporting while deep in the Socorro case investigation. The geographic proximity of Holloman and Socorro, combined with Lorenzen's credibility, was what first made Fox seriously consider the Holloman landing claim. Until reading Lorenzen, he had dismissed the Holloman reports entirely.
