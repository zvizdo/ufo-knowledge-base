# Gordon Gray

## Background

Gordon Gray served as Special Assistant for National Security Affairs to President Eisenhower — effectively the predecessor role to the modern National Security Advisor. A lawyer and former newspaper publisher from North Carolina, he previously served as Secretary of the Army.

Gray was a founding member and key figure in the **Psychological Strategy Board** (PSB), a Truman/Eisenhower-era interagency body that coordinated psychological warfare and information operations. The PSB is identified by [[matthew-pines]] as the institutional architecture that formalized the US government's approach to managing UAP information — including both suppression from the public and weaponization against Soviet adversaries.

## Psychological Strategy Board

The PSB comprised Gray, [[charles-douglas-jackson]], and [[philip-corso]] among others. Their mandate was explicitly to coordinate psychological operations; the extension of this mandate to UAP information — treating UAP knowledge as both a domestic suppression problem and a foreign-facing psyop asset — follows naturally from the [[walter-b-smith]] CIA memo of the same era.

Gray's involvement places the UAP classification architecture squarely within the formal national security policy apparatus of the Eisenhower administration, rather than in informal ad hoc arrangements. This is the machinery that [[harold-malmgren]] was operating around during his multi-administration career.

## Kissinger Connection

[[henry-kissinger]] consulted directly for Gray at the [[psychological-strategy-board]] in 1951, 1952, and 1955, placing Kissinger within Gray's institutional orbit while still a Harvard graduate student. Gray was the patron who drew Kissinger into the core psychological warfare apparatus. This connection is seen as one of the pathways through which Kissinger may have been read into UAP-related programs.

## Gravity Research and UNC Chapel Hill

While Gray was president of the University of North Carolina (1955–61), UNC's board of trustees — with his final approval — sanctioned a **proposal to establish an Institute of Pure Physics** to study and control gravity, as reported in a November 1955 *New York Herald Tribune* article ("Conquest of Gravity Aim of Top Scientists in United States"). This institute became the **Institute of Field Physics at UNC Chapel Hill**, which in 1956 hosted the **Chapel Hill Conference** — gathering all the world's top theoretical physicists to discuss gravity, sponsored by **Wright Field** (Wright-Patterson Air Force Base). The institute's director, Agnew Bahnson, also funded [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s gravity manipulation experiments.

Gray's sanctioning of gravity research at UNC — while simultaneously having oversight of the PSB's UAP psyop framework and being named in MJ12 documents — creates an unusual convergence of interests that researchers have noted as non-coincidental.

## MJ12 Connection

Gray is named as one of the advisers on the top-secret research and development organization established by Truman, as it appears in the leaked Majestic 12 documentation. His deep ties to military, psychological warfare, government, and academia — on top of the UNC gravity research connection — make a very plausible case that he was read into high-level UAP programs.
