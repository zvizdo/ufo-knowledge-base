# Diane Powell

Harvard-trained neuroscientist who conducted the primary research underlying [[ky-dickens]]'s *[[telepathy-tapes]]* documentary. Powell documented controlled experiments with non-verbal autistic children demonstrating apparent telepathic abilities — with hit rates typically at 90%+ that, in Dickens' description, "shatter" skeptical objections. Her medical license was subsequently revoked, though the specific basis for the revocation is not fully detailed in the source.

## Background

Medically trained at Harvard. Background in neuroscience. Researched the intersection of autistic savant abilities and anomalous cognition — apparently documenting the phenomenon of non-verbal autistic individuals reading others' minds in controlled settings before [[ky-dickens]] approached her.

Her trajectory is compared explicitly to [[john-mack]]: a mainstream, credentialed academic who crossed into anomalous research and faced institutional consequences.

## Notable Claims

Claims derived from Powell's research as reported by [[ky-dickens]]:

- Non-verbal autistic children achieve 90%+ hit rates in controlled telepathy experiments — identifying images, numbers, objects held in the mind of a facilitator separated by blindfold and physical barrier.
- The phenomenon is statistically significant and replicable across multiple subjects.
- The results are publishable in scientific literature; Powell appears to have published or attempted to publish.
- Powell's interpretation connects to [[consciousness-non-local]]: the autistic subjects appear to access a consciousness field rather than engage in conventional sensory perception.

## PEER Institute

Following [[john-mack]]'s death in a traffic accident in London in 2004, colleagues at his memorial urged Powell to take on and continue his Program for Extraordinary Experience Research ([[peer-institute]]). Powell subsequently found a house in Oregon whose construction date matched Mack's birthday — a synchronicity she found meaningful. She now stewards the institute Mack built as a haven for anomalous-phenomena research outside mainstream institutional control.

## Scalar Waves Discussion

In [[0qlppHc3-gg]] Powell engages Jesse Michaels's questions about [[scalar-waves]] and extended electrodynamics as a candidate mechanism for the telepathic transmission observed in non-speaking children. She identifies the scalar hypothesis as "a very interesting idea" and cites specific neurological regions — the caudate nucleus and putamen adjacent to the brain ventricles — as potentially the first encoders of the scalar-field signal.

## Strieber Corroboration (ABOP8ZJsyIk)

[[whitley-strieber]] independently confirms the pattern of institutional persecution Powell faces, explicitly linking her situation to [[john-mack]] and [[roger-leir]] as a recurring dynamic: credentialed researchers who cross into anomalous territory face coordinated career destruction. Strieber attributes the persecution pattern to forces connected to [[collins-elite]] and defense department actors.

Strieber also connects Powell's research on non-verbal telepathic autistic children directly to his own encounters with NHI hybrid beings — he describes encountering a hybrid woman at a grocery store who communicated telepathically. He sees Powell's autistic subjects as potentially displaying the same non-verbal telepathic faculty as the hybrid beings.
