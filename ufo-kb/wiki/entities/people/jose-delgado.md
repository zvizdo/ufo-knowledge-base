# Jose Delgado

Dr. Jose Delgado (1915–2011) was a Spanish-born neurosurgeon at Yale University, funded by the CIA and multiple branches of the US military. He is most notable for developing **remote-controlled brain electrode implants** and for his published advocacy for using this technology on the entire human population.

## The Technical Achievement

Delgado's key technical advance: you did not need a wire connecting the electrode to an external controller — a **remote transmitter** could activate the electrode from a distance. This meant a person with an implanted electrode could move freely through the world while their behavior was being remotely modulated.

Published experiments documented in medical journals include:
- A **16-year-old girl** with an electrode implant whose behavior cycled between normal, frenzied wall-pounding, and catatonia depending on which electrode was activated via remote transmitter — photographed in medical journals
- **Bulls** stopped mid-charge in the arena by remote activation of an electrode (Delgado entered the arena and stopped a charging bull by pressing a button)
- **Monkeys** with remote-controlled behavioral modifications
- Documented in **mainstream medical journals** (Ross notes he has published in the same journals)

## *Towards a Psycho-Civilized Society*

Delgado published a book arguing that the solution to social violence and dysfunction was to **implant electrodes in the brains of the entire population** — excepting the lead generals and top politicians. He framed this as creating a "psycho-civilized" society. [[colin-ross]] notes this was "stated in the book clearly."

## CIA / Military Funding

Delgado was funded by both the CIA and multiple branches of the US military simultaneously — placing him in the same top-tier of MK Ultra's cleared research community as [[martin-orne]] and [[jolly-west]].

## UCLA Violence Project

During Reagan's governorship of California, [[jolly-west]] was involved in the **UCLA Violence Project**, which proposed implanting electrodes in the brains of released sex offenders: when a subject moved outside their allowed geographic perimeter, a signal would be sent to **temporarily paralyze** them. The project was shut down before implementation, but Delgado's research provided the technical foundation for this proposal.

## Connection to Andrija Puharich

[[andrija-puharich]] and Delgado were close personal friends. Letters between them survive from the 1960s period. The correspondence itself was not particularly sinister — mostly coordinating to attend the same conferences — but the closeness was notable. Puharich operated his covert company [[intellect]] during the same decade as Delgado's most significant bull experiments, and filmmaker [[greg-malazzi]] believes the two were conducting adjacent and possibly connected research: Delgado on remote animal control via brain electrodes, Puharich on remote human thought-transmission via radio frequencies. Multiple people warned Malazzi not to pursue the Delgado–Puharich connection publicly.
