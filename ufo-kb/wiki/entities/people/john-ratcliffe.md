# John Ratcliffe

Director of National Intelligence under President Trump (2020–2021). Ratcliffe made a series of public statements about UAP that went further than most officials, including the assertion that UAPs "exhibit technologies that we do not have and cannot defend against." This is among the most significant on-record government acknowledgments of a UAP technology gap, coming from the nation's top intelligence official.

[[marik-von-rennenkampff]] cites Ratcliffe's statement as part of a cascade of official acknowledgments (alongside Rubio, Gallagher, and AARO director Kosloski) that constitute a coherent trajectory of institutional acceptance.

## Key Statement

> "UAP exhibit technologies that we do not have and cannot defend against."
— attributed to Ratcliffe in multiple media appearances, ~2021

This statement implies:
1. The objects are real (not misidentification or hoax)
2. Their performance exceeds known US aerospace capability
3. They represent a potential national security threat that cannot currently be countered
