# Harry S. Truman

33rd President of the United States (April 1945–January 1953). His presidency coincided precisely with the formative period of the modern UAP era and the construction of the national security architecture that UAP secrecy research argues has been used to conceal non-human intelligence contact.

**Key events during his presidency**:
- Kenneth Arnold sighting (June 1947) — the event that launched the "flying saucer" era in public consciousness
- [[1947-roswell]] (July 1947) — the crash and official retraction
- National Security Act of 1947 — created the CIA, the NSC, the Joint Chiefs of Staff, and the modern IC architecture
- Truman's Executive Order 9835 and subsequent security apparatus — established the clearance infrastructure under which UAP programs could be hidden
- 1947–1949: founding of Project Sign and Project Grudge as the Air Force's official UAP investigation programs

**MJ-12 allegation**: The disputed [[majestic-12]] documents purport to be a 1952 briefing for President Eisenhower, describing a secret group ([[majestic-12]]) that Truman allegedly authorized in 1947 to manage crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering. The documents include a purported Truman executive memorandum as an annex. Most document analysts consider the MJ-12 documents fabricated; their provenance is murky. No verified primary source establishes Truman's direct knowledge of recovered NHI technology.

**The 1950 press conference deflection**: Truman briefly addressed flying saucers at a 1950 press conference, giving an ambiguous deflection rather than a clear denial. Some researchers parse the phrasing as meaningfully non-denying; the mainstream view is that it was a standard political non-answer.

**Forrestal**: Truman's first Secretary of Defense [[james-forrestal]] broke down and died under disputed circumstances in 1949 — UAP researchers connect Forrestal's breakdown to his alleged knowledge of crash-retrieval programs and his later opposition to Truman's policies. The connection is speculative.
