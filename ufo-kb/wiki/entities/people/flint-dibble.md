# Flint Dibble

American archaeologist and professor who became publicly prominent through a 2024 debate with [[graham-hancock]] on the Joe Rogan Experience podcast. Dibble specializes in classical Greek and Bronze Age Aegean archaeology; his academic profile is that of a credentialed mainstream archaeologist without prior public presence in the ancient-civilizations debate.

**The Hancock debate**: Dibble and [[graham-hancock]] debated on the Joe Rogan podcast in early 2024 — a widely watched exchange given Rogan's massive audience. The debate covered Hancock's evidence for a pre-Göbekli Tepe advanced civilization, Younger Dryas comet impact evidence, underwater archaeology claims, and the mainstream archaeological response to each. Hancock describes Dibble as "a very intelligent individual who was well prepared" but characterizes Dibble's primary objective as winning the debate rather than mutual truth-seeking. Dibble subsequently claimed to have "destroyed" Hancock on social media, which Hancock disputes.

**Institutional posture**: Hancock uses the Dibble encounter as a case study in "how archaeology functions when it deals with people with alternative points of view" — arguing that the discipline's primary response to outsider challenges is reputational dismissal rather than evidence engagement. The debate's outcome in public perception (rather than in academic literature) illustrates the [[credibility-frameworks]] tension: Dibble held institutional authority; Hancock held a large popular audience and specific archaeological evidence claims.

**Separate context (Biondi)**: Dibble also made YouTube critiques of energy researcher Biondi's "Beyond Protocol" project — raising patent expiry and heat-at-depth objections. Biondi addressed these objections in subsequent responses ([[NvQXmtcwHA8]]). This is a separate track from the Hancock debate.

**KB significance**: Dibble appears in the KB primarily as the institutional-archaeology representative in the ancient-civilizations debate — not as a UAP figure. His significance is methodological: how mainstream credentialed experts respond to paradigm-challenging evidence, and what the debate format reveals about credibility and evidence-assessment in public discourse.
