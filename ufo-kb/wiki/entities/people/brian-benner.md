# Brian Benner

Politico journalist included in [[james-fox]]'s documentary *The Program* (2024). Benner's contribution is primarily analytical — commenting on the structural dynamics of Congressional UAP oversight rather than providing firsthand UAP testimony.

## Background

Benner covers national security and government for Politico. His appearance in *The Program* represents Fox's effort to include mainstream media and political journalism perspectives alongside UAP witnesses and whistleblowers.

## "Congress Can't Keep a Secret"

Benner's most-cited line from the documentary: **"Congress can't keep a secret."** The observation frames a core tension in the disclosure debate — Congressional oversight is one of the primary mechanisms proposed for forcing declassification and accountability, but legislative bodies are inherently porous. Classified briefings get leaked, members talk to constituents, and staff turnover is constant.

This comment was highlighted by Fox in his Joe Rogan interview as a notable moment from the documentary.
