# Filippo Biondi

## Background

Italian researcher who developed the [[beyond-protocol]] — a novel application of synthetic aperture radar (SAR) Doppler tomography using **phonon detection** (surface micro-vibrations) to image the interior and substructure of ancient sites. Research began ~2018; first major paper published 2022; presented publicly at the Malta 2024 conference. Uses data from the Italian [[cosmos-skymed]] satellite system.

His work received massive viral attention in 2025. A direct-to-camera interview with [[jeffrey-drum]] and [[jesse-michaels]] in [[NvQXmtcwHA8]] provides the most detailed technical account of his methodology and findings.

## Core Claims

Eight cylindrical tubular structures detected below the [[giza-plateau]]:
- ~20 m diameter each
- Extending from the bedrock surface to an estimated 600 m–1 km depth
- Present below all three pyramids and the [[great-sphinx]]
- Connected at depth to cubic/block formations
- The [[osiris-shaft]] detected with ~4 m margin of error (partial validation)

The signal tapers off at approximately 600 m depth. Biondi says the cause is "something I can't disclose it now" — implying defense/commercial constraints on the satellite data.

## Grand Sasso Proof of Concept (Malta 2024)

The strongest validation of Biondi's technology: the [[gran-sasso-laboratory]], a major underground physics facility 1.4 km below the Apennines (GERDA/MAJORANA particle experiment), was successfully imaged using a superior noise-averaging processing technique. The resulting images clearly show internal structures and are described as "transparent like a crystal."

This processing was not applied to the original Giza pyramid scans because it required ~2 months of compute time on a borrowed high-performance machine. Biondi acknowledges the quality gap directly: "transparent like a crystal" in the Gran Sasso scan vs. noisier pyramid results. He intends to apply equivalent processing once the Malta foundation's computing infrastructure is built.

## Future Plans

- **Malta Foundation**: non-profit being established in Malta with a solar-powered data center; intended to host an array of GPUs for real-time or high-quality pyramid substructure processing; seeking philanthropic funding ("millions")
- **Shaft-cleaning proposal**: being submitted with University of Ferrara and other academic partners to the Egyptian government — propose using robots to clear vertical shafts on the Khafre causeway as entrance points to the underground system
- **Collaboration with Jeffrey Drum**: Biondi accepts Drum's chemical manufacturing thesis, Dunn's power-plant thesis, and his own underground city interpretation as potentially complementary

## Criticisms and Conflicts

> ⚠ Conflict: Earlier framing in [[92XrddiKjHY]] describes the alternative community as "uniformly skeptical." [[NvQXmtcwHA8]] shows [[jeffrey-drum]] engaging with Biondi's data seriously and finding it partially credible. The community is divided, not uniformly opposed.

1. **Known chambers not detected**: the Queen's Chamber and King's Chamber (known structures) are absent from the scan. Biondi attributes this to granite density interference for small chambers.
2. **Validation gap**: the Gran Sasso-quality processing was not applied to the pyramid dataset. The original images have significant noise and ambiguity.
3. **Signal cut-off unexplained**: the ~600 m depth cut-off lacks a scientific explanation in the published material.
4. **Methodology (earlier critique)**: the original 3D models apparently superimposed King's Chamber geometry from a different pyramid onto Khafre's — Biondi did not address this directly in [[NvQXmtcwHA8]].
5. **AI framing**: earlier "AI infallibility" language critiqued by [[luke-caverns]] — not raised in [[NvQXmtcwHA8]] where Biondi presents more modestly.

[[graham-hancock]] texted Jesse Michaels: "I have no reason to believe the source here is being honest." This was in the context of the 2025 viral social media phase, not the direct technical interview.

## The Phonon-Photon Boundary Technique

In [[zrmG2jBckgk]], Biondi explains the physics more precisely than in earlier interviews:

- Radar (photons) cannot penetrate earth — only superficial interaction per classical Maxwell theory
- **Phonons** (vibration quanta) propagate inside matter; photons in free space
- At the **boundary** (earth surface), both are present simultaneously
- SAR satellites flying at ~7 km/s generate a generous Doppler bandwidth; the earth's surface vibrations frequency-modulate the returned signal
- By extracting vibration information from the surface boundary, tomographic inversion is possible to great depth
- Technique used successfully for: mining surveys, volcanic monitoring (Vesuvius paper with National Research Council of Italy), and the Gran Sasso validation

Biondi is explicit: **no AI involved.** The pipeline is: measurements → physical model → pseudo-inversion = tomographic slices.

## Water and Piezoelectric Hypothesis

New in [[zrmG2jBckgk]]: Biondi's working hypothesis about the spiral structures:

> "Maybe the water goes through these spirals, and the spirals are made by piezoelectric material. And while the water flowing through the spirals, are exciting this piezoelectric material that generates electricity."

He connects this to the work of [[giuliano-preparata]] and [[emilio-del-giudice]] on coherent quantum domains in water, and to cold fusion physics — though notes he is "not interested in cold fusion" per se, only passionate about the underlying physics.

## Similar Structures at Menkaure and Sphinx

New confirmation in [[zrmG2jBckgk]]: the 4+4 tube pattern at Khafre is not unique:
- **Menkaure pyramid**: 2+2 tubes — smaller version of the same pattern
- **Great Sphinx**: single cube structure descending beneath it
- All interconnected via a tunnel network
