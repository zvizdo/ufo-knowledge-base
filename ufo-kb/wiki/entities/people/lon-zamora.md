# Lonnie Zamora

Police officer in Socorro, New Mexico. On April 24, 1964, Zamora witnessed a landed egg-shaped craft and two small beings in white coveralls near a dry gully outside town. The craft emitted a roar and flame, then departed. Military officers documented the landing site including physical impressions and what a diagram in the Project Blue Book files depicts as beings' footprints.

## Significance for Hynek

[[j-allen-hynek]] received word of the Socorro case almost immediately after [[jacques-vallee]] had visited him at Wright-Patterson AFB and urged him to look into the "psychological" category reports in Blue Book files — those describing beings associated with craft. The Socorro case arrived within days. It is credited as the case that caused Hynek to do a "complete 180" on Close Encounters of the Third Kind — transitioning from dismissal to genuine investigation.

## James Fox's Research

[[james-fox]] is deeply invested in this case. He:
- Visited the National Archives with author Jay Sanford (who wrote the book on the case — possibly "Saucer in the Pentagon Pantry" or similar)
- Retrieved original Project Blue Book documents including a military diagram of the beings' footprints
- Became acquainted with Zamora's family
- Investigated in proximity because the case is geographically close to Holloman AFB (the alleged landing site he was also pursuing)

Fox describes finding the footprint diagram in the National Archives as a visceral confirmation moment: "Right there in black and white."

## Note on Carral Lorenzen

[[coral-lorenzen]] — a journalist considered highly credible in UFO research circles — wrote contemporaneous articles about a Holloman AFB landing around the same period, based partly on anonymous military officials and a pilot. Fox connected the two cases geographically (Holloman and Socorro are close) and credits Lorenzen's reporting as the first credible account of the Holloman event he encountered.
