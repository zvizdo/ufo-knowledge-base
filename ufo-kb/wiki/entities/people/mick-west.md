# Mick West

Retired video game programmer who transitioned into skeptical investigation after retirement. Starting with chemtrail conspiracy research and Wikipedia editing (~2006), he built [[metabunk]] into the primary public forum for structured UFO/UAP video debunking. His analytical approach — developed from video game debugging, plane-tracking, and mathematical modeling — became the main skeptical counterweight to the 2017 NYT-era UAP discourse.

## Background

West's entry into UAP came via the 2014 Chilean Navy video (released by [[leslie-kean]] in 2016–17), which he identified as a distant plane with exhaust plumes — a conclusion now widely accepted. This led him to investigate the [[2015-gimbal]], FLIR1, and [[2015-gofast]] videos released alongside the December 2017 NYT article.

He exchanged extensive emails and Zoom calls with [[marik-von-rennenkampff]] early in the latter's research, a process Merrick credits (ironically) with deepening his conviction that the videos were genuine anomalies rather than West's explanations.

## Analytical Position

### Gimbal Video
West argues the rotating "glare" in [[2015-gimbal]] is an optical or digital artifact — either a "glare" phenomenon or the result of de-rotation software (referenced in a FLIR patent). His position: the object is a distant jet, the shape is a camera artifact, and the rotation is the de-rotation system compensating for aircraft bank. Air-to-air infrared experts consulted by Merrick called the rotation "bizarre" and rejected both the SR-71 comparison and the glare explanation, though West disputes the framing of those expert opinions and notes they were preliminary impressions based on limited data.

### GOFAST / FLIR1
Acknowledges disagreements with Merrick on some videos in this family; broader agreement on the Chilean Navy case.

### The "Crappy Video Problem"
West's meta-argument: the consistent low quality of UAP footage is itself diagnostic. If better footage existed and showed something anomalous, it would be unambiguous. Since only ambiguous footage circulates, this is a selection effect — not evidence of evasion or suppression. He calls this the "low information zone."

### UAP Evasion Claim
West characterizes the claim that UAPs evade cameras as unfalsifiable: "They're evasive, but they appear 100 ft away where you'd be able to get wonderful 4K video if you had your camera with you. They're not really evasive. They're just evading cameras."

## Stated Priors

Accepts that alien life almost certainly exists somewhere in the universe. Does not see good evidence for visitation of Earth. Would not rule it out but requires hard data. Actively wants AARO to release the "true anomaly" footage Kosloski described.
