# Bryce Zabel

Bryce Zabel is a television writer-producer and UFO author. He co-created the 1990s drama series **Dark Skies** (one season), which explored UFO secrecy and [[majestic-12]] machinations in a fictionalized framework. He co-authored *[[after-disclosure]]* with [[richard-dolan]], a book examining what public and institutional life would look like in the immediate aftermath of formal UAP disclosure.

## Dark Skies

*Dark Skies* was a 1990s network television series dramatizing the MJ-12 structure and the government's management of extraterrestrial contact. While fiction, Dolan regards the series as notable for its level of seriousness about the subject matter — an attempt to work through the disclosure scenario in a cultural format.

## After Disclosure

*After Disclosure*, co-written with Dolan approximately 15 years before the GQlWf54K_7Y interview (~2010), explored the sociological, political, economic, and psychological implications of governments formally acknowledging the UFO reality. The book addressed questions like: How do existing institutions respond? What happens to religion? How do financial markets react?

[[richard-dolan]] references Zabel in passing when discussing the book's framing of post-disclosure society.
