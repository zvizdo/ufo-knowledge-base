# Jean Seberg

## Background

Jean Seberg (1938–1979) was an American actress best known for her role in Jean-Luc Godard's *Breathless* (1960). She was also an active supporter of the American civil rights movement and made financial contributions to the Black Panthers — activities that drew FBI surveillance under COINTELPRO.

## The FBI Image Cheapening Operation

The FBI's COINTELPRO program maintained a documented technique called **"image cheapening"** — planting false or damaging stories in mass media to destroy the public credibility of targets. Seberg is the most prominent documented victim.

According to [[hal-puthoff]] in [[iQOibpIDx-4]], and per the historical record:

- The FBI planted a story with journalist **Joyce Habber** in the *Los Angeles Times* falsely claiming that the baby Seberg was carrying was not her French husband's child, but the child of a Black Panther.
- The fabricated story, published while Seberg was pregnant, led to a **miscarriage** (likely stress-induced).
- Seberg suffered **ten years of suicide attempts** following the publication.
- She died in 1979 in what was ruled a probable suicide.

Puthoff uses this case to illustrate the institutional capability of the US government to deploy media smear campaigns — and draws the parallel to UAP-adjacent figures and programs.
