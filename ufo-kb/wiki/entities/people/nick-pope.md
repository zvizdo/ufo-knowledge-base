# Nick Pope

British journalist and former UK Ministry of Defence official who ran the MoD's UFO desk from approximately 1991 to 1994. Pope is one of the most publicly prominent former government UAP officials in the UK and a key contact in [[james-fox]]'s disclosure network.

## Background

Pope ran the MoD's UFO research section, formally known as Secretariat (Air Staff) 2a, for approximately three years in the early 1990s. During this period he investigated and filed reports on thousands of UFO cases submitted by the British public and military.

## Calvine Photograph

Pope is a key link in the history of the [[1990-calvine-photograph]]. He:
- Had a large poster of the Calvine photograph hanging on the MOD wall during his tenure
- Told James Fox about the Calvine case in the late 1990s — the first time Fox became aware of it
- Described the Calvine photo to Fox as "the best case he ever saw" in terms of a single photographic UAP document
- Published the first public account of the case in his book *Open Skies, Closed Minds* — the first time the Calvine case received public mention

## Disclosure Prediction (~2008–2009)

Pope was interviewed by Fox around 2008–2009 (around the time of a National Press Club event Fox organized with [[leslie-kean]]). Pope's prediction: "It's going to be disclosure with a small D, not a big D — it's going to trickle out slowly, you condition people, get people prepared, test the waters." Fox describes this prediction as accurate in retrospect.

## Publications

- *Open Skies, Closed Minds* — first public mention of the Calvine case; overview of his MoD UAP research tenure

## MoD Career — Extended Detail (B6WFLOIkyho)

21 years total at the UK Ministry of Defence ([[uk-ministry-of-defence]]), 1991–1994 at the UFO desk (Secretariat Air Staff 2a). The operation was a **one-person operation with admin support**, investigating 200–300 cases per year with access to radar specialists, JRIC imagery analysts, Met Office, Royal Greenwich Observatory, and astronomers.

Pope was constrained to "play it very straight" publicly — focusing on defense, national security, and pilots/radar cases even when he received abduction and high-strangeness reports. This self-censorship was "one of the most uncomfortable things of all." He drew a direct comparison to Project Blue Book: both were public-facing programs while classified work occurred in parallel.

Post-desk, he stayed at MoD for 21 years total, then became de facto BBC UFO correspondent and History Channel commentator. Currently runs the "Ancient Aliens Live" US tour (80+ dates) and speaks at events like Contact in the Desert.

## Personal Encounter — Virginia Beach (Firsthand, B6WFLOIkyho)

Pope discloses a personal UAP sighting:
- Location: Virginia Beach area, East Coast US
- Setting: nighttime jog along the beach
- Craft: "translucent and a purplish color — looked like a stingray but without the tail"
- Altitude: approximately 100 yards overhead (possibly less)
- Pope associates the encounter with "time slip" and "missing time" phenomena discussed in that segment
- He did not disclose this encounter during his MoD tenure

## 1993 Cosford Investigation

Pope personally investigated the [[1993-cosford-incident]] — a wave of UK sightings including a triangular craft that caused a driver to chase it with his screaming family, a farmer whose cows formed a ritual circle, and a London art shop with MIB-adjacent visitors who displayed money-confusion behavior. The shop attendant subsequently experienced missing time and "seeing somebody that looked like herself from the future." Pope describes this cluster as "the fringe encounter that still kind of troubles me to this day."

## Calvine — Poster, JRIC, and CGI

Pope submitted the 6 Calvine photographs and their negatives to JRIC ([[jric]]). The formal government conclusion: the craft was real, approximately 75 feet across, solid and structured, of unknown origin. He also commissioned a CGI recreation with an LA graphic artist — result was "pretty similar to the Craig Lindsay photo." The best Calvine image was blown up to poster size and hung in his MoD office "for years."

## Rendlesham Book — Dual Clearance

Co-authored a Rendlesham book with [[jim-penniston]] and [[john-burroughs]] requiring simultaneous security clearance review from both the UK MoD and the US government — the only book in Pope's experience to require this dual-nation clearance.
