# Robert Cutler

Robert Cutler Jr. served as Special Assistant to President Eisenhower for National Security Affairs (1953–1955 and 1957–1958), functioning as executive secretary of the National Security Council (NSC). He managed the flow of national security information to and from the President.

## UAP Relevance

In November 1957, during the Levelland, Texas UFO wave, [[wladyslaw-krasuski]] — a Polish POW who had observed a large circular aircraft near Berlin in 1944 — mailed a letter to Cutler at the White House describing his wartime experience. The FBI Director responded to Cutler's receipt of this letter by issuing an URGENT teletype to SAC Detroit ordering an immediate interview of Krasuski (FBI Case 100-26505).

Cutler's name also appears in disputed MJ-12 documents (the "Cutler-Twining Memo" of July 1954, purportedly originating from the NSC). The authenticity of this memo is debated; its relevance here is that it places Cutler at the center of alleged UAP-related NSC policy during the Eisenhower period.
