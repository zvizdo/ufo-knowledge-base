# Julia Mossbridge

Researcher cited by [[ky-dickens]] in the context of *[[telepathy-tapes]]* for her work establishing that **love** — specifically, the quality of emotional attunement and unconditional love between people — is an enabling variable for psychic and anomalous cognitive abilities. This finding aligns with the observed patterns in non-speaking autistic children, who demonstrate telepathic abilities most consistently with primary caregivers in loving, sacrificial relationships.

## Relevance to the Telepathy Tapes

Mossbridge's work provides a theoretical bridge between the controlled experimental observations and the family context: the 90%+ hit rates documented by [[diane-powell]] appear in child-caregiver dyads characterized by unusually deep love and sacrifice. Mossbridge's research suggests this is not coincidental — love may be both the activating condition and the channel through which non-local information access occurs.

[[ky-dickens]] frames this as consistent with what every family in the documentary reports: the telepathic connection is most vivid with the person the non-speaker loves most, and the first words many non-speakers communicate telepathically to teachers are instruction to "tune into my heart."
