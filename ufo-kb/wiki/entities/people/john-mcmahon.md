# John McMahon

## Background

John N. McMahon was a career CIA officer who served as Deputy Director of Central Intelligence in the 1980s under [[bill-casey|Bill Casey]]. He was previously Deputy Director for Operations and Director of the National Foreign Assessment Center.

## Notable Claims

### Operationalizing Stargate — "Do a Soviet Site"

[[hal-puthoff]] (Gf_tKn9TaP8) names McMahon as the official who pushed [[stargate-program|Stargate]] beyond scientific demonstration into espionage tasking. After [[pat-price|Pat Price]]'s accidental description of [[sugar-grove-naval-station|Sugar Grove Naval Station]] — a US classified NSA target produced from blind coordinates — McMahon said: "Okay, well, let's not waste it on our science for God's sakes. Do a Soviet site."

He provided coordinates for an R&D facility at [[semipalatinsk|Semipalatinsk]] in the Soviet Union. Price described a giant crane rolling over the top of a building. Satellite imagery later confirmed the description. This was the moment, per Puthoff, when the National Security Council and CIA leadership declared internally: "Okay, this stuff is real. It can be used. Let's go to work with it."
