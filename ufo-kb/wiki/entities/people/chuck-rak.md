# Chuck Rak

One of the four witnesses in the [[1976-allagash-abduction]]. During the encounter, while [[charlie-foltz]] signaled SOS to the craft and the hollow conical beam began to extend toward the canoes, Chuck went into a catatonic state — completely unresponsive — and remained so through the visible phase of the encounter.

He is not a primary speaker in [[QkuDvTFN7ms]]. His account survives primarily through [[raymond-fowler]]'s regression transcripts, which are described as broadly consistent with the other three witnesses on the fundamental structure of the encounter.

Of the four witnesses, Chuck is described as having undergone the most visible personality change following the event.
