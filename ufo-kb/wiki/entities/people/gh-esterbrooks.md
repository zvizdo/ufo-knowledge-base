# G.H. Esterbrooks

G.H. Esterbrooks was a psychologist at Colgate College (upstate New York) who is documented as the earliest known developer of the operational **"super spy"** hypnotic technique — a compartmentalized personality implanted in a military subject that could be triggered with a code phrase.

All of the following is documented in primary sources cited by [[colin-ross]]:

## The "Moon is Clear" Technique

Esterbrooks described in the published literature a specific operational technique:

1. Select a susceptible subject
2. Put them through conditioning (unspecified in public writings)
3. Implant a hypnotic trigger phrase: **"the moon is clear"**
4. The implanted alternate identity activates on the trigger
5. The army colonel gives the alternate identity its assignment (e.g., carry documents to Tokyo)
6. The subject travels to Tokyo in normal identity
7. The officer on the receiving end says "the moon is clear" — the alternate identity delivers its message and receives its response
8. Switch back to normal identity; subject returns home with no memory of the exchange

This exact same mechanism — verbal trigger, alternate identity, amnesia — appears in the CIA Bluebird/Artichoke documents describing experiments on secretaries and military personnel.
