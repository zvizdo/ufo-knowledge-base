# Chuck Schumer

US Senate Majority Leader (D-NY). Schumer co-authored the Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Disclosure Act of 2023 with Senator [[mike-rounds]] (R-SD), a bipartisan amendment to the NDAA that called for mandatory declassification of UAP records within 25 years and the creation of a review board modeled on the JFK Records Collection Act. The amendment was significantly weakened before final passage.

The Schumer-Rounds UAP Disclosure Act is considered the most significant legislative push for UAP transparency since AATIP, and its weakening in conference has been cited by researchers including [[danny-sheehan]] and [[dan-farah]] as evidence of continued suppression.
