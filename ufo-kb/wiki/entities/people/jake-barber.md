# Jake Barber

## Background

Jake Barber is a crash retrieval witness who publicly disclosed his account of participating in recovery operations for non-human craft. His testimony is notable for being among the most operationally specific accounts combining physical craft retrieval with firsthand psionic experience.

### Origin Story and Recruitment (dnnpyNuPdXs)

Barber describes a recruitment pipeline that he believes was designed to find children with a specific profile — what he calls "the four eyes": imagination, intellect, intuition, and instincts. His own profile: abusive stepfamily environment (physical, verbal, borderline sexual abuse by stepfather and stepbrother); Navy grandfather in the Scouts and Raiders (Seals precursor); Army grandfather; state science fair winner (color harmony project); IQ of 138 standard / **152 under stress-IQ test** (oxygen-deprivation protocol — he scored *higher* under stress). He was expelled his senior year; grandfathers arranged military placement.

Recruited ~1994 at age ~17 into the **GTE (Guaranteed Training Education)** program — a 10-year Air Force contract that fast-tracked him through Combat Control (Air Force Special Warfare) with 2.5 years of specialized training guaranteed from day one. After completing selection at Operating Location Hotel (OH) — graduating 9 from a class of 89 — Barber was mid-pipeline when two individuals approached him and suggested he become an airplane mechanic. This was his **cover and gateway**: Combat Control was the entry point; the actual program was different.

Posted to **Pope Air Force Base, North Carolina** after mechanic school. Deployed to Bosnia, participated in Clinton-era operations, later became a **red team expert** (opposition force, "OP-4") — charged with exploiting weaknesses in blue-force military exercises. Red team work brought him into exercises adjacent to UAP subject matter and honed the operational deception skills ("the first rule of war is deception") he now openly applies as a public whistleblower.

Air Force was, per Barber, *by design* the agency straddling the "blue/black line" since its formation in July 1947 — two weeks after the [[1947-roswell]] incident, simultaneously with the National Security Act, CIA, and DoD.

### Current Roles (as of December 2024)

Director of operations for helicopter company; program director for Gracie Jiu-Jitsu NorCal; flight instructor for airborne law enforcement; president of a corporation manufacturing wheelchair parts for veterans hospitals; services fitness centers on military bases. These roles establish Barber's continued operational and physical capability context.

## Notable Claims

### Helicopter Retrieval of Two Craft

Barber describes participating in retrieval operations involving two distinct craft:

1. **Egg-shaped craft** — recovered via helicopter; ~20 ft; flies at ~10,000 mph in dead silence; demonstrated post-program in broad daylight (August, exact year unclear). Retrieval profile: GPS coordinates received at last second; brief en-route readout of what's being picked up; ground crew with flashes; landing as if it's any other piece of equipment. In the [[dnnpyNuPdXs]] interview Barber describes the moment of encountering it: "there's just flying a helicopter, come over ridge, there's an egg sitting there."
2. **Eight-gon (octagonal) craft** — disc-shaped, 25–30 ft, dark matte gray/black, hard angles and textured panels, a spine structure. Biologics aboard (per hazmat disclosure at the scene). Emits a radiological signature — Barber became gravely ill (hair loss, skin peeling on arms, nausea) after a separate escort mission involving an unidentified heavy object; [[garry-nolan]] is now treating him for residual effects.

The retrieval methodology — helicopter-based, rapid-response to GPS coordinates — is consistent with accounts from other witnesses of standing retrieval teams operating under classified contractor or military oversight. Compare with the broader [[reverse-engineered-craft]] custodian chain.

### Psionic Consciousness Lock (Eight-Gon Craft)

While hovering near the octagonal craft via helicopter at night, Barber states he experienced an **involuntary consciousness connection** — an overwhelming flood of emotion he describes as "divine feminine," loving, and deeply sad (like a minor-key harmony). This was progressive: began at approach, intensified to the point of nearly incapacitating him mid-flight while carrying the craft back to the drop site. Ended immediately after deposition.

Two interpretations Barber holds simultaneously:
- The **craft's consciousness** connected to him — suggesting it is a sentient or semi-sentient object with projective capability.
- A **psionic asset's consciousness** was trapped inside the craft and reached out through Barber's proximity — consistent with the P3 program's use of psionic humans as biological components.

This is the most detailed firsthand account in the current KB of the **consciousness-interface mechanism** in [[psionics-uap-interface]].

### Psionic Methodology (dnnpyNuPdXs — most detailed account to date)

Barber describes the psionic asset training pipeline with more specificity than in earlier sources:

- **Goal**: Lower intellect → raise intuition → reintroduce intellect as *observer* not *driver*
- **Methods**: Stress inoculation (triggering fight/flight), meditation, dissociation (chemical or meditation-based), environmental satisfaction (not hungry, cold, afraid, tired)
- **Preferred candidates**: Children — naturally lower intellectual dominance, higher intuitive access, more visceral responses
- **Alternative path**: 30 years as a Shaolin monk achieves the same state; "easier cheats exist"
- **Warning**: "Some cheats have not fared well for those involved — including children." (Barber does not elaborate further in this interview.)
- **Two classes of summoning proven to work**: machine-based (signals and energy systems) and man-based (psionic operators via consciousness field)

Barber frames this explicitly as non-fringe: "like night vision goggles — it's there, we use it."

### Psionic Assets and Summoning

Barber's broader testimony (as characterized by [[matthew-pines]]) includes the claim that government-trained psionic operators can **summon UAPs into a field of consciousness** — implying active rather than passive interaction. This is consistent with [[steven-greer]]'s CE-5 protocol claims but from a classified military-operations framing rather than a civilian consciousness-advocacy framing.

### Radiation Exposure and Medical Care

During an earlier escort mission for unidentified "mystery boxes," Barber was exposed to what he believes was radiation — losing all body hair, skin peeling off arms, severe nausea. He was not informed of the hazard; no nuclear hazmat protocol was in place. Barber does not blame Air Force intentional negligence: "I don't think they knew what we were dealing with." He is now receiving care from [[garry-nolan]] for what are likely ongoing residual effects.

### New Jersey Drone/UAP Investigation (December 2024)

[[sky-watchers]] was deployed to [[pikatini-arsenal]] as part of a DoD/FBI task force. See [[2024-new-jersey-drone-flap]] for full account. Key Barber contributions:
- Orange orbs appeared within 30 minutes of arrival — assessed as likely conventional drones cycling lights
- Separate UAP-class phenomena (vertical atmospheric entry/exit) observed south of arsenal
- Government at "highest levels extremely confused"
- Barber's threat matrix: false flag, genuine Chinese actors, genuine NHI, or a mix

### Relationship with David Grusch

Barber explicitly positions himself and [[david-grusch]] on different ends of the same system: Grusch is at the "high-end administrative end," having interviewed 40 witnesses over 4 years and filed with the ICIG; Barber is at the "fingertips" — operational retrieval. They met *after* Grusch filed his complaint. Barber was *not* one of Grusch's 40 ICIG witnesses. Barber's assessment of Grusch: "Dave's your guy. He knows where the bodies are buried."

Both are going through the Dobster process simultaneously — submitting documents for publication under a fiction-as-veneer mechanism that can later be confirmed as fact.

His account is corroborated in structure (if not in detail) by [[michael-herrera]]'s Indonesia incident, which similarly involves a psionic asset team interacting with a non-human craft in a military-operational context. Both accounts fit the strategic logic [[matthew-pines]] develops: a global footprint of psionic assets, deployed with retrieval teams, capable of interfacing with recovered craft.

## Red-Team Mission and Defection (D2tKCFmJjks)

Barber initially approached [[michael-herrera]] at the 2023 National Press Club event using an alias, under orders from legacy programs to **red-team/infiltrate whistleblowers**. He subsequently went rogue from those programs.

[[steven-greer]] connected Barber and Herrera via a late-night email. Herrera flew to California; was picked up in an armored vehicle; met Barber in a conference room where Barber used an RF wand to sweep the room. During subsequent interactions — including a desert trip — Barber provided key disclosures:

1. **The containers = [[p3-psionic-assets]]**: the refrigerated containers in Herrera's [[2009-indonesia-ufo]] held psionic humans, not drugs. Legacy programs refer to these individuals as **"pink assets"** — biological equipment.
2. **Russia/China gentleman's agreement**: all three powers have retrieval teams and reverse-engineered NHI technology; an informal agreement determines that whichever team first reaches a downed craft claims it; the others wave off.
3. **Craft are shot down**: legacy programs actively shoot down NHI craft; retrieval is Barber's specialty. Barber expressed moral discomfort about this practice.
4. **Global sites**: operations occur in Southeast Asia, Mexico, Africa, South America — not just the US.
5. **Blue-on-blue**: Barber's account is structurally consistent with [[kirk-mcconnell]]'s SCIF confirmation of a blue-on-blue firefight involving a Lockheed retrieval team and transport of humans in reverse-engineered craft.

[[ross-coulthart]] broke Barber's story publicly; [[donald-trump-jr]] podcast interview preceded the disclosure.

## Sky Watchers

After defecting, Barber founded [[sky-watchers]] — an initiative conducting NHI contact operations without drug cocktails, without treating psionic assets as cattle, and with an eventual public disclosure goal.

## John Blitch's Independent Validation

[[john-blitch]] (Lt. Colonel ret., Delta Force / DARPA) applied behavioral tradecraft to Barber's story: he met with **all four members of Barber's retrieval team simultaneously** on his boat, watching specifically for eye-contact coordination, behavioral micro-inconsistencies, and cross-talk between team members. He found zero red flags. His stated confidence: approximately **3-sigma (~99%)** that Barber's core claims are genuine.

Blitch also independently notes that Barber's ontological relief framing — "it's not ontological shock, it's ontological relief" — is one of his most important takeaways from Barber's public testimony, citing it as a message society is ready to receive.
