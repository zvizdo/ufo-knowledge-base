# Richard Shaver

Richard S. Shaver was an American author best known for the "Shaver Mystery" — a series of stories published in [[ray-palmer]]'s *Amazing Stories* magazine beginning in 1945. Shaver claimed the stories were factual: malevolent underground creatures he called "Deros" (degenerate robots) used ancient machinery to torment surface humans through rays and mental projections.

## War Department investigation request (August 1947)

In August 1947 — during the height of the national flying saucer wave — the War Department specifically requested through Col. Golbranson that the FBI investigate Shaver. An anonymous telegram had suggested Shaver as a possible source of information about the origin of flying discs.

The FBI ran a check on Shaver at his Lily Lake, Illinois address. No further development appears in the accessed sections of the file. The War Department's specific interest in Shaver suggests that military intelligence was tracking the overlap between contactee and underground-entity narratives and contemporaneous UFO reports as early as August 1947.

## Significance

Shaver's case represents the first documented instance in the FBI file of military/FBI interest in a contactee/channeled narrative specifically in connection with UAP reports. The pattern of monitoring contactee organizations later appears extensively in Section 10 with [[afsca]].

[[ray-palmer]] simultaneously published the Shaver Mystery stories and paid Kenneth Arnold $200 to investigate the Maury Island incident — placing Palmer at the intersection of both the underground-entity narrative and the physical-evidence UFO reports in mid-1947.
