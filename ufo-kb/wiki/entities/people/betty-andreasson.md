# Betty Andreasson

New England housewife whose abduction/contact experiences began in the 1960s and were investigated extensively by researcher Ray Fowler, resulting in multiple books. Her encounters are distinctive for their explicitly religious framing.

## The Encounter

Gray beings entered her home and froze her entire family in place. Betty and her daughter Becky were taken — Betty believed to a location underground or underwater (not space). There she was brought before **tall, angelic-looking Nordic beings** who delivered a theological message:
- They claimed responsibility for bringing **Jesus Christ** into the world
- They stated they would bring him back again
- The grays, they explained, were **a mass surveillance system** that records the deeds of all humans
- She witnessed small gray beings entering a brilliant light — which the Nordics described as "the one" and "the source" — saying she could not enter

## Significance

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] cites this case in [[EzPmG_7WhXc]] to illustrate the [[grays-nordics-hierarchy]] and the [[ufo-religion-convergence]] — specifically that NHI beings have presented themselves as architects of humanity's religious systems. The Andreasson case is unusual in explicitly mapping NHI onto Christian theology from the NHI side.

The grays-as-surveillance-system detail connects to the broader [[nordic-control-system]] thesis: the gray entities are not independent — they serve a monitoring and record-keeping function for the Nordic overlord class.

## Credibility Assessment

Investigated under hypnotic regression by [[ray-fowler]] — methodology carries the standard caveats about regression-era abductee testimony. Betty's accounts are internally consistent across multiple sessions and years.
