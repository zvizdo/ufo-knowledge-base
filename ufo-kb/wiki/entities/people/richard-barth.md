# Richard Barth

USAF airman (air policeman), assigned to the **4392 Combat Defense Squadron** at [[vandenberg-afb]], California, from approximately 1963–1964. He was stationed there under the selective service requirement after washing out of a USAF navigator training program due to undiagnosed dyslexia (number transposition under pressure). His guard duties included securing Minuteman, Atlas, Thor, and Titan missile sites and related cryptographic facilities.

## Background

Barth initially enrolled in college as a pre-med major, then joined the USAF aiming for navigator training. He failed two test missions due to dyslexia-related number transposition, was washed out, and served the remaining ~13 months of his selective service as a security guard at Vandenberg — chosen partly for its proximity to his Los Angeles home. He was discharged in June 1964, but the encounter occurred before that, in September 1964 (a timing discrepancy in his account worth noting: he says he was discharged in June but the encounter was September — possibly he re-enlisted or the September date is approximate; this is flagged as an open question).

> ⚠ Note: Barth states he was getting out in June 1964, but says the core encounter was September 1964. This inconsistency is unresolved in the interview. Robert Hastings has his DD-214 records; the service dates there would clarify.

He came forward after hearing [[robert-hastings]] on Coast to Coast AM (hosted by George Noory). Previously had attempted contact with [[robert-salas]], citing a "relationship" between their experiences. He was eventually put in contact with [[bob-jacobs]] by Hastings; he and Jacobs spoke directly about the September 1964 timeline overlap.

## The September 1964 Encounter

### Phase 1: Approach and Paralysis

Approximately 2:00am on a foggy night, stationed at a Minuteman missile site (D-sector, site D5), Barth saw a humanoid figure emerge from the fog toward his guard shack. The figure wore what appeared to be a military trench coat and cap — initially plausible as an officer. As it closed to within yards, Barth felt it taking control of his mind. His right arm was paralyzed — he could not draw his .38 pistol. He was also unable to scream. He backed into the guard shack until he was pressed against the rear shelf. Then he went blank.

### Phase 2: Aboard the Craft

Barth came to in a circular room with a concave floor (he was at the lowest point, on his knees). A transparent 2.5–3 foot circular aperture in the floor showed the base below. He was instructed via telepathic command (no words — "you just know") to look through the aperture and not look at the figure to his right. Through the aperture, now at near-dawn light, he observed site D11 across the street — a flatbed truck with long pipes arriving, the guard opening the gate. Four silhouetted NHI beings sat on a bench opposite him, the bench being a continuation of the wall material (suggesting molded, seamless construction). The room was grayish, dark, smooth, no seams or rivets — "one giant piece of gray plastic." Beings had large round heads (baby-proportioned, not pointed chins), proportionate necks (unlike typical grey depiction), large dark eyes. No bright lighting; the figures were back-lit and silhouetted. The four beings were observing him; they did not communicate. Then he returned to the guard shack floor.

### Phase 3: Return and Apology

Barth came to lying on the guard shack porch, feet on the ground, with a being sitting beside him holding his head on its lap. The entity communicated:
- *"I'm sorry, we didn't mean to scare you."*
- They needed access to the site — specifically the intelligence/data from the missile's electronics room (the guidance computer at the top of the Minuteman silo).
- Barth should keep his eyes shut; it would leave; he should stay still.

Then the entity left. Barth lay still for ~15 minutes with his eyes shut. When he opened them, the sun was rising. He felt a profound sense of love and connection looking at a chipmunk near his feet — described as ecstatic unity with all living beings.

### Phase 4: Return to Base and Delayed Memory

His relief arrived around 6:30–7:00am. Barth rode back in the pickup truck bed, silent and confused. By the time he reached the base, the memory had faded — leaving only a nagging sense that something profound had occurred. Approximately two weeks later, the full memory returned in a single vivid flash while he was waking in the morning — not a dream, clearly distinct from dream states. He simultaneously received (or recalled) an implicit command not to tell anyone, which he believes was NHI-installed.

## NHI Communication Content

The NHI entity communicated three substantive messages:
1. **Nuclear intelligence gathering**: They needed the data from the missile's guidance electronics — this was why they needed access to the site and why Barth was "in the way."
2. **Nuclear deterrence intent**: Earth is important to them. They will not allow nuclear war. If push came to shove, they would intervene.
3. **Multiple species**: Not every species is as benevolent as they are. Other groups exist; if they (the benevolent group) stop all the missiles, a conflict over control could follow — implying the benevolent group may not have unchallenged dominance.

## Physical Evidence

- **First metallic object**: A chrome-like metal ball with a flat side expelled from his nose while blowing into a handkerchief, years after the 1964 encounter. Discarded. Not analyzed.
- **2001 nasal surgery**: During a deviated septum operation, his surgeon encountered an anomalously hard object in his right nostril — said he had "never seen anything like it in years of practice." Object was suctioned out and discarded as biological anomaly. Not retained for analysis.
- Barth did not pursue X-rays or implant analysis; he was "not prepared to go there."
