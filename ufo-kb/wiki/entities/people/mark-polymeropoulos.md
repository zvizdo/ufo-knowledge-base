# Mark Polymeropoulos

CIA operations officer who became one of the most prominent public faces of the Havana syndrome / Anomalous Health Incidents (AHI) controversy. Appeared on CBS 60 Minutes, where he said he loves the CIA but that it "betrayed him" by denying his injuries were related to directed energy attacks.

## Case

Polymeropoulos suffered symptoms consistent with the AHI cluster — details reported on 60 Minutes. His case is notable because:
1. He is a career CIA officer with institutional loyalty, making his public criticism of the CIA's denial highly credible as a non-partisan account
2. His 60 Minutes appearance elevated the AHI issue into mainstream media coverage
3. His framing ("betrayed," not "wronged") implies he sees the denial as willful rather than ignorant — consistent with [[eric-haseltine]]'s political suppression hypothesis

## Significance for AHI Investigation

[[eric-haseltine]] cites Polymeropoulos's case as evidence of the split within the CIA between those who acknowledge AHI and those who suppress it for strategic reasons. His case, alongside the unnamed 60 Minutes government employee who quit over "we need to turn down the temperature on this," suggests internal CIA dissent about the official denial.
