# Murray Gell-Mann

Murray Gell-Mann (1929–2019) was an American theoretical physicist at Caltech, awarded the 1969 Nobel Prize in Physics for his work on elementary particles — specifically the quark model and strangeness quantum number. He later co-founded the Santa Fe Institute, known for complexity theory research.
