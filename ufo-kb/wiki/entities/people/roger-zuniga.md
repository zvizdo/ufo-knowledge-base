# Roger Zuniga

Biologist at the University of Ika in Peru involved in the scientific investigation of the Nazca mummies. His key conclusion is that the M-type specimens are "humanoid, not hominid" — meaning they share structural traits with humans but do not fall within known hominid taxonomy.

## Background

Affiliated with the [[university-of-ika]], one of the primary institutional bodies that has hosted and defended the ongoing Nazca mummy research against repeated attempts by Peruvian government authorities to seize the specimens.

## Notable Claims / Role

Zuniga's biological assessment directly challenges the mainstream dismissal of the mummies as fabricated or misidentified remains. His "humanoid, not hominid" framing is one of the more precise scientific characterizations offered in the investigation, distinguishing morphological humanoid structure from genetic or phylogenetic classification within known hominid lineages.
