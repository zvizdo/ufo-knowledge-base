# Jay Anderson

Jay Anderson is the host and creator of [[project-unity]], a YouTube channel focused on UFOs/UAPs, secret science, ancient archaeology, and government secrecy. He is a recurring participant in the broader UFO disclosure media ecosystem and appeared in [[51N8OxqZIWY]] alongside comedian [[kurt-metzger]] to trace connections between [[jeffrey-epstein]]'s physics funding and classified propulsion research, [[cern]] portals, and transnational blackmail power structures.

## Background

Anderson's [[project-unity]] channel covers a wide range of adjacent topics — from mainline UAP sightings to ancient pre-history and covert government programs. He is not a military whistleblower or insider; his approach is investigative journalism and synthesis of public and semi-public sources. [[robert-hastings]] is cited in this episode as one of the more credible researchers in the field.
