# John Blitch

## Background

Lt. Colonel (ret.) John Blitch is one of the most credentialed military figures to publicly disclose firsthand alien abduction experiences. His career spans U.S. Army Special Forces (Delta Force / 1st SFOD-D, Green Berets), a decade-long tenure as a DARPA Program Manager, a brief stint as VP of a SAIC robotics division, and graduate education in cognitive psychology and mathematics/computer science at the Colorado School of Mines. He attended West Point and describes racking up extensive "punishment hours" there.

He identifies as a member of the [[invisible-college]] — the informal network of researchers, military personnel, and insiders who share UFO-relevant knowledge outside official channels.

### Military Career

- **Delta Force / 1st SFOD-D** — Hostage rescue, direct action; described as "all about violence for just reasons — we're going to break in and rescue these folks"
- **Army Special Forces (Green Beret)** — Served in the same unit as Dalton Fury (author of *Kill Bin Laden*); trained at Key West underwater operations; black belt (broke nose during five-on-one test)
- **DARPA (late 1990s–2002)** — Program Manager, Tactical Mobile Robotics ($130M portfolio: $65M robots, $65M stealth bio-camouflage SAP). Deployed to Afghanistan (Bagram Airfield, early 2002) to support bin Laden cave-search robot missions; mission demoralized when engagement-denial pattern for bin Laden was revealed
- **SAIC (briefly, 2001)** — VP hired to rescue troubled Littleton, CO robotics division; gave one internal briefing on micro-robots for infiltration; pulled back to DARPA within ~30 days; gave leave of absence and ultimately resigned
- **Oklahoma City bombing (1995)** — Volunteered in rubble with TS/SCI clearance; graphic first-responder experiences that triggered his meditative mountain bike rides

### Academic Credentials

- Bachelor's / master's in mathematics and computer science, Colorado School of Mines
- Doctoral work in cognitive psychology (same institution)
- Expert in behavioral science: applies normal-behavior baselines to assess witness credibility

## Abduction Experiences

### 1995 Daniels Park Missing Time

During a standard meditative mountain bike ride at [[daniels-park]] (south/southwest of Denver, CO), near a Native American burial ground — in the weeks following the Oklahoma City bombing — Blitch experienced:

1. An uncharacteristic compulsion to enter a fenced-off burial ground; he walked his bike in, out of respect
2. A spontaneous gushing nosebleed with no mechanical cause (not sweating, not exerting, had been living at Front Range altitude for a year)
3. Immediate loss of consciousness upon lying down — "didn't take more than a second"
4. Waking freezing, exhausted, and bonked, hours later (estimated ~5+ hours of missing time)
5. In a fuzzy memory during the gap: hauling his bike up to a mesa toward bright lights he rationalized as a "drilling rig" on the burial ground; approaching what appeared to be a fuel truck; looking into the cab at approximately a 20-degree angle and seeing a man in a red plaid shirt — at which point extreme anxiety set in

He describes this as textbook anomalous behavior: a behavioral scientist who knew his own baselines, unable to explain his own actions. He connects it to the broader pattern of nose-bleeds and blood appearing in experiencer accounts.

See [[1995-daniels-park-missing-time]] for the full incident record.

### Praying-Mantis Terror Encounter

Blitch's other core experience — a night encounter with an entity he describes as a "big bug" — is more vivid and more disturbing:

> "This big bug came like through the screen door on our deck and he's standing over me, his little triangle face opens up and these jaws open up and he starts tugging chunks of flesh off of me, you know, from my cheeks, my shoulders, and he's flinging it... He wanted me to know that they could rip me to shreds if they wanted to, but they couldn't get to my soul."

He woke with bruises. His interpretation: the entity deliberately used violence to communicate a specific message to someone whose warrior psychology required extreme force to penetrate — that the body is temporary but the soul is inviolable. He frames this in retrospect as a "gift," even though in his youth he responded with rage and broke hockey sticks and baseball bats in his backyard.

The entity belongs to the [[insectoid-beings]] category — praying-mantis morphology with a triangular face and articulating jaws.

## Views and Claims

### Abductions as the Elephant in the Room

Blitch's central thesis for his forthcoming book (*Fear Fighter*): abductions, not mere aircraft sightings, explain 80 years of extreme secrecy. The logic:

- Lights in the sky are not scary enough to justify harming military witnesses to keep them quiet
- Even disclosing that living beings pilot recovered craft is insufficient justification
- The **only** secret terrifying enough to rationalize the suppression of whistleblowers is: *we cannot protect you from them*
- World governments would have to admit to populations that a non-human intelligence can take any individual at any time, and nothing can stop it
- His demand for the legacy cabal: admit this fact and ask for amnesty for the cover-up, rather than face accountability for murders committed (or condoned) to maintain the secret

He explicitly names [[richard-doty]] and [[afosi]] as examples requiring this amnesty framework, and cites [[paul-benowitz]]'s suicide as an example of the cabal's most corrosive actions.

### DARPA as NHI Technology Conduit

Building on [[philip-corso]]'s account in *The Day After Roswell*, Blitch argues that DARPA — founded in 1958, contemporaneous with General Trudeau's mandate to distribute recovered materials into U.S. industry — functioned as the institutional vehicle for this distribution. Supporting smoke:

- DARPA's exceptional acquisition authority (explicit exception from federal acquisition regulations that all other national labs must follow) made it uniquely positioned for rapid, non-auditable tech distribution
- Blitch's own stealth bio-camouflage SAP was cancelled one month after approval with no explanation — consistent with the pattern of programs being terminated when the underlying technology already exists in a black program
- A 2004 Al Jazeera video (relayed by Katie Couric) appeared to show an invisible entity climbing onto a tank in Iraq — Blitch's immediate reaction was "that's why my program was cancelled"

### Radar-Induced UAP Crashes

See [[radar-induced-uap-crashes]] for the full hypothesis. Briefly: pre-WWII and early-WWII radar engineers were cranking transmitter power to extend range, unknowingly interfering with craft operating invisibly in the area. The 1941 [[1941-cape-girardeau-crash]] and the [[1933-magenta-crash]] both fit within radar range of known early installations. Blitch drove through Cape Girardeau en route to his AFRL posting in Dayton and walked the ground.

### Galactic Institutional Review Board

Blitch proposes that NHI non-intervention is governed by a framework analogous to a human IRB — a galactic oversight body preventing direct disclosure because it would invalidate the experimental conditions under which humanity is being studied. See [[galactic-irb]].

### Validation of Jake Barber and Randy Anderson

- **Jake Barber**: Blitch met with all four members of Barber's retrieval team on his boat — specifically watching for eye-contact coordination or behavioral inconsistencies among the group. Found none. Assigns ~99% (3-sigma) credibility to Barber's core claims.
- **Randy Anderson**: Assessed credible based on internal consistency and — critically — the details Randy *omitted* in his interview with Jesse. Blitch notes that authentic witnesses typically withhold the most distinctive structural details (the long elevator ride; the contrast between surface and underground facility quality) rather than lead with them. These omissions increase, not decrease, credibility.
- Blitch also connects Bob Lazar's sphere/orb independently to Anderson's levitating sphere at Crane, noting the specific repulsion-field phenomenon Lazar described.

### Edgar Mitchell and Astronaut Corps Suppression

During a dive trip to the Aquarius underwater habitat (~2008), an unnamed NASA flight surgeon told Blitch that Edgar Mitchell was effectively ostracized by the Apollo astronaut community — with the flight surgeon siding against Mitchell. Blitch's read: there was an implicit agreement among astronauts to "not talk about Fight Club" — i.e., not discuss the ETs "we have all seen." He cites this as anecdotal smoke (not flame) for the hypothesis that some Apollo astronauts encountered NHI in space.

- **Mars**: Endorses [[john-brandenberg]]'s nuclear-war-isotopic-ratio argument as compelling causation under Occam's razor; endorses [[richard-hoagland]]'s Cydonia work, noting the Maxwell's equations extension Hoagland advocates is now receiving NSF/NASA attention
