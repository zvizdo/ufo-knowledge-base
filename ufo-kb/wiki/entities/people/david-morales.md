# David Morales

CIA covert operator described by [[danny-sheehan]] as the "crack shot" of the 15-man [[s-force]] and the gunman who fired the kill shot from the grassy knoll on November 22, 1963. Known by the nicknames "El Indio" and "the Mexican."

## Background

Morales was among the anti-Castro Cuban exile operatives recruited by [[santos-trafficante]] into the S-Force — the triangular fire team originally assembled under CIA program [[zr-rifle]] to assassinate Fidel Castro, Raul Castro, and Che Guevara. The team was trained at [[clint-murchison-jr]]'s ranch in Oaxaca, Mexico, under [[carl-jenkins]].

His photograph was reportedly taken at Dealey Plaza, along with that of [[rip-robertson]], the S-Force field operations commander. Sheehan states photographs exist showing both men at the scene.

## November 22, 1963

Per Sheehan's reconstruction:
- Morales was positioned at the grassy knoll
- He took the shot from the knoll that struck Kennedy
- The triangular fire team structure (three gunmen in crossfire) created the pattern of witness confusion about shot origin
- Zapruder film frames were allegedly reversed to obscure the forward motion of Kennedy's head that would indicate a shot from the front (Sheehan cites [[dick-billings]] as his source for the doctoring)

## Open Questions

- Independent photographic evidence of Morales at Dealey Plaza has been claimed by other researchers but not confirmed definitively.
- Morales died in 1978, the same year the House Select Committee on Assassinations reopened the case.
