# Tom Bearden

Retired US Army Lieutenant Colonel and engineer who became the foremost popularizer of **scalar electromagnetic theory** in the UFO/alternative-physics community. His books — including *Fer-de-Lance*, *Energy from the Vacuum*, and *Gravitobiology* — outline a framework in which Oliver Heaviside's simplification of Maxwell's equations discarded a physically real class of electromagnetic phenomena (scalar, longitudinal, and helicoidal waves) with applications to free energy generation, directed-energy weapons, and non-local biology.

Bearden argued that scalar waves:
- Are a more fundamental form of EM radiation than transverse waves
- Interact with atomic nuclei rather than with electron clouds
- Can therefore affect radioactive decay rates
- Travel faster than light
- Have been weaponized in classified programs (he attributed various anomalous events to Soviet "scalar weather modification" and "psychotronic weapons")

[[steve-coburn]] names Bearden's framework as the primary reference for his own scalar wave definition in [[iPxbILOMQ08]] — specifically the E/B phase relationship (90° out of phase vs. in-phase for transverse waves) and the faster-than-light travel claim.

## Critical Assessment

Bearden's work has not been replicated or accepted in mainstream physics. His framework is internally consistent but based on a disputed interpretation of Maxwell's original equations. Critics note that Oliver Heaviside's reformulation discarded gauge terms that are physically real (confirmed by the Aharonov-Bohm effect) but do not carry energy in the way Bearden claims. His specific claims about Soviet scalar weapons and overunity free energy devices have not been independently confirmed.
