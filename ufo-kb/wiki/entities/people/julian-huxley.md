# Julian Huxley

British evolutionary biologist (1887–1975), grandson of Thomas Henry Huxley ("Darwin's Bulldog"), and brother of [[aldous-huxley]]. First Director-General of UNESCO (1946–48). Widely credited with coining the term **transhumanism** in his 1957 essay of the same name, framing it as humanity directing its own evolution.

## Transhumanism

Julian Huxley's 1957 essay "Transhumanism" argued that the human species could, and should, transcend its current form through science and technology — eugenics, genetic engineering, intelligence augmentation. He founded and shaped the early transhumanist intellectual movement.

## Brave New World as Plan (Jones's Account)

[[alex-jones]] claims that in a ~1962 Berkeley speech during the *Brave New World Revisited* tour, Julian Huxley stated explicitly that the 1931 novel was written based on plans developed over 60–70 years by elite families (Huxleys, Wedgwoods, Galtons) projecting where humanity was headed — not speculative fiction but a blueprint:

> "We wrote all this in '31 because this is what we were planning and what had been developed over the last 60–70 years by our families. It's where we thought humanity was going. It's not going to be a 1984 future... it's going to be a chemical system of biological control and genetic engineering."

If accurate, this is a significant claim: that *Brave New World* (1931) was a programmatic statement of elite intent, not a warning. Jones uses this to argue elite family networks (Galton → Francis Galton, eugenics; Wedgwood → Darwin family connection) have been consciously planning technocratic governance for 150+ years.

> ⚠ Jones's account of this speech is secondhand and the original speech text/recording has not been verified in this KB. Aldous Huxley's Berkeley lecture in 1962 is documented (KPFA archives); whether Julian Huxley made this specific claim there is unverified.
