# Walter Kern

Journalist and researcher acquainted with [[david-grusch]]. Jesse Michaels describes him as a friend. Kern relayed Grusch's characterisation of [[dick-cheney]] as the head of the UAP legacy pyramid — a claim that has since circulated widely in the discourse.

## Key Relay

According to Kern (as cited by Jesse Michaels in [[u7g5Sn1DJF4]]):

> "Dave Grush told him that Dick Cheney was the head of the UFO pyramid for quite a while... essentially when he left in 2009, that was the last time that these activities really had central leadership."

This framing — Cheney as apex, his 2009 departure creating a leadership vacuum — is one of the more specific structural claims in the disclosure literature and is cited by [[uap-gerb]] as "highly accurate, probably true."
