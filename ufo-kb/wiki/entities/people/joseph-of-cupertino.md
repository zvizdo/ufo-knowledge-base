# Joseph of Cupertino

## Background

Joseph of Cupertino (Giuseppe Maria Desa, 1603–1663) was an Italian Conventual Franciscan friar canonized in 1767. He is historically known for numerous reported levitation events, some witnessed by Church officials and lay observers including foreign dignitaries. The Catholic Church compiled depositions from witnesses during his lifetime, partly to evaluate the genuineness of the phenomenon and partly to manage its visibility.

## Notable Claims

### The 1628 Spanish Ambassador Episode

[[hal-puthoff]] (Gf_tKn9TaP8) cites the 1628 visit of the Spanish ambassador to Italy. The ambassador met Joseph in his monastic cell and was so impressed that he returned with his wife. Joseph entered the church where the couple hoped to meet him. Upon seeing the statue of Mary elevated 10 ft into the air, Joseph "flew over the crowd to the statue, prayed, flew back to the door, and returned home." The Church later took depositions from a number of people who were present that day; their accounts were consistent.

### Vacuum-Energy Reading

Puthoff's physics framing of Joseph's levitations: the only mechanism currently consistent with the physics we know is access to [[zero-point-energy|vacuum energy]] via coherence — i.e., if you cohered the otherwise-random vacuum fluctuations and made them non-random, "maybe that could do it." The proposed substrate is the ecstatic / extreme-devotional consciousness state. Puthoff's key analytic move: "That guy didn't have a nuclear power pack on his back," so the only candidate energy reservoir is the vacuum itself.

### Church Suppression as Authenticity Marker

Per Puthoff, the Catholic Church's handling of known levitators — sequestering them in monasteries to keep them out of public view — is itself evidence that the phenomenon was real rather than promoted. The Church found levitation events disruptive (e.g. a levitating priest in the middle of mass) and uncomfortable, not magical PR. The Inquisition's records, which Puthoff says he and colleagues have looked into, document the phenomenon under hostile institutional priors.
