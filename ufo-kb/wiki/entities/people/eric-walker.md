# Eric Walker

Dr. Eric A. Walker served as President of Pennsylvania State University from 1956 to 1970. In UAP research circles he is known as an alleged Majestic-12 adjacent figure — someone who, according to multiple researchers, had insider knowledge of government UAP programs.

## Kecksburg Presence

[[kevin-knuth]] found an official government document listing [[donald-hornig]] (presidential science advisor) as present at the [[1965-kecksburg]] crash site. [[eric-walker]] is separately reported — by researchers including [[stan-gordon]] and others who interviewed witnesses in the Kecksburg area — as also having been present at or briefed on the event.

## MJ-12 Alleged Connection

Walker is cited in several MJ-12 adjacent claims as a scientific advisor who had knowledge of recovered non-human materials. The specific nature and provenance of these claims has not been verified in the primary source literature accessible to this KB.

## The Walker Network — Cameron, Steinman, Kit Green

Three independent researchers each had separate interactions with Walker that converged on the same frustrating wall:

1. **[[grant-cameron]]**: Published a transcript of a conversation with Walker in a book. Walker neither confirmed nor denied insider knowledge but was clearly evasive.

2. **[[william-steinman]]** and a colleague (possibly Scott Crane): Visited Penn State to meet Walker in person. Walker reportedly called them "Don Quixote" — dismissing their UAP inquiry as chasing windmills. But he also asked: *"What do you know about the sixth sense? What do you know about psi?"* — implying that access to insider knowledge required a prior grounding in parapsychology/psi that Steinman lacked. He is quoted as saying, in effect: "Until you know about that, you don't know anything."

3. **[[kit-green]]**: Met Walker and reportedly asked directly about UFO secrets. Walker **kicked him out of the office** and refused to answer — despite Green's CIA credentials.

The pattern across all three interactions: Walker knew something, repeatedly declined to share it, and in Steinman's case set a "psi prerequisite" as the gatekeeper condition.

> ⚠ Conflict / Open question: Walker's "sixth sense / psi" prerequisite may link to the [[stargate-program]] / remote viewing cluster (Puthoff, CIA SRI). If psi access was the clearance gate for UAP knowledge, it suggests the US government had already integrated parapsychology research with crash retrieval intelligence by the 1970s–80s.
