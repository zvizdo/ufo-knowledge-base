# Carl Feindt

UAP researcher who wrote one of the earliest foundational books on UFOs and water/ocean phenomena, compiled through direct interviews with witnesses and researchers across multiple decades. [[tim-gallaudet]] cites Feindt's work alongside [[richard-dolan]] and [[preston-dennett]] as the key reference literature for the USO field.

The exact title of Feindt's book is not recalled precisely in this interview ("UFOs in the Water" or similar). He is also referenced by a description as "an Italian researcher who had been focused on underwater UAP for about 20 years" — though this may refer to a different figure; the Italian connection is uncertain.
