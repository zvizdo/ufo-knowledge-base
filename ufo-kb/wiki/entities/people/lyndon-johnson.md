# Lyndon Baines Johnson

36th President of the United States (1963–1969), having assumed the presidency following John F. Kennedy's assassination in Dallas on November 22, 1963. Previously Senate Majority Leader and Vice President under Kennedy.

## JFK Assassination Context

[[danny-sheehan]] cites multiple witnesses that LBJ was present at [[clint-murchison-jr]]'s Dallas ranch the night before the assassination, where the plot was allegedly discussed among attendees. On Air Force One after the assassination, [[jack-valenti]] witnessed LBJ lock himself in the bathroom repeating "they're going to kill us all." Despite this distress being reported, LBJ had reportedly been briefed the night before — suggesting the behavior was either a psychological reaction to the reality of what occurred or calculated performance.
