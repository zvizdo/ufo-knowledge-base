# Bernard Barker

Cuban-American CIA operative. One of the five burglars arrested at the Watergate complex on June 17, 1972 — along with [[frank-sturgis]], Virgilio González, Eugenio Martínez, and James McCord. Barker had a long history with the CIA's anti-Castro Cuban exile network, having participated in the Bay of Pigs invasion (1961).

In the UAP/deep-state context, Barker's arrest at the Watergate connects to the broader thesis that Watergate was not merely a political scandal but an operation by CIA covert-operations veterans — possibly attempting to recover or suppress documents related to Richard Nixon's knowledge of UAP programs ([[jfk-assassination-ufo]], [[larry-obrien]] call logs).
