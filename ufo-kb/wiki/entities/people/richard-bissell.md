# Richard Bissell

## Background

Richard Mervin Bissell Jr. served as CIA Deputy Director of Plans under Director [[allen-dulles]] — the most operationally powerful position in the Agency, overseeing covert action. In 1955, he is credited as the primary founder of Area 51, establishing the classified facility at the Nevada Test Site as the base for the U-2 spy plane program he also architected. He was one of the most influential figures in Cold War covert intelligence history.

Bissell planned and supervised the Bay of Pigs invasion of Cuba in 1961. After its failure, President Kennedy fired both Bissell and [[allen-dulles]] from the CIA. Bissell subsequently went to Brown Brothers Harriman — the same institution where Dulles landed — a firm described in source discussions as having effectively formed the CIA's institutional culture.

## Notable Claims

### Bluegill Triple Prime — Deliberate UAP Shootdown

Bissell privately told [[harold-malmgren]] that the October 1962 Bluegill Triple Prime high-altitude nuclear test was used deliberately to down a UAP "tag-along" that had been closely and persistently monitoring the detonation. In Malmgren's account, Bissell stated:

> "We downed a UFO that was monitoring — closely monitoring — that test and it tumbled into the ocean and the Navy picked it up."

This constitutes a direct, named-source allegation that the US government deliberately used a nuclear detonation as a [[directed-energy-uap-weapon]] to shoot down a non-human craft — and then recovered it. The claim sits at the intersection of [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] and active engagement with UAP, moving well beyond passive observation. For additional context on the Roswell-to-Bluegill chain, see [[pippa-malmgren]]'s relay of her father's disclosures.

### 1933 Magenta Crash — Named Privately Decades Before Public Disclosure

Bissell also told Malmgren about the 1933 Magenta, Italy UAP crash — a case that would not enter public discourse until David Grusch's 2023 congressional testimony. The quote attributed to Bissell: "He said this did not begin last week. He mentioned 1933 Magenta." This suggests that the Magenta crash was known within the senior CIA hierarchy as a foundational event in the history of UAP recovery, and that Bissell was willing to share it with cleared insiders he trusted. The independent corroboration strengthens the provenance of the Grusch testimony significantly.

[[allen-dulles]] and his brother John Foster Dulles are separately described as having arranged the transfer of the 1933 Magenta craft via OSS Project McGregor — making the Dulles–Bissell partnership foundational to both UAP recovery and secrecy management.

### CIA Hostility to UAP Transparency

Bissell is described in the source as having articulated why the CIA became institutionally hostile to UAP transparency: he believed UAPs "presented forces that were beyond CIA's knowledge or control and they interfered perhaps with what CIA was doing with private industry." This framing — UAP as a threat to CIA operational primacy rather than merely a scientific mystery — helps explain the shape of the [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] and its motivations in terms of institutional self-preservation rather than public safety alone.
