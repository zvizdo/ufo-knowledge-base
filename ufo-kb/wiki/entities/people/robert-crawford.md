# Robert Crawford

Robert Crawford served aboard the USS Delarof, a US transport ship, in the summer of 1945. He is the primary witness on record for the [[1945-uss-delarof]] incident near [[adak-island]] in the Aleutian Islands. Crawford spoke with multiple UFO researchers over the years, including the noted [[james-mcdonald]].

## Account

During the return voyage from Japan to Seattle, while the ship passed near Adak Island (approximately 500 miles from Hawaii in the Pacific), crew members witnessed a disc-shaped object emerge from the water. Crawford himself did not see the emergence, but saw the object immediately after. He describes it:

- Disc-shaped, approximately 200 feet in diameter
- Circled the ship **twice** in complete silence
- Conditions: high winds — but the object was "rock solid, totally stable" and not buffeted at all
- Departed to the east/southeast at extreme speed, leaving a visible flash of light

This case serves as the opening entry in [[richard-dolan]]'s USO research, and was the incident that "started me on this book project."
