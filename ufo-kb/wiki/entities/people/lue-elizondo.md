# Lue Elizondo

Former US Army counterintelligence officer who served in various sensitive roles before becoming director of the **Advanced Aerial Threat Identification Program (AATIP)** and a key figure in the **Advanced Aerospace Weapons Systems Application Program (AAWSAP)**. In October 2017, Elizondo resigned from the Pentagon citing bureaucratic obstruction of UAP research — and the same month, the New York Times broke the AATIP story, launching the modern UAP disclosure cycle. He subsequently joined the **To The Stars Academy of Arts and Science** alongside Tom DeLonge and others.

## Background

Elizondo's intelligence background is in counterintelligence; he has not disclosed the precise nature of all his assignments. He has consistently framed his UAP work as a national-security issue rather than a spiritual or paranormal one, which has made him the acceptable face of disclosure for mainstream media. He acknowledges being authorized to discuss certain material and not others, and is careful to delineate firsthand knowledge from second-hand claims.

## Notable Claims

### Multiple NHI Groups
Elizondo argues that the evidence is consistent with **more than one** non-human intelligence visiting Earth — not a single alien civilization but potentially several distinct groups with different craft morphologies, behaviors, and apparent agendas. This is a significant departure from the common assumption of a single alien presence.

### Dick Cheney as Classification Gatekeeper
He implies that [[dick-cheney]] occupied the apex of the legacy UAP classification structure. Significantly, he leaves open the possibility that even Cheney was not read into the full picture — suggesting the program architecture was designed to compartmentalize even its own custodians.

### UAP Propulsion Physics
Elizondo describes UAP propulsion as gravity manipulation: the craft do not burn fuel in any conventional sense but instead warp the local space-time metric to create a "propulsion bubble." He frames this as consistent with general relativity and E=mc² — enormous energy expenditure to achieve mass-equivalent effects on the surrounding spacetime. See [[gravity-manipulation-propulsion]].

### Binary Reality Framing
His signature rhetorical move: either UAPs are genuinely non-human craft, or we are witnessing coordinated mass hysteria spanning admirals, generals, top-secret-cleared pilots, nuclear weapons officers, and aerospace engineers. He presents this as the only epistemically honest framing.

### Battle-Space Preparation Hypothesis
Elizondo has suggested that some UAP activity — particularly long-duration passive presence in military operating areas — may represent **"prepping the battle space"**: a reconnaissance and signals-intelligence gathering operation, potentially by a non-human or advanced adversarial actor, designed to map US radar waveforms, communication frequencies, and operational tactics for exploitation in a future conflict. This framing is distinguished from active aggression but implies adversarial intent.

[[ryan-graves]] notes in [[6WC4o2yY9Ws]] that the East Coast UAP presence was consistent with passive recon: "Oh, we were setting ourselves up for failure. They could soak up our waveforms, decrypt our radios, observe our tactics — and if we just ignore them and let that happen, the next time we go into a major conflict, they know everything we're doing." However, Graves attributes this framing to the *initial* hypothesis his squadron considered — not necessarily to Elizondo specifically, and distinct from the full-scale NHI hypothesis.

### Nuclear Nexus
Elizondo confirms that UAP activity is disproportionately concentrated around nuclear weapons infrastructure — consistent with [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] and the testimony of [[robert-salas]] and [[robert-hastings]].

### Occult-Aerospace Thread
He notes that [[jack-parsons]], co-founder of JPL and a key figure in the development of American rocketry, recited an Aleister Crowley hymn before rocket tests. Elizondo raises this as one thread in a broader pattern connecting early aerospace development to esoteric traditions. See [[occult-aerospace-connection]].

## Danny Sheehan as Attorney; Controlled Disclosure Dimension

[[danny-sheehan]] is Elizondo's attorney and frames his 2017 New York Times disclosure as an **authorized managed release** — not a whistleblower act but a pre-cleared step in a managed disclosure pipeline. Sheehan notes:
- The three UAP videos released with the NYT story were government pre-cleared and deliberately chosen as the lowest-quality material available
- Elizondo and [[chris-mellon]] jointly drove the 2017 media strategy with institutional authorization
- The authorized nature of the release is itself significant data: it shows the intelligence community decided at a specific moment to move public awareness to a new level

Sheehan also notes that Elizondo and [[steven-greer]] have deep mutual antagonism — Sheehan calls it "unfortunate" but does not explain it publicly.

## JRE #2194 — Key New Firsthand Claims (9gLPtRwXgCM)

### Recruitment and Origin
Elizondo describes his 2009 recruitment in specific detail: two unnamed individuals approached him at the Pentagon; he was brought to an undisclosed DC-area facility; his first interviewer was **Dr. James Lacatski**, described as "probably the number one rocket scientist in the US government." His pre-AATIP academic background was microbiology/immunology/parasitology — he had no prior interest in UFOs.

His turning-point was a **dinner hosted by [[robert-bigelow]]** at a Washington DC hotel where **Brazilian General Uchoa** presented photographs and reports from the [[1977-colares-incident]]. Elizondo left "scratching my head."

### Classified Evidence Specifics
- Classified 4K ultra-high-definition UAP footage exists — far superior to the three public videos, which Elizondo calls "the least compelling" and "low-hanging fruit."
- A classified cockpit-close-pass video shows a **wedge/diamond-shaped silver metallic craft** passing approximately 15 feet from a pilot's cockpit; the pilot's expletive is audible on the recording.
- A classified underwater video shows a USO at **450–550 knots** — larger than the offshore oil derrick it passes.
- **Pentagon-cleared statement**: *"There is very compelling evidence to suggest that the US government is in absolute possession of exotic material that is not made by humans."*

### Puthoff SKIF Briefing
Elizondo attended a **~3-hour classified SKIF briefing by [[hal-puthoff]]** on the unified spacetime/gravity propulsion theory that explains all five UAP observables simultaneously — describes it as a collective "epiphany."

### Nuclear Interference Documents
Elizondo brought the **declassified Echo Flight ICBM shutdown report** (FOIA via [[john-greenwald]] / [[black-vault]]) to the Rogan studio on camera. He also states UAPs **activated** Soviet nuclear missiles — the inverse of Echo Flight.

### Vatican and Historical Accounts
Elizondo traveled personally to Italy, met a **senior Vatican academic**, who told him the Vatican *"doesn't have a problem with this topic."* Pre-1600s theology held it heretical to presume mankind was God's only representation. He also references a Vatican source's account of Roman military records describing **"flaming shields in the sky"** (ecus) following armies — deferring to [[jacques-vallee]] for elaboration.

### USO/Transmedium Accounts
- An unnamed Navy official told him: when tracking a USO larger than a US submarine at hundreds of knots — *"We go around."*
- A helicopter pilot told him of a **Caribbean USO incident** (late 1990s): a round object the size of a small island rose from the ocean floor and absorbed a cruise missile before submerging — [[david-fravor]] could corroborate the water-churning detail.
- The [[2013-aguadilla-incident]] (DHS/CBP thermal video) was shared with AATIP investigators — transmedium, no-splash water entry, splits into two objects.

### Personal History
- **Father**: fought with Castro against Batista; joined CIA-backed forces; participated in the **Bay of Pigs invasion**; spent two years in Castro's prisons being tortured. Told Elizondo months before his death (~June 2023) that the greatest threat to humanity is *"corruption — when you are willing to bypass your own moral code, your own ethics for something else."*
- **Mother**: died of cancer (coma); at the moment of passing, Elizondo describes a spiritual experience — *"something reached"* and told him she was gone; her eyes opened despite compromised brain function.
