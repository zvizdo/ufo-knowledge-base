# Arthur Trudeau

US Army General who headed the **Army's Foreign Technology Division (FTD)** — a distinct organization from the Air Force FTD at [[wright-patterson]]. Trudeau supervised [[philip-corso]] during the 1958–1962 period that Gerb and Michels describe as the **"golden age of Army R&D"**.

## Role in Reverse Engineering

Under Trudeau's oversight, the Army's FTD:
- Received and catalogued recovered craft materials (including Roswell debris)
- Distributed materials to private industry for seeded reverse engineering:
  - [[bell-labs]] — transistor/integrated circuit research
  - Sperry Rand — guidance systems
  - [[northrop]] — materials science
  - [[lockheed-martin]] — aerospace applications
  - Raytheon — radar/targeting systems

Corso documented this distribution program in his manuscript *Dawn of a New Age* (pre-publication version, regarded as more authentic than the published *The Day After Roswell* which was edited by a co-writer with additional material inserted).

## Context

Trudeau operated the Army FTD **separately** from both the Air Force's FTD and from Majestic 12 / the civilian contractor layer. This compartmentalization is consistent with the multi-track structure of UAP programs: military services ran parallel efforts without necessarily coordinating with each other or with the civilian contractor/FFRDC layer.
