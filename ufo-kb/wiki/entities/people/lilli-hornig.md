# Lilli Hornig

Lilli Hornig (née Schwenk) was a chemist who worked on the Manhattan Project at Los Alamos and later became a prominent advocate for women in science. She was the wife of [[donald-hornig]], who served as presidential science advisor to Eisenhower, Kennedy, and Johnson.

## Oppenheimer Film (2023)

Lilli Hornig appears as a character in Christopher Nolan's *Oppenheimer* (2023). Her best-known line — spoken when interviewers at Los Alamos asked how fast she typed — was reproduced verbatim: **"They don't teach typing at Harvard."** [[kevin-knuth]] confirmed this from a family dinner visit he made to Rhode Island before her death.
