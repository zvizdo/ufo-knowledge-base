# Jim Simons

Mathematician, NSA code-breaker, and founder of Renaissance Technologies (died 2024). Ran the **Medallion Fund** — widely considered the best-performing investment fund in history over a 30+ year period. Made fundamental contributions to geometry and theoretical physics (Chern-Simons theory, contributions to Yang-Mills equations).

## Background

- NSA codebreaker before academic career
- Contributed to Chern-Simons theory (fundamental to modern string theory and condensed matter physics)
- Founded Renaissance Technologies; Medallion Fund averaged ~66% annual returns before fees
- Major philanthropist through the Simons Foundation; major donor to Stony Brook University (which has a top-ranked physics department)
- His science advisor was [[david-spergel]]

## Connection to UAP

[[eric-weinstein]] has publicly speculated that Renaissance Technologies' extraordinary profits may have been directed (in part) toward funding classified UFO research — and that Simons's position at the intersection of NSA, elite mathematics, and elite physics would make him a plausible UAP-adjacent figure.

Jesse Michaels initially dismissed this as conspiratorial. But the connection sharpened in 2022 when NASA announced a UAP review panel and [[david-spergel]] — Simons's science advisor and primary contact at the Simons Foundation — was named the **main author** of the NASA UAP review panel report. Michaels: "What's going on? Is there some sort of connection there?"

The NASA UAP review panel's report was notably thin (~10 pages vs. France's 100-page COMETA report) — leaving open whether Spergel's involvement represents genuine UAP interest or institutional gate-keeping.

Knuth declines to confirm or deny the Simons-UFO hypothesis; the connection remains speculative.

## Weinstein's UAP Physics Attempt at Renaissance Technologies (xnxasfyHtfo)

[[eric-weinstein]] discloses in [[xnxasfyHtfo]] that he attempted to instantiate "at least the theoretical physics component" of a UAP legacy program at **Renaissance Technologies**. This is the most direct evidence connecting Simons's institution to UAP physics — not through Simons himself, but through Weinstein's own attempt to use the RenTech environment for UAP-relevant theoretical physics.

Whether Simons was aware of or supportive of this effort is not stated. Weinstein also flags the overlap between Simons as RenTech founder, [[david-spergel]] as head of the Simons Foundation, and Spergel's appointment to lead the NASA 2022 UAP review panel as "a very interesting overlap."
