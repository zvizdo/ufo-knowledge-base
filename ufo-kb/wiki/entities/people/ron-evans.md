# Ron Evans

US Navy aviator and NASA astronaut; Apollo 17 Command Module Pilot (December 1972). During the mission's re-entry, Evans described the fireball appearance from the rendezvous window as "a tunnel with a bright spot in the middle of the tunnel. Way down the tunnel, way back behind, I could see the fireball." Evans orbited the Moon alone for approximately 75 hours while Cernan and Schmitt conducted surface operations.
