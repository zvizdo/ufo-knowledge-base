# Catherine Austin Fitts

Catherine Austin Fitts served as Assistant Secretary of Housing and Urban Development under President George H. W. Bush in the late 1980s–early 1990s. Her attempts to audit HUD's books — she was denied access until she insisted — revealed a system apparently designed to *prevent* auditing. She describes library carts of paper, organized and disorganized, that "God could not pull apart."

## HUD Accounting

Fitts concluded that the HUD accounting system was architecturally designed to be un-auditable. She subsequently wrote a paper called "The Myth of the Rule of Law" analyzing the implications.

## Pentagon Missing Money

She has spent years documenting discrepancies in Pentagon and federal government accounting. Key data points cited by [[richard-dolan]]:
- **September 10, 2001**: Defense Secretary Rumsfeld acknowledged before the House Armed Services Committee that "$2.3 trillion in unaccounted expenditures" existed in Pentagon records — exact figure revised multiple times but originally $2.3 trillion. The story vanished after 9/11.
- **2002**: A man named Dov Zachheim took over Pentagon accounting and declared the discrepancies resolved to zero — a claim Dolan finds implausible.
- Fitts's view: the money is not necessarily "stolen" but the open-window financial system allows it to exit into unaccountable destinations.

## Convergence with Breakaway Civilization

Fitts independently reached the same conclusion as [[richard-dolan]]'s [[breakaway-civilization]] hypothesis through her financial investigations: significant funds appear to flow into deep-classified activities, likely including [[deep-underground-military-bases]] and possibly exotic technology programs. In a [[tucker-carlson]] interview, she mentioned Dolan's work directly and pointed toward "space" as a destination for missing funds.

## Arlington Institute Incident

[[john-peterson]] of the [[arlington-institute]] allegedly invited Fitts to a simulation of "what it would be like in a world where extraterrestrials are openly acknowledged as living among us" (~1998). Fitts declined, fearing it was a setup (she was being investigated for her financial disclosures at the time). She later came to regret missing the conference.

A separate claim (from [[linda-thompson]]): John Peterson allegedly promised to show Fitts "a UFO" if she would stop her research.
