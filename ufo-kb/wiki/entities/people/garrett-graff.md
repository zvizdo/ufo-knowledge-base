# Garrett Graff

Journalist, author, and Aspen Institute fellow. Authored *UFO: The Inside Story of the US Government's Search for Alien Life Here — and Out There* (2024), which [[michael-shellenberger]] characterizes as a sophisticated UAP debunking operation rather than impartial investigative journalism.

## The Alleged Dual Information-Management Role

[[michael-shellenberger]]'s central concern: Graff co-orchestrated a **Hunter Biden laptop pre-bunking** operation at the [[aspen-institute]] in summer 2020 alongside his supervisor [[vivian-schiller]] (then head of NPR, later Twitter media executive, NYT executive). This operation seeded the media environment with skepticism about the laptop's authenticity before it became news. Graff's subsequent turn to UAP debunking strikes Shellenberger as suspicious: "I find him a suspicious character."

The pattern Shellenberger identifies: both the laptop pre-bunking and the UAP book represent **information management operations** — one suppressing a politically sensitive document, one managing public perception of a sensitive government topic. Whether this represents coordinated institutional behavior or coincidence is unresolved.

## UAP Analytical Position

Graff's book is categorized by Shellenberger as a "more sophisticated" tier of debunker:
- Tier 1 (crude): [[sean-kirkpatrick]] / Stephen Greenstreet — dismissal and ridicule
- Tier 2 (technical): [[mick-west]] — systematic video analysis
- Tier 3 (sophisticated): Graff — acknowledges unexplained UAPs and orbs exist but argues the military/intelligence community "just doesn't know what they are"

Shellenberger disputes the latter claim: Graff cannot *know* the government is simply ignorant — and if he does know, that itself is a problem since Congress hasn't been told.
