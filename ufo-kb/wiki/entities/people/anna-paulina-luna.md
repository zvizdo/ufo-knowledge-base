# Anna Paulina Luna

US Representative (Republican, Florida). Cited on the American Alchemy episode with [[tim-alberino]] for her appearance on Joe Rogan's podcast, during which she recommended that people "read the Book of Enoch" in the context of UAP/alien disclosure — specifically referencing **interdimensional beings** as a framework for understanding the phenomenon.

## Significance

Luna is one of the very few sitting US politicians to have explicitly connected the biblical apocryphal text [[book-of-enoch]] to the modern UAP disclosure conversation in a public, high-profile media setting. Her Rogan appearance was notable enough that [[jesse-michaels]] mentioned it to Alberino as context for the Book of Enoch discussion.

Her framing — that the Book of Enoch describes "the fall of angels, thus creating really the precursor of civilization that led to the first flood" and that understanding what Enoch was talking about "kind of makes sense" as background for UAP origins — maps closely to the [[watchers-thesis]] advocated by Alberino.

She identified the beings as potentially **interdimensional** (consistent with congressional/government framing that emphasises non-physical rather than extraterrestrial origins).

## AARO Defunding / FBI Transfer Call (V00WcEiKRAY)

[[ross-coulthart]] reports that Luna tweeted that AARO is "a hopeless cause" and should be defunded, with the implication that the money should go to the FBI. Coulthart reads this as indicating the FBI's ongoing UAP crime investigation — focused on murders [[jake-barber]] alleged were committed to protect the secret — is the intended successor to AARO's role.
