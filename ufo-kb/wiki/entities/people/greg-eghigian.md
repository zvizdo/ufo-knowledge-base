# Greg Eghigian

History professor at **Penn State University** who wrote an academic history of UFOs. He spent several weeks at the [[archives-for-the-unexplained]] (AFU) in Sweden conducting research for the book. [[claus-svahn]] endorses him: "Very good researcher. I like him."

Eghigian appears regularly on television as a credentialed academic expert on the UFO phenomenon — one of the few historians willing to treat the subject with scholarly rigor.

## Significance in the KB

Eghigian occupies a rare niche: a mainstream academic historian who takes UAP history seriously enough to immerse in primary source archives. His Penn State affiliation creates an incidental connection to [[eric-walker]] (Walker was Penn State president 1956–1970), though no direct relationship is known.
