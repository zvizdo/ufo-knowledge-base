# Enrico Fermi

Italian-American physicist (1901–1954). Nobel laureate in Physics (1938) for work on induced radioactivity; designed and operated the first artificial nuclear reactor (Chicago Pile-1, 1942); senior figure of the [[manhattan-project]].

## Background

Fermi emigrated from Fascist Italy in 1938 and led key US nuclear-weapons work at Chicago and [[los-alamos]]. After WWII he returned to academic physics at the University of Chicago.

## Notable Claims / Roles

- **Fermi Paradox** (1950, Los Alamos lunch conversation): "Where is everybody?" — the foundational framing for SETI, the Drake equation, and contemporary [[great-filter]] / [[dark-forest]] discourse.
- **RS-33 (alleged, 1953)**: Per the [[guglielmo-marconi]] family testimony surfaced by [[jesse-michaels]], Fermi was vice-president of [[rs-33]], a clandestine Italian government UFO committee headed by Marconi and reactivated around the 1953 Varese case. If true, this places one of the founders of nuclear physics inside an Italian classified UFO body the year before his death.
