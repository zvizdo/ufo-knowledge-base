# Wladyslaw Krasuski

Polish civilian POW laboring at the agricultural estate Gut Alt Golssen, approximately 30 miles east of Berlin, 1942–1945. Also known as Walter Krasuski after emigrating to the United States (Detroit, Michigan).

## Key Actions

**1944 (Gut Alt Golssen, Germany)**: Observed a large circular craft approximately 75–100 yards in diameter and ~14 feet tall, consisting of a stationary dark gray top section (~5–6 feet), a stationary dark gray bottom section (~5–6 feet), and a rapidly rotating 3-foot middle section that blurred during operation. The craft emitted a high-pitched whine that stalled his tractor engine twice. It rose vertically from inside an earthwork enclosure (100–150 yards diameter, ringed by a 50-foot tarpaulin wall), then moved slowly horizontally after clearing the wall. Approximately 16–18 Russian, French, and Polish POWs were present alongside SS guards. Uninsulated copper cables (1.5–2 inch diameter) ran from the enclosure to a concrete column. Post-war inspection revealed the cables and concrete structure had been removed and the site flooded.

**November 1957**: Following media coverage of the Levelland, Texas UFO wave (multiple vehicle-stalling reports), Krasuski mailed a letter to [[robert-cutler]] (Special Assistant to President Eisenhower) describing his 1944 observation. The FBI Director responded with an URGENT teletype to SAC Detroit, resulting in an interview the following day.

**November 7, 1957**: Interviewed at his Detroit home by SA Cassius Rathbun (FBI Case 100-26505). Rathbun noted "No indication of irrational or otherwise abnormal behavior" — a direct institutional credibility endorsement.
