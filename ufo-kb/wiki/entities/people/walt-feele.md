# Walt Feele

USAF Lieutenant (later Colonel, ret.) who commanded Echo Flight at [[malmstrom-air-force-base]] approximately March 16, 1967 — eight days before [[robert-salas]]'s [[1967-oscar-flight]] incident. The [[1967-echo-flight]] incident is structurally identical: all 10 missiles go offline while UFO activity is reported overhead.

## Background

Feele had maintenance and security personnel already on-site overnight (preparing for morning modification work) when one missile began shutting down. He called out and was told, "We didn't do anything — and by the way, there are objects hovering above us." Shortly after, all 10 missiles went down. Retired as Colonel. Salas was in contact with him as recently as ~2025.

## Notable Claims

- The [[1967-echo-flight]] incident preceded Oscar Flight by exactly 8 days, on the same base — the pattern Salas cites as proof this is not anomalous.
- A strike team sent to one of the Echo Flight sites reported "a large round object directly over the site."
