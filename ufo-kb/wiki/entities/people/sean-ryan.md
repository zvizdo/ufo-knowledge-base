# Sean Ryan

Host of the **Shawn Ryan Show** (also spelled "Shawn Ryan" in various references; this is the same person). Ryan is a former military professional turned podcast host who covers intelligence community, military, and UAP topics.

## Jake Barber Interview (dnnpyNuPdXs)

Ryan hosted [[jake-barber]]'s first major on-camera interview — convened on 36-hour notice — for what Barber describes as a period of personal danger while awaiting a [[news-nation]] story to go live. Ryan's crew of five had no advance warning of who the guest was.

In that interview, Ryan acknowledged that he had recently retweeted claims from the [[matthew-livelsberger]] email — characterizing New Jersey mystery drones as "Chinese gravitic drones capable of vaporizing NYC or DC." Barber assessed this as sensationalist conflation of conventional drone activity and genuine UAP phenomena, and described it as "hell for us [the disclosure community] the last 2–3 weeks."

## Livelsberger Email Episode

Separately, Ryan interviewed **Sam Shoemate**, who relayed that Livelsberger had emailed him with claims that the New Jersey mystery drones were Chinese electrogravitic craft. The FBI subsequently confirmed the email's authenticity — frames Ryan and Shoemate as having reported accurately on a real communication, even if Barber disputes the interpretation.
