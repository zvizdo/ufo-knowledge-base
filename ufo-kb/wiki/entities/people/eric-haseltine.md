# Eric Haseltine

Neuroscientist, futurist, and career intelligence official. PhD from Indiana University. Worked at Hughes Aircraft on flight simulation before joining Walt Disney Imagineering as Executive Vice President. Then Director of Research at the NSA — effectively the "tip of the spear" for science and innovation at the US's most sensitive intelligence agency. After NSA, served as CTO of the US intelligence community via ODNI, then worked as a CIA analyst. Has 70+ patents and a background in psychotherapy. Grew up near Trona, California.

## UAP Stance

Haseltine's position is carefully calibrated by his intelligence background:

- **Official exposure**: As NSA Director of Research and ODNI CTO, he had a "Clockwork Orange" exposure to all compartmented programs. He saw nothing about UAP at the senior level — "not a femtosecond."
- **Guilty knowledge**: Despite this, he identifies "a few dozen" cases out of the many thousands on record where he is "confident saying there's something real there" — some with firsthand classified insight.
- **Methodological frame**: Applies the intelligence community's [[method-competing-hypotheses]] (method of competing hypotheses) to UAP. Enumerates plasma, drones, classified human craft, Russian Maskirovka, non-human terrestrial origin, NHI, and extra-dimensional physics as competing hypotheses.
- **Tic Tac assessment**: The plasma hypothesis does not fully account for the Tic Tac visual signature. "Those Tic Tacs — there's something real going on there." Endorsed by multi-sensor corroboration (radar, FLIR, visual) in the [[2004-nimitz-tic-tac]] incident.
- **Multiple distinct phenomena**: Tic Tacs, glowing orbs, triangles, and 1950s Washington fireballs are likely separate, unrelated phenomena.
- **NHI hypothesis preference**: If NHI is visiting, it is "probably technology, not biology — probably a [[von-neumann-probe]]." Advanced civilizations send AI before biology (citing human space exploration trajectory).
- **Extra-dimensional physics**: The most likely explanation for "impossible" UAP maneuvers is physics beyond 4 dimensions, not exotic biology.

## Havana Syndrome Investigation

The most firsthand and technically detailed section of Haseltine's public contributions. He and his wife [[chris-gilbert]] are actively interviewing Havana syndrome / AHI victims.

Key findings:
- 1,000+ US officials and family members affected; locations include Cuba, Russia, China, Austria, Germany, Switzerland, and near the White House
- Victims: 20s–40s, excellent physical condition; consistent otolith disruption confirmed by ENT specialists
- Symptoms: directional high-frequency sound, dizziness, cognitive impairment, ear pain, tinnitus, insomnia
- Symptoms disappear on leaving the room and return on re-entry
- Mechanism hypothesis: RF/microwave pulse train (nanosecond duration, high peak power, backpack-portable); also considers [[microwave-hearing-effect]], terahertz, millimeter wave, near-UV laser
- Most likely perpetrator: Russia. Russia has been technically superior in directed energy since the cold war. [[walter-stoessel]] (US Ambassador to Moscow) died of rare blood cancer from Soviet microwave beaming in the 1970s.
- Political suppression: acknowledging Russia has attacked US officials since the 1980s is an act-of-war finding — hence institutional suppression.
- CIA denial framed as psychological, not conspiratorial: officials "literally cannot believe it because their whole ego structure would collapse."
