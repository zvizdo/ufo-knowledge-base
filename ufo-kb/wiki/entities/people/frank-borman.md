# Frank Borman

USAF Colonel; NASA astronaut. Flew Gemini 7 (Dec 4-18 1965, CDR, with James Lovell — the 14-day endurance mission) and Apollo 8 (Dec 1968, CDR — first crewed lunar orbit; Christmas Eve "Earthrise" broadcast).

**Gemini 7 bogey report**: At approximately 4h 24m into the Gemini 7 mission, Borman reported "A BOGEY AT TEN O'CLOCK HIGH." Mission Control asked if it was the booster or a natural sighting; Borman confirmed it was a separate, actual sighting distinct from the Titan booster and the particle debris cloud. The PAO master tape of this exchange was dubbed and released as a UAP-relevant record.
