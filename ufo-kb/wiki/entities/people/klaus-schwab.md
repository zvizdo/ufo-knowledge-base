# Klaus Schwab

German economist (b. 1938); **founder of the World Economic Forum** (1971) and architect of the annual Davos summit. Author of *The Fourth Industrial Revolution* and *The Great Reset*. Trained at Harvard under Henry Kissinger.

**Alex Jones framing**: In the [[KhmUixSXxDo]] episode, Jones characterizes the [[world-economic-forum]] as the public operational arm of the [[bilderberg-group]] — activated in the 1990s after Bilderberg had operated privately for decades. In this framing, Schwab is "the real deal, not a joke" — "the head gopher who organized it and ran it" — meaning WEF's public Davos persona is the face of a coordination structure whose actual decisions happen at Bilderberg-level meetings. Jones describes Bilderberg as where "final deals and battle planning" happen, while Davos is the public presentation layer.

**Structural role in UAP discourse**: Schwab himself has made no UAP-related statements. In the KB, he functions as the anchor for the elite-coordination-of-disclosure hypothesis: the argument that decisions about technology release, post-disclosure governance, and the managed integration of advanced technology into the global economy would be made by the class of people who attend Davos, not by elected governments. The [[elite-global-governance]] synthesis uses WEF/Schwab as one node in the nested coordination structure (Bohemian Grove → Bilderberg → WEF → national policy) hypothesized to operate above democratic institutions.

**KB position**: Schwab is not a UAP claimant. His page exists to ground the "globalist-elite disclosure-timing" narrative in a specific named institution rather than vague reference. This is a guilt-by-association structural anchor — he is cited in conspiratorial-adjacent frames, not as a direct source of UAP evidence.
