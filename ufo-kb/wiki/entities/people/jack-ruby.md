# Jack Ruby

Born Jacob Leon Rubenstein; ran the Carousel Club, a Dallas striptease venue. On November 24, 1963, Ruby walked into the basement of Dallas police headquarters and shot Lee Harvey Oswald at point-blank range on live television — eliminating the only surviving suspect in the Kennedy assassination before he could stand trial or be interrogated further.

## Mob Connections

Ruby had documented connections to organized crime figures. He had made trips to Havana in the 1950s (during the mob's casino era there); he knew individuals in the [[sam-giancana]] / [[santos-trafficante]] sphere; and he had informant relationships with the FBI. The Warren Commission acknowledged these connections but concluded they were not relevant to the assassination.

## Access Anomaly

The central anomaly of the Ruby case: how did a Dallas nightclub owner gain access to the secure basement of the Dallas police department on one of the most scrutinized days in American history? The Warren Commission's explanation (Ruby slipped through a poorly guarded entrance) has never fully satisfied investigators. The HSCA (1979) concluded Ruby's killing of Oswald was likely not a spontaneous act but a planned one.

## Psychiatric Examination and Induced Psychosis

[[jolly-west]] — an active CIA psychiatrist — was eventually assigned to Ruby's case. West's appointment as psychiatrist was contested; the original judge blocked it. When West finally gained access, he spent approximately **one hour alone** with Ruby in his county jail cell with no cameras present. He immediately emerged to hold a press conference announcing that Ruby had suffered a **psychotic break from which he would never recover**, manifesting auditory hallucinations (including hearing the cries of Jewish children being boiled alive outside his cell) and visual hallucinations.

From that day forward, Ruby could not string three coherent sentences together. He died three years later — never having given coherent testimony about why he shot Oswald.

West's documented area of specialty included **inducing insanity in a person without their knowledge**. The Warren Commission was never told West had a conflict of interest as an active CIA asset. Ruby had previously told the Warren Commission directly that if taken out of Dallas, he could tell the truth — implying he was afraid to speak freely there.
