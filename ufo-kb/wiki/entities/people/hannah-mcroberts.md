# Hannah McRoberts

Canadian civilian witness; best known for an accidental UFO photograph taken on Vancouver Island, British Columbia, on October 8, 1981. McRoberts was driving with her husband near Kelsey Bay when she stopped to photograph a mountain whose cloud formation she found "a little nice." She did not notice any unusual object while composing and taking the photograph; only when the film was developed did the image reveal a classic dome-top flying saucer visible between the mountain peak and the cloud formation.

**Photograph characteristics**: The photograph shows a clearly disc-shaped object with a dome on top, in sharp focus, positioned in the frame as if part of the landscape photograph rather than superimposed. The film medium (rather than digital) and the accidental-capture narrative make photographic manipulation substantially more difficult to arrange. McRoberts had no known interest in UFOs before the photo, no history of making anomalous claims, and no apparent motive to fabricate the encounter.

**Credibility assessment**: [[claus-svahn]] — Scandinavian UFO researcher and co-author of *Files of the Unexplained* — investigated the case, spoke with McRoberts by telephone, and endorses her credibility. The accidental-capture mechanism is the strongest credibility element: a person who stages a UFO photograph plans to have a dramatic encounter story to tell; McRoberts's story is specifically that she saw nothing and only discovered the object in the photograph. This eliminates the core motive concern that plagues staged UFO photo cases.

**Case file location**: The photograph and case file are referenced by [[archives-for-the-unexplained]], the Scandinavian UFO archive organization, and appear in Svahn's published work.
