# Kirsten Gillibrand

US Senator from New York (D), member of the Senate Armed Services Committee. The most significant congressional champion of UAP transparency legislation as of 2023–2024. Gillibrand's position on Armed Services — the committee with jurisdiction over defense classification and special access programs — gave her the structural leverage to insert UAP disclosure requirements into the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA).

**UAP Disclosure Act (UAPDA)**: Gillibrand co-authored and championed the UAP Disclosure Act of 2023, which was inserted into the 2024 NDAA. The legislation created a new review board (modeled on the JFK Assassination Records Review Board) with authority to compel disclosure of UAP-related records from all government agencies and cleared contractors. The bill passed the Senate Armed Services Committee but was significantly weakened in conference with the House before final passage.

**Kirk McConnell**: Gillibrand's principal senior defense staffer, [[kirk-mcconnell]], drove much of the technical UAP legislative work. McConnell is described in [[D2tKCFmJjks]] as the staffer who developed the NDAA language and navigated the intelligence community opposition to the disclosure provisions.

**Significance**: Gillibrand's advocacy represents the most substantive congressional engagement with UAP classification since the [[jay-rockefeller]] initiative of the 1990s. Unlike predecessors, she tied disclosure to concrete enforcement mechanisms (review board with subpoena authority) rather than executive-branch voluntary disclosure.
