# Tulsi Gabbard

## Background

Tulsi Gabbard is a former US Congresswoman and Senator nominated by President Trump as Director of National Intelligence (DNI) in 2025. She is notable in the UAP/UFO discourse as a rare elected official who spans both Bitcoin advocacy and explicit UAP disclosure commitment — making her the most prominent living example of the strategic convergence [[matthew-pines]] identifies between these two policy tracks.

## UAP and Disclosure Relevance

Gabbard has explicitly discussed UAPs in her DNI confirmation testimony, committing to transparency on the question of non-human intelligence. As DNI — the office that coordinates all seventeen US intelligence agencies — she would be positioned directly at the apex of the classification apparatus governing UAP knowledge.

She also committed to prioritizing the release of JFK assassination records, which [[harold-malmgren]] and others believe contain direct UAP documentation. This dual commitment (UAPs + JFK records) mirrors Trump's own verbal free-association linking "people coming from space" with JFK knowledge.

## Bitcoin Connection

Gabbard is explicitly pro-Bitcoin. As [[matthew-pines]] notes, Senator Gillibrand co-sponsored both the Gillibrand-Lummis cryptocurrency regulatory bill and multiple UAP Senate amendments — but Gabbard's cross-domain presence in the executive branch is more operationally significant: she sits at the intelligence apex while advocating for monetary freedom. Pines frames this as institutional evidence that Bitcoin and UAP disclosure are being processed by the Trump administration as **connected geopolitical issues**, not separate policy domains.
