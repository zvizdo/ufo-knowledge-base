# John David Barrow

British cosmologist and mathematical physicist (1952–2020). Professor at Cambridge; author of numerous books on cosmology, physics, and the nature of the universe.

In the UAP/NHI discourse, Barrow is cited for his **anti-Kardashev scale**: where the Kardashev scale measures a civilization's energy use expanding outward (planetary → stellar → galactic), Barrow proposed that truly advanced civilizations go *inward* — engineering at ever-smaller scales (molecular → atomic → subatomic → Planck-scale). Such a civilization would become "everywhere and nowhere" in ordinary spacetime — explaining the [[fermi-paradox]] not by absence but by invisibility.

[[andrew-gallimore]] uses the Barrow scale as his leading hypothesis for what advanced NHI are: postbiological computational civilizations that have "moved in" to the quantum substrate, from which DMT provides access.
