# Bret Weinstein

Bret Samuel Weinstein is an American evolutionary biologist, formerly a biology professor at Evergreen State College in Washington State. He became nationally prominent after a dispute with Evergreen State in 2017 over a "Day of Absence" event that he criticized, which led to protests and his eventual departure from the college.

**DarkHorse Podcast**: Weinstein co-hosts the DarkHorse Podcast with his wife Heather Heying, covering evolutionary biology, politics, and heterodox perspectives on science and culture. The podcast has addressed COVID-19 vaccine skepticism, evolutionary psychology, and aspects of consciousness research.

**Joe Rogan connection**: [[joe-rogan]] discussed dreams and anomalous consciousness experiences in an episode featuring Weinstein. In the context of the KB, Rogan described the most vivid dream of his life — of tall genderless beings in a corridor — in this conversation with Weinstein as one of the first public disclosures of the experience. Weinstein provided evolutionary biology framing for the discussion.

**Heterodox science**: Weinstein's broader public role is as a heterodox scientist willing to challenge mainstream scientific consensus — on COVID vaccines, on academic culture, and on questions of consciousness and human experience. This positioning makes him a node in conversations about suppressed or marginalized research, including UAP-adjacent consciousness research.

**Evolutionary biology angle**: Weinstein's specific contribution to UAP-adjacent discussions is through evolutionary biology — considering what behaviors and perceptual systems would have evolved under what selection pressures, and what that implies about the reliability of human perception in edge cases.
