# Paul Benowitz

Albuquerque, New Mexico electronics businessman — owner of Thunder Scientific Corporation, a manufacturer of precision temperature and humidity instruments (still operating, run by his son). His home was in the "four hills" area of Albuquerque, on the north border of [[kirtland-afb]]; his company office was near the Wyoming gate of Kirtland. He was a licensed private pilot with aerobatic flying experience, a member of [[apro]], and had a masters degree in electrical engineering. In the early 1980s, he witnessed and filmed anomalous craft near Kirtland and began intercepting what he believed to be alien communications, which made him a target for an Air Force OSI disinformation operation run by [[rick-doty]].

Pre-affair photographs of Benowitz show a man with a "mischievous look on his face" — "a grown-up kid." By 1981–82, contemporaneous press photos show "long hair and a big beard... very serious." Greg Bishop notes he appeared to have pre-existing instability that AFOSI amplified rather than manufactured. (Greg Bishop, [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])

## The Doty Psyop

[[rick-doty]] — then an Air Force Counter Intelligence officer at Kirtland — infiltrated Benowitz's research, confirmed his beliefs, and then fed him an elaborate false narrative: that the signals were real alien communications, that aliens had underground bases in the American Southwest, and that the government had secret agreements with non-human intelligences. The disinformation was progressively escalated. Benowitz became convinced he was in direct contact with aliens via his computer.

The result: Benowitz experienced a psychological breakdown and was hospitalized psychiatrically. The operation is documented in detail in *[[project-beta]]* by [[greg-bishop]], which Jesse Michaels describes as a "great book" that introduced him to Doty.

## Significance

Benowitz represents the clearest documented case of intentional, deliberate destruction of a UAP researcher by US intelligence. He started from a genuine observation, and the CIA-OSI response was not debunking or ignoring him — it was actively amplifying and distorting his beliefs until he was incapacitated.

This raises the question of whether the same playbook was applied to other witnesses who had genuine encounters at nuclear facilities, and whether the "gray alien" mythology that exploded in 1980s popular culture was partly seeded by OSI's deliberate disinformation campaign through Benowitz and others.

## Brandenburg Corroboration (p0S0BfoZy0w)

[[john-brandenberg]] provides an independent firsthand corroboration of the Benowitz affair from proximity:

- Brandenburg drove past **Thunder Corporation** (Benowitz's company) every morning on his commute to Sandia National Labs. He noticed it: "That looks like an interesting place." He only later learned of the drama that had unfolded there.
- Brandenburg explicitly confirms Benowitz was **involuntarily committed** — not that he died in a mental institution (the transcript shows Brandenburg initially saying "I heard he died in a mental institution," then being corrected: "They involuntarily committed him"). This aligns with the known record: Benowitz was psychiatrically hospitalized but did not die there.
- Brandenburg also notes that **Bill Moore** confessed at a MUON conference to being a government agent monitoring the effectiveness of the SCOP operation on Benowitz "to drive him insane."
- Brandenburg's account is consistent with and independent of Greg Bishop's documentation — two separate informants describing the same events.
