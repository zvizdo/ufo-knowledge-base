# Brandon Fugel

Real estate entrepreneur and patron of frontier science. Purchased [[skinwalker-ranch]] from [[robert-bigelow]] and assembled a team of scientists to investigate the property's anomalous phenomena with the stated commitment to open-sourcing results — a departure from the classified approach of the [[bigelow-aerospace]] / [[aatip]] era.

## Background

Made his fortune in real estate. Has a personal interest in anomalous phenomena and frontier science. Describes himself as "an experiencer, not a believer" — meaning he considers personal encounter evidence more probative than belief systems. The purchase of the ranch is framed as both a personal pursuit and a scientific project.

## Ranch Stewardship

Under Fugel, the ranch has been documented in the History Channel / Netflix series *The Secrets of Skinwalker Ranch*. His team includes [[eric-barr]] (surveillance infrastructure) and has brought in outside experts including [[aaron-blunt]] (drilling/geology) and [[ravi-chandran]] (metallurgical analysis). Fugel's stated methodology: deploy comprehensive sensors, run controlled experiments, and share the data publicly.

Key findings under Fugel's tenure documented in this episode:
- **Mesa metallic object**: Ground-penetrating radar and soil resistivity located a large metallic structure; drilling confirmed a hard object ~400 ft wide. See [[skinwalker-mesa-metallic-object]].
- **Triangle anomaly**: A rocket launched into the "Triangle" sky patch deflected dramatically westward with no wind; a UAP appeared and disappeared simultaneously; gamma radiation spiked.
- **Biological effects**: Multiple visitors including the [[2016-skinwalker-illness-incident]] group have been hospitalized with no diagnostic explanation.

## Notable Claims

- **AATIP FOIA exemption by design**: Fugel confirmed that all data gathered 2007–2012 under [[aatip]] — which ran "under the auspices of [[bigelow-aerospace]]" — is both classified and FOIA-exempt through the proprietary-label protections applicable to private companies. He characterizes the structure as having been set up intentionally.
- **Private sector absorption of UAP programs**: "I don't believe that any of that [UAP study] has gone dark or ceased. I think it has only been shifted into the private sector into these special access programs, certainly aerospace corporations have knowledge in the UAP area that specifically are sequestered against FOIA."
- **Puzzle-piece siloing**: Fugel uses the puzzle metaphor: highly territorial factions hold different corners of the UAP picture and have no way to integrate — nobody has the whole image or knows what it looks like.
- **Personal sighting**: Saw a silver-grey disc ~100 ft above the Mesa; it moved 50 ft instantaneously then shot off like a bullet and vanished. This shifted his worldview completely.

## Maron Sphere Testing (V00WcEiKRAY)

[[ross-coulthart]] reveals that Fugel agreed to fly [[jim-maron]]'s anomalous sphere in his private jet to Utah for comprehensive lab testing at Skinwalker Ranch. Maron had previously resisted testing for years; Fugel's offer resolved the impasse. The sphere is reported to move under its own power on a perfectly level floor with no external stimulus.
