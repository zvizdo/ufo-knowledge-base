# Matthew Livelsberger

## Background

Matthew Livelsberger was a US Army Green Beret who on **January 1, 2025** detonated a Cybertruck packed with fireworks outside Trump Tower in Las Vegas. The act attracted media coverage as a Strange Than Fiction event; in its aftermath, podcaster [[sean-ryan]] interviewed a guest named Sam Shoemate who claimed Livelsberger had emailed him prior to the bombing.

## The Email

Livelsberger's email to Shoemate made two primary claims:
1. The **New Jersey mystery drones** (late 2024) were not prosaic — they were Chinese **electrogravitic propulsion craft** capable of carrying large payloads, with the ability to "vaporize New York or DC"
2. The email included coordinates that Jesse Michaels identifies as pointing to **Area 51**, which he frames as the locus of US gravitic propulsion operations

The FBI subsequently vindicated Shoemate: the email appears to have genuinely been sent by Livelsberger.

## Jesse's Analysis

Jesse Michaels treats the email with measured skepticism — he does not wholesale accept the "Chinese electrogravitic threat" framing, but considers it worth investigation. He uses the Livelsberger episode as a launching point for his broader argument that:
- The US and possibly China have cracked **anti-gravity in laboratory settings** (citing Thomas Townsend Brown and extended electrodynamics)
- The Area 51 coordinates in the email are notable because [[randy-anderson]] also worked there and observed a black triangle he believed was using electrogravitic propulsion
