# Mary Meyer

Artist and socialite; the wife of CIA counterintelligence official [[cord-meyer]] and John F. Kennedy's sustained romantic partner. She is significant in the JFK-UAP nexus as a consciousness-expanding influence on Kennedy at a critical moment in his presidency.

## Background

Mary Pinchot Meyer was a Washington D.C. artist who moved in Georgetown social circles. She married [[cord-meyer]] — a WWII veteran turned CIA officer who became the head of Operation Mockingbird (the CIA's media infiltration program). Their marriage deteriorated as Cord Meyer became, in Sheehan's characterization, "a warmonger" embedded in the Dulles intelligence network.

## LSD Sessions with JFK

Per [[danny-sheehan]]'s account (confirmed by published sources including Nina Burleigh's *Mary's Mosaic*):
- Mary Meyer had an ongoing, prolonged intimate relationship with JFK — "his actual sort of mistress"
- She and Kennedy conducted LSD sessions together; the substance was supplied by [[timothy-leary]]
- Sheehan frames these sessions as contributing to Kennedy's "metanoia" — a fundamental shift in his worldview regarding nuclear weapons and the military-industrial complex
- Kennedy's post-LSD trajectory included his American University speech (June 1963), the secret Khrushchev disarmament letters, and his demand for a UAP briefing
- Mary's motivation was partly to rein in the nuclear establishment her husband was embedded in

## Murder

Mary Meyer was shot and killed on the C&O Canal towpath in Georgetown on October 12, 1964 — less than a year after Kennedy's assassination. Her diary subsequently disappeared; [[james-angleton]] reportedly entered her studio immediately after her death and took the diary. The murder was never definitively solved.
