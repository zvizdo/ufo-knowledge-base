## Background

E. Howard Hunt was a career CIA officer and prolific spy novelist who became publicly known as one of the principal operatives in the Watergate break-in (1972), for which he was convicted and served prison time. Before Watergate, he was a veteran of the CIA's Bay of Pigs operation against Cuba — a covert action that drew on the same Cuban exile networks later implicated in various JFK assassination theories.

Hunt spent decades as a clandestine officer, and his career intersected with many of the most sensitive CIA operations of the Cold War era. He died in 2007.

## Notable Claims

- Before his death, Hunt told his personal lawyer [[douglas-caddy]] that the assassination of President John F. Kennedy "was about the alien presence." This deathbed statement is among the most direct attributions of a UAP motive to the JFK assassination from someone with documented CIA insider standing.
- Harald Malmgren cites Hunt's statement to Caddy as corroboration for his own assessment that UAPs were "probably the number one issue" behind JFK's killing — an assessment Malmgren grounds in his direct experience with UAP oversight and his knowledge of JFK's efforts to share UAP information with the Soviets.
- The CIA Bay of Pigs / Cuban exile network in which Hunt operated is also cited by researcher Danny Sheehan in connection with Nixon's 5412 committee, suggesting an overlap between the anti-Castro operational world and the UAP secrecy apparatus.
