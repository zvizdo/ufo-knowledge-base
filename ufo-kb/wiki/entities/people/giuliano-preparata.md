# Giuliano Preparata

Italian theoretical physicist at the University of Milan. Together with [[emilio-del-giudice]], he developed the quantum field theoretical basis for **coherent domains** in liquid water — the mechanism by which water molecules might spontaneously organize into coherent quantum states capable of facilitating energy transfer and, theoretically, catalyzing low-energy nuclear reactions.

## Cold Fusion Connection

Preparata and Del Giudice collaborated with [[martin-fleischmann]] and [[stanley-pons]] after the pair's controversial 1989 announcement of cold fusion results using palladium electrodes in heavy water. The Italian physicists provided a theoretical framework:

- **Coherent domains in water**: at low temperature and pressure, water molecules can align their oscillations into macroscopic coherent quantum states — like laser light but in a fluid medium.
- This coherence could create conditions favorable for bringing hydrogen nuclei close enough together for fusion without the Coulomb barrier requiring hot plasma.
- The "persuader" in Biondi's framing (in [[zrmG2jBckgk]]): "the atom of palladium persuades two hydrogen atoms to go together naturally without forcing them."

## Relevance to Pyramid Hypothesis

[[filippo-biondi]] cites Preparata and Del Giudice in the context of his water/pyramid hypothesis: if water flowing through the spiral underground structures generated coherent vibrations, these could theoretically interface with pyramid geometry in ways that go beyond conventional electromagnetic physics.

> Biondi in [[zrmG2jBckgk]]: "I suggest you to read the studies of Giuliano Preparata and Emilio Del Giudice."

## Fate

Biondi describes both Preparata and Del Giudice as having "disappeared" — clarifying he means they died (not in a mysterious way). Preparata died in the early 2000s; Del Giudice died ~2014–2015. Both are described as "wonderful" and "very clever."
