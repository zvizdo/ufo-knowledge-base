# Steven Bruell

PhD from Vanderbilt University; originally trained as a clinical psychologist before moving into biomedical research. Brings expertise in research design and statistics. Contacted [[beatriz-villarroel]] directly after learning of her [[vasco]] work and proposed a systematic statistical analysis of the relationship between nuclear tests and transient appearances.

## Background

Bruell's background in statistics and research design gave him the methodological tools to build a large longitudinal database. He compiled 2,700 days of data, recording: (1) whether a nuclear test occurred that day; (2) whether a transient was observed that day; (3) whether a UAP sighting was reported in the old Center for UFO Studies (CUFOS) database that day.

## Notable Claims

### Nuclear-Transient Correlation
- 134 nuclear tests occurred across the 2,700-day study period
- Transients were observed on ~300 of the 2,700 days
- Without a nuclear test: transients appear on **11%** of days
- With a nuclear test the previous day: transients appear on **~19%** of days
- This represents a **68% relative increase** in transient risk; **p = 0.008** (i.e., ~8 in 1,000 chance of occurring by error)

### Nuclear-UAP-Transient Triad
All three pairwise correlations — nuclear/transient, nuclear/UAP, UAP/transient — are statistically significant. This rules out artifact explanations that are local to the observatory or the film emulsion (e.g., cosmic rays from nuclear detonations could not produce a correlation with UAP sightings from the general public).

### 1956 Cessation
The last nuclear-test-correlated transient was March 17, 1956. Over the following full year, 38 additional nuclear tests were conducted without a single correlated transient. This temporal cutoff mirrors a separate finding by Larry Hancock (SCU): UAP activity at nuclear production facilities was high 1949–1953 and then abruptly ceased — even as more facilities came online. Bruell calls this "thoughtprovoking in an existential way," as it implies deliberate, intelligent interest that was then withdrawn.

### Plans to Improve Data Quality
Plans to use AI to clean the transient dataset — distinguishing plate defects and dust from real transients. Expects error reduction to increase the signal strength of observed correlations.
