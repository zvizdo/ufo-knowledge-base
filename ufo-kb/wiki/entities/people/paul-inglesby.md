# Paul Inglesby

A "maverick priest" referenced by [[nick-pope]] as having exerted "strong influence" on [[lord-hill-norton]] (retired UK Chief of the Defence Staff) regarding the UFO phenomenon. Inglesby held the view that UAP are demonic — not extraterrestrial or technological — in nature.

Pope notes that **intelligence analysts in both the US and UK** hold the demonic interpretation seriously; Inglesby is the clearest named UK advocate whose views shaped a senior defense official's engagement with the field.

The significance: [[lord-hill-norton]]'s parliamentary advocacy for UFO transparency was partly shaped by a theological framework in which UAP represent demonic activity — though Hill Norton's public arguments were framed in security terms. This is an under-documented thread in UK UAP discourse: the religious interpretation of the phenomenon influencing establishment-level advocacy.
