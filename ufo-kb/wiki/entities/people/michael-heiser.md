# Michael Heiser (deceased)

Evangelical biblical scholar and author. Trained in ancient languages and Second Temple Judaism. Best known in mainstream evangelical circles for *The Unseen Realm* (2015) and in the UFO/alternative-history research community for *Reversing Hermon* and his joint conferences with [[david-flynn]] and [[richard-hoagland]] on the Mars/Sidonia civilisation hypothesis.

Notably ran a website called **sitchiniswrong.com** critiquing [[zecharia-sitchin]]'s translations of Sumerian cylinder seals, on the grounds that Sitchin's interpretations were linguistically unsound. This is significant because Heiser, while debunking Sitchin, simultaneously affirmed the supernatural / historical reality of the Nephilim narrative from a rigorous scholarly perspective.

## Key Works

- ***The Unseen Realm*** (2015): Scholarly examination of the "divine council" worldview in the Hebrew Bible; argues the supernatural reading of Genesis 6 was the authentic ancient Israelite view
- ***Reversing Hermon*** (2017): Specifically addresses how Jesus's ministry in the New Testament reverses and undoes the damage caused by the Watchers' descent at Mount Hermon; argues for continuity between Enochian cosmology and the New Testament

## Significance for This KB

Heiser is the most academically credentialed corroborator of the Nephilim/Watchers thesis within the evangelical mainstream. [[tim-alberino]] recommends both books as "really really important" and "critical scholarly dissertations." The fact that a mainstream evangelical scholar with Heiser's credentials endorsed the supernatural interpretation of Genesis 6 and the historicity of the Watchers narrative lends the thesis significant scholarly weight it would not otherwise have.
