# Cecilia Levy

Cecilia Levy is a faculty member associated with [[u-albany-project-x]], the University at Albany (SUNY Albany) UAP research program co-directed by [[kevin-knuth]] and [[matthew-shostakus]]. The program is one of the few institutionally supported academic UAP research efforts in the world, distinguished by an endowment from donor Tony Gorman that provides ongoing funding.

**Research context**: The U Albany Project X program conducts field observation (including the 2023 *A Tear in the Sky* mission), material analysis (neutron-activation isotope analysis of alleged UAP debris including [[arts-parts]]), and peer-reviewed academic publications on UAP phenomena. Levy's specific research contributions within the program are not detailed in available KB sources.

**Institutional significance**: The U Albany program represents the small and growing sector of mainstream academic institutions willing to conduct serious scientific UAP research under institutional umbrella — as opposed to the predominantly civilian/independent researcher tradition that has historically dominated UAP investigation.
