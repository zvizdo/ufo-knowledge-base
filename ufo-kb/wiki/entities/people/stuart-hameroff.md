# Stuart Hameroff

Anesthesiologist at the University of Arizona; collaborator with physicist [[roger-penrose]] on the **Orchestrated Objective Reduction** (Orch OR) theory of consciousness. His key contribution: the identification of **microtubules** — tiny cylindrical protein polymers in neuronal cytoplasm — as the candidate site for quantum coherence in the brain.

## Microtubule Hypothesis

### Anatomy

Microtubules are cylindrical structures built from tubulin dimers, found in the cytoplasm of virtually all eukaryotic cells. In neurons they form part of the cytoskeleton and are involved in intracellular transport, signaling, and structural integrity. Hameroff noticed they have ordered, repeating ring structures ("indole rings" distributed throughout tubulin subunits) that could, in principle, maintain quantum superposition states.

### Anesthesia Argument

Hameroff's most compelling line of evidence for the microtubule hypothesis:
- When anesthesia is administered, it specifically disrupts microtubule quantum states
- All other brain functions continue normally under anesthesia (metabolic activity, neural firing, etc.)
- Only consciousness is switched off
- When microtubules resume their normal quantum states (anesthesia wears off), consciousness returns
- Conclusion: microtubules are the on/off switch for conscious perception — the quantum substrate Orch OR requires

### Orchestrated Objective Reduction

With [[roger-penrose]], Hameroff proposes that consciousness arises from quantum computations in microtubules. The "orchestration" is how the cell primes and times the quantum collapse. The "objective reduction" is a quantum collapse event (wave-function reduction) that is not random noise but governed by a real physical process at the Planck scale — each such event constituting a moment of conscious experience.

## Relevance to UAP/Consciousness Interface

If Orch OR is correct, then:
- The brain is literally a room-temperature quantum computer
- Quantum temporal non-locality ([[quantum-brain-precognition]]) is physically possible within the brain
- The microtubule is the physical substrate through which [[eric-wargo]]'s retrocausal signaling would operate
- [[efim-liberman]]'s goldfish experiment (cytoskeletal disruption → loss of spatial cognition) is independent corroboration of the cytoskeleton/consciousness link
