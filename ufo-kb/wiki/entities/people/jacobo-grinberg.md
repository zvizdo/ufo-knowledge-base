# Jacobo Grinberg

Mexican neurophysiologist and psychologist affiliated with UNAM who developed an influential theory of consciousness he called the **syntergic theory** — proposing that the brain interacts with a universal energy field he termed "the lattice" to construct holographic perceptual experience. He disappeared in 1994, shortly after what his associates describe as his most significant experimental findings.

## The Lattice Theory

Grinberg proposed that:
- Space is not empty but filled with a **"massive energy matrix"** — the lattice — permeating all of reality
- Human perception is mediated by the brain's interface with this lattice: the brain decodes lattice perturbations into subjective experience
- Individuals with highly trained cognition can synchronize with the lattice to a degree that allows alteration of perceptual reality — analogous (in the source's framing) to Neo's ability to rewrite the Matrix

This framework prefigures the kind of consciousness-as-information-processing-field models that have since emerged in quantum mind theories and is structurally adjacent to [[morphic-resonance]] and [[consciousness-non-local]] frameworks in this KB.

His experimental work reportedly included EEG transfer studies — measuring whether brain wave states could be transmitted between isolated subjects — which produced anomalous results suggesting non-local correlations.

## Disappearance

**Date:** December 1994  
**Circumstances:** Shortly after what his circle described as his most profound discovery, Grinberg disappeared. His laboratory was subsequently cleared out and his research records were erased. Only one item remained: a handwritten note.

> *"If you understand the system, you disappear."*

Whether this was written before his disappearance as a warning, or left by someone else afterward, is unknown.

No body was ever found. No charges were filed. His case remains officially open and unsolved.

## Connection to Andrija Puharich

[[greg-malazzi]] reveals that Grinberg and [[andrija-puharich]] actually met — in Mexico, after Puharich had gone into exile following the 1978 arson of his Ossining house. Grinberg's research (proving psychic phenomena are real, working in very similar areas to Puharich) made them natural colleagues.

The timing is extraordinary: Puharich died in January 1995. Grinberg disappeared in late 1994 / February 1995 — less than a month after Puharich's death. Both the [[stargate-program]] was officially terminated in 1995, and Puharich was lecturing openly about his Stargate-related research at the time of his death. Malazzi treats these simultaneous events as more than coincidental.

An anonymous message board post — since deleted — claimed that Puharich never actually died but was "taken in by the government and is still alive." Malazzi spent extensive time trying to find this post and could not locate it.
