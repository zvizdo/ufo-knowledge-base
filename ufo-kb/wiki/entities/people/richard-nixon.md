# Richard Nixon

37th President of the United States (1969–1974). As Dwight Eisenhower's Vice President, Nixon chaired the **NSC 5412 Committee** — the body that authorized CIA covert operations. [[danny-sheehan]]'s account positions Nixon as the architect of a covert assassination apparatus that connected Cuba policy, the JFK assassination, and Watergate into a single causal chain.

## Background

Vice President 1953–1961. Lost 1960 presidential election to Kennedy. President 1969–1974; resigned in the Watergate scandal. CIA Director for Ford: [[george-hw-bush]].

## The S Force — Sheehan's Account

Sheehan's reconstruction (based on multiple sources including [[santos-trafficante]]'s revelations to Sheehan's law firm):

1. As VP chairing the 5412 Committee, Nixon arranged to have [[howard-hughes]] call his lawyer [[robert-maheu]], who contacted Mafia bag man [[johnny-roselli]], who contacted [[sam-giancana]], who brought in [[santos-trafficante]], who had operated casinos in Batista's Cuba.
2. The team of ~15 operatives — the **S Force** — was ostensibly tasked with assassinating Castro, Raul Castro, and Che Guevara.
3. When Nixon lost the 1960 election, the S Force persisted without Kennedy's knowledge or authorization.
4. [[allen-dulles]] learned of Kennedy's secret correspondence with Khrushchev arranging mutual nuclear disarmament (18 secret letters). Dulles activated the S Force against Kennedy.
5. The S Force killed JFK on November 22, 1963. [[theodore-shackley]] was Miami CIA Station Chief that day.

## Watergate — The Real Motive

Sheehan's thesis (supported by the "smoking gun" tape of June 21, 1972):
- Nixon feared that [[larry-obrien]] (DNC chair and former Howard Hughes D.C. lobbyist) knew about the S Force through the Howard Hughes connection.
- Following the Mexico City bank account money (found in [[bernard-barker]]'s pocket) → [[carlos-marcello]] → Miami National Bank → the S Force funding chain would expose Nixon as the architect of JFK's murder.
- The Watergate break-in was not about political intelligence; it was about eliminating the evidentiary trail back to the assassination.

## UAP / UFO Connection

No direct UAP connection established for Nixon personally. Contextually: Nixon terminated [[project-blue-book]] in 1969 via the [[condon-commission]] findings, removing the Air Force's public-facing investigation of UFOs. This effectively ended any official investigative pathway.

## JFK Knowledge Claim

[[joe-rogan]] cites reporting that Nixon "apparently had been saying: 'I know who killed JFK and I know why they did it.'" This is consistent with [[danny-sheehan]]'s thesis that Nixon was the architect of the S Force and therefore knew the full operational chain behind Kennedy's assassination. Nixon's statement — if accurately reported — is an admission of knowledge that implicates him as the instigator even if not the trigger-puller.
