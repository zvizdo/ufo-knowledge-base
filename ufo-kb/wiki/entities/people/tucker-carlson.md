# Tucker Carlson

Tucker Swanson McNear Carlson (born 1969) is an American political commentator and television host. He hosted primetime programs on MSNBC, CNN, and Fox News (2016–2023) before departing Fox and launching independent media. His Fox News program *Tucker Carlson Tonight* was the highest-rated primetime cable news program during its run.

**UAP disclosure platform**: Carlson used his Fox News platform to cover UAP disclosure topics at a level unusual for mainstream political commentary. His interviews with figures including [[david-grusch]] (before the public Congressional testimony), former government officials discussing UAP programs, and researchers like [[lue-elizondo]] helped bring UAP disclosure topics to an audience that would not typically encounter them through specialist outlets.

**Catherine Austin Fitts interview**: [[catherine-austin-fitts]], the former Assistant Secretary of HUD who has documented the scale of missing federal funds and connected them to classified programs, appeared in a Carlson interview where she discussed [[richard-dolan]]'s [[breakaway-civilization]] hypothesis and pointed toward "space" as the destination for missing funds. This interview is the KB anchor for the Carlson/Fitts connection.

**Political context**: Carlson's political positioning (national conservative) influenced how UAP topics were framed in his coverage — emphasizing government deception and cover-up rather than scientific consensus questions. This framing, while amplifying disclosure, also associated UAP seriously with political movements in ways that some researchers consider problematic for scientific credibility.
