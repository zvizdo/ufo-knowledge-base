# Theodore Shackley

CIA Associate Deputy Director for Operations; one of the most operationally significant CIA figures across a 30-year span running from Miami anti-Castro operations through Southeast Asia to Iran-Contra. [[danny-sheehan]] identifies Shackley as a throughline connecting the JFK assassination operational environment to later CIA covert operations.

## Background

Career CIA officer. Known within intelligence circles as the "Blond Ghost" for his operational reach and low public profile.

## Key Roles (per Sheehan)

- **Miami CIA Station Chief, November 22, 1963**: Shackley was running the Miami station — the operational hub for anti-Castro covert activities — on the day Kennedy was assassinated. The S Force that Sheehan identifies as the JFK assassination team had its operational roots in this Miami apparatus.
- **Southeast Asia**: Ran assassination programs and covert operations during the Vietnam era; the same CIA-Mafia-heroin triangle that [[paul-helliwell]] and [[santos-trafficante]] operated in Batista's Cuba was extended through Shackley's Southeast Asia networks.
- **Iran-Contra**: Listed in [[danny-sheehan]]'s Christic Institute complaint as a key figure in the Iran-Contra network. [[felix-rodriguez]] (CIA operative involved in Iran-Contra) met with a [[george-hw-bush]] aide.
- **Plutonium shipments**: Sheehan alleges Shackley facilitated the movement of bomb-grade plutonium — connected to the [[karen-silkwood]] investigation — to Israel, and from there to Iran under the Shah. Chain of custody remains unresolved; this is one of Sheehan's open questions.
