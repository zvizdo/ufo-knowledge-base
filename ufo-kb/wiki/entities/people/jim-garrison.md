# Jim Garrison

Earling Carothers "Jim" Garrison (1921–1992) served as District Attorney of New Orleans from 1962 to 1973. He conducted the only criminal prosecution related to the Kennedy assassination — bringing [[clay-shaw]] to trial in 1969 for conspiracy. Shaw was acquitted, but Garrison's investigation uncovered a dense network of CIA-adjacent figures in New Orleans.

## Garrison's Files

[[peter-levenda]] went through Garrison's released investigation files and found striking personal connections. The files contained stationery from the [[american-orthodox-catholic-church]] — the intelligence-front church Levenda had briefly joined at age 17–18 to obtain a draft deferment. In the files were letters from the church leader ("Propheta," a Ukrainian bishop in the Bronx) addressed to Garrison, asking: *"When you're finished with Jack Martin, can you send him back to us?"* — revealing that [[jack-martin]], Garrison's informant, was a member of the same church Levenda had attended.

This produced a personal loop: Levenda's youthful intelligence-adjacent religious affiliation directly intersected with the investigation into Kennedy's assassination.
