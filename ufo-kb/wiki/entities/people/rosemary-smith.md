# Rosemary Smith

Remote viewer associated with the [[stargate-program]] (CIA/DIA). Her most documented session involved locating a crashed Soviet TU-22 spy plane somewhere on the African continent — one of the most operationally significant remote viewing successes on record.

## The TU-22 Africa Session

**Context**: In 1979, the US and USSR were in a race to locate a downed Soviet TU-22 reconnaissance aircraft somewhere in Africa. The plane carried sensitive Russian cryptographic equipment. [[dale-graf]] ran the psychic spy program at the time and tasked Rosemary Smith with the session.

**Task**: She was given **all of Africa** as the possible target area — an extraordinary starting constraint.

**Result**: She produced a drawing specifying a **3-square-mile area in Zaire** (now DRC). The plane was found exactly where she indicated.

**Presidential confirmation**: President [[jimmy-carter]] recalled this session as *"the most miraculous thing that occurred during my four-year presidential term"* and *"the only time that I have ever experienced something that was inexplicable"* as president.

## Significance

This session is among the strongest individual cases for operationally useful remote viewing:
- Extreme geographic compression: from all of Africa (~30 million km²) to 3 square miles
- Presidential-level confirmation; Carter had no motive for exaggeration
- Intelligence stakes were real (cryptographic equipment, Cold War sensitivity)
- Cross-corroborates the Stargate statistical record reviewed by [[jessica-utts]]
