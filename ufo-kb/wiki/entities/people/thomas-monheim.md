# Thomas Monheim

Inspector General of the Intelligence Community (ICIG), the senior oversight official responsible for receiving and investigating whistleblower complaints across the intelligence community. Monheim's office is the formal mechanism through which [[david-grusch]] and other UAP whistleblowers have filed classified disclosures.

## Relevance to UAP Disclosure

The ICIG is a critical bottleneck in UAP disclosure: whistleblowers with classified UAP knowledge who cannot go public must first file with the ICIG, who determines whether complaints are "urgent and credible" and passes them to the relevant congressional intelligence committees. Grusch's complaint to Monheim's office triggered the chain of events that led to his public testimony.
