# Dr. José Zalce

Formerly chief of the medical division for the Mexican Navy. One of the most credentialed and most personally imperiled researchers involved with the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]]. His institutional affiliation and the professional/legal consequences he suffered for investigating the mummies give his testimony unusual weight.

## Background

As a military forensic official, Zalce gained access to the mummy DNA results and examined the bodies. When he published his involvement and findings, the Mexican Navy imprisoned him for approximately five days on charges of dishonoring the Navy's credibility. He refused to cease the investigation, citing moral obligation to demonstrate the truth.

## Notable Claims

- The bodies are definitively real organisms (not constructed).
- DNA analysis of "Maria": ~70% known, ~30% completely unknown. Within the 70% known: the combination spans chimp/bonobo lineages from Africa and South Asian human primates — "a combination you would never see" in a single organism.
- The genetic dissimilarity is so great he speculates the beings may be extraterrestrial.
- His DNA interpretation relies heavily on data from Dr. David Ranel's analysis.

## Credibility Assessment

Military medical official; suffered genuine personal consequences (imprisonment). However, his genetics interpretation is secondhand — he is quoting Ranel's analysis, not running the sequencing himself. The 30% unknown figure requires independent replication. See [[credibility-frameworks]].
