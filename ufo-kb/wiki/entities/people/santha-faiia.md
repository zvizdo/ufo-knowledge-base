# Santha Faiia

Photographer and research companion to [[graham-hancock]]; they have been partners for decades across his fieldwork at ancient sites in Egypt, Mexico, Turkey, Indonesia, and elsewhere. Faiia is professionally a photographer and has contributed photographic documentation to Hancock's books and research projects.

**Philip Hancock inscription discovery**: Faiia is noted in the KB for a specific discovery she made with Hancock near the summit of the Great Pyramid: an inscription reading "P. Hancock — 5th April 1916." Hancock's grandfather Philip Hancock served with the British forces in Egypt during WWI. The family inscription was unknown to Graham before the discovery; its authenticity was confirmed by his father's wartime diary, which included the entry "Climbed Great Pyramid today" for the same date. The discovery has personal significance to Hancock and appears in his public talks on Egypt.

**Research partner role**: Faiia participates in Hancock's fieldwork and has been present at many of the sites central to his ancient-civilizations research — Göbekli Tepe, Gunung Padang, the [[giza-plateau]], Yonaguni. Her observations and documentation contribute to the research corpus without her typically being the named public voice.

**Critical framing**: Faiia appears in the KB primarily through her research partnership and the Pyramid inscription discovery rather than through independent claims. Her role is documented as part of the Hancock network rather than as an independent claimant.
