# Lee Hively

Physicist characterised by [[jesse-michaels]] as the **"godfather of extended electrodynamics in the modern context."** Published work on scalar longitudinal waves in collaboration with colleagues **Woodside** and **Low Bell**. Based in Colorado Springs.

Hively's work advances the theoretical case that scalar/longitudinal electromagnetic waves are physically real (not merely mathematical artifacts) and have measurable effects — part of the broader [[extended-electrodynamics]] program to restore the full Maxwell equations that Heaviside and Lorenz simplified away.

His proximity to Colorado Springs is notable: [[nikola-tesla]] also worked in Colorado Springs, where he conducted his high-voltage wireless transmission experiments and claimed to have observed overunity effects. The geographic overlap is at minimum coincidental, and at most suggestive of ongoing classified research infrastructure in that region (given [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s ties to Air Force R&D nearby).

## Aborted Meeting with Jesse Michaels

Jesse Michaels states he nearly met Hively but the meeting was cancelled, with Hively reportedly becoming nervous or the "door getting shut." This pattern — a credentialed physicist in a domain adjacent to UAP propulsion physics becoming suddenly unavailable — recurs in the extended electrodynamics community and is noted in [[physics-lockdown]] as circumstantial evidence of active suppression.

[[kirk-mcconnell]] mentioned knowing people who work directly with Hively, though he has not met Hively personally.
