# Helen McCall

Author of a white paper for the Soul Foundation on the intersection of UAP disclosure and financial stability risk.

## Financial Stability Paper

McCall argues that central banks and treasury departments are not tracking potential phase-transition risks from UAP disclosure, primarily due to social taboo and institutional resistance. [[matthew-pines]] endorses the analysis: the primary concern is not mass civilian panic but the potential functional collapse of bureaucratic institutions (Fed, Treasury, etc.) staffed by psychologically stressed technocrats who are already destabilized by political transitions.

Pines notes that UAP ontological shock dropped on top of already-stressed institutional populations could lead to non-show rates, payment system failures, and other systemic institutional failures that would cascade through financial infrastructure.
