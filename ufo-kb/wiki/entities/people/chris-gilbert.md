# Chris Gilbert

French-trained physician (MD and PhD from one of France's top medical schools). Specialist in internal medicine and nephrology; adolescent medicine background. Worked with Doctors Without Borders across four continents. Eric Haseltine's wife and co-author. Pioneer of "The Listening Cure" — a body-centered holistic medicine approach grounded in the concept of [[cellular-cognition]].

## Havana Syndrome Investigation

Gilbert and [[eric-haseltine]] are actively interviewing Havana syndrome / [[2016-havana-syndrome]] victims. Her medical training informs the physical symptomatology analysis:
- Directional high-frequency sound
- Otolith disruption (confirmed by ENT specialists in victims)
- Cognitive impairment, balance disruption, ear pain, tinnitus
- Symptoms that disappear on leaving the room and return on re-entry

## Biological Possibility Space

Gilbert presents a systematic survey of extremophile data to challenge assumptions about what biology can survive:

| Organism | Extreme condition survived |
|---|---|
| *Deinococcus radiodurans* | 3 years outside ISS; radiation, cold, vacuum, acid, dehydration |
| Tardigrades | 18 months in vacuum; 16,000 Gs; 30 years at 0°F; 10 years dehydrated |
| Fruit flies | 17,000 Gs |
| Nematode | Revived after 46,000 years in Siberian permafrost (2023) |
| Ants | 5,000 Gs |
| Round worms | 80,000 Gs |
| Mariana snailfish | 26,000 ft depth |

Argument: we should "never say never" about biological survival in interstellar G-forces and radiation — supporting [[panspermia]].

## The Listening Cure & Cellular Cognition

Gilbert's clinical practice is grounded in evidence that the body has distributed cognition:
- Single cells and plants can be classically conditioned
- Planaria (flatworms): the regrown head half (with no neurons after cutting) still knows a previously learned maze
- Each organ may have "a mind of its own"

This scientific framing makes her body-mind medicine approach more than metaphor. See [[cellular-cognition]].

## UAP / NHI Perspective

Gilbert and Haseltine together developed the [[exo-psychology]] methodology for imagining NHI cognition through inversion of human traits. Her contributions:
- Dark matter life hypothesis: NHI organisms made of dark matter, feeding on dark energy; to dark matter, "we appear as dark matter — we look dark"
- UAP may represent physics limitations, not biology: "it shows more the limitation of our understanding of physics than anything else"
- Multiple distinct phenomena: agrees with Haseltine that different UAP types are unrelated
