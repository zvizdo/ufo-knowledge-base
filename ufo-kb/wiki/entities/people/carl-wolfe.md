# Carl Wolfe

Carl Wolfe was a US Air Force veteran and photo technician who worked at [[nasa]]'s Langley Research Center. He is cited in UAP-adjacent and lunar anomaly research for claiming to have seen photographic evidence of artificial structures on the far side of the Moon while working in NASA's photo analysis section.

**Lunar anomaly claim**: According to accounts preserved primarily through the Disclosure Project and similar researcher archives, Wolfe was working in a NASA darkroom facility processing imagery from the Lunar Orbiter program when a colleague showed him photographs depicting what appeared to be artificially constructed structures on the lunar surface. Wolfe reportedly told colleagues and researchers about this observation but was never able to publicly testify in a formal setting.

**Death before testimony**: Wolfe died in what was characterized as a traffic accident — struck by a tractor-trailer while riding a bicycle — at a time when he had reportedly been preparing to make his account public through the [[disclosure-project]] or a related forum. His death before testimony is cited alongside similar cases (e.g., [[john-ford]], and others in the KB who died or were incapacitated prior to disclosure) as a pattern in which NASA and intelligence community insiders with anomalous knowledge are prevented from testifying.

**Credibility**: Wolfe's claim is unverifiable — his own account was secondhand and no photographic evidence he allegedly saw has been independently produced. The Lunar Orbiter imagery is publicly available; no artificial structures have been confirmed in that archive. His case is preserved primarily as a testimonial claim, not a documented evidentiary one.
