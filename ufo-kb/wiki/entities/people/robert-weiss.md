# Robert Weiss

Robert Weiss served as director of the Lockheed Skunk Works program after [[ben-rich]]. He is significant in this KB primarily as the Skunk Works director who collaborated with [[tom-delonge]] on the *Secret Machines* book series — a project that intertwined classified aerospace themes with Kabbalah and consciousness framing.

## Aerospace-Consciousness Nexus

[[nick-cook]]'s *[[hunt-for-zero-point]]* documents that the aerospace community has historically been more willing to engage "heretical" science (consciousness, parapsychology, psychokinesis) than mainstream science itself. The Weiss/DeLonge collaboration is a contemporary data point: the institutional heir to Skunk Works agreeing to co-author books that explicitly connect aerospace to Kabbalah/consciousness.

[[to-the-stars-academy]] — DeLonge's UAP disclosure vehicle — lists Weiss as an advisor or collaborator, situating him alongside former intelligence and military officials.

## Significance

Weiss's collaboration with DeLonge represents institutional aerospace legitimizing DeLonge's UAP disclosure project — the same kind of signal that [[ben-rich]]'s deathbed statements provided: that Skunk Works leadership privately believes the aerospace-consciousness-UAP connection is real enough to engage publicly (even if obliquely).
