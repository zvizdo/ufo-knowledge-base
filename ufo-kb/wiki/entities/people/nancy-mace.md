# Nancy Mace

Republican Congresswoman from South Carolina's 1st Congressional District; elected 2020. Member and at various points chair of the House Oversight and Accountability Committee. A former state legislator known for breaking from mainstream Republican positions on some issues; her UAP engagement reflects the broader cross-party coalescence around transparency demands that has characterized Congressional UAP oversight since 2021.

**2024 UAP hearing**: Mace chaired the subcommittee hearing on UAP oversight held at [[rayburn-house-office-building]] in November 2024 — a continuation of the Congressional UAP disclosure push that began with the July 2023 Grusch hearing. Witnesses at her hearing included [[michael-shellenberger]], who describes Mace expressing urgency about the need to "show some movement" on UAP — reflecting frustration that executive branch agencies were not cooperating with Congressional oversight requests. The hearing focused on whether programs existed that were concealing UAP-related information from oversight, and whether the UAP Disclosure Act provisions were being honored.

**Oversight context**: Mace's involvement illustrates that UAP has become a genuine oversight priority rather than a fringe issue within Congress. The [[house-oversight-committee]] is the primary Congressional body responsible for government accountability; its engagement with UAP signals that the topic has been elevated from the specialized defense committees where it originated ([[senate-intelligence-committee]], [[senate-armed-services-committee]]) to the general accountability-focused forum.

**Cross-party dynamics**: UAP oversight has generated unusual cross-party alignment — [[tim-burchett]] (R), [[anna-paulina-luna]] (R), [[mike-rounds]] (R), [[kirsten-gillibrand]] (D), [[marco-rubio]] (R) — cutting across normal partisan lines. Mace fits this pattern: her UAP engagement does not map cleanly onto other political positions, suggesting the issue is generating genuinely independent political energy.
