# Jon Kosloski

Director of the Department of Defense's **All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO)** since late 2024, succeeding Sean Kirkpatrick. Career intelligence-community technical official with prior NSA service.

In UAP discourse, Kosloski's most-cited public moment is his **2024 acknowledgment that AARO has reviewed cases that constitute "true anomalies"** — encounters his team has been unable to resolve to known phenomena. The statement is read as the most-credentialed sitting-IC-official acknowledgment that some UAP cases remain unexplained and consequential, in contrast to AARO's prior public framing under Kirkpatrick which emphasized mundane attribution.
