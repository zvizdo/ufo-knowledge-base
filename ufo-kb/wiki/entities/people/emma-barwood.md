# Emma Barwood

Phoenix, Arizona council woman who became the only elected official willing to publicly call for an investigation of the [[1997-phoenix-lights]]. She was "lambasted in the press" for the position. Her name was subsequently used to deceive at least one witness out of his video evidence.

## The Footage Confiscation

A Vietnam veteran had filmed the Phoenix Lights from his rooftop on a tripod — high-quality footage with audio of CB radio truckers describing the object. He contacted Barwood's office to share the footage. Before the official handoff, two men in dark suits arrived at his home claiming to be from Barwood's office. They asked one question: "Did you make any copies?" He said no. They took the footage.

When the vet called Barwood's office, she said she had never sent anyone. The footage has not resurfaced.

This is one of the cleaner documented [[men-in-black]] footage-confiscation cases: the impersonation of a named official, the targeted single question about copies, and the official's denial all on record.
