# George W. Bush

43rd President of the United States (2001–2009). Appears in the UAP discourse primarily through two threads: the failed AAWSAP SAP elevation attempt during his administration, and the NSC disclosure exercise described by [[hal-puthoff]].

## AAWSAP SAP Elevation Failure

[[harry-reid]] wrote a letter to [[william-lynn]] (Deputy Secretary of Defense) requesting SAP (Special Access Program) elevation for AAWSAP — a program that already held TSSI clearance. [[eric-davis]] explains in [[LnAiNChnuEQ]] why this failed:

> "Harry was using political muscle to bypass all that and the executive branch is taught where trained not to do that. Capitol Hill is not their Taskmaster. The Taskmaster is the president of the United States."

Davis argues the correct path would have been for Reid to approach Bush directly, or meet face-to-face. If the president had requested the SAP, Defense officials would have complied. Bush's NSA [[steven-hadley]] was the described clearing house for all UAP-related presidential matters — a direct call from Hadley to the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security would have achieved the result Reid wanted.

The fact that Reid used the wrong channel — sending a letter directly to the Deputy Secretary — signals either that Reid did not understand the SAP creation process or that he lacked access to the presidential channel needed to move it correctly.

## NSC Three-Body Problem Disclosure Exercise

In a separate account (relayed by [[hal-puthoff]] on Joe Rogan), the Bush-43 NSC under NSA [[steven-hadley]] read Liu Cixin's *The Three-Body Problem* as part of an exercise considering whether to disclose UAP. The conclusion was that disclosure was not worth it. [[ralph-moat-larson]], who knew Hadley well, said he had never heard of this exercise — neither confirming nor refuting it.
