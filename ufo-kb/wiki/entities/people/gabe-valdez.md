# Gabe Valdez

New Mexico state trooper who investigated cattle mutilations near [[dulce-new-mexico]] and became a key source for [[greg-bishop]]'s research on the [[paul-benowitz]] affair. Valdez is one of only three people Bishop interviewed who personally knew Benowitz during the critical period (the others being [[rick-doty]] and [[bill-moore]]).

## Firsthand Observations

Valdez made several significant firsthand observations that Bishop incorporated into *[[project-beta]]*:

**Benowitz's signal-intercept setup**: Valdez visited Benowitz's home and saw the elaborate electronics setup he had constructed to intercept what he believed were alien signals. He also witnessed the CRT display showing garbled video content — including images of dinosaurs and what appeared to be Roman battles — that Bishop believes was old film footage piped in by intelligence operatives from across the street. (Gabe Valdez, firsthand, via Greg Bishop, [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])

**The Archeleta Mesa crash site**: Valdez investigated a site in the Archeleta Mesa area (Jicarilla Apache reservation, northwestern New Mexico) where something had apparently crashed. He found "broken up brush and evidence that something had been picked up" and a pen marked "US Government Property." The crash predated or coincided with Benowitz's fixation on the area. Bishop suggests it may have been a stealth fighter or drone — a real event that AFOSI then used to further concentrate Benowitz's attention on the area rather than on Kirtland. (Gabe Valdez, firsthand, via Greg Bishop, [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])

**AFOSI props**: Valdez confirmed that AFOSI had placed jeeps and water tanks in the Archeleta Mesa area as stage props — physical props in a "24-hour stage play" — designed to give Benowitz something to focus on and photograph instead of Kirtland itself.

## Cattle Mutilation Investigations

Valdez was one of the primary investigators of the cattle mutilation wave in northwestern New Mexico, particularly near Dulce and the Jicarilla Apache reservation. His work in this area contributed to the broader [[dulce-underground-base]] mythology, though Valdez appears to have been a straightforward law enforcement investigator rather than a mythology promoter.
