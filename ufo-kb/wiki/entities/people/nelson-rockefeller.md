# Nelson Rockefeller

## Background

Nelson Aldrich Rockefeller (1908–1979) was one of the most powerful political figures in mid-20th-century America — Governor of New York (1959–73), Vice President under Gerald Ford (1974–77), and a perennial presidential contender. As heir to the Rockefeller family fortune, he commanded extraordinary resources for independent policy initiatives.

## Role as Kissinger's Patron

Rockefeller recruited [[henry-kissinger]] in 1955 to advise President Eisenhower on countering Soviet psychological warfare initiatives, forming an expert panel that first met at Quantico, Virginia. Two years later, he made Kissinger the director of the **Rockefeller Brothers Fund Special Studies Project** — a high-level panel producing classified assessments of US international security objectives that included [[edward-teller]].

Many recommendations from this project were reportedly integrated into Eisenhower's 1958 State of the Union address. Much of the classified output, however, remains locked in the **Rockefeller Archive Center** and has never been released.

Kissinger continued to work on Rockefeller's presidential campaigns (1960, 1964, 1968) even while consulting for the Kennedy White House, and many Rockefeller project alumni fed directly into both the Kennedy and Nixon administrations.

## MJ12 Connection

**Detlev Bronk**, who served as president of the **Rockefeller Foundation** during the 1950s, is named in MJ12 documents as one of the original Majestic 12 members. This places the Rockefeller institutional network directly adjacent to the alleged UFO oversight structure — suggesting that the Rockefeller Brothers Fund think tank work and MJ12 may have operated through overlapping personnel and resources.

Rockefeller's anti-Soviet psychological warfare mandate also provided a natural institutional umbrella for the kind of UAP information management that the [[psychological-strategy-board]] was pursuing simultaneously.
