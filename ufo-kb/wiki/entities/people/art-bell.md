# Art Bell

Host of Coast to Coast AM, the overnight radio program that reached 10–15 million listeners nightly at its peak. One of the most influential figures in popularizing UFO discourse in the 1990s-2000s.

## Background

Art Bell built Coast to Coast AM into the dominant late-night paranormal/UFO radio show in the United States. His willingness to take callers seriously and his own evident passion for the subject made him a trusted figure among UFO witnesses and insiders.

## Connection to UAP Evidence

Because of his high profile and evident genuine interest, Bell received by mail an unusual material now known as **[[arts-parts]]** — bismuth-magnesium nanolayers allegedly from the 1947 Roswell crash. The material's unusual properties (diamagnetic/paramagnetic pairing, bismuth's role as a high-k dielectric matching the Biefeld-Brown effect) have made it a subject of active scientific analysis:
- [[matthew-shostakus]] at University at Albany: neutron-activation isotope analysis
- [[garry-nolan]] at Stanford: mass spectrometry

[[kevin-knuth]] describes Bell as "obviously famous, Coast to Coast, amazing, you know, radio show host that reached you know, 10 to 15 million people every night. He was super into UFOs. And it's so interesting that he received these pieces cuz he was so passionate about this stuff."
