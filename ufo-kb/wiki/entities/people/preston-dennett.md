# Preston Dennett

Preston Dennett is a California-based UAP researcher and field investigator affiliated with MUFON (Mutual UFO Network). He has authored more than 30 books on UAP, paranormal phenomena, and related topics, making him among the most prolific researchers in the civilian UAP research community.

**USO specialization**: Dennett is particularly known for his research into Unidentified Submerged Objects (USOs) — UAP that are observed entering or emerging from bodies of water. His book *Undersea UFO Base* documents the history of anomalous USO and UAP activity in the Catalina Channel region off Southern California, which is one of the most consistently reported underwater UAP hotspot areas in the United States.

**Catalina Island**: [[catalina-island]] and its surrounding waters are central to Dennett's research — the region has accumulated decades of reports of anomalous underwater objects, unexplained lights descending into or rising from the ocean, and possible submersible UAP base hypotheses. His documentation of this area is the primary KB reference for Catalina UAP history.

**Nassau/Andros Island**: Dennett's research extends to the Bahamas region, particularly the waters around [[nassau-bahamas]] and Andros Island, where underwater cable installations and USO reports overlap with hypotheses about a potential underwater UAP presence near the AUTEC (Atlantic Undersea Test and Evaluation Center) facility.

**Research approach**: Dennett conducts field investigations, collects witness testimony, and synthesizes case histories. His work is in the civilian/independent researcher tradition rather than the government/academic track, but his case documentation provides primary source material for USO phenomenon analysis.
