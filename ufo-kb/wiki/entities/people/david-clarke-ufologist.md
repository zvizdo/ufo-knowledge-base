# David Clarke

UK-based UFO researcher and journalist who played the pivotal role in resurrecting the [[1990-calvine-photograph]] case. Clarke identified [[craig-lindsay]] through archival MOD documents and broke the story publicly before handing the thread to [[james-fox]] for the documentary treatment.

## Background

Clarke is one of the UK's most established UFO researchers, known for his work with Freedom of Information requests targeting the UK Ministry of Defence's UAP files and his academic/journalistic approach to the subject. His name is distinct from other "David Clarke" entries in the UFO literature.

## Calvine Discovery

Clarke's key contribution to the Calvine case:

1. Obtained a declassified (but partially redacted) MOD document related to the 1990 Calvine investigation
2. Found Craig Lindsay's name within that document — the RAF Press Officer who received a print from the local newspaper
3. Located Lindsay and confirmed he had retained one print for approximately 34 years
4. **Broke the story publicly** approximately two years before December 2024, placing the public disclosure around late 2022

## Introduction to Fox

Clarke subsequently introduced [[james-fox]] to Lindsay. Fox traveled to England specifically to meet Clarke, then continued on to Scotland to conduct Lindsay's first on-camera interview — included in *The Program* (2024). Clarke is credited in the documentary chain as the researcher who unlocked the Calvine case's surviving evidence.
