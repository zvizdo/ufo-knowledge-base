# Wernher von Braun

German-born rocket engineer who led the development of the V-2 ballistic missile under the Third Reich at Peenemünde, surrendered to US forces in 1945, and was brought to America under **Operation Paperclip**. He went on to direct NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center and was the chief architect of the **Saturn V rocket** that powered the Apollo Moon missions.

## Operation Paperclip Connection

Von Braun's US career began under **Operation Paperclip** — the post-WWII program that recruited former Nazi scientists into American military and civilian research. His colleagues from Peenemünde staffed early NASA and DoD programs. This pipeline is relevant to the KB because some researchers (e.g., those studying secret space programs) connect Paperclip's imported expertise with the alleged acceleration of black-program aerospace technology.
