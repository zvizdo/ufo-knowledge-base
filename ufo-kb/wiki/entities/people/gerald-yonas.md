# Gerald Yonas

## Background

Gerald Yonas is a long-time defense-science engineer best known publicly for directed-energy and pulsed-power work at Sandia National Laboratories; later affiliated with [[lockheed-martin]] where he co-filed the "Cognitive Enhancement using Feedback" patent with [[charles-chase]].

## Notable Claims

- **Closed-loop EEG patent.** The Chase/Yonas patent describes a system that reads brainwaves, compares to a target state, and applies light, sound, or electromagnetic pulses to nudge the brain toward that state — then re-measures. Same control architecture as [[biofusion]].
