# Sir Peter Horsley

British RAF officer who became the **closest aide and personal attaché to Queen Elizabeth I** (Jorjani's description — almost certainly Queen Elizabeth II is meant, given the 1950s timeframe). He had a remarkable encounter with apparently non-human individuals who demonstrated apparent foreknowledge of his future.

## The Nordic Encounter

In the 1950s — when the UK was first developing nuclear weapons — Horsley was invited to a meeting with two individuals identifying themselves as **Miss Markham** and **Mr. Janis**. He described them in retrospect:
- They presented themselves as Nordic in appearance
- Miss Markham was **completely expressionless** and "looked like Lady Dracula" — icy, pale, no emotional affect
- They invited him to help the UK **disarm its nuclear program**

## The Critical Exchange

Horsley challenged them: *If we disarm and the Soviets disarm, but then it turns out you people are up to no good — our nuclear weapons are our only defense against you.*

At this point, Miss Markham showed the **only emotion** she had displayed — a **"wicked smile"** — as if acknowledging he had understood the game.

He reported this meeting to authorities. When investigators went to the address, **the flat was empty** — the two had vanished.

## The Foreknowledge Element

Approximately **20 years later**, Sir Peter Horsley was placed in operational control of the British nuclear arsenal. [[jason-reza-jorjani]] argues in [[EzPmG_7WhXc]] that the beings who approached him had **foreknowledge of his future career trajectory** — they targeted a relatively junior RAF officer specifically because they knew he would one day control exactly the thing they wanted dismantled.
