# Ray Palmer

Ray Palmer was the editor of *Flying Saucers* magazine, one of the earliest dedicated UFO publications. In the UAP knowledge base he appears through a single but highly specific anecdote provided by [[charles-hall]]:

## The Roswell Fiber Optic Strip

As a child (approximately 5th or 6th grade, ~1951), Hall was in a store in London, Wisconsin, and saw Palmer displaying a piece of material approximately **one yard in length, woven like ceramic, that bent light without absorbing it**. Hall identifies this as a section of the ceramic fiber-optic coil material from the [[1947-roswell]] crash — the same type he would later encounter at [[indian-springs]] in the form of melted debris from [[small-grays]] craft.

Key properties as Hall recalls:
- Woven/braided, resembling ceramic construction
- Bent light through its length without absorbing or scattering it (fiber-optic behavior)
- Light exited from both ends of the yard-long strip
- Did not appear to be glass or conventional optical material

Hall argues this material — not the celebrated "memory metal" foil — was the scientifically significant Roswell debris. The foil was merely protective outer sheathing; the fiber-optic ceramic coils were the propulsion medium.
