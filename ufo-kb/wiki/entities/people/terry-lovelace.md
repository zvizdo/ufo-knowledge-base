# Terry Lovelace

Former USAF nuclear-base security officer who experienced a CE3 encounter during a camping trip with his partner Toby, reportedly in 1977 — the same year as [[mario-woods]]'s incident at [[ellsworth-air-force-base]]. Both cases involve a military security officer, a partner who subsequently disappeared or was unreachable, and the 1977 timeframe.

## The Encounter

Details are sparse in [[73YBwFvcmF8]]; Mario Woods describes Lovelace and Toby's encounter as a "camping trip" rather than an on-duty incident. This distinguishes it from the Woods case structurally.

Toby — Lovelace's partner — "never fully recovered" after the incident. According to Woods (relaying Lovelace's account), Toby developed a significant drinking problem from that period and is now untraceable. The VA gave him medication for anxiety. He is not in a mental health facility but has no traceable presence — similar to Michael Johnson's disappearance from the record.

Lovelace himself is still contactable ("Terry's still around," per Woods).

## Parallel to the Woods Case

Woods explicitly draws the parallel between his and Lovelace's situation:
- Both had a partner who became unreachable after the encounter
- Lovelace's partner Toby was also a light-skinned Black man — an observation Woods found worth noting
- Both incidents occurred in 1977

This pattern — a primary witness who persists and goes public, and a partner who disappears or deteriorates — appears more than once in the nuclear-site CE3 record and raises questions about differential treatment by the military/OSI apparatus, or differential psychological response to the experience.
