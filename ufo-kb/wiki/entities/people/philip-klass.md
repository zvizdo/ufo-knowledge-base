# Philip Klass

Philip Klass was one of the most prominent and persistent UFO debunkers in the United States from the 1960s through the 1990s. He was a technical editor at Aviation Week & Space Technology and wrote several books attacking UFO claims.

## Intelligence Community Allegations

[[richard-dolan]] describes Klass as "deeply deeply connected to the US intelligence community in my opinion, not just mine." This is not a marginal position — it has been raised by multiple researchers who observed the consistency and vigor with which Klass pursued not just specific cases but witnesses and researchers personally.

Whether Klass was a witting asset or simply an ideological skeptic who served the same function is an open question. His work unquestionably served the debunking mandate of the establishment position on UFOs.

## MJ-12 Attack

Klass's primary attack on the MJ12 documents focused on Truman's signature — claiming it was identical to a signature on another document, suggesting it was copied (i.e., the document was forged). Dolan's counter: autopen technology was in use during the Truman era, making signature duplication a possible artifact of authentic documents rather than proof of forgery. The New York Times was "very happy" to amplify Klass's debunking.
