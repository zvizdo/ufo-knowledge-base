# Richard Maita

A mysterious German high-voltage physicist who operated within **Schota Works** — the alleged Nazi black-programs division run by SS officer Hans Kammler. Maita's work involved the generation of extremely high voltages with extremely low currents across short distances — precisely the parameters [[thomas-townsend-brown]] had been investigating independently in the United States.

## Taom Mission Target

Maita's work bore an "uncanny resemblance" to Brown's own electrogravitics experiments. This parallel is why Brown was specifically recruited for the **Taom mission** — parachuted behind enemy lines into Nazi Germany in 1945, coordinated by MI6 spy [[william-stevenson]] ("Intrepid") and [[bill-donovan]] (OSS). The Taom mission reportedly helped capture Maita for the Allied Forces.

The convergence of Brown's and Maita's work in the same high-voltage, low-current regime suggests that multiple researchers on both sides of the Atlantic had independently discovered the same fundamental anomalous effect — or that there was some form of information leakage between them earlier in the war.

## Post-War: The Avrocar

By most accounts, Maita ended up in Canada after the war, where he worked on the **[[avrocar-project]]** — the joint US-Canada project run by [[john-frost]] to build a functional flying saucer. This is consistent with Operation Paperclip-era transfers of German aerospace talent to Allied programs. In the context of the avrocar, Maita was "probably trying to convince Frost that he needed to get the Schota Works band back together."

## The High-Voltage Lineage

Maita sits within a longer lineage of high-voltage physicists — from [[nikola-tesla]] at Wardenclyffe through Brown's own work — all operating in the domain of high voltage, low current, and short-distance charge across insulating materials. [[nick-cook]] frames Maita's work and Brown's as two nodes of the same suppressed physics program.
