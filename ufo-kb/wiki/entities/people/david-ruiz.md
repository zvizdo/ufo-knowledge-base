# Dr. David Ruiz

President of the Peruvian Society of Legal Medicine — the Peruvian equivalent of John McDow's role in the US. Has personally examined the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] bodies and their CT scans.

## Background

A conventional forensic physician with high institutional standing in Peru. His involvement carries particular weight because he risked professional backlash within the Peruvian establishment — the [[peruvian-ministry-of-culture]] was actively hostile to the case until at least September 2024.

## Notable Claims

- Asserts the beings are genuinely non-human but real organisms.
- States no artificial skull deformation markers (pressure lines) visible in Maria's skull tomography — unlike typical cranial binding artifacts.
- Argues that governments are suppressing the bodies because accepting them means accepting associated technology, which they want for reverse engineering and military supremacy. This is his most explosive claim.
- Invites scientists of the world to investigate rather than dismiss.

## Credibility Assessment

Credentialed institutional forensic physician with professional stake in accuracy. Took personal career risk. His speculation about reverse-engineering motivation goes beyond forensic expertise into political theory. See [[credibility-frameworks]].
