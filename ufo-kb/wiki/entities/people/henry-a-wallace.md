# Henry A. Wallace

Vice President of the United States under Franklin D. Roosevelt (1941–1945) and earlier Secretary of Agriculture. Long associated with esoteric and Theosophical currents — most notably his correspondence with Nicholas Roerich and his role in placing the Eye of Providence/pyramid on the US dollar bill in 1935.

## Background

Wallace cultivated relationships with mystics, occultists, and unconventional researchers throughout his political career. After leaving office he was named by [[andrija-puharich]] as one of the initial funders of the [[roundtable-foundation]] in Rockland, Maine — though [[greg-malazzi]]'s research suggests the bulk of the funding actually came from Army, Navy, CIA and an "AR Foundation" channel.

## Notable Claims

- Funded Puharich's Roundtable Foundation in its early years
- Linked through Theosophical/occult networks to figures who later appear in the [[the-nine]] and contactee literature
