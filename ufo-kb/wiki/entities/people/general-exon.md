# Brigadier General Thomas Jefferson Exon

USAF Brigadier General who was stationed at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base in the late 1940s. Exon is one of the most militarily credentialed firsthand witnesses to the Roswell retrieval, having stated in interviews that he personally witnessed unusual material and bodies being transported to Wright-Patterson following the 1947 incident.

## Firsthand Account

Exon reported that the material was unlike anything he had encountered, that bodies were part of the recovery, and that a small group of senior officials controlled all information about the recovery. He used the phrase "Unholy Thirteen" or similar to describe the oversight group — placing him in the same witness cluster as [[robert-sarbacher]] and other early-era military sources.
