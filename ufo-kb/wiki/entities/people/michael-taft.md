# Michael Taft

Michael Taft is a podcast host, author, and mindfulness teacher known primarily as the host of *Third Eye Drops*, a podcast exploring consciousness, psychedelics, neuroscience, meditation, and related topics. He has published books on meditation practice — notably *The Mind Illuminated* (co-authored with Culadasa / Dr. John Yates) — and is a leading figure in the secular, science-adjacent mindfulness community. His work attempts to integrate contemplative tradition with neuroscience rather than framing meditation in purely religious terms.

**Third Eye Drops podcast**: *Third Eye Drops* occupies a distinctive niche: a podcast willing to take seriously both the phenomenological claims of psychedelic researchers (DMT entity contact, psilocybin mystical experiences, ketamine dissociation) and the neuroscientific frameworks attempting to explain them. This dual seriousness — neither dismissing subjective experience nor uncritically accepting supernatural explanations — makes the podcast a bridge between the KB's psychedelic-consciousness thread and its NHI-contact thread. Guests include DMT researchers, consciousness philosophers, and contemplative practitioners.

**Gallimore connection**: [[andrew-gallimore]] — neuroscientist and DMT researcher known for proposing that DMT enables access to alternative reality frames populated by autonomous entities — appeared on *Third Eye Drops* to discuss his research into DMT entity contact, the pharmacology of the DMT experience, and the [[machine-elves]] and entity taxonomy that emerges from high-dose DMT sessions. This appearance is one of the public-facing discussions of Gallimore's thesis about DMT as a tuner for alternate realities.

**Secular contemplative tradition**: Taft's background in secular mindfulness is relevant to the KB's consciousness research thread. The secular approach — studying meditation through neuroscience and phenomenology rather than religious doctrine — has produced some of the most rigorous first-person data on altered states, which overlaps with the altered states framework used to interpret DMT entity encounters and consciousness-based NHI contact hypotheses.

**Positioning in the KB**: Taft represents the "podcast host as intellectual conduit" role — figures who do not themselves produce research but who enable high-quality public discussions between researchers, creating accessible records of specialized claims. His interview with Gallimore is the KB's primary source for Gallimore's public account of the DMT-entity thesis.
