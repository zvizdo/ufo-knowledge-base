# Leslie Groves

US Army Major General who commanded the [[manhattan-project]] from 1942 through its successful conclusion. Groves was the military director responsible for the overall project, working alongside scientific director [[j-robert-oppenheimer]] at [[los-alamos]] and supervising the entire network of Manhattan Project sites.

## Condon Conflict

[[edward-condon]] was brought onto the Manhattan Project as a senior physicist but clashed with Groves over what Condon perceived as excessive secrecy — specifically, compartmentalization so extreme that scientists couldn't communicate results across sections. Condon resigned or was forced out approximately six weeks in.

This conflict is significant in the context of [[debunkers-as-atomic-insiders]]: Condon left the Manhattan Project with deep institutional knowledge of its structure and the people involved, and later wrote the [[mcmahon-secrecy-act]] (1946) — the legal framework that governed atomic and, per Grusch's argument, UFO secrecy. His subsequent loss of clearance under McCarthy-era proceedings and his later appointment to head the [[condon-commission]] (to discredit UFO research) trace back to this network.
