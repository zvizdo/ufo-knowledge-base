# Amenhotep III

## Background

One of the most powerful pharaohs of the 18th dynasty (~1388–1351 BC). Father of [[akhenaten-pharaoh]]. His reign coincided with the height of Egypt's imperial power.

According to [[luke-caverns]]'s reading: by Amenhotep III's time, the temple priesthood — particularly the Amen priests at Karnak — had grown to control enormous economic resources (land, food offerings, donations) and were competing politically with the pharaoh. Amenhotep III was reportedly frustrated by this erosion of pharaonic supremacy.

Luke speculates that the young [[akhenaten-pharaoh]] grew up watching his father's rage against the priests and internalized it — which is why, when he became pharaoh, he went further than his father and simply abolished the entire institution.
