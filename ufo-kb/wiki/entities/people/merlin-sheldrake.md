# Merlin Sheldrake

Biologist and author of *Entangled Life* (2020), a widely acclaimed work weaving together fungal ecology, philosophy of biology, and the history of science. Son of [[rupert-sheldrake]]; his intellectual development was shaped by both his father's work and by [[terence-mckenna]], who stayed frequently at the Sheldrake family home in Hampstead, London. Merlin knew McKenna personally and engaged with his ideas during his formative years.

## Entangled Life

*Entangled Life* explores how fungi:
- Form the internet of forest ecosystems (mycorrhizal networks, "wood wide web")
- Demonstrate decision-making and memory without neurons
- Have challenged the plant/animal kingdom binary (fungi are a distinct kingdom; genetically they are closer to animals than plants)
- Take over insect behavior (the *Ophiocordyceps* / zombie ant fungus is the most dramatic example — fungi literally redirect an ant's behavior to climb a tree and sporulate)

[[jesse-michaels]] notes that the human microbiome, itself partly fungal, shapes human thought and behavior — connecting fungal intelligence to cognitive influence in a non-spectacular but continuous way.

## McKenna Influence and Panspermia

Jesse cites McKenna's claim — during a two-week Amazon expedition — that the mushroom spoke to him: **"Within my memory exists the knowledge for hyperlight drives."** This is the McKenna version of directed panspermia: fungi (or the entities mediated by psilocybin) as the cosmic information vector. [[francis-crick]] and Leslie Orgel (1973) proposed directed panspermia for bacteria; McKenna and the entheogenic tradition propose the same for fungal/mycelic life. Merlin's scientific work on fungal intelligence sits adjacent to this hypothesis without explicitly endorsing it.

The human susceptibility to fungal infection and influence (compared with plant/bacterial equivalents) is framed by Jesse as evidence of a deep biological compatibility — consistent with the panspermia model in which fungi (or their spores) arrived pre-adapted to interface with animal nervous systems.
