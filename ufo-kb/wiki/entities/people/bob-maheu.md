# Robert Maheu

Former FBI agent turned CIA contractor who served as Howard Hughes's chief executive deputy. Key intermediary between the CIA and organized crime in the Castro assassination plots (ZR/RIFLE program).

## Background

Maheu ran a private security firm used by CIA for deniable operations. He was the primary CIA contact with Santos Trafficante, Johnny Roselli, and Sam Giancana for Operation Mongoose anti-Castro plots. Later managed all of Howard Hughes's Nevada business interests.

## Notable Claims

- Central to the CIA-Mob collaboration documented by the Church Committee.
- Connected to Sheffield Edwards (CIA Security) in the S-Force assassination planning.
