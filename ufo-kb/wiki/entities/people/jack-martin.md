# Jack Martin

Jack Martin (real name Edward Stewart Suggs) was a figure in New Orleans's occult/intelligence fringe who held a position as a bishop in the [[american-orthodox-catholic-church]] — an organization that Levenda identifies as an intelligence front. He was an alcoholic and according to Levenda, a "go-to person for background intelligence."

## Role in the Garrison Investigation

On the day of the Kennedy assassination, Martin immediately telephoned Guy Banister's office and, in an altercation, gave police the name of [[david-ferrie]] as someone to investigate. This made him [[jim-garrison]]'s primary entry point into the Camp Street network. He survived the investigation, unlike Ferrie and others who died under suspicious circumstances.

## Connection to Levenda

[[peter-levenda]] had personally been a member of the [[american-orthodox-catholic-church]] as a teenager in New York (joining to obtain a draft deferment). When he later read Garrison's released files, he found letters from the church's leader ("Propheta") to Garrison asking for Martin to be returned — creating a direct personal intersection between Levenda's own background and the JFK assassination network.
