# Jean-Charles Duboc

Air France captain. On 28 January 1994, while commanding Air France Flight 3532 from Nice to London at approximately 39,000 feet over the Loire Valley, Duboc and co-pilot Valérie Chauffour observed a large, disc-shaped or lenticular craft estimated at ~250m diameter that remained visible for approximately 50 seconds. The object disappeared instantaneously (not by flying away).

## The 1994 Sighting

The [[1994-air-france-3532|Air France 3532 case]] is the primary French case documented in the [[cometa-report|COMETA Report]] (Chapter 1). The sighting was:

- Visually confirmed by Duboc and Chauffour
- Confirmed on primary radar at the Cinq-Mars-la-Pile Control and Detection Center (CDC); the track was recorded
- Classified as Category D (UAP-D, unexplained) under [[geipan|SEPRA's]] methodology

The craft's apparent diameter of 250m and its instantaneous disappearance are emphasized in COMETA as the key anomalous features distinguishing it from any known aircraft.
