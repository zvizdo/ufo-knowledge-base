# Felix Rodriguez

Felix Ismael Rodriguez Mendigutia is a Cuban-American CIA paramilitary officer and former operative. He is most publicly known for his direct role in the capture and execution of Ernesto "Che" Guevara in Bolivia in 1967 — Rodriguez was present at the schoolhouse where Guevara was killed and received his pipe as a personal trophy.

**Vietnam and Phoenix Program**: Rodriguez served in Vietnam working with the [[phoenix-program]] — the CIA's counterinsurgency and targeted assassination program. His work brought him into sustained contact with the senior covert operations network that would later become the Iran-Contra network.

**Iran-Contra involvement**: Rodriguez was a central figure in the logistics chain of the Iran-Contra operation — specifically the supply flights to Contra forces in Central America that also allegedly transported cocaine northward. [[danny-sheehan]]'s Christic Institute complaint, the most comprehensive legal case against the Iran-Contra network, named Rodriguez as a key figure. He met with a [[george-hw-bush]] aide (Donald Gregg) regarding the operation while Bush was Vice President — a meeting that became significant in the subsequent congressional investigation.

**Theodore Shackley connection**: [[theodore-shackley]] ("The Blond Ghost"), a senior CIA official, was Rodriguez's operational patron across multiple covert operations — from the anti-Castro operations (Rodriguez was a Bay of Pigs veteran) through Vietnam through Iran-Contra. The Shackley network is the KB's primary example of the "deep state" covert operations structure that persisted across administrations.

**Christic Institute filing**: [[danny-sheehan]]'s Christic Institute filed a civil RICO case against the Iran-Contra network in 1986, naming Rodriguez among those responsible for a pattern of drug trafficking, assassination, and political terrorism in service of covert policy objectives.
