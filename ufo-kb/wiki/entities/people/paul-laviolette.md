# Paul LaViolette

American physicist, author, and independent researcher. LaViolette holds a PhD in Systems Science from Portland State University and is the founder and president of the Starburst Foundation, a nonprofit research institute. He is best known within alternative physics and UAP circles for his theory of "subquantum kinetics" — an alternative to standard quantum mechanics and relativity — and for his extensive documentation of alleged classified electrogravitic propulsion research.

## Background

LaViolette's academic trajectory took him through conventional physics before he developed subquantum kinetics as an alternative cosmological framework. Unlike standard quantum field theory, his model posits a continuously transmuting ether of subquantum processes that gives rise to observable particles and fields. The theory is not accepted in mainstream physics, which largely does not engage with it, but LaViolette argues it makes predictions consistent with observation that standard models do not.

His engagement with electrogravitics began when he encountered [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s work and the 1956 Aviation Studies International report "Electrogravitics Systems" — a trade-press survey of the field that documented serious corporate interest (Bell, Douglas, Glenn L. Martin) before apparently going quiet. LaViolette concluded this represented not the death of the field but its classification.

## Key Works

**"Secrets of Antigravity Propulsion"** (2008, Bear & Co.) is LaViolette's primary contribution to the electrogravitics literature. The book covers:

- A detailed history of Thomas Townsend Brown's experiments and their reception
- The claim that the 1956 aviation press interest was a prelude to classification, not abandonment
- Analysis of the B2 Spirit bomber's leading-edge electrostatic charging system (publicly documented) as evidence of electrogravitic supplementation of conventional thrust
- Discussion of Project Skyvault (alleged USAF electrogravitic aircraft development)
- Theoretical framework in subquantum kinetics for why electrogravitic thrust should work

The B2 claim is LaViolette's most concrete and most contested specific argument. He notes that the B2 uses a high-voltage charging system on its leading edge (confirmed by a Northrop patent) and argues this is not merely for reducing radar cross-section (the official explanation) but creates electrostatic thrust by interacting with the atmosphere.

## Relationship to the [[biefeld-brown-effect]]

LaViolette is the most thorough public-domain synthesizer of the theoretical and historical literature connecting the [[biefeld-brown-effect]] to classified aerospace. His work provides the intellectual framework within which researchers like [[gary-mckinnon]] situate the exotic materials in the [[non-terrestrial-officers]] spreadsheet: if electrogravitics is real and classified, the barium/strontium/molybdenum transfers suggest a supply chain for high-K dielectric materials relevant to that propulsion system.

[[charles-buhler]]'s vacuum thrust results — published after LaViolette's major books — provide empirical support for the core claim LaViolette argued historically.

## Mainstream Assessment

LaViolette's subquantum kinetics is not recognized as a viable alternative to quantum field theory. His B2 claims rest on inference from public documents rather than insider testimony or direct evidence. Critics argue the B2 electrostatic system is fully explained by stealth function without invoking propulsion. His pulsar message claim (he argues certain pulsars encode messages from an advanced civilization using a prime-number-based code) is widely rejected by astronomers.

Within UAP/disclosure research, however, LaViolette is treated as a serious technical source because of the depth of his historical documentation and the specificity of his aerospace claims.
