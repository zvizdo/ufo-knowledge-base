# Harley Trent

Submarine specialist and remote viewer in the [[stargate-program]]. Described by [[joe-mcmoneagle]] as "a better viewer than I'll ever be." His specific domain expertise (submarines) made him a high-value contributor to the program's most significant operational intelligence task.

## TK-089 Soviet Submarine Session

Trent worked the **TK-089 prototype submarine target** simultaneously with McMoneagle. Their two independent sessions produced matching descriptions:

- 20 slanted launch tubes (unprecedented configuration — allowed launch while the submarine was underway)
- RS-27 missiles: 5 warheads each, approximately 50 megatons per warhead (Hiroshima was < 1 Mt)
- Submarine dimensions: 33 feet shorter than a Soviet aircraft carrier
- Could operate 100 miles off the US coast and destroy the country in 18 minutes

The convergence of two independent viewers on the same unusual configuration provided the corroborating evidence that made the report actionable at the National Security Council level, even before overhead photography confirmed it 114 days later.

## Death

Trent died relatively soon after the Stargate era. His session records — if preserved — are classified or unavailable. [[JpLThEF2dTM]] raises the question of whether his files are preserved and accessible.
