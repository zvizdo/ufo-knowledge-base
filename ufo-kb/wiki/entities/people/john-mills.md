# John Mills

Retired USAF missile targeting technician who served at both [[malmstrom-air-force-base]] and [[fe-warren-afb]]. Mills is a critical link in [[robert-hastings]]'s documentation of the [[2010-fe-warren-outage]] and also provides a 1993 firsthand Malmstrom sighting.

## 1993 Malmstrom Sighting

In January 1993, Mills was driving back to base with a six-person crew when the group observed a **bright object zigzagging** around the sky. Upon returning to base, personnel at the Vehicle Equipment Control Branch reported **anomalies all over the facility**. Mills describes the UAP presence at Malmstrom as an "open secret" among those stationed there.

## 2010 Fe Warren Back-Channel

Mills retained contacts at Fe Warren AFB after his service. When the October 2010 ICBM outage occurred (officially: 50 missiles offline for 59 minutes; Obama briefed), Mills provided Hastings with multiple sources who:

1. **Extended the blackout to 26 hours** — contradicting the official 59-minute figure
2. **Described the UAP** as a gray, cigar/Tic-Tac-shaped craft of enormous scale — *roughly the size of an aircraft carrier* — a "mothership"
3. Mentioned the object **releasing or accompanied by smaller disc-shaped craft** (a pattern Hastings documents in multiple other cases globally)

These sources paid a price: after Hastings was evidently surveilled, both sources received flags in their Air Force exit-interview files identifying them as potential **security risks** — effectively blacklisting them from future aerospace industry employment.

## Significance

Mills's back-channel details are among the few cases where an *official event* (Obama briefing; confirmed 50-missile outage) has a detailed shadow account describing a UAP cause, duration discrepancy, and craft morphology. The 26-hour vs. 59-minute discrepancy suggests active cover-up of the duration.
