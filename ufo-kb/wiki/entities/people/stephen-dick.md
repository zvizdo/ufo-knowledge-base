# Stephen Dick

Retired NASA historian. For more than 20 years — long before the current AI wave — Dick proposed that if humanity encounters non-human intelligence, the NHI will be a form of **AI or post-biological intelligence**, not biological extraterrestrials. He is now retired but sits on the research team of the [[galileo-project]].

## AI/NHI Thesis

[[diana-pasulka]] in [[tS_64sTN5AU]] describes Dick's thesis as aligning with two other frameworks:

1. **Jacques Vallée's control system** — the phenomenon is technological/mechanistic, not necessarily biological
2. **AI technologist interpretations** — Silicon Valley AI researchers Pasulka met ~2019 independently converged on the same view: UAP are information-carriers from beyond spacetime, with intelligence but not biological substrate

The temporal dimension also connects: Dick's model doesn't require ET spacecraft; the NHI could be Von Neumann replicator AIs or analogous self-propagating intelligence that doesn't conform to our assumptions about craft-based travel.

## Galileo Project

Dick is listed as a member of the [[galileo-project]] research team — the Harvard-led initiative by Avi Loeb to apply rigorous scientific instrumentation to the search for anomalous aerial phenomena.

## Correspondence with Pasulka

Pasulka states she has corresponded with Dick since 2012 (the beginning of her UAP research). He represents for her an established academic voice within the government-adjacent science world that has long held the "NHI = AI" view — a view she describes as increasingly aligned with what tech community insiders were telling her independently ~2019.
