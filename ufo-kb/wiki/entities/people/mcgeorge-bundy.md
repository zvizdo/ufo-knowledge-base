# McGeorge Bundy

## Background

McGeorge "Mac" Bundy served as National Security Adviser to both President Kennedy and President Johnson. As NSA, he was one of a handful of people with the most comprehensive access to classified national security information. He was a key figure in managing the Cuban Missile Crisis alongside [[harold-malmgren]], Robert McNamara, and others in the JFK administration.

## UAP Connection

In the [[09KP8XVf5nY]] episode, Bundy is mentioned as part of the inner national security circle that Malmgren inhabited during the Cuban Missile Crisis. Given Malmgren's own claims about UAP knowledge flowing to Q-cleared advisers, Bundy's position as NSA places him among the small group of officials who would have been on any UAP briefing distribution list above presidential level.

His proximity to JFK during the period when Kennedy was allegedly considering disclosing UAP knowledge to the Soviets makes him a significant — if indirect — figure in the [[jfk-assassination-ufo]] nexus.
