# Abraham Pais

Dutch-American theoretical physicist (1918–2000). Worked at the [[institute-for-advanced-study]] in Princeton alongside Albert Einstein and Niels Bohr. Author of *Subtle is the Lord* (1982) — considered the definitive scientific biography of Einstein — and *Niels Bohr's Times* (1991). His work on kaon physics contributed to understanding CP violation.

**In the KB**: Abraham Pais is referenced primarily through the [[pais-effect]] — a US Navy patent by [[salvatore-pais]] describing high-frequency electromagnetic field manipulation of inertial mass. Salvatore Pais named the effect after himself and, as a tribute, after Abraham Pais — both physicists who worked at the intersection of quantum mechanics and field theory. The two are not otherwise related; this is a naming coincidence exploited as a respectful tribute.

**Historical significance**: Abraham Pais's close relationship with Einstein during Einstein's Princeton years gave him access to unpublished thinking and personal conversations that appear in his biography. He was present at the Institute during the same period that [[john-von-neumann]] and [[solomon-lefschetz]] were there — a concentration of foundational 20th-century physics that is noted in the KB's Institute for Advanced Study cluster.
