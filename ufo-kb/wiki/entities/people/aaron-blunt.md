# Aaron Blunt

Drilling expert engaged by [[brandon-fugel]]'s team to physically investigate the anomalous object detected by ground-penetrating radar under the Mesa at [[skinwalker-ranch]].

## Drilling Findings

After ground-penetrating radar and soil resistivity surveys located a large metallic structure under the Mesa, Blunt was brought in to drill. The drill went horizontally into the Mesa, then when operators attempted to angle it upward, it encountered an impenetrably hard object it could not break through — despite geological conditions that should have allowed penetration. Blunt scouted the edges of the object and concluded it was approximately **400 feet wide**.

The drill collected hundreds of metal shards deposited at the drilling site, which were subsequently analyzed by metallurgist [[ravi-chandran]], who identified them as fused metal containing europium and tellurium — concluding the object was manufactured rather than naturally occurring. See [[skinwalker-mesa-metallic-object]].
