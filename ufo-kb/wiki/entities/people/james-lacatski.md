# James Lacatski

Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) analyst credited as a co-founder of [[aawsap]] — the broader classified UAP/paranormal research program that preceded and overlapped with [[aatip]]. Lacatski visited [[skinwalker-ranch]] and, according to *Skinwalkers at the Pentagon*, reported an anomalous experience there that motivated the AAWSAP program's focus on the ranch.

## AAWSAP and the Ranch

Lacatski is described as the government official whose personal encounter at Skinwalker Ranch helped generate institutional momentum for AAWSAP funding. He worked with the DIA to establish the program, which contracted [[robert-bigelow]]'s [[bigelow-aerospace]] entity to conduct investigations.

## Skinwalkers at the Pentagon

Co-authored [[skinwalkers-at-the-pentagon]] with [[colm-kelleher]] and [[george-knapp]]. The book is notable as a partially declassified insider account of AAWSAP's findings — including the **hitchhiker effect** (phenomena following investigators home), the range and severity of biological effects on ranch personnel, and the broader theoretical framework the program developed for understanding the multi-phenomenon environment.
