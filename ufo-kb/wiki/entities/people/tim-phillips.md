# Tim Phillips

Disclosure researcher with behind-the-scenes connections to both the UAP research community and figures associated with government-adjacent disinformation operations. Phillips appeared on the **Event Horizon podcast** and made claims about the AARO assessment of "Psalm 101" documents.

**Psalm 101 passage material claim**: Phillips claimed that [[aaro]] (the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office) conducted analysis and found that "Psalm 101" — a set of documents apparently circulating in the UAP disclosure community — constituted **passage material**: fabricated documents designed to be passed from source to source, eventually building enough apparent provenance to be treated as authentic intelligence. Passage material is a documented intelligence community technique — fabricate a document, inject it into a plausible circulation chain, allow it to acquire apparent chain of custody, then surface it as "evidence" of classified programs.

**Rick Doty connection**: Phillips is described as operating alongside [[rick-doty]] in behind-the-scenes discussions. Doty is a former AFOSI (Air Force Office of Special Investigations) agent with a documented history of disinformation operations against UAP researchers — most infamously his manipulation of Paul Bennewitz. Phillips's association with Doty situates him in a zone where the line between information and disinformation is actively contested.

**Majestic-12 relevance**: The Psalm 101 passage material claim is directly relevant to the broader [[majestic-12]] document debate. If AARO found that at least one category of documents circulating as "UAP insider evidence" is deliberately fabricated passage material, the implication is that the document ecosystem has been contaminated with fabrications designed to discredit legitimate research by association. [[uap-gerb]] cites this as a reason to treat all MJ-12-adjacent documents as auxiliary material rather than primary evidence.
