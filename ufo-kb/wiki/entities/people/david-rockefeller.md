# David Rockefeller

Chairman and CEO of Chase Manhattan Bank (1969–1981); co-founder of the Trilateral Commission (1973, with Zbigniew Brzezinski); long-serving member of the Council on Foreign Relations and regular Bilderberg attendee. Considered by many analysts to be among the most influential private citizens in 20th-century American foreign policy and finance.

**Trilateral Commission**: David Rockefeller co-founded the Trilateral Commission as a non-governmental forum for elites from North America, Western Europe, and Japan to coordinate policy positions on trade, security, and governance. Critics from both left and right have argued it constitutes an unelected transnational governance structure operating outside democratic accountability — a framing structurally analogous to how the KB characterizes UAP program management.

**Opposition to disclosure**: In the KB, David Rockefeller appears as a counterpoint to his brother [[laurance-rockefeller]], who actively lobbied for UAP disclosure in the 1990s through the [[1995-rockefeller-ufo-initiative]]. David reportedly opposed Laurance's disclosure push. The family split on this issue reflects a broader elite-network pattern: individuals with knowledge of or access to information about UAP are not uniformly pro-disclosure; some with the deepest establishment ties actively resist it.

**Rockefeller Foundation context**: David's stewardship of the Chase Manhattan–Rockefeller financial empire is distinct from the [[rockefeller-foundation]] philanthropic activities, but both reflect the same family's dual role as both institutional power-brokers and occasional reformers — a tension the KB traces in the UAP domain.
