# Steven Spielberg

Steven Spielberg (b. 1946) is one of the most influential filmmakers in Hollywood history, best known in the UFO/UAP context for *Close Encounters of the Third Kind* (1977) and *E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial* (1982). In UAP discourse, he is a recurring figure in the claim that Hollywood has served as a vehicle for controlled disclosure or cultural pre-conditioning.
