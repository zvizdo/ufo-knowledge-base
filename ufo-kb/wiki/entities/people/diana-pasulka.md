# Diana Pasulka

Professor of Religious Studies at the University of North Carolina Wilmington. Author of *Heaven Can Wait* (focused on Catholic purgatory) and a later work on the convergence between mystical/religious experiences and UAP/contact phenomenology.

## UAP Connection

[[danny-sheehan]] references Pasulka in the context of his work with [[john-mack]] and the broader project of cross-mapping anomalous experiencer accounts:
- Pasulka's research documented structural parallels between **Catholic accounts of apparitions and divine encounters** (particularly Marian apparitions and reports of purgatorial experience) and **modern alien contact/abduction accounts** — similar sensory details, similar emotional texture, similar recurring motifs.
- This convergence is cited by Sheehan as evidence that the NHI phenomenon is cross-cultural and trans-historical, appearing in the religious idiom of a given culture's framework.

## Tim Taylor Connection

[[bob-maguire]] adds a specific detail about Tim Taylor and Pasulka: Taylor escorted Pasulka to the **Vatican Observatory** and was admitted to the **Vatican archives without her and without an escort** — flashing multiple three-letter-agency credentials on demand. The Vatican acknowledged and admitted him. Ryan Bledsoe told Bob that Taylor claimed to Pasulka that he was part of a **secret time travel group in Nassau, Bahamas**, with [[thomas-townsend-brown]] as its president.

This places Pasulka as a documentarian who witnesses Taylor's extraordinary institutional access firsthand — her account of Taylor's Vatican credentials is corroboration for his claim of multi-agency reach.

## American Cosmic — 1947 Dividing Line Thesis

In [[I9VyyV4-3Js]], [[brian-muraresku]] cites Pasulka's book *[[american-cosmic]]* (2019) and her thesis that **1947 (Roswell) is a dividing line** in how the ancient contact phenomenon presents:

> Before 1947: angels, demons, genies, sylphs, fairies, gnomes, elves, leprechauns. After 1947: aliens and spacecraft.

Muraresku frames Pasulka as a key ally whose work bridges religious studies, UAP research, and the mystery-religion thesis. He cites *American Cosmic* as a book he "loves" and describes her approach as essential for understanding how the idiom of contact changes while the underlying phenomenon remains continuous. This thesis connects directly to [[trickster-intelligence]] and [[ufo-religion-convergence]].

## Catholic Aerial Phenomena 1200–1800

While researching Catholic purgatory in Vatican and other archives (for *Heaven Can Wait*, 2014), Pasulka unexpectedly found a long series of **aerial phenomena reports by European Catholics from 1200–1800**: discs, orbs, luminous phenomena, "flying houses" (e.g., the Flying House of Laredo). She logged them as anomalous data without connecting them to modern UAP.

When she later published findings about "angel contact events," **multiple aerospace company employees** (from multiple companies, not just one) contacted her asking for this data — specifically: what did people describe seeing, what color was the light, what did the objects look like, and **did people report injuries**? The injury question was particularly notable. This was the beginning of her entry into UAP research.

## Hereticon Panel (2026): Primary Speaker (aa9Xx5wI8Rw)

Pasulka appeared as primary speaker alongside [[carl-nell]] at Hereticon, with [[jesse-michaels]] hosting. Key material not previously covered:

### St. Francis Stigmata as UAP Electromagnetic Injury

Primary-source analysis of the Francis stigmata event (1224 CE): Brother Leo (age ~15) witnessed a seraphim with an eye in the center descend; it "dealt harshly with Francis." Francis's wounds (the stigmata) match **electromagnetic radiation burn injuries** documented by [[garry-nolan]]'s CIA-commissioned research on UAP experiencers — "comport one for one." Francis died likely from these wounds. The event was marked by institutionalized non-disclosure (Francis and Leo told no one). The word *stigma* itself encodes this: a mark that enforces silence. See [[ufo-electromagnetic-injuries]].

### Cosmism: US and Russian Space Programs

Traced the founding epistemologies of both space programs:
- **Russian**: [[konstantin-tsiolkovsky]] explicitly believed in NHI and wrote books on it; Slavic-Christian cosmism tradition.
- **American**: [[jack-parsons]] — not framing his NHI contact as demonic; viewed it as advancing the "common task" of getting humanity to space. Greco-Roman framework.

Named her book *[[american-cosmic]]* because this tradition "we hadn't talked about." See [[cosmism]].

### Space Program Patches

Insiders showed Pasulka mission patches with **Roman gods and first-century Latin** — dedications to "the sponsors" (the NHI the programs believe they work with). One collector had 150+ patches. The tradition is transmitted orally, not via memo; people sometimes don't know the true nature of their mission until they receive a patch years later. When she asked if the NHI read Latin: **"Absolutely."** See [[space-program-patches]].

### Plato Read Non-Allegorically

After witnessing UAP non-disclosure harassment firsthand, Pasulka re-read Plato's *Republic* and concluded the **allegory of the cave is not allegorical** — it describes how things actually are. Plato's answer to "can there be a just society?" is the **dialectic** — a mystical community-based conversation oriented toward justice/goodness — and the **myth of Er** (the soldier's NDE) she reads as Plato's literal belief in soul immortality, likely informed by his participation in the Eleusinian mystery rituals.

She identifies a lineage: Pyramid Texts (Egypt ~2500 BC) → Hermetic/Gnostic tradition → classical Antiquity → early Christian theology → the present.

### Tim Taylor at Vatican Observatory

Added detail: when Taylor's real name was announced at the Vatican Observatory (Castel Gandolfo) to a room of young European Space Agency scientists, **the whole room erupted into applause**. American researchers did not know who he was; Europeans did. Pasulka writes eulogies for deceased "Invisibles" (people like Tyler whose families couldn't eulogize them correctly because they didn't know what their work had been).

### René Girard

Pasulka's intellectual hero. She attended [[rene-girard]]'s colloquiums at Stanford while at Berkeley as a graduate student. His mimetic theory — all desire is imitative; social violence is channeled through scapegoating — was too large a "theory of everything" for either religious scholars (no transcendent metaphysics) or evolutionary biologists. She parallels her own double-rejection status to his. Connected to [[peter-thiel]]'s Antichrist analysis (Thiel also studied under Girard).

## American Alchemy Interview (tS_64sTN5AU)

The longest and most comprehensive single interview with Pasulka in the KB. Recorded in Palo Alto around the launch of *[[encounters-pasulka|Encounters]]*. Key material not covered elsewhere:

### Tyler's Protocols (Detailed)

Pasulka describes Tyler's ([[tim-taylor]]) information-download protocols in detail: he prioritized **sleep**, **hydration** (the body is ~80% water; water conducts electricity, so hydration matters for any bio-electric/signal-reception process), and a specific **waking state of receptivity**. He described receiving a "signal coming in" from which he would extract information used to found biotech companies and innovations. Pasulka began practicing these protocols herself because she was overworked and over-caffeinated.

### The Night Before the New Mexico Crash Site

The group (Pasulka, Tyler, [[garry-nolan]]) spent the night before visiting the New Mexico crash retrieval site relaxing with a beer. Garry Nolan took that opportunity to share roughly **two hours of his research** — photos, data, and findings on UAP-injured individuals. Pasulka was "horrified" because Nolan's motivating question was how to **stop this from happening to people** — the injuries were severe. This moment crystallized for Pasulka that the radiation/injury dimension was real and serious.

### Girard Extended

Pasulka describes attending [[rene-girard]]'s **Stanford colloquiums as a graduate student at Berkeley**. In [[tS_64sTN5AU]], Jesse elicits a detailed application of Girard's scapegoating theory to the Eucharist: the Mass is a ritualized sacrifice of a perfect innocent victim that has been repeated so many times that participants no longer consciously know they are re-enacting a killing. Pasulka frames the **UFO as a forcing function** that breaks through this automated programmatic behavior — analogous to how Girard's mimetic theory was too large a "theory of everything" for any single discipline to contain.

### Outer Limits / Betty and Barney Hill

Pasulka raises the temporal overlap between the **CBS Outer Limits** premiere (1963) and Betty and Barney Hill's abduction report: the show featured aliens with **horizontal slit-eyes wrapping around the rim of their heads** — essentially the same visual description the Hills reported. This is a flagship example of Pasulka's argument that **cultural media shapes the phenomenological overlay** of contact experiences.

### Four UAP Research Traditions

Pasulka introduces a taxonomy of four distinct UAP research lineages (fleshed out in *[[encounters-pasulka|Encounters]]*):
1. Intelligence/classified tradition (Hynek, invisible college)
2. Public hobbyist ufology
3. Academic scholarship (John Mack, Pasulka)
4. **Emergent fourth tradition** — accidental insider-civilian collaborations (Tyler + Pasulka) producing a new kind of transparency

### Muses Talk

Around the time of this interview Pasulka gave a talk titled **"The Muse Is Real"** (she asked it not be recorded). She traced muses from ancient Greece → Benjamin Franklin's **Masonic Muse Lodge** → modern space-program **mission patches**. Key claim: **museums** were originally conceived as containers for the spirit of the muses. This is an operational oral tradition, not allegory — the founding fathers knew the muses were real entities. See [[muses-as-real-entities]].

### Disclosure as Colonial Concept

Pasulka's sharpest political critique: **disclosure, as currently framed, is a Colonial concept** — it assumes the US government is the first legitimate authority to receive UAP knowledge, erasing Indigenous, Aboriginal Australian, Haitian Vodou, and other non-Western traditions with continuous contact frameworks.

### Personal Transformation / Forcing Function

In response to Jesse's coinage of "forcing function," Pasulka endorses it fully: contact experiences force people toward the **straight and narrow** — she herself stopped drinking and gave up caffeine. The phenomenon is like an amplifier of spiritual reality; it intensifies both good and bad influences, requiring hypervigilance (she invokes C.S. Lewis's *Screwtape Letters*). Her thesis: the only thing truly in any individual's power is **personal salvation** — not collective structural change.
