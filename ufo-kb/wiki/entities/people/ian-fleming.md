# Ian Fleming

British Naval Intelligence officer and author of the James Bond novels. Served in intelligence during WWII with direct access to covert operations.

## Spectre = ODESSA

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] argues that Fleming's real MI6 assignment was facilitating [[martin-bormann]]'s evacuation to Argentina after the war. This operational experience directly informed the James Bond franchise:
- The Spectre organization = [[odessa]] (Order of Former SS Officers)
- The global criminal-intelligence network with Nazi origins became the canonical Bond villain organization
