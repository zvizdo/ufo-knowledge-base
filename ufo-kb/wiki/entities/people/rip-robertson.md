# Rip Robertson

CIA covert operator who served as the field operations commander for the [[s-force]]. Per [[danny-sheehan]], Robertson is identifiable in photographs taken at **Dealey Plaza** on November 22, 1963 — placing him at the JFK assassination scene.

## Background

Robertson operated in the anti-Castro Cuban exile network in the Miami/Florida area, connected to the JM Wave station and the broader paramilitary operations infrastructure. He was a hands-on field commander, not a desk officer.

## Dealey Plaza

Sheehan states that photographs taken at Dealey Plaza show Robertson present — along with [[david-morales]] (the S-Force shooter). The presence of S-Force leadership at the scene supports Sheehan's reconstruction of the assassination as an S-Force operation rather than a spontaneous or CIA-institutional action.
