# Eric Hecker

Eric Hecker is a former [[raytheon]] contractor who claims he worked at the South Pole as part of a neutrino detection or emission project, and that this technology has weaponized applications beyond its stated scientific mission. He presented his claims publicly (the American Alchemy episode references him as a known community figure).

## Claims

### South Pole Neutrino Device

Hecker claims he was contracted by Raytheon to install or maintain a neutrino emitter/detector at the South Pole. The stated purpose was scientific — consistent with the IceCube Neutrino Observatory at the Amundsen–Scott South Pole Station. However, he alleges that water-cooler discussions among contractors revealed the device had dual use as a weapon or control system:

> "A lot of stuff was like overheard like water cooler talk."

He was physically present at the site and shared photographs as corroborating evidence of his presence.

### Christchurch Earthquake Connection

Hecker alleges the 2010 Christchurch earthquake (or the 2011 aftershock sequence) may have been caused or triggered by neutrino experiments at the South Pole. The mechanism is not elaborated. [[jesse-michaels]] treats this specific claim with appropriate skepticism:

> "I don't know if it's true, but I love that."

The Christchurch earthquake (2010: M7.1; 2011: M6.3 killing 185 people) was one of the most destructive natural disasters in New Zealand's modern history. No independent corroboration of a neutrino-trigger mechanism has emerged.

## Credibility Assessment

Hecker's credibility profile is mixed:
- **Positive**: Demonstrated physical presence at the South Pole (photographs); Raytheon employment is plausible and verifiable
- **Negative**: Core claims rest on "water cooler talk" (hearsay); earthquake-trigger mechanism lacks scientific basis; no documented whistleblower filings or congressional testimony

Jesse engages with his claims warmly but does not treat the earthquake allegation as established fact. Hecker enters the KB as a **whistleblower-adjacent figure** — someone with plausible proximity to classified defense technology but making claims that currently exceed his documented access.
