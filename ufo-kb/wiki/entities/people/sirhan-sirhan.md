# Sirhan Sirhan

Sirhan Sirhan (born 1944) was convicted of assassinating Senator Robert F. Kennedy at the Ambassador Hotel in Los Angeles on June 5, 1968. He is currently serving a life sentence. To this day he **claims no memory of firing his weapon**, a fact consistent with [[dissociative-identity-disorder|dissociative identity disorder (DID)]] or [[manchurian-candidate|Manchurian Candidate programming]].

## The Official Account vs. Physical Evidence

The official account: a 24-year-old Palestinian man shot Kennedy with a .22 caliber revolver, hitting him three times and wounding five others.

What the physical and forensic record shows (per [[colin-ross]] and other sources):
- The Los Angeles County Coroner measured bullet entry holes and found the total count **exceeded the capacity of Sirhan's single gun**
- RFK was hit from **close range from behind**; Sirhan was **4–6 feet in front** of Kennedy when firing
- A Lockheed Skunk Works bodyguard named **Eugene Thane Caesar** was standing directly behind Kennedy when he fell; Kennedy grabbed Caesar as he went down
- [[rfk-jr|Robert F. Kennedy Jr.]] has publicly stated: "I don't believe that Sirhan's bullets ever hit my father"

## MK Ultra / Programming Indicators

[[colin-ross]] assesses Sirhan's case:
- **Trauma history**: As a child in the Middle East, Sirhan's apartment was bombed; he witnessed dismembered bodies. This creates a dissociative foundation — the same kind of pre-existing trauma that MK Ultra experimenters sought in subjects.
- **MPD/DID**: A biography of Sirhan described him as having a fully developed system of alternate personalities — "Robert" as the head, multiple others. By clinical criteria, he had DID.
- **Amnesia**: No memory of the shooting, claimed consistently since the night of the crime.
- **Diary programming**: His diary contains repeated entries like "must kill RFK" — potentially indistinguishable from psychotic obsession or implanted programming.
- ***Catcher in the Rye* connection**: Before the shooting, Sirhan went to a bookstore and obtained *Catcher in the Rye* — the same book found with multiple other alleged triggered assassins (Mark David Chapman, etc.).

> ⚠ Speculation flag: Whether Sirhan was an active MK Ultra subject or an independent mentally ill individual cannot be determined from available evidence. The indicators (amnesia, DID, trauma foundation, Catcher in the Rye) are consistent with programming but not proof of it.
