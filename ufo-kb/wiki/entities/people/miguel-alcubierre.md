# Miguel Alcubierre

Mexican theoretical physicist best known for the **Alcubierre drive** (1994) — a solution to Einstein's field equations that allows, in principle, faster-than-light travel without locally violating special relativity. The mechanism contracts spacetime ahead of a vessel and expands it behind, creating a "warp bubble" in which the craft is effectively stationary relative to local space while the space itself moves.

## The Alcubierre Metric and UAP

[[jesse-michaels]] and [[ryan-graves]] discuss the Alcubierre metric as a theoretical framework consistent with the inertial anomalies observed in UAP — specifically the instantaneous acceleration and deceleration that would destroy any conventional aircraft. The mechanism requires **negative energy** (or exotic matter with negative mass density) to maintain the warp bubble. Crucially, the **Casimir effect** — a well-documented quantum phenomenon — produces a measurable negative-energy gradient between closely-spaced conducting plates, demonstrating that negative energy is physically real even if not yet harnessable at scale.

[[carl-nell]] has cited this theoretical model in public appearances as deserving serious scientific attention rather than reflexive dismissal.

**NASA** has studied the Alcubierre metric through researcher Harold "Sonny" White at the Johnson Space Center.
