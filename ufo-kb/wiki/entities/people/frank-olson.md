# Frank Olson

Frank Olson worked in the US Army's chemical warfare division at [[fort-detrick]] on [[mk-naomi]] — the biological warfare offshoot of [[mk-ultra]]. In 1953 he was beginning to have a crisis of conscience and appeared to be moving toward becoming a whistleblower.

## The Murder (documented)

[[sidney-gottlieb]] invited Olson to a party somewhere outside Washington, DC. Olson was covertly dosed with LSD and Quaaludes. He had what was described as "a bad trip," which became the official cover story: that the LSD trip had destabilized him mentally, leading him to jump from the 10th floor of the Statler Hotel in New York.

**The cover story unraveled when:**
- Eric Olson, Frank's son, physically examined the hotel window and found it was **too small** for a person to have jumped through — Olson must have been pushed
- [[harold-abramson]] (an MK Ultra-connected doctor) was involved in the story in ways that suggested coordinated management rather than independent psychiatric response
- [[pierre-lafitte]], a man with approximately 20 documented aliases, is the individual [[colin-ross]] believes pushed Olson from the window

**The Lafitte-Oswald Connection:**  
One of Lafitte's documented aliases was "**Hydell**" — the same alias used by [[lee-harvey-oswald]] (who used "Alex Hydell"). Both Lafitte and Oswald worked at the **Reilly Coffee Company** in New Orleans during the same period (1960–63). This overlap places Lafitte squarely inside the network that later converged on the Kennedy assassination.

## The Compensation

Frank Olson's family found a single paragraph in the **Rockefeller Commission Report** (1973) that described what had happened to their father — including the LSD dosing. This triggered their legal action. A photograph exists of the family in the Oval Office with the president handing them a **$700,000 compensation check**. This constitutes government acknowledgment of the murder.

## Significance

The Olson case is one of the most fully documented state murders in the CIA's domestic history: documented dosing, documented cover story, documented family compensation, and a documented connecting figure (Lafitte) to the Kennedy assassination network.
