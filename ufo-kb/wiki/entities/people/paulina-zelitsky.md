# Paulina Zelitsky

Canadian oceanographic archaeologist. In the early 2010s, she published sonar mappings of what she interpreted as **vast underwater ruins** off the western tip of Cuba — structures she believed might represent an ancient city, potentially connected to Atlantean or pre-Mayan civilization.

## Findings and Suppression

Zelitsky's sonar data was initially picked up by multiple news outlets, and National Geographic reportedly indicated the site needed investigation. She then traveled to Mexico to investigate a hypothesized connection to Yucatan ruins — and was **arrested by Mexican authorities**, severely intimidated, and sent back to Canada. She subsequently dropped all further investigation. The story disappeared from media.

This visit is cited as evidence of Ghislaine's active pursuit of Atlantean archaeological sites, connecting the Epstein network to genuine archaeological claims being suppressed.
