# John Anthony West

## Background

Alternative Egyptologist who collaborated with geologist [[robert-schoch]] to advance the argument that the [[great-sphinx]] shows water erosion from heavy rainfall — impossible in Egypt's current desert climate — implying construction during the African Humid Period well before the 4th dynasty. His work directly challenged the Egyptological consensus and inspired a generation of alternative researchers including [[luke-caverns]].

## Notable Claims

The erosion patterns on the Sphinx's body (vertical runnels from rainwater) could only have formed during a significantly wetter climate. Combined with [[robert-schoch]]'s geological dating, this places initial construction before ~7000 BC or earlier.

## Death and Legacy

West died in 2018. [[graham-hancock]] in [[9PJJiut0Iuw]] explicitly credits West as the *catalyst*: "it was John who first brought Robert Schoch to the Giza Plateau." This sequence — West identifying the problem, Schoch providing the geological methodology — is the core of the Sphinx water-erosion argument.
