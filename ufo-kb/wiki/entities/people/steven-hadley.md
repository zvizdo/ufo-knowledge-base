# Steven Hadley

National Security Advisor (NSA) to President [[george-w-bush]] (2005–2009). Appears in the UAP knowledge base through two separate accounts:

## UAP Clearing House Role (Davis Account)

[[eric-davis]] describes the NSA as the institutional clearing house for UAP presidential matters. If a government agency experienced a significant UAP event requiring a presidential decision, the NSA was the first call:

> "The National Security advisor is going to have to say okay here's a number at your agency who you're going to call to get a full briefing on the whole UFO thing and meanwhile I will schedule a meeting in the Oval Office with the president."

In the AAWSAP SAP elevation context, Davis argues [[harry-reid]] should have contacted the president or NSA (Hadley) directly, rather than writing to [[william-lynn]] (Deputy Secretary of Defense). A call from Hadley to the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security would have been the effective path to SAP elevation.

## Three-Body Problem NSC Exercise (Puthoff Account)

[[hal-puthoff]] described on Joe Rogan that Hadley's NSC read Liu Cixin's *The Three-Body Problem* as part of a UAP disclosure exercise. The conclusion was that disclosure was not worth it. [[ralph-moat-larson]], who knew Hadley well, was unaware of this exercise.
