# Sam Altman

CEO of OpenAI. Altman attended the [[sol-foundation]]'s inaugural symposium at Stanford in November 2023, where [[david-grusch]] spoke with him about the UAP disclosure question and AGI. Grusch describes Altman as "a little coy" — neither confirming nor denying that OpenAI had crossed an AGI capability threshold.

Grusch raises the speculative hypothesis (explicitly framed as his own inference, not sourced testimony) that Altman's dramatic board removal and reinstatement in November 2023 may have been triggered by an "AGI-cracked" scenario — the board may have invoked OpenAI's charter's safety clause upon believing AGI had been achieved. Grusch uses this as an analogy to the NHI disclosure problem: both AGI emergence and NHI reality, if acknowledged, would restructure power relationships in ways established institutions resist.

## OpenAI / Sol Foundation Context

Sam Altman (b. 1985) is CEO of OpenAI (since 2019) and previously was president of Y Combinator (2014-2019). His direct UAP-research involvement is limited but structurally interesting: Altman has appeared at the Sol Foundation symposium (a Stanford-affiliated UAP-research organization) and is reportedly a financial supporter of UAP-research efforts including Garry Nolan's biological-investigation work at Stanford.

Altman's UAP-adjacent posture is exploratory rather than advocacy: he has not made strong public claims about UAP existence or origins, but his attendance at Sol Foundation events and engagement with the Stanford UAP-research network signals that the topic is taken seriously within the AI / Silicon Valley elite circle. Altman's broader interest in "frontier questions" (AGI, life extension, fusion, UAP) is a recurring profile theme in 2022-2025 media coverage.
