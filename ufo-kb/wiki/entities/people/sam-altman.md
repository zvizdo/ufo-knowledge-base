# Sam Altman

CEO of OpenAI. Altman attended the [[sol-foundation]]'s inaugural symposium at Stanford in November 2023, where [[david-grusch]] spoke with him about the UAP disclosure question and AGI. Grusch describes Altman as "a little coy" — neither confirming nor denying that OpenAI had crossed an AGI capability threshold.

Grusch raises the speculative hypothesis (explicitly framed as his own inference, not sourced testimony) that Altman's dramatic board removal and reinstatement in November 2023 may have been triggered by an "AGI-cracked" scenario — the board may have invoked OpenAI's charter's safety clause upon believing AGI had been achieved. Grusch uses this as an analogy to the NHI disclosure problem: both AGI emergence and NHI reality, if acknowledged, would restructure power relationships in ways established institutions resist.
