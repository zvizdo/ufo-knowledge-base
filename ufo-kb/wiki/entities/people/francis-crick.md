# Francis Crick

British molecular biologist (1916–2004). Co-discoverer (with James Watson and Rosalind Franklin's crystallography data) of DNA's double helix structure in 1953; Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine (1962). Later pioneered computational neuroscience and proposed the "neural correlate of consciousness."

In UAP/consciousness discourse, Crick is cited for:

1. **Directed panspermia**: Proposed (with Leslie Orgel, 1973) that life on Earth was deliberately seeded by an extraterrestrial civilization — an early scientific version of the NHI-origin-of-life hypothesis.
2. **LSD and DNA**: Reports (including [[graham-hancock]]'s citation) that Crick used LSD during the period when he achieved the visual insight that led to the double helix model — framing altered states as a legitimate scientific tool.
