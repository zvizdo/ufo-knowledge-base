# Charles Lieber

**Charles Lieber** was the Chair of Harvard University's Department of Chemistry, a pioneer in nanotechnology, and one of the world's leading chemists. In December 2021, he was convicted of six federal felonies for lying to federal investigators and concealing his participation in China's [[thousand-talents-program]].

## Thousand Talents Participation

The [[wuhan-institute-of-technology]] paid Lieber approximately $50,000/month, more than $150,000 in living expenses, and over $1.5M to establish a nanotechnology research laboratory in China. These payments were made secretly while Lieber held his Harvard position and federal research grants, which required disclosure of foreign payments.

Jesse Michaels in [[ePdH01pphbk]] notes the geographical coincidence — that the Chinese institution paying Lieber for years before COVID was the Wuhan Institute of Technology, located in the same city as the Wuhan Institute of Virology. He raises this as an open question without asserting a connection.
