# Mark Felt

FBI Associate Director (effectively Deputy Director) and the anonymous "Deep Throat" source for Bob Woodward and Carl Bernstein's Watergate reporting at the Washington Post. [[danny-sheehan]]'s account adds a layer to Felt's motivation that goes beyond institutional loyalty or personal grievance.

## Sheehan's Account

Sheehan's reconstruction:
- After J. Edgar Hoover died, Felt expected to be named FBI Director. Nixon passed him over (appointing L. Patrick Gray instead).
- Felt's FBI investigation of Watergate was focusing on the **Mexico City bank account** — the bills in [[bernard-barker]]'s pocket — which led toward [[carlos-marcello]] → Miami National Bank → the S Force funding chain → Nixon as architect of JFK's assassination.
- Nixon wanted to shut down this Mexico City investigation (the "smoking gun" tape records Nixon telling Haldeman to have CIA Director Richard Helms and Deputy Vernon Walters call Acting FBI Director Gray to block it).
- Felt refused to be blocked. His leaking to Woodward and Bernstein was therefore not merely about Watergate political crimes — it was about preventing the suppression of an investigation whose terminus was the JFK assassination.

## Epistemic Status

Sheehan's account is an inference from the known documentary record (the "smoking gun" tape) combined with his broader S Force theory. Felt confirmed he was Deep Throat in 2005. Whether his motivation included the JFK angle is Sheehan's interpretation, not documented fact.
