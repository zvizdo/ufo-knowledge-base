# Leo Sprinkle

Psychologist and hypnotherapist affiliated with [[apro]] (Aerial Phenomena Research Organization) who worked with abductee witnesses in the early 1980s. One of the few researchers of the period who had experience conducting hypnotic regression sessions with experiencers, alongside James Harder, before Bud Hopkins's foundational abduction literature was published.

## Role in the Benowitz Affair

Leo Sprinkle was brought in by [[paul-benowitz]] to work with abductee [[myrna-hansen]], whose case Benowitz was investigating. Sprinkle came to Benowitz's home on at least two occasions to conduct regression sessions. On the second visit, Benowitz was already exhibiting clear signs of paranoia — he greeted Sprinkle while armed with a rifle and pistol and insisted "they're coming — you have to come in the house right now." This was, according to [[greg-bishop]], before Benowitz had even formally contacted the Air Force — suggesting the paranoia predated and was independent of (though later amplified by) the AFOSI disinformation campaign. (Greg Bishop, firsthand research, via [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])

## Significance

Sprinkle's firsthand observation of Benowitz's pre-AFOSI paranoia is one of the pieces of evidence that Bishop uses to support his assessment that Benowitz "was already kind of unstable to begin with." This is contested by those who prefer a narrative in which Benowitz was a fully stable researcher before government interference.
