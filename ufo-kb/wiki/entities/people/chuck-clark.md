# Chuck Clark

Self-described military watchdog based in the Rachel, Nevada area (near Area 51/S4). Clark wrote a handbook on the Area 51/S4 facility and worked as an amateur astronomer, mapping specific vantage points in the surrounding mountain ranges from which the base could be observed. He would take people out to these vantage points.

## FBI Property Seizure

Clark had his property — including computers and personal files — seized by FBI agents operating under a sealed search warrant. [[george-knapp]] covered this story for a local news station. The nature of what was confiscated is unclear; Fox speculates it may have included photographs of planes and hangars, or possibly the VHS footage described below.

## The 1995 VHS Footage

Clark called [[james-fox]] during production of Fox's first documentary (early-to-mid 1990s) saying: "When you see it your jaw's going to hit the floor." Fox drove ~12 hours to Rachel, Nevada. Clark played a VHS tape:
- Two young men on a desert road trip, filming themselves, listening to music
- They become alarmed at an object approaching or over them
- They argue in the car; one decides to get out against protests
- The dashboard lights up; shadows move from an external light source
- The man exits the car; an **orange disc-shaped craft appears, hovering and wobbling "like magic"**

Fox describes it as the best UFO footage he has ever seen (in its original state). The tape was approximately one week old when he viewed it. The footage was provided to Clark by a TV producer in Los Angeles, who reportedly had a master on Beta SP tape.

### Provenance Degradation

After 26 years of repeated VHS playback, the tape degraded severely. Clark repeatedly rebuffed Fox's requests to help obtain it ("ask me again and I'll never speak to you"). When Fox offered to find a buyer offering $30K, Clark cut contact entirely.

[[logan-paul]] subsequently visited Clark in Arizona with approximately $50,000–$100,000 in cash; Clark refused to sell. Logan Paul recorded the tape using button cameras but Clark threatened to sue if released. The footage Clark showed Paul was described as "seriously eroded" and "pretty degraded."

A separate avenue: the original Beta SP master from the Los Angeles TV producer may still exist — but the producer died, and his son was not cooperative. A woman at "the Little Alien" diner in Rachel told Fox: "You think we're that stupid? You don't think we made a digital copy?" — implying local awareness that the footage has been preserved somewhere.

## Relationship with James Fox

Fox collaborated with Clark during his first documentary. Over roughly 15–20 years Fox repeatedly asked for access to the footage; the final ask (when a $30K buyer emerged) ended the relationship permanently. Clark is described as a man of his word who will not speak to Fox again. Fox also asked Clark to introduce him to the original source of the tape; Clark refused.
