# Tim Coleman

Former BBC correspondent described by [[james-fox]] as "walking encyclopedia" and "super smart guy" who co-developed Fox's documentary "Out of the Blue" in the late 1990s.

## Varginha Seed

In the late 1990s, while Fox and Coleman were mapping out "Out of the Blue," Coleman urged Fox to investigate the [[1996-varginha]] case in Brazil. Fox's response was dismissive: "live aliens walking around the town of a UFO crash — please." Fox refused to investigate Varginha for approximately 10 years.

When Fox eventually made [[moment-of-contact]] after five visits to Brazil over 12 years, he called Coleman to apologize. Coleman's response: "Bloody hell mate, you wouldn't even look into it back in the day." Fox gave Coleman a special thanks credit in the film despite his having no production involvement.
