## Background

Jeffrey Kruchshank is an Australian intelligence analyst who independently investigated Operation Dominic and the Bluegill Triple Prime nuclear test event of 1962. His work represents the civilian and allied-intelligence analyst community that has been independently reconstructing the Bluegill Triple Prime UAP event from declassified documents, ship logs, and government records — often reaching further than official US channels.

## Notable Claims

- In 2023, Kruchshank submitted a mandatory declassification review (MDR) request to the US Department of Energy specifically targeting Operation Dominic and Bluegill Triple Prime film footage. An MDR request obligates the agency to search for and assess the material.
- The Department of Energy responded that the Kettle 2 film was "unable to locate." For an agency that manages nuclear test documentation — records considered among the most carefully preserved in the US government — this response is remarkable. It implies either that the footage was destroyed, transferred out of normal custody chains, or is being withheld under a classification level that removes it from standard inventory responses.
- Kruchshank's work corroborates Harald Malmgren's firsthand account of the Bluegill Triple Prime UAP event and the recovery of [[bluegill-uap-debris]]: if the footage were merely routine, there would be no reason for it to be unlocatable.
