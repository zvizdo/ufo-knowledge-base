# Steve Coburn

Materials scientist and analytical chemist who became [[roger-leir]]'s closest scientific collaborator on alien implant research. Coburn is unusual in the field: he combines a scientific background in analytical instrumentation with personal firsthand experience as a class-2 experiencer who has had an implant surgically removed and analyzed.

## Background

Coburn was working in Camarillo, California ("Camo") when he discovered [[roger-leir]] was practicing a few miles away in Thousand Oaks. He spent approximately five years as a researcher at the **UCLA Neuropsychiatric Institute** (where [[jolly-west]], MK-Ultra's UCLA node, held authority over that branch). He later founded **Neutron Star Technologies** and a spin-off, **Neutron Star Nano Technologies**, focused on carbon nanotube applications — directly informed by his implant research.

His father was a dentist and Colonel in the Army Reserve who sighted a cylindrical UFO with a red light and reported it to military authorities; he made veiled disclosures to Coburn late in life about possible UFO-related experiences, though he "kept things close to his chest."

## Experiences

### First Experience (Age 5, Oxnard CA)
Woke before dawn; observed a bright yellow disc-shaped UFO hovering over his parents' house. Blacked out. Woke at 8 AM with blood down the front and a vague memory of something inserted into his nose — likely the initial placement of a brain implant. Parents attributed it to a nosebleed.

### Toe Implant (Fillmore CA, Adult)
Woke at approximately 3 AM with a stinging pain in his toe. He knew immediately he had been implanted. Two small gray beings had visited. He walked to Leir's office, was prescribed an X-ray, and saw what looked like "a bent piece of wire." [[jaime-maussan]] funded the surgical removal. Coburn then analyzed the object at his workplace.

**Under regressive hypnosis**, Coburn recovered memory of the full abduction experience: taken aboard a disc ~50 ft in diameter (similar to [[s4-sport-model]]), via a blue-green tractor beam. The craft's interior included an airlock with both human and alien space suits, a thought-controlled pilot station with hieroglyphic-style labeling (including what appeared to be a **blue vector symbol** with two circles below), and four quadrants off a central airlock.

A **taller gray** (the scientist-engineer type) extracted Coburn's couch from the wall, inserted the implant using a black plastic handle with stainless steel tubing and fiber optics — UV light activates the device. Coburn communicated telepathically. Total time aboard: approximately one hour.

### Skull Implants
- Brain/frontal lobes (implanted age 5, he believes)
- Two small implants above each ear — "barely visible" on X-ray
- These began emitting **radio signals** during filming of a Japanese TV program

## Implant Analysis

See [[nhi-implants]] for full structural detail. In brief:

- **No immune/inflammatory response** — unheard of for foreign objects
- **Gray membrane** — near-impossible to cut; came back from the body without any tissue reaction
- **Bone-like intermediate layer**
- **Meteoric iron core with carbon nanotubes** embedded in the metal
- **Nerve cell connections** growing into the device (likely explains the progressive electrical-shock pain Coburn experienced in his toe)
- **Isotopic ratios**: boron and copper skewed by up to 30%; heavier isotopes over-represented — consistent with origin near galactic core (heavy neutron-capture supernovae) or with artificial isotope separation costing millions in centrifuge time

## Implant Detection Protocol

Coburn uses and trains others in [[roger-leir]]'s detection protocol:
1. **Stud finder** (small metal detector) — scan entire body; mark hits
2. **G-meter** (sensitive magnetometer) — scan stud-finder hit areas
3. Double hit (stud finder + G-meter) at same site = **almost certainly an implant**

Conducted ~400 scans; ~50% produced hits in this pre-selected experiencer audience.

## Critical Assessment

Coburn is a self-reporting experiencer claiming scientific credentials; his analyses are never independently verified with chain-of-custody conditions. The isotopic anomalies, if genuine, are potentially significant — but no peer-reviewed lab has confirmed his findings. The attempted JSE publication and its unusual reversal is worth tracking but unverifiable from public records. He represents a unique combination: firsthand claimant with relevant analytical expertise, but with multiple personal motivations that complicate standard credibility assessment.
