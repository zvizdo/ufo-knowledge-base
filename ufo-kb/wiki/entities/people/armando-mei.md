# Armando Mei

Italian researcher who collaborates with [[filippo-biondi]] and [[corrado-malanga]] on the [[beyond-protocol]] Giza substructure scanning project. He also pursues an independent theoretical line: connecting the dimensionless **fine structure constant (α ≈ 1/137)** to the Khafre pyramid.

## The 137 / Fine Structure Constant Claim

Mei argues that the number 137 — the approximate inverse of the fine structure constant in quantum physics — is encoded in the proportions of the [[khafre-pyramid]]. This claim situates the pyramid within a tradition of mathematical/cosmological encoding that includes:

- **Feynman's famous injunction**: Richard Feynman reportedly urged physicists to put a plaque bearing the number 137 on their lab walls and meditate on it daily — he considered it the deepest mystery in physics.
- **Leon Lederman (Nobel 1988)**: reported to have lived in a house numbered 137.
- **Quantum significance**: 137 is the ratio between the speed of light and that of the electron orbiting the nucleus of the hydrogen atom (α⁻¹ ≈ 137.036). It governs the strength of electromagnetic coupling — why atoms hold together and why light and matter interact.

Biondi in [[zrmG2jBckgk]] describes Mei as "a very nice person and scientist also" and encourages his work, though notes he has not yet fully studied Mei's books.
