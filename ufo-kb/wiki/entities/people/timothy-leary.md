# Timothy Leary

Harvard psychology professor and psychedelic researcher. Fired from Harvard 1963 for giving psilocybin to students. Became the face of 1960s psychedelic counterculture. His LSD research overlaps with MK-Ultra era CIA interest in psychedelics.

## Background

Known connection to Mary Meyer (JFK mistress) — she sought LSD from Leary and allegedly turned JFK on to psychedelics. Leary later claimed JFK was "turning on" and "tuning in" to alternative consciousness before assassination.

## Notable Claims

- Mary Meyer connection: Leary provided her LSD; she was working on a peace-oriented project with JFK.
- Claimed CIA infiltrated and co-opted the psychedelic movement.
