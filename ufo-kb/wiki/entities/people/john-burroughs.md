# John Burroughs

USAF Staff Sergeant stationed at RAF Bentwaters, UK, who was one of the primary witnesses to the [[1980-rendlesham-forest]] landing incident in late December 1980. Burroughs approached the craft on foot alongside [[jim-penniston]] and experienced the encounter at close range.

Cited by [[jake-barber]] in [[dnnpyNuPdXs]] as a historical precedent for UAP activity near military installations — alongside Penniston, framed as proof that the pattern has decades of documented military precedent.
