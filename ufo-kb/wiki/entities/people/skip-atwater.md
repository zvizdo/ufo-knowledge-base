# Skip Atwater

Frederick Holmes "Skip" Atwater was the operations and training officer for the US Army INSCOM remote viewing program ([[stargate-program]]) at [[fort-meade]], Maryland. He is not a remote viewer himself but the officer who designed challenge targets, managed session protocols, handled double-blind procedures, and delivered intelligence products to Pentagon clients. After leaving the Army, he became president of the [[monroe-institute]]. He is the most operationally detailed insider account of how the Stargate program functioned day-to-day.

## Background

Atwater was raised in Southern California (Glendale area) with early paranormal experiences. His mother had her own perceptual abilities; she responded to his childhood OBEs and UFO sightings with complete equanimity — "sometimes you're in your body and sometimes you're not." As a child, he saw five silver objects in the sky; his mother said "they call those UFOs, don't worry about them, they're fine."

He attended college (with Spanish fluency), was drafted 1A, and chose Military Intelligence based on a blue recruiting brochure. After a first assignment in Alabama (Vietnam ended six months later), he was assigned to teaching counterintelligence at Fort Huachuca, Arizona.

## Entry into Remote Viewing

At Fort Huachuca, Atwater and colleague Rob Coward found [[hal-puthoff]] and [[russell-targ]]'s book *Mind Reach* and immediately identified a counterintelligence gap: nobody was checking if adversaries were remote viewing US Army units. He brought the threat to his colonel; the colonel arranged a Pentagon assignment. That assignment was rerouted to Fort Meade by an INSCOM assignments officer — a redirection Atwater attributes to "the Oneness" guiding his path.

His mother's parting advice when he moved east: "You should look up Bob Monroe" — [[robert-monroe]], who Atwater didn't recognize at the time but later succeeded as president of the [[monroe-institute]].

At Fort Meade, after passing clearances, Atwater found three classified documents in a safe referencing **Project SCAN** and Soviet remote viewing programs — confirming the DoD had been tracking the phenomenon before the Army's formal program.

## Operational Role in Stargate

Atwater's role was operations and training officer — not viewer. He:
- Designed challenge targets and ran double-blind protocols (he himself often didn't know the target)
- Managed the trust relationship with [[joe-mcmoneagle]]: "I didn't fool around with him. He knew that I knew the questions that the Pentagon needed to know."
- Delivered session outputs to Colonel Scotty Watt, who would show results to Pentagon clients and signal whether to "schedule a second session"
- Ran "challenge targets" as training when operational tasking was low — an analogy to physical training for soldiers

He recruited approximately 200 Army personnel and civilians in the DC area using newspaper clippings about near-death experiences and psychic claims as a screening tool. [[hal-puthoff]] and [[russell-targ]] came to Fort Meade, evaluated six candidates, and selected all six at the same price as three.

He describes his relationship with [[joe-mcmoneagle]] with genuine affection: "I love Joe. I mean I really love that he made the day."

## Project 8200 — UFO Base Validation

Approximately 10 years after [[pat-price]]'s 1973 remote viewing sessions identifying four underground UFO bases in mountains, [[hal-puthoff]] passed Price's transcripts to Atwater after the issue had "quieted down." Atwater created **Project 8200** as a self-initiated training exercise: giving the four mountain coordinates ([[sugar-grove-naval-station]] connection aside, the mountains were Mount Hayes, Alaska; Mount Inyangani, Zimbabwe; Mount Perdido, Spain-France border; Mount Zeil, Australia) as blind challenge targets to six Stargate viewers.

Results from [[joe-mcmoneagle]] and Bill Ray's sessions on Mount Hayes (read verbatim by Atwater) described hollow mountains, silver metallic craft, thin unemotional beings with "programmed feelings," and concealment via programmed telepathy — matching Price's original descriptions from a decade earlier. See [[stargate-program]] for full Bill Ray session transcript excerpt.

## Firsthand NHI Encounter — Lawrence Livermore

While working at Fort Meade, Atwater had an experience (describing it as an OBE, declining to call it a dream) of standing on a grassy knoll area near [[lawrence-livermore-national-lab]] surrounded by ordinary people in a mesmerized state. Alien beings were moving among the people, interviewing them. Atwater announced "I teach people how to get out of their body," drawing alien attention. A small alien injected him in the leg with a device containing brownish fluid; he floated upward.

When he reported this to [[hal-puthoff]], Puthoff immediately asked: "Was it at the Lawrence Livermore Laboratory?" — confirming the site had a history of reported incidents.

Critically: **[[joe-mcmoneagle]] was among the mesmerized people in the experience.** The next morning, Atwater asked McMoneagle if anything happened. McMoneagle replied: "No, uh-uh — just all those aliens that you and I were with." They have never spoken of it again. Atwater: "I don't know the answer to that."

## Alien Spacecraft OBE — Alpha Centauri and the Rubik's Cube

In a separate OBE, Atwater found himself standing behind an alien on a small spacecraft. The alien said: "You shouldn't be here." Atwater asked how they travel to Alpha Centauri. The alien explained via a **Rubik's Cube analogy**: imagine a cube where each tile contains a periodic table. To travel, "you just twist" — relocating to where a different elemental configuration exists, rather than moving through space at velocity. "You have to stop thinking about going that way for a long time very fast — it doesn't work."

Three years later, Atwater arrived at an independent realization: "You don't have to twist the cube — you just turn it to the other surface. It's a different world." The alien responded telepathically: "I've been waiting for you to discover that." This connects to [[consciousness-non-local]] and the quantum non-locality framework Atwater uses to explain [[remote-viewing]].

## Pat Price Investigation

Atwater placed [[pat-price]]'s photograph in a sealed envelope and gave it to [[joe-mcmoneagle]]. McMoneagle's first response: "This guy does something like what I do." Location: "He's in an underground office in Virginia somewhere." Atwater stopped the session and reported to Major/Colonel Watt, then deliberately went no further: "I just kept my mouth shut." This remains the most significant unresolved thread in the Pat Price mystery — if McMoneagle's assessment was accurate, Price may have been taken and continued in a classified underground location.

## Monroe Institute

After his military career, Atwater became president of the [[monroe-institute]] — the consciousness research organization founded by [[robert-monroe]] (whom his mother had pointed him toward years before his Fort Meade assignment). He authored books on guided spiritual development. He connects [[gateway-process]] and Monroe's Hemi-Sync methodology to his own OBE training.

## Stargate Dismantlement

Stargate was publicly dismantled after two evaluators gave contradictory assessments. Atwater says it was "time for it to be gone," but volunteers: "They already had five other programs." He then deflects, noting he has been out of government for over 26 years. Connects to [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] — public program closure masking classified continuation.
