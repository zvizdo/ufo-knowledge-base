# Maxwell W. Hunter II

Aerospace engineer; Member of the Professional Staff, National Aeronautics and Space Council (NASC), Executive Office of the President (1963). Later worked at Douglas Aircraft and became a noted advocate for SSTO (Single Stage to Orbit) launch vehicles.

**1963 alien contact policy memo**: In July 1963, during deliberations on BNSP Task I, Hunter wrote "Thoughts on the Space Alien Race Question" to Robert F. Packard at the DoS Office of International Scientific Affairs. The memo systematically analyzed three alien contact scenarios (chemical Martians, nuclear civilization, FTL civilization) and their policy implications — concluding that no meaningful preparation was possible and that policy response would be determined by "grand panic."
