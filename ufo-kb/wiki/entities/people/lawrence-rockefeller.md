# Lawrence Rockefeller

Philanthropist of the Rockefeller family who, in the 1990s, mounted a serious private effort to advance UFO/UAP disclosure through philanthropic funding and political outreach. Described by [[whitley-strieber]] as "a good man with a good heart who was very curious and somehow knew this was real."

## Disclosure Funding (1990s)

Rockefeller funded a cluster of disclosure-adjacent research initiatives simultaneously:

- **[[john-mack]]** — Harvard psychiatry chair's alien abduction research program ([[peer-institute]])
- **[[steven-greer]]** — UFO researcher; Rockefeller-funded event in Mexico during which Greer reportedly called in a UFO (Strieber's account; Greer was accompanied by Bootsie Galbraith, a Rockefeller associate)
- **[[princeton-parapsychology-lab]]** — Mind-over-matter / psionic effects research; funded by Rockefeller

## Clinton Briefing Attempt

In the 1990s, Rockefeller attempted to interest [[hillary-clinton]] in UFO disclosure by gifting her Paul Davies's book *Are We Alone*. The attempt is notable as an example of a private citizen with serious resources attempting to leverage political access for disclosure purposes — and apparently failing to move the needle.

## JY Ranch — September 1993 Meeting with Greer

In [[areO7Mej44E]], [[steven-greer]] gives a first-person account of visiting Rockefeller at the **[[jy-ranch]]** — the Rockefeller family's private ranch in the Grand Tetons — in **September 1993**. The Clintons vacationed here (Rockefeller donated the surrounding land to form part of Grand Teton National Park but retained the ranch).

At a late-night session on the deck, Rockefeller told Greer his brother [[david-rockefeller]] (Chairman of Chase Manhattan Bank) and nephew Senator [[jay-rockefeller]] (Chairman of the Senate Intelligence Committee) were "already jumping up and down on my nuts for even having this meeting with you." This family opposition constrained Lawrence's ability to provide meaningful support. Early funding Lawrence did provide to Greer's work was later intercepted by CIA non-official cover (NOC) operatives who had embedded themselves on Rockefeller's team.

Greer characterizes Lawrence as "the philosopher king of the family" and a genuinely motivated disclosure supporter who was systematically neutralized by both his own family and the intelligence community's penetration of his network.

## Medway Gathering

Rockefeller hosted a weekend gathering at his Medway Plantation property in South Carolina that included [[edgar-mitchell]] (Apollo 14 astronaut) and others. Strieber describes the environment as one where serious people with access "were coy about what they knew."
