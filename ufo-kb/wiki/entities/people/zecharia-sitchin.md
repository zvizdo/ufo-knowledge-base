# Zecharia Sitchin

Russian-American author (1920–2010). Wrote *The 12th Planet* (1976) and the subsequent Earth Chronicles series, arguing that Sumerian cuneiform texts, when re-translated, reveal a history of extraterrestrial visitors called the Anunnaki who arrived from a planet named Nibiru on a 3,600-year elliptical orbit, genetically engineered Homo sapiens as a slave species, and are the origin of ancient Mesopotamian religion.

Sitchin's translations are disputed by mainstream Assyriology scholars. [[michael-heiser]] specifically ran a website (sitchiniswrong.com) cataloguing Sitchin's translation errors, arguing the Sumerian texts do not support his interpretations — while simultaneously affirming a supernatural/historical reading of the Nephilim narrative through orthodox scholarship.

Researchers like [[matt-lroy]] and others in the ancient-mysteries field often distance themselves from Sitchin's Nibiru framing while building adjacent arguments about pre-Flood civilizations and planetary catastrophe.
