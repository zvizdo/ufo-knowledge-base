# Alex Lee Moyer

Documentary filmmaker; director of *Alex's War* (2022), a feature documentary following [[alex-jones]] over multiple years. Released July 1, 2022, the film traces Jones's evolution from the populist philosopher visible in his *[[waking-life]]* (2001) cameo — where he delivers a megaphone monologue on human liberty and resistance to corporatocracy — to the polarizing "tinfoil-hat conspiracy theorist" figure he became in the 2010s and 2020s.

**Thesis of *Alex's War***: [[jesse-michaels]] describes the film as documenting the process by which Jones was "co-opted" from a genuine populist-philosophy position into the manufactured conspiracy-broadcaster persona. Moyer's documentary uses Jones's own footage, interviews, and public record to trace this arc, presenting the *Waking Life* Jones as the authentic original and the post-Sandy-Hook Jones as a figure shaped by enemies, lawsuits, and social incentives rather than purely his own beliefs.

**Interview context**: The [[KhmUixSXxDo]] American Alchemy episode was recorded during or around the *Alex's War* release, with Moyer's film linked in the episode description. The episode itself covers Jones's Bilderberg infiltrations, his assessment of Klaus Schwab and the WEF, his views on JFK, and the philosophy articulated in *Waking Life* — topics that parallel and promote the documentary's framing.

**KB significance**: Moyer appears primarily as a context figure for the Alex Jones / populist-philosophy-vs-conspiracy thread. Her documentary is cited as evidence for the [[scapegoat-mechanism]] analysis of how authentic dissent gets reframed as conspiracy — a pattern relevant to the broader KB theme of how UAP disclosure advocates are treated.
