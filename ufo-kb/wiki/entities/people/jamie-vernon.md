# Jamie Vernon

## Background

Jamie Vernon, known universally as "Young Jamie," is the producer and technical operator of the [[joe-rogan]] Experience (JRE), one of the world's most-listened-to podcasts. Beyond managing production logistics, Jamie has served as an informal research collaborator for Rogan on UFO/UAP topics, and by his own account has followed the rabbit holes extensively.

He is a golfer and friend of [[jesse-michaels]], whom he met in the run-up to Jesse's JRE appearance. They connected partly through a shared interest in UAPs and partly through Viktor Hovland's Austin visit, leading to the golf outing at Barton Pitch and Putt documented in [[SZBI85yvV5A]].

## UAP Research

Jamie's entry into UFO research followed the classic path of many in the public: the Pentagon UAP video releases (Gimbal, Go Fast, [[2004-nimitz-tic-tac]]), followed by [[bob-lazar]]'s account on JRE, then a slow accumulation of corroborating whistleblowers and witnesses. His position inside JRE has given him direct access to many significant UAP figures who have appeared on the show.

He has followed several advanced rabbit holes and is knowledgeable about specific cases such as:
- The 1933 Magenta, Italy crash (discussed with Jesse as cross-corroboration via [[richard-bissell]] → [[harold-malmgren]])
- [[jake-barber]] and the egg-shaped UAP and Sky Watchers protocols
- The Columbus, Ohio node — [[battelle-memorial-institute]], [[wright-patterson]], and their proximity

His frustration: "You don't really have anything concrete and like hard evidence yet. Even though there seems to be so much out there coming from your videos and like the deep dives that you do, it's unbelievable."

## Credibility

Jamie operates as a media figure and research aggregator rather than a firsthand witness or official. His value to the discourse is as a well-connected curator who has absorbed large volumes of UAP literature and testimony, and whose platform (JRE) has shaped mainstream awareness of UAP topics. See [[credibility-frameworks]].
