# Bill Richardson

US Secretary of Energy (1998–2001) under President Clinton; Governor of New Mexico (2003–2011); former US Congressman and UN Ambassador. One of the few senior US officials to publicly advocate for UAP disclosure.

**UAP advocacy**: As Governor of New Mexico — the state containing Roswell and many key UAP-related sites — Richardson publicly stated his belief that the US government was withholding information about UAP/UFOs. He wrote the foreword to a book on the Roswell Incident calling for declassification. This made him an unusual figure: a senior official with DOE security clearance who publicly questioned the official position.

**Epstein-Los Alamos connection**: In the KB (via [[d9tdJ2SkBKQ]]), Richardson is cited in connection with Jeffrey Epstein's unusual access to Los Alamos National Laboratory during Richardson's tenure as DOE Secretary. [[jeffrey-epstein]] had connections to multiple prominent figures and institutions during this period; the nature and purpose of his Los Alamos visits is not fully documented but has been noted in the context of nuclear secrets and intelligence operations.

**DOE context**: As DOE Secretary, Richardson oversaw the national laboratories (Los Alamos, Sandia, Lawrence Livermore, Oak Ridge) — facilities at the core of claims about classified UAP-related research. His access to the full scope of DOE programs would have potentially included any classified biological or materials research programs under the DOE umbrella.
