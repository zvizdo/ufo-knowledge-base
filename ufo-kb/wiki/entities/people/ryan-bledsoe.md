# Ryan Bledsoe

Son of [[chris-bledsoe]]; firsthand multi-witness participant in the [[2007-bledsoe-cape-fear]] encounter on the Cape Fear River in North Carolina and in the ongoing family contact experiences in the years following. Ryan's presence at the original January 2007 incident alongside his father and other family members provides multi-witness corroboration that distinguishes the Bledsoe case from a single-witness contactee account.

**2007 encounter witness**: On the night of the original Cape Fear River encounter, Ryan was among the group present when the lights appeared and the subsequent experiences began. The fact that multiple witnesses (including Chris Bledsoe and his sons) reported anomalous experiences independently reduces the confabulation/fabrication hypothesis compared to a lone-witness account. Ryan's account, while corroborating his father's, is an independent data point in the case.

**Ongoing family contact**: The Bledsoe case is notable for being described as an ongoing multi-year contact relationship rather than a single isolated incident. Ryan has continued to be part of the family's reported experiences. The multi-generational character of the contact — affecting both father and son, across years, in multiple locations — is one of the case's more unusual features.

**Research interest**: [[diana-pasulka]], the Catholic Studies academic who has researched UAP and belief systems, has interviewed the Bledsoe family including Ryan. Pasulka's academic engagement with the family places the case in the tier of contact experiences that have attracted serious scholar-level investigation rather than only independent UAP researcher interest.
