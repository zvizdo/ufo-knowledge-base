# Bill Moore

Bill Moore was the dominant UFO researcher of the 1980s — co-author of *The Roswell Incident* (with [[stanton-friedman]]) and one of the first recipients of the MJ12 documents (sent through his friend [[jamie-shandera]]). His career ended controversially when he publicly admitted to having cooperated with US Air Force intelligence operatives, including [[richard-doty]], in the disinformation operation against researcher Paul Bennewitz.

## The MUFON 1989 Admission

At the 1989 MUFON Symposium, Moore acknowledged that he had served as an intermediary between the intelligence community and the UFO research world. He said he had "allowed myself to get involved with those people because I believed I had the ability to separate the [disinformation] from the candy" — meaning he thought he could extract real information while feeding false information to Bennewitz. [[greg-bishop]] documented this arc in *[[project-beta]]*.

The admission effectively ended Moore's standing in the UFO research community. [[richard-dolan]] observes that [[robert-hastings]] had been tracking Moore's intelligence connections for years through the old MUFON journals — "Hastings was on him" — and that by July 1989, "the gig was up" and Moore had no choice but to come out with it.

## MJ-12 Connection

Moore was the close friend of [[jamie-shandera]], who received the anonymously sent MJ12 documents in 1984 — the documents were sent from near [[kirtland-afb]]. This connection to [[richard-doty]] and the intelligence community surrounding Kirtland has made Moore a central figure in debates about whether the MJ12 documents were authentic leaks, disinformation, or a hybrid.

## Assessment

[[stanton-friedman]] was close to Moore and generally defended him. [[richard-dolan]] takes a more nuanced view: Moore's rationalization ("separating candy from poison") makes some psychological sense, but his cooperation with the Bennewitz operation materially harmed a sincere researcher. Dolan declines to "wag the finger" while acknowledging the damage done.

## Open Questions

- To what degree was Moore an active participant vs. a manipulated asset?
- Did the real information Moore received justify the cost of the Bennewitz operation?
