# Vladimir Putin

President of the Russian Federation. Former KGB officer, director of the FSB, Prime Minister. He and [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]] have known each other since 1993, when Ilyumzhinov signed the first inter-regional agreement between St. Petersburg and Kalmykia during Putin's tenure as vice-governor under Anatoli Sobchak. Ilyumzhinov describes Putin's memory as "phenomenal" and notes that Putin always takes handwritten notes during any conversation, regardless of topic.

## UAP Interaction

Putin invited Ilyumzhinov to a meeting specifically to discuss Kalmykia and the chess championship. After the formal presentation, Putin took off his jacket and asked Ilyumzhinov to "tell me exactly how everything transpired, how it all unfolded." The conversation was polite and measured. Putin asked:

1. **"Were you in your right mind then? You weren't drunk, were you? Everything was normal?"** — Ilyumzhinov confirmed he was completely sober and clear-headed.
2. **"Why did you decide to speak about it?"** — Ilyumzhinov explained he believed someone had to speak the truth publicly and he wasn't a careerist.

Putin responded: "Yes, yes." Then the meeting ended and Ilyumzhinov continued with other business. The interaction was brief, non-committal, and carefully neutral — neither dismissive nor endorsing.

**Analytical note:** Ilyumzhinov is adamant that "Putin, the president of the Russian Federation, is doing everything to stop the war" (in the Ukraine context) and frames Putin as chosen by divine/cosmic force rather than mere political will. This may color his characterization of Putin's response, but the debriefing details themselves (questions asked) are concrete and consistent with a leader managing sensitive information — not investigating it and not dismissing it.

Ilyumzhinov also says: "I know for a fact that this [UAP issue] is of utmost importance and many presidents and governments, including Vladimir Putin, are involved."
