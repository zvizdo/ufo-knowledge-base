# Tomoyuki Yamashita

Imperial Japanese Army general (1885–1946) who commanded Japanese forces in the Philippines in the final phase of WWII. Tried by US military tribunal for war crimes, convicted, and executed in February 1946.

In UFO/black-budget discourse, Yamashita is referenced not for his combat record but for the **looted Asian treasure** allegedly buried by Japanese forces under his nominal command across the Philippine archipelago — typically estimated at $100–200 billion in modern terms. The post-war recovery of this treasure by US intelligence is the seed claim of the [[yamashita-gold-black-budget-network]] thesis.

## UAP-Adjacent Relevance

Per [[danny-sheehan]] in [[C4rSj5Aum7w]], the recovered Yamashita gold was funneled into off-books trusts (notably the [[anderson-trust-gold]]) that became one of the alleged funding mechanisms for compartmented programs operating outside Congressional appropriations — including, per the thesis, UAP retrieval and reverse-engineering work. [[charles-willoughby]] (MacArthur's intelligence chief) is the named recovery operator.

This makes Yamashita a structural anchor for the [[yamashita-gold-black-budget-network]] synthesis even though he himself had no UAP connection — his name attaches to the financial substrate.
