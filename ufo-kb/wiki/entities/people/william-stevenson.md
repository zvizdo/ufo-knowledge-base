# William Stevenson

The head of British intelligence operations in the Western Hemisphere during World War II, codenamed **"Intrepid."** Stevenson is described as the inspiration for the James Bond character and one of the key architects of modern Western intelligence. He operated from the Rockefeller Plaza building in New York, which served as his base and communication hub.

## The Taom Mission and Brown's Germany Infiltration

In 1945, as Nazi Germany's defeat was imminent, Allied forces were racing to capture exotic technology and key scientists. Stevenson coordinated — with [[bill-donovan]] (OSS director) — the **Taom mission**, which parachuted [[thomas-townsend-brown]] behind enemy lines into East Germany from a very low altitude.

The mission targeted [[richard-maita]], a German high-voltage physicist whose work bore "uncanny resemblance" to Brown's own electrogravitics experiments. The mission went wrong: Brown was captured by the Nazis and made a prisoner of war before being rescued by Allied forces. Schatzkin surfaced a telegram sent to Brown's wife from the Rockefeller Plaza building, signed "Intrepid," reassuring her: "Your husband is recuperating fine in an English Hospital. We'll send him home soon."

## Nassua and the Caroline Group

Stevenson's Nassau operation is intertwined with the [[caroline-group]] — a private intelligence and finance consortium that operated from the yacht *Caroline*. Nassau served as a base of operations for Stevenson, and when the Caroline made port in Nassau, Stevenson came aboard for dinner meetings. The Caroline Group appears to have provided the private sector intelligence network that channeled Brown's work from civilian hands into classified aerospace programs.

## Co-Founding Intelligence Agencies

With Roosevelt's quiet consent and without J. Edgar Hoover's knowledge, Stevenson laid the groundwork for what became the CIA. He worked closely with [[allen-dulles]] and was deeply involved in the early formation of all the major Anglo-American intelligence apparatus.
