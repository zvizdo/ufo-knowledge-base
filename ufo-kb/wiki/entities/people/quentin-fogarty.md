# Quentin Fogarty

New Zealand political correspondent of significant later renown. Was aboard the cargo aircraft during the famous [[1978-kaikoura]] UFO incident, during which elliptical metallic disc-shaped objects approached to cockpit-window proximity. Later achieved political journalism prominence in Australia (Dennis Grant, a fellow journalist on the aircraft, also went on to renown).

## Serendipitous Connection

[[ross-coulthart]] discovered Fogarty's connection to the Kaikoura story by pure coincidence: Fogarty's son **Ben Fogarty** was a chief cameraman at *60 Minutes* (Australia) where Coulthart was a reporter. This gave Coulthart direct access to Quentin's firsthand account while writing *In Plain Sight*.

## Role at Kaikoura

Fogarty was one of two journalists on the aircraft alongside the flight crew and radar controller confirmation. His account confirmed what the pilot, co-pilot, and the Wellington radar operator John Cordi all saw simultaneously — real solid objects on radar matching exact visual positions described in real time.
