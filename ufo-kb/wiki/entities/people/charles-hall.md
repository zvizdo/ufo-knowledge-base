# Charles Hall

Charles Hall is a former USAF airman who claims the most sustained and detailed firsthand contact account in the contemporary UFO literature — roughly 26 months of regular, nightly interaction with an alien species he calls the [[tall-whites]] while stationed at Nellis AFB's Indian Springs Gunnery Ranges (Areas 53–54, "Dreamland"), Nevada, from March 1965 to May 1967.

## Background

- Born in Brooklyn, Wisconsin; parents from England
- Enlisted in the US Air Force in 1964 at age 19
- Trained as a weather observer; scored 499/500 on the USAF math ability test
- Holds a master's degree in nuclear physics
- Stationed at Nellis AFB March 1965 – May 1967, assigned to Indian Springs Gunnery Ranges as the duty weather observer
- 80 years old at time of interview (American Alchemy, ~2024)
- Author of the 6-volume *Millennial Hospitality* series and the physics book *Beyond Relativity*; subject of two documentaries by Emil Marzac

## Selection and Assignment

Hall maintains he was never formally briefed or debriefed about the Tall Whites — by design. A CIA psychiatrist with multiple PhDs in psychiatry evaluated him and determined he uniquely deployed 70 of 105 known psychological defense mechanisms within 15 minutes of a first meeting, making him psychologically "unbreakable." The debrief could never happen, per Hall, because any thought a debriefer had about Tall White children would be immediately read by Tall White electronics — potentially triggering lethal retaliation.

Forty-one men were sent before Hall; all came back "compromised." Some had nervous breakdowns, at least one received a medical discharge for radiation injuries, one deserted.

## Interactions with the Tall Whites

Hall describes a graduated, multi-month trust-building process: the Tall Whites stalked him, appeared suddenly to gauge his reactions, and vanished. He developed protocols — singing to announce his presence, backing away when intimidated, never running — that allowed trust to build. His primary contacts were:

- **The Teacher** — a female Tall White, approximately 230 years old in 1965 (born ~George Washington era); his primary interlocutor; could read his thoughts electronically
- **Range for Harry** — a permanent male guard; Hall's closest Tall White relationship ("like a brother")
- **The Butterfly** — the Teacher's young daughter
- **Pamela** — a younger female who initially failed, then passed, a trust test involving Hall

The Tall Whites came heavily armed at all times, even when visiting as friendly parties. Their suits emitted radioactive decay particles visible in darkness; getting too close caused radiation burns (Hall saw x-rays of a prior weather observer injured this way). They used clean Nellis weather station bathrooms on base, played on the airstrips on summer nights, and brought their children to observe interesting humans.

Hall rode inside a Tall White scout craft twice — gently lifted, traveled up Indian Springs Valley to the scout craft hangar entrance at Areas 53–54. On one trip, American generals and medical personnel were already present. The craft's cargo hold was described as a simple ellipsoidal space with no protrusions.

## Hall Photon Theory

Hall's original contribution to physics (copyrighted 1997, predating public internet discussions of classified physics). Core claims:

- Einstein's Special Relativity is **incomplete** — it assumes light has only electric and magnetic force fields; there are many additional force fields, some with far lower permittivity → speeds many orders of magnitude faster than light
- Gravity travels faster than light because it has no electromagnetic interaction cross-section (only interacts with other gravitational fields)
- Neutrinos travel faster than light for similar reasons; they only slow to apparent light speed when forced into electromagnetic interaction
- Craft must be **convex ellipsoids** — any concavity or sharp edge causes force-field turbulence → heating → speed cap at ~80% light speed. This is why Ryan Graves's "sphere inside a cube" craft cannot exceed that limit
- Five classes of subatomic particles (including two beyond the Standard Model's six quarks) are used in ceramic fiber-optic propulsion coils; two more for military-only craft; two even more unstable ones for weapons

See [[hall-photon-theory]] for the full framework.

## The Roswell Thesis

Hall argues that **two** gray scout craft crashed at Roswell in 1947 — not one. His explanation of the debris field:

- **Melted "rocks"** at the field = melted ceramic fiber-optic coils (Al₂O₃ + SiO₂ + semiconductor coating; melts at 1,800–2,200°F when the power fails)
- **"Memory metal" foil** = trivial outer protective sheathing; not scientifically significant
- The actual significant debris — the fiber optic material — was left behind because investigators didn't recognize it
- He personally saw the same gray scout craft type many times in Nevada — still operational as of 1965–67

As a child (~1951), Hall saw *Flying Saucers* magazine editor **Ray Palmer** at a store in London, Wisconsin, displaying a ~1-yard strip of woven ceramic fiber-optic material from Roswell that bent light without absorption.
