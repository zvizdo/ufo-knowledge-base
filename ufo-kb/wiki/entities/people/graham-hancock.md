# Graham Hancock

British author and journalist whose work (*Fingerprints of the Gods*, 1995; *America Before*, 2019; *Magicians of the Gods*, 2015) argues for the existence of a technically sophisticated pre-Ice Age civilization destroyed in a global catastrophe approximately 12,800 years ago — the Younger Dryas Impact event — and whose knowledge was encoded in megalithic structures globally aligned to astronomical configurations from that era.

## Core Arguments

- Megalithic sites globally (Göbekli Tepe, Giza, Sacsayhuamán, Angkor Wat) show precession-aware astronomical alignments that could only have been designed by a civilization with centuries of observational astronomy
- Randall Carlson's geological work corroborates catastrophic flooding events consistent with Hancock's timeline
- Robert Schoch's erosion analysis of the Great Sphinx pushes its construction back to before 5000 BC at minimum

## UAP-Adjacent Relevance

Jesse Michaels engages Hancock's framework in [[73YBwFvcmF8]] in the context of Mario Woods's post-abduction pyramid obsession. The specific thread: Were megalithic structures (including pyramid complexes) "celestial ascent chambers" — portals or stargates used by or for beings capable of interstellar/interdimensional travel? Woods directly endorses this.

The convergence of Hancock's ancient-cataclysm thesis with the UAP disclosure narrative is structural: if NHI have been present on Earth for tens of thousands of years, and if a previous advanced civilization was destroyed or reset, the question of NHI involvement in that history becomes live. This is the conceptual bridge between the ancient-civilization thread and the NHI thread in the UAP discourse.

## Supernatural (Cave Art and DMT)

Hancock wrote *Supernatural* — one of his less well-known books — examining cave art from the upper Paleolithic (30,000–40,000 years ago). He makes the compelling case, shared with anthropologists working in the field, that the cave art depicts beings that are clearly nonhuman and not animal — humanoid-animal hybrid forms, in pitch-black caves. His argument: these civilizations were using darkness or psychedelics to access the same NHI layer that modern DMT users encounter. The motif of nonhuman beings visible in darkness retreats and deep caves is a direct predecessor to modern DMT entity encounters.

[[andrew-gallimore]] cites *Supernatural* in [[8XD1ZiuhXoY]] as supporting evidence for the antiquity and cross-cultural stability of NHI entity motifs. Hancock also wrote the foreword to Gallimore's *[[death-by-astonishment]]*.

## Updated Position on Pyramid Dating (2025)

As of the [[92XrddiKjHY]] episode (2025), [[luke-caverns]] reports that Hancock has **converged on the 4th dynasty (~2650–2450 BC) for the Great Pyramid** — a significant revision from earlier more speculative positions. [[ben-van-kerkwijk]] discussed this on a podcast filmed at the pyramids alongside [[randall-carlson]]. Hancock still maintains the [[great-sphinx]] is older (pre-dynastic), informed by [[robert-schoch]]'s water erosion analysis.

Hancock has also recently speculated that the sarcophagi inside the pyramids may be places for "consciousness elevation" rather than straight body storage.

## Origin Story: Ark of the Covenant

Hancock's investigation of the anomalous began not with the pyramids but with the **Ark of the Covenant**. As *The Economist*'s East Africa correspondent in the late 1970s, he repeatedly encountered the Ethiopian tradition that they hold the real Ark in Axum. He interviewed successive guardians — all developing cataracts, all expecting to die within ~3 years, all describing the Ark as "a thing of fire." His investigation produced *The Sign and the Seal* (1992) and permanently shifted his orientation: archaeology dismisses traditions that, at firsthand investigation, have coherent evidence behind them. The Ark was his first direct experience of this gap.

## Sphinx as Full Lion

Hancock argues the Great Sphinx was **originally a full lion** — not a lion body with a human head, but a lion body with a lion head. The pharaonic human-head aspect (wearing the nemes headdress) was carved later over a heavily eroded and damaged original lion head. This is consistent with [[sphinx-water-erosion-theory]]: the erosion preceded any dynastic-era modification.

## Atlantis: 9,600 BC

The specific Atlantis date — 9,600 BC — comes from Egyptian priests at the Temple of Neith at Sais in the Delta, who read it to [[solon-of-athens]] from hieroglyphs on the wall. Hancock equates this with meltwater pulse 1B, one of the rapid sea-level-rise events at the end of the last Ice Age. This is not incidental mythology; it is one of the only global flood traditions that provides a specific date — and that date matches a known geological event.

## Consciousness / DMT as the Better UFO Track

Hancock explicitly declines to endorse the "ancient alien" nuts-and-bolts hypothesis (physical aliens in spacecraft built the pyramids). He believes the more productive track is through consciousness. [[pablo-amaringo]]'s framing — that "flying sorcerers" (craft-like objects in his spirit-world visions) are "vehicles for entering and leaving the spirit world" — is, for Hancock, the most illuminating definition of the UFO phenomenon available. He cites two parallel tracks of UFO research: nuts-and-bolts hardware, and consciousness/DMT; argues both must be embraced to reach resolution.

## Gnostic Self-Identification

Hancock explicitly identifies as Gnostic in [[dfPfPB601hw]]:

> "If I were to put myself in a category, I would say Gnostic is the right category."

He is not a Christian, does not belong to any organized religion. His book *[[talisman]]* (co-authored with [[robert-bauval]]) traces the Gnostic transmission through history — the Gnostics were among the first burned at the stake when the Roman Catholic Church "pulled on the jackboot" and began persecuting its competitors. Hancock believes the transmission has continued underground through the Templars, Rosicrucians, and Freemasons, and now through diffuse carriers who "may not even know what they're transmitting anymore."

## Ark of the Covenant: The Full Ethiopian Narrative

In [[sign-and-the-seal]] and [[dfPfPB601hw]], Hancock lays out his evidence chain for the Ark's presence in Axum:

1. **Elephantine Island** (~650 BC): A Jewish community on a Nile island near Aswan was expelled around 400 BC after conflicting with Egyptian priests over ram sacrifices at a Khnum temple. This is historically documented — they were "driven out, not massacred."
2. **Beta Israel chain**: The Ethiopian Jews ([[beta-israel]]) claim their ancestors lived on a Nile island and fled south. The Blue Nile rises in **[[lake-tana]]** — the absolute center of the Beta Israel community.
3. **Axum**: The Ark is claimed to be in a small chapel beside the Church of Saint Mary of Zion in **[[axum]]**. Guardians develop cataracts and die within ~3 years. The Ark is described as "a thing of fire."
4. **Babylonian absence**: The Babylonians (587 BC) kept meticulous records of everything taken from Jerusalem's Temple. No mention of the Ark — it was already gone.

## James Bruce and Kill Winning Lodge No. 2

While researching *The Sign and the Seal*, Hancock investigated **[[james-bruce]]** of Kinnaird — the Scottish traveler who claimed to discover the source of the Blue Nile and who brought back the [[book-of-enoch]] from Ethiopia (without James Bruce, the Book of Enoch would not be known in the West). Hancock suspected Bruce was a Freemason but could not prove it until he tracked down the Earl of Elgin, whose estate was restoring Bruce's grave:

> "My dear boy, of course he was a Freemason — Kill Winning Lodge No. 2 in Edinburgh."

The Earl also confirmed Bruce was a **speculative Freemason** interested in the esoteric side of the order. Hancock's inference: Bruce's Ethiopian journey was motivated by Masonic interest in the Ark, not merely geographical exploration.

## Lalibela and the Templar Trail

Hancock's Templar-Ark speculation (from [[sign-and-the-seal]]):
- An Ethiopian prince named Lalibela was in exile in Jerusalem during the Crusades, when the Templars controlled the Temple Mount.
- Hancock speculates Lalibela disclosed the Ark's Ethiopian location to the Templars.
- A "strange force of foreigners" helped Lalibela return and seize the Ethiopian throne.
- The rock-hewn churches at **[[lalibela]]** contain: (a) a **Templar cross on the ceiling**, and (b) a fresco of **Al-Aqsa Mosque with a cross on top** — the latter only chronologically valid during the period the Templars occupied and placed their cross on the mosque.

## Ayahuasca Novel: Entangled

In 2006, Hancock drank Ayahuasca for the first time in Brazil over five sessions. He received the entire plot of a novel — *[[entangled]]* — as a direct creative gift. Mother Ayahuasca gave him a clear instruction at the end of the sessions: "Write it." He did. The novel features two women, 24,000 years apart, quantum-entangled in a battle against a demonic force traveling through time.

## Depression and SSRIs (Personal Account)

Hancock speaks candidly about a period of severe depression and his experience with SSRIs (seroxat/Prozac). He concluded pharmaceutical antidepressants were "absolutely useless" for him and withdrew gradually over six months by slicing pills with a razor blade. He notes recent scientific findings that placebos are as effective as SSRIs and that the serotonin-deficiency/depression link has been debunked — "we've been sold a myth."

## Michael Shermer: A Genuine Update

After *[[ancient-apocalypse]]* aired on Netflix, [[michael-shermer]] — whom Hancock had publicly debated on the Joe Rogan Experience in 2017 — emailed Hancock to say he felt Hancock had made a good case for "missing an episode of the human story." Hancock cites this as evidence of Shermer's intellectual integrity: willing to step out of an entrenched position when evidence warrants.

## Zahi Hawas Reconciliation (2025)

After a serious falling-out in 2015 (Hancock said "very harsh words"), Hancock reached out to [[zahi-hawass]] in 2025 to make peace. They had dinner with Hawas's son Kareim. Hancock made an explicit apology. The two may pursue a "respectful disagreement" public collaboration — possibly a debate-format exchange allowing each to strongly present his position without personal attacks.
