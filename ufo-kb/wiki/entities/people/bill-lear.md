# Bill Lear

Aviation inventor and entrepreneur known as the "autopilot wizard" — his development of the automatic aircraft direction finder and, later, the Lear Jet made him one of the most consequential figures in postwar aerospace. Lear Corporation is named in the [[1971-australian-jio-memo]] as one of the major aerospace companies involved in US anti-gravity programs.

## Connection to Townsend Brown

Lear's offices in 1950s Santa Monica were directly across the street from [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s Guidance Technologies. The proximity led to a close working relationship; Lear is listed as a witness to Brown's gravitator experiments. In this he joins [[edward-teller]], [[curtis-lemay]], and [[victor-brandus]] as senior figures who personally observed experiments they found inexplicable.
