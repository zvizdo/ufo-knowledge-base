# Donald Berliner

**Donald Berliner** was a UFO researcher who worked in the crash-retrieval tradition during the late 1970s and 1980s, a period defined by FOIA releases that made thousands of pages of military and CIA UFO documents publicly available for the first time.

He is grouped with [[leonard-stringfield]], [[stanton-friedman]], and Kevin Randle as researchers who systematically investigated crash-retrieval accounts using newly accessible FOIA material.
