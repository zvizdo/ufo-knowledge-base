# Bill Napier

British astrophysicist associated with Armagh Observatory. In collaboration with Victor Clube and others (going back to ~1980), Napier theorized that a giant progenitor comet (~50–60 miles diameter nucleus) was captured into a sub-Jovian orbit 25,000–30,000 years ago and began a hierarchical fragmentation process, ultimately creating the Taurid meteor stream. This provided the cometary-source framework for the Younger Dryas Impact Hypothesis.

## Taurid Progenitor Model

[[randall-carlson]] attributes to Napier the specific proposal that the **Taurid meteor stream** was the delivery mechanism for the cosmic material that triggered the Younger Dryas ~12,900 years ago. Key elements of Napier's model:

- A mega-comet captured into an elliptical orbit between the sun and Jupiter
- Hierarchical breakup into increasingly smaller nuclei over millennia
- Creation of the dense, ecliptic-plane Taurid stream
- Earth crossing the stream twice yearly (late June/early July; late October/early November)
- Dense clumps within the stream — not uniform — make some crossings far more dangerous

Carlson notes that Napier's work came slightly after the 2007 Firestone et al. paper, adding the Taurid-stream mechanism to the impact framework that had been proposed earlier.

## Related Researchers

Carlson groups Napier with **Victor Clube** and **William Asher** as the academic astrophysics cluster that independently validated the catastrophism framework he had been arguing since 1995.
