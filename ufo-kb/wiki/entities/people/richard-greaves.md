# Richard Greaves

Hotel kitchen cook who worked with the two unnamed poachers responsible for the [[1990-calvine-photograph]]. Greaves was not present at the encounter itself but provides the primary eyewitness account of the subsequent MIB intimidation that drove the witnesses out of the area.

## Background

Greaves was employed at the hotel where the two poachers also worked in the kitchen. He was present when they returned from the area near Calvine having photographed the diamond/disc-shaped object. Though he did not witness the UAP directly, he observed the immediate aftermath and the intimidation that followed.

## MIB Intimidation

Two to three nights after the photograph was taken, three men arrived at the hotel — a driver and two men in suits. They took the witnesses outside and interrogated them in the rain. When the witnesses returned, Greaves described them as returning "white as ghosts." They began drinking heavily shortly after and disappeared from the area entirely within weeks.

Greaves specifically confirmed that **Americans were also involved** in the intimidation — the contingent was not exclusively UK officials. This detail places the Calvine suppression in the broader context of transnational UAP cover-up operations.
