# George Howard

Independent researcher and co-author of the foundational 2007 **Younger Dryas Impact Hypothesis (YDIH)** paper, which proposed that a cometary or asteroidal impact event ~12,800 years ago triggered the Younger Dryas cooling period, mega-fauna extinctions, and the collapse of the Clovis culture in North America. [[randall-carlson]] lists Howard alongside [[richard-firestone]] and Allan West as co-authors of the paper that launched the formal YDIH research program.

**Cosmic Tusk blog**: Howard runs the **Cosmic Tusk** blog (cosmictusk.com), which has served since the mid-2000s as the primary aggregator of YDIH-related research publications, team replications, independent confirmations, and academic critiques. The blog has become a reference point for researchers tracking whether the hypothesis is gaining or losing institutional support — new platinum/iridium anomalies, nanodiamonds, magnetic spherules, and carbon signatures at the Younger Dryas boundary from sites around the world are catalogued there as they are published.

**Significance in the catastrophism research cluster**: Howard bridges the professional academic literature (co-authoring in peer-reviewed journals) and the independent researcher/public-dissemination layer (the blog). His KB significance is as the connective tissue between [[richard-firestone]]'s laboratory findings, [[bill-napier]]'s orbital mechanics work, and [[randall-carlson]]'s public-facing presentation of YDIH — all three threads converge in the Cosmic Tusk documentation record.
