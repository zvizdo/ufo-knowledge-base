# John Keel

American journalist and UFO researcher (1930–2009). Author of *The Mothman Prophecies* (1975), *UFOs: Operation Trojan Horse* (1970), and *Strange Creatures from Time and Space* (1970). Known for rejecting the extraterrestrial hypothesis in favor of what he called the **ultraterrestrial** hypothesis — that UAP phenomena are manifestations of a "superspectrum" of energy that has always been present on Earth and that interfaces directly with human consciousness and perception.

## Mothman and Silver Bridge

Keel documented a wave of paranormal events around Point Pleasant, West Virginia (1966–67) — Mothman appearances, UFO sightings, Men in Black encounters, and precognitive visions. The wave climaxed with the collapse of the Silver Bridge on December 15, 1967, killing 46 people. Keel reported that some individuals who were drowning in the river heard voices broadcasting numbers at them — "number 3004," "number 3,486, wake up" — which pulled them back to consciousness and allowed them to survive. This "number broadcasting" motif recurred in Keel's broader research.

## Men in Black

Keel's Men in Black accounts describe entities that materialize and dematerialize, behave inconsistently with human intelligence operative behavior, and have bizarre interactions with witnesses that suggest a non-human origin. He distinguished these from government agents.
