# Wang Huning

**Wang Huning** is the 4th-ranked member of China's Politburo Standing Committee — the top 7-person decision-making body — and the CCP's chief ideologist. He has been the ideological architect behind Xi Jinping Thought, the Chinese Dream slogan, and the Belt and Road Initiative.

## America Against America (1991)

In 1988, a young Wang Huning visited the United States and spent several months studying American society, culture, and institutions. His observations were compiled into a book called *America Against America* (1991). Jesse Michaels, citing Josh Rogin, describes the book as documenting "all of the administration's China policy" and specifically identifying American weak points: racial divisions, class ruptures, decadence, consumerism. Wang Huning has been exploiting this analysis in Chinese strategic influence operations for over 30 years.

His analysis of American decadence — especially the potential for media platforms to exacerbate internal divisions — is understood as foundational to China's TikTok/ByteDance strategy: exporting mindless content to American youth while restricting Chinese youth to STEM-focused material.
