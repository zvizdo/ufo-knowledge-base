# Danny Jones

Independent podcast host; part of the same social network as [[jesse-michaels]] and [[joe-rogan]]. Jesse and Joe both describe him as a friend; Rogan says Jones texts him clips from notable episodes.

## Notable Episodes

**Bart Sibrel vs. Charlie Duke**: Jones hosted a debate between [[bart-sibrel]] (moon-landing skeptic) and [[charlie-duke]] (Apollo 16 astronaut, ~90 years old). The episode was described by [[joe-rogan]] as significant; Danny Jones reportedly texted Rogan clips and told him the episode left him "maybe convinced we didn't go to the moon." Rogan added that Jones "also told me he thinks they were MK Ultra'd to think they went to the moon."

**Neil Armstrong episode**: Jones hosted an episode on the moon landing that [[joe-rogan]] also references in connection with the Gemini 15 faked photo and other evidence.

Jones appears to function as a significant independent node in the alternative-media UAP/conspiracy network — adjacent to but distinct from the American Alchemy / Rogan ecosystem.
