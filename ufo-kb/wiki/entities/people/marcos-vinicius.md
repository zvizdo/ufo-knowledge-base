# Marcos Vinicius

Junior neurosurgeon at [[hospital-regional-varginha]] in January 1996. A colleague and friend of [[italo-venturelli]], with 2–3 years of experience at the time (primarily trauma cases). He died in 2018, reportedly after years of increased stress from the encounter — his smoking markedly escalated after 1996. He collected Breitling and Omega watches, bought many gadgets online, owned racehorses, and shared his home generously. His wife's name is Rosalin.

## The Surgery

Vinicius performed an improvised minor procedure on the alien being — most likely sutures to close a wound. The room used was not a standard operating theatre but an improvised space (now an ICU). The surgical kit visible in the video was minimal: an amis basin and needle holder only — not a full neurosurgical kit. Venturelli interprets this as a simple wound-closure procedure.

Vinicius had not alerted Venturelli beforehand — he called him in after the procedure as an informal senior review, per their normal working relationship. He did not discuss the details of the procedure with Venturelli in depth; medical confidentiality culture and professional risk suppressed the conversation.

## The Video

Vinicius filmed the being on a large 1990s shoulder-mounted camera with a small screen. Based on Venturelli's description of the video:
- Black and white
- ~1.5–2 minutes long
- Shows the being lying semi-upright in bed, conscious, looking at the camera
- Surgical material visible on the side table (pre-procedure or post-procedure)
- Humanoid figure, teardrop skull, large eyes, calm

Vinicius showed this video repeatedly at his home — family, fellow doctors all saw it. He kept it in a personal cabinet (with his watch collection and CDs) in his bedroom.

## After 1996

Vinicius began chain-smoking after the encounter, which Venturelli attributes to extreme stress. He never discussed the details of the surgery publicly. He showed the video for years as a kind of personal proof, in controlled settings, to people he trusted.

He died in 2018. After his death, his house was robbed — some watches and items were taken. [[james-fox]] subsequently searched his house and had his computer taken to a São Paulo warehouse — the video was not recovered. Venturelli believes it may still be in the house, possibly among CDs and other storage in Marcos' bedroom cabinet, and may have been stolen unknowingly in the robbery.

James Fox has offered $1M for the video.
