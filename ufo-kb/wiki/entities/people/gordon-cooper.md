# Gordon Cooper

USAF test pilot and NASA Mercury astronaut. Held the record for most time in orbit among astronauts before the Apollo program began. Salas describes him as "no scrub" — a credibility marker given his elite aerospace background. Cooper spent more time in orbit than anyone before him as part of the Mercury missions.

Cooper wrote in his book (title not recalled by Salas) that [[wernher-von-braun]] told him during the informal weekend gatherings von Braun hosted for astronauts that his team in Nazi Germany had actually worked on and flown a craft operating on principles similar to a UFO. Salas cites this as evidence that [[reverse-engineered-craft]] activity predates the post-WWII US programs commonly discussed.

## Connection to Andrija Puharich

Cooper was deeply interested in psychic research and the UAP question. He visited [[andrija-puharich]]'s Ossining, NY research compound multiple times. In his book he wrote about attending Puharich's sessions, seeing the Faraday cage experiments, and witnessing the [[space-kids]] program. He was apparently genuinely curious about whether psychic phenomena and UAPs were connected.

## 1957 Military Base Encounter and Film Confiscation (QkuDvTFN7ms)

At a 1995/96 Portsmouth, Maine conference where [[jim-weiner]] and [[charlie-foltz]] (Allagash witnesses) also spoke, Cooper shared two firsthand UAP accounts with them:

**1957 military base landing:**
- Cooper was on a base with three photographers (a 16mm cinematographer, a 35mm still photographer, and a 4x5 press camera photographer)
- A tic-tac-shaped object was found resting on tripod legs on the ground; as their jeep approached within 300–400 yards, the landing legs retracted and the craft shot vertically upward
- Cooper: "Never saw them. Soon as we got back, they were all confiscated." — the films were immediately seized upon return
- A second craft was also observed: it flew over them, deployed three landing struts, rested on a dry lakebed while they filmed it, then retracted and departed at high speed

**Mercury orbit encounter:**
- During his orbital flight, a green-colored object came into proximity with Cooper's capsule (Charlie Foltz hedges this may be partially conflated with other astronauts' accounts)
- The base encounter (1957) predated the Mercury program (first flights 1958–59), establishing Cooper as a multi-event witness

Charlie notes that Cooper described both events at the same conference and spent the weekend with them, corroborating the detail and atmosphere. Cooper's 1957 encounter is significant as a documented case of physical film evidence being immediately confiscated by military authority — consistent with the suppression pattern Cooper described through his book about von Braun.
