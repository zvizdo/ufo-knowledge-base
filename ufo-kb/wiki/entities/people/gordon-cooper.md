# Gordon Cooper

USAF test pilot and NASA Mercury astronaut. Held the record for most time in orbit among astronauts before the Apollo program began. Salas describes him as "no scrub" — a credibility marker given his elite aerospace background. Cooper spent more time in orbit than anyone before him as part of the Mercury missions.

Cooper wrote in his book (title not recalled by Salas) that [[wernher-von-braun]] told him during the informal weekend gatherings von Braun hosted for astronauts that his team in Nazi Germany had actually worked on and flown a craft operating on principles similar to a UFO. Salas cites this as evidence that [[reverse-engineered-craft]] activity predates the post-WWII US programs commonly discussed.

## Connection to Andrija Puharich

Cooper was deeply interested in psychic research and the UAP question. He visited [[andrija-puharich]]'s Ossining, NY research compound multiple times. In his book he wrote about attending Puharich's sessions, seeing the Faraday cage experiments, and witnessing the [[space-kids]] program. He was apparently genuinely curious about whether psychic phenomena and UAPs were connected.
