# Harold White

## Background

NASA physicist based at Johnson Space Center who ran the **Eagle Works** laboratory — an advanced propulsion research group — through roughly the early 2010s. Best known publicly for claiming to have detected very small but non-zero thrust in an EmDrive (electromagnetic drive), and for attempting to verify whether the Alcubierre warp-drive metric could produce a detectable spacetime distortion in a laboratory setting using a modified Michelson interferometer.

## Eagle Works / Warp Drive Experiment

White's most influential public contribution was an experimental attempt to detect a metric distortion consistent with an Alcubierre warp bubble using interferometry with a 1 cm toroidal resonator. The results were reported as suggestive but below threshold for publication confidence. NASA ultimately released a statement describing the experiment as having produced "null results."

In the [[8TYMQOUDQBo]] interview, both [[salvatore-pais]] and co-host Jack note:
- White "hasn't published anything lately" — which they describe as "always a sign" of classified involvement
- He is now funded by **DARPA** and runs (or is associated with) a company called **Limitless Space**
- Pais affirms "Dr. White is right on the money" — a notable endorsement from the author of the competing Navy patent pathway

## Limitless Space / DARPA

The transition from public NASA researcher to DARPA-funded private entity mirrors a pattern seen with other researchers who get close to anomalous propulsion physics. The company name "Limitless Space" and its DARPA affiliation remain relatively unanalyzed in open-source literature.

## Relationship to Pais Patents

Both White's Eagle Works approach and Pais's patents are attempting to engineer Alcubierre-adjacent physics:
- White's approach: directly detect/generate a spacetime metric distortion using resonating electromagnetic fields in a toroidal geometry — close to Pais's "high-frequency gravitational wave generator" patent
- Pais's approach: drive quantum vacuum energy densities past the Schwinger limit to generate the exotic matter the Alcubierre metric requires

Pais's endorsement of White's direction despite the apparent null results suggests the null results may not tell the full story.
