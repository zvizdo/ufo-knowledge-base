# Robert Gates

CIA Director (1991–1993) and Secretary of Defense (2006–2011). During his time as a CIA Russia analyst, Gates evaluated the Stargate Program's TK-089 Soviet submarine remote viewing report — specifically, [[harley-trent]] and [[joe-mcmoneagle]]'s independently corroborating description of a prototype Soviet submarine with 20 slanted launch tubes and RS-27 missiles. Gates dismissed the report as "total fantasy." Overhead photography confirmed the submarine 114 days later.

## Nightline Lying-Through-Omission

McMoneagle describes Gates' behavior on ABC's Nightline regarding the Stargate Program. When asked whether a single-source intelligence report would ever be used, Gates said no — technically true under standard protocols. McMoneagle: "That's lying through omission. You never use single source unless it's verified — but that's exactly what we were doing, because we were providing intelligence that nobody else could provide."
