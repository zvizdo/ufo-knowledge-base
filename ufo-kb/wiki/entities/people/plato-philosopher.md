# Plato

Ancient Greek philosopher (c. 428–348 BC). Founder of the Academy in Athens; student of [[socrates-philosopher]]; teacher of Aristotle. Author of dialogues covering epistemology, ethics, politics, metaphysics, and consciousness.

## Atlantis & Mystery Tradition Context

Primary ancient textual source for the Atlantis account, transmitted through *Timaeus* and *Critias*, attributed to the Athenian statesman [[solon-of-athens]] who received the story from Egyptian priests at [[temple-of-sais]]. Whether Plato invented Atlantis as philosophical allegory or preserved real history is a central debate in the [[atlantis-thesis]] and [[lost-civilization-hypothesis]] literature.

Also cited in [[brian-muraresku]]'s work on Greek mystery religions and psychedelic sacraments at Eleusis.

## Cave Allegory & UAP Epistemology Context

Philosopher [[jim-madden]], in [[KRDE1i3LDuU]] and [[unidentified-flying-hyper-object]], offers a major reinterpretation of Plato's corpus relevant to UAP discourse:

**The Republic is about death, not politics.** The initiating question (what happens when we die?) is the real inquiry; justice is secondary scaffolding. The book is bookended by myths of the afterlife. The political program is Plato's exoteric cover — following the [[leo-strauss]] tradition of esoteric/exoteric reading.

**Plato was not a Platonist.** Received Platonism (Forms, soul immortality as flat doctrine) misreads the dialogues. Plato writes in drama and dialogue precisely to avoid doctrinal assertion; he is often testing the reader, being playful, or writing in multiple layers simultaneously.

**The Cave Allegory (*Republic* VII):** Prisoners chained facing a cave wall see only shadows of objects carried behind them. One prisoner freed and dragged out of the cave is blinded, then gradually learns to see — but if they return to the cave, they are rejected (and may be killed, as [[socrates-philosopher]] was). Applied to UAP: humanity is literally in the cave (400–700nm EM spectrum, credentialist epistemology); UFOs are the prior-busting phenomenon from outside; official disclosure is an attempted return-to-cave that will be rejected because the other prisoners have not undergone the perceptive transformation.

**The Recollection Doctrine (*Phaedo*, *Meno*):** Knowledge is not information transfer but anamnesis — remembering what the soul already knows. The geometry proof with the slave in *Meno* demonstrates primordial knowledge structure. Implication: enlightenment cannot be transmitted by authority, only unlocked internally.

**Good Lies / Noble Myths:** Plato states that even the ideal city's guardians must be told myths about afterlife rewards to sustain virtuous behavior — structural necessity of "good lies" at every level of institutional authority.
