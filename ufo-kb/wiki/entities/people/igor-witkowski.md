# Igor Witkowski

Polish writer and researcher focused on German secret weapon programs of World War II. Witkowski is the primary public source for the [[die-glocke]] (The Bell) story and claims to have seen an SS interrogation transcript referencing it.

## Cook's Meeting

According to [[nick-cook]] in [[G7Ns4Aq1tVc]], Witkowski contacted Cook during Cook's investigation and told him about Die Glocke — a device allegedly producing anti-gravitational and time dilation effects. Cook describes the visit to the site as "very atmospheric":

The site was a deep mine near **Walenberg/Wenceslaus** in what is now Poland. On the ground, a **hexagonal concrete structure** is still visible — which Witkowski identified as a test rig for either a disc-shaped craft or Die Glocke itself. The site showed evidence of heavy classified infrastructure: "very thick cabling — lots of electricity into this place."

## Cook's Assessment

Cook's own conclusion about Die Glocke's true nature evolved over time. He came to think the Wenceslaus facility was most likely "one of several sites the Germans — the SS in particular — were using to look into nuclear technology beyond the Heisenberg program" (which was officially stopped in 1942–43). Cook notes that if the project's name was "Kronos" (as Witkowski claimed), it could have been aptly named — Kronos being the Greek god of time.

This stops short of endorsing the time-travel interpretation while acknowledging the facility was genuinely secret and highly powered.
