# Chris Bledsoe

North Carolina-based contactee known for claims of repeated encounters with what he describes as a "divine feminine" figure or entity appearing from or associated with the **dark side of the Moon**. His case has attracted unusual attention from within the intelligence community.

## CIA Interest

Senior CIA officer **Jim Semivan** personally visited Bledsoe — an extraordinary level of institutional attention for a civilian contactee. Semivan's visit signals that the CIA (or at least senior figures within it) found Bledsoe's accounts operationally or phenomenologically interesting enough to warrant direct engagement.

## Encounter Description

Per [[danny-sheehan]]'s account:
- The entity Bledsoe encounters is described in feminine/Marian terms — consistent with the cross-mapping [[diana-pasulka]] documents between religious apparition accounts and NHI contact.
- The encounters reportedly have other witnesses, including family members.
- The "dark side of the Moon" detail is unusual within the contactee literature and connects to the broader discourse around Moon-as-artificial-object or Moon-as-inhabited-structure.

## Bob Maguire's Firsthand Visit

[[bob-maguire]] spent four days at Bledsoe's North Carolina property after [[jim-semivan]] opened the door. Key events on the final clear night:

- Two large orbs appeared over the treeline (~3–400 meters away, then one ~40 ft overhead directly above them). Fluctuating colors, non-linear movement (curves, zig-zags).
- A purple aura above the treeline grew slowly visible then vanished abruptly. Bledsoe said "they're gone now" without missing a beat — apparent telepathic signal from the phenomenon.
- Bledsoe's son Ryan also witnessed and filmed one of the orbs.
- After Bob returned home, orbs appeared around his property; security cameras captured a triangle of orbs rising vertically.

Bob states that he saw the orbs with his naked eye (not just via IR camera), confirming they were real electromagnetic phenomena.

## The Obama Camp David Napkin

[[grant-cameron]] posted a photo on Facebook showing an envelope addressed to Bledsoe and a napkin bearing the **US presidential seal from Camp David**. [[bob-maguire]] identified the seal. Copying the presidential seal is a federal crime; the only way such a napkin exists is if someone with Camp David access sent it.

Bob's conclusion: [[tim-taylor]] (the conduit between Bledsoe and Washington) arranged for Bledsoe's prophecies to be transmitted to **Barack Obama**. This is Bob's characterization of "Ironclad proof" that the story reached the president.

## The Bledsoe Prophecy

Bledsoe reportedly sees a prophetic vision each Easter. One key vision: missiles being fired at Iran while astronomical events occur over the pyramids. Tim Taylor's involvement implies this prophecy was taken seriously enough to reach the White House. [[ryan-bledsoe]] (Chris's son) has corroborated elements of these accounts.

## Roswell Material Test

[[tim-taylor]] brought a piece of the [[1947-roswell]] crash and placed it in Bledsoe's hand. Bledsoe reacted unexpectedly strongly. Taylor's response: "Why you?" — curious and surprised, not negative.

## The First Encounter — January 8, 2007

Full account now documented. See [[2007-bledsoe-cape-fear]] for the incident page.

Bledsoe held a **commercial pilot's license** at the time. He was at the lowest point of his life — housing business collapsed, living in a mobile home, Crohn's disease at maximum severity. The encounter on the Cape Fear River involved a 4-hour missing time and complete resolution of his Crohn's disease the same day.

## Memory Recovery

[[michael-oconnell]] — a Harvard psychiatrist trained directly by [[john-mack]] — worked with Bledsoe for years to recover suppressed memories of the 4-hour gap. The recovered memories began with an overhead view of Egypt: pyramids, Sphinx, Hathor's temple, hieroglyphic imagery. Bledsoe then studied Egyptian symbolism with [[robert-gilbert]] (Rosicrucian scholar/nuclear chemist, Asheville NC). O'Connell is now deceased.

## The 2012 Easter Encounter with the Lady

After five years of persecution by his Fayetteville community, Bledsoe walked out Easter Saturday night 2012 and renounced his public testimony. A wind knocked him backward; a translucent cow passed over him (visible stars through it). A luminous woman hovered 3 ft off the ground. *"You know why I'm here."* She identified herself as having many names: Hathor, Hera, Diana, Isis, Virgin Mary, Sophia, White Buffalo Calf Woman. She issued the compact: continue telling your story and I will allow you to film my presence. From 2012 onward Bledsoe has amassed terabytes of data.

The Lady communicated that humanity is at a threshold; that the apocalypse (*apokalypsis* = the unveiling) would expose darkness and reveal truth; and that God is one beneath all religious names.

## Camp David Napkin — Firsthand Account

In this interview Bledsoe confirmed directly that [[tim-taylor]] told him:
> *"I was at the dinner table with the president and handed me that [napkin]."*
Taylor had just returned from Camp David at Christmas. The napkin bore the presidential seal. [[bob-maguire]] independently identified the seal and concluded Bledsoe's story reached Barack Obama. Bledsoe never told Taylor he had briefed the president; he inferred it from the letter head and the napkin together.

## Tim Taylor: Dragon Control and the Hammer

Taylor's NASA console is called **Dragon Control**; he is the primary launch-monitoring station at Cape Canaveral. An entire chain of coverage (ground cameras → WB-57 at 50,000 ft → F-104 Starfighter at 100,000 ft → NRO satellites) feeds into a private room accessible only to Taylor once the rocket is in orbit.

Taylor told Bledsoe he works for "the Hammer" — pointing to the Philip K. Dick-derived film *The Adjustment Bureau* as the best description:
> *"In the movie, the Hammer is like an archangel — the one who handles what lesser beings can't. I work for the Hammer and the Hammer works for God."*

Taylor required Bledsoe to get a **dragon tattoo** to access the Cape Canaveral base. Tim also knew [[elon-musk]] (names came up; Taylor controls Dragon capsule launches). Taylor also worked at [[aerospace-corporation]] and is connected to the **NRO** (per Bledsoe: *"the NRO has the keys"*).

Every rocket launch attracts UAPs — especially when armed. NRO is the only organization with satellite eyes on what approaches the rocket.

## Zanesville Metal Recovery

[[tim-taylor]] took Bledsoe to **Zanesville, Ohio** — [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s birthplace — to recover a piece of glowing red metal found in a local man's yard 15 years earlier. The metal was sawed in a machine shop; Taylor kept one piece. Bledsoe subsequently brought the same source to [[hal-puthoff]], [[tom-delonge]], and [[lue-elizondo]], each receiving pieces.

The recovery site is near the **Indian Serpent Mound**. Taylor discussed Townsend Brown there; confirmed he was helping Brown's daughter [[linda-brown]] write her biography and had the manuscript on his phone. Taylor told Bledsoe that NHI had visited Townsend Brown directly — *"things coming and landing in his yard and getting out and giving him information."* This matches Linda Brown's own Reddit AMA account of pale-skinned beings visiting their kitchen.

## Vivex Biotechnologies

Bledsoe served as an **intuitive input source** for [[vivex-biotechnologies]] — [[tim-taylor]]'s biotech company (sold for $88M). As a carpenter, Bledsoe spent years reading scientific papers on proteins and stem cells sent to him by the team, then drawing cellular diagrams from intuitive downloads. His drawings were submitted weekly. The viograph built from recovered material performed bone regeneration (first US operation: Spanish woman with bone cancer; bone regrown in ~60 days).

## Faraday Cage and Consciousness

At the Monroe Institute (filmed for Beyond Skinwalker Ranch, with Brandon Fugal), Bledsoe was placed in a copper-walled Faraday cage in the basement. Dr. Thompson (neuroscience PhD) monitored him. Every time Bledsoe pointed toward the ceiling sensing a UAP, cameras above the building captured something. Conclusion from the group (including [[john-alexander]]): the mechanism is **consciousness, not electromagnetic** — Faraday shielding is irrelevant.

## Healing Accounts

Two healing narratives on record:
1. **Crohn's disease** (18 years): resolved completely after the January 2007 encounter.
2. **Stage-4 kidney cancer patient** (~2013–2015): orb appeared, flashed, a tall glowing figure (~6–7 ft) emerged and moved toward the woman. Monday scan: no cancer. On video.

Received around 2012 from the Lady: when Regulus (a blue star) appears **red on the horizon at dawn**, in the gaze of the Sphinx facing east — a sign that ushers in new knowledge for humanity. The darkness will be exposed; truth revealed. Bledsoe has spoken about this for 10–12 years and believes we are in that window now.
