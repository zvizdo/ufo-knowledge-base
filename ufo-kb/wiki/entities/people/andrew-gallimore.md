# Andrew Gallimore

British neuroscientist living and working in Japan. Author of *[[alien-information-theory]]* (2015) and the forthcoming *[[death-by-astonishment]]* (forward by [[graham-hancock]]). Core researcher at [[new-nautics]] (Florida). Has studied DMT for over 20 years, approaching it as a hard scientific problem rather than a spiritual practice — but one that requires going beyond conventional materialist explanations.

## Background

Neuroscientist with a focus on consciousness and pharmacology. Based in Japan. Has appeared on the Danny Jones podcast and the Third Eye Drops podcast ([[michael-taft]]). Spoke at the Psychedelic Science Conference in Denver. Works closely with [[rick-strassman]] (co-developed DMTX protocol 2015) and with [[new-nautics]] (Florida), which is funding endogenous DMT research at the University of Florida.

## Core Model: The Directed DMT World

Gallimore's central thesis is what he calls the **directed world model**. Perception works not by direct access to external reality but by the brain building a model tested against sensory inputs. In the DMT state:

- DMT makes the cortex unusually sensitive to external perturbation (confirmed by recent neuroimaging)
- Discarnate intelligences with no physical form gain access to the brain's world-modeling machinery
- They **direct** the visions — the brain becomes a stage; the entities perform on it
- The user does not break through *into* the DMT world; the DMT world breaks through *into* the user

This is a radicalization of the [[transmission-theory-consciousness]] (brain as receiver), adding active external agency: the signal is not passive, it is directed.

## DMTX Protocol

In 2015, Gallimore and [[rick-strassman]] co-developed the DMTX concept: repurposing target-controlled intravenous infusion (a standard anesthesiology technique) with DMT to sustain stable breakthrough states. The key pharmacological property enabling this is DMT's unusual **lack of tolerance** — repeated doses within minutes produce identical intensity. [[chris-timmermann]] at Imperial College implemented a refined version, extending the DMT state to 30 minutes. A group in Basel extended to 90 minutes; another extended to 6 hours at low dose.

Gallimore's vision: send trained specialists (mathematicians, geometers, linguists) into stable DMTX states to systematically study the entities and what they produce. [[andres-gomez-emilsson]] — a mathematician — has already observed DMT entities performing the four-color theorem instantaneously, an operation requiring hours for a human.

## Endogenous DMT Research

With [[new-nautics]], Gallimore is contracting with the University of Florida to isolate and characterize a peptide found in cerebrospinal fluid that inhibits INMT (the enzyme that produces DMT). This peptide keeps endogenous DMT levels low. Understanding it may allow "endoDMTX" — using a peptide injection to slide subjects into a stable natural DMT state without exogenous administration.

## Proposed Identity of DMT Entities

Three candidate hypotheses (not mutually exclusive):

1. **Postbiological civilizations** — advanced intelligences that transcended biology and instantiated in the computational substrate of spacetime (John Barrow's omega-minus civilization). Everywhere and nowhere; interact with physical minds via brain access.
2. **Higher-dimensional beings** — our 3D reality is embedded in a higher-dimensional structure; DMT unfolds the brain into it temporarily.
3. **Discarnate intelligences** — non-physical beings that have always been present but only accessible via specific molecular gates.

Gallimore is deliberately non-committal on which is true; he argues that mainstream scientific discourse already supports all three as non-crazy hypotheses.

## Notable Claims

All firsthand unless noted:
- DMT entities **cannot help but betray their intelligence** — they perform mathematical operations and geometric structures that are humanly impossible; the solution is to send the right trained observers in
- Cave art 30–40,000 years old depicts the same entity types as modern DMT reports — this is not coincidence; these beings go back to the beginning of human cognitive history
- Indigenous peoples who developed ayahuasca were taught the preparation by the plants/intelligences themselves — "the plants told us" is a precise empirical report, not metaphor
- The Yanomami *wusinari* (insectoid) and *heura* (multitudinous dancing) beings match insectoid aliens and machine elves exactly
- DMT may be a neuroprotective agent released at death; the sigma-1 receptor is the mechanism
- "DMT is not a coincidence — it is a message implanted in the natural world"

## Open Questions

- Are entities present outside the DMT state, simply inaccessible? (Gallimore hopes not)
- Does endogenous DMT released at death serve as a soul-transit conduit, or only as neuroprotection?
- Can DMTX demonstrate non-human intelligence in a scientifically falsifiable way?
