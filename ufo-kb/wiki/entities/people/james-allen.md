# James Allen

Documentary filmmaker who produced a film documenting [[mark-mccandish]]'s ARV / Flux Liner testimony. The documentary covered McCandish's account of the Alien Reproduction Vehicle he illustrated at the 2001 National Press Club Disclosure event organized by [[steven-greer]].

## Death

Allen was diagnosed with an aggressive cancer and died within approximately three weeks of diagnosis. [[uap-gerb]] notes that Allen's autopsy showed heavy metal poisoning. [[michael-herrera]] implies this is consistent with assassination via the weaponized form of a [[scalar-frequency-healing]] device — a scalar/frequency weapon that can reportedly induce fast-growing cancers remotely via a photograph of the target.

> ⚠ The scalar assassination claim is Herrera's interpretation. No independent investigation or official finding has corroborated it. Heavy metal poisoning could indicate conventional poisoning, environmental exposure, or other causes.

## Significance

If the account is accurate, Allen's death fits a pattern of researchers and documentarians associated with sensitive UFO/reverse-engineering topics dying of fast-moving illness. Comparable cases in the UAP discourse include Phil Schneider and others. The specific claim of scalar-weapon capability adds a new mechanism to the pattern.
