# Pete Conrad

NASA astronaut; commander of Gemini 11 (1966) and Apollo 12 (1969). The third human to walk on the Moon (Apollo 12, November 1969). Died 1999.

## Background

Conrad flew four space missions: Gemini 5, Gemini 11, Apollo 12, and Skylab 2. He was known as a skilled and irreverent pilot. His crewmate on Gemini 11 was Richard Gordon.

## Connection to UAP

During **Gemini 11** (September 1966), public NASA transcripts record a power anomaly — battery stack "Charlie" drained — coinciding with a report of an object flying over the spacecraft. Kevin Knuth notes this sequence in the transcripts is "almost a classic UFO sighting": object appears, flies over the ship, batteries drain.

The [[1966-gemini-11-ufo]] incident is separate from the later [[scott-simkinson]] lithograph controversy. Knuth's forensic work placed the "strange object" lithograph at the time of the tether experiment (not when the battery drain occurred); the battery drain happened approximately two-thirds of an orbit (~one hour) later.

Conrad's Gemini 11 crewmate [[alan-bean]] was his Apollo 12 crew member. Bean separately reported the Skylab UFO encounter to [[kevin-knuth]].
