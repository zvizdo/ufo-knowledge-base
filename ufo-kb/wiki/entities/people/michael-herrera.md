# Michael Herrera

## Background

Michael Herrera is a US Marine Corps veteran (31st Marine Expeditionary Unit, saw gunner by trade) who came forward as a witness to the [[2009-indonesia-ufo]] encounter — one of the most operationally specific multi-witness UAP accounts in the disclosure corpus. A Freemason (self-declared, open). Currently runs multiple businesses in Colorado. Went public through [[steven-greer]]'s networks after approximately eight years of silence, driven partly by the Gallagher Amendment whistleblower protections.

## Notable Claims

### The 2009 Indonesia Encounter

During a Marine humanitarian deployment in western Sumatra, Herrera's unit of six Marines observed a **~300-foot octagonal craft** hovering in a jungle clearing, emitting a low-frequency bass hum. Three-tiered concentric octagonal platforms; matte black with Vantablack-like absorptive panels; no rotor wash, no sonic boom on departure. A lower platform loaded refrigerated/HVAC containers via cargo trucks; the craft then rotated clockwise (revealing red/yellow/green/blue corner lights) and shot off at "several thousand miles per hour." See [[2009-indonesia-ufo]] for the full account.

The containers held [[p3-psionic-assets]] — humans with psionic ability used to attract NHI craft — as later disclosed by [[jake-barber]].

### Apprehension and Cover-Up

Eight unknown operators (American-accented English) apprehended the six Marines at gunpoint before they could close on the clearing. The operators openly discussed killing them. Herrera's photos were covertly destroyed at Subic Bay (camera accessed from a locked locker, battery and memory card removed). An Air Force Lt. Colonel at Camp Hansen, Okinawa had each Marine sign an NDA referencing "TSSCI" and "Indonesia" under threat of prison or execution.

### Disclosure Path

- **2018:** Met [[steven-greer]] at a Boulder book signing; told him he witnessed a "drug running operation using this technology"; Greer handed him a card and told him not to tell anyone
- **April 2023:** Testified to [[aaro]]; Black Hawk and CH-47 helicopters subsequently began hovering over his home, his father's residence, and his business
- **2023 NPC:** Publicly testified at Greer's National Press Club event; [[jake-barber]] was present under an alias on a legacy program red-team mission
- **Post-NPC:** [[jake-barber]] went rogue; connected with Herrera via Greer; revealed P3 assets, Russia/China agreement, and craft shoot-down doctrine; took Herrera on a desert trip to confirm his legitimacy

### Jake Barber Disclosures

See [[jake-barber]] for full details. Key points received by Herrera:
- The containers = [[p3-psionic-assets]]
- Russia/China have their own retrieval teams; "gentleman's agreement" on downed craft
- Legacy programs shoot down NHI craft; retrieval teams recover them
- Blue-on-blue firefight between Lockheed retrieval team and another team (confirmed by [[kirk-mcconnell]] in a SCIF) involving transport of humans in reverse-engineered craft

### Greer's Claim on the Craft

[[steven-greer]] told Herrera the 8-gon craft observed in Indonesia is a reverse-engineered version manufactured by [[lockheed-skunk-works]] at "North Brumit." Herrera treats this as Greer's speculation, not confirmed.

### CE-5 and Sedona

Attended multiple CE-5 events with Greer in Sedona. At one event (attended by [[paola-harris]] and Demi Lovato's group): blue/white orbs appeared behind Herrera, a gold-colored disc-shaped craft appeared and disappeared, and Herrera was physically struck on his side by an unknown force while orbs were reportedly behind him.

### Scalar Healing

Underwent treatment with an undisclosed [[scalar-frequency-healing]] device (word-of-mouth access). Reports parasites cured after sessions; dog's bladder mass resolved remotely via photo.
