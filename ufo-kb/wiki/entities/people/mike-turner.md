# Mike Turner

Rep. Mike Turner (R-OH) chairs the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence, making him one of the most powerful figures in US intelligence oversight. He has been a significant presence in UAP legislative battles — notably pushing back on or shaping the [[schumer-uap-amendment|Schumer-Rounds UAP Disclosure Amendment]], which sought mandatory declassification of UAP-related records.

Turner represents the institutional skeptic faction within Congress: not necessarily denying UAP reality, but concerned about intelligence equities, classification regimes, and the process of any disclosure.

## NDAA Eminent Domain Clause (D2tKCFmJjks)

[[michael-herrera]] identifies Turner as the congressman who **"basically gutted" the eminent domain clause** from the NDAA UAP language — the provision that would have allowed the government to compel UAP-related assets held by private contractors. Turner's top three donors are named by Herrera as **Boeing, Lockheed, and Raytheon** — the primary legacy aerospace contractors. Turner has since left Congress. Herrera frames his opposition to the eminent domain clause as self-exposure: it reveals which legislators are protecting the private-contractor custodians of UAP programs.

> Note: Turner represents the congressional district containing [[wright-patterson]] AFB — one of the most historically significant UAP-adjacent facilities.
