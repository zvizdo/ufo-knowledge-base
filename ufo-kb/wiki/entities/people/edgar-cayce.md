# Edgar Cayce

## Background

American psychic (1877–1945), often called "The Sleeping Prophet." While in trance states, he claimed to receive visions about past lives, healing, and ancient history. One of his most influential predictions was that a "Hall of Records" — a chamber containing the history of Atlantis — lies beneath the [[great-sphinx]] at [[giza-plateau]].

## The Hall of Records Claim

Cayce stated (ca. 1940s) that a chamber beneath the Sphinx holds records of the lost civilization of Atlantis. This prophecy became enormously popular and directly motivated [[zahi-hawass]]'s 1990s drilling near the Sphinx — a test that Hawas quickly abandoned after shallow, off-axis drilling found nothing, prompting ongoing suspicion about the methodology.

The claim is hearsay/psychic — no firsthand documentary or physical evidence. [[luke-caverns]] notes the story "goes back to Edgar Cayce" and maintains a "probably not, but maybe" stance.
