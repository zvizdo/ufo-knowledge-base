# Ellie Marculli

UFO/NHI researcher and close colleague of [[tim-alberino]]. Known for two key contributions documented in this transcript:

## Kandahar Giant Corroboration
Marculli received an independent account of the [[2002-kandahar-giant]] from a special operations soldier (branch unspecified):

- The soldier described the same basic incident but dated it to **2003** (vs. the C-130 pilot's ~2005)
- A team went missing; a second team found them in a cave; the giant **skewered one soldier with a spear**; the team killed it
- Marculli published this account; the C-130 pilot subsequently re-contacted [[steve-quail]] and confirmed Marculli's source was telling the truth — the pilot had deliberately withheld certain details from his own public account, and Marculli's source provided matching information for those details
- Whether the two accounts describe the same incident or two separate encounters remains unresolved

## Roger Lear / Implant Research
Marculli worked with [[roger-leir]] on implant extractions from abductees, and was present during some procedures:

- Described to Alberino how some implants were **evasive** — they move within the body, migrate away from surgical instruments, and are not rejected by the host
- Consistent with Alberino's and other researchers' observations about implant properties
