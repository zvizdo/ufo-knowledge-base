# George Hunt Williamson

A pseudonym. Real name: **Michel Drenovic** (or Drajenovic), described by [[jason-reza-jorjani]] as being from Serbian royalty. One of the key handlers/editors of [[george-adamski]]'s contactee publications.

## Fascist Background

Before becoming a contactee figure, Williamson was editor/contributor to ***Valor*** magazine — the publication of **William Dudley Pelley**'s **American Silver Shirts**, American fascists who openly supported an alliance with Nazi Germany in the 1930s–40s. Pelley was imprisoned for collaboration with the enemy. While in prison Pelley wrote *Star Guests* and founded a religion called **Soulcraft** — which transmitted the identical Nordic master-race narrative as Van Tassel and Adamski.

## Contactee Role

Williamson helped Adamski shape and publish his contact narratives. Both transmitted the same doctrine: Nordic beings from space seeded humanity; lower races are hybrid products of interbreeding with hominids; the master race is pure-lineage space human.

## Significance

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] argues that the fictional name "George Hunt Williamson" (with the first name "George" — shared by Van Tassel and Adamski) and the direct lineage from American Silver Shirts to contactee publishing makes the [[contactee-nordic-fascist-nexus]] explicit. This is not coincidental convergence but a deliberate network transmitting an ideology through the cover of alien contact narrative.

Jorjani notes this connection is a "clear taboo" in UFO research — mainstream researchers avoid it because of its implications for the field's respectability.
