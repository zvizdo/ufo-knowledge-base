# Jim Weiner

Artist and one of the four witnesses in the [[1976-allagash-abduction]]. Identical twin brother of [[jack-weiner]], who was also abducted and developed bilateral shin tumors in anatomically identical locations to Jim's bald patches. Lives in Massachusetts with wife Carol. Studied print making and photography on the GI Bill in Brooklyn in 1976. Primary speaker in [[QkuDvTFN7ms]].

## Pre-Allagash History

**Childhood anomalous experiences (Allentown, Pennsylvania):**
- Jim and Jack repeatedly experienced anomalous events in their shared bedroom: covers pulled off beds, balls of light, figures moving around the room at night
- Both heard a deep voice calling their names at night; Jim assumed it was his father; the family named the entity "Harry the ghost"
- Their mother disclosed to [[raymond-fowler]] — never to her sons — that she had seen a hooded being in her bedroom; Jim did not learn of this until after Fowler's investigation

**1973 Mansfield, Pennsylvania sighting (pre-Allagash):**
- While doing beaver-parachuting fieldwork at Mansfield State College, Jim and two friends observed a group of 6–8 star-like lights in a "little dipper" formation above the tree line, tumbling end-over-end like a slinky while slowly descending
- Jim blinked his flashlight at them; immediately, one light emitted a large amber flash-burst and expelled "a tube of light / blob of toothpaste kind of light"
- The entire formation reversed direction instantaneously and ascended, disappearing: "it just stopped and then reversed itself"
- The flashlight trigger pattern — craft responding to a deliberate light signal — later repeated at Allagash when Charlie Foltz's SOS signal drew the craft's beam

## The Allagash Encounter

On Allagash Lake, August 1976: Jim examined a large luminous sphere through binoculars; as soon as it came into focus it appeared to teleport behind the canoe. He told the others: "Oh, Christ. We are not going to make it back. Keep going. Don't look back." He had no conscious memory of the next ~3 hours.

**On-craft account (recovered under hypnotic regression):**
- Perceived the abduction beam as a hollow tube: particulate light on the outer sheath, dark interior with something moving inside
- Saw Jack, Jim (himself), Charlie, and Chuck seated passively on a bench; Jack was examined first
- Described the beings as insectoid: "They're bugs. They look like bugs." — face resembling an ant head; could not determine if actual face or mask; four opposable digits on each hand
- Received instantaneous telepathic communication when he looked into a being's eyes — "like two magnets grabbing one another instantly": "I wasn't in any danger. Just comply and everything will be all right."
- Beings demonstrated thought-reading: as soon as Jim contemplated grabbing a being's neck to escape, all nearby beings "moved away so fast it was like a blur"
- Placed on a table attended by six beings; a being produced two flat oval devices ("like a flattened hockey puck, but oval") placed on the outside of each shin — immediately unable to move his legs at all
- Sperm sample collected via a receptacle device (tubular with round bottom and narrower neck/flange); had to request permission to sit up
- Underwent a table examination; describes it as "step one, step two, step three, step four... almost like automated assembly line"; "We were like the chimps and they were like Jane Goodall"
- Had to be repeatedly brought out of hypnosis by [[raymond-fowler]] to prevent seizure from panic

**Physical aftereffects:**
- Hair ceased to grow on both shins at the exact oval-device contact points; bald patches persisted for "several years"
- Discovered a shallow circular punch-biopsy scar near his scrotum, inside his leg — no known prior origin

## Catalyst for the Investigation

Jim developed temporal limbic epilepsy following an injury and was admitted to Beth Israel Hospital for a sleep deprivation EEG study. A staff member left him a tray of books; one was Whitley Strieber's Communion. Jim had an extreme involuntary negative reaction to the cover image — he "flipped out," made a scene, and refused to look at it without knowing why. Dr. Jonathan Silverman (Beth Israel / Harvard Medical School) asked if he had ever seen a UFO and referred him to [[raymond-fowler]]. At a Marriott hotel conference, Jim told Fowler the story; Fowler was uninterested until Jim mentioned his identical twin brother was one of the witnesses — at which point Fowler asked him to start over.

## Post-Investigation

Jim attended [[john-mack]]'s Harvard-based support groups, where he aligned with the "nuts and bolts" camp (seeking physical evidence rather than the consciousness/multi-dimensional interpretation favored by the other camp). He characterizes Mack himself as leaning toward the consciousness camp.

He and Jack began experiencing simultaneous recurring nightmares approximately 12 years after the event while living in different states — identical clinical-setting scenarios with unknown beings — which ultimately motivated the formal investigation.
