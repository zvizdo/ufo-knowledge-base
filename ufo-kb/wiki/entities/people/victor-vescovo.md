# Victor Vescovo

Private equity investor and record-setting ocean explorer. Former intelligence officer in the NAILO reserve. Vescovo spent approximately $50 million designing and building his own manned submersible and hiring a ship to complete the **Five Deeps expedition** — the first human dives to the deepest point in each of the five world oceans. He dove Challenger Deep (the deepest point on Earth, ~35,000 ft / ~10,700 m in the Mariana Trench) fourteen times, surpassing any prior record. He also completed the Explorers Club Grand Slam: all seven continental summits (including Everest) plus skiing to both poles.

## Seafloor UAP Survey Partnership with Gallaudet

[[tim-gallaudet]] contacted Vescovo while Vescovo was planning a sonar shakedown cruise for a new state-of-the-art multibeam sonar system. Gallaudet shared:
- Coordinates for an unexplained feature on the Southern California seafloor that Gallaudet suspects is related to USO-seabed interaction
- Coordinates for the seafloor areas near the [[2004-nimitz-tic-tac]] UAP encounter
- Coordinates for the seafloor near the [[2019-uss-omaha-uso]] encounter area

Vescovo replied with "Send me the coordinates" and surveyed all the specified areas. His submersible was not ready for a visual dive, but the multibeam sonar data was collected and is being used by Gallaudet as a basis for planning an ROV expedition. Gallaudet notes that Vescovo's immediate, un-skeptical reaction is itself a signal of the topic's credibility among serious explorers.
