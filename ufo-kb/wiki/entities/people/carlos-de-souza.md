# Carlos de Souza

Brazilian ultralight pilot and geography teacher from São Paulo. Key witness to the [[1996-varginha]] UFO crash — the first person on the scene, the only civilian to handle physical debris, and the only known witness to receive a detailed Men in Black intimidation encounter with specific personal knowledge.

## Background

De Souza had no prior interest in UFOs and describes himself as highly skeptical. He first attributed the object he saw to a government experiment or aircraft accident. He spent ~8–9 months without telling anyone about the experience; only came forward to researcher Claudia Kovu when the Varginha creature sightings appeared in Brazilian media and he recognized the connection. He subsequently spent decades being ridiculed by family and media. He did not profit from any testimony and refused payment in all instances.

His consistency is notable: his 2021–2022 account to Marco Leal matched his account from ~1996 in every detail, which Leal confirmed.

## The Crash (January 1996)

Driving toward Varginha before 5:30 AM for a ultralight pilot graduation barbecue, de Souza observed a cylindrical metallic object trailing smoke — he assumed it was a blimp in trouble. He followed in his small pickup truck. The craft attempted to gain altitude twice before crashing in an open field: the rear section was destroyed with scattered debris, the front section slid ~100–150 m into a tree.

Smell at site: strong ammonia + rotten eggs — not aviation fuel. The craft emitted no lights — only reflected sunlight off its polished metallic exterior.

De Souza picked up a piece of curved debris. Weight: effectively weightless, like kitchen aluminum foil. When he crumpled it in his hand, it immediately returned to its original shape. See [[memory-metal]] — this property matches descriptions from Roswell (1947) and ARTS/Parts material. He was still holding it when a soldier arrived, threatened him at rifle-point ("I'm not asking. I'm giving you an order. If you don't leave, I'm going to blow your brains out"), and knocked the fragment from his hand.

The craft was approximately the size of a school bus/mini bus. Interior was visible only as a shadow/silhouette (burning eyes from the ammonia smell prevented close inspection). Exterior: polished metallic, blue-ish interior.

The road he took led directly to EZA (Escola de Sargentos das Armas — Brazilian Army Sergeant School), which likely explains the convoy's arrival within ~2 minutes.

## Men in Black Encounter

~10–15 minutes after leaving the crash site, two tall, muscular men in black suits arrived at a roadside café in a car with no license plates. One identified de Souza by name, recited his wife's name, home address, and business name. He warned: "You didn't see anything. You don't know anything. If you open your mouth to anyone, you're going to have serious problems for the rest of your life."

De Souza has asked himself "how did they get that information so quickly?" for 30 years. His tentative hypothesis: they tracked his vehicle license plate via radio — but he acknowledges this is speculation. See [[men-in-black]].

Subsequent surveillance: phone tapped (even after changing number), business subjected to extended tax audits lasting days, unmarked cars surveilling his company.

## Connections to the Varginha Case

Eight months later, when the Varginha creature sightings appeared in media, UFO researcher Claudia Kovu asked the public for crash information. De Souza called him. Kovu visited the crash site — the tree was still burnt, and vegetation inside an elongated circle remained absent 8–9 months later. Soil samples showed unknown chemical compounds. Kovu filmed everything on Super 8.

The specific road where de Souza followed the craft runs directly to EZA — a connection confirmed by the UFO research community that de Souza himself was not aware of when he first reported.

"Military X" — an anonymous military source interviewed in disguise in Moment of Contact — said "everything that happened in Varginha started at my farm," and de Souza's crash site is believed to be on or near that farm.

## Hypnotic Regression

Underwent regression with "Saga," a Brazilian hypnotherapist. The recording (which de Souza watched but does not consciously remember) shows:
- De Souza attempting to approach the being; becoming very agitated
- The being communicating mentally: "Calm down. Everything is fine. Go away. It's dangerous for you here. We're fine. Thank you for your concern."
- The being stating it would ensure de Souza would not remember this contact
- De Souza's heart rate visible as extreme — the hypnotherapist ended the session out of concern

De Souza explicitly frames this as speculative and more tentatively held than his waking-state testimony.

## Physical Evidence
- Crash site anomalous vegetation circle and burnt tree remained undisturbed for 26 years
- Soil samples from Claudia Kovu's collection showed unknown chemical compounds
- In 2022–2023, the crash site was bulldozed (7–8 meters of earth removed) shortly after Fox's documentary aired, at economically irrational expense for "soybean planting"
