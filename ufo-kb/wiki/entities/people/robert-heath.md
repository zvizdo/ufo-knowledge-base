# Robert Heath

**Robert Heath** was a Tulane University psychiatrist who received CIA and military funding to conduct brain electrode implant experiments from the 1950s through the 1970s. His experiments — including an attempt to "cure homosexuality" via electrode stimulation combined with pornography and a prostitute — were published in mainstream medical journals, illustrating how MK-Ultra-era research was laundered through legitimate academic channels.

Heath is cited in discussions of [[mk-ultra]] as evidence that the program extended deep into academic medicine and that its methods were not hidden from the scientific community but normalized within it.
