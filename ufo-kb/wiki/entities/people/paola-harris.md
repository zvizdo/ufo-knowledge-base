# Paola Harris

Italian-American investigative journalist and author specializing in UFO/UAP research. Co-authored *Trinity: The Best Kept Secret* with [[jacques-vallee]], documenting the alleged 1945 San Antonio, New Mexico crash — one of the earlier claimed US crash retrievals predating Roswell.

## Trinity: The Best Kept Secret (fyX8V1XXmQM)

Harris co-authored *[[trinity-best-kept-secret]]* with [[jacques-vallee]] after independently discovering the case through Italian UFO research networks. She had already interviewed both witnesses — [[remy-baca]] and [[jose-padilla]] — and possessed audio recordings before Vallée joined the project. In [[fyX8V1XXmQM]], Vallée credits her as having done the foundational fieldwork.

Key context Vallée provides about Harris's investigative methodology: she located the case through contacts in the Italian UFO research community, traveled to New Mexico, conducted recorded interviews with the two witnesses (by then elderly men), and brought the case to Vallée's attention because she felt it required his analytical framework and scientific credentials. The collaboration began from this starting point, with Harris providing the primary source record and Vallée providing the analytical structure.

A central unresolved question Vallée and Harris share: *would Oppenheimer have been told?* If the Manhattan Project had custody of the crash materials, the classification chain would run from Oppenheimer's group to the Atomic Energy Commission to the Department of Energy — a parallel clearance universe invisible to standard Pentagon SAP infrastructure.

## Role in CE-5 / Sedona

Harris attended a CE-5 event in Sedona, Arizona organized by [[steven-greer]], the same event at which [[michael-herrera]] was physically struck by an unknown force while orbs were reportedly present, and at which Demi Lovato's group was in attendance. Harris's presence as an author/researcher at this event adds context to the range of figures participating in Greer's contact protocols.
