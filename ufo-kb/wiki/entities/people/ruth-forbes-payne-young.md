# Ruth Forbes Payne Young

Ruth Forbes Payne Young was a Forbes family heiress and wife of [[arthur-young]] — the Bell helicopter co-inventor who participated in the 1952 Nine seance organized by [[andrija-puharich]]. Her significance in the JFK network analysis is her close friendship with [[mary-douglas]], who was Allen Dulles's mistress.

## Network Position

This connection places her as a social bridge between:
- The blue-blooded American occult-intelligence network (via the Nine seance and Arthur Young's esoteric circle)
- The CIA directorship (via Allen Dulles, through his mistress Mary Douglas)

Her daughter-in-law [[ruth-payne]] housed Lee Harvey Oswald, and Dulles subsequently cut off Ruth Payne's Warren Commission testimony about visiting Arthur Young's household.
