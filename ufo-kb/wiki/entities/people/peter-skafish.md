# Peter Skafish

Cultural anthropologist and co-founder of the [[sol-foundation]] alongside [[david-grusch]] and [[garry-nolan]]. Grusch describes Skafish's role as providing the humanistic and anthropological framework for understanding NHI contact — the dimension that STEM-only approaches to UAP typically neglect. The Sol Foundation's Truth and Reconciliation framework for UAP disclosure is partly shaped by Skafish's anthropological perspective on how human societies process paradigm-disrupting contact events.

## Background and Pasulka Connection

Peter Skafish is an anthropologist and philosopher; PhD in anthropology from UC Berkeley (2009); affiliated with the Centre national de la recherche scientifique (CNRS) in France. His academic work focuses on speculative metaphysics, ontology, and the anthropology of contemporary religious/spiritual movements.

In the UAP-research context, Skafish appears in Diana Pasulka's [[american-cosmic]] (2019) as one of the contemporary scholars working at the intersection of religious studies, philosophy, and UAP/contact phenomenology. His role in Pasulka's narrative is as an interlocutor and theoretical contributor rather than a primary subject; his contribution is the philosophical framework for treating "the experiencer" as a category worth taking seriously in anthropological terms.
