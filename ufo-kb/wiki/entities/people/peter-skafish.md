# Peter Skafish

Cultural anthropologist and co-founder of the [[sol-foundation]] alongside [[david-grusch]] and [[garry-nolan]]. Grusch describes Skafish's role as providing the humanistic and anthropological framework for understanding NHI contact — the dimension that STEM-only approaches to UAP typically neglect. The Sol Foundation's Truth and Reconciliation framework for UAP disclosure is partly shaped by Skafish's anthropological perspective on how human societies process paradigm-disrupting contact events.
