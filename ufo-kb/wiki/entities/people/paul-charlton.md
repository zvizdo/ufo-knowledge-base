# Paul Charlton

Officer stationed at [[norton-air-force-base]] (California) who processed — in the photographic/film development sense — footage of the [[1964-holloman-afb-landing]]. He showed this footage to filmmaker [[allan-sandler]] after a Colonel Coleman (Pentagon public relations, 1970s) called the base and said to cooperate with the UAP documentary team without realizing Charlton had just processed the sensitive film.

## Sequence of Events

1. Colonel Coleman calls Norton AFB: cooperate with the UFO documentary team ([[bob-emenegger]] / [[allan-sandler]])
2. Charlton, who had just processed the Holloman footage, takes Sandler into his facility and shows it to him
3. Either later that day or the following morning, men in suits arrive at Norton AFB
4. They confiscate the footage and instruct Charlton: "Don't ever talk about it"
5. Charlton asked the men who they work for; they did not answer

## Death

Charlton died in a head-on collision with his wife. [[james-fox]] notes this and is "conspiratorial at all about" the collision, answering: "It's a bummer, definitely a bummer." He had, before his death, appeared on a television show with [[bob-emenegger]] to discuss the incident.
