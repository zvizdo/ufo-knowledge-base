# Brett Stewart

Professional remote viewer who makes a living solely through RV work — a fact [[chris-ramsey]] found immediately significant as evidence of functional utility: if remote viewing is entirely illusory, it should not support a professional living. Stewart was retained by a hedge fund to train promising new remote viewers in **Associative Remote Viewing (ARV)** for stock market prediction.

## ARV Training Program

The fund's recruitment process: reached out to ~600 candidates across several communities, selected based on initial screening; Stewart was the training head for the cohort. He trained [[nelson-dellis]] one hour per day for one month before Dellis began live ARV sessions. The protocol deliberately keeps the viewer ignorant of what outcome they are predicting — they remote view a target image, not the market — to remove all possible feedback loops.

Ramsay notes that Stewart himself was trained under a similar government-adjacent program, suggesting a direct lineage from the [[stargate-program]].
