# J. Allen Hynek

Northwestern University astronomer who served as scientific consultant to [[project-blue-book]]. Developed the Hynek classification scale for UFO encounters (CE1–CE5), the foundational taxonomy for UFO phenomenology. Later became a genuine believer and founded the Center for UFO Studies (CUFOS).

When [[jacques-vallee]] found the [[pentacle-memo]] in Blue Book files and confronted Hynek asking if he knew about it and was complicit in concealing the real parallel investigation, Hynek reportedly "played dumb."

## Alleged Role in the Benowitz Affair

[[bill-moore]] told [[greg-bishop]] that Hynek showed up at [[paul-benowitz]]'s door with a pre-built computer — apparently containing fabricated alien communications and programmed video footage — that became central to the AFOSI disinformation operation against Benowitz. At the time (early 1980s), Hynek was reportedly still on the Air Force payroll.

Bishop gives Hynek benefit of the doubt on the question of knowing what the computer contained: "if he knew that's what it was, I don't think he'd have done it" — citing Hynek's apparent conscience in other documented cases. However, Bishop acknowledges this is "just a guess" and notes several other instances where Hynek could have been complicit but chose to play ignorant. (Greg Bishop, via [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])

This allegation remains unverified beyond Moore's testimony to Bishop. It is filed here as contested — Moore-to-Bishop hearsay — not as established fact.

## Operation Moonwatch (lbGE3EC6StE)

[[beatriz-villarroel]] reveals that Hynek helped [[fred-whipple]] (Menzel's PhD student) run [[operation-moonwatch]] — a 12-station citizen science network tracking satellites worldwide. This places Hynek's early career within [[donald-menzel]]'s academic orbit before Blue Book. The Moonwatch dataset contains at least 36 UFO reports from participating amateur astronomers.

## The Hynek/Sagan Split (lbGE3EC6StE)

Jesse Michaels notes a striking divergence: Carl Sagan and Hynek operated in the same era and both had access to the same UFO research landscape. Sagan publicly pivoted to skepticism around the same time Hynek "says you know actually I played a part in the cover up" and went in the opposite direction — becoming a genuine believer and founding CUFOS. The inversion is symmetrical and unexplained.
