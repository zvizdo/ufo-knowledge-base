# Elaine Douglas

UFO researcher active in the American UFO research community during the 1980s and 1990s. Douglas was a close personal friend of [[john-ford]] and a prominent voice asserting both that the [[1989-long-island-crash]] retrieval was a genuine event and that Ford's subsequent criminal prosecution was a politically motivated frame-up intended to silence him.

## Background

Active in the UFO research community during the decade when Long Island became a hotspot for both reported sightings and alleged crash-retrieval activity. Douglas's advocacy for Ford continued after his incarceration and became a touchstone case in discussions of witness intimidation and suppression within the UFO research community.

## Notable Claims / Role

Douglas maintained that Ford's arrest on an unrelated criminal charge shortly after he publicly pushed the Long Island crash story was not coincidental. Her sustained advocacy helped keep the Ford case in the awareness of the broader UFO research community and is cited in discussions of the social and personal costs borne by UFO researchers who challenge official narratives.
