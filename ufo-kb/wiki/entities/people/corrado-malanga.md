# Corrado Malanga

Italian researcher who heads the research group behind the [[beyond-protocol]] Giza scanning project. The team's division of labor: Malanga leads the research group, [[armando-mei]] provides theoretical/numerological analysis, and [[filippo-biondi]] is the radar technician who developed the SAR Doppler tomography method.

## Background

Per Biondi in [[zrmG2jBckgk]], the Giza project began in 2019 when Malanga proposed scanning the Khufu (Cheops) pyramid to see if the SAR Doppler tomography technique could reveal interior and subterranean structures. The collaboration then extended to the Khafre pyramid, which yielded the major discovery of eight cylindrical underground tubular structures.

## Role

- **Conceptual leadership** of the research program
- Motivates the project direction toward ancient sites
- Regular collaborator with Biondi and Mei; the three discuss their findings daily per Biondi's account
