# Peter Sturrock

Stanford University plasma physicist and solar physicist. Beyond his mainstream physics career, Sturrock conducted serious scientific UAP research and published an isotope analysis of the [[1957-ubatuba-orb|Brazilian magnesium fragments]]:

> "I was on his astrophysics staff for a couple of years looking at galaxies, the structure of the Sun, certain types of strange stars with special emissions. We also looked at UFO materials — especially a case from Brazil which he published. He got financial support to do the isotope analysis."

When Sturrock retired, he donated all his UAP material samples to [[jacques-vallee]], who subsequently passed them to [[garry-nolan]] at Stanford for NanoSIMS analysis. This created a continuous Stanford lineage for UAP materials science.

Sturrock died at over 100, still working in astrophysics at the time of his death.

## Significance

Sturrock represents the bridge between the first wave of serious academic UAP research (1970s–90s) and the current Stanford materials-science effort under Nolan. His willingness to publish isotope analysis of UAP materials under his own name at Stanford gave institutional credibility to the inquiry at a time when that was professionally hazardous.
