# Aubrey Marcus

Entrepreneur, podcaster (Aubrey Marcus Podcast), and psychedelic explorer with extensive experience of both exogenous DMT and darkness retreats. [[andrew-gallimore]] appeared on Marcus's show and they discussed darkness retreats as a potential endogenous DMT-triggering mechanism.

Marcus is the key firsthand witness cited by Gallimore for the darkness-DMT connection: after 4–5 days in a total darkness retreat, Marcus — who is "very experienced with DMT" — said: "This wasn't like DMT. This was a DMT." He explicitly equated the experience with a full psychedelic breakthrough, suggesting the extended darkness triggered endogenous DMT release at breakthrough levels.

This observation is now being tested: [[new-nautics]] and Gallimore are funding a study at University of Florida that will send subjects into darkness retreats and take blood/urine/saliva samples at intervals to test for DMT spikes.
