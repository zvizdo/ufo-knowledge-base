# Ty Roberts

UFO researcher and content creator; affiliated with the [[international-ufo-bureau]] (based in Oklahoma City) and the Total Disclosure media outlet. Roberts works in the independent UAP research and journalism space, producing content on UAP witness accounts and disclosure developments.

**Greg Rogers coverage**: Roberts was involved in publishing and amplifying [[greg-rogers]]'s account of the [[1992-cape-canaveral-saucer]] incident. Rogers — a retired US Air Force flight surgeon — had witnessed a metallic saucer-shaped craft at Cape Canaveral in 1992 and maintained silence for over 30 years before coming forward in 2024. [[josh-boswell]] of the UK Daily Mail broke the story on May 4, 2024; Roberts and [[chris-leto]] were among the independent-media journalists who followed with additional interviews and coverage, amplifying the story through the UAP research community.

**Total Disclosure**: Total Disclosure is a UAP-focused media outlet; Roberts's affiliation places him in the independent-research-media tier of the disclosure ecosystem. This tier amplifies stories from mainstream media breaks (like the Daily Mail's Rogers article) and conducts its own interviews with witnesses who are not yet in the major-media pipeline.

**International UFO Bureau connection**: Roberts is associated with the [[international-ufo-bureau]], the Oklahoma City-based organization on whose board Greg Rogers serves. This institutional connection means Roberts is part of the organizational network that specifically supports witnesses like Rogers in going public — a witness-preparation and media-coordination function that the independent disclosure media ecosystem provides.
