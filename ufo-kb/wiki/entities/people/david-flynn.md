# David Flynn (deceased)

Late independent researcher and author best known for his book ***Sidonia***, which [[tim-alberino]] describes as "the most consequential book I have ever read in my entire life." Flynn developed a comprehensive thesis — combining biblical analysis, ancient mythology, and astronomical data — arguing that Mars was once inhabited by an ancient advanced civilisation, that the gods of the ancients descended from Mars (and from a destroyed planet called Rahab), and that mystery schools from the Phoenicians forward have had the recovery of this Martian knowledge as a primary objective.

Flynn was closely associated with [[richard-hoagland]] and [[michael-heiser]]; the three gave joint conferences in the 1990s–2000s on the Mars/Sidonia civilisation thesis.

## Key Arguments in *Sidonia*

- The Sidonia region of Mars contains artificial structures (the Face, the D&M Pyramid) that are remnants of an advanced pre-Adamic civilisation
- Ancient peoples across cultures identified Mars as the origin world of their gods
- A planet called **Rahab** (named cryptically in Psalms, Job, and Isaiah) was destroyed in a pre-Adamic cosmic war; its explosion devastated Mars and formed the asteroid belt
- Mystery schools from antiquity to the present preserve and transmit the goal of returning to Mars to recover lost knowledge
- This is one of the primary hidden objectives of the Freemasonic tradition and related esoteric lineages

## Legacy

*Sidonia* was long out of print and sold for $2,000–$3,000 second-hand online. Flynn's daughter Ty reprinted it (now ~$25 on Amazon). Alberino, who is friends with Flynn's twin brother Mark and his daughter Ty, describes *Sidonia* as the intellectual substrate for his own book *Birthright*.
