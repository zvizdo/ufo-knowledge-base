# Steven Schiff

Republican US Congressman for New Mexico. After being briefed by New Mexico state representative [[jay-andrew-kissner]] on his 1994–1995 interviews with firsthand legacy-program personnel, Schiff launched a large GAO probe and congressional investigation into the 1947 Roswell incident.

The investigation found that the US Air Force had lost all relevant records related to Roswell. Schiff went on record stating that the destruction of records was "rampant" and "inappropriate."

Schiff died in 1998. His Roswell investigation is one of the few instances of a sitting US Congressman explicitly probing UAP crash-retrieval records through official government accountability mechanisms.
