# Garry Kasparov

13th World Chess Champion (1985–2000), widely considered the greatest chess player of all time. His rivalry with FIDE president [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]] was well-known; he founded breakaway Professional Chess Association (PCA) in 1993 over disputes with Ilyumzhinov's FIDE governance. In the UAP disclosure context, Kasparov represents the institutional backlash that Ilyumzhinov anticipated and experienced.

## Reaction to Ilyumzhinov's Alien Disclosure

According to Ilyumzhinov, when he went public with his 1997 alien encounter, Kasparov "immediately began to state: 'What kind of president of the World Chess Federation can Kersonen be? If he has met with aliens, he must be removed and replaced. How can he possibly lead the chess federation if he's mentally unstable?'"

This reaction was precisely what Ilyumzhinov had predicted when doing his political cost-benefit analysis — that going public would give opponents a weapon to call him unfit. Kasparov's response is archetypal of the institutional gatekeeping mechanism that keeps high-profile witnesses from disclosing: the credibility-destruction reflex attached to any UFO claim, regardless of other evidence of the claimant's competence.
