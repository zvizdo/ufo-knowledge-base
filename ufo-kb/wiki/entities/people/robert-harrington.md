# Robert Harrington

Chief astronomer for the U.S. Navy, working at the U.S. Naval Observatory. Harrington collaborated with [[thomas-van-flandern]] on analysis of Pioneer 10 and 11 probe data in the 1980s, identifying gravitational anomalies consistent with a dead stellar companion orbiting our sun.

Per [[matt-lroy]]'s account, [[thomas-van-flandern]] called Harrington after reviewing Pioneer 10 data. Both men pursued the binary companion hypothesis; their findings were published and subsequently discredited by Miles Standish. Harrington died of throat cancer — a non-smoker, as was Van Flandern. Lroy treats both deaths as suspicious given the timing and circumstances.

> ⚠ Note: The "Pioneer Anomaly" was conclusively resolved by ~2012 through thermal radiation pressure modeling; the binary star interpretation is not part of mainstream resolution.
