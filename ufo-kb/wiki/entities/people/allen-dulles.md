# Allen Dulles

## Background

Allen Welsh Dulles served as Director of Central Intelligence from 1953 to 1961, making him one of the longest-serving and most powerful CIA directors in the Agency's history. During World War II, he ran OSS operations out of Bern, Switzerland — operating deliberately beyond the reach of US law, a structural feature that would later inform how covert UAP programs were organized outside normal congressional oversight.

Dulles co-founded Area 51 with [[richard-bissell]] in 1955, establishing the facility at the Nevada Test Site that became the center of classified aerospace work. He was fired by President Kennedy after the failed Bay of Pigs invasion in 1961, along with Bissell. After his dismissal, Dulles based himself at Brown Brothers Harriman, described in the source as the firm that "formed the CIA" and which also became Bissell's post-CIA base — suggesting a continuity of the network outside official government.

His brother John Foster Dulles served as Secretary of State under Eisenhower, giving the two brothers combined control over both overt foreign policy and covert intelligence operations — an unprecedented concentration of national security power.

## Notable Claims

### OSS Project McGregor and the 1933 Magenta Craft

Allen Dulles, together with John Foster Dulles, is described in the source as having co-arranged the transfer of the craft recovered in the 1933 Magenta, Italy crash through OSS Project McGregor. This would place the Dulles brothers at the center of the earliest known US government acquisition of non-human technology — predating both Roswell and the formal establishment of the CIA itself. The transfer reportedly moved the craft from Italian/Mussolini control to US custody via OSS wartime infrastructure.

[[richard-bissell]] later named the Magenta crash to [[harold-malmgren]] — independently confirming within the CIA's inner circle that the 1933 event was considered foundational. This connects to [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] as a historically deep structure with pre-CIA roots.

### Paul Mellon and the Czech Recovery Site

Dulles and philanthropist Paul Mellon are reported to have physically stood on top of a recovered disc at a site in Czechoslovakia associated with Hans Kammler's Nazi advanced technology program. Kammler oversaw the most classified German aerospace and weapons projects at the end of World War II; his program's overlap with UAP-related technology is a recurring thread in [[reverse-engineered-craft]] accounts. The detail that Dulles was personally present at such a site reinforces his direct knowledge of recovered craft.

### Institutional Network: Brown Brothers Harriman and the CIA's Origins

The source describes Brown Brothers Harriman as the institution that "formed the CIA" — and both Dulles and Bissell ended up there after JFK fired them. This creates a picture of the [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] as having a private-sector institutional backbone that persists independent of any presidential administration's decisions. Dulles's firing by JFK connects directly to [[jfk-assassination-ufo]]: removing him from power did not remove him from the network.

### James Jesus Angleton and the Knights of Malta

Dulles is described as having been closely tied to [[james-angleton]], CIA Chief of Counterintelligence and a Knight of Malta. The Knights of Malta connection is treated in the source as a transnational custodian layer within the UAP secrecy structure, with Vatican-adjacent diplomatic and institutional reach. Dulles as Angleton's patron represents the intergenerational transmission of this network.

## The JFK Assassination — Sheehan's S Force Account

[[danny-sheehan]]'s account in [[2SQXAPCdmPE]] gives Dulles an even more active role in the JFK assassination than the UAP-motive thesis alone:

- Dulles discovered Kennedy's **18 secret letters to Khrushchev** arranging mutual nuclear disarmament — which would have dismantled the entire covert apparatus Dulles had built.
- Dulles then **activated the S Force** — the 15-man assassination team created by [[richard-nixon]] in 1960 to kill Castro — against Kennedy.
- Dulles was subsequently **appointed to the Warren Commission**, creating a direct structural conflict of interest: the man who ordered the assassination was also one of the chief investigators.
- This extends the existing Malmgren/Hunt UAP-motive account with an operational explanation for how Dulles moved from motivation to execution.

See [[1963-jfk-assassination]] for the full account.
