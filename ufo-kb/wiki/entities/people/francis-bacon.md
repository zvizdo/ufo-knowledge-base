# Francis Bacon

Sir Francis Bacon (1561–1626) was an English philosopher, statesman, scientist, and author who served as Attorney General and Lord Chancellor of England under James I. He is widely credited as a founding figure of the scientific method and empirical philosophy. In esoteric and alternative history contexts, Bacon is a recurring figure for a different reason: he is alleged to have been the true author — or a significant contributor — to the works attributed to William Shakespeare, and to have used that authorship as a vehicle for encoding esoteric and hidden knowledge accessible only to initiates.

## Background

Bacon was a member of the Elizabethan and Jacobean court and moved in circles that included figures associated with Rosicrucian and Hermetic traditions. His utopian work *New Atlantis* (1627) describes a society governed by scientific adepts operating in secrecy — a text that has made him a touchstone figure for researchers tracing the lineage of secret knowledge societies into modernity.
