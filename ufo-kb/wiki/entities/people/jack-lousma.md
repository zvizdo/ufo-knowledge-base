# Jack Lousma

NASA pilot-astronaut; pilot on Skylab 3 (July–September 1973) and commander of Space Shuttle STS-3 (1982).

## UAP Account

Lousma was the first to notice the rotating reddish object from the Skylab 3 wardroom window, approximately one week before splashdown. From the official SL-3 crew debrief (October 4, 1973): "It never did take the shape of an object but it was always brighter than any other star or planet in the night sky. It was much brighter." He also conducted T002 experiments and observed other satellites appearing as ordinary star-points — explicitly distinguishing those from the anomalous object.
