# Alex Jones

Host of *Infowars*, a media platform that has regularly reached millions of Americans despite being banned from most mainstream platforms. Known for high-intensity conspiratorial commentary, Jones frames himself as a populist anti-elitist warning about entrenched corrupt elites who exploit and wage war on the general population — a position he characterizes as non-partisan ("I don't see it as left or right").

## Background

Jones grew up with an unusual family background: his father had connections to the John Birch Society, his mother was interested in psychedelic research in San Francisco countercultural circles, and an uncle conducted clandestine activities in South America. He was an early reader of history — Roman history, WWI, WWII — from age 6–7. He began on Access TV in Austin ~1995, then transitioned to radio.

He has a long friendship with [[joe-rogan]] of ~23–24 years. Rogan is described by Jones as "the smartest person I know."

His first major public exposure in film was via Richard Linklater's *Waking Life* (2001), where he delivers a speech on human liberty and populist anti-corporatism.

## Bohemian Grove Infiltration (2000) — Firsthand

Jones successfully snuck into the [[bohemian-grove]] retreat in Northern California in 2000 and witnessed the **Cremation of Care** — a midsummer pagan ritual performed by approximately 1,000 men (business elite, policy decision makers) around a pond/lake.

He describes it as: "a composite pagan ritual... a fusion of Moloch worship — the bull out of the Middle East, Tyre and places — and Faustian occultism and Druidic occultism." He describes the men as taking it "extremely seriously" and characterizes their expressions as "satanic."

This firsthand account was captured on video and is associated with the documentary *Alex's War*.

## Bilderberg Group Infiltration

Jones has infiltrated [[bilderberg-group]] meetings on multiple occasions and been removed at gunpoint. He characterizes the Bilderberg steering committee as effectively "James Bond villains" and describes [[world-economic-forum]] (Klaus Schwab) as the public mouthpiece arm of Bilderberg, going operational in the 1990s.

## 9/11 Prediction (July 2001)

Jones claims to have predicted, on-air in July 2001, that the US government would orchestrate an attack on the World Trade Center and blame it on Osama bin Laden. He attributes the prediction partly to recurring intense dreams ("I would wake up sweating and usually throw up and it's like you've gone to the future"). A clip of this is referenced in *Alex's War*.

## Disinfo and QAnon

Jones describes being repeatedly "set up" with planted disinfo — third parties would circulate claims (e.g., Pizzagate, derived from New York Times / Washington Post coverage of 4chan), Jones would question them, and media would then attribute the original claims to Jones. When QAnon emerged, Jones explicitly called it "BS" and refused to endorse it, identifying it as a disinfo operation designed to co-opt and discredit the populist movement. Many of his listeners were angry at him for this. See [[flood-the-zone-disinfo]].

## Consciousness and Parapsychology

Jones endorses the [[transmission-theory-consciousness]]: "electrochemically the DNA is doing that now" — the nervous system does not generate consciousness but receives/transmits a pre-existing consciousness field. He references [[william-james]] and [[aldous-huxley]] explicitly. He also invokes the quantum observer effect (double-slit experiment) and [[roger-penrose]]'s Orchestrated Objective Reduction as evidence that human consciousness has a "disproportionate impact on material reality."

## Scapegoat Framing

[[jesse-michaels]] invokes [[rene-girard]]'s scapegoat theory to frame Jones's media role. Jones accepts this: he was "used as the archetype" to demonize the populist anti-establishment movement, functioning as a pressure-valve scapegoat so authorities could justify broad censorship of free speech. Jones observes this backfired, making him larger.
