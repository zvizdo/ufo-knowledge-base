# Amy Escridge

Alabama-based researcher who was publicly working on anti-gravity technology. Died in 2022; the official ruling was suicide by self-inflicted gunshot wound. Her close contacts dispute this, citing her explicit and repeated warnings that she believed her life was in danger.

## Death and Disputed Circumstances

A friend of Escridge who spoke to her approximately four hours before her death stated she repeatedly said: *"I'm not going to commit suicide. I am not going to have an accident. If there's something suspicious about my death, it's because it is."*

The official finding — self-inflicted gunshot — stands unresolved against this testimony.

Her case is the final entry in this episode's survey of scientists who died or disappeared in proximity to sensitive research.
