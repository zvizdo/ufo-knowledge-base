# Jack Valenti

Special assistant to President Lyndon B. Johnson; later president of the Motion Picture Association of America (MPAA).

## Air Force One — November 22, 1963

[[danny-sheehan]] reports that Valenti was with LBJ on Air Force One in the aftermath of JFK's assassination. Despite LBJ having been briefed the night before at [[clint-murchison-jr]]'s Dallas ranch that the assassination was about to happen, Johnson locked himself in the plane's bathroom saying "they're going to kill us all" repeatedly. Valenti had to physically retrieve him from the bathroom.

Sheehan presents this as evidence that LBJ's on-camera distress on Air Force One was at least partly performance (since he had pre-knowledge), but notes that pre-knowledge of an assassination and genuine psychological shock in its aftermath are not mutually exclusive.

## Significance

Valenti is named as a direct witness to LBJ's Air Force One behavior, corroborating the "at least three witnesses" Sheehan cites for LBJ having been pre-briefed at Clint Murchison's ranch.
