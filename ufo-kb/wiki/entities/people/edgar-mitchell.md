# Edgar Mitchell

Apollo 14 lunar module pilot; sixth person to walk on the Moon. Known in UFO discourse for publicly stating his belief in ET visitation and for reporting that [[wernher-von-braun]] personally came to him and shared knowledge about UFO/ET matters. Salas reports that von Braun was also apparently involved in facilitating Mitchell's remote viewing/parapsychology experiments from space during Apollo 14.

## ESP Experiment on Apollo 14 (zjpvfDFc4fg)

On his journey to the Moon, Mitchell conducted a **private ESP experiment** — attempting to send mental images back to Earth using Zener cards. Results scored by colleagues on Earth were better than chance. In a 1971 New York Times interview he said: *"We're too much uninformed about telepathy or ESP to project its uses. But I think once we understand the mechanism, then we can start talking about uses."*

[[wernher-von-braun]] was reportedly the one senior NASA figure who was **deeply supportive**, even encouraging Mitchell to find a site within NASA to study consciousness further. Von Braun attended one of Mitchell's early IONS fundraising dinners. Mitchell saw the overview effect — seeing Earth from space — as evidence that *"the cosmos might be conscious."*

## Institute of Noetic Sciences

Mitchell founded the **Institute of Noetic Sciences (IONS)** after his Apollo 14 mission, motivated by the profound consciousness shift he experienced during the flight. [[rupert-sheldrake]] is a fellow of IONS. In [[wT5-hXWIkzM]], Jesse and Sheldrake note that astronauts who have been to space "turn entirely inward" upon return — the subsequent work is consciousness-related, not hard science — and cite Mitchell's founding of IONS as the institutional expression of this pattern.
