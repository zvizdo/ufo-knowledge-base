# Mary Bancroft

American journalist and OSS intelligence operative who worked directly under [[allen-dulles]] in Bern, Switzerland during World War II, where she served as analyst, agent handler, and his romantic partner. She translated the memoirs of Hans Bernd Gisevius, a German anti-Hitler conspirator and Abwehr officer — work that gave her direct access to the German resistance network Dulles was cultivating.

In the KB's JFK cluster, Bancroft appears as a link in the social-network analysis of Dulles's circle: she vacationed on Nashon Island with [[ruth-payne]], Lee Harvey Oswald's landlady in Dallas — a connection that places a direct Dulles intimate in the immediate social environment of Oswald's family prior to the assassination. [[allen-dulles]] was fired by Kennedy after the Bay of Pigs, then served on the [[warren-commission]] that investigated Kennedy's murder.

Her connection to [[the-nine]] and [[andrija-puharich]] is indirect: both she and Puharich moved through the same mid-century intelligence-adjacent cultural networks in New York and Europe.
