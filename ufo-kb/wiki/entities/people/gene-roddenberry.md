# Gene Roddenberry

## Background

Gene Roddenberry (1921–1991) was the creator of the Star Trek franchise. According to tape recordings discovered in [[andrija-puharich]]'s archive by filmmaker [[greg-malazzi]], Roddenberry attended multiple channeling sessions hosted by Puharich in the mid-1970s. On the tape recordings Roddenberry makes reference to having participated "many times in the past," indicating this was a sustained involvement, not a one-time event.

## Channeling Sessions

The sessions involved Puharich hypnotically regressing channeler [[phyllis-schlemmer]], who would go into trance and channel [[the-nine]] — the same nine cosmic entities first contacted in 1952 through [[dr-venad]]. Roddenberry asked extended questions during a session lasting one to two hours.

## "The Nine" Script

Roddenberry was moved enough by his experiences to write a 130-page screenplay titled "The Nine," which changed the names and locations but drew directly from his experiences with Puharich and Schlemmer. Malazzi holds an original copy. The script never went into production — Roddenberry was reportedly struggling personally at the time, and the project fell apart.

## Star Trek: Deep Space Nine Connection

The connection between these channeling sessions and Star Trek: Deep Space Nine is explicit:
- The show's name references nine entities
- A character in Deep Space Nine is named "Venad" — directly mirroring the real 1952 channeler [[dr-venad]]

Malazzi states there is "no question" this connection is real.
