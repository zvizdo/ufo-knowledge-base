# William Dudley Pelley

American fascist organizer and founder of the **Silver Shirts** (officially: Silver Legion of America), an American Nazi movement active in the 1930s–40s. He publicly advocated for an alliance with Hitler rather than war against Germany. He was **arrested and imprisoned** during World War II for collaboration with the enemy.

## Soulcraft Religion

While in prison, Pelley wrote ***Star Guests*** and founded a religion called **Soulcraft**. Its doctrine was:
- The Nordic (white) race originated in space and came to Earth as colonists
- They found hominid life, interbred to produce "lower races"
- The pure-lineage Nordic space humans are the master race
- Spiritual advancement requires recovering the pre-hybridization purity

This is **the exact narrative** that [[george-van-tassel]], [[george-hunt-williamson]] (Pelley's employee on *Valor* magazine), [[george-adamski]], and [[billy-meier]] all transmitted as contactee "revelations" in the 1950s–70s.
