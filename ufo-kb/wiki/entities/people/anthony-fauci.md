# Anthony Fauci

American immunologist who directed the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID) from 1984 until his retirement in December 2022 — the longest tenure of any director of a U.S. federal medical research institute. He was the face of U.S. public health response to HIV/AIDS, SARS, H1N1, MERS, Ebola, and COVID-19.

In the KB, Fauci appears in the COVID-19 origins / gain-of-function research cluster. The NIH, under Fauci's directorship, funded EcoHealth Alliance through sub-grants that supported [[peter-daszak]]'s coronavirus research collaboration with the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology]]. Whether this funding constituted "gain-of-function research" — specifically, research that could enhance pathogen transmissibility — became a central dispute in Congressional hearings. Fauci's testimony that NIH did not fund gain-of-function research in China was later disputed by NIH's own acknowledgment in a letter to Representative James Comer.

In the KB's broader framing, the NIH/EcoHealth/Wuhan chain is treated as analogous to other programs in which controversial or dual-use research was conducted through nonprofit intermediaries to obscure direct government sponsorship — the same structural pattern identified in [[ffrdc-uap-architecture]] and other unacknowledged program architectures.
