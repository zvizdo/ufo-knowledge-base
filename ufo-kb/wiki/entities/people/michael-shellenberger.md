# Michael Shellenberger

Investigative journalist and author who publishes via the *Public* substack. Prior major work includes the **Twitter Files** (covering FBI/CIA information operations on social media, particularly the suppression of the Hunter Biden laptop story) and reporting on nuclear energy, climate policy, and the decline of San Francisco. His UAP work began after reading the David Grusch testimony and grew into the publication of the [[immaculate-constellation]] report — his most significant UAP contribution.

## Background

Shellenberger came to UAP journalism via his existing beat of government abuses of power — he frames UAP secrecy as a disclosure and accountability story, not a paranormal one. His first UAP piece was on the 1979 Varginha, Brazil case; he lived in Brazil and speaks Portuguese. He has since published approximately four to five UAP stories. He describes himself as "agnostic" on the essential nature of UAP phenomena: he reports what sources say while remaining non-committal on whether NHI are present.

He returned his Christian faith approximately six years before this interview. He frames this as relevant context for how he interprets the intersection of UAP and spirituality: he is neither dismissive of the paranormal angle nor uses it to override evidentiary standards.

## Notable Claims

### Immaculate Constellation Report
A whistleblower inside the Department of Defense provided Shellenberger with a detailed report describing a **USAP (unacknowledged special access program)** called Immaculate Constellation. The program functions as a strategic intelligence project integrating SIGINT, HUMINT, still imagery, and video into classified databases to catalog and classify UAPs. The report includes documentation of psychological and biological effects on UAP witnesses. After publication, a separate source told Shellenberger the program is "controlled by the White House but administered by the DoD" — a characterization he cites as single-source.

### Kuwait Orb Video (single source)
Shellenberger reported a **high-definition, full-color helicopter video** of an orb emerging from the ocean approximately 20 miles off the coast of Kuwait and being joined by a second orb. The helicopter platform would carry high-quality cameras, undermining the "methods protection" argument for non-release.

### Reasons for Secrecy
He explicitly rejects the "methods protection" rationale for UAP secrecy, arguing that advanced optics and helicopters are not secrets. His list of actual motivations: ego, legacy, psychology, politics, possible criminality, and ethics concerns.

### Circular Reporting Problem
Shellenberger describes methodological care to avoid **circular reporting** (a single claim being counted as two independent sources). He checked the Immaculate Constellation report against existing UAP lore to verify it didn't appear to be compiled from prior public material.

### Garrett Graff as Suspicious Actor
[[garrett-graff]] co-orchestrated a Hunter Biden laptop pre-bunking operation at the [[aspen-institute]] alongside [[vivian-schiller]]; he then published a 2024 UAP book ([[ufo-by-garrett-graff]]) that Shellenberger characterizes as a sophisticated debunking operation. The overlap Shellenberger flags: both operations managed information control around sensitive government-adjacent topics.

### John Greenwald Finding
He highlights [[john-greenwald]]'s FOIA work showing secrecy is *growing* not shrinking — a direct contradiction of the "we are on the path to disclosure" narrative. Greenwald also documented that the Pentagon justified UAP secrecy under a law enforcement provision in at least one FOIA response, suggesting a hidden law enforcement investigation.

## Methodology

- Always uses multiple sources; avoids two-source rule for fringe topics — uses more
- Distinguishes firsthand vs. hearsay in attribution
- Protects sources even under congressional cross-examination (resisted Congressman [[clay-higgins]] attempting to elicit source gender/agency)
- Remains agnostic on essential nature of phenomena; focuses on government conduct
