# Joe McMoneagle

US Army veteran designated **Remote Viewer Number One** — the most credentialed participant in the [[stargate-program]]. Awarded the Legion of Merit for **200+ instances of adding to Military Intelligence** through remote viewing. Served 13+ years overseas, including multiple Vietnam tours. His firsthand accounts of specific Stargate operations — with independently verifiable outcomes — represent some of the highest-credibility evidence for operationally useful remote viewing in the public record.

## Background

Trained as a military intelligence specialist. First formal contact with remote viewing came through the "Gondola Wish" project: General Roya assigned McMoneagle to investigate the project at Stanford Research Institute (SRI), where [[hal-puthoff]] and [[russell-targ]] were running experiments. McMoneagle scored so well on initial tests that he was recruited directly into the program, later promoted from Warrant Officer to Chief Warrant Officer.

He trained in controlled out-of-body experience techniques under [[robert-monroe]] at the [[monroe-institute]] — a DOD contractor for Stargate preparation.

## Near-Death Experience (~1970, Munich)

McMoneagle was poisoned in Munich or Austria; his heart stopped for approximately 40 minutes (possibly longer with minimal beats). Classic NDE sequence:

1. Out-of-body: stood on cobblestone road outside the clinic, watched friend perform CPR, watched wife collapse to pavement
2. Taken to emergency room; felt intense heat on neck; turned to see white light of "perfect love and peace"
3. Heard voice: "You can't stay, you have to go back"
4. A German patient in the ER reported McMoneagle's eyes turned "crystal blue with fire coming out" and fled the room
5. Returned to body; survived

**Aftermath:** Six months of suicidal depression — McMoneagle thought of 15 ways to kill himself daily. Ended when an entity woke him during sleep and "slapped" him: *"What are you that stupid? You know what happens when you die, why aren't you enjoying this place, teaching people?"* Complete personality change followed: no longer feared death, worked more effectively alone, enhanced spontaneous OBE capacity.

The NDE preceded and plausibly facilitated his remote viewing abilities — [[joe-mcmoneagle]] is a key data point linking NDE-induced perceptual shifts to subsequent verified psychic performance.

## TK-089 Soviet Submarine (c.1979)

Most operationally significant Stargate session. McMoneagle and [[harley-trent]] were given a blind target:

- Independently described a prototype Soviet submarine with **20 slanted launch tubes** — a design innovation not yet visible in overhead imagery, allowing missiles to be launched while underway
- Described **RS-27 missiles**: 5 warheads each, ~50 megatons per warhead (Hiroshima was < 1 Mt)
- Submarine dimensions: 33 feet shorter than a Soviet aircraft carrier; could sit 100 miles off the US coast and destroy the US in 18 minutes

Report submitted to National Security Council in 4 hours. **Robert Gates** (then head CIA Russia analyst, later CIA Director and Secretary of Defense) dismissed it as "total fantasy." Admiral Jake Stewart brought the report to the NSC.

**114 days later**: overhead photography confirmed the submarine tied up to an aircraft carrier, side of a building removed, trench cut to harbor. The intelligence collected in 4 days exceeded all previous Soviet submarine intelligence in history.

## MX Missile Program Remote Viewing

McMoneagle remote viewed the US Air Force's MX Missile Program — a hidden railroad system of 23 protective shelters. A white paper sent to the Situation Room predicted that remote viewers could locate the missiles every time. Tested for one year; accurate every time. The Air Force cancelled the program (cost: ~$100 billion). Senator William Cohen sent McMoneagle a thank-you letter crediting him with saving that amount.

## Mars DOD Session (c.1984)

Given **seven GPS coordinates** corresponding to locations on Mars. DOD tasking: "Mars one million BC." McMoneagle described:

- **Huge pyramid** — larger than the Great Pyramid of Giza — with "monster rooms"
- **Pyramidal structures** interpreted as hibernation/survival chambers
- **12-ft-tall humanoid beings** — twice normal human height (hearsay per Alberino elsewhere; firsthand in [[JpLThEF2dTM]])
- People trapped, atmosphere deteriorating, attempting to survive until rescue
- **Structures visible at JPL negatives** provided post-session: pyramids, old fort, collapsed walls, "The Shard" (4-sided pyramid thousands of feet tall on crater edge), 30-mile perfectly straight cliff edge, subterranean cavern with "wiring," bone-like surface objects
- 2001 Mars Global Surveyor image: square structure several km per side with clear right-angle geometry

> ⚠ Note: Alberino cites this session as "8–12 ft beings" (pre-[[CiQTBOQ1dTg]]); McMoneagle himself in [[JpLThEF2dTM]] says "12-foot-tall." This is likely a rounding difference, not a conflict.

McMoneagle holds ~60 photographs of Martian structures he considers "clearly alien."

## Protocol and Consciousness Theory

McMoneagle's model of how remote viewing works:
- Remote viewing is an innate human ability trained by **unlearning bad habits** (analytical overlay, ego engagement), not by learning new skills
- **Intent is the key variable**: when intent is imperative (life-saving), viewers override blocking conditions
- The unconscious mind has no language; it borrows images from memory to approximate data — the viewer must "down-analyze" rather than interpret
- **Separation of transmission from analysis** is critical; monitor and analyst are both functioning psychically
- Success rate: ~60% under normal conditions; 7× more successful with proper double-blind scientific protocols
- **Corpus callosum hypothesis**: the filter between left/right brain hemispheres may be the seat of the psychic filtering mechanism; people with agenesis of corpus callosum ([[kim-peek]]) and non-verbal autistics may have reduced filtering and enhanced psychic access

## Russian Psychic Program Flaw

McMoneagle's analysis of why the Soviet remote viewing program failed:
- Russian viewers couldn't distinguish target mice (high anxiety) from control mice (low anxiety)
- But the person **sorting mice** between groups (not listed as an official viewer) was the actual psychic — his unconscious sorting was the functioning phenomenon
- Once discovered, removing him destroyed the experiment's results
- This error explains why Soviet attempts to target senators, congressmen, and the president failed: the psychic function wasn't in the designated viewers

## Himiko Session

Remote viewed [[himiko-ruler]] — Japan's only female Empress (~249 AD) — in a public demonstration broadcast to 31 million Japanese viewers. Described Summer Palace, Winter Palace, Temple, mother's Temple, and burial mountain without prior target information.

## Baltic Sea Anomaly Remote Viewing

[[dennis-asberg]] contacted McMoneagle (and [[angela-ford]]) to remote-view the [[baltic-sea-anomaly]] — a 60-meter anomalous seafloor object in the Baltic Sea discovered in 2011. Multiple remote viewers (including McMoneagle) reportedly assessed the BSA as a constructed object and "some kind of energy source"; one viewer said it might be "made of gold." Full session transcripts have not been published. See [[aGqFOxuYRT0]] for Asberg's account.
