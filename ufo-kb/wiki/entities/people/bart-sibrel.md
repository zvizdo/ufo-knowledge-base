# Bart Sibrel

Moon-landing skeptic and documentary filmmaker; best known for ambushing Apollo astronauts (most famously [[neil-armstrong]] and [[buzz-aldrin]], who punched him) with a Bible and demanding they swear they went to the moon. His most substantive claim is that he obtained unedited NASA footage showing the Apollo 11 crew faking their distance from Earth.

## Key Claims

**Gemini 15 / Michael Collins Photo**: Sibrel identifies a photograph of [[michael-collins]] purportedly conducting a spacewalk as a confirmed training photo with the background blacked out. This is not disputed — it demonstrates NASA did produce faked imagery.

**Unedited Apollo Reel**: Sibrel claims to have obtained NASA footage in which the crew can be seen using the circular porthole to frame Earth, making it look distant when they were still in low orbit — i.e., actively staging the appearance of deep-space travel.

**Physical impossibility argument**: The Van Allen radiation belt delivers ~150× a lethal radiation dose; Werner von Braun reportedly described crossing it as impossible; no animals were tested crossing it and returned alive.

**Apollo 11 press conference**: Sibrel characterizes the post-flight press conference as a "hostage video" — Armstrong, Collins, and Aldrin appearing nervous, fumbling, and deceptive. A body language expert analysis supports this reading.

**Nothing can be reproduced**: Rogan's summary of a key Sibrel argument — name one technology from 1969 that is not today cheaper, faster, and easier to replicate. Moon travel is the only exception. Starship (the most capable rocket ever built) requires 10 sequential refueling operations in LEO before it can reach the moon; Saturn 5 allegedly did it in one shot on the first crewed attempt.

## Danny Jones Episode

Sibrel debated [[charlie-duke]] (90-year-old Apollo astronaut) on [[danny-jones]]'s podcast. Both [[joe-rogan]] and [[jesse-michaels]] reference the episode; Rogan says Danny texted him clips and became more skeptical of the moon landing after it.
