# Jean-Jacques Vélasco

Head of SEPRA (Service d'Expertise des Phénomènes de Rentrées Atmosphériques) within [[cnes|CNES]] (Centre National d'Études Spatiales), the French space agency. Vélasco led the agency that evolved from GEPAN (1977) through SEPRA (1988) and eventually became [[geipan|GEIPAN]] in 2005. Co-authored the 1999 [[cometa-report|COMETA Report]] and provided the technical grounding for its evidence methodology (the 4-category GEPAN/SEPRA classification system and the 500+ aeronautical UAP-D database).

## Role in COMETA

Vélasco supplied the primary technical evidence base for the COMETA Report: GEPAN technical notes (NT 1–18), the statistical analysis showing Category D cases at 4–5% of all reports, and the aeronautical case database (500+ cases since 1942, ~20% radar+visual). His presence as a serving government scientist gave the report institutional credibility unavailable to purely retired-officer groups.

Co-authored with Jean-Claude Bourret: *Ovnis, la science avance* (Robert Laffont, 1993), cited in the COMETA Report bibliography.
