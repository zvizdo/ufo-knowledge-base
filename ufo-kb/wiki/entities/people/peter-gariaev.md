# Peter Gariaev

Russian biophysicist and founder of "linguistic wave genetics" — the hypothesis that DNA functions as a wave-based information system that can be read, written, and transmitted via scalar and vector electromagnetic potentials. His work sits at the intersection of [[scalar-waves]], consciousness research, and biological healing.

## Work

Gariaev's core claim: DNA is not merely a chemical data-storage medium but a **wave-based antenna-transmitter system** tuned to **helicoidal scalar waves** (the same wave geometry proposed by [[constantine-meyl]]). This implies:

- Genetic information can be read out via coherent light (lasers) and transmitted to other biological systems
- The structure of DNA resonates naturally with helicoidal longitudinal waves — form follows function
- Disease involves disruption of these wave patterns; healing involves restoration

His practical experiments: patients would enter **wooden pyramid structures** equipped with lasers, semiconductors, and scalar-field-generating electronic circuits. The field created inside the pyramid allegedly healed various ailments — depression, physical illness — through the scalar/vector potential interaction with the patient's DNA and cellular lattice.

[[dave-rossi]] frames this through his lattice-polarization model: the pyramid setup polarizes the patient's biological lattice, invoking negative entropy (neg-entropy) to reverse cellular aging — bringing a "book 3" state (younger) into the present "book 10" frame.

## Linguistic Wave Genetics

Gariaev also proposed that DNA carries **semantic information** in its wave emissions — hence "linguistic" wave genetics. He connected this to [[luke-montagnier]]'s DNA water teleportation findings, suggesting both are different aspects of the same underlying scalar wave mechanism.

Papers and engineering methodologies remain accessible at his website (now maintained by his wife): **linguisticwavegenetics.com** (Russian, with English translation).

## Nobel Prize and Death

Gariaev was nominated for the Nobel Prize in 2019 or 2020. The nomination was subsequently retracted. He died — reportedly under mysterious circumstances — some time after. [[dave-rossi]] mentions this but declines to elaborate.
