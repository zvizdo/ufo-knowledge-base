# Jason Sanss

US Air Force Master Sergeant stationed at [[nellis-air-force-base]] with the 67th Intelligence Group at the time of the 1994 Nellis range UAP encounter that circulates in public as the "Nellis Range UFO" video.

## The 1994 Nellis Encounter

**Date:** August 1994  
**Location:** Nellis Air Force Base / Nellis testing range, Nevada

The public-domain Nellis range UAP footage shows an object flying past the "schoolhouse" (training area) and descending into the valley. Air traffic control ("E1 control") confirmed it did not know what type of aircraft it was. Sanss's account — reported by [[james-fox]] in [[the-program-fox]] — goes beyond the public footage:

Sanss describes a **face-to-face interaction** with what he characterizes as a figure that "did not appear to be human" wearing military-style clothing/uniform. Fox draws a connection between this account and [[gary-mckinnon]]'s discovery of a "non-terrestrial officers" list with ranks and names — speculating they may represent the same category of entity or program.

Fox's assessment: "I opted to report on it even though I can't prove that it happened. But I think it's a pretty compelling account." He notes: three other on-duty military personnel at the location, a confirmed date, and a confirmed location.

## Status in The Program

Sanss is the central witness account in [[the-program-fox]]. Fox frames the Nellis account in a lineage of Close Encounter of the Third Kind reports going back to the 1940s and documented by [[j-allen-hynek]].

## Additional Details (3yrKVu35uSE)

Fox elaborated on the Sanss account in his December 2024 Joe Rogan interview after *The Program* release:

- The entity **departed wearing Sanss's jacket** — a concrete detail that grounds the interaction physically
- The encounter produced what Sanss calls a **"K state"** — a specific altered cognitive or perceptual state he uses to describe the encounter's quality
- A metal associated with the object was given a classified name (transcribed as "tenellum" or "tillum" — the exact term was redacted through the **Doser pre-publication review** process, meaning the word itself was classified)
- Sanss's credentials were independently verified by Congressional committees as part of Fox's due diligence before including the account

Fox also noted that three other military personnel were on-duty at Nellis when the encounter occurred — potential corroborating witnesses who have not gone public.
