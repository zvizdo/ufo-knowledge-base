# Charles Suggs

US Navy personnel who allegedly claimed to have been present at the 1954 meeting between President Eisenhower and non-human entities at [[edwards-air-force-base]] (Muroc). The claim is known primarily through secondhand reporting by his grandson. Suggs himself (if he made the claim) has not provided documentary evidence, and the account exists as unverified witness testimony.

**Epistemics**: The Suggs claim is one of several independently circulating accounts of the 1954 Eisenhower meeting. Its evidentiary weight rests entirely on his grandson's transmission of what Suggs allegedly said; no contemporaneous documentation corroborates it. The KB treats this as a contested claims-thesis entry rather than an established fact. The grandson gave a recorded account circa 2005, which circulated in UFO researcher communities — it is this recording, rather than any primary document or contemporaneous witness statement, that introduced the claim to the public record.

**What is known**: Suggs's name appears in the UFO research literature primarily from around 2005 onward. He was reportedly a US Navy master sergeant. No independent witnesses or documents have surfaced to corroborate his grandson's account.
