# Anne Strieber

Whitley Strieber's wife of 45 years (maiden name Anne Maddox). Met through a 1970s Manhattan personal-ad service called *Mindmates* — the only non-fish surname on his returned match list. They were not separated for more than two weeks over the next 45 years. She died in the mid-2010s.

Anne functioned as the spiritual and perceptual center of Strieber's contact experiences, not merely as witness. Strieber credits her judgment — her selections of who could visit the cabin, her interpretations of encounters, her cultivation of compassion — as essential to the entire record.

## Role in Contact Experiences

- Anne's presence at the cabin appeared to *attract* visitors. Strieber explicitly credits her spiritual quality as a draw.
- She selected which researchers and friends were invited to the cabin, exercising discernment Strieber says he could not have managed alone.
- After her death, a period of no contact followed. When she became ill and after her death, contact resumed and intensified.
- Strieber believes Anne activated his 1989 ear implant in 2015 — the implant had been dormant for 26 years — so that communication could continue from beyond physical death.

## Reported Post-Death Communication

Anne's 3 AM communion sessions are among Strieber's primary ongoing contact events (2015–present). He describes the sessions as involving "dead people" and "the aliens" communing together, with Anne present. She appears to function as a mediator between the NHI and Strieber.

Messages Strieber attributes to her include:
- The implant activation and its guide function toward synchronicity-driven information.
- The core NHI message relayed via the communion: *"You are more than physical creatures; you have more value than you recognize."*

## Aphorisms

Two of the sharpest formulations Strieber attributes to Anne:

> "Enlightenment is what happens when there's nothing left of you but love."

> "Each of us is all we have." — her definition of compassion.

Her ethical instruction, relayed by Strieber: *"Don't judge them. You'll feel which ones can use your help and which ones can't. Just don't go to that part of your head."*

## La Guardia Park Dedication

Anne created a park at La Guardia Place / 520 La Guardia in Manhattan. A statue and plaque bearing the Strieber names remain there — a concrete public memorial to her physical presence in the world.
