## Background

William Joseph "Wild Bill" Donovan was a decorated World War I veteran, lawyer, and the founder and director of the Office of Strategic Services (OSS), the WWII-era US intelligence and special operations agency that was the direct predecessor to the CIA. He was one of the most powerful intelligence figures of the 20th century.

Donovan was also a Knight of Malta — a membership in the Sovereign Military Order of Malta that appears repeatedly in accounts of the UAP secrecy custodian network. His Knighthood is specifically cited as the reason he was selected to receive Vatican-channeled intelligence on the 1933 Magenta craft and to establish retrieval protocols through OSS Project McGregor.

## Notable Claims

- The Vatican back-channeled intelligence about a crashed non-human craft recovered near Magenta, Italy in 1933 to the Allied powers through Donovan's OSS network. The Vatican's stated motivation was fear that Nazi Germany would gain access to the craft and its technology. Donovan's Knights of Malta membership provided the trusted channel for this communication.
- Donovan established UAP retrieval protocols via OSS Project McGregor specifically in response to the Magenta intelligence — making this among the earliest known instances of a formal US government protocol for handling recovered non-human craft.
- When the OSS was dissolved after WWII and reconstituted as the CIA, the UAP-adjacent intelligence networks and protocols Donovan had established carried over into the new agency, contributing to the continuity of [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] infrastructure across institutional transitions.
