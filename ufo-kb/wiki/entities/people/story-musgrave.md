# Story Musgrave

NASA astronaut who flew on six Space Shuttle missions — the most experienced shuttle astronaut at the time of his retirement. Holds seven academic degrees. Known for performing the Hubble Space Telescope servicing mission (STS-61, 1993).

## Notable Claims

Musgrave reported seeing what he described as "snakes writhing in space" during his shuttle missions. [[kevin-knuth]] cites this in the context of a broader phenomenon people have reported for centuries — luminous or semi-biological-appearing entities in space and near maritime vessels.

Knuth frames this within a long historical pattern: "balls of light coming out of the water, hovering next to the ship, following the ship, and then taking off into the clouds" — cases going back to the 1800s.

Musgrave's account was **not mentioned in the NASA Commission's UAP report**.
