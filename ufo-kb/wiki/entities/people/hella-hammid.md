# Hella Hammid

## Background

Hella Hammid was an SRI laboratory staffer who became a control-condition test subject in the [[stargate-program|SRI remote-viewing program]] when the program's leadership wanted to test whether psi ability was concentrated in rare gifted individuals or distributed across the general population.

## Notable Claims

### First-Session Hit Under Skeptic Priors

[[hal-puthoff]] (Gf_tKn9TaP8) recounts the moment in detail. After a full medical workup on the program's top performers (seven-layer brain scans, neurology, psychology) found "these are just normal people," Puthoff wanted to test the implication: can ordinary people, with no priors, hit a target? Hammid was an SRI lab employee with explicit "give me a break, I don't believe in that stuff" priors.

In her first trial, the outbound team went via the standard random-protocol to a chain-link-fenced freeway overpass with "a very interesting structure." Hammid drew "this kind of trough up in the air, but it's got holes in it, so it couldn't carry water"; she described "something going by really fast." She "really nailed the place."

### The Bell Curve Discovery

This session, plus subsequent work with other non-self-selected subjects, produced what Puthoff frames as the program's biggest discovery: psi ability follows a bell curve like athletic or musical ability — superstars and duds at the extremes, but "to some degree, anybody could do it." This became the basis for the Army Intelligence training program (~Joe McMoneagle's cohort and beyond) — selection plus structured training, not just gift-spotting.
