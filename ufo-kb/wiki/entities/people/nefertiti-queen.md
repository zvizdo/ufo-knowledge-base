# Nefertiti

## Background

Chief wife of [[akhenaten-pharaoh]] during the Amarna period. One of the most recognizable figures of ancient Egypt. Her death (circumstances unclear) marked the end of direct Akhenaten-era rule and the transition to [[tutankhamun-pharaoh]]'s brief reign, during which the Amarna experiment was reversed.
