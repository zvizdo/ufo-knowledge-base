# Andrija Puharich

Croatian-American medical doctor (1918–1995) who became one of the most controversial figures at the intersection of parapsychology, classified government research, and UAP contact narratives. His career spans legitimate scientific work on ELF waves and consciousness to allegations of CIA involvement, psi operations, and direct contact with the "Nine" — a purported council of nine cosmic intelligences.

## Background

Puharich was trained as a physician and worked from the early 1950s on the biology of psychic phenomena. He founded the Round Table Foundation in Maine, which hosted a series of psychics and channelers in controlled experiments. He eventually became a contractor for Army/CIA research programs interested in non-standard intelligence collection.

## ELF Waves and Consciousness

His research on Extremely Low Frequency (ELF) electromagnetic waves as a substrate for consciousness and mind influence is cited in [[human-energy-field]] concept page. This work overlaps with classified military interest in non-lethal weapons and behavioral modification — placing him near the [[mk-ultra]] ecosystem. [[jesse-michaels]] in [[0qlppHc3-gg]] characterizes him bluntly as "tied in with MK-Ultra like CIA brainwashing stuff." The intelligence-funding context creates standing uncertainty about which findings were genuine, suppressed, or weaponized; [[operation-midnight-climax]] is the parallel CIA program operating in San Francisco in the same era.

## Scalar Waves and Extended Electrodynamics

Puharich's ELF and bone-induction research sits in the broader space of extended electrodynamics — the components of Maxwell's original equations that Heaviside's simplification dropped. [[jesse-michaels]] places him in a four-name lineage with [[nikola-tesla]], [[thomas-townsend-brown]], and [[constantine-meyl]] of researchers who probed [[scalar-waves]] empirically, working on effects involving high voltages and non-Hertzian wave forms. [[dave-rossi]] cites Puharich's ELF and semantic-information work as a direct predecessor to his own [[extended-electrodynamics]] framework. The same physics substrate is invoked for both UAP propulsion (TTB, Meyl) and non-local consciousness (Puharich, Rossi) — making Puharich the consciousness-side anchor of a single suppressed-physics tradition. See [[physics-lockdown]].

## Bone Induction

Puharich documented cases of patients receiving radio signals through metallic dental work — a non-standard EM-coupling phenomenon he attributed to bone induction. Lucille Ball reported the same effect during WWII. The TD-100 device he later developed at [[intellect]] formalized this principle into a directed thought-transmission tool.

[[jesse-michaels]] in [[73YBwFvcmF8]] connects this work directly to the [[1977-ellsworth-woods-abduction]] case: the flight surgeon's unexplained dental and gum inspection of [[mario-woods]] post-abduction may have been a search for or placement of an RF-reception implant of the type Puharich's research established as feasible. See [[nhi-implants]].

## Uri Geller

Puharich brought [[uri-geller]] to the United States and arranged his testing at [[stanford-research-institute]] (SRI) — the same institution that hosted the [[stargate-program]] remote viewing program. Puharich believed Geller was a genuine psychic and possibly an emissary of extraterrestrial intelligences.

## The Nine

Puharich documented his belief that Geller was in contact with a group of nine cosmic entities ("The Nine") — a narrative that has since been cited as either evidence of genuine contact or as a CIA-sponsored psychological operation designed to test channeling narratives.

## Army Specialized Training Program

Puharich enrolled at Northwestern University in 1947 as a medical student and was part of the Army Specialized Training Program — a military recruiting initiative that also included Henry Kissinger and Kurt Vonnegut. This was likely his first step into the military-intelligence world, predating the Roundtable Foundation.

## The TD-100 and Thought Transmission

At his covert company [[intellect]] in the 1960s, Puharich developed the TD-100 (Transdermal 100) — a device that could send a specific radio frequency to a person's brain, received either via a tooth-mounted transducer or directly via skull-transmission. He demonstrated this at the Pentagon. Close friend [[jose-delgado]] was simultaneously doing brain electrode research at Yale; Puharich used Delgado's animal-control findings as context for human thought-transmission research. Footage of women being experimented on at Intellect exists but has no sound.

## The Nine — Full Chronology

The Nine were first channeled at [[roundtable-foundation]] in 1952 through [[dr-venad]], then re-emerged through [[uri-geller]] in 1971 (when Puharich hypnotized him in Israel), and later through [[phyllis-schlemmer]] in the 1970s sessions attended by [[gene-roddenberry]], [[gordon-cooper]], and others. The same Nine also came through some of the [[space-kids]] when hypnotized, despite those children having no prior knowledge of the Nine.

## Death — January 1995

Puharich was found dead in January 1995 — the same month the [[stargate-program]] was officially terminated. [[jacobo-grinberg]] disappeared less than a month later. His house had been burned in a proven arson in 1978. Shortly before his death he had been approached by the CIA (via [[bob-beck]]) to head an ELF weapons department; he refused and threw Beck out of his house.
