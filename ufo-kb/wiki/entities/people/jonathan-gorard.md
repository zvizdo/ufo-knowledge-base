# Jonathan Gorard

Physicist at the Wolfram Institute. Working with Stephen Wolfram, Gorard developed the mathematical framework known as the Wolfram Model, providing what [[matthew-pines]] presents as the most compelling candidate for a theory of everything with direct implications for understanding NHI capabilities and UAP propulsion.

## Key Contributions (2020–2021)

**Causal graph → General Relativity**: Showed that discrete measures on the causal graph of a multi-way hypergraph rewriting system converge in the limit to the Einstein field equations. Derived a discrete Ricci tensor and Riemann manifold structure from purely combinatorial hypergraph structures — recovering general relativity without assuming background spacetime.

**Branchial graph → Quantum Mechanics**: Showed that the same system's branchial graph (representing all possible orderings of rule applications) yields measures converging to the Feynman path integral; branch space with suitable assumptions converges to projective Hilbert space — recovering quantum mechanics without additional axioms.

**Rulial space → Unified structure**: Analyzing the space of all possible rules applied to all possible hypergraphs, the infinite-limit structure is an "infinity groupoid" (Grothendieck's homotopy hypothesis). This explains why different observers see the same laws of physics: they are all nearby in rulial space, slicing the higher-dimensional structure similarly.

**Observer model**: An observer is mathematically characterized as a "Nupendix completion" — the procedure of equivalencing multiple branches into one perceived state. Quantum mechanics is what any observer necessarily sees when performing this collapse. The measurement problem is dissolved: measurement IS the completion procedure.

## NHI Implications (per Pines)

- Branchial non-locality: two events far apart in spacetime can be one edge apart in branchial space, providing a physical basis for non-local phenomena (entanglement, remote viewing, telepathy).
- Consciousness may directly access branchial space; psychedelics and meditation may "relax constraints" on how observers stitch the underlying structure together.
- "CIA wizards messing with timelines" can be cashed out as observers using consciousness-amplified technology to equivalence a future state indistinguishable from a past state — no causal paradox.
