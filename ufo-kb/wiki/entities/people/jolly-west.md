# Jolly West

Dr. Louis Jolyon "Jolly" West (1924–1999) was a prominent UCLA psychiatrist with documented CIA and USAF research connections. His career sits at the intersection of mainstream academic psychiatry and the government's covert behavioral modification programs of the Cold War era.

## MK-Ultra Research

West was a full MK-Ultra participant, not merely "adjacent." He conducted research into hypnosis, sensory deprivation, dissociation, and pharmacological behavior modification. He received CIA funding and corresponded using an alias with [[sidney-gottlieb|Sidney Gottlieb]] (the program's operational director, known as "the poisoner in chief"). His 1957 experiment administering LSD to an elephant (killing it) attracted public notoriety.

### The 1955 Memory Implantation Report

In 1955, West sent a report to the CIA stating he had successfully developed the technology to permanently **implant false memories** and **remove true memories** using drugs and hypnosis — without the subject's knowledge. This was the most strategically valuable achievement the program had been seeking for nearly a decade.

The version of this report submitted to Congress during the Church Committee hearings had this paragraph **removed**. The CIA re-drafted West's document, excising the confirmation of success. [[tom-oneill]] found West's original, unredacted report in the UCLA archives.

## Haight-Ashbury: The Manson Connection

In 1967, West conducted LSD research on hippies at the [[haight-ashbury-free-clinic]] in San Francisco — the exact time and location where [[charles-manson]] was building his cult and recruiting from the hippie community. West used an alias to report to [[sidney-gottlieb]] about these experiments.

West's geographic movement tracked Manson's: both moved from San Francisco to Los Angeles over the same period. [[tom-oneill]] presents this parallel trajectory as the central evidentiary thread connecting West to Manson's transformation from petty criminal to MK-Ultra's "ideal product."

## Jack Ruby Psychiatric Break

During Jack Ruby's trial for killing Lee Harvey Oswald, West was assigned as Ruby's psychiatrist. This was not immediate — the trial judge initially blocked West's access — but West eventually gained approximately **one hour alone with Ruby** in his county jail cell. Immediately afterward, West held a press conference announcing that within 48 hours Ruby had suffered a **psychotic break from which he would never recover** — with auditory and visual hallucinations.

From that day forward, Ruby could not string three coherent sentences together. He died three years later, never having given coherent testimony. West's specialty included **inducing insanity in a person without their knowledge** — precisely the outcome Ruby displayed.

The Warren Commission was never informed that West was an active CIA asset with a conflict of interest.
