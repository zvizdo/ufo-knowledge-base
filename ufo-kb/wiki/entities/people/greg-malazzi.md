# Greg Malazzi

## Background

Greg Malazzi is the director and researcher behind *Mind Traveler*, a documentary about [[andrija-puharich]] that took approximately ten years to produce. He became interested in Puharich after reading *The Sacred Mushroom* and accumulated access to Puharich's personal archives including hundreds of tape recordings, journals, and correspondence that had never been publicly heard.

## Research Methodology

Malazzi located the "Space Kids" (psychic children Puharich worked with in the 1970s) through names written in Puharich's journals, then spent approximately two years earning their trust before they agreed to be interviewed. He obtained five on-camera interviews. He also interviewed Uri Geller (who bent a spoon during the interview in a manner Malazzi couldn't explain), Hal Puthoff, and Annie Jacobson, among others.

## Key Discoveries

- **Puharich's 1953 DC journals** — meticulous records of meetings with figures including [[allen-dulles]] and [[thomas-townsend-brown]], suggesting Puharich was already deeply embedded in intelligence circles at the same time as The Nine seances
- **Gene Rodenberry's "The Nine" script** — 130-page unproduced screenplay about Rodenberry's experiences with Puharich and Phyllis Schlemmer's channeling; Malazzi holds an original copy
- **The TD-100 device** — Puharich's "transdermal 100" device that emitted specific frequencies to people without physical contact; footage of experiments on a woman at "Intellect" exists
- **The prosthetic finger device** — Puharich invented a prosthetic finger containing a chip emitting an ELF frequency that induced immediate hypnotic trance when touching someone's forehead; used on Space Kids
- **Intellect** — Puharich's covert 1960s company; almost no public records; only evidence found in Beardsley Graham's Berkeley archive; believed to be a CIA front
- **Valerie Ransone** — mysterious woman connected to the Nixon White House who claimed to channel aliens and sat on boards of ELF technology research organizations; was in contact with CIA's Kit Green in the late 1970s; Malazzi believes he has located her but has not yet interviewed her

## Claims About Puharich

- Puharich predated SRI's remote viewing program by ~20 years — doing equivalent work at Roundtable Foundation in early 1950s
- Puharich may have been a CIA double agent — genuine believer who was simultaneously conducting classified behavioral modification research
- Puharich's death in January 1995 was coincident with the end of the Stargate program; Jacobo Grinberg disappeared weeks later
- Puharich refused a CIA request to head an ELF weapons department; Bob Beck came to his house as CIA liaison; Puharich threw him out

## The Documentary: Mind Traveler

Released through Age of Disclosure. Available for purchase on iTunes and YouTube. Malazzi describes it as the first serious documentary treatment of Puharich that includes interviews with space kids who had never spoken publicly.
