# Darold Treffert

Psychiatrist who became the world's leading clinical authority on savant syndrome — rare cases of extraordinary mental ability in individuals with developmental or acquired brain differences. Served as a consultant on the film *Rain Man* and ran a dedicated savant research center in Wisconsin (referenced in [[0qlppHc3-gg]] as "the Tord Center there"). His work documented the phenomenon that anomalous cognitive capacity — extraordinary memory, mathematical ability, artistic skill — can emerge alongside or because of significant brain impairment.

## Role in This Context

Subject Amelia (a non-verbal autistic girl in Wisconsin) was brought to Treffert's center to be evaluated. The connection suggests Amelia's family was specifically seeking the most credentialed institutional assessment of her abilities. Treffert's Center represents the mainstream scientific establishment's closest institutional acknowledgment of savant phenomena.

## Relevance to Non-Local Consciousness

Treffert coined the phrase "islands of genius" for savant abilities. His work implies that whatever enables extraordinary memory or skill in savants is not simply conventional neural processing — providing indirect support for the [[consciousness-non-local]] transmission model that [[diane-powell]] and [[ky-dickens]] invoke.
