# David Ferrie

David William Ferrie (1918–1967) was a pilot and CIA-adjacent operative in New Orleans who was a central figure in [[jim-garrison]]'s investigation of the Kennedy assassination. He suffered from alopecia (total hair loss) and was known for wearing an unconvincing fake red wig and drawn-on eyebrows. He held white supremacist views and operated from the [[guy-banister]] Camp Street network.

## Role in the Garrison Investigation

Ferrie worked with Banister and was connected to [[clay-shaw]]. Garrison considered him a key node in the conspiracy network. Ferrie died on February 22, 1967 — officially from a brain aneurysm — in the early days of Garrison's investigation. His death is widely considered suspicious by researchers; Levenda describes it as a likely suicide or assisted death.
