# Eugene Thane Caesar

Security guard employed by Ace Guard Service and contracted to provide security at the Ambassador Hotel on the night of Robert F. Kennedy's assassination (June 5, 1968). Caesar was positioned directly behind Kennedy, in the crowd between Kennedy and the pantry exit, when the shots were fired — closer to Kennedy than Sirhan Sirhan, who fired from the front.

He admitted to drawing his gun after the shooting; photos and witness accounts confirm he had it in his hand. Ballistics in the case are anomalous: the bullets extracted from Kennedy entered from behind and the right side, inconsistent with Sirhan's position directly in front of Kennedy. The Los Angeles Police Department investigation largely ignored Caesar's position; he was never rigorously cleared.

Caesar had Lockheed clearances and reportedly sold a .22 caliber weapon shortly after the assassination — the same caliber used in the crime.
