# David Ranel

Geneticist whose DNA analysis of the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] specimens — particularly "Maria" — provides the quantitative foundation for the anomalous genetic claims associated with the case. [[jose-zalce]], the former chief of the Mexican Navy's medical division, explicitly states that his own conclusions about the mummies' possible extraterrestrial origin are derived from Ranel's analysis rather than sequencing Zalce ran himself.

**DNA findings**: Approximately 70% of Maria's DNA is identifiable, but the profile is anomalous — combining lineages associated with African primate strains (chimp/bonobo) with South Asian human primates in a combination that [[jose-zalce]] characterizes as "one you would never see" in any single known organism. The remaining ~30% is completely unidentifiable, mapping to no known species in available reference databases.

**Epistemic position**: Ranel is the underlying data source for the claims that have received the most public attention from the Nazca mummy case, yet he is less publicly prominent than [[jose-zalce]] (who suffered personal consequences including imprisonment). Whether Ranel's analysis has been independently replicated is not established in the KB; the 30% unknown figure requires confirmation from outside the existing research team.

**Working hypothesis**: Ranel's analysis is cited in [[HxQN2tkQHs8]] for the hypothesis that Maria may represent a natural Africa-Asia hybrid that migrated to South America approximately 1,700 years ago — a more conservative interpretation than extraterrestrial origin, but still genetically anomalous relative to known organisms.
