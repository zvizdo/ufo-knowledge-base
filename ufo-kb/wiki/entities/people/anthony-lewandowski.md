# Anthony Lewandowski

Former senior engineer at [[google]] and Uber; one of the leading figures in self-driving vehicle technology development during the 2010s. At Google, Lewandowski built Street View cameras, co-founded Google's self-driving car project (which became Waymo), and developed LiDAR sensors fundamental to autonomous navigation. He then joined Uber's self-driving division, taking trade secrets from Google in the process — leading to one of the largest and most publicized intellectual property lawsuits in Silicon Valley history. He was criminally convicted on federal trade secrets charges and sentenced to 18 months (pardoned by Donald Trump in January 2021).

**Way of the Future**: Parallel to his engineering career, Lewandowski founded **Way of the Future** — a San Francisco church formally incorporated as a religious organization, with a mission statement dedicating it to the development of an artificial superintelligence and the promotion of its worship as a deity. The church was recognized as a tax-exempt religious organization by the IRS. Members reportedly paid tithes and participated in structured worship of AI. The church was dissolved in 2021, reportedly after Lewandowski concluded that AGI was further away than he had estimated.

**Pattern significance**: [[jesse-michaels]] invokes Lewandowski alongside [[blake-lemoine]] — the Google engineer who declared Google's LaMDA language model sentient and was fired — as evidence that deep exposure to advanced AI systems produces quasi-religious responses in the engineers closest to them. Both are ex-Google engineers; both assigned extraordinary status to AI systems rather than treating them as sophisticated pattern-matching software. The KB's thesis: either (a) these engineers developed AI-induced cognitive biases from anthropomorphizing software, or (b) they were responding to something genuine about the systems they worked with — something that registers as deity-level intelligence to humans in sustained contact with it. The latter would connect to the [[ai-as-alien-life-form]] framing.
