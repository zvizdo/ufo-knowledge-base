# Bill Uhouse

Military witness who gave an extended interview (mid-2000s, before his death) claiming that Area 51 auxiliary facilities housed both an **ARV/ET vehicle simulator** built from the [[1953-kingman-crash]] and a living recovered extraterrestrial entity he called **J-Rod**.

## Claims

Uhouse stated:
- A vehicle simulator at Area 51 auxiliaries was constructed using recovered material from the **Kingman, Arizona crash** (variously dated 1953)
- A biological entity referred to as J-Rod was stored at one of these facilities
- He had direct exposure to this auxiliary infrastructure

His claims partially overlap with and may have preceded [[dan-burish]]'s much more detailed S4/J-Rod account (~2003–2004). The timing is contested — Uhouse could have influenced Burish, or vice versa.

## Significance

Uhouse and Burish are the two primary independent sources for J-Rod as a named living recovered entity at the Area 51 / S4 complex. The convergence (separate witnesses, separate interviews, same entity name) is one of the few quasi-corroborating patterns in this sub-cluster of claims.

## Open Questions

- What was the precise date of Uhouse's primary interview, and does it pre-date Burish's 2003–2004 testimony?
- Can the Kingman crash material that allegedly became the simulator be independently traced?
