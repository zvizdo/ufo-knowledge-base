## Background

Robert Sarbacher served as head of Washington National Laboratories and as a member of US government scientific advisory committees, giving him access to classified programs at the highest levels of the postwar national security state. His professional standing — a credentialed scientist with formal advisory roles — makes his statements on UAP classification among the most consequential on record.

Nuclear engineer [[stanton-friedman]] received Sarbacher's testimony and published it in UFO research literature, bringing it to wider attention within the research community.

## Notable Claims

- Sarbacher stated on the record: "UFO secrecy is classified two levels above the hydrogen bomb." This is one of the most direct and specific statements ever made by a credentialed government official about the classification level applied to UAP information. It implies that UAP programs sit above virtually all other compartmentalized programs in the US national security system.
- Sarbacher's claim is independently corroborated by Harald Malmgren's own experience: despite holding full Q clearance (the Atomic Energy Commission's highest clearance) and presidential clearance, Malmgren was explicitly denied access to UAP recovery information from [[bluegill-uap-debris]] and the Bluegill Triple Prime operation. The denial confirmed that the compartment existed above even his access level.

## Named as Co-Architect (5udx_SDdL3Y)

[[jesse-michaels]] cites [[david-grusch]]'s 2023 whistleblower testimony naming Sarbacher alongside [[j-robert-oppenheimer]] as the individuals who "actually set up the secrecy protocols for UFOs." This is the most direct claim on record about Sarbacher's structural role beyond simply knowing the classification level.

The proposed mechanism: Sarbacher and Oppenheimer designed the overlay of the [[atomic-energy-act-classification]] nuclear secrecy framework onto UAP programs — creating a system in which any recovered craft or material with radioactive properties is automatically "born secret" under nuclear law. See [[oppenheimer-ufo-classification-architect]] for the full claims-thesis.

## Strieber's Personal Account: Battel Molecular Grid and Death (ABOP8ZJsyIk)

[[stanton-friedman]] introduced [[whitley-strieber]] to Sarbacher before *Communion* was published in 1987. Sarbacher asked Strieber to write up his experiences and mail the account. Strieber sent the manuscript overnight via UPS. The next morning, the UPS driver called to say Sarbacher had died the previous night.

**Sarbacher's last disclosure to Strieber** (conveyed before the manuscript was sent):
- Materials recovered from crashed craft were studied at [[battelle-memorial-institute]].
- When electron microscopes became operational, researchers discovered a **molecular grid** — a designed internal structure within thin material that gave it exceptional strength far beyond conventional engineering.
- This molecular grid was subsequently reverse-engineered into military and civilian technology.

Sarbacher also confirmed to Strieber that [[thomas-townsend-brown]] was part of the broader program: Sarbacher personally picked up Brown in a Cadillac and was connected to Brown's anti-gravity work, including results achieved in Paris.

**Death circumstances**: The death certificate says "natural causes." The UPS driver said Sarbacher had "fallen off his boat." Strieber notes the coincidence of the timing with the manuscript delivery — the very night he mailed a detailed account of experiences Sarbacher had asked him to document.

> ⚠ No independent documentation of the Battel molecular grid claim exists. Strieber's account is firsthand (direct conversation) but uncorroborated. Ross Coulthart is noted as having data on Sarbacher and nuclear engineers at the Woomera test site in Australia.
