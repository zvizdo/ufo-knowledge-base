# John Paul DeJoria

## Background

John Paul DeJoria is a billionaire entrepreneur best known as a co-founder of Paul Mitchell Systems and Patrón Spirits. He is publicly identified in this knowledge base context as a financial and operational partner of [[hal-puthoff]] on advanced physics research.

## Notable Claims

### Quantum-Communication Partnership With Puthoff

[[hal-puthoff]] (Gf_tKn9TaP8) describes DeJoria ("JP") as his active collaborator on a [[subrosa-quantum-communication|quantum-communication program]]. Puthoff demonstrated the proof-of-principle on a classified contract in the 1990s but shelved it because the required cryogenic detector hardware was not mature. The hardware bottleneck has now been broken by quantum-computing investment — Josephson junctions operating at ~3.7 K are commercially viable. (See [[josephson-junctions]].)

Architecture per Puthoff:
- **Transmit side:** explicitly designed antenna geometries whose electric and magnetic field components cancel, while preserving an underlying quantum signal.
- **Receive side:** quantum-only detectors (Josephson junctions) — ordinary EM detectors can't pick up the signal because the E and B components are gone.

Application targets:
- Through-shielding communication (the same scenario that motivated CIA's initial 1972 worry about Soviet remote viewers reading classified documents from superconducting safes).
- Submarine communication: seawater blocks EM, doesn't block the quantum signal.
- Plasma-blackout communication during atmospheric reentry.
- Possible application as a substrate for biological-quantum (e.g. [[microtubule-quantum-consciousness|microtubule]]) signal-detection research.
