# Josh Boswell

British investigative journalist at the UK Daily Mail with a focus on US national security and government accountability. In the UAP context, Boswell's primary contribution was breaking the [[1992-cape-canaveral-saucer]] story on May 4, 2024 — the first public account of [[greg-rogers]]'s testimony, an encounter Rogers had kept private for over 30 years.

**The Greg Rogers story**: Rogers was a US Air Force flight surgeon stationed at Cape Canaveral in 1992 when he witnessed a metallic saucer-shaped craft at close range. The encounter was significant because of Rogers's medical-officer credentials, his inability to explain what he saw, and the three decades he maintained silence before coming forward. Boswell located Rogers, obtained his detailed account, and published it with the Daily Mail's investigative resources — including corroboration efforts and documentation of Rogers's military record.

**Publication dynamics**: The Daily Mail UK is a mass-circulation tabloid with a large online presence that regularly reaches American audiences. Its willingness to publish UAP stories that US mainstream outlets like the New York Times typically decline places the Daily Mail in the same category as [[the-debrief]] — a publication willing to carry credible UAP accounts. Boswell's article precipitated follow-up interviews by [[chris-leto]] (Letto Files) and [[jesse-michaels]] (American Alchemy), demonstrating the amplification pattern from initial publication through specialist UAP media.

**Journalist profile**: Boswell represents a class of mainstream-media journalists who have engaged seriously with UAP disclosure — alongside [[leslie-kean]] (New York Times/The Debrief) and Ralph Blumenthal. His UK institutional base may reduce some of the editorial pressure that affects US journalists covering UAP, given that British national security equities in the story are lower.
