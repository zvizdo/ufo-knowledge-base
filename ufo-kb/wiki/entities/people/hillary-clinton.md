# Hillary Clinton

American politician who served as U.S. Senator from New York (2001–2009), Secretary of State (2009–2013), and was the Democratic nominee for President in 2016. In the UAP context, Clinton is notable for two things:

**Rockefeller Initiative (mid-1990s)**: [[lawrence-rockefeller]] gifted her Paul Davies's book *Are We Alone* and attempted to use his Clinton White House access to advance UAP disclosure. Clinton was the primary target of the external-pressure component of the initiative, while her husband's administration simultaneously tasked [[webster-hubbell]] with finding UAP files through internal channels.

**2016 Campaign**: Clinton's campaign chairman John Podesta — a longtime UAP disclosure advocate — influenced her public statements on the subject. In a January 2016 interview with the Conway Daily Sun, Clinton said she would "get to the bottom of" UFOs if elected, pledging to declassify UFO files. This made her the first major-party presidential candidate to explicitly discuss UAP disclosure as a policy position.

Neither the Rockefeller-era pressure nor the 2016 campaign commitments resulted in known disclosure actions.
