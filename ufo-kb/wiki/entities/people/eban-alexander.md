# Eben Alexander

Neurosurgeon who contracted bacterial meningoencephalitis — inflammation of the brain and meninges due to E. coli — causing a week-long coma during which his cortex was, by his own medical assessment, entirely non-functional. He emerged with full cognition intact and a detailed account of a near-death experience that he subsequently published as *Proof of Heaven* (2012).

## Background

Alexander's scientific training and medical expertise make him an unusual NDE witness: he spent his career explaining away NDE accounts on neurobiological grounds and treating them as hallucinations or REM intrusions. His own experience was physiologically distinct in that the E. coli infection inflamed his cortex directly — ruling out the usual neurological explanations. He concluded that consciousness had survived independently of brain activity.

## The NDE Account (kRO5jOa06Qw)

[[jesse-michaels]] summarizes Alexander's experience in [[kRO5jOa06Qw]] in the context of discussing higher-dimensional reality and the UAP phenomenon:

- During the coma, Alexander experienced what he describes as a *"higher-dimensional plane"* — another level of reality inaccessible in ordinary consciousness
- It was characterized by an *"overarching loving energy"* — a recurring motif across many NDE accounts and certain alien abduction reports
- When he returned and attempted to write the experience down, he found *"he could not find the human words to translate it"* — a symbol-rate problem: the felt reality exceeded what language could encode
- He describes his recovery as miraculous given the severity of the cortical damage
