# John Podesta

Former White House Chief of Staff under President Bill Clinton and chair of Hillary Clinton's 2016 presidential campaign. Podesta has been one of the most prominent mainstream political figures to express serious interest in UFO/UAP disclosure, including publicly lamenting at the end of the Obama administration that he had failed to secure UFO disclosure as he had hoped.

## UAP Relevance

[[jesse-michaels]] describes Podesta as "extremely into this topic" — citing him alongside [[dick-cheney]] and George W. Bush as senior political figures who may have been exposed to classified UAP information during their tenures and whose public statements hint at more knowledge than they disclose. Michaels frames them as living witnesses who could, in principle, make statements about what they know.

The Podesta email leaks (2016) included correspondence with astronaut [[edgar-mitchell]] about UAP and efforts to brief presidential candidates. His repeated public statements about wanting UFO disclosure — combined with his senior access during two presidential administrations — make him one of the more plausible political figures to have been read into relevant material.
