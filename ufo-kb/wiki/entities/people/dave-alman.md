# Dave Alman

Dave Alman is the media manager for the [[vasco]] project (Vanishing & Appearing Sources during a Century of Observations), a citizen-science astronomical effort led by [[beatriz-villarroel]] that compares 1950s sky survey plates against modern imagery to identify objects that have appeared or disappeared.

**1952 date connection**: Alman's key contribution to the VASCO research is credited as identifying that the two clusters of anomalous transient point sources found in the Mount Palomar archive — July 19 and July 27, 1952 — coincide exactly with the two weekends of the [[1952-washington-dc-flyover]], the most documented peacetime UAP mass-sighting event in US history. This date alignment is the central observation making the [[1952-nine-transients]] finding UAP-relevant rather than merely astronomically anomalous.

**VASCO context**: The VASCO project is an international academic initiative that has produced peer-reviewed findings on vanishing stars, transient objects, and anomalous astronomical sources. Alman's media role involves communicating findings to public and media audiences; his date-connection insight was apparently made during this communication work rather than in the formal scientific analysis.
