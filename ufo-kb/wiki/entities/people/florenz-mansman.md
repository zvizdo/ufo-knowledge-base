# Florenz Mansman

USAF Major stationed at **Vandenberg Air Force Base** in 1964; the immediate superior of [[bob-jacobs]] when Jacobs operated the telescope-camera tracking system that filmed a UAP interacting with a test missile.

## Role in the 1964 Vandenberg Incident

During the [[1964-vandenberg-bob-jacobs]] incident, Mansman was Jacobs's commanding officer. According to Jacobs, Mansman was present when CIA personnel arrived to confiscate the film and instructed Jacobs never to speak of what he had seen.

For years after Jacobs went public, the US government denied Jacobs had ever worked at Vandenberg. Mansman — after the denials became untenable — eventually confirmed that:
1. Robert Jacobs did in fact work at Vandenberg
2. Jacobs managed a team of approximately **130 people** (making his role and credibility hard to dismiss)
3. Implicitly confirmed the incident by no longer denying it

This makes Mansman a second-source corroboration for the Vandenberg incident — upgrading the corroboration level from single-witness to multi-witness.

## Significance

The pattern of initial denial followed by corroboration is consistent with the mechanism described in [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]]: personnel are instructed to deny, but eventually the institutional pressure to maintain the lie exceeds individuals' willingness to continue when specifics become untenable.
