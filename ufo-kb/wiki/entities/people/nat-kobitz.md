# Nat Kobitz

Nat Kobitz served as Director of Science and Technology for the US Navy. Near the end of his life — dying of cancer — he spoke with [[ross-coulthart]] and disclosed that he had personally seen UAP technology at [[wright-patterson]] Air Force Base.

## Testimony

Kobitz described the material as involving "bonded metals fabricated at the atomic level" and provided other technical details that [[richard-dolan]] found significant enough to want to review. The deathbed context — Kobitz had no career protection left to lose — gives the testimony additional weight in the credibility framework used by researchers.

## Significance

Kobitz's position as Navy Director of Science and Technology placed him at a level of institutional authority where access to classified UAP materials would be plausible. His identification of Wright-Patterson as the location is consistent with a long line of testimony placing exotic materials at that facility.

## Full Disclosure Narrative (V00WcEiKRAY)

[[ross-coulthart]] provided a complete account of how he found Kobitz and what Kobitz disclosed:

**How Coulthart found him**: Using knowledge from his [[echelon]] investigation of Five Eyes surveillance, Coulthart wrote ~160–170 handwritten letters to potential sources (avoiding phones/email due to metadata trails). Kobitz was a "brazen" target — Coulthart did not expect a response.

**Three months of building trust**: They spoke about Kobitz's work in early drone technology (a drone crashed on George W. Bush's ranch after hitting a Secret Service frequency blocker), the lunar lander, and electron beam welding. Kobitz was an expert in electron beam welding — his company made the C-47 nose wheel — and the method leaves no bead.

**The disclosure**: One day Kobitz said "Why don't you ask me the question?" (three times). When Coulthart asked if he had been read into a UAP/alien technology program, Kobitz answered **"Yes" before the question was finished**. Coulthart didn't hear him the first time — he was expecting a denial.

**Additional confirmation**: When separately asked "Have you ever been given anomalous technology that defied prosaic explanation?" — Kobitz gave the same beat response and affirmed, then told the Wright-Patterson story.

**The material**: Taken underground at Wright-Patterson, shown a UAP bulkhead piece under heavy guard. Nearly weightless. Under electron scanning microscope: **two metals bonded at the atomic level, atom by atom** — "Whoever built this had layered it atom by atom." Not a technology Kobitz was aware of, and as a world expert in electron beam welding, he would have known.

**Multiple craft**: Kobitz confirmed the US has recovered multiple craft and implied Roswell, Aztec, and Kingman were all real incidents.

**Source facilitation**: Kobitz then facilitated introductions that became Coulthart's cascade of confirmations. Coulthart has an authorized audio recording; withholds it pending family consent.
