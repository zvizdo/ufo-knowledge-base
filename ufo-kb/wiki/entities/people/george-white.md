# George White

George White was a senior officer in the Federal Bureau of Narcotics (FBN — the predecessor of the DEA) who ran [[operation-midnight-climax]] on behalf of the CIA. He ran the San Francisco safe houses (with some operations in New York) where unwitting subjects were dosed with LSD, observed through one-way mirrors, and studied for behavioral responses.

## The Quote

[[colin-ross]] quotes White as having said in public statements:

> "Where else could a good American boy rape, murder, and kill with impunity?"

This was White's own characterization of his role. [[colin-ross]]'s framing: "That's what he said in public. That was the head of the equivalent of the DEA at the time." White's career trajectory — drug enforcement officer → CIA safe house operator running dosing and observation experiments → head of the successor drug enforcement agency — illustrates the permeability between law enforcement, intelligence, and covert operations.

## Significance

White is not a marginal figure — he was the **operational head** of one of the most morally egregious sub-programs within MK Ultra. His quote is a document of institutional culture: the CIA's covert operations environment created space for acts that would be serious crimes outside that context, and White acknowledged this openly.
