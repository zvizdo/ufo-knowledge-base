# Fabio Furtado

Brazilian farmer whose property was the landing/crash site of the January 1996 Varginha UAP craft. Furtado is a **new witness** brought to light in James Fox's updated film *Moment of Contact: New Revelations of Alien Encounters* (2025 cut); his testimony establishes the final segment of the craft's flight path and confirms it came down on private farmland.

## Background

Furtado had no prior UFO interest. His testimony was obtained by [[james-fox]] in the course of his multi-year Varginha investigation. He did not come forward anonymously — he is named and on camera in the updated film.

## The Sighting

In the early hours of January 1996, Furtado and at least one other person were at the Furtado farm. The craft flew directly over his house — he described it as a cigar shape. It emitted a distinctive "w" sound consistent with a vehicle in distress. After passing over the house it made a final maneuver and landed (or crashed) further away on the property.

His account is consistent with witness [[carlos-de-souza]]'s description of the craft's trajectory: both saw a cigar-shaped object in apparent distress heading toward the same area. Fox's updated film includes a drone survey and a map showing the cumulative sightings, with Furtado's farm as the landing endpoint.

## Significance

Furtado's testimony fills a key gap: previous witness accounts placed the craft before the crash site (de Souza from a distance, the couple's sighting from ~20 m away). Furtado places himself *at* the landing point. His testimony is first-time public; he received no payment.
