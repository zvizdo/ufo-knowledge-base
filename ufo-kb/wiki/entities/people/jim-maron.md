# Jim Maron

Former rock promoter based in Texas, known for having worked with Willie Nelson. Came to UAP attention via [[ross-coulthart]]'s 2021 Australian television story about metallic spheres with anomalous movement.

## The Sphere

Maron possesses a metallic sphere that reportedly moves under its own power on a perfectly level floor — with no perceptible external stimulus. Coulthart and colleagues observed it stationary, then spontaneously move toward a pole, apparently circle it, and reverse — while all observers were seated still. The floor had just been relaid and was verified level by a spirit level prior to the demonstration.

Maron resisted formal testing for years. Coulthart had secured a free industrial X-ray offer from a Chicago contact, but before this could happen, [[brandon-fugel]] arranged to fly the sphere in his private jet to [[skinwalker-ranch]] in Utah for comprehensive lab testing. That arrangement is described as in place as of the recording.

## Connection to Patrick Jackson

[[ross-coulthart]] also references meeting a sphere in the home of [[patrick-jackson]], a Texas man whose sphere may now be in Maron's possession or is a distinct object in the same category.
