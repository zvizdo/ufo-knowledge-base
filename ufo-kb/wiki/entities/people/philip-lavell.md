# Philip Lavell

Philip Lavell is a JFK assassination researcher described by [[richard-dolan]] as knowing the assassination "as well as any person alive." Lavell was present at the same conference where the GQlWf54K_7Y interview was filmed, and Dolan references him as endorsing the UFO component among JFK's assassination motives.

## JFK–UFO Connection

Dolan notes that Lavell accepts the UFO motive alongside the more conventionally documented motives (Federal Reserve, Vietnam policy, Operation Mongoose, Cuba). While Dolan defers to [[danny-sheehan]] for the most detailed reconstruction of the assassination's operational mechanics, he cites Lavell's expertise as independent validation that the UFO thread is not an outlier hypothesis among serious assassination researchers.

> "[Philip Lavell] is someone who I think knows the JFK assassination as well as any person alive."

## Note on Possible Name Confusion

The transcript transcript may conflate Philip Lavell (JFK researcher) with [[paul-laviolette]] (electrogravitics physicist cited separately by Dolan). The two are distinct individuals — LaViolette's electrogravitics paper is what caused the B2 colonel's "that's bullshit" reaction at the Las Vegas conference. Lavell is a separate figure present at the filming location.
