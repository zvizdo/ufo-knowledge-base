# Burkhard Heim

German physicist (1925–2001). A laboratory explosion at age 19 left him deaf and nearly blind, forcing him to work in almost complete isolation. Despite this, he independently developed a unified field theory that attracted serious attention from aerospace research establishments.

**Heim theory**: Heim's unified field theory (published in German, largely untranslated) operates in a 6-dimensional extended spacetime (4 ordinary + 2 "metadimensions"). The theory notably predicted the masses of elementary particles with accuracy exceeding the Standard Model of Heim's era — a result that attracted attention from physicists who found the predictions too accurate to dismiss as coincidence. Later extensions (by Walter Dröscher and Jochem Häuser) expanded to 12 dimensions.

**Propulsion implications**: The extended Heim theory implies a mechanism for converting electromagnetic energy to gravitational energy — which would enable field propulsion capable of exceeding light speed. A rotating magnetic field at sufficient field strength would theoretically generate a gravitophoton field enabling near-FTL travel.

**Lockheed Martin connection**: Per the KB source transcript ([[d9tdJ2SkBKQ]]), Heim's work was recognized by Lockheed Martin in the 1950s. This sits alongside claims that US aerospace contractors explored theoretical gravity manipulation frameworks during the same era that [[electrogravitics]] research was being classified. The six-force framework parallels the theoretical structure needed for UAP propulsion.
