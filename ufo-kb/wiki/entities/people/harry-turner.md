# Harry Turner

Head of the nuclear division of the [[australian-joint-intelligence-organisation]] — Australia's equivalent of the CIA. In 1971, Turner authored a classified memo documenting his assessment of US UFO and anti-gravity research activity through the 1950s and 1960s. The memo is the primary source document discussed in [[5udx_SDdL3Y]].

## The 1971 Memo

See: [[1971-australian-jio-memo]]

Turner's central conclusion was that [[project-blue-book]] was a deliberate deception operation — designed not only to manage the US public but to mislead allied intelligence services into believing American UFO research was superficial. Behind Blue Book, Turner argued, were "real programs far more vital" that represented genuine US investigation and possibly exploitation of UAP phenomena.

The memo:
- Named [[j-robert-oppenheimer]], [[edward-teller]], and [[freeman-dyson]] as deeply involved in US anti-gravity research
- Listed CIA-backed academic outposts (including [[unc-chapel-hill]] as one of them)
- Listed major aerospace contractors involved: Bell Aircraft ([[bell-aircraft]]), Martin Corporation ([[martin-corporation]]), Lear Corporation ([[lear-corporation]])
- Documented all major US inquiries into anti-gravity and UFOs in the 1950s–60s

The fact that a foreign intelligence official — specifically a nuclear division head, not a UFO enthusiast — compiled this assessment lends it institutional weight as a secondary source.

## Extended Career: Maralinga and Cross-Pollination (V00WcEiKRAY)

[[ross-coulthart]] provides additional career detail not in prior sources:

- Turner began his UAP work as a **young physicist recently qualified from Melbourne University** — invited by the Australian government to analyze UAP sightings (a more open-minded era than present).
- Australia was collaborating with the US on Project Blue Book at the time, providing Australian and New Zealand sighting data.
- Turner then became the **supervising physicist at [[maralinga]]** during British nuclear tests in the 1950s–60s. He was asked to investigate multiple UAP sightings that occurred during the tests; those reports remain in Australian archives.
- Similarly associated with the adjacent [[woomera-test-range]] where ICBM tests occurred.
- Australian scientists including Turner were invited to **Pacific nuclear test observations** — a diplomatic concession for allowing British bombs in Australia. Coulthart believes these Pacific interactions were key vectors for knowledge transfer from US nuclear physicists who were aware of the early US legacy UAP program. This explains how Turner could write his 1972 analysis of US crash retrievals with such specificity.
- [[robert-sarbacher]] is specifically mentioned as having visited Australian scientists (in prior sources) — Sarbacher was a significant US-side connection.

## Reese Morgan / Woomera Connection

Coulthart also mentions Reese Morgan, whose grandfather ran the Woomera test range and was secretly involved in British government UAP investigations from the late 1940s–50s. A young researcher (Reese Delton Moore) has published on this; Coulthart posted his article.

## Personal Connection via Bill Chalker

[[bill-chalker]], described as "probably Australia's preeminent UAP researcher," knew Turner personally and helped document his significance. Chalker is Coulthart's bridge to Turner's work.
