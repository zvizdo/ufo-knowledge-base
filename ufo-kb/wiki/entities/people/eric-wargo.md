# Eric Wargo

Researcher and author; guest on [[RofQnByLwOo]] (American Alchemy / Jesse Michaels). His central thesis: the brain is a **hybrid classical/quantum computer**, and the quantum component enables **temporal non-locality** — information from a future knowledge state can propagate backward in time, producing what we experience as precognition, intuition, and déjà vu.

## Background

Wargo synthesizes findings from quantum physics, quantum biology, parapsychology research (especially Stargate), and neuroscience into a unified, materialist model of precognition. His approach is explicitly non-mystical: he is trying to explain anomalous cognition within physical law rather than invoking spirit or supernatural cause.

## Core Thesis: Brain as Quantum Time Machine

### Quantum Coherence in Biology

Wargo points to [[quantum-biology]] as foundational evidence that warm, wet biological systems can maintain quantum coherence:
- Enzyme tunneling (quantum tunneling in catalysis)
- Cryptochrome (Cry4) protein in robin eyes — electron spin used to sense Earth's magnetic field at room temperature
- Possibly quantum entanglement in plant photosynthesis

### Temporal Non-Locality

In quantum mechanics, entangled particles exhibit not only spatial non-locality (Einstein's "spooky action at a distance") but **temporal non-locality**: measurement of Photon B in a delayed-choice double-slit experiment appears to affect the earlier measurement of Photon A retroactively. This is experimentally documented retrocausality.

In quantum computing, **cubits exist in superposition** — not in state 0, not in state 1, not in both, not in neither. Crucially, cubit states can be **reversed** in quantum computation, opening a theoretical channel for information to propagate backward in time.

If the brain contains a quantum subsystem (Penrose-Hameroff [[microtubule-quantum-consciousness]] / [[quantum-biology]]), it could theoretically access its **future knowledge state** through the same temporal non-locality mechanism.

### The Confirmatory Feedback Model

Wargo's most operationally specific claim: remote viewers are not perceiving the present target directly — they are perceiving the **future moment when they receive confirmation** that a target was correct. This explains why:
- Remote viewing works better when feedback is provided promptly and richly
- Remote viewers can perceive a target accurately even before it is selected
- [[hal-puthoff]] confirmed this model as consistent with his view of the program's mechanics

### Evolutionary Advantage

Why would temporal non-locality evolve? Because survival decisions under uncertainty benefit enormously from access to outcome information. Wargo notes humans (and animals) are most intuitively accurate in **life-and-death situations** — consistent with evolutionary selection pressure for retrocausal signaling in survival contexts.

### Anatomical Locus

[[garry-nolan]]'s finding that the **caudate nucleus and putamen** (basal ganglia, reward-prediction system) light up during intuition-based decisions in Go players and show higher neural density in anomalously capable individuals is consistent with Wargo's model: the reward system processes anticipated future outcomes, which is precisely the anatomical location you'd expect a retrocausal signal to surface.

## Self-Fulfilling Prophecy Mechanics (hXYdkcv5TtY)

In [[hXYdkcv5TtY]] Wargo elaborates the full structure of how precognition must work in a [[block-universe]]:

**The physics**: 1980s wormhole physicists found closed timelike curves are self-consistent — no paradox is possible. A billiard ball sent through a wormhole to deflect itself always ends up entering the wormhole. The universe resolves its own loops. Any information transmitted backward in time is already part of the backstory that produced the transmission.

**The implication**: precognition is always self-fulfilling. The future signal changes behavior; the changed behavior produces the prophesied outcome. This is not a limitation — it explains *why* precognitive signals are symbolic, oblique, and not consciously actionable. Full clarity would enable conscious evasion, which would require the universe to produce a paradox. Obliqueness is the universe's paradox-prevention mechanism.

**The Oedipus archetype**: Oedipus fulfills the prophecy precisely through his effort to evade it. Wargo argues this is the structural template for all genuine precognition. The modern cultural analog: [[sigmund-freud]] built his career denying precognition while patients brought him precognitive dreams he explained away — and then lived out a precognitive time loop with his own 1895 dream prefiguring his ~1923 oral cancer.

**[[carl-jung]]'s scarab beetle**: the canonical simple case — a rationalist patient dreams of receiving a golden scarab; as she tells Jung, a live scarab lands on the window; Jung hands it to her. The dream deflected her toward that session context; the session produced the gifting; the gifting fulfilled the dream. She could not have consciously arranged it.

**[[morgan-robertson]]'s Futility (1898)**: described the sinking of SS Titan — largest liner, iceberg, April, North Atlantic, insufficient lifeboats — 14 years before the Titanic. Wargo acknowledges the Skeptics' cherry-picking objection but argues artist precognition is dense and systematic, not rare.

**[[michael-rolando-richards]]**: sculptor who portrayed himself impaled by airplanes in multiple works (including *Tar Baby vs. St. Sebastian*, 1999; also works showing him on a burning meteor, and *Fallen Angel* — torso with wings). The recognition his work received led to a Twin Tower studio; he alone among that cohort stayed overnight on September 10, 2001.

## UFOs, Time Travel, and Quantum Tesseract (hXYdkcv5TtY)

Wargo endorses [[michael-p-masters]]'s Extratemporalis model as the most plausible core hypothesis for genuine UAP — time-traveling humans or time-traveling technology. Key elaborations in this interview:

**Quantum computer as temporal bridge**: a quantum server left plugged in for years/decades/centuries theoretically develops a backward information channel to its own start-up moment. Wargo calls this the "Tesseract" (from science fiction). [[william-gibson]]'s *The Peripheral* is the best available narrative: a quantum server enables a future Londoner to communicate with and direct a drone operator in the present — and to transmit 3D-printing instructions for future technology to present fabricators. This could explain UAP as craft built in the present from future-transmitted designs, operated remotely by future humans.

**[[die-glocke]] as time-dilation device**: Wargo discusses the Die Glocke not primarily as an antigravity device but as a *time dilation* machine — the SS program may have involved creating a different inertial reference frame via high voltage (linked to [[walter-gerlach]], a gravity physicist). Every second outside = a fraction of a second inside, like the time dilation gradient near a black hole. Wargo connects this to Thomas Townsend Brown's interest in gravity-time linkage.

**Multiple future factions**: if time travel becomes possible at any point in the future, it has always been possible in a block universe — time travelers have always been present throughout history. Different future factions (Russian vs. American, etc.) may be competing to nudge timelines to their advantage, much as nations compete now. This explains the incoherence of UAP behavior and the apparent variety of agendas.

**Nuclear sites as leverage points**: UAP appearing consistently near nuclear sites may reflect future humans identifying nuclear weapons as the highest-leverage intervention point for timeline management — the Archimedes lever for humanity's trajectory.

## Philip K. Dick and Jacques Vallée (hXYdkcv5TtY)

Wargo argues [[philip-k-dick]] and [[jacques-vallee]] independently precognized each other's work in 1974–75:

- Vallée was writing *The Invisible College* (1975)
- Dick was writing the first draft of what became *VALIS* — later published posthumously as *Radio Free Albemuth*
- Neither could have read the other's manuscript at that time
- Both featured: a "teaching satellite" / awakened people receiving signals from a distant source designed to save humanity from peril; overlapping conceptual structures

Wargo's interpretation: mutual precognitive convergence, consistent with his thesis that artists absorb future information from the discourse environment they will inhabit.

Dick's *Close Encounters* precognition dream: in ~1974 Dick had a dream featuring small diminutive beings emerging from a spaceship, giving him a piece of technology, and a brownie scored by a fork around its edges labeled as belonging to "Betty Field" (which Dick associated with UFO abductee Betty Hill). Three years later, *Close Encounters of the Third Kind* was released — featuring the exact scene of a character fork-scoring food into a relief map of Devil's Tower, linked to a UFO encounter. The name "Betty Field" → "Betty Hill" was Dick's conscious association; Wargo argues the dream was precognizing the *film* rather than an actual UFO event.

Wargo considers Dick the only artist he has studied who **consciously accepted** that he was precognitive and that his work was influenced by his future.

## Decision Augmentation and Ed May (hXYdkcv5TtY)

[[ed-may]] (who led the Stargate research arm's final decade) developed [[decision-augmentation]] theory: micro-PK results in RNG experiments may be explained entirely by researcher precognition guiding trial start-times. No psychokinesis required.

Wargo considers this potentially "hugely important across science" — if researcher precognition can bias start-timing decisions in RNG experiments, it could be a hidden factor in the replication crisis and any experiment where a human decides when to begin recording.

## The Tesseract and DNA (hXYdkcv5TtY)

Wargo speculates (with admitted tentativeness) that DNA may itself be a form of quantum server — encoding evolutionary information from the organism's future, consistent with Schrödinger's 1944 suggestion in *What is Life?* about aperiodic crystals as information structures. If so, biological evolution might be understood as 3D-printing organisms from instructions transmitted backward through evolutionary time — a radical extension of the retrocausal model.

The helicoidal EM wave type (from [[extended-electrodynamics]] research by Constantine Meyl and others) is shaped like DNA — an intriguing but unconfirmed potential resonance mechanism.

## Open Questions

- Can Wargo's model make falsifiable predictions distinguishing retrocausality from clairvoyance?
- Is confirmatory feedback *required* or merely *facilitative* for psi effects?
- What is the specific quantum mechanism in neurons — microtubules, as Hameroff proposes?
- Does decision augmentation explain the replication crisis in psychology and health sciences?
- Can DNA be shown to exhibit quantum information storage properties consistent with retrocausal encoding?

## Connection to Future Humans Hypothesis (Y26iMB0r-f8)

[[michael-p-masters]] references Wargo and his book *Time Loops* as a theoretical bridge between the remote viewing phenomenon and the future-humans hypothesis. Masters frames: if the brain is a quantum system (Penrose), quantum computers can send information backward in time, and remote viewers may be **precognizing their own future states** — essentially glitching into the future.

This connects to Masters' broader thesis: experiencers may be pre-experiencing future human contact events (through temporal non-locality), and the UFO experience itself may be partially a Time Loop — the experience (in a dream-like or altered state) affects attentional patterns that cause the event to manifest (the scarab beetle / Carl Jung model). Masters quotes Wargo: remote viewing is really precognition, and viewers always need that "deliverable" — they see the future moment of receiving confirmation, not the present target.
