# Byron Ristvet

## Background

Byron Ristvet was a Defense Nuclear Agency (DNA) scientist and senior official at the Department of Energy with specialized knowledge of the 1962 Marshall Islands nuclear test program. His institutional position made him one of the custodians of the test film archives.

## UAP Connection

[[jeffrey-kruchshank]] contacted Ristvet seeking the [[kettle-footage]] — footage from the Kettle 2 camera position during the [[bluegill-triple-prime]] test that allegedly captured the UAP event. According to Kruchshank's account in [[09KP8XVf5nY]], Ristvet told him in 2023 that the DOE was "unable to locate" this specific footage.

The disappearance of precisely the footage that would document the [[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown]] is an open question explicitly flagged in the source. Whether the Kettle 2 footage was deliberately suppressed, reclassified under a different reference, or genuinely lost is unknown.
