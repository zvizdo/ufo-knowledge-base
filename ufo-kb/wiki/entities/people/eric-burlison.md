# Eric Burlison

Republican Congressman from Missouri's 7th Congressional District; former Missouri state legislator. Member of the House Oversight and Accountability Committee. In the UAP context, Burlison is notable for the specificity of his questions at the [[2024-congressional-uap-hearing]] — going beyond general UAP acknowledgment to ask about classified biological and genetic programs.

**Biologics and genetic manipulation question**: During the 2024 hearing, Burlison asked witnesses about "biologics" and specifically about a rumored classified DoD project involving the manipulation of human genetics with non-human genetic material, potentially for human capability enhancement. He framed this as information that had "come up to the hill" — meaning it had been briefed to Congressional staff or members through official channels. The specificity of his question (non-human genetic material, capability enhancement) suggests this was not a general fishing expedition but an attempt to get witnesses to confirm or deny a specific claim he had received through official channels.

**Gatekeeper question**: Burlison also asked who the "gatekeepers" of the UAP program were — who held the access and made decisions about what Congress could and could not see. This question directly addresses the compartmentalization structure the KB documents under [[ufo-secrecy]] and [[ffrdc-uap-architecture]]. [[chris-sharp]]'s Liberation Times reporting about [[glenn-gaffney]] (alleged CIA UAP program gatekeeper) is directly responsive to this question — suggesting that Congressional members asking who the gatekeepers are have been receiving partial answers pointing toward specific individuals.

**Congressional significance**: Burlison's questioning at the 2024 hearing places him alongside [[tim-burchett]], [[anna-paulina-luna]], and [[mike-rounds]] as members of Congress who have received enough classified or confidential briefing to ask very specific questions about program structure, rather than treating UAP as a topic they know nothing about.

**Mexico City Nazca-mummies hearing**: Burlison attended a follow-up congressional hearing in Mexico City focused on the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]]. (His name is rendered "Eric Berles" in the [[HxQN2tkQHs8|American Alchemy episode]] — a transcription/audio artifact of the same person.) Initially skeptical ("there's no way that's real"), he changed his mind after a private conversation with [[david-grusch]] — the Pentagon UAP whistleblower from the 2023 Schumer-amendment testimony. Per Burlison, Grusch told him that the **physiology of some (not all) Nazca mummies is congruent with what he has seen in classified settings** — and qualified this carefully, acknowledging that "some are faked, but some of the mummies… based on the physiology, are congruent." The qualification's specificity reads as authentic engagement rather than blanket endorsement.
