# Victor Brandus

Technical representative from Wright Air Development Center (see [[wright-patterson]]), working under Colonel Albert Boyd who ran the Flight Test Division. Brandus traveled to Los Angeles to witness [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s gravitator experiments — the asymmetric high-voltage capacitor that appeared to produce anomalous thrust or anti-gravity effects.

## The Witnessed Reaction

Brandus's direct quote after witnessing the experiments:

> "Believe it or not, I think I just saw a flying saucer and it frightened me."

This reaction — from a technical representative of the Air Force's premier flight test facility — is one of the most direct documented links between Townsend Brown's experiments and official Wright-Patterson interest. It suggests that WADC was actively monitoring Brown's work through personnel visits, not merely through document review.

## The Surveillance Pattern

[[jesse-michaels]] notes that Wright-Patterson's interest in Brown may have been intensive enough that Brown's daughter Linda reported a man named **Charles Wyatt Miller** — identified as a technical consultant from Wright Field — living with the Brown family and following them wherever they went. Brandus's documented visit is the formal, on-record layer of what appears to have been a more comprehensive surveillance operation.
