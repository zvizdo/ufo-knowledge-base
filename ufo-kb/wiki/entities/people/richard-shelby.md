# Richard Shelby

Richard C. Shelby (b. 1934) served as US Senator from Alabama from 1987 to 2023, making him one of the longest-serving senators of his era. He served for many years on the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence, eventually as chairman (1997–2001), and on the Senate Appropriations Committee (chairman 2015–2021).

**Intelligence Committee role**: Shelby's long tenure on the Senate Intelligence Committee gave him institutional access to classified intelligence programs, including any UAP-related programs that fell within the committee's oversight mandate. He is cited alongside [[jay-rockefeller]], [[ron-wyden]], and [[mario-biaggi]] as a congressional figure with the committee position to seek — and theoretically receive — information about UAP programs.

**Oversight context**: Congressional intelligence committee members who have sought UAP briefings have generally faced compartmentalization that exceeds their oversight authority — a pattern documented across multiple committee members who report being denied access or given incomplete information. Shelby's position as committee chairman during 1997–2001 (overlapping with significant disclosure-adjacent activity, including the [[1997-phoenix-lights]] and the peak of the Greer-era public pressure) places him in the relevant period.
