# Alfred Loedding

Aeronautical engineer at the Air Materiel Command (AMC), Wright Field, Dayton, Ohio. In September 1947, Col. [[harold-mccoy]] transmitted a copy of Loedding's "LD-2" flying disc patent drawing to USAF Washington (Major General George McDonald, AC/AS-2) alongside the Horten RAE Technical Note AERO 1703. Because of patent rights, each reviewer of the drawing was required to witness it with signatures and dates directly on the document.

Loedding's disc design was intended as a comparison reference: AMC T-2 was using it to assess whether any of the reported disc phenomena could correspond to a known US experimental aircraft design. Col. McCoy's letter asking that the drawing be returned after use, and that it be officially recorded with the Patent Office, indicates the design was considered proprietary and possibly secret at the time.

Loedding became one of the principal technical consultants to [[project-sign]] through 1947–1948, contributing aeronautical analysis to Project SIGN's investigation of disc reports.
