# Lou Branscomb

Physicist and national security policy figure. PhD student under [[donald-menzel]] at Harvard Observatory. Connected to the Jason Advisory Group and the US defense establishment.

## The Condon Clearance Connection

In 1966, Branscomb gave [[edward-condon]] his security clearance back. Condon had lost his clearance during the McCarthy era (early 1950s). The restoration of his clearance was the precondition for Condon's appointment as head of the [[condon-commission]] — the University of Colorado UFO study funded by the Air Force that produced a 1968 null-result report and was used to terminate [[project-blue-book]].

The chain:
**[[donald-menzel]]** → PhD advisor → **Lou Branscomb** → restored clearance → **[[edward-condon]]** → ran Condon Commission → killed Project Blue Book

This makes Branscomb a structural node in the [[debunkers-as-atomic-insiders]] network: Menzel's academic lineage directly enabled the institutional mechanism that shut down official UFO research.
