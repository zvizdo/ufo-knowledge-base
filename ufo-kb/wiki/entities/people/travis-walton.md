# Travis Walton

Arizona logger who was struck by a beam from a UFO on November 5, 1975, near Snowflake, Arizona, while working with a six-man logging crew. He disappeared for five days before reappearing in a dazed state on a roadside. All six crew members passed polygraph tests about witnessing the initial incident. The case became the basis for the 1993 film *Fire in the Sky*.

## The Encounter

According to Walton's account, after being struck by the craft's beam he regained consciousness in a medical-type setting surrounded by **small gray beings**. Being a "big strapping fellow," he fought free of their apparent control and fled down a corridor. He then encountered **tall, muscular, broad-shouldered Nordic-type beings** in a room containing a star-map projection device. One of these beings, described as human-looking (someone who "could pass in a crowd on Earth"), led him outside the craft — which was parked inside a much larger room or structure — into another area where additional Nordic beings rendered him unconscious. He awoke on the roadside and saw the craft hovering briefly before it shot straight up.
