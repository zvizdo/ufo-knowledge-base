# Spiro Agnew

## Background

Spiro Theodore Agnew served as Vice President of the United States from 1969 to October 1973 under Richard Nixon. He resigned the vice-presidency under a *nolo contendere* plea to a single tax-evasion count, in the context of a broader Maryland kickback investigation that began during his time as Governor of Maryland.

## Notable Claims

### Pat Price's Pre-Resignation Prediction

[[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]] (Gf_tKn9TaP8) recounts that [[pat-price|Pat Price]] came into the SRI office during the Nixon administration and reported a remote-viewing session of the Oval Office. Price's findings, as Puthoff describes them:

- Something in the Oval Office would harm Nixon, and Nixon would not get through his second term.
- Spiro Agnew would not take over as president because "he goes first."

Puthoff reported the first finding to his CIA contract monitor. A sweep team checked for hidden microwaves and toxic substances and found nothing. The Watergate tape recorder ultimately drove Nixon out. The second finding tracked Agnew's October 1973 resignation, which preceded Nixon's August 1974 resignation by ~10 months.

Puthoff's framing: "The statistical odds of independently predicting both an aborted second term and the VP going first are essentially zero." The episode is one of his standard credibility-illustration anecdotes for [[remote-viewing|remote viewing's]] operational reach.
