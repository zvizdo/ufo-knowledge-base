# Jack Weiner

One of the four witnesses in the [[1976-allagash-abduction]]. Identical twin brother of [[jim-weiner]]. Lives in Vermont. Not a primary speaker in [[QkuDvTFN7ms]] — described primarily through Jim's account and regression transcripts.

Jack and Jim have Rh-negative blood (as does their father). As children in Allentown, Pennsylvania, both shared anomalous experiences in their bedroom — voices, balls of light, covers being pulled off beds — and named the apparent entity "Harry the ghost."

## Physical Aftereffects

The most significant physical evidence from Jack's case is bilateral shin tumors that grew in precisely the same anatomical locations as the bald patches on [[jim-weiner]]'s shins — the exact spots where oval restraint devices had been placed during the on-craft examination of Jim.

- Tumors were pink, bubble-gum-colored, and hard
- Surgically removed at a Vermont teaching hospital; pathology department could not identify the tissue type
- Samples forwarded to the **Air Force Institute of Pathology** (near Washington, D.C.) — received and signed for by a colonel (physician of that rank)
- Air Force pathology finding: tissue most closely resembled wound-healing growth formed when bone marrow is extracted from bone; no definitive identification achieved

## Regression Account — Divergent Detail

Under independent hypnotic regression (conducted separately from all other witnesses, no inter-witness communication permitted), Jack recalled the sperm collection procedure differently than Jim:
- Jim recalled a receptacle device (tubular with a round bottom)
- Jack recalled a conical device with a spike or needle that was inserted into his penis

This discrepancy is notable as evidence of independent rather than collaborative recall — identical twins who underwent the same event separately remembered a specific procedural detail differently.

## The Simultaneous Nightmares

Approximately 12 years after the 1976 event, Jack and Jim — living in different states — independently began having recurring nightmares featuring all four Allagash witnesses in a clinical setting with unknown beings. When Jim told Jack not to worry about it (assuming ordinary nightmares), Jack revealed he had been having the same nightmare repeatedly without telling Jim. This simultaneous surfacing of repressed material drove Jim to seek medical help and ultimately led to [[raymond-fowler]]'s investigation.
