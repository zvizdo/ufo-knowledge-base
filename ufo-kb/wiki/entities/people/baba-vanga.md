# Baba Vanga

Blind Bulgarian prophet born Vangeliya Pandeva Gushterova in 1911 in Strumica (then Ottoman Empire, now North Macedonia); died 1996 in Petrich, Bulgaria. Lost her sight at age 12. Initially drew visitors from nearby villages, then from across Europe and the world. Appointments were booked a year in advance by English bankers, Arab sheikhs, prime ministers, and heads of state. The French and Belgian Academies of Sciences officially recognized her in the 1970s as "humanity's number one phenomenon." Her prediction accuracy rate is reported (by Ilyumzhinov, who knew her personally for decades) at approximately 90–95%.

## Relationship with Kirsan Ilyumzhinov

[[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]] met Baba Vanga 20–40 times over many years, beginning when he was 29 years old and planning to pursue business in Moscow, Switzerland, or New York — not politics. She immediately predicted he would "go back home" and "be the leader." He dismissed it. Six months later he was nominated for the presidency of Kalmykia and won a fiercely contested election.

In April 1995 she predicted he would lead "a very very large global organization of the smartest people — chess players." On November 24, 1995, he was elected [[fide]] president. She sent him a telegram the night before the 1993 election results were announced — addressed to "President Kiren."

Of her 10 major predictions for Ilyumzhinov, 8 came true.

## Mechanism of Vision

Baba Vanga described her gift as a "channel or corridor" that opened — she didn't know how. Modern language she offered: she was like a "cosmic computer" antenna, accessing a database containing all past and future information recorded on a "hard drive." She saw images; she read the information; she said it aloud.

She could read minds the way [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]] described NHI communication — answers before the question was finished.

## NHI Awareness and Subterranean Beings

During walks together, she would stop at flowers and converse with invisible presences. When asked who she was speaking with, she named specific deceased individuals and entities. Ilyumzhinov once asked directly: "Are we alone here?" She reportedly hit him lightly and said: "What's wrong with you? Can't you see? **It's all around you. There it is. There it is. It's absolutely everywhere — on the ground and underneath the ground and up in the sky.**"

She pointed downward when Ilyumzhinov asked about underground beings and said "they live there." This aligns with the [[subterranean-civilizations]] hypothesis and the Kalmyk Jangar epic descriptions of underworld creatures.

She described her experience as a torment: simultaneously present in multiple time frames — speaking to the dead and to those not yet born — while needing to constantly return to ordinary reality. On some days she could not receive anyone: "I don't hear anything. I don't see anything."

She said that materialized entities (extraterrestrials) might be "souls from past lives" — aligning with [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]]'s Buddhist worldview where souls reincarnate through 108 lives.
