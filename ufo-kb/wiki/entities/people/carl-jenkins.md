# Carl Jenkins

CIA paramilitary operations officer; identified by [[danny-sheehan]] as a core member of the covert operations network that included [[david-morales]], [[rip-robertson]], and others who Sheehan claims carried out the JFK assassination. Jenkins operated within the [[s-force]] structure and was part of the Cuba-focused paramilitary apparatus that included [[operation-40]] and [[zr-rifle]].

Jenkins is described as a trainer and operator within the CIA's anti-Castro operations, later allegedly repurposed for domestic operations.
