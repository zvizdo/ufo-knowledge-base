# Doug Wolf

Doug Wolf is named by [[uap-gerb]] as the **first director of the CIA's Office of Global Access (OGA)** — a key node in the UAP legacy program administrator layer. His career trajectory gives him a uniquely wide view across the intelligence community's collection and acquisition apparatus:

- 16 years in the [[nro]] (National Reconnaissance Office), including serving as **executive assistant to the director**
- CIA **DS&T** (Directorate of Science & Technology) deputy director
- Founded the **Office of Global Access** (OGA)
- **DDNI ATNF** — Deputy Director for National Intelligence for Acquisition, Technology, and Facilities — with **direct oversight over NRO acquisitions**

In UAPGerb's pyramid model, the CIA DS&T is one of the intelligence-agency directorates identified as a legacy-program administrator.
