# Myrna Hansen

New Mexico woman whose abduction account — developed under hypnotic regression — became one of the foundational sources for the [[dulce-underground-base]] mythology. Her case was investigated by [[paul-benowitz]] in the early 1980s as part of his broader research into anomalous activity near [[kirtland-afb]].

## The Case

Benowitz worked with Hansen and sought the involvement of psychologist [[leo-sprinkle]] to conduct hypnotic regression sessions. Under regression, Hansen described being taken to an underground facility — accounts that Benowitz interpreted as confirmation of an alien base in the Dulce / Archeleta Mesa area of northwestern New Mexico. Greg Bishop notes that at the time, Bud Hopkins's foundational abduction literature had not yet been published, so Leo Sprinkle was one of the very few researchers who knew how to work with abductee accounts.

According to [[greg-bishop]], the content of Hansen's regression sessions "seeded so much of the UFO lore afterward." He acknowledges that independent people later claimed similar experiences but cannot determine whether those corroborations were genuine or reflected contamination from the same AFOSI disinformation environment that was simultaneously feeding Benowitz.

## Epistemic Status

The Myrna Hansen case sits at the intersection of genuine experiencer testimony and one of the most documented disinformation operations in UFO history. Whether her account:
- Reflects a genuine abduction experience
- Was shaped by leading questioning from Benowitz (who was himself being manipulated)
- Was amplified or distorted by the AFOSI disinformation environment

...cannot be resolved from available evidence. Greg Bishop does not dismiss it but also does not endorse it as verified. (Greg Bishop, via [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])
