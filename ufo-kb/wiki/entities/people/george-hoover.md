# George Hoover

US Navy Commander who held top-secret clearance throughout most of his career and is cited by [[michael-p-masters]] as one of the earliest and most credentialed insider corroborators of the [[future-humans-hypothesis]]. Hoover privately stated that the beings recovered at Roswell were **time-traveling humans from the future** — not extraterrestrials.

## Claim

Masters cites Hoover as having admitted (to researchers or associates; details of the disclosure context not given in the source) that the beings involved in the [[1947-roswell]] crash were time-traveling humans. His top-secret clearance throughout most of his career gives this claim insider weight — he would have had access to classified information about the Roswell recovery.

Hoover is paired by Masters with [[herman-oberth]]'s time-space coordinate statement as the two strongest pre-existing institutional corroborators of the Extratemporalis model — making them the "George Hoover and Herman Oberth" anchor pair for the [[future-humans-hypothesis]].
