# Josh Goldberg

Physicist (PhD from Syracuse University) who led the **Aerospace Research Laboratory** at Wright Air Development Center — the physics research group within WADC (see [[wright-patterson]]) whose explicit institutional mandate was to figure out how to beat gravity.

## Significance

Goldberg's group represents the formal, institutionalized face of WADC's anti-gravity interest. While [[victor-brandus]]'s reaction to Brown's experiments and [[barry-goldwater]]'s blocked access to restricted rooms represent the phenomenological/security layer, Goldberg's Aerospace Research Laboratory is the theoretical physics layer — the Air Force formally employing physicists to work on general relativity and anti-gravity.

His group's existence confirms that Wright-Patterson's interest in anti-gravity was not informal or peripheral: it was an explicitly funded, directorate-level program.
