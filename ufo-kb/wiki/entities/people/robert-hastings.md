# Robert Hastings

Journalist and independent researcher best known for *UFOs and Nukes: Extraordinary Encounters at Nuclear Weapons Sites* ([[ufos-and-nukes]]), which documents 167 security-cleared witnesses to UAP activity at nuclear weapons facilities. Described by Michaels as "probably the authoritative chronicle" of UFO-nuclear incidents.

## Background

Hastings is the son of a **special weapons officer** stationed at [[malmstrom-air-force-base]], Montana — a detail that places him inside the nuclear-security establishment from childhood ([[TH10mD9hQas]]). In the second half of March 1967 — likely overlapping with the [[1967-oscar-flight]] and [[1967-echo-flight]] incidents — Hastings was a teenager working as a janitor at the base. An **FAA supervisor** casually mentioned he was tracking five unknowns on his scope and called Hastings over to look. This single observation set him on a decades-long investigation. The existing page described this person as "a radar operator"; [[TH10mD9hQas]] specifies him as an FAA supervisor.

## Notable Claims

- 167 Q-cleared witnesses documented; many signed NDAs immediately after their sightings.
- The UFO-nuclear link is "ubiquitous and widespread" across multiple facilities globally.
- Of the 167 documented witnesses, approximately **7–8% had CE3 encounters** (beings sighted) — and Hastings notes that number is almost certainly an undercount given military cultures of secrecy around CE3 disclosures. At a nuclear base with a partner present "always under the twin rule," the proportion who experienced CE3 but never volunteered the detail is likely much higher.
- [[richard-barth]] is case #170 in his corpus (as of his American Alchemy interview). Hastings acquired Barth's DD-214 service records, called three separate friends to confirm Barth told them the same story before UAP became mainstream, and connected Barth to [[bob-jacobs]]. Hastings himself is a UFO experiencer (described in *Confessions*, his memoir), including a nasal implant of his own.
- [[mario-woods]] is in his corpus; Hastings told him: "Either you were in the right place at the right time, or the wrong place at the right time — but you were there."

## Kevin Knuth's Entry Point

[[kevin-knuth]] credits the Hastings 2010 press conference (hosted by Hastings with six Air Force veterans including Robert Salas) as the pivotal moment that drew him into UAP research. In 2015, while preparing a lecture, Knuth stumbled onto the press conference video and recognized the Malmstrom missile-shutdown story — which he had been told as a "running joke" in grad school in 1988 — was real and had been ongoing for 20 years before he first heard it. He credits Hastings's assembly of the witness testimony as the wake-up call that made him take the phenomenon seriously.

## AARO Betrayal and Communications Surveillance (fzvwBBSmWYA)

In his detailed American Alchemy interview, Hastings describes two critical developments:

**AARO betrayal:** In early 2023, retired Senate Armed Services Committee investigator [[kirk-mcconnell]] contacted Hastings asking him to assemble nuclear-site veterans for AARO interviews. Roughly 11 veterans agreed and spoke with AARO. Outcome: (1) AARO did not record a single session — only "took notes"; (2) [[sean-kirkpatrick]] then told Congress "no evidence of extraterrestrials" point-blank. Veterans wrote to Hastings saying "Arrow betrayed us." Hastings calls AARO "the Condon Committee 2.0."

Additionally, Kirkpatrick's résumé includes: DOE grant at age 17 for work at Brookhaven Lab; research at Oakridge National Laboratory; a company called "Nonlinear [something] LLC" — Hastings notes nonlinear algebra is used in gravity research and some of the "weirder physics." Kirk Patrick reportedly went to check on the Big Sur film in a Pentagon safe after Elizondo disclosed its location, then claimed it was not there.

**Communications surveillance:** After each phone interview with nuclear-site veterans in early 1982, within minutes of hanging up, the phone would ring and there would be "nothing but heavy breathing." Hastings is certain his communications were monitored.

**Confession:** His second book is a personal memoir titled *Confession* revealing his own UAP experiences, including a nasal implant. He noted some concern about disclosing this as it might affect credibility, but stated it aligns with what ~7-8% of his 167 witnesses also experienced (CE3 cases).
