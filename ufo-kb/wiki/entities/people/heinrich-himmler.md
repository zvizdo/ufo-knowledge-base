# Heinrich Himmler

Reichsführer-SS (1929–1945); head of the SS, Gestapo, and concentration camp system; Minister of the Interior (1943–1945). Second most powerful figure in Nazi Germany after Hitler. Committed suicide by cyanide on May 23, 1945 after capture by British forces.

**SS-Ahnenerbe connection**: Himmler founded the [[ss-ahnenerbe]] (Ancestral Heritage Research and Teaching Society) in 1935 as a research arm of the SS to study German prehistory, racial science, occultism, and "ancestral heritage." Under his direction, the Ahnenerbe conducted archaeological expeditions (Tibet, Scandinavia, Greece), paranormal research, and investigations into anomalous phenomena. In the KB, the Ahnenerbe is cited as evidence of official Nazi engagement with occult and potentially UAP-adjacent phenomena, and as the institutional precursor to post-war US intelligence programs that employed Ahnenerbe-connected scientists.

**Post-war intelligence connections**: Several Ahnenerbe-connected scientists and researchers were absorbed into US programs via [[operation-paperclip]] (Operation Paperclip). The lineage from Nazi occult-science research to US classified programs is part of the broader KB thesis about how pre-war Nazi interest in anomalous phenomena fed into post-war American classified research.
