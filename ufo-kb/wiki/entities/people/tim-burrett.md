# Tim Burrett

Tennessee Congressman who has been attempting to investigate the [[scientists-targeting-pattern]] — the cluster of top US researchers in sensitive fields who went missing or died in 2025–2026. Burrett is, per the [[wlsQIbpEKSM]] episode, the only elected official who has publicly acknowledged the pattern as a legitimate investigation priority.

**The investigation and the stonewalling**: Burrett told the Daily Mail: *"The numbers seem very high in these certain areas of research. I think we'd better be paying attention and I don't think we should trust our government."* He claimed that US intelligence agencies were actively blocking his inquiries — refusing cooperation, failing to provide information, and in at least one case preventing congressional staff access to relevant records. No specific findings from his investigation have been disclosed publicly as of the source date.

**Context**: The 2025–2026 cluster includes [[neil-mccasland]] (AFRL commander, disappeared 8 days after Trump's UAP announcement), [[nuno-lorero]] (MIT plasma physicist, murdered), [[carl-gilmore]] (Caltech/JPL astronomer, murdered), [[monica-resza]] (Mandeloy co-inventor, disappeared), and [[melissa-casillas]] (Los Alamos admin, disappeared). Each case individually has a plausible individual explanation; the cluster pattern at the intersection of classified defense research and UAP-adjacent science is what Burrett treats as significant.

**Significance as official acknowledgment**: Congressional investigation of the scientists-targeting pattern is qualitatively different from independent researcher documentation — it creates a paper trail, triggers statutory obligations, and makes stonewalling an official act rather than an informal one. Burrett's public disclosure of the stonewalling is itself a data point for the pattern: if the deaths were unremarkable, intelligence agencies would have no motive to obstruct a congressional inquiry.
