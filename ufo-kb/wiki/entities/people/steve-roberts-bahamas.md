# Steve Roberts (Bahamas)

US Army colleague of [[joe-mcmoneagle]] stationed at the downrange Cape Canaveral tracking center on Eleuthera Island (the Bahamas) in the mid-to-late 1960s. Present during the [[0000-mcmoneagle-bahamas-disc-encounter]] — a rotating disc with a cone of light that McMoneagle and Roberts encountered while walking back across sand dunes to their quarters after an outdoor movie and bar visit.

## The Encounter and Aftermath

Roberts was hospitalized at **Homestead Air Force Base** the morning after the encounter. Medical finding: burn injuries severe enough that the **pattern of his shirt was burned into his chest** (Roberts was pale-skinned from not swimming; McMoneagle was dark-skinned from diving, which reduced his injury). The attending Navy medic at the tracking station tore up McMoneagle's paperwork immediately when McMoneagle mentioned a "circular thing," gave him iodide pills for two weeks, implied radiation poisoning, and told him never to bring it up again.

**Roberts' memory:** He retained only one account of the incident: "taking the top off a radiator on a red truck, sprayed by hot water." There are no red trucks on the naval base — all are Navy blue or white. He could not remember the movies, the bar, or anything before or after the encounter except this confabulated memory. When McMoneagle told him what actually happened: "What do you mean?" — no recognition. Years later, McMoneagle introduced Roberts to his wife during a vacation. Roberts still maintained only the red truck account.

**Tracking station equipment:** All screens at the tracking station were "whited out" — pure snow — for the entire duration of the encounter.

The pact: McMoneagle and Roberts agreed that night never to speak of it, since they both valued their Bahamas assignment over being rotated to Vietnam.
