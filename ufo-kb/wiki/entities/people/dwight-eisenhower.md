# Dwight D. Eisenhower

34th President of the United States (1953–1961). Supreme Allied Commander in Europe during WWII. Former Army General of the Army (5 stars). In the KB, Eisenhower is primarily referenced in the context of claimed NHI contact and the institutional architecture of early UAP program secrecy.

**1954 Meeting Claim**: Multiple testimonies assert Eisenhower made an unannounced departure from a Palm Springs vacation to travel to [[edwards-air-force-base]] (or Holloman AFB) for a meeting with non-human entities. See → [[1954-eisenhower-ufo-meeting]]. Not confirmed in primary sources; contested.

**"Eisenhower Treaty" era**: [[hal-puthoff]] and other KB sources refer to an "Eisenhower treaty" — an alleged formal arrangement between the US government and NHI in the mid-1950s, purportedly covering non-interference, technology transfer, and human abduction limits. The specific terms vary across accounts and no primary documentation is in the public domain.

**Institutional architecture**: Eisenhower's administration established or restructured many of the agencies and programs that frame UAP secrecy in the KB: the NSA (1952, pre-presidency but signed by Truman at Eisenhower's direction), the U-2 program under the CIA, and the framework for deep black Special Access Programs. His famous farewell warning about the "military-industrial complex" (January 1961) is cited in KB contexts as the outgoing acknowledgment of exactly the unaccountable private-contractor programs that UAP secrecy may operate through.
