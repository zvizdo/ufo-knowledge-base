# Bob Emenegger

Documentary filmmaker who, with [[allan-sandler]], produced "UFOs: Past, Present and Future" in the 1970s — a production notable for unprecedented access to active US Air Force bases and active/retired military personnel. The film included a deliberately veiled reference to a landing at [[holloman-air-force-base]]: phrased as an event that "may have already happened or may happen."

## Confirmation to James Fox

[[james-fox]] interviewed Emenegger (doing an extended recorded session) about the Holloman event. Emenegger confirmed to Fox: the event happened, they had obtained footage, and at the last minute it was pulled. He did not specify who pulled it or why. Fox quotes him saying they were "going to get the video and feature it in the film" and then "at the last minute they pulled it."

Emenegger had done Fox's extended interview before dying. Fox notes Emenegger was "dead" by the time the Logan Paul/Chuck Clark story broke — suggesting this was in the 2020s.

## The "UFOs: Past, Present and Future" Plausible Deniability

Fox's interpretation: every other element of the film was "so carefully choreographed" with unprecedented military cooperation — which itself signals something significant was happening with that production. The Holloman reference was left in a deliberately ambiguous tense as plausible deniability.

## Note: Emenegger Never Knew Allan Saw the Footage

[[allan-sandler]] never told Emenegger that he had personally seen the Holloman footage at [[norton-air-force-base]]. Fox put them on the phone together; the revelation came out only then.
