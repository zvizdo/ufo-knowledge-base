# Johnny Roselli

Italian-American organized crime figure who served as the CIA's primary mob liaison for the Castro assassination plots. Born Filippo Sacco; changed identity after an early deportation order. Rose through the Chicago Outfit and established himself as the mob's representative in Las Vegas and Hollywood. His relationship with [[robert-maheu]] (the CIA/[[howard-hughes]] fixer) formalized the CIA-mob partnership in [[operation-mongoose]].

## CIA-Mob Connection

Roselli was recruited by [[robert-maheu]] at CIA direction circa 1960 to enlist organized crime in killing Fidel Castro. He in turn brought in [[sam-giancana]] (Chicago boss) and Santos Trafficante Jr. ([[santos-trafficante]]). The plots never succeeded. The CIA's Inspector General report of 1967 documented these relationships; they became public via the Church Committee (1975–76).

## Church Committee and Death

Roselli testified before the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence (Church Committee) in 1975 about the Castro plots. He was murdered in July 1976 — his dismembered body found in a floating oil drum in Miami — before he could testify further. The Church Committee considered his murder a potential act to silence testimony. [[sam-giancana]] had been murdered the year before (June 1975), also before his planned testimony.

## Relevance to JFK and UAP Discourse

In [[danny-sheehan]]'s S Force thesis and the [[jfk-assassination-ufo]] framework, Roselli occupies a key structural position: as a CIA asset with mob enforcement capability, he represents the covert action infrastructure that — Sheehan argues — was also used to enforce UAP secrecy. The same personnel (Maheu, Roselli, Giancana, Trafficante) who ran MONGOOSE were, in some theories, involved in JFK's assassination as blowback for Kennedy's apparent wavering on the Cuba project.
