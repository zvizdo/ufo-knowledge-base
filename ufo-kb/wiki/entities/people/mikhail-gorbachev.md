# Mikhail Gorbachev

General Secretary of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union (1985–1991) and last head of state of the USSR. Architect of glasnost and perestroika; Nobel Peace Prize 1990. In the UFO/UAP discourse he appears as a relay node: he did not personally witness the Nixon-Brezhnev UFO folder meeting, but he received a direct account from [[yuri-andropov]] who was present, and subsequently relayed it to [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]].

## UAP-Relevant Role

### The Nixon–Brezhnev UFO Folder (Hearsay from Andropov)

According to [[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]]'s account, Gorbachev told Ilyumzhinov the following story attributed to [[yuri-andropov]] (KGB chairman):

At the first major meeting between [[leonid-brezhnev]] and President [[richard-nixon]], after the formal session had ended and only a few people remained, Nixon opened a small folder and showed its contents to Brezhnev. Andropov was present in his capacity as KGB chairman. The contents of the folder were never specified, but the immediate aftermath was: all Soviet and US lunar exploration funding was halted completely and never resumed. Both sides simultaneously abandoned advanced Moon missions that were already being prepared.

This story was relayed in a private three-way meeting at Ilyumzhinov's Moscow residence involving Gorbachev, [[henry-kissinger]], and Ilyumzhinov. Gorbachev told the story and then pushed Kissinger: "Come on, Henry, tell him. You should tell him everything." Kissinger smiled but said nothing at that meeting. In a later meeting Kissinger spoke more openly of his familiarity with UAP documents.

### Comments on UFO/NHI

Gorbachev separately said (according to Ilyumzhinov) that "there are various civilizations that have made contact with representatives of our [civilization]." This is presented as Gorbachev's own commentary, distinct from the Andropov story.

- Gorbachev is two degrees of separation from the actual Nixon-Brezhnev meeting (Andropov → Gorbachev → Ilyumzhinov)

- His pushing of Kissinger to "tell everything" implies he believed Kissinger had direct knowledge, not just the same secondhand account
