# Michael Mazzola

Documentary filmmaker who has spent years covering the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] case. Author of the book *Catastrophic Disclosure* and director of the forthcoming documentary *This Is Not a Hoax*. Jesse Michaels credits Mazzola with facilitating access to the Peru trip.

## Background

Has conducted extensive fieldwork including visiting the supposed discovery cave with Leandro. His frank assessment that Leandro is probably showing the wrong cave — and that the real source is a larger subterranean tunnel system — is a significant acknowledgment undermining part of the provenance chain.

## Notable Claims

- The cave Leandro shows tourists (charging ~$3,000/tour) is too small to have contained 200+ body parts; he believes it is not the real discovery site.
- The actual source is likely a large subterranean tunnel system in the region.
- Documents the [[peruvian-ministry-of-culture]]'s bad-faith actions, including press conference raids.
