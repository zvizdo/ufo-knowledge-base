# Dr. Venad

## Background

Dr. Venad (also spelled "Venade") was an Indian academic — a professor at a university in Bombay — and a practitioner of theosophy and spiritualism. He was a close friend of Mahatma Gandhi. At the time of the events described, he was on a lecture tour of the United States, speaking at Rotary clubs and dinner parties about theosophy and spiritualism.

Very little information about him has been recoverable by researchers. [[greg-malazzi]] notes that despite extensive searching, the historical record on Venad is extremely thin.

## The Nine Channeling — 1952

Dr. Venad arrived at [[andrija-puharich]]'s [[roundtable-foundation]] in Rockland, Maine, through recommendation from one of Puharich's contacts. On the first night of his visit — New Year's Eve 1952 — he unexpectedly fell into a trance. Someone grabbed a tape recorder (the recordings still exist in Puharich's archive), and Venad began speaking in a voice that identified itself as "the Nine," delivering extensive philosophical and cosmological content.

This was the founding event of [[the-nine]] contact tradition — the same nine entities that would later communicate through [[uri-geller]] in 1971 when Puharich hypnotized him in Israel.

The character "Venad" in Star Trek: Deep Space Nine traces back to this historical figure — [[gene-roddenberry]] attended Puharich's later channeling sessions and incorporated the name.

## Subsequent Disappearance from Record

Venad remained at the Roundtable Foundation through approximately 1953–54, then returned to India. Puharich — despite this being one of the most consequential events of his life — almost never mentioned Venad again in his public writing or recorded conversations. Malazzi identifies this conspicuous silence as the beginning of the conspiracy around what the Nine sessions really were.

## Significance

Venad's background as a Gandhian intellectual and theosophist stands in contrast to Puharich's later (self-serving) characterization of him as a random psychic who spontaneously appeared. The suppression of Venad's biography by Puharich raises questions about whether the 1952 channeling was orchestrated rather than spontaneous.
