# Charles Chase

Charles Chase served as **director of Lockheed's Revolutionary Projects Organization** within [[lockheed-skunk-works]], one of the most classified aerospace divisions in the world.

## Levitation Briefing

Chase invited Dr. [[jeffrey-kripal]] (Rice University, philosophy and religious studies) to give a **private talk to top Skunk Works engineers on levitation**. The subject: shamans, saints, and monks who reported floating during intense spiritual or meditative states. Chase wanted the engineers to hear these accounts — framed as serious research material, not curiosity. This suggests Chase believed consciousness-based effects might be relevant to advanced propulsion research.

## Secret Machines Advisory Role

Chase, alongside [[robert-weiss]], [[james-ryder]], and Air Force Research Lab Commander Neil McCaslin, formally advised [[tom-delonge]] and [[peter-levenda]]'s *[[secret-machines]]* project. According to DeLonge and Levenda, these elite scientists frequently circled back to **human consciousness** as the central variable in their conversations — and the core thesis of *Secret Machines* (that UAP are "mystical machines") reflects this advisory input.

## Significance

Chase represents the clearest named institutional connection between a classified aerospace program and formal inquiry into consciousness as a propulsion or contact mechanism. Not a fringe enthusiast — a director of revolutionary projects at Skunk Works.
