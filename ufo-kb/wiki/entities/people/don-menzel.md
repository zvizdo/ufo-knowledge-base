# Don Menzel

Harvard astrophysicist, director of the Harvard College Observatory, and the most prominent public UFO debunker of the 1950s–1960s. Wrote three books arguing that all UFO sightings had prosaic explanations. Publicly, he was the scientific establishment's most visible skeptic on the topic.

## The JFK Contradiction

Per [[harold-malmgren]] in [[09KP8XVf5nY]], Menzel privately admitted to President Kennedy that he held **some of the deepest clearances across the board** — CIA, NSA, and Navy — despite his public posture as a debunker. Malmgren cites this as evidence that JFK had access to the full picture of UAP reality through personal relationships with insiders even before taking office.

This creates the same pattern as James Jesus Angleton's relationship with UFO programs: a figure who presents publicly as a skeptic or unrelated actor, but privately operates within the classification structure.

## Significance

If Menzel's debunking was cover (consistent with his disclosed clearances), it represents one of the most strategically placed disinformation agents in UAP history — shaping the scientific consensus against UAP seriousness from the most credible possible institutional position.
