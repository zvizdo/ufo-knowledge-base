# Dr. Krangle

Named corroborating witness who went on record with [[jeremy-corbell]] and stated: "I was in security briefings with Bob Lazar the physicist at Los Angeles." The partial accuracy — "Los Angeles" is likely a transcription artifact for "Los Alamos" given the context — does not undercut the core claim.

Krangle is described by Corbell as someone who "came forward," suggesting voluntary disclosure. This makes him distinct from other witnesses who refused to go on camera because they are still employed at classified facilities.

## Significance

[[bob-lazar]]'s Los Alamos employment was one of the most aggressively contested aspects of his account in the 1980s and 1990s. Krangle's on-record statement is one of the few independent human confirmations (alongside the 1982 phone directory and the Los Alamos newspaper article) that Lazar was a recognized physicist there.
