# Mark Croa

Commercial fisherman based in the Florida Keys. Tim Gallaudet met him through his fishing guide while visiting Key West "last year" (c. 2023). They spoke by phone.

## UAP Sightings

- Has observed **large, lit, cylindrical UAP** in the skies off the Florida Keys for more than ten years
- Regularly sees **jets from a local Florida Air Force base scramble** in pursuit of the objects
- Has been publicly interviewed about his sightings

## Jackie Gleason Story

As a youth, Croa worked as a janitor and assistant at a Florida golf clubhouse where [[jackie-gleason]] was a regular player. Later in life, he met and spoke with Gleason's widow. She told him that the story of [[richard-nixon]] taking Gleason to a southern Air Force base to see crashed UAP wreckage and alien bodies was "100% legit" — and that Gleason had been so disturbed that he did not eat for a week and his "whole world view had been changed."
