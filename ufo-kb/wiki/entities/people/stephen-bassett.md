# Stephen Bassett

Professional UAP disclosure lobbyist and activist; founder of the **Paradigm Research Group (PRG)**, the only registered lobbying organization in Washington DC specifically focused on UAP disclosure. Bassett coined or popularized the term "Paradigm Research Group" and has been a continuous presence in the disclosure advocacy space since the 1990s.

[[danny-sheehan]] identifies Bassett as an associate with whom he has had the standard public-interest attorney-client relationship discussion — explicit agreement that both the client's interests and the broader public interest in disclosure are being represented. This suggests Sheehan has provided legal counsel or consultation to Bassett or PRG at some point.

## Paradigm Research Group

PRG has organized multiple advocacy events, including the Citizens Hearing on Disclosure (National Press Club, 2013) — a mock congressional hearing with former members of Congress as committee members and UAP witnesses testifying. Bassett has been the consistent organizer of the broader disclosure-advocacy coalition that now includes [[new-paradigm-institute]], MUFON, and other groups.
