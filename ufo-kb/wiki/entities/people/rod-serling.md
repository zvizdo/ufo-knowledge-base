# Rod Serling

American screenwriter and television producer (1924–1975), creator of *The Twilight Zone* (1959–1964) and *Night Gallery* (1969–1973). One of the principal mid-century channels by which UFO and paranormal themes reached mainstream American audiences.

## Background

[[jesse-michaels]] disclosed in his appearance with [[diana-pasulka]] that Serling was the godfather of his father — a connection Jesse had not previously stated publicly. Per Jesse, Serling produced what he describes as "the most famous UFO documentary" of the late 1950s / 1960s era.

## Notable Claims / Cultural Output

- *The Twilight Zone* episodes recurringly engaged UFO/contactee tropes
- Hosted/narrated *UFOs: Past, Present, and Future* (1974) — one of the more widely seen mainstream UFO documentaries of the era
- Embodied a model of "UFO-curious mainstream entertainment figure" decades before such positions were common
