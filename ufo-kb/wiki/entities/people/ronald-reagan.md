# Ronald Reagan

40th President of the United States (1981–1989), former California Governor and Hollywood actor. In the UAP context, Reagan is notable for documented statements suggesting personal belief in or awareness of the alien/NHI scenario:

**E.T. Screening (1982)**: After a private White House screening of Steven Spielberg's *E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial*, Reagan reportedly leaned to Spielberg and said: "You know, there are a number of people in this room who know that everything on that screen is absolutely true." The statement has been cited by researchers as possible signaling about classified knowledge. Spielberg has described the remark publicly.

**Geneva Summit with Gorbachev (1985)**: Reagan reportedly told Gorbachev that the U.S. and Soviet Union would immediately cooperate if Earth faced an alien threat. Gorbachev confirmed this exchange publicly in 1987. The remark occurred outside scheduled proceedings.

**UN General Assembly Speech (1987)**: Reagan stated publicly: "I occasionally think how quickly our differences worldwide would vanish if we were facing an alien threat from outside this world." The speech was widely covered.

**SDI**: His Strategic Defense Initiative ("Star Wars") was ostensibly designed to shoot down Soviet ICBMs; some researchers argue the system's true purpose included defense against non-terrestrial craft.
