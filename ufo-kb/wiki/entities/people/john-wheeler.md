# John Wheeler

## Background

John Archibald Wheeler (1911–2008) was one of the 20th century's most influential theoretical physicists. Key contributions:

- Coined the term **"black hole"**
- Coined the phrase **"it from bit"** — the claim that all physical reality is constituted by information (bits), not matter or energy in themselves
- Supervised Richard Feynman's PhD thesis (from which the Feynman path integral and QED emerged)
- Supervised Hugh Everett's PhD thesis (the Many Worlds / Multiverse interpretation)
- Co-authored the standard graduate text *Gravitation* (with Misner and Thorne)
- Developed the **participatory universe** model — the observer doesn't just measure reality; observers participate in generating it; connects directly to the Wheeler observer model that [[donald-hoffman]]'s consciousness theory and [[bob-maguire]]'s hypergraph mapping try to formalize

## Bob Maguire's Personal Encounters

[[bob-maguire]] worked at the [[institute-for-defense-analysis]] in New Jersey. His wife was a nurse at a retirement community where Wheeler lived. She arranged an autograph; Wheeler invited Bob to visit. Bob became a **regular visitor**, and occasionally served as Wheeler's chauffeur to talks at the Institute for Advanced Study at Princeton, where Bob sat in the back of the room watching "these people cow to Wheeler."

Freeman Dyson was also an IDA Advisory Board member during this period, and Bob interacted with Dyson separately (at church and IDA meetings).

Wheeler shared his understanding of gravitation with Bob, clarifying parts of the textbook. Their conversations ranged from physics to the nature of observation and information.

## "It from Bit"

Wheeler's "it from bit" formulation: what constitutes the universe around us are **bits of information** that describe configuration and state. Physical events reduce ultimately to yes/no questions — binary observations — from which the appearance of continuous reality is constructed. Bob struggled with the low-level meaning: he interpreted it as the wave-function collapse reducing to a series of binary choices (which eigenstate is selected), grounding in information theory rather than classical matter.

This connects directly to:
- Everett's Many Worlds — if wave function collapse never actually occurs, you just get universes branching (each branch representing one set of binary choices)
- Wolfram's hypergraph model — which derives physics from discrete computational structures
- Hoffman's conscious agents — which produce the appearance of spacetime as a rendering interface

## Eric Weinstein's Secret Science Lineage

[[eric-weinstein]] has argued publicly that there is a lineage of secret science in the US, with Wheeler at the center: **Wheeler → Freeman Dyson → Bryce DeWitt → Hugh Everett**. [[bob-maguire]] endorses this. Weinstein's claim: the most productive theoretical directions explored by this lineage were classified or deflected; what remains in public physics is a deliberately incomplete version.
