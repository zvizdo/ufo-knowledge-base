# Doug Pasternak

## Background

Doug Pasternak is a journalist who interacted with [[john-norsine]] and surfaced his work in public reporting. His description of [[biofusion]] is the source for the often-quoted "no delay, no latency" framing of Norseen's brain-machine-interface aspiration.

## Notable Claims

- Pasternak: Norseen "believed that a pilot could fly a plane by merely thinking. Basically, no delay, no latency."
- Pasternak's description of the helmet's *mind-correction loop* — pilot says "right 090°" but processes 080°; the helmet "catches the mistake and injects the right number into the pilot's mind. Electromagnetic waves straight to the dome."
