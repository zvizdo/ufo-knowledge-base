# Scott Crane

Scott Crane was a UFO researcher who sought out [[eric-walker]] — Penn State University president (1956–1970) and alleged MJ-12-adjacent figure — for extended private conversations about UAP. After those conversations, Crane converted to Christianity and ceased UAP research entirely.

[[peter-levenda]] (in [[Y7PLeu5rTv4]]) and [[jesse-michaels]] use this as an archetypal case of what Jesse calls the "gnostic quest" model: the researcher is essentially poking the bear until God reveals himself through esoteric inquiry. Once sufficient certainty about NHI (or something adjacent to it) is obtained, the need to research disappears — the researcher just wants to "tend their garden and be nice to their neighbor."

What Walker specifically told Crane is not publicly documented. The conversion outcome implies that whatever Crane learned produced enough certainty to make continued inquiry unnecessary or spiritually irrelevant.
