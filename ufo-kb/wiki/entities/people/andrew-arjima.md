# Andrew Arjima (Drew)

Engineer with approximately 35+ years of experience, co-founder and principal experimenter at [[exodus-propulsion-technologies]]. Arjima approached the [[biefeld-brown-effect]] / [[exodus-force]] from the **electrovitics** tradition — the Townsend Brown lineage — rather than from the electrostatics side, which is what distinguished his approach from [[charles-buhler]]'s field-momentum conversion framing.

## Origin of the Collaboration

A mutual friend named Mike knew both Arjima and Buhler were independently working on closely related anomalous electrostatic thrust problems. Mike deliberately kept each party unaware of the other — "playing dumb" — to see if they would converge independently. When Buhler visited Arjima's home setup (a high-voltage needle inside a Teflon casing), Buhler's wife Janessa was the one who pointed out that Arjima was likely observing the same force as Buhler, just with a different apparatus geometry. Buhler returned to his lab, built a needle variant with a tube and scotch tape enclosure, and the device moved 3–4 feet in the air. He sent video to Arjima; collaboration began immediately (approximately 2016).

## Experimental Infrastructure

Arjima is unusually well-equipped for a non-institutional researcher:
- **Vacuum chamber at home** — a significant size, self-funded, used for the majority of the Exodus team's vacuum tests.
- **Second, walk-in-scale vacuum chamber** — being brought online; funded through the company.
- **2,000+ test articles** built and tested since the collaboration began (each tested multiple times across different configurations — pendulum, spinner, rotator, scale, in-air, and vacuum).

The iteration rate has impressed independent observers including [[david-chester]], who called it "phenomenal" from his vantage point on APE and through private communications with Arjima.

## Electrovitics Framing

Where Buhler resists the label "electrogravitics" as "pretentious," Arjima uses the term "electrovitics" — consistent with the Townsend Brown community. Arjima also uses the term "warp drive," which Buhler explicitly distances himself from. The practical collaboration proceeds despite this framing gap; both are measuring the same anomalous force regardless of theoretical label.

## Investor Interactions

At least one person who learned of the experiment built a variant in their own garage achieving ~1 mN thrust before being connected to Exodus as a potential investor. This replication-by-investor is the team's most significant independent validation on public record as of the episode date.
