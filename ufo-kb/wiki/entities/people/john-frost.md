# John Frost

British aerospace engineer who directed the [[avrocar-project]] — a 1952 joint operation between the CIA's Office of Scientific Intelligence and British aircraft design groups. The Avrocar was a saucer-shaped experimental craft using a radial gas turbine, based in Canada (Avro Canada) before being taken over by Wright Air Development Center (see [[wright-patterson]]).

The project was eventually declassified and officially acknowledged as a failure to fly well. [[jesse-michaels]] raises it as context for why WADC was interested in anti-gravity propulsion — a saucer-shaped craft that used conventional jet propulsion might logically require a better propulsion theory (anti-gravity) to be effective.
