# Lawrence Bell

Founder and president of [[bell-aircraft]] Corporation, the company that broke the sound barrier in 1947 with Chuck Yeager in the Bell X-1. Bell was among the most credible aerospace figures of the postwar era. His quoted statement in the 1956 *Young Men's* magazine article ([[1956-g-engines-article]]) is cited by [[jesse-michaels]] as evidence that the anti-gravity research boom was not fringe enthusiasm but was being pursued by the heads of leading aerospace firms.

## The 1956 Quote

Bell's quote in the [[1956-g-engines-article]]:

> "We are working with nuclear fuels and equipment to cancel out gravity."

This is a direct statement from the head of one of the most credible aerospace companies of the era, made publicly in a recognized aviation publication. It corroborates the claim in the [[1971-australian-jio-memo]] that listed Bell Aircraft as one of the major aerospace companies involved in US anti-gravity programs.
