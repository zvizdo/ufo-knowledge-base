# Clay Higgins

Republican Congressman from Louisiana's 3rd Congressional District; former law enforcement officer (police corporal, St. Landry Parish). Higgins is known for a plain-spoken, sometimes provocative style. He serves on the House Homeland Security Committee and the House Oversight and Accountability Committee.

**2024 UAP hearing questioning**: During the [[2024-congressional-uap-hearing]], Higgins questioned [[michael-shellenberger]] on the identity of the anonymous source who had provided information about the [[immaculate-constellation]] program. Rather than asking directly, Higgins attempted to elicit source identification details through indirect questions — asking about the source's gender, agency, and position. Shellenberger, recognizing the technique, resisted disclosing the information. Higgins subsequently self-deprecatingly acknowledged that he had tried to "trick" Shellenberger but was "partially successful" at drawing out some information.

**Significance of the exchange**: The Higgins-Shellenberger exchange is notable for what it reveals about Congressional interest in source identity versus source protection. Congressional members have strong institutional motives to identify insider sources — to verify the claims, to determine which agencies are leaking, and potentially to facilitate referral to security clearance review. Shellenberger's journalist-source protection posture directly conflicted with this Congressional interest. The exchange illustrates the structural tension between Congressional oversight and journalist source protection in UAP disclosure.

**UAP positioning**: Higgins's participation in the hearing does not characterize him as a strong UAP disclosure advocate — his questioning was more investigative (trying to identify the source) than supportive of disclosure. He represents the contingent of Congress that is skeptical of anonymous sources and wants to verify claims through official channels rather than rely on journalist-mediated insider accounts.
