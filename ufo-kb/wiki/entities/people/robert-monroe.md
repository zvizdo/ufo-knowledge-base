# Robert Monroe

Author of *Journeys Out of the Body* (1971), one of the foundational texts on out-of-body experiences (OBEs). Founder of the **Monroe Institute** in Virginia, which developed Hemi-Sync audio technology claimed to facilitate altered states of consciousness including OBE induction.

## DOD Stargate Connection

Per [[joe-mcmoneagle]], Monroe was **hired by the Department of Defense** to train Stargate personnel — including McMoneagle — in controlled out-of-body experiences. The DOD's decision to contract Monroe indicates that induced OBE states were considered operationally relevant to the [[stargate-program]]'s intelligence collection mandate.

McMoneagle trained at the Monroe Institute as part of his preparation for remote viewing work, suggesting the program's leadership saw OBE induction and remote viewing as related or complementary practices — both potentially accessing non-local consciousness states.
