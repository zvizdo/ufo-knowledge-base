# Christopher Dunn

British-American machinist and engineer who has studied the Great Pyramid of Giza from a mechanical and engineering perspective. His primary thesis — that the pyramid was built as an acoustic resonance power generator rather than a pharaonic tomb — is documented in his book *The Giza Power Plant* (1998) and subsequent works.

## Core Thesis

Dunn argues that:
- The precision of the granite work inside and around the Great Pyramid exceeds what copper and wooden chisels could achieve; the tools required do not exist in the conventional archaeological record
- The internal chamber geometry is consistent with acoustic resonance engineering rather than funerary architecture
- The plateau was purpose-built to support the structure's weight before the pyramids were constructed on top of it — implying a level of engineering planning incompatible with the "pharaoh commissioned this" narrative

[[mario-woods]] endorsed Dunn's theory as "absolutely correct" during [[73YBwFvcmF8]], noting the acoustic/tool analysis and placing it alongside [[graham-hancock]], [[robert-schoch]], and Randall Carlson as convergent lines of evidence for advanced pre-historical technology.

## Connection to UAP / NHI Discourse

Woods's post-abduction interest in pyramids — experienced as a "download" or installed obsession after his 1977 encounter — connects directly to Dunn's framework. This is a documented pattern: some experiencers report post-encounter knowledge downloads about ancient civilizations, zero-point energy, or pyramid technology. Dunn's work provides an engineering framework into which these intuitions can be mapped.

The broader question — who had pyramid-building technology, and what happened to it — is part of the [[time-travel-uap]] and breakaway-civilization discussion, as Jesse Michaels engages it in the same episode.
