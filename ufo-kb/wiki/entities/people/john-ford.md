# John Ford

John Ford headed the Long Island UFO Network and investigated what researchers believe was a UFO crash or retrieval event near [[brookhaven-national-lab]] on Long Island in 1989. His investigation was cut short by his arrest and subsequent decades-long incarceration on charges that [[richard-dolan]] and others regard as almost certainly fabricated.

## Long Island Investigation

Ford's group believed there may have been more than one anomalous event on Long Island around that time — the first alleged crash near Moriches Bay (~1989, per Dolan) near Brookhaven, with possible additional events. The area's proximity to Brookhaven — the first US particle accelerator facility (the Cosmotron) — is cited as potentially significant, given documented patterns of UAP interest in nuclear and high-energy physics installations.

## The "Persecution"

Ford was charged with conspiring to poison a Nassau County or Suffolk County official's toothpaste with radioactive material. Dolan: "Like, are you kidding me, man?" He was incarcerated for decades, potentially still alive. Close friend [[elaine-douglas]] (a prominent UFO researcher in her own right) was a strong believer in the reality of the Long Island crash and in Ford's innocence.

Dolan's assessment: "It really looked like they just went after him. It seems really wrong what they did to him." He notes uncertainty about whether Ford was "just unstable" but comes down on the side of persecution.

## Pattern Recognition

Dolan connects Ford's case to the broader historical pattern — Galileo-type persecution of truth-seekers by establishments. The specific charge (radioactive toothpaste) has the quality of intelligence-community fabrications: bizarre enough to be discrediting, technical enough to sound real.
