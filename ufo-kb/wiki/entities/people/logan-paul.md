# Logan Paul

American YouTuber and entertainer (b. 1995). In the UAP KB, Paul appears in two distinct contexts: as a holder/presenter of ~1995 Area 51 footage, and as someone who attempted to purchase related VHS footage.

**The 1995 orange disc footage**: In the [[d9tdJ2SkBKQ]] episode (the Bob Lazar American Alchemy interview), Logan Paul makes a brief cameo presenting footage from ~1995 near [[area-51]] showing an orange-corona wobbling disc. [[bob-lazar]] views the footage in real time and identifies it as an "Omicron configuration" — the S-4 craft running in an unstable wobble during Wednesday-night test flights. Lazar notes that the visual profile (orange corona, unstable wobble) matches multiple independent witness descriptions from the 1989–2000 period, including Gene Huff and John Lear on March 22, 1989. The footage had not been released publicly before this episode.

**The Chuck Clark VHS**: Separately, Paul reportedly offered $100,000 to [[chuck-clark]] for another piece of VHS footage showing an orange disc hovering near Rachel, Nevada (~1995). Clark refused. [[james-fox]] has independently pursued the same footage for over 26 years and believes a digital copy may exist locally.

**Significance**: Paul functions as an amplification node in the soft-disclosure-through-entertainment tier — bringing UAP content to a very large mainstream audience. The footage he presented to Lazar, and Lazar's real-time identification of it, is cited in the KB as one of the stronger independent-corroboration data points for the S-4 Omicron craft profile.
