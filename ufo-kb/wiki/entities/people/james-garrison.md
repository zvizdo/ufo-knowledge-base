# James Garrison

> ⚠ **Disambiguation**: James H. Garrison (Sheehan's nuclear-disarmament classmate, Gorbachev Foundation) is a distinct individual from [[jim-garrison]] (Earling Carothers "Big Jim" Garrison, the New Orleans District Attorney who prosecuted the JFK assassination case). The two are frequently confused in UAP contexts because both appear in Sheehan's network.

James H. Garrison was [[danny-sheehan]]'s classmate at Harvard Divinity School and a key figure in the nuclear disarmament movement of the 1980s. He later became the founding president of the **Gorbachev Foundation USA** and co-founded the **State of the World Forum** with Mikhail Gorbachev and Sheehan.

## Early Career — Jesuit Headquarters and Cambridge

After Harvard Divinity School, Garrison worked at the **Jesuit National Headquarters** in Washington DC under Sheehan as part of the Social Ministry Office. Sheehan arranged for him to pursue a doctoral degree at Cambridge University under theologian J.T. Robinson. While at Cambridge, Garrison met physicist [[fritjof-capra]] (author of *The Tao of Physics*), who was writing a book on nuclear disarmament funded by **Mrs. Rose Cruz** (wife of an English brigadier general). Through Cruz, Garrison was connected to members of the Royal Academy of Science, who wrote letters to their Soviet Academy of Science counterparts — opening the channel to Moscow.

## Path to Gorbachev

Garrison traveled to Moscow and worked his way through the Soviet scientific and political network:
- Connected to **Brezhnev's personal physician** and co-produced a 3-hour documentary on the effects of nuclear war with him
- After Brezhnev's death, worked through **Shevardnadze** (Soviet Foreign Minister), who told Garrison to bypass the dying Andropov and focus on **Mikhail Gorbachev**, then Secretary of Agriculture and identified by Shevardnadze as a future Soviet leader
- Met Gorbachev in the Kremlin approximately 1980–81; Gorbachev spoke English privately but not publicly
- Gorbachev's conversation: acknowledged nuclear weapons could not be fully dismantled because China — which could put "a billion men in the field, as proven in Korea" — remained a nuclear deterrent requirement; said "they will come for me in the night" when he starts dismantling; asked Garrison to become president of a foundation that could continue the disarmament work regardless of Gorbachev's political fate

## The Gorbachev Foundation and Kidnapping

Garrison became president of the **[[gorbachev-foundation]]** (US arm). Gorbachev was subsequently removed in a coup/kidnapping lasting approximately one week; Boris Yeltsin rode tanks into Red Square to free him; Yeltsin won the presidency. Gorbachev signed the dissolution of all Soviet Socialist Republics on **December 31, 1991**. Garrison's foundation maintained organizational continuity during this period.

## State of the World Forum

Garrison co-founded the **[[state-of-the-world-forum]]** with Gorbachev and Sheehan — an annual gathering addressing global civilizational challenges. Sheehan was present at Esalen Institute when Garrison recounted the full Gorbachev story to him in a hot tub.

## UAP Relevance

The Garrison-Gorbachev connection is UAP-relevant through Sheehan's broader framework: the 1992 Cheney Defense Department Policy Planning Guidance document (which established full-spectrum military dominance as U.S. policy in response to the Soviet collapse) foreclosed the cooperative civilizational transformation that the Gorbachev Foundation was trying to enable. For Sheehan, UAP disclosure politics play out within this geopolitical architecture — the same military-industrial complex that wrote the 1992 PPG also controls the UAP programs.
