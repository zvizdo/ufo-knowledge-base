# Dylan Borland

UFO witness who observed a large triangular craft with an active **electrooptic cloaking** system over [[langley-cia]] / Langley, Virginia. UAPGerb cites Borland's account as evidence that signature-management (cloaking) derived from non-human technology has been operationalised on US triangular airframes.

## Account

Borland observed a black metallic-flake triangular craft with a gold/lava-like plasma fluid flowing over and around its exterior surface. The craft was beneath him for 2–3 minutes; the center light flashed 2–3 times; the craft then climbed to commercial-jet altitude with no audible sound. He immediately felt static electricity over his body and smelled ozone. At altitude, he could only see the center light — as if the triangle had vanished around it, suggesting the signature-management system deactivated or the craft flew beyond visible range.

Post-sighting, Borland gained some exposure into legacy programs and inferred the craft was human-built (not NHI), based on what he learned about derivative technology programs. UAPGerb, however, maintains it is a US triangular airframe with NHI-derived cloaking — not a genuine NHI craft.

Borland also appeared on the Weaponized podcast and claimed DTRA (formerly AFSWP) held Atomic Energy Commission records stating UFOs are of extraterrestrial origin.
