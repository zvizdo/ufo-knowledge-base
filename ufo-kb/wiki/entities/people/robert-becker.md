# Robert Becker

Dr. Robert O. Becker (1923–2008) was an orthopedic surgeon and researcher who pioneered the study of bioelectromagnetics — the role of electromagnetic fields in biological processes. His book *The Body Electric* (1985) is the foundational text for the field.

## Key Findings

- **Salamander limb regeneration**: Salamanders naturally regenerate severed limbs. Becker showed this process is electrically controlled via **ion gradients** at the wound site. By applying specific ion gradients artificially, you can cause a salamander to regenerate a limb — or create a **two-headed salamander** by applying the head's ion gradient pattern to the wound
- **Tadpole limb regrowth**: Tadpoles do not normally regenerate limbs; by applying the correct electromagnetic charge to a severed tadpole arm, Becker induced regeneration
- **Implication**: Biological form and healing are **upstream of biochemistry** — the electromagnetic/ion-gradient information precedes and determines the biochemical activity
