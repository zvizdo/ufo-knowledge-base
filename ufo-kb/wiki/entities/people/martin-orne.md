# Martin Orne

Martin Orne was one of the **two top-secret-cleared** MK Ultra psychiatrists specifically documented by [[colin-ross]] — meaning he knew his research was funded by the CIA, unlike the approximately one-third of MK Ultra project investigators who were unwitting (received CIA money funneled through cutout front organizations).

## MK Ultra Role

The CIA project documents for Orne's work explicitly state the purpose: **"studying hypnotic and dissociative states."** This is the core capability the Manchurian Candidate programs required — the ability to create and exploit dissociative states for compartmentalized identity construction. Orne was aware he was researching for the CIA.

He was also aware of [[gh-esterbrooks]]'s prior work ("Martin Orne was aware of G.H. Esterbrooks and back and forth," per Ross).

## False Memory Syndrome Foundation

In 1992, Orne co-founded the **[[false-memory-syndrome-foundation]]** alongside [[jolly-west]] and others.

[[colin-ross]]'s analysis of the Foundation's mixed motives:
1. Memory researchers who disbelieve in dissociative amnesia (academic disagreement)
2. People wrongly accused of incest (legitimate grievance)
3. Actual incest perpetrators needing a defense
4. Those who think Freudian theory is outdated
5. **West and Orne**: covering up the Manchurian Candidate programs because therapists were beginning to hear stories from DID patients about being taken to military bases, being spun in vestibular disorientation devices, and undergoing experimental alter-creation procedures

Ross's thesis: the Foundation served as a **strategic cover operation** to discredit therapists who were starting to document MK Ultra survivors in their clinical practices.
