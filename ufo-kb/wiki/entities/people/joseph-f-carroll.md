# Joseph F. Carroll

> *Stub. Expand with full biography, USAF career, founding of AFOSI and DIA, and any additional UAP-related primary documentation as it enters the KB.*

USAF Brigadier General (1910-1991). Founding Director of the [[afosi|Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI)]], established 1948. Later became the founding Director of the [[dia|Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA)]] in 1961.

## Role in 1948–1950 UAP Collection

Per [[dow-uap-d17-sandia-base-1948-1950]], Carroll served as **Director of Special Investigations, HQ USAF, Washington 25 DC** during 1949-1950. In that role he was the senior addressee of the 25 May 1950 "Summary of Observations of Aerial Phenomena in the New Mexico Area, December 1948 - May 1950" from **Lt Col [[doyle-rees]]** (Commander, 17th OSI District at Kirtland AFB). The summary documented 209+ sightings and forwarded LaPaz's scientific analyses; its distribution to Carroll's office established HQ USAF-level institutional awareness of the foundational green-fireballs corpus.

Carroll's office received 6 copies (the largest single-recipient allocation) of the summary letter.
