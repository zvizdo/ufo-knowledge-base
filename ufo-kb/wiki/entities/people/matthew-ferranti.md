# Matthew Ferranti

## Background

Matthew Ferranti holds a PhD from Harvard in economics (supervised by Ken Rogoff) and has worked as an economist for the US intelligence community. He is a fellow at the [[bitcoin-policy-institute]].

## Bitcoin Reserve Asset Paper

Ferranti authored the Bitcoin Policy Institute white paper: **"The Case for Bitcoin as a Central Bank Reserve Asset."** In [[9QMrhcpJq8I]], [[matthew-pines]] cites this paper as the intellectual foundation for the strategic-reserve argument: as Bitcoin's volatility decreases with adoption, its risk-weighted allocation in sovereign portfolios should rationally increase, creating a self-reinforcing adoption flywheel.

The paper argues that any central bank or sovereign wealth fund should consider Bitcoin as a diversifying reserve asset given its:
- Fixed supply (unlike gold, immune to asteroid mining or technological extraction advances)
- Correlation structure improving overall portfolio sharp ratio
- Potential to serve as a global reserve asset independent of any single sovereign

## Intelligence Community Background

His background as a US intelligence community economist gives him unusual standing to make this argument: he bridges the strategic-national-security framing of Bitcoin with rigorous monetary economics.
