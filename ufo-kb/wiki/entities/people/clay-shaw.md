# Clay Shaw

Clay Lavergne Shaw (1913–1974) was a New Orleans businessman who served as a CIA contract agent. He was the only person brought to criminal trial — by New Orleans District Attorney [[jim-garrison]] — for conspiracy in the assassination of President Kennedy. He was acquitted in 1969. His CIA status was later acknowledged by CIA Director Richard Helms in a separate civil case.

## The Garrison Investigation

Garrison's theory held that Shaw used his business contacts and CIA connections to facilitate the plot against Kennedy. Shaw's documented relationships with [[fred-chrisman]] and [[david-ferrie]] were central to Garrison's case. Chrisman connected Shaw to the 1947 Maury Island UFO incident orbit; Ferrie connected him to the anti-Castro Cuban exile networks that overlapped with the Camp Street operation.

## CIA Status

While Shaw denied CIA connection throughout his trial, CIA Director Richard Helms subsequently acknowledged under oath in a civil proceeding that Shaw had been a CIA contact — confirming Garrison's basic factual claim, if not his broader conspiracy theory.
