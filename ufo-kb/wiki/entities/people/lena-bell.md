# Lena Bell

Managing editor in the Wall Street Journal's opinion section. Met with [[jesse-michaels]] at WSJ headquarters (introduced via a News Corp vice president) to discuss publishing the suppressed [[carl-nell]] op-ed co-authored with a former cabinet-level official on UAP transparency.

## The Nell Op-Ed Rejection

Despite what Michaels describes as a positive and earnest personal exchange, Bell appeared "powerless" to advance the op-ed's publication. Michaels describes "some undetectable dark matter at play" — an unexplained institutional block preventing the piece from running.

Notably, the WSJ rejected an op-ed co-authored by a cabinet-level official arguing for civilian oversight of UAP programs — while simultaneously publishing [[joel-shectman]]'s article claiming those programs don't meaningfully exist.

## David Spergel as WSJ UFO Gatekeeper

Bell disclosed to Michaels that [[david-spergel]] — astrophysicist, head of the Simons Foundation, chair of the NASA UAP working group — is the WSJ's "de facto adviser on UFOs." Spergel's stated position is that UFOs are "space trash, light reflections, and temperature inversions" — a formulation identical to [[donald-menzel]]'s 1950s debunking.
