# Edgar A. Fouché

USAF Master Sergeant who came forward with claims about the **TR3B** — a large triangular reverse-engineered craft allegedly operating out of [[area-51]] and related facilities. Fouché's Area 51 employment in the 1970s is regarded as independently verifiable, lending his structural claims some evidential floor even while specific program details remain unconfirmed.

## The TR3B Claim

Fouché described a reverse-engineered triangular craft with the following parameters (as cited in [[nTiFs8LudUo]]):

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Shape | Triangle |
| Size | 200–600 ft diameter (multiple versions) |
| Crew | 4 personnel |
| Propulsion | Reverse-engineered by [[sandia-national-laboratories]] and [[lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory]] |
| Program ownership | [[nsa]], [[cia]], [[nro]] |
| Primary contractors | [[northrop-grumman]], [[lockheed-martin]], [[boeing]] |
| Pilot pipeline | USAF Test Pilot School, Edwards AFB (412th Test Wing) |
| Mission | Logistics/transport for alleged secret space fleet |

The TR3B reportedly achieves **vertical takeoff and landing in complete silence with no ground-dust disturbance** — a behavioral signature corroborated by:
- [[randy-anderson]] (Green Beret who observed a triangular craft at Area 51)
- [[paul-benowitz]] (who observed exotic vertically-landing craft at [[kirtland-afb]])

## Relationship to the X-Files Design

[[mark-mccandish]] documented that the triangular ARV design he illustrated (the **XF-131 Super Sentinel**) matched descriptions given to him by engineers from Northrop and Lockheed who had become frustrated with classification. The X-Files pilot episode (contracted to McCandish by **Chris Carter**) used this design — suggesting the same hardware entered both classified and entertainment channels from the same engineering source.
