# Michael Lazoski

AFI-trained documentary filmmaker who directed *Sleeping Dogs* — [[jeremy-corbell]]'s 2026 documentary centered on [[bob-lazar]], the FBI raids, and Corbell's broader UAP investigation work.

## Working Relationship with Corbell

Corbell describes a trust-building process before granting Lazoski access:

- Corbell initially refused: "I ain't going to trust you."
- His wife's counsel changed this: "You've got to trust somebody."
- Corbell gave Lazoski **20 years of privately held footage** (99.9% previously unreleased), plus ongoing access to new filming.
- Lazoski's quality signal: when Corbell told a story verbally, Lazoski would return saying "Dude, it was cooler than you remembered" — having found corroborating footage in the archive.

Corbell characterizes Lazoski as "a master filmmaker" and "right out of AFI, probably top of his class." He describes Lazoski's artistic choices — the "code word for cut" filming technique, the hand-gesture cross-cuts, preserving unflattering moments — as reflecting a deliberate strategy to establish Corbell's credibility *before* presenting evidence, so audiences trust the evidence when it comes.

## Single Undisclosed Restriction

Corbell placed exactly one restriction on Lazoski's use of the footage. He declines to say what it is: "I like leaving things undone so you stay curious."
