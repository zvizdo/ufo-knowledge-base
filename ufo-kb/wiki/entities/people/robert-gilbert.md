# Robert Gilbert

A Rosicrucian scholar and nuclear chemist who lived in Asheville, North Carolina. Described by [[chris-bledsoe]] as "a world of knowledge" and someone who helped him understand that "this world is far more complex." Jesse Michaels identifies him as a "famous Rosicrucian scholar." Gilbert died of cancer, apparently in 2025 or early 2026.

## Work with Bledsoe

After [[michael-oconnell]]'s hypnotherapy sessions unlocked Bledsoe's recovered memories, Bledsoe saw imagery that prominently featured **ancient Egypt** — overhead views of the pyramids and Sphinx, Hathor's temple, and hieroglyphic animal/bird imagery. Gilbert helped Bledsoe decode this symbolism within a broader framework that connected Egyptian cosmology to Judeo-Christian scripture:

- The deities of Egypt depicted as animals and birds represent **forces of nature and humanity** — not supernatural beings in a Western sense.
- The same beings appear in scripture: Ezekiel's "wheel within a wheel" (the cherubim with animal faces); the Book of Daniel's angel-like figure "gleaming like glass."
- Jesus's flight to Egypt during infancy is coherent because **Egypt was under Greek control for 500 years** before his birth — the cosmological and philosophical systems were already linked.
- **Pendulums** are the most frequently found artifact under Egyptian soil — millions of them — pointing to a sophisticated understanding of resonance, subtle forces, and measurement.
- Washington DC's layout (L'Enfant plan), the obelisks in Central Park, London, and Paris (all taken from Egypt), and the interior of the Freemasonic temple in DC are all direct derivatives of Egyptian sacred geometry.

## Sacred Geometry Thread

Gilbert operated within the Rosicrucian tradition — a Western esoteric stream that claims continuity with Egyptian mystery-school knowledge. His specific bridge between nuclear chemistry and sacred geometry makes him an unusual combination: hard-science credentials applied to esoteric frameworks. His proximity to Asheville, NC (near Bledsoe's Fayetteville base) enabled repeated visits.
