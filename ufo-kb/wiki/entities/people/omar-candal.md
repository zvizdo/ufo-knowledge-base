# Omar Candal

Co-founder of **[[yes-theory]]**, the adventure/challenge YouTube channel (~10M+ subscribers) known for saying yes to uncomfortable experiences. Recurring co-host and supporting figure in the [[american-alchemy]] / [[jesse-michaels]] UAP podcast network; described as a primary financial and promotional supporter of the American Alchemy project.

**Soul Conference connection**: Candal met [[whitley-strieber]] at [[garry-nolan]]'s **[[soul-project-stanford|Soul Conference]]** at Stanford — the annual gathering of credentialed researchers and experiencers at the intersection of consciousness and anomalous phenomena. This meeting led to the [[ABOP8ZJsyIk]] episode, a full-length American Alchemy interview with Strieber recorded in Austin, Texas.

**Whitley Strieber interview**: In [[ABOP8ZJsyIk]], Candal co-hosts alongside Jesse Michaels in an extended conversation with Strieber covering NHI motivations, the 1952 Skinner box program, Strieber's 1989 ear implant, blue dwarf phenomena, and the [[collins-elite]] persecution. Candal closes the episode with the Eden Phillpotts quote: *"The universe is full of magical things waiting, patiently waiting for our wits to grow sharper."* He also cites the Ariel School Zimbabwe child's words: *"In space there is no love. But down here there is."*

**KB significance**: Candal represents the soft-disclosure pathway through large mainstream platforms (Yes Theory's audience skews young and non-UAP-specialist). His involvement in American Alchemy is both an amplification mechanism and a credibility signal to that audience.
