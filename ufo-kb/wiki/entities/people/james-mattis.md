# James Mattis

Retired US Marine Corps four-star general (commander of US Central Command, 2010–2013) and 26th US Secretary of Defense (January 2017 – January 2019).

Per [[lue-elizondo]] in [[1f16VvXaSSE]], in early 2017 Elizondo and [[chris-mellon]] attempted to brief Defense Secretary Mattis on the [[aatip]] findings — and were physically intercepted at the door by bureaucratic intermediaries who refused to allow the briefing to proceed. Elizondo identifies this blocked-briefing moment as the immediate trigger for his decision to resign and take the three Pentagon UAP videos to The New York Times — leading to the December 2017 article that triggered the modern disclosure wave.

## Background

- 1950–. b. Pullman, Washington.
- US Marine Corps career officer; commanded the 1st Marine Division in the 2003 invasion of Iraq.
- Commander, US Joint Forces Command (2007–2010).
- Commander, US Central Command (2010–2013).
- 26th US Secretary of Defense (Jan 20, 2017 – Jan 1, 2019); resigned over policy differences regarding Syria withdrawal.

## Notable Claims

- **The blocked briefing**: per Elizondo, Mattis himself was not the obstacle — bureaucratic intermediaries shielded him from the AATIP briefing. The implication is that the Secretary of Defense was being insulated from UAP information by lower-level program protectors, consistent with the [[ufo-legacy-program-pyramid]] thesis.
- The Mattis briefing failure is the immediate causal antecedent of the December 2017 NYT article that publicly launched the modern disclosure era.
