# Jose Padilla

One of two primary witnesses to the [[1945-san-antonio-trinity-crash]]. Son of a local New Mexico cattle rancher. At the time (approximately August 1945) he was approximately nine years old.

## Account

Padilla and his companion [[remy-baca]] (age ~7) observed the crashed avocado-shaped metallic object from a distance. Where Baca refused to approach, Padilla entered the area near the craft. He later recalled that the craft's floor was flat and that there was roughly one foot of clearance below the floor — implying a significant internal volume beneath. [[jacques-vallee]] notes this is consistent with a propulsion or engine compartment somewhere within the oval structure.

He also observed the three humanoid creatures: approximately three feet tall, human-like facial features, two eyes, two arms, three fingers per hand, two legs — moving by sliding rather than bipedal walking.

His testimony was recorded on audio tape by [[paola-harris]] before she co-authored *[[trinity-best-kept-secret]]* with Vallée. By the time of the interviews, both Padilla and Baca were elderly men.
