# Alan Earas

Population geneticist. Engaged with Jesse Michaels on the genetics and bioelectricity dimensions of the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] case.

## Background

Offers a unifying hypothesis for how the mummies' unusual phenotype (tridactyl morphology, elongated skull) could arise without requiring wholesale genetic engineering: bioelectric fields direct spatial gene expression. The osmium/cadmium implants could be the mechanism modulating this bioelectric layer.

## Notable Claims

- Bioelectricity is a real mechanism for modulating which genes get expressed where — not fringe science.
- The metal implants may operate at the bioelectric layer, producing phenotypic changes without requiring large-scale genetic modification — which would explain why the known 70% DNA doesn't obviously encode tridactyly.
- Explicitly notes the case "should be the most fascinating thing from an anthropology perspective" but is avoided because of its stigma.

## Credibility Assessment

Population geneticist bringing relevant domain expertise (bioelectricity is a legitimate and growing research field, not pseudoscience). His hypothesis is speculative but scientifically grounded. Jesse found him credible. See [[credibility-frameworks]].
