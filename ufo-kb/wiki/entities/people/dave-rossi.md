# Dave Rossi

Self-taught physicist and UFO researcher operating under the handle "Zed," host of the *Generation Zed* podcast. A former construction worker with no formal science education, Rossi claims a spontaneous contact experience with a blue humanoid plasma being (~2021) prompted him to self-teach electrical engineering, quantum physics, and extended electrodynamics. He has since been noticed by [[hal-puthoff]] and [[eric-davis]], and consults privately for defense-adjacent and private-sector groups.

## Background

Rossi worked in construction before COVID lockdown; started the Generation Zed UFO podcast around 2020 to apply an "open mind" approach to the subject. About 1–1.5 years in, with no prior scientific training (failed math throughout high school), he had a contact experience and awoke with an overwhelming drive to study physics. He spent the next two years intensive self-study — reading Soviet physics papers, older North American literature, and experimental reports on zero-point energy technology.

## Blue Being Contact Experience

Around 2021, while falling asleep, Rossi experienced what he describes as neither fully physical nor purely spiritual:

- Opened his eyes to white light around a convex structure; a blue energy humanoid, significantly taller than a human, stood near his head
- The being had no dense physical features — two arms, two legs, but essentially plasma-like — and communicated visually
- Key download: longitudinal scalar waves, visualized as **slinkies** existing all around us in the quantum vacuum
- Next morning: complete reorientation toward physics; no precedent for this interest prior

He explicitly avoids the word "abducted" due to the lack of negative connotation. He does not believe he is uniquely chosen — he suspects hundreds of thousands of people worldwide are receiving similar downloads, including children who spontaneously describe geodesic timelike curves.

## Physics Framework

Rossi's work centers on **[[extended-electrodynamics]]** / **[[scalar-waves|scalar physics]]**:

- Classical EM (U1, flat spacetime) only permits transverse Hertzian waves
- Heaviside's simplification of Maxwell's equations dropped scalar and vector potential terms (the "Lorenz re-gauging") — Rossi describes this as taking 12 pizza slices and telling people there's only 1
- Restoring those terms unlocks **longitudinal scalar waves**, **helicoidal waves**, and the ability to curve spacetime electromagnetically
- The **Aharonov-Bohm effect** provides experimental proof that potentials are more fundamental than E/B fields
- To access these effects: induce spacetime curvature via spinning capacitors (mechanical), or polarize superconductor lattice holes (solid state)

He builds on [[hal-puthoff]]'s EarthTech papers, [[salvatore-pais]]'s patents, and the historical work of [[thomas-townsend-brown]] and [[nikola-tesla]], as well as Soviet-era physics literature.
