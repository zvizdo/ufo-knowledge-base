# Pippa Malmgren

## Background

Pippa Malmgren is an economist, author, and policy adviser. She served as Special Assistant to President George W. Bush on the National Economic Council, placing her inside the senior White House advisory circle during a period when UFO-related discussions were reportedly occurring at the executive level.

She is the daughter of [[harold-malmgren]], the presidential adviser who served under JFK, LBJ, Nixon, and Ford and who made extensive firsthand claims about UAP knowledge. Pippa was a co-guest in the source interview, present alongside the host to relay, contextualize, and corroborate her father's disclosures — some of which she received directly from him in his final days.

## Notable Claims

### Deathbed Disclosure: Roswell Survivor and Directed-Energy Weapon

Pippa relayed that her father told her a Roswell survivor provided the United States with the conceptual basis for the directed-energy shootdown technology later used in the [[directed-energy-uap-weapon]] event described as the 1962 Bluegill Triple Prime nuclear test. This claim, if accurate, creates a direct causal chain from the 1947 Roswell recovery to the 1962 deliberate downing of a UAP — a remarkable compression of official secrecy history. See [[richard-bissell]] for the original account of the Bluegill shootdown.

### Los Alamos Material as Deliberate Test

Pippa provided additional context for [[harold-malmgren]]'s account of handling UAP debris at Los Alamos. She stated that the episode — in which Lawrence Geist (AEC Albuquerque) placed material on a desk for her father — was a deliberate test of his reaction. This reframes the event from an incidental encounter to a controlled assessment, suggesting a more organized protocol for evaluating cleared officials' responses to recovered material. This connects to [[reverse-engineered-craft]] and the material sciences questions surrounding recovered debris.

### George W. Bush and UFO Disclosure

Pippa stated that President George W. Bush considered undertaking UFO disclosure during his administration and discussed it with National Security Adviser Steven Hadley. She reported that Liu Cixin's *The Three-Body Problem* was used as a conceptual framework in those discussions — specifically as a model for thinking about non-human intelligence (NHI) and the risks of making Earth's position known. This is one of the few accounts linking a sitting US president's inner deliberations directly to [[catastrophic-disclosure-fear]] at a policy level.

### The "No Onramp" Heuristic

Pippa formulated and articulated what she calls the "no onramp" test for identifying technologies potentially derived from [[reverse-engineered-craft]]:

> "Look for the technologies where there's no onramp. You can't find the research history that led to it. Those are the interesting ones."

This heuristic — searching for discontinuities in the public record of technological development — is a methodologically significant contribution to UAP research independent of any single claim. It points toward the Bell Labs / transistor claims made by [[philip-corso]] as a case study worth examining under this framework.
