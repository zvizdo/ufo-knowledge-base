# William Bramley

Author of *The Gods of Eden* (1989), a work [[jason-reza-jorjani]] considers "absolutely indispensable" and unusually credible precisely because Bramley wrote it as a one-off — he never returned to the UFO field, distinguishing him from career grifters. Bramley started as a conventional researcher investigating systemic war profiteering and encountered UFO evidence along the way.

## The Gods of Eden Thesis

Bramley's methodology: He noticed that **UFO sightings systematically preceded or accompanied major historical events**, particularly plague outbreaks. His archival research into the Black Death period revealed:

- At the time the Black Death broke out, there were **numerous UFO sightings** across Europe — objects landing in fields, aerial phenomena
- In the French court of **Charlemagne**, "tyrants of the air" sightings were so widespread that Charlemagne reportedly considered declaring war on them
- The hysteria became the subject of a recorded account (later discussed by Vallée as the Magonia/Merovingian period): three men and a woman were seen descending from aerial ships in Lyon; the crowd assumed they were enemy sorcerers; Bishop Agobard (a proto-rationalist) intervened and got them released
- **Most disturbing**: the plague broke out *after* these entities landed in fields and moved through towns wearing hoods and carrying **spray devices** — walking door-to-door, spraying doorways. The plague followed these sprayings. The shrouded figures with spray canisters became the iconographic basis for the **Grim Reaper** (hooded figure with a scythe = stylized spraying implement)

Bramley's conclusion: the Black Death was **biological warfare** delivered by entities connected to the same faction that has lorded over humanity through the Sumerian epoch. The plague derailed what would have been an early Renaissance (the late 1200s were already showing proto-empiricist and proto-scientific development), forcing a retreat into religious fundamentalism.

## Significance

The Black Death/UFO connection is central to [[jason-reza-jorjani]]'s [[nordic-control-system]] thesis: the same "bad guys" who maintain the control system actively sabotage periods of human intellectual growth by engineering catastrophes. The 1300s plague reset is paralleled to the Jesuit suppression of the 16th-century Rosicrucian/Hermetic movement.

## Credibility Notes

Bramley is praised for doing archival research rather than relying on secondary UFO literature. His work draws on historical chronicles of the era. The plague-spraying interpretation extrapolates from historical accounts of robed/hooded figures at disease sites; the leap from "historical oddity" to "biological warfare by NHI" is significant and not independently corroborated.
