# Jamie Shandera

Jamie Shandera is a UFO filmmaker who, in 1984, received a film canister in the mail containing the now-famous MJ12 documents — the first appearance of the alleged classified briefing documents establishing the existence of a secret government UAP management group. He was a close friend of [[bill-moore]], making the pipeline to Moore immediate.

## MJ12 Document Receipt

The documents were sent anonymously. [[richard-dolan]] notes they were "probably sent from somewhere near [[kirtland-afb]]" — pointing to [[richard-doty]] as the probable origin. The Shandera-Moore-Doty triangle is central to understanding the provenance of the MJ12 documents: were they authentic leaks, disinformation designed to flood the UFO research community with plausible-but-deniable material, or something in between?

## Intelligence Connections

Dolan notes that Shandera had "his own very interesting Intel connections I believe." This has never been fully documented publicly, but it places him in the same ambiguous space as Moore — researchers whose connections to the intelligence community made them natural conduits for managed information flows.
