# Colin Ross

Dr. Colin A. Ross is a Canadian-born psychiatrist practicing in Texas. He specializes in [[dissociative-identity-disorder|dissociative identity disorder (DID)]] — formerly called multiple personality disorder — and has published extensively on trauma-related psychiatric conditions. He is the author of *CIA Doctors*, which documents the medical malpractice of psychiatrists affiliated (officially or unofficially) with the CIA's mind-control programs.

## Entry into MK Ultra Research

Ross was not researching intelligence programs when a DID patient at his hospital (Plano, TX, ~1989) handed him a sheaf of papers about MK Ultra — frightened and not wanting them. This was his first exposure to the program. He subsequently read two existing books, then traveled to CIA headquarters in northern Virginia (~1992) to read the primary documents. After navigating an unusual access process (the taxi driver routed him through the Pentagon grounds; the CIA reading room was staffed by military officers behind "secure line: DIA only" and "secure line: CIA only" signs), he read all 15,000 pages of the 149 surviving MK Ultra sub-project files over multiple visits.

## CIA Doctors

*CIA Doctors* documents:
- Specific psychiatrists who ran or participated in MK Ultra and its sub-programs

This was published in a peer-reviewed electrical engineering journal. The implication: the optic nerve, being a large electrical cable exiting directly, concentrates and focuses brain-wave signals into an ocular beam — **extramission**.

He cites the physics case:

Proposed device: an array of high-sensitivity electrodes/antennas scanning the whole body — a **tricorder** — that could detect disease at the electromagnetic level before it manifests biochemically.
