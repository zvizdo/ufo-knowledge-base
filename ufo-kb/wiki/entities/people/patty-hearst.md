# Patty Hearst

Patricia "Patty" Hearst (born 1954) is the granddaughter of media magnate William Randolph Hearst (the model for Orson Welles' *Citizen Kane*). Her 1974 kidnapping and subsequent transformation into SLA soldier "Tania" is one of the most documented civilian cases of apparent [[manchurian-candidate|Manchurian Candidate-type behavioral modification]].

## The SLA Kidnapping

The Symbionese Liberation Army came to Patty Hearst's apartment, knocked out her boyfriend (who happened to have been observed at the [[peoples-temple]] location in Ukiah, California months earlier), and kidnapped her. She was:

- Held in a closet for weeks
- Subjected to sensory deprivation and sensory isolation
- Terrorized

She emerged as "**Tania**" — a new identity — and participated in a **bank robbery** with the SLA.

## The Donald DeFreeze / Psychological Warfare Connection

SLA leader [[donald-defreeze]], code-named "CINQUE," was:
- A petty criminal who was **transferred within the prison system** to a new facility
- **Walked out of prison** (not officially released — just walked out)
- Subsequently met by a **psychological warfare expert** who had served in Vietnam
- Given the CINQUE code name and organized into the SLA under cover of the Black Cultural Association

This pattern — petty criminal transformed into charismatic violent extremist after contact with a military psychological warfare specialist — mirrors the [[charles-manson]] pattern.

## The Jolly West Connection

At Patty Hearst's trial, [[jolly-west]] testified that she had been **mind-controlled** — that the sensory deprivation and isolation had produced a genuine dissociative state in which she was not acting under her own volition. This testimony runs counter to the prosecution's position that she was a willing participant.

The irony: West, who created Manchurian Candidates, was testifying that someone else had been Manchurian Candidated. Whether this was genuine testimony or another form of management of the narrative is unresolved.

## Jim Jones and Jonestown Connection

Hearst's boyfriend was observed at the **People's Temple** location in Ukiah before the kidnapping. [[colin-ross]] and the host [[jesse-michaels]] flag this as part of the broader web of interconnections — the People's Temple, Patty Hearst, and the SLA all existing in the same Bay Area intelligence-adjacent universe.
