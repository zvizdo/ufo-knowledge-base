# Patrick Jackson

Texas-based researcher who has accumulated knowledge of anomalous metallic spheres and developed a classification framework. [[ross-coulthart]] describes him as having "an eclectic intelligence" and being "fun" while acknowledging his theories rest on speculation.

## Theory

Jackson proposes that the metallic spheres observed globally in UAP contexts constitute a **global alien defense system** — deployed by an extraterrestrial or non-human intelligence to defend Earth from hostile actors. He classifies observed spheres into **type one** and **type two** categories based on physical characteristics or behavior (exact criteria not specified in the source).

Supporting observation: photographs of UAP events often show the main large object beside aircraft plus additional smaller metallic sphere dots in close proximity.

## Coulthart's Assessment

[[ross-coulthart]] respects Jackson as clever but is uncertain about how Jackson could actually know the spheres' purpose. The "global defense system" interpretation is treated as speculative but interesting.
