# Douglas Caddy

## Background

Douglas Caddy was an attorney who represented E. Howard Hunt — CIA operative and Watergate plumber — in legal matters. This relationship placed him in close proximity to one of the most significant figures at the intersection of the CIA, Watergate, and JFK's assassination.

## Notable Claims

### Howard Hunt's Deathbed Disclosure

According to Caddy, E. [[howard-hunt]] told him shortly before Hunt's death that President Kennedy's assassination "was about the alien presence." This is one of the most direct attributed statements — from a career CIA officer with firsthand proximity to the Kennedy assassination — connecting the killing to UAP/NHI knowledge.

In [[09KP8XVf5nY]], [[harold-malmgren]] independently arrived at the same conclusion — that JFK's intent to share UAP information with the Soviets was "probably the number one issue" in his assassination. Caddy's account provides corroboration from a completely separate chain: Hunt → Caddy, independently of Malmgren → Bissell → Malmgren.
