# Joseph Farrell

## Background

Prolific independent author with a doctorate in patristics from Oxford. Writes primarily about Nazi secret weapons programs, suppressed physics, the breakaway civilization hypothesis, and connections between occult organizations and aerospace development. Books include *Reich of the Black Sun*, *The SS Brotherhood of the Bell*, and many others. Frequently cites declassified documents, patent records, and obscure technical literature.

## Nenza Hypothesis

Farrell (along with [[walter-bosley]]) has written about **Nenza** — a hypothesized secret scientific society or breakaway group that may have been behind the 1890s airship wave in the United States. The "airship mystery" refers to thousands of witness reports across 1896–97 of a structured, powered craft — described as a cigar-shaped or egg-shaped object with lights — observed across multiple states, decades before the Wright Brothers. The Nenza hypothesis proposes that this was not NHI but a human breakaway group with advanced technology derived from physics available in the 1890s.

In the [[8TYMQOUDQBo]] interview, [[salvatore-pais]] invokes Farrell's work when discussing whether Oliver Heaviside's 1890s physics could have been applied by "powerful families" to engineer exotic craft, and whether the airship wave represents evidence of a human breakaway civilization.

## Breakaway Civilization

Farrell, alongside [[richard-dolan]], is among the most systematic proponents of the breakaway civilization hypothesis: that a subset of humanity — operating via secret programs funded through off-books means — has had access to dramatically advanced technology for decades (or longer), effectively constituting a "second civilization" running parallel to and hidden from the public world.

This framework is cited by Pais approvingly in the context of asking why no legacy UAP programs contacted him after his patents were published — his tentative answer being that they "must have already figured it out."

## Roswell Artifact Analysis (ABw7EsWh2Us)

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] cites Farrell's *Saucer Swastikas and SCOPS* (likely *Reich of the Black Sun* or *Saucer Swastikas and the Third Reich*) as analyzing [[philip-corso]]'s Roswell artifact claims — specifically the **perforated metal** and **Kevlar** found at Roswell — and concluding these match **Nazi aeronautical engineering** rather than extraterrestrial manufacture. This is a key plank of [[nazi-ufo-hypothesis]]: if Corso's artifacts are Nazi-made, Roswell was a Nazi craft.
