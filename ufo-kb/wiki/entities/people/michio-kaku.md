# Michio Kaku

Theoretical physicist and popular science communicator; co-founder of string field theory. Professor at City College of New York.

## UAP Statement (2020)

In or around 2020, Kaku made a public statement that journalist [[george-knapp]] cites as a significant mainstream-science shift:

> "The burden of evidence about these not being extraterrestrial craft is now on the scientific community."

This reversed the traditional epistemic burden — from "prove UFOs are alien" to "prove they aren't" — citing the weight of physical evidence and the fact that both the US government and its scientific advisors were taking the topic seriously.

Knapp noted this statement as evidence that mainstream scientists were beginning to engage with UAP evidence rather than dismissing it.
