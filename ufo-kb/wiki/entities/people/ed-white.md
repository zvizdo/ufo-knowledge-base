# Ed White

Edward Higgins White II (1930–1967) was a US Air Force officer and NASA astronaut who became the first American to walk in space during the Gemini 4 mission (June 3, 1965), performing a 23-minute extravehicular activity (EVA). He was selected as Senior Pilot on Apollo 1 under Commander [[gus-grissom]], and died with Grissom and Roger Chaffee in the Apollo 1 cabin fire on January 27, 1967.

**Gemini 4 EVA**: White's spacewalk during Gemini 4 was preceded by Soviet cosmonaut Alexei Leonov's EVA (March 1965) by approximately 10 weeks. White used a handheld maneuvering unit and reported enjoying the experience so much that he had to be persuaded to return to the capsule.

**Apollo 1 fire**: The fire that killed White, Grissom, and Chaffee ignited during a ground test. Post-accident investigation found numerous design deficiencies in the Command Module, quality control failures by contractor North American Aviation, and inadequate safety protocols for a pure oxygen environment at higher-than-atmospheric pressure.

**KB context**: White appears in the KB primarily as a crewmate of [[gus-grissom]] in the Apollo 1 context — Grissom's pre-accident criticism of the program and his unexplained reference to "CIA presence on the launch pad" the night before his death are the primary UAP-adjacent elements of the Apollo 1 narrative.
