# Udo Wartena

American miner who, in the 1940s, had a contact experience that included a guided technical tour of a UAP craft. [[michael-p-masters]] cites Wartena because — uniquely among historical contactee accounts — he received a specific engineering explanation for the craft's propulsion without possessing any prior knowledge of electromagnetism or physics.

## Account

While working at his mining claim, Wartena encountered a disc-shaped craft that had landed nearby. The operators invited him aboard and gave him a brief tour. During this tour they explained the propulsion mechanism:

**Counter-rotating flywheel system**:
- Two or more flywheels spinning in opposite directions
- Electromagnets drive the flywheels around a frictionless maglev surface (analogous to maglev trains: magnetic levitation eliminates friction, enabling very high rotational speed at low energy cost)
- The **counter-rotation** of the charged flywheels generates the EM field responsible for both anti-gravity propulsion and the manipulation of SpaceTime (frame-dragging)

Masters notes this is exactly what would be needed for a Tipler-disc type time machine: the counter-rotating mass creates the frame-dragging effect, and the electromagnetic field is a byproduct of the charged rotating mass.

Wartena is noted as knowing "nothing about UFOs or electromagnetics" — his account of this mechanism predates its theoretical articulation in the open physics literature.

## Significance

The Wartena case provides an engineering detail corroborating both:
1. The electromagnetic field anomalies consistently reported around UAP
2. The Tipler/frame-dragging time machine mechanism
