# Martin Cannon

UFO/mind-control researcher; author of *The Controllers* (1989, samizdat-circulated essay), one of the first major attempts to argue that abduction phenomena and **MK-Ultra-style mind-control programs** structurally overlap — that some apparent UAP encounters are explainable as classified neuro-electromagnetic experimentation on civilians, while others remain genuinely anomalous.

Cannon's work is referenced as a primary anchor for the [[mk-ultra-mind-control-ecosystem]] synthesis and the broader thesis that the abduction-phenomenon category includes both genuine NHI contact and human-staged interventions designed to imitate it.
