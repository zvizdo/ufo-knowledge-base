# Senior Chief Wiggins

Active-duty U.S. Navy Senior Chief Petty Officer who publicly raised his hand and testified about UAP incidents, becoming one of the named targets of the **[[arrow-organization]]** witness intimidation campaign described by [[jeremy-corbell]].

## Congressional Testimony

Wiggins testified publicly — "raised his hand, active Navy" — providing on-record UAP witness testimony to congressional or public forums. Corbell facilitated the broader pool from which witnesses for the UAP hearings were drawn and describes Wiggins as someone who ultimately came forward despite the Arrow pressure.

## Arrow Pressure Campaign

Following his public testimony, Wiggins was subjected to a structured intimidation sequence Corbell describes in detail:

1. **Initial contact**: Arrow called under the pretext "I'm not interested in people. I'm just interested in events" — designed to extract names of other witnesses while appearing to conduct neutral event reconstruction.
2. **Entrapment risk**: Arrow sought to "weaponize his answers" — having him name people in the room (which Corbell already knew and had provided publicly) in a context that could construct a false entrapment scenario.
3. **Refusal**: Wiggins declined follow-up interviews — "I told them everything I know."
4. **Escalation**: Arrow contacted his superior with menacing calls.
5. **Flex threat**: Arrow's escalation threat was "we'll go to Pete Hegseth" — invoking the Secretary of Defense. Corbell's reaction: "That made me laugh. That is the worst possible flex they could do."

> ⚠ Arrow's stated justification — "I'm just interested in events, not people" — is identified by Corbell as a deliberate lie, as all names were already in the public record.
