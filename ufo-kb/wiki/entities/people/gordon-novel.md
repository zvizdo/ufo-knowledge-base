# Gordon Novel

Gordon Novel was a colorful figure at the intersection of the CIA, JFK assassination research, and UAP. He appeared in Jim Garrison's New Orleans JFK investigation and subsequently claimed involvement in classified UAP programs. In later years he worked with [[robert-bigelow]] and [[nids]] on private UAP research, claiming to have information about government UAP retrieval efforts.

Novel's claims are difficult to verify — he operated in the murky zone between intelligence asset, grifter, and genuine insider. His connections to Bigelow/NIDS, however, place him in proximity to the serious private UAP research network that preceded AAWSAP.
