# Oliver Sacks

Neurologist and author of acclaimed case-study books including *Awakenings* (Parkinson's patients awakened by L-DOPA) and *The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat*. Known for studying "quirky syndromes" at the edges of neurological understanding. [[diane-powell]] cites meeting Sacks in 1986 as the beginning of her interest in autism and savant abilities — specifically his documentation of institutionalized autistic twins who played games using six-digit prime numbers with no known algorithm, simply "seeing" the numbers.

## Significance Here

The twins Sacks documented represent an early canonical case of cognition that cannot be explained by conventional neuroscientific models. They did not calculate prime numbers — they *saw* them. This is the same structural challenge posed by [[kim-peek]]'s memory and by the children in *[[telepathy-tapes]]*: the information appears to be accessed rather than computed.
