# Arthur Young

Arthur M. Young (1905–1995) was the co-inventor of the Bell helicopter and a philosophical mystic whose interests spanned geometry, consciousness, and esoteric traditions. His significance in the UAP/JFK network lies in his participation in the 1952 Nine seance and the connections that linked his household to both the intelligence community and the Kennedy assassination.

## Background

Young developed the Bell helicopter rotor mechanism during WWII and later devoted himself to philosophy, writing *The Reflexive Universe* (a geometric theory of process and consciousness). He was deeply interested in esoteric traditions and was part of the elite social network that convened for the 1952 Nine seance.

## Notable Connections

### The Nine Seance (1952)
Young attended the [[1952-nine-seance]] organized by [[andrija-puharich]] in Maine — a gathering of blue-blooded American society figures (old money, establishment families) who made contact with what purported to be nine non-human intelligences.

### Ruth Forbes Payne Young
His wife was [[ruth-forbes-payne-young]] — a Forbes family heiress and best friend of [[mary-douglas]], who was Allen Dulles's mistress. This places Young two degrees from the CIA director who would later sit on the Warren Commission.

### Ruth Payne — The Oswald Connection
His daughter-in-law [[ruth-payne]] was the Quaker woman living in Texas who housed Lee Harvey Oswald, his wife Marina, and their children in the weeks before the Kennedy assassination. Ruth Payne obtained Oswald his job at the Texas School Book Depository. When Ruth Payne was testifying before the Warren Commission, [[allen-dulles]] personally cut off her testimony at the exact moment she was about to describe her visit to Arthur Young's household.
