# Plutarch

Greek historian and biographer (c. 46–119 AD). Best known for *Parallel Lives* (biographies of famous Greeks and Romans) and *Moralia* (essays on philosophy, religion, and ethics). Served as a priest at Delphi.

Relevant to this KB primarily through [[matt-lroy]]'s citation: Plutarch names "Elder Priest Sanas" as the source who transmitted the Atlantis account to [[solon-of-athens]] — adding a layer to the transmission chain from Egyptian priesthood → Solon → Plato's dialogues. Lroy argues this makes the Atlantis account a real historical record rather than Plato's philosophical invention.

Also cited in the context of [[temple-of-sais]] and the Egyptian mystery tradition connecting to the [[atlantis-thesis]].
