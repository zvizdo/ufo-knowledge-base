# Brigham Buhler

Healthcare entrepreneur who founded [[ways-to-well]], a functional medicine clinic in Austin, Texas. Former pharmaceutical and medical device sales representative who became disillusioned with the industry and pivoted to preventative, holistic medicine. Friend and collaborator of [[jesse-michaels]]; met through a UAP lecture Jesse attended in Austin.

## Background

Buhler worked inside the medical establishment in pharmaceutical and device sales before concluding the system is built to monetize chronic disease rather than prevent it. He describes having "played a million roles in this space" and knowing "where the traps are." He was instrumental in compounding pharmacy operations for peptides and fought regulatory battles with the FDA over access to treatments excluded from the insurance-driven mainstream.

## Senate Testimony

Testified before the US Senate alongside [[rfk-jr]] on healthcare corporate capture, opening with Eisenhower's warning (the less-cited second half of the "military-industrial complex" speech) about corporate capture of the scientific-industrial complex stifling innovation. His testimony focused on:
- Removing soft drinks and ultra-processed foods from government nutrition programs for poverty-stricken families
- The broken incentive structure that rewards managed chronic disease over prevention
- The [[american-heart-association]]'s opposition to removing sugar-laden products — funded by PepsiCo/Coca-Cola

## Claims on Pharmaceutical Innovation

Between 2011 and 2019, 356 blockbuster drugs reached market. Buhler claims approximately zero to two of these were originated by pharmaceutical companies — the rest came from taxpayer-funded research (universities, NIH, DARPA) that was then licensed or acquired by pharma at minimal cost once Phase 3 risk was gone. GLP-1 weight-loss drugs are his primary example — discovered through research on Gila monsters by an academic scientist.

## UAP Interest

Buhler met Jesse Michaels because of his longstanding interest in UAP/alien phenomena. His [[ways-to-well]] clinic in Austin features alien-themed murals (alien hand reaching for DNA; a "girl and alien" mural) and an AI healthcare assistant. He is a regular consumer of UAP content from Jesse Michaels, Chris Ramsey, Jeremy Corbell, and George Knapp. He believes the UAP question and the health reform question will eventually converge — an advanced NHI intelligence would presumably have solved human health optimization.

## Gain of Function / COVID

Buhler frames gain-of-function research as a biowarfare euphemism — no such program has ever produced a cure for the pathogen it engineered. He references the 2017 DARPA-rejected EcoHealth Alliance SARS modification proposal that then proceeded at the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology]], and cites Tulsi Gabbard's warnings about bioweapon labs in active war zones.
