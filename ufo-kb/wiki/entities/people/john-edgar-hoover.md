# J. Edgar Hoover

First Director of the Federal Bureau of Investigation (1935–1972); Director of the FBI's predecessor the Bureau of Investigation from 1924. Hoover served as FBI Director under eight presidents, from Coolidge through Nixon, making him the longest-serving head of any US national security agency. He died in office in 1972.

**Surveillance and COINTELPRO**: Hoover built the FBI's domestic surveillance infrastructure, including COINTELPRO (Counter Intelligence Program, 1956–1971), which targeted civil rights leaders, socialist organizations, and political dissidents. COINTELPRO is documented as using psychological operations, false documents, informants, and harassment — techniques that overlap with the [[mk-ultra]] research context.

**MK-Ultra adjacency**: The FBI and CIA operated parallel mind-control research interests during the Cold War era, with some documented coordination. In the KB, the connection appears through [[abduction-research]] — certain MK-Ultra survivor testimony research intersects with abduction case claims, and some researchers have pointed to FBI records as evidence of institutional awareness of these programs.

**UAP connection**: Hoover's FBI did accumulate records on UFO sightings, and some declassified FBI documents from the late 1940s show correspondence between Hoover and military officials about Roswell-era UAP reports. However, these connections are peripheral in the KB context.
