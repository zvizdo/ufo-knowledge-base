# Doyle Rees

> *Stub. Expand with biography, career arc, post-1950 assignments, and any additional UAP-related documentation as it enters the KB.*

Lt Col USAF; District Commander of the [[17th-osi-district|17th District USAF Office of Special Investigations]] at [[kirtland-afb]], NM, during the foundational 1948–1950 New Mexico green-fireballs collection period. Per [[dow-uap-d17-sandia-base-1948-1950]], Rees was:

- The **principal addressee** of all seven [[lincoln-lapaz|Lincoln LaPaz]] "Anomalous Luminous Phenomena" reports (Second through Seventh) — the foundational scientific analyses of the 1948-1950 New Mexico green fireballs corpus
- The **signatory** of the 25 May 1950 "Summary of Observations of Aerial Phenomena in the New Mexico Area, December 1948 – May 1950" to **Brig Gen [[joseph-f-carroll]]** (Director of Special Investigations, HQ USAF) — the foundational consolidation document of the 17th OSI District's UAP collection program
- The **signatory** of the 18 May 1949 cover letter forwarding the 19 April 1949 Summary of Information to Sandia Base (with 11-recipient distribution list including AMC Wright-Patterson, FBI Albuquerque, AESS, Holloman AFB, AEC Los Alamos)

Rees's command made the 17th OSI District the **institutional epicenter** of US-government UAP collection during the period that produced Project Sign / Grudge / Twinkle.
