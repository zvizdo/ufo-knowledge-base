# Luke Caverns

## Background

Real surname Reagan — a distant cousin of Ronald Reagan. The "Caverns" pseudonym was chosen for privacy and to avoid search-result confusion with the former president. His family's archaeological lineage stretches back to the 1890s: ancestors searched for lost Spanish gold in the American Southwest (Reagan Canyon, Big Bend, TX) and later discovered 450-year-old Spanish gold mines in southern New Mexico before the company collapsed.

Trained under archaeologist [[ed-barnhart]] and counts [[graham-hancock]] and [[randall-carlson]] as intellectual inspirations. Self-described "vigilante archaeologist" who critiques the sterilization of academic archaeology while maintaining epistemic humility — his default position on grand claims is "probably not, but maybe."

Active fieldwork (as of late 2024): Peru (11 days), Egypt (20 days), Guatemala (9 days) in consecutive weeks. Has access to the tunnels under [[sacsayhuaman]] through a personal connection with the head archaeologist there.

## Notable Claims

### Sphinx and pre-dynastic structures
[[robert-schoch]] and [[john-anthony-west]]'s water erosion argument makes the [[great-sphinx]] the strongest physical evidence for a pre-dynastic monument in Egypt. The [[osireion]] at [[abydos]] and the Valley Temple adjacent to the Sphinx share the same enormous red Aswan granite construction style and almost certainly share a pre-dynastic builder — the same architect responsible for all three.

[[graham-hancock]] has converged on the Great Pyramid being 4th dynasty (~2650–2450 BC). The popular "Egypt is 12,000 years old" claim is not backed by evidence beyond the Sphinx.

### Ka-burial thesis (pyramid purpose)
No hieroglyphs inside 4th-dynasty pyramid interiors — yet older tombs (Saqqara step pyramid) do have interior decoration. Luke's hypothesis: by the 4th dynasty the pharaoh was no longer a pious king serving a god but *was* the god, and the pyramid housed only the divine *Ka* (not the body). Pharaohs' physical remains may have been buried in the south at [[abydos]], as suggested by Amenemhat II's dual burial-site practice and southern precedent.

### Osiris Shaft and Giza underground
[[osiris-shaft]] beneath the Giza causeway contains a sarcophagus on an elevated platform surrounded by a water-filled chamber — matching Herodotus's 5th-century BC description of a tomb on an island surrounded by a lake under the pyramids. The Giza Plateau is a "honeycomb" of natural caves, shaft tombs, and labyrinths from multiple eras.

### Tayos Cave / Library
Post-moon-landing, [[neil-armstrong]] went to Ecuador with a BBC film crew to find the [[tayos-cave]]. He failed; [[luis-felipe-san-salvador]] claims Armstrong was sent in the wrong direction and that his family holds the real coordinates.

### Mainstream vs. alternative archaeology
Archaeology releases are timed marketing campaigns: discoveries are held for months or years before press releases to maximize tourism impact. Cites Tuthmose II's tomb (found early 2024, published late 2025) and the Sacsayhuaman tunnels as examples.
