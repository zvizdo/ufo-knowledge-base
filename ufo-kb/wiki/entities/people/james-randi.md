# James Randi

Stage magician and professional skeptic (1928–2020). Co-founded the Committee for the Scientific Investigation of Claims of the Paranormal ([[csicop]]) in 1976 and the James Randi Educational Foundation in 1996.

## Background

Randi made his name first as an escape artist and then as a debunker of psychic claimants — most prominently [[uri-geller]], whom he pursued through television confrontations, books (*The Truth About Uri Geller*), and a long-running campaign questioning the methodology of [[andrija-puharich]]'s research circle.

## Notable Claims

- Argued that all of Geller's demonstrated effects could be reproduced by stage magic
- Designed methodological critiques of remote-viewing protocols at SRI
- The "One Million Dollar Paranormal Challenge" — never awarded; critics argued the protocol was structured to be effectively unwinnable

## Role in the Skepticism Cluster

Randi is the public face of the organized-skepticism movement that opposed [[stargate-program]], [[remote-viewing]], and parapsychology generally. The "sheep–goat effect" debate ([[gertrude-schmeidler]]) raises the question of whether his presence in psi experiments suppressed effects rather than disproved them.
