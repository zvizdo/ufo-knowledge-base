# John von Neumann

Hungarian-American mathematician (1903–1957), widely regarded as one of the most important mathematicians of the 20th century. His contributions span quantum mechanics (von Neumann algebras, measurement problem), computer architecture, game theory, and the Manhattan Project.

## UAP Connection (Alleged)

In the UAP discourse, von Neumann's name surfaces in two contexts:

1. **Wave-function/NHI manifestation**: [[whitley-strieber]] claims von Neumann wrote a suppressed paper arguing that NHI can manifest into physical reality via wave-function collapse — and that once a civilization accepts this, the door "opens only once and only in one direction, forever," creating a unique suppression motive.
2. **UAP briefings**: The [[wilson-davis-memo]] network and associated sources suggest von Neumann was among early cleared scientists briefed on crash-retrieval material, given his role in government defense science during the late 1940s.

Both claims are unverified and rest on second- or third-hand accounts.
