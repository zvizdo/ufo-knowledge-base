# Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez

Democratic Congresswoman representing New York's 14th congressional district. Was present at the July 2023 congressional UAP hearing where [[david-grusch]], [[ryan-graves]], and David Fravor testified. Ocasio-Cortez questioned Grusch about the national security and oversight implications of classified programs operating entirely outside Congressional visibility — the constitutional accountability dimension of the UAP program secrecy issue.

## UAP-Disclosure Legislative Role

Ocasio-Cortez has been a vocal House Democratic co-signer of UAP-transparency efforts since 2023, notably supporting the Schumer-Rounds UAP Disclosure Act framework when it was pending in Congress. As a House Oversight Subcommittee member (under James Comer's chairmanship), she participated in the July 2023 public UAP hearing with David Grusch, David Fravor, and Ryan Graves.

Her public-statement posture on UAP is measured: she has emphasized intelligence-community-transparency and whistleblower-protection framings rather than NHI-existence claims, treating UAP disclosure as part of a broader civil-libertarian critique of executive-branch classification practice. She is not on the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence (where the actual UAP-disclosure legislative gatekeeping happens), limiting her direct legislative leverage on the issue.
