# Thomas F. Mantell

Kentucky Air National Guard P-51 Mustang pilot and flight leader; killed January 7, 1948 while climbing in pursuit of an unidentified aerial object over Godman Field, Fort Knox — the first aviation fatality attributed to UAP pursuit in US military records.

## Role

Mantell was flight leader of a NG formation that encountered the object while in transit. He broke off to pursue it, climbing without supplemental oxygen past his operational ceiling. His last radio transmission: "I can see the object — it's metallic and of tremendous size." His aircraft subsequently crashed.

The official Air Force explanation (planet Venus + hypoxia) was contested within [[project-sign]]; the Venus explanation is geometrically implausible for the reported visibility conditions.
