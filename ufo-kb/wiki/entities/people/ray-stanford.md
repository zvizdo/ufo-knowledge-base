# Ray Stanford

American UFO investigator and author who became the most detailed analyst of the [[1964-socorro]] landing case in New Mexico. Stanford wrote *Socorro Saucer on a Pentagon Pantry* (1976) — the definitive early account of the case — and maintained a close relationship with Lonnie Zamora's family.

## Background

Stanford (now deceased — "RIP" per James Fox) investigated the Socorro incident over many years. He befriended Zamora's wife Mary and their children Michael and daughter. He compiled newspaper clippings, national archives documents, and conducted extensive interviews.

## Role in Holloman Investigation

[[james-fox]], while conducting a five-year deep dive into [[1964-socorro]], met Stanford and through him discovered the trail that led to the [[1964-holloman-afb-landing]] investigation. In the process of reading Stanford's collected articles, Fox encountered [[coral-lorenzen]]'s UFO Bulletin pieces describing a landing at Holloman witnessed by military personnel — a discovery that launched Fox's years-long Holloman inquiry.
