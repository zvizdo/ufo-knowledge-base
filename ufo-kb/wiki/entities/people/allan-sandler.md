# Allan Sandler

Documentary filmmaker who, with [[bob-emenegger]], produced "UFOs: Past, Present and Future" — a film notable for its unprecedented cooperation with active-duty military personnel and Air Force bases in the 1970s. The film included a veiled reference to a landing at [[holloman-air-force-base]] that "may have already happened or may happen."

## The Footage Claim

Sandler told [[james-fox]] that he personally viewed footage of the [[1964-holloman-afb-landing]] at [[norton-air-force-base]]. The footage was shown to him by [[paul-charlton]], an officer at Norton who had processed (developed/transferred) the film.

Sandler's description of what he saw:
- Black and white footage
- Three disc-shaped craft at relatively high altitude, escorted by military jets
- Two craft peel away; one comes down in a floating, leaf-like wobble (possibly in distress)
- Craft lands on three pods; a sliding door opens (no visible seam) and a ramp extends
- Two beings with Egyptian-style headgear, big eyes with vertical-slit pupils, large noses, minimal mouths/chins flank the ramp
- A primary being with what appears to be a communication device descends
- Beings meet with military officers; get into a Jeep and leave
- Film ends with the disc still sitting on the base

Sandler told Fox he drove over to his car, closed his eyes, and verbally described the film in real time — Fox pulled over and recorded the description.

## Provenance Chain

Sandler was never told Bob Emenegger about seeing the footage. Fox learned about it, got the two of them on the phone together — Sandler's explanation to Emenegger was essentially: "You never asked."

Men in suits arrived at Norton AFB either the same day or the morning after Sandler saw the footage, confiscated it, and told [[paul-charlton]]: "Don't ever talk about it." Charlton did not know who the men worked for.

The original footage was provided to Clark by a Los Angeles TV producer who reportedly had the master on Beta SP. That producer has since died; his son was unresponsive when Fox's team contacted him.
