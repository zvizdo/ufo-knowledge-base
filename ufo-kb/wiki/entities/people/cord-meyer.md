# Cord Meyer

CIA officer (1920–2001); headed the CIA's International Organizations Division, coordinating covert influence operations through labor unions, student groups, and media outlets (Operation Mockingbird era). Former World Federalist; became a committed Cold Warrior after WWII.

Most relevant to this KB through his connection to [[mary-meyer]] — his ex-wife, who became John F. Kennedy's closest mistress and was murdered on October 12, 1964, on the C&O Canal towpath in Georgetown. James Angleton reportedly retrieved her diary from her home hours after her murder. The circumstances of Mary Meyer's death and her relationship to both JFK and Cord Meyer make her case a node connecting CIA, JFK, and the broader covert intelligence world.
