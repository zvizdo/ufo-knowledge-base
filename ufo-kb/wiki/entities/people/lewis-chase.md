# Lewis Chase

USAF Lt. Colonel serving as both base operations officer and designated UFO officer at [[malmstrom-air-force-base]] in 1967 (the latter role created at USAF direction to assist the [[condon-commission]] Condon Committee investigation). On the night of March 24, 1967, Chase went to [[belt-montana]] to investigate a UFO reported by a truck driver and highway patrol. He wrote official reports to USAF HQ and Condon describing the UFO activity *and* noting he had "coordinated in writing" with the wing command post officer about the UFO-related missile shutdowns.

When Condon investigator Roy Craig visited Malmstrom in October 1967 and interviewed Chase, Chase denied any knowledge of the missile shutdowns or their connection to UFO activity. Salas holds copies of Chase's own contemporaneous documents that contradict his Craig interview denial.

## Background

Ray Fowler ([[sylvania-corporation]]) gave Condon investigator Roy Craig detailed information, names, and phone numbers of contractor witnesses who saw UFOs connected to the missile shutdowns. Craig went to Malmstrom and interviewed Chase, who stonewalled. Salas characterizes Chase's denial as deliberate, driven by the Condon Commission being a pre-determined whitewash (as confirmed by the [[pentacle-memo]] and [[robert-low]]'s internal memo).

## Notable Claims

The October 1967 Craig interview (captured on audio; Salas has a copy) shows Chase explicitly denying knowledge of the missile shutdown connection while his own earlier written reports state the opposite.
