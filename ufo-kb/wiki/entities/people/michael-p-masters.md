# Michael P. Masters

Biological anthropologist and professor at Montana Tech University. Author of three books developing the **Extratemporalis model** — the theoretical framework that UAP/NHI phenomena are best explained as future Homo sapiens who have developed time travel and are returning to the past for research, genetic banking, and timeline management.

## Background

Masters trained as a biological anthropologist with expertise in human evolution, primate anatomy, and the fossil record. His interest in the UAP phenomenon began at age 8 with what he describes as a moment of conscious precognition — a visualization of an early human, a modern human, and a Gray alien together, with associated thoughts he interprets as information transmitted backward in time from his future self (the classic "closed time-like loop of sorts" in his framing).

He later changed his academic major specifically to biological anthropology to study the humanoid form associated with UAP entities — motivated, he believes, by a subtle prompt from the future humans he would eventually encounter.

## The Extratemporalis Model

### Core Biological Argument

The suite of characteristics attributed to Gray-type entities — large heads relative to body, diminished sensory organs (small nose, ears), large eyes adapted to screen-time, reduced musculature, child-like proportions — maps precisely onto the **predicted evolutionary trajectory of modern humans** given ongoing domestication, reduced physical demands, and increased cognitive/digital life:

- **Neoteny**: A species' distant descendants can resemble the children of its current form (paedomorphosis). Grays are consistently described as child-sized and child-like.
- **Reproductive compatibility**: Entities can breathe human air, have sex with humans, produce offspring with humans — biologically impossible between species that diverged on different planets.
- **Linguistic compatibility**: Many contactees report entities speaking human languages. This is inexplicable for extraterrestrials.
- **Probabilistic proximity**: Just as you are most likely to encounter people from nearby geographic regions, temporal proximity means you are most likely to encounter beings from the nearer future rather than from cosmically distant times.

### Physical Evidence from PEER Study

The Edgar Mitchell Foundation for Research into Extraterrestrial Encounters (PEER) accumulated ~5,000 contactee/abductee experiencer accounts. Masters extracts:
- **52% of respondents** encountered humanoid beings
- **85% of those** reported friendly or neutral experiences
- Respondents encountering non-humanoid forms (mantis-like, insectoid) had more negative experiences — consistent with the model that more temporally distant future visitors are rarer and less relatable

### Gamete Extraction and the Intertemporal Breeding Program

Reproductive procedures are among the most ubiquitous themes across abduction literature (1970s–90s especially). Masters' explanation:
- Male sperm counts have dropped ~60% in the developed world over 40 years (50% globally); below 40 million per milliliter, fertility problems emerge
- Future humans facing reproductive collapse return to the past to extract viable genetic material from ancestors whose genome is still intact
- Antonio Villas Boas (1957): sexual intercourse with a female NHI — reproductive compatibility argument
- Barney Hill: reluctantly disclosed semen sample extraction
- Jim Penniston / Rendlesham: explicitly told future humans were "having problems with reproduction" and taking gametes

This is not a hybrid program in the traditional sense — it is **genetic banking from the ancestral past**.

### Physics: Tipler Disc as UAP Form Factor

Masters connects to Frank Tipler's 1970s work: a finite, rapidly-rotating ring/sphere/disc creates sufficient frame-dragging (general relativity) to bend light cones backward — enabling closed timelike curves. This is the smallest-possible time machine geometry within general relativistic physics. Key connection: **the Tipler disc is exactly the UAP disc/saucer form factor** ("form follows function" in biology).

Supporting effects reported near UAP:
- Time dilation: grass growing faster, watches showing days elapsed, beard growth
- Blue-shifted light from Alcubierre-style warp bubble (causing skin burns, temporary blindness)
- EM field anomalies: craft interfere with electronics; portal sites show persistent EM drain

### Corroborating Quotes from Prior Researchers

- **George Hoover** (Navy Commander, top-secret clearance): Roswell beings were time-traveling humans
- **Herman Oberth** (father of German rocketry): UFOs "jump from one time-space coordinate to another"
- **Steven Spielberg** (1993, to Robert Liefeld): "What's easier to believe — that we are being monitored by aliens from another world, or that we are monitoring ourselves and studying us from the future?"
- **Tom DeLonge** (Newsweek): "These things aren't coming from other planets; these things might be coming through time"

### Timeline Wars

Masters' third book (*Revelation: The Future Human Past*) addresses the possibility of **multiple future-human factions with competing timeline interests** — some guiding humanity toward ascension, others attempting to control or extract from it. This explains why some UAP encounters are friendly, others malevolent.

## Personal Contact Experience

At a Phoenix, Arizona UFO conference (approximately one year before this interview), Masters experienced what he describes as a firsthand encounter with two future humans who manifested physically:

1. One individual approached and sat so close their knee was in Masters' lap; said "I need to be this close for this to work"
2. Stated: "We know you've been thinking about quitting lately and we'd really prefer you didn't do that" — Masters had not spoken this thought aloud
3. When Masters asked how they knew, replied: "Once you know who we are, you'll know how we know that"
4. Masters telepathically communicated the thought "future humans"; they confirmed
5. Communication became entirely telepathic; both beings shared the same voice in his head
6. With consent, they blacked out his eyes and loaded three pieces of partitioned information into his brain — which he cannot consciously access
7. He then slept 13 hours straight and cried for hours afterward — ongoing as they moved through conference

One of the two beings reappeared briefly in an empty conference hallway, placed its hand on his shoulder, and asked "Are you okay?"

Masters identifies this with the "hive mind" model: he was not talking to two individuals but to a unified consciousness that knew his private thoughts, could reference past interactions, and could partition stored information in human neural architecture.
