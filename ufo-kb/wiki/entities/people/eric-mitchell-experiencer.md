# Eric Mitchell (Experiencer)

> ⚠ **Name disambiguation**: This page is for Eric Mitchell, a self-described "super experiencer" present in [[iPxbILOMQ08]] alongside [[steve-coburn]]. He is distinct from [[edgar-mitchell]], the Apollo 14 astronaut. The host refers to doing "an amazing show with yesterday" with Eric Mitchell, implying a separate episode exists.

## Known Information

Eric Mitchell is described as a close friend of [[steve-coburn]] and a "super experiencer" — meaning he would qualify as a class-2 experiencer in Coburn's typology (deep NHI program involvement, multiple implants, abduction history).

### Implant Scan Results
Coburn scanned Eric with the stud-finder and G-meter protocol. Hits detected at:
- **Back of neck**
- **Arm**
- **Knee** (weak signal; Leir considered it tentative)

### Neodymium Magnet Treatment
[[roger-leir]] mailed Eric a 100 lb neodymium magnet and instructed him to hold it to his knee ~10 minutes per day. Leir's theory: the magnet would create an eddy effect to loosen any implant from surrounding tissue and draw it toward the skin surface. Coburn reports this **worked** — the object apparently migrated. No surgical removal was performed.

### Near-Miss with Leir
Eric was scheduled to meet [[roger-leir]] in Eureka Springs, Arkansas approximately two weeks before Leir's death. The meeting — presumably for further evaluation or surgical removal — never occurred.
