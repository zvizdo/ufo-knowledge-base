# Dan Kimball

Dan Kimball served as US Secretary of the Navy from 1949 to 1953. He is notable in UFO history for having had a firsthand UAP sighting while serving in that capacity — giving his subsequent actions an unusual quality of personal conviction.

## The 1950 California-Hawaii Sighting

According to [[donald-keyhoe]]'s account (as relayed by [[richard-dolan]]), Kimball was flying from California to Hawaii in ~1950, accompanied by Admiral [[arthur-radford]]. The two aircraft were flying some miles apart. An unidentified object circled Kimball's aircraft — and then Radford's aircraft separately — before departing.

Kimball's response was direct: he decided the Navy would conduct its own independent UFO investigation, entirely separate from the Air Force. Per Dolan's account, this effort "didn't seem to take off. Didn't really go anywhere" — but the impulse itself reflects how seriously Kimball took the encounter.

## Navy vs. Air Force Context

Kimball's experience and subsequent attempt to establish independent Navy investigation fits into the broader pattern documented by Keyhoe: Air Force personnel would board Navy ships, assert dominance over UFO investigations, and Navy officers would resent it. The "revolt of the admirals" (~1949) — a simultaneous bureaucratic battle over naval aviation and nuclear weapons — provides the backdrop for understanding why Kimball might want to assert naval independence on the UFO issue as well.
