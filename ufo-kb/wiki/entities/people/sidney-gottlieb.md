# Sidney Gottlieb

Sidney Gottlieb (1918–1999) was the CIA's chief chemist and director of [[mk-ultra]], nicknamed "the poisoner in chief." He oversaw the program's most aggressive phases: non-consensual LSD dosing in prisons, [[operation-midnight-climax]] safe houses, and the assassination plots that used chemical and biological agents.

## Role in MK-Ultra

Gottlieb ran the Technical Services Staff division responsible for MK-Ultra's pharmacological research. He authorized experiments on non-consenting subjects across prison populations and the general public via Operation Midnight Climax. He also personally delivered a lethal dose of shellfish toxin to CIA station chiefs — poison that Frank Olson allegedly discovered and may have been killed for knowing about (1953 Olson death).

## Connection to Jolly West

[[tom-oneill]] discovered in [[jolly-west]]'s UCLA papers that West was writing to Gottlieb using an **alias** — reporting on experiments in San Francisco in 1967 that paralleled Charles Manson's time at the Haight-Ashbury Free Clinic. Gottlieb is identified as the head of the program to whom West reported. This alias use suggests the correspondence was intended to be deniable.

## Post-Program

Gottlieb retired in 1973, the same year [[richard-helms]] ordered the MK-Ultra files destroyed. He died in 1999, days after Helms (his superior who had ordered the files' destruction). He had long since claimed the program had failed and the work was misguided — consistent with the CIA's managed public posture following the Church Committee.

## The Frank Olson Murder

In 1953, [[frank-olson]] — a Fort Detrick [[mk-naomi]] biological warfare researcher — was beginning to develop a conscience crisis and appeared to be moving toward whistleblowing. Gottlieb:
1. Invited Olson to a party outside Washington, DC
2. Covertly dosed him with LSD and Quaaludes
3. Had [[pierre-lafitte]] push Olson from the 10th floor of the Statler Hotel in New York

The Olson family eventually received a **$700,000 White House compensation check** after finding a Rockefeller Commission Report paragraph describing the LSD dosing. [[colin-ross]] presents this as a documented state murder.
