# Melissa Casillas

Administrative assistant at [[los-alamos]] National Laboratory who disappeared June 26, 2025 from the Ranchos de Taos area of New Mexico, approximately one hour from the lab.

## Disappearance

**Date:** June 26, 2025  
**Location:** Ranchos de Taos, New Mexico (State Road 518; headed toward Carson National Forest)

Timeline:
- ~6:15 AM — Melissa drops husband Mark at LANL campus; he watches her swipe her badge at the gate
- Melissa drives home; tells daughter Sierra she forgot her badge and will work from home
- **Discrepancy:** Mark says he watched her badge in; she told Sierra she forgot it — one of them was wrong, or Melissa was not being truthful
- Sierra leaves for work; Melissa's boss calls Mark around 1:30 PM — she never showed
- 2:15 PM — doorbell cameras catch Melissa walking along State Road 518 in a turquoise shirt, blue jeans, and maroon sweatshirt around her waist, heading toward Carson National Forest (~3 miles from home)
- A witness reported a blue Dodge truck following her at the time she appeared on camera
- At home: car in driveway; door locked; found inside — purse, wallet, keys, both work and personal phones (personal phone factory-reset), an uncashed check, and some dollar bills
- Items she appeared to take: toothbrush, hair iron, other personal items

**Source's assessment:** The factory-reset phones (removing message history) and the personal items taken (toothbrush, hair iron — suggesting running electricity and a mirror) point toward voluntary departure rather than abduction or suicide. Financial stress (a car accident settlement for daughter Sierra that fell through, accumulating medical costs) is cited as a plausible personal pressure. The source includes her case but explicitly states: "we're not including her as evidence of something darker."

**However:** Her [[los-alamos]] connection placed her in public focus regardless of whether her administrative role touched classified material.
