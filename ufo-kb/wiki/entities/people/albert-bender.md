# Albert Bender

1950s UFO researcher who founded the **International Flying Saucer Bureau** (IFSB), one of the earliest civilian UFO research organizations. Shut it down abruptly in 1953 after allegedly being visited by three Men in Black.

## The Three Men in Black Encounter

Bender's account (documented in *Flying Saucers and the Three Men*, 1962):
- Three men appeared with **orange glowing eyes** and a strong **sulfur smell**
- They conveyed information about UFOs through some form of telepathic or direct communication
- Bender reported being **teleported to an Antarctic underground base**
- After the encounter he was visibly shaken and declined to publicly reveal what he had been told for years

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] reads Bender's account as directly connecting the Men in Black phenomenon to the Antarctic Nazi/antediluvian facility thread — the same base the Navy SEAL described (polished black stone, Schwarze Sonne, submarine access).

## Significance

The Bender case is one of the earliest documented MIB encounters and connects the silencing-of-researchers pattern to a specific geographic location (Antarctica) rather than simply government harassment.
