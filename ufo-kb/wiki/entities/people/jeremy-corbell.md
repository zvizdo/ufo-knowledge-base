# Jeremy Corbell

Documentary filmmaker and investigative journalist who has become one of the most consequential figures in the modern UAP disclosure cycle through a combination of documentary work and leaked video releases.

## Bob Lazar Documentary (2018)

*Bob Lazar: Area 51 and Flying Saucers* (2018) revived mainstream interest in [[bob-lazar]]'s account, featuring new interviews and serving as the proximate cause of Lazar's wider re-emergence in UAP discourse. The documentary introduced Lazar to an audience unfamiliar with the 1989 Knapp broadcasts.

[[joe-rogan]] reports this documentary is the #1 most-watched episode on his YouTube channel, with approximately **63 million views** — making it the single most-viewed piece of UAP content in the modern era. Rogan credits it as a primary driver of the public appetite for UAP disclosure that preceded the 2017–2023 wave.

## Pentagon Video Leaks (2021–2023)

Corbell (often in partnership with [[george-knapp]]) has released multiple UAP videos that triggered formal Pentagon responses:
- The "USS Omaha" sphere video (2021) — showing a spherical UAP descending into the Pacific
- The "Mosul Orb" video — a spherical object filmed by US military in Iraq
- The "Pyramid UFOs" / USS Russell triangle video (2021)

Each release generated Pentagon confirmation that the videos were genuine, which served as a ratchet in the disclosure process — each confirmation making subsequent denials less credible.
