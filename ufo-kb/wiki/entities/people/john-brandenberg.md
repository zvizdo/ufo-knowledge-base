# John Brandenburg

Plasma physicist and author. Brandenburg's career spans government weapons research (Lawrence Livermore, Sandia National Labs), Pentagon consulting (Mission Research Corporation), independent Mars anomalies research (Stanford Research Institute), and theoretical physics (GEM theory). His two central claims — isotopic proof of an ancient nuclear war on Mars and experimental evidence for gravity modification — connect him to both the UFO cover-up and advanced classified programs.

## Background

- Grew up in Oregon; physics + math at Southern Oregon University; inspired by an uncle who worked on the [[manhattan-project]]
- Graduate research at **[[lawrence-livermore-national-lab]]** (fusion research, laser fusion, hydrogen bomb development); [[edward-teller]] was the program mentor — Brandenburg had "several pleasant conversations" and found him "creative and enlightened"
- Then **[[sandia-national-laboratories]]** (~3 years, directed energy weapons)
  - Witnessed the classified 1983 ABLE ARCHER crisis: "We were as close to a nuclear war as we were during the Cuban missile crisis but it was secret. Only people on the lab premises inside the fence knew how bad things were."
  - Felt despair; discovering Mars anomalies gave him "hope for the future for the human race"
- Moved to Washington DC; joined **Mission Research Corporation** as a consulting physicist to the Pentagon and intel agencies

## UFO Cover-Up Confirmation (Firsthand)

Brandenburg independently confirmed the existence of a UFO cover-up from two direct sources:

1. **Joint Chiefs staffer**: Brandenburg asked a colonel on the Joint Chiefs staff directly. Response: "Yes. That's all I can tell you. The rest you can figure out for yourself."
2. **Space Command staffer**: Brandenburg called Space Command and stated he'd heard of an extraterrestrial threat whose existence was classified and wanted to help. The staffer said "let me see if I can connect you with somebody" — then never called back.

Both contacts were during his Pentagon consulting period (early 1980s), before he claims he saw institutional evidence through his weapons work.

## Mars: Isotopic Evidence

His central scientific argument:

- **[[viking-landers]] (1976)** detected massive anomalous abundance of:
  - **Xenon-129**: produced by supernova explosions and hydrogen bomb detonations; no supernova possible on Mars; no other mainstream explanation exists
  - **Argon-40**: produced when potassium is irradiated by neutrons — the standard byproduct of nuclear explosions; super-abundant on Martian atmosphere samples
- The same isotopic signature was found in **Martian meteorites** — this is how scientists confirmed the meteorites' Martian origin (the signature is unique in the solar system)
- **ALH84001** meteorite: veins of carbonate in lava with organic matter; appeared to contain bacterial fossils; Brandenburg found this evidence "rather convincing"
- Brandenburg: "The consensus among the real experts on isotopes is that there is no explanation for the xenon-129 super-abundance or the argon-40 super-abundance."

## Independent Mars Investigation at SRI (1980s)

Brandenburg worked in the **Independent Mars Investigation** headquartered at [[stanford-research-institute]] — "the same place where they found out later that the remote viewing program was just down the hall."

- Role: gathering Viking images of the Face and D&M Pyramid at Cydonia at different lighting angles
- Key finding: **second face in the Utopia region** — another face resembling the Sidonia face, inside a helmet
- Published at a conference; after announcement, NASA renamed the Utopia region to **"Galaxias Chaos"** — Brandenburg believes this was deliberate suppression ("I'd thrown somebody's galaxy into chaos")
- **Discovered the Martian paleo-ocean**: followed the zero-kilometre elevation contour around the entire planet; below it was smooth old ocean floor with all river channels feeding in; above it was rough terrain. Announced at a **1986 Mars scientists conference** — NASA has never credited him; he calls it the "Brandenburg Ocean"
- Viking lander targeting of Cydonia was overruled by an unknown group ("boys downtown say you can't land there") — Brandenburg believes this was because Mariner 9 had already photographed the Face and Pyramid there

## Carl Sagan (Firsthand Phone Calls)

[[carl-sagan]] called Brandenburg twice:

1. **First call** (hostile): "Why are you causing this trouble?" — implied Sagan had non-public knowledge and was disturbed by Brandenburg's public research
2. After the second face was found, Sagan called again, apologized for being short, and asked for photos of the second face
   - Photos mailed to Carl Sagan, Cornell University — **never arrived** (Brandenburg watched the mailbox truck take the envelope)
   - Faxed copies instead; Sagan said the second face was "very interesting" (about two-thirds the size of the Sidonia face) — then the exchange ended

Brandenburg connecting the isotopic evidence with the Cydonia anomalies was an emotionally devastating moment: "I wept like a child for about four minutes. They were all dead. It looked like a thermonuclear holocaust." After four minutes he collected himself: "Don't be sad. Be happy. Now you know the universe has no safety net."

## GEM Theory (Gravity-Electromagnetism)

See [[gem-theory]] for the full account. Key points:
- Unifies gravity and electromagnetism via the **Poynting vector** and radiation pressure
- Interprets gravity as radiation pressure from zero-point vacuum fluctuations (similar to [[andrei-sakharov]]'s and [[hal-puthoff]]'s independent work)
- Predicts the gravitational constant Big G with no free parameters
- Derives the proton-electron mass ratio 1836; square root = **42.85** (Brandenburg was unaware of *Hitchhiker's Guide* when he derived this)
- Presented at APS meeting and a string theory conference (they got mad at him)
- [[darpa]] funded $200k; report accepted politely; Brandenburg believes relevant parts "went black" to Area 51

## Roswell (Firsthand Veteran Accounts)

At the "Reminiscences of Roswell" conference, Brandenburg sat at a banquet with firsthand witnesses — survivor officers and relatives of key figures. Key details they provided:

- The 509th Bomb Group at Roswell stored **all nuclear weapons on Earth** (five total)
- Black Widow (P-61) night fighters were relocated from Europe/Japan to patrol the nuclear stockpile
- **Colonel Blanchard** (base commander): liked and popular; announced the crash in a newspaper before receiving orders; was berated by phone from Fort Worth
- A **red-haired colonel** from an unknown command appeared and took over the base for ~a week; gathered all wreckage with deuce-and-half trucks of troops; I-beams with hieroglyphics and unusual plates described
- Col. Blanchard's quote at subsequent staff meeting: **"Boy did we screw up."**
- Separate second crash at **[[1947-san-agustin-crash|Plains of San Agustín]]** (~100 miles away, morning of July 4): intact saucer with big hole, dead aliens (coyotes had reached some), and **one live alien recovered**; same red-haired colonel; civilian witnesses threatened with death

## Intel Agency Recruitment Attempt

An unidentified intel agency (Brandenburg declines to name it) invited him to "discuss things more deeply" at headquarters. Before he entered the meeting, the person who'd recruited him apologized — signaling that someone powerful had blackballed Brandenburg. He was subjected to a "very bruising interrogation" designed to disqualify him. He was excluded from further contact. Brandenburg is relieved: "I I decided I was probably fortunate because I would have ended up at site 51 and a lot of bad stuff happens out there."

## Paul Benowitz Proximity

Brandenburg drove past **[[paul-benowitz]]'s Thunder Corporation** every morning on the way to Sandia Labs. He confirms Benowitz was **involuntarily committed** (not killed), consistent with the existing page but correcting any ambiguity about his fate.

Brandenburg heard multiple independent accounts of a military clash at [[dulce-underground-base]] from [[leonard-stringfield]], who had spoken directly to military veterans. The accounts differed only in casualty count (human and alien). Brandenburg found them credible given the consistency.
