# General Uchoa

Senior Brazilian military general who led the official Brazilian government investigation into the [[1977-colares-incident]]. He is the most senior military official publicly associated with that investigation's findings.

## Bigelow Dinner (circa 2009)

[[robert-bigelow]] hosted a dinner at a Washington DC hotel where General Uchoa presented the findings of the Colares investigation — photographs, reports, and documentary evidence produced by the Brazilian government. Uchoa brought his daughter to translate from Portuguese to English. He presented from the head of the table; [[lue-elizondo]] sat near the far end.

Elizondo describes this dinner as a critical **intellectual turning point** — he left "scratching my head" and began taking UAP seriously. An unnamed colleague who also attended the dinner had an equally significant reaction; Elizondo cannot publicly name this colleague as of 2024.

The presentation was the first time Elizondo encountered a senior government official from another country presenting organized, government-sourced UAP evidence.
