## Background

Nikola Tesla was a Serbian-American inventor and electrical engineer whose foundational contributions include alternating current (AC) electricity systems, radio transmission, X-ray technology, and resonance-based energy systems. He is widely regarded as one of the most consequential scientists of the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

Harald Malmgren stated: "In my mind, he was equal to or even stronger than Einstein." Malmgren's broader framing — "Einstein put a governor on our physical progress in reality" — positions Tesla as a figure whose more radical theoretical directions were effectively foreclosed by the dominance of Einsteinian physics, contributing to what is now catalogued as [[physics-lockdown]].

Tesla died in January 1943 in New York City. His papers were confiscated by the FBI almost immediately after his death and reviewed by John G. Trump, an MIT professor, leading radar expert, and the uncle of Donald Trump. Trump reportedly concluded the papers contained nothing of particular significance — a conclusion that Harald Malmgren and others treat with open skepticism.

## Notable Claims

- At his laboratory in Colorado Springs, Tesla claimed to have received communications from extraterrestrials via signals that resembled shortwave radio transmissions — an account that parallels [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s later claims of contact with "space brothers" via a similar device.
- Tesla's papers have never been publicly released despite decades of interest. Remarkably, there has been almost no organized public campaign for their release comparable to the sustained pressure applied to JFK assassination files — a disparity that researchers note as itself significant.
- Foreign intelligence sources told Malmgren that Tesla and [[thomas-townsend-brown]] collaborated directly on anti-gravity research and possibly on the transmutation of elements. If accurate, the papers may contain material far beyond what John Trump's review acknowledged.
