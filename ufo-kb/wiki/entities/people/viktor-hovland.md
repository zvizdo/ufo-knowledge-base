# Viktor Hovland

## Background

Viktor Hovland is a Norwegian professional golfer and one of the top players on the PGA Tour. He is notably one of the youngest players in the top 10. In his spare time he reads Plato, follows UAP discourse, and seeks out ancient and anomalous sites near tournament locations — including a personal visit to [[serpent-mound]] in Ohio during a Columbus tournament and Stonehenge during a London event.

He reached out to [[jesse-michaels]] via DM after discovering American Alchemy, citing it as something he was a fan of. The two connected and, when Viktor visited Austin in late April, participated in a golf outing at Barton Pitch and Putt with [[jamie-vernon]].

## Ancient Mysteries Interest

Viktor's curiosity spans UAPs and ancient history, and he appears to have genuine intellectual engagement rather than casual interest:

- Watched Jesse's [[graham-hancock]] interview and found it compelling
- Witnessed the Hancock vs. [[flint-dibble]] debate on JRE and felt both sides were "coming in not willing to budge"
- Visited [[serpent-mound]] in Ohio (during the Memorial tournament week)
- Visited Stonehenge during a London event
- Questions why the pyramids are not mentioned in the Bible, and why the Exodus narrative doesn't reference building them

## UAP Position

Viktor represents the thoughtful skeptic-adjacent position: high conviction that something real is being covered up (based on accumulation of witnesses and corroborating stories), frustrated by the absence of concrete physical evidence, but open-minded and unwilling to dismiss the topic.

His framing: "It's like in everything in life — you have to question everything, and in doing so you find that either you get rid of the beliefs that you have or you believe in the things you believe in even more deeply."

He finds the Malmgren interview especially compelling ("like you can't argue with his credentials") and raises the interesting question of why Bezos and Musk are investing in chemical rockets if the government already has exotic propulsion technology.
