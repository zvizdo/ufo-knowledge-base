# Matt Gaetz

Republican congressman from Florida's 1st district; one of the congressional members involved in UAP transparency efforts alongside [[tim-burchett]] and [[anna-paulina-luna]].

## Florida Pilot Briefing Confrontation

Gaetz, Burchett, and Luna flew to Florida after Gaetz was told that military pilots had UAP photographs. When military officials substituted a Chinese-infiltration briefing, Gaetz confronted them:

> "You're going to jerk me around."

Then threatened: "Here's what's going to happen — you're going to jerk me around. I'm going to subpoena you all. We're going to call y'all back to Washington."

Burchett, who was watching from the side, spotted CIA/military intelligence "suits" in the corner getting visibly nervous. Within an hour, the military reversed and brought the pilots in (still in flight gear). Burchett praised Gaetz: "You say what you want to about Matt Gates, but he's a great orator and he's a great attorney."

## Alien-Human Hybrid Whistleblower Claim

Gaetz claimed a whistleblower told him of a program involving captured extraterrestrials mating with abducted humans from war zones and migrant caravans to create hybrids for intergalactic communication — at 6–12 US facilities. The whistleblower sought coordinated congressional visits to prevent relocation.

**Gaetz did not verify any of this information.** Burchett and Rogan treated the claim with laughter and significant skepticism. This claim may represent disinformation injected to discredit UAP disclosure efforts, or it may be a genuine but extraordinarily unverified whistleblower account.
