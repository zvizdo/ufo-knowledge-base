# Jeff Natelli

USAF security officer stationed at [[vandenberg-afb]] between approximately 2003 and 2005, during a period of elevated UAP activity at the base. Natelli is cited by [[jesse-michaels]] as a "interesting witness" in the [[Qe8br8yYEDM]] interview with [[richard-barth]].

## Notable Claims

### October 14, 2003 — The Vandenberg Red Square

Boeing contractors reported a **massive glowing red square** silently hovering over two missile defense sites. The object drifted east onto the base and vanished over the hills. Natelli was on duty that night. Over the radio, he heard security guards at a critical launch site describing a fast-moving object approaching over the ocean — voices escalating to: *"It's coming right at us. It's coming right for us. And now it's right here."* — followed by the object shooting off and disappearing.

When Natelli arrived on scene, five shaken witnesses described a **massive triangular craft larger than a football field** that hovered silently for approximately 45 seconds over their entry control point before accelerating away at impossible speed. This event is now known as the **[[2003-vandenberg-red-square]]**.

### Record and Oversight

Official Air Force records of the Red Square event are said to be in the possession of **AARO** and the **FBI**. The incident was referenced by **Representative Luna** at a congressional UAP hearing. Between 2003 and 2005, Natelli counts **five total UAP incidents** at Vandenberg — suggesting a recurrence pattern consistent with the base's established UAP history stretching back to the 1960s.
