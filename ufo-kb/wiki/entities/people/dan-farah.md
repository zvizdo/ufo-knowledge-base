# Dan Farah

Filmmaker who produced *The Age of Disclosure* (2025), described by [[jesse-michaels]] as "one of the very best documentaries on the whole UAP/UFO phenomenon." The documentary's distinguishing feature is the level of its interview subjects: former Directors of National Intelligence, CIA directors, and sitting senators confirming or strongly implying knowledge of non-human vehicles.

## The Age of Disclosure Documentary

The film features [[james-woolsey]], [[james-clapper]], [[john-brennan]], [[marco-rubio]], and [[chuck-schumer]] — all individuals who held or hold the highest intelligence clearances. Their testimony is described as going well beyond diplomatic hedging: they present the UAP reality as a serious national-security matter with geopolitical urgency.

Farah's core thesis: the United States and China have both retrieved non-human vehicles and are running competing back-engineering programs. The race is analogous to the Manhattan Project but on a larger scale — and unlike nuclear weapons, the first nation to successfully back-engineer anti-gravity or metric-warping propulsion gains a generational military advantage.

## The Amnesty Problem

Farah and [[joe-rogan]] agree on a structural obstacle to disclosure: **amnesty must precede any official revelation**. The gatekeepers (black-program operators, defense contractors, intelligence officials) cannot come forward without legal protection because disclosure would expose:
- Decades of lying to Congress (potential felonies)
- Massive misappropriation of funds (billions, possibly trillions)
- Anti-competitive allocation of back-engineered technology to specific defense contractors (potential antitrust violations and lawsuits from competitors who went under)

Without amnesty, the people holding the information have more to lose from disclosure than from silence.
