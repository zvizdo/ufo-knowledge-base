# Tom DeLonge

Co-founder and lead singer of Blink-182 who became one of the most consequential private-sector actors in the modern UAP disclosure movement. DeLonge has described receiving briefings from senior intelligence officials — verified in part by WikiLeaks-released emails between DeLonge and Hillary Clinton campaign chairman John Podesta, and between DeLonge and USAF General William McCasland — over several years before going public.

## To The Stars Academy

In October 2017, DeLonge co-founded **To The Stars Academy of Arts and Science (TTSA)** with [[lue-elizondo]], [[hal-puthoff]], [[chris-mellon]], and others. Within weeks of launch, the *New York Times* published its AATIP story and released the Nimitz Tic-Tac (→ [[2004-nimitz-tic-tac]]), FLIR/Gimbal (→ [[2015-gimbal]]), and GoFast (→ [[2015-gofast]]) videos — videos that had been government-pre-cleared for release through the TTSA pipeline. The simultaneity of TTSA's launch and the NYT story reflects the managed disclosure character of the event described by [[danny-sheehan]].

## Intelligence Contact Claims

DeLonge's WikiLeaks emails document that:
- Former USAF General William McCasland communicated with him about UAP matters
- John Podesta encouraged him and vouched for his seriousness in internal emails
- Multiple contacts from the aerospace and intelligence world were engaging with him privately

This is notable because it partially corroborates his claim of high-level contacts — the communications exist; what precisely was shared is not documented.

## Future Humans Statement (Y26iMB0r-f8)

[[michael-p-masters]] references a Newsweek article in which DeLonge was quoted:

> "These things aren't coming from other planets. These things might be coming through time."

This is cited by Masters alongside the Hoover and Oberth quotes as a contemporary public-figure endorsement of the [[future-humans-hypothesis]] / time-travel interpretation. DeLonge is also paraphrased (via Masters citing Paul Heineck, one of John Hynek's sons): *"How would they find us? Because we're their ancestors. Why would they care about this planet? Because they're said to inherit it."*

## Credibility Tension

DeLonge's public statements about the phenomenon are often more cosmological and mystical than his intelligence-community colleagues (Elizondo, Mellon) — references to ancient civilizations, consciousness, and a decades-long contact history that goes far beyond what Elizondo and Mellon are willing to confirm publicly. Whether this reflects deeper briefings or DeLonge's own synthesis is unclear.
