# Fritjof Capra

Austrian-American theoretical physicist; author of *The Tao of Physics* (1975), one of the foundational texts connecting quantum mechanics to Eastern philosophical traditions. His work influenced the post-1962 Jesuit philosophical framework that Sheehan was trained in — the integration of quantum physics ontology into Catholic/Jesuit theology.

## Role in the Garrison-Gorbachev Chain

While [[james-garrison]] was at Cambridge University for his doctoral work under J.T. Robinson, he encountered Capra, who was in residence writing a book on nuclear disarmament. The book was funded by **Mrs. Rose Cruz** (wife of an English brigadier general). Cruz connected Garrison to members of the Royal Academy of Science, who wrote letters to their Soviet Academy of Science counterparts — the connection that eventually enabled Garrison to reach Brezhnev, then Gorbachev.

Capra is thus the accidental link in a chain that runs: Cambridge residency → Garrison-Capra meeting → Cruz introduction → Royal Academy → Soviet Academy → Garrison in Moscow → Brezhnev's physician → Gorbachev → Gorbachev Foundation.

## *The Tao of Physics* and UAP Relevance

Capra's core thesis — that quantum mechanics dissolves the classical materialist framework and parallels Eastern non-dual consciousness traditions — became a touchstone for the post-Vietnam generation of scientists, lawyers, and activists working on consciousness and UAP. Sheehan's quantum wave-function collapse framework (consciousness as constitutive of reality; NHI operating through consciousness rather than around it) draws on this tradition.
