# Mark Esper

27th United States Secretary of Defense, serving under President Trump from July 2019 to November 2020. According to [[david-grusch]], a PhD-level source with access to an active reverse-engineering program arranged a briefing for Esper and Deputy SecDef [[david-norquist]] on that program in Fall 2019. Grusch cites this briefing as evidence that program knowledge reached the SecDef level.

## Defense Secretary Tenure and UAP Posture

Esper served as Secretary of Defense from July 2019 through November 2020 under the first Trump administration. His tenure covered the period during which (a) the Navy released the three official UAP videos (FLIR1, Gimbal, GoFast) in April 2020 confirming their authenticity, and (b) the UAP Task Force was stood up in August 2020 under DOD oversight.

Per David Grusch's testimony, Esper and Deputy Secretary [[david-norquist]] received a UAP retrievals briefing in Fall 2019. Esper has not publicly confirmed or denied the briefing. His 2022 memoir *A Sacred Oath* does not discuss UAP topics substantively, though it does mention the UAP Task Force in passing. Esper was fired by Trump in November 2020; he has not made UAP-disclosure-related public statements since.
