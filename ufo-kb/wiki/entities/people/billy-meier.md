# Billy Meier

Swiss UFO contactee best known for producing hundreds of photographs and films of alleged Pleiadian spacecraft from his farm in Hinterschmidrüti, Switzerland, beginning in the 1970s. He lost his left arm in a bus accident in Turkey.

## Intelligence Background (Jorjani's Claim)

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] claims in [[EzPmG_7WhXc]], based on research in *Closer Encounters*, that:
- Meier's real name was **Edward Meyer** (or Eduard)
- He acquired the nickname "Billy" while working in **Tehran** as a field intelligence operative in the Middle East
- He had access to Arab royalty and heads of state, including the Shah of Iran and the King of Jordan
- He was working for "somebody" — and Jorjani connects this to the post-war **Nazi international network** (Die Spinne / ODESSA), building on [[joseph-farrell]]'s research
- People who worked with Meyer in the Middle East years claim he **introduced tall Nordic people to Arab leaders** in meetings about policy direction
- His arm was severed in a bus accident in Turkey, after which he retired to Switzerland and became the famous Billy Meier UFO contactee

## Photographs and Vallée

[[jacques-vallee]], despite general skepticism about contactee photos, assessed **the best of Meier's photographs** as genuinely difficult to fake for the era. Vallee's analysis: they could be fakes, but creating them would have required access to **ILM-level special effects capability** — implausible for a one-armed farmer in rural Switzerland.

## Svahn's Physical Analysis

[[claus-svahn]], who has studied more UFO photographs than almost anyone alive, offers direct analysis of the Meier photos:
- A **weapon/gun** visible in one photo is clearly a **toy-store prop** — not a genuine alien device
- Meier's claimed "dinosaur photograph taken during time-travel to the past" is a **photo lifted directly from a printed book** — Svahn owns the source book and compared them side by side; it is the same image
- Svahn's conclusion: Meier was a deliberate self-promoter aiming to position himself as a "new Jesus" — not a genuine experiencer

This goes beyond prior critique to specific photographic debunking with named evidence. Svahn was unaware of the Jorjani MI6 theory when asked about it, suggesting independent convergence on skepticism.

## Contact Narrative

Meier's Pleiadian contacts (he called them *Plejaren*) — Nordic beings — transmitted the same basic narrative as [[george-van-tassel]], [[george-adamski]], and [[george-hunt-williamson]]: humanity's cosmic origins, the Nordic race as the elder lineage, a hierarchical universal order. Jorjani notes the same racialist framework, same Nordic beings, across all four 1950s–70s contactee figures.

## Post-War Nazi Network Connection

Jorjani builds on [[joseph-farrell]]'s research to suggest that Edward Meyer was a high-level operative of the **international post-war Nazi network** (variously called ODESSA or Die Spinne). The pattern of introducing Nordic-type beings to Arab leaders aligns with the Nazis' post-war project of embedding in the Arab world (building V2/Scud successors for Nasser, etc.).
