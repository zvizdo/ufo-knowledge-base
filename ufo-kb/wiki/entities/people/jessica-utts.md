# Jessica Utts

Statistician and former President of the American Statistical Association; described by [[joe-mcmoneagle]] as one of the top five statisticians in the world. Brought in to analyze [[stargate-program]] data as part of the 1995 American Institutes for Research (AIR) review requested by the CIA.

## Stargate Data Analysis

Utts concluded that the remote viewing effect demonstrated by Stargate participants was statistically real:

- P-values were "off the chart way, way more replicable than psychology" — per McMoneagle
- Effect was robust across meta-analysis of program data
- Her conclusion: the phenomenon is genuine and warrants continued research

Her co-reviewer [[ray-hyman]] — a dedicated skeptic — disputed the methodological adequacy, and the CIA used Hyman's critique to justify shutting down the program in 1995 despite Utts's positive statistical verdict. This institutional choice to prioritize the negative interpretation over the positive statistical finding is cited by [[joe-mcmoneagle]] as evidence of motivated reasoning against the program.

## Significance

Utts's analysis represents the highest-credibility statistical endorsement of remote viewing in the public record. Her institutional standing (ASA president, top statistician) makes her endorsement harder to dismiss on methodological grounds than most psi-positive researchers. The CIA's decision to terminate despite her conclusions raises the question of whether the closure was based on scientific merit or institutional interest.

## Full Quote (RofQnByLwOo)

Jesse Michaels reads Utts's published conclusion verbatim: *"Using the standards applied to any other area of science, it is concluded that psychic functioning has been well established. The statistical results of the studies examined are far beyond what is expected by chance. Arguments that these results could be due to methodological flaws in the experiments are soundly refuted."*

Utts also stated that it would be a **waste of resources to continue looking for proof** of psychic phenomena — resources should instead be directed to the more pressing question of *how* the ability works. This is an explicit call to move from existence-proof research to mechanism research; [[quantum-brain-precognition]] is the kind of framework she was calling for.

## Photo Experiment Protocol

The transcript describes the experimental protocol Utts evaluated: 200 National Geographic photos in a randomized set; remote viewer placed in a completely isolated room; experimenter randomly selects 5 photos as target batch; viewer draws each photo under tightly controlled double-blind conditions; independent third-party judges matches. **Overall hit rate: 35%** — replicated across multiple labs (University of Edinburgh, Rhine Research Institute at Duke). The hit rate was found to be consistently above chance across all trials.
