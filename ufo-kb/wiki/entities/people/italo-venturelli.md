# Italo Venturelli

Head neurosurgeon at [[hospital-regional-varginha]] since 1983; born in São Paulo, trained at the FMUSP school and completed his residency there. He has been practicing in Varginha since 1983 under Dr. Tasidu and Dr. Domingos who had an established neurosurgery service. He managed three hospitals as financial or technical director. He testified in the presence of guest co-host [[michael-shellenberger]] (Portuguese-speaking journalist) at an Austin, Texas recording for [[UkKwa4jU0fc]], coming off a 48-hour shift.

## Background

Before January 1996, Venturelli had no interest in UFOs or alien phenomena, stating explicitly: "I'm a neurosurgeon. We spend most of our time in the operating room." He traveled to Egypt once for a course but had no prior interest in the subject.

Venturelli's credentials and professional reputation make him one of the most significant direct-contact alien witnesses in the public record. His reluctance to come fully forward — attributable to fear of losing his medical license, professional ridicule, patient confidentiality norms, and a pulmonary thrombosis/heart attack during a 2023 interview — are consistent with the risks his profession faces in Brazil.

## The Encounter (January 1996)

### The Video
After performing surgery on a child with Crouzon syndrome, Venturelli returned to the hospital (which was cordoned off with army trucks) through a pedestrian entrance. His colleague [[marcos-vinicius]] — a younger neurosurgeon — called him over: "Come see what I did." He showed Venturelli a brief black-and-white video on a large shoulder-mounted 1990s camera with a small screen. The video: a humanoid figure with a teardrop-shaped enlarged skull, lying on a hospital bed, fully conscious, looking at the camera. Surgical material (an amis basin and needle holder) visible on a side table — indicating a minor procedure had already been completed.

Venturelli initially suspected Crouzon syndrome (which involves cranial abnormalities). He asked: "What is this?" Marcos moved him to the room.

### Seeing the Being Directly
The same being was alive in the hospital bed:

**Physical description**:
- Body: humanoid; arms, chest, forearm, hand, three fingers + opposable thumb visible; one hand under surgical drape, one outside
- Height: approximately equivalent to a 7-year-old child lying down
- Skin: white, very clean — no blood visible, no foul smell
- Skull: teardrop/drop shape, slightly enlarged
- Eyes: teardrop-shaped, lilac-colored (compared to Elizabeth Taylor's eyes)
- Mouth: very small; neck: very thin
- No hair or fur; gender indeterminate (no visible secondary sex characteristics)
- No nipples observed; could not assess lower body (covered)

**Condition**:
- Conscious, awake, not in pain, breathing normally
- Not intubated, no IV drip, no monitors
- Not restrained; free to look around
- Looked out the window periodically — Venturelli interprets this as the being orienting itself spatially
- Bed positioned semi-upright (standard neurological practice)

**The gaze**:
Venturelli returns to the being's gaze across multiple descriptions: "A look of understanding, very calm, non-judgmental — like it had known me for a long time." He describes it as "paternal and compassionate," like an angel — "forgiving, moving forward, don't dwell." He becomes emotional when recounting it. A sense of time dilation during the 3–4 minutes. The being seemed fully aware and "in control of the room."

Venturelli's clinical assessment: "Not bleeding. Not in pain. Breathing normally. No emergency." He gave Marcos his implicit sign-off and left.

### Surgery Details
Marcos Vinicius performed the procedure alone before calling Venturelli. From the video and the material on the table: likely a few sutures. The surgical kit was minimal — only a needle holder and forceps, consistent with wound closure, not complex surgery. No specialized neurosurgical equipment present. Location was improvised — the room is now an ICU. Venturelli speculates Marcos had the being brought there because there were major wounds requiring closure; the being must have been bleeding significantly before treatment.

## After the Encounter

Army trucks were at the hospital entrance. The being was removed at some point after Venturelli left. He did not witness the removal.

Folklore (which Venturelli acknowledges as unverified) says the being was transferred to Humanitas hospital → Campinas → EZA Campinas → possibly Area 51.

A female reporter from TV Globo was stationed outside asking "Did you operate on the ET?" as Venturelli returned through the pedestrian entrance — indicating media had already been informed.

The hospital has a plaque at the front stating "This is the path of the ET. Came here to this hospital on January 20, [1996]." Staff openly confirm it happened.

## The Video (Unrecovered)

[[marcos-vinicius]] showed the video at his home many times — family, fellow doctors, all confirmed it. After Marcos' death in 2018, [[james-fox]] searched his house and retrieved his computer to a warehouse in São Paulo — the video was not found. Venturelli believes it may still be in the house, possibly among CDs in a locked cabinet in Marcos' room. A robbery after Marcos' death may have taken it unknowingly. James Fox offered $1M for the video.

## 2023 Inconsistency

In a 2023 interview with Marco Leal, Venturelli said only that he "saw the video." He explains this by: fear for his career, medical confidentiality norms, being in ICU with pulmonary thrombosis and heart attack at the time. He is explicit in this 2025 episode that he both saw the video and saw the being in person.

## Decision to Come Forward

Venturelli had a heart attack followed by cancer. While lying in the ICU staring at the ceiling, he decided: "If I get through this, I can't take this to the grave." His coming forward was catalyzed by the near-death experience, not by external pressure.

He showed the being's hospital room to Fox on camera — physically walking into what is now an ICU bay and pointing to the bed. He became emotional in a café afterward. Fox notes: "He got kicked out of the hospital — I think he might have just lost his job."

He is a concert pianist, shown playing piano in the documentary's end credits. He would testify under oath and put his hand on a Bible.

The entire regional medical community in the Varginha area has rallied behind his disclosure. Additional doctors at Humanitas Hospital are reportedly considering coming forward when the time is right.
