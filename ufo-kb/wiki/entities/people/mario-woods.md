# Mario Woods

Former US Air Force Security Police officer stationed at [[ellsworth-air-force-base]], South Dakota, 1975–1983. Held Q clearance (the DOE/DOD classification for nuclear materials access) as part of the Personal Reliability Program (PRP). In November 1977 (age 23), he and partner Michael Johnson experienced a CE3 abduction event at [[november-5-silo]] — a Minuteman II ICBM installation — resulting in ~5 hours of missing time, physical burns, unexplained leg marks, and a partner who was never located afterward.

Post-service, Woods continued working with the US Department of Energy as a security inspector at Los Alamos, Sandia, Oak Ridge, y-12 facility, and General Electric Neutron Devices (Largo, Florida). He retained his clearances for ~20 more years after the incident.

## The 1977 Incident

For the complete account, see [[1977-ellsworth-woods-abduction]]. Summary:

On a ~9°F night in November 1977, Woods and vacation-relief partner Michael Johnson were dispatched to a dual-prong alarm at [[november-5-silo]]. Upon arriving, they found a massive sphere UAP ("the size of a Walmart building") hovering 10 ft above the blast door of a live 1.2-megaton warhead. A blinding light was directed at the truck; Woods lost his ability to breathe; a black ball-shaped probe circled the vehicle. He received a telepathic message — "Do not fear" — as a vibration through his body. He then observed three [[small-grays]] and one [[tall-gray]] gliding alongside the truck before losing consciousness.

~5 hours later he regained awareness. Michael Johnson was still catatonic, rigid at the steering wheel. The truck was at [[new-lake-reservoir-dam]], 8.5 miles away, in mud, in sub-zero temperatures, on a 5-ft road that offered only one viable exit — which the vehicle was already positioned to take.

## The OSI Debrief

Debriefed at length by Master Sgt. Joel Gray, Col. Spraker (commander), Capt. Jack Reed (OSI lead), [[rick-doty]] (then brand-new OSI Counter Intelligence officer), and an unidentified man in a gray tweed suit. Woods **did not disclose seeing the beings** — to preserve his PRP clearance, Q clearance, and job. He signed a non-disclosure agreement. Rick Doty's stated role: retrieve medical records for both Woods and Johnson.

A flight surgeon examined Woods, documented burns above his right eye and right hand, took skin samples, and — notably — inspected his teeth and gums specifically. Jesse Michaels connects this to [[andrija-puharich]]'s bone-induction research: the question of whether investigators were looking for (or planting) a dental implant with RF-reception capability.

## Michael Johnson

Johnson — Woods's partner that night — was light-skinned, Black, from Chicago, Illinois. He visited Woods ~18 days post-incident; both drew independent depictions of the craft that matched exactly. After that visit, Johnson was never found again. Woods, AARO, law enforcement contacts, and others have been unable to locate him.

## Hypnotic Regressions

Two sessions: first with Bob Upton (MUFON-affiliated, Jacksonville, FL) — produced cardiac arrhythmia (AFib) requiring cardioversion; second with Yavon Smith (California). Regression revealed:
- Inside craft: large sphere, beehive-like multi-level interior filled with small Grays going about tasks
- Force-field gurney (pyramidal field holding his body ~4 ft off the floor)
- Tall Gray performed wrist procedure; telepathically communicated "we don't hurt you"
- A "museum room" containing: Singer sewing machine table, 1950s floor lamps, antique tube radio in wood cabinet, steam iron
- A round window with a pie-slice removed from it — Mario was invited twice to look out but was too afraid

## 1961 Childhood Sighting

At age ~5-6, witnessed three white/silver synchronized flying saucers over De Queen Elementary, Port Arthur, Texas, alongside mother and sister and hundreds of schoolchildren/teachers. Full account: [[1961-port-arthur-saucer-sighting]]. His mother had seen craft before; his father saw USOs at sea. Possible family lineage.

## Physical Evidence / Marks

- Burns above right eyebrow and back of right hand (documented by flight surgeon; skin samples taken)
- Two identical marks on lower legs — same depth, same size, dimpled center; noticed post-service; doctor (deceased, COVID) said no known life-saving procedure could account for them — see [[nhi-implants]]
- No memory of healing from them

## Post-Incident Life

Strong interest in pyramid technology — experienced as an installed compulsion after the encounter. Endorses [[christopher-dunn]]'s pyramid-as-power-generator theory and [[graham-hancock]]'s pre-Ice Age civilization thesis. Has post-1977 experiences including a December 2017 incident (4 AM, ~4.5 ft glowing hole in bedroom wall, all house electrical systems disrupted, Georgia Power found only his house was affected and the board downtown "lit up like the Fourth of July").

Reported to [[aaro]] in 2023: 4h 19m conversation; no recording provided; no follow-up. Willing to testify before Congress.
