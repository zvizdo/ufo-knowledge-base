# Matt Bell (Limitless Podcast)

Podcast host and independent researcher financing robotic investigation of the [[great-pyramid|Great Pyramid]]'s Queen's Chamber northern shaft — the unexplored counterpart to the southern shaft where Rudolf Gantenbrink's Upuaut II robot found a limestone "door" with metal handles in 1993.

## Queen's Chamber Shaft Investigation

The Queen's Chamber has two small shafts (roughly 20×20 cm cross-section) extending toward the north and south. The southern shaft was robotically explored in 1993 (Gantenbrink's Upuaut II, finding a door at ~65 m depth; a follow-up 2002 Pyramid Rover mission drilled through and found a second door beyond). The northern shaft has never been fully robotically explored.

Matt Bell is funding equipment and logistics for a robotic expedition into the northern shaft. If the shaft system detected by [[filippo-biondi|Biondi]]'s SAR below the Queen's Chamber floor is real, the northern shaft may provide the first access route into that deeper infrastructure.
