# Tim Gallaudet

Rear Admiral and former head of **NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration)**. After leaving NOAA, Gallaudet began publicly pursuing underwater UAP anomalies — unidentified submerged objects (USOs) — as a research topic.

## Significance

Gallaudet represents a rare public official bridging the naval underwater operational world and UAP research. His background at NOAA gives him access to oceanographic data; his naval career means he understands what unusual subsurface contacts look like and how they're typically explained away. [[jesse-michaels]] frames the underwater domain as severely underexplored relative to aerial UAP, noting that underwater contacts are **less likely to be confused with other traffic** (no dense commercial undersea traffic analogous to air traffic) — making a genuine anomaly proportionally more conspicuous.

## USOs and NEURO

Michaels mentions **NEURO** — described as the "little brother to the NRO" for the underwater domain — as a classified body that has likely detected anomalous subsurface objects. Gallaudet's public interest in USOs suggests some bridge between that classified world and open research.

[[ryan-graves]] notes that submarine service personnel he has spoken with are "very cagey, very secretive" about unusual contacts — consistent with a suppression dynamic similar to what he experienced in the surface-aviation community.
