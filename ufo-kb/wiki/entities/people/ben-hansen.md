# Ben Hansen

Television investigator and host of the show *UFO Witness* (Discovery+). Hansen investigated the [[point-mugu]] undersea mesa feature off the Southern California coast as a possible alien base or USO installation. [[tim-gallaudet]] notes that USGS bathymetric data identifies the Point Mugu feature as a natural geological formation, but that the broader SoCal undersea region has multiple anomalous features warranting investigation.

*UFO Witness* investigated [[sycamore-knoll]] as well — Gallaudet specifically cites the Sycamore Knoll episode as the one that drew his attention to the feature before it was removed from Google Earth. [[preston-dennett]] has also written a book covering the broader SoCal USO corridor that overlaps with Hansen's TV investigation territory.

Hansen's work represents the media-investigation tier of the UAP credibility ecosystem — not whistleblower testimony or classified evidence, but systematic physical investigation of reported USO areas with camera crews and equipment.
