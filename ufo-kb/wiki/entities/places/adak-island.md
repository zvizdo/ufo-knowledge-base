# Adak Island

Adak Island is the westernmost inhabited island in the Aleutian Islands chain, located approximately 1,200 miles west of the Alaska mainland and roughly 500 miles from the Hawaiian Island chain (per Dolan's approximation in the source). During World War II, Adak was a significant US military base used in the Aleutian Campaign against Japan (1942–1943).

## UFO/USO Significance

Adak Island is the nearest landmark to the [[1945-uss-delarof]] USO incident, which occurred in summer 1945 as the USS Delarof was returning from Japan to Seattle. The incident is the founding case in [[richard-dolan]]'s USO database.

## Geographic Context

The Aleutian Islands are an arc of volcanic islands extending from the Alaska Peninsula toward Russia's Kamchatka Peninsula. Their remote location and oceanic surroundings — deep Pacific water on all sides — make them consistent with USO activity near deep-ocean zones.
