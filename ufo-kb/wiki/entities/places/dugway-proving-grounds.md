# Dugway Proving Grounds

U.S. Army installation covering approximately 800,000 acres in Tooele County, Utah — one of the largest military installations in the United States. Established during World War II for chemical and biological weapons testing. Located approximately 85 miles southwest of Salt Lake City in the Great Basin Desert.

In the KB (documented in the [[ntifs8luduo]] transcript), [[uap-gerb]] and [[steven-greer]] assert that 35–40% of Area 51 programs relocated to Dugway Proving Grounds in recent decades, making it the current primary U.S. UAP research hub. The specific entities cited: Battelle Institute (operator of a secret UAP compartment per the [[project-stork]] reference), Weinbrenner, and Boeing all have confirmed relationships with Dugway.

The [[1992-cape-canaveral-saucer]] case is also connected to Dugway in the KB, suggesting that recovered or test craft from Eastern launch sites were routed to Dugway for analysis or storage.

Dugway's combination of extreme remoteness, enormous land area, chemical/biological research cover, and existing cleared contractor infrastructure makes it an ideal successor to Area 51 for programs that have been compromised by public attention.
