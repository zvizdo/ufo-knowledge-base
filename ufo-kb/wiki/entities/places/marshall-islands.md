# Marshall Islands

## Description

The Marshall Islands served as the US Pacific Proving Grounds for nuclear weapons testing from 1946 (Operation Crossroads) through 1962 (Operation Dominic). Bikini Atoll and Enewetak Atoll were the primary test sites, with dozens of nuclear detonations conducted across multiple test series.

## UAP Significance

The Marshall Islands test site is central to [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] evidence. According to [[harold-malmgren]] and the account relayed through [[richard-bissell]], UAP "tag-alongs" were a normalized, if unstated, presence at the test series by 1962 — military vocabulary that suggests the phenomenon had been observed often enough to warrant a casual working term.

The [[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown]] — the alleged deliberate downing of a UAP by nuclear X-ray pulse during Bluegill Triple Prime — occurred here. The [[bluegill-uap-debris]] was subsequently recovered by the US Navy operating in the same Pacific theater.

[[jeffrey-kruchshank]] and [[alfred-starbird]] (commander of the test program) are connected to the documentary and photographic record from these tests, including the [[kettle-footage]] that remains "unable to be located" per [[byron-ristvet]].
