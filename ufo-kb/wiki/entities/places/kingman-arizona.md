# Kingman, Arizona

Town in northwestern Arizona; in UAP literature, the locality referenced in the **alleged 1953 Kingman UFO crash** account, in which engineer [[arthur-stansel]] (writing under various names in different accounts) reported being flown to Phoenix and bussed under blacked-out windows to a classified site near Kingman to assess what was described as a "secret test vehicle" — and what he subsequently described as a recovered non-human craft and one small entity.

The Kingman case is one of the more durably-cited firsthand-witness crash claims in the field, alongside Roswell, Aztec, and Cape Girardeau.
