# Wewelsburg Castle

> *Stub page — expand with primary source import.*

Wewelsburg is a Renaissance triangular castle in Westphalia, Germany, acquired by Heinrich Himmler in 1934 and redesignated as the ideological and ceremonial headquarters of the SS. Himmler undertook extensive renovations to transform it into an "SS-Camelot" — a center for SS mythology, occult ritual, and racial ideology.

## Notable Features

- **North Tower crypt**: the "Grail room" with a central fire pit; site of SS order ceremonies
- **Black Sun mosaic**: the Schwarze Sonne floor symbol embedded in the North Tower — one of the most cited esoteric Nazi symbols, later adopted by neo-Nazi movements and appearing in UFO-adjacent occult literature
- **SS-Ahnenerbe research node**: Himmler funded paranormal, archaeological, and pseudo-scientific research from this location
