# Mount Perdido

## Description

Mount Perdido (Spanish: *Monte Perdido*; literally "Lost Mountain") is a peak in the Pyrenees in Aragon, Spain. In the [[OOr98r51Sj0]] decoding of [[stargate-program|Stargate]] [[remote-viewing]] sessions, Mount Perdido is one of four mountain sites named in 1973 by [[pat-price]] as a candidate non-terrestrial base.

## Associated Incidents

- **1973 Stargate remote viewing.** [[pat-price]] named Mount Perdido alongside Mount Hayes, Mount Inyangani, and Mount Ziel.
- **Joe McMoneagle blind replication — with a twist.** [[joe-mcmoneagle]], given the coordinates blind, confirmed an underground command center at Mount Perdido, but additionally reported seeing it **being built by humans** — with helicopters, cargo crates, and human personnel moving materials into the mountain. Per [[OOr98r51Sj0]], this collapses the "alien base" framing into a "[[breakaway-civilization]]-built underground base" framing for at least this node.
