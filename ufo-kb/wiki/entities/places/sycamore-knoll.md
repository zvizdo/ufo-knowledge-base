# Sycamore Knoll

An undersea topographic feature off the Southern California coast. Sycamore Knoll was investigated by [[ben-hansen]] on the TV show *UFO Witness* as a possible alien base or USO-related installation. The US Geological Survey (USGS) bathymetric database identifies the feature as natural geology; [[tim-gallaudet]] confirms this from the USGS data.

However, Gallaudet notes a striking anomaly: after he began publicly discussing UAP-seabed interaction hypotheses, Sycamore Knoll was **removed from Google Earth**:

> "Weirdly, when I started talking out about this in Google Earth, the feature was filtered out entirely. This Sycamore Knoll, now you can't even see it. It's plain as day in the USGS database. Why would someone go to Google Earth and have that feature removed? Who did that? Why?"

The Google Earth removal is unexplained. Whether it reflects routine data management or deliberate suppression is unknown.
