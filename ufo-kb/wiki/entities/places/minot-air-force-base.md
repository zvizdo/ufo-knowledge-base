# Minot Air Force Base

USAF base in North Dakota, assigned to [[strategic-air-command]]. Houses Minuteman ICBM launch facilities dispersed across the surrounding countryside. Along with [[malmstrom-air-force-base]], Minot is one of the primary sites in the documented pattern of UFO/UAP encounters with nuclear missile systems.

## Associated Incidents

### September 1966 — Minot UFO/Missiles
One of three incidents [[robert-salas]] cites in a 6-month window (Sept 1966 – March 1967) in which UFO activity correlated with missile shutdowns or anomalies. [[1966-minot-ufo-missiles]] — 8 Minuteman missiles reported affected during a UFO sighting by airborne crew.

### 1968 — Minot B-52 Encounter
A B-52 crew and ground radar tracked a large UFO near Minot in October 1968. Both airborne radar and ground radar confirmed an unidentified object that followed the aircraft. Multiple witnesses across crew positions.
