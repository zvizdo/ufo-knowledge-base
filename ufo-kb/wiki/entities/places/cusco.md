# Cusco

## Description

The former capital of the Inca Empire, located at ~3,400 m elevation in the Andes. [[luke-caverns]] calls it possibly the best city he has ever visited: clean, safe, beautiful, with extraordinary surviving Inca stonework integrated into colonial architecture.

## Key Archaeological Sites

- **Qorikancha** (Coricancha): The Inca sun temple in the city center. Built from gray andesite with trapezoidal, inward-leaning blocks of razor-sharp precision. Luke considers this the most impressive stonework anywhere in the ancient world — surpassing Egypt.
- **[[sacsayhuaman]]**: Megalithic fortress above the city; accessed underground tunnels in September 2024.
- **Sacred Valley**: Series of Inca and pre-Inca sites descending from Cusco toward Machu Picchu.
- **Ollantaytambo**: Another megalithic site in the Sacred Valley.
