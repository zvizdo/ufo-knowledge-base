# Ariel School

Primary school in Ruwa, Zimbabwe, approximately 20 km east of Harare, where the [[1994-ruwa-zimbabwe]] incident occurred on September 16, 1994. During a school break, 62 children between the ages of 8 and 12 reported seeing one or more craft land in a field adjacent to the school, followed by non-human beings who emerged and communicated with the children through what witnesses described as "images in their minds" — apparent non-verbal or telepathic communication.

The case is notable for:
- Scale of witness pool: 62 independent witnesses with consistent accounts
- Age profile: children as witnesses are considered less susceptible to deliberate confabulation
- Harvard psychiatrist [[john-mack]] and researcher Cynthia Hind conducted extensive follow-up interviews; accounts remained consistent over years
- BBC journalist Tim Leach filmed interviews with children in the days after the incident

The children described a being with large eyes that communicated what some interpreted as environmental concern. Several children reported lasting psychological effects. Mack considered this one of the most compelling close encounter cases he investigated, meeting the standard he applied to adult experiencers.
