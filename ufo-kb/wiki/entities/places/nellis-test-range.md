# Nellis Test Range

The Nevada Test and Training Range (NTTR), controlled by Nellis Air Force Base and covering approximately 12,000 square miles of restricted airspace and 3 million acres of land in southern Nevada. The largest airspace and ground-test complex in the U.S. Department of Defense, encompassing Area 51 (Groom Lake) and the Nevada Test Site (Department of Energy nuclear testing ground).

In the KB, Nellis Test Range appears through [[jason-sands]]'s whistleblower testimony. Sands describes serving on perimeter security duty for a classified UAP program at Nellis, during which he saw a blue alien being on the premises. He also reported being told that "NHI want their stuff back and will kill everyone in the program" — a claim consistent with the KB's broader theme of adversarial NHI-government-program dynamics.

The range's relationship to [[area-51]] and [[dugway-proving-grounds]] positions it as one of three nodes in the alleged core U.S. UAP research infrastructure: Nellis/Area 51 for testing and some storage, Dugway for the major current programs, and the FFRDC-network for analytical and development work.
