# Pope Air Force Base

## Description

Pope Air Force Base (now Pope Field, part of Fort Bragg / Fort Liberty in North Carolina) is a US military installation historically associated with airlift support of special-operations forces. In [[sentinels-of-ether]] (as decoded in [[OOr98r51Sj0]]), Pope Air Force Base is the location of [[jsoc]]'s Joint Operations Center during the novel's fictional 2004 Coyame ambush — the site where General Dale Steinmaker takes the Secretary of Defense's "black on black" call.

## Associated Incidents

- **Fictional 2004 Coyame ambush command-and-control hub** in [[sentinels-of-ether]]: JSOC OPS center watches the Predator drone feed of an unsanctioned execution and helicopter exfiltration through the Coyame canyon system; lead JSOC commander coordinates with the Secretary of Defense and dispatches F-18s.
