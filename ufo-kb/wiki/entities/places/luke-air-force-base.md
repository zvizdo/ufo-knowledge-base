# Luke Air Force Base

US Air Force installation in Glendale, Arizona, in the western Phoenix metropolitan area. Home to the 56th Fighter Wing, operating F-16 and F-35 aircraft. Luke AFB is approximately 20 miles west of central Phoenix.

## Phoenix Lights (1997)

[[james-fox]] reports in [[3yrKVu35uSE]] that at least one pilot from Luke AFB who observed the [[1997-phoenix-lights]] from the air had such a severe physiological or psychological reaction to the encounter that he **had to be carried from his cockpit** after landing. This account suggests the encounter produced effects beyond visual shock — consistent with physiological effects documented in other high-credibility close encounter cases.

The official Air Force position was to deny any unusual aircraft were in the area that night.
