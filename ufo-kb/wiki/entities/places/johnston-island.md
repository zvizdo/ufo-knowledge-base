# Johnston Island

Johnston Island (Johnston Atoll) is a remote US military atoll located approximately 750 nautical miles southwest of Hawaii in the Central Pacific. It was the primary staging area for US high-altitude nuclear tests during Operation Dominic in 1962 — a period when the United States was conducting some of its largest atmospheric nuclear detonations.

## Operation Dominic and UAP Activity

During the Operation Dominic tests (1962), multiple UAP sightings were reported near Johnston Island. [[richard-dolan]] describes an account from a witness aboard the USS Finch (October 25, 1962 — around the Cuban Missile Crisis):

- UAPs would "hang out" monitoring the tests and **disappear just before** each detonation
- On the date in question, after the crew was ordered below deck per protocol, selected sailors were brought up to witness a **long cigar-shaped object** zoom past horizontally
- Radar operators later confirmed the object and discussed recovery ships being dispatched

This is distinct from — but temporally adjacent to — the [[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown]] account involving a different vessel.

## Bob Jacobs and the 1964 Vandenberg Scuttlebutt

[[bob-jacobs]] (Vandenberg, 1964) reported that the "water-cooler conversation" at Johnston Island among engineers and technicians was that a Soviet nuclear weapon had been shot down from orbit using the Bluegill test — a theory that circulated among the technical personnel who were there just two years earlier.
