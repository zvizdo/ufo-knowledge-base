# Zanesville, Ohio

A small Ohio city best known in this knowledge base for two interlocking facts: it is the **birthplace of [[thomas-townsend-brown]]** (1905), and it became a **pilgrimage site** for the Taylor/Bledsoe network — the locus of a glowing red metal recovery near a local **Indian Serpent Mound**.

## Brown's Birthplace

[[thomas-townsend-brown]] was born in Zanesville in 1905. Local newspaper clippings document him building a **wireless telegraph at age 12** — an early indication of the trajectory that would lead to the [[biefeld-brown-effect]], the [[townsend-brown-gravitator]], and the alleged [[odlum-northrop-b2-pipeline]].

## The Taylor–Bledsoe Pilgrimage

[[tim-taylor]] brought [[chris-bledsoe]] to Zanesville specifically to retrieve a piece of **glowing red metal** found near the local Indian Serpent Mound. Taylor attributed the activity-concentration in the area to the **land itself** — implying a place-bound anomaly rather than an event-bound one.

Taylor was simultaneously helping [[linda-brown]] write the biography of her father — the partial manuscript was on Taylor's phone at the time of the Zanesville visit. The pilgrimage therefore had a dual purpose: (1) retrieve the anomalous metal, (2) physically connect Bledsoe to Brown's birthplace as part of the biographical research.

Pieces of the recovered Zanesville metal were subsequently distributed via Bledsoe's facilitation to:

- [[hal-puthoff]]
- [[tom-delonge]]
- [[lue-elizondo]]

The metal joins the [[1947-roswell|Roswell]] sample that Taylor previously placed in Bledsoe's hand as part of the documented physical-material chain Taylor has access to.
