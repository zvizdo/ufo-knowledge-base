# Bethesda Naval Hospital

Bethesda Naval Hospital (National Naval Medical Center) is a US military medical facility in Bethesda, Maryland. In the UFO research context, it is significant for two historical events separated by 14 years.

## 1949 — James Forrestal's Death

[[james-forrestal]], the first US Secretary of Defense, was hospitalized here after his forced resignation in early 1949. Against the advice of his attending physicians, national security personnel ordered him placed on the **16th floor** — not the ground floor, which would have been standard for a potentially suicidal patient. He died on May 22, 1949, officially by falling from a 16th-floor window. See [[1949-forrestal-death]] for the full reconstruction.

The hospital setting was significant: as a military medical facility, access to Forrestal could be controlled by intelligence-adjacent personnel in ways not possible in a civilian hospital. The duty corpsman who had befriended Forrestal went AWOL on his final night; a replacement was present when Forrestal "jumped."

## 1963 — JFK Autopsy

Bethesda Naval Hospital was also the site of President Kennedy's official autopsy after his November 22, 1963 assassination. [[richard-dolan]] and others note this convergence: the same military facility that may have been the site of Forrestal's murder 14 years earlier became the site of the official autopsy of the president who, per multiple sources, had been briefed on UFOs by Forrestal and had independently sought to share UAP data with the Soviets.

Whether coincidental or structural, the pattern — intelligence-controlled military medical facility as the site of death or autopsy of officials who knew too much — is noted by multiple researchers.
