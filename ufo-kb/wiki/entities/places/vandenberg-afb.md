# Vandenberg Air Force Base

Vandenberg AFB (renamed Vandenberg Space Force Base in 2021) sits along the California coastline in Santa Barbara County and has served since the late 1950s as the primary US launch site for polar-orbit satellites and ballistic missile test flights. Its remote coastal geography and long downrange ocean track made it ideal for testing ICBMs without overflying populated areas.

The base entered UAP history through the 1964 incident documented by Lt. [[bob-jacobs]], then a USAF film officer assigned to record a dummy warhead test flight. His team captured on a telescopic camera what appeared to be a disc-shaped object circling the re-entry vehicle and firing beams at it before the warhead tumbled out of trajectory. The footage — known as the Jacobs film — was seized by senior officers and Jacobs was ordered to sign a security oath and never speak of it. The incident is one of the most cited cases in the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] literature.

Also in September 1964, USAF air policeman [[richard-barth]], guarding a Minuteman missile site in D-sector, claims a CE3 encounter: paralysis by an approaching humanoid NHI entity, abduction aboard a hovering saucer, and direct NHI communication about their nuclear surveillance program and intent to prevent nuclear war. This places two independent witnesses reporting extraordinary UAP activity at Vandenberg in the same month.

In 2003–2005, five more UAP incidents occurred at Vandenberg, including the [[2003-vandenberg-red-square]] — a massive glowing red square reported by Boeing contractors over missile defense sites, followed by a triangular craft larger than a football field witnessed by five USAF security personnel and documented by [[jeff-natelli]]. Official Air Force records of the Red Square event are held by AARO and the FBI; the incident was referenced by Representative Luna in congressional testimony.

Vandenberg is described in the UAP literature as one of the most persistently active nuclear-base UAP hotspots alongside [[malmstrom-air-force-base]].
