# Woomera Test Range

Australian military test range in South Australia. While [[maralinga]] to the south was used for nuclear bomb tests, Woomera was used for ICBM and missile testing. Both ranges share a legacy of British and American military activity in Australia's remote outback.

## Current Activity (Alleged)

[[ross-coulthart]] states that Woomera is where Americans secretly test hypersonic missile technology. This is described as an ongoing arrangement under the US-Australia alliance — exploiting Australia's remote geography and low population density.

## UAP Reports

Aboriginal communities near Woomera have reported **silent black triangles** — a craft type associated elsewhere with advanced US aerospace programs and with some NHI interpretations. The remoteness of these communities means their accounts rarely enter mainstream UAP discourse.

## Historical Significance

During the 1950s–60s, Australian scientists were invited to observe Pacific nuclear tests as a diplomatic concession for allowing British bomb tests on Australian soil. These Pacific observation trips appear to have been a key vector for knowledge-sharing between US nuclear physicists (with UAP awareness) and Australian scientists like [[harry-turner]] and others in the [[australian-joint-intelligence-organisation]].
