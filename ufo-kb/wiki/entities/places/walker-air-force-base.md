# Walker Air Force Base

Walker Air Force Base was the renamed successor to [[roswell-army-air-field]] at Roswell, New Mexico. The installation was renamed Walker AFB in 1948 in honor of Brigadier General Kenneth Newton Walker, a WWII bomber commander killed in action over New Guinea in 1943.

**Strategic Air Command era**: Following its redesignation, Walker AFB became a Strategic Air Command (SAC) bomber base — housing nuclear-capable B-29, B-47, and B-52 bombers throughout the Cold War. The [[509th-bomb-group]]'s nuclear weapons legacy was thus institutionally continued at Walker through the SAC era.

**1947 historical significance**: The base's historical significance derives from its status as [[roswell-army-air-field]] — the home of the 509th Bomb Group in July 1947 at the time of the [[1947-roswell]] incident. The physical base is the same; only the name changed.

**Closure**: Walker AFB was deactivated in 1967 as part of Secretary of Defense McNamara's base closures. The former airfield is now operated as the Roswell International Air Center, a civilian airport that serves the Roswell, New Mexico area and includes the Roswell UFO Museum and Research Center.

**Roswell tourism**: The city of Roswell has built an economy around the 1947 incident through museums, annual UFO festivals, and tourism. Walker AFB's grounds and former infrastructure underlie the civilian airport that serves this tourism economy.
