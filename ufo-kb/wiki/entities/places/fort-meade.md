# Fort Meade

U.S. Army installation covering approximately 5,000 acres in Anne Arundel County, Maryland, between Baltimore and Washington DC. Best known as the headquarters of the National Security Agency (NSA) — the largest intelligence agency in the United States by personnel and budget — and of U.S. Cyber Command.

In the KB, Fort Meade appears through [[dan-sherman]]'s account of Project Preserve Destiny: Sherman traveled to the NSA complex at Fort Meade for the EA280 Intermediate Electronics Intelligence continuation course, during which a captain called him to the main NSA building for his read-in to the classified intuitive communicator program. The briefing included disclosure that Sherman's genetics had been pre-conditioned by aliens before his birth, and that his role would be to receive alien transmissions telepathically and relay them to his NSA handlers.

Fort Meade is also the location where [[stargate-program]] adjacent remote viewing work would have been coordinated through NSA channels, and where signals intelligence on UAP contacts would be processed under [[echelon]] and Five Eyes protocols.
