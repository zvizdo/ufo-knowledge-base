# South Haven

South Haven is a hamlet in Brookhaven, Long Island, New York — directly adjacent to the campus of [[brookhaven-national-lab]], the US Department of Energy's major particle accelerator facility on Long Island.

## UAP Crash Claim

Per [[51N8OxqZIWY]], [[jay-anderson]] claims there is "a famous crash in the US at South Haven right next to Brookhaven National Lab." Witnesses are described as including the local police chief and community residents. No date is provided for the alleged incident in this episode.

The episode uses the South Haven crash as the American parallel to alleged portal/beings phenomena at [[cern]] in Europe — both involving major particle accelerators and UAP events in their immediate vicinity. This forms the core of the [[particle-accelerator-ufo-nexus]] concept.
