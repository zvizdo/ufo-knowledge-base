# Skinwalker Ranch

A 512-acre property in the Uintah Basin of northeastern Utah, known in UFO/UAP and paranormal research circles since at least the 1990s. The Sherman family reported dramatic anomalous phenomena in the mid-1990s — cattle mutilations, UAP sightings, poltergeist-like events, and encounters with large predatory animals impervious to injury — which brought the ranch to the attention of Robert Bigelow, who purchased it in 1996 for study.

## AAWSAP Investigations

The ranch became the primary field investigation site for [[aawsap]], the classified Pentagon program. Robert Bigelow's **Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies (BAASS)** entity managed the on-site research under contract. [[lue-elizondo]] was associated with the program during this period. The investigations produced extensive documentation of anomalous events — the details of which have not been fully made public.

The ranch's significance in the AAWSAP context is that it became a kind of natural laboratory for the full spectrum of UAP-associated phenomena: aerial anomalies, physiological effects on witnesses, animal effects, and apparent poltergeist activity occurring in the same physical space. This led AAWSAP to adopt a broader "hitchhiker effect" model — the hypothesis that UAP phenomena can follow investigators home.

## Brandon Fugel Era (post-Bigelow)

The ranch was sold to [[brandon-fugel]], a real estate mogul and frontier science patron, who assembled a team of scientists committed to open-sourcing their data — a deliberate contrast with the classified Bigelow/AAWSAP approach. Key findings under Fugel documented in the American Alchemy episode ([[7W7iOt57fOo]]):

- **Buried metallic object**: Ground-penetrating radar and soil resistivity located a large metallic structure under the Mesa. Drilling by [[aaron-blunt]] confirmed an impenetrable hard object ~400 feet wide. Samples analyzed by metallurgist [[ravi-chandran]] showed fused metal containing **europium and tellurium** — manufactured, not natural. See [[skinwalker-mesa-metallic-object]].
- **The Triangle**: A patch of sky above the ranch where instrumentation consistently malfunctions. A 15-foot rocket launched into the Triangle deflected abruptly westward with no wind; cameras captured a UAP entering and exiting the sky simultaneously; gamma radiation spiked.
- **Biological effects continue**: The [[2016-skinwalker-illness-incident]] saw a former Hells Angel paralyzed for ~10 minutes and hospitalized for 3-4 weeks with no diagnostic explanation. Effects disproportionately affect aggressive/"alpha male" personality types — consistent with [[basal-ganglia-uap-predisposition]].
- **FOIA exemption confirmed**: Fugel confirmed all AATIP data (2007–2012) is both classified and FOIA-exempt because the program ran under [[bigelow-aerospace]] as a private company. See [[uap-private-sector-foia-exemption]].
- **Trickster interaction**: [[eric-barr]]'s surveillance challenge ("show me") was immediately answered with a screen artifact spelling "LIVING." See [[trickster-intelligence]].
- **As Above So Below**: An old engraving bearing this Hermetic phrase was found on the property. See [[as-above-so-below-engraving]].

The ranch is documented in the History Channel / Netflix series *The Secrets of Skinwalker Ranch*.

## AATIP Data Gap

All data from the 2007–2012 AATIP/AAWSAP investigations at the ranch is held by Bigelow Aerospace as proprietary — FOIA-exempt and classified. The primary public window into that period is [[skinwalkers-at-the-pentagon]] (Kelleher, Knapp, Lacatski, 2021) and [[hunt-for-the-skinwalker]] (Knapp, Kelleher, 2005).
