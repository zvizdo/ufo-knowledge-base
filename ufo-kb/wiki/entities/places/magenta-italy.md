## Description

Magenta is a town of approximately 23,000 people in the Metropolitan City of Milan, Lombardy, in northern Italy. It is situated on the Ticino River, west of Milan. The town is historically known for the 1859 Battle of Magenta during the Second Italian War of Independence.

In UFO/UAP history, Magenta is the site of what is arguably the earliest well-documented government-confirmed UAP recovery event: the June 1933 crash of a disc-shaped non-human craft in the vicinity of the town, during the height of Benito Mussolini's fascist regime.

The Magenta crash is significant not merely as a historical event but as a test case for the corroboration structure of UAP testimony. The crash was:

1. Independently named to Harald Malmgren in private by Richard Bissell (CIA Deputy Director for Plans), well before David Grusch's 2023 public congressional testimony about the same event.
2. Investigated by a credentialed scientific panel (the [[gabinetto-rs33|RS/33]]) that included Nobel laureate Guglielmo Marconi, whose conclusion was that the craft was non-human.
3. Subject to a Vatican intelligence operation (Pius XII's back-channel to the Allies) that was activated specifically to prevent the craft from falling into Nazi hands — a decision requiring the Vatican to have credible intelligence about the craft's nature and location.
4. The object of a dedicated Allied wartime intelligence operation ([[oss|OSS]] Project McGregor) to retrieve it.

The convergence of these independent chains of evidence — Italian state investigation, Vatican intelligence, OSS operational retrieval, and CIA-level private disclosure decades later — constitutes an unusually robust corroboration structure for a pre-Roswell UAP recovery event.

## Associated Incidents

**The June 1933 Crash**
A disc-shaped craft approximately 10–12 meters in diameter came down near Magenta in June 1933. Italian military and government personnel recovered the object. Mussolini was personally informed and immediately ordered:

- A total media blackout through the Stefani state news agency.
- The official position that the craft was a foreign experimental aircraft of terrestrial origin.
- The convening of the [[gabinetto-rs33|Gabinetto RS/33]] to investigate the craft in secret.

The craft was transferred to hangars operated by SIA-Marchetti, a leading Italian aerospace manufacturer in the Varese/Milan area, where the RS/33 investigation was conducted.

**The RS/33 Investigation (1933–1945)**
Guglielmo Marconi (radio pioneer, Nobel laureate) participated in the investigation and concluded the craft was non-human. Italian Air Marshal Italo Balbo was also involved. The RS/33's internal conclusion contradicted Mussolini's official position but did not change the public stance. The craft remained at SIA-Marchetti hangars for over a decade.

**Pope Pius XII's Vatican Back-Channel**
During the war, Pope Pius XII used Vatican diplomatic channels to alert Allied intelligence about the Magenta craft, specifically motivated by concern that it might fall into Nazi hands as German forces occupied northern Italy. This back-channel was the triggering intelligence for OSS Project McGregor. The Vatican's decision to share this intelligence indicates that Catholic Church leadership had been tracking the craft's existence and location throughout the decade of Italian custody.

**OSS Project McGregor (1945)**
The Allied retrieval operation, directed by Wild Bill Donovan's [[oss|OSS]], succeeded in securing the craft from Italian custody in 1945 in the final months of the war in Europe. The craft was subsequently transported to [[wright-patterson|Wright-Patterson Air Force Base]] in the United States, where it entered the US UAP secrecy infrastructure.

**Bissell's Private Disclosure to Malmgren**
Richard Bissell, CIA Deputy Director for Plans and founder of [[area-51|Area 51]], privately named the Magenta crash to Harald Malmgren. This disclosure pre-dated David Grusch's 2023 public testimony about the same event by many years, providing an independent corroboration from a CIA official at the highest operational level.
