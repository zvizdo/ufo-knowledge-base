# Manzano Base

> *Stub. Expand with full operational history, declassified weapons-storage record, and any additional UAP-related primary documentation as it enters the KB.*

US nuclear weapons storage facility tunneled into Manzano Mountain on the southeast edge of Albuquerque, NM. Active 1947–1992 as a primary nuclear weapons stockpile site; adjacent to [[kirtland-afb]] and physically integrated with the AFSWP / Sandia Base institutional complex documented in [[dow-uap-d17-sandia-base-1948-1950]]. Replaced after 1992 by the Kirtland Underground Munitions Storage Complex (KUMSC).
