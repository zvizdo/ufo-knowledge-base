# CIA Headquarters (Langley)

CIA Headquarters — the George Bush Center for Intelligence — is located in McLean, Virginia (commonly referred to as "Langley" after the nearby neighborhood). It is the operational hub of the Central Intelligence Agency and houses both administrative functions and operational/analytical divisions.

**Dylan Borland sighting**: [[dylan-borland]] is a UAP witness who observed a large triangular craft with active **electrooptic cloaking** over the Langley area. The craft's cloaking system was actively cycling — briefly visible during transitions — and the platform was assessed by [[uap-gerb]] as evidence that signature-management technology derived from non-human sources has been operationalized on US triangular airframes. The Langley location is significant: the observation was directly over CIA headquarters.

**Intelligence archives**: CIA Langley would house any classified UAP-related intelligence that the CIA has collected or analyzed. The agency's role in the Robertson Panel, its documented involvement in counterintelligence operations against UAP researchers (including surveillance of civilian organizations), and its position at the center of the US intelligence apparatus make Langley a primary institutional location for UAP-related classified material.

**[[aatip]] and UAPTF nexus**: While AATIP was nominally a DIA program and the UAPTF was DoD, CIA involvement in the broader UAP oversight structure is documented through personnel connections and the IC-wide access mandated by the UAP Disclosure Act and AARO's all-source mandate.

**Northern Virginia intelligence corridor**: Langley is part of the Northern Virginia intelligence community corridor, which also includes NSA facilities, NRO, DIA, and numerous private intelligence contractors — the geographic nexus of the US intelligence apparatus.
