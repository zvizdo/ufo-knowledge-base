# Black Mailbox

A mailbox along Nevada Highway 375 (now officially the "Extraterrestrial Highway") near [[area-51]] and [[papoose-lake]]. [[bob-lazar]] used it as the designated meeting point for friends to watch S4 craft test flights from the highway in 1989.

## History

When Lazar began confiding in friends about his S4 work and wanted to show them something real, he needed a landmark they could find in the dark Nevada desert. The black mailbox — belonging to a rancher — became the rendezvous point.

On **March 22, 1989**, Lazar brought [[john-lear]] and [[gene-huff]] to the area. Using Lear's Celestron 8-inch telescope, they confirmed a disc-shaped craft through the scope before it was also visible to the naked eye. This was while Lazar was still employed at S4.

At a subsequent viewing, Lazar's group was caught by security, ending his access to S4.

## Cultural Impact

After Lazar's public disclosure through [[george-knapp]] in 1989, the location became one of the most visited UFO pilgrimage sites in the United States. The mailbox itself was replaced multiple times due to tourist vandalism. The road (Highway 375) was renamed the "Extraterrestrial Highway" by the State of Nevada in 1996.
