# Nazca, Peru

The Nazca region is a high arid plateau in the Ica Region of southern Peru, approximately 400 km south of Lima. It is most widely known for the [[nazca-lines]] — enormous geoglyphs etched into the desert surface by the Nazca culture (c. 100–800 CE). In the UAP and alternative history context, the region is also the primary site for the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] discovery.

## The Nazca Lines

Geometric and figurative geoglyphs covering approximately 450 km² of desert plateau, visible only from altitude. The mainstream archaeological view holds they were created by the Nazca people for ritual or astronomical purposes. The [[ancient-astronaut-hypothesis]] (popularized by [[chariots-of-the-gods]]) reinterprets them as landing strips or signals for alien craft.

## The Tridactyl Mummy Discovery (2015)

In 2015, local huaquero [[leandro-gravedigger]] discovered a cave near the Nazca region containing over 200 body parts, isolated heads, limbs, and intact bodies covered in diatomaceous earth. The specimens are referred to as [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]]. See [[2015-nazca-mummy-discovery]] for the discovery chronology.

Anonymous sources cited in the research have reported that the mummies came from a large subterranean tunnel system in the region, with local reports of strange nocturnal beings near the tunnel entrances. See [[deep-underground-military-bases]], [[subterranean-civilizations]].

## Peruvian Government Suppression

The Peruvian Ministry of Culture has been documented acting in bad faith: conflating genuine mummy specimens with tourist figurines in official statements (which became Reuters debunks), raiding press conferences, and making at least 7 attempts to seize bodies from [[university-of-ika]]. See [[government-suppression-ufo]].
