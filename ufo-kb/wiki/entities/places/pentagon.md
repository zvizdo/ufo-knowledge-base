# The Pentagon

Headquarters of the U.S. Department of Defense, located in Arlington, Virginia. The five-sided building houses the offices of the Secretary of Defense, Joint Chiefs of Staff, and dozens of defense agencies. In the UAP context, the Pentagon is the institutional home of all official UAP investigation programs: [[aatip]] (2007–2012), [[uaptf]] (2020), [[aaro]] (2022–present), and the classified Special Access Programs that UAP whistleblowers allege exist within it.

The Pentagon's role in UAP is dual: (1) as the institutional driver of UAP classification and suppression, and (2) as the site of internal pressure for disclosure from reform-minded officials.
