# Savannah River Site

A top-secret nuclear production facility in South Carolina operated by DuPont for the Atomic Energy Commission. In July 1952 it was the **most secretive atomic site in the United States**, having just become operational producing plutonium and tritium for hydrogen bomb components. It is one of the most striking examples in the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] record.

## 1952 UAP Sighting

Young nuclear engineer **John Anderson** was required to attend a DuPont company anniversary event at the site on what Hastings establishes was likely **July 19, 1952** — the peak day of the [[1952-washington-dc-flyover]].

Anderson and colleagues observed a **green glowing, angular-shaped object** darting back and forth erratically at high speeds and altitudes with "phenomenal maneuverability." The event was kept secret; the site itself was so classified that its existence was not public knowledge at the time.

## Nuclear-DC Flyover Synchronicity

The concurrence is striking:
- Savannah River Site came online as the most sensitive atomic facility in America — in July 1952
- The 1952 DC flyover peaked on July 19–20 and July 26–27
- The sighting at Savannah River coincided with July 19
- [[beatriz-villarroel]]'s Palomar plate analysis independently found anomalous stellar transients on **July 19 and July 27, 1952** — matching both peaks of the DC flyover exactly

[[jesse-michaels]] suggests the UFOs may have been attempting to send a message to the federal government; the DC flyover targeted the seat of power while simultaneously the most classified nuclear facility in the US experienced a sighting.

## James McDonald

Physicist [[james-mcdonald]] from Arizona State University witnessed a UFO at Savannah River Site. His subsequent public advocacy for UFO research resulted in intense public ridicule — he became one of the cases Hastings cites of a serious researcher destroyed by going public.
