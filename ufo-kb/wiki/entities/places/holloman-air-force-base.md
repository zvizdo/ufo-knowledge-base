# Holloman Air Force Base

US Air Force installation near Alamogordo, New Mexico. Best known in UAP discourse for an alleged landing of extraterrestrial craft in the early 1960s — possibly 1964–65 — that was reportedly filmed, briefly screened at [[norton-air-force-base]], and then confiscated by men in suits.

## The Alleged Landing

Geographic and thematic proximity to the [[1964-socorro]] case drew [[james-fox]] to investigate. Fox's reconstruction:

- Three disc-shaped craft appeared over Holloman AFB, escorted by military jets
- Two craft peeled away; one descended in a floating, leaf-like wobble (possibly indicating mechanical difficulty)
- The craft landed on three pods
- A seamless sliding door opened and a ramp extended
- Two beings flanked the ramp, described as having Egyptian-style headgear, large eyes with vertical-slit pupils, large noses, minimal mouths and chins
- A primary being with what appeared to be a communication device descended
- Beings met with military officers, entered a Jeep, and drove off
- The film ended with the disc remaining on the base

Fox's conclusion after years of investigation: "I kind of believe it happened." He cannot prove it.

## Disinformation Layer

Fox argues that the widely circulated story — that **President Eisenhower** met with aliens at Holloman — was deliberately seeded as disinformation adjacent to the real event. By attaching a more sensational, unprovable element, the entire case could be discredited when that element was debunked.

[[coral-lorenzen]] wrote contemporaneous articles about the Holloman landing with military official sources in the early 1960s — the most credible near-contemporaneous reporting Fox found.
