# Malmstrom Air Force Base

[[strategic-air-command]] Minuteman ICBM base near Great Falls, Montana. Operated approximately 200 Minuteman I missiles in 1967, organized into 20 missile flights of 10 missiles each (e.g., Oscar Flight, Echo Flight). Site of at least four documented UFO-related incidents between 1966 and 1975 that Salas is aware of, making it the most densely documented single-site UFO-nuclear hotspot in the public record.

## Description

The base hosts underground launch control capsules (LCCs) for each flight, staffed by two-officer crews. Launch facilities (LFs) where the missiles themselves are housed are dispersed ~1–2 miles from each LCC. Triple-shielded cable bundles (8–9 inch diameter) connect the LCC to each LF.

[[robert-hastings]] worked as a janitor at the base in the second half of March 1967.

## Associated Incidents

- [[1967-echo-flight]] — ~March 16, 1967: all 10 Echo Flight missiles go offline during UFO reports overhead
- [[1967-oscar-flight]] — March 24, 1967: all 10 Oscar Flight missiles go offline; red-orange oval object at perimeter
- ~1968: additional incidents cited in Salas's book (part of 13 listed)
- 1975: NORAD report of UFO sightings over Malmstrom missile bases
- 1993: John Mills reports his group saw a UFO hovering over Malmstrom before returning to the base, which "had been swarmed"
