# Patrick Air Force Base

Patrick Air Force Base (Patrick AFB) is the headquarters facility of the [[45th-space-wing]] on Florida's Space Coast, located approximately 30 miles south of [[cape-canaveral]] along Highway A1A. The 45th Space Wing administers both Patrick AFB and Cape Canaveral Air Force Station as a unified command.

## Role in Rogers' Tenure

[[greg-rogers]] was stationed at Patrick AFB from 1989 as chief of aerospace medicine for the 45th Space Wing. His role encompassed:
- Medical group oversight for all of Cape Canaveral AFST, Patrick AFB, and the Eastern Space and Missile Center
- Chief flight surgeon for the astronaut rescue and recovery team at [[kennedy-space-center]]
- Oversight of [[egng]]'s medical chief (EG&G ran the on-base healthcare clinics)
- EG&G-escorted tours of contractor buildings across the cape as part of his medical oversight responsibilities

The 1992 saucer sighting (see [[1992-cape-canaveral-saucer]]) occurred at Cape Canaveral AFST; Rogers drove the 30-minute return to Patrick on Highway A1A while deciding not to report the incident.
