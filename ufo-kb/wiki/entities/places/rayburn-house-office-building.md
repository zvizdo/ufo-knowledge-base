# Rayburn House Office Building

## Description

The Rayburn House Office Building is the largest and most recently constructed of the three House office buildings on Capitol Hill, completed in 1965. Located on the south side of Independence Avenue SW, it houses committee hearing rooms and member offices. Room 2154 — a large formal hearing room — is where major committee hearings take place.

In UAP history, the Rayburn Building is the physical location of the July 26, 2023 hearing before the House Oversight Committee's National Security Subcommittee chaired by [[nancy-mace]], at which [[david-grusch]], [[ryan-graves]], and [[david-fravor]] gave sworn testimony. The hearing was one of the most significant public UAP events since the 1960s congressional UFO hearings.

The building was also the scene immediately surrounding the dramatic moment when journalist [[michael-shellenberger]] tweeted the existence of the [[immaculate-constellation]] program — reportedly timed to coincide with or immediately follow the hearing testimony, amplifying its impact.
