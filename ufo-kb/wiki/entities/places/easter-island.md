# Easter Island

Remote South Pacific island (Rapa Nui) approximately 3,700 km off the Chilean coast, administered by Chile. Famous for the enigmatic Moai stone statues and, in the context of ancient mysteries research, for the **Rongo Rongo** script — an undeciphered glyph system that remains one of the only instances of an independently developed writing tradition in the Pacific.

## Rongo Rongo and Plasma Discharge

[[robert-schoch]] and his wife Dr. Katie investigated Rongo Rongo on Easter Island. Standard assumption: Rongo Rongo is a writing system. Schoch's alternative interpretation (cited by [[matt-lroy]]): **Rongo Rongo glyphs are not writing at all, but recordings of plasma discharge visual patterns** — the same geometric and figural shapes that appear in the upper atmosphere during massive coronal mass ejection events.

This places Easter Island in the same analytical category as [[nazca-lines]]:
- Nazca: ground-level geoglyphs imitating plasma sky patterns
- Rongo Rongo: hand-held tablet glyphs recording the same patterns

If Schoch is correct, Rongo Rongo preserves direct visual memory of an ancient catastrophic CME event — a civilization's attempt to record the terrifying phenomena they witnessed.
