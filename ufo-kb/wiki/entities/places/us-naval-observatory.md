# US Naval Observatory

The US Naval Observatory (USNO) in Washington DC. Historically mapped stars for ship navigation in the age of sail; in the modern era, maintains the celestial reference frame that GPS satellites use to determine their positions. The Vice President of the United States lives in the former Superintendent's residence on USNO grounds.

[[tim-gallaudet]] served as Superintendent of the USNO. He credits his time there — working alongside astrophysicists, updating star catalogs, and engaging with cosmology — as a key inflection point in his thinking about extraterrestrial life:

> "I got to know a lot of astrophysicists and I learned a lot of cosmology and I realized the universe is so giant. We'd be arrogant to think that we're the only species that's evolved to travel between celestial bodies."

The USNO's role in star mapping and its scientific community of astrophysicists made it a formative environment for Gallaudet's eventual engagement with the Galileo Project.
