# F.E. Warren Air Force Base

US Air Force base in Cheyenne, Wyoming, that houses ICBM nuclear missile silos. Part of [[robert-hastings]]'s documentation of 167 UFO cases at every US nuclear base.

## 2010 Missile Outage

In 2010 — just before Hastings retired — F.E. Warren experienced an approximately one-hour outage at its nuclear missile silos. This represented a major National Security concern, as ICBM silos going offline is precisely the scenario the US most fears from adversary action.

Hastings conducted back-channel interviews with base personnel who attributed the outage to an **oblong, Tic-Tac shaped object** flying around the base and apparently interfering with the silos' electronics. See: [[2010-fe-warren-outage]].
