# Pikatini Arsenal

A US Army weapons research, development, and manufacturing facility in Morris County, New Jersey. The installation is associated with ordnance and explosives research — not nuclear weapons per se, but weapons-adjacent in a way that aligns with the documented [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] pattern of UAP appearing near defense-critical infrastructure.

> Note: The transcript spells the location "Pikatini Arsenal." The standard spelling is Picatinny Arsenal — both forms refer to the same installation. `pikatini-arsenal.md` is used here as the canonical slug matching the transcript rendering.

## Description

Large military campus in the New Jersey highlands. Historically associated with ammunition, propellant, and weapons systems development for the US Army. Not a nuclear site but a high-value military installation whose proximity to Salem Nuclear Power Plant (NJ) and the general northeastern US defense infrastructure gives it strategic sensitivity.
