# Johnson Space Center

NASA's main center for human spaceflight, located in Houston, Texas. Home of Mission Control ("Houston, we have a problem"). If a classified manned spaceflight operation with exotic materials logistics existed, JSC would be the natural headquarters — it focuses exclusively on human spaceflight, unlike other NASA centers.

## Building 8 — Photo Processing Lab

Building 8 at JSC is a satellite and launch photography processing facility. [[donna-hare]] (a NASA photographic specialist with secret clearance) disclosed that Building 8 contained satellite imagery showing UAP — and that these objects were routinely airbrushed before the imagery was sold commercially.

[[gary-mckinnon]], acting on Hare's testimony, identified Building 8 machines using Windows network audit commands (which report building numbers alongside machine serial numbers). He found ~12 accessible machines there with desktops bearing only two folders: **"raw"** and **"processed"** — a literal match to the workflow Hare described. On one machine he loaded a NASA-proprietary image showing a smooth cigar-shaped craft in lateral orbit above Earth. He was remotely disconnected before the image fully loaded.
