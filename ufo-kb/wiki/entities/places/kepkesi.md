# Kepkesi

An archaeological site in the [[lake-van]] region of eastern Turkey, investigated extensively by [[matt-lroy]]. The site features:

- **Giant square basalt blocks** — basalt has a Mohs hardness of approximately 7/10, making it extremely difficult to work with conventional tools.
- **Machine-cut surfaces** — precision cuts inconsistent with known ancient tool technologies.
- **Vitrified faces** — surfaces melted to an obsidian/glass-like state, indicating exposure to temperatures exceeding 2,000°C.

[[robert-schoch]] visited Kepkesi alongside [[matt-lroy]] and confirmed the vitrification firsthand. This confirmation by a credentialed geologist strengthens the physical evidence case.

## Interpretation

The vitrification is the central puzzle. Options:
1. **Volcanic heat**: [[mount-nemrut]] erupted in the same region during the Older/Younger Dryas period. Lroy considers this possible but insufficient for the observed pattern.
2. **Plasma discharge**: [[robert-schoch]]'s [[plasma-discharge-theory]] — a massive CME event created electrical plasma discharges that melted stone surfaces globally.
3. **Unknown ancient technology**: Some alternative researchers propose advanced ancient energy weapons or technology.

Lroy favors plasma discharge. The vitrification pattern (surface-only melting on exposed faces) is consistent with directed energy rather than ambient volcanic heat.
