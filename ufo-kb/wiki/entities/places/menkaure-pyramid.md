# Menkaure Pyramid

Third and smallest of the three major 4th-dynasty pyramids on [[giza-plateau]]. Built for Pharaoh Menkaure (~2510 BC). Height ~65 m (originally ~66 m); base 105 × 103 m. Notably, its lower casing courses are red Aswan granite rather than Tura limestone.

## Subsurface Studies

Like [[khafre-pyramid]] and the [[great-pyramid]], Menkaure's pyramid has been subject to muon tomography and ground-penetrating radar surveys. Less data is publicly available compared to the other two.
