# Indian Springs

Indian Springs refers to the Indian Springs Gunnery Ranges — a remote stretch of the Nevada desert administered by [[nellis-air-force-base]]. [[charles-hall]] identifies the northern end of Indian Springs Valley (Areas 53–54, which he calls "Dreamland") as the location of the [[tall-whites]] base and his primary duty station from March 1965 to May 1967.

> ⚠ Conflict: Popular usage conflates "Area 51," "Groom Lake," and "Dreamland." Hall draws a sharp distinction: **Area 51 = Groom Lake** (just the entry area to a numbered range system extending to Area 60). **Dreamland = Areas 53 and 54** — the northern Indian Springs Valley, Dog Bone Valley, and the western valley including French Peak. These are distinct sites ~40+ miles apart.

## Geography

- **Indian Springs Valley** — The primary valley where Tall White operations centered; scout craft observed along the eastern valley wall; Tall Whites played on Indian Springs Air Base runways on summer nights
- **Dog Bone Lake** — Adjacent dry lake used by Tall Whites as the primary landing field; deep spacecraft arrived and departed from here during the full-moon window
- **Areas 53–54 (Dreamland)** — The restricted areas at the northern end of the valley where the Tall White base and scout craft hangar were located; accessible via a road that Hall's Air Police could not approach
- **French Peak** — Part of the broader Dreamland area (western valley)
- **Range 3 / Range 4** — Specific gunnery ranges within the complex where Hall most frequently made weather observations and encountered Tall Whites; Range 4 associated with the "Range for Harry" legend

## The Desert Game Range

[[charles-hall]] makes a striking historical claim: the **Desert Southwest Game Range** (Desert Game Range), established ~1935–37 by FDR executive order ostensibly to protect desert bighorn sheep, was actually created to protect an already-present Tall White presence. Evidence Hall cites:

- Anti-aircraft guns with live ammunition were stationed at the "sheep preserve" pre-WWII
- Sheep Peak (within the range) had no documented human ascent until 1970
- A single Army colonel apparently convinced FDR to sign the executive order without broader consultation

If accurate, the Tall White presence at Indian Springs **predates** the post-Roswell government UFO programs by at least a decade — and possibly caused the military infrastructure to be placed around it, rather than the reverse.

## The Air Police Boundary

Hall describes strict compartmentalization enforced at the base:
- Air Police could not approach beyond a road junction approximately one mile from the Nellis weather station
- Hall could only be contacted in the chow hall parking lot between noon and 12:30
- Commanders were required to check in before and after any contact with Hall
- Hall's barracks could not be inspected

This administrative architecture was designed to prevent any military personnel — whose thoughts the Tall Whites could read electronically — from inadvertently triggering a response by thinking about Tall White children.

## Creech Air Force Base (Current Designation)

Indian Springs Air Base was renamed **Creech Air Force Base** (now the USAF's primary drone operations center). [[tim-taylor]] attended a ribbon-cutting ceremony at Creech and was reportedly warned not to drive up the road toward the former Tall White base area. Whether the Tall White base remains operational under the Creech designation is unresolved.
