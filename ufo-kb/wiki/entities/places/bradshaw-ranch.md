# Bradshaw Ranch

A ranch near Sedona, Arizona, formerly owned by Linda Bradshaw, who photographed what she describes as an interdimensional portal opening in a specific area of the property. The ranch is now fenced and nominally classified as an academic research station — though the cover story has anomalous features.

## Cover Story Problems

Officially, the ranch appears to be used by Northern Arizona University for research. However:
- None of the supposed university professors have ever been observed on site.
- The property is equipped with CCTV and a solar-powered camera, but **the camera points inward toward the area where [[linda-bradshaw]] says the portal appeared** — not outward toward the perimeter as security logic demands.
- The installation has no obvious connection to normal academic field operations.

## Physical Infrastructure (GPR Confirmed)

[[ross-coulthart]]'s Australian associate (GPR operator Paul) conducted ground-penetrating radar sweeps of the road around Bradshaw Ranch. Findings:
- **A 50-foot-wide reinforced steel-concrete structure detected just below the road surface** — clearly visible on radar; interpreted as a tunnel or underground chamber.
- **Two large three-phase power supply boxes** found at the fenced ranch perimeter, humming, with cables clearly running into the ground; significant electrical capacity going underground.

## Tunnel Trajectory

Extrapolating the tunnel direction from wall angles, the line runs directly to the **Clark Dale Cement Plant** — located midway between two mountain ranges. The cement plant is posited as a secondary egress point for the underground facility. Twenty years ago, intense drilling shook items off bookshelves in nearby Clarkdale, and residents were compensated for structural damage — consistent with large-scale tunneling beneath the community.

## Portal History

Linda Bradshaw photographed what appeared to be a portal opening in a specific patch of ground on the ranch. That exact patch is now the fenced area under camera surveillance.
