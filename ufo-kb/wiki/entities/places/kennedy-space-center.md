# Kennedy Space Center

NASA's primary human spaceflight launch facility, located on Merritt Island, Florida, adjacent to [[cape-canaveral]]. KSC has served as the launch site for the Apollo, Space Shuttle, and Artemis programs.

## UAP-Adjacent Significance

Flight surgeon [[greg-rogers]], who witnessed the [[1992-cape-canaveral-saucer]] incident during his assignment with the [[45th-space-wing]], worked in a medical support role serving KSC astronaut recovery operations. The physical proximity of KSC and Cape Canaveral AFS made Rogers's chain of command span both NASA and USAF missions.

[[egng]] (EG&G) served as a key contractor at Cape Canaveral and, by extension, supported KSC launch operations — the same contractor present at [[area-51]] and across the nuclear testing infrastructure.
