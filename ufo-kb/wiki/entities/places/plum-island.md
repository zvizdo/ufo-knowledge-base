# Plum Island

Federal animal-disease research installation on a small island off the eastern tip of Long Island, New York. Operated by the USDA from 1954, transferred to DHS in 2003; functions are migrating to the new National Bio and Agro-Defense Facility in Manhattan, Kansas.

## Description

Officially a Biosafety Level 3 / 3-Ag facility studying foreign animal pathogens. Persistent unofficial claims hold that the island has hosted classified biowarfare research and Operation Paperclip-era German tick / vector research — the basis for the contested thesis that [[lyme-disease]] originated as an escaped or released bioweapon, given the proximity of the original Lyme, Connecticut outbreak (1975) directly across Long Island Sound.
