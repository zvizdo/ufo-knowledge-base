# Uinta Basin

Geographically: a large structural basin in northeastern Utah, bordered by the Uinta Mountains to the north and the Wasatch Plateau to the west. Home to the Uintah and Ouray Reservation and the Duchesne County area that includes [[skinwalker-ranch]].

Historically cited as a persistent UAP hotspot — "Everybody who lives there has basically seen them: balls of light, orbs, structured craft, daylight, nighttime" (George Knapp in [[Hc6pbG4wICA]]).

## Research

- **Frank Salisbury (1974)** — Utah State University botanist who conducted a systematic study of UAP reports from the basin; published *The Utah UFO Display* (1974); one of the earliest academic treatments of a regional UAP hotspot.
- **Junior Hicks** — local schoolteacher who assembled a multi-decade collection of witness accounts from the area, eventually comprising hundreds of cases across the basin's communities.
