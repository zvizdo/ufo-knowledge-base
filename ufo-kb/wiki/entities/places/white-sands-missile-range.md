# White Sands Missile Range

Major US military testing facility in the Tularosa Basin, southern New Mexico. One of the most important sites for US missile, rocket, and weapons testing during and after World War II. The Trinity nuclear test site is nearby.

## Near-Earth Satellite Project (1953–1954)

In 1953, a military-funded project was established at White Sands to track small natural satellites of Earth (mini-moons). Led by [[lincoln-lapaz]] and [[clyde-tombaugh]]. The project's establishment coincided exactly with [[donald-keyhoe]]'s public claim that the Air Force was tracking two unknown artificial satellites at 400–600 miles altitude.

In August 1954, LaPaz published a statement in *Aviation Week and Space Technology* confirming two unknown objects had been identified — but claimed they were "natural asteroids." The contradiction: (1) these objects never appear in any subsequent asteroid or Near-Earth object literature; (2) the project's own final report concludes there are **no natural satellites of Earth**. The "natural asteroid" explanation was likely cover for something else.

## Nuclear Context

White Sands is adjacent to several key nuclear testing and research sites. LaPaz's previous work (Project Twinkle green fireballs at nuclear sites) connects the two projects — he moved from investigating anomalous phenomena *at* nuclear sites to investigating anomalous objects *above* Earth in the same era.
