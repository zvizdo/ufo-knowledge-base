# Dalton School

Elite private preparatory school on the Upper East Side of Manhattan, founded in 1919 by Helen Parkhurst. Known for progressive educational methods (the "Dalton Plan" of individualized learning contracts); consistently ranked among the most prestigious private schools in New York City. Alumni include media figures, financiers, and public officials across multiple generations.

**Donald Barr as headmaster**: In the KB, Dalton's significance derives from [[donald-barr]]'s tenure as headmaster (1964–1974). Barr was a former OSS (CIA predecessor) officer with intelligence community connections running through his family — a background not typically publicized during his tenure at an elite prep school. During his time at Dalton, Barr wrote *[[space-relations-book|Space Relations]]* (1973), a science fiction novel about an aristocratic intergalactic elite that trades underage sex slaves as a power currency among ruling-class families. The novel's content is cited in KB discourse as suggestive of unusual insider familiarity with elite trafficking dynamics.

**Epstein hiring**: During Barr's headmastership, Dalton hired [[jeffrey-epstein]] as a mathematics teacher in 1974 — despite Epstein having no teaching credentials and having dropped out of Cooper Union. The hiring was on Barr's personal recommendation. Epstein taught at Dalton for approximately two years before leaving to work for Bear Stearns (where he was hired by a Dalton parent), beginning his ascent through elite financial circles.

**William Barr connection**: Donald Barr's son is [[william-barr]], who served as U.S. Attorney General under George H.W. Bush (1991–93) and Donald Trump (2019–20). The second Barr AG tenure coincided with Epstein's 2019 re-arrest on federal sex trafficking charges and his subsequent death in federal custody at Metropolitan Correctional Center — a death officially ruled a suicide but widely disputed. The KB treats the chain Donald Barr → Epstein hiring → William Barr AG during Epstein death as a data point in the [[deep-state-continuity]] thesis.
