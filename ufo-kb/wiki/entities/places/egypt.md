# Egypt

Egypt (the Arab Republic of Egypt) is a northeastern African nation with one of the world's earliest and most sophisticated civilizations. In the UAP/KB context, Egypt appears through several intersecting themes: sacred geometry encoded in its monuments, mystery school traditions that later thinkers connect to Freemasonry and esoteric lineages, and the personal background of [[farooq-el-baz]] — the Egyptian-American geologist who selected Apollo lunar landing sites.

**Giza plateau**: The [[great-pyramid]] and Sphinx at Giza are the primary Egyptian monuments in KB analysis. [[graham-hancock]], [[randall-carlson]], and [[john-anthony-west]] argue that the Giza complex encodes advanced astronomical and geodetic knowledge; the [[sphinx-water-erosion-theory]] dates the Sphinx to ~10,500 BCE; and the pyramid's geometric proportions (encoding π, φ, the speed of light, and Earth's dimensions) are central to [[geodesy-sacred-geometry]] discourse.

**Mystery school lineage**: Egyptian mystery traditions — specifically the priests of Amun, Thoth, and Osiris — are proposed as the ultimate origin of the Western esoteric chain that runs through Hermeticism, Neoplatonism, Rosicrucianism, and [[freemasonry]]. The [[ancient-mysteries]] concept frames Egyptian knowledge as the source from which later secret societies drew their symbolic and cosmological vocabulary.

**El-Baz and Apollo**: [[farooq-el-baz]]'s Egyptian origin and his father's background as an Islamic/Egyptian religious scholar create an unusual resonance: the man who selected where humans would land on the Moon was steeped in Egyptian sacred knowledge tradition. Apollo astronauts called him "the King" — a title with Egyptian pharaonic connotations.
