# Edwards Air Force Base

**Type**: USAF installation and flight test center  
**Location**: Mojave Desert, California  
**Formerly**: Muroc Army Air Field (renamed 1949)

## Significance in UAP Discourse

Edwards AFB is the US military's premier flight test facility and has appeared repeatedly in UAP accounts:

- **1954 Eisenhower meeting**: Multiple accounts (including [[charles-suggs]] testimony via grandson) claim President Eisenhower visited Edwards for a meeting with NHI entities — presented as the first formal government-NHI contact. Not confirmed in primary sources; contested.
- **Proximity to Holloman AFB accounts**: Sometimes conflated with Holloman AFB in NHI-contact narratives from the 1950s.
- **Test aircraft cover stories**: Edwards is the institutional source of much "misidentified advanced aircraft" debunking of UAP sightings — Lockheed U-2, SR-71, and other black programs.
- **[[bill-uhouse]] claims**: Alleged insider testimony places NHI at Edwards meetings in the 1950s.

## Connection to Cover Story Architecture

Edwards' black aircraft program provides the most credible mundane explanations for many UAP sightings (U-2 account for ~50% of Blue Book unknowns per CIA study). This makes Edwards simultaneously the source of real exotic aircraft *and* the alibi used to dismiss genuine UAPs — a dual role that complicates analysis.
