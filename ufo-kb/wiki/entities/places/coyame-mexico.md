# Coyame, Mexico

## Description

Coyame is a small border-region town in Chihuahua state, northern Mexico (population ~1,230). The surrounding badlands are characterized by "messes ripped sideways from tectonic violence, rust and bone-white rock layers streak down canyon walls, boulders sit in bizarrely impossible stacks" — a landscape that resembles Mars and, per [[OOr98r51Sj0]], "the perfect place to disappear." Sky is dark and free of light pollution; nights are conducive to either UAP observation or covert aerial activity.

Locally, Coyame is known as "Mexico's Roswell" — the title of researchers [[noi-torres]] and [[ruben-uriarte]]'s book *[[mexicos-roswell]]*. Residents constantly report **colored lights winking in and out around the mountain peaks** and **little orbs and square objects that seem to come out of the landscape itself**.

## Associated Incidents

- **[[1974-coyame-ufo-crash]]** — August 1974 midair collision between a Mexican aircraft and an unknown craft; entire Mexican military recovery team perished; object airlifted out by US forces. Documented in *[[mexicos-roswell]]*; primary leaked memo surfaced in 1990s.
- Fictional 2004 events depicted in [[sentinels-of-ether]] place a holographic / programmable-matter "mountain door" inside a Coyame canyon — used by [[jesse-michaels]] to motivate FOIA targeting of NORAD/Northcom/Navy logs for unknown-aircraft incidents at **guard frequency 243 MHz** in the Coyame corridor in 2004.

## Suppression pattern

Per [[OOr98r51Sj0]]: locals "don't talk about the lights anymore. And if you ask too many questions, you might find out why." Investigators have flagged Coyame as a probable clandestine-ops sandbox — perfectly isolated, geographically anomalous, and low-traffic.
