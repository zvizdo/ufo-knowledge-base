# Maralinga

British nuclear weapons test range in the South Australian outback, active primarily in the 1950s and 1960s. [[harry-turner]] was the supervising physicist on the range during the nuclear tests, during which he was also tasked with investigating multiple UAP sightings.

## UAP Activity

UAP sightings during the nuclear tests were numerous and documented in official archives. Turner investigated several and his reports remain in Australian archives. These sightings were made by physicists and military personnel with high clearances.

Turner's time at Maralinga appears to be where he began cross-pollinating with American nuclear physicists — many of whom were aware of the early stages of the US legacy UAP retrieval program. This is Coulthart's hypothesis for how Turner was able to write his 1972 JIO analysis alleging US crash retrievals with such specificity.

## Companion Range: Woomera

The adjacent [[woomera-test-range]] was used for ICBM testing. Both ranges remain operationally significant — Woomera is where Americans secretly test hypersonic missile technology. Aboriginal communities near Woomera have reported silent black triangles.

## Maralinga Scandal

The 1988 Maralinga Royal Commission exposed that Aboriginal people and British servicemen were exposed to lethal radiation levels that were covered up for decades. Cancer rates showed a statistically significant increase that was known to authorities and suppressed. [[ross-coulthart]] covered this story; it deepened his understanding of how governments hide inconvenient truth using national security justifications.

## UAP-Nuclear Connection

Maralinga fits the broader pattern in which nuclear test sites are UAP hotspots — see [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]]. The co-presence of atmospheric nuclear tests and UAP monitoring is consistent with Malmgren and Hastings testimony about tag-alongs.
