# Great Pyramid of Giza

## Description

The Great Pyramid of Giza is the oldest and largest of the three major pyramids on the Giza Plateau, constructed during the Fourth Dynasty of the Old Kingdom (conventionally dated to approximately 2560 BCE) and attributed to Pharaoh Khufu (known in Greek as Cheops). At an original height of approximately 146.5 meters (480.6 feet), it was the tallest man-made structure in the world for over 3,800 years.

The pyramid is located near the 30th parallel north — a geodetic fact that figures in arguments about ancient surveying precision and the intentional placement of major monuments at geographically significant positions (see [[geodesy-sacred-geometry]]).

## Alternative Research Theses

Multiple alternative researchers have advanced non-tomb theories of the Great Pyramid's original function:

**Filippo Biondi's phonon SAR imaging**: Using synthetic aperture radar (SAR) analysis interpreted through phonon propagation modeling, Biondi claimed to detect 8 cylindrical structures at depths of 600 meters to 1 kilometer beneath the pyramid — structures inconsistent with natural geology and suggestive of constructed chambers or shafts at extraordinary depth. See [[filippo-biondi]] and [[beyond-protocol]].

**Jeffrey Drum's chemical manufacturing thesis**: Drum argues that the Great Pyramid's internal geometry, shaft orientations, and chemical residues are consistent with its use as a large-scale chemical manufacturing facility — possibly for the production of hydrated hydrogen or similar compounds. See [[jeffrey-drum]].

**Christopher Dunn's power plant thesis**: Dunn argues in *The Giza Power Plant* (1998) that the Great Pyramid was engineered as an acoustically-driven power generation facility, converting vibrational energy into microwave radiation via a hydrogen-based chemical process in the King's Chamber. See [[christopher-dunn]].

### 5. Geodetic Earth Model (Randall Carlson)
The most mathematically detailed geodetic argument: the Great Pyramid is a **scale model of the Earth's northern hemisphere at 1:43,200** — a scale factor that is itself one of the key numbers in the [[sacred-number-canon]] (= seconds in half a day; appears in the Vedas and Sumerian king list).

**Evidence** (from J.H. Cole's 1925 professional survey):
- Height with sockle: 482.757 feet × 43,200 = **3,949.83 miles** — within 313 feet of satellite-measured polar radius
- The two base measurements (with/without sockle) encode the east-west vs. north-south difference of one square minute of latitude at the equator
- In 2 seconds of Earth rotation, a point on the equator moves a distance equal to the pyramid's full base perimeter

The 313-foot discrepancy is within the Earth's own tidal variability (Moon and Sun on same side shift the polar radius by several hundred feet). Carlson argues the pyramid enshrines a measurement of Earth's polar radius "as accurate as those determined by modern satellite surveys."

See [[randall-carlson]] for methodology and [[geodesy-sacred-geometry]] for the broader Parthenon and Gothic cathedral comparisons. Source: [[R7oyZGW99os]].
