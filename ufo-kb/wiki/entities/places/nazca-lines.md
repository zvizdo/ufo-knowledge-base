# Nazca Lines

A series of large geoglyphs (~200 BC to 600 AD) in the Nazca Desert of southern Peru. The figures — including animals, plants, humanoids, and abstract geometric shapes — are visible only from aerial vantage points. Their construction purpose remains debated by mainstream archaeology (ritual?, astronomical calendar?, water cult?).

## Plasma Discharge Interpretation

[[robert-schoch]] (via [[matt-lroy]]): The geometric and animal Nazca designs are **imitations of plasma discharge patterns** visible in the sky during massive CME events. During a coronal mass ejection, the upper atmosphere fills with electrically charged plasma that forms structured geometric and animal-like visual patterns — documented in laboratory plasma experiments.

Ancient peoples witnessing such events would have been terrified and reverent. The Nazca Lines are their ground-level recreation of what they saw above them, visible "as above, so below."

## Parallel Recordings Thesis

Lroy assembles a global pattern:
- **Nazca Lines** (Peru) — geoglyphs imitating plasma sky patterns
- **Rongo Rongo** ([[easter-island]]) — script possibly encoding plasma designs
- **[[colossus-of-memnon]]** (Egypt) — directional burn marks from plasma event
- **[[kepkesi]]** (Turkey) — vitrified stone from plasma/CME heat

If this thesis is correct, the Nazca Lines are not astronomical calendars but disaster memorials — civilizational memory of catastrophic solar events.
