# JM/WAVE

The CIA's Miami Station, codenamed JM/WAVE, was the largest domestic CIA installation in US history during its peak operational period (1961–1965). Operating under the cover of "Zenith Technical Enterprises" on the University of Miami's south campus, it employed approximately 300–400 CIA officers and coordinated between 3,000–5,000 Cuban exile operatives.

JM/WAVE was the operational headquarters for anti-Castro activities including [[operation-40]] and is cited by [[danny-sheehan]] as the base from which the [[s-force]] network operated. Key figures based at or coordinating through JM/WAVE included [[david-morales]] (Chief of Operations), [[carl-jenkins]], and [[rip-robertson]].

In Sheehan's JFK assassination thesis, JM/WAVE is the institutional home of the operational team that carried out the assassination — the same team later involved in the Watergate operation.
