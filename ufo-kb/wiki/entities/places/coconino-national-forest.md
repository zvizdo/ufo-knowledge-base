# Coconino National Forest

## Description

Coconino National Forest is a US national forest of roughly 1.8 million acres surrounding [[sedona|Sedona]], Arizona. The forest contains [[bradshaw-ranch]], [[secret-mountain-sedona]], and Secret Canyon — locations associated with persistent UAP sightings and reports of armed insignia-free operators.

## Associated Incidents

- Hikers in Coconino National Forest near Sedona repeatedly encounter **"armed men in black, insignia-free uniforms, M-16s in hand"** — a pattern that [[OOr98r51Sj0]] flags as identical to the unidentified paramilitary in the [[2009-indonesia-ufo|2009 Indonesia incident]] and the [[sentinels-of-ether]] Coyame ambush.
- Bradshaw Ranch (now nominally a US Forest Service "climate research" site partnered with [[doe]] and [[oak-ridge-national-laboratory]]) sits within the forest's boundaries; persistent reports of underground infrastructure and orb activity escorted by Blackhawk helicopters.
