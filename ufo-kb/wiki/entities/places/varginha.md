# Varginha

City in Minas Gerais, Brazil; population roughly 120,000. Known within the UFO research community as the site of Brazil's most documented UAP incident — the [[1996-varginha]] event involving a craft crash, multiple alien being sightings, military recovery, hospital treatment, and the death of a military officer from an unprecedented bacterial infection.

## The 1996 Event

In January 1996, a tic-tac-shaped craft crashed on the outskirts of the city. Multiple alien beings (at least 3 recovered by military, possibly more per James Fox's later assessment) were encountered in the city. The sequence:

1. Craft crashes near the city early morning — witnessed by [[carlos-de-souza]], a farmer (who heard a boom and felt house shaking), and the EZA sergeant school proximity suggests military had foreknowledge
2. Days later, three young women encounter an alien being in the city — these are the "three Varginha girls" who became the public face of the case
3. Military police officers [[marcos-charez]] and [[eric-lopes]] capture a live being; Charez handles it with bare hands
4. Being treated at [[hospital-regional-varginha]] by neurosurgeons [[marcos-vinicius]] (operated on it) and [[italo-venturelli]] (observed it)
5. Charez dies ~2 weeks later from unprecedented bacterial infection; forensic pathologist [[jean-bachista-janini]] documents the anomalous bacteria

## The UFO Statue

The city constructed a large model UFO statue to commemorate the event. It is a public landmark and the incident is an "open secret" — the former mayor admitted it happened, and administrative staff at the hospital will confirm it.

## Witnesses

James Fox estimates ~12–15 witnesses who saw a craft, and 5–6 witnesses who saw alien body/bodies (including the three girls, Venturelli, nurses, Fernando Jane who saw the foot, and others). Multiple additional witnesses gave informal accounts to Fox when he visited the town square.

## US Involvement

[[carlos-de-souza]] reports being told by researchers that a US Air Force plane landed near Varginha without authorization from the Brazilian government — a secret operation. This is corroborated by the speed of the Men in Black encounter with de Souza (within 10–15 minutes, with specific personal information no local operation could gather that fast) and by anonymous military testimony in Fox's films. See [[crash-retrieval-research]].

## Physical Evidence Status (2025)

- Crash site: undisturbed for 26 years, then bulldozed in 2022–2023 after Fox's documentary
- A vacant lot in Varginha (where beings appeared) is fenced off; occasional visitations reportedly still occur
- Soil samples from crash site (Claudia Kovu collection, held by his daughter/widow) have not been fully analyzed
- Marcos Charez's remains: potential for bacterial DNA analysis pending court authorization
