# Randolph Air Force Base

U.S. Air Force base in San Antonio, Texas, now officially designated Joint Base San Antonio–Randolph. One of the primary Air Training Command and later Air Education and Training Command bases; also served as headquarters for Air Force Personnel Center. As a major USAF installation in the 1940s–1960s with connections to military aviation research, Randolph AFB existed within the same institutional network as the classified programs the KB tracks.

**Strieber connection**: [[whitley-strieber]] grew up in San Antonio and has referenced Randolph AFB in connection with his childhood experiences — suggesting early contact or conditioning events near the base. The [[1952-randolph-skinner-box]] case (an alleged early conditioning incident in Strieber's accounts) is located in this geographic cluster. Strieber's later implant — surgically removed in 1989 — was analyzed by [[southwest-research]], *also in San Antonio*, creating a geographically coherent loop: the city where Strieber reports childhood contact events is the same city where his implant was professionally analyzed decades later.

**Geographic significance**: The San Antonio cluster in Strieber's accounts is not incidental. San Antonio was and remains a heavily militarized city — Lackland AFB, Brooks AFB (now Brooks City-Base), Fort Sam Houston, and Randolph AFB all lie within or adjacent to the city. The density of military and intelligence-community presence in San Antonio gives context to Strieber's suggestion that his childhood experiences were not random but were connected to organized human programs.

**Air Force institutional role**: Beyond the Strieber connection, Randolph AFB's role as a major Air Force training and personnel headquarters means it was an institutional node through which thousands of USAF officers and aircrew passed — making it a plausible hub for the kind of informal UAP information flow that the KB tracks under [[ufo-secrecy]] and pilot-encounter suppression.
