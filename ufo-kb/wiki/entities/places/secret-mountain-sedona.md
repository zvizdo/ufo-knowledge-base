# Secret Mountain (Sedona)

Named mountain within approximately 57 km of Sedona, Arizona — the name itself is described by [[ross-coulthart]] as "somebody's secret joke." [[ross-coulthart]] and local researchers [[jeremiah-hostman]] and [[melinda-leslie]] conducted on-site investigation that yielded firsthand observation of anomalous activity.

## Orb Activity

At approximately 1:30 AM during Coulthart's visit, a **large golden pulsing plasma orb** was observed rising from behind Secret Mountain and moving off into the distance. The orb was accompanied by multiple military Blackhawk helicopters. Coulthart estimates the helicopter operation cost at $50–100K for that single evening's fuel alone. The orb had no visible means of propulsion.

## Armed Patrols on Public Land

Multiple credible accounts of **armed men with automatic weapons** — assessed as tier-one special forces by their movement patterns — ordering civilians off public parkland near Secret Mountain with explicit threats of lethal force. This represents a remarkable assertion of de facto military jurisdiction over nominally civilian public land.

## Alleged Underground Facility

Coulthart states with certainty that a **deep underground military bunker built in the 1950s** lies beneath Secret Mountain. Evidence includes:
- Hydraulically operated doors reportedly observed in mountain faces
- Adjacent [[bradshaw-ranch]] GPR data confirming 50-ft-wide reinforced steel-concrete tunnel
- Multiple three-phase power infrastructure boxes at Bradshaw Ranch feeding underground
- Tunnel trajectory extrapolated to the Clark Dale Cement Plant (alleged secondary egress)
- 20 years ago, local Clarkdale residents were compensated for property damage from intense underground drilling

## Psionic Reports

Coulthart mentions that people with claimed psionic abilities have reported detecting NHI presence in the area. Sedona more broadly has a strong local reputation for anomalous events.

## Adjacent Site

[[bradshaw-ranch]] is the most infrastructure-rich documented portion of this complex, with the GPR confirmation and the portal claim from [[linda-bradshaw]].
