# Hanford Nuclear Reservation

The Hanford Site is a nuclear production complex in southeastern Washington State. During the Manhattan Project, it served as the primary site for producing plutonium — the fissile material used in the Trinity test device and the Nagasaki bomb.

## UAP Connection

In 1945 — before Hanford was fully operational and before significant radioactive material was present — pilot [[bud-clem]] observed UFOs ("bogeys") over the site while flying in the area. He reported the sighting; a Lieutenant Commander Brown was directed to take off and challenge the object.

[[kevin-knuth]] cites this in the context of an SCU (Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies) finding: UAPs were statistically more likely to appear at nuclear sites **before construction was complete and before radioactive material was installed**. This is the Hanford case's key significance: it removes radiation as the attractant and implies NHI have advance intelligence about human nuclear programs.

The Hanford UFO encounter is one of the earliest nuclear-site UAP records in the public domain — predating the 1947 Roswell incident and the 1967 Malmstrom incidents by years.
