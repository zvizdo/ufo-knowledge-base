# Mount Inyangani

## Description

Mount Inyangani (also spelled Nyangani) is the highest peak in Zimbabwe, in the Eastern Highlands. In the [[OOr98r51Sj0]] decoding of [[stargate-program|Stargate]] [[remote-viewing]] sessions, Mount Inyangani is one of four mountain sites named in 1973 by [[pat-price]] as a candidate non-terrestrial base.

## Associated Incidents

- **1973 Stargate remote viewing.** [[pat-price]] named Mount Inyangani alongside Mount Hayes, Mount Perdido, and Mount Ziel.
- **Joe McMoneagle blind replication.** [[joe-mcmoneagle]], given the same coordinates blind, confirmed an underground command center at Mount Inyangani — and described the mountains as nodes of a connected network.
