# Belt, Montana

Small town approximately 35 miles southeast of Great Falls, Montana. On the night of March 24, 1967 — the same night as the [[1967-oscar-flight]] missile shutdown — a truck driver observed a bright white light pacing his vehicle, watched it land in a large ravine, and called highway patrol. A chain of witnesses accumulated: truck driver → highway patrol → county sheriffs → Air Force. The Air Force officer who responded was [[lewis-chase]] (Malmstrom's base ops/UFO officer).

## Associated Incidents

### Belt Canyon UFO Landing (March 24, 1967)

A truck driver driving through Belt saw a bright blinking white light pacing him. He stopped, watched it land in a ravine, then called highway patrol. The patrolman arrived and also observed it. The light rose, flew off, returned. County sheriffs arrived; they inspected the landing site and found broken branches. They called Malmstrom AFB.

[[lewis-chase]] came out. He deferred a helicopter investigation until morning due to lack of night-operations training. Meanwhile, objects were observed on radar at Malmstrom and seen by base airmen.

At the command post that night, [[bob-jameson]] reported via radio that personnel had visual contact with a craft at the bottom of the canyon — with blue and green lights visible from the rim. The canyon walls were too steep to descend at night. At dawn, helicopters were sent down; the craft flew up through the helicopter formation and departed.
