# Catalina Island

Island off the Southern California coast, approximately 22 miles from Los Angeles. Known in UAP research circles as a hotspot for USO (Unidentified Submerged Object) activity and anomalous aerial phenomena.

## UFO Research History

Preston Dennett's book *Undersea UFO Base* documents the history of anomalous USO and UAP activity in the Catalina Channel region. The area is consistently cited by researchers as one of the most active UAP hotspot regions in the contiguous US.

[[tim-taylor]] (NASA mission controller, referenced in Diana Pasulka's *American Cosmic*) has reportedly identified five oceanic hotspot regions globally with elevated UAP sighting rates — Catalina Island is associated with the Southern California offshore zone.

## U Albany Project X Mission (2021)

[[kevin-knuth]] and [[matthew-shostakus]], as part of what would become [[u-albany-project-x]], ran a five-day observation mission:
- **Base team**: rooftop in Laguna Beach (on the mainland)
- **Remote team**: Catalina Island

One of the first university-supported UAP data collection missions. Observations were filmed and became the basis of the documentary *A Tear in the Sky* (2023) and a peer-reviewed scientific paper (2024, Shostakus first author).

**Key findings (scientific paper)**:
- A bright light that excited the team was identified as the ISS (daylight savings time bug in their satellite tracking app; Shostakus reconstructed ISS size from pixel analysis to within one foot)
- Flare camera "objects falling into the sea" was identified as a known malfunction mode of that camera type (confirmed with the engineers who designed it)
- Knuth personally observed in infrared (night vision goggles) what appeared to be an object drop into the sea at the same time as the flare camera was triggering — but cannot confirm because it wasn't recorded and the flare camera issue wasn't understood until later
