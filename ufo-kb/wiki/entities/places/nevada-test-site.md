# Nevada Test Site (NTS)

The **Nevada Test Site** (now officially the **Nevada National Security Site**, NNSS) is a 1,375 square mile reservation in southern Nevada operated by the Department of Energy/NNSA. Over 1,000 nuclear tests were conducted here between 1951 and 1992. The site encompasses multiple facilities critical to UAP discourse:

- [[area-51]] (Groom Lake) — CIA/USAF black aircraft development and alleged UAP reverse engineering
- [[s4]] — alleged UAP craft storage and NHI contact, per Bob Lazar and Dan Burish
- [[area-6]] — Lockheed Martin aerial operations facility for DTRA/DARPA/NRO/NGA
- [[tonopah-test-range]] — classified aircraft testing
- [[papoose-lake]] — location cited in Lazar's S4 account

## UAP Relevance

The Nevada Test Site sits at the intersection of the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] (UAP clustering near nuclear infrastructure) and the [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] (extreme classification architecture developed for nuclear testing extends to UAP programs collocated in the same geography). [[egng]] (EG&G) was the primary contractor running the NTS for the [[atomic-energy-commission]].
