# Pine Gap

The Joint Defence Facility Pine Gap (JDFPG) is a classified US-Australian intelligence installation located approximately 18 km southwest of Alice Springs, Northern Territory, Australia. Operated jointly by the CIA, NRO, NSA, and Australian intelligence agencies (DSD/ASD), it functions as a ground control station for US reconnaissance satellites and as a signals intelligence collection facility.

**Functions**: Pine Gap is believed to operate:
- Ground terminals for geosynchronous SIGINT satellites (part of the Canyon/Chalet/Mentor series)
- Missile warning satellite ground control (DSP program)
- Communications intelligence collection from Asia-Pacific satellite links
- Possible imagery intelligence satellite control elements

**UAP tracking context**: In [[u7g5Sn1DJF4]], Pine Gap is cited as the "NRO Program B ground element" in the Indo-Pacific region — a candidate facility for tracking UAP in Asia-Pacific airspace and waters. Given its wide-area satellite surveillance coverage, Pine Gap would be among the facilities most likely to have data on UAP operating in the Indo-Pacific — including potential ocean-based UAP activity tracked by signals and imaging satellites.

**Political sensitivity**: Pine Gap has been a source of tension in Australian domestic politics — its functions are classified and successive Australian governments have had limited insight into its full activities. Its operation requires a bilateral treaty with the US that constrains Australian sovereignty.

**UAP policy adjacency**: The [[five-eyes]] intelligence architecture includes Pine Gap as a key node; any UAP information flowing through Pacific area satellite surveillance would pass through facilities like Pine Gap, giving it potential relevance to Five Eyes UAP information-sharing (or compartmentation).
