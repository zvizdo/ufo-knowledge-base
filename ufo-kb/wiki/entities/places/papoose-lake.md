# Papoose Lake

Dry lake bed in the Papoose Mountain Range of Nevada, approximately 11 miles south-southwest of [[area-51]] (Groom Lake). [[s4]] — the alleged classified facility where [[bob-lazar]] claims to have worked on recovered nonhuman craft — is situated in or adjacent to hillside escarpments overlooking Papoose Lake.

## USGS Map Alteration

The most precise piece of documentary corroboration for S4's existence:

- Lazar made his first anonymous public disclosure on **May 15, 1989** (KLAS-TV, [[george-knapp]] broadcast).
- USGS topographic maps were updated on **May 23, 1989** — exactly **8 days later**.
- The specific modification: the road leading from the public highway to the **S4 side** of Papoose Lake was removed. The road to the western edge of the lake was left intact.
- This was a surgical edit — not a blanket classification of the entire area, but the targeted removal of the specific access route Lazar had described.

The 8-day timeline implies a government response to Lazar's broadcast, not a routine cartographic update.

## Satellite and Aerial Corroboration

- **Google Earth (2022)**: Filmmaker [[luigi-vendetti]] found historical satellite imagery of the Papoose Lake area that had not yet been subjected to the systematic blurring applied to the broader Nevada Test Site region. Imagery showed vehicle tracks visible in multiple directions consistent with sustained operational activity.
- **Aerial photograph**: A pilot flying approximately 17 miles from Papoose Lake took a high-quality aerial photo; when contrast was enhanced by Vendetti's team, slanted rectangular hangar doors were visible, consistent with Lazar's description of hangar doors built into the hillside at an angle.

## Lazar's Description of S4

Lazar described [[s4]] as built into/alongside a hill or escarpment near Papoose Lake. The facility:
- Has approximately 9 hangar bays with doors that blend into the hillside
- Was accessed via bus with blacked-out windows from [[area-51]]
- Had an interior corridor painted in alternating light and dark green
- Had a hand-scanner biometric entry system logging all arrivals and departures
