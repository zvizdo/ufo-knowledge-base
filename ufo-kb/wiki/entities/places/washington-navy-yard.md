# Washington Navy Yard

Historic US Navy installation in Washington DC's Southeast quadrant; the oldest shore establishment of the US Navy, in continuous operation since 1799. Currently serves as the home of Naval Sea Systems Command (NAVSEA), Naval Criminal Investigative Service (NCIS) headquarters, and numerous Navy headquarters and command functions. The facility covers approximately 70 acres along the Anacostia River.

**UAP relevance — Salvatore Pais connection**: The Washington Navy Yard appears in the [[8TYMQOUDQBo]] American Alchemy episode on [[salvatore-pais]] as a location connected to the anonymous source [[jack-pseudonym]] and Navy research into the [[pais-effect]] (Pais's Navy-patented physics involving electromagnetic field manipulation for propulsion and effects). Jack, whose identity is anonymous in the episode, is described as having knowledge of the Navy's relationship with Pais's patents and the classified programs they may be connected to.

**NAVSEA significance**: As NAVSEA headquarters, the Washington Navy Yard is the institutional home of the Navy's major systems acquisition and research command. NAVSEA manages ship-building, weapons systems, and advanced naval technology programs — including the command structure through which Navy research contracts with entities like the Naval Air Warfare Center Weapons Division (Crane, Indiana) are managed. The Yard's position in Navy acquisition chain makes it relevant to questions about how the [[pais-effect]] patents were processed and whether they represent documented classified research or speculative patent applications.

**2013 mass shooting**: The Washington Navy Yard was also the site of a mass shooting in September 2013, unrelated to UAP research — noted here only to distinguish the facility from the event, which is sometimes searched alongside the yard's name.
