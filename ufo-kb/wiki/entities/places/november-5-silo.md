# November 5 Missile Silo

One of 150 Minuteman II ICBM missile sites associated with [[ellsworth-air-force-base]] in South Dakota, organized into 15 launch control facilities of 10 missiles each. November 5 was on the November Flight — the closest silo to November 1 Launch Control Facility (~5.5 miles as the crow flies; ~8–10 miles by road).

The silo is located on Orman Road, north of Newell, SD (~30 miles north of Sturgis). The Minuteman II warhead was a 1.2-megaton weapon housed in a hardened underground structure with a nine-ton octagonal blast door at ground level.

## The 1977 Encounter

On the night of November 5, 1977 (week of November 18), a dual-prong alarm — both above-ground and below-ground sensors triggered simultaneously — dispatched [[mario-woods]] and Michael Johnson from November 1. At the time the alarm was triggered, the ambient temperature was 9–13°F.

As the two officers approached on the access road and rounded the final dog-leg turn, they found a massive sphere UAP hovering approximately 10 ft above the blast door. The object's surface showed a boiling/plasma-like energy field. No wings, portholes, or thrusters were visible.

This is a particularly significant detail in the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] context: the UAP was physically positioned above the blast door of a live 1.2-megaton warhead during an active alarm, suggesting direct engagement with — or interference of — the silo's monitoring systems. See [[ufos-disable-nuclear-weapons]].

## Current Status

[[ellsworth-air-force-base]]'s ICBM mission was decommissioned approximately 1992. The missile silo was subsequently destroyed (silo collapsed/detonated per treaty requirements). A small cattle-processing facility has been built on the hardened bedrock foundation; the fencing remains. The site is visible from Orman Road.

Mario Woods returned to the site in 2021 for documentary filming.

## Pythagorean Triangle

Woods notes that the three points — November 1 Launch Control Facility, November 5 silo, and [[new-lake-reservoir-dam]] (where the truck was found after missing time) — form a Pythagorean triangle. He speculates the craft was hovering over the reservoir when first observed from November 1 at 9:00–9:40 PM.
