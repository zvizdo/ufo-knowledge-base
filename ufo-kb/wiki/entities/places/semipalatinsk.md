# Semipalatinsk

## Description

Semipalatinsk (now Semey, Kazakhstan) was the Soviet Union's principal nuclear-weapons test range, the Semipalatinsk Test Site (STS), in operation from 1949 to 1989. The wider Semipalatinsk region also hosted classified R&D activity including the cited "giant crane" facility identified via remote viewing.

## Associated Incidents

### Pat Price Operational Targeting (1970s)

Per [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]] in [[Gf_tKn9TaP8]], CIA Deputy Director [[john-mcmahon|John McMahon]] redirected the SRI program away from US scientific demonstrations and toward Soviet operational targets. The first target: coordinates for an R&D facility in the Semipalatinsk region.

[[pat-price|Pat Price]] described a giant crane rolling over the top of a building. Puthoff: "it sounded like science fiction." Subsequent satellite imagery confirmed the drawings. Per Puthoff, this was the moment when the National Security Council declared internally: "Okay, this stuff is real. It can be used. Let's go to work with it." The Semipalatinsk hit converted [[stargate-program|Stargate]] from a research project into an espionage tool.
