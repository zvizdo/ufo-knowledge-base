# Atomic Testing Museum

The National Atomic Testing Museum in Las Vegas, Nevada — a Smithsonian-affiliated institution that documents the history of nuclear weapons testing at the Nevada Test Site. In the UAP context, it functions as an informal gathering venue for figures in the UAP research community.

## Relevance to UAP Research

[[jeremy-corbell]] filmed [[john-lear]] at the Atomic Testing Museum for the *Sleeping Dogs* documentary. In that footage, Lear is shown eating (in Corbell's description, "stuffing his face") while wearing a tinfoil hat shaped like a fedora. **Colonel [[john-alexander]]** was also present in this scene.

Corbell frames Lear's theatrical appearance at the museum — with Alexander present — as revealing "the agent provocateur truth of what was going on" with Lear's persona: the deliberate theatrics deployed in an environment associated with nuclear history, alongside a figure with deep IC/military connections, suggests a performative dimension to Lear's public "UFO conspiracy" role.
