# Norton Air Force Base

Former US Air Force installation in San Bernardino, California (closed 1994). In UAP discourse it is significant as the location where [[paul-charlton]], an officer who had processed footage of the [[1964-holloman-afb-landing]], showed the film to documentary filmmaker [[allan-sandler]].

## The Footage Incident

Charlton had processed (developed/transferred) the Holloman landing footage. A Colonel Coleman (Pentagon PR, 1970s) called the base and asked personnel to cooperate with the UFO documentary team ([[bob-emenegger]] and [[allan-sandler]]) without realizing Charlton had just handled the sensitive material. Charlton showed Sandler the footage.

Men in suits arrived the next morning, confiscated the footage, and told Charlton: "Don't ever talk about it." Charlton did not know who they worked for.

This location is the single known point where a living witness ([[allan-sandler]]) claims to have viewed the Holloman footage.
