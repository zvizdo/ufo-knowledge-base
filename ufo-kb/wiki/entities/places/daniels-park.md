# Daniels Park

A park located south/southwest of Denver, Colorado on the Front Range. In the mid-1990s it was a semi-remote ranch area; it has since been largely developed into its own community. Notably contains a **Native American burial ground** — Blitch has partial Sioux heritage (great-grandfather took a Sioux wife) and was consistently respectful of such sites.

## Description

- Situated on the edge of the Front Range desert; temperature drops sharply after sunset without exertion
- In 1995, surrounded by scrub oak and relatively undeveloped
- The burial ground was fenced; accessing it required climbing over the fence

## Association with the 1995 Missing-Time Incident

In 1995, [[john-blitch]] — then a DARPA program manager living in a Denver suburb — was making routine meditative mountain bike rides in the area to decompress from his volunteer work at the Oklahoma City bombing rubble. On the specific ride that became the incident:

1. He felt an uncharacteristic, inexplicable obsession to enter the burial ground — behavior he recognized as anomalous for himself
2. He walked his bike in (out of respect — riding in a cemetery is taboo)
3. He experienced a spontaneous gushing nosebleed with no physical cause
4. He lost consciousness almost instantly upon lying down
5. He woke hours later (~5+ hours of missing time) freezing, exhausted, and with a fuzzy memory of bright lights on a mesa and a man in a red plaid shirt in the cab of a "fuel truck"
