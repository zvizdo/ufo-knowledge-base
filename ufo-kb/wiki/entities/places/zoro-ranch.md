# Zoro Ranch

## Description

Zoro Ranch (sometimes called Victoria's Secret Ranch) is a large property in Stanley, New Mexico, purchased by [[jeffrey-epstein]] in 1993. It is located in the high desert of New Mexico, placing it geographically near [[los-alamos|Los Alamos National Laboratory]] — the premier US nuclear weapons research facility and a major node of the [[doe-secret-science-nexus]].

## Epstein's Stated Purpose

[[eric-davis]] confirms in [[xnxasfyHtfo]] that Epstein purchased the ranch "with the explicit intent of being near retired Los Alamos physicists." The DOE security apparatus at Los Alamos was, per Davis, "so lax" during this period that this was feasible — retired LANL physicists could be accessed informally.

This account adds operational specificity to the broader [[epstein-physics-nexus]] thesis: Epstein was not merely funding physicists at conferences or on his island — he was geographically positioning himself adjacent to the most classified physics facility in the United States to access its retired personnel.

Bob Lazar (who independently had connections to the Los Alamos / Nevada corridor) reportedly drove past the ranch regularly — an additional geographic convergence noted in the KB.

## Nordic Eugenics Context

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] notes (in [[EzPmG_7WhXc]]) that the Zoro Ranch ground plan resembles the shape of Atlantis. Multiple women involved in Epstein's operations claim they were brought to Zoro Ranch specifically as part of a breeding program. These details are separate from the physics-access thesis but overlap in the same property.

## The Bannon Tape

Eric Weinstein references in [[xnxasfyHtfo]] that Epstein had a tape with Steve Bannon where Epstein was "clearly talking to people who knew what they were talking about, but he was a garbled version" — analogous to Bob Lazar's role as a technically incomplete conduit for real classified information. The precise content and location of this tape is unknown.
