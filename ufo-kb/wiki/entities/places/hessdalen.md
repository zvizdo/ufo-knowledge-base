# Hessdalen

A valley in central Norway (Trøndelag region) that has become Europe's best-documented and most persistent UAP hotspot. Anomalous lights — the "Hessdalen lights" — have been observed by locals and investigators for decades.

## Characteristics

- **Lights emerge from the ground upward** — not entering from above. This directional signature distinguishes Hessdalen from typical aerial sighting reports.
- High witness saturation among the local population: [[claus-svahn]] reports that at Hessdalen, **up to 80–90% of the local population** has had direct anomalous experiences — compared to the typical inversion elsewhere, where 10–15% are open to reporting. At Hessdalen, "you're the pariah if you haven't seen a UFO."
- Not just lights: local testimony includes structured craft and entity encounters alongside the more commonly discussed light phenomena.

## AFU Expeditions (1980s)

The **[[archives-for-the-unexplained]] (AFU)** participated in early 1980s field expeditions at Hessdalen, capturing:
- Photographic evidence
- A radar return — one of the few times Hessdalen phenomena were instrumented in that period

## Scientific Attention

The phenomenon has attracted ongoing scientific attention beyond AFU, including the Project Hessdalen initiative (supported by Østfold University College and others). The radar-instrument correlation is a recurring focus of research.
