# Tonopah Test Range

US government-controlled airfield and weapons test range located in central Nevada, adjacent to and frequently confused with [[area-51]] / Groom Lake. Operated by Sandia National Laboratories under DOE and Air Force contracts.

## Description

The range hosts classified aircraft testing — most famously the [[f-117-nighthawk]] stealth program (operationally based here 1982–1992) — and weapons effects testing. It is part of the broader [[nevada-test-site]] / Nellis range complex that also contains [[area-6]] and Area 51.

## Associated Incidents

- F-117 stealth fighter operational basing (1982–1992)
- Recurring sightings of unidentified craft attributed alternately to test programs and to genuine UAP
