# Mount Ziel

## Description

Mount Ziel is a peak in the western MacDonnell Ranges of Australia's Northern Territory. In the context of the SRI [[stargate-program|Stargate]] [[remote-viewing]] program, the location was named informally as a candidate site for a non-terrestrial base.

## Associated Incidents

### SRI Remote Viewer UFO-Base Identification

[[hal-puthoff]] (Gf_tKn9TaP8) recounts that "one of our remote viewers that was really good" — strongly implied to be [[pat-price|Pat Price]] — came in one day and reported: "I was looking around, and I think I found a UFO base on Earth." One of the named locations was Mount Ziel in Australia.

Puthoff's CIA contract monitor said: "I know this station keeper, CIA station keeper out in Australia. I think I'll call him and I won't tell him why I'm asking, but I'll ask him about Mount Ziel area." The station-keeper's unprompted reply: *"Oh, you mean where the UFOs are always flying around?"*

This anecdote sits alongside Pat Price's broader UFO-base list — four mountains globally, the transcripts of which Puthoff later passed to [[skip-atwater]] for Project 8200 (per the [[OwrDI7GvenQ|Atwater interview]]).
