# Bermuda Triangle

> *Stub page — expand with primary source import.*

The Bermuda Triangle is a loosely defined North Atlantic region roughly bounded by Miami, Bermuda, and Puerto Rico, associated since the 1950s with anomalous disappearances of aircraft and ships. The term was popularized by Charles Berlitz's 1974 book.

## Notable Incidents

- **[[1945-flight-19]]** — Five Navy TBF Avenger torpedo bombers on a training flight vanished December 5, 1945, along with a search-and-rescue plane; no wreckage was recovered
- Numerous other disappearances attributed to the zone, though statistical analysis has not found significantly higher loss rates than comparable ocean regions

## Hypotheses

- Methane hydrate eruptions destabilizing buoyancy and combustion
- Electromagnetic anomalies affecting instruments
- UAP activity — the zone overlaps with reported USO (unidentified submerged object) activity in some accounts
- Conventional explanations: Gulf Stream drift, human error, weather, inadequate record-keeping

## McMoneagle Fort Lauderdale Pilot Account

[[joe-mcmoneagle]] recounts a firsthand report from a Fort Lauderdale-based pilot who flew the Fort Lauderdale → Bimini route weekly for 2.5 years. On one return flight:
- Entered "a weird cloud"; wingtips began to disappear into it
- A bright light opened ahead; a huge object came directly at the plane; he nosedived to avoid it
- Dropped altitude and flew low the rest of the way to Fort Lauderdale
- **Landed 2 hours early with a full fuel load** — physically impossible without time-space anomaly
- Air traffic control immediately prompted: "Identify yourself" — unusual for a routine flight
- The pilot never flew from Fort Lauderdale again; shifted to Miami

McMoneagle: "I think it's an entry point or an exit point. Some type of portal maybe." He connects this to his own Bahamas disc encounter — same region, same physics. He also notes the craft-related time anomaly (2 hours gained) as consistent with the instantaneous-transit model required for genuine interstellar or interdimensional travel.

The pilot had been written up in "a couple of books" (unnamed) and as of the interview maintained he had no explanation.
