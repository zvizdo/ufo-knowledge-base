# Fort Detrick

US Army installation in Frederick, Maryland that served as the center of American biological and chemical warfare research from World War II onward. Home to the Army's Chemical Warfare Division and later to USAMRIID (US Army Medical Research Institute of Infectious Diseases).

## MK Naomi

Fort Detrick was the primary base of operations for [[mk-naomi]] — the CIA's parallel biological warfare program running alongside [[mk-ultra]]. The Army's biological research infrastructure made Fort Detrick the natural institutional home for a program that developed biological agents and delivery systems under CIA direction.

## Frank Olson

[[frank-olson]] worked at Fort Detrick as a biological warfare researcher before he was dosed with LSD by [[sidney-gottlieb]] and subsequently killed in 1953. Olson's role in MK Naomi — and his growing ethical crisis about the program's activities — is the key link connecting Fort Detrick to the broader CIA black program network.
