# Dugway Proving Ground

US Army testing and evaluation facility in the Great Salt Lake Desert, Utah. Primarily known as a chemical and biological weapons testing range. In UAP discourse, it appears as the proposed primary successor to [[area-51]] following a relocation of classified programs in the post-Cold War era.

## "New Area 51" Thesis

[[uap-gerb]] and [[jesse-michaels]] in [[nTiFs8LudUo]] advance the thesis that Dugway has replaced or significantly displaced [[area-51]] as the primary US UAP research hub. Supporting evidence cited:

1. **[[steven-greer]]** — claims 35–40% of Area 51 programs relocated to Dugway
2. **Boeing technician "ah"** (2000 testimony) — described same relocation independently
3. **[[wein-brener]]** — Foreign Technology Division colonel, deathbed statement that *dead extraterrestrials are housed at a Utah facility*
4. **Witness account** — craft observed hovering inside and being tested in a Dugway hangar
5. **[[battelle-memorial-institute]]** — constructed enclosed laboratory facilities at Dugway; Battelle is the operator of [[project-stork]] (secret UAP materials analysis program)

## Facility Context

Dugway is significantly more remote than Area 51 and has fewer civilian workers, making it harder to investigate. It is part of the broader **Utah Test and Training Range (UTTR)** network, which also encompasses the Nevada Test Range. The facility's existing chemical/biological weapons mandate provides natural classification cover for exotic materials storage.
