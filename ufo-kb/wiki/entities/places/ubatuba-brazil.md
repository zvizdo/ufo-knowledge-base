# Ubatuba (Brazil)

Ubatuba is a coastal municipality in São Paulo state, Brazil, situated along the Atlantic coast approximately 200 km from the city of São Paulo. It is the site of the [[1957-ubatuba-orb]] incident — among the most scientifically significant UAP physical evidence cases in the KB.

**The 1957 incident**: In September 1957, a fisherman at the Ubatuba beach witnessed a glowing disc-shaped object flying erratically over the ocean before it exploded. The explosion produced debris that fell toward the water. The fisherman recovered fragments — metallic pieces that proved to be nearly pure magnesium.

**Fragment journey**: The recovered fragments eventually entered Brazilian researcher records, were analyzed in the 1960s, and decades later came into [[jacques-vallee]]'s sample collection. Vallée arranged for analysis by [[garry-nolan]] using the NanoSIMS (Nanoscale Secondary Ion Mass Spectrometer) at Stanford University.

**Anomalous isotope ratios**: Nolan's analysis found that the magnesium isotope ratios in the Ubatuba fragments deviated from terrestrial norms — the ratio of magnesium-26 to magnesium-24 was anomalous in a direction inconsistent with any known terrestrial manufacturing or geological process. This represents one of the few empirically demonstrable physical anomalies in the UAP evidence base.

**Significance**: The Ubatuba location is significant primarily as the geographic anchor for the fragment chain-of-custody. The scientific significance lies in the fragments themselves; the physical location matters for understanding how the fragments entered the documented record.
