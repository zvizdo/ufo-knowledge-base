# UNC Chapel Hill

University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Named in the [[1971-australian-jio-memo]] as one of the CIA's Office of Scientific Intelligence–backed academic outposts — institutions implicitly funded or backed by intelligence agencies to pursue research of strategic interest.

## 1957 Gravity Research Conference

The most significant event for UAP/anti-gravity discourse. Organized by [[agnew-bahnson]] and sponsored by Wright Air Development Center (see [[wright-patterson]]), the conference gathered:
- [[bryce-dewitt]]
- [[john-wheeler]]
- [[peter-bergmann]]
- [[richard-feynman]]
- [[lewis-whitten]] (Martin Corporation / RIAS representative)

The sponsorship by a US Air Force flight research center — and the attendance of an aerospace contractor's anti-gravity division representative — reveals the conference as a military-industrial-academic nexus, not a purely theoretical gathering. [[john-wheeler]] reportedly complained about the conference's emphasis on applied anti-gravity, saying he just wanted to study why Einstein breaks down at subatomic scales.

## The CIA Outpost Connection

The Australian JIO memo's identification of UNC Chapel Hill as a CIA-backed outpost, combined with the conference's WADC sponsorship, suggests deliberate cultivation of the academic gravity research community by intelligence/military interests.
