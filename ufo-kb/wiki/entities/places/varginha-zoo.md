# Varginha Zoo

The municipal zoo in [[varginha]], Minas Gerais, Brazil. During the week of the [[1996-varginha]], multiple zoo animals died from causes that could not be explained by normal illness or environmental factors. The timing coincided with the reported capture of one or more non-human entities by Brazilian military and police.

## Animal Deaths

Animal deaths at the zoo in the same week as the entity recoveries are documented in local press accounts. The cause of death was not established officially. Researchers including [[roger-leir]] and others investigating the Varginha case noted the deaths as potentially consistent with biological contamination or an unusual contagion carried by the recovered beings.

## Significance

The zoo deaths are a secondary data point in the broader [[1996-varginha]] cluster, cited in analyses of [[post-contact-biological-contamination]] — the hypothesis that close proximity to NHI biologics produces measurable biological effects on nearby fauna.
