# Vera Rubin Observatory

Large-scale astronomical survey facility in Chile, named for dark matter pioneer Vera Rubin. Produces more than 1,000 images per night, covering the entire southern hemisphere sky from horizon to horizon in 30-second tile exposures. It is one of the most ambitious astronomical surveys ever undertaken.

## Pentagon Imagery Filter

Every image produced by Vera Rubin passes through an intelligence community review before reaching the scientific community. This is publicly documented (per *The Atlantic*):

1. A government agency contributed $5M toward a dedicated secure data network
2. Each 30-second tile image is immediately encrypted and transmitted to a secure California facility
3. An automated system compares the new image against prior images of the same tile
4. The system cuts "postage stamps" of any new object detected
5. Postage stamps that might depict classified US space assets (spy satellites, classified orbital hardware) are **filtered out**
6. The remainder — one minute after image capture — is transmitted to public astronomical alert services

The same practice occurred with PanSTARRS (the smaller all-sky survey that discovered 'Oumuamua), where the Air Force redacted entire swaths of imagery — sometimes disrupting legitimate astronomical work.

## Interstellar Object Discovery Potential

Current catalog: only 3 confirmed interstellar objects have ever been detected entering the solar system. Astronomers estimate Vera Rubin will detect **50** interstellar objects during its operational lifetime. It revealed 2,000+ previously unknown asteroids on its first night of operations.

Given that 'Oumuamua (discovered by PanSTARRS 2017) already prompted speculation about alien artifact status ([[avi-loeb]]), and that [[beatriz-villarroel]] found tens of thousands of anomalous transient objects in pre-Sputnik Palomar Observatory plates, Vera Rubin's discovery potential for anomalous objects is significant — but all anomalous imagery passes through the Pentagon filter first.

## Carl Gilmore Connection

[[carl-gilmore]] was working on Vera Rubin before his murder (February 16, 2026), specifically developing new instrumentation at [[palomar-observatory]] to monitor meteor impacts on the Moon during a lunar eclipse — directly related to Rubin's operational testing phase. A colleague described the project as "really exciting."

The source speculates — explicitly without evidence — whether Gilmore may have seen early Vera Rubin data containing something classified, which would have been filtered from the public version but might have reached him during the instrumentation testing phase.
