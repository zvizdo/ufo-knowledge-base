# Khafre Pyramid

The middle of the three 4th-dynasty pyramids at [[giza-plateau]], attributed to pharaoh Khafre (Chephren). Appears taller than the [[great-pyramid]] due to its elevated bedrock position. Contains the causeway linking to the [[great-sphinx]] and Valley Temple — and the [[osiris-shaft]] on that causeway.

## The Biondi / Beyond Protocol Substructure Discovery

The most significant recent claim about this structure comes from [[filippo-biondi]]'s [[beyond-protocol]] SAR Doppler tomography work, initiated by [[corrado-malanga]] in 2019 and published/presented publicly ~2022–2025:

### Eight Cylindrical Structures
- **4 + 4 tubular structures** (not 4×4 = 16; eight total) descend from the base of the pyramid
- Each tube has a **spiral (coil) nature** wrapping around a central core column
- Depth: approximately **1 km** (the deepest scan of this type performed at Giza)
- Bottom terminus: estimated **~80 m wide and ~80 m tall chambers** at the base of the tubes
- All eight tubes connect the pyramid base to these deep chambers
- Confirmed by **four independent satellite systems**: Capella Space, Umbra, ICEye, COSMO-SkyMed

### Nature of the Structures
Per Biondi in [[zrmG2jBckgk]]:
> "They are man-made. 100%. You never find anything in nature that coils perfectly."

The spiral nature suggests one of: descending staircases, coiled cables, helical structural columns, or — per Biondi's hypothesis — **piezoelectric coils** through which flowing water would generate electrical energy or "information."

### Comparative Findings at Other Pyramids
- [[menkaure-pyramid]]: similar structures at smaller scale — **2 + 2 tubes**
- [[great-sphinx]]: **one cube** structure descending beneath it
- All are interconnected via a network of underground tunnels across the plateau

## The 137 / Fine Structure Constant Connection

[[armando-mei]] argues that the proportions of the Khafre pyramid encode the **fine structure constant** (α ≈ 1/137), the dimensionless quantum constant governing electromagnetic coupling — the same number physicist Richard Feynman called the "greatest mystery" in physics.

## SAR Validation

The [[osiris-shaft]] — a known multi-level underground structure on the Khafre causeway — has been partially confirmed by Biondi's technique at ~37 m depth. This provides in-region validation of the method.

## Shafts Between Khafre and the Sphinx

[[trevor-grassi]] identified surface shafts on the plateau between this pyramid and the [[great-sphinx]] as potential secondary entrances to the underground network. The [[khafre-research-project]] proposes cleaning debris from these shafts as the first in-situ step.

## Muon Scan (1960s)

[[luis-alvarez]] performed the first muon radiography scan of this pyramid in the late 1960s: no chambers found above the burial chamber. A 1970s Stanford muon scan confirmed this and additionally found two bedrock cavities in the surrounding ground.
