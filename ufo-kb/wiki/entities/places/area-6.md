# Area 6

**Area 6** is an aerial operations facility located at **Yucca Flat** on the Nevada Test and Training Range (NTTR), distinct from [[area-51]] (Groom Lake) and the Tonopah Test Range. It is served by [[janet-airlines]] — the same red-striped classified airline that serves Area 51 — and houses a **Lockheed Martin aerial operations facility** that conducts work for:

- [[dtra]] (Defense Threat Reduction Agency)
- [[darpa]]
- [[nro]] (National Reconnaissance Office)
- [[nga]] (National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency)

UAPGerb describes it as "super super super kind of like held lock and keep" — almost nobody outside the research community knows about this facility.

## Janet Airlines Connection

According to **Lazy G Ranch** (a blog that tracks FAA flight radar data on Janet Airlines), employees destined for the S4 area of Tonopah Test Range must be transported directly by Janet and **cannot drive on site** like other Tonopah employees — a security anomaly that differentiates S4-area access from the rest of the NTTR. Area 6, similarly, is accessible by Janet from McCarran Airport (Las Vegas).

## Significance

Area 6 represents an under-documented node in the NTTR/Nevada Test Site complex — a Lockheed-controlled aerial operations facility, adjacent to known exotic-program hubs, with a multi-agency classified mandate. Its existence suggests the NTTR infrastructure is denser and more layered than public accounts of Area 51 and Tonopah alone imply.
