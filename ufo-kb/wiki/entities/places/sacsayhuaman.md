# Sacsayhuaman

## Description

A massive ceremonial complex and fortress above [[cusco]], Peru. Built from enormous stones — some weighing 100+ tonnes — fitted together with such precision that no mortar was needed; a sheet of paper cannot be inserted between the joints. The construction methodology remains contested: conventional archaeology attributes it to Inca labor, while alternative researchers note that earlier, massive blocks appear to belong to a different construction phase.

## Underground Tunnels

[[luke-caverns]] visited and accessed the underground tunnel system at Sacsayhuaman in September 2024 — months before any public announcement — through a personal contact: a friend on the head archaeologist's team whose members had seen Luke's YouTube channel. The tunnels became a public press release "within the last couple months" before the April 2026 podcast recording.

Luke's point: discoveries are routinely held by governments as planned marketing campaigns to maximize tourism impact, not released as they are found. This is not a conspiracy — it is standard institutional practice.

## The Qorikancha Connection

The Qorikancha (Coricancha) — the Inca sun temple in central [[cusco]] — features the most precise stonework [[luke-caverns]] has ever seen anywhere, including Egypt. Built from gray andesite (as hard as granite), with razor-sharp trapezoidal block profiles that lean inward. In Luke's view, the Qorikancha surpasses Egyptian precision entirely.
