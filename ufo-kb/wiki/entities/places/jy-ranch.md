# JY Ranch

The Rockefeller family's private ranch in the Grand Teton region of Wyoming. [[lawrence-rockefeller]] hosted [[steven-greer]] and others here in **September 1993** for discussions about UFO disclosure — a meeting that Greer describes as his formal engagement with Rockefeller's disclosure network.

## September 1993 Meeting

Greer describes a late-night session on the ranch's deck where Rockefeller told him that his brother [[david-rockefeller]] (chairman of [[chase-manhattan-bank]]) and nephew Senator [[jay-rockefeller]] (chairman of the Senate Intelligence Committee) were "jumping up and down on my nuts for even having this meeting with you." This family opposition constrained Lawrence's ability to support Greer's work; early funding Rockefeller did provide was later intercepted by CIA non-official cover operatives on his team.

The meeting occurred the same month Greer briefed CIA director James Woolsey (Clinton's DCI) on December 13, 1993 — suggesting Greer was deeply embedded in official-adjacent disclosure outreach during this period.

## Rockefeller Land Gift

The Rockefeller family eventually donated much of the surrounding Teton land to the US government, creating part of what is now Grand Teton National Park. The retained ranch served as a private retreat; the Clintons vacationed here during summers, according to Greer.
