# Baltic Sea Anomaly

An anomalous object resting on the floor of the Baltic Sea at approximately 90 m depth, approximately 60 km from the Swedish-Finnish coast. Discovered by [[dennis-asberg]] and [[peter-lindberg]] of [[ocean-x]] on June 19, 2011, via sidescan sonar. The object is approximately 60 m in diameter and exhibits multiple characteristics inconsistent with natural geological formation.

## Physical Description

- **Shape**: Circular when viewed from above; rough, jagged surface — not a smooth disc
- **Diameter**: ~60 m (~3,000 m² surface area); circumference ~180 m
- **Surface**: Dark grey-black; extremely hard (diver [[stefan-hogborn]] could not extract core samples)
- **Corridors**: Internal or surface features with 90° right-angle walls; flat wall sections up to ~30 m long
- **Hole**: A ~2-meter-diameter hole with a smaller 15–20 cm circular orifice showing rhythmic sand movement ("breathing" — material flows in and out)
- **Triangle feature**: A triangular mark with a perfect circular hole (~15 cm) visible in film footage
- **Track**: A groove or track beneath the object visible in original sonar image

## Anomalous Properties

| Property | Observation |
|---|---|
| EM interference | GPS loss, ROV shutdown, compass deviation — reproducible on every expedition including Stockholm University's own ship |
| Temperature | Near-freezing at the object (~90 m norm = 4–5°C; diver measured near 0°C) |
| Radar | Phantom objects appear on radar 150 m from ships above the site |
| Lightning | Video of lightning striking water above the object |
| Gas/smoke | Video of smoke or mist rising from water above the object |
| Biological material | Organic burned material in loose surface samples (tested in Israel; anomalous — no biological matter expected in this dead-sea zone) |
| Separation | Sub-bottom profiling (2025 expedition) indicates the object is **separated from the seafloor** — not embedded in or part of a geological formation |

## Hypotheses

### Natural Formation
Could be a volcanic pillow-lava formation, moraine deposit, or similar. Skeptics have proposed this; Asberg remains open to it. Counter: no volcanoes in the Baltic; no known geological process produces 90° angles at this scale; no crater (ruling out meteor impact).

### Pre-Ice Age Artificial Structure
If built before the Younger Dryas (~9600 BC, [[younger-dryas-impact]]) when the Baltic basin was land or shallow sea, the structure could be ancient human or non-human construction that was submerged by meltwater pulse 1b flooding. Jesse references this hypothesis explicitly.

### USO / Non-Human Intelligence
The EM interference, rhythmic "breathing" hole, detected energy output, biological material, and scale suggest an operational craft or installation rather than a relic. The "made to purpose" framing in [[immaculate-constellation]] aligns with the BSA's apparent internal structure.

## Corroboration Chain

1. [[dennis-asberg]] + [[peter-lindberg]] — original sonar discovery (2011)
2. [[stefan-hogborn]] — first physical dive; temperature anomaly, wall/corridor firsthand
3. Finnish scientific team + ROV operator — assessed as "man-made"; detected high-voltage energy signature on instruments (on tape)
4. Stockholm University ship captain (Tomas) — GPS failure confirmed independently on university-grade equipment
5. International experts consulted by Asberg — none had seen anything like it
6. Remote viewers [[joe-mcmoneagle]] and [[angela-ford]] — assessed as constructed, possibly an energy source

## Military Interest

NATO-nation ships (French, German, American, British) plus a Swedish corvette were filmed by [[ocean-x]] encircling the site during multiple dives. Swedish military headquarters met explicitly with Asberg about the BSA; denied any involvement; could not explain his footage of their ships. Asberg believes they have conducted their own dives.

## Next Investigative Steps (as of 2025)

- Core drilling around (not on) the object to establish age from sediment layers
- ROV insertion into the 2 m hole
- Core sample from the object itself (requires expensive equipment)
- Geiger counter measurements
- Radioactivity testing
- Additional biological material sampling from multiple surface points
