# Cydonia

Region on Mars at approximately 40°N, 9°W, containing the "Face on Mars" mesa and nearby pyramid-like structures known as the D&M Pyramid. Center of debate over evidence for ancient Martian civilization.

## Description

First photographed by Viking 1 in 1976. The "Face" is a mesa approximately 3 km long. Surrounding area includes geometric mound configurations studied by researchers including Vincent DiPietro, Gregory Molenaar, and Richard Hoagland.
