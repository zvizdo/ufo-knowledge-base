# Hospital Regional Varginha

Regional public hospital in [[varginha]], Minas Gerais. Site of one of the most extraordinary events in the 1996 Varginha case: the live alien being was treated here by the surgical team.

## The 1996 Event

The being was transported to the hospital — reportedly considered taking it to the Varginha Zoo initially but the hospital was chosen instead (proximity, facilities). [[marcos-vinicius]], a junior neurosurgeon, performed a minor procedure (suturing) on the being. His senior colleague [[italo-venturelli]] was called in to review and spent 3–4 minutes face-to-face with the being.

The hospital was cordoned off with Brazilian army trucks at the entrance. TV Globo had a reporter outside asking "Did you operate on the ET?" that same evening — indicating the story had leaked quickly.

The being was subsequently removed by the military. Witnesses include hospital nurses, a radiology technician who saw a closed box, Venturelli, Vinicius, and reportedly other doctors. The hospital administration did not order secrecy: staff openly confirm the event to this day.

## The Plaque

The hospital has a plaque at the front entrance stating (translated): "This is the path of the ET. Came here to this hospital on January 20th, 1996." The plaque is openly visible and photographed by James Fox.

## Transfer Chain

After Hospital Regional, the being was reportedly moved to: Humanitas hospital → EZA (Army Sergeant School, Varginha) → Campinas military facilities → possibly to the US (rumors, unverified). The "Moment of Contact" series by [[james-fox]] covers this chain.
