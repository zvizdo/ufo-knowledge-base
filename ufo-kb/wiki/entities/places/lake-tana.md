# Lake Tana

Ethiopia's largest lake, situated in the Amhara highlands at ~1,800 meters altitude. The source of the **Blue Nile**. Sacred in Ethiopian Orthodox tradition — several ancient monasteries occupy islands in the lake.

## Role in the Ark of the Covenant Hypothesis

[[graham-hancock]]'s reconstruction in [[sign-and-the-seal]]:

1. The Jewish community on **Elephantine Island** (Nile, opposite modern Aswan) was expelled around 400 BC after conflict over their sacrifice of rams at a temple dedicated to the Egyptian god Khnum.
2. Expelled northward through a hostile Egypt being impossible, the community fled south via the Nile system.
3. The Nile divides: White Nile (rising in Uganda) and **Blue Nile** (rising in Lake Tana, Ethiopia).
4. Lake Tana is "the absolute center of the area in which the Ethiopian Jews lived" — the **Beta Israel** (also known as the falashas, though they call themselves the House of Israel).
5. The community eventually moved from Lake Tana north to Axum, where the Ark is now claimed to be held in a chapel beside the Church of Saint Mary of Zion.

Lake Tana is thus the intermediate waypoint in the Ark's proposed 2,400-year journey: **Jerusalem → Elephantine Island → Lake Tana → Axum**.
