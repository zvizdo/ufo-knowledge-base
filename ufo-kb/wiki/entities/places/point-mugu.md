# Point Mugu

Naval Air Weapons Station Point Mugu — a US Navy facility on the Southern California coast in Ventura County. The area includes an undersea mesa or topographic feature adjacent to the station that has been discussed in the context of UAP/USO phenomena.

[[ben-hansen]] on *UFO Witness* investigated the Point Mugu undersea mesa feature as a possible alien base or USO installation. [[tim-gallaudet]] notes that USGS bathymetric data shows the feature as natural geology. However, the broader SoCal undersea region — including [[sycamore-knoll]] and an anomalous ridge section Gallaudet identified (a two-kilometer horizontally displaced section of rock) — collectively suggest the area warrants systematic investigation.

Preston Dennett's book on SoCal USO phenomena documents eyewitness accounts from the broader Point Mugu/Southern California offshore region.
