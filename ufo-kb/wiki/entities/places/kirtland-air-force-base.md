# Kirtland Air Force Base

US Air Force installation in Albuquerque, New Mexico, collocated with Sandia National Laboratories and adjacent to Manzano Base (underground nuclear weapons storage). Site of reported UAP incursions and security incidents.

## Description

Home to the Air Force Special Weapons Center and nuclear weapons storage. The Manzano Mountain underground storage facility nearby is allegedly a hotspot for UAP activity reported by security personnel. Paul Bennewitz's surveillance of alleged UAP signals near Kirtland led to AFOSI counterintelligence operations.

## Associated Incidents

- Paul Bennewitz affair — Bennewitz surveilled alleged UAP signals from his home adjacent to Kirtland; AFOSI used this as a disinformation operation.
- Sandia UAP security reports — referenced by multiple sources.
