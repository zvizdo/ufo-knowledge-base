# Temple of Sais

Ancient Egyptian temple at Sais in the Nile Delta, dedicated to the goddess Neith. The temple is primarily significant in the UFO/ancient-history discourse as the source of the Atlantis transmission: when [[solon-of-athens]] visited Egypt (~600 BC), priests at this temple read from hieroglyphs on the walls — records of a civilization destroyed "9,000 years ago" (i.e., 9,600 BC).

## The Transmission Chain

Standard account ([[plato-philosopher]]'s *Timaeus* and *Critias*):
> Egyptian priests → Solon → Solon's family lineage → Plato

[[matt-lroy]]'s augmentation via [[plutarch-historian]]:
> Plutarch's account specifically names the priest: **Elder Priest Sanas** of the Temple of Sais. The named priest (vs. anonymous "priests") is Lroy's primary evidence that this is real history, not Platonic allegory. Plato used allegory because of [[socrates-philosopher]]' execution; fear of speaking plainly about forbidden knowledge.

## Destruction

The Temple of Sais was destroyed within approximately 500–1,000 years of late antiquity. Its records — including, presumably, the hieroglyphic record Solon read — were lost. Lroy frames this as deliberate erasure of historical memory.

The Temple of Neith at Sais is historically attested; the site exists in the archaeological record at Sa el-Hagar. The specific Atlantis-record hieroglyphs have never been found because the temple was destroyed.
