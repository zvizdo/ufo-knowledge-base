# Sary Shagan Anti-Ballistic Missile Test Range

> *Stub. Expand with operational history, system tests, and additional UAP observations as they enter the KB.*

Soviet (USSR; since 1991 Kazakhstan, leased to Russia) anti-ballistic missile and air-defense weapons testing range located in the Karaganda Oblast of Kazakhstan, north of Lake Balkhash in the Betpak-Dala desert. Established in 1956 by GRAU as the primary ABM test site; hosted the development and live-fire test programs for [[abm-1-galosh-system-300]], the System-300/Aldan, the Azov, and later the A-135 ABM system. Co-located with the [[terra-3]] directed-energy weapons complex (the Soviet ABM laser program), referenced as rumor in [[cia-uap-d1-sary-shagan-1973]].

## Numbered Sites and Unit Designators (per CIA HUMINT)

- **V/Ch 03080**: Regional headquarters
- **Site 4 / V/Ch 03142**: Warhead checkout unit for System-300/Aldan and System-75 (SA-2) missiles
- **Site 35**: Launch complex (where Site-4-checked warheads are transferred for firing)
- **Site 7**: Housing/admin compound (location of the 1973 UAP observation)

(Numbering follows the convention in [[cia-uap-d1-sary-shagan-1973]]; modern Russian/Kazakh sources use different numbering schemes.)

## UAP Relevance

- Site of [[1973-sary-shagan-green-uap]] (firsthand HUMINT-reported single-witness observation; CIA-UAP-D001)
- Co-located with the [[terra-3]] DEW research complex; potential confound for any visible-luminous phenomena observed at the range during ABM/DEW testing periods
