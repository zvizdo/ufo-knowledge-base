# Ellsworth Air Force Base

US Air Force installation located near Rapid City, South Dakota. Hosts B-1 Lancer bombers and has historically hosted ICBM silos and nuclear weapons storage. Personnel stationed here hold Q clearance (the DOE/DOD classification for nuclear materials access).

## UAP / Abduction Connection

[[mario-woods]], a security officer guarding ICBMs at Ellsworth, reported an abduction experience during his service. The post-abduction medical examination included inspection of his teeth — a detail Jesse Michaels connects to [[andrija-puharich]]'s bone-induction research and the broader hypothesis that metallic implants or dental work may function as non-conventional EM receivers.

Ellsworth joins a pattern of nuclear facilities associated with UAP incidents and encounters: [[malmstrom-air-force-base]] (Oscar and Echo flight shutdowns), [[los-alamos]], and others. The pattern of UAP attention to nuclear assets is documented under [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]].
