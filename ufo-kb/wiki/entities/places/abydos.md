# Abydos

## Description

One of the oldest cities in ancient Egypt, located in Upper Egypt along the Nile. Considered the religious capital of ancient Egypt — the spiritual center associated with Osiris, god of the dead and resurrection. The earliest dynastic pharaohs (1st and 2nd dynasties, ~3100–2686 BC) were buried here, predating the Giza pyramids by centuries.

## Significance for the Pyramid Debate

[[luke-caverns]] argues that the southern location of Abydos as the "true" burial ground for pharaohs has implications for the [[pyramid-purpose-debate]]: if the earliest pharaohs were buried at Abydos, and if Amenemhat II (Middle Kingdom) gave himself *two* burial sites — including one at Abydos — then this suggests continuity of the Abydos burial tradition. Luke's Ka-burial thesis holds that 4th-dynasty pyramid pharaohs were also physically buried in the south (Abydos), while the pyramid housed only the divine Ka.

## The Osireion

Contains the [[osireion]] — a massive megalithic structure in red Aswan granite that [[luke-caverns]] and others argue predates Seti I's 19th-dynasty temple built above it.
