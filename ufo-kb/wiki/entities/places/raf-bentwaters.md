# RAF Bentwaters

**Type**: Royal Air Force base, NATO facility  
**Location**: Suffolk, England, UK  
**Active**: 1944–1993 (closed after Cold War drawdown)  
**Operator**: UK RAF; leased to USAF 3rd Air Force during Cold War

## Significance

RAF Bentwaters (along with adjacent RAF Woodbridge) is the site of the **[[1980-rendlesham-forest]]** incident — one of the most documented UAP cases in military history. The base was NATO's largest tactical nuclear storage facility in Europe at the time of the incident.

## UAP Context

- **Nuclear nexus**: Bentwaters stored nuclear weapons under USAF custodianship; the incident occurred yards from nuclear storage igloos. Fits the pattern documented in [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]].
- **Witnesses**: [[charles-halt]] (Deputy Base Commander) recorded audio log during the event; filed official memo to UK Ministry of Defence. [[john-burroughs]] and [[jim-penniston]] made ground-level contact with craft first night.
- **USAF [[halt-memo]]**: Official document acknowledging the events; key piece of primary-source evidence.
- **Physical evidence**: Landing marks, radiation readings, animal disturbance.
