# New Lake Reservoir Dam

A reservoir dam located approximately 4.5 miles off the main road north of Newell, South Dakota. Sits approximately 8.5 miles from the [[november-5-silo]] by direct distance. A narrow (~5 ft wide) road runs along the front of the dam, bordered on one side by the dam wall (~50 ft high) and on the other by a deep holding pond.

## Connection to the 1977 Abduction

After approximately 5 hours of missing time following the [[1977-ellsworth-woods-abduction]], [[mario-woods]] came to consciousness with the truck parked at the center point of this dam wall — in mud, despite sub-freezing temperatures. Michael Johnson was still catatonic on the passenger side.

Key observations:
- The road is only 5 ft wide; a two-wheel-drive F-150 with road tires could not have turned around
- The truck was in the only position from which it could exit — perfectly positioned to drive out without going over the edge into the holding pond
- There was only one set of tire tracks; nothing had skidded, slid, or corrected
- Woods had never visited this location before and didn't know it existed

Wing Security Control triangulated the truck's position over approximately 20–25 radio check-ins (one-minute intervals). By the time Sgt. Garza arrived, dawn was breaking in the east — confirming ~5 hours elapsed.

## Pythagorean Triangle

Woods notes that November 1 Launch Control Facility, [[november-5-silo]], and this dam form a Pythagorean triangle. He speculates the craft was hovering over the reservoir when he first observed it as a stationary light from the facility at ~9:00 PM — 5–7 miles away at a 30° angle to the east. The dam is approximately that distance.

## UAP-Water Nexus

UAPs are frequently reported hovering over bodies of water. The positioning of this dam as both the likely craft location at 9 PM (during the light-flashing exchange) and the missing-time endpoint (after the CE3 event) may not be coincidental. The deepest point of the reservoir is approximately 40 ft.
