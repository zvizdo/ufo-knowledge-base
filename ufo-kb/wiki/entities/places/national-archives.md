# National Archives (NARA)

Official U.S. government repository for federal records, located primarily in Washington D.C. and College Park, Maryland. In the UAP context, [[danny-sheehan]] alleges that in 1977, while working with the Jesuit Washington Office, he gained access to a classified section at the National Archives and viewed photographs from Project Blue Book files — including images of a crashed disc with symbols around the dome base.
