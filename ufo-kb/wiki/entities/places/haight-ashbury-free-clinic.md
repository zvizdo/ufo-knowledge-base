# Haight-Ashbury Free Clinic

Free medical clinic established in 1967 to serve the Haight-Ashbury neighborhood's large countercultural population. Founded by Dr. David Smith. Became the institutional locus of the most significant three-way overlap that [[tom-oneill]] documents in his investigation of the Manson murders and [[mk-ultra]].

## The Three-Way Overlap

In 1967, simultaneously operating at or through the Haight-Ashbury Free Clinic:
1. **[[jolly-west]]** — CIA-connected psychiatrist conducting LSD research on hippies; corresponding under alias with [[mk-ultra]] chief [[sidney-gottlieb]]
2. **[[charles-manson]]** — building his cult, the Manson Family, recruiting from the same hippie population
3. **[[roger-smith]]** — Manson's parole officer and LSD/amphetamine researcher; associated with the clinic

This geographic and temporal concentration — a CIA behavioral-modification researcher, an alleged MK-Ultra subject, and a government drug researcher all operating in the same small neighborhood clinic context — is central to O'Neill's case for Manson's CIA connection.

## Foundation

Dr. David Smith founded the clinic in response to the 1967 Summer of Love, which brought enormous numbers of young people to the Haight-Ashbury neighborhood, many of them using drugs and lacking medical care. The clinic's open-door policy made it an ideal observation point for anyone studying the effects of LSD and counterculture drug use.
