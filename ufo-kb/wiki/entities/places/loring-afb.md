# Loring Air Force Base

Strategic Air Command (SAC) nuclear installation in Aroostook County, northern Maine. In the mid-1970s, Loring AFB had a documented UAP incident in which a craft was observed conducting what appeared to be a systematic laser-mapping pass over the nuclear base.

## UAP Incident

The Loring AFB incident, occurring in the same period as the [[1976-allagash-abduction]] (approximately 90 miles away), fits the broader pattern of UAP anomalous attention to nuclear facilities — a recurring theme in the historical UAP record. The craft appeared to be methodically surveying the base's layout, rather than simply transiting the area.

## Connection to the Allagash Case

[[charlie-foltz]] — one of the four Allagash witnesses — had a nuclear submarine Navy background. The geographic proximity of Loring AFB to the Allagash Waterway (~90 miles) and the temporal overlap with the mid-1970s period raise questions about whether UAP activity in northern Maine during this period was concentrated around nuclear infrastructure.

Jesse Michels explicitly notes the UAP-nuclear nexus pattern in the context of Charlie's nuclear submarine service, Loring AFB's proximity, and Charlie's own 1970 USS Holland UAP sighting while serving in the Navy.
