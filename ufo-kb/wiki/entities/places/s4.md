# S4

Alleged classified installation approximately 11 miles from [[area-51]] (Groom Lake facility), generally located near Papoose Lake in the Nevada desert. S4 is the most frequently cited alleged location for U.S. government storage and study of recovered alien craft, and for direct contact/communication with extraterrestrial beings.

## Associated Claims

### Bob Lazar Account
[[bob-lazar]] claimed to have worked at S4 in the late 1980s as a reverse-engineering physicist on recovered alien craft. His account describes nine craft of varying designs, a gravity-wave propulsion system using Element 115 as a fuel, and a sport-model saucer he was assigned to work on.

### Albert Stein Deathbed Account
[[albert-stein]], a U.S. Army clerk typist assigned to [[project-blue-book]], told [[danny-sheehan]] on his deathbed that he was transported to S4 and witnessed his commanding officer conduct **telepathic communication with an extraterrestrial being** who described itself as part of a multi-star-system monitoring team. The ET used the phrase "what you would call God, but it is very different from what you think."

These two accounts are complementary but distinct: Lazar's is technical/engineering-focused; Stein's is communication/consciousness-focused.

## Description (Claimed)

- **Location**: Built into/alongside a hill or escarpment overlooking [[papoose-lake]], Nevada Test Site area
- **9 hangar bays** with slanted doors built into the hillside — designed to be invisible from aerial observation
- **Access**: Personnel transported from [[area-51]] via bus with blacked-out windows; no knowledge of route
- **Interior corridor**: Painted alternating light and dark green
- **Entry system**: Biometric hand-scanner logging all arrivals and departures; Lazar issued an access card tied to his scan
- **Craft present**: Nine craft simultaneously visible when hangar bay doors were open; each structurally different but same material and identical reactors

## Corroborating Evidence (d9tdJ2SkBKQ)

### USGS Map Alteration
The USGS topographic map of the [[papoose-lake]] area was modified exactly **8 days** after Lazar's first anonymous broadcast (May 15, 1989 → modification May 23, 1989). The specific road removed was the one leading to the S4 side of Papoose Lake; the road to the western edge was left intact. This surgical edit implies government awareness and direct reaction to Lazar's disclosure.

### Google Earth (2022)
Filmmaker [[luigi-vendetti]] found historical Google Earth satellite imagery of the area that had not yet been blurred. Vehicle tracks were visible in multiple directions around the putative S4 site — consistent with sustained operational activity.

### Aerial Photography
A high-quality aerial photograph taken by a pilot approximately 17 miles from Papoose Lake, when contrast-enhanced, shows slanted rectangular hangar doors matching Lazar's description of doors built into a hillside.
