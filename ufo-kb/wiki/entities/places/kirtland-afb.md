# Kirtland Air Force Base

Kirtland AFB is located on the southeastern edge of Albuquerque, New Mexico (35.05°N 106.61°W), and serves as a major hub for nuclear weapons research, storage, and oversight — hosting both [[sandia-national-laboratories]] and the Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center. Its proximity to **Manzano Base** (underground nuclear weapons storage in Manzano Mountain) has made it one of the most sensitive installations in the US arsenal. Home to the Air Force Special Weapons Center; absorbed [[sandia-base]] in 1971.

The base became significant in UAP lore primarily through the early-1980s [[paul-benowitz]] affair, in which AFOSI agent [[rick-doty]] and colleagues ran a deliberate disinformation campaign against Benowitz after he began monitoring anomalous electromagnetic signals he believed were extraterrestrial communications near the base perimeter. The operation, later acknowledged by participants, is considered one of the most documented examples of government agents actively manipulating a civilian UAP investigator. Kirtland has also appeared in documents and testimony alleging underground UAP crash-material storage, though these claims remain unverified.

## The Benowitz Affair

Paul Benowitz's house was on the north border of Kirtland in Albuquerque's "four hills" area; his company (Thunder Scientific) office was near the Wyoming gate. He could "walk out his front door and down a street two minutes and be at the fence." This proximity drove his observations of anomalous orbs lifting off from within the base perimeter and flying over a mountain. AFOSI's decision to engage (rather than dismiss) him was motivated by wanting to know what a civilian could detect from the base perimeter. (Greg Bishop, [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])
