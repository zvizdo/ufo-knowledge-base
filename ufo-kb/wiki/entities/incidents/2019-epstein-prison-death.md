# 2019 Epstein Prison Death

On August 10, 2019, [[jeffrey-epstein]] was found dead in his cell at the Metropolitan Correctional Center in New York City, where he was being held pending trial on federal sex trafficking charges. The New York City Medical Examiner ruled his death a suicide by hanging. The ruling was contested by Epstein's legal team, who commissioned a forensic pathologist (Michael Baden) who concluded that the injuries were more consistent with homicide by strangulation.

## Surveillance Video Anomaly

Per [[51N8OxqZIWY]], [[jay-anderson]] cites Wired Magazine reporting that metadata from the surveillance video of Epstein's prison cell indicated the footage had been edited — specifically using Adobe Premiere software — with a minute-long section removed from the video. The official narrative required unedited surveillance footage to establish the suicide timeline; the editing makes the documentary chain of custody unreliable.
