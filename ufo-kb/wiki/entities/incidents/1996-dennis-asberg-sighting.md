# 1996 Dennis Asberg Sighting

Early summer 1996. [[dennis-asberg]] woke spontaneously at approximately 3:30 AM in his apartment near a Swedish military installation. He had been sleeping well and had no reason to be awake.

## Account

Sitting on the edge of his bed, looking through a large window facing the military installation, Asberg saw something that should not have been there. It took him several seconds to process what he was seeing. He began swearing and tried to wake his wife; she came toward him frightened by his behavior. When he turned back to the window, the object was still present.

**Appearance**: From the side, the object appeared elongated — like a cigar or hot dog, brownish-yellow with sunlight reflecting off it, with darkness beneath it. As he watched, the object rotated, revealing a circular disc profile. It then accelerated away in approximately one to one-and-a-half seconds.

**Duration**: 14–15 seconds of observation.  
**Distance**: 1–1.5 km from his window.  
**Location**: Directly above the military installation (named "Fua" in the transcript — a Swedish military site).  
**Estimated size**: After visiting the area and comparing against the known installation, Asberg estimated the craft at 600–700 m in diameter.

The object was gone before his wife reached the window. His two children also woke. The family sat at breakfast that morning while Asberg tried to describe what he had seen.

## Aftermath

Asberg investigated through local newspapers, military websites, and a military friend. Found nothing. Emailed [[ufo-sweden]] — no reply. He decided to stay completely silent about the sighting; only his family and closest friends knew for years.

He later confirmed that the military installation handles nuclear material, including plutonium. See [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]]. He believes the craft may have been monitoring the nuclear facility — a pattern he came to recognize as common worldwide.

> "That was the time that I began to think about, okay, we are not alone. Something is here."

This sighting, while kept private until the [[2011-baltic-sea-anomaly]] investigation gave Asberg a public profile, fundamentally changed his worldview and arguably provided the personal conviction that sustained 15 years of BSA investigation.
