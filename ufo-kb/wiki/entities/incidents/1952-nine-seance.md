# 1952–53 Nine Seance

The Nine seance was organized by [[andrija-puharich]] (medical technologist and parapsychology researcher) and held in rural Maine on New Year's Eve, approximately 1952–53. It brought together a remarkable collection of American establishment figures in an attempt to contact non-human intelligences.

[[peter-levenda]]'s characterization: *"I mean, everybody that represents the blue-blooded brahminss of American society, old money people were at a freaking seance in the woods in Maine on New Year's Eve."*

## The Nine

The seance reportedly made contact with nine non-human intelligences who identified themselves as the "Council of Nine" — a group that claimed governance over the affairs of Earth. This became the founding event for a sustained channel of contact that Puharich pursued for decades.

## Participants

- [[andrija-puharich]] — organizer; medical technologist; parapsychology researcher
- [[arthur-young]] — Bell helicopter co-inventor; his presence connects the event to the mainstream military-industrial complex
- [[ruth-forbes-payne-young]] — Arthur's wife; Forbes heiress; best friend of Allen Dulles's mistress

Levenda notes the social composition: *"Old money people"* — the blue-blooded American establishment, not fringe figures. The combination of elite social standing and explicit occult/NHI contact practice is precisely what makes this event significant in the KB context.

## The URI Geller Connection

Puharich later brought [[uri-geller]] (Israeli psychic) to the United States and ran channeling sessions with him that produced contact with the same Nine intelligences. These Nine were subsequently identified in Puharich's accounts as being aboard a UFO in low Earth orbit — establishing the connection between the seance tradition and the physical UAP phenomenon.

## The JFK Network Connection

[[arthur-young]]'s presence at this seance is the pivotal node for Levenda's JFK network analysis:
- Young → his daughter-in-law [[ruth-payne]] housed Lee Harvey Oswald
- Young's wife → best friend of Allen Dulles's mistress
- [[allen-dulles]] on the Warren Commission → cut off Ruth Payne's testimony about visiting Young's household

The Nine seance is thus the origin point of a social network that runs through the heart of the Kennedy assassination investigation.

## Significance

This event represents the first documented instance in the modern era of elite American establishment figures in formal NHI contact through occult methodology — predating and in many ways prefiguring the later government-sponsored psychic research programs (Stargate, Gateway Process).

## The Channeler: Dr. Venad

The medium who unexpectedly channeled the Nine was [[dr-venad]], an Indian academic (professor in Bombay) and friend of Gandhi, who was on a US lecture tour discussing theosophy and spiritualism. He fell into trance on his first night at the Foundation. Puharich subsequently suppressed Venad's background in his own public accounts — never mentioning the Gandhi connection or the university affiliation — which filmmaker [[greg-malazzi]] identifies as the first suspicious gap in Puharich's self-narrated story.

## The Recordings

Tape recordings of the 1952 sessions exist in Puharich's archive. Malazzi's *Mind Traveler* documentary draws on these recordings. Thousands of pages of session transcripts were produced.
