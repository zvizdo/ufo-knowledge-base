# 1955 Kelly-Hopkinsville Encounter

On the evening of August 21, 1955, the Sutton family — eight adults and three children — at a farmhouse near Kelly, Kentucky (approximately 7 miles north of Hopkinsville) reported a sustained multi-hour encounter with multiple small unidentified entities.

The entities were described as approximately **3.5 feet tall** with glowing suits or skin. When family members fired at the beings with shotguns and rifles, **the bullets appeared to ricochet** off the creatures without apparent effect; the beings would fall, then get up again. The encounter continued for several hours before the family evacuated to a local police station to report the incident.

The case was investigated by multiple researchers and law enforcement and remains one of the most extensively documented close-encounter cases in US history in terms of witness count (11 people), duration, physical effects attempted (shooting), and institutional documentation.

The encounter's most significant institutional acknowledgment: it was cited **verbatim** in the United States Air Force Academy's required textbook *Introductory Space Science, Volume II*, Chapter 33, as a credible UAP-related case — one of three to four documented cases cited in a chapter that posited the existence of multiple alien groups operating on Earth. The chapter's inclusion of Kelly-Hopkinsville in official Air Force Academy educational material represents the highest-level US military educational acknowledgment of the case.

[[jeremy-corbell]] and [[george-knapp]] specifically highlight the AF Academy textbook citation as institutional corroboration: the military establishment, in its own training materials, treated this encounter as credible evidence.
