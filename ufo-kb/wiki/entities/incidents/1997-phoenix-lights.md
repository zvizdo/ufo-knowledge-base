# 1997 Phoenix Lights

Mass sighting event over Arizona on the evening of March 13, 1997. A boomerang-shaped object of extraordinary size — witnesses estimated 1 to 2 miles across — flew silently over populated areas. Multiple independent witness groups reported it.

## Account

The craft was described as:
- Boomerang-shaped (V-shaped)
- Massive — some witnesses said 1 mile across, others 2 miles
- Absolutely silent
- Flying slowly enough that it took approximately **5 minutes to pass overhead** in one account
- Close enough that witnesses could see compartments in the metallic structure
- Lit with lights; some witnesses described multiple separate UFOs as well as the primary object

[[james-fox]] interviewed a group of hospice workers (5–6 people) who were sitting together having tea when the craft passed directly overhead. They watched it for 5 minutes and then went right back to their conversation — did not call 911, did not chase it, did not alert anyone. Fox asked why; they said "I don't know, we just didn't do anything." Fox interprets this as evidence of cognitive overwhelm or an induced passivity.

## Emma Barwood and the Suppressed Footage

[[emma-barwood]], a Phoenix council woman, was the only elected official willing to publicly call for a government investigation of the lights. She was "lambasted in the press" for the stance.

A Vietnam veteran filmed the event from his rooftop on a tripod — high-quality footage with CB radio audio of truckers on I-10 describing the object in real time. He contacted Barwood's office to share it. Before the official handoff, two men in dark suits arrived at his home claiming to be from Barwood's office. Their only question: "Did you make any copies?" He said no. They took the footage and left. Barwood confirmed she had never sent anyone. The footage has not resurfaced. See [[men-in-black]].

## Kurt Russell — Airborne Pilot Witness

[[kurt-russell]], American actor and licensed pilot, was flying into Phoenix with his son when the object flew in. He described it as so large that he felt he "could land his airplane on it." His pilot's perspective provides an airborne scale assessment that corroborates the extraordinary dimensions described by ground witnesses.

## Governor Symington

[[fife-symington]], then sitting Governor of Arizona, was a firsthand witness. In the aftermath, he held a press conference and brought out an aide dressed in an alien costume to mock the sightings — a classic debunking move. He reversed position years later, publicly confirming he had witnessed the craft himself and could not explain it.

Symington appeared on Larry King Live with [[james-fox]] and [[buzz-aldrin]] to discuss the event. He also appeared in Fox's documentary [[the-phenomenon-fox]].
