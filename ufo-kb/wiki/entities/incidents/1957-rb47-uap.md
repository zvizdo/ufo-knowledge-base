# 1957 RB-47 UAP

**Date**: 17 July 1957  
**Location**: South-Central US (Mississippi, Louisiana, Texas, Oklahoma corridor)  
**Platform**: USAF RB-47H electronic intelligence (ELINT) aircraft  
**Crew**: Six USAF officers

## Observation

A USAF RB-47 reconnaissance aircraft on a training mission encountered an unidentified object that tracked alongside and interacted with the aircraft for approximately 1.5 hours across several US states. The six-officer crew experienced:

- **Visual observation**: crew saw a large bright object matching the radar track
- **Airborne radar**: the aircraft's own radar tracked the object
- **Ground radar**: independent ground radar stations confirmed the track
- **Microwave detection**: the aircraft's electronic countermeasures (ECM) equipment detected **microwave emissions** from the object on a specific frequency, confirming the object emitted active electromagnetic radiation

The object repeatedly approached the aircraft, appeared to maneuver in response to the aircraft's actions, and at times outpaced it.

## Significance

The RB-47 case is uniquely valuable in the COMETA Report (Chapter 2) because it provides **four independent data streams** (crew visual, airborne radar, ground radar, and ECM microwave detection) — making it one of the most comprehensively documented pre-1960 UAP cases. COMETA notes that Philip Klass's debunking is unpersuasive.

AIAA's UFO subcommittee designated it as a "sample case" for serious scientific study.
