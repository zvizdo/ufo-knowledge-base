# 2025 Casillas Disappearance

On June 26, 2025, [[melissa-casillas]] — an administrative assistant at [[los-alamos]] National Laboratory — disappeared from the Ranchos de Taos area of New Mexico, approximately one hour from the laboratory. Her disappearance became a notable case in part due to the anomalous circumstances surrounding it and her LANL employment.

**Timeline**: That morning, Casillas dropped her husband Mark at LANL's campus gate; he watched her badge in. She returned home, telling their daughter Sierra she had "forgotten her badge" and would work from home — creating a direct contradiction with her husband's account of watching her swipe in. Sierra left for work; Casillas's boss called Mark around 1:30 PM when she failed to appear. Doorbell camera footage from 2:15 PM caught Casillas walking along State Road 518 in distinctive clothing, heading toward Carson National Forest, approximately three miles from her home.

**Anomalies**: Her phone was factory-reset prior to or around the time of disappearance. The badge swipe discrepancy (husband says she badged in; she told daughter she forgot her badge) is unresolved. Financial stress was identified as a possible motivating factor for voluntary departure. The factory-reset phone, in particular, suggests either deliberate erasure of evidence (consistent with planned voluntary departure) or forced erasure by a third party.

**KB context and calibration**: The case is documented in the KB's [[scientists-targeting-pattern]] context, which tracks unexplained incidents involving individuals at sensitive government laboratories. Casillas was an administrative employee rather than a researcher — this is a meaningful distinction, since the targeting pattern (if it exists) would presumably be focused on individuals with technical access to classified programs. Her LANL connection is documented as the reason for KB inclusion, but the KB explicitly does not assert that her disappearance is related to her employment in any non-mundane sense. The case remains unresolved as of the KB's last update.

**Pattern calibration**: The KB's inclusion of this case in the scientists-targeting pattern creates a calibration challenge: administrative employees at classified labs disappear for ordinary reasons (mental health crises, financial stress, domestic situations) as frequently as any other demographic. Including this case requires noting that the pattern, if real, would need to be distinguished from the base rate of missing persons from any large employer.
