# 1953 Kingman Crash

## Account

On or shortly after 19 May 1953, during [[operation-upshot-knothole|Operation Upshot-Knothole]] at the Nevada Test Site, a nuclear device nicknamed "Harry" detonated with 32 kilotons of force — twice the power of Hiroshima. Engineer [[arthur-stansel]] was among approximately 16 specialists then covertly reassigned:

- Ordered to leave all personal belongings.
- Flown to Phoenix.
- Transported by bus with blacked-out windows to a classified location near Kingman, Arizona.
- Told they were assessing a "secret test vehicle."

At the floodlit site, Stansel observed:
- A **30-foot-diameter disc**, shaped like two deep saucers fused together, made of dull brushed metal.
- Buried approximately **20 inches in the sand** with no landing gear, no visible damage, and a small open hatch.
- Inside a **nearby tent**: several **4-foot-tall humanoid figures** laid out on tables.

Stansel's assigned task was to calculate impact velocity.

## Recovery and Aftermath

Former intelligence official [[chris-mellon]] confirmed in a later public statement that the Kingman craft was recovered and studied over subsequent decades. The analysis of recovered material apparently fell to the **Department of Special Studies at [[wright-patterson]]** under Dr. [[eric-wang]], who was subsequently alleged by his widow to have reported directly to [[henry-kissinger]].

In 1956, Wang's department relocated to [[sandia-national-laboratories]] at [[kirtland-air-force-base]], New Mexico — consistent with centralizing exotic-material research within the nuclear complex.

## Evidence

- Stansel's confirmed presence at Upshot-Knothole: supported by declassified Operation Upshot-Knothole records.
- Stansel's military record: three Purple Hearts, Bronze Star — establishes credibility per [[credibility-frameworks]].
- Chris Mellon's independent confirmation: secondhand but from a senior intelligence official.
- No physical evidence, photographs, or government documents directly confirming the crash site have entered the public record.

## Relationship to the Nuclear Test

Whether the crash was caused by the "Harry" detonation or was coincidental timing remains unclear. The episode suggests the nuclear blast may have downed the craft (paralleling the [[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown]] argument), but this is not stated explicitly in Stansel's account — he was told it was a classified US test vehicle.
