# 1966 Westall UFO Incident

Mass UAP sighting in Westall, Melbourne, Victoria, Australia in 1966. A craft was seen by teachers and students near a school; it briefly landed, leaving physical traces, and was witnessed by a large number of people.

## The Witness Intimidation — Mr. Greenwood

The science teacher at the school, referred to as "Mr. Greenwood" by [[james-fox]], was a key witness who spent approximately 50 years refusing to come forward. UFO researchers had been trying to get him on camera for decades. Fox describes how he persuaded him: "Just get me in the room with him." The details of how Fox managed this are not disclosed.

Once on camera, Greenwood described the aircraft and then described a subsequent intimidation visit: men in dark suits showed up at his door, identifying themselves as being from some unnamed government agency. They pressured him to stay silent — "putting the fear of God in him."

## Significance

The Westall case is significant because:
1. It is a mass-witness event (students, teachers, civilian bystanders)
2. The craft reportedly landed briefly, leaving physical ground traces
3. Sustained official suppression followed — via intimidation of witnesses
4. Greenwood's testimony, decades delayed, matches the pattern of other men-in-black intimidation accounts globally
