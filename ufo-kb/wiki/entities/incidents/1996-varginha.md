# 1996 Varginha UFO Incident

Brazil's most thoroughly documented UAP incident: a tic-tac-shaped craft crashed near [[varginha]], Minas Gerais in January 1996. Multiple alien beings were recovered. At least one was treated alive at [[hospital-regional-varginha]]. A military police officer who captured a being died from an unprecedented bacterial infection. James Fox's "Moment of Contact" series (2022, and sequel) assembled the most complete witness chain.

## Timeline

**Before dawn, ~January 13, 1996 (approximately)**:
- [[fabio-furtado]], a farmer, observes the craft fly low over his farm making a "w" distress sound, then land further away on his property. He and another person witness this together. This is the terminal segment of the craft's flight path, confirmed by a drone survey map in Fox's 2025 updated film.

**Early morning, ~January 13–20, 1996**:
- Before 5:30 AM: [[carlos-de-souza]] is driving toward Varginha and witnesses a cylindrical metallic craft trailing smoke. He follows it to a crash site on a dirt road outside the city. He handles debris (memory metal). Brazilian Army arrives in ~2 minutes and threatens him at gunpoint; the debris fragment is confiscated.
- Within ~10–15 minutes: Two Men in Black approach de Souza at a roadside café with specific personal information and warn him to stay silent. See [[men-in-black]].

**Days later (~January 20, 1996)**:
- Three young women (known as the "three Varginha girls") encounter an alien being in the city near a vacant lot. The being is humanoid, crouching, with an unusual appearance. This becomes the public-facing story of the Varginha case.
- Military police officers [[marcos-charez]] and [[eric-lopes]] locate and capture a being (approximately 3 hours after the girls' sighting).
- Charez captures the being with his bare hands; the being had an oily, greasy substance on its skin.
- The being is transported to [[hospital-regional-varginha]]. [[marcos-vinicius]] performs minor surgery (sutures). [[italo-venturelli]] observes the being for 3–4 minutes. Army trucks are present at the hospital entrance; TV Globo reporters are outside.
- The being is subsequently removed and transferred to other facilities (Humanitas, EZA Campinas); US involvement is alleged (Air Force plane without Brazilian government authorization).

**Weeks later (February 1996)**:
- [[marcos-charez]] dies from a rapidly progressive, unprecedented bacterial infection — septicemia following a skin lesion drainage procedure. Forensic pathologist [[jean-bachista-janini]] analyzes bacterial samples; describes them as unlike anything he has seen in 45–50 years. He later (2024–2025) states they may be alien or genetically engineered.

**Same week**:
- Animals at the Varginha Zoo die with unknown cause of death, possibly from biological contact with the alien beings.

## The Craft

The craft was cylindrical/tic-tac-shaped, approximately the size of a school bus (~7–10 m), metallic with a highly polished exterior that reflected sunlight. No lights visible. Smell at crash site: ammonia + sulfur/rotten eggs (not aviation fuel). The front section slid into a tree; the rear was destroyed. Debris was very light, with memory-metal properties (crumpled then returned to shape immediately).

The crash site showed anomalous features: the burnt tree and a vegetation-free elongated circle remained unchanged for 26 years. Soil analysis (Claudia Kovu, 1996) found unknown chemical compounds. The crash site was bulldozed in 2022–2023, shortly after James Fox's documentary aired.

## The Being(s)

Physical description of the one seen by Venturelli (and filmed by Vinicius):
- Humanoid, approximately child-sized (7-year-old equivalent)
- White/pale skin, no hair, teardrop-shaped skull
- Lilac, teardrop-shaped eyes
- Three fingers + opposable thumb; long arms
- Very small mouth; thin neck
- Calm, conscious, non-restrained; seemed to communicate non-verbally
- No smell; no blood visible; not in distress

Per James Fox's broader research: possibly 3+ beings were recovered; the number remains unclear. Multiple witnesses describe different states (dead vs. alive), suggesting multiple individuals.

## US Involvement

Per researcher and witness testimony, a US Air Force plane landed near Varginha without authorization from the Brazilian government — a secret recovery operation. The Men in Black encounter with de Souza (with immediate personal-data access in pre-internet 1996 Brazil) points to US intelligence involvement in the recovery phase.

## Aftermath

Witnesses were ridiculed by mainstream Brazilian media (TV Globo / Fantástico program) for years. None of the key witnesses profited from their testimony. [[carlos-de-souza]] was harassed, surveilled, and subjected to tax audits. The three Varginha girls were bullied and had to change schools.

James Fox's ongoing investigation includes: three trips to Brazil per year, plans for further soil analysis, bacterial exhumation, and ground-penetrating radar on the crash site.
