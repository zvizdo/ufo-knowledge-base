# 2023 Pacific Time Zone UAP D51

**Date**: March 2023 (night)
**Location**: Pacific Time Zone — adjacent to a national security facility (precise location redacted under 1.4a)
**Witness**: One civilian; observed via personal cellular device

## Observation

A civilian reported a large blue featureless triangular object with a solid, unwavering silhouette emitting powerful "whitish blue" light from multiple points along its perimeter. The object hovered stationary near or above a national security facility for approximately 3 minutes, then moved to a higher position in the observer's field of view, with the motion described as "backing up" in a "jerking" or "jumping" manner inconsistent with smooth jet propulsion. Total observation time: approximately 8 minutes.

The observer noted: no discernible front or rear; no defined flight path; no vapor trail; no apparent data collection capability; did not appear to be a drone.

## Reporting Chain

The incident generated an AFOSI Intelligence Information Report (IIR) classified SECRET//NOFORN. An OUSD(I&S) Information Disclosure Analyst submitted a classification review request; the [[afosi]] OSI CI Collections and Operations PM approved an UNCLASSIFIED derivative classification of the summary — noting it was the first time this pathway was used for an IIR in this context. The full IIR remains classified.
