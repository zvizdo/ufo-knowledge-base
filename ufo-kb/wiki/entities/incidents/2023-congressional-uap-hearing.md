# 2023 Congressional UAP Hearing

House Oversight Subcommittee on National Security, National Security, and Foreign Affairs hearing, July 26, 2023. The three witnesses — [[ryan-graves]], [[david-fravor]], and [[david-grusch]] — represented three distinct testimony types: ongoing routine sightings (Graves), a single high-credibility historical encounter (Fravor), and insider programmatic allegations (Grusch).

## Account

Lines outside the hearing room stretched around a corner with ~500+ people. Inside, the questioning was notably **bipartisan** — members from both sides of the aisle cooperated rather than competing or attacking witnesses, which Graves described as unusual for any congressional hearing. [[ryan-graves]] uses the carrier landing metaphor: "locked in, trying to push away all the doubts and other thoughts."

Both Fravor and Grusch pushed Graves to the front of the lineup before they walked in.

### The Three Testimonies

**[[ryan-graves]]** — Opening statement: "My name is Ryan Fobs Graves and I'm a former F-18 pilot with a decade of service in the US Navy. I have experienced Advanced UAP firsthand and I'm here to voice the concerns of more than 30 commercial air crew and military veterans who have confided their similar encounters with me." Primary content: the [[2014-east-coast-uap]] incident cluster; routine sightings; aviation safety; the cube-in-sphere near-miss; under-reporting.

**[[david-fravor]]** — Primary content: the [[2004-nimitz-tic-tac]] encounter; the most corroborated single-encounter testimony in modern UAP history.

**[[david-grusch]]** — Primary content: a covert crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering program operating outside Congressional oversight; non-human biologics recovered; "I can tell you the location, let's get in a SCIF." The ICIG characterized his complaint as "urgent and credible" and Grusch submitted over 1,000 pages to the inspector general.

## Significance

- First hearing in which a credentialed intelligence insider alleged specific programmatic crash-retrieval activity with specific locational knowledge
- The juxtaposition of firsthand sighting witnesses (Graves, Fravor) alongside a programmatic insider (Grusch) was deliberate and mutually reinforcing
- Bipartisan participation demonstrated UAP is a rare issue where ideological divides collapse: [[jesse-michaels]] notes "AOC and Matt Gates completely sympatico"
- The hearing catalyzed further Congressional legislation and additional whistleblower contact with AARO

## Aftermath

Multiple additional former government officials contacted Congressional oversight after the hearing. The UAP Disclosure Act (post-Nimitz-era iteration) gained momentum, though its review panel provision was removed before passage.
