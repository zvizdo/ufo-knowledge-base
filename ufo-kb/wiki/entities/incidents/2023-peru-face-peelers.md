# 2023 Peru Face Peeler Incursions

A series of nighttime assaults by armoured figures on advanced hover-technology in the Alto Nanai region of the Peruvian Amazon, culminating in at least one attempted surgical face-removal (the "face peeler" phenomenon). Investigated on the ground in summer 2023 by [[tim-alberino]] and Doug Thornton.

## Background

The term *pelacaras* (face peelers) had existed in Alto Nanai folklore since the mid-1980s, used by parents as a boogeyman figure. Since then, sporadic attacks were reported in which victims' faces were partially or wholly removed post-mortem, and bodies were found with harvested organs.

In June–July 2023 the phenomenon intensified dramatically, generating viral social media footage from multiple villages. The primary affected community was **San Antonio de Pintoyaku**, a Kichwa settlement on the Pintoyaku tributary of the Alto Nanai River (approximately two days by boat from Iquitos). Incursions also occurred in surrounding villages and, critically, in the city of **Nauta** (population ~36,000) with an active Peruvian Navy presence.

## Account

### Assailant Description
- Height: consistently estimated at 6.5–8 ft tall (Alberino estimates ~6.5–7 ft conservatively, accounting for possible exaggeration)
- Full-body **black armoured suits**, hermetically covering the body
- Helmets with **almond-shaped tinted eye lenses** (described by multiple witnesses as resembling the "Green Goblin" from Spider-Man); some described yellow lenses, some green — likely night-vision optics
- One witness reported a **silver suit** rather than black; may represent a variant or different personnel class

### Hover Technology
- Primary conveyance: circular **hoverboard discs** attached to the feet; when activated (by touching a boot component), the operator floated ~1 metre off the ground
- Operators "surfed" on the boards; two individuals were sometimes seen sharing a single disc
- 16-gauge birdshot fired at point-blank range had no effect — audible "dinking" as BBs struck armour; one shot knocked an assailant back onto the ground, but he immediately rose and departed
- A **secondary craft** was also reported: acorn/almond-shaped (lying on its side), seen by multiple witnesses independently. Descriptions:
  - Matte black, hovering silently with no visible propulsion at ~30–40 ft altitude
  - One witness described a **translucent version** with visible mesh exterior — he could see two beings inside (one at front, one in the belly); the craft emitted stubby protrusions with blinking lights before moving away with a low-frequency hum

### Key Incident: Talia's Attempted Abduction
A 15-year-old girl named Talia was the subject of the most detailed documented attack:

1. While retrieving a piece of fruit from her backyard near dusk, she felt a gust of wind; looked up to see an armoured assailant on a hoverboard arriving from a nearby gully
2. A second assailant arrived from the front
3. Both grabbed her and lifted her off the ground using the hoverboard tech, carrying her behind a thatch chicken coop
4. One assailant injected something up her nose with a syringe — she became disoriented
5. They applied a numbing cream to her face; one said to the other (in Spanish): **"Be careful. Don't put too much on her face — it'll ruin the flesh"** (to prevent the skin from separating from underlying tissue during surgical removal)
6. They began making an incision with what appeared to be a **laser scalpel**
7. Talia made a last effort to push up the helmet of the assailant restraining her; he released her to pull his helmet down (unwilling to be identified)
8. She screamed; her brother and neighbours arrived immediately (the village was on armed night patrol); the assailants fled upward through the forest canopy
9. Alberino observed a visible scar on her face during his investigation

Talia exhibited severe PTSD and trembled visibly when she first saw Alberino and Doug Thornton, associating them with her attackers.

## Alberino's Field Investigation

In summer 2023, Alberino and former infantry marine / DHS special operator **Doug Thornton** travelled to San Antonio de Pintoyaku after obtaining an official invitation from the village chief (APU). Key details:
- Chartered a river boat from Iquitos; escorted upriver by the APU and village men
- Brought medical/food supplies; also brought night-vision goggles with recording capability, thermal binoculars, high-powered flashlights, and quality radios
- Hired two active-duty Peruvian Navy jungle commandos as security
- Found the entire male population armed, conducting nightly patrols; villagers had not been farming; severe collective PTSD
- Conducted on-camera interviews with the APU, Talia, her father, and numerous other witnesses
- Footage and full investigation published as a YouTube video on Alberino's channel

## Official Response

Two provincial police officers, accompanied by a Navy escort, conducted an investigation before Alberino's visit. They concluded the attacks were perpetrated by **river miners with jetpack technology** attempting to drive villagers off their land.

Alberino and multiple observers noted this explanation is implausible: Peruvian river miners are impoverished, operate rusting dredge boats, and have no access to jetpacks. The Nauta city incursions further refute the "miner harassment" narrative.

The story was widely reported in Peruvian and US media before the jetpack narrative terminated coverage. NewsNation reportedly treated the story dismissively after the official explanation was issued.

## Concurrent Military Activity

**Operation Resolute Sentinel** — a joint military exercise involving the US Marines, Air Force, Navy, Space Force, Coast Guard, UK forces, and multiple South American militaries (Peru, Uruguay, Ecuador, Brazil) — was running simultaneously in Peru.

Alberino speculates this could be coincidental, a cover for an operation to pursue/capture the assailants, or (less preferred) connected to whatever the assailants were doing.

## Interpretation and Links to Other Cases

[[tim-alberino]] links the face-peeler incidents to [[michael-herrera]]'s Indonesia encounter: in both cases, paramilitary actors with advanced aerospace craft appear to be operating independently of — or beneath — national military command structures. Alberino raises the possibility of a **rogue paramilitary entity** with access to reverse-engineered or exotic technology.

The "face peeling" — surgical removal of skin — is compared to cattle mutilation methods (precision incisions, apparent anaesthesia) and to the apparent goal of harvesting biological material.
