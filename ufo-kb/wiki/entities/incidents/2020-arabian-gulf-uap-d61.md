# 2020 Arabian Gulf UAP Formation D61

482 ATKS / 432 AEW ISR aircraft observed a formation of unknown flying objects traveling NE-NW along the Arabian Gulf coast for 2 minutes before losing PID in cloud cover, 27 August 2020.

## Key Facts

- **DTG**: 271527Z–271529Z AUG20 (2 minutes)
- **Platform**: 482 ATKS / 432 AEW; OKAS, Saudi Arabia; redacted sensor type (1.4a SENSOR)
- **Location**: MGRS 39RVM38 (observer); 39RVM81 (formation, coastal)
- **UAP description**: "FORMATION OF FLYING OBJECTS" — "FORMATION OF UNK FLYING OBJECTS" (official activity description)
- **UAP trajectory**: NE-NW along the coast
- **Duration**: 2 minutes; PID lost in cloud cover; aircrew unable to regain PID
- **Guard call**: Iranian Air Defense at 0532Z (PROFESSIONAL tone); no mission impacts
- **Same sortie**: IL-76 CANDID at Abu Musa Island AFLD; Naser-class WAP dead in water
