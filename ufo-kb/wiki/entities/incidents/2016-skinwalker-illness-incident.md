# 2016 Skinwalker Ranch Illness Incident

On **October 14, 2016**, a group of 11 people touring [[skinwalker-ranch]] under [[brandon-fugel]] experienced acute medical effects. The group included a prominent doctor and his security detail, among whom was "George" — described as 6'4", former Hells Angels member.

## Account

While ascending the Mesa, cell phone batteries across the group drained from ~80% to zero simultaneously. The group paused to let their devices recharge.

"George," who had remained standing in the open-air Polaris UTV, was found in an unusual state. When Fugel approached and called his name, George's eyes opened slowly. He looked down and said "whoa, that was weird." He reported that when the group jumped out of the vehicle, he stood up and was immediately frozen, everything went black, and he had no awareness of the intervening time. He asked Fugel how long he had been standing there — it had been approximately 10 minutes.

George described experiencing **scopesthesia** (the sense of being watched or of being the object of predation) before losing consciousness. The following morning he checked himself into a hospital in Las Vegas — he could not walk and was completely immobilized. No biomarkers were found to explain his condition. He was out of commission for **3–4 weeks**.

During the same tour, on the drive back to retrieve their phones, a member of the group spotted a **silver-grey disc-like object** approximately 100 feet above the Mesa, hovering stationary. It then moved ~50 feet instantaneously and accelerated to a dot in the blink of an eye and vanished.

## Biological Effects Discussion

[[brandon-fugel]] noted a pattern: the individuals who experience the most severe biological effects seem to be "alpha male" / aggressive types. He speculated this may be related to [[garry-nolan]]'s [[basal-ganglia-uap-predisposition]] research: Nolan's thesis is that people with higher neural density in the caudate-putamen region are *predisposed* to UAP encounters before any exposure — and may be targeted by the phenomenon for that reason.

## Evidence

- Multi-witness confirmation of battery drain
- Fugel's direct observation of George's 10-minute paralytic episode
- Medical documentation of George's subsequent hospitalization (no biomarkers found — unexplained incapacitation)
- Multiple witnesses to the disc sighting on the same tour (though temporal link to incapacitation is not established)
