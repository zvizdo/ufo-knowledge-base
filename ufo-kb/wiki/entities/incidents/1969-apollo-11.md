# Apollo 11 (1969)

First crewed lunar landing mission. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed on the Moon on July 20, 1969; Michael Collins orbited above. Primary source: official Technical Crew Debriefing (July 31, 1969, CONFIDENTIAL / GROUP 4, declassified ~1981).

## Documented Anomalous Observations (Primary Source — Debriefing)

**1. Unidentified object in translunar coast (~1 day from Moon)**: All three crew observed an object of "sizeable dimension" through a monocular. S-IVB was confirmed at 6,000 miles — ruled out. Shape appeared different depending on observation method:

- Naked eye: "L shape" (Aldrin), "like an open suitcase" (Armstrong)
- Sextant out-of-focus: "cylinder" / "two connected rings" (Aldrin/Armstrong)
- Sextant focused: "hollow cylinder — you could look right down in its guts. It was tumbling." (Collins)
- Sextant refocused: "replaced by this open-book shape. It was really weird." (Collins)

No conclusion reached. Collins: "We really don't have a conclusion as to what it might have been, how big it was, or how far away it was. It was something that wasn't part of the urine dump." Collins tentatively proposed spacecraft debris (Mylar, high gain antenna part) but the observed shape didn't match.

**2. Cabin light flashes during coast**: Aldrin observed flashes ~1/min in the darkened cabin, throughout transit. Later observed double flashes "separated by maybe a foot" and "a line with no direction of motion." Armstrong (~50 observations in final night): suggested "a neutron or some kind of an atomic particle that would be in the visible spectrum." Now recognized as cosmic ray phosphenes.

**3. Bright light in lunar orbit** (approaching CDH burn): Aldrin observed a bright light source as Earth rose above the lunar horizon; initially unidentified. Later revised to probable Sun reflection off a lake.

## UAP Connection (Hearsay — Not in Debriefing)

Additional hearsay claims circulate:
- Armstrong's alleged oblique "truth's protective layers" speech (1994) — see [[neil-armstrong]]
- Ham radio operators claiming transmissions were cut — unverified
- Claims of Armstrong's post-mission reclusiveness and anomalous statements

> ⚠ Conflict: The hearsay claims are not in the official debriefing. The debriefing's unidentified translunar object is a primary-source observation with no conclusion; it does not confirm NHI contact.
