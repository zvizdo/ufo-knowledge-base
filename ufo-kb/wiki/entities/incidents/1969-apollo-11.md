# Apollo 11 (1969)

First crewed lunar landing mission. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed on the Moon on July 20, 1969; Michael Collins orbited above. Armstrong's famous "small step" broadcast was watched by ~600 million people.

## UAP Connection

Multiple claims circulate about anomalous aspects of the mission:
- Armstrong's alleged oblique public statements about what they saw.
- Michael Collins's *Carrying the Fire* account contradicting other astronaut statements about lunar features.
- Ham radio operators claiming to have heard transmissions cut from official record.
- Claims that Armstrong was deeply affected by what he saw and became increasingly reclusive.

> ⚠ Conflict: These claims are largely hearsay; no firsthand Armstrong statement explicitly confirms UAP contact. Moon-landing skeptics (Bart Sibrel school) dispute the mission itself — a separate claim not endorsed by most UAP researchers.
