# 1985–1986 Brewster, New York UFO Flap

A multi-night UFO wave centered in Brewster, New York (southeast New York State) in approximately 1985–1986. Reported in local newspapers and on television weekly. The event is notable in this KB because it was the formative sighting experience of [[charles-buhler]], which he credits as the origin of his career direction toward exotic propulsion research.

## The Sightings

Multiple eyewitnesses reported a formation of **six bright white lights**, silent, hovering at treetop level, moving slowly without conventional aircraft sound or structural features. Convoys of cars from neighboring areas drove to Brewster to observe the lights. The phenomenon continued **over multiple nights** (possibly several days to weeks) with the lights appearing in different parts of the town each night.

At least one eyewitness described a triangular configuration: *"I looked up and right over my head virtually, there was a rim of lights in the shape of a triangle... it was anywhere from wingtip to wingtip about 50 or 60 yards."*

Separate from the lights, Buhler also reported hearing of **two individuals in 1920s-era formal dress** (consistent with the "Men in Black" typology) who appeared in the area telling children: *"If you see something weird, just close your eyes and tell it to land."* This detail was not widely documented and Buhler notes that "Men in Black" as a concept was not yet publicly known in the early 1980s.

## Significance

[[charles-buhler]] was approximately 11–12 years old at the time. He observed the formation of lights at close range during a Halloween haunted-house event at his family's garage in Brewster. A friend pointed the lights out to him; he watched silent white lights hovering above the trees, tracking slowly along the road in formation with no sound. The experience was his first encounter with the unexplained and, by his account, the direct catalyst for his career interest in physics and exotic propulsion.

Buhler later learned that [[thomas-townsend-brown]] had a similar formative experience — a glowing orb that approached him at Catalina Island as a teenager — and that Brown said this orb encounter was the source of all his subsequent electrogravitics discoveries.

## Corroboration

The flap was covered in local newspapers and on television, with multiple named eyewitnesses interviewed. Buhler recommends searching "Brewster, New York, 1985 or 1986" to locate contemporary press coverage. The sighting is consistent with the broader Hudson Valley UFO wave of the mid-1980s, which generated significant multi-witness documentation in New York State.
