# 2019 USS Omaha USO Incident

In July 2019, the USS Omaha (a combat ship) tracked an unidentified spherical object off the Southern California coast. The object descended into the ocean and was not recovered. [[jeremy-corbell]] released the video in May 2021, triggering renewed attention to transmedium UAP.

[[tim-gallaudet]] cites the USS Omaha video as one of his leading examples of USO evidence. He gave [[victor-vescovo]] the encounter coordinates; Vescovo surveyed the seafloor at those coordinates with state-of-the-art multibeam sonar as part of his shakedown cruise, though his submersible was not available for a visual dive.
