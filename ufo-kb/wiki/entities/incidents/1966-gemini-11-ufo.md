# Gemini 11 UFO Encounter (1966)

During the Gemini 11 mission (September 1966, Pete Conrad commander), NASA transcripts record an anomalous sequence: an object flew over the spacecraft and the crew's battery "stack Charlie" drained. This follows the classic UAP power-interference pattern seen in maritime and nuclear contexts.

A separate artifact: in Scott Simkinson's NASA personal archive, a lithograph was found labeled "Strange object as seen by Gemini 11 astronauts." Kevin Knuth assessed this lithograph as a commemorative mock-up rather than an actual photograph, with important implications.

## Account

From NASA's mission transcripts: the crew reported "something just fly over you" — Houston replied "affirmative." Shortly after, battery stack C (Charlie) was reported drained. Approximately 6 minutes of transcript appear to have no record.

The sequence mirrors the classic pattern: UAP observed → power anomaly follows.

## The Simkinson Lithograph

[[scott-simkinson]] (described by Knuth as possibly "the first engineer hired by NASA") maintained a personal archive of NASA files with his secretary Emily Erdle. After their deaths, the archive was auctioned; Ed Wilson purchased it and found a lithograph labeled "Strange object as seen by Gemini 11 astronauts."

Lithograph characteristics (Kevin Knuth's analysis):
- A printed photograph (lithograph = dot-matrix printing like a comic book)
- Shows Earth with a specific cloud pattern in the background and a structured UAP object
- Knuth found the **original photograph** in University of Arizona archives: same Earth/cloud background, **no UFO present**
- Conclusion: the lithograph is a **mock-up or artist's rendition** of what the astronauts reported seeing, not a captured photograph
- The tether experiment (depicted in the Earth background) occurred **~one orbit (~one hour) before** the actual UFO encounter — the lithograph's background is from the wrong time

The lithograph inscription says "Strange object as seen by Gemini 11 astronauts." If it's a deliberate mock-up, it implies that someone inside NASA (Simkinson, or those around him) knew the encounter was real and created a record of it — just not via official channels.

Knuth: "If there's a painting of a UFO in that photo and it's found in his records, that implies there's almost institutional NASA knowledge of the fact that the Gemini 11 astronauts did see a UFO."

## Omission from Official Record

Not mentioned in the NASA UAP Commission report. Knuth lists this alongside the Skylab encounter and Musgrave's "snakes" as examples of documented astronaut encounters ignored by the official inquiry.
