# 2026 McCasland Disappearance

## Account

On February 27, 2026, retired Air Force Major General [[neil-mccasland]] disappeared from his home on Quail Run Court in Albuquerque, New Mexico, at the edge of the Cibola National Forest.

**Timeline:**
- ~10:00 AM — repairman sees McCasland at home
- ~11:10 AM — wife Susan leaves for a medical appointment (for anxiety, sleep, and memory issues that both were seeing a doctor for)
- ~12:04 PM — Susan returns; McCasland is gone
- Left behind: prescription glasses, phone (switched off), smartwatch
- Missing: red backpack, wallet, .38 caliber revolver with holster
- 3:07 PM — Susan files a missing person report
- Next day — Silver Alert issued

**Context:**
- McCasland had cycled 60 miles the prior week; regularly hiked the Sandia foothills; knew every trail
- Susan stated he had said things like "if his brain and body keep deteriorating, he didn't want to live like that" — but framed as venting, not a threat
- His physician confirmed: intelligent and capable, not confused or disoriented

**Search:** The Bernalillo County Sheriff's Office, FBI Albuquerque field office, Air Force OSI, New Mexico State Search and Rescue, Albuquerque Mountain Rescue, horseback teams, three dog types, drones, and helicopters were all deployed. Surveillance footage from both ends of his street showed no confirmed direction of travel despite the era of ubiquitous cameras.

**Only potential evidence:** A gray Air Force sweatshirt found a mile east of his house; DNA testing could not confirm it was McCasland's.

**Timing:** 8 days after President Trump posted on Truth Social directing the Pentagon and other agencies to begin identifying and releasing UAP files.

## Witnesses

- [[susan-mccasland]] — wife; filed the report; provided all known context; her Facebook statement on Roswell is the only cryptic public disclosure

## Aftermath

As of April 2026: no confirmed sightings, no body, no resolution. One of the most intensive missing-person searches in New Mexico's recent history produced next to nothing.
