# 2008 Iraq Gimbal Video

Thermal video recorded by a US Marine operating a LAV-25 (Light Armored Vehicle) thermal gunsight during overwatch/reconnaissance operations in Anbar province, Iraq, approximately 100 miles from the Syrian border in 2008. The video came to public attention via a Jeremy Corbel interview with the Marine who recorded it.

## Account

The witness described the object as rising off the desert floor, ascending approximately 25 meters, then moving approximately 30–50 meters to his left, returning to center, moving about 30 meters to his right, returning to center, and then slowly climbing. He began recording the thermal sight screen at that point. The footage shows a bright oval/disc-shaped object with what appears to be a rotating or morphing shape.

## The Gimbal Visual Comparison

[[marik-von-rennenkampff]] presents this video as visual corroboration for the [[2015-gimbal]] morphology: the Iraq video predates the USS Roosevelt encounters by ~7 years and was recorded by a different sensor type in a completely different operational context, yet shows the same characteristic oval/disc shape. If both videos are camera artifacts, Merrick argues it would require the same artifact to appear independently in two different sensor systems, years apart.

[[mick-west]] responds that: (1) the comparison mixes white-hot and black-hot thermal modes with very different contrast curves; (2) the video is very low quality with heavy compression; (3) the most likely explanation is landing lights of an aircraft, with the motion being camera vibration from the vehicle engine.

West notes a possible higher-quality version of the footage was uploaded by a UFO-focused Twitter account to mega.co.nz, which might resolve some ambiguities.

## Significance

The Iraq video is structurally interesting because it is a non-Navy, non-US-coastal encounter that produces a visually similar signature. If genuine, it broadens the geographic and operational context of the observed phenomenon beyond the USS Roosevelt theater. The witness retained their military clearance and there is no indication of disciplinary action following the report.
