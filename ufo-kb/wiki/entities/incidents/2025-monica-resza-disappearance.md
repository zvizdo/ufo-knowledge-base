# 2025 Monica Resza Disappearance

## Account

On June 22, 2025, [[monica-resza]] was hiking near Mount Waterman in the Angeles National Forest with a group of companions. She was approximately 30 feet behind the group when she disappeared — no sound, no struggle, no indication of what happened. When companions looked back, she was simply gone.

**Search and rescue:**
- 8 days of official SAR by land and air (LA County Sheriff's Department, Montrose Search and Rescue)
- Only item recovered: her beanie, found ~400 yards off the trail (photographs show it tucked into her pack's hip belt that morning, meaning it came loose or was placed there)
- After 8 days: official SAR mission concluded; case transferred to the homicide bureau / missing persons unit
- Volunteer groups (including expert mountain rescue teams) continued searching beyond the official perimeter for months
- Last volunteer Facebook group entry: November 18, 2025 — 150 days after her disappearance — still planning expeditions

**Physical environment:**
- Terrain is steep but civilian searchers described it as "not steep enough to be fatal if someone fell"
- No mountain lion or bear den found in the area
- San Gabriel Mountains is mountain lion country — but no evidence of animal predation

**Alternative scenarios discussed:**
- Mountain lion/bear attack — no den or remains found
- Fall/injury — terrain not described as fatal; no body in likely zones
- Abduction/intercept — conceivable via trail exit to a vehicle on a different highway section
- No conclusion is definitive

## Aftermath

150+ days without resolution. The volunteer search targeted a recovery, not a rescue, by the end. No charges filed. Case remains open.
