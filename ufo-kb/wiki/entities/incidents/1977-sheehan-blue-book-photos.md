# 1977 — Sheehan Viewing of Classified Blue Book Photos

## Account

In January 1977, [[danny-sheehan]] was working as special legal counsel to the Congressional Research Service (Science and Technology Division, directed by [[marsha-smith]]) on a UAP study commissioned by incoming President [[jimmy-carter]]. Carter, who had personally seen a UFO in 1969 with 11 other witnesses, arrived at the White House intending to pursue genuine disclosure.

The CRS study required access to [[project-blue-book]] records held in the National Archives. Sheehan was granted access to files in the **basement of the Madison Building** of the National Archives in Washington D.C.

While reviewing files on a microfiche machine, Sheehan came across a set of classified photos that had not been included in the publicly released Blue Book materials:

- A **saucer-shaped craft approximately 40 feet wide**, embedded in a **snow-covered embankment**, at an angle as if it had struck the hillside.
- **U.S. Air Force personnel** visible in the photos surrounding the craft — confirming the scene was an official U.S. military recovery operation.
- A **dome on the upper surface** of the saucer, around the base of which were **a series of symbols** of unknown origin/language.
- Sheehan reports that he **traced the symbols onto the back of a legal pad** — his only documentation of what he saw, as he had no camera.

The symbols have never been published. Sheehan has not released or discussed the tracing in detail in public.

## Context: Why He Had Access

- Sheehan was acting as counsel to the CRS study, which gave him government-document access for research purposes.
- The photos were apparently stored in a classified section of Blue Book's records — not destroyed, as publicly stated, but retained in the Archives.
- The Carter administration's commission to study UAP gave Sheehan the legal pretext to request and access the classified materials.

## Significance

This is one of the most specific firsthand accounts of a physical crash-retrieval photograph in the public record:
- Unlike other accounts (which are typically secondhand or describe document summaries), Sheehan claims to have **personally seen the photographs** and describes specific visual details (40 ft diameter, snow, AF personnel, dome symbols).
- The tracing of symbols is a unique corroborating detail — it is the kind of specific, retrievable artifact that distinguishes genuine firsthand accounts from constructed narratives.
- The classified section of Blue Book records apparently survived intact in the National Archives, contrary to the official position that Blue Book was fully released.

## Open Questions

- What became of Sheehan's traced symbols? He has never published them.
- Were these photos subsequently found or removed from the Archives? No independent corroboration of their existence has emerged.
- The snow-covered embankment suggests a cold-weather location — possibly a northern U.S./Canadian incident not in the public Blue Book record.
