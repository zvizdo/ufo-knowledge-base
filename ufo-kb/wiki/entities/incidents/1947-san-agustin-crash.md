# 1947 Plains of San Agustín Crash

A second, largely separate crash retrieval event alleged to have occurred the **morning of July 4, 1947**, approximately 100 miles west of Roswell on the Plains of San Agustín, New Mexico. [[john-brandenberg]] relayed these accounts from firsthand veteran witnesses he spoke with at a conference called "Reminiscences of Roswell."

## Account

Per Brandenburg's veterans:

- The morning of July 4, 1947, an object crashed on the Plains of San Agustín — a flat, remote high-altitude basin in western New Mexico.
- The object was described as a **saucer with a large hole in its side** (contrasting with the Roswell debris field, which was a scattered explosion of wreckage from something that apparently broke up in midair).
- **Multiple dead alien bodies** were recovered; coyotes and vultures had reached some of the bodies before military teams arrived.
- **One live alien** was recovered.
- A team of **archaeologists** investigating nearby Indian ruins were present and witnessed the recovery.
- **Civilian campers** were also present.
- A **red-haired colonel** arrived the morning of July 4 — screaming orders, commanding the recovery operation, and threatening civilian witnesses: *"If they told anybody about what they had seen, the government was going to kill them and their families too."* This colonel later appeared at Roswell Army Air Field for the second phase of the cleanup.

## Relation to the Roswell Debris Field

Brandenburg frames these as two separate but simultaneous events:
1. **Plains of San Agustín** (July 4 morning): intact saucer with hole in the side; bodies and live alien recovered.
2. **Debris field near Roswell** (slightly later, discovered by Mac Brazel): scattered aerial explosion of wreckage; no bodies described; became the public "Roswell Incident."

The appearance of the same red-haired colonel at both sites suggests a unified recovery operation — one team handling both locations in sequence.

## Brandenburg's Interpretation

Brandenburg speculates the craft were monitoring the nuclear weapons stockpile at Roswell (the 509th Bomb Group stored all five of Earth's nuclear weapons there). During a severe lightning storm, the extremely sensitive electromagnetic monitoring equipment on the craft was overwhelmed by natural and man-made EM interference. The P-61 Black Widow night fighters patrolling the area — using vacuum tube technology impervious to the same interference — may have been involved in the encounter.

## Witness Intimidation

The red-haired colonel's threat ("kill them and their families") reflects wartime security culture: less than two years after World War II, "loose lips sink ships" remained the operating norm. The same colonel took over Roswell Army Air Field for approximately a week before disappearing with all recovered material.
