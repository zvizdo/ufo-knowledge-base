# 1965 Gemini 7 Bogey

**Date**: December 5, 1965, approximately 4 hours 24 minutes into the mission
**Location**: Low Earth Orbit
**Witnesses**: [[frank-borman]] (CDR) and [[james-lovell]] (Pilot)

## Observation

Frank Borman called out "A BOGEY AT TEN O'CLOCK HIGH." Mission Control asked "is that the booster or is that a natural sighting?" Borman confirmed it was separate: "WE HAVE DEBRIS UP HERE — THIS IS AN ACTUAL SIGHTING."

Three objects were simultaneously visible:
- The **bogey** (unidentified): 10 o'clock high position
- The **Titan II booster**: 2 o'clock position; tumbling slowly; described by Lovell as "a brilliant body in the sun against a black background with trillions of particles on it"
- A **particle cloud**: "hundreds of little particles" 3–4 miles to the left, heading into polar orbit

## Resolution

The PAO commentary refers to "the third and unidentified object" as "a brown bogey." No further identification was provided. The tape was specifically dubbed and released because it contained UAP references.
