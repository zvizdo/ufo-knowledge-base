# 1957 Antonio Villas Boas Abduction

**Date**: Night of 15–16 October 1957  
**Location**: São Francisco de Sales, Minas Gerais, Brazil  
**Witness**: [[antonio-villas-boas]]

## Account

While plowing fields at night, [[antonio-villas-boas]] was approached by an oval craft with a rotating upper section. Short beings in gray suits and helmets took him aboard, stripped him, covered him in a liquid substance, and drew blood. A female entity then entered — described as fully human anatomically, with slightly elevated cheekbones, a pointier chin, wide-set large blue eyes, and platinum blonde hair. Sexual intercourse occurred twice. Before he was returned, she gestured toward her abdomen and then toward the sky.

## Evidence

- Medical examination by Dr. Olavo Fontes documented radiation-exposure-consistent symptoms: nausea, fatigue, skin sores, leg lesions
- Case was investigated and published by Fontes and journalist Joao Martins
- Villas Boas maintained his account consistently until his death

## Aftermath

The case was suppressed for years (not published until 1964 in the Flying Saucer Review) due to its sexual content. It became foundational in abductee literature as the first documented sexual/reproductive contact case.

## Significance

[[michael-p-masters]] uses this as the **earliest evidence for reproductive compatibility** between humans and UAP entities — arguing that sexual intercourse capable of producing viable offspring is only possible between the same species, supporting the [[future-humans-hypothesis]] over an extraterrestrial model.
