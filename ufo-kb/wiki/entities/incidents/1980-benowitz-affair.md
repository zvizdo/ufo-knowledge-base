# The Benowitz Affair (c. 1980–1984)

The deliberate Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI) disinformation campaign against civilian UFO researcher [[paul-benowitz]], widely considered the most thoroughly documented intelligence operation against a UFO investigator in US history. The operation is the subject of *[[project-beta]]* (2005) by [[greg-bishop]].

## Origins

[[paul-benowitz]] was an Albuquerque electronics businessman (owner of Thunder Scientific, manufacturer of precision temperature/humidity instruments) who lived and worked immediately adjacent to [[kirtland-afb]]. He was a member of [[apro]] (Aerial Phenomena Research Organization) and held a private pilot's license with aerobatic flying experience.

Beginning around 1977–1978, Benowitz observed anomalous glowing orbs lifting off from inside the Kirtland base perimeter, flying over a mountain, and descending on the other side. He filmed them with 8mm cameras and later with early video equipment. He also built elaborate radio-intercept equipment to detect what he believed were alien communications.

When Benowitz contacted the Air Force to report what he was seeing, they did not dismiss him — they invited him to share his findings, because they needed to know exactly what a civilian could detect from base perimeter. (Greg Bishop, [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])

## The Operation

The AFOSI operation was orchestrated by retired CIA Soviet specialist [[harry-rositzki]] ("Falcon") and executed primarily by AFOSI counter-intelligence agent [[rick-doty]] with the participation of UFO researcher [[bill-moore]] as a civilian asset.

**Phase 1 — Surveillance and contact**: AFOSI identified Benowitz as a potential security concern (what could he detect? who was he sharing information with?) and a potential vehicle for counterintelligence. They maintained contact under the guise of cooperation.

**Phase 2 — Disinformation seeding**: Moore was instructed to deliver the "Aquarius document" to Benowitz — a fabricated government memo that first used the term "MJ-12" ("MJ Telve") and validated some of Benowitz's beliefs. Moore later said he told Benowitz to "take it with a grain of salt" and hid the handover in a supply closet with a radio playing to mask their conversation.

**Phase 3 — The computer**: [[j-allen-hynek]] apparently delivered a pre-built computer to Benowitz's door. The computer, according to Bill Moore (relayed by Bishop), was programmed — likely by NSA — with fabricated alien communications. Operatives across the street piped signals into it at the same frequency Benowitz was monitoring. Benowitz began "decoding" these signals as alien messages: that aliens had given the US eight craft, that an alien invasion was planned, that Earth women were needed to propagate a dying alien race.

**Phase 4 — Fake video**: A CRT display connected to the computer showed garbled video footage — likely old movie clips of dinosaurs and Roman battle scenes — which Benowitz interpreted as windows into the alien planet and Earth's past/future. [[gabe-valdez]] personally saw this setup at Benowitz's home.

**Phase 5 — Dulce redirection**: AFOSI flew Benowitz by helicopter to Archeleta Mesa near [[dulce-new-mexico]], staging the site with props (jeeps, water tanks) and encouraging him to concentrate his research there rather than at Kirtland. A real crash (likely a classified drone or stealth aircraft) had occurred in the area, giving the redirection additional verisimilitude.

## Result

Benowitz's family became alarmed by his deteriorating state — sleeplessness, chain-smoking, paranoid ideation, armed standoffs with visitors. They committed him to a psychiatric facility for approximately one to two months. He was discharged, recovered to a functional baseline, and continued to be interested in the subject but it no longer dominated his life. He did not die in a mental institution (a persistent false rumor). His sons continued to operate Thunder Scientific after his death. (Greg Bishop, [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])

## Bill Moore's Confession

At the 1989 [[mufon]] Symposium, Moore publicly admitted his role in the operation. He said he had allowed himself to get involved because he believed he could separate the disinformation from the genuine material. Bishop, who has known Moore for 35+ years, defends his overall credibility while acknowledging the moral dimension of his participation.

## Scale and Context

Moore told Bishop: "Imagine the Benowitz operation as a 24-hour long stage play. Benowitz is five minutes in one scene — not even five minutes." The operation was one small node in a vast Cold War counter-intelligence effort; the only reason it became famous in UFO circles is that it intersected with the UFO research community. (Greg Bishop, [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])

## Lasting Effects

The operation substantially generated or amplified:
- The [[dulce-underground-base]] mythology (via Myrna Hansen's regression + AFOSI amplification + John Lear's admitted fabrications)
- The first written appearance of "MJ-12" (in the fake Aquarius document)
- The template for using the UFO subject as an intelligence tool — documented and named for future analysis
