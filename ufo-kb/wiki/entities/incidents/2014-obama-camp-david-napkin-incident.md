# Obama Camp David Napkin Incident

A documented chain in which [[tim-taylor]] allegedly conveyed a Camp David **presidential-seal napkin** to [[chris-bledsoe]] after dining with President Barack Obama. The napkin is interpreted as evidence that Bledsoe's prophecies were transmitted into the highest level of the US executive branch.

## The Photo

[[grant-cameron]] posted a photo to Facebook showing an envelope addressed to [[chris-bledsoe]] alongside a napkin partially obscuring the address. [[bob-maguire]] identified two elements:

1. **The address** — Chris Bledsoe's address.
2. **The napkin** — bore the **presidential seal of the United States** from Camp David.

Copying the presidential seal is a federal crime; the napkins exist only at Camp David and can only be sent by someone with access to the White House compound.

## The Taylor Chain

Per Bob Maguire's reasoning: the only person who could have both the napkin and knowledge of Bledsoe's story is Tim Taylor — who had the access and was the documented conduit between the Bledsoe family and Washington intelligence networks.

In [[bM18PJY6_Zc]], Bledsoe confirmed firsthand that Taylor told him directly:

> *"I was at the dinner table with the president."*

Taylor handed him the napkin at Christmas, just back from Camp David. The napkin chain therefore runs: **Obama dinner at Camp David → Taylor → Bledsoe (by hand, Christmas)**.

## Significance

- The strongest single artifact tying Bledsoe's prophecies to a named sitting US President.
- Independent corroboration of Taylor's institutional reach beyond NASA into Camp David / White House access.
- The Christmas handoff timing rules out a mail-only delivery: Taylor brought it back personally, which means he was physically present at Camp David in the relevant window.
- The pattern matches Taylor's other custodian-layer behaviors (Vatican archive access without escort, multi-agency credentials, Roswell material handling).
