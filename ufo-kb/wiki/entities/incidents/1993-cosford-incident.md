# 1993 Cosford Incident

A **wave of UAP sightings** in 1993, investigated by [[nick-pope]] during his tenure at the [[uk-ministry-of-defence]] UFO desk. Pope describes it as the "fringe encounter that still kind of troubles me to this day." The case is notable not as a single event but as a cluster of simultaneously occurring, independently anomalous witnesses.

## Witness Account A — Car Chase

A man "was so stunned by this huge triangular shaped craft" that he "leapt into his car and started trying to chase it with his family in the back screaming at him to stop."

Craft characteristics:
- **Shape**: enormous triangular
- **Reaction**: caused immediate, impulsive response — witness abandoned rational behavior

## Witness Account B — Farmer and Cattle Reaction

A farmer observed the craft at extremely low altitude over his field — "so low he thought it maybe it landed." He walked to the field and found "all the cows in the field were standing in a circle like facing each other." Pope: "straight out of the X-Files."

The cattle behavioral anomaly (animals forming geometric patterns after UAP proximity) is a recurring motif in UAP literature. The craft apparently hovered very low before departing.

## Witness Account C — London Art Shop (MIB-Adjacent)

This account is the most anomalous. During the wave, a central London art shop received a visit from "a whole bunch of normal-looking people":

- Browsed for an extended period without clear purpose
- Approached the counter and made "a big show" of buying a single pencil — an unusual level of ceremony for a trivial purchase
- Paid with a **very high denomination bank note**
- When given change, "they looked confused like they didn't understand the concept of getting their change back"
- The shop attendant was so struck that she phoned someone immediately and said "There are aliens in my shop"

### Aftermath
Pope "got to know this woman quite well" through follow-up contact. She subsequently reported:
- **Missing time** — periods she could not account for
- The experience as "more of a time slip"
- "Seeing somebody that looked like herself from the future"

The money-confusion detail is consistent with documented MIB behavioral patterns in other cases — MIB figures repeatedly reported unable to understand basic currency or commercial transactions.

## Significance

Pope described this cluster as persistent in troubling him despite his years of investigation — it does not resolve into a conventional explanation. The triangular craft is consistent with many 1990s UK sightings; the cattle circle and London MIB incident represent the "high strangeness" Pope argues is systematically under-reported.
