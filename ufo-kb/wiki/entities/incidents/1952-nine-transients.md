# 1952 Nine Transients (VASCO)

A finding from the [[vasco]] project (Vanishing & Appearing Sources during a Century of Observations), described publicly by [[beatriz-villarroel]] in [[lbGE3EC6StE]]. While comparing 1950s Mount Palomar plates to modern Pan-STARRS imagery, Villarroel and her collaborators identified two statistically significant clusters of transient point sources — objects that appear in one image and are gone in the next — concentrated in narrow regions of sky on **July 19 and July 27, 1952**. These two dates coincide exactly with the two weekends of the [[1952-washington-dc-flyover]].

## Account

- **July 19, 1952**: Three point sources discovered by [[enrique-solano]] (VASCO collaborator) appearing in close spatial arrangement, then absent in subsequent images.
- **July 27, 1952**: Five point sources arranged in a narrow band — statistically anomalous spatial alignment.
- A 10.4-meter Gran Telescopio Canarias optical follow-up at all positions found *nothing* — no faint counterpart, no expected stellar remnant.
- Cross-checked by satellite expert: **pre-Sputnik** by 5 years, so artificial-satellite reflection or space debris cannot explain the signal.

- Statistical alignment analysis (developed with Lash) for narrow-band positional anomaly significance

## Aftermath

The VASCO finding sits in unresolved tension with conventional astrophysics: there is no known natural mechanism for multiple simultaneous transients in a small spatial region pre-Sputnik. Villarroel's framing in [[lbGE3EC6StE]] is that this is a candidate signature of pre-1957 reflective artifacts in Earth orbit — i.e. potential evidence of non-human probes — and serves as the starting point for her [[exoprobe]] project.

In 2021 Villarroel published the formal paper with [[geoff-marcy]] as collaborator. **The same week the paper was accepted**, the National Academy of Sciences voted to expel Marcy; *Science* and *Nature* published critical coverage of his continued collaborations. Villarroel and [[jesse-michaels]] both note the timing as suspicious in [[lbGE3EC6StE]].
