# IM1 — Interstellar Meteor 1

The first confirmed interstellar object to impact Earth. Designated IM1 (Interstellar Meteor 1). Impacted in the Bismarck Sea off Papua New Guinea in **2014**. Its interstellar origin — confirmed by Space Force trajectory data — makes it the first physical material of confirmed interstellar origin ever recovered from Earth.

## Discovery and Confirmation

- US Space Force data confirmed the object's interstellar trajectory — it was moving too fast and on the wrong trajectory to be a solar system object
- The object entered the atmosphere and impacted in the ocean; its heat-of-entry behavior was anomalous: it burned up **more slowly than expected**, implying a higher material hardness than typical meteorites (not iron composition)
- The impact location (Bismarck Sea, off Papua New Guinea) was estimated using trajectory data

## Avi Loeb's Expedition (2023)

[[avi-loeb]]'s [[galileo-project]] team funded and conducted an ocean expedition to recover fragments from the impact site. The method: magnetic sled dragged across the seafloor to collect metallic particles.

Key findings from recovered material:
- Material assessed as **not of earthly origin**
- Contains **isotope ratios never found in natural Earth materials**
- The combination of anomalous hardness and non-natural isotope ratios led Loeb to call it extraterrestrial material

Loeb is planning a second expedition (April 2025) using an **ROV** to search the seafloor for larger undissipated fragments; if the anomalous material is confirmed to be a manufactured object, Loeb has joked he will "push the button."

## Tim Gallaudet's Role

[[tim-gallaudet]] assisted Loeb's team in two ways:
1. Provided **ocean current and atmospheric data** to model where the debris field would have drifted in the ocean after impact — helping constrain the search area for the magnetic sled expedition
2. Connected Loeb with a NOAA/NASA team that had previously done magnetic sled meteorite recovery in the Olympic Coast National Marine Sanctuary — providing methodological precedent and potential expertise

## Significance

If IM1's recovered material is confirmed to be of artificial manufacture (not just natural but exotic interstellar material), this would represent the first physical evidence of interstellar technology. Loeb treats this as an empirical research question. The UAP connection: Loeb's search for interstellar probes in the Galileo Project's mandate includes looking for objects like IM1 — whether natural or artificial.

## Open Questions

- Are the isotope ratios found in IM1 material consistent with natural nucleosynthesis processes, or do they require an industrial origin?
- Will Loeb's ROV expedition find larger fragments that retain identifiable structure?
- Are there other IM-class interstellar impactors in existing ocean/land records that have not been recognized as such?
