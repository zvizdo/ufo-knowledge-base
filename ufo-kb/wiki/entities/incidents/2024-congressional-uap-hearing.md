# 2024 Congressional UAP Hearing (Rayburn)

Congressional UAP oversight hearing held at Rayburn House Office Building in November 2024, chaired by [[nancy-mace]]. [[michael-shellenberger]] testified and had just published the [[immaculate-constellation]] report the same day, tweeting it immediately before entering the building. [[lue-elizondo]] also testified. The hearing is described as the follow-up to the July 26, 2023 hearing (see [[2023-congressional-uap-hearing]]).

This hearing is Shellenberger's 13th Congressional testimony across multiple issue areas. He characterizes the atmosphere as unusually bipartisan — every committee member on both sides acknowledging that UAPs represent a real and serious issue, with no uncomfortable laughter or character assassination.

## Context

The hearing was held shortly after the 2024 presidential election — Donald Trump had been elected but had not yet taken office (described as "president elect"). Shellenberger and Michaels discuss Trump's potential role in disclosure, and Shellenberger expresses that if Trump does not produce disclosure in the next couple of years, his optimism would shift to pessimism.

## Key Moments

- Shellenberger entered the hearing with 15 members of the public admitted
- He had just tweeted the Immaculate Constellation report, framing it as a live document drop accompanying his testimony
- [[clay-higgins]] attempted to elicit source details during questioning; Shellenberger refused to narrow the universe of who his sources might be
- [[eric-burlison]] asked about biologics and human genetics manipulation with non-human material
- [[nancy-mace]] expressed urgency about showing movement on UAP progress

## Significance

The hearing marks the transition from the 2023 Grusch-era hearings (which established that a retrieval program allegedly exists) toward policy pressure on the incoming Trump administration. Shellenberger describes it as a strategic pivot: the UAP movement should focus less on proving NHI exists and more on demanding transparency, regardless of what the phenomena turn out to be.
