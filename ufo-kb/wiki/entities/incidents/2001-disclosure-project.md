# 2001 Disclosure Project

On May 9, 2001, [[steven-greer]] organized a press conference at the National Press Club in Washington D.C. — the most prominent credentialed-witness UAP testimony event held before the 2023 Congressional hearings. Twenty-one witnesses testified, with hundreds more ready to testify before Congress under oath (Greer's organization claimed to have collected 400+ sworn testimonies).

## Key Witnesses

- **[[mark-mccandish]]** — Presented detailed technical drawings of the "Flux Liner" ([[alien-reproduction-vehicles]]) based on Brad Sorensen's [[1988-norton-afb]] account. This was the most technically detailed ARV account in the public record.
- **[[clifford-stone]]** — Claimed to have worked as a "translator" during crash retrievals, with access to a 57-species entity catalog maintained by the US government
- **[[robert-salas]]** — Described UAPs disabling nuclear missiles during the [[1967-oscar-flight]] incident
- **[[danny-sheehan]]** — Legal context for witness protections; connected the event to constitutional and international law frameworks

## Significance

The event was significant for several reasons:
- First large-scale attempt to move from individual anecdotes to collective witness testimony in a formal setting
- Demonstrated that credentialed insiders (military, government) were willing to go on record
- The ARV / Flux Liner testimony (McCandish) remains the most technically specific public account of alleged reverse-engineered craft
- Established Greer's CSETI/Disclosure Project as the primary pre-TTSA civilian disclosure vehicle

## Limitations

- Organized by [[steven-greer]], whose subsequent CSETI claims (CE-5, alien contact protocols) are widely disputed even within the UAP community
- No Congressional testimony followed, despite promises of hundreds of witnesses
- Some witnesses' accounts were not independently corroborated
