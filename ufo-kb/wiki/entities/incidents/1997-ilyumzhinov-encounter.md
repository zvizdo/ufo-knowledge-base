# 1997 Ilyumzhinov Alien Encounter

The most institutionally significant public contactee claim in the post-Cold War period: a sitting dual-president (Republic of Kalmykia + FIDE) claims to have been taken aboard an alien spacecraft from his Moscow penthouse apartment, with three witnesses corroborating his unexplained absence and reappearance.

## Account

**Date:** Night of September 17, 1997 (Ilyumzhinov was scheduled to fly to Elista, capital of Kalmykia, at 1 PM the next day)

**Location:** Moscow, top-floor penthouse apartment; only the roof above; open window

**Pre-event:** Ilyumzhinov had returned from his Republic of Kalmykia office in Moscow around 10 PM, turned on television, and lay down. He then experienced what he describes as "a vision, like a half dream, but reality."

**The encounter:**
- Heard a sound or call from the window
- Approached and found a translucent glowing pipe or corridor — described as "made of some matter, or maybe some kind of energy"
- Chose to enter ("everything unknown is interesting to me")
- Found himself in a very large open space — "like several football fields large"
- Many beings present, resembling humans but different; tall; wearing yellow garments
- No one paid attention to him; all were busy with their own tasks
- One being suggested he press somewhere on his chest — this allowed him to breathe more easily (the air had "a lack of air" initially); after pressing, "the air smelled like the sea or the forest"
- He spoke; they answered via thought-transmission (no spoken language)
- He asked to be shown around; one being led him to a large porthole
- Through the porthole: something like a fiery planet giving off heat; beings were loading cylindrical containers/boxes from it into the ship — possibly sample collection from another planet, possibly the far side of the moon, possibly fourth-dimensional space
- He asked why they didn't broadcast on CNN or Russia Today to announce their existence; they replied: "It's still early. You have not yet reached the level in your civilization's development to communicate with us."
- They gave the ants analogy: "Do you converse with ants? … We kind of created you here."
- They cited 117 armed conflicts, environmental destruction, weapons development as evidence of civilizational immaturity
- He asked about time — was afraid an hour there would mean 1,000 years had passed on Earth; they said "we'll return you to where we took you from"
- He asked where they were from; they showed him mathematical formulas "like in space" — symbols and formulas he could not later recall clearly
- The main regret: when he returned, his friends immediately distracted him and he never wrote down the details

**Return:**
- He reappeared from his bedroom — a room his chief of staff, driver, and friend had entered and exited "10 times already"
- They had called the police; security at the building confirmed no one had left or entered; cameras had not recorded anyone leaving
- He felt completely normal; no physical marks; told them "I was flying with friends. They were showing me their ship."
- His friends touched him repeatedly to confirm he was alive

## Witnesses

**Vasili (chief of staff)** — worked with Ilyumzhinov ~40 years; arrived at apartment with keys, rang bell (no answer), found all personal items in place (clothes, two mobile phones, slippers), confirmed Ilyumzhinov was not in the apartment. Searched for an hour. Eventually Ilyumzhinov walked out of the bedroom as if from nowhere. First thought on seeing him: "teleportation maybe." Confirmed: "He's a very honest man. An honest person."

**Driver and mutual friend** — both present during the search; the friend called Vasili after confirming Ilyumzhinov was missing

## Phenomenology Classification

- **Hynek category:** CE5 (Close Encounter of the Fifth Kind) — or CE3 (encounter with beings) + CE4 (abduction/transport)
- **Ilyumzhinov's framing:** Explicitly NOT "abduction" — "I was invited by them." He chose to enter.
- **Duration:** Estimated at over 1 hour from witnesses; subjective experience unclear
- **Physical effects:** None reported; normal mental state on return; no implants, no marks, no amnesia

## Aftermath

- Ilyumzhinov initially kept the encounter private while doing a lengthy cost-benefit analysis
- Went public despite the political risks
- [[garry-kasparov]] immediately called for his removal from FIDE leadership
- [[boris-yeltsin]] quietly acknowledged: "I know, I heard about it. Is everything okay?"
- [[vladimir-putin]] asked: "Were you in your right mind? You weren't drunk?" and moved on
- NASA representatives later contacted Ilyumzhinov for a friendly lunch; they told him 4,000 UFO cases are officially recorded annually
- Multiple other politicians (including a G7 president) privately contacted Ilyumzhinov to share their own experiences after his disclosure
