# 1995 Bariloche Airport UAP

**Date**: 31 July 1995  
**Location**: San Carlos de Bariloche Airport, Argentina  
**Witnesses**: Airline crew, air traffic controllers, airport personnel, civilians in the city

## Observation

During final approach at Bariloche Airport, an unidentified object was observed flying in parallel to an incoming commercial aircraft. Simultaneously:

- The airport's electrical power failed
- All runway lights went out mid-approach
- The city of San Carlos de Bariloche experienced a citywide power outage

The crew, controllers, and ground personnel were all witnesses. The power outage during the UAP's presence and its restoration after the object departed was documented.

## Significance

The [[cometa-report|COMETA Report]] uses the Bariloche case (Chapter 2.5 and referenced in Chapters 11 and 13) to illustrate two points:
1. **Electromagnetic interference at airport infrastructure scale** — not just avionics disruption but full grid failure
2. **Preparedness deficit** — the surprise of a mid-approach power outage during a UAP encounter illustrates why aeronautics personnel need specific training protocols

COMETA cites Bariloche alongside Tehran as the primary examples of apparent UAP-induced infrastructure disruption.
