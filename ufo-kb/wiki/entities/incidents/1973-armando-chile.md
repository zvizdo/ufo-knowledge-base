# 1973 Corporal Armando Chile Time Displacement

**Date**: c.1973  
**Location**: Chile  
**Witnesses**: Corporal Armando (surname withheld); 6 platoon members  
**Corroboration**: Multi-witness disappearance/reappearance; physical evidence (watch, beard)

## Account

During a military operation in Chile, Corporal Armando disappeared entirely from the sight of the other six men in his platoon. He was gone for **15 minutes** by external timekeeping. When he reappeared:

- His **wristwatch showed 5 days had elapsed** (it read 5 days in the future relative to the platoon's watches)
- His **beard had grown out** despite having just shaved before the incident
- He was confused and disoriented; physically he had experienced 5 days

No craft was observed by the platoon; Armando's account of what occurred during the 5-day subjective period is not described in Masters' summary.
