# 1948–1951 Los Alamos / Jemez Mountains Green-Light Sightings

## Key Facts

- **Date range**: 1948 through 1951 (date placeholders 1948-01-01 / 1951-12-31 for ordering; the cluster spans the period; specific dates per sighting are alleged to be in the Los Alamos Protective Force logs but those logs are not in this release)
- **Location**: [[jemez-mountains]] range immediately west and northwest of Los Alamos townsite, observed from Los Alamos
- **Time of day**: Usually 9:00–11:00 pm ("early part of the night")
- **Object morphology**: Green lights — multi-event cluster; described as "weaving in and out of mountain peaks"
- **Daytime variant**: One reported daytime sighting of "five objects flying over Los Alamos in the afternoon. They were flying from southeast to northwest and appeared to be flying in formation." Five or six witnesses including at least one Los Alamos Protective Force member (name redacted under FOIA b(6)).
- **Witness count**: Multi-witness across multiple events; specific names redacted under b(6) in the released letter
- **Reporting chain**: All sightings reported to and logged by the Los Alamos Protective Force Headquarters
- **Documentary trail**: Recalled in writing in November 1970 by a Los Alamos colleague (b(6)-redacted) in correspondence to [[james-l-tuck]], who was actively investigating the cluster from a Los Alamos site-internal perspective. Released by DOE in 2026 as part of [[doe-uap-d2-james-tuck-correspondence-1970-1976]].

## Significance

- **First Los Alamos site-internal documentary corroboration of the green-fireballs corpus**: The wider [[green-fireballs]] / Project Twinkle (1949–1951) record is documented primarily through [[lincoln-lapaz]]'s Air Force-investigation channel. The Tuck correspondence is independent: a Los Alamos colleague's site-internal first-person recollection of multi-year sightings logged by the LANL Protective Force.
- **Identifies an actionable archival target**: The Protective Force logs (LANL security organization 1948–1951) are alleged in the letter to contain time/date records of every reported green-light sighting in this cluster — a tractable target for any future declassification request specifically pursuing those logs.
- **Adds a daytime formation sighting**: The five-object southeast-to-northwest daytime sighting is structurally different from the nighttime green-light cluster and adds previously-undocumented daytime UAP material to the Los Alamos record.
- **Establishes a UFO-curious senior Los Alamos physicist**: That [[james-l-tuck]] (Manhattan Project veteran, fusion-research leader) was actively soliciting Los Alamos colleagues' recollections of these sightings 19+ years after the fact places him in the small lineage of senior physics-establishment figures researching the UAP corpus.
