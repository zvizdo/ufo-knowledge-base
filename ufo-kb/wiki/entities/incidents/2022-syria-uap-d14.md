# 2022 Syria UAP D14

USEUCOM 50 ATKS / 432 AEW ISR platform observed one UAP over the Eastern Mediterranean on 30 May 2022 (300117Z MAY22) while conducting ISR against Russian naval vessels and aircraft in the Syria/Lebanon coastal region.

## Key Facts

- **DTG**: 300117Z MAY 2022
- **Platform**: 50 ATKS / 432 AEW, Sigonella AB (LICZ); FL240, 142 KIAS
- **UAP behavior**: Flew north to northeast; followed as long as possible
- **UAP location**: MGRS 37SBV28 area (Eastern Mediterranean)
- **Result**: Screener could not get positive ID
- **Assessment field**: Blank (not recorded — unique among AARO D-series)
- **Prior event**: RFAF SU-30 flew directly under observer orbit at FL190, ~5 NM, 2.5 hours before UAP
