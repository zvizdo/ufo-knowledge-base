# 1949 Kodiak Alaska UFO Sighting

On April 8, 1949, US Marshal Paul Herring, USN Lieutenant Commander Shepard, and two civilians observed a greenish-blue object at approximately 2,500 ft altitude estimated at 1,500 mph over Kodiak, Alaska. Filed CONFIDENTIAL under FSR 200-4 to AMC Wright-Patterson MCIAXO-3. The associated Office of Naval Intelligence (ONI) report evaluated the case as "probable."

## Details

- Witnesses: US Marshal Paul Herring (civilian federal law enforcement), Lt. Cdr. Shepard (USN), 2 additional civilians
- Location: Kodiak, Alaska
- Color: greenish-blue
- Altitude: approximately 2,500 ft
- Estimated speed: 1,500 mph (well beyond any 1949-era aircraft)
- ONI follow-up: case evaluated "probable" — meaning the ONI considered it a probable genuine unidentified object, not an identified aircraft
- Classification: CONFIDENTIAL
