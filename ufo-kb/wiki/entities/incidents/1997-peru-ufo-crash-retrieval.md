# Peru UFO Crash Retrieval

Undated crash-retrieval incident in Peru documented by researcher UAP Gerbant through whistleblower [[jonathan-wagant]]'s testimony.

## Account

**Year**: 1997. **Context**: [[operation-laser-strike]], a US counter-narcotics operation shooting down drug flights over Peru.

USMC Lance Corporal [[jonathan-wagant]], Sergeant Atkins, and Sergeant Allen were part of a team of 8–10 Marines told there was a "downed friendly or foreign plane" and dispatched to investigate. After trekking through jungle, they found:

- An **enormous egg-shaped / teardrop-shaped craft** embedded at ~45° in a cliff face, with significant **ballistic damage on the rear** — Wagant attributes this to a **Peruvian Hawkman-23 missile** strike
- A **swirling oil-on-water / mother-of-pearl electromagnetic field** around the craft's hull
- A **ring of light** on the craft's circumference that appeared to be slowing down
- A **four-fingered arm** hanging limply from an open hatch, dripping a **purplish-green syrup-like liquid** (described as "everywhere")

Wagant reported feeling **telepathic communication** from a being inside: *"They were not going to harm me... everything is going to be all right. Just help us get out."*

**Post-encounter silencing**: All three Marines were intercepted by **8–12 operators in all-black, no insignia, no name tags**, held at gunpoint. They were subsequently given what they were told were **anthrax vaccine boosters** — a silencing mechanism [[uap-gerb]] identifies across multiple independent crash-retrieval cases.

Wagant's fellow Marines Atkins and Allen later "threw him under the bus." [[uap-gerb]] documented Wagant's account through direct contact.
