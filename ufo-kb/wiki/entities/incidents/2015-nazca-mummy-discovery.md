# 2015 Nazca Mummy Discovery

In 2015, a huaquero named [[leandro-gravedigger]] was walking along the edge of a hillside in the [[nazca-peru]] region when he tripped and caused a wall to collapse, revealing a cave filled with bodies. He dug out clumps that revealed heads and body parts.

## Account

The cave contained over 200 body parts: isolated heads, limbs, appendages, and many complete intact bodies. All were covered in diatomaceous earth — a naturally occurring desiccant that dried them out and preserved organs, tendons, cartilage, and bones. Leandro describes the cave as having been "all up to the ceiling" with bodies.

## Witnesses

- [[leandro-gravedigger]] — sole firsthand witness to the original discovery

## Evidence

- 200+ bodies and body parts
- Diatomaceous earth preservation (not typical human burial mummification)
- Carbon dating range: ~700 years to ~18,800 years, mostly Nazca period (100–800 CE)
- Three distinct body types (M, J, S types)
- CT scans, DICOM files, DNA analysis conducted post-discovery

## Aftermath

- Leandro met [[thierry-jamin]] in 2016 → [[enari-institute]] organized systematic investigation
- [[peruvian-ministry-of-culture]] began active suppression campaign
- International forensic teams examined bodies
- Significant ongoing scientific debate about authenticity, provenance, and nature of beings
