# 2023 Syria UAP D74 — Spherical Object

USCENTCOM AARO MISREP filed under Operation INHERENT RESOLVE. Platform: redacted ISR aircraft conducting FMV/SIGINT target development in Syria. At 2153Z on 9 November 2023, while RTB, aircrew observed a UAP.

## Observation

- **Shape**: Spherical ("bouncy ball"); solid physical state
- **Approach**: From the south, near co-altitude with the ISR platform
- **Behavior**: Dropped altitude and passed safely past the aircraft
- **Speed**: ~424 knots, consistently maintained for at least 7 minutes before going out of sensor range
- **Emissions**: None detected
- **RF**: None
- **Propulsion**: Unknown; no visible propulsion signature
- **Intelligent control**: No (observer assessment)
- **Maneuverability**: None observed
- **Threat assessment**: Benign
- **Equipment effects**: None
- **Personnel effects**: None
- **Materials**: Not recovered
