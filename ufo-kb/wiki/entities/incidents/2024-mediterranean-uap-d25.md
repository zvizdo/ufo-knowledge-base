# 2024 Mediterranean UAP D25

AFSOC 33rd Special Operations Squadron aircraft observed one UAP during a transit phase of a Mediterranean ISR mission, 25 January 2024.

## Key Facts

- **DTG**: 250509Z JAN 2024; duration ~2 minutes
- **Platform**: AFSOC 33 SOS, Larissa AB (LGLR), Greece; FL250, 176 KTS
- **UAP morphology**: Round diamond shape with a straight, non-maneuverable probe/tail at the bottom
- **UAP sensor**: SWIR WHT only — not detected by FMV or other onboard sensors
- **UAP speed**: ~434 knots; altitude FL200; trajectory W; altitude oscillating but heading constant
- **Intelligent control**: UNKNOWN; maneuverability: NONE
- **Assessment**: BENIGN; no effects on persons/equipment; not engaged
