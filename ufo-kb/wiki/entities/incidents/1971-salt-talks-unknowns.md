# 1971 SALT Talks — Unknowns Coordination

A set of declassified transcripts from the SALT (Strategic Arms Limitation Talks) negotiation period (~1971) in which [[henry-kissinger]] and Soviet Ambassador **Anatoly Dobrynin** discussed unconventional phenomena: high-frequency microwave beams targeting US embassy staff in Moscow, illnesses among diplomats, and the broader question of how the two superpowers should handle "unknowns" observed by their respective militaries near sensitive installations.

The transcripts are cited across the KB as a structural precedent for two distinct ideas: (1) US-Soviet coordination protocols on phenomena that mainstream science publicly dismissed as nonexistent, and (2) the historical-record paper trail behind what is now called [[2016-havana-syndrome]].

## Account

In the cited exchange:

- Kissinger raises Soviet microwave beams targeting the US embassy in Moscow as a serious matter
- Dobrynin replies "you are joking"
- Kissinger doubles down: "I am serious" — and gets specific about hours of beam exposure documented at the embassy
- The American ambassador to the USSR at the time, [[walter-stoessel]], was eventually medically discharged with a rare blood cancer attributed to long-term microwave exposure

The same transcripts (per [[jesse-michaels]] in [[5udx_SDdL3Y]] and [[1f16VvXaSSE]]) include US-Soviet coordination on "unknowns in airspace" — language consistent with mid-twentieth-century classified-context terminology for UFOs.

## Witnesses

- [[henry-kissinger]] — US Secretary of State; raised the issue formally
- **Anatoly Dobrynin** — Soviet Ambassador to the United States; counterparty
- [[walter-stoessel]] — US Ambassador to USSR; medically discharged from microwave exposure-attributed illness

## Evidence

- Declassified SALT II transcript record
- Stoessel medical record (rare blood cancer attribution)
- Subsequent on-record CIA / State Department references to the Moscow embassy microwave program

## Aftermath

The Moscow embassy microwave program is now a documented historical case — see [[1976-moscow-embassy-microwave-incident]]. The SALT talks transcripts establish that at the highest levels of US-Soviet diplomacy, microwave-beam health effects were treated as established fact decades before [[2016-havana-syndrome]] surfaced as a public phenomenon.

[[jesse-michaels]] uses these transcripts in [[5udx_SDdL3Y]] and [[1f16VvXaSSE]] as a "forgotten timeline" demonstrating that:
- Highest-level state actors have on-record taken seriously phenomena dismissed by contemporary public science
- A US-Soviet coordination model existed for unknowns
- The Havana Syndrome dismissal pattern (hysteria → confirmed phenomenon) has direct precedent
