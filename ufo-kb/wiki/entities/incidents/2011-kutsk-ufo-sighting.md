# 2011 Kutsk Oblast UFO Sighting

February 2011. Mass UFO sighting in the Irkutsk (Kutsk) Oblast region of southeastern Siberia, Russia. Reported on Russian National Channel 1.

## Account

Multiple witnesses including pensioner Nelly Stean and local children observed luminous spheres hovering above the treetops. Spheres broke apart and merged back into a single object. Witnesses reported an unusual smell ("like from exploded little bumps boys like to blow up") and pink light illuminating the area. One witness heard a rumble. Multiple emergency services responded: civil aviation specialists, search and rescue center, military, FSB, police for transport.

Official explanation: meteorite fall scenario (per Sergey Yazev, Irkutsk State University Astronomical Observatory). Villagers and Russian UFO researchers were not satisfied.

## Nuclear Connection

UAP community member Steven Pearson noted that this region of Siberia hosts the **29th Guards Missile Division** — a live nuclear-armed mobile ICBM force. Nuclear warheads are stored locally at Object 644, not in silos but in bunkered magazines. Jesse Michaels: this connection is consistent with the global pattern of UFO sightings near nuclear assets, adding to his conviction this sighting was real.
