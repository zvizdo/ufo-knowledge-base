# Philadelphia Experiment

Alleged [[us-navy|US Navy]] experiment in which the destroyer escort USS Eldridge was said to have been rendered invisible to radar and/or physically teleported between Philadelphia and Norfolk, Virginia, on October 28, 1943. The story traces primarily to letters and marginal annotations by Carlos Allende (Carl Allen) in a copy of the Varo edition of Morris Jessup's book on unified field theory.

## Origins of the Account

No credible official documentation supports the experiment. The primary source is Carlos Allende, who claimed to have witnessed the event from a nearby merchant vessel. Allende's claims were first communicated through handwritten annotations in a book sent to the Office of Naval Research in 1956.

## Townsend Brown Connection

[[t-townsend-brown|T. [[t-townsend-b]]rown|Townsend Brown]]'s high-voltage field experiments and reports of temporal disorientation effects among operators are sometimes cited in the same context — the temporal/spatial displacement effects are structurally similar to what Allende described.
