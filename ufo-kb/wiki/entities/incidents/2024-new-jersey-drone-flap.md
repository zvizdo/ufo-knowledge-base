# 2024 New Jersey Drone/UAP Flap

A multi-week wave of drone and UAP sightings across New Jersey and adjacent areas, peaking in December 2024. Centered on [[pikatini-arsenal]] but extending to nuclear power plants at Salem (NJ), Lake and Heath (NC), and Brunswick/Duke Energy (NC). The incident attracted simultaneous response from DoD, FBI, 59+ local law enforcement entities, and private legacy program contractors — none coordinating with each other.

[[jake-barber]]'s [[sky-watchers]] organization was deployed as part of a DoD/FBI task force, providing the most publicly available operational account.

## Account

### Timeline
- **Pre-Thanksgiving 2024**: A Temporary Flight Restriction (TFR) was quietly established over the Pikatini Arsenal area, restricting unmanned aerial systems (UAS) but notably *not* general aviation — creating a collision hazard the TFR failed to address.
- **December 2024**: Public awareness of drone sightings escalates dramatically; media coverage intensifies.
- **Sky Watchers arrival**: Within 30 minutes of Barber's team arriving at Pikatini Arsenal, orange orbs appeared in the sky.

### Orange Orbs Over Pikatini Arsenal
Barber's assessment of the orange orbs: most likely **conventional drones** violating FAA regulations — operating lights-off above 400 ft, then cycling lights on (producing the "orb" appearance). The 40–45 minute observation windows are consistent with battery-powered consumer or commercial drones. The lights-on/lights-off behavior suggests either multiple craft toggling or a single craft with a light cycle.

This is *not* a dismissal — Barber emphasizes these were still illegal incursions over a sensitive military installation.

### UAP-Class Activity South of Arsenal
Separately, Barber's team observed phenomena that did not behave like conventional drones: objects apparently entering from the outer atmosphere, hovering briefly, then returning to space — a vertical entry/exit profile consistent with non-conventional propulsion. These objects were observed south of Pikatini Arsenal and are distinct from the orange orbs.

Barber describes this as the genuinely anomalous layer of the incident, largely absent from public media coverage which focused on the drone narrative.

### Nuclear Facility Pattern
The flap extended to multiple nuclear facilities: Salem Nuclear Power Plant (NJ), and plants associated with Lake and Heath and Brunswick (Duke Energy) in North Carolina. This aligns with the historical pattern documented by [[robert-hastings]] in [[ufos-and-nukes-hastings]] — UAP systematically monitoring nuclear weapons and energy infrastructure. See [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]].

## Chris Mellon on Scale (Fox/Rogan, December 2024)

In [[3yrKVu35uSE]], [[james-fox]] cited [[chris-mellon]]'s assessment of the New Jersey drone wave: Mellon stated that **up to 50 objects were coming in from the ocean** simultaneously during the peak incursion period. This figure — from the former Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence — significantly exceeds the public media portrayal of isolated drone sightings and implies a coordinated, large-scale incursion consistent with either a state-level adversary or an anomalous phenomenon.

Fox also noted that **Patterson Air Force Base was temporarily shut down** during the incident — a detail absent from most public coverage that indicates the incursion reached the threshold for base-level operational response.
