# 1958-detroit-uap

Civilian David Weaver (23 years old, son of Detroit policeman, CAP experience) observed a circular object with a "crystal-type dome that reflected lights" traveling northward over Detroit at 4:08 AM on April 17, 1958. The object crossed the city approximately 3 blocks south of Six Mile at Lamphere Street. Weaver attempted to contact Selfridge Field AFB but was unable, and then called the FBI Detroit Field Office directly.

## Additional Context

The 1958 Detroit area UAP sighting cluster occurred during a multi-year period (1957-1960) of elevated US UAP activity that included the major waves of November 1957 (the "Levelland sightings" in Texas + the EM-effects vehicle-stop pattern) and the 1959 Father Gill encounter in Papua New Guinea. The Detroit incident is one of several Great Lakes regional reports from this era.

Detroit's UAP-relevance is partly geographic (proximity to Selfridge AFB and the Great Lakes interceptor airspace) and partly demographic (high-population urban observer base producing multiple-witness corroboration). The 1958 incident appears in Project Blue Book records and was treated by Blue Book analysts as one of the late-1950s "unknown" classifications rather than dispositioned to a prosaic explanation.
