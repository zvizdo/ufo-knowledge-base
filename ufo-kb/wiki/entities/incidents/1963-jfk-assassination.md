# 1963 — JFK Assassination

## Account

President John F. Kennedy was shot and killed in Dealey Plaza, Dallas, Texas on November 22, 1963. The Warren Commission concluded Lee Harvey Oswald acted alone. [[danny-sheehan]]'s account provides a detailed alternative reconstruction:

### The S Force — Who and How

- In 1960, VP [[richard-nixon]], chairing the NSC 5412 Committee, organized a 15-man assassination team (the "S Force") targeting Castro through a chain: Nixon → [[howard-hughes]] → [[robert-maheu]] → [[johnny-roselli]] → [[sam-giancana]] → [[santos-trafficante]].
- When Nixon lost the 1960 election, the S Force continued operating without Kennedy's knowledge.
- [[allen-dulles]], fired by Kennedy after the Bay of Pigs, discovered Kennedy was conducting **18 secret letters of correspondence with Khrushchev** arranging mutual nuclear disarmament — which would have dismantled the entire covert nuclear deterrence apparatus Dulles had built.
- Dulles activated the S Force against Kennedy.
- **David Morales** fired the shot from the grassy knoll — Sheehan identifies him as the "dead-eye shot" of the team.
- [[theodore-shackley]] was Miami CIA Station Chief that day; the S Force's operational base had been the Miami station.

### Oswald's Role

- Oswald was a **witting patsy** — he knew he was being positioned as the fall guy, and he believed he would be protected.
- Crucially, Oswald did not know about the grassy-knoll shooter or the other team members — he thought he was part of a larger protective operation, not the designated assassin.
- When he realized that **Officer Tippit** (who had been assigned to eliminate him) was coming for him, Oswald killed Tippit in self-defense.
- [[jack-ruby]] killed Oswald in custody; [[jolly-west]] subsequently spent 24 hours with Ruby in his jail cell, after which Ruby reportedly had a "psychotic break" consistent with MK Ultra-adjacent behavioral disruption.

### UAP Connection

The UAP-motive thesis (documented separately in [[jfk-assassination-ufo]]) is that Kennedy's intent to share NHI intelligence with the Soviets was an additional motivation — overlapping with the nuclear disarmament motive. The two are not mutually exclusive: Kennedy's secret letters to Khrushchev may have included or anticipated UAP information sharing.

The **JFK-to-McCone letter** of November 12, 1963 (ten days before the assassination) — see [[jfk-letter-mccone]] — requesting UAP data from CIA for Soviet deconfliction purposes, fits as an operational step toward the cooperative nuclear/UAP framework Kennedy was building with the Soviets.

## The Watergate Throughline

Sheehan's critical insight: the JFK assassination doesn't end in 1963. The S Force funding chain (Nixon → Hughes → mob → Mexico City bank account) is exactly what the Watergate investigation threatened to expose. The break-in, and [[richard-nixon]]'s desperate moves to shut down the Mexico City investigation, make sense only if what lay at the end of that chain was not just political embarrassment but evidence of organizing a presidential assassination.
