# 2016-latakia-sea-skim-d55

P-8A Poseidon maritime patrol aircraft (CTG 67.1) observed an unidentified low-flying object near Latakia, Syria on 18 Nov 2016. Sea-skimming at ~500 KTS, southeasterly heading outbound from a Russian naval contact group (KCTG). Lost visual after passing between Russian ship INGUL ARS and an unidentified vessel. Assessment: possible missile, standard KCTG activity. First such observation by P-8 aircraft in the Eastern Mediterranean.


## Source Document Detail (from [[dow-uap-d55-mission-report-syria-2016-11-18]])

USCENTCOM MISREP from CTG 67.1; P-8A Poseidon maritime patrol aircraft; Eastern Mediterranean; 18 Nov 2016; SECRET//REL TO USA, NATO. Declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison USCENTCOM COS. PURSUE Release 01.
## Observation

- **Date/Time**: 18NOV2016, 1310Z; lost visual 1312Z (~2 minutes total)
- **Location**: 55 NM NW of Latakia, Syria; P-8 at 26 NM south of object at 16,121 ft (altitude detail 1.4a)
- **Object**: Unidentified low-flying object (BLUF characterization); EO/IR detection; sea-skim mode; ~500 KTS; southeasterly heading outbound from KCTG
- **Loss of visual**: ~40 NM NW of Latakia; object passed between Russian ship INGUL ARS and 1 unidentified vessel
- **Weather**: Clear, no range limitations

## CTG 67.1 Assessment

> "The mission commander for P-8 characterized the interaction as safe. While this was the first observed occurrence of possible missile activity by P-8 aircraft in the Eastern Mediterranean it is assessed to be standard activity consistent with the assessed activity of the KCTG."

- **Resolution**: Assessed as "possible missile" consistent with KCTG (Contact Group — likely Russian naval surface group); NOT resolved as confirmed missile; retained as UAP/unidentified in AARO batch despite assessed explanation.
