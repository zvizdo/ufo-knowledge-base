# 1961 Vallée Retrograde Orbit Observation

On July 11, 1961, [[jacques-vallee]] and colleagues at the Paris Observatory — then part of the nascent satellite tracking program — observed an unidentified object in **retrograde orbit**: traveling in the opposite direction to Earth's rotation. The object was **as bright as Sirius** (the brightest star in the night sky, magnitude -1.46). It was observed at multiple other observatories and photographed by American tracking stations, who also could not identify it.

## Account

Vallée and colleagues first noticed the anomaly while working on the satellite tracking program. The object did not reappear in successive weeks. The following morning, observatory director [[paul-mueller]] confiscated the tracking tape and destroyed it, terminating any possibility of systematic follow-up.

Vallée later recounted that this experience made him realize: "Scientists were human beings like the rest of us. When their reputations were threatened, when their ideas were challenged, they reacted by eliminating the data." This was the inciting incident that set him on his lifelong path as the foremost independent UFO researcher.

## Why Retrograde Orbit Matters

Entering a retrograde orbit is difficult — requiring a rocket to fight against Earth's rotational momentum, which most launch facilities exploit by placing themselves near the equator. Retrograde orbits are often polar orbits, which allow a satellite to image the **entire Earth's surface** as the planet rotates below. If the object were an observation platform, retrograde/polar orbit is the optimal geometry.

## The Corona Satellite Explanation (Insufficient)

Some have attributed the observation to the Corona program — CIA-sponsored reconnaissance satellites first launched in 1960 with nearly polar orbits. Problems:
1. Corona satellites were built with low-reflectivity materials to be inconspicuous; the observed object was as bright as Sirius — far brighter than any Corona satellite
2. Corona satellites did not reappear across weekly intervals because they did re-enter — but the specific brightness and simultaneous observation by multiple independent tracking stations is inconsistent with misidentification

## Evidence

The tracking tape was destroyed by [[paul-mueller]]. Vallée's later interviews confirm the events. Multiple observatories and US tracking stations made independent observations.
