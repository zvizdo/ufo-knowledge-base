# 2026 Department of War UAP Document Release

## Account

On **May 8, 2026**, the US Department of War (rebranded from the Department of Defense under the Trump administration) released hundreds of UAP-related documents, video, and audio files. President Trump publicly ordered the release with a "very pointed message to the American people." Within the next two weeks, the Department of War's UFO release web page reportedly logged over **500 million page views** — exceeding the total US population.

Per [[OOr98r51Sj0]] (released same day), the release is characterized as **a "UFO starter pack"** rather than a "vault" — "no alien autopsy tape, no pictures of historical crash retrievals like Roswell, no Little Gray in a freezer at Wright Patterson." Most of the documents had been "hiding in plain sight for ages" and are now receiving "a stamp of approval" rather than being newly declassified.
