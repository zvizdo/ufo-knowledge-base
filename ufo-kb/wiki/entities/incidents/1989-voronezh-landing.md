# 1989 Voronezh UAP Landing

September 27, 1989. A public park (Yuzhny Park) in Voronezh, Russia (then USSR). Multiple witnesses — primarily children and a few adults — reported observing a large pink or light-blue sphere descend into the park. A hatch opened and beings emerged: tall (3+ meters) with three eyes. The beings reportedly used a device on one child who screamed and was briefly paralyzed or disappeared, then reappeared after the craft departed.

**Official Soviet acknowledgment**: TASS, the Soviet state news agency, reported the incident. This makes Voronezh notable as one of the few cases where the government of a major power officially acknowledged a close encounter event. Soviet scientific teams subsequently visited the site and reported finding physical ground traces — changes in soil composition and the presence of unusual rocks.

**Significance in the KB**: The Voronezh incident is cited in the broader KB context of Soviet-era official UAP investigation (→ [[russia]]). The Soviet Union conducted its parallel to US programs through official science bodies rather than purely military channels. The TASS report — a state agency confirmation — represents a data point about state-level acknowledgment of landing events.

**Credibility notes**: The primary witnesses were children between roughly 7 and 14 years old. Some adult witnesses corroborated seeing an unusual object. The combination of TASS reporting and physical ground traces places this above purely anecdotal close-encounter accounts, though the small entity descriptions are contested.
