# 2011 Kutsk Snow Alien

March 2011. A video filmed by two Russian teens (Timor, age 18, and Kir, age 19) in the Irkutsk Oblast region of Siberia shows what appears to be a small alien body lying in the snow. The video has 12 million YouTube views.

## Account

The teens filmed the body and uploaded it to YouTube. They were subsequently taken in by police, questioned, and apparently confessed to hoaxing it using breadcrumbs, plasticine, and chicken skin. This debunk was widely accepted.

Jesse Michaels is skeptical of the confession for several reasons:
1. The body closely resembles the Nazca J-type beings and the beings from *Close Encounters of the Third Kind*.
2. A mass UFO sighting ([[2011-kutsk-ufo-sighting]]) occurred in the same region just one month before.
3. The chicken-skin confession echoes the pattern of Manuel Caceras's wooden figurines being used to debunk the Nazca bodies — false debunks using substituted fake objects.
4. The debunking expert analysis was weak ("you'd expect to see alien blood or fluids" — dismissed as poor reasoning by Jesse).

Jesse's gun-to-head take: probably still fake (teens were at prime pranking age), but the morphological similarity and nuclear-sighting proximity make it worth noting.
