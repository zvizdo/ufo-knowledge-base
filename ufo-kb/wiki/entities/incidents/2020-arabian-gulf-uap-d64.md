# 2020 Arabian Gulf UAP D64

482 ATKS / 432 AEW ISR platform observed two UAP via FMV over the Arabian Gulf on 2 November 2020, 5 minutes apart, with an Iranian Air Defense guard call earlier in the same sortie.

## Key Facts

- **DTG**: 022143Z NOV20 (Obs 1) and 022148Z NOV20 (Obs 2; 5 minutes later)
- **Platform**: 482 ATKS / 432 AEW; OKAS, Saudi Arabia; ANDAS4 + GMESH + VORTEX
- **Location**: Arabian Gulf; MGRS 39RWK58 (Obs 1), 39RWK68-69 (Obs 2)
- **Obs 1**: 1× UAP bearing 080T from observer; altitude UNKNOWN; FMV; no further description
- **Obs 2**: 1× additional UAP traveling NW; FMV
- **Guard call**: Iranian Air Defense at 1012Z (11h 31m before UAP); PROFESSIONAL tone; no mission impact
- **Weather**: Haze precluded IMINT collection
- **Total mission**: 20h 42m; 18h 55m on-station
- **Unit context**: Third 2020 Arabian Gulf/Hormuz MISREP from 482 ATKS / 432 AEW (alongside D62 Sep 2020, D63 Oct 2020)
