# 1933 Magenta Crash

**Date**: 1933  
**Location**: Near Magenta, Italy

## Account

A disc approximately 10–12 meters in diameter crashed near Magenta, Lombardy, Italy in 1933, under Mussolini's fascist government. Two separate lines of evidence corroborate the basic event:

**Italian source chain** (via [[robert-salas]] / Roberto Pinotti): [[guglielmo-marconi]] was assigned by Mussolini to a secret investigation group (the [[gabinetto-rs33]], sometimes written RS/33) to study the craft. Marconi's grandson confirmed this in a video interview with Ogo magazine. Italian Air Marshal Italo Balbo was also involved. Mussolini insisted publicly that the craft must be of terrestrial origin; the RS/33 investigation concluded otherwise. The Stefani fascist state news agency imposed a complete media blackout. The craft was stored at SIA-Marchetti aerospace hangars for analysis.

**US source chain** (via [[harold-malmgren]] / [[09KP8XVf5nY]]): [[richard-bissell]] — CIA Deputy Director of Plans and founder of [[area-51]] — privately told Malmgren: "He said this did not begin last week. He mentioned 1933 Magenta." This conversation predated [[david-grusch]]'s 2023 public testimony about the Magenta crash by decades, constituting unusually strong corroboration from a named senior CIA official.

**Vatican channel**: Pope Pius XII alerted the Allies to the Magenta craft, specifically fearing Nazi access. This back-channel triggered OSS [[project-mcgregor]] — [[bill-donovan]]'s operation to retrieve the craft from Italian custody in 1945.

**Transfer to US custody**: Via OSS Project McGregor, coordinated by [[allen-dulles]] operating out of Switzerland; the craft was transferred to [[wright-patterson]] Air Force Base (Hangar 18) in 1945. Note: the earlier hypothesis (from the first source) that it was transferred to *Nazi Germany* is not supported by the Malmgren account — the Malmgren/Bissell chain has it going to the US via the OSS, not to Germany via Mussolini.

> ⚠ **Conflict**: [[robert-salas]] (via Roberto Pinotti) speculated the craft may have been transferred to Nazi Germany before WWII, potentially informing von Braun's claimed familiarity with UFO craft. The Malmgren/Bissell account has it remaining in Italian custody until 1945, then transferred to the US via OSS Project McGregor. These are incompatible unless multiple craft or partial materials were involved.

## Jorjani Account (ABw7EsWh2Us) — Additional Details

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] in [[ABw7EsWh2Us]] adds several specific details not in earlier sources:

- **Neutral third country**: The joint working group was placed in **Czechoslovakia** deliberately — "to respect the Italians by putting this project in a third country that was neither Germany nor Italy." This is the [[kammler-stab]] / [[kammler-stab]] complex near Prague.
- **Italian technical gap**: "The Italians needed the German technical expertise... they had to call the Germans in." The Germans were brought in specifically for reverse-engineering, not as initiators.
- **Three-party working group**: The Vatican, Italian scientists, and SS scientists all participated. The Vatican's involvement was as an observer/overseer, not merely an intelligence conduit.
- **Gehlen exfiltration**: [[reinhard-gehlen]] organized the eventual exfiltration of the craft (or its technical knowledge) via Nazi rat lines using [[knights-of-malta]] logistics — per [[harold-malmgren]]'s testimony as relayed by Jorjani. This is the mechanism by which the technology entered [[operation-paperclip]] and US programs.

> ⚠ **Conflict note (Jorjani vs. earlier accounts)**: The existing account has the craft transferred to US custody via OSS [[project-mcgregor]] in 1945, coordinated by [[allen-dulles]]. Jorjani's account via Malmgren adds Gehlen's rat lines as a parallel or complementary exfiltration mechanism. These may be compatible (Gehlen providing logistics for the Dulles-directed transfer) or represent different claims about what was moved when.
