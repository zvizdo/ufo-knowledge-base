# 1945 Flight 19

On December 5, 1945, five US Navy TBF Avenger torpedo bombers designated as "Flight 19" departed Fort Lauderdale Naval Air Station, Florida on a routine training mission over the Atlantic. All five aircraft and their 14 crewmen disappeared. A PBY Martin Mariner rescue aircraft sent to search for them also disappeared — 13 more men lost. No wreckage attributable to Flight 19 was ever definitively found.

## The Banana River NAS Connection

A search aircraft departed from Banana River Naval Air Station — the predecessor installation to what became [[cape-canaveral]]. This places the Flight 19 disappearance in the geographic cluster of anomalous activity around the Florida Space Coast that includes the [[1992-cape-canaveral-saucer]] incident.

## Bermuda Triangle

Flight 19 is the defining case in [[bermuda-triangle]] mythology. The pilots' final radio transmissions suggest compass malfunction and complete spatial disorientation — both consistent with electromagnetic interference effects reported in other UAP proximate events.

## Competing Explanations

- **Conventional**: navigational error, fuel exhaustion, crash in bad weather
- **Electromagnetic anomaly**: compass/instrument failure consistent with UAP electromagnetic effects
- **Bermuda Triangle mythology**: triangular energy vortex, sea monster, dimensional rift — the full spectrum of speculative explanations
