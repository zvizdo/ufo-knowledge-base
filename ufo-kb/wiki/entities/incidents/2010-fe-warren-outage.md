# 2010 F.E. Warren ICBM Outage

In 2010, nuclear ICBM missile silos at [[fe-warren-afb]] in Cheyenne, Wyoming experienced an approximately one-hour outage — meaning the silos were inoperable for that period. This is classified as a critical National Security event, as the ICBMs are the US's strategic nuclear deterrent.

## Account

[[robert-hastings]] conducted back-channel interviews with base personnel following the incident. Those personnel attributed the outage to an **oblong, Tic-Tac shaped object** that was observed flying around the base during the outage window. Personnel interviewed held **PRP clearance** (Personnel Reliability Program) — the highest mental-fitness screening for nuclear base personnel — which is cited as elevating their credibility.

The incident occurred just before Hastings retired and was included as one of the 167 cases in his compilation [[ufos-and-nukes]].

## Witnesses

Unnamed PRP-cleared F.E. Warren base personnel (back-channel interviews conducted by [[robert-hastings]]).

## Evidence

- Back-channel testimony from PRP-cleared personnel
- The outage itself is an official event (power failure at a nuclear missile installation would be documented)
- Craft type matches the "Tic-Tac" profile consistent with 2004 [[2004-nimitz-tic-tac]] and other cases

## John Mills Back-Channel — 26 Hours Not 59 Minutes (fzvwBBSmWYA)

[[robert-hastings]]'s primary source for this incident was [[john-mills]], a retired missile targeting technician who retained contacts at Fe Warren. Mills provided multiple sources who described:

1. **The blackout lasted over 26 hours**, not the officially stated 59 minutes
2. The UAP was described as **gray, cigar-shaped (Tic-Tac), approximately aircraft-carrier scale** — a "mothership"
3. The mothership released smaller disc-shaped craft (pattern Hastings documents in other cases)

The Atlantic magazine ran an article noting "President Obama was briefed this morning on a power failure at F.E. Warren... that took 50 nuclear intercontinental ballistic missiles... temporarily offline." Notably, the article appears to have edited the text in real-time: "power failure" was struck through and replaced with "engineering" — visible in a screenshot. The event was large enough to warrant a presidential briefing.

Mills's sources received flags in their Air Force exit-interview files designating them as "security risks" — suggesting surveillance of their communications with Hastings.
