# 2020 Strait of Hormuz UAP D63

USCENTCOM 482 ATKS / 432 AEW ISR platform observed one UAP near end of a Strait of Hormuz NAVCENT ISR mission on 2 October 2020, following sustained Iranian Air Defense tracking throughout the mission.

## Key Facts

- **DTG**: 021829Z OCT 2020 (6 minutes before RTB clearance)
- **Platform**: 482 ATKS / 432 AEW, OKAS; ANDAS4 FMV + AIRHANDLER
- **Iranian guard calls**: 5 calls over 5.5 hours (0727Z–1315Z), all professional/directive tones
- **UAP form data**: Absent from released document
- **Assessment**: Not recorded
