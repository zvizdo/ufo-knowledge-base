# 1977 Giraud Mirage IV UAP Intercept

**Date**: 7 March 1977  
**Location**: Dijon region, France  
**Platform**: Mirage IV aircraft (French Air Force)  
**Witness**: Colonel Hervé Giraud + two additional ground/air witnesses

## Observation

During a flight near Dijon, Colonel Giraud observed an unidentified object that executed a tight turn at 3–4g, then a second turn at 6.5g. The object was moving at supersonic speed yet produced no sonic boom. Critically, the object was **not detected by radar** at the time — ruling out conventional aircraft or missile explanations that would generate a radar return. Three independent witnesses confirmed the observation.

## Anomalous Features

- **G-force maneuvers (3–4g, then 6.5g)** at supersonic speed without any sonic boom
- **Radar-invisible** despite supersonic flight profile
- **Three independent witnesses**: elevated evidentiary value per COMETA methodology

## Significance

Giraud's Mirage IV case opens the COMETA Report's French military testimony section (Chapter 1) and is used to establish that UAP encounters involving French military pilots in high-performance aircraft are not easily dismissed. The combination of radar-invisibility and anomalous g-forces is presented as physically inconsistent with any known 1977 aircraft.
