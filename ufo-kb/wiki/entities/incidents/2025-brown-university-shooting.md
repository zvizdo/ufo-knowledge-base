# 2025 Brown University Shooting

On December 13, 2025, Portuguese national Claudio Emanuel Neves Valente entered an open lecture hall in Brown University's Barus and Holley engineering building during final exam period and opened fire with a 9mm pistol, killing two students and wounding nine others. Valente had conducted months of prior surveillance on the campus, renting a storage unit in Salem, New Hampshire and returning repeatedly to scout the specific building.

**Barus and Holley context**: The Barus and Holley building houses Brown University's School of Engineering and the physics department, making it a gathering point for physics and engineering graduate students during final exam period. The choice of this specific building — rather than a more general campus location — is consistent with the subsequent Lorero murder in indicating an interest in physics/engineering personnel rather than random targeting.

**Escape and Lorero connection**: After the shooting, Valente evaded police for approximately 48 hours using a burner phone, European SIM card, and swapped license plates. On December 15, 2025 — two days after the Brown attack — Valente shot MIT plasma physicist [[nuno-lorero]] in the foyer of Lorero's Brookline, Massachusetts apartment. Lorero died during surgery. Security cameras placed a suspect matching Valente's description near Lorero's home earlier that day.

**Valente's background**: Valente and Lorero had studied physics together at Instituto Superior Técnico in Lisbon approximately 20 years before the attacks. Valente was reportedly top of his class but failed his MIT graduate school admissions test; Lorero was admitted and went on to a distinguished career in plasma physics and nuclear fusion research. Valente subsequently dropped out of Brown and effectively disappeared. His confessional videos, recorded at his New Hampshire storage unit after the Lorero shooting, confirm both attacks were intentional.

**KB interpretation**: The KB's inclusion of this incident in the [[scientists-targeting-pattern]] context requires calibration. The most straightforward interpretation is a targeted personal grievance attack: Valente blamed Lorero (and secondarily the physics academic community at Brown) for his career trajectory. The attack on Barus and Holley rather than Lorero's lab directly suggests Valente wanted mass casualties in a physics space, not exclusively a targeted assassination of Lorero. Whether Lorero's proximity to classified fusion programs (SPARC, CFS) has any relevance to the attack is not established in KB sources — the personal grievance explanation is sufficient without invoking external targeting.

**Significance for pattern analysis**: The incident is documented in the KB not as an asserted conspiracy but as a data point in the broader pattern of unexplained incidents involving nuclear/fusion researchers. The personal-grievance framing is well-documented; the KB notes the case without asserting that Valente was acting on behalf of anyone other than himself.
