# 1980 Rendlesham Forest UAP Incident

**Date**: 26–28 December 1980  
**Location**: Rendlesham Forest, Suffolk, UK  
**Witnesses**: [[jim-penniston]], [[john-burroughs]], [[charles-halt]] (plus additional personnel)  
**Corroboration**: Multi-witness military; audio recording (Halt tape); physical landing traces

## Account

Over three consecutive nights, USAF personnel from RAF Bentwaters / Woodbridge reported unusual lights in Rendlesham Forest. On the first night, [[jim-penniston]] and John Burroughs physically approached a small craft resting on the forest floor. Penniston describes:

- A **"sphere of influence"** surrounding the craft — an area where time and movement felt perceptibly altered, "like walking through water"
- The craft itself as triangular, metallic, with a smooth surface bearing engraved hieroglyphic-like symbols
- He physically touched the craft; noted heat and texture
- A bright flash when he touched certain symbols

On the third night, [[charles-halt]] and a larger team observed aerial phenomena and recorded audio (the Halt tape). The physical landing site showed broken branches and radiation readings above background.

## Penniston's Contact (Recalled via Hypnosis)

Under hypnotic regression, [[jim-penniston]] recalled a contact experience embedded within the physical encounter:

> "We are you. We come from 8,000 years in the future."

Additional recalled content:
- The visitors were experiencing **reproductive problems** causing a genetic diversity crisis
- They were visiting the past specifically to collect **gametes** from ancestral humans
- They wanted "to maintain genetic diversity and not get wiped off the planet"
- The "sphere of influence" time distortion was an effect of the craft's time-manipulation field
