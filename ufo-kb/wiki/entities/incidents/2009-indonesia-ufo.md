# 2009 Indonesia UFO Encounter

The account of [[michael-herrera]] and five fellow Marines who observed a ~300-foot octagonal craft loading operations in a jungle clearing in western Sumatra, Indonesia, during what was officially a humanitarian assistance mission. One of the most operationally specific multi-witness UAP accounts in the KB; notable for involving the apparent loading of [[p3-psionic-assets]] in refrigerated containers, an armed apprehension by unknown American operators, and a subsequent NDA under threat of execution.

## Account

### Mission Context

The 31st Marine Expeditionary Unit (aboard the USS Denver) was originally tasked to Operation Katsana in the Philippines following an earthquake/tsunami, then rerouted to western Sumatra. Six Marines were assigned LZ security for helicopter operations at a hasty jungle clearing — no civilians were present, which Herrera noted as unusual. Obama family members were mentioned in the ward room briefing; a SEAL team was reportedly in theater to extract them if necessary.

### The Craft

From elevated high ground through partially obscuring vegetation, the Marines observed an object in the clearing below:

- **Dimensions:** ~300 feet in diameter; enormous relative to the clearing
- **Shape:** Three-tiered concentric octagonal platforms — most accentuated/pyramidal on top, medium in the middle, most spread at the base; like stacked concentric rings reducing in relief
- **Surface:** Matte black transitioning to matte grey; specific panels described as "Vantablack" — absorbing light to a degree beyond ordinary matte black, appearing to consume ambient light "like a black hole"; color shifted as sunlight hit it at different angles during descent
- **Sound:** Persistent low-frequency bass hum, no conventional propulsion signature; friend "Joey" independently recreated a similar sound
- **Hover:** Below or at treetop level; appeared to hover without any support structure contacting the ground
- **Platform:** A lower platform extended beneath the craft; cargo trucks (Tundra or F-350-type) with refrigerated/HVAC containers and Pelican cases drove up the platform ramp; the platform levitated or merged with the craft from below — not structurally attached from above

### Cargo

Herrera initially suspected the containers held drug cargo. [[jake-barber]] later told him the containers held **[[p3-psionic-assets]]** — humans with psionic capability recruited to attract non-human craft, sometimes from third-world countries, sometimes given drug cocktails to raise consciousness states.

### The Apprehension

Before the Marines could fully descend to the clearing, an eight-man armed force had already established a pincer (4+4 formation) around them:

- American-accented English
- Openly discussed killing the Marines: "should we smoke these guys right now?"
- Disarmed the Marines: magazines removed, chambers checked; Marines were in Condition Three and had no opportunity to respond
- Biometrically processed the Marines via a BAT-like device and a thin CavCom smartphone-like device; identified each by name, rank, and SSN

### Departure

After the last trucks loaded onto the platform:
- Platform rose; craft descended or merged with it
- Craft rose above the treetops and rotated **clockwise** — revealing red, yellow, green, and blue lights at the corners
- Lights shut off; craft shot off to the left (toward the ocean) at "several thousand miles an hour"
- Appeared as a "black blur"; no rotor wash, no disruption of surrounding vegetation, no sonic boom
- Appeared to exist within its own environmental envelope

### Aftermath

1. **Camera sabotage at Subic Bay:** Herrera's personal Panasonic camera (which had documented the craft) was accessed from his locked locker without visible forced entry; found on his rack with battery and memory card removed; camera could not be powered on afterward
2. **NDA at Camp Hansen, Okinawa:** An Air Force Lt. Colonel in dress blues (silver oak leaf, jump wings, no pilot badge) had Herrera sign an NDA referencing "TSSCI" and "Indonesia"; threatened prison or execution; same procedure applied to each of the six Marines separately
3. **Silence:** Herrera kept silent for approximately 8 years before connecting with [[steven-greer]] at a Boulder book signing in 2018
