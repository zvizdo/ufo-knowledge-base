# 2024 Iraq UAP D28 (AC-130 Gunship, Live Fire)

AFSOC 16 SOS / 27 SOW AC-130 gunship observed a UAP traverse its sensor field during a live precision-guided munitions shot over Ayn Al Asad Air Base, Iraq, September 2024.

## Key Facts

- **DTG**: 202027:59Z SEP24
- **Platform**: AC-130 gunship (SOTU 016); 16 SOS / 27 SOW; OKAS; FL130; 170 KIAS; HDG 096
- **Sensors**: MX-20 + MX-25 dual EO/IR targeting pods
- **Primary witnesses**: WSO (Weapons Systems Officer) and CSO (Combat Systems Officer)
- **Location**: MGRS 38SKC59 (Ayn Al Asad ROZ RAINDROP, Iraq)
- **Timing**: Immediately after AGM-176 release; "in between munition release and munition impact"
- **UAP description**: Solid; IR lens flare on both sensors (significant heat source); high rate of speed; traversed sensor field and was not reobserved
- **Possible object detachment**: Unknown whether object detached from primary UAP before leaving sensor field
- **UAP response to detection**: None; "path of movement appeared predetermined"
- **Assessment**: BENIGN
- **Munition outcome**: AGM-176 hit desired target (weapons impact not affected)
- **UAP Event Serial Number**: 202027ZSEP2024-CENTCOM
