# Gary McKinnon Hack (2001–2002)

Between February 2001 and March 2002, British hacker Gary McKinnon accessed 97 U.S. military and [[nasa]] computer systems — including systems at the [[pentagon]], [[johnson-space-center]], and others — specifically searching for UAP evidence. McKinnon was operating under a dial-up connection and left notes on some systems ("Your security is crap"), leading to his eventual identification and a decade-long extradition fight.

## Claimed Discoveries

McKinnon claims to have found:
1. A list of "non-terrestrial officers" — names and ranks not corresponding to any known branch of the U.S. military
2. A list of "fleet-to-fleet transfers" between ships with non-terrestrial designations — not any known Navy vessels
3. A photograph of a large, elongated craft in orbit, apparently taken from a satellite or space-based platform — which he did not save and which was subsequently deleted

All claimed discoveries are unretrievable; the files McKinnon saw have been deleted or never existed as he describes.
