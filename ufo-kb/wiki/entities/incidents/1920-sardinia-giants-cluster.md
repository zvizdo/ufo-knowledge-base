# Sardinian Giant Evidence Cluster

A collection of alleged giant skeletal discoveries documented by [[tim-alberino]] through on-site investigation in Sardinia, Italy, across at least three separate visits. The discoveries span from the 1920s to the 1980s and were reported by elderly locals who excavated or witnessed the remains before they were removed by unknown officials.

## Archaeological Context: Nuragic Culture

The nuragic culture built over **30,000 circular stone towers** (*nuraghi*) across Sardinia, standing up to 100 ft tall. Associated with these towers are the *tombe dei giganti* — **tombs of the giants** — megalithic structures shaped like wombs (or bull's heads in a lateral view). Alberino links the nuragic people to **Canaanites** displaced by Joshua's conquest of the Promised Land; a Greek historian reportedly recorded an inscription in Tunisia reading "We are the Canaanites who fled before the face of Joshua son of Nun."

## Tombs of the Giants: Design and Purpose

Two configurations:
1. **Triilithon entry**: Large megalithic stones forming a doorway tall enough to stoop through; spacious gallery inside with stone bench
2. **Stele with doggy door**: A large megalithic stone with a small rectangular opening at the base — only a child (12–13 year old) could enter

Both configurations are shaped like wombs (uteri). Archaeological consensus is that they were used for **right of incubation** — young initiates were sealed inside the tomb to commune with the power/spirit of the giant supposedly buried beneath it. This is interpreted as Canaanite necromancy: attempting to contact or become inhabited by the disembodied spirits of dead Nephilim (per the Book of Enoch, these became demons).

## Church of Sant'Anastasia Excavation (c. 1978–1981)

Two excavators, interviewed **separately** by Alberino, gave identical accounts of work at the Church of Sant'Anastasia (Sardinia's capital region, Cagliari):

- Hired by the municipality to excavate the nuragic site beneath the Catholic church
- Discovered entire skeletal remains of giants (≥9 ft) alongside gold and silver jewelry, goblets, plates, and other artifacts
- All material placed inside the church on tables; **every night, unknown persons removed everything** — no artifacts were found on subsequent mornings
- One excavator laid out three complete giant skeletons (≥9 ft each) outside the church yard; they were gone the next morning
- The individual he reported to was affiliated with the **University of Cagliari** (according to the witness)
- One witness found a giant skull so large that he and his wife had to carry it together; the vertebrae was still attached; bodies were not bare skeletons but had preserved tissue/sinew
- One witness entered a hermetically sealed chamber inside a nuraghi tower (buried suddenly by what appeared to be a tidal wave deposit of earth) and found a **giant seated at a table, apparently mid-meal**: a giant plate of oysters, a giant goblet, giant furniture — all proportionally consistent with a 9–12 ft individual; the body began to crumble on exposure to air

## 101-Year-Old Witness (c. 1920s–30s)

A 101-year-old man (still spry, with translator) described how he was tilling a field to plant a vineyard near a nuraghi tower when workers simultaneously began unearthing dozens of giant bodies arranged in cross-pattern pairs. The field owner halted work and called authorities. The witness hid himself when vans arrived; he watched officials photograph the remains, load them into vans, examine and seal a downward passage in the nearby tower, and depart.

## Pattern

In every case described by Alberino:
- Remains photographed and removed by government/university representatives
- Workers instructed not to photograph and never to speak about it
- No photographic evidence remains in public hands (note: most discoveries occurred pre-digital era)

Alberino argues suppression is driven primarily by **occult interests** — an elite that uses giant remains for necromantic practices (Baal/Canaanite cult), not primarily by scientific embarrassment or biblical implications.
