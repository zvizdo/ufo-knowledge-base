# 1990 Calvine UFO Photograph

The Calvine photograph is one of the most significant suppressed UAP photographic cases in UK history. Two poachers (publicly described as "hikers") near Calvine, Scotland encountered and photographed a large diamond/disc-shaped object hovering silently, with a Harrier jet (99% confident identification) visible in the frame flying around the object.

## The Incident

- **Date**: 1990, Scotland
- **Location**: Remote area near Calvine (a small village in Perthshire, Scotland)
- **Witnesses**: Two unnamed poachers; hotel kitchen worker Richard Greaves was working with them at the time and separately witnessed the aftermath
- **Documentation**: 5-6 prints taken on 35mm film; witnesses contacted the local newspaper
- **Object**: Diamond/disc-shaped, hovering silently at close range; a Harrier jet flew around it — strongly suggesting RAF or military awareness of the object at the time
- **Departure**: Object took off "straight vertical, fast, faster, faster" and was gone

## MOD Suppression

- The local newspaper was immediately contacted; the MOD took over the investigation and suppressed the story
- MOD took all the prints from the newspaper and from the witnesses
- [[nick-pope]], who ran the MoD UFO desk at the time, confirmed a large poster of the Calvine photograph hung on the MOD wall during his tenure; he described it as "the best case he ever saw"

## Craig Lindsay — The Surviving Print

- [[craig-lindsay]] was an RAF Press Officer in Scotland in 1990; received a print from the local newspaper as part of his role
- After the MOD took over, Lindsay kept one print hidden for approximately 34 years without disclosing it
- [[david-clarke-ufologist]] (researcher) discovered Lindsay's existence through a declassified but redacted MOD document; broke the story to the public approximately two years before the Fox/Rogan interview
- Clarke introduced [[james-fox]] to Lindsay; Fox traveled to England, then to Scotland, to film Lindsay's first ever on-camera interview — now included in *The Program* (2024)

## Witness Intimidation

- **Richard Greaves** (hotel kitchen cook who worked with the poacher witnesses): 2-3 nights after the photograph, three men arrived at the hotel — a driver and two men in suits; they interrogated the witnesses in the rain outside
- Witnesses returned "white as ghosts," began drinking heavily, and disappeared from the area within weeks
- **Americans were reportedly also involved** in the intimidation — not just UK officials
- The intimidation pattern is consistent with [[project-beta]]-era disinformation operations

## JRIC Assessment — Official UK Government Finding (B6WFLOIkyho)

New detail from Pope's 2025 interview: the MoD held **6 photographs and the associated negatives** — not just a single image. The best of these was blown up to poster size and hung in Pope's office "for years."

The photographs were submitted to **[[jric]]** (Joint Air Reconnaissance and Intelligence Centre) — the UK military's imagery analysis body. The JRIC formal conclusion:

> "Yeah, it's real. It's whatever it is. It's like 75 ft across. It's a solid structured craft, but we have no idea what it is."

This is the UK government's **official imagery intelligence assessment**: the photographs were genuine, showing an unidentified solid structured craft approximately **75 feet across**. JRIC could not identify the craft.

Pope also commissioned a CGI recreation with a Los Angeles graphic artist; the result was "pretty similar to the Craig Lindsay photo" — providing independent corroboration that Pope's memory of the images is consistent with what Lindsay publicly disclosed.

When the Calvine file was eventually declassified, the released file contained only "a photocopy of a line drawing" — the original photographic documentation was withheld from the declassified release.

## Nick Pope's Confirmation

- Pope told Fox about the Calvine case in the late 1990s — among the earliest times Fox heard of it
- Pope confirmed he had a large poster of the photograph on the MOD wall during his UFO desk tenure
- Pope described Calvine as "the best case he ever saw" and the first public mention appeared in his book *Open Skies, Closed Minds*
