# 2007 Bledsoe Cape Fear River Encounter

**Date:** January 8, 2007
**Location:** Cape Fear River, near Fayetteville (Fateville), North Carolina — river bottom below a cornfield

The foundational event in [[chris-bledsoe]]'s contact history. Bledsoe had walked from the riverbank (where his son Ryan and three companions were fishing) up a quarter-mile to a hilltop cornfield to look for wildlife, at approximately 5:10 PM.

## Account

At the crest of the hill, Bledsoe saw **two large balls of fire** (~40 ft in diameter, red-orange, with flames curling off them and small tips shooting out) hovering approximately 100–200 ft above the treeline, ~300 yards away at the highway end of the field. He stood frozen between amazement and terror for 5–10 minutes.

When he turned to run back downhill, he looked back one final time. A **third orb** that had been invisible above him suddenly appeared and shot straight down in front of him. 5:15–5:30 PM in January — full daylight. The next thing Bledsoe registers is walking back to the riverbank approximately **4 hours later** at ~9:00 PM.

His son Ryan was missing — had spent the intervening hours searching for his father on foot and gotten lost. The companions were upset and confused.

## Physical Effects

Upon return, Bledsoe realized his **Crohn's disease** — suffered for 18 years, requiring up to 20 bathroom visits per day — had completely resolved. No recurrence.

## Witness Context

- Bledsoe holds a **commercial pilot's license** and could rule out conventional aircraft immediately.
- His companions were on the riverbank ~400 meters away during the core event; Ryan was the first to search for him.
- Ryan Bledsoe subsequently had his own encounter during the search.

## Aftermath

The event coincided with Bledsoe's financial collapse (housing bubble crash, lost home and income). The next **five years (2007–2012)** were characterized by community persecution in the heavily fundamentalist Christian local community: social services visits, children questioned at school, zero ability to film phenomena (the orbs would vanish whenever he raised a camera).

Memory recovery of the 4-hour period was accomplished years later through hypnotherapy with [[michael-oconnell]], a Harvard-trained psychiatrist trained by [[john-mack]]. The recovered memories began with imagery of Egypt — pyramids, Sphinx, Hathor's temple — and remained fragmentary.
