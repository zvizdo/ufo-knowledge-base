# 1967 Echo Flight UFO Incident

**Date**: ~March 16, 1967 (8 days before Oscar Flight)  
**Location**: Echo Flight launch control facility, [[malmstrom-air-force-base]]

## Account

[[walt-feele]] was on duty when one missile began shutting down. He called out to the launch facilities where maintenance and security personnel had stayed overnight (preparing for morning modification work). They reported: "We didn't do anything — and by the way, there are objects hovering above us." Shortly after, all 10 Echo Flight missiles went offline.

A strike team sent to one LF site later reported seeing "a large round object directly over the site."

This incident preceded [[1967-oscar-flight]] by approximately 8 days. [[robert-salas]] initially conflated his own Oscar Flight incident with this one because they are so structurally similar. [[fred-maywald]] clarified the distinction around 1996 when Salas first contacted him.

## Witnesses

- [[walt-feele]] — underground LCC, firsthand
- Maintenance and security personnel at LF sites — reported UFO objects overhead
- Strike team member — reported large round object over the LF site

## Evidence

- SAC HQ commissioned investigation; the investigative telegram (cited by Salas) names two [[boeing]] employees by name and states this is "of grave concern because we don't have any idea what happened"
- [[boeing]] bench investigation origin story: SAC's telegram about Echo Flight triggered the Boeing investigation that eventually concluded an unknown external signal caused the failures

## Aftermath

Wing command post told [[fred-maywald]] (post-Oscar Flight) that the same thing had happened at "another flight" — referring to this incident. [[condon-commission]] apparently never received the Echo Flight documentation either.

## Salas/Jameson Account (fzvwBBSmWYA)

In the Hastings interview, [[robert-salas]] describes receiving a call from his flight security controller reporting "strange lights in the sky making odd maneuvers," then a second call with the guard "screaming" about a red glowing object hovering over the front gate. Shortly after, missiles began going into no-go condition one by one. [[bob-jameson]] confirms: "All 10 missiles were off alert. All 10. Whole flight was off alert. All red."

A detail not previously documented: a **UFO was reportedly seen going down into a canyon near Belt, Montana** — the canyon walls were too steep to send men down at night, but helicopters were dispatched at daylight and the UAP "went right through the helicopters" and departed.
