# 2023 US Transport Facility UAP

**Date:** September 2023 (7:15–7:30 am)  
**Location:** US transport facility, restricted test area (location redacted); restricted airspace at time of incident  
**Witnesses:** At least 3 US-citizen contractors; primary witness = 15-year facility employee with extensive military aircraft/drone exposure  
**Investigation:** FBI 302 interviews (Serials 3, 4, 5); FBI Lab composite sketch (April 30, 2024)

## Description

Cigar-shaped object, **metallic bronze** in color, approximately **2–3 Blackhawk helicopters long** (~120–180 ft). Extremely bright diamond-white light with a ring visible at the eastern end, pointing southeast — described as "like looking into the sun." Object was hovering/nearly stationary then moving slowly **east to west**. Completely silent. Altitude approximately 500–3,000 feet above the tree line (~1 mile southwest of observer).

A second witness at longer range observed a bright white stationary light that moved right and disappeared in ~10 seconds.

## Gate Anomaly

Concurrent with the sighting, an electronic gate malfunctioned — opening partially on three consecutive attempts, then fully on the fourth. No prior or subsequent operational issues with the gate were noted.

## Investigation

FBI opened a formal investigation; 302 interviews conducted of at least three witnesses. The FBI Lab produced a composite sketch with site-photo overlay on April 30, 2024 (~7 months post-incident). Serial 5 is fully redacted; no content available. No photos or video taken by any witnesses.
