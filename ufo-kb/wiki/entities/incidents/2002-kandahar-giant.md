# Kandahar Giant Incident

Alleged US military encounter with a living giant humanoid in the Kandahar region of Afghanistan, resulting in the death of an entire reconnaissance team and the subsequent killing and airlifting of the giant's body. The story was first brought to public attention by researcher [[steve-quail]] and investigator [[tim-alberino]], and has since become one of the most widely circulated claims in the giants-Nephilim research community.

## Account

### Pilot Account (c. 2005)

A C-130 cargo pilot (unnamed, active duty at the time of interview, identity partially withheld) told the story first to Steve Quail and then was flown to Bozeman, Montana to be interviewed on camera — in silhouette — for the *True Legends* documentary series by Quail and Alberino.

According to the pilot:

- He was flying a routine classified mission to **Bagram Airfield** (~2005) to pick up what were typically described as "high-value assets" (e.g., Taliban detainees)
- Upon landing, he was met on the tarmac by individuals he described as "babysitters" — apparently air force or army intelligence — which was unusual; he normally went directly to the hangar
- Before any conversation, the babysitters told him: **"This never happened. No pictures. Don't ever talk about it."**
- A forklift brought over a large air-cargo pallet (~9 ft long). The pallet's cargo was covered by a tarp but parts protruded beyond the tarp's edge
- The first thing that struck him was the **smell**: a pungent odour of "body odour and death," unlike anything he had encountered
- He could see that the tarp did not fully cover the figure, which was in the **fetal position** but still extended past the edges of a 9-ft pallet
- Visible characteristics: **red hair**, **pale/white skin**, **six fingers on each hand**, **six toes on each foot**, feet wrapped in primitive canvas bindings
- Personnel were comparing their boots to the feet — the creature's feet were roughly two to three times the size of adult military boots
- The body felt **solid as a rock** when tapped — either extreme musculature or rigor mortis
- The load master weighed the pallet; the creature's body alone weighed approximately **1,100 lb**
- Estimated height based on fetal-position extension: **10–12 feet tall**
- The pilot was told second-hand (could not confirm directly): A recon team had gone missing in the Kandahar region. A second team found the first team at or near a cave — all dead. The giant was found **eating** one of the soldiers. The second team killed it
- The body was loaded onto the C-130 and transferred to **a base in Qatar**
- The pilot later heard through the grapevine (unconfirmed) that the body ended up at **[[wright-patterson]] Air Force Base**
- He reported withholding specific details from his public account and was able to use those details to vet subsequent witnesses (see below)

### Ellie Marculli / Special Ops Account (c. 2003)

[[ellie-marculli]] independently received an account from a special operations soldier (branch unspecified) describing a very similar incident:

- Date given: **2003** (vs. the pilot's ~2005 — discrepancy unresolved)
- A team went missing; a second team was sent to find them; they encountered the giant in a **cave**
- In this version, the giant **skewered one of the soldiers with a spear**
- Most other physical details aligned closely with the pilot's account

After Marculli published his account, the original C-130 pilot re-contacted Steve Quail and told him the Marculli source was telling the truth — confirmed using the withheld details as a verification key.

## Witnesses

- **C-130 cargo pilot** — primary witness; transported the body from Bagram to Qatar; interviewed in Bozeman, Montana by Alberino and Quail
- **Ellie Marculli's special ops source** — secondary independent witness; 2003 date, cave encounter, spear weapon
- **Additional informal corroborators** — several soldiers and officers have reportedly acknowledged the story to Alberino; at least one government contact reacted with visible alarm and warning when the subject was raised
- **Jesse Michaels' source** — host of American Alchemy noted that years before meeting Alberino, a mutual acquaintance told him of a special forces soldier who claimed to have fought a giant in Afghan caves — a wholly independent origin for the claim

## Evidence

- No photographs, film, or physical material in the public domain
- Pentagon reportedly issued a statement denying the story
- The weight (~1,100 lb), six-digit extremities, red hair, and pale skin are consistent across the two primary independent accounts
- Body reportedly disposed of / secured within classified military infrastructure (Wright-Patterson per hearsay)

## Aftermath

The story became widely circulated in the conspiracy and ancient giants research community. Alberino states it remains one of the most highly classified subjects he has encountered — generating more visible alarm in military/government contacts than UFO crash retrieval topics.

He argues the extreme secrecy is not primarily about biblical corroboration but about **occult interests** — a Baal-worshipping elite that uses giant remains for necromantic practices (see [[nephilim-giants-thesis]]).
