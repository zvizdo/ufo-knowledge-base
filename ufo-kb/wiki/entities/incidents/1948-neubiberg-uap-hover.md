# 1948 Neubiberg Air Base UAP Hover

An unidentified flying saucer hovered over **Neubiberg Air Base** — a USAF installation near Munich in the American occupation zone of West Germany — for approximately 30 minutes. The incident is cited in USAFE Teletype #1524 (USAFE 14, 4 Nov 1948) as one of a pattern of "recurring reports on flying saucers" across the European theater that the USAFE Directorate of Intelligence concluded "cannot be disregarded and must be explained on some basis which is perhaps slightly beyond the scope of our present intelligence thinking."

No further detail (altitude, shape, speed) is recorded in the teletype; the hover duration of 30 minutes is the primary data point.
