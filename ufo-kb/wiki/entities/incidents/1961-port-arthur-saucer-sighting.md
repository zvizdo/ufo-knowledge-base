# 1961 Port Arthur Saucer Sighting

## Account

In 1961, while being driven to De Queen Elementary School in Port Arthur, Texas by his mother, [[mario-woods]] (then approximately 5–6 years old) witnessed three white/silver flying saucers hovering approximately 25–50 ft in the air directly over a church at the corner of De Queen Blvd and the school entrance.

Woods's mother stopped the car when she saw them, saying "Look, Anthony — look at those flying saucers." She stepped out of the car, and Woods and his sister stood beside her watching. His sister said she was scared; his mother responded: "It'll be okay, honey — they're not here to hurt you."

The saucers were visible long enough for Woods to take a deliberate mental survey of the surroundings:
- All children on the adjacent playground (kindergarten through 12th grade) were pointing upward
- Teachers and students were leaning out of open windows of the school building pointing at the craft
- Nobody in this crowd of hundreds said a word about it afterward — the entire school resumed normal activity as if nothing had happened

### Craft Description

- Three craft, synchronized in behavior
- **White/silver** — classic saucer configuration (dome on top, dome on bottom)
- Lights around the outer rim, all synchronized
- Departure: straight up, all at once, extremely fast — from full visible size to a pinpoint to nothing in an instant
- No sound described
