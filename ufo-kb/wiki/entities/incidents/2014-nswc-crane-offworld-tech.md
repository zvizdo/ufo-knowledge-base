# 2014 NSWC Crane Off-World Technology Division

## Account

In March 2014, [[randy-anderson]] — a US Army Green Beret (18B Weapons Sergeant) — and one other unnamed Green Beret were taken underground at [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]] after excelling at an advanced weapons training course (Mark 44 minigun, SCAR iterations, armorer certification).

After completing standard foreign weapon review (captured Russian/Chinese systems), cadre escorted them through a second SCIF-level checkpoint and down a hallway to a section labeled **"Off-World Technology Division"** — a placard on the wall, either permanent or staged.

### Room Contents

**First artifact — the Levitating Orb:**
A metallic basketball-sized sphere hovered above a podium with visible gap beneath it. The metal appeared distinctly unnatural in appearance. Cadre stated it was:
- Recovered from a **crashed craft**
- A **control mechanism** for that craft
- Something that **interacted with consciousness**

See [[nswc-crane-levitating-orb]].

**Second artifact — the Gauntlet Device:**
A metallic arm-mounted object (gauntlet/shield form factor) sat on a table and bore a rectangular crystalline display screen. While Anderson looked at it, **shifting hieroglyphic symbols** appeared and cycled through sequences. Cadre noted someone was **harmed or killed during retrieval** due to an energy discharge from this device.

See [[nswc-crane-gauntlet-device]] and [[nhi-hieroglyphic-symbols]].

### Departure

The pair was returned upstairs and told not to discuss the experience with the rest of the training class or outside the program. No additional paperwork was signed. No further contact from cadre occurred.

## Witnesses

## Aftermath

The experience triggered a permanent worldview shift in Anderson. He entered meditation practice, studied consciousness research, connected with [[michael-herrera]], and eventually came forward publicly through [[jesse-michaels]]'s American Alchemy platform, citing [[david-grusch]]'s courage as precedent.
