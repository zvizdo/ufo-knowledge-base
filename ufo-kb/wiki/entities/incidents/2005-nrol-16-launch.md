# 2005 NROL-16 Launch

The **NROL-16** was a classified [[nro]] satellite launch on **April 30, 2005** (Walpurgisnacht) at **12:50 AM** from Cape Canaveral, Florida.

## Parameters (per John Ramirez)

According to former CIA officer [[john-ramirez]], the launch had the following features:

| Parameter | Value | Occult Significance |
|-----------|-------|---------------------|
| Date | April 30 (Walpurgisnacht) | Traditional pagan holiday: gates between worlds open |
| Time | 12:50 AM (~midnight) | Necronomicon: best time for "ceremony of walking" |
| Direction | Northeast (toward Big Dipper) | Necronomicon: face north/northeast when Big Bear rises |
| Payload name | Prometheus | Fire-bringer; associated with esoteric knowledge transfer |
| Rocket | Titan 4 | — |
| Patch | Pelican, sun, 5 pentagrams, Bigfoot, palm trees | 5 pentagrams; number 5 sacred per Necronomicon ritual |
| Year | 5th year of millennium | Number 5 alignment |
| Pelican | Aviary code name of CIA scientist Ron Pandolfi | Identifies Pandolfi as involved |

Ramirez claims **[[ron-pandolfi]]** was directly involved in this launch, and that the pelican on the patch was specifically Pandolfi's aviary identifier.

## Necronomicon Alignment

The **[[necronomicon]]** (1977 grimoire by Simon) describes a ritual called the **"ceremony of walking"** — a spiritual journey to the stars — as most effective:
- Around midnight
- Facing north, with emphasis on **northeast**
- When the **Great Bear hangs by its tail** in the sky
- The number **5 is sacred**

The NROL-16 launch matches these parameters on every axis. Peter Levenda has noted receiving letters from inside the US military praising the Necronomicon as "spot on," including from Strategic Air Command officers.

## Aftermath

Following this launch, the **remaining Titan 4 rockets were retired**:
- One went to the **National Museum of the US Air Force** at [[wright-patterson]] Air Force Base
- One went to **McMinnville, Oregon** — the site of one of the most famous UAP photographs ever taken (1950)

## Significance

NROL-16 is the most concrete 21st-century data point for the claim that US space/intelligence launches are deliberately structured around occult ritual templates. Whether this reflects individual actors (Pandolfi, others), institutional practice, or coincidence is unresolved.
