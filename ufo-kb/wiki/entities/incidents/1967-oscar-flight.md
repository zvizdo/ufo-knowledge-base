# 1967 Oscar Flight UFO Incident

**Date**: March 24, 1967  
**Location**: Oscar Flight launch control facility, near Roy, Montana (~100 miles east of Great Falls), [[malmstrom-air-force-base]]

## Account

Around 9:00 PM, [[robert-salas]] (on duty in the underground LCC, with commander [[fred-maywald]] resting) received a call from the topside Flight Security Controller (FSC) reporting strange lights — moving at high speed, stopping in midair, reversing course, executing 90° turns with no engine noise.

Salas initially dismissed the report. Five minutes later the FSC called back "frightened, screaming, babbling" — he had called all guards out, weapons drawn, pointed at a large red-orange pulsating oval object hovering at the perimeter. The FSC described it as roughly 40 feet long with what appeared to be a solid oval object inside the light envelope. One guard had been injured (unrelated to the UFO — fence climbing). The FSC hung up to attend to the injury.

Salas went to wake Maywald. Before he could fully explain, the alarm sounded — one missile went from green (ready) to red (no-go). Immediately after, all 10 went down one by one. The failure code on all 10: **guidance and control system failure**.

Guards sent to two of the launch facilities reported incursion lights (possible intrusion). Additional guards sent out observed bright lights hovering above two LF sites and lost radio contact returning.

Salas called topside: the red-orange object had departed. Wing command post was notified. [[boeing]] was commissioned to investigate. Maywald was told by the command post that the same thing had happened "at another flight" (the [[1967-echo-flight]] incident eight days earlier).

## Evidence

- SAC HQ investigative telegram characterizing the event as "of grave concern because we don't have any idea what happened"
- [[boeing]] bench test: confirmed a guidance logic coupler could be triggered by an externally injected signal but could not explain how such a signal penetrated triple-shielded cable simultaneously across 10 missiles
- All 10 missiles restarted smoothly — as if nothing had happened — ruling out physical damage
- [[lewis-chase]]'s contemporaneous written reports to USAF HQ and the [[condon-commission]] documenting the UFO-missile connection (later denied by Chase in Craig interview)
