# 1965 Kecksburg Crash

On December 9, 1965, a fireball was seen across multiple US states before an unknown object came down near Kecksburg, Pennsylvania. Military personnel arrived quickly, cordoned off the area, and removed something on a flatbed truck. The official explanation — variously a Soviet satellite, a meteor, or nothing at all — has been rejected by researchers who have investigated the case.

## Account

Multiple local witnesses described an acorn-shaped object with writing or markings around its base (described as hieroglyphic-like). The object sat in a depression in the woods. Military personnel arrived in force and secured the area, and the object was removed.

Local radio journalist [[frank-murphy]] was among those who attempted to cover the story and had his career destroyed as a result. Dolan: "It wrecked his life. It destroyed his life."

## Dolan's Assessment

[[richard-dolan]] is unequivocal: "I don't think [it was a Soviet satellite]. I do not believe that. I do not believe that at all." He credits [[stan-gordon]]'s multi-decade investigation with establishing the best available case: "I think he's got the goods on it."

Kecksburg appears in Dolan's "real or hoax" rapid-fire: **Real**. He notes some researchers compare the acorn shape to the Nazi [[die-glocke]] device — Dolan pushes back on this: the Glocke was not a flying device, it was a high-voltage torsion chamber. The shape similarity may be superficial.

## NASA FOIA Battle

[[stan-gordon]] and others pursued NASA through FOIA for records related to Kecksburg. NASA eventually acknowledged it had records and then claimed they had been "lost" — a standard suppression technique.

## Presidential Science Advisor on Site

[[kevin-knuth]] (physicist, University at Albany) discovered that his father's cousin, [[donald-hornig]] — who served as **presidential science advisor to Eisenhower, Kennedy, and Johnson** and designed the electronic triggers for the Trinity plutonium bomb — is listed as **present** in an official government document relating to the Kecksburg crash. President Johnson was in office on December 9, 1965. Hornig being dispatched to or briefed at such an event would be procedurally consistent with his role.

[[eric-walker]] (president of Penn State; alleged Majestic-12 adjacent figure) was also reportedly present at Kecksburg.
