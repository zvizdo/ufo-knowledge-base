# 1964 Vandenberg CE3 — Richard Barth

In **September 1964**, USAF air policeman [[richard-barth]], while on night guard duty at Minuteman missile site D5 (D-sector), [[vandenberg-afb]], experienced a CE3 encounter with non-human intelligence.

## Account

Around 2:00am a humanoid figure in a military-style trench coat emerged from dense fog toward Barth's guard shack. As it approached, Barth felt his right arm paralyzed and was unable to draw his weapon or scream. He backed into the shack until pressed against the rear wall, then lost consciousness.

He regained awareness aboard a circular craft hovering above the base. The room had a smooth, seamless, gray molded interior (no seams, no rivets — described as "one giant piece of plastic"), a concave floor, integrated bench seating, and a 2.5–3 foot transparent aperture in the floor. Through the aperture, Barth observed site D11 across the street from D5: dawn light, a flatbed truck with pipes arriving, a guard opening the gate. Four silhouetted NHI beings sat on the bench — large round heads with baby-like proportions, proportionate necks, large dark eyes, backlighting from the wall creating silhouettes. The beings observed him silently. The humanoid entity to his right (the same figure he encountered on the ground?) communicated by telepathy (no words, direct knowing): *"Don't look at me. Just look through the hole."*

Barth was then returned to the guard shack porch — lying with feet on the ground, head in the lap of a being. The entity communicated an apology for the fright, explained the need for data from the missile's guidance electronics room, said it was leaving, instructed Barth to keep his eyes shut. Barth complied. Sun was rising when he finally opened his eyes; he felt overwhelming love and connection toward a chipmunk nearby.

Total time: approximately 2am–6am (3–4 hours of lost time).

## Timing Convergence with Bob Jacobs Incident

This encounter falls in September 1964 — the same month as the [[1964-vandenberg-bob-jacobs]] filmed incident, in which [[bob-jacobs]]'s team photographed a UAP disabling a test missile from the Big Sur telescope site. The two events are independent — Barth and Jacobs did not know each other at the time — and together suggest elevated NHI activity at Vandenberg in September 1964.

Earlier in 1964 (May), Barth also observed a UAP "tagalong" tracking an Atlas missile launch at Vandenberg from below (different from the CE3; no entity contact). This is consistent with the broader [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] pattern of routine surveillance.

## NHI Message

Three elements communicated to Barth:
1. They needed intelligence from the missile guidance electronics.
2. They will not allow nuclear war; Earth is important to them; they will intervene.
3. Multiple non-human species exist; not all are as benevolent; control of the situation would shift if this group disabled the missiles.
