# 2023 Arabian Gulf UAP D23

Two UAP contacts during a single 50ATKS ISR mission supporting OP SPARTAN SHIELD (NAVCENT), 24 October 2023.

## Observations

**UAP 1 — 0241Z**
- Location: MGRS 39RYJ vicinity, Arabian Gulf
- Speed: ~320 MPH (estimated)
- Physical state: Solid
- **Thermal signature: COLD** — no detectable heat emission
- Shape: Unknown
- Maneuverability: None; no intelligent control

**UAP 2 — 0322Z** (41 minutes later, MGRS 39RXK6)
- Speed: ~440 MPH (estimated)
- Physical state: Solid
- Thermal signature: Unknown
- Shape: Unknown
- Maneuverability: None; no intelligent control

Both: Benign assessment; no effects on personnel or equipment; no materials recovered.

## Geopolitical Context

Iranian Air Defense issued a guard call to the aircraft at 0145Z: "MAINTAIN SAFE DISTANCE FROM BORDER." The UAP observations occurred ~1 hour later. Both entities may represent Iranian drone/surveillance assets, unknown technology, or a third category. The cold thermal signature on UAP 1 is inconsistent with any known conventional propulsion system.
