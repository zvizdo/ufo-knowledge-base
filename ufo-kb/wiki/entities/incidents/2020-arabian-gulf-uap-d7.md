# 2020 Arabian Gulf Balloon-Like UAP Weapons Track (AARO D7)

Balloon-like UAP at 31,000 ft MSL traveling with winds. Crew obtained a weapons-quality 1 radar track, performed a simulated intercept ("next to shoot"), and visually identified the UAP via TFLIR. Similar to a prior 48th Fighter Wing (RAF Lakenheath, UK) report.
