# 1948 Netherlands Unidentified Aircraft (307th Bomb Group)

Three crews of the 307th Bomb Group, participating in **Operation Daggar**, observed an unidentified aircraft at 1402Z on September 5, 1948, off the west coast of Holland at coordinates 5155N/0355E, altitude 30,000 ft. The object was initially cruising at a normal jet speed on heading 120°. It then produced smoke trails and condensation trails while undergoing sudden acceleration and steep climb. The observers generally agreed it appeared to be "a single jet propelled A/C employing probably rocket assists with tremendous reserve power, more than normal cruising speed for jets of the 1947 variety." It never came within identification range and its course "did not indicate its purpose." Evaluation: **B-2**.

Reported by USAFE OI OB to USAFE Directorate of Intelligence via TT #1524 (USAFE 10, 4 Nov 1948); forwarded to [[cp-cabell|Gen. Cabell]], USAF Director of Intelligence.
