# 2017 NYT UAP Story

On December 16, 2017, the New York Times published "Glowing Auras and 'Black Money': The Pentagon's Mysterious U.F.O. Program," written by investigative journalists Helene Cooper, [[ralph-blumenthal]], and [[leslie-kean]]. The article disclosed the existence of the Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program ([[aatip]]), a $22 million black-budget program funded between 2007 and 2012 through a Senate earmark secured by Senator [[harry-reid]] and administered through Bigelow Aerospace. The article was accompanied by the release of the Nimitz "FLIR1" cockpit video, with the Gimbal and GOFAST videos following in subsequent weeks.

The story had been in preparation for months and was coordinated with former AATIP director Luis Elizondo and [[chris-mellon]], both of whom had joined the To The Stars Academy (TTSA) to drive controlled disclosure. The publication broke a roughly 50-year period during which major mainstream news outlets had treated UFO/UAP coverage as professionally toxic. Within days it was front-page news globally, prompted official DoD comment, and set in motion a congressional response that within three years produced the UAP Task Force mandate and eventually [[aaro]]. It is the single most consequential media event in UAP history since the 1947 Roswell coverage.
