# 1949 Hickam AFB Flying Disc Sighting

On January 4, 1949, Capt. Paul R. Stoney (AO 36278), Assistant S-2 Officer of the 1810th AACS Group at Hickam AFB, observed a round flat white oscillating disc from approximately 1407–1435 local time (28 minutes). The object appeared to be "under absolute control at all times," executed wide, decisive turns with rhythmical oscillation completing approximately once per second, and departed climbing on a straight course of 25° magnetic. Filed SECRET with full investigative follow-up.

## Details

- Observer: Capt. Stoney, Asst. S-2 Officer, 1810th AACS Group — a twin-engine pilot and intelligence officer
- Duration: 28 minutes (1407–1435 local)
- Object: large round piece of flat white disc, oscillating rhythmically, ~AT-6 size
- Top surface: dark, non-reflecting; underside: brilliant white
- Speed: ~85 mph during observation; increased on departure
- Departure: straight course 25° magnetic, climbing
- "Definite gyrations being executed" — "under absolute control at all times"
- Follow-up: C-54 in area investigated as possible source; ruled out. Weather service confirmed updrafts insufficient to explain described behavior.
- Commanding officer's assessment: Stoney is "an extremely cool and level-headed person, who is not given to the making of rash statements or using poor judgment"
- Classification: SECRET
