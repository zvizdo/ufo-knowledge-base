# 1967 Shag Harbor USO Incident

One of the most significant USO cases in the public record. In October 1967, multiple witnesses observed a UAP descend into the waters off Shag Harbor, Nova Scotia. Canadian naval vessels were dispatched to investigate.

## Account

- Multiple civilian witnesses observed a craft or set of lights descend into Shag Harbor
- Canadian Navy ships were vectored to the location
- Canadian Navy divers investigated the underwater site
- Divers reported observing two **disc-shaped craft underwater**, with what appeared to be occupants outside the craft appearing to **make repairs** to one of the discs
- Video footage was allegedly captured of the underwater objects
- The location and fate of the video tapes are reportedly unknown

## Associated NATO Encounter

[[tim-gallaudet]] notes that the Shag Harbor incident is linked to an earlier NATO mine warfare exercise (early 1960s) during which UFOs were seen entering the water near Canada. The two events are documented together in the book [[sweep-clear-five]].

## Documentation

The primary documentation is the book **[[sweep-clear-five]]** (subtitle: *NATO's Underwater UFO Encounter*), written by a researcher who interviewed the Canadian divers involved in the investigation. Gallaudet cites this as one of the most compelling underwater UAP cases in the historical record.

> Note: The Shag Harbor incident is also documented in other sources outside this interview and is well-established in UFO research literature.

## Significance

The unique aspects of this case:
- **Multi-witness civilian sighting** of descending UAP
- **Official naval investigation** with divers deployed
- **Firsthand diver testimony** of seeing two craft and apparent occupants conducting repairs — not just an anonymous contact but close visual observation
- **Video evidence allegedly captured** but subsequently lost/unavailable
