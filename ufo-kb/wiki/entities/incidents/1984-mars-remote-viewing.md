# 1984 CIA Mars Remote Viewing Session

Declassified [[stargate-program]] session in which [[joe-mcmoneagle]] was tasked with remote viewing sealed coordinates on Mars, with the target date set to "approximately 1 million BC." The session transcript is publicly available.

## Account

McMoneagle describes viewing huge pyramid structures, a degrading atmosphere, and extremely tall humanoid figures (approximately 12 feet) appearing to be in distress or dying. He reported ~60 photographs of Martian surface structures held by him as corroboration (JPL negatives provided post-session were said to match described areas).

## Evidence

- Declassified session transcript available via FOIA.
- McMoneagle claims JPL negatives post-session matched his described areas.
