# 1945 San Antonio (Trinity) Crash

An alleged crash retrieval event near San Antonio, New Mexico, documented by [[jacques-vallee]] and [[paola-harris]] in their book *[[trinity-best-kept-secret]]*. The crash occurred approximately **20 days after the first atomic test** (July 16, 1945, [[1945-trinity-test]]) and **two days after Japan's formal concession** — placing it in mid-August 1945, in the immediate aftermath of the Hiroshima and Nagasaki bombings.

## Account

A metallic, avocado-shaped object fell from the sky near San Antonio, New Mexico — roughly adjacent to the Trinity test site. The craft lost one panel on impact but retained structural integrity. Two sons of local cattle ranchers — [[remy-baca]] (age 7) and [[jose-padilla]] (age 9) — observed the craft from a distance through the gap created by the missing panel. Inside, they saw **three humanoid creatures**: approximately three feet tall, two eyes, two arms, three fingers, two legs. The beings moved by sliding rather than walking in a conventional bipedal gait.

Remy (age 7) refused to approach; Jose entered the area. The craft was large enough to have a flat floor; Vallée estimated it at three to four tons.

## Significance to the Field

Vallée identifies three reasons the Trinity case updates modern ufology:

1. **Pre-dates the term "flying saucer"** — The encounter occurred in 1945; Kenneth Arnold coined "flying saucer" in 1947 (the same year as Roswell). The UFO story in America — indeed in the world — precedes the post-war vocabulary invented to describe it. This supports Vallée's core thesis (*[[passport-to-magonia]]*) that the phenomenon has been occurring continuously for millennia and is not a product of the postwar atomic/space age.

2. **Unknown to Pentagon-cleared officials** — When Vallée investigated, no one he knew with Above Top Secret clearances had heard of the case. His conclusion: the classification track that holds the Trinity materials is not the Pentagon SAP system, but a separate atomic-energy lineage: **Manhattan Project → Atomic Energy Commission → Department of Energy** — with its own distinct clearance tier. [[chris-mellon]] provides independent corroboration: he nearly met a source from the program (inside a commercial aerospace company) who died of a heart attack two weeks before the meeting.

3. **Paradigmatic absurdity** — A three-ton avocado crash 20 days after the first nuclear test, witnessed by children, apparently unknown to most of the national security establishment, managed by a separate classification apparatus — Vallée frames this as the *Zen koan* of crash retrievals: "it stops your brain." The behavior is not *take me to your leader*; it is conspicuously oblique. See [[control-system-hypothesis]] and [[trickster-phenomenon]].

## The Craft's Interior

Jose Padilla recalled that the craft's floor was flat and that the vehicle appeared to have an engine or propulsion system somewhere within its oval form. The interior geometry was consistent with a three-to-four ton object, implying significant internal volume.

## Witnesses' Later Testimony

[[paola-harris]] — who introduced the case to Vallée after locating it via Italian UFO research networks — had already interviewed both witnesses (by then elderly men) and possessed audio recordings before co-authoring the book with Vallée. Their accounts remained consistent across decades.

## Clearance Architecture Implied

The absence of any Pentagon awareness suggests a strict compartmentalization by energy/atomic classification rather than intelligence/military. Vallée and Harris speculate that **Oppenheimer** (as Manhattan Project director) would have had custody, with the material transitioning to the AEC and then the DOE. The DOE's "born secret" provision under the Atomic Energy Act creates a parallel classification universe invisible to standard DoD SAP oversight. See [[atomic-energy-act-classification]].
