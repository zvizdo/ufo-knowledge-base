# 1949 San Andres Islands Caribbean Disc Sighting

On February 6–7, 1949, Capt. McBride of the S.S. Antigua (United Fruit Line) tracked three disc-shaped objects near the San Andres Islands in the Caribbean by sextant. The objects displayed a sequential color shift from silver to yellow to red. The US 6th Weather Squadron analysis of the sighting included an explicit investigation of two Soviet 300-ton sloops in the area — the Omar and the Blesk — as a possible source.

## Details

- Observer: Capt. McBride, Master of S.S. Antigua, United Fruit Line
- Number of objects: 3 disc-shaped objects
- Tracking method: sextant (precision navigation instrument)
- Color progression: silver → yellow → red
- Investigative follow-up: 6th Weather Squadron explicitly investigated two Soviet 300-ton sloops (Omar and Blesk) in the area as possible source; outcome not recorded in available documents
- Report filed by: 6th Weather Squadron, Canal Zone
