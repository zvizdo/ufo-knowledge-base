# 2024 Langley AFB Incursion

## Account

A 17-day period in late 2024 during which drone-like objects repeatedly entered restricted airspace over **Langley Air Force Base** (Hampton, Virginia). Witnesses described the objects as sounding like lawnmowers — slow, relatively large drones — flying over a strategically critical installation (Langley houses the Air Combat Command and F-22 Raptor squadrons).

[[chris-mellon]] publicly stated that the incident may have involved a **mothership** — a larger craft from which smaller craft were being deployed, hovering beyond detection range.

## Pais's Interpretation

In [[8TYMQOUDQBo]], Pais is asked about the Langley incursion. He poses a crucial diagnostic question:

> "Was anybody killed? Yes or no?"

His logic: if no one was killed, there should be an attributable explanation — i.e., the technology was man-made. If it were ET craft deliberately testing military defenses (the battlefield-preparation hypothesis advanced by [[lue-elizondo]]), Pais implies the behavior might have been more aggressive or conclusive. He declines to speculate further.

This creates a useful diagnostic framework: lethality (or lack thereof) as an ET vs. man-made discriminator for anomalous incursions.
