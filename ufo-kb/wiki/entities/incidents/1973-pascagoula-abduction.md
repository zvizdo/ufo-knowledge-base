# 1973 Pascagoula Abduction

The Pascagoula abduction is one of the most evidentially solid CE4 (contact with beings) cases in UFO history. On an October 1973 evening, [[charles-hixon]] (in his 40s) and [[calvin-parker]] (age 19) were fishing near Pascagoula, Mississippi when they encountered a dome- or cone-shaped craft hovering above the water. Both were taken aboard.

## The Encounter

The craft hovered and the two men were taken by entities described as distinctly bizarre-looking — with "carrot ears" and features that did not match the popular "grey alien" template. [[richard-dolan]] regards this non-standard appearance as a point in favor of authenticity: witnesses reporting what the cultural zeitgeist did *not* expect.

## Police Hidden Recording Test

When Hixon and Parker reported the incident to local police, the deputy sheriff placed them in a room with a hidden recorder. They did not know they were being recorded. Their private conversation showed Parker in visible distress — "freaking out" — and both men processing the experience sincerely, without any performance for an audience. Law enforcement concluded they were "totally convinced" the witnesses were honest and sincere.

This is one of the most unusual credibility tests in abduction case history: an organic, unguarded reaction captured by police before the witnesses knew their story would become public.

## Geographic Context

Pascagoula at the time was home to the **largest nuclear submarine production facility in the United States**. [[richard-dolan]] notes this explicitly and plans to develop the connection further in volume 2 of his USO book. The pattern of UAP/USO interest in nuclear installations — documented across many cases — gives this sighting's location additional interpretive significance.

## Subsequent USO Incident

Within a week or two of the Hixon/Parker encounter, a **genuine USO incident** was reported in the same Pascagoula area. Dolan: "That is in my book and it's a good it's a really good case." This sequential pairing of an abduction encounter and a USO incident in the same small geographic window is unusual.

## Calvin Parker's Late Disclosure

[[calvin-parker]] largely disappeared from public view after 1973. He re-emerged toward the end of his life and independently confirmed that he too had been abducted — not merely present alongside Hixon. This late-life confirmation was made without obvious incentive and adds to the case's weight.
