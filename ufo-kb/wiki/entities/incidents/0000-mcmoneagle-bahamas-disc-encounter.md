# McMoneagle Bahamas Disc Encounter

Undated firsthand encounter reported by [[joe-mcmoneagle]] and corroborated by the hospitalization of his colleague [[steve-roberts-bahamas|Steve Roberts]]. Occurred during McMoneagle's first military assignment at a **downrange tracking station from Cape Canaveral** on what he calls "the Luther" — likely **Eleuthera Island** in the Bahamas (a historical US tracking station location). The Bahamas government was in the process of going independent from Britain; US personnel remained at the tracking facilities.

**Approximate date:** Mid-to-late 1960s, based on McMoneagle's career timeline (first assignment, pre-Vietnam main tours, before Fort Meade assignment). Exact year unknown.

## Encounter

McMoneagle and [[steve-roberts-bahamas|Steve Roberts]] watched a double feature at an outdoor theater on the base and had drinks at the bar afterward. Instead of walking the mile down the road to quarters, they took a shortcut across sand dunes behind the tracking station. Midway across:

- "The whole place lit up like high noon"
- Looking up: **a slowly rotating disc**; panel lines visible on the surface; individual lights
- **A cone of light** projected downward onto them
- Duration: approximately 8 seconds
- "Before we could adjust our eyes, it disappeared"

McMoneagle: "It could have just folded out of time-space. I don't know." His sense at the time: "It sped away." After reflection: the speed was so extreme it may have been instantaneous disappearance rather than rapid departure.

## Physical Effects

**Steve Roberts:**
- Taken by ambulance to **Homestead Air Force Base hospital** at 4:00 AM the following morning
- Burned so severely that the **pattern of his shirt was burned into his chest**
- Roberts was pale-skinned (rarely outdoors), which made the burns more severe than McMoneagle's
- Retained no memory of the encounter; only a confabulated account involving "a red truck" and a hot radiator

**Joe McMoneagle:**
- Woke feeling like he had the flu
- Saw sick call at the naval facility; Navy medic stopped him mid-account when he mentioned a "circular thing," **tore up the paperwork**, told him never to bring it up again, and gave him iodide pills to take for two weeks — implying suspected radiation poisoning
- McMoneagle was dark-skinned from extensive diving, which reduced his visible injury

**Equipment:**
- All tracking station screens were "whited out" — pure snow — for the **entire duration of the encounter**; nothing was recorded

**Spatial anomaly:**
- McMoneagle clearly remembers standing next to a small palm tree (knee-high, growing from a coconut placed in the sand) when the light appeared. When it disappeared: the palm tree was in a noticeably different position. McMoneagle: "We walk through some kind of a time shift or something. My watch was the same time — there's no way to check that."

## Memory Effects — Steve Roberts

When McMoneagle later visited Roberts in the hospital and asked what he remembered: "What do you mean?" Roberts had no memory of the movies, the bar, or the shortcut. His only account: "I took the top off a radiator on a red truck. It sprayed me with hot water — that's how I got my burns." McMoneagle: "There's no red trucks on the base. They're all Navy blue or white." Years later, when McMoneagle introduced Roberts to his wife during a vacation: Roberts still maintained only the "red truck" account. "To this day, it's a red truck."

## Aftermath and Recruitment

When [[joe-mcmoneagle]] later told [[hal-puthoff]] about this encounter during Stargate recruitment discussions, Puthoff's response was immediate: **"We want Joe in this group."** The Bahamas encounter was explicitly cited by McMoneagle as a direct contributor to his recruitment into the [[stargate-program]].
