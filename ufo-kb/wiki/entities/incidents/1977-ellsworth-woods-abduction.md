# 1977 Ellsworth AFB Mario Woods Abduction

## Account

On the night of November 5 (week of November 18), 1977, [[mario-woods]] (age 23) and partner Michael Johnson were dispatched from November 1 Launch Control Facility at approximately 12:20–12:25 AM to investigate a dual-prong alarm at [[november-5-silo]] — a Minuteman II ICBM facility 8–10 miles away by road. Ambient temperature: 9–13°F.

### Pre-Encounter: Light-Flashing Sequence

Earlier that evening (~9:00–9:40 PM), Woods had observed a stationary light 5–7 miles to the east from the front of the facility. He flashed the facility's exterior lights three times in succession. The object responded with the same pattern. This exchange occurred four times before the object went dark. Bill Hollman (Staff Sgt.) and Michael Johnson both witnessed the object, though Johnson was unimpressed.

### Arrival at November 5

Traveling on clay roads through the prairie, Woods spotted a pulsating orange-yellow-white glow from miles away. As they rounded the final dog-leg turn on the access road, they found a massive sphere hovering approximately 10 ft above the blast door — described as the size of a large Walmart building. The energy surface resembled a boiling/plasma effect; no tail sections, wings, portholes, or thrusters.

### Close Encounter

Upon pulling within ~60 ft at a 45° angle to the cattle gate, an intense light was directed at the vehicle. Woods could see the veins behind his eyelids. He lost the ability to breathe. He prayed for relief, leaned out the window with his flashlight, and flashed at the object — which produced partial relief.

A black ball approximately six times the size of a beach ball (with long vent-like markings) then shot over the hood of the F-150 pickup and circled the vehicle rapidly — front, back, left, right — before vanishing.

A strong ammonia-like stench permeated the sealed cab. Woods experienced tunnel vision and began losing consciousness.

### Beings

Through the right side window, as he was blacking out, Woods observed:
- **Three small Grays**: identical height, identical appearance; wearing small uniform-like garments; gliding above the ground (no walking); one held a small wand with a yellow tip at the waist belt
- **One tall Gray**: a chest and a head taller than the others; had a pulsating chest device matching the color of the wand tip; appeared to be in command of the others

Telepathic transmission: the phrase/feeling **"Do not fear"** traveled through Woods's body as a wave-like vibration — no sound, no ambiguity, repeated.

### Missing Time

Woods lost consciousness. When he came to, it was total darkness — the craft and its light were gone. Michael Johnson was still rigid, hands locked on the steering wheel, eyes open, non-responsive. The vehicle was parked 8.5 miles from November 5, in mud, behind the center of [[new-lake-reservoir-dam]] — in temperatures that froze everything else. The dam road is only 5 ft wide; the truck was in the only position from which it could exit.

Radio check-ins (Woods made ~20–25 one-minute checks) were used by Wing Security Control to triangulate location. It was getting light to the east by the time Sgt. Garza's alert team arrived. Missing time: approximately 5 hours.

### Aftermath

Garza escorted them back to November 1. Michael Johnson, still catatonic, was separated from Woods on arrival. Woods was debriefed intensively by Master Sgt. Joel Gray, Sgt. Hawkins, and later by Col. Spraker (commander), Capt. Jack Reed (OSI lead), [[rick-doty]] (brand-new OSI Counter Intelligence officer), and an unidentified man in a gray tweed suit.

Woods **did not** report the beings he saw — he disclosed everything else. Had he mentioned CE3 details, he would have lost his Personal Reliability Program (PRP) clearance, his Q clearance, and his job. He signed a non-disclosure agreement.

Flight surgeon examination: burns above right eyebrow (matching arc of his Air Force glasses) and on the back of his right hand (no glove worn). Skin samples taken. **Gums/teeth specifically inspected** — a detail that connects to [[andrija-puharich]]'s bone-induction dental implant research.

Rick Doty's stated purpose: to retrieve the medical records of both Woods and Johnson and bring them to his office.

## Aftermath

Woods reported to AARO in 2023. Michael Johnson was never found. Rick Doty later came forward as an OSI disinformation agent, running the [[paul-benowitz]] psyop. Woods connected with him via email after ~5 years. Doty confirmed being present at the debrief.

Woods underwent two hypnotic regression sessions: Bob Upton (first; triggered AFib requiring cardioversion) and Yavon Smith (California). The regression revealed time inside the craft, the force-field gurney, the museum room, and the wrist procedure.

In 2021, Woods returned to the sites with a documentary film team. Confirmed New Lake Reservoir Dam and November 5 silo locations. Multiple local Newell residents observed the filming.
