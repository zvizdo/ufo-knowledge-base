# 1957 Väddö Island Incident

Two carpenters were driving late at night on Väddö Island (northeast of Stockholm) when they saw a craft coming from the east, making a U-turn, and hanging motionless in front of their car. The car's engine died completely. The occupants sat watching the object until it departed the way it came. They then got out and found a **metallic piece on the road** — so hot it burned their hands. The car restarted, and they drove away.

## The Artifact

The metallic piece was analyzed and identified as **tungsten carbide (wolframite)**:
- Impurity profile consistent with 1957-era tungsten production — earthly metallurgy
- Tungsten is an excellent heat conductor and should equilibrate to ambient temperature quickly
- The fact that it was burning hot in the middle of a cold Swedish night strongly corroborates that the craft actively heated or ejected it

The physical artifact is held by the [[archives-for-the-unexplained]] (AFU). [[claus-svahn]] personally analyzed the piece and presented it during the American Alchemy episode [[Rjr3Yq-dMUE]].

## Significance

- One of the rare Swedish cases where a **physical artifact survives** and has been independently analyzed
- The earthly metallurgy rules out exotic alloy claims but doesn't rule out UAP involvement — the anomalous heat signature remains unexplained
