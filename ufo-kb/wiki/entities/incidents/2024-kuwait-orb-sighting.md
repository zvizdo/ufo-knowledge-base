# Kuwait Orb Sighting (Helicopter Video)

A **single-source report** from [[michael-shellenberger]] describes a classified **high-definition, full-color helicopter video** showing an orb emerging from the ocean approximately 20 miles off the coast of Kuwait. The orb is then joined by a second orb. The video was shot from a military helicopter and is described as high-quality — "high definition full color" — on equipment that would be expected on a military helicopter platform.

## Significance

Shellenberger uses this case to undermine the "methods protection" rationale for UAP non-disclosure: helicopters and HD cameras are not classified technologies, so their involvement cannot justify secrecy. This implies the real reasons for non-disclosure are something else — which he lists as ego, politics, legacy, potential criminality, and ethics concerns.

The case is also notable as an **ocean emergence** event — the orb comes out of the water rather than descending from above — placing it in the broader [[uso-ocean-portals]] category. The Persian Gulf location is strategically sensitive, overlapping with areas of US military operations.

## Corroboration Status

Single-source at time of publication. Shellenberger is careful to flag this throughout his Immaculate Constellation reporting. Not corroborated by other witnesses or independent journalism as of 2026-04-26.
