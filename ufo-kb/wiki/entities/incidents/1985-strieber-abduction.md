# 1985 Strieber Abduction

[[whitley-strieber]]'s foundational NHI contact experience at his [[cabin-upstate-new-york]] on the night of December 26, 1985. Strieber and his family were staying at their upstate New York cabin when he experienced missing time and found himself outside with no memory of how he got there. Under subsequent hypnotic regression sessions conducted with Budd Hopkins's assistance, Strieber recalled an encounter with non-human beings — a variety of entities including small beings he described as having large, almond-shaped dark eyes.

**Communion and cultural impact**: The experience became the basis for *Communion* (1987), published as a non-fiction personal account. The book sold over two million copies in its first year, spent 16 weeks on the *New York Times* bestseller list, and became the dominant popular image of the "grey alien" abduction archetype. A 1989 film adaptation starring Christopher Walken extended the cultural reach further. *Communion* is the single most impactful mass-audience document of the abduction phenomenon; it set the cultural template for what abduction experiences are understood to look like.

**Multiple witnesses**: [[anne-strieber]], Whitley's wife, was also present at the cabin and had her own anomalous experiences during this period — providing independent corroboration that *something* unusual was occurring, separate from Strieber's hypnotic recall. The presence of a second witness reduces the pure confabulation critique.

**Ongoing contact relationship**: Strieber has described the 1985 encounter not as an isolated event but as the beginning of an ongoing and irreversible contact relationship ([[nhi-open-door-problem]]) — with subsequent encounters extending for decades, including the 1989 implant removal documented at [[southwest-research]]. The 1985 event is the entry point into this long-term contact narrative.

**Credibility note**: Strieber is a professional fiction author, and the boundary between his fiction and his non-fiction UAP accounts has been contested. The KB treats his accounts as firsthand testimony to be evaluated on the usual credibility criteria rather than as either proven fact or simple invention.
