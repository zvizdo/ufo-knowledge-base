# 2023 Aegean Sea UAP D33

AFSOC 33 SOS / 27 SOW special operations aircraft observed a circular UAP performing multiple 90-degree turns at 80 MPH just above the ocean surface over the Aegean Sea, 27 October 2023, before losing it from the FMV feed 3 minutes after contact.

## Key Facts

- **DTG**: 270035:12Z OCT23; en route to tasked target (not RTB)
- **Platform**: 33 SOS / 27 SOW / 56 SOIS; Larissa AB (LGLR), Greece; AN/DAS-4; G-MESH
- **Location**: MGRS 35SKD59 area, Aegean Sea
- **UAP description**: "SEEMINGLY CIRCULAR, TOO SMALL TO MAKE OUT DETAILS" (matches D35 exactly)
- **UAP behavior**: "THE UAP TOOK MULTIPLE 90 DEGREE TURNS AT AN ESTIMATED 80 MPH" — sea-skimming
- **UAP speed**: 80 MPH; first coord 35SKD95, last coord 35SKD90
- **UAP maneuverability form field**: "SHARP 90 DEGREE TURNS"
- **UAP state**: Solid; BENIGN; no signatures; no advanced capabilities stated
- **Outcome**: Lost from FMV feed at 0038Z (3 minutes after contact)
- **Unit context**: Same detachment and base as D35 (33 SOS / 27 SOW, Larissa AB, 29 Oct 2023, 48 hours later)
