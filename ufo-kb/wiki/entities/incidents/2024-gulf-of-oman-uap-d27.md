# 2024 Gulf of Oman UAP D27

AFSOC 3 SOS / 27 SOW ISR aircraft observed one UAP during return-to-base over the Gulf of Oman, 7 June 2024.

## Key Facts

- **DTG**: 070457Z JUN 2024; during RTB
- **Platform**: AFSOC 3 SOS / 27 SOW, Al Dhafra AB (OMAM); AN/DAS-1 EO/IR sensor
- **UAP description**: "GLOWING HOT SPHERICAL UNIDENTIFIED OBJECT WITH A VERTICAL UNWAVERING CYLINDRICAL POLE/BAR ATTACHED ON THE BOTTOM. POSSIBLE REFLECTION IN THE WATER."
- **UAP behavior**: Flying straight just over the water at 140 knots; no response to observer
- **UAP altitude**: Very low — sea-skimming (possible water reflection visible)
- **UAP trajectory**: South (crossed from MGRS 40R to 40Q grid zone)
- **Physical state**: Solid; assessment: BENIGN
