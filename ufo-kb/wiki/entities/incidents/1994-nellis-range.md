# 1994 Nellis Range UAP Encounter

**Date:** August 1994  
**Location:** Nellis Air Force Base testing range (Nevada Testing and Training Range), Nevada

## Public Record

Video footage of an unknown object flying past the "schoolhouse" (training area) and descending into the valley has circulated publicly as the "Nellis Range UFO." Air traffic control ("E1 control") confirmed on tape that it could not identify the type of aircraft. The footage is considered among the more credible early 1990s UAP videos because of the accompanying radar data and ATC acknowledgment.

## Jason Sanss Account

[[jason-sanss]], USAF Master Sergeant stationed at [[nellis-air-force-base]] with the 67th Intelligence Group at the time, provides an account that extends beyond the public video. He describes:
- A UAP event "this previous week" before the schoolhouse passage
- A **face-to-face interaction** with a figure that "did not appear to be human," dressed in military-style clothing

[[james-fox]] is careful: "I opted to report on it even though I can't prove that it happened. I certainly can't. But I think it's a pretty compelling account. We have a location. We have a date. And we have three other active, at the time on-duty, military personnel."

## Speculative Connection to Non-Terrestrial Officers

Fox connects Sanss's account speculatively to [[gary-mckinnon]]'s discovery of a spreadsheet labeled "non-terrestrial officers" on a US Navy network — a list of names and ranks of apparent military personnel with no Earth-based ship assignments. Fox wonders whether the entity Sanss encountered belongs to the same programmatic infrastructure McKinnon glimpsed.

> ⚠ Note: The Fox/McKinnon connection is speculative inference, not direct evidence. McKinnon himself does not link his findings to Nellis.
