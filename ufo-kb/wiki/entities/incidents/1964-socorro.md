# 1964 Socorro Landing

One of the most thoroughly documented Close Encounter of the Third Kind (CE3) cases in the official US government record. On April 24, 1964, police officer [[lon-zamora]] was on duty outside Socorro, New Mexico when he saw a shiny egg-shaped object and two small figures in white coveralls near a dry gully. The craft emitted a roar and blue-orange flame before departing.

## Account

Zamora pulled his police cruiser over after seeing an unfamiliar vehicle down in an arroyo. He observed:
- An egg-shaped or oval metallic craft resting on leg-like supports
- Two small figures near the craft in white coveralls
- The beings appeared startled when they noticed Zamora
- The craft emitted loud noise and flame as it took off vertically
- Physical ground impressions and burn marks were found at the scene

A military officer arriving as first responder documented the scene, including a diagram of what appeared to be beings' footprints — this document is in the Project Blue Book files and was retrieved by [[james-fox]] from the National Archives.

## Significance: Hynek's Reversal

[[jacques-vallee]] had visited [[j-allen-hynek]] at Wright-Patterson AFB in 1964, urging him to investigate Project Blue Book reports classified as "psychological" — those where witnesses described beings associated with craft. Hynek was skeptical. Within roughly a week, the Socorro call came in. It is cited as the case that caused Hynek to do a "complete 180," transitioning from dismissal to genuine investigation of CE3s.

## James Fox's Investigation

Fox became deeply involved:
- Visited the National Archives with author Jay Sanford (who wrote a book on the case)
- Retrieved original Project Blue Book documents including the footprint diagram
- Got to know Zamora's family
- Investigated alongside the [[1964-holloman-afb-landing]] case because Holloman AFB is relatively close to Socorro geographically

## Evidence

- Zamora's firsthand testimony (police officer on duty)
- Physical landing impressions in the ground
- Military diagram of footprints in Blue Book files
- Burn marks at the scene
- Contemporaneous official documentation
