# Trinity Test (1945)

First detonation of a nuclear weapon, July 16, 1945, at the Alamogordo Bombing and Gunnery Range, New Mexico. Code name: Trinity. Yield: approximately 21 kilotons.

## Account

The plutonium implosion device ("Gadget") was detonated atop a 100-foot steel tower. The test preceded the atomic bombings of Hiroshima (August 6) and Nagasaki (August 9) by three weeks.

## UAP Connection

[[joe-mcmoneagle]] claims UFOs were observing humanity within 7 days of the Trinity test, establishing the nuclear-UAP temporal correlation hypothesis. The first post-war UAP wave (1946 Swedish ghost rockets, 1947 US wave) follows closely.

## Witnesses

Manhattan Project personnel including Robert Oppenheimer, Enrico Fermi, and Vannevar Bush.

## Evidence

Fully documented; yield confirmed by instrumentation and Soviet intelligence.

## Aftermath

Triggered the post-war nuclear arms race and coincided with the onset of the modern UFO era. Central to the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] thesis.
