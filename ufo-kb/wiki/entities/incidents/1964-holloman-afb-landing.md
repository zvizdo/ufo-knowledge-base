# Holloman AFB Landing

Alleged UAP landing at [[holloman-air-force-base]], New Mexico, in the early 1960s (most likely 1964–65 based on [[coral-lorenzen]]'s contemporaneous reporting and proximity to the [[1964-socorro]] incident).

## Account

Three disc-shaped craft appeared over the base, escorted by military jets. Two broke away; one descended in a floating, leaf-like motion that some interpreted as indicating distress — a description [[james-fox]] notes he has heard from multiple witnesses about other craft. The descending disc landed on three pods.

A seamless door slid open (no visible seams, according to [[allan-sandler]]'s description of the footage) and a ramp extended. Two beings with Egyptian-style headgear, large vertically-slit eyes, large noses, and almost nonexistent mouths/chins flanked the ramp. A primary being carrying what appeared to be a communication/interpretation device descended. The beings met with military officers on the ground. They then entered a Jeep and drove away. The disc remained on the base.

## The Footage Problem

The footage exists (or existed) on black-and-white film. It was:
1. Provided to [[paul-charlton]] for processing at [[norton-air-force-base]]
2. Shown to [[allan-sandler]] by Charlton
3. Confiscated the next morning by unidentified men in suits ("don't ever talk about it")

The original footage was supplied by a Los Angeles TV producer who had a Beta SP master. That producer died. His son was not cooperative. Whether the master survives is unknown.

[[bob-emenegger]] and [[allan-sandler]]'s film [[ufo-past-present-future]] (1970s) included the event in deliberately veiled language — the event that "may have already happened or may happen" — with unprecedented military cooperation otherwise throughout the film.

## Sandler vs. Charlton Interpretation

A significant new detail from [[hBrfe4hFuM0]]: the two men who saw the Holloman footage drew opposite conclusions about it.

[[allan-sandler]] — who was shown the footage at [[norton-air-force-base]] by [[paul-charlton]] — concluded the craft was real but the beings were *costumed humans*: our own disc technology with staged occupants. He believes it was a US government-manufactured event ("our technology").

[[paul-charlton]], who processed the footage and organized the viewing, believed absolutely it was a genuine ET landing. He held this conviction until his death.

The interpretive split between two firsthand viewers is itself significant: it means either the footage was ambiguous enough to support both readings, or one viewer's analysis was wrong.

## Bush and the Holloman Footage

[[eric-davis]] reportedly told [[james-fox]] that President George H.W. Bush was either briefed on or shown the Holloman footage during his time as CIA Director (Fox is uncertain whether Bush viewed the footage directly or received only a briefing on its contents). If accurate, this would establish presidential-level awareness of the event during the 1970s.

## Disinformation Layer

A competing story circulates that President Eisenhower met with aliens at Holloman. [[james-fox]] believes this was deliberately seeded as disinformation adjacent to the real event — "the best disinfo is slightly adjacent to the truth." The goal: attach something more sensational and unprovable, debunk that element, and the core event goes down with it.

## Aftermath

[[paul-charlton]] died in a head-on collision with his wife after the events. Fox notes the suspicious timing but does not claim certainty.
