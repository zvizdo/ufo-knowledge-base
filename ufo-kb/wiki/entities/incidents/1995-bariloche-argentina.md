# 1995 Bariloche Argentina UFO Incident

## Account

In 1995, a commercial pilot near Bariloche, Argentina — in the vicinity of a civilian nuclear grid — reported that a UFO was chasing his aircraft and caused an electrical outage that prevented him from landing. The precise mechanism and duration of the outage are not detailed in the source.

[[jesse-michaels]] cites this incident as evidence that the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] extends beyond US military bases to civilian nuclear infrastructure internationally — undermining the Wall Street Journal's claim that nuclear-base UFO cases are explained by US Air Force hazing rituals or secret EMP tests.

## Significance

Together with the Fukushima civilian nuclear grid sightings documented by Japanese residents, the Bariloche case suggests the pattern of UAP interest in nuclear sites is:
- **Not US-specific** — occurring in Argentina
- **Not military-specific** — occurring near civilian power infrastructure
- **Not era-specific** — consistent across 1960s-1990s timeframes

This makes any single-country or single-institution explanation (EMP tests, USAF hazing) structurally inadequate.
