# 1948 Aztec Crash

The Aztec crash refers to an alleged UFO/UAP crash recovery near Aztec, New Mexico in March 1948 — approximately nine months after the more famous [[1947-roswell]] incident. The standard skeptical position is that the Aztec story originated as a hoax in Variety magazine reporting from Frank Scully's 1950 book *Behind the Flying Saucers*, citing fraudulent sources. UFO researchers contest this dismissal.

## Scott and Suzanne Ramsay Investigation

Per [[51N8OxqZIWY]], [[jay-anderson]] references [[scott-ramsay]] and [[suzanne-ramsay]] as having conducted extensive primary research into the Aztec crash, including interviews with surviving witnesses. They are presented as credible researchers who built a substantial evidentiary case.

Their research is presented in the episode as an example of serious crash-retrieval investigation that goes beyond the initial hoax accusation — consistent with the broader argument that early UFO crash claims were dismissed by the same "lone actor" / "hoax" narrative machinery used on other inconvenient events.

## Richard Dolan's Assessment (GQlWf54K_7Y)

[[richard-dolan]] affirms the Aztec crash as real and provides important historical context:

**Aztec was first**: Dolan emphasizes that Aztec was **the first crash case to appear in print** — Frank Scully's *Behind the Flying Saucers* (1950) preceded any published account of Roswell. The deliberate, systematic smearing of the Aztec story was therefore the first major instance of the crash-retrieval suppression pattern.

**The debunking was driven by resentment**: Dolan is pointed about JP Cahn, the journalist who viciously debunked the story in 1950:
> "JP Cahn — a really vicious debunking in the 1950s. But it turned out he had a personal vendetta. His family were actually family friends with J. Edgar Hoover... It wasn't really honest research; it was agenda-driven."
The FBI connection to Cahn's family is treated by Dolan as a structural relationship, not mere coincidence.

**Wikipedia as active disinformation**: Dolan notes that the Aztec Wikipedia page lists the crash as "a hoax" — a label maintained by skeptical organizations that actively manage Wikipedia UFO content. This represents the modernized version of the same suppression mechanism used by Cahn in 1950.

**Ramsey rehabilitation**: Scott and Suzanne Ramsay spent years interviewing surviving witnesses and rebuilt the evidentiary case from scratch. Dolan endorses their work.
