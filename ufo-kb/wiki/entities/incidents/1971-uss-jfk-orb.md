# 1971 USS JFK Orb Incident

On the evening of July 2, 1971, an unidentified glowing spherical object appeared alongside or above the USS John F. Kennedy aircraft carrier shortly after the ship completed a series of qualification tests. The incident caused a complete blackout of ship communications, took weapons systems offline, and resulted in at least one sailor being sedated. The ship went to general quarters for approximately 20 minutes.

## Account

Primary witness: [[james-copley]], communications officer. He documented the incident ~2000–2001 and testified to the [[disclosure-project]] in 2000.

Timeline of events:
1. ~8:30 PM: All inbound communications begin producing gibberish (first sign)
2. Over the ship's intercom: a sailor screams **"It's God. It's the end of the world."**
3. Copley goes to deck: sees a glowing orange/yellow/reddish orb — approximately beach-ball size at arm's length
4. Multiple sailors observe; Copley watches ~20 seconds
5. At least one sailor **has to be sedated**
6. Ship goes to **general quarters** (battle stations)
7. Copley returns to communications station
8. Weapon systems are reported **offline** during the general quarters period (~20 minutes)
9. Orb departure: not witnessed by Copley

**Captain's announcement** afterward: "As you all know, uh, certain things happen aboard a US naval vessel that have to remain on the vessel and not to be discussed anywhere else. I hope I made myself clear."
