# 1989 Long Island UFO Crash

An alleged UFO crash or retrieval event occurred on Long Island, New York in ~1989, near Moriches Bay and in the vicinity of [[brookhaven-national-lab]] — which housed the first US particle accelerator (the Cosmotron). Multiple anomalous events on Long Island during this period were investigated by [[john-ford]] and the Long Island UFO Network. Ford believed there may have been more than one event.

## Account

Eyewitnesses reported a massive security presence in the area consistent with a crash retrieval operation. Ford's investigation documented these accounts. [[richard-dolan]]'s assessment: "I actually think that's that's a real possible one." He notes the location — adjacent to the US's first particle accelerator and a major physics research facility — fits the documented pattern of UAP interest in "tip-of-the-spear science and high energy physics."

## John Ford's Persecution

Following his investigation of the Long Island event(s), [[john-ford]] was charged with conspiring to poison a Nassau County or Suffolk County official's toothpaste with radioactive material. He was incarcerated for decades. The charge was so bizarre — radioactive toothpaste — that Dolan regards it as almost certainly fabricated: "It really looked like they just went after him."

Ford's friend [[elaine-douglas]] (a prominent UFO researcher who knew Ford well) strongly believed both the Long Island crash was real and that Ford was framed.

## UAP-Physics Infrastructure Connection

[[richard-dolan]] observes: "UFOs are definitely attracted to nuclear [facilities] and then I wonder if you could generalize that to 'tip the spear' science and to high energy physics and particle accelerators. [I think] yes." The Cosmotron at Brookhaven was the first US particle accelerator and a leading-edge facility in the late 1980s.
