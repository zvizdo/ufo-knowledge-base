# 1954 Eisenhower UFO Meeting

Alleged meeting between President Dwight D. Eisenhower and non-human entities in February 1954, at either [[edwards-air-force-base]] (formerly Muroc Army Air Field) or Holloman Air Force Base in New Mexico. Multiple independent accounts claim Eisenhower made an unannounced departure from a Palm Springs vacation (officially attributed to a dental emergency) to travel to the base for this meeting.

**Accounts in circulation**:
- [[charles-suggs]]'s alleged testimony (via his grandson): Suggs claimed to be present at the meeting at Muroc/Edwards, describing craft landing and beings engaging with Eisenhower.
- A purported account from William Moore and others describing a 1955 Holloman meeting (sometimes listed separately).
- Former US Representative Henry McElroy reportedly stated (in a 2010 video) that as a New Hampshire state rep he had seen a brief memo to Eisenhower about the reality of extraterrestrial beings.

**Epistemics**: No primary source documentation confirms the meeting. Eisenhower's schedule has gaps consistent with an unannounced departure from Palm Springs, and the dental appointment explanation was disputed at the time. However, the entire claim rests on secondhand and tertiary testimony without chain-of-custody documentation. The KB treats this as a contested claims-thesis rather than an established incident.

**Relationship to Holloman**: A separate but related claim places an Eisenhower-NHI meeting at Holloman AFB in 1955. The two accounts are sometimes conflated. [[hal-puthoff]] and others in the KB's sources discuss the "Eisenhower treaty" in a way that implies formal government-NHI contact in this era, consistent with (but not confirming) either meeting account.
