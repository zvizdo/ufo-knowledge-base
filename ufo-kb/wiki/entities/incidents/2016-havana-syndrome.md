# Havana Syndrome / Anomalous Health Incidents (AHI)

## Account

Beginning approximately 2016, US government officials and their family members stationed in Havana, Cuba began reporting a cluster of unusual symptoms: a sudden sense of directional pressure or vibration, high-frequency directional sound, headaches, dizziness, and cognitive disruption. The symptoms were tied to specific rooms or locations — disappearing when the person left and returning when they re-entered the same space.

The phenomenon spread to US officials in Russia, China, Austria (Vienna), Germany, Switzerland, and in proximity to the White House in Washington DC. As of investigation, over **1,000 individuals** have been affected — primarily US State Department, CIA, and other intelligence personnel and their family members, including children.

## Symptoms

Consistent ENT-confirmed pattern:
- Sudden onset of directional high-frequency sound
- Dizziness and balance disruption (otolith damage confirmed by ENT specialists)
- Ear pain, tinnitus, hearing loss
- Cognitive impairment: degraded memory and processing speed
- Insomnia, irritability, depression
- Room-dependent onset: symptoms disappear when the person leaves the affected room and reappear on return

Russia is the most likely perpetrator in Haseltine's assessment:

> ⚠ **Conflict**: The 2023 ODNI inter-agency assessment concluded that the most likely explanation for the majority of AHI cases is pre-existing medical conditions, environmental factors, and stress — effectively a psychogenic/psychosomatic finding. This directly contradicts Haseltine's directed-energy/Russia attribution and Polymeropoulos's firsthand injury account. The NIH's own 2023 neuroimaging study found objective brain differences in some AHI patients, which pushes back against a purely psychogenic explanation. The conflict is between: (1) official institutional conclusions (psychosomatic/environmental), (2) investigator/victim accounts (directed-energy, Russia), and (3) the NIH neurological evidence (objective anomalies without assigned cause). Neither side has produced the weapon system, and neither side can fully explain all cases.
