# 1966 Minot AFB UFO Missile Shutdown

**Date**: September 1966  
**Location**: [[minot-air-force-base]], North Dakota

## Account

[[david-shindell]]'s incoming crew was briefed by departing security guards that UFOs had been observed over the facility the previous night. Upon entering the launch capsule, all 10 missiles were off alert. Shindell's statement: "After entering the capsule, our eyes were immediately transfixed on the launch control console, which showed that all missiles were off alert and unlaunchable. We had never seen such a thing before. The outgoing crew briefed us on the wild events that transpired overnight and indicated that the missiles malfunctioned at the time the object was hovering directly above the capsule and next to the main gate."
