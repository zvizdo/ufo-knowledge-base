# 2008 Stephenville UFO

A mass UFO sighting over Stephenville, Texas in January 2008. Dozens of witnesses across a wide area reported a large, silent craft. The incident attracted significant media attention and drew comparison to the [[1997-phoenix-lights]] in scale and character.

## The Sighting

Multiple civilian witnesses in and around Stephenville reported a large object moving silently overhead in January 2008. The object was described variously as a massive structure with bright lights on its underside.

## Ricky Sorrels — Close-Range Observation

[[ricky-sorrels]], a hunter in the Stephenville area, observed the craft at particularly close range while in the field. He aimed his rifle scope at the craft's underside and examined it at magnification — finding **no rivets, seams, or panel lines**. The surface appeared completely seamless. After he aimed his scope at the craft, it departed at extraordinary speed.

## Witness Intimidation

Following his report, Sorrels was subjected to repeated low-level military helicopter surveillance over his property. He subsequently went silent and had not spoken publicly for years as of December 2024 per [[james-fox]].

## In "The Program"

Sorrels' account is documented in [[the-program-fox]] (December 23, 2024). Fox expressed concern about Sorrels' prolonged public silence during the Fox/Rogan JRE #2246 interview.
