# 1917 Fatima — Miracle of the Sun

On October 13, 1917, a crowd of **30,000–70,000 people** gathered at Cova da Iria near Fátima, Portugal, after three child shepherds (Lúcia dos Santos, Francisco Marto, and Jacinta Marto) had been receiving monthly apparitions from a female figure claiming to be the Virgin Mary since May 1917. The children promised a miracle would occur on that date.

Witnesses reported the sun appearing to spin, change colors (rainbow hues), and descend toward the Earth in a zigzag motion before returning to its normal position. Multiple photographs were taken. The Vatican officially recognized the event as a miracle in 1930.

## Structure of the Event

The Fatima case has a two-layer structure that [[chris-ramsey]] flags as analytically important:

1. **Apparition layer**: Three children privately received prophetic messages from a female figure over several months — the "Secrets of Fatima," partly still classified by the Vatican. This is intimate, verbal, prophetic contact.
2. **Physical phenomenon layer**: A large crowd observed an erratic, multi-colored aerial object behaving like no known astronomical body. This is an instrumented, multi-witness aerial event.

Ramsay notes that these are **two different phenomena** being attributed to the same source — the Virgin Mary. The first might be interpreted as psychic/contactee communication; the second looks like a nuts-and-bolts UAP display. Whether they are genuinely connected or coincidentally adjacent is a key open question.

## UAP Interpretation

Several UAP researchers, including [[diana-pasulka]] (who studies the phenomenological overlap between Catholic apparitions and contact accounts), treat the Fatima aerial phenomenon as a probable UAP event interpreted through the culture's available religious framework. Key features:
- Witnessed by a massive, mixed crowd (believers and skeptics alike)
- Erratic motion inconsistent with solar behavior
- Multi-chromatic light effects consistent with reported UAP luminescence
- Structured approach and departure ("descended toward Earth, then returned")
- Preceded by months of claimed contact with a communicating entity

The [[ufo-religion-convergence]] concept aggregates this pattern: NHI appearances adopt the idiom of the host culture's dominant spiritual framework.

## Chris Ramsay's Analysis

Ramsay uses Fatima as a magic lens case study: the crowd was primed over months by repeated prophecy that a miracle would appear on this specific date. From a stage-magic perspective, you could reverse-engineer the Fatima event as a preparation-and-execution psyop — but, as Ramsay notes, "knowing the method doesn't mean it was the explanation." The fact that 30,000–70,000 people saw something anomalous in the sky on the promised date remains regardless of how the interpretive framework is built around it.

He distinguishes the aerial phenomenon (plausible UAP) from the prophetic messages (less reducible to material explanation) — and suggests they may be genuinely separate events that became entangled through the expectation structure.
