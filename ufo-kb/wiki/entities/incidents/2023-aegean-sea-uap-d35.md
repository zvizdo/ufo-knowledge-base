# 2023 Aegean Sea UAP D35

AFSOC 33 SOS / 27 SOW special operations aircraft observed a circular UAP sea-skimming toward land during RTB over the Aegean Sea, 29 October 2023.

## Key Facts

- **DTG**: 290811:00Z OCT23; during RTB
- **Platform**: 33 SOS / 27 SOW / 56 SOIS; Larissa AB (LGLR), Greece; armed (Hellfire missiles); AN/DAS-4
- **Location**: MGRS 35SMV3 area, Aegean Sea
- **UAP description**: "SEEMINGLY CIRCULAR, TOO SMALL TO MAKE OUT DETAILS"
- **UAP behavior**: "FLYING JUST ABOVE THE SURFACE OF THE OCEAN WATER... STRAIGHT ABOVE THE OCEAN TOWARDS LAND"
- **UAP speed**: 30 MPH (estimated)
- **UAP state**: Solid; BENIGN; maneuverability NONE; signatures NONE
- **Outcome**: Lost from FMV feed at 0811Z (lost while still in flight)
