# 1946 Babalon Working

The **Babalon Working** was a series of ceremonial magic rituals conducted by [[jack-parsons]] in early 1946, with **[[l-ron-hubbard]]** as his ritual partner. It was conducted at Parsons' Pasadena mansion (the "Parsonage") and drew on Aleister Crowley's *Moonchild* novel and Thelema system.

## Intent and Nature

The working aimed to summon the **divine feminine archetype known as Babalon** — described in Thelema as the "Scarlet Woman" or Whore of Babylon in a positive, world-soul sense. Inspired by Crowley's *Moonchild*, it aimed not for literal birth but for **transformation of the self and opening of higher-dimensional contact**. The rituals were elaborate: invocations of gods and spirits, sexual-magical practices, and ceremonial workings spread over several weeks.

Parsons did not see these as fantasy or performance. He believed they had **real power to open doorways and make contact with NHI**.

## L. Ron Hubbard's Role and Aftermath

Hubbard participated as Parsons' ritual partner. Shortly after the working concluded, Hubbard **ran off with Parsons' girlfriend and a significant portion of his money**. Parsons, furious, conducted a magical counterattack in a Miami hotel room — invoking **Bartabel**, the spirit of Mars. Hubbard's yacht subsequently encountered a storm, and a court later forced him to repay Parsons.

Whatever one makes of the metaphysics, Hubbard went on to found **Scientology**, and the shared origin with a Thelemic sex-magic working is an unresolved biographical connection.

## The UAP Wave Hypothesis

Parsons believed the Babalon Working had succeeded — that it had **opened something**. In 1946–1947, reports of unidentified aerial phenomena skyrocketed (the 1947 wave, Kenneth Arnold sighting, Roswell). [[jesse-michaels]] raises the question in [[zjpvfDFc4fg]]: did the working trigger the UAP wave, or is this coincidental temporal proximity?

[[diana-pasulka]] and others in the NHI-consciousness research tradition take seriously the possibility that Parsons was attempting to contact the same category of entity that modern UAP experiencers encounter.

## Mojave Nordic Encounter (Alleged)

According to [[jacques-vallee]] (per hearsay), there were rumors of a ritual in the **Mojave Desert** during this period where a **blonde woman appeared and claimed to be from Venus**. If accurate, this would be the **first recorded encounter with a Nordic-type NHI entity**, predating similar contactee stories by years.
