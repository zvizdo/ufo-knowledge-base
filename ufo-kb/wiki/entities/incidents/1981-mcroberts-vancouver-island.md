# 1981 McRoberts Vancouver Island Photo

On October 8, 1981, **Hannah McRoberts** was driving with her husband on Vancouver Island, British Columbia. She stopped to photograph a well-known mountain because "the cloud seemed a little nice over the top." Using a film camera, she took the shot and thought nothing of it.

On developing the film, she discovered a **classic dome-top flying saucer** clearly visible in the frame — an object she never saw with her naked eye. She had not been trying to photograph a UFO.

## Analysis

[[claus-svahn]] spoke with McRoberts on the telephone. Key points:
- The image was captured on **old film stock** — not a digital artifact
- Svahn believes she did not fake it; the accidental-capture story holds up under interview
- The object appears as a structured disc with a dome — highly legible

Svahn does not dismiss the photo, unlike his verdict on all known Swedish 1970s photos (all hoaxed by young men). He presents it in his book *Files of the Unexplained*.

## Significance

- Accidental capture mechanism eliminates the staged-photo critique
- Film medium with pre-digital provenance reduces manipulation concerns
- Witness credibility: Svahn describes her as a convincing, consistent interviewee
