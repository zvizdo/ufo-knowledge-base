# 1974 Coyame, Mexico UFO Crash

## Account

Per the leaked Mexican military memo summarized in [[mexicos-roswell]] (researchers [[noi-torres]] and [[ruben-uriarte]]) and revisited in [[OOr98r51Sj0]]:

In **August 1974**, a small Mexican civilian aircraft collided midair with an unknown craft over the Coyame region of northern Chihuahua. Both went down. A Mexican military team was dispatched to the crash site.

**The entire Mexican recovery team died at the scene.** The object — variously described as disc-shaped or spherical — was subsequently **airlifted out of Mexican territory by US forces** before Mexican authorities could complete a full investigation.

## Witnesses

The incident's primary documentation is a leaked memo (Mexican military origin) that surfaced publicly in the **1990s**. Local witnesses in Coyame report decades of subsequent strange aerial activity in the same area but have become reticent to speak ("locals don't talk about the lights anymore… if you ask too many questions, you might find out why").

## Evidence

- Leaked Mexican military report (1990s leak)
- Civilian-research compilation: *[[mexicos-roswell]]* (Torres / Uriarte)
- Local witness testimony — colored lights in mountain peaks; orbs and "square objects" emerging from the landscape
- Persistent intimidation pattern documented by Torres/Uriarte and rehearsed in [[OOr98r51Sj0]]

## Aftermath

- The object's final destination is unknown publicly; US-controlled disposition.
- The cause of death of the Mexican recovery team is unknown — speculation includes radiation, biological hazard, or direct kill.
- Coyame has remained a site of recurring anomalous activity reported by locals; [[sentinels-of-ether]] uses the surrounding canyon system as the fictional setting for a 2004-era holographic-mountain-base operation.
