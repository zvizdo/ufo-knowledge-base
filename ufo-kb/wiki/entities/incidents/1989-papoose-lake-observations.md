# 1989 Papoose Lake Observations

Series of weekend observation events in 1989 at Groom Lake Road's overlook of [[papoose-lake]] / S-4, undertaken by [[bob-lazar]] and a small group of civilian companions including [[gene-huff]] and [[john-lear]]. The observation events came after Lazar's reported employment at S-4 ended; he claimed to know the test schedule for the disc craft he had worked on.

## Account

On three separate Wednesday-night observations, Lazar's group reported witnessing a glowing/disc-shaped object ascend, hover, and maneuver in the airspace over Papoose Lake — consistent with Lazar's prior description of the S-4 craft's flight tests. The third observation event ended with the group being detained by Lincoln County sheriff's deputies and Wackenhut security personnel.

## Witnesses

[[bob-lazar]], [[gene-huff]], [[john-lear]], and a small number of additional civilian observers across the three nights.

## Evidence

Video footage of light-source-class objects (low resolution given consumer cameras of the era); witness testimony; the law-enforcement contact establishes provenance of group's presence at the location.

## Aftermath

The detention sequence ended Lazar's direct site-monitoring; the footage and witness accounts entered the broader Lazar-narrative apparatus. The events function in the KB as a multi-witness corroboration anchor of the broader [[bob-lazar]] account, irrespective of the disposition of his employment claims.
