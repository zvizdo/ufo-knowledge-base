# 1948 Sweden Lake Crash (UFO)

A Swedish technical expert (unnamed) observed an object crash or land in a lake near his home. He carefully noted its azimuth from his observation point. The [[swedish-air-intelligence-service|Swedish Air Intelligence Service]] considered his account sufficiently credible to dispatch a naval salvage team to the lake. Divers discovered a **previously uncharted crater on the floor of the lake** — which Swedish intelligence concluded "was in fact caused by a flying saucer." No further information on the salvage outcome was available as of the November 4, 1948 teletype.

This incident was reported to [[cp-cabell|Gen. Cabell]], USAF Director of Intelligence, as part of USAFE Teletype #1524 (USAFE 14), alongside the Swedish Air Intelligence Service's formal assessment that flying saucers represent technology "which cannot be credited to any presently known culture on earth."
