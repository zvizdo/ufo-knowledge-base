# 1953 Varese UAP Case

The 1953 Varese case is a UAP incident alleged to have occurred in the Varese province of Lombardy, northern Italy, in 1953. It is cited primarily in connection with the alleged reactivation or reconstitution of the Italian government's classified UFO investigation committee — [[rs-33]] (or its 1950s successor) — under [[guglielmo-marconi]].

**RS-33 and Fermi connection**: According to accounts surfaced through Marconi family testimony (cited by [[jesse-michaels]]), the Varese incident was significant enough to prompt a formal government response involving the reconstitution of the RS-33 structure. In this framework, [[enrico-fermi]] served as vice-president of the reconstituted RS-33 committee — placing one of the most eminent physicists in history inside an Italian classified UAP body in the final year of his life (Fermi died in November 1954). The Varese case would represent the triggering event for this structural response.

**Varese province context**: Varese province is in the Italian pre-Alps of Lombardy, near the Magenta area that was the site of the [[1933-magenta-crash]]. The geographic proximity — both incidents occurring in the same general region of northern Lombardy — is noted in KB analyses as potentially significant, whether due to UAP operational patterns, Italian classification geography, or coincidence.

**Source quality**: The RS-33/Varese/Fermi connection derives from Marconi family testimony, which is secondary and not independently corroborated in public documents. The case is preserved in the KB as a claimed historical incident whose evidentiary basis is the Marconi family's private account.
