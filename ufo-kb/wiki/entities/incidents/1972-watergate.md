# Watergate

The Watergate affair (1972–74) — the break-in, cover-up, and Nixon resignation — is the best-documented case of the covert action apparatus that [[deep-state-continuity]] theorists argue underpins UAP secrecy. Key nodes in the Watergate network (Howard Hunt, [[mark-felt]], [[bernard-barker]], G. Gordon Liddy) had direct CIA and organized crime connections. [[danny-sheehan]]'s thesis places Watergate as a node in a continuous institutional lineage running from the CIA-mob Castro plots through the JFK assassination to present-day UAP secrecy enforcement.

The cover-up's architecture — compartmentalized deniability, parallel structures of authority, use of intelligence assets for domestic operations — mirrors the structural requirements for long-term UAP secrecy identified by researchers like Sheehan and [[lue-elizondo]].
