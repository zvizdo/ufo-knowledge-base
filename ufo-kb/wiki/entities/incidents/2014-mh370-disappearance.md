# 2014 MH370 Disappearance

## Account

Malaysian Airlines Flight MH370 departed Kuala Lumpur on March 8, 2014, bound for Beijing with 239 people aboard. After deviating from its planned flight path, the aircraft vanished. Despite one of the largest multinational search operations in aviation history, no confirmed wreckage was found for years; questions about the cause remain officially unresolved.

The mainstream explanation centers on a deliberate diversion (potentially by the captain) followed by fuel exhaustion over the South Indian Ocean, based on satellite ping analysis. Pieces of confirmed MH370 debris have washed up on Indian Ocean coastlines since 2015.

## UAP Hypothesis (Ashton Forbes)

[[ashton-forbes]] argues that video footage shows the aircraft being **surrounded and teleported by UAP orbs**. Forbes became notable for advancing this hypothesis on social media and podcasts circa 2023–2024.
