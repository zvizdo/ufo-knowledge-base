# 1977 Colares Incident

A major mass UAP event in 1977 over **Colares**, an island municipality in Pará state in northern Brazil. The incident involved repeated low-altitude UAP encounters by dozens of witnesses over an extended period.

## The Incident

Witnesses across the Colares area reported aerial objects of varying shapes — including disc-type craft — that flew at extremely low altitudes, sometimes reportedly emitting beams of light that struck witnesses and produced physical injuries. Local residents described fear and disorientation; some reportedly required medical treatment for burns or other physical effects attributed to the encounters.

## Operation Prato

The Brazilian Air Force launched **Operation Prato** (Operation Plate/Saucer), an official military investigation into the Colares events. The investigation produced:
- Photographs of UAP
- Medical documentation of injuries attributed to UAP encounters
- Written reports from military investigators

[[general-uchoa]] is associated with the leadership of this investigation at a senior military command level.

## Presentation at Bigelow Dinner

[[general-uchoa]] brought photographs and reports from the Colares investigation to a **Washington DC hotel dinner hosted by [[robert-bigelow]]**, approximately 2009. He presented at the head of the table; his daughter translated from Portuguese to English.

[[lue-elizondo]], attending this dinner, describes it as a critical intellectual turning-point: he left "scratching my head" and began taking the UAP phenomenon seriously. An unnamed colleague who also attended was equally affected. This is one of Elizondo's most specific early turning-point anecdotes.
