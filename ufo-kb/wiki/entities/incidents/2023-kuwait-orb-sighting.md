# Undated Kuwait Orb Sighting

The undated Kuwait orb sighting is a UAP incident reported by [[michael-shellenberger]] based on a single source: a high-definition, full-color video taken from a helicopter platform showing an orb emerging from the ocean approximately 20 miles off the Kuwaiti coast and subsequently being joined by a second orb.

**Source characteristics**: Shellenberger reported this case from a single source, meaning independent corroboration is not documented in public records. The description — helicopter-based, high-definition, full-color video — would suggest higher evidentiary quality than typical UAP photographs if the footage exists as described. Helicopter platforms carry professional-grade cameras, making "methods protection" (the government's usual rationale for withholding UAP imagery that would reveal surveillance platform capabilities) a less convincing reason for non-release.

**Evidentiary status**: The video has not been publicly released or independently verified. The case rests entirely on Shellenberger's reporting of his source's account. As a single-source unreleased video, it falls into the [[claims-thesis]] category.

**Ocean emergence pattern**: The orb-from-ocean pattern — UAP observed entering or exiting bodies of water — is a recurring category in UAP phenomenology documented across multiple independent cases (see [[uso]] and [[water-world-ufo-habitat]]). The Kuwait case, if accurately described, would be a high-quality recent instance of this pattern in an operational military surveillance environment.
