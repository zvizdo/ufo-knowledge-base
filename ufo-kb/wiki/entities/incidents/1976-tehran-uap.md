# 1976 Tehran UAP

**Date**: 18–19 September 1976  
**Location**: Airspace over Tehran, Iran  
**Witnesses**: Imperial Iranian Air Force F-4 Phantom crews (two aircraft), ground radar operators, civilian witnesses

## Sequence of Events

1. Multiple civilian reports of a large bright object in Tehran airspace overnight.
2. Imperial Iranian Air Force scrambles first F-4; pilot approaches and experiences **complete electronics failure** — all instrumentation goes dark; pilot breaks off and electronics restore.
3. Second F-4 scrambled; crew makes visual and radar contact with the primary object (estimated very large). While tracking, a **sub-object is launched** from the primary craft and approaches the second F-4 at high speed; pilot attempts to fire an AIM-9 missile but the **weapons systems fail**. Sub-object returns to the primary craft.
4. A second sub-object is launched and descends to the ground. The F-4 crew follows to the area and observes a bright light on the ground but finds nothing at daylight.

## DIA Assessment

The Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) classified the incident report and produced an assessment that described it as **"an outstanding report... a classic which meets all the conditions required for a legitimate study of the UFO phenomenon."** The report was eventually obtained via FOIA.

## Official Assessments

- **DIA**: classified "outstanding report," met all study criteria
- **COMETA**: one of three primary international cases in Chapter 2; electronics-failure + sub-object + DIA rating make it the strongest multi-sensor case
- **Philip Klass**: attempted debunking; COMETA characterizes this as unconvincing "reducing disinformation"
