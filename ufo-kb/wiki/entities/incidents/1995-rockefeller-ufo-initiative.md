# 1995 Rockefeller UFO Initiative

The most resourced civilian disclosure-pressure campaign of the modern era — funded by **[[laurance-rockefeller]]** and coordinated through his **[[jy-ranch]]** property. Active across roughly 1993–2000.

## Account

The initiative pursued multiple parallel tracks:
1. **Engagement with the Clinton White House** — including documented [[jy-ranch]] meetings; Hillary Clinton attended a 1995 visit, and [[webster-hubbell]] later confirmed that Bill Clinton tasked him with looking into UFO records.
2. **The *Best Available Evidence* briefing document** — coordinated by Marie Galbraith and team; distributed to Congress and government officials; designed as a high-credibility brief on the strongest non-classified UAP evidence as of 1995.
3. **Funding of consciousness-research and academic-UAP-research projects** — including support for John Mack's experiencer work and the UFO Research Coalition.
4. **Direct patronage** — including private engagement with named-disclosure figures of the period.

## Witnesses

[[laurance-rockefeller]] (financier and convener); Marie Galbraith (briefing coordinator); Scott Jones (Rockefeller's UFO-disclosure liaison); Hillary Clinton (visiting attendee); various Clinton-administration figures.

## Evidence

Documented through FOIA-released White House visitor records, the *Best Available Evidence* document itself, and contemporaneous press coverage.

## Aftermath

The initiative wound down in the late 1990s without achieving formal disclosure but established the modern playbook for elite-funded disclosure-pressure work. Its closest contemporary analog is To-The-Stars-Academy / TTSA in operational structure (private vehicle channeling disclosure-adjacent material toward government engagement).
