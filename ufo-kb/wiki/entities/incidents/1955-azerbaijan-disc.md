# 1955 Azerbaijan Disc Observation (USSR)

## Account

At approximately 1910 hours on 4 October 1955, Senator Richard Russell observed the first disc aircraft ascending from a Soviet facility area south of the Trans-Caucasus rail line while traveling by train between Atjaty and Adzhijabul in the Azerbaijan SSR. Russell immediately alerted Lt. Col. E.U. Hathaway and Ruben Efron, who reached the window in time to clearly observe the second disc one minute later. All three witnesses were members of a US Senate delegation traveling through the USSR.

**UAP 1 (Russell only — brief window)**
- Round/circular shape; outer surface revolving clockwise
- Two stationary interior lights positioned near the top
- Sparks/flame visible from the bottom
- No protrusions, wings, fuselage, or markings
- Ascended from area ~1–2 miles south of the rail line where searchlights were pointing vertically skyward
- Climbed to approximately 6,000 ft altitude, then accelerated sharply northward

**UAP 2 (Russell, Hathaway, Efron — all three confirmed)**
- Identical morphology to UAP 1
- Appeared from same launch area ~1 minute after UAP 1 had cleared
- Same vertical ascent and northward acceleration pattern

**Soviet response**: Immediately following the observation, Soviet trainmen closed all window curtains and refused passenger access to the windows for the remainder of that stretch — a deliberate, coordinated information-control response.

## Official Assessment

- Cable classification: Top Secret / NOFORN
- Cable precedence: OPERATIONAL IMMEDIATE (to HQ USAF, Washington)
- Prepared by: Lt. Col. Thomas S. Ryan, US Air Attaché, Prague
- USAIRA source reliability: B (Usually Reliable)
- USAIRA information rating: 2 (Probably True)
- Combined: **B-2 — high confidence in both source and content**

The report was declassified decades after the event. The Russell sighting is cited in UAP literature as the strongest Cold War-era case for Soviet disc technology — or for genuinely anomalous disc craft operating in Soviet airspace, with Soviet knowledge.

## Hynek Classification

**DD (Daylight Disc) / CE1**: Clear visual observation at dusk of craft with defined shape and structure, no physical contact. Three witnesses including the Chairman of the Senate Armed Services Committee.
