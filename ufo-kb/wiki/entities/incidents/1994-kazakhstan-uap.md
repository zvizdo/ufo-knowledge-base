# 1994 Kazakhstan UAP (Tajik Air 747SP)

**Date:** January 27, 1994  
**Location:** ~45°N 55°E, Kazakhstan, at approximately 41,000 ft MSL (Boeing 747SP cruise altitude)  
**Witnesses:** [[ed-rhodes]] (Chief Pilot, Tajik Air; AMCIT; Pan Am veteran) and two American pilot colleagues  
**Source:** US Embassy Dushanbe cable 94 DUSHANBE 259, filed January 31, 1994  

## Observation

Crew first observed the object as a "bright light of enormous intensity" approaching from over the eastern horizon at high speed and at a much higher altitude than their own. The observation lasted approximately 40 minutes. Maneuvers observed:
- Circles
- Corkscrews
- 90-degree turns at rapid speed "under very high G's"

After the maneuver phase, the object "adopted a horizontal high-speed course and disappeared over the horizon." Dark conditions prevented shape identification; the light was described as having a **"bow wave"** — resembling the trailing shock wave visible in a high-speed bullet photograph.

## Physical Trace Evidence

Approximately 45 minutes after the initial sighting, as the sun was rising, the aircraft flew under contrails left by the object. Estimated altitude: **~100,000 feet**. Aircraft speed at time of underpass: over 500 knots. The contrail patterns directly reflected the maneuvering sequence — circles, corkscrews, etc.

Rhodes noted that conventional aircraft cannot produce contrails at ~100,000 feet due to insufficient air density and moisture, making the origin of the contrails anomalous.

## Resolution

Unresolved. Embassy comment: "We have no opinion and report the above for what it may be worth." Rhodes and crew concluded the object was "extraterrestrial and under intelligent control."
