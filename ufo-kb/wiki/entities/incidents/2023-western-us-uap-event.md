# 2023 Western US UAP Event (Federal Law Enforcement)

**Date:** 2023 (two separate days — dusk and pre-dawn observations)  
**Location:** Western United States, restricted zone  
**Witnesses:** USPER1–USPER7 (seven federal law enforcement special agents in three two-person teams plus one additional); multiple independent corroborations from varying vantage points

## Phenomena Observed

1. **Orange mother orb launching red offspring orbs**: An orange orb appeared, launched 2–4 red orbs, and disappeared within 1–2 seconds; repeated at least 5 times across two days. Six witnesses (USPER1–USPER6) from multiple vantage points.

2. **Large fiery orange orb at rock pinnacle**: Hovering orange orb ~12–18 meters in diameter (AARO measurement), at ~1,050 meters from observers (AARO measured), "hovering with zero resistance or movement." Silent. Duration ~1 min. USPER6 observed a possible "spindle connecting it from underneath to the rock formation."

3. **"Dark Kite" / triangular object**: Initially mimicked a ground vehicle with two lights (red + white) in a restricted zone. When agents pursued on foot to within a few hundred feet, the object moved laterally off the road over the desert at ~15–20 mph without changing its orientation relative to the observers and stopped ~100 m away with lights off. NVGs showed a ~4-foot-wide horizontal "thin line." AARO follow-up identified this as triangular.

4. **"Transparent Kite"**: ~30 min after the dark kite, a kite-shaped object appeared ~6 m off the ground, floating slowly with the wind; partially transparent (stars visible through it on NVGs). A spotlight beam was observed stopping at ~50 yards "on nothing in particular" — consistent with an invisible or near-invisible object absorbing or blocking the beam.

## AARO Involvement

AARO conducted post-event measurements (establishing fiery orb at 1,050 m and 12–18 m diameter) and follow-up witness discussions (eliciting triangular description for "Dark Kite"). Active AARO engagement documented.
