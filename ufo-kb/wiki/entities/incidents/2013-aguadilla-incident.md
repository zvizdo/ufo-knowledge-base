# 2013 Aguadilla Incident

A transmedium UAP incident captured on thermal video by a **Department of Homeland Security (DHS) / Customs and Border Protection (CBP)** helicopter over **Aguadilla, Puerto Rico** in April 2013. The video was released publicly and has been analyzed extensively by independent researchers and reportedly by AATIP.

## The Incident

A DHS/CBP helicopter crew on a routine patrol mission captured thermal footage of an unknown object. The object exhibited the following sequence of behaviors:

1. **Initial appearance**: balloon-like or at least consistent with a slow-moving aerial object
2. **Unusual maneuvers**: shifted to performing movements inconsistent with a balloon or conventional aircraft
3. **Water entry**: the object **entered the water without creating a visible splash** — no surface disturbance
4. **Underwater tracking**: the object was tracked continuing to move while underwater
5. **Re-emergence**: the object surfaced from the water
6. **Splitting**: the single object **split into two distinct objects** before the sequence ended

## AATIP Relevance

[[lue-elizondo]] explicitly references the Aguadilla case in JRE #2194 as something **shared with AATIP investigators** and analyzed repeatedly by experts. He cites it as a canonical transmedium case — an object demonstrated air-to-water transition without the physical disruption that would accompany any conventional vehicle.

## Significance

- One of the few publicly available videos showing apparent **transmedium transit** (air-to-water without surface impact signature)
- The splitting behavior — one object becoming two — is a recurring feature in other high-credibility UAP cases
- DHS/CBP as the recording agency provides an institutional provenance that separates this from amateur footage
