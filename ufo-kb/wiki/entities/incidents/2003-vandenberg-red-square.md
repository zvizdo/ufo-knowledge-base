# 2003 Vandenberg Red Square

On **October 14, 2003**, [[vandenberg-afb]] experienced one of its most documented modern UAP incidents. The event unfolded in two distinct phases and involved multiple independent witness groups.

## Account

### Phase 1: The Red Square (Boeing Contractors)

**Boeing contractors** reported a massive glowing red square silently hovering over **two missile defense sites** at Vandenberg. After several minutes, the object drifted further east onto the base and vanished over the hills. Its silence and anomalous geometry ruled out conventional aircraft.

### Phase 2: The Triangular Craft (USAF Security)

Later that night, while [[jeff-natelli]] was on duty, USAF security guards at a critical launch site reported a bright, fast-moving object approaching over the ocean. Natelli heard the escalating radio traffic:

> *"It's coming right at us. It's coming right for us. And now it's right here."*

Moments later: the object shot off and was gone. When Natelli arrived on scene, **five shaken witnesses** described a **massive triangular craft larger than a football field** that had hovered silently for approximately 45 seconds over their entry control point before departing at impossible speed.

## Broader Pattern: 2003–2005

This October 2003 event was the first of **five UAP incidents at Vandenberg between 2003 and 2005** documented by Natelli and colleagues.

## Official Status

- Official Air Force records of the Red Square event are said to be in possession of **AARO** and the **FBI**.
- The incident was referenced by **Representative Luna** at a congressional UAP hearing — making it one of the few modern Vandenberg UAP events to reach the congressional record.

## Historical Continuity

The 2003–2005 cluster follows the same Vandenberg pattern established in the 1960s: [[1964-vandenberg-bob-jacobs]] (instrumented UFO footage, missile interaction), [[1964-vandenberg-richard-barth]] (CE3, NHI nuclear intelligence gathering), and the broader [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] documentation by [[robert-hastings]].

## In "The Program" and Fox/Rogan Interview

[[james-fox]] featured Natelli's account in [[the-program-fox]] (released December 23, 2024) and read his written statement on-air during the JRE #2246 interview ([[3yrKVu35uSE]]). Fox also confirmed additional incidents not in the original account: an **orb on the Vandenberg flight line** (separate from the Red Square event) and at least three further incidents, bringing the confirmed 2003–2005 cluster to five events total.
