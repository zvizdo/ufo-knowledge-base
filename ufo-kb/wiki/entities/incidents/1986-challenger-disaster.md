# 1986 Challenger Disaster

The Space Shuttle Challenger (STS-51-L) broke apart 73 seconds after launch on January 28, 1986, due to O-ring failure in the right solid rocket booster. All seven crew members were killed. The disaster prompted a major review of NASA safety culture and a 32-month halt to shuttle launches.

In the KB, the Challenger disaster appears primarily through [[ted-owens]]'s documented letters. Owens, who claimed psychokinetic powers granted by mantid entities, wrote letters to official parties predicting the Challenger disaster before it occurred. The letters are documented and were reviewed by [[jason-reza-jorjani]], who treats the prediction as genuine anomalous data rather than coincidence.

The significance: Owens's Challenger letters are among the few verifiable predictive claims in the KB's anomalous-abilities cluster. If the prediction was genuine precognition (rather than lucky guessing or post-hoc document manipulation), it would constitute evidence for the type of future-state information access that [[quantum-brain-precognition]] (Eric Wargo's retrocausality thesis) and remote viewing research attempt to explain mechanistically.
