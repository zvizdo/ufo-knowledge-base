# 2020-gulf-of-aden-uap-d57

Round, cold-IR object tracked over the Gulf of Aden on 04 Sep 2020 by an O-3 pilot from 2 ATKS during an ISR mission. The contact appeared bright white in black-hot IR polarity, indicating ambient-cold temperature. Moving heading 168° at speed 277; range closed from 8.8 to 6.1 KM during the 8-minute observation.
