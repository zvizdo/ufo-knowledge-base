# 2025 Western US UAP Orb Swarm

FBI 302 interview of a senior US intelligence official documenting a multi-hour UAP encounter at and near a classified facility in the western United States, late 2025.

## Key Facts

- **Date**: Late 2025 (specific date redacted; nighttime encounter 2202–2241hrs)
- **Platform**: State Partner Organization helicopter (CALL SIGN 1) with two pilots; LP/OP ground team (FEDERAL PARTNER 4) with FLIR and NVGs
- **Primary witness**: WITNESS 1 (senior US intelligence official, FBI 302 subject); naked-eye observation
- **Additional witnesses**: WITNESS 2 (senior US intelligence official); 5 military aircraft crew (two minutes to station at 2218hrs)
- **Location**: Mountain range west of classified SITE CODE NAME, western United States (MGRS coordinates redacted)
- **Origin**: Search for previously reported "orbs/lights" and "thuds" near classified facility; same day as unspecified "successful test" at site
- **UAP morphology**: "Super-hot" orb under FLIR; naked-eye appearance oval shaped, orange, white/yellow center, emitting light in all directions
- **UAP behavior**:
  - Orb split into two objects; something emerged from two objects traveling in a third direction at high rate of speed
  - Single orb came within 10 feet of the helicopter (CE1); could not be matched in speed
  - Swarm of lights "too many to count" at 2218hrs
  - Sequential horizontal flaring display: 4–5 orbs flaring up one at a time, then down in reverse order — repeated three times (west of helicopter at 2227hrs, above military aircraft at 2228hrs, to the east at 2233hrs)
  - Orbs appeared to "break off from [CALL SIGN 1] and pursue the [MILITARY AIRCRAFT]" at the moment of military response arrival
- **Photo evidence**: 24 photo PDFs (b1–b24) submitted; image-only, content not extractable from text
- **Classification**: SECRET//NOFORN; released PURSUE Release 01
