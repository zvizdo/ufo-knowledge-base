# 1950 Philadelphia Angel-Hair Incident

On September 26, 1950, two Philadelphia police officers reported a six-foot lavender luminous object that descended from the sky into a vacant lot.

**Characteristics:**
- Size: approximately six feet
- Color: lavender
- Weight: no measurable ground deformation — the object did not depress the weeds beneath it
- Duration: approximately 15–25 minutes before complete evaporation
- Residue: slight sticky material recovered from the site after evaporation

The "angel-hair" material — evaporating shortly after deposition — is consistent with similar incidents documented in France (1954 Oloron-Sainte-Marie) and Australia (1954 Quirindi). This FBI-documented case is among the earliest in the modern record.
