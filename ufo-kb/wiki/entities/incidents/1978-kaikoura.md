# 1978 Kaikoura UFO Incident

One of the most thoroughly documented multi-modal UAP encounters in Pacific history. A cargo Argosy aircraft flying up the east coast of New Zealand's South Island in December 1978 was visited by elliptical metallic disc-shaped objects that came to cockpit-window proximity, glowing but clearly solid and structured.

## Witnesses

- **Flight crew** (pilot and co-pilot) — observed objects approach cockpit window; described as elliptical metallic discs, glowing but clearly structured; saw them go to the west
- **Journalist passengers** — including [[quentin-fogarty]] and Dennis Grant (political correspondent); described objects in real time
- **Wellington radar operator John Cordi** — cross-Cook Strait, simultaneously confirmed solid radar returns moving in the exact directions the visual witnesses described, in real time

## The Contemporaneous Corroboration

The Wellington radar operator was in voice contact with the aircraft and confirmed strong radar targets matching the visual positions and movement directions reported in real time. This multi-modal, contemporaneous corroboration — visual observation + cockpit witness + independent radar operator — gives the case exceptional evidentiary weight under any [[credibility-frameworks]] analysis.

> Audio record: "We just heard from Wellington radar that we've got an object about a mile behind us and it's following us. Let's hope they're friendly. … There's a strong target right in formation with you now. … Your target has doubled in size."

## Official Cover-Up

The NZ Air Force and government moved with unusual speed to produce an official explanation: **Japanese squidboat lights on the horizon**. The theory proposed that the pilots and co-pilots of two separate aircraft, flying at 14,000 ft, were misidentified lights below them as objects at cockpit level through atmospheric refraction.

[[ross-coulthart]] later confirmed from released 2011 NZ archives that NZ Air Force officers were explicitly instructed to "find a [expletive] solution as fast as possible" and "get this off the front page" — under direct pressure from the prime minister's office.

## US Intervention

[[robert-muldoon]], NZ Prime Minister at the time, told Coulthart as a young journalist: **"Yeah, the Americans wanted that one shut down pretty quickly."** This is the only known statement from a sitting head of government confirming US intervention in allied UAP suppression.

## Context: Frederick Valentich

The Kaikoura incident occurred in the same December 1978 period as the [[1978-valentich-disappearance]] — Frederick Valentich, a young pilot who disappeared over Bass Strait in October 1978 after reporting a craft above his Cessna. The proximity in time framed the media climate and may have contributed to the urgency of official debunking.
