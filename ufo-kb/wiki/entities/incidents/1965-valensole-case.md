# 1965 Valensole Case

**Date**: July 1, 1965  
**Location**: Valensole, Provence, France  
**Witness**: Maurice Masse, lavender farmer

## The Encounter

Maurice Masse was a lavender farmer in southern France. On July 1, 1965, he arrived at his field at approximately 5 AM to water his crop before the midday heat. He encountered an egg-shaped craft about the size of a medium truck, with two short humanoid beings (approximately 3–3.5 feet tall) outside it.

The beings:
- Were humanoid in appearance — similar description to the beings in the Trinity (1945) and Socorro (1964) cases
- Breathed air (no breathing equipment)
- **Paralyzed Masse**: he could stand and observe but could not move (motor nerve inhibition)
- **Communicated with him** in a way that he described as transcending his ordinary life, giving a sense of "bigger meaning for life — the other side of life" — not about Divinity or God specifically, but a deeper significance

After the beings returned inside the craft, it took off "like a shot out of a gun" and vanished in midair.

## Investigation

Five French government agencies investigated the case. Physical traces at the landing site were confirmed and documented. The local police initially responded; the FBI happened to be in the area on an unrelated case and helped preserve trace evidence.

Masse refused to discuss the content of the communication with the beings with most investigators. He told [[jacques-vallee]] (who visited with a French government diplomat as a local contact) that he thought the beings were likely to return, and was sworn to secrecy about the nature of the communication.

## Significance in Trinity Republication

This case is one of three egg-shaped cases in the republished *[[trinity-best-kept-secret]]* by Vallée and [[paola-harris]]. All three share:
- Egg-shaped craft
- Beings approximately 3–3.5 ft tall, breathing air
- Physical traces
- Witness paralysis (consciousness maintained, motor nerves inhibited)

Vallée's central question: "What kind of extraterrestrial comes here and breathes the air? We don't go to the Moon without breathing equipment."
