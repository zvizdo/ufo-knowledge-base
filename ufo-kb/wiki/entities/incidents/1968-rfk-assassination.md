# 1968 RFK Assassination

Senator Robert F. Kennedy was shot on June 5, 1968 at the Ambassador Hotel in Los Angeles, hours after winning the California Democratic primary. Sirhan Bishara Sirhan was apprehended at the scene and convicted of the murder.

## The MK Ultra Hypothesis

[[danny-sheehan]] — who has won the only successful civil lawsuit against [[mk-ultra]] — argues that Sirhan Sirhan was a programmed [[mk-ultra]] subject deployed as a distraction/scapegoat:

- Sirhan appears to have no memory of firing the shots; under hypnosis he expressed confusion about the events
- Ballistic analysis (including a 2006 acoustic study and the work of researcher Paul Schrade) suggests the fatal shot came from *behind* Kennedy — Sirhan was *in front* of him
- [[eugene-thane-caesar]], a Lockheed security guard positioned behind Kennedy, drew his gun and has never been rigorously cleared
- **William Jennings Bryan II** — hypnotist who allegedly worked with Sirhan at the Santa Anita racetrack in the period before the assassination — has been cited as a possible handler. [[jolly-west]], the CIA-connected psychiatrist, also examined Sirhan post-arrest

## Sheehan's Legal Work
