# 2025–2026 Gilmore Murder

## Account

**Background incident (December 20, 2025):**
[[carl-gilmore]] called police to report a trespasser on his sprawling rural lot in Lano. Deputies found 29-year-old Lano resident **Freddy Snyder** wandering the property carrying a loaded, unregistered rifle. Snyder claimed he was heading to the post office — but the post office was in the opposite direction of both Snyder's and Gilmore's homes. Snyder was arrested on a felony weapons charge, booked at Palmdale Station Jail (where he attempted to escape before his court appearance), then released on his own recognizance by a judge citing his lack of prior criminal record.

**Murder (February 16, 2026):**
At 6:10 AM, deputies responded to a 911 call for assault with a deadly weapon at Gilmore's property. He had been shot on his porch. Paramedics pronounced him dead at the scene. A simultaneous carjacking call came in from nearby; Freddy Snyder was arrested for the carjacking and subsequently charged with Gilmore's murder.

Bail: approximately $3 million. Arraignment postponed from March 26 to April 29, 2026.

**No established motive.** No evidence that Snyder and Gilmore were acquainted. No explanation for why Snyder began a crime spree that started and ended with Gilmore.
