# 2023 Grusch Congressional Testimony

On July 26, 2023, the House Oversight Subcommittee on National Security held a hearing titled "Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena: Implications on National Security, Public Safety, and Government Transparency." Three witnesses testified:

- **[[david-grusch]]** — former NGA and NRO intelligence officer; testified under oath to the existence of multi-decade programs involving non-human craft retrieval and recovery of "non-human biologics"
- **[[ryan-graves]]** — former US Navy F/A-18 pilot; testified to the regularity of UAP encounters among active-duty pilots
- **[[david-fravor]]** — former Navy commander who encountered the Tic-Tac UAP in 2004; corroborated Graves's account of extraordinary performance envelopes

Grusch's testimony was the most significant for disclosure: he stated under oath that he had interviewed individuals with direct knowledge of crash retrieval programs, that injuries and deaths had been caused by these programs, and that Congress was being illegally denied access.

[[danny-sheehan]] characterized the testimony as a step in [[controlled-disclosure]] — Grusch received ICIG authorization before speaking, meaning the disclosure was managed rather than a system failure.
