# 1978 Valentich Disappearance

Frederick Valentich, a young Australian pilot, disappeared over Bass Strait in October 1978 while flying his Cessna. In his final radio communications he reported a large, fast craft hovering above him that he could not identify. Radio contact cut off in mid-sentence and neither Valentich nor his aircraft were ever found.

## Significance

Mentioned by [[ross-coulthart]] in the context of the [[1978-kaikoura]] incident — both occurred within weeks of each other in the same Southern Pacific / Australia-NZ media zone. Coulthart notes that as a teenager, the coincidence of both cases in the Christmas holiday news cycle made it easy to accept the official "media beat-up" framing at the time.

The Valentich case remains one of the most documented pilot-UAP encounter-disappearances on record. The radio recording exists and has been analyzed by researchers.
