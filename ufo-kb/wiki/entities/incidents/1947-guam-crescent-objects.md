# 1947 Guam Crescent-Shaped Objects

On August 14, 1947, three US enlisted men of the 147th AACS (Airways and Air Communications Service) Squadron at Harmon Field, Guam reported observing two crescent-shaped objects.

**Characteristics:**
- Altitude: approximately 1,200 feet
- Speed: approximately twice fighter speed (~600–700 mph)
- Course: zig-zag, westerly direction
- Shape: crescent

This is a multi-witness, uniformed military sighting at a US installation during the height of the 1947 national wave, extending the wave's documented geographic scope to the Pacific theater.
