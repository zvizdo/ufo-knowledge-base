# 1950 Farmington Armada

> *Stub. Expand with full witness accounts, newspaper coverage, and post-event investigation as relevant primary source documentation enters the KB.*

Mass-witness multi-object UAP event in Farmington, New Mexico (Four Corners area) on 17 March 1950. Hundreds of Farmington residents — variously estimated as a substantial fraction of the town's then-3,600 population — witnessed an estimated 200-500 silvery / metallic objects in the sky over multiple hours. Widely covered in the *Farmington Daily Times* the following day. Officially attributed by USAF to a misidentified Skyhook balloon at altitude; the explanation was widely disputed by witnesses.

## 17th OSI District Documentation

The 17th OSI District catalog in [[dow-uap-d17-sandia-base-1948-1950]] documents the **16 March 1950 precursor cluster** as Sightings #191 and #192:

- **#191**: 16 March 1950, 1100 hours, Farmington NM, 10 observers (Unk reliability), N to NE; "Flat spheriodic" 1-6"; "Bright luminous as tin foil"; **"Turned on their axis & maneuvered up & down"**; "Faster than conventional aircraft"; 3-5 mts duration (Eval 2)
- **#192**: 16 March 1950, 1000 hours, Farmington NM, 10 observers (Unk reliability), NE; over 20,000' altitude; "Skyward at 60°-80°"; "Bright aluminum"; oval & oblong 1/16"-1/2" at arm's length; "Faster than conventional aircraft"; 30 mts duration

These precursor entries — preceding the famous mass-witness 17 March event by one day — are the **earliest formal-government documentation** of activity at the Farmington site, recorded in classified format before the public mass-witness event.

## Significance

- One of the largest mass-witness UAP events in US history by raw observer count
- Geographic position: Four Corners area, distant from Los Alamos / Sandia Base but within the broader 17th OSI District jurisdiction
- The 17 March 1949 Camp Hood TX Q-Area 8-light event (per [[dow-uap-d17-sandia-base-1948-1950]]) occurred on the exact same calendar date one year earlier — possibly an anniversary phenomenology pattern worth examining
- Officially-classified balloon explanation is the canonical skeptical position; not directly addressed in the 17th OSI District documentation
