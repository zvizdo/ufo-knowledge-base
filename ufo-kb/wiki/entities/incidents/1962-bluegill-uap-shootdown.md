# 1962 Bluegill UAP Shootdown

The 1962 Bluegill UAP Shootdown is the alleged incident in which an unidentified aerial phenomenon (UAP) that had been monitoring the [[bluegill-triple-prime|Bluegill Triple Prime]] high-altitude nuclear test on October 26, 1962, was downed by the X-ray pulse from the detonation. The craft reportedly fell into the Pacific Ocean, was recovered by the US Navy, and the recovered material was transported to Los Alamos National Laboratory.

This incident is the primary case study for the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] concept at the shootdown level — not merely passive UAP observation of nuclear activity, but the documented (alleged) downing of a UAP as a direct consequence of a nuclear detonation.

## Account

Richard Bissell, CIA Deputy Director for Plans (DDP), told Harald Malmgren that during the Bluegill Triple Prime test, a UAP "tag-along" — a craft that had been shadowing the test, as was reportedly routine at nuclear tests by 1962 — was positioned so close to the detonation point that the X-ray burst from the nuclear device reached it. The craft "tumbled into the ocean."

The US Navy recovered the craft (or significant material from it) from the Pacific. The recovered material was transferred to **Los Alamos National Laboratory**, where it was held under the authority of **Lawrence Geist** (AEC Albuquerque Division).

At Geist's personal request, **Harald Malmgren** — present at Los Alamos in his capacity as a senior government advisor — handled the recovered material. His reported observations:
- The material was **physically present** (solid, occupying space) but felt **weightless**.
- Its color was described as **"the color of something in space"** — a color outside the range of normal terrestrial reference.
- While handling it, Malmgren experienced what he described as **telepathic communication** — words appearing in his mind without auditory input. He later connected this experience to the modalities investigated by the [[stargate-program]].

Malmgren subsequently relayed this account to **Kirk McConnell**, a senior staffer on the Senate Intelligence Committee, in the context of briefing congressional staff on the UAP phenomenon.

## Witnesses

- **Richard Bissell** (CIA DDP) — Relayed the shootdown account to Malmgren; primary government official source for the core claim.

- **Jeffrey Kruchshank** — Independent researcher; analyzed the partially declassified Kettle 1 film footage from the Bluegill Triple Prime test; filed the 2023 mandatory declassification review (MDR) request that produced the DOE's "unable to locate" response for the Kettle 2 film.
- **Kirk McConnell** — Senate Intelligence Committee staff; received Malmgren's account directly; represents the congressional intelligence oversight community's exposure to the claim.

## Evidence

**Film footage — Kettle 1:**
Filmed by a KC-135 aircraft operated by Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory. Partially declassified in 1998. One frame contains a white triangle redaction applied by **Byron Ristvet** of the Defense Special Weapons Agency. Kruchshank's analysis of this footage identified anomalies consistent with a "second thermal source" during the detonation sequence.

**Film footage — Kettle 2:**
Filmed by a KC-135 aircraft operated by Los Alamos National Laboratory. Reported **"unable to locate"** by the Department of Energy (DOE) in response to Kruchshank's 2023 mandatory declassification review request. This is the only Fishbowl test with unaccounted film records.

**Flight Dynamics Laboratory report (Wright-Patterson AFB):**
A post-test official report identified a **"second thermal source"** in the Bluegill Triple Prime footage. The report attempted to explain this anomaly through conventional mechanisms without success.

**FOIA to National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA) — June 2022:**
A FOIA request to the NNSA implied the existence of responsive records related to the test anomalies but resulted in no substantive release.

**AVCO Mark IV / Atlas 8F (September 1962 — related):**
A separate incident at Cape Canaveral one month before Bluegill Triple Prime involved a UAP that "phased into existence" alongside an AVCO Mark IV re-entry vehicle on an Atlas 8F booster traveling at Mach 18. The official report on this event stated the "origin could not be determined" — an admission without explanation.

## Aftermath

- The recovered material was held at Los Alamos; its subsequent disposition is unknown.
- Malmgren's handling of the material and his proximity to the account appear to have resulted in his name being entered into talent-tracking books maintained by multiple intelligence communities, including what he referred to as "the majestic" — see [[majestic-12]].
- The X-ray pulse mechanism observed in this incident became the technical basis for Edward Teller's Project Excalibur and, subsequently, the [[strategic-defense-initiative]] — whether deliberately or as an emergent consequence of the Fishbowl research findings.
- The Kettle 2 film's "unable to locate" status as of 2023 means that the most direct visual record of the incident (if it exists) remains outside public or reviewed access.
