# 2020-arabian-gulf-uap-d38

Round object tracked intermittently during an ISR mission over the northern Arabian Gulf (~28°N, 49°E) on 14 May 2020 at 20:40Z. Platform at 20,000 ft. Object appeared solid white in TN/Black-Hot/Lin IR polarity; flew through sensor FOV; performed erratic movements; was at very low altitude with ocean waves visible in background. Sensor operator continuously repositioned sensor. MDR 26-0019.
