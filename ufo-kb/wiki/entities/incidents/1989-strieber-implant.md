# 1989 Strieber Implant (Discovery and Analysis)

In 1989, [[whitley-strieber]] discovered an anomalous object behind his ear. The object produced an anomalous magnetic field. It was sent for analysis to [[southwest-research]] (Southwest Research Institute, San Antonio), which found:
- Metallic composition with cilia-like biological structures
- Emission of a classified radio signal, "still under study" as of the mid-2000s

[[whitley-strieber]] later identified [[constantine-raudive]] — a Latvian parapsychologist who died circa 1974 — as the apparent inventor of the device, implying either:
1. The device was developed before Raudive's death and placed later, or
2. A successor program continued his work posthumously.

The 2015 death of [[anne-strieber]] was experienced by Whitley as activating the implant — suggesting a long-term programming function.
