# 1947 West Rindge NH Metal Fragments

On July 7, 1947 — during the peak of the national flying saucer wave — metallic fragments were recovered in West Rindge, New Hampshire. The fragments were submitted to MIT for analysis.

**MIT findings:**
- Material similar to inner lining of a V-2 rocket
- Diameter: approximately 14 inches
- Thickness: 3/16 inch
- Manufacturing method: machine-tooled (not cast)
- Thermal evidence: subjected to terrific heat

The fragments were transferred to Army Air Forces after analysis. No disposition record appears in available declassified records.

If authentically associated with an aerial object, these fragments represent the earliest laboratory-analyzed anomalous aerial debris in the FBI's documented UFO record — predating the Roswell incident by ten days.
