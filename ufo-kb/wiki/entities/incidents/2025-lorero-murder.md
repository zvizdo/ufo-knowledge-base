# 2025 Lorero Murder

## Account

**Related incident (December 13, 2025):** Claudio Emanuel Neves Valente — a Portuguese national — entered an open lecture hall at Brown University's Barus and Holley engineering building during final exams and opened fire with a 9mm pistol, killing two students and wounding nine others. Valente had conducted surveillance on the Brown campus for months, renting a storage unit in Salem, NH, and returning repeatedly to scout the building. He evaded police for two days using a burner phone, European SIM card, and swapped license plates.

**The Brookline murder (December 15, 2025):**
At 8:30 PM, the doorbell rang at [[nuno-lorero]]'s Gibb Street apartment. His youngest daughter answered, saw a man in a yellow vest with gray stripes carrying what appeared to be a delivery package with a barcode, and returned to the card game her family was playing. Lorero came to the foyer; the family heard gunshots.

Lorero was wounded in the upper left chest, upper abdomen, right thigh, and a graze wound through his left thigh. He was conscious and responsive when paramedics arrived. He answered questions by shaking his head. He did not speak. He died during surgery; pronounced dead the following morning.

Security cameras recorded a masked suspect matching Valente's description on Commonwealth Avenue earlier that day. The Nissan Sentra he had rented was tracked to the area. After the shooting, he drove to his New Hampshire storage unit, where he recorded a series of confessional videos before his eventual apprehension.

**Primary suspect:** Valente. He and Lorero had studied physics together at Instituto Superior Técnico in Lisbon ~20 years earlier. Valente was reportedly top of his class but failed his MIT graduate school admission test; Lorero gained admission and went on to a distinguished career while Valente dropped out of Brown and effectively disappeared.

## Unresolved Anomalies

1. **Motive vagueness:** Valente's confessional videos confirm both attacks were intentional but leave his specific grievance against Lorero maddeningly vague. The decades-old academic rivalry is the primary inference.
2. **Timeline gap:** ~48 hours between the Brown shooting and the Brookline attack are only partially accounted for. How did Valente locate Lorero, confirm his address, and verify he was home? Lorero had just returned from a DC trip.
3. **Surveillance pattern:** Brown was meticulously surveilled over months; Lorero's home was approached with less apparent planning. Was Lorero a target of longer standing, or was Lorero's targeting an opportunistic extension once the Brown mission was complete?
4. **Behavior on the day:** Cameras record Valente pacing near the Lorero home for hours — described in the source as "having cold feet or awaiting instruction."
5. **The TAE merger:** Two days after Lorero's death, [[tae-technologies]] announced a $6B merger with Trump Media — a competitor of [[commonwealth-fusion-systems]], whose scientific pipeline ran through the PSFC that Lorero directed.
