# 1947 UFO Wave

The 1947 UFO wave — also called the "flying saucer wave" — was the first mass-scale aerial anomaly event in the modern era. It was triggered by [[kenneth-arnold]]'s June 24, 1947 sighting over Mount Rainier and sustained by thousands of subsequent reports across the continental United States and US territories through August 1947.

## Scale and Scope

The FBI's 62-HQ-83894 case file documents reports from 39+ states, with physical evidence cases including the [[1947-west-rindge-nh-fragments]] (MIT analysis, V-2-type metal) and the [[1947-maury-island]] incident. Military sightings extended to the Pacific theater, including the [[1947-guam-crescent-objects]] observation by three enlisted men of the 147th AACS Squadron.

## Institutional Response

The wave prompted General [[george-schulgen]] of AAF Air Intelligence Requirements Division to write to FBI Director [[j-edgar-hoover]] on July 9, 1947, requesting Bureau assistance. Schulgen's letter confirmed that no US military programs were responsible for the sightings. The formal cooperation agreement — Bureau Bulletin No. 42, July 30, 1947 — included Hoover's handwritten demand for access to any recovered discs.

The wave also coincided with the [[1947-roswell]] crash and the subsequent institutional responses that shaped UAP information management for the following two decades.
