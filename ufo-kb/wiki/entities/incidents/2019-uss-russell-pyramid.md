# 2019 USS Russell Pyramid UAP

In 2019, the destroyer USS Russell, operating in the Pacific Ocean, encountered an unidentified aerial object described in official Pentagon briefing materials as **"triangle by angle of observation, pyramid in shape."** The object hovered approximately **700 feet above the deck** of the ship.

A classified photograph of the object was included in briefing slide packages prepared by the Pentagon for senior military leadership, including the Joint Chiefs of Staff, and for Congressional briefings. [[jeremy-corbell]] obtained and released the photograph publicly.

The **Department of Justice** subsequently announced a criminal investigation into how the classified image entered the public record — a response that [[jeremy-corbell]] describes as a deterrent against further leaks rather than a genuine criminal inquiry. Corbell's position: briefing material that Congress has seen should be accessible to the public; the DOJ response confirmed the photograph's authenticity by treating its release as a serious security matter.

The briefing's official characterization — "triangle by angle of observation, pyramid in shape" — indicates the Pentagon's own analysts determined the object's three-dimensional shape based on multiple viewing angles or additional imagery. This stands in contrast to the Nimitz/Gimbal/Go Fast footage, where object shape is interpreted from two-dimensional sensor output.

The USS Russell encounter is described alongside the [[2019-uss-omaha-uso]] sphere incidents as part of a cluster of documented 2019 Navy UAP events that together prompted accelerated Congressional attention.
