# 1947 Roswell Crash

The 1947 Roswell crash is the most widely known alleged UAP retrieval event in US history. In July 1947, one or more unidentified craft came down near Roswell, New Mexico. The US Army Air Force initially issued a press release acknowledging recovery of a "flying disc," then retracted the statement and substituted a cover story attributing the debris to a weather balloon (later revised to Project Mogul).

The broad outlines of Roswell — craft recovery, biological material, cover-up — are extensively documented in other entries. This record focuses specifically on the **new claims introduced in [[09KP8XVf5nY]]** through Harald Malmgren's testimony as relayed by his daughter Pippa Malmgren.

## Account

Per Pippa Malmgren's account of her father Harald's **deathbed statement**, Harald Malmgren had seen a video recording of an interview with the **sole surviving non-human entity** recovered from the Roswell crash. The entity was alive, recovered by US authorities, and formally interviewed on video — a recording that Malmgren reportedly viewed.

The specific and consequential claim is that the surviving entity disclosed to US officials the concept of using **directed energy — specifically a nuclear X-ray pulse — to disable or shoot down UAPs**. This would mean that the mechanism later used (incidentally or intentionally) to down the UAP at [[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown|Bluegill Triple Prime in 1962]] was not independently discovered by US physicists but was disclosed by a non-human intelligence that had its own reasons — strategic, communicative, or otherwise — for providing this information.

This creates a profoundly recursive loop:
- A non-human entity discloses the directed-energy concept after surviving a crash.
- US physicists and weapons designers develop the concept through [[operation-dominic|Operation Dominic]] and Project Excalibur.
- Fifteen years later, US nuclear testing incidentally (or deliberately) uses that mechanism to down another UAP at Bluegill Triple Prime.
- The US then formalizes the capability into the [[strategic-defense-initiative]] — a nationally declared defense program that may be, at one level of its hierarchy, a weapons program directed against the civilization that originally provided the concept.

**Source reliability:** This account is **hearsay** — Pippa relaying Harald's deathbed statement. Harald himself was not present at the Roswell event; his claimed knowledge was from viewing the interview video at some undisclosed point in his career. The reliability of deathbed accounts is contextually variable, and Pippa's transmission adds a second remove. These caveats are fundamental to interpreting the claim.

## Witnesses

- **Harald Malmgren** — Claims to have viewed a video interview with the surviving Roswell entity; the primary source for this specific disclosure claim; account relayed posthumously.
- **Pippa Malmgren** — Harald's daughter; relayed his deathbed account; the actual speaker in [[09KP8XVf5nY]].

The broader Roswell case involves dozens of firsthand witnesses (Mac Brazel, Jesse Marcel, Frankie Rowe, Walter Haut, Glenn Dennis, and others) whose accounts are documented extensively elsewhere and are not reproduced here.

## Evidence

The evidence specific to the new claims in [[09KP8XVf5nY]] is limited to Pippa Malmgren's testimony. No corroborating document or independent witness has surfaced for the specific claims of:
- A surviving non-human entity.
- A video interview with that entity.
- Disclosure of the directed-energy concept by the entity.

The broader Roswell case has substantial evidentiary grounding in declassified documents, witness testimony, and physical evidence claims — all predating and independent of the Malmgren account.

## Aftermath

- Wright-Patterson Air Force Base received Roswell material and, allegedly, biological material; this is consistent with the [[project-mcgregor|Project McGregor]] / Magenta disc transfer to Wright-Patterson in 1945, suggesting Wright-Patterson was already the designated repository for anomalous material.
- The alleged survival and interview of a non-human entity would represent the US government's first direct contact with non-human intelligence — predating by 15 years the indirect, material-mediated contact Harald Malmgren reportedly experienced at Los Alamos.
- If the directed-energy disclosure occurred as claimed, the Roswell survivor's motivations remain unexplained: why provide a potential weapon against one's own kind?

## Tim Taylor and the Roswell Material Test

[[tim-taylor]] (NASA Mission Controller, multi-agency credentials) brought a **piece of the 1947 Roswell crash** to [[chris-bledsoe]]'s North Carolina home and placed it in Bledsoe's hand. Bledsoe reacted unexpectedly strongly. Taylor's response: "Why you?" — curious and not negative, suggesting Taylor was testing for a specific sensitivity and was surprised by the intensity of Bledsoe's reaction. Ryan Bledsoe also handled the material.

This account (relayed by [[bob-maguire]]) is significant because:
1. It confirms Tim Taylor has access to physical Roswell material — implying multi-agency access at the custodian level.
2. The material produces a measurable experiential effect in at least some individuals.
3. The "Why you?" response suggests Taylor expected a reaction but not this one — implying some experiencers show more material-sensitivity than others.

## Jorjani's Nazi Psyop Hypothesis (ABw7EsWh2Us)

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] advances a competing interpretation in [[ABw7EsWh2Us]]: "I'm not suggesting that all these crashes have to do with Nazi psychological operations, but the particular incident at Roswell, I think did."

His argument:
1. **Saucer sightings from Spain/Mediterranean coast** launching toward America were reported approximately one year before Roswell — Jorjani attributes these to Nazi test flights using [[die-glocke]]-powered craft
2. **Curtis LeMay** commanded the **59th Atomic Bomber Squadron stationed at Roswell** in July 1947 — adding suspicious military context
3. **Philip Corso's artifact findings** (perforated metal, Kevlar) are analyzed by [[joseph-farrell]] (*Saucer Swastikas and SCOPS*) as matching Nazi aeronautical engineering rather than extraterrestrial manufacture
4. The **CIA was founded in September 1947** — two months after Roswell — and immediately absorbed [[reinhard-gehlen]]'s Nazi intelligence apparatus; Jorjani argues the timing is not coincidental
5. **Operation Paperclip** brought Nazi scientists including Die Glocke-adjacent researchers into US programs; Roswell could be a handoff event or test within this transfer

> ⚠ **Major conflict**: This interpretation directly contradicts the Malmgren/Pippa Malmgren deathbed account (primary source in this entry) of a **surviving non-human entity** recovered from Roswell. A Nazi test crash would not produce a living non-human entity. These accounts cannot both be fully correct. Possible resolutions: (a) multiple crash events conflated under "Roswell"; (b) one account is disinformation; (c) the Nazi craft itself contained NHI-derived technology that Jorjani's framework misattributes as purely human manufacture.

## Brandenburg Veteran Accounts (p0S0BfoZy0w)

[[john-brandenberg]] attended the "Reminiscences of Roswell" conference, where he sat at a banquet with firsthand witnesses — survivor officers, daughters of the sheriff, and other participants. These accounts are one remove from firsthand (veterans → Brandenburg):

**Nuclear Context**: The **509th Bomb Group** at Roswell stored all five of Earth's nuclear weapons — the entire global nuclear arsenal. **P-61 Black Widow** night fighter squadrons, relocated from Europe and Japan, patrolled the perimeter to protect the stockpile. These were vacuum tube aircraft (immune to electromagnetic interference), which may be relevant to the crash event.

**Colonel Blanchard**: The base commander was "a very well-liked fellow, very popular." On receiving reports of the crash wreckage found by Mac Brazel, Blanchard called the local newspaper and announced they had recovered a crashed flying saucer — without orders one way or another regarding UFOs. He was immediately berated by phone from Fort Worth. His quote at the subsequent Monday morning staff meeting: **"Boy did we screw up."**

**The Red-Haired Colonel**: An unidentified colonel — described as red-haired and known for "just screaming at everybody" — arrived with multiple deuce-and-a-half trucks of troops and took over Roswell Army Air Field for approximately a week. He gathered all wreckage across football-field-sized areas, including I-beams with hieroglyphics and unusual plates. Troops walked side-by-side across fields picking up every fragment. Some apparently pocketed small pieces. All material was flown out before the colonel departed.

**Brandenburg's Interpretation**: The craft were monitoring the nuclear weapons stockpile. A severe lightning storm created massive electromagnetic interference that overwhelmed the craft's extremely sensitive monitoring equipment. The Black Widows may have been involved in the encounter. Brandenburg explicitly does not credit the "directed energy weapon" origin story — he believes it was environmental EM and possible miscalculation during a nuclear survey mission.

**Separate Event at San Agustín**: Brandenburg relays that the same red-haired colonel appeared separately at the [[1947-san-agustin-crash|Plains of San Agustín]] (~100 miles away) the same morning (July 4), where an intact saucer with a big hole in its side was recovered along with dead alien bodies and **one live alien**. Civilian witnesses at that site were threatened with death.
