# Stanford University

Stanford University is a private research university in Stanford, California (near Palo Alto, Silicon Valley). It is one of the world's leading research universities and a key institutional node in the KB's UAP science and intelligence-adjacent research network.

**Garry Nolan**: [[garry-nolan]], a professor in Stanford's School of Medicine, has conducted peer-reviewed research on brain imaging of individuals claiming UAP contact, reporting structural brain differences in some subjects. He has also been involved in isotopic analysis of alleged UAP material samples and co-founded the [[sol-foundation]].

**Harold Puthoff**: [[hal-puthoff]] received his PhD at Stanford and worked at Stanford Research Institute (SRI) — an independent research institute spun off from Stanford — where he co-ran the [[remote-viewing]] program (STARGATE) in the 1970s–80s. Puthoff's later work on zero-point energy and advanced propulsion connected SRI's anomalous research tradition to the AATIP program.

**SRI International**: Stanford Research International (now SRI International) is the independent contractor that hosted the government-funded remote viewing research. The SRI–Stanford connection is a recurring element in the KB's mapping of academic institutions that intersected with classified government anomalous research programs.

**Silicon Valley nexus**: Stanford's relationship with the technology industry — [[elon-musk]], [[mark-andreessen]], and other figures who appear in the KB's NSC classified-physics disclosure narrative — makes it an institutional hub connecting academic UAP research to the tech investor community.
