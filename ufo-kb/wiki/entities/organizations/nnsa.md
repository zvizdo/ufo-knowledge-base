# NNSA

The **National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA)** is a semi-autonomous agency within the [[doe|Department of Energy]] responsible for maintaining and securing the US nuclear weapons stockpile, managing nuclear nonproliferation programs, and overseeing the US nuclear weapons complex. It was established in 2000.

## Nevada Site (Las Vegas)

The NNSA operates a significant presence in the Las Vegas, Nevada area, associated with the Nevada National Security Site (formerly the Nevada Test Site) — one of the most active US nuclear weapons testing and research locations during the Cold War.

## Connection to Wilson-Davis Memo

[[eric-davis]] disclosed in [[xnxasfyHtfo]] that his contacts at the **NNSA Nevada site** — specifically the **Director of Intelligence** and the **Director of Counter-Intelligence** — were instrumental in facilitating the Wilson-Davis meeting.

Both officials were members of the **Association of Former Intelligence Officers (AFIO)** alongside Davis, giving him a trusted-contact channel outside formal institutional lines. These NNSA contacts helped arrange the meeting that produced the Wilson-Davis memo, making the NNSA a previously underappreciated node in the Wilson-Davis story.

This detail places the Wilson-Davis facilitation chain as:  
**AFIO contacts (NNSA Nevada)** → [[john-alexander]] (who also had NNSA Las Vegas connections) → Davis → [[thomas-wilson]]

## DOE Jurisdiction and UAP

Under the [[atomic-energy-act-classification|Atomic Energy Act of 1954]], any material with a radioactive signature falls under DOE/NNSA jurisdiction automatically. The NNSA's role in nuclear security means it sits at the intersection of the most classified US government activities and the DOE pipeline that, per [[eric-davis]] and others, routes crash retrieval material toward aerospace contractors like [[lockheed-martin]] and [[northrop]].
