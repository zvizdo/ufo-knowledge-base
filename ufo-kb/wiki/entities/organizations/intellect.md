# Intellect

## Background

Intellect (also called "Intellecton" in some references) was [[andrija-puharich]]'s covert research company operating during the 1960s. Its location was in the Elmsford, New York area. It is extraordinary for how completely it has vanished from the public record: filmmaker [[greg-malazzi]] searched exhaustively and could find almost nothing. The only surviving documentation came from papers with an Intellect letterhead found in the Berkeley archives of NASA consultant [[beardsley-graham]], who was an outside consultant to the company.

## Research Activities

### TD-100 Device

The Transdermal 100 (TD-100) was Puharich's primary invention at Intellect: a device that could send a specific radio frequency to a person at short range — initially across a room, with ambitions for longer distances — that would be picked up by the brain's audio-processing centers. In early demonstrations it used a physical tooth implant; later experiments attempted to bypass the implant entirely and beam the frequency directly to the skull.

The TD-100 was demonstrated at the Pentagon: Puharich fitted a tooth device to a general, had a broadcaster in the hallway, and the general heard the transmission — reportedly saying "God damn it, I hear it, the bastard did it."

### Brain Implant Research

Puharich was close friends with [[jose-delgado]], the Yale neurosurgeon famous for remote-controlling bulls via brain electrodes. Letters between them survive; they were in regular professional contact during the Intellect period. Malazzi believes the implant research being conducted at Intellect was direct heir to Delgado's work, extending it from animals toward human subjects.

### Classified Experiments

Footage exists of an experiment on a woman conducted at Intellect: she is in a separate room, connected via the TD-100 device, and the footage clearly shows the transmission working. No audio accompanies the footage.

## The Documented Gap

The 1960s represent a strange gap in Puharich's record: abundant documentation exists for the Roundtable Foundation (1948–1955) and for the Ossining Space Kids period (1970s), but the 1960s are nearly empty. Malazzi believes this is because Intellect's classified activities required total documentary silence.

Puharich never discussed Intellect publicly. Even family members appeared to not know about its activities. It "went dark" around 1968.

## Later Relevance

Puharich brought the TD-100 research into the [[space-kids]] program in the 1970s, using related frequency devices on the children — including the prosthetic finger ELF device — without telling colleagues what he was doing.
