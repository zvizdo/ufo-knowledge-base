# Consolidated Nuclear Security, LLC (CNS LLC)

> *Stub. Expand with corporate history, security-incident track record, and any additional UAP-relevant documentation as it enters the KB.*

DOE/NNSA prime management & operations (M&O) contractor for two facilities in the US nuclear weapons complex:
- [[pantex-nuclear-facility]] (Amarillo, Texas — nuclear weapons assembly/disassembly/modification)
- Y-12 National Security Complex (Oak Ridge, Tennessee — uranium processing and HEU storage)

CNS took over the combined contract from previous management arrangements (Mason & Hanger at Pantex, B&W Y-12 at Oak Ridge) in **July 2014**, with a contract value totaling tens of billions of dollars over the original 10-year base + options period. The consortium is led by Bechtel National with Leidos, Orbital ATK (now Northrop Grumman Innovation Systems), and SOC LLC as members.

- **Producer of [[doe-uap-d1-pantex-image-fragment]]**: CNS LLC's letterhead and the "Managed and Operated by Consolidated Nuclear Security, LLC" subheading identify CNS as the issuer of the formal "Pantex Unidentified Object Incident Report" partially released in PURSUE Release 02 (2026-05-22).
