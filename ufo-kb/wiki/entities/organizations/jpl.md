# Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)

The **Jet Propulsion Laboratory** is NASA's primary facility for planetary science and robotic space missions, managed by [[caltech]] on behalf of NASA. It is located in Pasadena, California.

## Founding

JPL grew from an informal rocketry group formed in the mid-1930s by [[jack-parsons]], Frank Malina, Ed Foreman, and engineering student Theodore von Kármán, who began testing experimental engines in the **Arroyo Seco** — a dry gulch just outside Pasadena. The group was known informally as the "Suicide Squad" for the dangerous nature of their early tests.

The JPL site (now in La Cañada Flintridge, CA) grew from that original test location. The early JPL team:
- Developed **JATO (Jet-Assisted Take-Off)** — solid fuel boosters to help overloaded aircraft take off; first successful test in 1941; by 1942 the US military was ordering 20,000 units/month
- Avoided the word "rocket" in official documents because it sounded too much like science fiction
- Eventually attracted formal military funding and became the institutional foundation for American rocketry

## "Jack Parsons Lives"

Some JPL insiders reportedly still call it **"Jack Parsons Laboratory"** rather than Jet Propulsion Laboratory — an acknowledgment that Parsons' simultaneous identity as occultist and rocket engineer has not been entirely forgotten within the institution.
