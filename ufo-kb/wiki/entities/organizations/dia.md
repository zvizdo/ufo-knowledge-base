# DIA (Defense Intelligence Agency)

**DIA** is the US Department of Defense's primary all-source intelligence agency, providing military intelligence to warfighters and defense policymakers.

## UAP Relevance

- **AAWSAP/AATIP**: DIA administered the [[aawsap]] contract to Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies (BAASS) starting 2008; DIA also housed or supported [[aatip]] under Luis Elizondo.
- **Stargate Program**: DIA was a primary sponsor of the [[remote-viewing]] / [[stargate-program]] program alongside CIA and Army.
- **Eric Davis reports**: [[eric-davis]] produced classified technical reports for DIA on UAP reverse engineering and related physics topics; at least one report (on "warp drives and anti-gravity") was released via FOIA.
- **Wilson-Davis memo**: The alleged meeting between [[thomas-wilson]] (DIA/Joint Staff) and [[eric-davis]] is at the center of the Wilson-Davis document controversy.
