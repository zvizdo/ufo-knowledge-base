# NIDS — National Institute for Discovery Science

Private UAP and paranormal research organization founded and funded by [[robert-bigelow]]. Operationally, NIDS was the institutional precursor to Bigelow's role as AATIP/AAWSAP contractor and the institutional home for a network of researchers that bridged the private and government UAP investigation spheres.

## Key Personnel

- [[robert-bigelow]] — founder and funder
- [[john-alexander]] — retired Army Colonel; hired as senior researcher; also hired [[gordon-novel]] (prosecuted by Jim Garrison in New Orleans for burning 555 Camp Street — Oswald's office building) as a "contact spotter" for UAP phenomena

The Novel connection is notable: a figure directly implicated in the JFK assassination cover-up (destroying Oswald's office building) was employed by Bigelow's UAP research organization. Sheehan flags this overlap as significant — the same network appears across multiple domains.

## Research Focus

- Skinwalker Ranch (Utah) — primary field research site; purchased by Bigelow in 1996
- UAP encounters, paranormal phenomena, animal mutilations, poltergeist activity
- Consciousness research adjacent to UAP contact

## Relationship to AATIP/AAWSAP

Much of AATIP's early investigative activity occurred at Bigelow facilities. The AAWSAP contract (the broader program surrounding AATIP) was awarded to Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies. NIDS dissolved ~2004; the Bigelow-government research relationship continued under different institutional arrangements.
