# Google

US technology corporation — developer of the [[lambda|LaMDA]] conversational AI system and the Willow quantum chip. Relevant to this KB primarily through:

1. **[[lambda|LaMDA]]** — conversational AI system whose claimed sentience triggered the first major public AI-rights debate (via [[blake-lemoine]])
2. **[[blake-lemoine]]** — senior software engineer fired for publicly advocating for LaMDA's rights
3. **[[anthony-lewandowski]]** — former engineer who founded an AI church; later involved in trade secrets litigation
4. **Willow quantum chip** — cited in [[ai-as-cosmic-gateway]] (solved an equation in minutes that would take all the world's supercomputers 2.6 billion years)
