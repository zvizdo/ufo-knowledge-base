# DARPA

Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency. Created in 1958 in response to Sputnik, DARPA funds high-risk, high-reward military technology programs that private industry and conventional military R&D would not pursue. Its exceptional acquisition authority — an explicit exception from federal acquisition regulations — distinguishes it from all other national laboratories and makes it uniquely capable of rapidly seeding technologies into the private sector without standard oversight.

## DARPA as NHI Technology Conduit (Blitch/Corso Thesis)

[[john-blitch]] builds a case — first articulated at a St. Patrick's Day DARPA party in 2001 — that DARPA was designed in part as the institutional successor to General Trudeau's mandate (per [[philip-corso]]'s account in *The Day After Roswell*) to distribute recovered NHI component technologies into the U.S. free-market economy:

1. **Founding timing**: DARPA was founded in 1958 — the same era as Trudeau's purported alien-tech distribution program in Army R&D
2. **Exceptional acquisition authority**: DARPA can move contracts from concept to award in days vs. the 6–12 month standard at other labs — exactly the mechanism needed for opaque, rapid technology seeding
3. **Unexplained cancellations**: Blitch's own $70M stealth bio-camouflage SAP (reverse-engineering cephalopod skin crypsis) was cancelled with no explanation one month after approval — consistent with the pattern of programs being terminated once underlying capabilities are confirmed to exist in a black program
4. **2004 tank-invisibility video**: Blitch saw an Al Jazeera/Couric clip of what appeared to be an invisible entity climbing onto a U.S. tank in Iraq — his immediate reaction: "that's why my program was cancelled"

This thesis does not require DARPA leadership to know what they're distributing — the mechanism could be functional even if individual program managers are unaware of the NHI origin of the technology they're developing toward.

## John Blitch's Programs

[[john-blitch]] served as a DARPA Program Manager in the late 1990s through early 2000s:

- **Tactical Mobile Robotics** (~$65M): miniature robots for cave reconnaissance (WMD search, bin Laden tunnel mission), rubble navigation, infiltration through small spaces; deployed to Bagram Airfield 2002
- **Stealth bio-camouflage SAP** (~$65M): classified program to reverse-engineer cephalopod (octopus/squid/cuttlefish) skin crypsis for military camouflage applications; sold on the basis of Roger Hanlon's MBL Woods Hole cephalopod video; **cancelled one month after approval with no explanation**

## Afghanistan Mission (2002)

Blitch was called back from his SAIC VP role by DARPA director Art Morris for a by-name request from former boss "Jumo Brown" — to deploy miniature robots for cave/tunnel reconnaissance at Bagram Airfield. The mission was demoralized when Blitch learned that bin Laden had been in U.S. sniper sights three times and engagement was denied each time (per *Kill Bin Laden* author Dalton Fury). He subsequently resigned after his demand to handpick his robot operator team was refused.
