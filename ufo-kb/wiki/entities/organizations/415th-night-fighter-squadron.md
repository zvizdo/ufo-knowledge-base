# 415th Night Fighter Squadron

USAAF unit assigned to XII Tactical Air Command during WWII; flew night interdiction missions over occupied France and Germany (Rhine valley, Alsace region). Primary source of the first systematic pilot documentation of the [[foo-fighters]] phenomenon.

## Foo Fighter Encounters

Between December 1944 and January 1945, 415th NFS crews repeatedly encountered unexplained luminous phenomena during night missions. GCI radar control consistently reported "negative" (no conventional aircraft in area) when queried during these events. The unit's 30 January 1945 endorsement memo explicitly coined the term "Foofighters" as the name "given these phenomenon by combat crews of this Squadron."

Key encounters reported by the unit:
- Lights blinking on/off changing colors, flying formation with aircraft
- Objects following aircraft for up to 5 minutes despite evasive action
- One object came within 100 ft, "peeled off" during evasive maneuver but resumed pursuit
- Lights "appearing to be under perfect control at all times"
- Propwash felt from one object that came alongside

These reports were escalated through XII TAC → SHAEF G-2 → Air Ministry, which closed the investigation in March 1945 with the conclusion: "The whole affair is still something of a mystery."
