# Wuhan Institute of Technology

The **Wuhan Institute of Technology** (WIT) is a Chinese state university distinct from the **Wuhan Institute of Virology** (WIV). Its primary connection to the UAP knowledge base is through the Charles Lieber case.

## Charles Lieber Payments

WIT served as the primary funder of Harvard chemistry chair Charles Lieber's secret participation in China's [[thousand-talents-program]]. Payments included $50,000/month in salary, $150,000+ in living expenses, and $1.5M to establish a nanotechnology laboratory in China. These payments continued for multiple years before the COVID-19 pandemic emerged in Wuhan in late 2019.

Jesse Michaels in [[ePdH01pphbk]] notes: "I've never even heard somebody broach the topic" of the geographical coincidence — that the institution paying off one of America's leading nanotechnologists was located in the same city as the pandemic's origin, for years before the outbreak. This is offered as a question, not a conclusion.
