# Arrow (Organization)

A coordinating authority described by [[jake-barber]] in [[dnnpyNuPdXs]] that he works *with* (not for) following his defection from and reorganization out of legacy UAP programs. Barber alternately spells this "Arrol" and "Arrow"; both appear to refer to the same entity.

> Note: Arrow/Arrol should not be conflated with AARO (All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office) — Barber expresses skepticism about AARO's effectiveness and discusses Arrow separately as the entity actually coordinating his operations.

## Overview

Arrow appears to function as the operational coordination layer for Barber's retrieval activities:
- Issues GPS coordinates to Barber's helicopter at the last moment before a retrieval
- Provides en-route mission briefings (description of object being picked up)
- Authorized Barber's public disclosure (per Barber's characterization)
- Organized the post-program egg demonstration in August (exact year unclear)

Barber's framing positions Arrow as distinct from the legacy programs he defected from — a reorganization that allows the same operational capabilities to be used under different auspices and (implied) ethical norms.
