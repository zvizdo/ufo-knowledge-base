# Disclosure Project

The Disclosure Project is [[steven-greer]]'s organization, best known for hosting the **2001 National Press Club event** in Washington DC — one of the highest-profile UAP witness events in history, at which approximately 20 military and government whistleblowers testified on the record about UAP encounters, recovered materials, and cover-up operations.

## Project Starlight — Private Phase (mid-1990s)

The Disclosure Project's private-phase code name was **Project Starlight**. During this period Greer was conducting classified briefings for government officials — including Clinton's CIA Director James Woolsey (briefed December 13, 1993) — while building the archive and witness network that would go public in 2001.

## The 2001 Press Club Event

On May 9, 2001, the Disclosure Project presented witnesses including former military radar operators, FAA officials, and other credentialed sources before a large press contingent. Per Greer in [[areO7Mej44E]], the event reached approximately **800 million people** globally. The event preceded 9/11 by ~four months; Greer explicitly claims 9/11 was engineered as a deliberate diversion to displace the disclosure momentum. UAP coverage was rapidly eclipsed.

The testimony is still cited as among the most credentialed multi-witness affirmations of UAP reality.

Witnesses connected to this event: [[donna-hare]] (NASA photo retouching allegation), and others documented in the Disclosure Project's formal witness testimony archive.

## CSETI

The Disclosure Project grew from Greer's earlier Center for the Study of Extraterrestrial Intelligence (CSETI), which promotes CE-5 (human-initiated contact) protocols — coherent thought sequencing, light signaling, and meditation to attract or communicate with NHI. See [[steven-greer]] for the CE-5 controversy.

## Criticisms

- Greer's insistence that **all NHI are benevolent** is contested by other researchers who document mixed-motivation or hostile encounters
- His CE-5 methodology is unverifiable and risks conflating atmospheric phenomena with genuine NHI response
- Some witnesses from the 2001 event later distanced themselves from Greer's framing
