# Naval War College

## Overview

The US Naval War College is the Navy's senior military education institution in Newport, Rhode Island. In [[OOr98r51Sj0]], the Naval War College is the venue at which [[john-norsine]] had a planned lecture two weeks before he was found dead in a motel room — and the destination referenced in his cryptic semiotic email signoff: *"I RVed the upcoming lecture — John not heard nor seen."*
