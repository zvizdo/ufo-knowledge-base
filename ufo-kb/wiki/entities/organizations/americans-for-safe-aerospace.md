# Americans for Safe Aerospace

Non-profit organization founded by [[ryan-graves]] to aggregate UAP encounter reports from commercial and military aviation personnel. Originally focused on military aviators, it expanded to commercial pilots after Graves became a public figure. It extends the function Graves tried to perform through official Navy channels — providing a stigma-free reporting mechanism.

## Overview

The organization has become Graves's primary platform for advocacy on aviation safety dimensions of UAP. As of the 2023 congressional testimony, more than 30 commercial air crew and military veterans had confided their encounters to Graves through this channel. The organization frames UAP primarily as an **aviation safety and national security issue**, deliberately bracketing questions about ultimate origin or nature.

## Key People

- [[ryan-graves]] — founder; primary public voice

## Programs / Activities

- Collecting UAP incident reports from commercial and military pilots
- Aggregating cases that go unreported through official channels (DoD SAPR/HAZREP, FAA) due to career stigma
- Providing testimony and policy input to Congressional oversight
- Connecting with AARO reporting mechanisms
