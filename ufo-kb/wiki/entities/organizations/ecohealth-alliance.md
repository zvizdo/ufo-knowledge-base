# EcoHealth Alliance

US-based nonprofit organization, president: [[peter-daszak]]. In the KB's biowarfare and origins context, EcoHealth Alliance appears primarily as the organizational conduit for federally-funded bat coronavirus research at the [[wuhan-institute-of-virology]] in China.

**DARPA DEFUSE proposal**: EcoHealth Alliance submitted a 2017 proposal to DARPA's PREEMPT program, co-authored with the Wuhan Institute, proposing to insert furin cleavage sites into SARS-like coronavirus spike proteins — modifications DARPA reviewers assessed would enhance human transmissibility. DARPA rejected the proposal specifically on biosafety grounds, noting the proposed work would be classified as gain-of-function research. DARPA's rejection memo is a key document in the COVID-19 origins debate.

**NIH funding chain**: EcoHealth Alliance received grants from [[anthony-fauci]]'s NIAID (National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases) and sub-granted portions to the Wuhan Institute of Virology. This funding structure is the mechanism by which federally prohibited gain-of-function research — under a 2014 moratorium — may have continued through a nonprofit intermediary in a foreign lab.

**KB relevance**: In [[q7czo77qhha]], the EcoHealth Alliance/Wuhan/DARPA chain is presented as a case study in dual-use research governance failure: research with obvious weaponization potential conducted through nonprofit intermediaries, with civilian NIH funding, in a foreign facility outside US biosafety oversight, even after DARPA specifically rejected it on exactly those grounds.
