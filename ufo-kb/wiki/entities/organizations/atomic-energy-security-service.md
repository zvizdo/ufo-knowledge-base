# Atomic Energy Security Service (AESS)

> *Stub. Expand with full organizational history, command structure, and post-1955 successor lineages as relevant primary source documentation enters the KB.*

Civilian-led security force established by the [[aec|Atomic Energy Commission]] in 1946 to provide protective security at AEC nuclear installations including [[los-alamos]], [[sandia-base]], Oak Ridge, and Hanford. AESS Inspectors patrolled and guarded the most sensitive perimeters of the early US nuclear-weapons complex.

## UAP Observer Role (1948–1950 NM Corpus)

Per [[dow-uap-d17-sandia-base-1948-1950]], AESS personnel were among the most-cited trained-observer categories in the 17th OSI District's New Mexico UAP collection program 1948-1950:

- **20 December 1948 Los Alamos fireball** — four AESS Inspectors named in the LaPaz Third Report formed the primary observation team: **William D. Wilson, Buford G. Truett, Clifford E. Strang**, plus **Physical Security Inspector George S. Skipper**
- **December 6, 1948 Sandia Base** — AEC Security Agent observer (catalog entry, page 74)
- **December 7, 1948 Los Alamos** — AESS Inspector
- **27 February 1949 Los Alamos** — AESS Lieutenant
- **2 March 1949 Los Alamos** — AESS Inspector
- **3 March 1949 Los Alamos** — AESS Sergeant
- **8 March 1949 Los Alamos** — AESS Inspector (NOTE 7 — observed "elliptical, rather stubby, aluminum body" object)
- **5-7 April 1949 Los Alamos** — multiple AESS Inspector observations
- The 16 February 1949 Los Alamos green-fireball conference included **Mr. Maxwell** representing AESS
- The 25 May 1950 17th OSI District summary letter explicitly lists **"Los Alamos Security Inspectors"** as a primary observer category alongside scientists, OSI agents, airline pilots, and military pilots
