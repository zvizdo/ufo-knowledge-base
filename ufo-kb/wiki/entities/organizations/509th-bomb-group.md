# 509th Bomb Group

The 509th Bombardment Group (later Bomb Group) was the US Army Air Forces unit that carried out the atomic bombings of Hiroshima (August 6, 1945) and Nagasaki (August 9, 1945). Activated in 1944 and trained under absolute secrecy for the atomic mission, the 509th was the world's only nuclear-capable bomber unit at the end of World War II.

**Roswell posting**: In 1947, the 509th was based at Roswell Army Air Field, New Mexico. This made Roswell Army Air Field the base of the most sensitive military unit in the United States — the only one with nuclear weapons capability — at exactly the moment when the [[1947-roswell]] and [[1947-san-agustin-crash]] incidents occurred.

**Nuclear context for crash sites**: The 509th's presence at Roswell provides context for the extraordinary security response to the crash events. Whether the recovered material was a weather balloon, a classified Mogul balloon array, or something else entirely, the security infrastructure and personnel competence for managing an extreme-secrecy situation was already in place at the base. Critics of the Roswell alien hypothesis argue this makes a classified balloon recovery — handled to nuclear-weapons standards because that was the culture of the unit — more plausible as the origin of the "crash retrieval" narrative.

**San Agustín connection**: The [[1947-san-agustin-crash]] account references the 509th as providing nuclear weapons context for why aircraft were in the area — the nuclear monitoring pattern ([[nhi-monitoring-nuclear-civilizations]]) suggests that UAP interest in the Roswell/San Agustín area may have been specifically directed at the 509th's nuclear capability.
