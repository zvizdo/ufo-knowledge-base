# Society for Psychical Research (SPR)

British scientific organization founded in 1882 by [[frederick-myers]], Henry Sidgwick, and Edmund Gurney to apply rigorous, systematic methods to the investigation of telepathy, mediumship, hauntings, and related anomalous mental and physical phenomena. Co-founded the American Society for Psychical Research (ASPR) with [[william-james]] as a transatlantic partner.

The SPR produced the first large-scale, systematically collected dataset on telepathy (the 1886 *Phantasms of the Living*, and the Census of Hallucinations). It remains the oldest continuously operating organization dedicated to scientific study of these phenomena.

Referenced in [[0qlppHc3-gg]] as the 150-year precedent for what [[diane-powell]] and [[ky-dickens]] are doing with the *[[telepathy-tapes]]* documentary — establishing that credentialed academic investigation of telepathy is not a recent fringe development but a long-standing if marginalized scientific tradition.

## Key People

- [[frederick-myers]] — co-founder; coined "telepathy"; primary theoretical architect
- [[william-james]] — American partner; provided Harvard institutional legitimacy; investigated medium Leonora Piper under SPR auspices
- Henry Sidgwick — first president; philosopher at Cambridge
- Edmund Gurney — co-founder; principal researcher on *Phantasms of the Living*

## Programs / Activities

- *Phantasms of the Living* (1886) — first systematic case collection on telepathic experiences at moments of crisis or death
- Census of Hallucinations (1894) — large-scale survey of anomalous perceptual experiences in the general population
- Mediumship investigations — long-running controlled studies of individual mediums including Leonora Piper and Eusapia Palladino
- Survival research — systematic investigation of whether individual consciousness persists after death
