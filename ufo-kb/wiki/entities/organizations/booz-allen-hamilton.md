# Booz Allen Hamilton

Major US defense and intelligence consulting firm. In the context of this KB, notable for its **psychological operations consulting practice for the Department of Defense**, which is publicly described on employee LinkedIn profiles.

## UAP-Relevant Role

The article did not disclose Goff's employer or her psyops specialization.
