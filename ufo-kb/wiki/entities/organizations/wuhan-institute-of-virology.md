# Wuhan Institute of Virology

Chinese Academy of Sciences research institute in Wuhan, Hubei Province; China's first BSL-4 (Biosafety Level 4 / "P4") laboratory in mainland China. Established 1956; BSL-4 wing added 2018. Specializes in emerging infectious diseases, particularly bat coronaviruses. COVID-19 (SARS-CoV-2) emerged in the same city in late 2019.

**DARPA DEFUSE proposal**: In 2017, [[ecohealth-alliance]] (led by [[peter-daszak]]) submitted a DARPA PREEMPT grant proposal (DEFUSE) to engineer SARS-like coronaviruses with furin cleavage sites — modifications that would enhance transmissibility in human airway cells. WIV was a primary research partner in the proposal. DARPA rejected it as too dangerous. Internal DARPA documents (leaked 2021) confirmed both the proposal and the rejection. The WIV subsequently received EcoHealth sub-grants from [[anthony-fauci]]'s NIAID to conduct related coronavirus research.

**COVID-19 origins**: SARS-CoV-2 contains a furin cleavage site absent in all known related bat coronaviruses — the same modification that DEFUSE proposed to engineer. Whether this resulted from natural evolution, accidental laboratory escape, or deliberate release remains officially unresolved. The [[gain-of-function-research]] debate centers on WIV's research program.
