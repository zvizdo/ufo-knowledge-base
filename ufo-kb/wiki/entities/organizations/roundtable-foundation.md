# Roundtable Foundation

## Background

The Roundtable Foundation was [[andrija-puharich]]'s research laboratory established circa 1948 in Rockland, Maine. It occupied a large former barn-style building on the coast and operated as both a lab and residence with a full staff. Puharich described receiving funding from Vice President [[henry-a-wallace]], and from "heirs to two of the wealthiest families in the world, the Aschers and the DuPonts." In reality, per [[greg-malazzi]]'s research, it was substantially government-funded — by the Army, Navy, CIA, and an "AR Foundation" connected to the Army.

## Research Activities

- **ESP card testing** — standard Zener card and similar protocols
- **Faraday cage experiments** — one of the first labs to run ESP tests inside a Faraday cage; Puharich placed psychics inside the shielded cage to test abilities stripped of external EM interference
- **Remote viewing** — Puharich was conducting what would now be called remote viewing ~20 years before the official SRI Stargate program began
- **Psychedelic research** — experimented with Amanita muscaria mushrooms and their effects on ESP abilities; documented in Puharich's book *The Sacred Mushroom*
- **Bone-conduction radio reception** — discovered when a patient received radio signals through metallic dental fillings; led to development of the tooth implant device

## The Nine — 1952

The most historically significant event at the Roundtable Foundation was the New Year's Eve 1952 channeling of [[the-nine]] through Indian psychic [[dr-venad]]. Tape recordings of this session survive in Puharich's archive.
