# DTRA — Defense Threat Reduction Agency

The **Defense Threat Reduction Agency** is the current institutional successor to the [[afswp]] (Armed Forces Special Weapons Project, est. 1947), after a series of intermediate reorganisations. It manages US nuclear deterrence, threat reduction, and WMD defence programs.

## UAP Relevance

[[dylan-borland]] appeared on the **Weaponized podcast** and stated that DTRA **retains old Atomic Energy Commission (AEC) records** asserting that UFOs are of **extraterrestrial origin**. If accurate, this would mean that the institutional lineage running from the 1947 AFSWP → present DTRA has continuously held — and perhaps suppressed — official AEC documentation of the ET nature of UAP.
