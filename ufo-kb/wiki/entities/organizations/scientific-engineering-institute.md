# Scientific Engineering Institute

A CIA proprietary organization created by the [[oard]] (Office of Research and Development — the post-MK-Ultra behavioral control program) as its covert research vehicle. Located near Boston with a rural facility on several hundred acres. The institute existed to conduct behavioral control research at sufficient remove from the CIA to provide plausible deniability.

## Research

The Scientific Engineering Institute served as OARD's primary research arm, conducting the same expanded behavioral control program:
- Remote brain electrode experiments on animals (including crows — the only activity a researcher would partially confirm when pressed)
- Behavioral modification research beyond MK-Ultra's LSD/hypnosis scope
- Animal delivery systems for chemical/biological weapons (reptiles as covert delivery vectors)
- Possible continuation of human subjects research via [[holmesburg-prison]] pipeline

## Privatization Chain

The institute was a CIA proprietary, but in the late 1970s the CIA transitioned it into the private sector:

1. **Rebranded as SURL Metadata**
2. **Acquired as a subsidiary of GD SURL** — a pharmaceutical company
3. **GD SURL was chaired by [[donald-rumsfeld]]**
4. **In 1985, Rumsfeld orchestrated the sale of SURL/SEI to [[monsanto]]**

This chain moved a covert CIA behavioral research program — including its personnel, data, and research apparatus — into the corporate structure of the world's leading agrochemical company, which already had extensive government contracts and no transparency requirements.

## Significance

The Scientific Engineering Institute represents the clearest documented case of:
- **CIA behavioral research privatized into pharma/agribusiness** rather than terminated
- **The revolving door** between intelligence programs and corporate entities (via Rumsfeld)
- The institutional precedent for how classified government research programs continue beyond their official endings
