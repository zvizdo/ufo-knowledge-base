# Martin Corporation

Major US aerospace contractor of the postwar era (predating its merger with Lockheed). Named in the [[1971-australian-jio-memo]] by [[harry-turner]] as one of the top aerospace companies involved in US anti-gravity programs.

## RIAS — Research Institute for Advanced Study

Martin Corporation's internal anti-gravity division, led by VP [[george-trimble]]. RIAS sent [[lewis-whitten]] to represent the company at the 1957 gravity research conference at [[unc-chapel-hill]], which was also attended by [[bryce-dewitt]], [[richard-feynman]], [[john-wheeler]], and [[peter-bergmann]], and was sponsored by Wright Air Development Center (see [[wright-patterson]]).

Trimble publicly predicted in 1956 that beating gravity could be done "in about the time it took to build the first atom bomb" — a direct Manhattan Project comparison that appeared in *Young Men's* magazine ([[1956-g-engines-article]]).
