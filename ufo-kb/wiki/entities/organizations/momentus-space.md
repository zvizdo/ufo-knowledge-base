# Momentus Space

A commercial space company providing ride-share and in-space transportation (space tug) services. Operating in the small-satellite launch market, Momentus uses dedicated in-space transfer vehicles to deliver satellites to precise orbits after primary launch vehicle deployment.

## Brandenburg Thruster Connection

Momentus is the company that flew **[[john-brandenberg]]'s patented [[water-microwave-electrothermal-thruster]]** in orbit:

- Brandenburg holds the patent for a water-based microwave electrothermal thruster — a propulsion system that uses distilled water as propellant, heated by microwaves.
- Momentus waited until the patent expired, then hired Brandenburg as a summer consultant to get the system operational.
- The thruster was successfully fired in space on a Momentus space-tug vehicle to raise the vehicle's orbit — a real operational demonstration of the technology.

Brandenburg: "I worked there as a consultant for a summer and got everything running for them and… it was great fun."

## Significance

The successful orbital flight of Brandenburg's thruster is one of his conventional scientific credentials — evidence that his physics work (outside the contested Mars and GEM domains) has produced flight-proven engineering. It provides context for credibility assessments of his other claims.
