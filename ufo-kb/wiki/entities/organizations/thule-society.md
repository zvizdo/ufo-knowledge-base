# Thule Society

The **Thule Society** (*Thule-Gesellschaft*) was a German völkisch secret society operating roughly 1918–1933. It combined Germanic paganism, Atlantis myths, and Aryan esotericism into a belief system with explicit claims of contact with non-human intelligences.

## Beliefs and Practices

- Performed **séances and rituals** explicitly intended to contact **NHI** — which they described as ancient Aryan progenitors from the far north, beneath the pole star (circumpolar)
- Believed the **Nazi party's ideological roots** trace partly to the Thule Society network
- Conducted rituals at symbolic locations (including mountain sites) on significant pagan nights — notably **Walpurgisnacht** — aligning with the tradition of gates opening between worlds
- Promoted the idea that the **circumpolar stars** (the pole star region) were the origin point of their ancestral "star beings"

## Connection to Nazism and Aerospace

The Thule Society provides the direct link between:
1. **Nazi occultism** (which [[ss-ahnenerbe]] later institutionalized under Himmler)
2. **NHI contact belief** — framed in racial/Aryan terms rather than extraterrestrial, but phenomenologically similar to other contactee traditions
3. **Rocket science** — via the downstream chain: Thule Society → Nazi ideology → SS Ahnenerbe → Nazi rocket program → [[operation-paperclip]] → NASA

The fact that senior NASA personnel (von Braun's team) passed through institutions steeped in this tradition is part of the [[occult-aerospace-connection]] argument.

## Vril Society Connection

The **[[vril-society]]** is a related, partially overlapping group said to have channeled anti-gravity technology and psychic powers from an ancient subterranean race. Whether the Vril Society had a formal institutional existence or was primarily a mythological construction is disputed.
