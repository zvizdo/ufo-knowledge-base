# Stanford Research Institute

Independent non-profit research institute spun off from Stanford University in 1970 (renamed SRI International 1977). Conducted classified US government research on remote viewing and parapsychology under contract from the CIA and DIA from 1972 onward.

## Overview

The primary institutional home of the early remote-viewing research program. Hal Puthoff and Russell Targ led the psi research program there, testing subjects including Ingo Swann and Pat Price under controlled conditions. Their positive results led to government funding and eventual creation of the [[stargate-program]].
