# SpaceX

Space Exploration Technologies Corp (SpaceX) is a private aerospace company founded by [[elon-musk]] in 2002, headquartered in Hawthorne, California. It became the first private company to successfully launch, orbit, and recover a spacecraft, and has become the dominant provider of commercial orbital launch services.

**Key programs**:
- **Falcon 9**: First operational reusable orbital rocket; recovered first stage boosters allow near-weekly launch cadence
- **Starship**: The world's largest rocket (first fully-reusable orbital-class launch vehicle); under development for lunar, Mars, and deep-space missions
- **Starlink**: Constellation of over 6,000 (and growing) low-Earth orbit communications satellites; provides broadband internet globally; has significant military and intelligence applications

**UAP relevance**: SpaceX appears in the KB primarily through [[elon-musk]]'s public statements and position. Musk has made various statements downplaying or dismissing UAP claims, while simultaneously operating the Starlink constellation that provides unprecedented global coverage and the Falcon 9 launch vehicle that places DoD payloads in orbit. SpaceX holds significant DoD launch contracts, making Musk a major defense contractor even as he publicly dismisses government UAP programs.

**Starlink military use**: The US military uses Starlink for secure communications, including in the Ukraine conflict. The constellation's low Earth orbit and global coverage also have passive surveillance utility — the same infrastructure that enables commercial internet also captures RF emissions and potentially optical data at scales previously limited to government intelligence satellites.

**Musk and disclosure**: Musk has publicly stated he would know if there were non-human technology in US government possession. This statement — interpreted by some as authoritative denial and by others as evidence of Musk's actual classification status — is cited in KB discussions of disclosure dynamics.
