# Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit (IPU)

The **Interplanetary Phenomenon Unit** was an alleged in-house US Army counter-intelligence unit that existed, per Army Counter Intelligence Corps FOIA responses (1986–87 to researcher William Steinman), **only in institutional memory** — all documentary records wiped. Some debunkers (Kevin Randall) proposed the IPU was simply an internal processing unit for Army mail room handling. UAPGerb dismisses this as strained.

## Discovery

The IPU's existence was surfaced through FOIA requests in **1986–1987** by researcher **William Steinman**, who received responses from **Army Counter Intelligence Corps** acknowledging the unit existed but that all records had been destroyed or were held under a J2 classification node.

## The IPU Field Report

Documents attributed to the IPU — obtained circa **1994–1995** by **Tim Cooper** from a pseudonymous source "Thomas Kwell" — include a **July 1947 field report** alleging to describe how the Roswell retrieval was carried out:

- A **Special Engineering Detachment (SED)** — a Manhattan Project engineering unit — was dispatched
- Under **Colonel Housebrook** of the [[afswp]] (Armed Forces Special Weapons Project)
- To attend to the Roswell wreckage
- Sandia-related details appear in the field report

UAPGerb notes IPU docs deserve significant attention alongside (not subordinate to) the broader Majestic 12 document set.

## Credibility Notes

The IPU's existence is partially corroborated by official FOIA acknowledgement. The field report itself falls into the contested Majestic document space — useful as auxiliary evidence if a strong independent thesis exists, but not standalone proof. See [[credibility-frameworks]].
