# Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies (SCU)

The Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies (SCU) is a US non-profit civilian organization focused on rigorous, peer-review-compatible UAP research. It draws on credentialed researchers — physicists, engineers, data scientists — to apply scientific methodology to UAP investigation.

## Nuclear Site Frequency Study

SCU produced a statistical analysis showing that UAPs appear at nuclear sites **before construction is complete and before radioactive material is present** — not merely during operational periods. [[kevin-knuth]] cites this as evidence of **precognitive or intelligence capability** beyond passive monitoring: whatever is appearing at nuclear sites knows they will be nuclear before the nuclear material arrives. This thread connects to [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]], [[robert-hastings]]'s ICBM interference data, and [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] more broadly.

## The Catalina Island Field Mission

SCU hosted or co-hosted the 2023 five-day rooftop observation mission at Laguna Beach and Catalina Island led by Kevin Knuth. This mission produced the documentary [[a-tear-in-the-sky]] and the peer-reviewed scientific paper authored by [[matthew-shostakus]].

## Significance

SCU represents the attempt to bridge civilian UAP investigation and peer-review scientific standards — making its work harder to dismiss using the credibility attacks directed at individual witnesses. Its nuclear site study is the clearest statistical argument for intentionality in UAP behavior.
