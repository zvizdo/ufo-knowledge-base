# Blue Origin

American private aerospace company founded by Jeff Bezos in 2000, focused on reusable launch vehicles and suborbital/orbital space tourism. Its New Shepard rocket achieved commercial suborbital flights; its New Glenn orbital rocket reached orbit in 2024.

In the KB ([[jeff-bezos]] page), Blue Origin is noted in connection with the Bezos family's genealogical link to the UAP custodian network: Bezos's maternal grandfather was Lawrence Geist, the Atomic Energy Commission's Albuquerque Director, who briefed [[harold-malmgren]] on UAP-related materials recovery. The implication noted by Matthew Pines: Bezos's decision to found a private space company — and his specific focus on propulsion and reusability — may be informed by familial awareness of what is achievable beyond the public technological frontier.

Blue Origin has no confirmed UAP-specific program; the connection is genealogical and circumstantial. But the broader private-aerospace cluster (Bezos/Blue Origin, Musk/SpaceX, Bigelow Aerospace, Branson/Virgin Galactic) is notable for concentrating enormous private capital in propulsion research at exactly the moment when UAP disclosure pressure is increasing.
