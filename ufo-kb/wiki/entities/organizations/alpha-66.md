# Alpha 66

Cuban-Exile paramilitary organization founded in 1961 in Miami in the immediate aftermath of the failed Bay of Pigs invasion. Originally comprised of 66 founding members (hence the name), it was the most militant of the post–Bay of Pigs Cuban-Exile organizations, with the explicit operational mandate of invading Cuba and overthrowing Fidel Castro through paramilitary action. Active from 1961 to the present, with most kinetic activity concentrated in the 1960s–70s.

Cited by [[lue-elizondo]] in [[1f16VvXaSSE]] as the organization his father (Lou Sr.) joined after originally fighting alongside Castro and then breaking with the Cuban Communists. Per Elizondo, his father had previously participated in the [[cia]]'s Bay of Pigs invasion before joining Alpha 66 — meaning Elizondo grew up in a household with deep operational ties to the Cuban-Exile / CIA paramilitary network. This is the family backdrop for Elizondo's later career in US Special Operations and the US Army Intelligence Support Activity (Gray Fox).

## Overview

- Founded 1961 in Miami; initially 66 members.
- Operational mandate: armed invasion of Cuba, sabotage of Cuban infrastructure, assassination of Cuban officials.
- Conducted multiple raids on Cuban shipping and coastal targets through the 1960s–70s.
- Maintained operational ties to the [[cia]] during the active anti-Castro phase; later relationships became more autonomous.
- Continues to exist as a small organization in Miami; current activity is primarily political-cultural rather than operational.

## Key People

- Antonio Veciana — co-founder; former CIA contact (per congressional Church Committee)

- "Lou Sr." (Elizondo's father, as named by Lue Elizondo on [[1f16VvXaSSE]]) — joined after breaking with Castro and participating in the Bay of Pigs
