# Hawkeye 360

## Overview

Hawkeye 360 is a Virginia-based geospatial intelligence company that [[bob-maguire]] co-founded and whose core patents he holds. The company builds and operates small satellite constellations that can detect, locate, and analyze any RF-emitting source on Earth's surface.

**Core capability**: Using time-difference-of-arrival (TDOA) measurements across multiple satellites and highly accurate atomic clocks, the system triangulates the position of any RF transmitter on the ground to approximately **one square meter accuracy**. The system is passive (it only listens; the emitter doesn't know it's being tracked) and covers the entire Earth continuously.

## How It Works

1. Multiple satellites pass over a region simultaneously.
2. A signal emitted on the ground arrives at each satellite at a slightly different time (TDOA).
3. Knowing the satellites' precise positions and clock readings, the system solves the geometry to localize the emitter.
4. Signal characteristics (frequency, modulation, timing) allow classification of what kind of device is transmitting.

Primary customers: NRO, NGA. Tish Long (first female director of a three-letter agency, former NGA director) sits on the Advisory Board.

## UAP and Implant Significance

Two UAP implications that [[bob-maguire]] draws:

### 1. NHI Implant Tracking

Bob's girlfriend has two implants (leg and forehead) that pulse RF bursts. Bob's realization: **Hawkeye 360 is exactly the technology that would locate these beacons across the entire planet**. If NHI entities have placed RF-transmitting implants in abductees/contactees globally, Hawkeye's satellite network could map the distribution of those beacons in real time.

Bob estimates a hypothetical 1.6 GHz tracking frequency — which he states Hawkeye has "almost certainly" searched — would allow locating every implant-bearing individual on Earth.

### 2. UAP Detection via RF Signature

At the 2022 SCUAP meeting in Huntsville, AL, Space Force and three-letter agency personnel approached Bob about applying Hawkeye's capabilities to detecting UAP RF emissions on the ground. Bob directed them to current Hawkeye staff (his clearance had lapsed). The implication: UAP may emit characteristic RF frequencies that could be passively tracked from orbit.
