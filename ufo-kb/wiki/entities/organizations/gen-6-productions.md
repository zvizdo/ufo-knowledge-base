# Gen 6 Productions

Film production company co-founded by [[tim-alberino]] and [[steve-quail]], based in Montana. Named after Genesis 6 — the biblical passage describing the descent of the sons of God and the creation of the Nephilim.

## True Legends Documentary Series
Gen 6 Productions' primary output is the **True Legends** documentary series, which investigates physical evidence for biblical giants and Nephilim. Key episodes include:

- On-site investigation of **Sardinia** — nuragic towers, tombs of the giants, and interviews with elderly Sardinians who excavated giant skeletal remains
- Documentation of the **[[2002-kandahar-giant]]** account — the C-130 pilot was flown to Bozeman, Montana and interviewed in silhouette
