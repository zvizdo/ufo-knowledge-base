# Collins Elite

An alleged military-intelligence group within the US Air Force that interprets the UFO/UAP phenomenon through an evangelical Christian lens — categorizing non-human entities as demonic and opposing all research and disclosure on theological grounds. Documented in Nick Redfern's book *Final Events* (2010).

## Beliefs and Doctrine

The Collins Elite hold that:
- Non-human intelligences contacting humans are demonic entities, not extraterrestrials.
- Engaging with, studying, or disclosing this phenomenon is spiritually dangerous and must be suppressed.
- Researchers and experiencers who publish on this subject are either deceived by or "in league with demons."

This position leads them to active suppression rather than mere bureaucratic compartmentalization — they view their work as a moral and religious imperative.

## Connection to Strieber

[[whitley-strieber]] identifies the Collins Elite as the primary architects of a social engineering campaign that destroyed his career:

- They reportedly coordinated media attacks and ridicule campaigns against him.
- A *South Park* episode satirizing Strieber is mentioned as part of the campaign that made him a public laughingstock.
- The career destruction forced his bankruptcy and loss of his cabin.
- Defense Department figures connected to this group "do not like me and do not want my work out there."

Strieber refers to *Beyond the UFO* Episode 3 for a detailed account of the Collins Elite campaign against him.

## Wright Airfield / Stack Five

The Collins Elite is associated with a study called "Stack Five" at Wright Airfield — the Air Force base with the longest continuous association with UFO research (also home to the Foreign Technology Division and USAF UFO investigation programs).

## Researcher Persecution Pattern

The Collins Elite represent one node in a broader pattern of institutional persecution of UFO/UAP researchers. Their unique feature is ideological motivation (religious) rather than purely security-based suppression:

- [[john-mack]] — Harvard psychiatry; faced career-ending investigation
- [[roger-leir]] — implant researcher; license threatened
- [[diane-powell]] — Harvard neuroscientist; medical license revoked
- [[whitley-strieber]] — author/experiencer; career and finances destroyed

> ⚠ Conflict: The existence and organizational coherence of the Collins Elite is unverified. Strieber and Redfern are the primary named sources; no official documentation has been declassified confirming the group's existence or activities.
