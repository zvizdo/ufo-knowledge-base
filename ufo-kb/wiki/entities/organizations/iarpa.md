# IARPA

## Overview

IARPA (Intelligence Advanced Research Projects Activity) is the intelligence community's equivalent of DARPA — created after the establishment of the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) to fund high-risk, high-reward research with potential intelligence applications. It sits under the DNI structure.

## DNA Encoding for Intelligence

Around 2013–14, IARPA approached [[bob-maguire]]'s team at the [[hume-center]] with a specific research question: **Could CRISPR-Cas9 be used to encode intelligence information into DNA?**

Bob declined to submit a proposal (too busy with other work), but team members at the Hume Center did develop and work on a proposal.

This request reveals that IARPA was already exploring DNA as a data storage and covert intelligence carrier medium in the early 2010s — before CRISPR became widely known outside specialized molecular biology circles.

## Implications

Bob's extension of the IARPA concept: a cleared intelligence officer could carry terabytes of classified schematics in their own DNA — walks through any security checkpoint undetected, indistinguishable from a normal person. The human as a one-time pad. If detected, decoding requires knowing the person is carrying the data and having the sequence/key.

The further extension Bob draws: if NHI implants are tracking devices and the entities have this technology a thousand years more advanced, the implants may not just track — they may read or write genetic information.
