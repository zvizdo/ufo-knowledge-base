# AFSWP — Armed Forces Special Weapons Project

The **Armed Forces Special Weapons Project** was established in **1947 at Sandia Base, Albuquerque, New Mexico** — co-founded by Secretary of Defense [[james-forrestal]] (and one other individual). It managed US nuclear weapons programs and the relationship between the military and the Atomic Energy Commission.

## Roswell Connection

The [[interplanetary-phenomenon-unit]] field report (July 1947) alleges that AFSWP sent a **Special Engineering Detachment (SED)** — a Manhattan Project engineering team — under **Colonel Housebrook** to attend to the Roswell wreckage. This would make AFSWP the organisational vehicle for the first documented crash-retrieval operation.

## Forrestal / Sandia Connection

The AFSWP was established at **Sandia Base** at almost exactly the same time that **Los Alamos Z Division** (the weaponisation arm of Los Alamos) established operations at the same base — the origin point of what would become [[sandia-national-laboratories]]. This institutional co-location of AFSWP and Sandia at the same moment as the Roswell crash is a notable confluence.

[[james-forrestal]], AFSWP's co-founder, is the same figure listed in the [[majestic-12]] Eisenhower Briefing Document as MJ-12's first member. Forrestal was subsequently thrown from a window at Bethesda Naval Hospital in 1949 — and the Eisenhower Briefing Document records that Walter Bedell Smith was read into MJ-12 as Forrestal's replacement on the same dates as actual Truman/Smith meetings.

## Evolution into DTRA

AFSWP went through a series of institutional evolutions and today exists as the **Defense Threat Reduction Agency** ([[dtra]]). [[dylan-borland]] stated on the Weaponized podcast that DTRA retained old Atomic Energy Commission records stating UFOs are of extraterrestrial origin.
