# Harvard College Observatory

Astronomical research observatory founded in 1839, part of Harvard University; merged with the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory in 1973 to form the Center for Astrophysics | Harvard & Smithsonian.

In UAP-adjacent discourse, Harvard Observatory is referenced through **[[fred-whipple]]'s** directorship and his involvement in **[[operation-moonwatch]]** (1956–1975) — a civilian-volunteer satellite-tracking network that the SAO/Harvard ecosystem coordinated. Operation Moonwatch is one of the field's documented examples of civilian-network sky-monitoring infrastructure that pre-dated and partly seeded modern citizen-science UAP-tracking efforts.
