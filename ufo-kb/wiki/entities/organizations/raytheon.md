# Raytheon Technologies

## Overview

Raytheon Technologies (now trading as RTX Corporation) is one of the largest US defense contractors, with major business lines in missiles and defense systems, aerospace systems, intelligence systems, and advanced sensing technologies. It employs hundreds of thousands of people globally and holds significant classified contracts across the US defense and intelligence community.

In the UAP knowledge base context, Raytheon is relevant primarily through [[eric-hecker]], a Raytheon contractor who worked at the South Pole's [[south-pole-neutrino-detector]] facility (IceCube Neutrino Observatory). Hecker made claims about the facility's possible use for purposes beyond its stated scientific mandate, and his Raytheon contractor status raises questions about the overlap between defense contractor operations and scientific facilities in remote locations.
