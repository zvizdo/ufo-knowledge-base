# Naval Sea Systems Command (NAVSEA)

Naval Sea Systems Command (NAVSEA) is the largest of the US Navy's six systems commands, responsible for designing, building, delivering, and maintaining ships, submarines, and combat systems. It is headquartered at the [[washington-navy-yard]] in Washington, D.C.

**Acquisition role**: NAVSEA manages the Navy's most complex engineering projects, including nuclear submarine programs, surface combatants, and advanced weapons systems. It oversees contracts with major defense contractors and research laboratories across the naval-industrial complex.

**Pais Effect connection**: In UAP-adjacent discourse, NAVSEA is relevant because [[salvatore-pais]]'s controversial physics patents — describing room-temperature superconductors, compact fusion reactors, and a "hybrid aerospace/undersea craft" using high-energy electromagnetic field manipulation — were filed by Pais as a Navy employee and processed through the Navy's patent system. The question of whether NAVSEA's acquisition architecture provides a mechanism for classified research to be patented and potentially developed without public disclosure is part of the analysis of the Pais patents.

**NCIS co-location**: The Naval Criminal Investigative Service (NCIS) is also headquartered at Washington Navy Yard, meaning that both the acquisition command (NAVSEA) and the counterintelligence/law enforcement function (NCIS) share the same installation — relevant to questions about how classified naval research is protected.
