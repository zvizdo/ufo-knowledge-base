## Overview

Gabinetto RS/33 (also written RS33) was the Italian fascist state's special investigation body created by Benito Mussolini following the recovery of a disc-shaped craft near [[magenta-italy]] in June 1933. It stands as the earliest known formally constituted government body tasked with investigating a recovered non-human craft.

The designation "RS/33" is believed to reference the investigative mandate (RS) and the year of establishment (33). The Gabinetto operated under direct state authority and reported to the highest levels of the fascist government. Its existence and findings were subject to a total media blackout, enforced through the Stefani agency — the official fascist state news wire — at Mussolini's personal direction.

The central tension within RS/33's investigation was Mussolini's political insistence that the craft must be of terrestrial (and preferably foreign) origin, versus the investigators' conclusion that the craft was genuinely non-human. Mussolini's public position — that the object was a foreign experimental aircraft — was contradicted by the RS/33's own internal findings, which held that the craft was not of any known human manufacture.

## Key People

- **Benito Mussolini** — Italian dictator; ordered the creation of RS/33 and imposed the media blackout; insisted publicly and politically that the craft was of terrestrial origin while RS/33 investigators concluded otherwise.
- **Guglielmo Marconi** — Nobel Prize laureate and radio pioneer; participated in the RS/33 investigation. Marconi was one of the most technically credentialed scientists in Italy and his involvement signals the seriousness with which the investigation was conducted. He concluded the craft was non-human.
- **Italo Balbo** — Italian Air Marshal; involved in the RS/33 investigation. As the senior aviation official in fascist Italy, Balbo's participation provided both aeronautical expertise and institutional authority.

## Programs / Activities

**Recovery and Initial Investigation (1933)**
Following the June 1933 crash near [[magenta-italy]], Italian military and government personnel recovered a disc approximately 10–12 meters in diameter. The craft was transferred to hangars operated by SIA-Marchetti, a prominent Italian aerospace manufacturer, where RS/33 conducted its technical investigation. The SIA-Marchetti facility provided the infrastructure and technical personnel needed for a serious examination of an exotic craft.

**Media Blackout via Stefani Agency**
Mussolini directed the Stefani news agency — the fascist state's official press distributor — to impose a complete blackout on all reporting related to the crash and recovery. This represented one of the first known uses of state information control mechanisms to suppress public knowledge of a UAP recovery event.

**Investigative Conclusion vs. Official Position**
The core finding of RS/33 was that the recovered craft was not of human origin. This contradicted Mussolini's official political position and created a classified internal contradiction: the state publicly denied the non-human nature of the craft while its own investigators had concluded the opposite. This pattern — internal acknowledgment of non-human origin paired with public denial — prefigures the post-Roswell structure of US UAP secrecy.

**Custody and Transfer**
The craft remained in Italian custody at SIA-Marchetti hangars for over a decade. As the war turned against Italy and then against Germany, the risk that the craft would fall into Nazi hands became acute. Pope Pius XII, aware of the craft's existence and location through Vatican intelligence networks, back-channeled this information to the Allied powers — triggering [[oss|OSS]] Project McGregor, which retrieved the craft in 1945.
