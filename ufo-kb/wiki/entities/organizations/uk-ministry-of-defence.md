# UK Ministry of Defence

The United Kingdom's central defence ministry and primary institutional body for official UAP investigation in the UK. Operated a dedicated UFO research desk — **Secretariat (Air Staff) 2a** — which was the MoD's public-facing UAP inquiry mechanism.

## UFO Desk (Secretariat Air Staff 2a)

- [[nick-pope]] ran the UFO desk from approximately **1991 to 1994** as a civilian employee
- The desk was a **one-person operation** with admin support
- Investigated **200–300 cases per year**, primarily from civilian witnesses
- Had access to a network of expert resources: radar specialists, JRIC imagery analysts, Met Office, Royal Greenwich Observatory, astronomers, meteorologists
- Explicitly modeled as "public-facing, like the old US Air Force [[project-blue-book]]" — most work was unclassified, though classified investigation occurred in parallel
- Pope was required to "play it very straight" — focusing on defense, national security, and safety of flight angles, not the broader phenomenon

## Key Cases Investigated

- **[[1993-cosford-incident]]**: Wave of sightings including triangular craft, animal anomalies, and MIB-adjacent encounters; Pope's personal investigation
- **[[1980-rendlesham-forest]]**: Major military UAP incident at NATO nuclear bases; UK intelligence assessment on radiation exposure of witnesses
- **[[1990-calvine-photograph]]**: 6 photographs and negatives analyzed by JRIC; JRIC concluded the craft was real, ~75ft, solid structured, unknown

## JRIC Analysis

Photographs submitted to JRIC ([[jric]] — Joint Air Reconnaissance and Intelligence Centre) for imagery analysis — the UK military's dedicated intelligence imagery assessment body. For the Calvine case, JRIC concluded:
- Images were genuine
- Craft was "solid structured" and approximately 75 feet across
- No identification could be made

## Declassification

Under an **open government initiative**, MoD began releasing UFO files. This permitted Pope and others to discuss declassified cases publicly. However:
- The Calvine declassified file contained only "a photocopy of a line drawing" — original photographic documentation was not included
- Witnesses remained bound by the **Official Secrets Act** for classified content
- The dual UK-US security review of the Rendlesham book (Pope, Penniston, Burroughs) reflects the transnational classification complexity
