# WINPAC — Weapons Intelligence, Non-Proliferation and Arms Control

A **multi-agency CIA program** (not exclusively CIA) that fuses Personnel and resources from CIA, NSA, NRO, and other intelligence community components to address weapons intelligence, proliferation threats, and arms control verification.

## Multi-Agency Structure

[[eric-davis]] describes in [[LnAiNChnuEQ]] that when he was hired, the hiring manager briefed him on two long lists of organizations involved:

> "He wrote down two long lists on a couple of columns they weren't that long but they were all the agencies that were fused together to run wi the winpack operation."

WINPAC seeks resources and personnel from multiple agencies to accomplish different sub-tasks. This is a different model from a single-agency program — it operates as a coordinated fusion cell rather than a CIA-only operation.

## Davis's Near-Hire (2003)

In late 2003, Davis was offered the position of **branch chief scientist** at WINPAC. He accepted the hiring manager's briefing about the role (interfaces with CIA, NSA, NRO, and other agencies) but ultimately did not start the position (reasons not specified in the transcript).

This hiring context is significant: Davis's trajectory into the CIA's S&T orbit in 2003 is contemporaneous with his later interaction with [[george-hw-bush]] (per Davis's account in the same interview, he had conversations with HW Bush "back in 2003") and his continued work on classified UAP-adjacent programs.

## Connection to Glenn Gaffney

[[glenn-gaffney]] served as Deputy/Director of the CIA's Science and Technology Directorate — the directorate that WINPAC operates under. Davis did not work directly with Gaffney, but the same directorate that owns WINPAC also owned the UAP portfolio Gaffney managed.
