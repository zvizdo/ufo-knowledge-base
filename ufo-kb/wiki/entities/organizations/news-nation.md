# NewsNation

**Type**: US national cable television news network  
**Owner**: Nexstar Media Group  
**Launched**: 2020 (as rebranded WGN America)

## Significance in UAP Discourse

NewsNation became a primary outlet for UAP disclosure news in 2023–2025, breaking or amplifying several significant stories:

- **[[david-grusch]] initial interview** (June 2023): Grusch's first on-camera interview after his congressional complaint aired on NewsNation with journalist Ross Coulthart; effectively launched the 2023 disclosure push.
- **[[ross-coulthart]]**: Australian investigative journalist who joined NewsNation and became the network's primary UAP correspondent; conducted multiple high-profile insider interviews.
- **[[jake-barber]] interviews**: Barber's accounts of Sky Watchers program and NJ drone flap broke through NewsNation/Coulthart.
- **Congressional testimony context**: NewsNation reporting preceded and contextualized the July 2023 House Oversight Committee hearing.

## Editorial Position

NewsNation positioned itself as a "down-the-middle" network, which gave its UAP coverage an air of mainstream credibility while legacy outlets (NYT, Washington Post) remained skeptical. This positioning is analyzed in [[disclosure-narratives]] as a media-strategy component of the 2023 disclosure push.
