# Battelle Memorial Institute

Nonprofit research and development organization based in Columbus, Ohio. One of the largest contract research organizations in the U.S., with extensive government and defense contracts. In the UFO context, Battelle was contracted by the USAF to statistically analyze UFO sighting reports in the early 1950s (Project Stork / Bear — the precursor statistical work to Project Blue Book's public database).

## UFO Investigation Role

Battelle's most significant UFO-related output was the [[pentacle-memo]], authored by [[howard-cross]] in January 1953. The memo reveals that Battelle scientists were not neutral analysts but active participants in shaping how UFO investigations would be structured and what conclusions they would reach.

Battelle also produced "Project Blue Book Special Report No. 14" — a statistical analysis of 3,200 cases that found ~21.5% were "unknowns" even after rigorous screening. This figure was higher than the Air Force publicly acknowledged, and the report was classified for years.

## DOE Nuclear Site Management (SZBI85yvV5A)

In [[SZBI85yvV5A]], [[jesse-michaels]] and [[jamie-vernon]] flag Battelle's role as manager of US nuclear sites, situating it within the broader [[doe-secret-science-nexus]]. Key points raised:

- Battelle manages all major US nuclear sites on behalf of the Department of Energy
- Under the Atomic Energy Act of 1954, anything emitting alpha/beta/gamma radiation is "born secret" — restricted data upon discovery — giving DOE (and its contractors including Battelle) automatic classification authority over exotic materials found in or near their facilities
- The largest particle accelerator in the US is at Brookhaven National Laboratory — a facility with Battelle management ties; "a lot of the secret science stuff is going on at these DOE sites"
- Geographic proximity to [[wright-patterson]] Air Force Base (Columbus to Dayton, ~70 miles) creates a structural node: DOE nuclear management + alleged USAF UAP reverse-engineering in the same regional cluster
