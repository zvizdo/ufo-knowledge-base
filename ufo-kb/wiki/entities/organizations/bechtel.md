# Bechtel Corporation

Bechtel is one of the largest private engineering and construction companies in the world, with deep roots in US defense and intelligence infrastructure. Its known portfolio includes nuclear plant construction, classified government facilities, and extensive work with agencies including the DOE, DOD, and CIA.

## UAP-Adjacent Claims

[[lue-elizondo]] has claimed that individuals pursuing him (as part of efforts to discredit his UAP disclosures) are connected to both [[monsanto]] and Bechtel. The Monsanto–Bechtel connection, as framed by Jesse Michaels in [[q7Czo77qhHA]], implies that these contractors may have been part of a biological specimen storage or handling network — possibly relating to alleged NHI biological material.

The specific claim: Bechtel's classified construction capabilities (secure vaults, temperature-controlled storage for nuclear/biological materials, sites with extreme security clearance requirements) would make it a plausible candidate for long-term storage of anomalous biological specimens in a compartmentalized fashion.

## Institutional Context

Bechtel's history places it squarely in the [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] network:
- Built many of the US nuclear power plants and DOE facilities
- Constructed classified sections of government installations
- Revolving-door relationships with Reagan, Bush administrations (George Shultz, Caspar Weinberger were Bechtel executives)
- Extensive international projects including Saudi Arabia, UK, and Eastern Europe
