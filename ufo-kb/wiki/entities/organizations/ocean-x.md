# Ocean X

Swedish underwater exploration company co-founded by [[dennis-asberg]] and [[peter-lindberg]]. Originally focused on Baltic Sea shipwreck retrieval (cognac, wine, champagne cargo; historical vessels). Primary investigating body for the [[baltic-sea-anomaly]] since its accidental sonar discovery on June 19, 2011.

## Key People
- [[dennis-asberg]] — co-founder; public face of the BSA investigation
- [[peter-lindberg]] — co-founder; co-discoverer of BSA
- [[beatrice-varel]] — scientific collaborator (joined c. 2023)
- [[stefan-hogborn]] — affiliated expert diver; conducted first physical BSA dive (2012)

## Programs / Activities

### Shipwreck Retrieval
Found multiple historically significant wrecks in the Baltic Sea, including:
- A cargo ship with 1600-vintage wine (among the oldest in the world) and Tsar-bound cognac
- A Russian Tsar-era submarine (built 1904; used in Russo-Japanese War) in Swedish waters (2015)

### Baltic Sea Anomaly Investigation
Multiple expeditions from 2011 through 2025, employing:
- Sidescan sonar (discovery and initial characterization)
- ROV (multiple vehicles; frequently fails near the object due to EM interference)
- Expert diver deployments
- Sub-bottom profiling (latest expeditions; confirmed object is separated from seafloor)
- Multi-beam 3D imaging (conducted with Stockholm University ship)

### Scientific Partnerships
Now works with Stockholm University scientists and their research vessel. Those scientists independently confirmed the GPS interference on their own equipment.
