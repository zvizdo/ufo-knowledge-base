# FBI (Federal Bureau of Investigation)

US domestic intelligence and federal law enforcement agency, operating under the Department of Justice since 1935. Director J. Edgar Hoover held the office from 1924 to 1972, during which the Bureau developed extensive files on UFO/UAP reports.

## UAP-Adjacent Activity

**Guy Hottel Memo (1950)**: FBI agent [[guy-hottel]] sent Hoover a memo reporting that three flying saucers had been recovered in New Mexico, each piloted by three bodies "dressed in metallic cloth." The memo is one of the most-discussed FBI documents in UAP disclosure research. It is available via FOIA and the FBI Vault.

**G.H. Esterbrooks**: [[gh-esterbrooks]] appears in FBI records in contexts related to hypnosis research — relevant to the [[mk-ultra]] / mind control ecosystem.

**COINTELPRO**: The FBI's domestic intelligence program surveilled UFO researchers and contactee groups, treating them as potential security threats.

## Relationship with CIA

The FBI and [[cia]] have historically maintained tension over domestic vs. foreign intelligence jurisdiction. In UAP contexts this matters because recovered craft/materials would fall into military/CIA jurisdiction while domestic witnesses and researchers would fall under FBI purview.
