# Joint Air Reconnaissance Intelligence Centre (JRIC)

The UK Ministry of Defence's dedicated **imagery analysis and intelligence imagery** assessment body. In the UAP context, JRIC is notable primarily for conducting the official UK government analysis of the [[1990-calvine-photograph]] case.

## Calvine Assessment

[[nick-pope]] describes submitting the Calvine photographs to JRIC during his MoD UFO desk tenure (1991–1994). The JRIC conclusion — representing the UK government's official imagery intelligence assessment — was:

> "Yeah, it's real. It's whatever it is. It's like 75 ft across. It's a solid structured craft, but we have no idea what it is."

This is the most authoritative UK government statement on the Calvine images: a positive finding that the photographs were genuine and showed an unidentified solid structured craft approximately **75 feet across**. The assessment was unable to identify the craft.

JRIC's conclusion was reached with access to all 6 photographs and the associated negatives that were in MoD custody — not just the single print that [[craig-lindsay]] retained.

## Significance

JRIC's role in UAP cases represents the UK institutional analog to US intelligence imagery analysis of UAP evidence. The Calvine assessment, if ever officially published, would constitute one of the most direct government confirmations of unidentified physical craft in any nation's declassified record.
