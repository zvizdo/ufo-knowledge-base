# UFO Sweden

Swedish civilian UFO investigation organization; one of the most established UFO research bodies in Scandinavia. Founded in 1970, UFO Sweden maintains a case archive, publishes a journal (*UFO-Aktuellt*), and coordinates civilian investigation of Swedish UAP reports. [[jesse-michaels]] describes it as "the official civilian investigation UFO" organization for Sweden.

**Scandinavian UAP context**: Scandinavia has a disproportionately rich UAP history. The 1946–1947 "ghost rocket" wave — unidentified objects observed by thousands of witnesses across Sweden, Finland, Norway, and Denmark, many tracked on military radar — was one of the earliest major postwar UAP events and was investigated by both Swedish and US military intelligence. The Swedish Air Force chief of staff called it "a very real phenomenon" in 1946. UFO Sweden's archives capture decades of Scandinavian encounter reports in this historically active region.

**Dennis Asberg contact**: [[dennis-asberg]], who had a UAP sighting over a Swedish military installation in 1996, contacted UFO Sweden by mail — the only official civilian reporting channel he could identify. He received no reply. His account illustrates the gap between the formal existence of civilian reporting organizations and their practical capacity to process and respond to reports, particularly from individuals who are not already networked into the UAP research community.

**Relationship to government**: UFO Sweden has no formal relationship to the Swedish government or military; it is a purely civilian volunteer organization. This distinguishes the Scandinavian model from the French GEIPAN (which is a government body) and illustrates the variation in how different countries have institutionalized civilian UAP reporting.
