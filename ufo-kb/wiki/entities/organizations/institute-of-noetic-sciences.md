# Institute of Noetic Sciences (IONS)

**IONS** was founded in 1973 by Apollo 14 astronaut [[edgar-mitchell]] following his profound consciousness experience during the return journey from the Moon. The institute studies consciousness, psi phenomena, and the intersection of science with inner experience.

[[rupert-sheldrake]] is a fellow of IONS. The institute represents the institutionalization of the "astronaut turns inward" pattern — multiple Apollo astronauts had consciousness-transforming experiences in space and subsequently pursued consciousness research.

## UAP Relevance

IONS sits at the intersection of [[consciousness-non-local]], [[remote-viewing]], and UAP discourse — the same conceptual space where psi research, NHI contact, and consciousness theory overlap. Edgar Mitchell was one of the first high-credibility figures to publicly advocate for UFO reality.
