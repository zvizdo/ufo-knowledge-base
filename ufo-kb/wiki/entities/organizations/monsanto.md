# Monsanto

American agrochemical and agricultural biotechnology corporation, founded 1901 and headquartered in St. Louis. Originally a chemical manufacturer, Monsanto became one of the dominant forces in industrial agriculture, GMO seed patents, and herbicide production. In 2018 it was acquired by [[bayer]] for approximately $63 billion — formally merging two corporations with parallel histories of large-scale harm and institutional protection.

## Agent Orange and Vietnam

Monsanto was one of the primary manufacturers of Agent Orange — a chemical defoliant used in Operation Ranch Hand during the Vietnam War to destroy vegetation concealing enemy positions. The manufacturing rush to produce millions of gallons created a contaminant: **dioxin** (TCDD), a persistent organic pollutant that:
- Does not dissolve in water
- Resists environmental breakdown for decades
- Accumulates in fat tissue
- Causes cancer, severe birth defects, and epigenetic mutations that pass across generations

In the mid-1980s, Monsanto paid millions in compensation to US veterans exposed to Agent Orange. Vietnamese civilians were excluded from the case. Monsanto continued to operate and grow.

## Roundup and Glyphosate

By the late 20th century, Monsanto rebranded as a biotech powerhouse, pioneering genetically modified crops engineered to withstand heavy use of its flagship herbicide, Roundup. The active ingredient is **glyphosate**, which:
- Has been linked to cancer (the International Agency for Research on Cancer classified it as "probably carcinogenic to humans")
- Has permeated global soil, water supplies, and the human bloodstream
- Brigham Buhler and Jesse Michaels describe as "water-soluble Agent Orange"
- Bayer has paid out billions in settlements over cancer claims while continuing to sell the product

## The CIA Connection: Scientific Engineering Institute

In 1985, [[donald-rumsfeld]] — who chaired GD SURL (the pharmaceutical company that housed the former CIA behavioral research unit) — orchestrated the **sale of the [[scientific-engineering-institute]] to Monsanto**. The Scientific Engineering Institute was a CIA proprietary organization created by the [[oard]] (Office of Research and Development, MK-Ultra's successor program) as its covert research vehicle. The transfer moved a behavioral experimentation entity with CIA origins into the corporate structure of a company with extensive government contracts and no public accountability.

The host notes: "The line between covert government operations, human experimentation, and big pharma is very, very blurry."

Separately, [[lue-elizondo]] has claimed that some of the people pursuing him are connected to Monsanto, and that there is a Monsanto–[[bechtel]] connection from the period when these companies may have handled biological specimens — possibly including NHI-related material.
