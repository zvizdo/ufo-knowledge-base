# U Albany Project X

UAP research program at the University at Albany (SUNY Albany). Co-led by [[kevin-knuth]] and [[matthew-shostakus]], with [[cecilia-levy]] as additional faculty member. Originally founded as UAPx (which has since disbanded); the research program moved to U Albany and became Project X when UAPx disbanded.

## Funding

Received a major donation from local businessman Tony Gorman, creating an **endowment** — the program is funded "in perpetuity to some degree." This is significant: most UAP academic research is unfunded or dependent on individual effort.

## Research Activities

1. **Field Observation**: The *A Tear in the Sky* (2023) mission — five-day rooftop observation from Laguna Beach + a team on Catalina Island; scientifically documented; published paper (2024, Shostakus first author).

2. **Material Analysis**: Matthew Shostakus is developing and applying a non-destructive **neutron-activation isotope analysis** technique to study debris from alleged UAP crashes, including [[arts-parts]] (Art Bell's Roswell material), samples from Ukraine, and samples from Israel. Government representatives have expressed interest in this work.

3. **Academic Publications**: Knuth published a review paper on the history of UAP scientific study (including Bud Clem's 1945 Hanford sighting). They are producing peer-reviewed science on UAP topics.

## Significance

One of the very few *institutionally supported*, *publicly academic* UAP research programs in the world. The endowment distinguishes it from individual researcher efforts. "We are here to stay," Knuth says.
