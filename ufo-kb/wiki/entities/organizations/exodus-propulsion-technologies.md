# Exodus Propulsion Technologies

Florida-based advanced propulsion startup co-founded by [[charles-buhler]] (NASA Kennedy Space Center lead electrostatics scientist) and [[andrew-arjima]] (engineer, 35+ years). The company is focused on the discovery, validation, and commercialization of an anomalous directional thrust that appears when high voltage is applied across asymmetric electrostatic configurations — a force that Buhler believes is distinct from all four known fundamental forces and is quantum-electrodynamic in origin.

The company's name is a deliberate reference to the idea that this force could free humanity from chemical-rocket dependence — the "exodus" from Newton's third law as a constraint on space travel.

**Website**: exoduspropulsion.space (DIY replication videos publicly available)

## Key People

- [[charles-buhler]] — co-founder, NASA lead electrostatics scientist, incoming president of the Electrostatic Society of America
- [[andrew-arjima]] (Drew) — co-founder, lead experimenter, engineer, 35+ years experience

## Programs / Activities

### Core Experiment Program

Since collaboration began (approximately 2016), Buhler and Arjima have built and tested **over 2,000 distinct test-article variants** with the following documented results:

- **Current thrust level**: 5–10 millinewtons additive — sufficient for lunar/orbital applications (satellite station-keeping, orbital altitude adjustment, interplanetary transit in micro-gravity)
- **Vacuum survival**: confirmed in hard vacuum (high-vacuum chamber; Bluetooth-actuated to avoid mechanical coupling); a 14 mm deflection of a dual-thruster spinner corresponds to ~2.5 mN. Ion wind and Coulomb attraction to chamber walls both ruled out.
- **Power-off persistence**: thrust continues after DC power disconnected; charge retention in the dielectric drives the field effect. Analogous to the Casimir effect — no ongoing energy input required.
- **Weight-reduction demo**: on a calibrated scale, the device loses measurable weight at −480 V; direction reverses when the thruster is flipped — ruling out scale attraction.
- **Faraday cage enclosure**: all tests run inside a grounded Faraday cage to eliminate Coulomb attraction to surrounding surfaces.
- **Voltage reduction trend**: from ~30–40 kV a decade ago to ~480 V in recent air tests; further reductions expected with optimized materials and geometry.

### Initial Market Focus

The team has stated Earth-gravity "unity" (sufficient thrust to lift the device plus support hardware) is not yet achieved. Near-term market: **orbital applications** — satellite station-keeping, deorbiting, inter-orbit maneuvering, deep-space transit. The concept of a **self-launcher** (a device that can lift its own weight including power supply and structure) is the long-term product vision.

### Patent Position

- **First patent**: possible national security hold (Invention Secrecy Act 1952 may apply); status unclear.
- **Second patent**: currently undergoing examiner peer review, which Buhler treats as equivalent to scientific peer review; affidavits from experiment witnesses are being collected.

### Open Replication

The Exodus website hosts DIY replication videos. At least one individual built a garage version achieving ~1 mN of thrust independently, which Buhler cites as spontaneous third-party validation.
