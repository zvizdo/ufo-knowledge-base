# The Debrief

Independent U.S. national security and technology journalism outlet founded in 2020. Covers defense, intelligence, emerging technology, and UAP with a staff of journalists including former military and intelligence community members. Distinguished from legacy media by willingness to publish UAP stories that mainstream outlets spike under editorial pressure.

**The Grusch story**: The Debrief gained UAP-field prominence in June 2023 when it published [[david-grusch]]'s whistleblower story — the article [[leslie-kean]] had originally attempted to place at the New York Times, where it was blocked by national security correspondent Julian Barnes (who has a documented pattern of debunking UAP stories). The Debrief's June 5, 2023 article — co-bylined by Kean, Ralph Blumenthal, and The Debrief's own Tim McMillan — was the first major-outlet report on Grusch's specific claims: the U.S. government possesses multiple non-human origin craft and biologics, and programs managing this material have been concealed from Congressional oversight.

**Disclosure sequence trigger**: The Debrief publication directly triggered the July 2023 Congressional hearing at which Grusch, [[ryan-graves]], and [[david-fravor]] testified publicly. The sequence — story published June 5, hearing scheduled within weeks, testimony July 26 — demonstrates that the publication of the right story in the right outlet can shift the disclosure timeline on a compressed timescale.

**Other UAP coverage**: The Debrief has also covered [[aaro]] inspector general complaints, DoD UAP sensor coverage, and the legislative history of the UAP Disclosure Act — functioning as the primary mainstream-adjacent venue for serious UAP journalism.
