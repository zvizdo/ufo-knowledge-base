# Strategic Air Command (SAC)

The USAF command responsible for nuclear deterrence operations throughout the Cold War, including operation of Minuteman ICBM fields. SAC headquarters was directly involved in the response to the [[1967-echo-flight]] and [[1967-oscar-flight]] UFO-related missile shutdowns at [[malmstrom-air-force-base]].

## Overview

SAC operated approximately 200 Minuteman missiles at Malmstrom AFB in 1967, organized into 20 flights of 10 missiles each. When all 10 missiles in a flight go offline simultaneously, it represents a catastrophic failure of nuclear deterrence capacity — the primary mission of SAC.
