# Jane's Defence Weekly

Prestigious British military and defense publication with a long history as the authoritative open-source reference on military hardware, aviation, and defense programs worldwide. Jane's has historically been trusted at the highest levels of government and military — giving its journalists unusual access to classified-environment facilities and senior officials.

## UAP/Anti-Gravity Relevance

[[nick-cook]] and [[bill-sweetman]] worked as editors at Jane's in the 1980s and 1990s, using the publication as the institutional platform for their black-world aircraft investigations. Cook notes he had "a pretty much unlimited global travel budget" and could "go anywhere" — a level of institutional backing that enabled his extended anti-gravity and classified aircraft investigations.

The Jane's brand also conferred credibility: when Cook or Sweetman requested interviews with Skunk Works heads ([[ben-rich]], [[jack-gordon]]) or senior Air Force officials ([[george-muellner]]), the Jane's name opened doors that would have been closed to a UFO-focused journalist.

[[bill-gunston]], another Jane's editor, used his Jane's credentials and aerospace expertise when he assessed the B2's anomalous performance data and invoked [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s electrogravitics as the explanation — before self-censoring.
