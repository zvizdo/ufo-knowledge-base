# Renaissance Technologies

**Renaissance Technologies** is a quantitative hedge fund founded by mathematician [[jim-simons]]. It is relevant to UAP discourse through two threads:

1. **Eric Weinstein's UAP physics attempt**: [[eric-weinstein]] discloses that he attempted to instantiate "at least the theoretical physics component" of a UAP legacy program at Renaissance Technologies — a private-sector effort to fund serious UAP physics research outside academia and government. The attempt did not produce a permanent program.

2. **Simons/Spergel overlap**: Jim Simons also founded the **Simons Foundation**. [[david-spergel]] (astrophysicist who led NASA's 2022 UAP review panel) served as president of the Simons Foundation. Weinstein flags this overlap as "very interesting" — the same philanthropic network that employs the NASA UAP panel chair is also where he attempted to build a UAP physics program.
