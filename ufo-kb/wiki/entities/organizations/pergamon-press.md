# Pergamon Press

British scientific publisher (1948–1991), built into a global STEM-publishing power by **Robert Maxwell**. Pergamon was the primary corporate vehicle through which Maxwell built the Maxwell Communication Corporation publishing empire.

In UFO/intelligence discourse, Pergamon is referenced for two structural reasons:
1. Maxwell himself was widely alleged to have been an intelligence asset (variously named as Mossad, KGB, MI6) — a claim revived after his 1991 death-at-sea.
2. Pergamon's translation/access program for Soviet scientific journals through the Cold War made it a documented channel for East-West technical-intelligence transfer in physics and engineering.
