# United States Air Force

The air warfare branch of the U.S. Armed Forces, established as an independent service in September 1947 — the same year as the modern UAP era's beginning (Kenneth Arnold sighting June 1947) and the same year as the National Security Act creating the CIA. The Air Force inherited most of the Army Air Forces' infrastructure, including the early UAP investigation programs (Project Sign, Project Grudge, Project Blue Book).

In the KB, the Air Force is the institutional home of the most documented UAP suppression activity:
- **Project Blue Book** (1952–1969): officially closed with "insufficient evidence" conclusion; subsequent declassification suggests active debunking rather than neutral investigation
- **[[dan-sherman]]**: retired USAF Technical Sergeant; served in NSA's Project Preserve Destiny as an intuitive communicator
- **Area 51 operations**: Air Force controlled access and denied the facility's existence for decades
- **[[space-force]]**: the sixth branch (2019) absorbed Air Force Space Command and now holds formal responsibility for space-domain UAP awareness

Multiple UAP witnesses and whistleblowers in the KB — including [[john-lear]], [[bob-lazar]] adjacent figures, and numerous fighter pilots — served in or were processed through Air Force channels.
