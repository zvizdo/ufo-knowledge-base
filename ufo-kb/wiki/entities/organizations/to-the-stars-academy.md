# To The Stars Academy (TTSA)

Organization co-founded by musician Tom DeLonge, former Pentagon AATIP director [[lue-elizondo]], and former Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence [[chris-mellon]] in October 2017. TTSA functioned as the primary civilian vehicle for the modern UAP disclosure era:

1. **November 2017**: Partnered with the New York Times on the [[2017-nyt-uap-story]] that revealed AATIP and released the FLIR1/Nimitz footage
2. Obtained and released additional Navy UAP footage (GIMBAL, GOFAST)
3. Advocated for congressional UAP oversight hearings
4. Entered a Cooperative Research and Development Agreement (CRADA) with the U.S. Army to research exotic materials

TTSA's core achievement was converting a long-suppressed topic into mainstream political and media discourse in under six months.
