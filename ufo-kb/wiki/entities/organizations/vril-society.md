# Vril Society

The **Vril Society** (also called the *Luminous Lodge* or *Vril-Gesellschaft*) is an alleged German occult group, most active in the 1920s–1940s, said to have:

- **Channeled anti-gravity technology** from an ancient subterranean race ("inner earth" beings)
- Pursued **psychic powers** and contact with non-human intelligences described as pre-human or subterranean
- Had connections to both the **[[thule-society]]** network and early Nazi exotic propulsion research

## Status of Evidence

Whether the Vril Society had a formal institutional existence — with membership lists, meeting records, and documented activities — or whether it was primarily a mythological construction that later attracted embellishment is disputed among researchers. The term "Vril" comes from Edward Bulwer-Lytton's 1871 novel *The Coming Race*, which described a subterranean race powered by a mysterious energy force called "Vril."

What is documented: Nazi Germany funded genuine exotic research through [[ss-ahnenerbe]], and there were real attempts to develop unconventional propulsion. The Vril Society may have been the mythological overlay on actual institutional research.

## Relevance

The Vril Society narrative is relevant because:
1. It frames Nazi exotic propulsion research as **NHI-channeled technology** — the same basic claim made in modern UAP crash-retrieval discourse
2. It connects to the broader **Nazi-UFO-technology** strand (alleged saucer tests in Czechoslovakia, etc.) that appears in other KB sources
3. It places NHI contact at the origin of what became [[operation-paperclip]] technology transfer
