# Nassau Group

An alleged classified group or program referenced by [[tim-taylor]] to [[chris-bledsoe]] — described as based in or connected to [[nassau-bahamas]]. Taylor reportedly told Bledsoe directly that *"time moves differently in Nassau"* and implied membership in a group operating there. In context of Taylor's other claims (Dragon Control, Hammer, Adjustment Bureau framing), the Nassau Group is interpreted as a time-travel-adjacent operation.

Taylor mentioned the Nassau Group to Bledsoe firsthand around 2008. The reference appears in coded form in Taylor's autobiography [[launch-fever]], where Nassau cargo flights and temporal anomalies are described without naming the group explicitly.

## Thomas Townsend Brown as Alleged President

Ryan Bledsoe relayed (to [[bob-maguire]]) that [[tim-taylor]] claimed [[thomas-townsend-brown]] was the **president** of the secret time travel group headquartered in Nassau. Taylor reportedly told this to [[diana-pasulka]] as well. The claim is consistent with:

- Brown's documented time spent in the Bahamas (frequent meeting location for Brown and [[robert-sarbacher]])
- Brown's lifelong theoretical obsession with the gravity–time coupling in general relativity
- The [[caroline-group]] account that Nassau served as the coordination hub for meetings between Brown and Sarbacher

If accurate, the Nassau Group is the institutional bridge between Brown's mid-century electrogravitics research and the later Taylor-network operations. Brown died in 1985, so the group either continued after his death under successor leadership or has been intermittent.
