# KUFOS

KUFOS (Kentuckiana UFO and Paranormal Research Organization, or similar naming) is a civilian UAP research organization. Its primary significance in the KB is a single hearsay connection to [[neil-armstrong]]: according to an account relayed by Stanton Friedman's son (reported in a transcript), Armstrong discussed with Stanton Friedman the possibility of creating a new UFO research organization but ultimately decided against public involvement due to the stigma attached to the UFO topic — and KUFOS may have been the organization that resulted from that conversation without Armstrong's public participation.

**Neil Armstrong context**: Armstrong's post-Apollo career included several UAP-adjacent activities (the [[tayos-cave]] expedition, his carefully worded public statements about lunar experiences) while he maintained a very private public profile. The pattern of declining to publicly associate with UFO research despite private interest fits Armstrong's broader approach to controversial topics.

**Hearsay status**: The connection between Armstrong and KUFOS is second-generation hearsay — Stanton Friedman's son relaying what his father told him about a private conversation with Armstrong. It is preserved in the KB as a data point about Armstrong's private interest rather than as a verified organizational history.

**Civilian research context**: KUFOS sits in the same category as [[hynek-cufos]] and other civilian regional research organizations — the network of non-academic, non-governmental groups that have maintained continuous UAP documentation since the 1970s.
