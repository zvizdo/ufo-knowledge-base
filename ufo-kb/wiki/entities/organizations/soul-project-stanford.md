# Soul Project (Stanford)

Research initiative at Stanford University focused on investigating the nature of consciousness and what is variously called the "soul" — the non-physical aspect of personhood that may persist beyond bodily death, communicate non-locally, or constitute a fundamental feature of reality distinct from physical brain states. Very little public information is available about the specific project structure, principal investigators, or funding.

**Riz Virk connection**: The Soul Project is mentioned by [[riz-virk]] in [[aKZ_MUbuk_Q]] alongside the [[galileo-project]]: "I'm an adviser of the Galileo Project and peripherally involved with the Soul Project as well at Stanford." The pairing is significant — Virk advisers both a serious scientific UAP-detection initiative (Galileo) and a consciousness/soul-research project. This dual involvement reflects the KB's recurring pattern of figures who operate simultaneously at the UAP-physics frontier and the consciousness-research frontier, treating them as converging rather than separate domains.

**Stanford consciousness research context**: Stanford has hosted serious consciousness research through the Stanford Research Institute (SRI) — where the [[remote-viewing]] program was developed under [[hal-puthoff]] and [[russell-targ]] — and through various faculty with interests in anomalous phenomena. The Soul Project is adjacent to this lineage, though whether it has institutional or personnel continuity with the SRI work is unknown.

**KB significance**: The Soul Project represents the academic-tier consciousness-research strand that runs parallel to the UAP-science strand in the KB. If consciousness is fundamental to physical reality (the [[consciousness-non-local]] thesis), then studying its nature at a major research university is directly relevant to understanding NHI contact, remote viewing, and the broader phenomenology the KB documents.
