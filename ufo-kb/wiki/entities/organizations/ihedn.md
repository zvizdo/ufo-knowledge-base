# IHEDN

Institut des Hautes Études de Défense Nationale — France's senior national defense studies institute. IHEDN runs annual seminars for senior military officers, civil servants, diplomats, and business leaders on French and European security strategy. Completion of the program produces "auditors" (anciens auditeurs) who form a network of France's strategic elite.

Most members of the [[cometa-report|COMETA]] group were former IHEDN auditors, including General [[bernard-norlain]] (former director). The first IHEDN UFO study group produced a report 20 years before COMETA; the COMETA Report explicitly credits that earlier work in its Conclusions section.
