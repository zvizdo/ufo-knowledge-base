# Five Eyes

The multilateral signals intelligence (SIGINT) sharing alliance between the United States (NSA), United Kingdom (GCHQ), Canada (CSE), Australia (ASD), and New Zealand (GCSQ). Formalized through the UKUSA Agreement (1946), the alliance coordinates surveillance activities across global communications infrastructure and shares finished intelligence products under a framework that permits member states to spy on each other's citizens on each other's behalf.

In the KB, Five Eyes is relevant through [[echelon]] — the SIGINT network the alliance operates — and through the question of how UAP secrecy is maintained across national intelligence communities. If retrieved UAP materials or UAP-related intelligence exists, Five Eyes coordination means the UK, Australia, Canada, and New Zealand are likely participants in the secrecy architecture, not merely bystanders.

Witnesses in the KB (including military pilots and intelligence officials in the UK and Australia) have reported UAP incidents through national military channels that feed into Five Eyes collection. The transnational nature of UAP secrecy — the fact that it survives across different governments and political systems — is consistent with a Five Eyes-level coordination mechanism.
