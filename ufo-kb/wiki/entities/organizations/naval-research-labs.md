# Naval Research Laboratory

**Type**: Federal research laboratory  
**Established**: 1923  
**Operator**: US Navy  
**Location**: Washington, DC (primary); Stennis Space Center, MS

## Relevance to UAP Discourse

The NRL appears in several UAP-adjacent contexts:

- **[[salvatore-pais]] patents**: Pais filed several exotic-propulsion patents (room-temperature superconductor, high-frequency gravitational wave generator, inertial mass reduction) through the Navy; NRL is the institutional parent of Navy basic research and the pipeline through which Pais-class patents move.
- **[[pais-effect]]**: The "Pais Effect" (high-frequency rotation generating electromagnetic fields that reduce apparent mass) is the theoretical basis for the hybrid aerospace-undersea craft patent. NRL's materials science division is the natural home for any follow-on research.
- **Historical UAP research**: NRL monitored the [[1952-washington-dc-flyover]] with radar; their E-3 radar data fed into the scrambled intercept response.
- **Project Vanguard** (1955–58): NRL ran the first US civilian satellite program; scientists involved overlapped with early UAP technical analysis.
