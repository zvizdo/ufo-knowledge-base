# Lucis Trust

**Lucis Trust** is an esoteric publishing organization founded originally as the **Lucifer Publishing Company** before rebranding. It publishes deeply theosophical and occult knowledge drawing on the Alice Bailey/Theosophy tradition.

Its UAP relevance in the knowledge base comes from a specific event: **Jim Ryder** (VP of Space Systems at Lockheed Martin) spoke at a Lucis Trust event. His topic — "Garment of the God"; devas; fairies; the unveiling of hidden realities (apocalypse in its original revelation sense); psychophysics; mind-matter connections — is extraordinary for an executive in charge of Lockheed's space systems program.

[[peter-levenda]]'s synthesis (from [[Y7PLeu5rTv4]]): "He's not VP of HR. He's VP of Space Systems." The Lucis Trust talk implies that the occult-aerospace bridge is not merely historical (Thule Society, SS Ahnenerbe) but present-day at the executive level of the most classified aerospace programs.

Levenda connects this to the Lucifer = Prometheus = civilization-jumpstarting NHI thesis: the original "light bearer" figures in this tradition are the same entity as the NHI who defied higher authorities to gift humanity with knowledge.
