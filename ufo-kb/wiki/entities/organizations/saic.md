# SAIC (Science Applications International Corporation)

Major US defense contractor historically based in San Diego / McLean, Virginia. SAIC has extensive classified program involvement across the intelligence community, Department of Defense, and Department of Energy.

## Relevance to UAP Research

### Stargate / Remote Viewing (1972–1995)

SAIC (operating through its Palo Alto research division) ran a significant portion of the CIA's **[[stargate-program]]** (psychic spy program) during the 1980s and 1990s. SAIC managed operational remote viewing sessions, assessed viewer performance, and served as the institutional home for later program operations. Key figures from the Stargate era — including [[hal-puthoff]] — passed through or collaborated with SAIC.

### Consciousness Research

SAIC's Palo Alto operations conducted research into consciousness and anomalous cognition well beyond remote viewing — the same research cluster that intersects with **[[psionics-uap-interface]]** doctrine. This history makes SAIC a plausible institutional operator for consciousness-related components of classified UAP programs.

### John Blitch's Brief Tenure (2001)

[[john-blitch]] was recruited to SAIC as VP to rescue a troubled robotics division in Littleton, Colorado. He had approximately 30 total days on site before being recalled to DARPA for the Afghanistan robot mission. He gave one internal briefing on micro-robot infiltration for confined spaces. He was never read into any classified programs during his tenure. However, he notes — from the outside — that SAIC's documented psychotronics/consciousness research history makes it a plausible contractor for the [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]] psionic testing [[randy-anderson]] witnessed.

### NSWC Crane Contracting (per UAPGerb)

[[uap-gerb]] has researched **SAIC contracting links to [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]]** specifically. Crane's Off-World Technology Division — the section where [[randy-anderson]] observed the levitating orb and gauntlet device in 2014 — may be operated or supported by SAIC or successor contractors. The institutional fit (defense contractor + consciousness research history + Crane involvement) is flagged by Jesse Michaels in the context of the Randy Anderson interview.
