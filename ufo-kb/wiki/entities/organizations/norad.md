# NORAD

North American Aerospace Defense Command. Established in 1958 as a bi-national U.S.-Canadian command to provide aerospace warning and control of North America. NORAD's network of radars and satellite sensors tracks all airborne objects — including anomalous ones.

## Relevance to UAP

NORAD's surveillance infrastructure is the primary system through which officially "uncorrelated targets" (objects that cannot be matched to a known aircraft or natural phenomenon) are detected. In the context of the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]], NORAD-adjacent systems would have tracked any UAP approaching [[malmstrom-air-force-base]], [[minot-air-force-base]], or other SAC installations.

NORAD's classified "uncorrelated target" lists — referenced in the [[1zRWi_r3HRM]] context by [[beatriz-villarroel]] — date to the early 1960s and are not publicly released. [[space-force]] (successor to some NORAD functions) maintains these lists.
