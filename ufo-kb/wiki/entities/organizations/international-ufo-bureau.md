# International UFO Bureau

UFO research and documentation organization based in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. Focuses on collecting and publishing firsthand UAP witness accounts, particularly from military, aviation, and government-connected witnesses whose accounts carry institutional credibility weight. Associated with [[greg-rogers]], the US Air Force flight surgeon who witnessed the [[1992-cape-canaveral-saucer]] incident, and [[ty-roberts]] of Total Disclosure.

**Greg Rogers connection**: [[greg-rogers]] serves on the International UFO Bureau's board after going public with his 1992 Cape Canaveral sighting (first reported by [[josh-boswell]] in the UK Daily Mail on May 4, 2024). Rogers's decision to affiliate with the IUFOB after decades of private silence reflects the typical trajectory of credentialed witnesses who come forward: they gravitate toward organizations that take their testimony seriously and provide institutional context for their account.

**Organizational niche**: The IUFOB occupies the civilian-advocacy tier of the UAP research landscape — below the formal scientific organizations ([[scuap]], [[galileo-project]]) and the government-adjacent research organizations ([[aaro]], [[aatip]]) but above purely informal networks. Its Oklahoma City base gives it regional presence in the US interior, away from the coastal concentration of most UAP research activity.

**Ty Roberts and Total Disclosure**: [[ty-roberts]], associated with the Total Disclosure media project, provides a media-distribution dimension to the IUFOB's witness-account documentation work — connecting the organization to the broader disclosure-media ecosystem.
