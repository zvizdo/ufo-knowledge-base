# New Nautics

Florida-based research and retreat organization working with [[andrew-gallimore]] on the cutting edge of DMT science. Two current initiatives:

## 1. Endogenous DMT Research (University of Florida Contract)

New Nautics is funding a team at the University of Florida to **isolate and characterize an endogenous peptide** found in cerebrospinal fluid that inhibits INMT (indolethylamine N-methyltransferase) — the key enzyme that converts tryptamine to DMT. This peptide keeps mammalian brain DMT levels low. Understanding its structure would enable "endoDMTX": using a peptide injection to modulate the endogenous DMT system rather than administering exogenous DMT, potentially producing sustained natural DMT states.

## 2. Darkness Retreat DMT Study

New Nautics is also funding a study to test whether extended darkness triggers endogenous DMT spikes — sending subjects into darkness retreats and measuring DMT levels in blood/urine/saliva at intervals. Motivated by [[aubrey-marcus]]'s report of a full DMT-equivalent experience on day 4–5 of a darkness retreat.

## 3. Eleusis Mind Retreat Center

New Nautics is developing **Eleusis Mind** — a DMTX research and retreat center in St. Vincent and the Grenadines, the Caribbean. The center will offer:
- Preparation with various sub-breakthrough DMT doses
- Full DMTX breakthrough experiences in a clinical/tropical environment (anesthesiologists, psychiatrists, nurses on site)
- Integration support afterward

The center has a government license from St. Vincent and the Grenadines to work with any substance, including DMT and potentially ergot alkaloids. Gallimore explicitly mentions the possibility of reconstructing the Eleusinian Mystery ergot preparations at the site. Website: newnautics.org; retreat: elusismind.com.

## Name and Concept

The name invokes exploration of novel inner territory — "new nautical voyaging." The retreat is explicitly named after the Eleusinian Mysteries, connecting the modern DMTX program to the ancient Greek psychedelic tradition.
