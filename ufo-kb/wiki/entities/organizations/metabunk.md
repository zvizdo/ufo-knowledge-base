# Metabunk

Online forum (metabunk.org) founded and run by [[mick-west]]. Started as a general conspiracy-theory debunking site, grew significantly more focused on UAP after the 2017 NYT story. West describes it as a community forum where members analyze videos and claims using mathematical and technical methods.

Notable for open-source tools — West released "sitrec" (SItuation RECorder), a free open-source tool for analyzing UAP video geometry and flight parameters.

## Programs / Activities

- UAP video analysis (Gimbal, GOFAST, FLIR1, Chilean Navy, Aguadilla, 2008 Iraq gimbal)
- Older conspiracy theory analysis (911, chemtrails, flat earth, false flags)
- Open-source video analysis tooling (sitrec)
