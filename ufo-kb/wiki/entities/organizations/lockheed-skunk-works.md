# Lockheed Skunk Works

Lockheed Martin's Skunk Works (formally Advanced Development Programs, or ADP) is the legendary advanced aerospace R&D division founded by Kelly Johnson in Burbank, California in 1943. Operating under deep secrecy with a small, hand-picked team, Skunk Works produced some of the most consequential aircraft in history: the U-2 high-altitude reconnaissance plane, the Mach-3 SR-71 Blackbird, the F-117 stealth fighter, and later the F-22 Raptor. Its working methods — small teams, extreme classification, direct contract relationships with senior government officials, minimal bureaucracy — became the template for black-budget advanced programs broadly.

The Skunk Works became a focal point in UAP reverse-engineering discourse primarily through [[ben-rich]], who succeeded Kelly Johnson as director and led the division from 1975 to 1991. Rich is alleged to have made a series of statements in the final years of his life (he died in 1995) to the effect that "we now have the technology to take ET home" and that Skunk Works had worked on vehicles "not of this earth." These statements are reported second- and third-hand; no documentary corroboration has emerged, and Rich's surviving colleagues have given conflicting accounts. Nonetheless the alleged Rich statements are among the most-cited pieces of claimed insider testimony in the reverse-engineering literature.

## Nick Cook's Palmdale Visit and ASTRA (G7Ns4Aq1tVc)

[[nick-cook]] was given rare access to interview Skunk Works head [[jack-gordon]] inside the actual classified Palmdale headquarters building. During the interview, Gordon stated he had overseen **15 real flying aircraft** in his career but could only discuss **12**. The three unacknowledged programs remain unknown.

On leaving the building, Cook spotted an org chart with the designation **ASTRA** at the top — above all currently acknowledged aircraft. He was immediately escorted out. The visit date was April 1st — creating ambiguity about whether this was a deliberate disinformation breadcrumb or a genuine slip.

A subsequent Skunk Works director confirmed the incident was well-known internally, stated ASTRA "hasn't flown yet" and "will make an appearance soon." As of 2024, it has not appeared publicly. See [[astra-program]].

Cook also documents [[kelly-johnson]] (Skunk Works founder) being anti-stealth at the end of his career — in conflict with [[ben-rich]] who championed stealth and pushed through the F-117.

## "North Brumit" Claim (D2tKCFmJjks)

[[steven-greer]] told [[michael-herrera]] that the ~300-foot octagonal craft observed in the [[2009-indonesia-ufo]] encounter is a reverse-engineered version manufactured by Lockheed Skunk Works at a facility called **"North Brumit"**. Herrera explicitly treats this as Greer's speculation rather than confirmed fact.

This would make Skunk Works not merely a custodian of RE research but a **manufacturer of operational RE craft** — a significantly stronger claim than the Ben Rich deathbed statements imply.

## Retrieval Team (Blue-on-Blue)

[[kirk-mcconnell]] confirmed to [[michael-herrera]] in a SCIF that he was aware of a **blue-on-blue firefight between a Lockheed retrieval team and another team** involving transport of humans in reverse-engineered craft. If accurate, Lockheed's Skunk Works division operates retrieval teams capable of and tasked with recovering downed NHI craft — consistent with the Ben Rich "taking ET home" claims but now in an active, operational framing.
