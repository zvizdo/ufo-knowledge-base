# Monroe Institute

Non-profit research organization founded by Robert Monroe in 1974 in Faber, Virginia. Develops audio technology (Hemi-Sync) to induce altered states of consciousness including out-of-body experiences, remote viewing states, and non-local awareness.

## Overview

Gateway Process (declassified 1983 CIA report) grew directly from Monroe Institute techniques. Multiple remote viewers trained at or inspired by Monroe Institute protocols, including key [[stargate-program]] personnel.

## Key People

- [[robert-monroe]] — founder; author of *Journeys Out of the Body*, *Far Journeys*, *Ultimate Journey*
- [[joe-mcmoneagle]] — trained under Gateway; became top Stargate viewer

## Programs / Activities

- Gateway Experience — residential altered-states training
- Hemi-Sync audio technology — binaural beats for brain hemisphere synchronization
- Remote viewer training — directly fed into government programs
