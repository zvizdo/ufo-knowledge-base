# American Orthodox Catholic Church

The American Orthodox Catholic Church was a schismatic religious organization in the United States that [[peter-levenda]] characterizes as an intelligence front. It was led by a Ukrainian bishop known as "Propheta" operating from the Bronx, New York, and had membership spanning from New York to New Orleans.

## Intelligence Front Characteristics

Levenda notes that [[j-edgar-hoover]]'s FBI personally vetted clergy in Propheta's church — an unusual level of government interest in a small religious organization. This, combined with the appearance of its members across intelligence-adjacent networks, supports the intelligence-front characterization.

## JFK Network Connection

[[jack-martin]] — the alcoholic bishop who was [[jim-garrison]]'s primary informant in the Kennedy assassination investigation — was a member of this church. [[david-ferrie]], another central figure in Garrison's investigation, was also connected to it. When Garrison obtained files from Propheta, the letters asked: *"When you're finished with Jack Martin, can you send him back to us?"* — indicating the church expected its member back after the investigation concluded.

## Levenda's Personal Connection

Levenda joined the church as a teenager around age 17–18 as a way to obtain a religious draft deferment during the Vietnam War. He and a friend incorporated a related organization (the Czechoslovak Orthodox Church of North and South America) for the same purpose. Later, when going through Garrison's released files, Levenda recognized church stationery he had personally typed on — creating a direct personal loop between his own history and the Kennedy assassination network.
