# New Mexico School of Mines

> *Stub. Renamed New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology (NMT / "New Mexico Tech") in 1951. Expand with detailed history and additional UAP-related documentation as needed.*

Public research university in Socorro, New Mexico, founded 1889. Renamed New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology in 1951.

## UAP Relevance

Per [[dow-uap-d17-sandia-base-1948-1950]], the School's **Research and Development Division** under [[w-d-crozier|W. D. Crozier]] developed the impactment-based airborne particle-collection apparatus used in the 1949 attempt to recover physical material from the New Mexico green fireballs. The 7/25-8/1/1949 field campaign — sampling 9 locations across New Mexico — recovered anomalous copper-bearing particles and three perfectly spherical cobalt-indicating 12-micron particles, constituting the primary physical-evidence investigation in the corpus. The work was sponsored by the 17th OSI District through Lincoln LaPaz.
