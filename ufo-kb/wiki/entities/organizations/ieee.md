# IEEE

## Overview

The Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) is the world's largest technical professional organization for electronic and electrical engineering. It publishes peer-reviewed engineering journals including the flagship *Proceedings of the IEEE*.

## Key People / Activities

### Proceedings of the IEEE — 1976 Remote-Viewing Paper

[[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]] and [[russell-targ|Russell Targ]] published their foundational [[remote-viewing|remote-viewing]] paper in the *Proceedings of the IEEE* in 1976. Per Puthoff (Gf_tKn9TaP8), as engineers themselves, they targeted the engineering venue rather than a parapsychology journal to maximize technical scrutiny.

The editor (head of communications at [[bell-labs|Bell Labs]]) initially balked after receiving a one-sentence review: "This is the kind of thing I wouldn't believe in even if it were true." Puthoff demanded a presentation to Bell Labs engineers, who became enthusiastic about the mechanism question; the editor still balked. Trump card: a full blind replication at Bell Labs, with Bell engineers and Bell-internal target-list makers. Bell Labs replicated; the paper was published.

[[bob-jahn|Robert Jahn]] published his time-decoupled-remote-viewing replication in the same journal ~2 years later (~1978), giving the IEEE Proceedings the canonical academic remote-viewing publication record.
