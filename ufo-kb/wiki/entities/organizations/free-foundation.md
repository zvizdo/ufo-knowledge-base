# FREE Foundation

The Foundation for Research into Extraterrestrial Encounters (FREE) is a nonprofit organization co-founded by Rey Hernandez (an estate planning attorney who reported a personal UAP encounter) along with academic advisors including Dr. Rudy Schild (Harvard astrophysicist, retired) and clinical psychologist Dr. Jon Klimo. FREE conducts structured, large-scale quantitative surveys of individuals who report contact experiences with NHI.

**Survey methodology**: FREE's approach applies structured questionnaire methodology to the experiencer population — rather than relying on self-selected testimonials or hypnotic regression cases. The surveys cover types of contact (physical, telepathic, OBE, NDE, CE5 protocols, apparitions), physical effects, emotional and psychological impact, and witness demographics. The scale (several thousand respondents across multiple survey waves) allows statistical analysis impossible with individual case studies and enables comparisons across contact types.

**Beyond UFOs (2018)**: The survey findings were published in *Beyond UFOs: The Science of Consciousness and Contact with Non Human Intelligence* (2018), a multi-author academic volume. Key findings included: a majority of experiencers report positive transformative effects on their psychology and worldview; a significant minority report negative effects; physical effects are common (including apparent healing and physical evidence); the contact phenomenon spans many categories beyond "alien abduction" — most experiencers report multiple contact modalities, not a single event type.

**Consciousness-contact connection**: FREE's data showed substantial overlap between UAP contact experiences and other altered-state phenomena — NDE, OBE, meditation-contact, and CE5 protocols produced similar experiencer profiles. This overlap is cited in the KB's consciousness-technology and NHI-contact frameworks as evidence that the contact phenomenon is consciousness-mediated rather than purely physical.

**KB relevance**: FREE represents the move from anecdotal case documentation (Budd Hopkins, John Mack methodology — deep case studies of individuals under hypnotic regression) toward systematic quantitative research on the experiencer population — analogous to the shift from case studies to epidemiology in medicine. Its findings are cited in [[experiencer-research]] as the institutional context for academic legitimization of the field.
