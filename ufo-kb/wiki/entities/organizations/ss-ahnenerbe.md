# SS Ahnenerbe

The *Ahnenerbe* (literally "ancestral heritage") was an organization founded in 1935 by Heinrich Himmler within the SS to pursue research into Germanic and Aryan prehistory, occult traditions, and esoteric knowledge. It became one of the primary institutional expressions of Nazi occultism.

## Research Programs

[[peter-levenda]] notes that the Ahnenerbe conducted research that would be considered bizarre within any other institutional framework:

- **Tibetan expeditions** — searching for Aryan racial origins and spiritual-occult knowledge in Tibet
- **Yoga-rune postures** — systematic study of how yoga asanas corresponded to runic symbols, with the premise that yoga preserved an ancient Aryan body-knowledge tradition
- **Non-Jewish Kabbalah** — Himmler was interested in Kabbalistic traditions that could be appropriated without the Jewish attribution; the Ahnenerbe studied mystical traditions selectively
- **Ancient history reconstruction** — SS officer Karl Wiligut claimed photographic memory of Nordic prehistoric civilization and provided "recovered" ancient knowledge

## Documentation

The Ahnenerbe's files are substantially preserved in the US National Archives, having been captured at the end of World War II. Levenda cites this documentation as evidence against the common dismissal of Nazi occultism as myth — the institutional apparatus was real and well-documented.

## Wewelsburg Castle

The Ahnenerbe's most visible physical expression was [[wewelsburg-castle]] — Himmler's SS castle, which he was building into an occult center and "SS Vatican." [[michael-aquino]] later conducted a satanic ceremony there in a deliberate act of esoteric reclamation.

## Significance to UAP Research

The Ahnenerbe connects to UAP history through the [[occult-aerospace-connection]] concept: the same institutional framework that studied ancient wisdom traditions was embedded within the SS that also ran the secret weapons programs at [[kammler-stab]] (under [[hans-kammler]]). Whether occult knowledge was intended to inform technological development or remained separate remains an open question.
