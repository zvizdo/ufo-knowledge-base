# Department of Defense

## Overview

The United States Department of Defense (DoD), headquartered at the Pentagon, is the executive branch department responsible for coordinating and supervising all agencies and functions of the government directly related to national security and the US military. Established by the National Security Act of 1947, the DoD replaced the earlier War Department and unified oversight of the military services under a single civilian secretary. In the UAP context, the DoD is the primary institutional custodian of classified UAP-related programs, the employer of the key UAP oversight bodies (AATIP, UAP Task Force, AARO), and — per sources cited by researchers including Michael Shellenberger — the administrator of the alleged [[immaculate-constellation]] UAP tracking program.
