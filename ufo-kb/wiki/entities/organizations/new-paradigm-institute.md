# New Paradigm Institute

[[danny-sheehan]]'s current legal and advocacy organization for UAP disclosure. Successor to the [[christic-institute]] in Sheehan's organizational trajectory.

## Role in UAP Legislation

The New Paradigm Institute is one of the civilian organizations **authorized under statute** by the Senate UAP disclosure bill to recommend candidates for the independent UAP review board. This gives Sheehan's organization formal legal standing in the disclosure process — unusual for a civil society group in this space.

Per Sheehan's account in [[C4rSj5Aum7w]]:
- Will recommend board members it considers "almost beyond criticism" — people the president cannot easily dismiss
- Challenge: the president ultimately appoints the board and could ignore recommendations
- Working to get Crenshaw (new House Intelligence Committee chair) to greenlight the full 64-page bill or at least the board-appointment portion

## Current Disclosure Strategy (as of early 2025)

- Focus on Senate re-passage of 64-page UAP bill (Rounds to sponsor, Schumer to co-sponsor)
- Target the House Intelligence Committee under [[dan-crenshaw]] (viewed as more tractable than predecessor Mike Turner)
- Immediate deadline: National Archives September 2025 military/intel UAP file submission
- Sheehan gives 51% odds of meaningful release by then
