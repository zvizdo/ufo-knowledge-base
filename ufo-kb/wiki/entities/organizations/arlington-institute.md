# Arlington Institute

The Arlington Institute is a think tank founded by [[john-peterson]], a figure who appeared on "many many short lists for DoD Secretary of Defense position for years." Its existence at the intersection of defense policy circles and UAP-adjacent inquiry makes it unusual.

## The 1998 Extraterrestrial Simulation Conference

In approximately 1998, the Arlington Institute convened a small gathering — described as a "mini conference" — to simulate and discuss "what it would be like in a world where extraterrestrials are openly acknowledged as living among us."

[[catherine-austin-fitts]] was invited to this conference by Peterson. She declined out of fear: at the time she was under investigation for her financial disclosures about the US government and drug trafficking connections, and she feared the invitation might be a setup. She came to regret not attending.

This conference — at the intersection of DoD-adjacent personnel and extraterrestrial open-contact simulation — is notable for occurring years before the public disclosure movement became mainstream. That a figure of Peterson's DoD stature was organizing it suggests awareness at institutional levels of the UAP issue.

## John Peterson and Catherine Austin Fitts

[[richard-dolan]] also references a separate claim (from researcher [[linda-thompson]]): that Peterson allegedly offered to show Fitts "a UFO" if she would stop her financial investigations of the US government. If accurate, this implies Peterson had access to UAP reality and was willing to use it as a bargaining chip.
