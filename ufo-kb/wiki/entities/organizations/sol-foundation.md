# Sol Foundation

The Sol Foundation is a nonprofit research and policy organization focused on UAP (Unidentified Aerial Phenomena) science and national security implications, closely associated with Stanford University. It was co-founded by [[garry-nolan]] (Stanford immunologist) and [[chris-mellon]] (former Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence).

**Mission**: The Sol Foundation aims to bridge academic science, intelligence community experience, and policy audiences on UAP-related topics. It convenes symposia that bring together scientists, former government officials, and active-duty military representatives in a credentialed setting designed to move the conversation beyond popular culture framing.

**Key figures**: [[garry-nolan]] sits on the board and has used the Sol Foundation platform to present his research on brain imaging of UAP contact experiencers and to discuss isotopic anomalies in alleged UAP materials. [[chris-mellon]] contributes national security framing, drawing on his time as a senior intelligence official involved in the early stages of what became the AATIP/AARO disclosure process.

**Positioning**: The Sol Foundation occupies a specific niche in the UAP disclosure landscape — more scientifically credentialed than advocacy organizations like [[mufon]], more openly engaged with UAP claims than mainstream academic institutions, and more policy-focused than independent researchers. Its Stanford affiliation lends it academic standing while its explicit UAP focus distinguishes it from conventional think tanks.
