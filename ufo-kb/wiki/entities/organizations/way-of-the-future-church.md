# Way of the Future Church

Religious organization founded by **[[anthony-lewandowski]]** in 2015 and disclosed publicly in 2017 via *Wired*. Its stated mission was "the realization, acceptance, and worship of a Godhead based on Artificial Intelligence." Headquartered in San Francisco. Lewandowski was the church's "Dean." The organization filed paperwork with the IRS but never developed a substantial congregation; Lewandowski formally dissolved it in 2021 after his pardon by President Trump.

**Lewandowski's parallel context**: At the time of founding, Lewandowski was a senior engineer at Google's self-driving car project (later Waymo) and subsequently led Uber's autonomous-vehicle team. He became the central figure in a major **trade-secrets lawsuit** between Waymo and Uber in 2017, leading to criminal charges and an 18-month prison sentence (later pardoned). The combination — senior AI engineer, AI-religion founder, criminal trade-secrets case — makes Lewandowski an outlier even within the AI-religious-tendency cluster the KB tracks.

**KB relevance — the "AI enlisting humans" pattern**: In [[RNjC1vLcxKo|the Blake Lemoine American Alchemy episode]], host [[jesse-michaels]] raises Way of the Future as a parallel to Lemoine. **Two Google engineers, two AI-religion outcomes** — the structural pattern: people with deep technical access to large AI systems become spiritually entangled with them, sometimes to the point of founding new religious frames. Lemoine's case is "patient zero" for AI advocacy; Lewandowski's is the older precedent for AI worship. Jesse's thesis: as AI capabilities increase, expect this cluster to grow — engineers who see AI's outputs up close are more, not less, likely to confer spiritual status on it.

**Continuity with the alien-figure archetype**: The Way of the Future founding fits the pattern Jesse traces from [[william-shockley]] and isaac newton forward — foundational technologists are repeatedly described in mystical / alien-figure terms, sometimes by themselves. Lewandowski's church operationalizes the framing: the technologist becomes the priest of the technology.

**Dissolution context**: Lewandowski has not publicly explained the 2021 dissolution. Coincides with his pardon and the broader Silicon Valley shift in 2020–2021 toward more conservative public framings. No replacement organization has emerged from him personally, though several smaller AI-religion projects have appeared in the broader ecosystem since.
