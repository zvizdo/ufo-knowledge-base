# 45th Space Wing

The 45th Space Wing (45th SW) is the US Air Force unit responsible for administering the eastern space launch range, encompassing [[cape-canaveral]] (Cape Canaveral AFS), [[patrick-air-force-base]], and the Eastern Space and Missile Center.

## Key People

- [[greg-rogers]] — Chief of aerospace medicine and chief flight surgeon, 1989–~1994; senior flight surgeon for astronaut rescue/recovery team at [[kennedy-space-center]]

## Connection to the 1992 Saucer Sighting

Rogers' medical oversight role gave him access to roughly 80% of buildings on Cape Canaveral under [[egng]] escort — the unusually wide operational footprint that led indirectly to his [[1992-cape-canaveral-saucer|1992 sighting]].
