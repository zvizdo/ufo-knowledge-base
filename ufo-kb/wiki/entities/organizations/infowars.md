# Infowars

Media platform founded and hosted by [[alex-jones]], headquartered in Austin, Texas. Operates as a news/commentary website, radio show, and video channel covering alternative and conspiracy-oriented political commentary. Despite being banned from YouTube, Apple, Facebook, Spotify, and other platforms since 2018, the platform continues to reach millions of Americans daily.

## History

Jones began broadcasting on Access TV in Austin around 1995, transitioned to radio, and eventually built Infowars into a major alternative media brand. His early work was explicitly anti-establishment but not partisan: in the early 2000s he was "very anti-George W. Bush, anti-the war in Iraq, and against neoconservatism."

The platform became a major flashpoint during the 2016 US election cycle and was broadly deplatformed following the 2018 Sandy Hook defamation litigation.

## Role in UAP-Adjacent Discourse

Infowars regularly covers conspiracies adjacent to UAP discourse: secret societies, government false flags, mind control, breakaway civilization, consciousness suppression. Jones's Bohemian Grove infiltration footage was a major Infowars production.
