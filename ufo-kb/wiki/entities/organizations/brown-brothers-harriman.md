# Brown Brothers Harriman

Elite American investment bank formed in 1931 through the merger of Brown Brothers & Company with W.A. Harriman & Company. [[danny-sheehan]]'s account identifies BBH as the institutional precursor to the CIA and the source of the covert operations legal authority that enabled everything from the JFK assassination to UAP secrecy.

## Third Reich Financing

Per Sheehan:
- **George Herbert Walker** (grandfather of [[george-hw-bush]]) set up the **Union Bank of New York** in 1924, capitalized by BBH investors, to funnel money to finance Hitler's rise and the Third Reich.
- **Fritz Thyssen** ran the Dutch subsidiary (Bank of Shipping and Commerce, Netherlands) that channeled this investment into the German war machine.
- **Henry Ford** provided the ideological infrastructure — his idealization of the Nazi economic model and anti-Semitic publications preceded and informed this financial relationship.
- The investors calculated that a militarized Germany would generate enormous returns on industrial investment.

## CIA Creation

- **Robert Lovett** (BBH senior partner) wrote the foundational memo to Truman recommending the creation of the CIA and, specifically, the **NSC 5412** directive — the legal authority for CIA covert operations on foreign soil.
- **Allen Dulles** and **John Foster Dulles** — the nephews of former Secretary of State John W. Foster and grandsons of Robert Lansing — were BBH-adjacent figures who drafted the Versailles Treaty reparations provisions that BBH investors then financed and extracted wealth from.
- The same network that financed Hitler effectively created the legal and institutional architecture of American covert intelligence.

## The Throughline

Sheehan's argument: BBH → CIA → S Force (JFK assassination) → Watergate cover-up → Iran-Contra → UAP secrecy protection is not a conspiracy theory but a traceable institutional genealogy. The same families and organizational DNA appear across these events.

**General Smedley Butler's 1934 testimony** to Congress that a BBH-adjacent circle recruited him to lead a fascist coup against FDR is an early data point in this lineage — the same network that financed Hitler attempted to install fascism in the U.S.
