# Counter Intelligence Corps (CIC)

## Overview

The Counter Intelligence Corps (CIC) was the US Army's premier intelligence and security body, functioning essentially as the Army's FBI. During and after WWII it played a central role in:
- **Denazification** of occupied Germany
- **Operation Paperclip** — the secret importation of 1,500+ Nazi scientists into the US
- **Securing Nazi advanced technology**, including exotic propulsion research
- Hunting war criminals and safeguarding military secrets

The CIC is deeply embedded in early UFO/UAP accounts, including the Roswell crash and early Majestic 12 leaks.

## Key People

- **[[henry-kissinger]]** — CIC officer; assigned to Oberammergau 1945; placed at the center of Nazi scientist recruitment and possible exotic propulsion intelligence
- **[[philip-corso]]** — CIC agent who later served on Eisenhower's NSC and claimed to have distributed alien technology to US industry
- **Neil Gershimer** — CIC agent who coerced [[victor-schauberger]] into signing over his anti-gravity IP to the US
- **[[harold-malmgren]]** — mentioned in sources as having CIC-adjacent intelligence ties

## Role in UAP Programs

MJ12 documents reference CIC involvement in UFO investigations including Roswell. CIC agents in Kissinger's operational area (Germany, 1945) interrogated [[victor-schauberger]] for nine months on anti-gravity propulsion — agents reportedly considered this more important than Nazi nuclear research. The IP was deposited at [[brookhaven-national-lab]].

The CIC thus served as a funnel for both human talent ([[operation-paperclip]]) and intellectual property from the Nazi exotic-technology program into US classified research.
