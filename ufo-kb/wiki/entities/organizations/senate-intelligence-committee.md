# Senate Select Committee on Intelligence

The US Senate's permanent committee with jurisdiction over the intelligence community. Oversees CIA, NSA, DIA, and other IC components. Key UAP venue: classified briefings on UAP programs have been held here; Senators Mark Warner (chair), [[marco-rubio|Marco Rubio]], and later [[chuck-schumer|Chuck Schumer]] used this committee as the platform for UAP disclosure legislation.

## UAP Legislation

The UAP Disclosure Act (UAPDA, 2023-2024 National Defense Authorization Act amendments) was primarily advanced through the Senate via this committee and through Armed Services. Rubio's early amendment to the 2020 Intelligence Authorization Act mandating the UAP Task Force report also came through here.
