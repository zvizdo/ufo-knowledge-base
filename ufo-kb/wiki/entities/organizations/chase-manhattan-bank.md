# Chase Manhattan Bank

Chase Manhattan Bank was one of the largest US commercial banks of the 20th century, headquartered in New York City. It merged with J.P. Morgan & Co. in 2000 to form JPMorgan Chase, now one of the world's largest financial institutions.

**David Rockefeller connection**: In the UAP KB context, Chase Manhattan Bank is relevant primarily as the institutional base of [[david-rockefeller]], grandson of Standard Oil founder John D. Rockefeller, who served as chairman of the bank from 1969 to 1981. David Rockefeller was one of the most influential financiers and establishment figures of the late 20th century.

**Opposition to UAP disclosure**: [[laurance-rockefeller]]'s disclosure initiative — the [[1995-rockefeller-ufo-initiative]] — brought Steven Greer to the family's [[jy-ranch]] in Wyoming for meetings with the Clintons. Per Greer's account, Laurance told him that his brother David (Chairman of Chase Manhattan) and nephew Senator [[jay-rockefeller]] were "jumping up and down on my nuts for even having this meeting with you." David's opposition — representing the family's establishment-financial wing — constrained Laurance's ability to support disclosure efforts. Early funding Laurance did provide was reportedly intercepted by CIA operatives on his team.

**Structural significance**: The Chase Manhattan/David Rockefeller opposition to Laurance's disclosure efforts illustrates the internal family tension in elite networks around the UAP question: the same family produced the most dedicated elite-level disclosure advocate (Laurance) and one of its most institutionally powerful opponents (David).
