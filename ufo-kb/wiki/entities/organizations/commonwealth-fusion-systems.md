# Commonwealth Fusion Systems (CFS)

MIT spinout fusion company founded in 2018, widely regarded as one of the leading private ventures in the race to achieve commercial nuclear fusion. Its SPARC tokamak design uses high-temperature superconducting (HTS) magnets to achieve plasma confinement at smaller scale than traditional fusion designs.

## Relationship to MIT and Nuno Lorero

CFS's scientific foundation was built on decades of MIT plasma physics research conducted at the **MIT Plasma Science and Fusion Center (PSFC)**. The PSFC directly collaborates with CFS and contributes fundamental plasma physics — including research on magnetic confinement, MHD stability, and magnetic reconnection — to CFS's reactor design.

[[nuno-lorero]] was deputy director of the PSFC at the time of his murder in December 2025. As the institution's second-ranking researcher overseeing its full plasma and fusion research portfolio, his death effectively removed CFS's most direct institutional scientific ally at MIT.

## The TAE Technologies / Trump Media Context

Two days after Lorero's murder (December 18, 2025), [[tae-technologies]] — one of CFS's main competitors in the private fusion race — announced a $6 billion merger with Trump Media and Technology Group. The merger was announced without any corresponding technical breakthrough. Most analysts attribute any fusion company's success to federal government support given the technology's cost and infrastructure requirements.

Whether Lorero's death benefited CFS's competitors is a structural observation; no causal connection has been established.
