# 31st Marine Expeditionary Unit

## Overview

The 31st Marine Expeditionary Unit is a forward-deployed Marine Air-Ground Task Force based in Okinawa, Japan. In [[OOr98r51Sj0]], the unit is referenced as the operational umbrella under which [[michael-herrera]] (with 2nd Battalion, 5th Marines, described as "the most decorated infantry battalion in the entire Marine Corps") deployed to Indonesia / the Philippines in 2009 for **Operation Kitsana** — the humanitarian-assistance operation during which Herrera witnessed the [[2009-indonesia-ufo|300-ft octagonal vantablack craft loading psionic-asset shipping containers]].
