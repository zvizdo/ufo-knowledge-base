# National Reconnaissance Office

The National Reconnaissance Office (NRO) is the U.S. intelligence community agency responsible for developing and operating reconnaissance satellites that collect signals, imagery, and measurement intelligence for the DoD and IC. Established in 1961, its very existence was classified until September 1992 — 31 years — making it one of the longest-hidden major government agencies in American history. It operates in close coordination with the NSA, CIA, and DIA to task and exploit satellite collection.

**UAP sensor coverage**: NRO reconnaissance satellites carry electro-optical, radar, and signals-intelligence payloads covering the full Earth surface. Any anomalous aerial object of significant radar cross-section or thermal signature operating over areas under NRO collection tasking would be captured in the NRO database. Researchers including [[danny-sheehan]] have argued that NRO imagery of UAP events — if collected — would be classified at the highest Sensitive Compartmented Information (SCI) tiers, accessible to fewer than a hundred officials worldwide.
