# Quantum Generative Materials

Quantum computing startup founded by [[deep-prasad]] with the explicit dual mission of advancing materials simulation via quantum hardware and reverse-engineering UAP propulsion/material properties from DoD-documented observable behaviors.

## Mission and Funding

Raised $15 million. The operational goal is to solve the many-body Schrödinger equation at scale — a computation that classical computers cannot perform for more than ~3 atoms — to simulate arbitrary combinations of atoms and predict their properties before physical synthesis. The UAP application: simulate metamaterials whose modeled properties match the DoD's [[five-observables]], on the hypothesis that UAP propulsion is a [[macroscopic-quantum-uap]] phenomenon.

## Methodology

See [[quantum-computing-uap-reverseengineering]] for the full concept framework.

The company performs "hardcore research into how to map material science problems onto a quantum computer." Initially using quantum computer simulators (classical emulations of quantum algorithms) rather than physical quantum hardware, due to decoherence limitations of current NISQ devices. The mathematical challenge is identifying the correct representation of the many-body Schrödinger equation that maps efficiently onto qubit-based algorithms.

## Significance for ufo-kb

The company is unusual in the UAP disclosure landscape: rather than relying on whistleblowers or document leaks, it attempts to approach UAP physics from first principles using publicly available DoD data (the five observables) and openly documented quantum computing research. This positions it as the first known startup explicitly engineering toward UAP-observable capabilities from the outside.
