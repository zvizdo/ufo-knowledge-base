## Overview

The Office of Strategic Services (OSS) was the United States' primary wartime intelligence and special operations agency, operating from 1942 until its dissolution in September 1945. It was the direct institutional predecessor to the Central Intelligence Agency, and many of its personnel, networks, and — critically — its most sensitive intelligence holdings carried over into the CIA when that agency was established in 1947.

The OSS's most significant action in the UFO/UAP context was Project McGregor: the covert Allied wartime operation to locate and retrieve a non-human craft that had crashed near [[magenta-italy]] in June 1933 and had been held by Mussolini's fascist government for over a decade. The operation succeeded in 1945, with the craft ultimately transferred to [[wright-patterson]] Air Force Base.

The OSS's founding director, Wild Bill Donovan, was a [[knights-of-malta|Knight of Malta]], and the Vatican back-channel through which Pope Pius XII alerted the Allies to the existence of the Magenta craft ran directly through networks that intersected with the Order. This means that the chain of custody for the most significant pre-Roswell non-human craft recovery in the historical record runs from the Vatican through the Knights of Malta through the OSS — not through a purely governmental chain of command.

## Key People

- **Wild Bill Donovan** — Founder and Director of the OSS; Knight of Malta. Donovan designed and directed Project McGregor. His dual role as OSS chief and Knight of Malta positioned him at the intersection of the governmental and transnational custodian networks that would manage UAP secrecy into the Cold War era.
- **Allen Dulles** — OSS station chief in Bern, Switzerland. Dulles ran OSS operations in Europe from Switzerland, operating beyond the reach of US law and with significant autonomy. He coordinated with his brother John Foster Dulles and would later become CIA Director. His European networks were central to the intelligence picture that made Project McGregor possible.
- **John Foster Dulles** — Allen Dulles's brother; coordinated with OSS European operations; later served as Secretary of State.

## Programs / Activities

**Project McGregor**
Project McGregor was the OSS operation — initiated on the basis of Pope Pius XII's Vatican back-channel intelligence — to retrieve the 1933 Magenta craft from Italian custody before it could fall into Nazi hands or be destroyed in the chaotic final months of the war in Italy. The operation targeted advanced technology in northern Italy and succeeded in securing the craft in 1945. The recovered object was subsequently transported to [[wright-patterson]] Air Force Base, where it entered the US UAP secrecy infrastructure.

The operation's success depended on: (1) the Vatican's willingness to share intelligence about the craft's existence and location; (2) the OSS's covert operational capability in occupied and contested Italian territory; and (3) the [[gabinetto-rs33|RS/33 investigation]] having already established the craft's location and condition at [[sia-marchetti]] aerospace hangars.

**Swiss Operations Under Allen Dulles**
Allen Dulles's OSS station in Bern served as the nerve center for OSS operations in Europe. Operating from neutral Switzerland placed it beyond the legal constraints that applied to OSS activities on US soil and gave Dulles unusual autonomy. The intelligence gathered through these operations — including on Axis advanced technology programs — fed directly into Project McGregor's targeting.

**Transition to the CIA**
When the OSS was dissolved in September 1945, President Truman initially distributed its functions across existing departments. The CIA was established in 1947, and OSS personnel — including Dulles and many of Donovan's key officers — reconstituted the agency. The UAP-related intelligence holdings and networks, including the custodianship structures surrounding the Magenta craft, carried over into the new agency's most sensitive compartments.
