# Russia / Soviet Union

In the KB, Russia and its predecessor state the Soviet Union appear primarily in three contexts:

## Directed-Energy Weapons

Russia is [[eric-haseltine]]'s primary suspect for the [[2016-havana-syndrome]] attacks. Soviet directed-energy research predates the US by decades. The [[1976-moscow-embassy-microwave-incident]] (1970s) documents Soviet microwave beaming of the US Embassy in Moscow; US Ambassador [[walter-stoessel]] later died of a rare blood cancer attributed to the exposure. The [[microwave-hearing-effect]] is a Soviet-origin research program.

## Maskirovka

[[maskirovka]] (Russian military deception doctrine) is one of Haseltine's competing hypotheses for UAP: Russia possesses exotic propulsion and stealth technology, and some UAP sightings may be deliberate deception operations designed to mislead US military and intelligence. The doctrine has Cold War precedents in ghost aircraft, false radar returns, and deception-by-demonstration.

## UFO Research

The Soviet Union ran its own UAP research programs. The [[1989-voronezh-landing]] and other Soviet-era sightings were investigated by official Soviet scientific bodies. Yevgeny Primakov ([[yevgeny-primakov]]) and other senior Soviet figures had documented interest in the paranormal.
