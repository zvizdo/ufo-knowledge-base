# Citizen Hearing on Disclosure

A 2013 mock congressional hearing organized by [[ruben-langden]] at the **National Press Club** in Washington D.C. The event featured former members of Congress hearing sworn testimony from UAP witnesses and researchers, structured to simulate an actual congressional hearing — years before UAP testimony became a formal congressional activity.

## Purpose and Format

Langden's design goal was to establish a congressional-format precedent: credentialed witnesses, former legislators in the role of questioners, sworn testimony, public record. The event anticipated the 2023 congressional UAP hearings by a decade.

[[jeremy-corbell]] describes Langden as "the unsung hero of the citizen hearing" — crediting him with creating the template that normalized the concept of congressional-format UAP testimony.

## Financial Collapse

The event went significantly over budget and resulted in Langden's personal bankruptcy, attributed to "misappropriation of funds and everything that was going on." The financial fallout left Langden in a state of personal crisis — which paradoxically led to his reaching out to Corbell and ultimately facilitating the [[oscar-wolf]] deathbed interview through his network.

## Legacy

The Citizen Hearing predated the congressional UAP hearing wave of 2022–2023 by nearly a decade. Its witnesses and methodology anticipated the kind of public testimony that eventually became institutionally validated. Whether it directly influenced subsequent congressional action is unclear, but Corbell frames it as a significant step in the long arc of UAP disclosure normalization.
