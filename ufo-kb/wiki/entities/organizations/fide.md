# FIDE — World Chess Federation

Fédération Internationale des Échecs, founded 1924 in Paris. Headquartered in Lausanne, Switzerland. Approximately 200 member nations — effectively a global body. Governs competitive chess at all levels from amateur to world championship.

## Ilyumzhinov's Presidency (1995–2018)

[[kirsan-ilyumzhinov]] was elected FIDE president on November 24, 1995 — a date predicted by [[baba-vanga]] six months earlier to the day (April 1995). He served for 23 years, making him one of the longest-serving presidents in FIDE history.

During his tenure Ilyumzhinov pursued a goal he frames in explicitly civilizational terms: 1 billion chess players = 1 billion intelligent people who look multiple moves ahead = a world more capable of preventing war, redirecting military budgets to science and medicine, and eventually making contact with advanced civilizations. Chess, on this view, is not just a game but an alien-gifted tool for human cognitive development.

He also introduced chess as a compulsory school subject in [[kalmykia]] and lobbied the UN General Assembly annually to redirect weapons budgets to brain research and cancer treatment.

His governance was contested — [[garry-kasparov]] founded the breakaway Professional Chess Association (PCA) in 1993, and after Ilyumzhinov's alien disclosure, Kasparov publicly called for his removal on grounds of mental unfitness.
