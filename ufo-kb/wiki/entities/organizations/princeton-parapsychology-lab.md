# Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research (PEAR)

Research program at Princeton University's School of Engineering, founded in 1979 by Dean of Engineering Robert Jahn. PEAR ran for 28 years, producing peer-reviewed research demonstrating small but statistically robust correlations between human intention and random event generators (REGs), and reproducing remote perception results under controlled conditions.

PEAR is significant in the UAP/consciousness nexus because its results — showing non-local mind-matter interaction — provide an empirical backbone for claims about consciousness-based UAP phenomena and psionic interfaces.

## Key Personnel

- [[bob-jahn]] — founder, Princeton Dean of Engineering; plasma physicist; 28 years running PEAR; co-authored *Margins of Reality* and *Consciousness and the Source of Reality* with Brenda Dunne
- Brenda Dunne — laboratory manager, co-author of most PEAR publications
- [[herb-metz]] — parapsychologist; 10+ years on REG experiments; designed the Mind Lamp; author of *The Selection Effect*
