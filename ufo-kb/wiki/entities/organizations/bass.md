# BASS — Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies

The contractual entity through which **[[bigelow-aerospace]]** (Robert Bigelow's company) held the AAWSAP government contract. BASS (also written BAASS) was the primary contractor for the DIA's Advanced Aerospace Weapons Systems Application Program (AAWSAP), which ran from approximately 2007 to 2012 with an initial $22 million appropriation.

## Role in AAWSAP

BASS administered field investigations, contracted specialized researchers, and managed the production of Defense Intelligence Reference Documents (DIRDs). [[eric-davis]] and [[hal-puthoff]] worked within the BASS/AAWSAP research ecosystem through their affiliation with [[earthtech]].

## Crash Retrieval Material Access Attempt

[[eric-davis]] describes in [[LnAiNChnuEQ]] that during the AAWSAP period, BASS (under DIA auspices) attempted to get a defense contractor (identified in context as [[lockheed-martin]]) to share crash retrieval materials they had held since before the 1989 CIA shutdown.

The attempt required SAP-level clearance that AAWSAP did not yet have (it held TSSI; Harry Reid was simultaneously trying to get SAP elevation, which [[william-lynn]] denied). [[glenn-gaffney]] (CIA Deputy Director of S&T) intervened and blocked the transfer, saying "absolutely not."

This is the most operationally specific firsthand account of what BASS was actually trying to accomplish — not just conducting open-ended research, but attempting to access physical crash retrieval materials from legacy corporate custodians.
