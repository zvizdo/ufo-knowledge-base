# CNES

Centre National d'Études Spatiales — France's national space agency. CNES has housed France's official UAP investigation body in all three of its forms: **GEPAN** (1977–1988), **SEPRA** (1988–2005), and **[[geipan|GEIPAN]]** (2005–present). This institutional continuity — a government UAP investigation unit sitting within the national space agency — is cited in the [[cometa-report|COMETA Report]] as a model for other nations.

Former CNES President [[andre-lebeau]] wrote the foreword to the COMETA Report. [[jean-jacques-velasco|Jean-Jacques Vélasco]] headed SEPRA within CNES and co-authored the report.
