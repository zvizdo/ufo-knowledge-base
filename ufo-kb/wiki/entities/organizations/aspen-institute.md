# Aspen Institute

The Aspen Institute is a Washington DC-based non-profit think tank and conference center. In the UAP context, it is flagged as a potential nexus between intelligence-community information management operations and mainstream media narratives — specifically through the activities of its affiliates [[vivian-schiller]] and [[garrett-graff]].

## The Laptop Pre-Bunking Operation

In summer 2020, Aspen Institute hosted an exercise co-orchestrated by Vivian Schiller (then NPR president) and Garrett Graff (Aspen fellow) that seeded media skepticism about the authenticity of the Hunter Biden laptop before the story broke publicly. [[michael-shellenberger]] identifies this as a pre-bunking operation: manufacturing doubt in advance to inoculate the media environment.

Graff subsequently authored *UFO* (2024), a book Shellenberger characterizes as sophisticated UAP debunking. The conjunction — information-management operation on behalf of a political figure, followed by a UAP debunking book — raises the question of whether the same institutional network manages both political and UAP information landscapes.
