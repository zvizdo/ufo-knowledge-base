# University of Ferrara

Italian research university founded in 1391. In the context of this KB, notable as the institutional home of [[filippo-biondi]] and the SISAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) research group responsible for the [[khafre-research-project]] ground-penetrating radar surveys of the Giza plateau.

## Relevance to ufo-kb

The Ferrara radar group applied SISAR technology to the Khafre pyramid complex, claiming to detect a spiral underground channel system beneath [[khafre-pyramid]]. This data underpins Biondi's hypothesis about pyramid function as an acoustic or electromagnetic resonator.
