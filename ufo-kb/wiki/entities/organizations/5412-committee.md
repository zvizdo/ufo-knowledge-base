# 5412 Committee

NSC subcommittee (NSC 5412/2) established under Eisenhower to authorize and oversee covert CIA operations. Named for the classified directive that created it. Predecessor to the 303 Committee (Johnson) and 40 Committee (Nixon).

## Overview

The primary oversight body for CIA covert action during the Eisenhower-Kennedy era. Any major covert operation — including Operation Mongoose (Castro assassination plots) — required 5412 Committee authorization.

## Key People

- Gordon Gray — Eisenhower's national security advisor, chaired the committee
- Allen Dulles — CIA Director, presented operations to the committee
- McGeorge Bundy — Kennedy-era participant
