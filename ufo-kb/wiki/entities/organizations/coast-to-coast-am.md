# Coast to Coast AM

Coast to Coast AM is a late-night North American radio talk show focused on paranormal, UFO/UAP, and fringe science topics. Under [[art-bell]]'s hosting (1988–2003), it became the dominant mass-market platform for UFO disclosure claims — reaching millions of listeners at a time when mainstream media dismissed the subject.

## Role in UAP Discourse

- **Amplifier**: Many UAP witnesses and researchers — including [[bob-lazar]], [[john-lear]], [[whitley-strieber]], and later disclosure-era figures — made their first major public appearances on C2C.
- **Legitimacy signal**: Invitation to C2C was a measure of standing in the UFO community.
- **Contradiction surface**: The show hosted credible witnesses and outright fabricators on equal footing, making it a case study in the [[credibility-frameworks]] problem in UAP research.

## Key Hosts

- [[art-bell]] — founder and defining host; retired multiple times
- George Noory — current primary host (2003–present)
