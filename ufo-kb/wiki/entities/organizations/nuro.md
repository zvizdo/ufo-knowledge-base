# NURO — National Underwater Reconnaissance Office

The **National Underwater Reconnaissance Office** (NURO) is identified by [[uap-gerb]] as the **sixth major US intelligence agency** — alongside NSA, DIA, CIA, NRO, and NGA — and as one of the **legacy-program administrators** in the UAP pyramid structure.

## Key Facts

- Established **1969**
- As of UAPGerb's research, still **not publicly disclosed or acknowledged**
- By analogy with the NRO (which runs imagery/signals intelligence satellites for SIGINT/IMINT), the NURO presumably manages undersea intelligence collection
- Named by UAPGerb specifically because most discussions of "the big five" intelligence agencies omit it

## Significance in UAP Context

In UAPGerb's pyramid model, the program-administrator layer consists of directorates within the six major intelligence agencies. The NURO's non-public status makes it a particularly opaque node — one where a UAP legacy-program directorate could operate with virtually no congressional visibility.

The undersea domain is increasingly relevant to UAP discourse given numerous documented transmedium UAP events (craft entering or exiting water) and the existence of USAPS that may relate to underwater phenomena.
