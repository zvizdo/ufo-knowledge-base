# AFOSI — Air Force Office of Special Investigations

USAF counterintelligence and criminal investigation branch. Salas describes a consistent pattern: after a UFO-nuclear incident, an AFOSI agent appears in the squadron commander's office and administers NDAs to all involved personnel. This pattern is corroborated by other witnesses in Salas's network.

## Overview

After the [[1967-oscar-flight]] incident, an AFOSI agent was already present in Salas's squadron commander's office when Salas and [[fred-maywald]] were called in after being relieved. The agent's sole task was to have them sign non-disclosure agreements. The squadron commander (a WWII B-17 veteran) was described as "white as a sheet" and stated explicitly there had been no test involved.

Salas: "That seems like a recurring pattern — an AFOSI agent always shows up around nuclear UFO incidents and then swears you to secrecy."

## The Benowitz Disinformation Operation (c. 1980–1984)

The most extensively documented AFOSI operation against a civilian UFO researcher. Counter-intelligence agent [[rick-doty]] ran a multi-year disinformation campaign against [[paul-benowitz]], an Albuquerque electronics businessman who had filmed anomalous orbs near [[kirtland-afb]]. Working with CIA handler [[harry-rositzki]] ("Falcon") and UFO-community asset [[bill-moore]], AFOSI escalated Benowitz's beliefs into a paranoid breakdown requiring psychiatric hospitalization.

Key operational elements: delivery of a pre-programmed computer (allegedly via [[j-allen-hynek]]) containing fabricated alien signals; NSA-style signals piped from across the street; fake historical video on a CRT display; physical props (jeeps, water tanks) staged at Archeleta Mesa near [[dulce-new-mexico]] to redirect his attention. The operation substantially generated the [[dulce-underground-base]] mythology. (Greg Bishop, [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])

AFOSI also targeted [[linda-moulton-howe]] in 1982, feeding her fabricated presidential UFO briefing documents and material on "Project Garnett."
