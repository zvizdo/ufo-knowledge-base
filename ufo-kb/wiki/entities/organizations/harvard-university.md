# Harvard University

Harvard University is a private research university in Cambridge, Massachusetts, founded in 1636 — the oldest institution of higher education in the United States. It is consistently ranked among the world's leading universities, with particular strength in law, medicine, business, and the natural and social sciences.

**John Mack**: Professor [[john-mack]] of Harvard Medical School was a Pulitzer Prize-winning psychiatrist who, after years of conventional practice, began investigating UFO abduction experiencers in the early 1990s. His books *Abduction* (1994) and *Passport to the Cosmos* (1999) constituted a serious academic treatment of the experiencer phenomenon. Harvard's administration attempted to investigate Mack for his unorthodox research, but after a formal process, he was exonerated. Mack died in 2004.

**Crane Brinton**: [[crane-brinton]], Harvard historian known for his work on revolutions (*The Anatomy of Revolution*, 1938), gave a 1968 lecture cited in the KB connecting the consciousness revolution of the 1960s to broader historical patterns of paradigm change.

**Epstein penetration**: The Epstein network's documented penetration of Harvard — through donations to Harvard Law, connections to the Department of Economics (Larry Summers), and the Epstein-funded Program for Evolutionary Dynamics — is cited in KB contexts discussing the Epstein network's broader infiltration of academic and scientific institutions.

**UAP-adjacent research**: Harvard has also been the institutional home for the Galileo Project (Avi Loeb), which attempts scientific detection of extraterrestrial artifacts in near-Earth space, and has hosted various anomalous cognition research programs over the years.
