# Capella Space

San Francisco-based commercial satellite company founded in 2016; operates a constellation of synthetic-aperture radar (SAR) imaging satellites capable of high-resolution Earth observation in all weather conditions, day or night. SAR satellites emit microwave-frequency radar pulses and measure the reflected signal, producing imagery that is independent of cloud cover and optical lighting — capabilities that distinguish SAR from optical satellite photography.

**SAR capabilities and limitations**: Synthetic-aperture radar is powerful for surface and near-subsurface analysis. SAR at certain wavelengths (particularly L-band and P-band) can penetrate shallow dry material — sand, dry soil, thin rock — revealing subsurface voids, structures, or geological features. However, the interpretation of SAR data for specific subsurface claims (e.g., "void beneath Pyramid X") requires careful modeling of dielectric properties of the subsurface material, signal processing assumptions, and independent validation. Capella's commercial X-band SAR has higher resolution but lower subsurface penetration than longer-wavelength systems.

**Archaeological research applications**: In the KB, Capella's SAR data is invoked by [[filippo-biondi]] and adjacent researchers as a potential source for subsurface analysis of the [[great-pyramid]] and other anomalous archaeological structures (the [[khafre-research-project]]). Biondi has made claims about subsurface voids detected via SAR analysis. These claims are contested: critics argue that the SAR data interpretation requires assumptions that aren't warranted by the data resolution and that independent verification is necessary.

**Dual-use context**: Commercial SAR satellites like Capella's operate in the same technical domain as classified government reconnaissance satellites — the distinction being resolution and access, not fundamental capability. Capella's public data releases have been used in conflict monitoring (Ukraine, Gaza), demonstrating that commercial SAR provides actionable intelligence-grade imagery.
