# Office of Global Access (OGA)

The **Office of Global Access** is a CIA program/directorate focused on gaining access to denied areas and materials. Its first director was [[doug-wolf]], who brought 16 years of NRO experience and senior ODNI-level authority to the role.

## Significance in UAP Context

In [[uap-gerb]]'s pyramid model, CIA DS&T is one of the six intelligence-agency directorates at the program-administrator layer. The OGA — as a specifically access-oriented CIA office — is a natural candidate for involvement in crash-retrieval operations: acquiring access to downed craft, recovery sites, or foreign programs. Wolf's NRO background (satellites/overhead intelligence) alongside his OGA founding role suggests the office may bridge collection and retrieval functions.
