# Tridactyls.org

Tridactyls.org is a public online database maintained by the [[enari-institute]] (led by [[thierry-jamin]]) providing open access to CT scan DICOM files, research reports, and documentation on the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]].

## Significance

The public availability of DICOM files is significant because it enables independent researchers worldwide to examine the raw imaging data without relying on ENARI's interpretive reports. Independent analysts — including the bioinformatics expert [[elena-hardy]], who identified the GLI3 digit-development mutation in NCBI data — have used this open access to contribute findings that ENARI itself did not originate.

The open-data approach also makes fabrication harder to sustain: if the CT scans showed obvious manipulation or inconsistency with claimed morphology, independent radiologists would have flagged it.
