# University of Ika

Peruvian university that has taken custody of and is actively studying the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]]. Located in the Ica region of Peru, close to the Nazca discovery site.

## Overview

The university's involvement represents the most institutionally legitimate ongoing scientific investigation of the mummies within Peru. Biologist [[roger-zuniga]] studies the bodies there. The [[peruvian-ministry-of-culture]] has made at least 7 documented attempts to seize the mummies from the university, each time bringing police and prosecutors.

## Key People

- [[martin-alcron]] — vice president; documents government seizure attempts
- [[roger-zuniga]] — biologist studying the bodies

## Programs / Activities

- Scientific study and custody of the M-type and other mummy bodies
- Resistance to Ministry of Culture seizure attempts
- Tacitly endorsed by the Peruvian government's September 2024 shift in position
