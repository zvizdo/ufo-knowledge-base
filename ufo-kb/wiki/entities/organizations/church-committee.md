# Church Committee

The Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities (1975–76), commonly known as the Church Committee after its chairman, Senator Frank Church (D-ID). The most comprehensive investigation of US intelligence community abuses in American history.

**What it revealed**: The Church Committee produced 14 reports documenting:
- **COINTELPRO**: FBI's domestic counterintelligence program targeting civil rights leaders, socialist organizations, and political dissidents through infiltration, harassment, and psychological operations
- **[[mk-ultra]]**: CIA's mind-control research program involving LSD administration, hypnosis, and psychological torture of unwitting subjects, including the death of Army biochemist Frank Olson
- **Assassination plots**: CIA plots against foreign leaders including Fidel Castro, Patrice Lumumba, and others, using organized crime intermediaries
- **Mass surveillance**: NSA's warrantless interception of international communications (Operation SHAMROCK and MINARET)
- **FBI surveillance**: Wiretapping of Martin Luther King Jr., Eleanor Roosevelt, and thousands of others

**Frank Olson connection**: The Committee's investigation of MK-Ultra surfaced the 1953 death of [[frank-olson]], an Army biochemist who died after being covertly dosed with LSD in a CIA experiment. His son [[eric-olson]] used the Church Committee revelations as the starting point for his decades-long investigation into his father's death.

**William Barr connection**: [[william-barr]] interned at the CIA during the period of the Church Committee investigations — a formative experience in his career during a time of maximum institutional stress on US intelligence agencies.

**Legacy**: The Church Committee led directly to the creation of the Senate and House Intelligence Committees (permanent oversight), FISA courts (judicial oversight of surveillance), and Executive Order 11905 (prohibition on assassination). It established the template for [[uap-disclosure-act]]-style investigations: a special committee with subpoena power, access to classified programs, and mandate to produce public reports.
