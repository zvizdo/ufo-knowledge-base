# American Alchemy

Independent podcast hosted by [[jesse-michaels]], covering UAP/UFO disclosure, consciousness research, parapsychology, ancient mysteries, and adjacent topics. **This podcast is the primary seed corpus for this knowledge base** — the bulk of the wiki's entity and concept pages were created from American Alchemy episode transcripts.

## Format and Style

Long-form interview format; typically 1.5–3 hours per episode. Michaels brings a skeptic's instinct but genuine openness — he pushes guests on weak evidence while engaging seriously with the phenomenon. The show's co-host roster has included [[chris-ramsey]] (of the [[area-52]] podcast), particularly for live tour events.

## Thematic Focus Areas

- **UAP disclosure**: Whistleblowers, government programs (AATIP, AAWSAP, Stargate), congressional developments
- **Consciousness research**: NDE, remote viewing, telepathy tapes, autistic savant phenomena
- **Historical UFO events**: Roswell, Nimitz, nuclear-UFO nexus
- **Adjacent politics**: Deep state, CIA-mob, JFK, Epstein connections to science and secrecy
