# General Dynamics

General Dynamics Corporation is one of the largest US defense contractors, producing a wide range of military systems including nuclear-powered submarines (Virginia-class, Columbia-class), armored vehicles (M1 Abrams tank), combat aircraft (historically F-16, F-111), the Gulfstream line of executive jets, and advanced electronics and information technology systems.

**Major systems**: 
- **Electric Boat division**: Designs and builds US Navy nuclear submarines, including the Virginia-class attack submarines and Columbia-class ballistic missile submarines
- **Land Systems division**: Produces the M1 Abrams main battle tank
- **Information Technology**: IT and services contracts for the US government, including classified programs
- **Gulfstream Aerospace**: Private and government executive jet aircraft

**UAP relevance**: General Dynamics appears in the KB primarily as one of the [[military-industrial-complex]] contractors that operates classified defense programs. Along with Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, Raytheon, and Boeing, GD is one of the "Big Five" defense contractors whose classified programs and SAP work are cited in UAP reverse-engineering claims as potential repositories of anomalous technology.

**Aerojet connection**: The [[aerojet-rocketdyne]] entry in the KB references General Dynamics in the context of propulsion system development — GD historically had its own rocket and missile propulsion programs before the industry consolidated.
