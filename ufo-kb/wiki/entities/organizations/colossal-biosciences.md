# Colossal Biosciences

US biotechnology company specializing in de-extinction through ancient DNA analysis. Advised by George Church of Harvard, described as "maybe the top geneticist in the world." Achieved global recognition for de-extincting the direwolf.

## Overview

Jesse Michaels approached [[ben-lamb]] at Colossal to arrange ancient DNA testing of the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]]. The company agreed, but the tests require Peruvian government permission to take samples from Peru to the US.

Jesse frames this as a "forcing function": if the [[peruvian-ministry-of-culture]] refuses the world's leading ancient DNA firm permission to test the mummies, that refusal itself becomes evidence of suppressive intent.
