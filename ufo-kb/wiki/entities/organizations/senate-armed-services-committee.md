# Senate Armed Services Committee

## Overview

The Senate Armed Services Committee (SASC) is one of the most powerful committees in the US Senate, with jurisdiction over defense policy, the Department of Defense budget, military operations, and intelligence programs insofar as they involve defense appropriations. In the UAP context, SASC is the institutional home of [[kirk-mcconnell]], a senior staff member whose work on UAP oversight and concerns about AARO's independence have made him a significant figure in the congressional UAP oversight landscape.

## Key People

- [[kirk-mcconnell]] — senior SASC staff member; raised concerns about [[sean-kirkpatrick]]'s post-AARO employment at [[oak-ridge-national-laboratory]] as a potential conflict of interest
- [[frank-church]] — historical figure; chaired the Church Committee (Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations), whose 1975–76 investigations of CIA abuses provide institutional precedent for current UAP oversight debates

## Programs / Activities

- Defense authorization processes that include UAP-related legislative riders and requirements
- Oversight of AARO and DoD UAP programs
- Institutional context for the [[schumer-uap-amendment]] negotiations
