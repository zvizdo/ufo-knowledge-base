# JASON Advisory Group

An independent group of elite US scientists — primarily physicists, chemists, and mathematicians — who provide classified analysis and recommendations to the US Department of Defense and other agencies on defense-related science and technology questions. Founded in 1960, JASON meets annually at classified facilities and produces classified reports.

Historically contracted through the Institute for Defense Analysis (IDA) and later the MITRE Corporation. Members are cleared to the highest levels and work on problems ranging from nuclear weapons design and submarine warfare to advanced propulsion concepts.
