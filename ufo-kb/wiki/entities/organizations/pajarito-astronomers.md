# Pajarito Astronomers

> *Stub. Expand with full club history, current status, and additional UFO/UAP-related programming as it enters the KB.*

Los Alamos-area amateur astronomy club. P.O. Box 1092, Los Alamos NM 87544. Sponsored by **Club 1663**, the historic Los Alamos employee social organization named after the Manhattan Project's wartime postal address (PO Box 1663, Santa Fe). The club's officer-mail-stop conventions (President MS K553, Vice President MS E531, Treasurer MS F665) confirm that 1986-era leadership was drawn from active [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|LANL]] technical staff. The name "Pajarito" refers to the Pajarito Plateau on which LANL and Los Alamos townsite sit.

## UAP/UFO-Related Programming

Per [[doe-uap-d3-pajarito-astronomers-1986]] (1986 meeting announcement released by DOE 2026), the club scheduled a 29 May 1986 lecture in the Ranch Room at Fuller Lodge by Dr. John Warren (LANL AT-6 / Accelerator Technology Division Group 6) titled "Why Should a Scientist be Concerned about UFO's?" The talk's advocacy-posture title and the club's institutional placement (LANL staff, sponsored by Club 1663) document that UFO topics had standing as legitimate astronomy-club material at Los Alamos in 1986.
