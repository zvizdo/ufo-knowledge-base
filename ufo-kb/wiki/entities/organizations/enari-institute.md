# Enari Institute

Organization founded and led by explorer [[thierry-jamin]]. Since 2016, has been the primary institutional organizer of scientific research on the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]]. Arranged CT scans, invited international forensic and genetics experts, published DICOM files publicly via [[tridactyls-org]].

## Overview

Operates at the intersection of archaeological exploration and UAP research. Jamin's exploration mandate (seeking Incan lost cities, El Dorado) provided both the network and credibility to organize an international research effort on the mummies. The Enari Institute functions as the institutional umbrella for what is otherwise a diffuse, informal research network.

- Systematic CT scanning and DICOM publication of mummy bodies

- Publication of research findings and public database (tridactyls.org)
