# Neutron Star Technologies

Neutron Star Technologies (also Neutron Star Nano Technologies) is a materials science and analytical chemistry company founded by [[steve-coburn]], a materials scientist and personal UAP experiencer who collaborated with [[roger-leir]] on the analysis of alleged alien implants removed from abduction experiencers.

**Analytical capabilities**: Coburn's company specializes in isotopic spectroscopy and electron microscopy techniques capable of detecting anomalous isotopic ratios and nanoscale structures in materials. Applied to the alleged implants, these methods found: isotopic ratios deviating up to 30% from terrestrial norms, meteoric iron as a base material, and carbon nanotube structures — findings Coburn argues are inconsistent with known terrestrial manufacturing processes.

**Personal experiencer context**: Coburn's professional analysis work is intertwined with his personal experience: he had a toe implant removed that showed the anomalous isotopic ratios he later documented in other implants. He also reports two skull implants (brain/frontal lobe area, above ears) that emitted radio signals during a Japanese TV taping.

**Roger Leir collaboration**: Leir, a podiatric surgeon who performed surgical removal of alleged alien implants from experiencers, collaborated with Coburn on laboratory analysis of removed objects. Coburn's NST conducted approximately 400 implant scans on experiencers, with roughly half producing anomalous results.
