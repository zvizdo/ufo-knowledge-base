# Johns Hopkins University

Major research and medical university in Baltimore, Maryland. Founded 1876. Known for its medical school, Bloomberg School of Public Health, and Applied Physics Laboratory (Johns Hopkins APL — a major defense research FFRDC).

**KB connection**: [[detlev-bronk]] served as president of Johns Hopkins and is cited in KB sources as a member of the alleged [[majestic-12]] committee — the purported executive body overseeing US UAP programs in the late 1940s and 1950s. If accurate, this would place the president of a major medical research institution within the inner circle of UAP oversight, connecting NHI biology research to the university's medical infrastructure. The claim rests on disputed MJ-12 documents.

**Applied Physics Laboratory (APL)**: Johns Hopkins APL is a major DOD FFRDC (Federally Funded Research and Development Center) with deep classified research relationships — including work on naval weapons systems, missile defense, and space surveillance. APL's relationship with classified defense programs is part of the KB's broader FFRDC-UAP-architecture context (→ [[ffrdc-uap-architecture]]).
