# APRO (Aerial Phenomena Research Organization)

APRO was an American civilian UFO investigation organization founded in 1952 by **James and Coral Lorenzen** in Alamosa, Colorado (later headquartered in Tucson, Arizona). It was one of the two major civilian UFO research organizations of the Cold War era, the other being [[nicap]].

## Founding and Mission

James and Coral Lorenzen established APRO to systematically collect and analyze UFO reports from civilian and military witnesses. The organization maintained a network of scientific consultants and field investigators. It operated until Coral Lorenzen's death in 1988.

## Dolan's Assessment

[[richard-dolan]] names the Lorenzens alongside [[donald-keyhoe]] as his primary institutional inspirations when he entered the field:

> "James and Coral Lorenzen — I'm a huge admirer of theirs."

Dolan credits APRO's methodical case collection and refusal to sensationalize as models for serious civilian UFO research. He went through their books carefully early in his career.

## Paul Benowitz and the Disinformation Period

[[paul-benowitz]] was an APRO member, having applied and been accepted as a technical contributor (citing his electrical engineering expertise). Greg Bishop has seen his membership application. [[bill-moore]] was also on APRO's board during this period — both were APRO-connected when the AFOSI disinformation operation targeting Benowitz began. The operation exploited Benowitz's APRO membership as one channel for tracking who he was in contact with. (Greg Bishop, [[WBTqsbwJyqU]])

APRO's founders (Jim and Coral Lorenzen) split off from the Midwest UFO Network — the precursor to [[mufon]].
