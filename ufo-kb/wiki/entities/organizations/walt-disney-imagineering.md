# Walt Disney Imagineering

Walt Disney Imagineering (WDI) is the creative engineering and development arm of the Walt Disney Company, responsible for designing, building, and operating Disney theme parks and attractions worldwide. It employs engineers, artists, and technologists to create large-scale immersive environments.

**Eric Haseltine's tenure**: [[eric-haseltine]] — who later served as Director of Research at the NSA and then CTO of the US intelligence community (ODNI) — served as Executive Vice President at Walt Disney Imagineering before his intelligence career. This trajectory — Imagineering → NSA → ODNI — is the most unusual intelligence community career path in the KB and raises questions about the relationship between entertainment technology and classified technology development.

**Plasma voxel system**: At Imagineering, Haseltine developed a Q-switched Nd:YAG laser system capable of ionizing free air to create plasma voxels — small balls of plasma created in mid-air without any physical substrate. CEO Michael Eisner refused to commercialize the system. Haseltine has stated publicly that this plasma technology creates massless objects with "impossible" kinematics if mistaken for aircraft — directly relevant to the analysis of UAP orb phenomena. The existence of such technology at a *civilian entertainment company* implies it may also exist in classified military applications.

**Broader significance**: WDI's role in the KB is primarily through Haseltine's plasma research and career trajectory. The organization demonstrates that advanced photonic/plasma technology with direct UAP-mimicry potential was being developed outside of classified military channels in the 1990s.
