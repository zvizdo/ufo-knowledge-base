# Liberation Times

UK-based investigative journalism outlet specializing in UAP/UFO disclosure politics, founded and run by [[chris-sharp]]. Distinct from American outlets in its British editorial perspective while covering predominantly US government UAP secrecy.

## Key Reporting

[[michael-shellenberger]] cited a Liberation Times article by Chris Sharp covering the **history of UFO crash retrieval programs** — specifically the role of [[glenn-gaffney]] as a CIA gatekeeper who allegedly blocked the transfer of a craft held by Lockheed to [[aatip]]-adjacent research (reportedly to Robert Bigelow's company). This is the most detailed public account of the CIA's structural role in blocking crash retrieval program consolidation.
