# MUFON (Mutual UFO Network)

MUFON is the largest civilian UFO investigation organization in the United States, founded in 1969. It maintains a global network of field investigators, a standardized sighting report database, and publishes the *MUFON UFO Journal*. By volume of documented cases, it holds the most comprehensive public record of UFO/UAP sighting reports.

## Role in UAP Discourse

- **Database**: Tens of thousands of sighting reports filed by witnesses across the US and internationally.
- **Investigator network**: Trained field investigators conduct local interviews and physical evidence collection.
- **Credibility debates**: MUFON's open membership and inconsistent investigative standards have drawn criticism; its reports vary widely in rigor.

## Notable Connections

- [[john-lear]] was actively involved with MUFON prior to and during the [[bob-lazar]] revelations — a period when Las Vegas's UFO community was particularly active.
- MUFON has intersected with government programs: some researchers allege intelligence community monitoring of MUFON's caseload.
