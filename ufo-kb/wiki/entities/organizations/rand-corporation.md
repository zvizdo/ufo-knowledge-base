# RAND Corporation

## Overview

The RAND Corporation grew out of Project RAND, established by the Air Force in 1948 to perform long-range research for the military. It quickly became the premier defense-policy think tank in the US, staffed with physicists, economists, and game theorists — many of whom had worked on the Manhattan Project and in WWII defense research.

## UAP Connection

RAND produced early classified analyses of UFO reports and the strategic implications of the phenomenon. Its role as a bridge between the military-intelligence world and academic researchers placed it in the institutional network where UAP policy analysis would have occurred.

In [[09KP8XVf5nY]], RAND is referenced as part of the broader Cold War research complex — the institutional network Malmgren inhabited — within which UAP knowledge was managed alongside nuclear weapons strategy. The cross-pollination between RAND analysts and figures like [[hal-puthoff]] (later at [[stargate-program]]) reflects RAND's role as a talent incubator for intelligence-adjacent research.
