# The Black Vault

The largest private FOIA (Freedom of Information Act) archive in the United States, founded by [[john-greenwald]] in 1996. The site hosts hundreds of thousands of declassified and FOIA-obtained documents spanning UFO/UAP incidents, intelligence programs, MK-Ultra, JFK files, and related topics. It is the primary institutional home for document-based civilian UAP research.

## Key Activities

- Systematic submission of FOIA requests to DoD, CIA, NSA, NORAD, and other agencies
- Public archive of received documents with search functionality
- Tracking of FOIA denial patterns and exemption types — source of Greenwald's finding that UAP secrecy is growing
- Documented Pentagon inconsistency on UAP video releases (see [[john-greenwald]])

## Significance

[[michael-shellenberger]] describes The Black Vault as underappreciated in UAP discourse because Greenwald is "neither fish nor fowl" — his FOIA-based findings are not aligned with either the NHI-advocate or skeptic camps. The organization provides the clearest **official-channel evidence** for the proposition that secrecy around UAP programs is expanding, not contracting. See [[disclosure-narratives]] and [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]].
