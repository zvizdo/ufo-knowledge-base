# Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

The Air Force Research Laboratory at [[wright-patterson]] Air Force Base, Ohio, is one of the largest defense science and technology research organizations in the US military. Its $2.2B portfolio (as of 2011–2013) spans advanced materials science, directed energy weapons, space systems, and exotic propulsion technologies.

## UAP Relevance

In UFO discourse, AFRL occupies a structurally significant position:
- [[wright-patterson]] is the most commonly cited destination for recovered UAP hardware (the alleged Roswell crash debris; the 1933 Magenta craft)
- AFRL's materials science directorate is the institutional context in which technologies derived from recovered exotic materials would most naturally be developed
- The [[mandeloy-alloy]] — a superalloy developed under AFRL contracts — sits in a lineage of nickel-based alloys with roots in the same Wright-Patterson research ecosystem as the pre-Roswell Batelle nickel-titanium studies
- [[neil-mccasland]] commanded AFRL 2011–2013 and was subsequently named in Tom DeLonge's emails as a central insider aware of UAP programs
