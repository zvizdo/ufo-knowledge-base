# RIAS — Research Institute for Advanced Study

The Research Institute for Advanced Study (RIAS) was [[martin-corporation]]'s internal anti-gravity research division, headed by corporate VP [[george-trimble]]. Its existence within a major aerospace contractor — dedicated to anti-gravity — is one of the clearest institutional data points for the [[anti-gravity-manhattan-project]] claims-thesis.

## Personnel and Activities

- **[[george-trimble]]** (VP, director): Publicly predicted beating gravity "in about the time it took to build the first atom bomb"
- **[[lewis-whitten]]** (physicist): RIAS's representative at the 1957 UNC Chapel Hill gravity conference; reported [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s bismuth anti-gravity results; was contracted by Wright Air Field to work on gravity
- **[[bryce-dewitt]]**: Nearly joined RIAS before choosing an academic path

## The 1957 UNC Conference

RIAS sent Whitten to represent Martin Corporation at the gravity research conference at [[unc-chapel-hill]] organized by [[agnew-bahnson]] and sponsored by Wright Air Development Center (see [[wright-patterson]]). This placed RIAS in direct contact with [[richard-feynman]], [[john-wheeler]], [[peter-bergmann]], and [[bryce-dewitt]].
