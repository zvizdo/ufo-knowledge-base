# Army Futures Command

US Army major command established in August 2018, headquartered in Austin, Texas. Mission: modernize the Army by accelerating development and adoption of new technologies and warfighting concepts. First commanding general: General John "Mike" Murray.

**In the KB**: [[carl-nell]] is the primary connection. Nell established or significantly built Army Futures Command in his capacity as a senior Army leader, and is described in KB sources as using its mandate and culture to create space for unconventional science discussions — including UAP. The command's physical location in Austin (outside the DC beltway, near the technology industry) was deliberate: to embed military modernization in the technology ecosystem.

**Significance for UAP context**: Army Futures Command represents one of the institutional footholds where UAP-relevant physics discussions could be framed as legitimate military technology research rather than fringe speculation. Nell's subsequent transition to UAP-adjacent research work is partly explained by the institutional legitimation he built at AFC for blue-sky technical thinking. AFC's funding mandate also covers cross-domain technologies — artificial intelligence, hypersonics, directed energy — that overlap with UAP phenomenology.

**Cross-domain mandate**: AFC's eight "cross-functional teams" (CFTs) cover: next-gen combat vehicle, long-range precision fires, future vertical lift, network, air and missile defense, soldier lethality, assured positioning/navigation/timing, and synthetic training environment. The mandate for emerging technology across all domains creates institutional permission for discussions of exotic propulsion and UAP-adjacent physics.
