# EarthTech International

Private research institute based in Austin, Texas, co-founded by [[hal-puthoff]]. Operates under the banner of the **Institute for Advanced Studies at Austin (IASA)**. EarthTech has been a key institutional home for publicly available exotic-propulsion physics research — the kind of work that bridges classified and open scientific inquiry.

## Research Output

EarthTech produced a body of peer-reviewed and technical papers covering:
- Metric engineering and Alcubierre-type warp drives (Puthoff, Davis)
- Traversable wormholes and faster-than-light travel
- Vacuum zero-point energy / quantum vacuum fluctuations
- Polarizable vacuum models of gravity (Puthoff's Physical Review paper — praised by [[salvatore-pais]])

These papers are frequently cited by cleared UAP researchers and have circulated within classified programs as theoretical foundations for understanding what NHI propulsion might employ.

## Role in UAP Disclosure Chain

[[eric-davis]] was based at EarthTech/IASA during his AAWSAP/ASAP period. The institute's classified-adjacent work made it a natural contractor/consultant for DIA-sponsored programs. Davis's AAWSAP-era research, the data he accumulated through NIDS and BASS, and his CIA interactions all ran through this institutional affiliation.

[[dave-rossi]] (self-taught physicist) cites EarthTech papers as his primary mentorship resource — "Puthoff's work as mentor guide."

## Connection to BASS

**BASS** (Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies) was the primary AAWSAP contractor. EarthTech/IASA served in a research capacity within the BASS ecosystem. Davis notes that BASS was trying to get access to Lockheed Martin's crash retrieval materials through a classified channel under DIA — a request that [[glenn-gaffney]] blocked.
