# Penn State University

Pennsylvania State University (Penn State) is a major public research university headquartered in University Park, Pennsylvania. It is one of the largest US universities with a significant research and graduate programs.

**Greg Eghigian**: [[greg-eghigian]] is a history professor at Penn State who wrote an academic history of UFOs — one of the few mainstream academic historians to treat the subject as a serious historical topic worthy of archival primary-source research. He spent weeks at the [[archives-for-the-unexplained]] (AFU) in Sweden and has appeared on television as a UAP expert. [[claus-svahn]] endorses him as a rigorous researcher.

**Eric Walker**: The more significant UAP connection is historical. [[eric-walker]] served as Penn State's president from 1956 to 1970. Walker is alleged — based on documented statements he made in his later years — to have been a member of the [[majestic-12]] committee, having worked on classified UAP programs at a high level during his earlier academic and defense roles. If Walker's claims are accurate, Penn State was, during his presidency, led by one of the most senior figures in the classified UAP program structure.

**Significance**: The co-presence of Eghigian (mainstream academic UAP historian) and Walker (alleged MJ-12 insider) at the same institution is an incidental but notable overlap. No direct connection between their work is documented.
