# State of the World Forum

Annual gathering co-founded by [[mikhail-gorbachev]], [[james-garrison]], and [[danny-sheehan]] to address global civilizational challenges. Associated with the [[gorbachev-foundation]]. Held in San Francisco and other locations through the 1990s and 2000s.

The Forum brought together world leaders, scientists, philosophers, and activists to work on problems — nuclear weapons, environmental collapse, economic inequality — that individual nation-states could not address unilaterally. Its founding vision was that the end of the Cold War had opened a window for genuine civilizational transformation that the 1992 Cheney full-spectrum dominance doctrine effectively closed.

For Sheehan, the State of the World Forum represents the road not taken: a post-Soviet world that could have oriented toward cooperative governance rather than permanent American military supremacy. UAP disclosure fits into this framework as another instance of the same choice — whether civilizational transformation or power consolidation wins determines whether the human relationship with NHI is managed cooperatively or covertly.
