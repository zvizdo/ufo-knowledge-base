# FFRDC (Federally Funded Research and Development Centers)

**FFRDCs** are research organizations that receive the majority of their funding from the US federal government but operate outside normal government agencies. They provide independent analysis, R&D, and systems engineering to government sponsors while maintaining contractor status. Key examples relevant to UAP discourse include:

- [[mitre-corporation]] — national security FFRDC; frequently cited in UAP program context
- RAND Corporation
- Aerospace Corporation
- MIT Lincoln Laboratory

## UAP Relevance

FFRDCs occupy a structural ambiguity: they do classified government work but are not subject to congressional personnel reporting requirements in the same way agencies are. This makes them potential nodes in the [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] architecture — able to hold programs and personnel at arm's length from direct oversight.
