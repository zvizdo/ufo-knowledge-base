# Federated Wireless

US wireless technology company founded in 2012 and headquartered in Arlington, Virginia; specializes in Citizens Broadband Radio Service (CBRS) spectrum sharing and spectrum access systems (SAS). CBRS (3.5 GHz band) enables shared use of spectrum previously reserved for DoD naval radar systems — a spectrum-coordination challenge that requires real-time radio frequency management across civilian and military users.

**Bob Maguire connection**: [[bob-maguire]] is referenced in connection with Federated Wireless in the American Alchemy network. Maguire has an intelligence-community career background and has discussed UAP-adjacent communications research. His connection to a spectrum-access company operating in a band previously reserved for naval radar is noted in the KB as part of the broader pattern of intelligence-community and DoD-adjacent personnel embedded in commercial communications technology companies.

**Spectrum sharing and UAP**: Spectrum management is technically adjacent to UAP detection and communication: radar, RF anomalies, and sensor network design are tools used in both commercial spectrum operations and UAP monitoring programs. A specialist in spectrum access systems — particularly in bands shared between DoD and civilian users — has institutional knowledge of both the technical and classification infrastructure relevant to signals-based UAP detection.

**Defense-commercial interface**: Federated Wireless's core business (managing spectrum access between DoD naval radar and civilian broadband users) places it at the intersection of defense and commercial communications infrastructure — the same organizational niche as companies like [[mitre-corporation]] and the spectrum-management offices within the National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA).
