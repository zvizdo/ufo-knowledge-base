# Archives for the Unexplained (AFU)

Sweden-based research archive widely regarded as the **world's largest UFO archive**. Founded in 1973 by [[claus-svahn]] and colleagues. The AFU occupies a **16-room building** and holds:

- 22,000+ formal UFO case files from Sweden
- Original first-generation documents that no longer exist anywhere else
- Witness reports, photographs, audio recordings, radar data, and video footage
- Files from multiple countries including Japan and Russia
- Material related to the [[borderland-science-research-association]] (oldest UFO group in the world)
- A dedicated **time travel** research section
- Material on [[thomas-townsend-brown]]

## Digitization

Active digitization program running, but single-person operation ([[leif-ostrand]] — the "sole digitizer under an avalanche"). AFU is seeking funding to scale this effort.

## Field Research

AFU has not only been a passive archive but has conducted active field research, including:
- Participating in 1980s [[hessdalen]] expeditions (Norway) — capturing photos and a radar return
- Door-to-door surveys of 1,600 Swedes finding 10% had anomalous sightings
- Physical sample analysis (e.g. [[1957-vaddo-island-incident]] tungsten piece)
- 2005 northern Sweden radar case (witnessed personally by Svahn)
