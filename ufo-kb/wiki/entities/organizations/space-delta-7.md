# Space Delta 7

**Space Delta 7** is the **intelligence wing** of the **[[space-force]]** (United States Space Force). Its emblem is notable in the context of the [[occult-aerospace-connection]] and [[big-dipper-nhi-nexus]] patterns:

## Emblem Symbolism

The Space Delta 7 emblem shows:
- **A bear holding a key**
- Set against the **Big Dipper** (Ursa Major)

This places Space Delta 7's visual identity directly within the constellation of symbolism that [[jesse-michaels]] argues spans:
- Ancient Egyptian dipper-shaped ritual tools used to send pharaohs into the afterlife
- Daoist sages tracing dipper stars in ascension rituals
- Ptolemy's claim that souls ascend through Orion's belt
- The **[[thule-society]]**'s identification of circumpolar stars as the origin of NHI progenitors
- [[chris-bledsoe]]'s contactee NHI (the "Lady") being linked to the Big Dipper; her companions calling themselves the **"Guardians"**
- Space Force's own term: **Guardians** (the name for all Space Force personnel)

The convergence of "Guardians" as the Space Force term and [[chris-bledsoe]]'s NHI self-identification is a striking nomenclature alignment, whether intentional or coincidental.
