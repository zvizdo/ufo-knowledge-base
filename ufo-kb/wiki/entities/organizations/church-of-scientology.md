# Church of Scientology

Religious organization founded in 1954 by [[l-ron-hubbard]], growing out of his earlier Dianetics movement. Appears in this KB at the intersection of remote viewing and intelligence programs.

## Scientology-Stargate Connection

Three of the most significant figures in the SRI [[stargate-program]] remote viewing program had documented Scientology connections:

- **[[ingo-swann]]** — the psychic who developed Coordinate Remote Viewing (CRV); was a practicing Scientologist. His Scientology OT levels were cited in some CIA assessments as potentially relevant to his demonstrated abilities.
- **[[pat-price]]** — a former Burbank police commissioner who became the most operationally useful SRI viewer; also a Scientologist.
- **[[hal-puthoff]]** — co-director of the SRI program with [[russell-targ]]; was an OT VII Scientologist at the time of founding the program (later departed the organization).

This concentration of Scientologists in a CIA/DIA-funded psi research program is either coincidence (Scientology's early OT training focused on perceptual exercises that may have selected for individuals with the trait being measured) or suggests a deliberate recruitment channel.

## Hubbard and Alien Contact

L. Ron Hubbard's later writings (Scientology's OT III materials) describe an elaborate cosmology involving an alien galactic confederacy, mass murder of thetans (souls) by the overlord Xenu 75 million years ago, and implanting of false memories. These materials — treated as secret doctrine in the organization — sit adjacent to but distinct from the broader UFO/NHI discourse. Some researchers note parallels with contactee traditions of the same era.

## Intelligence Connections

Multiple published accounts (including the Church's own critics) allege intelligence agency connections to Hubbard and early Scientology. These remain largely speculative in terms of primary documentation.
