# Ordo Templi Orientis (OTO)

The **Ordo Templi Orientis** is an esoteric order founded in the late 19th century and dramatically reshaped by **Aleister Crowley** in the early 20th century under his **Thelema** system. Thelema combines ceremonial magic, Eastern mysticism, and **tantric sex magic** (which Crowley spelled "magick" to distinguish from stage performance) into a unified practice of willful self-transformation and contact with higher intelligences.

## Jack Parsons and the Agape Lodge

The OTO's direct relevance to the UFO/UAP knowledge base is through **[[jack-parsons]]**, who:
- Led the **Agape Lodge** (Pasadena chapter) of the OTO in the 1940s
- Lived in a Pasadena mansion with a rotating cast of artists, intellectuals, and magicians
- Conducted elaborate invocations of gods, spirits, and forces described as residing in higher dimensions
- Performed the **[[1946-babalon-working]]** — the most infamous of his workings — with [[l-ron-hubbard]] as his ritual partner
- Simultaneously co-founded JPL and made fundamental advances in solid-fuel rocket chemistry

This overlap makes the OTO the organizational nexus for the [[occult-aerospace-connection]] — the most documented case of a major aerospace figure operating simultaneously as an occult order leader.

## Babalon Working and UAP Wave

Parsons himself believed the [[1946-babalon-working]] had "opened something." In the years immediately following 1946, reports of unidentified aerial phenomena skyrocketed. Whether this is coincidence, confirmation bias, or causation is an open question.
