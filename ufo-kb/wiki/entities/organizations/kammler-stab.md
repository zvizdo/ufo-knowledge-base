# Kammler-Stab

Secret Nazi SS weapons facility located in Czechoslovakia (in modern-day Poland), operated under SS General Hans Kammler. Among the most secretive of the Nazi advanced weapons programs, Kammler-Stab worked on unconventional propulsion and anti-gravity concepts alongside conventional weapons development.

## Key Researchers (transferred to US via Paperclip)

- **[[victor-schauberger]]** — designed the "impeller" flying saucer based on vortex propulsion; Cook visited family diary confirming Kammler-Stab locations
- **[[richard-miethe]]** — conducted high-voltage experiments parallel to Thomas Townsend Brown's electrogravitics
- **[[henry-coanda]]** — lenticular aerodyne design (1936 patent); later consulted on the Avrocar at Wright-Patterson
- **[[rudolph-schriever]]** — German technician; designed disc-shaped craft in Prague; first test flight February 1945 (claimed 40,000 ft in 3 min)

## Transfer to Wright-Patterson

Following Germany's defeat in World War II, these researchers were brought to the United States under [[operation-paperclip]], with [[wright-patterson]] as a primary receiving base. [[jesse-michaels]] cites the Kammler-Stab tech transfer as one of three explanations for why WADC developed such intense interest in anti-gravity research in the late 1940s and 1950s.

## SCOD Works / Škoda Works (Jorjani Naming, ABw7EsWh2Us)

[[jason-reza-jorjani]] in [[ABw7EsWh2Us]] refers to this same Czechoslovakian SS facility as **"SCOD Works"** — likely a phonetic transcription artifact for **Škoda Works** (the Czech arms manufacturer headquartered in Pilsen, kept under Nazi control during WWII). Jorjani locates it "just outside Prague" under SS General Hans Kammler — geographically and command-structure consistent with Kammler-Stab, justifying treating it as the same entity under different speaker conventions.

Jorjani adds a narrative layer not present in the Cook/Michaels framing:

- **Joint SS–Italian working group**: Italian scientists and SS scientists studied the [[1933-magenta-crash]] craft together at this facility under [[vatican]] oversight.
- **Neutral-third-country location rationale**: Jorjani says the choice of Czechoslovakia was deliberate — "they wanted to respect the Italians by putting this project in a third country that was neither Germany nor Italy."
- **Magenta → Die Glocke chain**: German aeronautical engineers combined a saucer-shaped airframe (solving boundary-layer aerodynamics) with electrogravidic propulsion derived from Magenta reverse-engineering. [[die-glocke]] was developed and tested here — initially with rotating rocket motors, then with the Bell's own electromagnetic system.
- **Personnel exfiltration**: Facility's research absorbed into US via [[operation-paperclip]]; [[reinhard-gehlen]] organized rat-line exfiltration.

This Magenta-crash-reverse-engineering frame is Jorjani-specific; it should be treated as one source's claim, not as established consensus alongside the Cook/Michaels Schauberger/Schriever/Miethe documentation.
