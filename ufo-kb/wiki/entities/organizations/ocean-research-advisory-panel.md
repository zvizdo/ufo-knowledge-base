# Ocean Research Advisory Panel (ORAP)

White House advisory committee on ocean research priorities. Provides input into the annual White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP) R&D priorities memo — the document that directs federal agency research budgets.

[[tim-gallaudet]] is a member and is actively working to have underwater UAP/USO phenomena listed as a national ocean R&D research priority. If successful, this would formally direct federal agencies to allocate research resources toward studying ocean anomalous phenomena — a significant policy lever that Gallaudet is uniquely positioned to pull given his background as former NOAA head.

During the Trump administration, the White House R&D priorities memo included artificial intelligence and ocean exploration; Gallaudet aims to add UAP as a third pillar in any future memo.
