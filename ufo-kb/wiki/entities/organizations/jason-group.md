# JASON Group

JASON is an independent scientific advisory group that has provided classified scientific analysis to the US government since 1958. Its members — typically 30–60 active scientists, predominantly physicists and mathematicians at elite universities — convene each summer to study classified technical problems posed by the Department of Defense, DARPA, DOE, and the intelligence community. JASON operates under the MITRE Corporation as its secretariat.

**Origin**: JASON grew out of the post-Sputnik concern that the US government needed access to the best scientific minds for classified technical problems. Its name is sometimes explained as an acronym (Julius, Alphonse, and others) or as a reference to Jason and the Argonauts. It was closely tied to the [[institute-for-advanced-study]] at Princeton and the same post-war defense-science nexus as DARPA and the Institute for Defense Analyses.

**Scope of work**: JASON has produced classified studies on topics including nuclear weapons effects, antisubmarine warfare, missile defense, the feasibility of various weapons programs, climate change (early assessments), and counterterrorism. Its studies are among the most technically rigorous classified analysis available to the government.

**UAP relevance**: JASON appears in the KB primarily through [[ron-pandolfi]], a CIA analyst associated with the intelligence community's engagement with anomalous phenomena. The JASON Group's membership overlaps with the scientific community that participated in the 1980s–90s government-sponsored remote-viewing and anomalous cognition research, and JASON studies of advanced propulsion and exotic physics would be directly relevant to any serious government UAP analysis program.

**Membership selectivity**: JASON membership is by invitation only and constitutes a form of credentialing within the classified science world. Alumni include some of the most decorated physicists in the post-war era.
