# Department of Homeland Security

## Overview

Cabinet-level department of the US federal government, created in November 2002 by the Homeland Security Act in response to the September 11, 2001 attacks. DHS combined 22 existing agencies, including Customs and Border Protection, the Coast Guard, FEMA, and the Transportation Security Administration.

## Programs / Activities

### Kona Blue (Proposed UAP SAP)

Per [[hal-puthoff|Hal Puthoff]] in [[Gf_tKn9TaP8]], following the wind-down of [[aatip|AATIP]], the same network — Puthoff, colleagues, [[robert-bigelow|Bigelow]] alumni — approached DHS to set up a new special access program for UAP investigation under the codename [[kona-blue|Kona Blue]]. Puthoff: "We built up a stack of documents that would go to the ceiling here about what needed to be done." The Kona Blue codename has since been declassified through [[aaro|AARO]]. The program was not approved.
