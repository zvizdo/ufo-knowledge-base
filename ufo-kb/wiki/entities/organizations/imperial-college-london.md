# Imperial College London

Research-intensive university in South Kensington, London; one of the world's top ten research universities by multiple rankings. Strong in engineering, natural sciences, medicine, and computing. In the UAP/consciousness context, Imperial College is the institutional home of [[chris-timmermann]]'s Psychedelic Research Group — the team that developed and ran the first clinical DMTX (extended-state DMT infusion) trials.

**DMTX trials**: Standard intramuscular or intravenous DMT produces a 10–20 minute experience, after which the compound is metabolized. [[andrew-gallimore]] and Timmermann developed the [[dmtx-protocol]] — a continuous IV infusion regimen that maintains plasma DMT concentrations at a target level for 30+ minutes, allowing subjects to remain in a sustained DMT state for far longer than conventional administration. This was the primary technical obstacle to systematic NHI/entity contact research: the experience was too brief for controlled investigation. The ICL trials removed this obstacle.

**Research significance**: Timmermann's group at ICL has published on the neuroscience of DMT states — documenting changes in brain connectivity, activation of regions associated with visual processing and default-mode network disruption, and subjective report phenomenology. The DMTX trials extended this research into sustained-state territory. The KB tracks these trials under the [[dmt-nhi-hypothesis]] because the sustained DMT state appears to produce contact experiences with consistent entity phenomenology — which, combined with Timmermann's neuroimaging work, raises the question of whether the entities encountered are internally generated or are in some sense real.

**Institutional legitimacy**: ICL's research prestige means Timmermann's DMT work carries institutional credibility that affects how the findings are received in scientific and policy contexts. The [[credibility-frameworks]] analysis applies: the same claims made by an independent researcher would be dismissed; made by a team at Imperial College, they enter the academic record.
