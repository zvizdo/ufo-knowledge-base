# House Oversight Committee

## Overview

The House Committee on Oversight and Accountability (informally the House Oversight Committee) is one of the oldest and most powerful standing committees in the US House of Representatives. Its jurisdiction covers government operations, executive branch accountability, and federal programs. In July 2023, its National Security Subcommittee — chaired by Representative [[nancy-mace]] — held the most consequential Congressional UAP hearing in history, featuring sworn testimony from [[david-grusch]], [[ryan-graves]], and [[david-fravor]].

## Key People

- [[nancy-mace]] — chaired the National Security Subcommittee that held the UAP hearing
- [[david-grusch]] — testified under oath about UAP crash retrieval programs and non-human biologics
- [[ryan-graves]] — testified about sustained Navy UAP sightings and aviation safety implications
- [[david-fravor]] — testified about the 2004 Nimitz Tic-Tac intercept
