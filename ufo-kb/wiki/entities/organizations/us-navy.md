# US Navy

## Overview

The United States Navy is one of the six branches of the US Armed Forces. In the UAP context it is the most significant military service branch: the majority of the high-quality documented UAP incidents from the modern era involve Navy personnel, Navy airspace, and Navy-operated sensor systems. The Navy was also the institutional home of the UAP Task Force and the filing entity for the controversial Salvatore Pais exotic propulsion patents.

## Key People

- [[ryan-graves]] — Navy F/A-18 pilot who observed UAPs over the US East Coast for years; testified before Congress in 2023/2024
- [[david-fravor]] — Navy Commander who led the 2004 Nimitz Tic-Tac intercept
- [[salvatore-pais]] — Navy aerospace engineer who filed patents for exotic propulsion concepts (the [[pais-effect]])
