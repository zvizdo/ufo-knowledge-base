# Brookhaven National Laboratory

Brookhaven National Laboratory (BNL) is a multi-program national laboratory operated under contract to the US Department of Energy, located in Upton, Long Island, New York. It operates one of the United States' most powerful particle accelerators, the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC), and previously operated the Alternating Gradient Synchrotron (AGS).
