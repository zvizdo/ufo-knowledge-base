# Swedish Air Intelligence Service

The Swedish Air Intelligence Service is the military air intelligence branch within Sweden's armed forces. In 1948, when USAFE officers visited and raised the question of flying saucers, Swedish Air Intelligence delivered the following formal assessment:

> "These phenomena are obviously the result of a high technical skill which cannot be credited to any presently known culture on earth."

They were therefore "assuming that these objects originate from some previously unknown or unidentified technology, **possibly outside the earth**."

Importantly, the Swedes acted on this conclusion: when a Swedish technical expert reported observing a flying saucer crash into a lake, Swedish intelligence dispatched a naval salvage team. Divers found **a previously uncharted crater on the lake floor**, which the Swedish Air Intelligence concluded was caused by the object. See [[1948-sweden-lake-crash]].

This is among the earliest documented cases of a NATO-allied government's military intelligence organization formally adopting an extraterrestrial technology hypothesis in writing — and it was communicated to USAF Director of Intelligence [[cp-cabell]] in November 1948, five years before the [[robertson-panel]]'s 1953 debunking mandate.
