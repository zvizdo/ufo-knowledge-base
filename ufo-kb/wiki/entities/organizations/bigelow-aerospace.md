# Bigelow Aerospace / BAASS

Private aerospace and research company founded by real-estate billionaire [[robert-bigelow]]. In the UAP context, Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies (BAASS) was the sole bidder and awardee of the Pentagon's $22 million AAWSAP contract (2008–2010), run through DIA. BAASS employed dozens of researchers and produced hundreds of technical reports on UAP, paranormal phenomena, and advanced propulsion.

## Key Role

BAASS subcontracted to [[nids]] alumni and hired personnel including [[james-lacatski]] as government program manager and [[hal-puthoff]] as a senior adviser. The company's work fed directly into what later became [[aatip]] under [[lue-elizondo]].

Bigelow also purchased [[skinwalker-ranch]] in 1996 through NIDS and used it as a live research site for anomalous phenomena through 2016.

## The Lockheed → Bigelow Spacecraft Transfer (V00WcEiKRAY)

[[ross-coulthart]] reveals that [[lockheed-martin]] was planning to transfer an alien spacecraft to Bigelow Aerospace — and this is what the 2017 New York Times "metamaterials" language was actually about. Coulthart: *"It wasn't metamaterials. It was a spacecraft."* The transfer was blocked by [[glenn-gaffney]] (CIA, Office of Global Access or related body).

The craft involved may be the [[1953-kingman-crash]] vehicle. This is confirmed from multiple directions: a senior Lockheed executive told Coulthart "We want to do it"; Coulthart's account is consistent with [[eric-davis]]'s firsthand description of Gaffney blocking material access during the AAWSAP period.

> ⚠ "Office of Global Access" is an alternate organizational name for Gaffney's role; Coulthart expresses some uncertainty whether it was Gaffney specifically.
