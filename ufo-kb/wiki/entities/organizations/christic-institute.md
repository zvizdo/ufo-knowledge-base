# Christic Institute

Washington D.C.-based public interest law center founded and directed by [[danny-sheehan]], located at 1324 North Capitol Street. The Institute was the institutional vehicle for Sheehan's most significant national security litigation, including the Iran-Contra civil complaint.

## Key Activities

### Iran-Contra Complaint
The Christic Institute filed a civil complaint naming ~29 individuals — including [[theodore-shackley]] and other CIA/military figures — for their roles in the Iran-Contra network. The complaint alleged a continuing criminal enterprise running from Southeast Asia drug operations through the Nicaraguan contra-support network. A federal judge in Florida (James Lawrence King) sealed the complaint. The Institute was ultimately sanctioned and the case dismissed, which Sheehan characterizes as a politically motivated outcome.

### Karen Silkwood Investigation
Investigated the death of Karen Silkwood — killed en route to a New York Times reporter with documents about plutonium irregularities at the Kerr-McGee plant. Sheehan's investigation connected the missing 40 lbs of bomb-grade plutonium to a chain involving [[theodore-shackley]] and eventual transfer to Israel.

### MK Ultra Litigation
Won the only successful case against the MK Ultra program — a Long Island tennis pro who was dosed twice with LSD at a New York City hospital and died of a heart attack. [[jacob-javits]] (Army lawyer at the time) and Warren Burger (later Chief Justice, serving as intelligence-community counsel) had covered up the death. The case settled when the judge threatened to expose Burger's role.

## Dissolution

The Iran-Contra sanctions effectively destroyed the Institute financially and organizationally. Sheehan subsequently founded the New Paradigm Institute as a successor educational organization.
