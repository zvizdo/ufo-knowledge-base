# Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane Division

US Navy weapons research and development facility in Crane, Indiana. One of the largest military installations in the world by area. Specializes in electronics, cyber, and weapons systems. Referenced in connection with deep underground military bases and — via [[randy-anderson]]'s firsthand 2014 account — an **"Off-World Technology Division"** housed in a modern underground level.

## Overview

NSWC Crane is in Crane, Indiana (Martin County), roughly **3 hours from [[wright-patterson]] Air Force Base** in Dayton, Ohio. In addition to its documented role in Navy weapons development and nuclear readiness (coordination with the Department of Energy), Jesse Michaels's contacts within Navy Special Warfare describe Crane as the **Navy's center for off-world reverse-engineering efforts**, in the same way Wright-Patterson is framed as the Air Force's center.

Known documented functions include:
- Electronics, cyber warfare, and weapons systems R&D
- Naval Readiness (NAVC program)
- Coordination with the Department of Energy on nuclear capabilities
- Advanced weapons training (including Mark 44 minigun, SCAR iterations, armorer certification)

## Off-World Technology Division (Alleged)

In March 2014, [[randy-anderson]] — a US Army Green Beret (18B) attending an advanced weapons training course — was escorted underground after performing exceptionally well. After passing a standard checkpoint and examining captured foreign weapon systems, he and one other Green Beret passed through a second **SCIF-equivalent checkpoint** and into a hallway bearing a placard:

> **"Off-World Technology Division"**

Inside, they were shown two artifacts:
1. A **levitating metallic sphere** (basketball-sized) recovered from a crashed craft — described as a consciousness-interfacing control mechanism. See [[nswc-crane-levitating-orb]].
2. A **gauntlet-like arm device** with a crystalline display showing **shifting hieroglyphic symbols** — retrieval reportedly caused a casualty. See [[nswc-crane-gauntlet-device]].

See full account at [[2014-nswc-crane-offworld-tech]].

## SAIC Connection

[[uap-gerb]] has independently researched SAIC (Science Applications International Corporation) contracting links to Crane, including connections to consciousness research programs. SAIC is known to have run significant operations for the CIA's [[stargate-program]] (psychic spy program, 1972–1995). This raises the possibility that Crane's underground exotic programs involve SAIC or successor contractors as primary operators.
