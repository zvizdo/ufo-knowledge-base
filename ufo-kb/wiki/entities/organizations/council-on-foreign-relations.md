# Council on Foreign Relations (CFR)

Influential U.S. foreign policy think tank and membership organization, founded 1921, headquartered in New York. Publishes *Foreign Affairs*. Membership includes former secretaries of state, CIA directors, and senior diplomats.

In the UAP context, [[harold-malmgren]] — a 50-year CFR member — described the CFR and [[knights-of-malta]] as "complementary rather than competing structures" within the broader [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]]. Both organizations operate with transnational reach and membership that crosses official governmental lines.

## Kissinger's Study Group (1955)

In 1955, [[henry-kissinger]] was invited to direct a CFR **study group on nuclear war and foreign policy**. He was briefed by [[j-robert-oppenheimer]] personally and instructed to turn the findings into a book (*Nuclear Weapons and Foreign Policy*). The study group was chaired by Gordon Dean (former head of the Atomic Energy Commission) and included top military officials, government insiders, and scientists.

The CFR study group's membership overlapped significantly with figures named in MJ12 documents — including **Richard Bissell**, CIA Deputy Director and Area 51 co-founder, who was also a CFR study group participant and who later privately briefed [[harold-malmgren]] on "otherworld technologies." This overlap suggests CFR study groups and the alleged MJ12 oversight structure operated in adjacent, potentially cross-pollinating circles.

Walter Isaacson's biography of Kissinger describes CFR study groups as elite closed sessions exploring specific policy questions — a format that would be equally suitable for managing UAP-related intelligence outside normal bureaucratic channels.
