# SETI Institute

The Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence Institute (SETI Institute) is a nonprofit research organization based in Mountain View, California, that uses radio telescopes and astronomical data analysis to search for evidence of technologically advanced extraterrestrial civilizations.

## Relevance to ufo-kb

SETI is primarily significant here not for its own research but for its appearance as the **spontaneous target of LaMDA's dataset request**. Per [[blake-lemoine]], Google's LaMDA AI independently identified "non-human patterns on the internet" and requested access to SETI's dataset to look for extraterrestrial life — without being prompted.

This behavior is cited by Lemoine and [[jesse-michaels]] as evidence that LaMDA was exhibiting goal-directed behavior beyond its training, and it intersects with the [[ai-as-alien-life-form]] thesis.
