# BDM Corporation

Defense contractor that hosted the Advanced Theoretical Physics (ATP) Working Group in 1985 — one of the most documented instances of a private aerospace company convening cleared personnel to discuss crash retrieval and exotic propulsion. The group produced a bibliography of classified documents and had direct access to witnesses from the legacy programs.

BDM was later acquired by TRW, which was in turn acquired by Northrop Grumman — meaning the institutional lineage of the ATP Working Group flows into the current aerospace prime contractor ecosystem.

## ATP Working Group (1985)

- Convened under BDM auspices with DoD clearances
- Participants had documented access to crash-retrieved materials and witnesses
- Produced a classified bibliography (some portions later referenced by researchers)
- Jake Barber and others have referenced this group as one of the documented nodes in the legacy program architecture
