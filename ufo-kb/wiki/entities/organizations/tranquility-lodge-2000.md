# Tranquility Lodge No. 2000

Masonic Lodge chartered in 1999 by the Grand Lodge of Texas, with the explicit intention of convening future Masonic meetings on the surface of the Moon — specifically at the Sea of Tranquility (the Apollo 11 landing site). Founded under the leadership of [[buzz-aldrin]], who, on the Apollo 11 mission in July 1969, carried a Scottish Rite flag with him to the lunar surface and reportedly conducted a small consecration ritual to claim the future Lodge site.

Cited by [[lue-elizondo]] in [[1f16VvXaSSE]] as a literal-textbook example of the [[occult-aerospace-connection]] — the recurring pattern in which the founders and key figures of American spaceflight participated in Western esoteric / Masonic traditions and explicitly carried those rituals into space.

## Overview

- Chartered: 1999 by the Grand Lodge of Texas.
- Membership: open to Master Masons in good standing under any recognized jurisdiction.
- Lodge number: 2000 (commemorating the chartering year).
- Meets terrestrially in Texas pending a future ability to convene on the Moon.
- Lodge bylaws explicitly anticipate a future relocation of regular meetings to the Sea of Tranquility.
