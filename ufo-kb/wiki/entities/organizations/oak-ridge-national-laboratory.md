# Oak Ridge National Laboratory

## Overview

Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL) is the largest US Department of Energy science and energy laboratory, located in Oak Ridge, Tennessee. Founded in 1943 as part of the Manhattan Project (originally known as the Clinton Laboratories), it has since grown into a major multi-program research institution with work spanning nuclear science, materials research, computing, and national security applications.

In the UAP context, ORNL is relevant primarily because [[sean-kirkpatrick]] — the first director of AARO — accepted a position at Oak Ridge following the end of his AARO tenure. [[kirk-mcconnell]] of the [[senate-armed-services-committee]] flagged this employment transition as a potential conflict of interest, raising questions about whether advisory contracts or consulting arrangements between AARO/DoD and Oak Ridge may have influenced Kirkpatrick's conduct as AARO director.
