# Northrop

Major US aerospace and defense contractor. In the context of ufo-kb, Northrop is primarily significant as the recipient of [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s exotic propulsion IP and as the manufacturer of the [[b2-stealth-bomber]].

## Townsend Brown Connection

Per [[RTEWLSTyUic]]: Northrop published an electro-aerodynamics paper approximately **three months after Brown's Gravity Rand project was shut down** — a timing widely interpreted as evidence that Brown's research was transferred to Northrop via the [[caroline-group]] (the private intelligence consortium that channeled Brown's IP). 

The [[b2-stealth-bomber]]'s leading-edge electrostatic charging system (which ionizes air ahead of the wing) is cited by multiple sources as a possible operational application of the [[biefeld-brown-effect]].

## Close Encounters of the Third Kind Connection

Per [[SZBI85yvV5A]], two large Northrop-labeled crates appear in *Close Encounters of the Third Kind* — a detail [[jesse-michaels]] reads as deliberate disclosure seeding.
