# United Nuclear

[[bob-lazar]]'s commercial company, founded approximately 1999–2000. United Nuclear sells radioactive materials, scientific equipment, and related products to the public, as well as to government agencies including the Department of Homeland Security and the FBI for training and calibration purposes.

## Government Harassment

Despite prior formal clearances from the FBI, ATF (Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, and Firearms), and the U.S. Postal Service for its operations, United Nuclear was raided by the **Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)** with a full SWAT team. During the raid, agents pointed an M16 at Lazar's wife's face.

Lazar regards this raid as consistent with the pattern of harassment he has experienced since going public in 1989 — targeted regulatory action despite compliance. The CPSC raid occurred years after Lazar had been formally cleared by other agencies for the same activities.

## Significance

United Nuclear represents Lazar's post-S4 career trajectory: he pivoted from exotic physics to running a legal commercial operation dealing with exactly the kinds of materials and equipment that intelligence agencies find useful for training. The company's dual commercial/government supply role, combined with the CPSC raid, fits the broader pattern of state-adjacent harassment without formal prosecution.
