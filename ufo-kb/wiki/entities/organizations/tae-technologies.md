# TAE Technologies

Long-running private fusion company pursuing aneutronic fusion using hydrogen-boron fuel, which theoretically produces no neutrons and enables direct energy conversion. Unlike deuterium-tritium approaches, hydrogen-boron fusion runs at much higher plasma temperatures and remains scientifically more challenging. TAE is pre-revenue and has not yet demonstrated net energy gain.

## Trump Media Merger

On **December 18, 2025** — two days after [[nuno-lorero]]'s murder — TAE Technologies announced a merger with **Trump Media and Technology Group** (parent of Truth Social), valuing the combined entity at approximately **$6 billion**.

The announcement coincided with no specific technical breakthrough. Analysts note that any fusion company will need federal government support to build commercial infrastructure — making a politically connected merger strategically logical regardless of technical progress.

[[commonwealth-fusion-systems]] (MIT spinout) is TAE's primary competitor in the private fusion race. Lorero, murdered days before this announcement, directed the PSFC whose research underpins CFS's scientific foundation.

The source explicitly declines to insinuate causal connection between the murder and the merger announcement while noting the timing warrants acknowledgment.
