# Vivex Biotechnologies

A biotech company associated with [[tim-taylor]] that was sold for approximately **$88 million**. According to [[chris-bledsoe]], the company's foundational technology was a **viograph** — a bone scaffold system whose properties derived from recovered UAP material, infused with stem cells to promote bone regeneration.

## The Viograph

The viograph involved:
1. Taking a piece of **exotic recovered metal** and wrapping it around the damaged bone structure.
2. Injecting stem cells into the scaffold.
3. The combination stimulated bone regeneration within approximately **60 days**.

The first operation using this device in the United States involved a **Spanish woman with bone cancer in her thigh**. Two inches of her bone were removed; the viograph replaced the section. Within ~60 days, her bone had fully regrown, pain was gone, and she could walk again. Bledsoe has video of this operation.
