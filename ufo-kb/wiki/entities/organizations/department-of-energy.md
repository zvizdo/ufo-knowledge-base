# Department of Energy

**Type**: US federal executive department  
**Established**: 1977 (successor to ERDA and the [[aec]])  
**Mandate**: Nuclear weapons stewardship, energy research, and nuclear materials security

## Relevance to UAP Discourse

The DOE sits at the intersection of nuclear secrecy and UAP secrecy through several mechanisms:

- **Born-secret doctrine**: Under the [[atomic-energy-act-classification]], any radioactive material recovered at a UAP incident is automatically classified at nuclear-secrecy level, routing it outside normal UAP intelligence channels and into DOE jurisdiction.
- **[[ralph-moat-larson]]**: DOE Director of Intelligence 2005–2008; was never briefed on nuclear-site UAP incursions despite his clearance level — attributing this to a deeper compartment or aggregation failure.
- **Uranium enrichment sites**: UAPs documented at pre-operational nuclear facilities (per SCU/[[kevin-knuth]] data) include DOE-managed Hanford Site construction (1945).
- **[[mcmahon-secrecy-act]]**: Key legislative instrument that created the classification architecture DOE inherits.

## Open Questions

- Does DOE maintain a separate UAP-related compartment distinct from the intelligence community's UAP programs?
