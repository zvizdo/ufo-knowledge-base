# The Carlyle Group

Global private equity firm founded in 1987 in Washington DC. Known for its unusually dense connections to intelligence community veterans and former government officials, including [[george-hw-bush|George H.W. Bush]], James Baker, [[frank-carlucci|Frank Carlucci]] (former Secretary of Defense and CIA Deputy Director), and John Major.

## UAP-Secrecy Relevance

Cited in discussions of how private equity bridges government and private aerospace in ways that enable UAP program continuity outside congressional oversight. The Carlyle Group's model — former officials moving into private roles with security clearances — is described as one mechanism for sustaining legacy programs.
