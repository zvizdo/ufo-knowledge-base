# Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL)

**Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory** is a US Department of Energy national laboratory focused primarily on nuclear weapons design and national security science. Operated by a UC/Bechtel consortium.

## UAP Relevance

- **Nuclear nexus**: As a primary nuclear weapons lab, LLNL sits at the intersection of the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] — UAP frequently appear near nuclear facilities and tests.
- **Derivative technology**: LLNL is cited in discussions of [[derivative-technology]] — reverse-engineered non-human technology filtering into classified US weapons and energy programs through DOE channels.
- **[[doe-secret-science-nexus]]**: DOE/LLNL classification apparatus is a major node in how advanced physics knowledge is kept out of public academia.
- **[[ufo-legacy-program-pyramid]]**: LLNL appears in theories about where recovered hardware analysis occurs within the national laboratory system.
