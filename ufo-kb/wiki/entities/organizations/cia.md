# Central Intelligence Agency (CIA)

## Overview

The CIA was established in 1947 — the same year as the [[1947-roswell]] crash — and from its earliest years under Director [[allen-dulles]] became deeply embedded in UAP secrecy management. Its predecessor organization, the [[oss]], was involved in the [[1933-magenta-crash]] recovery via [[project-mcgregor]].

## Key People

- [[allen-dulles]] — Director who institutionalized UAP secrecy; co-founder of [[area-51]]
- [[richard-bissell]] — Deputy Director of Plans; founded Area 51; disclosed UAP shootdown to [[harold-malmgren]]
- [[james-angleton]] — Counterintelligence chief; embedded in the same custodian network
- [[john-mccone]] — Director under JFK; at the intersection of CIA and AEC UAP classification
- [[chase-brandon]] — Official Entertainment Liaison; publicly claimed to have seen Roswell confirmation documents

## Programs / Activities

- [[area-51]] — Founded primarily by CIA under Bissell for U-2 program; UAP advanced research co-located
- [[majestic-12]] — The alleged interagency governance body managing UAP; CIA representation central
- [[project-mcgregor]] — OSS/CIA operation that allegedly transferred the 1933 Magenta craft to the US

## Institutional Stance on UAP

[[richard-bissell]] reportedly articulated the CIA's position: UAPs "presented forces that were beyond CIA's knowledge or control and they interfered perhaps with what CIA was doing with private industry." This framing — UAP as a threat to CIA operational primacy — explains the institutional hostility to transparency as self-preservation rather than public safety.

The [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] as described by Malmgren places the CIA as one of multiple custodian layers, with the [[knights-of-malta]] and AEC operating above or alongside it with different jurisdictional authorities.

## Domestic Operations: MK-Ultra and Operation CHAOS

Two CIA programs targeting U.S. citizens — both technically illegal under the CIA's founding charter — are directly relevant to the KB's broader themes:

- **[[mk-ultra]]** (1953–1973): non-consensual behavioral modification using LSD, hypnosis, and sensory deprivation. Operated through [[operation-midnight-climax]] (SF and NYC safe houses). Key figure [[jolly-west]] successfully developed permanent memory implantation/removal by 1955; this finding was censored from Congressional records after files were destroyed by [[richard-helms]].
- **[[operation-chaos]]** (1967–1974): domestic surveillance and infiltration of the counterculture and Black militant movements. Ran parallel to FBI COINTELPRO. The 1969 Tate-LaBianca murders ([[1969-tate-labianca-murders]]) functionally served CHAOS's documented objective of destroying the counterculture.

These operations demonstrate the CIA's documented willingness to apply coercive and non-consensual techniques against U.S. citizens — the same institutional character required to maintain UAP secrecy against elected officials.
