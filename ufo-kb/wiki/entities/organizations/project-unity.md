# Project Unity

YouTube channel operated by [[jay-anderson]], focused on UAPs/UFOs, secret science, ancient archaeology, and related disclosure topics. Functions as an interview-based media outlet within the independent UFO research and disclosure ecosystem, hosting researchers, whistleblowers, government insiders, and investigative journalists.

**Format and audience**: Project Unity produces long-form interview content, typically 60–120 minutes per episode, allowing guests to develop complex positions at length. This format is characteristic of the independent UAP podcast/YouTube space that expanded dramatically after the 2017 [[aatip]] revelations — a distributed alternative media ecosystem that operates outside legacy media editorial constraints.

**Institutional position**: Project Unity occupies the independent-research-media tier of the disclosure ecosystem — below the major mainstream platforms (Netflix documentaries, congressional hearings) but above purely amateur forums. It functions as a distribution channel for research that hasn't yet reached mainstream platforms, often interviewing figures before (or instead of) mainstream outlets. [[jay-anderson]] has interviewed figures including government insiders, former intelligence personnel, and researchers connected to the KB's core network.

**Donald Barr/Epstein episode**: The [[51N8OxqZIWY]] source episode in the KB covers the [[donald-barr]]–[[jeffrey-epstein]]–[[dalton-school]] connection — including the science fiction novel *Space Relations*, Epstein's hiring at Dalton, and the [[william-barr]] chain. This represents Project Unity covering the intelligence-network/elite-trafficking cluster that intersects with the UAP secrecy thesis under [[deep-state-continuity]].
