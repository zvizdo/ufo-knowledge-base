# Neuralink

American neurotechnology company founded by Elon Musk and others in 2016; focused on developing brain-computer interface (BCI) implants. Its first human clinical trial (PRIME study) began in 2024; the first patient demonstrated cursor control through neural signal decoding.

In the KB ([[jared-birchall]] and [[51N8OxqZIWY]] pages), Neuralink appears through [[jared-birchall]] — Musk's personal fixer and Mormon elder who sits on the Neuralink board or serves as its president, while simultaneously managing Musk's family office and all Bitcoin/crypto transactions. The KB's analysis treats Birchall's role as structurally analogous to the "Mormon Mafia" (Frank Gay et al.) that controlled [[howard-hughes]]'s affairs and ran CIA operations through Hughes's companies in the 1960s–70s.

The broader significance: if Neuralink achieves functional BCI at scale, it becomes the technological realization of what the KB's consciousness research cluster discusses in theoretical terms — direct, two-way communication between human neural systems and external information networks. The [[gate-program]] and [[space-kids]] cluster raises the question of whether systematic identification of psychic-ability children was an early analog to this goal.
