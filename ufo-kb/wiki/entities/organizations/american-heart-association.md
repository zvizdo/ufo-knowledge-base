# American Heart Association

The American Heart Association (AHA) is a major US nonprofit organization dedicated to cardiovascular disease prevention, treatment, and research. It is one of the most recognized health authorities in the United States and produces widely followed dietary and lifestyle guidelines.

**Institutional capture (Buhler's claim)**: [[brigham-buhler]] — a health and longevity advocate who testified before the US Senate alongside [[rfk-jr]] on the "Make America Healthy Again" (MAHA) platform — cites the AHA as an example of institutional capture in medicine. Buhler argues that the AHA's dietary guidance on sugar-laden products has been compromised by corporate funding from PepsiCo and Coca-Cola, and claims the AHA withdrew from Senate testimony after public pressure highlighted this conflict of interest.

**Broader institutional pattern**: In the KB, the AHA's alleged corporate capture is a data point in the institutional capture theme — where organizations that should serve public health interests are argued to have been influenced by commercial actors with contrary financial interests. This theme runs parallel to similar arguments about the FDA, CDC, and NIH in the KB's medical-establishment skepticism thread.

**Buhler's open question**: Buhler raises the specific question of whether the AHA withdrew from Senate testimony under public pressure regarding its industry funding — a claim that, if documented, would be a concrete example of institutional-capture-driven behavior change.
