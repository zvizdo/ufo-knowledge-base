# Naval Information Warfare Center

## Overview

The Naval Information Warfare Center (NIWC), formerly the Space and Naval Warfare Systems Center (SPAWAR SSC) in San Diego, is a US Navy research-and-engineering command. In [[OOr98r51Sj0]] the center is the institutional source of the **contract prefix N66001** under which [[john-norsine]]'s [[biofusion]] R&D was filed — publicly described as an "aviation weather information systems project," but internally mirrored against Lockheed Martin documents that show the same contract number tied to Norseen's adaptive-cockpit neuroweapons work.
