# National Security Agency

The National Security Agency (NSA) is the US government's primary signals intelligence (SIGINT) agency, operating under DoD authority and responsible for the collection, processing, and analysis of foreign communications and electronic signals. Founded in 1952, the NSA was itself a classified organization whose existence was not widely publicly acknowledged for decades — earning the sardonic backronym "No Such Agency."

The NSA's relevance to UAP research comes from several directions. The agency acknowledged holding a set of UAP-related documents following FOIA litigation in the 1980s; when released, most were heavily or entirely redacted. NSA intercepts of foreign military communications discussing anomalous aerial objects have been cited as a secondary data stream that would capture reactions to UAP globally. Some researchers argue that the NSA's SIGINT capabilities — including monitoring of communications near known UAP hotspots — would produce data that falls under the highest classification compartments. The agency's documented role in mass surveillance (confirmed by Edward Snowden in 2013) demonstrates the scale of collection programs that can remain hidden from the public and most of Congress for years.

## Project Preserve Destiny

The most operationally specific alleged NSA-UAP intersection in the KB comes from [[dan-sherman]] ([[Rfmy5oW_r9c]]). Sherman claims he was recruited into **[[project-preserve-destiny]]** (PPD) — a "gray project" concealed inside a conventional ELINT black project — during an NSA continuation course at the Laurel, Maryland headquarters complex (~1991–92). As an "intuitive communicator" (IC), Sherman would open a dedicated dialogue box (code: Staunch 118) inside a C-van and receive telepathic transmissions from alien entities, which he relayed to his handler chain.

The NSA's response to a FOIA request for all PPD documents is described by [[jesse-michaels]] as "fairly telling" — ambiguous rather than a categorical denial. PPD is the most direct allegation of NSA-run NHI communication in the KB, nested inside a classification architecture (gray-inside-black) that would make it invisible to most cleared personnel.
