# Aerojet Rocketdyne

US aerospace propulsion contractor formed by the 2013 merger of Aerojet and Pratt & Whitney Rocketdyne; subsequently acquired by L3Harris Technologies in 2023. Designs and manufactures liquid-fueled and solid rocket motors, electric propulsion systems, and hypersonic-class engines for civil-space, launch vehicle, and defense applications. Major government contracts include propulsion for ICBM systems (Minuteman), space launch vehicles (Atlas, Delta, SLS), and tactical missiles.

**Defense contractor profile**: Aerojet Rocketdyne's position as a prime propulsion contractor for ballistic missile and space systems gives it deep security-clearance infrastructure and DOE/DoD interface on nuclear-capable delivery systems. Its facilities include classified manufacturing and test sites in Sacramento, Redmond (WA), Camden (AR), and Alabama. The combination of classified propulsion work, materials research capabilities, and government integration with the nuclear weapons delivery chain places it in the same organizational tier as [[lockheed-skunk-works]], [[northrop-grumman]], and [[general-dynamics]] in discussions about where UAP reverse-engineering work might be distributed.
