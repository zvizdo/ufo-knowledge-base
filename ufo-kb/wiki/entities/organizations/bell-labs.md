# Bell Labs

Bell Telephone Laboratories — the research arm of AT&T (later Lucent, Nokia Bell Labs). Originator of the transistor (1947), information theory, the laser, Unix, and many foundational 20th-century technologies.

## Overview

Bell Labs is the most-cited corporate node in [[philip-corso]]'s account of the Foreign Technology Division's 1958–1962 program of seeding Roswell-derived materials into private industry under [[arthur-trudeau]]. The transistor's official 1947 invention coincides almost exactly with the [[1947-roswell]] crash — a coincidence Corso and others treat as causal rather than chronological.

## Programs / Activities

- Transistor research (Shockley, Bardeen, Brattain, 1947)
- Recipient of materials per Corso's *The Day After Roswell*
- Long defense-research relationship with the US government
