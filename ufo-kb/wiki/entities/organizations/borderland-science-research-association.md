# Borderland Science Research Association (BSRA)

The Borderland Science Research Association is widely considered the **oldest UFO/anomalous phenomena research group in the world**, founded in the 1940s and based in Eureka, California. [[claus-svahn]] personally traveled to Eureka, met a member at his garage, and shipped the BSRA's archive to Sweden where it is now preserved at the [[archives-for-the-unexplained]] (AFU).

## Intelligence Connections

Svahn notes that BSRA members "were more than intelligence in certain cases in the '40s" — implying active CIA/intelligence community connections or overlap during the formative post-WWII UAP era, when intelligence agencies were also deeply interested in the phenomena.

## Research Focus

The BSRA explored the boundary regions of science — electromagnetism, consciousness, etheric physics, and anomalous phenomena — well before these topics entered mainstream UFO discourse. Their archive reportedly includes material related to [[thomas-townsend-brown]] and [[biefeld-brown-effect]] adjacent research, consistent with the time period and geographic network.
