# Global Marine Corporation

Global Marine Corporation was a CIA front company operated through [[howard-hughes]] that constructed and operated the **Glomar Explorer** — a purpose-built vessel with a massive moon-pool capable of lifting objects from extreme ocean depths.

## The Glomar Explorer Operation

In the 1970s, the Glomar Explorer recovered a sunken Soviet submarine from approximately three miles below the surface of the Pacific Ocean. The recovery broke the submarine in half; the US obtained roughly half of it. The US government gave a proper military burial to recovered Soviet sailors — an act that was later acknowledged and became one of the few confirmed details of the operation.

## Significance for USO Retrieval

[[richard-dolan]] cites the Glomar Explorer as direct proof that the US government possesses the technical capability to recover objects from extreme ocean depths and has exercised that capability covertly. His inference: "I believe that's probably a yes" — when asked whether the US has a USO crash retrieval program.

Additionally, there is an unverified single-source account (related to Dolan in a letter) that the USS Nautilus nuclear submarine, during a mid-1960s refit, had a "side hatch" installed specifically designed to acquire disc-shaped objects from shallower ocean shelf areas. Dolan notes this carefully as unverified but reports it for the record.
