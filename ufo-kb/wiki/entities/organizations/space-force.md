# U.S. Space Force (USSF)

The sixth branch of the U.S. Armed Forces, established by the National Defense Authorization Act in December 2019. Responsible for organizing, training, and equipping forces for space operations, including satellite communications, GPS, space surveillance, and missile warning. Carved out of the Air Force, Space Force absorbed the former Air Force Space Command's units.

## Space Domain Awareness and UAP

Space Force's Space Delta 2 (Space Domain Awareness) operates sensors that track objects in orbit and near-Earth space. In principle, this infrastructure would capture anomalous objects in the space domain — but Space Force maintains no public UAP reporting channel separate from AARO.

Tim Taylor ("Tyler") — described in Diana Pasulka's *American Cosmic* as a NASA Mission Controller — has a connection to Space Force that was mentioned in the KB context. His role suggests cross-institutional awareness of anomalous phenomena at the intersection of NASA and military space programs.

The Congressional UAP disclosure push (Schumer/Rounds UAPDA 2023) explicitly includes space-domain anomalies in its mandate, requiring that any retrieved UAP materials held across all DoD components — including Space Force — be reported to AARO.
