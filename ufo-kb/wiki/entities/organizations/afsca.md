# Amalgamated Flying Saucer Clubs of America (AFSCA)

AFSCA was a US civilian organization bringing together flying saucer contactees and researchers in the 1960s. Editor Gabriel Green presided over the newsletter and organized national conventions, including a notable gathering in Reno, Nevada.

## FBI Surveillance (1966)

Section 10 of the FBI's 62-HQ-83894 case file documents Bureau surveillance of AFSCA in 1966. The trigger: a letter from Mrs. Levi J. Dow (Goffstown, New Hampshire) to FBI Director Hoover asking whether AFSCA was Communist-backed. The basis for her concern was the content of AFSCA's July 1966 newsletter, which included a channeled message from an entity identifying itself as "Kalen-Li Retan of Korendor" addressing Vietnam War policy and the Military-Industrial Complex.

Hoover's response stated that the FBI "makes no evaluations" of organizations for members of the public. Before drafting this boilerplate response, Bureau staff reviewed AFSCA's newsletter content — generating the primary archival documentation of the Korendor message.

## Korendor message significance

The Kalen-Li Retan channeled communication is one of the few primary-source contactee texts in an FBI case file. Its political content (anti-Vietnam, anti-Military-Industrial Complex) was what attracted the Communist-connection inquiry — the extraterrestrial-origin framing was secondary to the political messaging in the FBI's surveillance logic.
