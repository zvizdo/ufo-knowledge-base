# S-Force

A 15-man covert assassination team assembled by [[santos-trafficante]] from among his anti-Castro Cuban exile network in Miami and Florida, nominally under CIA cover, trained to execute a triangular-fire ambush. Originally commissioned to kill Fidel Castro, Raul Castro, and Che Guevara; ultimately activated against President John F. Kennedy on November 22, 1963.

## Origins — Operation 40 / ZR RIFLE (1960)

The chain of commission (per [[danny-sheehan]]):
1. **[[richard-nixon]]**, VP and chair of the NSC [[5412-committee]], wanted Castro dead
2. Nixon contacted **[[howard-hughes]]** (5412 Committee consultant) via secure phone on the day Nelson Rockefeller withdrew from the 1960 Republican primary — the day Nixon knew he would be the nominee
3. Hughes sent **[[bob-maheu]]** to approach **[[johnny-roselli]]** (Las Vegas mob bagman)
4. Roselli referred to **[[sam-giancana]]** (Chicago mob boss)
5. Giancana referred to **[[santos-trafficante]]** (Havana/Tampa mob boss; his territory, his gunmen)
6. **[[sheffield-edwards]]** (CIA Chief of Security under Dulles) appeared at the Fontainebleau Hotel to give official green light
7. Trafficante handpicked 15 anti-Castro Cuban exile gunmen from five paramilitary bases (No Name Key, Swan Island, Everglades, two Lake Pontchartrain sites)

## Training

- Private planes picked up recruits from five paramilitary bases across Florida/Louisiana
- Flown to Fort Huachuca (Arizona) for processing; then disappeared from records
- Transferred to Clint Murchison Jr.'s ranch in **Oaxaca, Mexico**
- Trained under **[[carl-jenkins]]** (CIA officer) in the triangular fire team technique: three gunmen in crossfire positions against a moving target
- Code name for the program: **[[zr-rifle]]**
- Funded by casino skim money from Las Vegas → New Orleans (Carlos Marcello audit) → Miami National Bank → wire to Banco Internacional, Mexico City → Oaxaca ranch expenses

## Activation Against JFK

The S-Force persisted after Nixon lost the 1960 election; Kennedy was never briefed on its existence.

When Kennedy began secret nuclear disarmament negotiations with Khrushchev via [[norman-cousins]] (18 letters), and then on June 5, 1963 demanded a UFO briefing from CIA director [[john-mccone]], [[richard-bissell]] informed [[allen-dulles]] in Georgetown. Dulles assessed that Kennedy's actions (nuclear disarmament + UAP disclosure to Soviets) constituted treason against the China Lobby's interests — the nuclear option against China would be lost. He re-activated the S-Force.

Dulles's instruction was simple: "get up off their neck" — allow the team to proceed.

## Watergate Connection

[[richard-nixon]], fearing [[larry-obrien]] (new DNC chair and former Hughes lobbyist) knew about the S-Force funding chain, ordered the break-in at DNC headquarters to bug O'Brien's phones. **[[james-mccord]]** — Dulles's favored operative, on CIA retainer (index 4) — led the break-in team. Nixon feared O'Brien would use the information in the 1972 election campaign.
