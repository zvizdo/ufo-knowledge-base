# House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence

Standing committee of the US House of Representatives responsible for oversight of the intelligence community. A critical gatekeeper for UAP disclosure legislation — [[michael-turner]]'s chairmanship (until Jan 2025) was the primary obstacle to House passage of the [[controlled-disclosure-campaign-act]], which Turner prevented from reaching the floor. His successor [[brad-wenstrup]] is expected by [[danny-sheehan]] to be more receptive to disclosure legislation.

## Committee Composition and UAP Track Record

HPSCI has 25 members (typically 13 majority, 12 minority); chair is by majority-party appointment. Members hold TS/SCI clearances and are read into the most sensitive intelligence programs. The committee is the House counterpart to the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence (SSCI).

UAP-relevant history:
- **2020**: HPSCI added the UAP-disclosure rider to the Intelligence Authorization Act
- **2022**: HPSCI Subcommittee on Counterterrorism, Counterintelligence, and Counterproliferation held the first public UAP hearing in 50+ years
- **2023**: Under Turner's chairmanship, the committee declined to advance the broader Schumer-Rounds UAP Disclosure Act
- **2024**: Schumer-Rounds re-introduced; HPSCI again blocking
- **Jan 2025**: Wenstrup succeeds Turner as chair; UAP-disclosure advocates expect renewed legislative momentum
