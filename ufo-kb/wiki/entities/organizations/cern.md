# CERN

The European Organization for Nuclear Research (CERN) operates the world's largest and most powerful particle physics laboratory, located on the Franco-Swiss border near Geneva. Its primary accelerator, the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), is a 27-kilometer ring that accelerates particles to near light speed and collides them to study fundamental forces and particles.

## CERN in UFO/UAP Discourse

Within the UAP discourse, CERN features in two overlapping claims:

### Portal / Beings Claim
Per [[51N8OxqZIWY]], [[jay-anderson]] asserts that "physicists from CERN testified to beings coming in and out of portals." No named witnesses are cited; this is secondhand hearsay. The claim is that high-energy collisions at the LHC produce anomalies — potentially opening or activating inter-dimensional passages.

This connects to a broader pattern in the episode: particle accelerators as inadvertent or deliberate mechanisms for accessing other dimensions or attracting non-human intelligence (NHI) phenomena.

### Comparison to [[brookhaven-national-lab]]
[[jay-anderson]] draws an explicit structural parallel: CERN (European) :: [[brookhaven-national-lab]] (American). The Long Island facility is described as "our CERN equivalent," and a UAP crash at [[south-haven]] adjacent to Brookhaven is presented as the American analog to whatever anomalies CERN physicists allegedly witnessed.
