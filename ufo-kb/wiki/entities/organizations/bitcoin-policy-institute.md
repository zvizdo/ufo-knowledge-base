# Bitcoin Policy Institute

## Overview

The Bitcoin Policy Institute (BPI) is a US-based policy and research organization focused on Bitcoin's role in national and international monetary policy, financial security, and digital infrastructure. Its framing is explicitly national-security-adjacent: Bitcoin is treated not merely as a monetary innovation but as a **strategic asset** with geopolitical implications comparable to oil or gold reserves.

## Key People

- [[matthew-pines]] — Executive Director; primary public voice on Bitcoin-UAP-AI strategic convergence

## Programs / Activities

BPI's work intersects with the Trump administration's reported consideration of Bitcoin as a US strategic reserve. This trajectory — from fringe monetary experiment to presidential policy discussion — mirrors the UAP disclosure arc that [[matthew-pines]] explicitly parallels.

BPI's national-security framing positions it at the intersection of monetary heterodoxy and intelligence-community thinking about strategic assets. Pines himself exemplifies this dual orientation, applying national-security analytical frameworks to both Bitcoin and UAP phenomena.
