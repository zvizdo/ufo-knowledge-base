# Hume Center for National Security and Technology

Virginia Tech's national security research institute, named after Cyrus Hume, a Virginia Tech alumnus who donated to support national security research. Conducts work on radar, signals intelligence, spectrum analysis, communications security, electronic warfare, and cybersecurity. Employs cleared researchers with active security clearances and maintains direct ties to NSA, DoD, IARPA, and DHS.

**Cleared academic environment**: The Hume Center operates in the structural category the KB identifies as "cleared academic environments" — universities or university-affiliated institutes that hire personnel with active government security clearances, conduct classified research alongside unclassified academic work, and serve as a pipeline between academia and the intelligence community. This is the same structural role as the [[mit-lincoln-laboratory]], [[johns-hopkins-applied-physics-lab]], and similar academic-adjacent defense research organizations.

**Bob Maguire connection**: [[bob-maguire]], who appears in the KB in connection with UAP-adjacent communications research and IARPA contract work, is associated with the Hume Center through his intelligence community career. Maguire's trajectory — cleared academic research environment, then UAP-adjacent work — illustrates how personnel move between cleared institutional environments and the less-formalized UAP research ecosystem.

**UAP-adjacent significance**: The Hume Center's work on radar and signals intelligence is directly relevant to UAP sensor coverage: radar anomalies, transient signal detection, and spectrum analysis are the technical tools through which UAP are detected and tracked. Whether the Hume Center has done classified work touching on anomalous signals in these domains is unknown, but its institutional capabilities place it within the technical ecosystem relevant to UAP detection.

**IARPA relationship**: IARPA (Intelligence Advanced Research Projects Activity) funds cutting-edge research on intelligence-relevant technologies. Hume Center researchers have received IARPA funding, extending the institute's connections beyond DoD into the IC research funding structure.
