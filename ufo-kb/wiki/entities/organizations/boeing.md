# Boeing

US aerospace and defense company. In 1967, Boeing had the Minuteman I guidance and control system contract. Following the [[1967-echo-flight]] and [[1967-oscar-flight]] missile shutdowns, [[strategic-air-command]] HQ commissioned Boeing to investigate — naming two specific Boeing engineers by name in the investigation telegram.

## Key Findings

Boeing conducted a bench test of the Minuteman I logic coupler (an onboard guidance computer component). They found they could cause it to malfunction momentarily by injecting an electrical signal at a specific frequency, voltage, and duration — reproducing the guidance-and-control failure mode observed in the incidents. However:

- They could not explain how such a signal could have been injected simultaneously into 10 separate missiles
- The missiles used triple-shielded 8–9 inch cable bundles specifically designed to resist EMI (electromagnetic interference)
- Their report concluded the failures were caused by an **externally generated signal** whose penetration pathway was unknown

Their investigation thus excluded internal equipment failure, excluded human-operated EMP (at the technology levels available in 1967), and pointed toward an unknown external mechanism.
