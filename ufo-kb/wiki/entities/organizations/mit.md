# MIT (Massachusetts Institute of Technology)

**MIT** is a leading US research university with deep ties to government-classified research (Lincoln Laboratory is an MIT FFRDC). Relevant to UAP discourse as:

- Institutional home of researchers adjacent to UAP topics
- Part of the elite academic physics network critiqued by [[eric-weinstein]] and [[eric-davis]] as structurally hostile to UAP physics research ([[physics-lockdown]])
- [[hal-puthoff]] has MIT connections in his academic background
- [[avi-loeb]] (Harvard, not MIT, but the institutional comparison is made) leads the Galileo Project as a counterpoint to mainstream silence
