# MITRE Corporation

MITRE is a **Federally Funded Research and Development Center (FFRDC)** — government-owned but contractor-operated — that serves as a subject-matter expert and intermediary for classified US government programs across DoD and the intelligence community.

## Role in UAP Legacy Programs (per UAPGerb)

In UAPGerb's pyramid model, [[ffrdc]] institutions like MITRE occupy a crucial intermediary tier between intelligence-agency program administrators and defense-industrial prime contractors. The key advantage: because FFRDCs are government-*owned*, the DoD/IC retains access control, classification authority, and personnel management over materials — while the FFRDC provides the R&D expertise that the government itself cannot house internally. This prevents legacy programs from truly "running away" to private contractors.

MITRE specifically appears as:
- A tier where some of the US's best legacy-program scientists likely work
- Deeply involved with **Sean Kirkpatrick**'s post-AARO career (his LLC subcontracts under MITRE for US Space Command)
- Possibly connected to **Ronald Moltrie** (who UAPGerb believes may have also had MITRE ties)
- A place where personnel rotate from ODNI, CIA DS&T, NRO etc. — the revolving-door mechanism that preserves program compartmentalization

## AARO Connections

[[sean-kirkpatrick]]'s post-AARO LLC now subcontracts under MITRE for US Space Command — suggesting MITRE as a landing pad for officials who pass through AARO's orbit. [[ronald-moltrie]], who structured AARO, may also have MITRE ties (unconfirmed).
