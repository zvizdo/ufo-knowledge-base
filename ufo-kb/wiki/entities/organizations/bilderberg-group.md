# Bilderberg Group

Annual invitation-only meeting of approximately 130 Western political, financial, academic, and media figures, founded in 1954 at the Hotel de Bilderberg in Oosterbeek, Netherlands. Proceedings are not published; no press; no minutes released. Participants have included heads of state, central bank governors, CEOs, and intelligence figures.

## Alex Jones's Account

[[alex-jones]] claims to have infiltrated Bilderberg meetings on multiple occasions and been removed at gunpoint. He characterizes the meeting in [[KhmUixSXxDo]]:

> "If there's a place where you've got like half of them are James Bond villains, that's it."

He is careful to note: "Not everybody that goes to Bilderberg is bad." But he characterizes the *steering committee* as the operative power center.

Jones's structural theory: [[world-economic-forum]] (Klaus Schwab) began as the public/"mouthpiece" arm of Bilderberg in the 1970s and went "really operational in the 90s." The WEF's "Great Reset" agenda is, in this framing, the operational expression of Bilderberg planning.

## Notable Attendees (historically documented)

Henry Kissinger, David Rockefeller, various heads of state, senior NATO officials, major financial institution heads. In recent decades, tech executives (Google, Microsoft) have been regular invitees.
