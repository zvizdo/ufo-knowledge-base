# ODNI — Office of the Director of National Intelligence

The Office of the Director of National Intelligence coordinates and oversees the 18 agencies of the US Intelligence Community. Created in 2004 by the Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act following the 9/11 Commission recommendations. The DNI is the principal intelligence advisor to the President.

## UAP Relevance

ODNI has been centrally involved in UAP reporting and oversight:
- Issued the 2021 Preliminary Assessment on UAP (mandated by Congress in the FY2021 NDAA)
- Issued classified and unclassified UAP assessment reports annually from 2021 onward
- The 2023 ODNI inter-agency assessment attributed most Havana Syndrome / AHI cases to pre-existing medical conditions rather than directed-energy attacks — a contested conclusion (see [[2016-havana-syndrome]])
