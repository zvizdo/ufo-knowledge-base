# Sandia National Laboratories

US Department of Energy national laboratory managed by Sandia Corporation (Honeywell). Located primarily at Kirtland Air Force Base, Albuquerque, New Mexico. Responsible for non-nuclear components of nuclear weapons (arming, fuzing, firing systems).

## Overview

One of three primary US nuclear weapons labs (with Los Alamos and Lawrence Livermore). Its colocation with Kirtland AFB and Manzano Base (nuclear storage mountain) makes it a nexus of both nuclear and alleged UAP activity.
