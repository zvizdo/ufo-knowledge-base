# Peruvian Ministry of Culture

Peruvian government agency responsible for cultural heritage. Has taken a consistent hard-line position against the [[nazca-tridactyl-mummies]] case, which multiple researchers characterize as bad-faith suppression rather than legitimate archaeological oversight.

## Overview

Key actions documented by Jesse Michaels, Michael Mazzola, and Pavle (podcast Activo):
1. Endorsed [[flavio-estrada]]'s conflation of tourist wooden figurines with real mummies, which became the global debunking narrative via Reuters (2024).
2. Raided at least one press conference — brought in federal police, federal prosecutors, and took over the microphone while researchers were presenting.
3. Made 7 documented attempts to seize the mummies from the [[university-of-ika]], each time using police and prosecutors.
4. Labeled credentialed scientists like Dr. Roger Zuniga "grave robbers" to discredit their research.

In September 2024, the Peruvian government began to shift, tacitly endorsing research at the University of Ika — a change attributed partly to pressure from Jaime Maussan's $300M defamation lawsuit.
