# Gorbachev Foundation

The Gorbachev Foundation was established to continue Mikhail Gorbachev's work on nuclear disarmament and global civilizational transformation after his removal from Soviet leadership. The U.S. arm was presided over by [[james-garrison]], [[danny-sheehan]]'s Harvard Divinity School classmate who had cultivated the relationship with Gorbachev through Soviet Academy of Science connections beginning around 1980–81.

## Origin

When Gorbachev met [[james-garrison]] in the Kremlin approximately 1980–81, Gorbachev predicted he would be removed from power when he began dismantling nuclear weapons. He asked Garrison to establish an organizational structure that could survive his personal political fate and continue the disarmament work. The Foundation became that structure.

Gorbachev was subsequently removed in the August 1991 coup attempt — kidnapped for approximately one week; Boris Yeltsin rode tanks into Red Square to free him. Gorbachev signed the dissolution of all Soviet Socialist Republics on December 31, 1991. The Foundation then operated as an independent institutional memory and advocacy vehicle for the civilizational transformation Gorbachev had envisioned.

## State of the World Forum Connection

The Foundation's most visible output was the **[[state-of-the-world-forum]]** — co-founded by Garrison, Gorbachev, and Sheehan — an annual gathering addressing global civilizational challenges including nuclear weapons, environmental crisis, and (eventually) UAP-related institutional reform.

## UAP Relevance

For Sheehan, the Gorbachev Foundation's history is directly relevant to UAP disclosure politics. The 1992 Cheney Defense Department Policy Planning Guidance document — drafted the week after Gorbachev signed the Soviet dissolution — foreclosed the post-Cold War peaceful transition the Foundation was trying to enable. The same military-industrial network that wrote the 1992 PPG controls the UAP programs. UAP disclosure requires dismantling the same power structure that prevented the post-Soviet transformation.
