# World Economic Forum

Swiss non-profit organization founded in 1971 by [[klaus-schwab]], hosting the annual Davos conference attended by heads of state, CEOs, central bankers, and international organization heads. Publishes reports, advocates for stakeholder capitalism, and has promoted the "Great Reset" framework since 2020.

## Bilderberg-WEF Relationship (Jones Theory)

[[alex-jones]] frames the WEF as the public operational arm of the [[bilderberg-group]]:

> "He [Schwab] was really the head gopher at the Bilderberg group that kind of organized it and ran it, and they decided by the 70s to have a public arm that then went really operational in the 90s."

In this framing, the WEF's "Great Reset" agenda — "you will own nothing and be happy," stakeholder capitalism, digital ID, ESG — is the publicly legible expression of planning conducted privately at Bilderberg meetings since the 1970s.

## Klaus Schwab

Jones characterizes Schwab as simultaneously powerful and cartoonish: "he's the real deal" while wearing "cartoonishly villainous outfits." Schwab's *The Great Reset* (2020) and *The Fourth Industrial Revolution* (2016) are the WEF's primary programmatic documents.
