# Temple of Set

The Temple of Set was founded in 1975 by [[michael-aquino]] after his departure from Anton LaVey's Church of Satan. It centers on the Egyptian deity Set as a principle of individual consciousness and self-deification — distinct from LaVey's Satanism in emphasizing genuine magical practice and esoteric metaphysics rather than atheistic self-interest.

## Connection to Intelligence Community

Aquino's dual identity as Temple of Set founder and US Army PSYOPS officer creates an unusual institutional overlap: the same person who ran psychological warfare operations in Vietnam (the Phoenix Program) also led an occult organization focused on consciousness manipulation. This overlaps with [[operation-often]] — the CIA's parallel study of occult and consciousness phenomena.

## Wewelsburg Ceremony

Aquino conducted a satanic ceremony at [[wewelsburg-castle]] — Heinrich Himmler's SS castle, which the [[ss-ahnenerbe]] had been developing as an occult center. Aquino described this as a ritual act of esoteric significance, reclaiming the site's power. [[peter-levenda]] notes this during his broader discussion of Nazi occultism and the intelligence-occult intersection.
