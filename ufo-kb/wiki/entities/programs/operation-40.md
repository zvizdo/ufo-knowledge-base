# Operation 40

CIA covert paramilitary unit established in March 1960 under the Special Group (5412 Committee) during the Eisenhower administration. Originally created to follow CIA-backed invaders into Cuba after the anticipated success of the Bay of Pigs invasion and to take control of key governmental positions. After the Bay of Pigs failure, Operation 40 evolved into a broader assassination and destabilization unit.

[[danny-sheehan]] identifies Operation 40 members as overlapping with the [[s-force]] and [[zr-rifle]] network that he alleges carried out the JFK assassination. Key figures linked to Operation 40 include [[david-morales]], [[carl-jenkins]], [[rip-robertson]], and [[frank-sturgis]].
