# ZR RIFLE

CIA program code name for the training and operational infrastructure behind the [[s-force]] — the 15-man triangular fire team assembled by [[santos-trafficante]] for the CIA to assassinate Cuban revolutionary leaders. Training was conducted under CIA officer [[carl-jenkins]] at [[clint-murchison-jr]]'s ranch in Oaxaca, Mexico.

## Program Structure

- **Objective**: Train Cuban exile gunmen in a triangular crossfire technique against a moving target — originally designed to hit Castro, Raul Castro, and Che Guevara simultaneously
- **Trainer**: [[carl-jenkins]] (CIA officer)
- **Training site**: Clint Murchison Jr.'s ranch, Oaxaca, Mexico
- **Cover processing**: Fort Huachuca, Arizona (men signed in then "disappeared" from records)
- **Funding**: Casino skim from Las Vegas → New Orleans (audited by Carlos Marcello) → Miami National Bank → wire to Banco Internacional, Mexico City → ranch costs

## Operational Outcomes

The training program produced a team capable of executing the triangular fire team plan against any high-value target. When President Kennedy ordered the dismantling of the anti-Castro paramilitary network (No Name Key, Everglades) in 1962, the S-Force remained operational — unknown to Kennedy — as a dormant capability that [[allen-dulles]] could re-activate.

## Historical Note

ZR RIFLE is a documented CIA program in the public record (Church Committee) related to assassination planning; the specific Sheehan details about training at Murchison's ranch and the operational use against JFK are Sheehan's interpretive reconstruction.
