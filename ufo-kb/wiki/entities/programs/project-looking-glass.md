# Project Looking Glass

One of three classified programs [[bob-lazar]] encountered briefing documents for during his first full day at [[s4]]. Looking Glass's stated subject was affecting and/or observing the flow of time.

## What Lazar Knows

Lazar read only a brief overview of the program — it was not his assignment ([[project-galileo-s4]] was). When asked directly whether the program had a stated operational purpose, Lazar answered "No" — meaning he either didn't read far enough into the documents to find one, or the overview he read didn't specify.

Lazar does not know whether Looking Glass was operational, experimental, or merely theoretical at the time of his assignment. He does not know whether it was connected to the reactor/craft technology he worked on, or a separate research program.

## Significance

Looking Glass is notable for being the only program among the three [[bob-lazar]] briefings (Galileo, Looking Glass, Sidekick) that points toward time-manipulation rather than propulsion or weaponization. If genuine, it suggests the S4 program had interests extending beyond mere reverse-engineering of propulsion — into the implications of a craft that may manipulate spacetime.

Lazar himself has increasingly come to believe the [[s4-reactor]]'s force affects the flow of time (not just space), and that what he witnessed — frozen candle flames, stopped watches — may be consistent with localized temporal effects rather than purely gravitational effects.
