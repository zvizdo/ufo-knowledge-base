# TR-3B (Claimed Triangle Program)

The TR-3B is a claimed classified USAF triangular aircraft, most extensively described by [[edgar-fouche]], who asserts service-record access to classified aerospace programs at Area 51 and Groom Lake. Under Fouché's account, the TR-3B is a large triangular reconnaissance vehicle powered by a nuclear thermal rocket with a rotating plasma ring that reduces the craft's effective weight by approximately 89% — enabling performance far beyond conventional aircraft.

**Edgar Fouché's claims**: Fouché described the TR-3B in his 1998 book *Alien Rapture* and in subsequent public presentations. He claims the program achieved operational status in the early 1990s and that the craft is approximately 600 feet across. The propulsion system — a Magnetic Field Disruptor (MFD) — allegedly creates a plasma vortex that partially cancels local gravity and inertia.

**UAPGerb synthesis**: [[uap-gerb]] (Sammy) has conducted extensive research into the TR-3B program, integrating Fouché's claims with the 1988 [[norton-air-force-base]] ARV display account and the [[bdm-corporation]] FFRDC architecture. UAPGerb situates TR-3B within a post-1947 reverse-engineering program chain culminating in operational triangular platforms by the early 1990s.

**Belgium wave connection**: The [[black-triangle-uap]] Belgium wave (1989–1990) is sometimes cited as the first major public observation of TR-3B-class vehicles. However, the global distribution of black triangle sightings — including reports predating plausible classified development timelines — makes a purely terrestrial explanation difficult.

**Credibility assessment**: No declassified documentation of a TR-3B program exists. Fouché's claims are unverified. Mainstream researchers treat TR-3B as an unconfirmed claim that may explain *some* black triangle sightings but cannot account for all such reports.
