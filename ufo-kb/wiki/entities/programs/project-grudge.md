# Project Grudge

Successor to [[project-sign]], established February 1949. Unlike Sign, which had produced a classified estimate sympathetic to the extraterrestrial hypothesis, Grudge was structured around debunking. The project's 1949 report concluded that UFO reports were explainable through misidentification of conventional objects, hoaxes, or psychological factors.

## Significance

Grudge institutionalized the debunking posture that would define official U.S. government UFO investigations through the [[project-blue-book]] era. [[edward-ruppelt]], who later ran Blue Book, described Grudge as actively hostile to serious investigation — analysts were under pressure to "explain away" cases rather than evaluate them.
