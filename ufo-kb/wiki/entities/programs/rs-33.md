# RS-33

Allegedly classified Italian government committee studying UFO/anomalous-craft phenomena. Named for "Reparto Sviluppo 33" (Development Unit 33) or similar — sources differ on exact expansion.

## Mandate

Per [[guglielmo-marconi]]'s grandson (interviewed by [[jesse-michaels]]), Marconi headed RS-33 as a secret Italian government committee. The committee is said to have investigated:

- The 1933 [[1933-magenta-crash]] (Lombardy region)
- The 1953 Varese, Italy UFO incident (the trigger for the 1953 reactivation)

In the 1953 configuration, [[enrico-fermi]] is named as vice-president of the committee — placing two of Italy's most decorated 20th-century physicists at the head of a clandestine UFO body.
