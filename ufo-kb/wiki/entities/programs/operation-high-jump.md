# Operation High Jump

## Overview

Operation High Jump (Task Force 68) was a large US Navy expedition to Antarctica in 1946–47, commanded by Rear Admiral Richard Byrd. It involved 13 ships, 33 aircraft, and approximately 4,700 men — unusually large for a scientific survey mission. The official mandate was to establish the Antarctic research base Little America IV and conduct scientific observation.

## UAP Allegations

Operation High Jump has accumulated a body of UFO/UAP-related claims in the discourse:
- Alleged encounters with unidentified craft emerging from and submerging into Antarctic waters
- Admiral Byrd's alleged statements about encountering "aircraft of an unknown origin" in the region
- Connections to alleged Nazi survival scenarios and Antarctic bases ("Base 211")
- The expedition's early return — eight months before schedule — is cited as potentially anomalous
