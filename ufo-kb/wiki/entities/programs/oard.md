# OARD — Office of Research and Development

The CIA's Office of Research and Development was the classified successor division to [[mk-ultra]] that quietly continued behavioral control research while MK-Ultra was officially being wound down in the 1960s. According to John Marx's *The Search for the Manchurian Candidate*, OARD operated under director **Steven Aldrich** and represented a significant escalation — not a termination — of the CIA's behavioral control program.

## Mandate

OARD's focus moved beyond LSD and hypnosis into a more technologically sophisticated behavioral control program:
- **Remote-controlled brain electrode implants** in animal brains to control movement
- **Psychosurgery** — surgical alteration of brain tissue to modify behavior
- **Memory erasure** — research into chemically or surgically eliminating specific memories
- **Biological weapons delivery** — discussion of using reptiles (snakes, lizards) to deliver chemical or biological weapons in covert assassination operations

## Research Venue: Holmesburg State Prison

OARD ran experiments on inmates at Holmesburg State Prison in Philadelphia — a full decade after MK-Ultra had officially been terminated. This demonstrates that the public and congressional narrative of "shutdown" was false; the program continued in renamed form with upgraded capabilities.

## The Scientific Engineering Institute

OARD created the **[[scientific-engineering-institute]]** (SEI) — a CIA proprietary organization near Boston, with a rural research facility on several hundred acres — as its primary covert research vehicle. SEI allowed OARD to conduct research at arm's length from the CIA, using academic and private-sector cover.

One researcher, when questioned, admitted only that they had "looked at zapping parts of the brains of crows to control their behavior" — the minimum disclosed version of what was presumably a much broader animal behavioral control program.

## Privatization

In the late 1970s, the CIA transitioned SEI into the private sector, rebranding it as SURL Metadata — a subsidiary of GD SURL, a pharmaceutical company chaired by [[donald-rumsfeld]]. In 1985, Rumsfeld sold SURL/SEI to [[monsanto]]. This chain transferred a CIA covert behavioral research program into the corporate structure of the world's leading agrochemical company.

## Significance

OARD is the documented bridge between:
- The 1953–1973 MK-Ultra era of non-consensual human behavioral experimentation
- The 1980s pharmaceutical-industrial complex (Rumsfeld / G.D. Searle / Monsanto)

It demonstrates that the "termination" of MK-Ultra in the Congressional record was a limited hangout — the program continued, relocated, and eventually became part of a private corporation with no oversight.
