# Montauk Project

Alleged set of secret US government experiments said to have taken place at Camp Hero, a decommissioned USAF radar station at Montauk Point, Long Island, New York. The claims entered wide circulation through a book series begun by Preston Nichols and Peter Moon (starting 1992) which alleged classified programs running from the 1970s through the 1980s involving psychotronic mind-control devices, time travel, teleportation portals, and the weaponization of psychic phenomena. In some versions, Montauk is depicted as a continuation of the [[1943-philadelphia-experiment]] mythology.

**Evidence assessment**: No credible documentary evidence has emerged to support the core claims. No declassified records, no whistleblower testimony corroborated by verifiable documents, no physical traces. Camp Hero is a real facility — it exists, and a large Cold War radar dish (AN/FPS-35) remained on-site after the base closed, providing visual anchoring for the narrative. But visual anchoring is not corroboration. The KB evaluates Montauk as unsubstantiated fringe-tier narrative operating in the same cultural space as MJ-12 documents: real facilities and real programs used as scaffolding for unverifiable claims.

**Contamination risk**: Montauk is a significant contamination risk in UAP discourse. It occupies the same thematic space as real documented programs ([[mk-ultra]], [[stargate-program]]) and borrows their credibility patterns — government secrecy, decommissioned facilities, anonymous insider testimony. Researchers who uncritically accept Montauk-tier claims alongside credible programs degrade the overall evidentiary standard of the field.

**Cultural impact**: The Montauk Project narrative influenced the Netflix series *Stranger Things*, which borrowed Camp Hero as a setting, the psychic government lab concept, and the interdimensional portal theme. The cultural dissemination of Montauk mythology through mainstream fiction is itself a [[disclosure-narratives]] data point — fictional framings prime audience reception for related non-fictional claims.

**Philadelphia Experiment connection**: Montauk is often framed as a continuation of the [[1943-philadelphia-experiment]] — the alleged 1943 USS Eldridge invisibility experiment. Both claims share a template: naval/military base, exotic physics experiments, cover-up. Both are evaluated as unsubstantiated by the KB.
