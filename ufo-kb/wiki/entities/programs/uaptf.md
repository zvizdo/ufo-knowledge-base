# UAP Task Force (UAPTF)

The UAP Task Force was established by the Pentagon in August 2020 under the Office of Naval Intelligence (ONI), succeeding [[aatip]] as the official government UAP investigation effort. It was directed to standardize UAP reporting, analyze data, and report to Congress.

Its most significant public output was the June 25, 2021 *Preliminary Assessment: Unidentified Aerial Phenomena* — the first official government UAP report in decades. The assessment covered 144 reports from government sources between 2004 and 2021, could only explain one conclusively, and notably did not rule out extraterrestrial origin for the others.

The UAPTF was replaced by [[aaro]] (All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office) in 2022.
