# Project McGregor

Project McGregor was an OSS wartime operation focused on the identification and retrieval of Nazi advanced technology assets in northern Italy in the final stages of World War II. Its most significant alleged acquisition was the 1933 Magenta craft — an unidentified disc-shaped object that had been held under Mussolini's authority since its recovery in northern Italy in 1933.

## Mandate

The operation was tasked with locating and securing advanced technology before it could be destroyed, hidden, or captured by Soviet forces advancing from the east. Northern Italy, where much of Mussolini's wartime industrial and scientific infrastructure was concentrated, was a priority theater for OSS technology retrieval efforts in 1944–1945.

The specific target of McGregor's most consequential acquisition was a disc-shaped craft recovered near Magenta, Italy in 1933. After its initial discovery, Mussolini had established the **Gabinetto RS/33** — a special investigative cabinet — to study the object. The craft was stored at **SIA-Marchetti hangars** in northern Italy during the intervening years.

**Vatican involvement:** Pope Pius XII, fearing that Nazi forces would gain access to the Magenta craft as their control of northern Italy became precarious, alerted the Allied powers to its existence and location. This communication was back-channeled through Vatican diplomatic contacts. The Pope's concern reflected an understanding — likely traceable to pre-war Italian investigations — of the craft's potential strategic and theological significance.

**Knights of Malta:** Wild Bill Donovan, OSS founder and Knight of Malta, was involved in establishing the retrieval protocols for McGregor. The Knights of Malta's involvement in custodianship of recovered non-human material traces from this operation and persists into later alleged oversight structures including [[majestic-12]].

## Personnel

- **Allen Dulles** — OSS station chief operating out of Berne, Switzerland; coordinated the McGregor operation from his Swiss base, which served as the primary liaison point for Italian resistance networks and Vatican channels.
- **Wild Bill Donovan** — OSS founder and director; established the retrieval protocols; his affiliation with the Knights of Malta created the institutional link between the OSS retrieval operation and subsequent Catholic-adjacent custodianship structures.
- **Pope Pius XII** — Alerted the Allies to the Magenta craft's location; the back-channel communication through the Vatican initiated Allied action to secure the object before Nazi access.

## Outputs / Findings

- The 1933 Magenta disc was successfully transferred from SIA-Marchetti hangars to US custody at the end of the war.
- The craft was transported to **Wright-Patterson Air Force Base** in 1945, where it joined other retrieved anomalous material.
- The operation established a precedent and a personnel network — particularly through the Knights of Malta connection — for the management and custodianship of recovered non-human objects. This network is alleged to have fed directly into post-war structures like [[majestic-12]].
- Allen Dulles's subsequent career trajectory (CIA director, 1953–1961) positioned him to carry institutional knowledge of the Magenta retrieval into the heart of the Cold War intelligence apparatus.
