# UAP Task Force

The Unidentified Aerial Phenomena Task Force (UAPTF) was formally established by Deputy Secretary of Defense David Norquist in August 2020, following a Senate Intelligence Committee directive embedded in the FY2021 intelligence authorization bill. It was housed within the Office of Naval Intelligence and tasked with detecting, identifying, and attributing UAP, as well as assessing whether they represented threats to US personnel or airspace.

The task force represented the first official, publicly acknowledged DoD UAP investigative body since the closure of [[project-blue-book]] in 1969. It produced the landmark Preliminary Assessment on UAP in June 2021 — a nine-page unclassified report covering 144 UAP reports from 2004 to 2021, acknowledging that 143 remained unexplained. That report and the legislative pressure that created the task force directly led to the establishment of its successor, the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office ([[aaro]]), in 2022.

## Personnel

- **[[carl-nell]]** — Army representative to the UAPTF; the most senior military figure to have spoken publicly about serving in this capacity as of the Hereticon panel (2026). Nell confirms the task force had access to classified UAP material and that the evidence it reviewed was, in his assessment, "indisputable."
