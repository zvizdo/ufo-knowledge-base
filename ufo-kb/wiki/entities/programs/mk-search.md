# MKSEARCH

**MKSEARCH** was a CIA covert program that continued the mind-control and behavioral modification research begun under [[mk-ultra]] after that program's nominal termination in 1964. It ran until approximately 1972 and retained many of the same subprojects, contractors, and researchers.

## UAP Context

MKSEARCH is cited in discussions of:
- **[[human-staged-abductions]]**: hypothesis that some UFO abduction experiences were induced by government programs using [[mk-ultra]]/MKSEARCH techniques
- **[[manchurian-candidate]]**: behavioral programming as a documented government capability
- **[[dissociative-identity-disorder]]**: MKSEARCH experimentation with DID induction is documented in the record
