# Kona Blue

Proposed US government UAP-focused special access program; submitted by former [[aawsap]] / [[aatip]] personnel and contractors to the US Department of Homeland Security around 2009–2011, in the wake of [[aawsap]]'s funding lapse. Per public reporting (and [[lue-elizondo]] in [[1f16VvXaSSE]]), Kona Blue was intended to continue UAP material custody and reverse-engineering work but was either never formally approved at SAP level, or was approved at a lower classification and then terminated.

Elizondo's framing on [[1f16VvXaSSE]]: "It's Kona Blue — they just released [it] I think by accident." He uses it as evidence against the public assumption that AAWSAP/AATIP were the only or most-secret UAP programs — arguing there is a hidden infrastructure of program names that surface only sporadically through accidental disclosures.

## Mandate

- Continue UAP material custody, characterization, and reverse-engineering work that AAWSAP could not complete after [[lockheed-martin]]'s proposed material divestment to [[bigelow-aerospace]] was killed by inter-agency dispute (per [[james-ryder]] and reported by [[david-grusch]]).
- Provide a non-DOD home for UAP work that could attract civilian-scientist talent without the DOD compartmentation barriers.
