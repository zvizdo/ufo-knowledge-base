# MK Naomi

MK Naomi was a CIA biological warfare program running parallel to [[mk-ultra]], based primarily at [[fort-detrick]] (the Army's biological warfare research center in Frederick, Maryland). [[frank-olson]] — the researcher who was dosed with LSD by [[sidney-gottlieb]] and subsequently murdered — worked on MK Naomi.

## Overview

MK Naomi focused on:
- Development of biological agents for use in covert operations
- Collaboration with the Army's Chemical Warfare Division at Fort Detrick
- Research into biological delivery mechanisms

Olson's conscience crisis — which led to his murder — appears to have been triggered by knowledge of what MK Naomi was developing or had done. The specific operation that troubled him has not been publicly documented.

## The Olson Murder Nexus

The murder of [[frank-olson]] is the most documented state crime connected to MK Naomi. Olson's work on biological warfare at Fort Detrick, his growing ethical crisis, and [[sidney-gottlieb]]'s decision to have him dosed and killed all point to MK Naomi as a program with secrets serious enough to require murder to protect.
