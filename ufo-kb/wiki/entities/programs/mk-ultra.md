# MK Ultra

CIA program studying and applying non-consensual mind control and behavioral modification techniques — primarily using LSD, hypnosis, sensory deprivation, and psychological coercion. Officially terminated in 1973 following the Church Committee investigations. [[danny-sheehan]] won the only successful civil litigation against the program and believes its tools were applied to key figures in the JFK and RFK assassinations.

## Mandate

Develop techniques to:
- Extract information from resistant subjects without their awareness
- Create behavioral dispositions (including violence) in subjects who would not consciously consent
- Disrupt or alter the memory and mental state of targeted individuals post-event

## Program Structure (per [[colin-ross]])

[[colin-ross]] read all 15,000 surviving pages. The 149 sub-projects fall into three tiers:
1. **~50 projects**: straightforward chemical procurement or industrial contracts — no human experiments
2. **~50 projects**: investigators are **unwitting** — CIA money funneled through front organizations (cutouts); professors and researchers don't know who is funding them
3. **~50 projects**: **top-secret-cleared** — investigators know it is CIA money; these include [[martin-orne]] (dissociative/hypnotic states) and [[jolly-west]] (multiple functions)

Precursors: [[bluebird]] (April 1952) → [[artichoke]] (1951–53) → MK Ultra (1953) → [[mk-search]] (into early 1970s). Parallel: [[mk-naomi]] (biological warfare, [[fort-detrick]]).

The 7 surviving document boxes are a [[limited-hangout]] — a curated release. The full record (including [[jolly-west]]'s 1955 memory-implant success paragraph) was destroyed by [[richard-helms]] and [[sidney-gottlieb]] in 1973.

## Key Personnel (Sheehan's account)

- **[[jolly-west]] (Louis Jolyon West)** — psychiatrist with MK Ultra connections; spent **24 hours** with Jack Ruby in his jail cell after Oswald's murder; Ruby subsequently had a "psychotic break," changed his story, and descended into paranoia. Sheehan implicates West in behavioral disruption of Ruby to prevent him from coherently revealing what he knew.
- **William Jennings Bryan II** (distinct from the historical Bryan) — appears to have spent time with Sirhan Sirhan at the Santa Anita racetrack in the period before RFK's assassination; Sheehan suggests possible programming involvement.

## Connection to Political Assassinations

Sheehan's thesis:
1. **Jack Ruby**: West's 24-hour cell visit induced a "psychotic break" — consistent with MK Ultra disruption techniques — that prevented Ruby from giving coherent testimony about why he killed Oswald.
2. **Sirhan Sirhan**: Sheehan (with RFK Jr.'s support) believes Sirhan was not the actual RFK shooter (Eugene Thane Caesar was); Sirhan may have been an MK Ultra subject deployed as a distraction/scapegoat, with his programming making him believe he fired shots he did not fire.
3. **Charles Manson**: Investigator Tom O'Neal documented evidence that Manson was an MK Ultra subject whose "family" was used to discredit the counterculture movement at a critical political moment.

## The Sheehan Victory

Sheehan won the only successful civil case against MK Ultra: a **Long Island tennis pro** was dosed twice with LSD at an unnamed New York City hospital without consent and died of a heart attack. [[jacob-javits]] (serving as Army lawyer at the time) and Warren Burger (serving as intelligence community counsel, later Chief Justice) covered up the death. When Sheehan's investigation exposed Burger's role, the judge ordered a settlement rather than allow Burger to be named in court proceedings.

## Additional Experiments Documented by Ross

- **[[jose-delgado]]** at Yale: remote-controlled brain electrode implants; control of behavior in a 16-year-old girl; *Towards a Psycho-Civilized Society*; funded by CIA and multiple military branches
- **[[robert-heath]]** at Tulane: brain electrode implants funded by CIA/military; "curing homosexuality" experiment; experiments published in mainstream medical journals
- **Dolphin brain electrodes**: documents describe remote-controlling a dolphin via brain electrode to deliver a bomb under a vessel — combining Delgado's technique with [[john-lilly]]'s dolphin neuroanatomy research
- **[[gh-esterbrooks]]**: documented the super spy hypnotic trigger "the moon is clear" in open publications before MK Ultra began; contracted with War Department; corresponded with CIA and J. Edgar Hoover
- **[[tuskegee-experiment]]**: Ross uses this as the foundational precedent — demonstrating institutional willingness to run long-duration, medically harmful experiments on non-consenting populations; ran 1932–1972 in parallel with MK Ultra

## Post-MK-Ultra Continuation: OARD

Per John Marx's *The Search for the Manchurian Candidate*, cited in [[q7Czo77qhHA]]: as the CIA was officially winding down MK-Ultra in the 1960s, a successor division — the [[oard]] (Office of Research and Development), led by **Steven Aldrich** — quietly continued the work with better funding and fewer oversight constraints. OARD:
- Expanded from LSD/hypnosis into brain electrode implants in animals (remote control of movement), psychosurgery, memory erasure, and reptile-delivered biological weapon delivery systems
- Ran experiments on inmates at **Holmesburg State Prison** in Philadelphia — a full decade after MK-Ultra's official shutdown
- Created the **[[scientific-engineering-institute]]** (a CIA proprietary near Boston with a rural farm lab) as its research vehicle

In the late 1970s, SEI was privatized, rebranded as SURL Metadata, acquired by pharmaceutical company GD SURL (chaired by [[donald-rumsfeld]]), and in 1985 sold to [[monsanto]]. The MK-Ultra behavioral research apparatus ended up inside the world's largest agrochemical company.
