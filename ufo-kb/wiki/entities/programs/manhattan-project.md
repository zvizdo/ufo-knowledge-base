# Manhattan Project

The U.S.-led secret program to develop nuclear weapons during World War II, headquartered at [[los-alamos]] (New Mexico), Oak Ridge (Tennessee), and Hanford (Washington). Directed by [[vannevar-bush]] and [[george-c-marshall]] at senior levels; scientific direction by J. Robert Oppenheimer at Los Alamos.

## Relevance to UAP History

The Manhattan Project established the template for extreme compartmentalization that later characterized alleged UFO retrieval programs:
- Scientists and engineers worked in complete isolation by compartment; most had no idea of the broader program.
- Black budget accounting through obscured congressional appropriations.
- A large standing body of cleared scientists with security clearances that could be mobilized for follow-on secret projects.

Many figures who appear in early UAP reverse-engineering claims had Manhattan Project connections: [[vannevar-bush]] (headed the Office of Scientific Research and Development), [[edward-teller]] (thermonuclear weapons), [[john-trump]] (MIT; classified post-war investigation of Nikola Tesla's papers at [[tesla-papers]]).

[[harold-malmgren]]'s account of [[bluegill-uap-debris]] handling at [[los-alamos]] is structurally continuous with how Manhattan-Project-era cleared scientists handled classified materials at the same institution.
