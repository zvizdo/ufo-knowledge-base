# BrainSTORMS

## Mandate

BrainSTORMS — *Brain System To Transmit Or Receive Magneto-electric Signals* — is [[battelle-memorial-institute]]'s Phase 2 deliverable under [[darpa-n3|DARPA's N3 program]], developed in partnership with [[afrl|Air Force Research Lab]] and Carnegie Mellon University. The system uses **nanoscale magneto-electric devices injected into the bloodstream** and guided into specific regions of the brain with external magnets, as part of a bidirectional neural interface designed for war-fighter use cases including UAV control.

## Personnel

- Battelle (prime contractor)
- AFRL (military partner)
- Carnegie Mellon University (academic partner)

## Outputs / Findings

- Per Battelle's 2020 press release: progressed to N3 Phase 2.
- In [[OOr98r51Sj0]], BrainSTORMS is presented as the public-record evidence that nanoscale, injectable, magnetically-targeted neural interfaces are a real and funded research area — making the [[mind-controlled-craft]] claim more plausible than it sounds when read cold.
