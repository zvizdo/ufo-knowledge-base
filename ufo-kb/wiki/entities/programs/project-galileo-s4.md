# Project Galileo (S4)

The primary classified program [[bob-lazar]] was assigned to at [[s4]], as described in briefing documents he read on his first full day. Not to be confused with Avi Loeb's public [[galileo-project]] (a civilian asteroid/UAP search).

## Mandate

According to Lazar, Project Galileo had two explicit directives:

1. **Reverse-engineer the propulsion system** of the recovered craft using materials available on Earth — determine how it works and build an analog using terrestrial technology.
2. **Remotely disable the system "at all costs"** — develop a means to neutralize the craft's propulsion and potentially the craft itself.

Lazar was told — or understood from context — that **directive 2 was the higher priority**. This is significant: the program was not primarily about understanding the technology or replicating it for human use, but about neutralizing it as a potential threat.

## Lazar's Assignment

Lazar was assigned specifically to the [[s4-sport-model]] and its [[s4-reactor]]. His task was to work on the propulsion system — specifically to understand the [[element-115]] reactor's output and the gravity-wave amplification mechanism.

Lazar was presented as an "outside the box" thinker brought in because previous scientists (described as using conventional approaches) had made no progress. His background in particle accelerator physics at [[los-alamos]] and his reputation as an unconventional problem-solver were cited as reasons for his selection.

## The "Canary Trap"

Briefing documents about the occupants of the craft contained a deliberate disinformation element — Lazar and [[barry-castillo]] were told that certain passages, including the claim that "aliens made 65 corrections to human evolution," were intentionally inserted to help identify leakers (each set of documents had slightly different language). This is a known intelligence technique called a "canary trap."

Lazar applies this framing cautiously: the trap language was in the human-genetics content, not necessarily in the technical material he was working with. He cannot determine which elements of what he read were genuine and which were false.

## Status

Lazar believes the program long ago left [[s4]] and [[area-51]]. He speculates the South Pacific was the preferred relocation target, though cost-prohibitive. Current location unknown.
