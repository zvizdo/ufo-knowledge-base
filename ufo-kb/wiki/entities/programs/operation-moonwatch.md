# Operation Moonwatch

Citizen science program created by [[fred-whipple]] at Harvard Observatory, with [[j-allen-hynek]] as a key collaborator. Twelve stations distributed around the Earth staffed by amateur astronomers who tracked satellites visually across the sky — originally designed to support early space program tracking before dedicated radar networks were operational.

## UFO Reports

[[beatriz-villarroel]] notes that she and "Jacqueline" found **36 UFO reports** within the Operation Moonwatch dataset. These reports came from the participating amateur astronomers — people with genuine sky-observation experience — who encountered anomalous phenomena while conducting their tracking work. The reports have been largely ignored but are now under Villarroel's investigation.

This is significant because it means an officially sanctioned scientific program, embedded in the Menzel network, inadvertently accumulated primary-source UFO encounter data.
