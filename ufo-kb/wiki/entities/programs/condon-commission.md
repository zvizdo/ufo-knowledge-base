# Condon Commission

Air Force-funded UFO study based at the University of Colorado, headed by physicist [[edward-condon]] (1966–1968). Its 1968 report concluded there was nothing scientifically interesting in UFO reports and recommended no further study. The Air Force used this conclusion to terminate [[project-blue-book]] in 1969.

## Mandate

To conduct an ostensibly independent scientific review of USAF UFO data and recommend whether continued investigation was warranted.

## Personnel

- [[edward-condon]] — director
- [[robert-low]] — deputy director; pre-determination memo author
- Roy Craig — lead investigator; interviewed [[lewis-chase]] at Malmstrom
- Designated base UFO officers at various USAF bases (including [[lewis-chase]] at Malmstrom) were created specifically to support Condon

## Outputs / Findings

- 1968 Condon Report: null result; no scientific value in UFO study
- Internal memo by [[robert-low]] pre-dating the study states the conclusion was pre-determined
- Investigation of [[1967-oscar-flight]] and [[1967-echo-flight]]: Craig visited Malmstrom, interviewed Chase (who denied all knowledge), and left without accessing the missile-shutdown documentation that Chase himself had previously sent to the Commission
- [[robert-hiper]] told [[robert-low]] the missile-shutdown material was "probably too highly classified" — the Commission never saw it
- A June 1967 meeting with base UFO officers allegedly produced agreed language for the final report ("nothing to see here") before investigations were complete
