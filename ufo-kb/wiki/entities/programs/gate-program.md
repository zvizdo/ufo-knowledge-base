# GATE Program

The Gifted and Talented Education (GATE) program is a U.S. federal initiative, formally established by the Gifted and Talented Children's Act of 1978, providing funding and curriculum frameworks for schools to identify and serve students with exceptional intellectual, creative, or academic abilities.

In the KB, GATE appears in connection with [[space-kids]] — [[andrija-puharich]]'s program at his Ossining compound that gathered children with apparent psychic abilities for channeling sessions with [[the-nine]]. A person interviewed by Greg Malazzi (in the [[space-kids]] page) claimed to have been in GATE and recognized strong structural parallels between GATE's talent-identification methodology and the Space Kids program.

The hypothesis: GATE's nationwide testing and identification pipeline, combined with the documented CIA/MK-Ultra history of interest in psychic phenomena in children ([[mk-ultra]] included experiments with hypnosis and altered states in minors), suggests that GATE may have served — officially or unofficially — as a screening mechanism for identifying children with anomalous cognitive abilities that intelligence programs would subsequently contact through other channels (private researchers like Puharich acting as cutouts).
