# Project Twinkle

US Air Force investigation into unexplained green fireballs appearing over American nuclear weapons installations, primarily in the Southwest. Led by meteorite expert [[lincoln-lapaz]] (Lincoln LaPaz).

## Mandate

Determine the nature and origin of highly anomalous luminous green fireballs that appeared repeatedly over nuclear-sensitive sites including [[los-alamos]] and Holloman Air Force Base. The concern was obvious: if something unknown was flying over the most sensitive nuclear installations in the United States, that required explanation.

## Findings

[[lincoln-lapaz]] concluded that the green fireballs were **not a known natural phenomenon** and that they appeared to propel themselves intelligently. This is a remarkable conclusion from a mainstream, credentialed meteorite scientist.

## Significance

Project Twinkle is one of the earliest documented official acknowledgments of anomalous phenomena specifically associated with nuclear sites — predating and foreshadowing the larger pattern catalogued by [[robert-hastings]] in [[ufos-and-nukes]]. The same lead investigator (LaPaz) later ran the Near-Earth Satellite Project at [[white-sands-missile-range]], suggesting continuity of concern about anomalous aerial phenomena at and above nuclear sites.
