# COINTELPRO

**COINTELPRO** (Counter Intelligence Program) was an [[fbi]] domestic intelligence operation that ran from 1956 to 1971, targeting organizations deemed subversive — including civil rights groups, antiwar activists, and fringe political/religious movements. UFO researcher and contactee groups were surveilled under the same broad mandate.

## UAP Relevance

COINTELPRO provides a documented historical precedent for [[government-suppression-ufo]]: the FBI demonstrably targeted groups whose beliefs the government found threatening or destabilizing. This is cited as evidence that:

1. The government had a systematic mechanism for disrupting UAP-related communities.
2. The "no interest" public posture of agencies like the FBI was contradicted by actual operational targeting.

The program is also connected to [[spahn-ranch]] (Manson family surveillance context) and [[fbi]] general activity.
