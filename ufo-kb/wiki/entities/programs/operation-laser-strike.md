# Operation Laser Strike

**Operation Laser Strike** was a US counter-narcotics operation in Peru, approximately **1997**. Its official mandate was to shoot down narcotrafficking planes bringing drugs from Colombia, Bolivia, and Peru into US airspace.

## UAP Connection

[[jonathan-wagant]], a USMC Lance Corporal, was deployed on Operation Laser Strike. During the operation, his 8–10 man Marine team (including Sergeant Atkins and Sergeant Allen) was told there was a "downed friendly or foreign plane" — standard procedure — and dispatched to investigate after a combination of driving and trekking through jungle terrain.

What they found was not a plane: an enormous **egg-shaped / teardrop-shaped craft embedded in a cliff face** with extensive ballistic damage on the rear (consistent with a Peruvian Hawkman-23 missile strike). The craft had a swirling oil-on-water / mother-of-pearl electromagnetic field around it. A four-fingered arm hung from an open hatch, dripping purplish-green fluid. Wagant felt a being communicating telepathically, saying "don't be scared, help us get out."

Following the encounter, all three Marines were intercepted by 8–12 black-ops operators (no insignia, no name tags) at gunpoint, and subsequently given what they were told were **anthrax vaccine boosters** — which UAPGerb identifies as a known silencing mechanism across multiple crash-retrieval cases.
