# Khafre Research Project

A proposed in-situ archaeological and scientific research initiative by [[filippo-biondi]], [[corrado-malanga]], and [[armando-mei]], in collaboration with the [[university-of-ferrara]] and potentially other academic and commercial partners. The project aims to translate the [[beyond-protocol]] SAR findings into physical ground-truth by gaining Egyptian government authorization to explore surface shafts on the [[giza-plateau]].

## Mandate

The project proposal (being compiled as of the [[zrmG2jBckgk]] interview, ~2025) focuses on:

1. **In-situ seismic measurements**: Using seismic reflectivity methods (not SAR) to independently map underground structures and validate Biondi's SAR data from the ground.
2. **Water table mapping**: Precisely charting the underground water level across the plateau — key to the piezoelectric/water energy hypothesis.
3. **Shaft identification and cleaning**: Known blocked shafts between [[khafre-pyramid]] and [[great-sphinx]] — tipped by [[trevor-grassi]] — would be cleaned of debris to allow descent.
4. **Underground exploration**: Following shafts downward to identify the secondary entrances to the main underground facility.

## Key Constraint: Egyptian Authorization

The project explicitly does **not** request entry into the pyramids themselves (too politically difficult). Instead it targets the **plateau surface shafts** — an approach Biondi believes may be easier to authorize. His stated goal for 2026: complete the Giza SAR scanning, submit the proposal to Egyptian authorities, and potentially begin in-situ work.

> Biondi (translated from Italian proverb): "Chiedere umano est. Ottenere autem diabolicum." — Asking is human; obtaining is another matter entirely.
