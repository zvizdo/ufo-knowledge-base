# Operation Often

> *Stub page — expand with primary source import.*

Operation Often is an alleged CIA program from the early 1970s, reportedly run under the aegis of [[mk-ultra]] director [[sidney-gottlieb]] (or his successors), that explored the intelligence applications of occult practices, demonology, and paranormal phenomena. The program's name allegedly came from the frequency with which its operatives were instructed to repeat experimental procedures.

## Claims

- Employed astrologers, tarot readers, practitioners of Satanism, and professional occultists as consultants
- Tested whether occult rituals could produce psychological or physical effects useful for operations
- Connected to [[george-white]]'s safe-house operations and the broader MK Ultra infrastructure

## Evidentiary Status

Unlike [[mk-ultra]] (confirmed by surviving financial records and FOIA), Operation Often is primarily documented through journalistic accounts (Gordon Thomas's *Journey Into Madness*, 1989). No confirmed FOIA release has yet produced its operational files. The claim is plausible given CIA's documented interest in non-standard psychology but remains unverified at the primary-document level.
