# Project Excalibur

## Overview

Project Excalibur was a classified program at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory to develop a nuclear-pumped X-ray laser as a missile defense weapon. It was part of the broader [[strategic-defense-initiative]] ("Star Wars") research portfolio and was championed by [[edward-teller]].

## UAP Connection

In [[09KP8XVf5nY]], the [[directed-energy-uap-weapon]] lineage is traced from the [[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown]] through Project Excalibur to SDI. The argument is that the discovery during Bluegill Triple Prime — that a nuclear X-ray pulse could physically affect a UAP — created institutional awareness of a weapons modality, which Excalibur then developed into a deployable technology.

The [[pippa-malmgren]] relay of her father's account frames the Roswell survivor's communications as having seeded the concept of using directed energy against UAP. Excalibur would represent the second-generation implementation of that concept: a purpose-built system rather than an accidental nuclear test byproduct.

The dual-use nature — missile defense publicly, UAP engagement capability covertly — is a key question left open in the source.
