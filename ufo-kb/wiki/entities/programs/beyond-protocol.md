# Beyond Protocol

Research program led by [[filippo-biondi]] applying **Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) Doppler tomography** to ancient structures, starting with the [[giza-plateau]]. The program's novel contribution is the use of **phonons** — surface micro-vibrations — as the detection signal rather than traditional penetrating radar returns.

## Methodology

### SAR Doppler Tomography
Uses data from the Italian [[cosmos-skymed]] satellite system (2nd and 3rd generation). Key parameters:
- **Footprint**: 5 × 5 km scan area
- **Resolution**: ~1 m × 1 m (submetric)
- **Integration**: 15 seconds per image (single pass is sufficient statistic for tomography)
- **Orbital period for repeat geometry**: 16 days

### Phonon Detection
Standard SAR measures radar backscatter. Biondi's innovation: measuring **micro-vibrations** (phonons) on the surface of the structure. Differences in phonon patterns between solid material and cavities reveal internal structure. The paper abstract states: "Khufu becomes transparent like a crystal when observed in the micro movement domain."

### Tomographic Line Extraction
From the 2D SAR image, any pixel-to-pixel line can be selected to extract a vertical cross-section (depth profile). This allows flexible targeting of any sub-area within the scan footprint.

### Improved Processing (Grand Sasso variant)
For the [[gran-sasso-laboratory]] proof-of-concept (presented Malta 2024), Biondi applied a superior noise-averaging processing technique over 2 months of computation time. This produced significantly cleaner images than the original Giza pyramid scans. The improved technique is computationally expensive and requires an "array of GPUs" / mainframe — not yet applied to the full Giza pyramid dataset due to resource constraints.

## Findings (Giza Plateau)

- **Eight cylindrical tubular structures (4+4)** below [[khafre-pyramid]]: extending ~1 km into bedrock. Each tube has a **spiral (coil) nature** wrapping around a central core column, terminating at ~80 m wide/tall chambers at depth. Confirmed by four satellites: Capella Space, Umbra, ICEye, COSMO-SkyMed ([[zrmG2jBckgk]])
- **2+2 smaller versions** of same tubular structure beneath [[menkaure-pyramid]] ([[zrmG2jBckgk]])
- **Single cube structure** descending beneath [[great-sphinx]] ([[zrmG2jBckgk]])
- **[[osiris-shaft]]** detected with ~4 m error margin — used as partial validation
- **Big Void** above the Grand Gallery detected; position differs slightly from the [[scan-pyramids-project]] muon team's detection
- **Underground tunnel network** interconnecting all structures across the plateau
- Vertical shafts adjacent to the Khafre–Sphinx causeway identified as potential clean-up/entry targets; [[trevor-grassi]] independently confirmed a "corridor of rooms" at these locations

## Criticism
Critics (including [[luke-caverns]] in [[92XrddiKjHY]]) note:
1. Known pyramid chambers are *not* detected, undermining confidence in the claimed deeper structures
2. The original AI "infallibility" framing is methodologically indefensible on n=1 objects
3. Quality gap between Gran Sasso processing and pyramid processing is not satisfactorily explained
4. The signal cut-off at ~600 m is unexplained (Biondi: "there is something I can't disclose it now")

[[graham-hancock]] texted Jesse Michaels: "I have no reason to believe the source here is being honest." Though this referred to the 2025 social media viral phase.
