# Project Artichoke

Project Artichoke was the CIA mind-control program that succeeded [[bluebird]] and preceded [[mk-ultra]], running in the early 1950s. It continued the same core research into compartmentalized hypnotic identities for use in intelligence courier and control operations.

## Key Facts (per [[colin-ross]])

Artichoke documents describe:
- Experiments where subjects were hypnotized, given tasks, and induced to have **amnesia** for those tasks
- Testing of **verbal triggers** to switch subjects between normal and programmed identities
- Use of available personnel — "secretaries and whoever they could get their hands on"
- Methods consistent with [[gh-esterbrooks]]'s published "super spy" technique

## Significance

Artichoke is the documented bridge between Bluebird's initial formalization of hypnotic courier techniques and MK Ultra's vastly expanded 149-project research program. The continuity of methods across Bluebird → Artichoke → MK Ultra → MK Search shows a decades-long operational commitment to developing [[manchurian-candidate]] capabilities, not an isolated experiment.
