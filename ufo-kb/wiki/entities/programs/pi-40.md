# PI-40

## Overview

PI-40 is an alleged successor to or evolved version of [[majestic-12]], described in some research circles as a specialized think tank that provided intellectual and strategic backbone to the UAP oversight structure as it evolved from a small Truman-era committee into a more institutionalized program under Eisenhower and beyond.

The name "PI-40" and "MJ12" are sometimes used interchangeably by researchers, reflecting uncertainty about whether the structure was renamed, reorganized, or whether they were always distinct parallel bodies.

## Alleged Kissinger Connection

Some researchers have argued that [[henry-kissinger]] was recruited into MJ12 or PI-40 through his association with [[nelson-rockefeller]] and the Rockefeller Brothers Fund Special Studies Project — with the claim that his Harvard academic career partially served as cover for deeper intelligence advisory work within this structure.

The argument rests on:
- The Rockefeller Foundation connection to MJ12 (via Detlev Bronk)
- Kissinger's consulting relationship with the [[psychological-strategy-board]] under [[gordon-gray]] (MJ12-named)
- Kissinger's CFR study group overlap with MJ12-adjacent figures like [[richard-bissell]]
- Maria Wang's claim (via [[william-steinman]]) that her husband reported directly to Kissinger in the "flying saucer program"

## Private Industry Shift

Researchers proposing PI-40's existence often connect it to the shift, beginning in the late 1950s, of UAP reverse-engineering work into private aerospace and defense contractors — allowing congressional oversight to be bypassed by compartmentalizing within private corporations. This shift would implicate Kissinger through his Rockefeller connections.

> ⚠ PI-40 is an alleged structure with no confirmed documentary basis. All claims regarding its existence derive from UFO research community sources, not verified government documents.
