# Project Preserve Destiny

An alleged NSA classified program in which human "intuitive communicators" (ICs) — individuals whose genetics were reportedly altered by non-human intelligences (NHI) before birth — were trained and deployed to telepathically receive and relay alien transmissions. The sole named firsthand source is [[dan-sherman]], a USAF Technical Sergeant and ELINT specialist who served as IC Staunch 118.

The program is described in Sherman's book **[[above-black]]** and in an American Alchemy interview ([[Rfmy5oW_r9c]]).

## Mandate

According to Sherman's account:
- Recruit humans pre-conditioned by NHI to have latent IC faculty
- Train them in a biofeedback framework to consciously access and use the IC channel
- Deploy them at classified ELINT facilities overseas, where IC work was concealed within conventional ELINT black projects
- Relay alien communications to a handler chain; relay reports of apparent abduction activity

The program's content was categorized as a **"gray project"** — concealed inside a conventional **"black project"** as a further layer of classification. The standard "onion" briefing (multiple classification layers) was used, but even the black project handlers were kept unaware of the gray (NHI) layer.

## Structure

**Classification architecture:**
- Black project (conventional classified ELINT)
- Gray project (PPD / NHI layer) nested inside the black project
- Right seat = IC work; left seat = conventional ELINT; the two operators shared a C-van but did not cross-inform

**Handler chain (as described by Sherman):**
- Captain White — initial NSA contact officer; Sherman's primary initial briefer; knew the administrative shell but little NHI content
- Local unit captain at overseas base — knew Sherman had "the gray project" role but not the content
- Sergeant Larson — in the room during base indoctrination; knew the black layer, unaware of the gray layer

**IC code:** Staunch 118

**Platform:** C-vans (communication vans) at classified ELINT detachments; two-person requirement at all times for security

## Training

Sherman was recruited during continuation course EA280 at NSA headquarters, Laurel, Maryland. Training ran ~3 months concurrently with his conventional EA280 schooling:
- Van with headphones; tone to mentally hum
- Sine wave monitor: when IC mental contact established, the sine wave visually shifted
- No alien direct contact during training; training was purely biofeedback calibration

## Known IC Communications

Sherman's alien contacts, self-named after *Star Trek* characters:
- **Spock** — first contact; logical, minimal; abruptly shut down disallowed questions; communication ended when Sherman's base was decommissioned
- **Bones** — second contact; different "tapestry"; more open to informal questions; confirmed via a "closer communication plane" that Sherman developed

Key alien-communicated claims (all hearsay, attributed to NHI via Sherman):
1. Multiple alien races exist
2. Human-alien interbreeding experimented with historically
3. NHI have been on Earth for very long time; long presence pre-dates human civilization-to-civilization communication
4. Human travel refers to void-creation ahead of craft (Alcubierre-type EM manipulation)
5. Humans are "water beings/vessels"
6. Intelligent creation and non-intelligent creation are distinct; humans and NHI are both in intelligent creation
7. More than one country involved in NHI interactions (not necessarily PPD itself)

**Late-stage mission shift:** Toward the end of Sherman's service, transmissions began carrying what he interpreted as real-time abduction monitoring data — location potentials, pain levels, and recall indicators for abductees. This prompted his discomfort and eventual exit from the program.

## FOIA Status

An independent researcher submitted FOIA requests to both the US Air Force and the NSA for all files related to Project Preserve Destiny. Per [[jesse-michaels]]'s account, the responses from both agencies were "fairly telling" — ambiguous rather than categorical denials. The specific text of these responses was discussed at the end of the interview.

## NSA Director of Research Corroboration

In [[jMCavr3dVP0]], [[eric-haseltine]] — who served as NSA Director of Research and later CTO of the entire US intelligence community — was asked about PPD and Dan Sherman. His response: "I heard about something like that." He offered no confirmation of the specific program details but did not categorically deny it, and his "guilty knowledge" framing suggests awareness of at least something in this territory. This is the highest-credentialed corroboration in the public record — an actual NSA senior official neither confirming nor fully denying.
