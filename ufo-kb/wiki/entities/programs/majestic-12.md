# Majestic 12

Majestic 12 (MJ-12) is an alleged elite committee of military officers, intelligence officials, and scientists established to govern US policy on the UFO/UAP issue, including the management of retrieved non-human material and the suppression of public disclosure. It first entered public awareness through a set of disputed leaked documents in the 1980s and 1990s.

## Mandate

The alleged mandate of MJ-12 encompasses:
- Oversight of all recovered non-human craft and biological material.
- Management of reverse-engineering programs.
- Control of information flow regarding the UAP phenomenon within the US government and allied intelligence communities.
- Talent tracking and vetting of individuals who come into contact with sensitive UAP-related information — whether through official programs or incidental encounters.

The committee is alleged to operate outside normal congressional oversight and budgetary accountability, funded through unacknowledged special access programs (USAPs).

## Personnel

The original alleged MJ-12 membership (per the disputed Eisenhower Briefing Document) included figures such as Vannevar Bush, James Forrestal, Roscoe Hillenkoetter, and others. Whether these names reflect an actual committee or a disinformation construct remains unresolved.

What is notable in the context of [[09KP8XVf5nY]] is that Harald Malmgren does not use the term "MJ-12" directly, but references the same structure using the phrase **"the majestic"** — treating it not as a rumor or a legend, but as a routine operational reality he had encountered in his career. A senior official told Malmgren:

> *"Your name went in all the important books of talent keeps — not only CIA but you know all the majestic, all these guardians, they also themselves to protect the world."*

This framing — "the majestic" alongside "the guardians" — suggests Malmgren understood these to be distinct but related custodial structures, not a single monolithic committee. His casual, unremarkable use of the term is treated in [[09KP8XVf5nY]] as one of the more significant pieces of indirect corroboration for MJ-12's existence: a senior government insider referring to it as a known entity rather than a conspiracy theory.

Malmgren's own entry into talent-tracking books across multiple intelligence communities — CIA, "the majestic," and "the guardians" — appears to have occurred following his handling of recovered UAP material at Los Alamos after the [[1962-bluegill-uap-shootdown]] and his proximity to other sensitive information.

## Outputs / Findings

- The leaked MJ-12 documents (Eisenhower Briefing Document, Cutler-Twining memo, etc.) remain forensically contested; the FBI concluded some were fabricated, but has not resolved the question of whether the underlying committee existed.
- Malmgren's testimony in [[09KP8XVf5nY]] provides a qualitatively different form of corroboration: not documents, but an insider's casual reference to the structure as a functioning reality.
- The "guardians" framing — those who "protect the world" — aligns with the [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]] concept: a self-appointed custodial class that has decided, unilaterally, that the public cannot be trusted with UAP-related information.

## JFK–Dulles Document Corroboration (June 28, 1961)

A 1995 MJ12 leak (discussed by Ross Coulthart and Jeff Krushank on NewsNation) describes a top-secret JFK request to CIA director [[allen-dulles]] dated **June 28, 1961**, asking for a review of MJ12 intelligence operations as they relate to Cold War psychological warfare. When the 2022 JFK files were released, they confirmed that Dulles had two meetings scheduled with Kennedy on that exact date — a fact impossible for a forger to have known in 1995. This is treated as documentary cross-corroboration of at least this MJ12 document's authenticity.

JFK's inquiry into MJ12 came just two months after the Bay of Pigs disaster (April 1961), and preceded Dulles's dismissal by five months (November 1961). The sequence raises questions about whether JFK's cleaning house at the CIA overlapped with a confrontation over MJ12 activities — a connection developed further in [[jfk-assassination-ufo]].

## MAGIC — Greer's Current Name (1990s NRO Document)

[[steven-greer]] provides a more recent name in [[areO7Mej44E]]: **MAGIC** — Majority Joint Intelligence Committee (or variant "MAJIC"). Greer states this is the last name he can pinpoint on a document from the 1990s, originating from the NRO (National Reconnaissance Office). He characterizes MAGIC as:
- **Transnational** — not a US-only structure; supersedes national governments
- Built directly from the WWII OSS network of [[allen-dulles]] and [[paul-mellon]]
- Funded by fascist-sympathizer billionaires (Mellon, Ford, IBM's Watson, Prescott Bush; Union Bank, Brown Brothers Harriman, Chase Manhattan network)
- Operationally above congressional and presidential oversight
- Using surveillance and consciousness-based technologies that "penetrate time and space beyond conventional electromagnetic encryption"

Greer claims to have personally met MAGIC members. General [[albert-stubblebine]]'s May 1992 $2 billion bribe attempt is interpreted by Greer as an explicit MAGIC-affiliate contact.

## Detlev Bronk: The Rockefeller-Johns Hopkins Node (q7Czo77qhHA)

[[detlev-bronk]] is named in the disputed MJ-12 documents as one of the 12 members. In UFO lore he appears specifically as the physician who performed **alien autopsies** at [[1947-aztec]] and possibly [[1947-roswell]]. Jesse Michaels (in [[q7Czo77qhHA]]) draws attention to Bronk's simultaneous institutional roles:
- **President of the Rockefeller Foundation** — the philanthropic arm that funded the [[flexner-report]] and structured the modern US medical monopoly
- **President of Johns Hopkins University** — clearing ~$8 billion/year in medical research with extensive government funding

If the MJ-12 documents are accurate, the alleged alien biological specimen handling was being done by the same person who ran the institutions that determine what American medicine is and is not allowed to be. This creates a structural link between the UAP biological recovery program and the institutional suppression of alternative medicine — the same Rockefeller apparatus governing both.
