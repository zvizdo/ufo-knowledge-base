# DARPA N3 — Next-Generation Non-Surgical Neurotechnology

## Mandate

DARPA's N3 program (Next-Generation Non-Surgical Neurotechnology), launched in 2018, openly funds the development of **bidirectional brain-machine interfaces** for military application. The program brief explicitly names "control of unmanned aerial vehicles" as a use case — making it the most-on-the-record public US analog of the [[mind-controlled-craft]] hypothesis.

## Personnel

- [[battelle-memorial-institute]] — Phase 2 prime, partnered with [[afrl|Air Force Research Lab]] and Carnegie Mellon University on the [[brainstorms-bci|BrainSTORMS]] system

## Outputs / Findings

- Phase 2 milestone (Battelle press release, 2020): BrainSTORMS — "Brain System To Transmit Or Receive Magneto-electric Signals" — uses nanoscale magneto-electric devices injected into the bloodstream and magnetically guided into specific brain regions for bidirectional neural interfacing.
- The program's existence is used in [[OOr98r51Sj0]] to argue that the public-record BCI lineage already supports the "psionic asset connected to exotic craft via neural interface" claim made by [[jake-barber]].
