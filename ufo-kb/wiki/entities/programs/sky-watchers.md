# Sky Watchers

Sky Watchers (publicly branded as **Skywatcher**) is a private technology company dedicated to UAP detection, classification, and eventual contact with non-human intelligence. It operates as both an ethical alternative to legacy classified UAP programs and as a sophisticated civilian sensor platform operating at government-adjacent sites. The organization is co-led by [[jake-barber]] (co-founder, helicopter pilot, psionic and retrieval background) and [[james-valor]] (Lead Operations Officer, Army Special Operations ret., sensor systems architect).

## Origin

In 2021, during a government-contracted war game exercise, [[james-valor]]'s team accidentally discovered a Class 1 formation of seven UAPs while scanning from a rooftop. Camera operator Mr. Ko spotted the objects at 7 km, 2-3,000 ft overhead — invisible to human senses. Initial assumption: US classified technology. After two weeks of continued observation, they concluded the phenomenon was non-human origin and began building the technology company around it.

[[jake-barber]] joined later, bringing his background in helicopter retrieval of NHI craft (egg-shaped, mid-2000s California) and his connections to the psionic asset community.

## Mandate

Sky Watchers conducts:

1. **Systematic multi-spectrum UAP detection** — nomadic deployment across operational sites in California, West Texas, and other locations
2. **NHI contact operations** — using the dog-whistle signal and psionic assets to attract specific UAP classes
3. **Public data release** — toward eventual disclosure; five years of terabytes of data in storage
4. **Ethical psionic asset engagement** — unlike legacy [[p3-psionic-assets]] program: no drug cocktails, no treating operators as "pink assets"

The framing positions Sky Watchers as the ethical positive case: same capabilities as classified programs, fundamentally different ethics and transparency trajectory.

## Technology Platform

Mobile, multi-spectrum detection array deployed nomadically:

| System | Purpose | Note |
|--------|---------|------|
| Radar | Primary detection | Class 7 jellyfish caused 2+ hr sustained jamming |
| Electro-optical (EO) | Standard visual | Cannot detect UAPs emitting only in IR bands |
| Shortwave infrared (SWIR) | IR band 1 | Captures UAP infrared emissions |
| Medium-wave infrared (MWIR) | IR band 2 | Additional IR layer |
| RF signal detection + direction-finding | Geolocation of UAP signals | Real-time in-sky positioning |
| Helicopter | Interception attempts | Flown by [[jake-barber]]; immobilized by field in Feb 2025 |

The team maintains four independent detection channels simultaneously — when an object appears on radar, SWIR, MWIR, and RF simultaneously, prosaic explanations are ruled out.

## The Dog Whistle

An electromagnetic/RF frequency discovered by Mr. K that attracts UAPs to the operational area. Evidence for causal attribution:

- **January 2025 controlled-absence test**: Equipment deployed *without* dog whistle → zero detections
- **Standard operations with dog whistle**: Consistent daily UAP appearances
- The frequency and mechanism cannot be publicly disclosed

Combined with psionic assets (Jordan's team), the dog whistle is the central summoning mechanism. See [[uap-summoning-protocols]].

## Key Events

### 2021 — Discovery (War Game)
Class 1 formation of seven UAPs spotted during government exercise. Foundation of company.

### 2023 — 200-UAP Sortie Event
Approximately 200 Class 1 UAPs observed in 4-hour window, ingressing vertically from space. Three Class 1s descended below ridgelines and did not resurface. Searches found nothing.

### 2024 — Four Crash Events
Four events where UAPs (Class 8 and Class 1) descended to terrain and disappeared. No wreckage recovered from multi-hour ground searches.

### December 2024 — New Jersey Deployment
Deployed to [[pikatini-arsenal]] as part of DoD/FBI task force investigating [[2024-new-jersey-drone-flap]]. Orange orbs appeared within 30 minutes of arrival (assessed: conventional drones). Separate vertical atmospheric entry/exit phenomena observed to the south (assessed: genuine UAP). See full account in [[2024-new-jersey-drone-flap]].

### February 2025 — "Conflict in the Sky"
Most significant multi-class engagement to date. Jordan's prediction confirmed; naked-eye Class 7 hover; helicopter immobilization; two-hour radar jamming; Tic Tac terrain-hiding and intent-reading; two Class 7s flanking symmetrically at 6 km offset. See [[y_8IKKcTntQ]] for full account.

## Relationship to Legacy Programs

Barber described Sky Watchers explicitly in contrast to the legacy apparatus (see [[p3-psionic-assets]]). Same capabilities — psionic summoning, multi-spectrum detection — fundamentally different ethics. No drug cocktails. No treating operators as biological equipment. Goal: public disclosure, not operational secrecy.

Valor adds the government-relations dimension: Sky Watchers operates at government-contracted sites without NDAs, suggesting implicit government awareness and non-interference. His thesis: this is intentional [[soft-disclosure-civilian-observation]].
