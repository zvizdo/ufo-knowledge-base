# Avrocar Project

Declassified experimental saucer program, originally a joint operation in 1952 between the CIA's Office of Scientific Intelligence and British aircraft manufacturer Avro Canada, directed by engineer [[john-frost]]. The craft was saucer-shaped and used a radial gas turbine engine (not anti-gravity propulsion). It proved unable to fly effectively above a few feet.

## Significance in the Anti-Gravity Context

[[jesse-michaels]] raises the Avrocar for two reasons:

**1. Nazi tech transfer pipeline**: [[operation-paperclip]] brought German anti-gravity researchers ([[victor-schauberger]], [[richard-miethe]], [[henry-coanda]]) into the US, and the Avrocar context intersects with this transfer — [[henry-coanda]] consulted on the project at [[wright-patterson]].

**2. Propulsion motivation**: The saucer shape's aerodynamic limitations using conventional jet propulsion may have motivated the concurrent push for genuine anti-gravity propulsion theory — explaining why Wright-Patterson sponsored theoretical physics conferences ([[unc-chapel-hill]]) and maintained Josh Goldberg's [[aerospace-research-lab-wadc]].
