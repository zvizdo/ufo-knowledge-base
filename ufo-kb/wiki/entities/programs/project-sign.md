# Project Sign

The first official U.S. Air Force investigation of UFO reports, established in late 1947 following the wave of sightings that began with Kenneth Arnold's June 1947 report and the [[1947-roswell]] incident. Headquartered at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base.

## The Estimate of the Situation

Project Sign's most significant output was a classified document called the [[estimate-of-the-situation]], produced in 1948. It concluded — on the balance of evidence — that UFOs were most likely extraterrestrial in origin. The document was forwarded up the chain of command to [[horace-vandenberg]] (then Air Force Chief of Staff), who rejected it as unsupported and ordered all copies destroyed. The report effectively ended Project Sign's ET-hypothesis-sympathetic phase.

## Termination and Successor

Project Sign was renamed [[project-grudge]] in February 1949. The new name reflected the shift in institutional attitude: where Sign had at least entertained the ET hypothesis, Grudge was structured from the outset to debunk and dismiss reports.
