# Operation Mongoose

Kennedy administration CIA covert operations program targeting Castro's Cuba — economic sabotage, sugar field burning, assassination attempts. Operationally headquartered at a **University of Miami hangar**. Supervised by Robert Kennedy, theoretically; practically run by the CIA's Miami station under [[theodore-shackley]].

## Mandate

Destabilize and ultimately overthrow the Castro government through a combination of:
- Economic sabotage (sugar field burning, infrastructure disruption)
- Assassination attempts against Castro, Raul Castro, and Che Guevara
- Support for anti-Castro exile operations

## The S Force Connection

[[danny-sheehan]]'s account: Operation Mongoose and the S Force are operationally the same apparatus. The S Force was not a separate organization — it was the assassination team embedded within the broader Mongoose structure. When Kennedy believed he was running controlled anti-Castro operations, the S Force was simultaneously operating as an independent lethal capability that would later be turned against him.

## Key Personnel

- [[theodore-shackley]] — Miami CIA Station Chief; operational commander of the Mongoose apparatus
- [[howard-hunt]] — CIA officer involved in Operation Mongoose and the Bay of Pigs apparatus
- [[frank-sturgis]] — liaison to Swan Island; Mongoose operative; later Watergate burglar
- [[santos-trafficante]] — Mafia partner in the assassination pipeline embedded in Mongoose

## Aftermath

Kennedy terminated Mongoose following the Cuban Missile Crisis resolution in late 1962 — infuriating CIA/military figures who had been building toward a full Cuba invasion. [[curtis-lemay]] promoted [[operation-northwoods]] as an alternative approach. The frustrated anti-Castro apparatus — S Force included — continued operating with its own agenda.
