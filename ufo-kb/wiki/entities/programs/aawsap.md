# AAWSAP

The **Advanced Aerospace Weapons Systems Application Program** was a classified Pentagon / DIA program initiated in 2007, primarily funded through a $22 million congressional appropriation secured by Senator Harry Reid. It was administered in part through **Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies (BAASS)** — Robert Bigelow's contractor entity — and included extensive investigations at [[skinwalker-ranch]] in Utah.

## Mandate

AAWSAP's mandate was broader than its successor [[aatip]]: it investigated not only aerial anomalies but the full spectrum of reported paranormal phenomena associated with UAP contact — poltergeist activity, animal mutilations, physiological effects on witnesses, and anomalous biological phenomena. Its scope reflected an institutional willingness, circa 2007–2012, to treat UAP as embedded within a larger phenomenon rather than a narrowly aerospace question.

## Personnel

- [[lue-elizondo]] — was associated with AAWSAP and later became director of [[aatip]]
- Robert Bigelow — funded contractor entity (BAASS) that administered much of the investigation
- Senator Harry Reid — key congressional champion; secured the initial funding

## Outputs / Findings

The program produced a series of Defense Intelligence Reference Documents (DIRDs) — technical reports on topics ranging from warp drive physics to psychotronics to UAP propulsion. Many of these have been released or leaked, providing one of the few paper trails from the modern classified UAP research era. The [[skinwalker-ranch]] investigations produced extensive anomalous event documentation that has not been fully made public.

## Crash Retrieval Material Access Attempt (Davis Account)

[[eric-davis]] reveals in [[LnAiNChnuEQ]] that a central operational goal during AAWSAP was gaining access to physical crash retrieval materials held by [[lockheed-martin]] (unnamed by Davis but identifiable in context). AAWSAP held TSSI clearance but needed SAP-level access to receive the materials. [[harry-reid]] tried to get SAP elevation through [[william-lynn]] (wrong channel). [[glenn-gaffney]] (CIA S&T Director) independently blocked the material transfer. Both efforts failed. The materials remained with Lockheed.
