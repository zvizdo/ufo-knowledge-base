# Soviet UAP Program

> *Stub page — expand with dedicated Soviet UAP documentation imports.*

The Soviet Union conducted classified investigation of UAP reports throughout the Cold War, parallel to the US [[project-blue-book]] and the USAF Scientific Advisory Board investigations. Soviet programs included military incident documentation, KGB file collection, and scientific analysis. Partially declassified Soviet files show:

- Systematic UAP documentation near nuclear weapons facilities
- Incidents analogous to the US [[malmstrom-air-force-base]] missile-deactivation events
- Scientific committees that occasionally reached conclusions similar to the US JANAP 146 reporting framework

The **1955 Azerbaijan disc sighting** ([[1955-azerbaijan-disc]]) adds a specific datum: a controlled launch area with searchlights pointing skyward from a Soviet facility implies that at minimum, Soviet authorities were aware of disc-type vehicles operating in their airspace and had infrastructure in place to monitor or direct them.
