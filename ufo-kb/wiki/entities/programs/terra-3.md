# Terra-3 / 5K17 / Omega — Soviet ABM Laser Weapons Complex

> *Stub. Expand with system technical details, post-1991 disclosures, and UAP-related cross-references.*

Soviet directed-energy weapons research complex located at the [[sary-shagan-test-range]] in Kazakhstan SSR. The Terra-3 system and its Omega/5K17 sub-system were developed by KB Vympel under GRAU sponsorship for ABM (anti-ballistic missile) and ASAT (anti-satellite) experimental engagement. The program ran from the early 1970s through the 1980s; the complex has been documented extensively in post-1991 Russian-language and Western open-source publications.

## Earliest CIA Trace (1973-vintage HUMINT)

[[cia-uap-d1-sary-shagan-1973]] (CIA Directorate of Operations cable FIRK-311/01638-77, distributed 20 December 1977, released 2026 as part of DoW PURSUE Release 02) contains one of the earliest US-IC paper trails:

> "According to hearsay, experiments involving laser weapons were being conducted at an unknown location at the range. Supposedly, the tests involved powerful antennas (no further details)."

The same cable contains a separate UAP observation by the source at Site 7 of the same range in late summer 1973 ([[1973-sary-shagan-green-uap]]); the CIA case officer makes no link between the two paragraphs, but their co-location in the same report is itself a noteworthy archival feature.

## UAP Relevance

- Co-located with the 1973 Sary Shagan green concentric-ring UAP observation; potential (but not asserted by the document) confound for any visible-luminous phenomena observed during DEW testing
- Reagan-era reports of Terra-3 illuminating US satellites (KH-9, Challenger STS-41-G) over Sary Shagan are part of the broader Cold War DEW history relevant to UAP analyses
