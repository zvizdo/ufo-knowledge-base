# NASA UAP Review Panel

The NASA Independent Study Team on UAP was announced in 2022 and produced its final report in 2023. It was chaired by astrophysicist [[david-spergel]], then president of the Simons Foundation (the philanthropy of hedge fund billionaire [[jim-simons]]).

## Report and Criticisms

The final report runs approximately 10 pages. [[kevin-knuth]] criticizes it for:
1. **Failure to engage the astronaut record** — The Alan Bean/Skylab UFO and Gemini 11 battery-drain incident (both disclosed by Knuth on American Alchemy) were not included or assessed. NASA's own astronauts reported UAP phenomena that the panel did not address.
2. **Insufficient depth** — France's [[cometa-report]] (1999) is approximately 100 pages and explicitly concludes the extraterrestrial hypothesis is plausible. The NASA panel, backed by NASA's institutional resources, produced a fraction of the analysis.
3. **Contrast with AARO** — While the [[aaro]] (All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office) operates within DoD, the NASA panel was intended as an independent scientific voice. It did not play that role.

## The Spergel-Simons Connection

[[eric-weinstein]] had previously speculated that Jim Simons might be channeling wealth into UFO research — a theory [[jesse-michaels]] had dismissed. When the panel was announced with Spergel (Simons's top science advisor) as chair, the connection suddenly became visible. [[kevin-knuth]] does not confirm or deny this hypothesis.
