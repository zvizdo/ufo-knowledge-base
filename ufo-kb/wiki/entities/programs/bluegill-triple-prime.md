# Bluegill Triple Prime

Bluegill Triple Prime was the final successful high-altitude nuclear test of [[operation-dominic|Operation Fishbowl]], detonated on October 26, 1962, above Johnston Island in the Marshall Islands. It is the only Fishbowl test whose complete film record has not been accounted for, and it is the alleged site of the first known US downing of a UAP — achieved not intentionally, but as a consequence of the test's X-ray pulse.

## Mandate

The test was part of [[operation-dominic|Operation Dominic]]'s Fishbowl sub-series, designed to measure nuclear weapon effects at high altitude, including X-ray propagation, EMP generation, and effects on re-entry vehicles. Bluegill Triple Prime was itself a remediation attempt — earlier Bluegill iterations had failed due to range safety destructions and other issues, making this the third and final attempt at the configuration.

Two KC-135 aircraft were tasked with filming the detonation under the "Kettle" program:
- **Kettle 1** — operated by Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory; partially declassified in 1998.
- **Kettle 2** — operated by Los Alamos National Laboratory; reported "unable to locate" by the Department of Energy (DOE) in response to Jeffrey Kruchshank's 2023 mandatory declassification review (MDR) request.

A post-test report from the **Flight Dynamics Laboratory** (Wright-Patterson Air Force Base) noted a "second thermal source" in the footage — an anomaly that official investigators attempted to explain through conventional means.

The white triangle visible in one frame of the partially declassified Kettle 1 footage was applied as a sanitizing redaction by **Byron Ristvet** of the Defense Special Weapons Agency during the 1998 partial release.

## Outputs / Findings

**The UAP Shootdown (alleged):** Richard Bissell told Harald Malmgren that a UAP "tag-along" was monitoring the Bluegill Triple Prime detonation so closely that the X-ray burst from the nuclear device downed it. Bissell stated that the craft "tumbled into the ocean" and was recovered by the US Navy.

**The Recovered Material:** The material was transported to Los Alamos National Laboratory. Malmgren, at Geist's request, handled it there. His reported observations:
- The material felt **weightless** despite being a physical object.
- It appeared the **"color of something in space"** — not a color easily described in terrestrial terms.
- It appeared to **telepathically communicate** with Malmgren — he heard words in his mind, an experience he connected retroactively to the [[stargate-program|Stargate Program]]'s findings on non-local consciousness.

**The AVCO / Atlas 8F Parallel (September 1962):** A separate but related incident at Cape Canaveral in September 1962 involved a UAP that "phased into existence" alongside an AVCO Mark IV re-entry vehicle riding an Atlas 8F booster at Mach 18. The official report acknowledged the anomaly but stated the "origin could not be determined."

**The SDI Lineage:** The X-ray pulse mechanism that incidentally downed the UAP at Bluegill Triple Prime became the conceptual basis for Edward Teller's Project Excalibur (X-ray laser anti-missile weapon), which in turn seeded the [[strategic-defense-initiative]]. Per the argument developed in [[09KP8XVf5nY]], if the directed-energy anti-UAP concept was originally provided by the Roswell survivor (see [[1947-roswell]]), then the US was weaponizing alien-origin intelligence against subsequent UAP visitors.
