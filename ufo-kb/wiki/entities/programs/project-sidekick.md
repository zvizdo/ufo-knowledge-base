# Project Sidekick

One of three classified programs [[bob-lazar]] encountered briefing documents for during his first full day at [[s4]]. Sidekick's stated focus was on weaponizing the craft's propulsion system.

## What Lazar Knows

According to Lazar's reading of the briefing documents, Project Sidekick investigated using the [[s4-sport-model]]'s three gravity-wave emitters to focus a **directed-energy particle beam** as a weapon. The three emitters — when operating in "delta mode" — were already configured to focus all three gravity amplifiers onto a single destination point. Sidekick appears to have explored extending this focusing capability as a weapon system.

Sidekick was not Lazar's assignment; he was assigned to [[project-galileo-s4]] (propulsion reverse-engineering). He read only the overview.

## Significance

The existence of Sidekick, alongside the high-priority "remote disable" directive within [[project-galileo-s4]], suggests the program's military principals were at least as interested in the craft as a weapons platform as they were in replicating its propulsion. This is consistent with the overall intelligence posture Lazar describes — the program operated as if the craft were a potential threat to be neutralized or exploited, not simply a scientific curiosity.
