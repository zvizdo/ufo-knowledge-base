# Gateway Process

The Gateway Process is a structured consciousness-expansion program developed by [[robert-monroe]] and the [[monroe-institute]] using Hemi-Sync binaural beat audio technology. Participants train to achieve specific altered states — including out-of-body experiences (OBEs), deep relaxation, and non-ordinary perception — by listening to audio tracks that synchronize left and right brain hemispheres.

## CIA Study

In 1983, the US Army commissioned an analysis of the Gateway Process as part of broader interest in psychic phenomena and human potential. Analyst Wayne McDonnell produced a classified report — "Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process" — which concluded the program was scientifically coherent and potentially useful for intelligence applications. The report remained classified until FOIA release in 2003, and gained viral attention after 2021.
