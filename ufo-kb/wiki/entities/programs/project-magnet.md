# Project Magnet

Project Magnet was a Canadian government study of UFOs conducted between 1950 and 1954 under [[wilbert-smith]], a Department of Transport radio engineer with a keen interest in UAP propulsion physics.

## Origin — The Sarbacher Meeting

The program's launch is directly traceable to Wilbert Smith's intelligence-gathering. In 1950, Smith attended a meeting in Washington and spoke privately with [[robert-sarbacher]], a defense scientist and consultant. Sarbacher disclosed to Smith:

- Flying saucers exist
- They are classified "two points higher than the H-bomb" — the most secret subject in the US government
- [[vannevar-bush]] was leading the recovery investigation
- The propulsion involved manipulation of the **Earth's magnetic field**

Smith memorialized the Sarbacher conversation in a classified memo to Canadian authorities, which was later obtained via FOIA and remains one of the most credible contemporaneous documents in the UAP record — it predates any public UFO discourse on these topics.

## Research Program

Based on Sarbacher's hint about geomagnetism, Project Magnet focused on:
- Measuring anomalies in Earth's geomagnetic field that might correlate with UAP presence
- Exploring whether a UAP propulsion system could exploit geomagnetic flux
- Developing a detection apparatus sensitive enough to identify craft using this principle

The research was formally ended in 1954 after a geomagnetic anomaly was detected at the Shirley's Bay station that could not be explained — a result Smith privately believed was a UAP measurement.

## Significance

Project Magnet represents the only known case of a government UAP research program explicitly initiated because a government official received an insider briefing from the US national security apparatus. All other programs (Project Blue Book, AATIP, etc.) were reactive; Magnet was proactively founded on foreign intelligence.
