# Special Access Program Oversight Committee (SAPOC)

The body within the Pentagon responsible for reviewing and approving every **special access program (SAP)** in the Department of Defense. SAPs account for approximately 75–80% of all classified programs at the most sensitive tier — this is where next-generation weapons systems, classified craft, and any programs involving recovered UAP materials would nominally reside.

The SAPOC operates under the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Acquisition, Technology, and Logistics (OSD AT&L).

## McCasland as Executive Secretary

From June 2009 to May 2011, [[neil-mccasland]] served as Director of Special Programs at OSD AT&L — and per his Wikipedia biography, was executive secretary of SAPOC. In this role, he was effectively the administrative gatekeeper for every SAP in the Pentagon — including any programs involving UAP reverse engineering, exotic propulsion, or recovered materials.

This position makes McCasland the single most credentialed figure in UAP disclosure discussions when it comes to documented access to the apex of the classification system. It is the position that Tom DeLonge's email to Podesta references when calling McCasland "the man in charge of all of this stuff."

## Why This Matters for UAP

If any UAP reverse engineering program exists within the Pentagon's SAP structure, SAPOC's executive secretary would have formal administrative awareness of it — not necessarily operational access to classified details, but at minimum knowledge of the program's existence and structure. McCasland's subsequent move to command AFRL (with its alleged Roswell debris connections) follows directly from his SAPOC tenure.
