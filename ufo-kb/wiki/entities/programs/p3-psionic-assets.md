# P3 Psionic Asset Program

The informal name ("P3" — Psionic Potential Predisposition) for an alleged classified program that identifies, recruits, and operationally deploys humans with innate psionic ability to attract and interface with non-human craft. First disclosed to [[michael-herrera]] by [[jake-barber]] as the explanation for the [[2009-indonesia-ufo]] encounter's container cargo.

## Mechanism

Per Barber's account:

1. **Identification:** Individuals with high psionic potential are identified — often from third-world countries, though also reportedly via gifted-and-talented programs within the US (see [[psionics-uap-interface]])
2. **Induction:** Drug cocktails may be administered to raise consciousness states and amplify psionic receptivity
3. **Deployment:** Assets are transported (in refrigerated/HVAC containers with Pelican cases) to operational sites where NHI craft are expected or can be attracted
4. **Asset retention:** Those who "don't make the cut" are retained at black sites as support/maintenance workers — effectively unable to leave
5. **Internal designation:** The legacy programs refer to these individuals as **"pink assets"** — a dehumanizing term treating them as biological equipment

## The 2009 Indonesia Case

The refrigerated containers and cargo trucks loading onto the octagonal craft during [[michael-herrera]]'s encounter were, per Barber, carrying P3 assets — not drugs as Herrera had originally suspected. This recasts the encounter entirely: the "loading operation" was the deployment of psionic humans aboard or associated with the craft.

## Ethical Context

[[jake-barber]] cited moral discomfort with how the P3 program treats its assets as a reason for defecting from legacy programs and founding [[sky-watchers]], which conducts similar contact operations without drug cocktails and without treating operators as cattle.

> ⚠ All claims here derive from [[jake-barber]]'s account to [[michael-herrera]]; no independent verification or documentary corroboration. The "P3" designation is Barber's informal label, not necessarily the program's actual classified name.
