# Project STORK

Classified research program reportedly operated by [[battelle-memorial-institute]] as the **hidden counterpart to [[project-blue-book]]**. While Blue Book was the public-facing Air Force UFO investigation, Project STORK allegedly handled the classified materials science side of the same program — analyzing physical samples from crash retrievals, particularly the 1947 Roswell materials.

## Classification Mechanism

Project STORK was classified under the **CIA Office of Scientific Intelligence** using the **McMahon Secrecy Act** (the legislative predecessor to the Atomic Energy Commission classification framework). This mechanism allowed Battelle to receive and work on materials that would otherwise fall under Air Force or DoD classification, routing the work through the atomic energy secrecy apparatus rather than conventional military channels.

## Materials Work

Per [[nTiFs8LudUo]], Battelle's 1949 publications included research on:
- **Shaped metal alloys** with exotic physical properties
- **Nickel-titanium composites** (the precursor to [[nitinol]])
- Materials with extreme strength-to-weight ratios achieved via molecular grid structures

[[robert-sarbacher]] is described as working with Battelle on materials analysis during this period. Sarbacher later described to [[whitley-strieber]] the **molecular grid structures** present in recovered materials — consistent with the nanoscale engineering Battelle's 1949 work touched.

## Fort Detrick / Biological Materials

Battelle's scope at this time also apparently included **biological materials research** at [[fort-detrick]] — consistent with the broader program's suspected custody of non-human biological entities and the [[nhi-biologics]] dimension of crash retrievals.

## Dougway Connection

Battelle was involved in the construction of **enclosed laboratory facilities at [[dougway-proving-grounds]]** in Utah, which supports the "Dougway as new Area 51" thesis. A witness described seeing a craft hovering inside a Dougway hangar during this period.

## Relationship to Blue Book

The Project STORK / Blue Book duality is structurally similar to the AATIP / AAWSAP duality decades later: a public program absorbs UAP reports and debunks/dismisses them, while a classified parallel program does the real analysis. Battelle's role as an FFRDC made it well-suited to serve as the insulated technical layer.
