# ZODIAC Program

Alleged crash retrieval and reverse-engineering oversight program reportedly operated by [[trw]] during the approximately 1988–2001 window. If the account is accurate, ZODIAC represents one link in the evolutionary chain from [[majestic-12]] through various successor names as the oversight structure adapted to new political and contractor environments.

## Origins and Evidence Base

**1998 UFO Magazine article:** A story written under the pseudonym **"Greg Halifax"** described a group called the "Sedge Masters" associated with a program called ZODIAC. [[richard-dolan]] subsequently tracked down and identified the likely author, concluding the account had internal consistency and investigative credibility.

**Email chain:** [[kit-green]] (CIA), [[hal-puthoff]] (physicist, AATIP), and [[eric-davis]] (exotic propulsion specialist) exchanged emails discussing the ZODIAC program. The content of these exchanges has not been fully disclosed publicly, but their existence suggests the program's name circulated in informal channels among cleared researchers.

**TRW context:** TRW — a defense/aerospace contractor — was acquired by [[northrop-grumman]] in **2002**, one year after the alleged end of ZODIAC's active period. The transition from TRW to Northrop Grumman mirrors the broader consolidation of aerospace primes that occurred in the late 1990s/early 2000s and may have restructured program ownership.

## Evolutionary Chain

Per [[nTiFs8LudUo]], the oversight group managing recovered craft materials apparently moved through several names:

1. **[[majestic-12]]** (1947 founding) — original crash retrieval oversight
2. **UFO Working Group** (1986) — renamed per [[philip-corso]]'s *Dawn of a New Age* manuscript, created under Army INSCOM by [[albert-stubblebine]]
3. **ZODIAC** (~1988–2001) — TRW-operated; "Sedge Masters" account
4. **Post-2002** — CIA's Office of Global Access (OGA) created 2002; TRW absorbed by Northrop; program likely migrated to new contractor shell

**[[eric-walker]]** (Penn State president, alleged Majestic 12 attendee, 1965 [[1965-kecksburg]] witness) in 1987 gave cryptic responses to researchers suggesting the group was still active under some name: "chasing windmills" and a reference to the impossibility of understanding the program without ESP. His remarks suggest continuity through the 1987 period.

**[[john-alexander]]'s** contradictory claim — that Majestic 12 was a continuity program with no UAP connection — is noted as a counter-claim, possibly consistent with need-to-know compartmentalization rather than actual non-existence.

## Relationship to Wilson-Davis Memo

[[thomas-wilson]] (former J2 Joint Chiefs of Staff) met [[eric-davis]] in a parking lot in 2002 and expressed frustration at being **cut out of rogue program groups** he had identified as operating outside official oversight. Wilson's account is consistent with the ZODIAC/contractor-run model: programs operating through contractor shells that senior military officials cannot access even with appropriate clearance. The Wilson-Davis memo documents this dynamic. See [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]].
