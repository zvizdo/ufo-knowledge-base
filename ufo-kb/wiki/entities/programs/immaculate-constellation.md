# Immaculate Constellation

A whistleblower report published by journalist [[michael-shellenberger]] in November 2024 describes a classified US government UAP program called **Immaculate Constellation**. Shellenberger's source identified it as a USAP — unacknowledged special access program — meaning it is not officially acknowledged even within the broader classified community and falls outside normal Congressional oversight.

## Mandate

Per the report, Immaculate Constellation functions as a **total-information strategic intelligence project** for UAP evaluation. It integrates:
- **SIGINT** (signals intelligence)
- **HUMINT** (human intelligence reports)
- **Still imagery and video** collected across multiple collection platforms
- Databases of UAP incidents, classified and compartmentalized from mainstream defense channels

The report includes a section categorizing different UAP types and documenting **psychological and biological effects** reported by witnesses — a level of detail that suggests systematic long-duration collection, not ad hoc incident response.

## Structural Attribution

Two sources gave Shellenberger different organizational framings:
1. **Pre-publication source**: Immaculate Constellation is a **DoD program**.
2. **Post-publication source**: It is "a USAP controlled by the White House but administered by the DoD."

Shellenberger reports the second characterization as single-source and unverified at time of publication. The inconsistency may reflect genuine structural ambiguity, deliberate obfuscation, or source compartmentalization. This mirrors the broader pattern described by Shellenberger: UAP programs span DoD, White House, CIA, NSA, and private contractors without clear consolidated ownership. See [[ufo-secrecy-cabal]].

## Congressional Response

The report was tweeted by Shellenberger immediately before the [[2024-congressional-uap-hearing]] at [[rayburn-house-office-building]] and discussed during that session. Congressional members sought to understand the program's oversight structure.

[[lue-elizondo]], asked if he was "read into" Immaculate Constellation, responded: "I can neither confirm nor deny." Shellenberger notes that if the program did not exist, a flat denial would be the natural response — making the non-denial significant.

## Broader Context

The existence of a program with this architecture — integrating multiple intelligence streams, categorizing witness effects, active over an extended period — is presented as evidence that the government's UAP engagement goes far beyond the "we only have blurry videos" public posture. The program's name was apparently chosen by someone; it echoes Catholic imagery (the Immaculate Conception), a recurring motif in UAP program nomenclature alongside religiously-tinged classified project names.

## Matthew Brown Disclosure (aGqFOxuYRT0)

A second independent disclosure attributed to whistleblower [[matthew-brown-whistleblower]] (via [[jeremy-corbell]] and [[george-knapp]]) describes Immaculate Constellation as a document characterizing **trans-medium UAP** — objects that transit between aerial and underwater environments. Key claim from this source:

- **Made to purpose**: Some trans-medium objects are engineered for specific missions. A craft designed for deep-sea operation is optimized for that mission profile rather than being a general-purpose vehicle.
- [[jesse-michaels]] applies this framing to the [[baltic-sea-anomaly]]: an object with 90° angles, corridors, a "breathing" hole, and apparent energy output may be "built to spec for some specific purpose" — a seafloor installation rather than a crashed vehicle.

The Brown disclosure and the Shellenberger disclosure may describe the same program from different angles (intelligence-collection architecture vs. categorization of objects), or may represent different compartments within the same program.
