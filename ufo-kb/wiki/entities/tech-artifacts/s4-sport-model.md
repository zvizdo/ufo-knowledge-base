# S4 Sport Model

The recovered nonhuman craft [[bob-lazar]] was assigned to reverse-engineer during his ~1987–1989 tenure at [[s4]]. It was one of nine craft simultaneously present in S4's hangar bays; Lazar worked specifically on this one.

## Physical Description

### Exterior
- **Diameter**: ~52–53 feet
- **No seams, rivets, panels, buttons, wiring, or visible controls** anywhere on the exterior
- **Color**: "Pewter stainless" at close range — a flat, metallic silver-gray
- **Distance effect**: At any meaningful distance, the craft looks "cartoonish" — like an optical illusion or animation artifact; [[luigi-vendetti]] reproduced this accidentally in his CGI model without intending to; Lazar confirmed it as accurate
- **Bottom**: Three large "trash can"-sized amplifier/emitter structures (also called the "three humps")
- **Upper ring**: An insulator ring; above it are small square/rectangular openings that appear "blacker than black" — deeper than the surrounding material; Lazar describes them as seemingly bottomless pits; assumed to be planar array sensors for stellar navigation
- **Hull material hypothesis**: Lazar and Vendetti increasingly believe the hull material is an **electret** — a material holding a permanent stored electric field. High voltage was detectable on the craft's exterior during operation. This connects to [[charles-buhler]]'s electrostatics research and [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s foundational asymmetric capacitor work.

### Interior
- **Floor span**: 52–53 feet with no visible central support column; [[luigi-vendetti]]'s CGI structural analysis confirms no Earth material could support this span — a physics impossibility under known engineering constraints
- **Height**: Cannot stand fully upright except near the center
- **Three humps**: Described as "seats" by personnel, but Lazar believes they are the amplifier structures
- **Archway**: Can become transparent — Lazar and Vendetti believe this is how the occupants view the outside
- **3D floating symbols**: Korean-like characters visible on the interior wall — not on a screen, not a projection, but freestanding three-dimensional characters in the air

## Propulsion System

The craft uses three operational modes:
- **Omicron mode** (local hover): Single emitter active; craft hovers with characteristic unstable wobbling motion
- **Delta mode** (travel): All three emitters focused on a single destination; craft locks in space, then "brings the destination to itself" by warping/bending spacetime; pulsed at approximately 10-millisecond intervals

Power source: [[s4-reactor]] using [[element-115]] as fuel.

## The Structural Impossibility

[[luigi-vendetti]] built a hyperrealistic CGI model of the craft to Lazar's stated specifications. The engineering analysis showed the 52–53 ft floor at the stated thickness has no support structure — no Earth material would prevent catastrophic collapse across that span. Lazar confirmed: "That's 100% true."

This is arguably the most rigorous single piece of evidence that the craft is not of human manufacture: not a witness statement, not a document, but a physics constraint derived from Lazar's own description.

## Provenance

[[barry-castillo]] told Lazar the sport model was recovered by the U.S. Navy from an underwater "archaeological dig." This was not in the briefing documents Lazar read. The body of water is unknown. Barry's characterization of it as an "archaeological dig" implies the craft was not recently crashed but had been there for an extended period.

## Nine Craft / Manufacturing Implication

Nine craft were simultaneously present at S4, all structurally different in shape but made of the same material and all with identical reactors. Lazar's inference: **they were manufactured, not independently crashed**. You don't find nine identical-reactor craft from nine separate crashes. A manufacturing facility produced them — implying an ongoing NHI capability rather than ancient artifacts from a single event.
