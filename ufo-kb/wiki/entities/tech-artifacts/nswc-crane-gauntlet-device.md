# NSWC Crane Gauntlet Device

## Description

A metallic arm-mounted object observed by [[randy-anderson]] in the underground **Off-World Technology Division** at [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]] in March 2014. Anderson described it as something one would wear on the arm — comparable to a gauntlet or arm-mounted shield — with a rectangular **crystalline display screen** on or adjacent to it. The object sat on a table and was not levitating (unlike the [[nswc-crane-levitating-orb]] nearby).

When Anderson looked at the display, **shifting hieroglyphic symbols** appeared and cycled — not static, but moving and changing, resembling the countdown display on the Predator wrist device. The effect appeared integrated into reality rather than holographic in a discernible light-projection sense.

## Cadre Briefing

Instructors noted that:
- Someone was **possibly harmed or even killed** during the retrieval of this item
- The cause was an **energy discharge** from the device

No further details about the source, recovery operation, or current status were given.

## Hieroglyphic Symbols

The shifting symbols observed on the device's display are a significant cross-reference point. Multiple independent accounts describe visually similar hieroglyphic or geometric symbols in UAP contexts:

- **[[danny-sheehan]]** (1977): Sheehan viewed Blue Book classified microfiche photos of a crashed UFO in a snow-covered embankment. Using a helipad as a transparent overlay on the microfiche viewer, he traced a ring of symbols from the dome of the craft. He subsequently drew them from memory. The symbols match those Anderson described seeing years later.
- **DMT research** (laser diffraction): Researchers doing DMT experiments shone a laser through a diffraction grating in a manner replicating the double-slit experiment. Participants under DMT reported seeing the same symbols in their visual field as appeared in the diffraction pattern — interpreted as a "source code" layer of reality. Anderson's symbols visually resemble the images from these experiments when shown documentary footage.

See [[nhi-hieroglyphic-symbols]] for the full cross-reference analysis.

## Analysis / Findings

The arm-mounted form factor with a control/display screen raises the possibility of a **wearable interface device** — consistent with the [[nswc-crane-levitating-orb]]'s claimed consciousness-interface function. If both objects are from the same craft, they may represent the control-input (gauntlet) and propulsion-interface (orb) components of a unified flight system.

The energy discharge that reportedly harmed the retrieval operative is consistent with other accounts of field-distortion or radiation effects near recovered NHI hardware. See [[bluegill-uap-debris]] and [[arts-parts]] for related material discussions.
