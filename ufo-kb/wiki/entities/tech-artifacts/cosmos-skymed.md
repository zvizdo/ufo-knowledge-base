# Cosmos SkyMed

Italian Earth-observation satellite constellation operated by the Italian Space Agency (ASI) and the Italian Ministry of Defense. Provides Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imaging with civilian and military applications.

## Specifications (2nd Generation, Spotlight-2 mode)
- **Scan footprint**: 5 × 5 km
- **Resolution**: ~1 m × 1 m (submetric)
- **Integration time**: 15 seconds per pass
- **Repeat geometry**: 16-day orbital cycle for same-target geometry
- **Product used**: "Spotlight 2" (civilian application)

A 3rd satellite of the 2nd-generation constellation was added to orbit ~2024.

## Relevance to Giza Research

[[filippo-biondi]]'s [[beyond-protocol]] uses Cosmos SkyMed data as the primary input for his SAR Doppler tomography scanning of the [[giza-plateau]]. The satellite's 2022-era data formed the basis of the original Giza substructure paper; updated passes continue to inform ongoing analysis.
