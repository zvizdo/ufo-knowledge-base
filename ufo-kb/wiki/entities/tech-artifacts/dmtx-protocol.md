# DMTX Protocol

A pharmacological technology for **sustained stable DMT breakthrough experiences**, repurposing a standard anesthesiology technique (target-controlled intravenous infusion, TCII) to maintain precise brain concentrations of DMT over extended periods. The key enabling property: unlike LSD or psilocybin, **DMT shows no tolerance build-up** — repeated doses at 30-minute intervals produce identical intensity.

## Development History

1. **1990s**: [[rick-strassman]] documents that DMT tolerance does not build; identifies the pharmacological anomaly that will later enable DMTX
2. **2015**: [[andrew-gallimore]] and [[rick-strassman]] co-author a paper proposing DMTX — using TCII with DMT to sustain breakthrough states; Strassman provides archived blood-sampling data for pharmacokinetic modeling; paper establishes theoretical feasibility; media attention for "alien communication machines" and "the matrix machine"
3. **Imperial College (~2021+)**: [[chris-timmermann]] implements DMTX with a more refined pharmacokinetic model; first successful human trials; extends DMT state to **30 minutes**; no major adverse physiological effects; anxiety peaks at onset then settles; intensity stable throughout
4. **Basel group (Matias Lekai)**: extends to **90 minutes**
5. **Unknown group**: extends to **6 hours** at low dose
6. **New Nautics / endoDMTX**: [[andrew-gallimore]] and [[new-nautics]] developing "endoDMTX" — using peptide injection to modulate endogenous INMT inhibition rather than administering exogenous DMT; relies on characterizing the regulatory peptide (University of Florida research)

## The Proposed Research Program

[[andrew-gallimore]]'s agenda: send trained specialists into DMTX states to systematically study what entities do:
- **Mathematicians and topologists**: describe geometric structure of the space; evaluate mathematical operations performed by entities
- **Linguists**: analyze entity communication patterns; assess whether they follow linguistic structure rules (informational density, syntax)
- **Phenomenologists**: map entity typology, hierarchy, and behavior systematically

Gallimore's metaphor: DMTX is "going from scuba diving to deep-sea diving — you have a direct line back to the boat, you can stay at the bottom and do real work."

[[andres-gomez-emilsson]]'s four-color theorem observation (via standard DMT, not DMTX) is the prototype of what this research program aims to generate systematically.

## Eleusis Mind Retreat

[[new-nautics]] is developing **Eleusis Mind** retreat center in St. Vincent and the Grenadines — providing DMTX experiences in a structured, medically supervised setting (anesthesiologists, psychiatrists, nurses) with preparation, titration, and integration. Website: elusismind.com.
