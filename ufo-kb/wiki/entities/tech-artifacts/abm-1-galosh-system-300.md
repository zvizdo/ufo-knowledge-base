# ABM-1 GALOSH / System-300 / Aldan

> *Stub. Expand with operational history, deployment timeline, and any other UAP-adjacent intelligence reporting as it enters the KB.*

Soviet anti-ballistic missile of the A-35 Moscow ABM defense system. The NATO designation is ABM-1 GALOSH; Soviet system designators include System-300, Aldan, A-350Zh, and 5V61. The missile was deployed around Moscow from the late 1960s through replacement by ABM-3 GAZELLE under the A-135 system in the 1980s-1990s. Designed for exo-atmospheric nuclear interception of incoming ballistic-missile reentry vehicles.

Per CIA HUMINT cable FIRK-311/01638-77 (released 2026 as [[cia-uap-d1-sary-shagan-1973]]), warhead specifications observed at [[sary-shagan-test-range]] Site 4 (warhead checkout unit V/Ch 03142) in 1972–1973:

- Warhead length: ~2 metres
- Warhead diameter: 80–100 centimetres
- Warhead weight: ~400 kilograms
- Contents: black cassettes 45×12 cm, "unrecalled number," each housing "an unknown number of metal balls (shariki)" — fragmentation-type warhead
- Source had no knowledge of nuclear loadings; opined "all systems could be equipped with nuclear warheads"; all work in source's department was "basically experimental (opytnaya)"
- Warhead checkout at Site 4 → missile transferred to Site 35 for launch
