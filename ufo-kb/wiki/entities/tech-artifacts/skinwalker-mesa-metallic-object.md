# Skinwalker Mesa Metallic Object

A large metallic anomaly buried under the Mesa — the rocky ridge forming the northern edge of [[skinwalker-ranch]]. Discovered and partially characterized under [[brandon-fugel]]'s ownership using ground-penetrating radar, soil resistivity surveys, and direct drilling.

## Discovery

The Mesa had long been identified as the focal point of electromagnetic anomalies on the ranch. Under Fugel's team, systematic ground-penetrating radar (GPR) and soil resistivity surveys established the presence of a large metallic structure beneath it. The surveys defined the basic geometry: a large, hard object.

[[aaron-blunt]] was brought in to drill. The drill went horizontally, then when angled upward, hit an impenetrable hard object. Blunt scouted the edges and concluded the object was approximately **400 feet wide**. The team could not break through it.

A snake camera sent to get visual access was blocked by a large boulder at the chamber entrance.

## Analysis

Hundreds of metal shards collected during drilling were sent to metallurgist [[ravi-chandran]]. Findings:
- The material is **fused metal** — not naturally occurring aggregate
- Contains **europium** and **tellurium** — rare earth elements whose co-occurrence in a fused manufactured state is not consistent with natural geological formation
- Chandran's conclusion: *"This material was manufactured as opposed to a natural occurrence."*

## Significance

The europium/tellurium composition is unusual. Europium is used in phosphors, lasers, and some nuclear control rods; tellurium has semiconductor properties and is used in thermoelectric devices. Their fused co-occurrence at depth under a site with intense electromagnetic anomalies raises questions about:
- Whether the object is the source of the EM anomalies that plague the Mesa
- Whether it constitutes a UAP propulsion or power system buried on/in the mesa
- The relationship between the object and the recurring UAP activity in "the Triangle" area above it (as above so below?)

The [[as-above-so-below-engraving]] found elsewhere on the property may be topically relevant.
