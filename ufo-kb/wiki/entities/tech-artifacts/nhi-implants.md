# NHI Implants

## Description

Alleged devices implanted in human subjects by NHI entities during close encounter or contact events. They appear in experiencer accounts globally and have been a contested subject in UFO research for decades. [[bob-maguire]] brings an unusual technical perspective: as the founder of [[hawkeye-360]] (a satellite RF geolocation company), he can directly assess the signal characteristics.

## Bob Maguire's Girlfriend's Implants

Bob's girlfriend has two implants:
- **Left leg**, near the femur
- **Forehead** — she occasionally experiences headaches and has the sense that "this thing is going on"

Bob tested the forehead implant with an **electromagnetic field sensor** (field meter). He moved the meter slowly across her head. Result: the meter detected **pulsed RF transmissions** localized to the implant site. Bob has posted video of this online.

Key technical observations:
- Human bodies do not generate pulsed RF transmissions.
- The signal is **strobing** — meaning digital burst transmission rather than continuous RF emission.
- This pattern is consistent with a **digital communication system** — the implant is not just passively reflecting; it is actively transmitting encoded data.

Bob has not yet decoded the modulation scheme or determined the encoding of the data, partly due to time constraints. He acknowledges that if the entities "did it right," the transmission is encrypted and he would never break the encryption.

## Hawkeye 360 and Global Tracking

The moment that crystallized Bob's belief in implants was realizing the connection to his own technology:

> "I didn't believe these stories at all about these implants and them being tracking — until I wrote the patent that tells you how they do it."

Hawkeye 360 finds RF emitters on the ground from satellites using TDOA triangulation. If NHI implants transmit at a known or discoverable frequency, a satellite network could:
- Locate every implant-bearing individual on Earth to one-square-meter accuracy.
- Track their movements continuously.
- Detect anomalous gatherings (e.g., a group of implant-bearers assembling).

Bob estimates **1.6 GHz** as a hypothetical frequency worth testing — and states Hawkeye has "almost certainly" looked at 1.6 GHz in interesting locations.

## Whitley Strieber's 1989 Ear Implant (ABOP8ZJsyIk)

[[whitley-strieber]]'s case is the most fully documented firsthand implant account in the KB:

**Placement (May 1989, upstate New York cabin)**: Two humanoid figures entered Strieber's locked, alarmed cabin. A male voice said "Condition red"; a woman's voice spoke soothingly; a powerful hand pushed his head down; a flash of light in the woods; complete paralysis. Aftermath: the alarm system was destroyed; an alarm technician measured a magnetic field strong enough to destroy electronics — the gas meter needle "disappeared off scale." Days later, ear pain indicated an implant.

**Southwest Research analysis** (Bill Malow, head of material science): Metallic base with cilia-like or cellular-appearing structures. When Strieber was present in a classified signals acquisition lab with Malow, the implant activated and emitted a radio signal. Lab technicians described it as "the strangest signal we've ever acquired" and classified the findings. At a San Antonio book signing 20+ years later, a signals technician approached Strieber unprompted and confirmed the signal was "still under study."

**Attempted removal (1994-96)**: Doctor attempted surgical extraction; the implant — visible as a white disc — moved down into the earlobe and disappeared under the skin. Surgery abandoned. The ear burned afterward.

**Dormancy and activation**: The implant was inert from 1989 until 2015. Strieber believes [[anne-strieber]] activated it from beyond death after her passing. Since 2015 it turns on during 3 AM communion sessions, feels warm/hot, and guides him toward needed information via synchronicity.

**Inventor**: Two visitors in the mid-2000s identified the inventor as [[constantine-raudive]] — the Latvian EVP researcher who died in the early 1970s, predating the 1989 implantation by ~15 years. Strieber interprets this as evidence the technology was developed before Raudive's death or continued by successors. A close friend of Strieber's, later discovered to be "one of the world's leading experts on Constantine Raudive," also has a similar eye-slit implant — Strieber reads this coincidence as evidence of a designed network experiment.

## Steve Coburn's Implant Analysis (iPxbILOMQ08)

[[steve-coburn]] is [[roger-leir]]'s closest scientific collaborator and provides the most technically detailed description of implant structure in this KB. He analyzed the implant removed from his own toe using workplace analytical equipment.

### Structural Layers (outside in)
1. **Gray membrane** — extremely hard to cut with a scalpel; no inflammatory/immune response despite being a foreign object in soft tissue (the membrane prevents the body from recognizing it as foreign)
2. **Bone-like intermediate layer** — similar to bone or mother of pearl; a biological hard part
3. **Metallic core** — meteoric iron with **carbon nanotubes** embedded in the metal
4. **Nerve cell connections** — nerve cells growing into and connecting to the device (probably explains the progressive electrical-shock pain Coburn experienced in his toe over several days after implantation, as the nerves grew in)

### Isotopic Anomalies
Coburn used mass spectrometry-class analysis. Elements including **boron** and **copper** showed isotopic ratios skewed by up to **30%** relative to terrestrial norms. Heavier isotopes were systematically **over-represented**. Three possible explanations:
- Origin near the galactic core, where frequent supernovae produce intense rapid neutron-capture (r-process) nucleosynthesis creating heavy isotope enrichment
- Deliberate artificial isotope separation (centrifuge cost: estimated millions of dollars — ruling out practical terrestrial production "just for this")
- Exposure to a massive neutron flux (reactor or zero-point energy generator)

Coburn notes this pattern appeared consistently across three analyzed implants, ruling out coincidence.

### Detection Protocol

The **Leir/Coburn protocol** for detecting implants in living subjects:
1. **Stud finder** (small conductive-object detector): scan entire body; map any hits
2. **G-meter** (sensitive magnetometer): scan over stud-finder hit areas
3. A simultaneous hit on both instruments at the same anatomical site = **almost certainly an implant** — because NHI implants consistently generate magnetic fields

Coburn has performed approximately **400 scans** on experiencers; roughly **50%** produced hits (note: pre-selected audience).

### Class 1/2 Experiencer Typology and Implant Prevalence

Coburn distinguishes two experiencer classes:
- **Class 1**: Biological sample collection only; ~3–5% of the American population (~10–15 million); do not typically receive implants
- **Class 2**: Deeper program involvement — taken aboard craft, given implants, assigned a handler; ~1 in 1,000 Americans (~**350,000 people**)

For class-2 experiencers: **brain implants are standard procedure**. These allow NHI to access sensory information and thought in real time, connecting the experiencer to the [[gray-hive-mind]]. Coburn himself has three skull implants (frontal lobes + two above-ear implants that have emitted radio signals).

### Behavioral Effects

Coburn noticed a subtle "governor" effect on his thoughts while his toe implant was in place — he felt more free mentally after its removal. People around him reported he had a gray complexion during the implant period. Whether these effects were caused by the implant or are psychosomatic/retrospective attribution is unknown. The implied function may be more than passive monitoring.

### Nanotechnology Assessment

The carbon nanotube electronics embedded in the metallic core are beyond current Earth manufacturing capability. The devices appear to be **grown** rather than assembled by conventional machining — Coburn describes them as resembling "mechanical life," grown by some form of nanotechnological process analogous to biological growth. This is consistent with an origin from a civilization potentially millions of years ahead technologically.

## Provenance and Skeptical Considerations

Implant claims are widespread in abductee literature but historically difficult to verify:
- Dermal objects have been recovered in some cases (e.g., the Leir retrievals) but lab analyses have been contested.
- The RF emission from Bob's girlfriend is the most technically specific publicly claimed verification: a specific measurable signal at a specific location.
- No independent lab has confirmed the signal or analyzed the object — Bob's evidence is firsthand but single-source.

## Mario Woods — Leg Marks and Dental Examination

[[mario-woods]]'s case adds two distinct implant-related data points:

### Unexplained Leg Marks
Two identical marks on Woods's lower legs, noticed post-service:
- **Same depth** — confirmed by his doctor
- **Same size** — top to bottom
- **Dimpled center** — like a drop hitting water, both marks identical
- Doctor (deceased from COVID; his children attended the same school as Woods's): "No known life-saving procedure — including car accident response — could account for these. I find that really odd."
- Woods has no memory of injury or healing that would explain them

The marks were not specifically attributed to the 1977 incident during the interview — Woods says he doesn't know when they appeared. They could be connected to the wrist procedure recalled under regression (the tall Gray lifting his wrist and performing something while communicating "we don't hurt you").

### Dental Examination
The flight surgeon examination after the 1977 incident included a specific and apparently unusual inspection of Woods's gums and teeth — index finger and thumb pressed around all teeth and gums systematically for several minutes. No explanation was given. Jesse Michaels connects this to [[andrija-puharich]]'s bone-induction research: the hypothesis that metallic dental work or implanted structures could receive RF transmissions via bone-conducted low-frequency waves, making them communication or tracking devices implanted in the oral cavity. The possibility: investigators were either looking for an existing implant, or placing one.
