# S4 Reactor

The power plant and core hardware of the [[s4-sport-model]], and the primary focus of [[bob-lazar]]'s reverse-engineering work at [[s4]]. Nine craft at S4 all had identical reactors — one of the key facts suggesting they were manufactured rather than separately crashed.

## Physical Description

- **Shape**: A hemisphere (approximately basketball-sized) mounted on a 15-inch square plate
- **Operating temperature**: Room temperature — no conventional cooling required
- **Color**: Not specified in detail; handled with normal gloves

## How It Works (Lazar's Account)

The reactor converts [[element-115]] bombardment into a propulsive force:

1. **Element 115 mass** is placed in the reactor
2. The reactor accelerates protons and bombards the element 115 (an internal tube/cyclotron structure visible on X-ray imaging)
3. Proton bombardment creates **element 116** (livermorium)
4. Element 116 immediately decays
5. Decay produces a **force wave** — the reactor's primary output
6. A waveguide couples this output to the amplifier system
7. Three emitters/amplifiers beneath the craft focus and direct the force

**X-ray findings**: Imaging revealed a hollow tube running through the reactor and a loop at the base — consistent with an internal accelerator (small cyclotron or linear accelerator structure).

## The Force Field

When the reactor is active and the emitter is in position, it produces a repulsive force around itself. Lazar's description:
- **Elastic**: Easy to push at distance; becomes nearly impossible to push through as you get closer — logarithmic resistance
- **No polarity**: Unlike a magnet, the force has no preferred direction; it acts outward in all directions simultaneously
- **Non-transferable**: The reactor itself does not slide or move when the force is applied — the force acts on matter around it, not on the reactor
- **Strength**: "You could probably sit a car on top of it and nothing would change" at close range

## Demonstration Experiments

[[barry-castillo]] conducted three key demonstrations for Lazar:

1. **Golf ball**: Threw a golf ball at the active reactor; it bounced off the force field, dislodged a ceiling tile; required cleanup before [[dennis-mariani]] returned
2. **Candle flame**: Placed a lit candle at the focal point of the active emitter; the flame stopped flickering — froze in position — while remaining lit and visible
3. **Black ball**: Rotated the emitter; a small black ball appeared in the air at the focal point — no light escaping, resembling a miniature black hole

Additionally ([[luigi-vendetti]]'s account, not in film):
4. **Mechanical watch**: A watch placed at the focal point stopped ticking while still visible

## The Force — Gravity or Something Else?

Lazar originally described the reactor's output as producing a "gravity wave." He increasingly doubts this framing. The force:
- Freezes candle flames (suggesting it affects the thermodynamic/chemical process of combustion)
- Creates a region of no-light escape (consistent with extreme spacetime curvature OR with a different fundamental force)
- Stops mechanical watch mechanisms (suggesting it affects physical processes generally, not just electromagnetic or gravitational)
- Has no polarity (unlike any known force carrier)
- Affects the flow of time (Lazar's increasing belief)

Lazar's current view: this may be a **previously unidentified fifth or sixth fundamental force** — not gravity, not electromagnetism, but something that affects matter, light, time, and energy in ways that don't fully match any known force.

## Home Laboratory Research

As of the episode's filming, Lazar is conducting experiments in his home laboratory attempting to produce the effects he observed at S4. He has obtained "interesting results" but will not elaborate. He is "100% confident" he can replicate the force at scale. His advantage: he "knows exactly what not to do" — meaning S4's failed approaches give him a significant head start.

## Nine Identical Reactors

All nine craft simultaneously present at [[s4]] had identical reactors. This is Lazar's basis for inferring the craft were **manufactured** rather than individually crashed. The probability of nine separate NHI crashes all producing identical power plants is, in Lazar's assessment, effectively zero.
