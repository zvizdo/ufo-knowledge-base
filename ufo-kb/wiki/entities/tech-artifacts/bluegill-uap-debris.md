# Bluegill UAP Debris

## Description

The Bluegill UAP debris refers to the physical remains of the craft allegedly downed by the nuclear X-ray pulse of the [[bluegill-triple-prime]] test on October 26, 1962. According to [[richard-bissell]]'s account relayed to [[harold-malmgren]], the craft "tumbled into the ocean and the Navy picked it up."

[[harold-malmgren]] subsequently claims to have physically handled a piece of this material at [[los-alamos]] National Laboratory, placed on a desk in front of him by [[lawrence-geist]] (AEC Albuquerque Division). He described it as feeling "weird because it didn't feel anything" — apparently lacking normal thermal and tactile properties. While holding it, Malmgren states he heard words telepathically, which he later forgot.

## Provenance Chain

1. UAP was downed over Pacific test zone during Bluegill Triple Prime (Oct 26, 1962)

## Analytical Notes

The material's described properties — no apparent thermal signature, no normal tactile properties, apparent telepathic resonance — are consistent with other alleged UAP material descriptions in the literature. The use of the material for a deliberate telepathic-response test on Malmgren is unusual and echoes [[nhi-telepathic-communication]] patterns described elsewhere.
