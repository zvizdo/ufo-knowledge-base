# Die Glocke (The Bell)

Alleged Nazi SS antigravity/advanced propulsion device developed at the [[kammler-stab]] (and/or [[kammler-stab]]) facility in Czechoslovakia under SS General Hans Kammler, approximately 1943–1945. Described as bell-shaped; reportedly involved rotating electromagnetic fields that produced anti-gravity effects.

The device is the technical center of [[jason-reza-jorjani]]'s Nazi UFO hypothesis: Jorjani argues it was initially tested in combination with rotating rocket motors under a saucer-shaped airframe, then the rockets were replaced with the Bell's electrogravidic system to produce a fully functional flying saucer:

> "They had rotating rockets underneath these saucer shaped craft… They took the rockets out, put the bell in it, and there's your flying saucer."

This synthesis — conventional saucer aerodynamics solved by Nazi engineers (who had cracked boundary layer aerodynamics) combined with electrogravidic propulsion derived from the 1933 Magenta craft reverse-engineering — is Jorjani's explanation for why saucer-shaped craft were observed launching from Spain toward America in 1946–47, approximately one year before [[1947-roswell]].

## Technical Framework (Jorjani's Account)

1. **Saucer airframe**: Nazi aeronautical engineers independently solved boundary layer aerodynamics problems that made saucer-shaped craft viable. This was natural evolution of German aviation engineering.
2. **Magenta reverse-engineering**: The [[1933-magenta-crash]] craft provided a non-human technological template for electrogravidic propulsion; SS scientists at [[kammler-stab]] worked to replicate this.
3. **Combination**: Rotating rocket motors (initial propulsion) were replaced with the Bell's electromagnetic system.
4. **Result**: An all-wingless, electrogravidically powered craft matching [[nikola-tesla]]'s drawings — the prototype for what would later appear at Roswell.

## Relationship to Tesla

Jorjani draws a connection between Die Glocke's electrogravidic principles and Nikola Tesla's experimental work on rotating electromagnetic fields and high-voltage discharges. Whether this represents an independent development convergence or direct intellectual lineage is not specified.

## Post-War Fate

If the Die Glocke program was successful, [[operation-paperclip]] and [[odessa]] are the proposed mechanisms by which the technology, personnel, and knowledge were transferred to US programs (directly) and potentially to underground Nazi networks (via ODESSA). [[joseph-farrell]]'s *The SS Brotherhood of the Bell* is the primary published source on the Die Glocke claim outside of Jorjani's synthesis.

## Nick Cook's Site Visit (G7Ns4Aq1tVc)

[[nick-cook]] visited the Wenceslaus mine site in Poland with [[igor-witkowski]], describing it as "very atmospheric." The physical evidence he observed:
- A **hexagonal concrete structure** on the ground — Witkowski identified as the test rig
- Evidence of **very thick cabling** carrying large amounts of electricity into the site
- The site was clearly a classified installation of some kind

Cook's own evolving conclusion: the facility was most likely used for **German nuclear research beyond the known Heisenberg program** — i.e., a secret SS nuclear program, not necessarily a time-dilation Bell device. However, Cook acknowledges the project name "Kronos" (if accurate) is "aptly named" for something involving time effects.

## Wargo's Time-Dilation Framing (hXYdkcv5TtY)

[[eric-wargo]] offers a distinct reading of Die Glocke in [[hXYdkcv5TtY]], different from Jorjani's antigravity emphasis: Wargo frames it specifically as a **time dilation device**. The mechanism: apply extreme high voltage over a short distance inside a bell-shaped chamber to create a different **inertial reference frame** inside — analogous to the time dilation gradient near a black hole in general relativity. One second outside = a fraction of a second inside; step out and you have effectively advanced into the future.

Wargo links this to the personnel he understands to be involved: [[walter-gerlach]] (Walther Gerlach) — a leading German physicist whose research focused on gravity — and the SS medical division head (potentially [[ernst-grawitz]]), implying live human subjects were tested in the chamber.

This time-dilation interpretation connects Die Glocke to [[thomas-townsend-brown]]'s parallel American interest in gravity-time linkage, and to the broader [[time-travel-uap]] framework. It is distinct from the antigravity/propulsion reading of Jorjani: same device, different primary effect claimed.

> ⚠ **Divergent interpretations**: Jorjani reads Die Glocke primarily as an electrogravidic propulsion device for saucer craft; Wargo reads it as a time-dilation chamber. These are not mutually exclusive (a sufficiently strong gravitational effect could produce both propulsion and time effects), but the emphasis differs significantly.

## Provenance and Skepticism

The Die Glocke claim originates primarily with [[igor-witkowski]] (based on alleged SS interrogation transcripts) and was popularized by [[joseph-farrell]]. No physical Die Glocke has been recovered or authenticated. The claim sits in the intersection of documented Nazi advanced weapons programs (which were extensive) and unverified Black Sun / SS occult mythology.

> ⚠ **Credibility note**: Die Glocke is among the most contested claims in Nazi secret weapons research. Farrell's analysis is secondary-source synthesis; the primary "SS interrogation transcripts" Witkowski cites have not been independently authenticated. Jorjani's synthesis adds the Magenta-derived motivation for the technology but does not add new primary evidence.
