# Anderson Trust

A secret trust established in Houston, Texas, by the Truman administration in 1945–46 to hold proceeds from the recovery of 12 of General Yamashita Tomoyuki's 176 Philippine treasure troves. Named after [[robert-anderson]], one of three Brown Brothers Harriman–connected deputy secretaries of war who were brought into the secret. Used as a covert funding mechanism for Cold War operations.

> ⚠ Sheehan explicitly states the Anderson Trust has **no connection to UFO program financing** — it is a covert political/paramilitary funding mechanism, not a black-budget UAP program. Some researchers (Chris Ramsey cited in the episode) had suggested a UAP link; Sheehan corrects this.

## Discovery Chain

1. **General Yamashita** (Japanese army) buried 176 treasure troves across the Philippines in the final months of WWII, using POW labor then burying the diggers with the gold. A Japanese royal prince accompanied him to record all 176 locations.
2. **[[ed-lansdale]]** (OSS/G2, Manila) learned of the troves when Yamashita surrendered. He captured and had tortured Yamashita's driver (Santo Mario/Santa Mario), extracting locations of 12 troves.
3. Lansdale verified the first trove — underground caverns 500–600 ft deep with armpit-high stacks of gold bullion and 50-gallon drums of jewels.
4. Lansdale flew to Singapore to brief **General MacArthur**, who sent **[[charles-willoughby]]** (his G2) with Lansdale back to the Philippines and then to Washington.
5. They briefed **[[clark-clifford]]** (Army chief of staff), who brought them to **President [[harry-truman]]**.
6. [[harry-truman|Truman]] called in **Secretary of War [[henry-stimson]]**, who brought his three deputies: **[[robert-lovett]]**, **[[robert-anderson]]**, and **[[john-j-mccloy]]** — all senior partners at [[brown-brothers-harriman]].

## Structure

- **Trust name**: Anderson Trust (Houston, Texas)
- **Gold destination**: US military transports flew the gold to the **International Credit Bank in Geneva, Switzerland**
- **Instrument**: Gold certificates issued against the deposits
- **Use**: Certificates distributed to right-wing anti-communist political candidates across Europe post-WWII; covert operations funding ongoing through the Cold War era
- **Oversight**: Off-books, unknown to Congress; Allen Dulles and the CIA-adjacent network knew about it
- **Amount**: ~$1.2 trillion at $32/oz (1945 gold price); the remaining 164 troves are reportedly still buried and unfound

## The Remaining Troves

Of 176 troves:
- 12 were recovered by Lansdale
- 2 were found by Ferdinand Marcos (via torture of a finder's son — conducted by Marcos's torturer Colonel Ver)
- ~162 remain unfound; Philippine government has a "Blue Ribbon Commission" tasked with locating them

## Connection to Covert Ops

The Anderson Trust gold funded covert anti-communist operations in Europe and Asia without Congressional oversight — the same model later used in Iran-Contra (which Sheehan litigated through the [[christic-institute]]). The Brown Brothers Harriman partners who controlled it were the same network that built the CIA.
