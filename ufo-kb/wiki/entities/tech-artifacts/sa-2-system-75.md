# SA-2 GUIDELINE / System-75

> *Stub. Expand with operational history and any UAP-adjacent intelligence reporting as it enters the KB.*

Soviet medium-to-high-altitude surface-to-air missile. NATO designation SA-2 GUIDELINE; Soviet system name System-75 / S-75 Dvina; missile designation V-750. Entered service 1957. The world's most widely-deployed SAM through the 1960s-1980s, used by Soviet PVO and exported globally; famously brought down the U-2 piloted by Francis Gary Powers on 1 May 1960 over Sverdlovsk.

Per CIA HUMINT cable FIRK-311/01638-77 (released 2026 as [[cia-uap-d1-sary-shagan-1973]]), warhead specifications observed at [[sary-shagan-test-range]] Site 4 (warhead checkout unit V/Ch 03142) in 1972–1973:

- ~100 grey cassettes
- Each cassette: 30 × 7 centimetres
- Each cassette held ~200–300 metal balls (shariki)
- Each ball: ≤1.5 cm diameter
- Fragmentation-type proximity warhead
