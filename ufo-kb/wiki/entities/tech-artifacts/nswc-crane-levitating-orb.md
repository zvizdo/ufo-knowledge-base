# NSWC Crane Levitating Orb

## Description

A metallic sphere approximately the size of a basketball, observed by [[randy-anderson]] and one other Green Beret in the underground **Off-World Technology Division** at [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]] in March 2014. The sphere hovered above a podium with a visible gap between object and surface — no obvious magnetic or mechanical support was discernible to the witnesses. The metal had an appearance Anderson described as **distinctly unnatural** — unlike any material he could compare it to.

The room carrying this object produced a palpable sense of "unease" and "ominousness" even before any briefing was given.

## Cadre Briefing

The instructors accompanying Anderson stated:
- The orb was **recovered from a crashed craft**
- It functioned as a **control mechanism** for that craft
- It **interacted with consciousness** (specific mechanism not elaborated; Anderson wanted to ask how they knew but did not)

## Provenance Chain

Unknown crash site → recovery by unspecified program → custody of [[naval-surface-warfare-crane]] Off-World Technology Division → observed by Anderson in March 2014.
