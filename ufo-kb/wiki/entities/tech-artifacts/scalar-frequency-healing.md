# Scalar Frequency Healing Device

An undisclosed scalar/frequency diagnostic and therapeutic device described by [[michael-herrera]] based on his personal treatment experience. Available only via word-of-mouth referral; kept off the commercial market, allegedly due to FDA dual-use concerns and pharmaceutical industry opposition.

## Description

### Mechanism

- Emits 886,000–1.2 million different frequencies sequentially at the body
- When a frequency "pings" (resonates), it identifies a specific element, pathogen, or condition — down to the DNA and atomic/molecular level
- Can diagnose pre-symptomatic conditions without the patient disclosing medical history
- Can operate **remotely** via a symmetrical facial photograph — physical presence not required

### Treatment Protocol (Herrera's Experience)

1. **Initial scan:** ~12 hours; full-body diagnostic across all frequencies
2. **Second session:** ~6 hours; targeted follow-up
3. **Destruction sequence:** ~4.5 hours; frequencies targeted to destroy harmful cells, parasites, or disease states
4. **Programmed water:** Purified water is programmed via the device and ingested twice daily; no medications or surgery involved

### Results Claimed

- Herrera cured of parasites after the sessions
- Herrera's dog (13–14 years old, bladder mass, suspected cancer) treated remotely via photo; mass resolved within weeks; confirmed by Herrera's father

## Weaponized Form

The same underlying technology is allegedly used offensively:
- Can remotely induce fast-growing cancers by targeting a victim's body via a photograph
- [[james-allen]] (documentarian of [[mark-mccandish]]'s ARV / Flux Liner story) is alleged by Herrera to have been killed this way; Allen died of an aggressive cancer in approximately three weeks; autopsy reportedly showed heavy metal poisoning

## Suppression Context

Herrera states the device is not commercialized because:
1. FDA regulatory concerns around its dual-use (healing/weapon) potential
2. Pharmaceutical and medical industry opposition

Commercial approximation: **Energy Enhancement Systems (EES)** — screens emitting scalar-adjacent healing frequencies; Herrera describes these as a distant, approximate version of the same principle.
