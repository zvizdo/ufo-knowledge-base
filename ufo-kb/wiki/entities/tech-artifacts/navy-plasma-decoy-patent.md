# Navy Plasma Decoy Patent

US Navy / DoD patent describing a class of **plasma-based decoy** generated mid-air to present a false radar/optical signature of a high-performance aircraft. Cited in UAP discourse as a documented existence-proof that **plasma decoys are a real US capability** rather than a hypothesized one.

The patent is referenced as the load-bearing object in two distinct argumentative directions:
1. **Mundane-attribution direction** — some fraction of UAP encounters may be observations of US-side plasma decoys (or peer-state equivalents) that personnel mistake for anomalous craft.
2. **Maskirovka direction** — see [[maskirovka]] — Russian-side plasma-decoy capabilities deliberately seeded into US sensor pools as deniable disinformation about UAP categorization.

[[eric-haseltine]] explicitly invokes the plasma-decoy frame in [[jMCavr3dVP0]] as the *first* hypothesis to test before reaching for non-human attribution — and notes it was ruled out for the [[2004-nimitz-tic-tac]] case because plasma does not explain the Tic Tac visual signature.

## Description

Plasma generated by directed RF or chemical means produces a localized region with altered radar cross-section and (in some configurations) visible-spectrum emission, simulating an aircraft signature.

## Provenance Chain

US patent record (declassified); cited in modern AAWSAP/AATIP-era literature surveys.

## Analysis / Findings

Plasma decoys are demonstrably real but do *not* explain the visual-morphology, sustained-flight-character, and instrumented-radar-track corroboration of high-confidence UAP cases.
