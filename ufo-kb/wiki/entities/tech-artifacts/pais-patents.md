# Pais Patents

## Overview

Five patent applications filed through the **US Navy / Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD)** by physicist [[salvatore-pais]] between approximately 2016 and 2019. Three became granted patents; two were denied. The five are interdependent — the granted patents logically require the denied room-temperature superconductor as enabling technology.

## The Five Patents

### Granted

1. **High-Energy Electromagnetic Field Generator (HEEMFG)**
   — Concentrates an electromagnetic field in an extremely small space to create enormous field gradients; subjects the quantum vacuum to these fields. Basis: Heaviside-Maxwell scaling analysis showing E_max ~ ω² and B_max ~ ω².

2. **High-Frequency Gravitational Wave Generator (HFGWG)**
   — Uses the HEEMFG output to generate gravitational waves at high frequency. Requires a high-Q resonator — which in turn requires the room-temperature superconductor. The patent examiner (Philip Bonzel) initially objected that "neutron stars cannot produce these electric fields and magnetic fields" (10¹⁸ V/m). Pais's rebuttal: the scaling analysis self-validates via E_max/B_max = c.

3. **Craft Using Inertial Mass Reduction Device**
   — The most exotic of the three; uses the outputs of the HEEMFG to manipulate the stress-energy tensor, reducing the inertial mass of a craft to near zero and warping local spacetime for propulsion. This is the "UFO" patent.

### Denied

4. **Room-Temperature Superconductor (RTSC)**
   — Lead zirconate titanate (PZT) subjected to the [[pais-effect]] to stabilize a bipolaron condensate at room temperature. Denied despite [[victor-lachinov]]'s published arXiv support and despite being the enabling technology for Patent #2 (HFGWG requires high-Q resonator that requires this superconductor).

5. **Hybrid Craft Using an Inertial Mass Reduction Device**
   — The application combining the above into an actual vehicle design. Denied.

## Patent Examiner Interaction

The Navy attorney (Mr. Marloot) facilitated all five applications. Patent examiner **Philip Bonzel** initially rejected the HFGWG and craft patents as physically impossible; a phone conversation was held where Pais walked through the mathematics, and a PDF of the supporting derivation was submitted. This PDF is in the public domain (submitted to a USPTO primary examiner via a documented phone conversation). Bonzel ultimately granted three patents after reviewing the mathematics.

The inconsistency in granting patents 1–3 while denying patent 4 (which 2 requires) has not been explained by the USPTO.

## Technical Summary of the Physics

The complete derivation chain:
1. Heaviside's four-equation Maxwell formalism coupled to the harmonic oscillator (Hook's law)
2. Scaling analysis yields E_max ~ ω², B_max ~ ω² (confirmed by E/B = c check)
3. At terahertz frequencies: E_max ~ 10¹⁸ V/m, B_max ~ 10⁹ T
4. These fields approach/exceed the **Schwinger limit** (~10³³ W/m²)
5. At Schwinger limit: quantum vacuum pair production, micro black hole formation, wormhole propulsion

The xenon gas in the annular channel of the craft patent is driven by ~100 THz alternating electric field; the standing wave amplification is the ω² mechanism in operation.
