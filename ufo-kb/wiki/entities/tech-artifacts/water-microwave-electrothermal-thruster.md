# Water-Based Microwave Electrothermal Thruster

A space propulsion device invented by [[john-brandenberg]]. The system uses ordinary **distilled water** as propellant, heated to a high-energy plasma state by microwave energy and expelled through a nozzle to generate thrust. Electrothermal thrusters in general convert electrical energy to thermal energy in the propellant; the use of water (rather than hydrazine or xenon) makes this design notable for safety and cost.

## Development and Patent

- Brandenburg invented the device and holds the patent.
- The patent has since expired.
- After expiration, [[momentus-space]] hired Brandenburg as a summer consultant to bring the system to flight readiness.

## Flight Heritage

Successfully fired in space aboard a [[momentus-space]] space-tug vehicle; the thruster raised the vehicle's orbital altitude. This constitutes **flight-proven** status — the system performed its designed function on orbit.

## Significance in Brandenburg's Profile

The thruster represents one of Brandenburg's conventionally-validated engineering achievements — a physical device that works in the real world, developed from theoretical physics principles, and flight-demonstrated. Brandenburg cites this as evidence that his broader physics framework (including [[gem-theory]]) is grounded in real understanding rather than speculation.
