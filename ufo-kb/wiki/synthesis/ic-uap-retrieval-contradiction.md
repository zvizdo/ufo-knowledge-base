# IC Insider UAP Retrieval Contradiction

## The Contradiction

Two sets of senior US intelligence community figures, with overlapping institutional access, give directly opposing accounts of whether UAP crash retrieval and reverse engineering programs exist.

### Denial Camp

**[[ralph-moat-larson]] (Rolf Mowatt-Larssen)** — Former CIA case officer (1983–2016), DOE Director of Intelligence and Counterintelligence (2005–2008), Moscow Station Chief. In a 2026 interview: "I swear to God I know nothing about UFOs." In a separate interview: would "a thousand percent publicize" any knowledge of retrieval programs.

**[[eric-haseltine]]** — Former NSA Director of Research, ODNI CTO. Had a "Clockwork Orange" exposure to all compartmented US intelligence programs. Spent "not a femtosecond" officially on UAP. Could not access any program he didn't know about. Applies method-of-competing-hypotheses; does not eliminate NHI but cannot personally confirm retrieval.

**Pattern**: Both officials report having had comprehensive institutional access. Both report seeing nothing about UAP at that level. Both frame this as genuine ignorance, not deliberate concealment.

### Assertion Camp

**[[jim-semivan]]** — CIA officer with documented anomalous personal experiences; asserts active crash retrieval and reverse engineering programs in "The Age of Disclosure."

**[[chris-mellon]]** — Former Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence; asserts active crash retrieval programs in public interviews and advocacy work; drove the AATIP disclosure strategy.

**[[david-grusch]]** — Former NRO-detail UAPTF representative; filed formal classified complaint with Congressional IGs asserting active illegal crash retrieval programs; complaint validated by IGs as credible. Congressional testimony 2023.

**[[eric-davis]]** — Former NIDS/AAWSAP researcher; received classified briefings from program insiders; asserts programs exist but have made no reverse engineering progress; "no physicists doing legitimate physics" is his explanation for failure.

## Resolution Space

> ⚠ **Unresolved Contradiction**: This is one of the most important open conflicts in the KB. Both sides have comparable IC credentials. Resolution requires choosing between:

1. **Ultra-compartmentation**: Programs are compartmented at a level that genuinely excludes even ODNI CTOs and DOE Intelligence Directors. The denial-camp officials are truthful and their access was insufficient. The denial-camp's very confidence in their denial may be the designed effect of compartmentation.

2. **Exaggeration/secondhand**: The assertion camp is operating on secondhand briefings, interpretation, or advocacy-driven framing. Semivan and Mellon lack the specific program access Davis has; Davis's "no physicists, no progress" claim may mean the programs exist in name only.

3. **Disinformation layer**: One or both camps includes deliberate disinfo. The denial camp may be managing expectations as per standard IC practice. The assertion camp may be a controlled narrative.

4. **Domain-specific compartmentation**: Programs may exist outside the DNI/DOE/NSA chain — in private aerospace contractors under AEA born-secret jurisdiction — which would explain why even the most senior ODNI and DOE figures genuinely know nothing.

The fourth resolution is the most structurally consistent with the available evidence: [[atomic-energy-act-classification]] provides a legal mechanism by which DOE-contracted programs could operate outside standard IC oversight chains, meaning even ODNI-level access doesn't reach them.

## Significance

This contradiction is more epistemically significant than any individual claim about crash retrieval. If the denial camp is correct, the assertion camp is largely composed of sincere but misled insiders or deliberate disinformation. If the assertion camp is correct, compartmentation is operating at a level that bypasses the most senior IC officials — which itself constitutes an extraordinary claim about the governance of classified programs.
