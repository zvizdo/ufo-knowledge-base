# Physics Suppression and Consciousness Suppression Are the Same Operation

## Key Finding

The institutional suppression of electrogravitic/anti-gravity physics (→ [[physics-lockdown]]) and the institutional suppression of psi/consciousness research (→ [[consciousness-non-local]]) are structurally unified. Both involve: classified government programs that operated in parallel to publicly dismissed fields; funding deflection toward false targets (string theory for physics; behaviorism for consciousness science); and active discrediting of researchers who produced results. The unifying motive: if UAP propulsion requires exotic physics AND involves non-local consciousness interaction, then both domains must be kept outside mainstream science simultaneously to preserve the knowledge asymmetry.

## Evidence

### Physics Side: Documented Suppression Pattern

[[physics-lockdown]] establishes the timeline:
- 1947–1957: Active classified research in electrogravitics (Townsend Brown / [[biefeld-brown-effect]], [[lewis-whitten]] / Martin anti-gravity, [[hyman-rickover]]'s "we're already on it")
- 1956: [[jacque-corneau]] (French Air Force technical representative) facilitated Brown's experiments at the Montgolfier facility in Paris in a vacuum of at least 10⁻⁶ torr; the residual thrust observed was several orders of magnitude larger than residual ionization could account for. Corneau confirmed this in a 2008 deathbed interview. This is the most rigorous public documentation that the Biefeld-Brown effect is *not* ionic wind.
- 1957: The transition point. At the same conference where [[herman-bondi]]'s negative-mass question pointed toward warp drives, funding flows shifted. Whitten stopped publishing. Brown's program went dark.
- Post-1957: [[string-theory-as-deflection]] — massive institutional investment in a direction that cannot produce propulsion physics, absorbing exactly the talent that would otherwise pursue the suppressed direction

### Consciousness Side: Parallel Suppression Pattern

[[stargate-program]] ran from 1972–1995 with CIA and DIA funding — $20M+ and 20+ years of operational remote viewing. The 1995 AIR evaluation: statistician Jessica Utts found a statistically real effect; Ray Hyman found methodological issues; CIA cited Hyman and closed the program despite Utts's positive conclusion.

The same pattern: classified operational use while publicly dismissing the field. [[russell-targ]] and [[hal-puthoff]] documented the effect in *Nature* (1974); their careers were marginalized. [[dean-radin]]'s 100+ peer-reviewed papers show consistent positive effects; mainstream psychology treats the field as beyond consideration.

Meanwhile, behaviorism (the "string theory" of consciousness science) dominated academic psychology from the 1950s onward — precisely by denying the existence of anything that could be remotely viewed.

### The Unifying Mechanism

The [[atomic-energy-act-classification]] framework: any physics touching nuclear processes can be classified from birth. If UAP propulsion involves quantum vacuum manipulation or sub-nuclear processes (as [[salvatore-pais]] patents imply), then the physics is automatically classifiable. If NHI contact involves consciousness-mediated information transfer, then the consciousness research that would characterize this channel is also a national security concern.

[[bob-maguire]] connects both sides: his classified career at CIA/NSA/NRO spanned both physics and consciousness research domains. He asserts — based on access rather than public disclosure — that both are within the classified program.

### The Savant Connection

[[autistic-savant-telepathy]], [[josiahs-fire]], and [[transmission-theory-consciousness]] add a third thread: non-verbal autistic individuals demonstrating apparent telepathic communication raises the same mechanistic question as Stargate remote viewing. The [[peer-institute]] (John Mack's legacy organization) and [[diane-powell]]'s controlled experiments exist at the same margin from mainstream acceptance as Targ/Puthoff were in the 1970s. The suppression mechanism is funding starvation and peer rejection rather than direct classification — but the effect is the same.

## Caveats

- The strongest evidence for physics suppression is mostly indirect (funding pattern, career trajectories, timing of silencing). The strongest direct evidence for psi is Stargate's documented 20-year classified existence.
- Maguire's claim that classified programs unified both domains is unverifiable; he remains the only source making this specific claim explicitly.
- The pattern could reflect independent institutional conservatism (not coordination) in two separate fields that both threaten paradigms.
