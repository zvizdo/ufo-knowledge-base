## The Grissom Node

[[gus-grissom]] is the strongest single node in this cluster:
- Most publicly critical internal voice (**lemon protest**, documented)
- Most likely candidate for first Moon landing (most experienced astronaut)
- Wife reports CIA presence on the launch pad hours before the fire — the first time he'd ever seen them there
- Died the next day in [[1967-apollo-1-fire]]

His death removed both the obstacle (public critic) and the threat (the most credentialed voice who could expose the program) simultaneously. The CIA connection threads directly into [[jfk-assassination-ufo]] — the same institutional apparatus.

## NASA Deception ↔ UAP Suppression

The structural connection to the UAP thesis: if NASA was willing to stage imagery and silence critics for the Apollo program, the same institutional willingness explains why NASA would suppress UAP data from astronomical surveys, astronaut accounts, and mission footage. [[bart-sibrel]] and [[dan-farah]] are cited in this context — the former as the primary Apollo skeptic researcher, the latter as connecting the dots to the broader disclosure landscape.

[[charlie-duke]] adds an anomalous data point: an Apollo 16 astronaut who became a Christian minister and spoke of unusual experiences — neither fully corroborating nor denying the skeptic account.
