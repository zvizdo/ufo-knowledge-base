# Western US UAP Event — Federal Law Enforcement Witness Slides (2023)

Summary slides covering statements by 7 federal law enforcement special agents (USPER1–USPER7) who independently witnessed multiple distinct UAP types over the western United States in 2023, at dusk and pre-dawn. Released 2026-05-08 in PURSUE Release 01. AARO conducted follow-up measurements on at least two phenomena.

## Phenomenon 1 — "Orbs Launching Orbs"

**Witnesses**: Three teams of two federal law enforcement special agents (USPER1–USPER6)  
**Time**: Dusk on two separate days  
**Event**: An orange orb appeared, launched groups of 2–4 smaller red orbs (consensus: ~3 per launch), then disappeared within 1–2 seconds. This occurred **at least five times**. The red orbs generally moved in horizontal paths after launch, though some were described as moving "heading up at an angle" or "swooping down." Multiple teams observed from varying locations/vantage points. Whether a single orange "mother orb" or multiple were responsible is unknown.

## Phenomenon 2 — "Large, Fiery Orb"

**Witnesses**: USPER5, USPER6  
**Time**: Dusk  
**Object**: Glowing orange orb perched close to a rock pinnacle. Witnesses initially estimated ~500–600 meters away; **AARO later measured: ~1,050 meters**. Size initially estimated as "small helicopter cockpit"; **AARO later measured: 12–18 meters in diameter** (~40–60 feet). Silent. USPER6: object "did almost appear it might have had a small spindle or something connecting it from underneath to the rock formation." Described as "similar to the Eye of Sauron from Lord of the Rings, except without the pupil, or maybe an orange Storm Electrify bowling ball." USPER6: "seemed to be hovering with zero resistance or movement, or to be suspended." Duration: ~1 minute.

## Phenomenon 3 — "Dark Kite"

**Witnesses**: USPER5, USPER6  
**Time**: Pre-dawn  
**Event**: Object initially appeared to be a car in a restricted zone — two lights (one red, one white), ~2–3 feet off the ground. Agents pursued; at a few hundred feet distance, the object suddenly moved **off the road over the desert without changing its orientation relative to the observers**, at ~15–20 mph. No height change. Stopped ~100 meters off the road, then turned off its lights. Visual description (from behind): USPER6 saw a "thin line"; USPER5 saw an "ill defined, dark kite shape with some rounded width to the sides." USPER6 used NVGs once the object stopped: "a very thin line" remained, ~4 feet wide, horizontal. Object then moved again at ~15–20 mph, "moved up in height… but remained a flat line." Lost from view after a few seconds. **AARO follow-up note**: In later discussions with AARO, the object was described as **triangular**.

## Phenomenon 4 — "Transparent Kite"

**Witnesses**: USPER5, USPER6, USPER7 (USPER7 did not see the object)  
**Time**: Pre-dawn, ~30 minutes after "Dark Kite"  
**Location**: Few hundred meters from "Dark Kite" site; same restricted area  
**Object**: Kite-shaped, similar lighting pattern to "Dark Kite"; ~6 meters off the ground, canted at angle (lower right to upper left); **appeared to be floating slowly with the wind**. USPER5 (NVGs): "could vaguely see a bright star or two in the distance **through** the object, though somewhat more faint" — indicating **partial transparency**. After losing sight of the object, USPER5 used a spotlight and observed: "my beam went from shining far into the distance to **stopping about 50 yards away on nothing in particular** — it just was not projecting into the distance and then it was." When pointed at the spot where the beam was stopped, this time the light "was projecting into the distance again." Note: outline was not definitive, but lights remained in exact formation.

## AARO Follow-Up

AARO conducted measurements on the "Large, Fiery Orb" — determining it was ~1,050 meters from the observers and 12–18 meters in diameter. AARO also conducted follow-up discussions with witnesses that clarified the "Dark Kite" was triangular. This confirms AARO's active engagement with this incident beyond initial reporting.

## Significance

1. **Multi-witness, multi-phenomenon event**: Seven federal law enforcement agents independently witness at least four distinct UAP types across two days; multiple teams from varying vantage points converge on consistent descriptions.
2. **Mother orb / offspring orbs**: The "orbs launching orbs" behavior — orange mother orb releasing 2–4 red orbs at least 5 times — is a documented recurring pattern in the KB (cf. tag-along-orbs, USS Russell pyramid footage).
3. **AARO measurements**: AARO's post-event measurements (1,050 m distance, 12–18 m diameter for the fiery orb) provide quantified physical parameters — confirming the object was not a small drone.
4. **Transparency / spotlight absorption**: The "Transparent Kite" appearing to absorb a spotlight beam — which stopped projecting into the distance then resumed — is consistent with reported light-absorbing or light-bending UAP properties.
5. **Federal law enforcement witnesses**: Agents in a restricted zone conducting patrols; witnesses with training to distinguish anomalous from ordinary phenomena.
