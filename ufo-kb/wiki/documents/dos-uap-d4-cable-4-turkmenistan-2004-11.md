# DOS UAP Cable 4 — Turkmenistan: Civil Society and UFOs, November 2004

US Embassy Ashgabat cable (MRN: 04 ASHGABAT 1028; DTG 120851Z NOV 04). Classification: UNCLASSIFIED. Released in Full. Routed to SECSTATE, CIA, DIA, NSC, USCENTCOM, SECDEF, JOINT STAFF, CIS Collective, USOSCE, USEU Brussels.

## Subject: The Union of UFOlogists of Turkmenabat

On November 5, 2004, the DCM and USAID Director met with the Board of the **Union of UFOlogists (UOU) of Turkmenabat**, Lebap Welayet (province), Turkmenistan. The UOU is:

- The **first NGO to register** after Turkmen independence (1992) and the first to successfully re-register under the 2003 NGO law
- Working on an **$8,532 USAID grant** (via Counterpart International) to help other local NGOs navigate the registration process
- An umbrella organization with 9 member organizations (sports, youth, business consulting) and 1,000+ members
- Running fee-based courses including computer, accounting — and UFOlogy and massage

## UAP-Relevant Content

**Key claim (UOU President Ovezberdy Muradov)**: The Turkmen military and government authorities had **consulted him about mysterious occurrences in Turkmen airspace**; however, he said there had been "no confirmed sightings of UFOs in Turkmenistan."

Members attended international UFO fora and published on the subject, but most told the DCM they had "limited or no interest" in UFOs. The UOU runs regular UFO seminars attended by local authorities, with "everyone is interested in UFOs" as the UOU's explanation for their political access.

## Civil Society / Diplomatic Context

- USAID considering a $15,000 capacity-building grant; a $30k newsletter-printing equipment proposal awaits a stronger proposal
- The UOU plans a 999-copy independent newsletter (below the 1,000-copy threshold requiring a print license in Turkmenistan)
- Cable tone: lightly amused — "Crazy? Like a fox; and worthy of USG attention and support"
- The cable is Cable 4 of the five-cable DOS UAP series; contrast with Cable 1 (Papua New Guinea 1985 sighting), Cable 3 (Georgia 2001 sighting), Cable 5 (Mexico 2023 Congress hearing)

## Significance

1. **Routing disproportionate to content**: An UNCLASSIFIED civil society meeting note was routed to CIA, DIA, NSC, USCENTCOM, SECDEF, and JOINT STAFF — the same distribution list used for national security intelligence. The UAP context (Turkmen airspace consultations) likely explains the routing.
2. **Turkmen government consultation with UFOlogists**: Muradov's statement that Turkmen military/government consulted his organization about "mysterious occurrences in Turkmen airspace" is the primary UAP data point; it implies ongoing unexplained aerial activity in Central Asian airspace in the early 2000s.
3. **USG funding a UFOlogy organization**: USAID funding a UFOlogy NGO is an unusual instrument of civil society engagement — the embassy found the UOU to be a reliable partner precisely because of its local authority relationships built through UFO interest.
