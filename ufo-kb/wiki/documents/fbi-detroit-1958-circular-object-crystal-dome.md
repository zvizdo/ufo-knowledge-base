# FBI Detroit 1958 — Circular Object with Crystal Dome (Serial 844)

FBI Office Memorandum from SA Robert Ross Reynolds, Detroit Field Office; April 17, 1958; File 100-DE-18221, Serial 844. Declassified via FBI Automatic Declassification Guide (May 24, 2007); screened by NARA (RF) 11-25-2025; FOIA #90288, DOCID: 34715588. PURSUE Release 01.

## Witness Report

Civilian witness **David Weaver**, 15801 Decosta, Detroit, MI (age 23, son of Detroit policeman, Civil Air Patrol experience):

- **Time**: 4:08 AM, April 17, 1958 — called FBI directly
- **Object**: Circular with a "crystal-type dome that reflected lights"
- **Direction**: Traveling northward from southwest; crossed Detroit approximately 3 blocks south of Six Mile at Lamphere Street
- Witness attempted to contact Selfridge Field (Air Force base) first but could not reach them
- Witness was returning home from work when he observed the object
- FBI **Recommendation**: Advise proper air force authorities

## Significance

One of four FBI field-office UAP memos in the PURSUE Release 01 batch (alongside Germany 1957, Detroit, and the 2023 transport facility 302). The "crystal-type dome" descriptor and the nighttime reflective quality are historically notable — the dome-with-dome-on-top morphology recurs across 1950s civilian reports. Witness's CAP background adds modest observational credibility; FBI's recommendation to forward to Air Force is the standard 1950s handling.
