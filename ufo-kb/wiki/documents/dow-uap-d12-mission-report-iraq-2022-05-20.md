# AARO MISREP D12 — Iraq UAP, 196 ATKS / 163 AW, 2022-05-20

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 196 ATKS / 163 AW / 609 CAOC / ACC, from OKAS, Saudi Arabia. May 20, 2022. Classified SECRET//REL TO USA, FVEY; declassification date: 20470521. Six MDR numbers cited (MDR 25-0094 through MDR 25-0099 / JS-250710-TM8S).

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 20–21 May 2022; takeoff 200542Z MAY22, land 210036Z; 18h 54m total; 18h 14m on-station
- **Platform**: 196 ATKS / 163 AW (California Air National Guard); OKAS; ANDAS4 + AHv2 (AIRHANDLER v2)
- **Mission**: OP PHANTOM FLEX; RECONNAISSANCE; support for MAG (Military Advisory Group) IVO MGRS 38SMC36; Killbox 89AS; precoord 136 minutes; SATISFACTORY
- **Operations center**: 609 CAOC / USCENTCOM / ACC
- **Weather**: Not a factor; took off late due to comm issues
- **Intel gap filled**: YES — "1.4a WAS EFFECTIVE"

## UAP Observation

- **DTG**: 202043Z MAY22
- **Observer location**: MGRS 38SMC85... (Iraq)
- **Observer altitude**: 18,000 FT
- **UAP first seen**: MGRS 38SMC79... 
- **MDS/Tail**: UNK (unknown aircraft type and tail number)
- **UAP trajectory**: North to northeast
- **UAP alt/velocity**: UNK
- **Gentext**: "(S//REL) OBSERVED A UAP AT 2043Z FLY NORTH TO NORTH EAST AND FOLLOWED AS LONG AS POSSIBLE. SCREENER COULD NOT GET A POSITIVE ID ON THE UAP."
- **Physical state/signatures/effects**: Not recorded

## Significance

D12 stands out from the rest of the gov-release MISREP batch in three ways:

1. **196 ATKS / 163 AW (California Air National Guard)**: Every other MISREP in the batch originates from 482 ATKS. D12 is the only report from a different squadron — the 196 ATKS, a California ANG ISR unit. This confirms multiple squadrons were simultaneously deployed to OKAS in 2022, and suggests UAP reporting was occurring across multiple units, not just the 482 ATKS.
2. **"Followed as long as possible"**: The crew tracked the UAP "as long as possible" before losing it — the only MISREP where active pursuit is stated. This contrasts with D18 ("DID NOT FOLLOW") and D60 (no follow-up). The screener (DGS analyst) attempted positive ID and failed.
3. **Intel gap filled: YES** — All other MISREPs in the batch report Intel gap filled: No. D12 is the only exception, suggesting the mission was judged effective overall despite the UAP not being identified. The gap was likely filled for the primary reconnaissance objective, not the UAP observation specifically.
4. **OP PHANTOM FLEX**: A different operation from INHERENT RESOLVE referenced by D10 and D18 — suggesting distinct mission context (possibly counterproliferation or weapons-cache surveillance, given "MAG" as supported unit).
