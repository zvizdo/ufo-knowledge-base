# DOS UAP Cable 5 — Mexico, September 2023 (Mexican Congress UAP Hearing)

US Embassy Mexico City weekly political blotter cable, September 16, 2023 (MRN: 23 MEXICO 2544; DTG 160150Z SEP 23). Classification: UNCLASSIFIED. Released in Full. Routed to NSC, White House, VP, DNI, CIA, DIA, NORTHCOM, DHS, DOJ, SOUTHCOM. Note: source filename reads "2003-09" but the cable is unambiguously 2023 (MRN "23 MEXICO" prefix, 2023 political context).

## UAP Content — Paragraph 11: Mexican Congress UAP Hearing (September 12, 2023)

The cable's eight political items include one UAP item: the Mexican Congress UAP hearing of September 12, 2023.

- **Witnesses**: [[jaime-maussan]] (Mexican journalist/UFO researcher) and [[ryan-graves]] (former [[us-navy]] pilot; previously testified before US Congress)
- **Legislative context**: The hearing was to debate language on UAP in the **Aerial Space Protection Law** — described by the cable as legislation that "if approved, would make Mexico the first country to formally acknowledge the presence of alien life on Earth"
- **Expert requests to Congress**: Recognize UAP officially; guarantee airspace security; allow UAP study
- **Maussan presentation**: Two alleged "alien corpses" and videos of Mexican military pilots' encounters with "fast-moving flying objects during flight"
- **Graves reaction**: "Lamented the display took away from his and other pilots' experiences with UAP and expressed disappointment with Maussan's 'unsubstantiated stunt'"
- **Scientific status**: Cable notes "scientists have discredited previous alleged alien corpses Maussan presented as evidence of alien life"
- **Cable tone**: Neutral reporting; no State Dept editorial on the broader UAP policy implications; the item is nested among routine political reporting (elections, security appointments, criminal attacks on prosecutors)

## Significance

1. **US government documentation**: US Embassy officially cabled this event to NSC, CIA, DIA, DNI, and the White House — confirming high-level US government awareness of Mexico's UAP legislative developments
2. **Graves/Maussan schism on record**: The cable captures the core tension in UAP advocacy: pilot-witness credibility vs. sensationalist presenter; Graves publicly distancing himself from Maussan's stunt is on record in US diplomatic traffic
3. **Mexico Aerial Space Protection Law**: First documented mention in US government diplomatic traffic of Mexico's UAP legislation effort; would have made Mexico the first nation to formally recognize alien life
4. **Routing list significance**: NSC/WH/VP/DNI/CIA/DIA/DHS routing suggests the US government treated Mexico's UAP legislative action as a foreign policy and national security matter, not merely a cultural oddity
