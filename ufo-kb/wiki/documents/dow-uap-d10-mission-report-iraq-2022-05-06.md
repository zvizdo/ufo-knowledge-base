# AARO MISREP D10 — Iraq UAP, 5x Observations Including Possible Missile, 2022-05-06

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 432nd Air Expeditionary Wing / 609 CAOC / AFCENT, Iraq. Operation INHERENT RESOLVE. Classification: SECRET//REL TO USA, FVEY. Declassification date: 20470506.

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 6–7 May 2022; takeoff 060246Z, on-station 060958Z, off-station 062036Z, land 070004Z
- **Platform**: 1 aircraft, AFCENT / 432 AEW; callsign, unit, base all redacted (1.4a)
- **Primary sensors**: FMV + SIGINT
- **Mission type**: REC/XCAS; 1 IMINT + 1 SIGINT tasking (Target Development)
- **Operations center**: 609 CAOC / USCENTCOM AFCENT
- **Operation**: INHERENT RESOLVE
- **Weather**: DUST HINDERED MOST FMV COLLECTION OF THE GROUND
- **FMV exploitation**: DGS1

## UAP Observation

- **DTG window**: 1514Z–1934Z, 6 May 2022 (4 hours 20 minutes)
- **Total UAP count**: 5× (one observation episode spanning 4h 20m)
- **Observer location**: MGRS 38SMC53 (Iraq); mission area 38SMC54
- **Method**: FMV (field of view observation)

**Observation 1 (1514Z):** "A UAP WITH THE VISRECCE OF A POSSIBLE [REDACTED] MISSILE FLY ACROSS THE FOV IVO 38SMC53." The first object was assessed by the crew as having the visual reconnaissance profile of a possible missile; the specific missile type is redacted under 1.4a.

**Observations 2–5 (1514Z–1934Z):** "4X OTHER UAPS IN THEIR FOV UNTIL 1934Z. ALL 4X REMAINING UAP FIT CLOSER TO THE PROFILE OF POSSIBLE BIRDS."

- **Resolution**: None definitive — first object "possible missile" (specific type redacted); remaining four "possible birds"
- **Observer assessment**: Not recorded; no BENIGN/UNKNOWN field visible in release
- **Advanced capabilities**: Not recorded
- **Effects on persons/equipment**: None stated

## Significance

The 5-count observation window (4h 20m) is the largest UAP count in a single D-series MISREP in this batch. The bifurcation in assessments — one object assessed as a possible missile (type redacted), four assessed as possible birds — is significant: the crew was discriminating actively, yet the first object's missile-profile assessment could not be confirmed or dismissed (the specific type is under 1.4a exemption). Dust degradation of FMV ground collection did not prevent aerial object observation, which adds a layer of environmental context. The mission took place under Operation INHERENT RESOLVE (Iraq/counter-ISIS theater). The 432 AEW / 609 CAOC reporting chain is the same unit cluster as D62, D63, D64, D65, and D75.
