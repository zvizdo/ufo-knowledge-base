# AARO MISREP D60 — Arabian Gulf UAP, 482 ATKS, 2020-08-08

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 482 ATKS / 432 AEW / 609 CAOC / ACC, from OKAS, Saudi Arabia. August 8, 2020. The **earliest** in the five-MISREP 482 ATKS cluster from OKAS in 2020 (D60 Aug 8, D61 Aug 27, D62 Sep 16, D63 Oct 1, D64 Nov 2). Declassification date: 20450301.

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 8–9 August 2020; takeoff 080337Z AUG20, land 090045Z; 21h 8m total; 20h 18m on-station
- **Platform**: 482 ATKS / 432 AEW; OKAS, Saudi Arabia; ANDAS4 (primary FMV) + AH/GMESH
- **Mission**: AREC; NAVCENT support; IRGCN/IRIN vessel characterization + pattern of life in Arabian Gulf / Strait of Hormuz / Gulf of Oman; 1 IMINT + 1 SIGINT tasking prosecuted
- **Operations center**: 609 CAOC / USCENTCOM / ACC
- **Weather**: DENSE CLOUD COVERAGE INTERMITTENTLY IMPACTED FMV COLLECTION

## UAP Observation

- **DTG**: 080726Z AUG20
- **Observer location**: MGRS 39RWK95... (redacted)
- **UAP location**: MGRS 39RVL90... / 39RWL08... (coastal Arabian Gulf)
- **Method**: FMV
- **Gentext**: "AT 0726Z, 1.4a OBSERVED 1X UAP IVO 39RWL081.4a NO IMPACT TO MISSION."
- **Observed Activity Description**: Heavily OCR-corrupted (illegible)
- **Duration/behavior/morphology**: Not recorded — this is the most minimal UAP gentext in the gov-release D-series batch

## Significance

D60 is the **earliest** MISREP from the 482 ATKS / 432 AEW OKAS cluster (Aug 8, 2020), predating D61 by 19 days. The UAP observation is the most content-sparse in the batch — a single sentence stating an unidentified aerial phenomenon was observed with no morphology, duration, behavior, or resolution. Dense cloud coverage on the same sortie may explain the lack of detail (FLIR returns degraded). The Iranian Air Defense guard call near Abu Musa Island is consistent with the other 482 ATKS 2020 MISREPs (D61 at 0532Z, D64 at 1012Z), establishing this patrol area as a recurring Iranian-proximity contact zone.
