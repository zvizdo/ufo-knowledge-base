## Significance

Two features stand out. First, the **UAP was observed during RTB** — same as D27 (Gulf of Oman) — and flew sea-skimming straight toward land before being lost from the FMV feed, consistent with the sensor-evasion pattern. Second, this is **the same unit and same base as D25** (33 SOS / 27 SOW, Larissa AB, Mediterranean, January 2024, diamond+probe/tail, SWIR-only), observed just three months earlier. D35 and D25 together document that the 33 SOS / 27 SOW AFSOC detachment at Larissa encountered UAP in consecutive missions three months apart — two distinct morphologies (circular sea-skimmer, January 2024 diamond+probe). Combined with D27 (3 SOS / 27 SOW, Gulf of Oman, June 2024), the 27th SOW unit cluster accounts for three separate UAP observations in less than a year. D33 (Aegean Sea, 27 October 2023 — two days before D35, same area) should be checked for the same unit when imported.
