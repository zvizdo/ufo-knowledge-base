# USCENTCOM MISREP D32 — Syria UAP, 20 October 2024

Standard AARO MISREP filed by 12 SOS / 27 SOW under OP INHERENT RESOLVE, USCENTCOM. UAP event during a 20-hour ISR mission from Al-Muwaffaq Salti Air Base (OJMS), Jordan, over Syria. FMV/SIGINT platform at 20,088 ft, 144 knots, orbiting.

## UAP Observation (1559Z–1644Z, 20 Oct 2024)

- **Initial DTG**: 201559Z Oct 2024
- **Duration**: 45 minutes
- **Location**: MGRS 37SFU36, Syria
- **Physical state**: **Plasma**
- **Description**: "MISHAPEN AND UNEVEN BALL OF WHITE LIGHT"
- **Behavior**: Multiple light/glare events at different angles and directions; three instances of light/glare crossing directly on the FMV camera feed (1559Z, 1602Z, 1644Z); two instances of a "light/glare halo effect at the top of FMV feed" (1609Z, 1620Z)
- **Kinetic data**: No altitude, velocity, or trajectory recorded (all fields estimated/blank)
- **Intelligent control**: No
- **Response to observation**: No change
- **Assessment**: Benign; aircrew explicitly assessed this was NOT a lasing event
- **Effects on personnel or equipment**: None

## Platform and Mission Context

- **Unit**: 12 SOS, 27 SOW (Special Operations Air Force)
- **Operations Center**: 609 CAOC
- **Operation**: INHERENT RESOLVE
- **Primary sensor**: FMV (SANTA FE pod); SIGINT also available
- **Total mission time**: 20 hours 24 minutes (20-21 October 2024)
- **Takeoff/land**: OJMS (Al-Muwaffaq Salti Air Base, Jordan)
- Three FMV ISR tasking periods completed; full-motion video exploited by GET

## Comparison with D74 (Syria, Nov 2023)

Both D32 and [[dow-uap-d74-mission-report-syria-2023-11-09]] are Syria MISREP incidents from the same 609 CAOC chain under INHERENT RESOLVE. Key differences:

| | D74 (Nov 2023) | D32 (Oct 2024) |
|---|---|---|
| Physical state | Solid (spherical) | Plasma |
| Kinetics | 424 kn, consistent 7 min | No velocity; no trajectory |
| Signature | Spherical "bouncy ball" | Misshapen uneven ball of white light |
| Camera interaction | FMV observation | Crossed directly on FMV camera feed |
| Lasing? | N/A | Explicitly ruled out |

The plasma classification is notable — it places D32 alongside the 2020 Arabian Gulf and Strait of Hormuz cases that also describe plasma-state UAP in CENTCOM theater.
