# AARO MISREP D25 — Mediterranean Sea UAP, SWIR Diamond Shape, 2024-01-25

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 33rd Special Operations Squadron (33 SOS) / 27th Special Operations Wing / AFSOC, departing Larissa Air Base (LGLR), Greece. Operations centers: 603rd AOC (USAFE) and 609 CAOC Det 1. Declassification date: 20490125.

## UAP Observation

- **DTG**: 250509Z JAN24; event serial 250509ZJAN2024-CENTCOM 001
- **Duration**: approximately 2 minutes (0509Z–0511Z), during transit
- **Observer aircraft**: FL250, heading 162°, 176 KTS; MGRS 35SQT44 area (Mediterranean Sea)
- **UAP shape**: "ROUND DIAMOND SHAPE WITH STRAIGHT, NON MANEUVERABLE 'TAIL'" — described as a probe or appendage at the bottom
- **UAP signature**: SWIR WHT (Short-Wave Infrared, white) — appeared **only on the SWIR camera**; not detected on any other sensor
- **UAP speed**: approximately 434 knots
- **UAP altitude**: FL200 (below observer at FL250)
- **UAP kinetic trajectory**: W (west)
- **UAP behavior**: MAINTAINED STEADY FLIGHT PATH, INCREASED AND DECREASED ALTITUDE PROFILE, DID NOT CHANGE TRAJECTORY
- **UAP maneuverability**: NONE
- **Intelligent control**: UNKNOWN
- **Advanced capabilities**: NO
- **Observer assessment**: BENIGN
- **Effects on persons/equipment**: NONE; no objects recovered

## Significance

Two features distinguish this observation. First, the UAP appeared **only on the SWIR camera** and was not detected by FMV or other sensors — a sensor-selective signature that raises questions about the object's surface emission properties. Second, the morphology — "round diamond with a non-maneuverable probe/tail" — is an unusual shape not found in other AARO MISREPs in this batch. The object's altitude-oscillating behavior (gaining and losing altitude while maintaining a constant westward heading) adds to the anomaly profile. The platform is an AFSOC special operations aircraft, adding a tier of credibility (special operations crews are trained for discriminating observation). The SWIR-only signature is directly comparable to documented optical-invisible UAP in the broader AARO dataset.
