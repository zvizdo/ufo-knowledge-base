# AARO MISREP D75 — Gulf of Aden UAP, ISR Platform, 2024-07-14

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 124th Attack Squadron (124 ATKS) / 132nd Wing / 432nd Air Expeditionary Wing supporting NAVCENT/AFCENT target development. Approved for AARO release. Declassification date: 2049-07-14. MDR 25-0072; release recommended by MG Brandon R. Tegtmeier, USCENTCOM Chief of Staff, 2 June 2025.

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 14 July 2024; takeoff 140222Z JUL24, on-station 140300Z, retasked 140041Z
- **Platform**: Redacted ISR/attack aircraft (124 ATKS / 132 Wing / 432 AEW)
- **Sensor**: FMV (primary)
- **Operations center**: 609th CAOC / USCENTCOM
- **Mission**: NAVCENT/AFCENT target development support, Gulf of Aden

## UAP Observation

- **Time**: 140517Z JUL24
- **Location**: IVO 38P MT 53, Gulf of Aden
- **Maneuverability**: STRAIGHT FLIGHT PATH AT SAME ALTITUDE
- **Key anomalous characteristic**: "UAP'S SPEED WAS FASTER THAN THE [aircraft] FLYING SPEED" — the primary anomalous descriptor; velocity redacted (1.4a / 1.4g)
- **Trajectory**: NW (northwest); altitude: LOW
- **Last known position**: 38P MT 22 (tracked until distance became too great)
- **Intelligent control**: UNKNOWN
- **Advanced capabilities**: UNKNOWN
- **Assessment**: BENIGN
- **Effects on persons/equipment**: NONE; no objects recovered

## Significance

The defining characteristic of this observation is speed: the UAP outpaced the observing ISR aircraft in straight-and-level flight. Unlike the cold thermal signature of [[2023-arabian-gulf-uap-d23]] or the redacted-gentext encounters of [[2020-arabian-gulf-uap-d65]], the D75 report preserves one unambiguous datum — velocity exceeding the platform — while withholding the aircraft's actual airspeed. Three USCENTCOM ISR UAP encounters now documented in the Gulf region (D23/Arabian Gulf, D65/Arabian Gulf, D75/Gulf of Aden) within a 4-year window.
