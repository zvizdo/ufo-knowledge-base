# DOE-UAP-D003 — Pajarito Astronomers Meeting Announcement (1986)

A single-page meeting announcement dated Tuesday, 20 May 1986, from the Pajarito Astronomers (a Los Alamos staff astronomy club sponsored by the Los Alamos employee social organization Club 1663) to its members. The announcement publicizes the club's 29 May 1986 meeting in the Ranch Room at Fuller Lodge (the historic 1928 John Gaw Meem-designed building in downtown Los Alamos), 7:30 p.m. The guest speaker was Dr. John Warren of AT-6 (Los Alamos National Lab Accelerator Technology Division, Group 6), whose talk title was "Why Should a Scientist be Concerned about UFO's?" The signature is redacted under FOIA b(6). Archive index A-86-014-91-3; cross-index EHF-100-334.

## Headline Claims

- A Los Alamos National Lab scientist (Dr. John Warren of AT-6) was openly scheduled to give a UFO-focused lecture to a Los Alamos staff astronomy club in May 1986 — under the talk title "Why Should a Scientist be Concerned about UFO's?" — and the meeting announcement was preserved in the Los Alamos / DOE archives sufficient to be released 40 years later as part of PURSUE Release 02.
- The talk venue (Ranch Room, Fuller Lodge) and sponsoring organization (Club 1663, the Los Alamos employee social club named after the Manhattan Project's wartime PO Box 1663) place the event squarely within the standard Los Alamos staff social/intellectual circuit — UFO interest was treated as a legitimate astronomy-club topic, not as fringe or taboo.
- Club officer mail-stops (President MS K553, Vice President MS E531, Treasurer MS F665) confirm the Pajarito Astronomers' membership and leadership were drawn from LANL technical staff (MS = Mail Stop, the internal LANL postal designator).

## What's Revealed

- **Pajarito Astronomers club**: a Los Alamos-area astronomy club centered on LANL staff, P.O. Box 1092, Los Alamos NM 87544, sponsored by Club 1663. Officers identified only by mail-stop (no names): President MS K553, Vice President MS E531, Treasurer MS F665. The club's existence and active 1986 programming is itself a previously-undocumented (in this KB) data point on Los Alamos UFO-interest culture.
- **Speaker**: Dr. John Warren of [[los-alamos-national-laboratory|LANL]] AT-6 (Accelerator Technology Division, Group 6). The "AT" division at LANL during the 1980s was the Accelerator Technology Division; AT-6 was one of several technical groups within that division.
- **Talk title**: "Why Should a Scientist be Concerned about UFO's?" — phrasing implies an *advocacy* posture (UFO topic is worth scientific attention) rather than a *debunking* posture; the title structure mirrors the "Why Should X Care About Y" persuasive-essay form, not the "Why X Is Wrong About Y" debunking form.
- **Venue**: Ranch Room, Fuller Lodge, 7:30 p.m. Fuller Lodge is the 1928-built former Los Alamos Ranch School dining hall, retained by the AEC/DOE post-1943 as a historic Los Alamos community building; standard venue for LANL staff non-classified social functions through today.

## What's Redacted

- **Club officer signature** (b(6)): the actual signatory of the announcement letter is redacted. The "Yi Bergman H-183" handwritten annotation top-right of the released PDF appears to be an archival routing/tracking notation, not the signatory.
- No body content is redacted; the announcement is essentially fully released.
- The redaction is standard FOIA personal-privacy treatment; no substantive content is concealed.

## Notable Quotes

## Open Questions Raised

- **Was the talk given?** The announcement is from 20 May 1986; the meeting was scheduled for 29 May 1986. Whether the meeting occurred, what Warren actually said, whether minutes/notes survive in any Pajarito Astronomers archive or LANL records — all unanswered.
- **Who is John Warren of AT-6?** The name "John Warren" is common; the AT-6 affiliation narrows but does not uniquely identify. No published Los Alamos technical-report or accelerator-physics author list immediately surfaces a "John Warren" / AT-6 c.1986. Identification would be a low-cost archival lookup.
- **Why was this in the DOE archive at all?** A members-only meeting-announcement letter to a staff astronomy club is not normally an archived federal record. Its retention in the DOE archive (file A-86-014-91-3) and inclusion in PURSUE Release 02 suggests either (a) the document was preserved in [[james-l-tuck|James L. Tuck]]'s or a similar staff member's personal UFO-research correspondence file (parallel to DOE-UAP-D002), or (b) a separate Los Alamos staff member retained a UFO-interest correspondence collection that the DOE later accessioned. The numbering ("A-86-014") suggests 1986-vintage accession; consistent with retention in active staff files at the time, then migrated to DOE archives.
- **What was the Pajarito Astronomers' broader programming?** Was UFO programming a recurring topic, or was this a one-off? The KB has no other Pajarito Astronomers documents; future archival pulls would need to surface complete club correspondence to determine the institutional weight of this single talk.
