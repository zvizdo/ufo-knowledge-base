# AARO MISREP D16 — Syria UAP, 89 ATKS / 432 AEW, 2022-07-31

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 89 ATKS / 432 AEW / 609 CAOC, from Muwaffaq Salti AB (OJMS), Jordan. July 30–31, 2022. Classification: SECRET//NOFORN; declassification date: 20470731.

## Mission Context

- **Date**: July 30–31, 2022; takeoff 301822Z JUL22, land 311519Z JUL22; total 20h 57m; on-station 18h 44m
- **Platform**: 89 ATKS / 432 AEW; MQ-9 ISR/AREC (armed reconnaissance); OJMS (Muwaffaq Salti AB, Jordan); FL195 (19,359 FT); 116 KTS
- **Mission**: OP INHERENT RESOLVE; ARMED RECCE; FORCE PRO ISO TF CHOSIN (Force Protection in Support of Task Force Chosin); FMV exploited by DGS1; IMINT and SIGINT collect; 103-minute precoord
- **Operations Center**: 609 CAOC / 603 AOC

**ISR activity**: Extended surveillance IVO 37SFU (northern Syria / Deir ez-Zor area): multiple ADM (armed dismounted military) follows; vehicle convoy tracking; 2× TELARs with 3 rockets loaded observed inactive at 1134Z; possible artillery systems on technicals; compound surveillance.

The observation occurred 7+ hours into the mission, during the night, while the crew was scanning for EEI (Essential Elements of Information) in KP 9 (a defined patrol/killbox area in Syria).

## Significance

1. **89 ATKS / 432 AEW**: Distinct from D14 (50 ATKS / 432 AEW, Syria May 2022) — a different squadron from the same wing, reporting a UAP in the same theater two months later
2. **Sub-minute duration**: One of the shortest UAP observations in the gov-release batch; "less than a minute" in the GENTEXT is explicit
3. **Force Protection mission**: Unlike most MISREP ISR missions (pattern-of-life surveillance), this is FORCE PROTECTION in support of a counterterrorism task force (TF CHOSIN), adding operational context to the UAP appearance
4. **Syria cluster**: D16 joins D14 (50 ATKS, May 2022), D19 (F-15E, Feb 2023), D20 (F-16CM, Mar 2023), D74 (ISR, Nov 2023), D32 (ISR, Oct 2024) — six UAP MISREPs from Syria/ESSA theater across 2022–2024
