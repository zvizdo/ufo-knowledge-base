# COMETA — UFOs and Defense: What Should We Prepare For? (1999)

Landmark French government-adjacent UAP report produced by [[cometa-report|COMETA]] (Committee for In-Depth Studies), a group of ~15 senior French military officers, intelligence officials, scientists, and aeronautics experts, most of them former auditors of [[ihedn|IHEDN]] (Institut des Hautes Études de Défense Nationale). Chair: General [[denis-letty]] (ret., French Air Force). Foreword by [[andre-lebeau]] (former CNES President); Preface by General [[bernard-norlain]] (ret., IHEDN). Presented to the French President and Prime Minister. First published in the magazine *VSD* (July 1999), approximately 90 pages. The most substantive official-adjacent UAP analysis made public by any Western government to that date.

## Document Structure

- **Part 1 (Chapters 1–4)**: Documented UAP cases — French military pilot testimonies (Chs. 1–2), international radar/visual cases (Ch. 2), ground sightings and [[landing-trace-evidence|landing traces]] (Ch. 3), abduction-contact reports (Ch. 4)
- **Part 2 (Chapters 5–8)**: Analysis — history of [[geipan|GEPAN/SEPRA]] (Chs. 5–6), photographic analysis (Ch. 7, François Louange), propulsion and physical hypotheses (Ch. 8, Christian Marchal/ONERA + [[jean-pierre-petit]])
- **Part 3 (Chapters 9–14)**: Defense implications — US/UK/Russia research programs (Ch. 9), strategic planning (Ch. 10), aeronautical (Ch. 11), scientific/technical (Ch. 12), political/religious (Ch. 13), media (Ch. 14)
- **Conclusions and Recommendations**: 6 formal recommendations
- **Appendices**: French radar systems, astronomers' sightings, life in universe, space colonization, Roswell/disinformation, historical UAP chronology, psychological/political analysis

## Key Cases Documented

**French military (Part 1)**

- **[[1977-giraud-mirage-uap]]** — Col. Hervé Giraud (Mirage IV, 7 Mar 1977, Dijon region): unidentified object performed 3–4g then 6.5g turns, not detected by radar, no sonic boom despite supersonic speed. Three independent witnesses.
- **Bosc T-33 (3 Mar 1976, Tours)**: Pilot Claude Bosc + 2 witnesses observe green sphere ~1–2m diameter.
- **[[1994-air-france-3532]]** — Capt. [[jean-charles-duboc]] + copilot Valérie Chauffour (Air France Flight 3532, 28 Jan 1994): 250m craft visible for 50 seconds, confirmed on primary radar at the Cinq-Mars-la-Pile Control and Detection Center.

**International radar/visual (Chapter 2)**

- **[[1956-lakenheath-uap]]** — RAF/USAF Lakenheath, 13–14 Aug 1956: RAF Venom pursued by and unable to shake a radar-tracked object; multi-station radar confirmation; DIA later called it "a classic case."
- **[[1957-rb47-uap]]** — RB-47 aircraft, 17 Jul 1957: 6 USAF officers; microwave emissions, simultaneous radar (ground + airborne) and visual tracking across South-Central US; object responded to aircraft maneuvers.
- **[[1976-tehran-uap]]** — 18–19 Sep 1976, Iran: two F-4 jets scrambled; electronic systems disabled on approach; sub-object launched from primary object; DIA classified the report and later noted it "meets all the conditions required for a legitimate study of the UFO phenomenon."
- **Russia/Maltsev 1990** (Pereslavl-Zalesski, 21 Mar 1990): Aviation General Maltsev's signed account; 100+ military witnesses; silent discoid object, hovering to speed "two or three times faster than a modern fighter jet"; published in *Rabochaya Tribuna*.
- **[[1995-bariloche-uap]]** — San Carlos de Bariloche, Argentina, 31 Jul 1995: airport power outage, lights extinguished on runway during approach; parallel flight by unidentified object; dozens of witnesses.

## GEPAN/SEPRA Methodology

[[geipan|GEPAN → SEPRA → GEIPAN]] was established within [[cnes|CNES]] in 1977. Jean-Jacques [[jean-jacques-velasco|Vélasco]] headed SEPRA. COMETA presents the agency's full 4-category classification system:

- **Categories A–C**: explained (balloons, aircraft, meteors, etc.)
- **Category D (UAP-D)**: remain unexplained after expert analysis — representing **4–5% of all cases**

Of 500+ well-documented aeronautical sightings since 1942, approximately 20% have both radar and visual confirmation. COMETA presents GEPAN technical notes (NT 1–18) as primary French evidence base.

## Physical Hypotheses (Part 2)

- **MHD (magnetohydrodynamic) propulsion**: Jean-Pierre Petit (CNRS/ONERA) model analyzed at length; COMETA concludes MHD is a scientifically credible mechanism for UAP-D propulsion, consistent with silent supersonic flight, electromagnetic effects on nearby vehicles, and observed plasma sheaths.
- **Particle beam propulsion**: reviewed but assessed as secondary.
- **Twining 1947 letter**: quoted verbatim from Appendix R of the [[condon-committee|Condon Report]] — COMETA treats it as authoritative evidence of physical reality.
- **ETH conclusion (Ch. 8.3.6)**: "The extraterrestrial hypothesis is by far the best scientific hypothesis." A 0.1% difference in evolutionary rate produces a civilization thousands of years ahead. Jean-Claude Ribes and Guy Monnet scenario: O'Neill-type asteroid habitats → antimatter propulsion → interstellar colonization — plausible under known physics.

## US, UK, Russia Research (Chapter 9)

- **US**: [[project-blue-book|Blue Book]] closed Dec 1969 on Condon Report. Bolender letter (Oct 1969) cited verbatim: Blue Book closure "will not put an end to military reports concerning UFOs that constituted a threat to national security — these were not part of the Blue Book system and would continue to be handled in accordance with JANAP 146 and Air Force Manual 55-11."
- **AFR 200-2 + JANAP 146**: penalties of 10 years imprisonment and $10,000 fine for unauthorized disclosure; applies to commercial airline pilots and merchant marine captains.
- **Robertson Committee (CIA, Dec 1952)**: recommended "stripping the UFO phenomenon of its aura of mystery" and monitoring ufological associations; CIA infiltrated these associations.
- **Goldwater**: COMETA cites [[barry-goldwater|Barry Goldwater]]'s statement that certain UFO files are classified above "top secret."
- **Col. [[philip-corso|Philip Corso]]'s book** (*The Day After Roswell*, 1997): COMETA gives qualified credence; Strom Thurmond's foreword noted as significant; Corso's claim that Army R&D (1961–62) distributed Roswell foreign-technology materials to US industry.
- **UK ([[nick-pope|Nick Pope]])**: MOD UFO desk (Sec(AS)2a), 1991–94; Lord Hill-Norton and Ralph Noyes suggest a secret parallel study department. Both Lords suspect US–UK collaboration.
- **Russia**: KGB declassified 124 pages in 1991 (Cases 1982–90, 17 regions); Col. Boris Sokolov sold 1978–88 military investigations to ABC News in 1994.
- **[[laurance-rockefeller|Laurance Rockefeller]]**: funded Marie Galbraith's 1995 briefing document (*Best Available Evidence*) distributed to 1,000+ world leaders and US congressmen; funded 1997 Pocantico scientific colloquium (chaired by [[peter-sturrock|Peter Sturrock]]).

## Strategic Analysis (Part 3)

Chapter 10 classifies states by UFO-contact depth (four levels: no knowledge → investigative capacity → possible contact with ETs). COMETA assigns the US to level (d) — possible contact. Chapter 13 models four-phase ET contact: (1) remote observation, (2) in situ sampling, (3) influencing local civilizations, (4) direct contact — used to reason about ET intentions toward Earth. Chapter 14: media implications — Orson Welles 1938 panic model; disinformation classification.

## Roswell Appendix

COMETA's detailed Roswell analysis identifies two types of disinformation:
- **Reducing disinformation**: Air Force Sept 1994 Mogul report shortens witness affidavits; [[philip-klass|Philip Klass]] cherry-picks testimony.
- **Amplifying disinformation**: Bennewicz affair — AFOSI/Doty induced Bennewicz to hallucinate an ET/human joint base program to protect Kirtland microwave weapons research and Groom Lake classified programs; Bill Cooper/John Lear amplification into CFR/EBE conspiracy.
- **GAO finding** (1995): base administrative records Mar 1945–Dec 1949 destroyed; Roswell radio messages Oct 1946–Feb 1949 destroyed; no destruction order documented.

## Conclusions and Six Recommendations

1. Inform political/military decision-makers and all flight/controller training schools
2. Expand SEPRA's resources (investigation capacity, foreign relations, global data collection)
3. Make UFO detection an explicit objective for French and allied space surveillance systems
4. Create a high-level inter-ministerial unit to produce prospective hypotheses and fund research
5. Diplomatic demarches to the US (individually and through the EU) to end secrecy and share data
6. Prepare contingency plans for overt contact scenarios (landing before witnesses, first contact)
