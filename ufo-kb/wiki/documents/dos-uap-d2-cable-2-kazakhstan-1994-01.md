# DoS Cable D2 — Tajik Air Pilots Report UFO Over Kazakhstan (January 1994)

**MRN:** 94 DUSHANBE 259. DTG: 310000Z Jan 94. From: AMEMBASSY DUSHANBE. Action: SECSTATE WASHDC ROUTINE, AMEMBASSY MOSCOW ROUTINE. Filed Jan 31, 1994. PURSUE Release 01. Released in full by John Powers, Acting-Director, DoS, 2/25/2026.

## Sighting Details

On January 27, 1994, [[ed-rhodes]] — Tajik Air Chief Pilot and American citizen — and two American pilot colleagues reported a UFO while flying a Boeing 747SP at 41,000 feet over Kazakhstan, at approximately **45°N 55°E** (near the Aral Sea region).

**Object approach and behavior:**
- First observed as "a bright light of enormous intensity approaching from over the horizon to the east at a great rate of speed and at a much higher altitude"
- Crew watched the object for approximately **40 minutes**
- Maneuvers observed: circles, corkscrews, and **90-degree turns at rapid rates of speed and under very high G's**
- After maneuvering, "adopted a horizontal high-speed course and disappeared over the horizon"
- Dark conditions prevented discerning shape; only the light was visible

**Physical description of the light:**
- Described as having a **"bow wave"** — "resembling a high-speed photo of a bullet in flight, in which a very small object gives off a much larger trailing wave of heat/light"

**Physical trace evidence (contrails):**
- Approximately 45 minutes after the sighting, as the sun was rising, the aircraft flew **under contrails the object had left behind**
- Aircraft was making over 500 knots when it passed under the contrails
- Rhodes estimated contrail altitude at approximately **100,000 feet**
- Rhodes noted: "there is too little air/moisture at that extreme altitude to enable the creation of contrails by the propulsion mechanisms of ordinary aircraft which might be able to reach that height"
- The contrail paths **reflected the maneuvers of the object** (circles, corkscrews, etc.)

**Photography:** Rhodes took several photos with a pocket Olympus camera; the cable notes he would send copies to the Embassy and the Tajikistan Desk (Lowry Taylor at DoS) "if they come out." No further record of the photos in this document.

## Witness Assessment

When the Embassy suggested a meteor explanation, Rhodes and his crew (described as Pan Am veterans with extensive commercial aviation experience) were **"adamant that they had seen thousands of falling stars and other space junk entering the atmosphere"** in their years flying for Pan Am. "This, they insisted, was nothing like a meteor."

Rhodes expressed the opinion, which his crew seemed to support: **"the object was extraterrestrial and under intelligent control."**

## Embassy Comment

"We have no opinion and report the above for what it may be worth." — Signed: ESCUDERO.

## Significance

1. **Contrail evidence**: Physical contrails at ~100,000 feet that trace the object's maneuvering pattern (circles, corkscrews) constitute independent physical evidence separate from the visual observation. Rhodes's analysis of why conventional propulsion cannot produce contrails at that altitude adds a technical dimension.
2. **Experienced witnesses**: Pan Am veterans with thousands of flight hours; explicitly familiar with atmospheric re-entry phenomena; actively rejected the meteor explanation.
3. **Released in full**: Unlike most DoS cables in this series, this one is UNCLASSIFIED and was released in full — suggesting no information was deemed still sensitive at release.
4. **Series context**: Cable 2 of the five-document DoS UAP cable series in the PURSUE Release 01 batch.
