# AARO MISREP D63 — Strait of Hormuz UAP, 5 Iranian Guard Calls, 2020-10-01

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 482nd Attack Squadron (482 ATKS) / 432nd AEW, departing OKAS (Saudi Arabia). 609th CAOC / NAVCENT support. Declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison, USCENTCOM Chief of Staff, 16 March 2026. Declassification date: 20450301.

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 1–2 October 2020; takeoff 012249Z, on-station 012300Z, ISR 020117Z–021835Z; land 021953Z
- **Total mission time**: 21 hours 4 minutes
- **Platform**: 482 ATKS / 432 AEW, OKAS, Saudi Arabia (1 aircraft)
- **Sensors**: ANDAS4 (FMV primary), AIRHANDLER SIGINT, GMESH
- **Mission**: AREC/ISR ISO NAVCENT; Arabian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz, Gulf of Oman; characterize IRIN/IRGCN vessels and UAS activity, establish pattern of life

## ISR Context: Iranian Naval Surveillance

ISR against Iranian/IRGCN presence in the Strait of Hormuz region:
- 1× UI aircraft at Abu Musa Island Airfield (1244Z; possibly ATR 72-500 at 1344Z assessment)
- 1× POSS NASER WAP vessel docked near Bushehr IRIN boatyard (1657Z)
- Heavy haze precluded IMINT analysis during portions of the mission

## Five Iranian Guard Calls (0727Z–1315Z)

The platform received 5 guard calls from Iranian Air Defense over approximately 5.5 hours:
- **0727Z** (MGRS 40RCP76, FL180, heading 020°)
- **0854Z** (MGRS 40RDP78, FL180, heading 145°)
- **1122Z** (MGRS 40RCQ75, FL160, heading 264°, tone: Directive)
- **1236Z** (MGRS 40RCP36, FL160, heading 128°)
- **1315Z** (MGRS 40RCP area, FL160, heading 227°, tone: Directive)

All responded with STANDARD RESPONSE 1; no impact to mission. The sustained pattern of 5 calls across 5.5 hours suggests persistent Iranian tracking of the aircraft's orbit.

## UAP Observation

- **DTG**: 021829Z OCT20 — 6 minutes before RTB clearance (021835Z)
- **Number**: 1× UAP
- **Form data**: UAP observation form section absent from released document (cut off or fully redacted)
- **Context**: Observation occurred after 5 Iranian guard calls; just before RTB

## Significance

D63 is notable for the sustained Iranian Air Defense attention (5 guard calls across 5.5 hours — more than any other MISREP in this batch). The UAP appearing at 1829Z, 6 minutes before RTB, with no form data surviving in the release, leaves the encounter as a data point without characteristics. The IRIN/IRGCN surveillance mission context (Abu Musa Island, Bushehr boatyard) establishes a high-Iranian-proximity operational environment. Three 2020 NAVCENT ISR UAP encounters now documented: D63/Strait of Hormuz, D65/Arabian Gulf, and sister Strait of Hormuz D62.
