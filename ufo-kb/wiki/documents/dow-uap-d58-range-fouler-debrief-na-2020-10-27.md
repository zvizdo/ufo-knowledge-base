# AARO Range Fouler Debrief D58 — DCA Mission, Chevron + Jamming, 27 Oct 2020

US Navy SPEAR Range Fouler Debrief; O-3 pilot, 77 EFS squadron; DCA (defensive counter-air) mission; location redacted; 27 Oct 2020 (night); declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison; PURSUE Release 01.

## Observation

- **Date/Time**: 27OCT2020, 01:12:21Z (night)
- **Location**: Redacted; contact altitude 26,000 ft (MSL); bullseye reference ZIM 248/17 (redacted 1.4a)
- **Contacts**: 2× unknown contacts
- **Shape**: **Chevron** (explicitly checked as shape; "Other Shape" field)
- **Stable trackfile**: Yes; radar lock obtained; tally (visual) achieved
- **Movement**: Yes — heading 060°, speed 20; no apparent propulsion listed
- **Electronic warfare**: Noise jamming indicated by two [witnesses/platforms]; EA indications checked
- **Narrative** (heavily redacted): "KINGPIN DIRECTED ID OF UNKNOWN CONTACT. [1.4a] OBTAINED RADAR LOCK AND TARGET [redacted] TO GET CLOSE [redacted] SECOND, THE CHEVRON [redacted] JAMMING WAS RECEIVED. NOISE JAMMING WAS INDICATED BY TWO [redacted]. FIRST DETECTION OF THE RANGE FOULERS WERE AT B/E ZIM 248/17 [1.4a]."
- **Resolution**: Unknown; KINGPIN controller unable to identify

## Significance

This is the only range-fouler form in the gov-release batch to report active noise jamming attributed to the unidentified contact, and the only one to record a non-round morphology (chevron shape). The combination of stable trackfile, radar lock, tally achieved, and simultaneous jamming is operationally significant: the contact was identifiable by multiple sensors yet could not be classified.
