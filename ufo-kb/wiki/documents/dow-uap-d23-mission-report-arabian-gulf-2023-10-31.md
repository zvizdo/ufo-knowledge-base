# AARO MISREP D23 — Arabian Gulf Two UAP (Cold Thermal)

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 50th Attack Squadron (50ATKS) / 432nd Air Expeditionary Wing, departing Al Dhafra AB (OMAM). Operation SPARTAN SHIELD, supporting NAVCENT. Approved for AARO release. Declassification date: 2048-10-25.

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 24 October 2023 (0015Z–2058Z); total mission time: 20 hours 43 minutes
- **Platform**: Redacted ISR/attack aircraft, 1 aircraft, from Al Dhafra AB (UAE)
- **Sensors**: SIGINT (AIRHANDLER), IMINT (FMV pod ANDAS1 + APKWMESH data link)
- **Mission**: ISR support to OP SPARTAN SHIELD (NAVCENT), Arabian Gulf
- **Operations center**: 609th CAOC

## Iranian Air Defense Guard Call

At 0145Z (shortly before the UAP events), Iranian Air Defense issued a guard call to the aircraft: "MAINTAIN SAFE DISTANCE FROM BORDER." The aircraft responded: "THIS IS A COALITION AIRCRAFT CONDUCTING ROUTINE OPERATIONS IN INTERNATIONAL AIRSPACE." Calls ceased at 0145Z. This establishes the aircraft was operating near the Iran-Arabian Gulf border at the time of the UAP encounters.

## UAP Observations

Two separate UAP contacts during the same mission, 41 minutes apart:

**UAP 1 — 0241Z (MGRS 39RYJ vicinity)**
- UAP Physical State: Solid
- UAP Speed: ~320 MPH (estimated)
- UAP Signatures: **THERMAL SHOWED COLD** — object was thermally cold, not generating heat detectable by FMV/IR sensors
- UAP shape: Unknown (UNK)
- Maneuverability: None
- Intelligent control: No
- RF: Unknown
- Advanced capabilities: Unknown
- Observer assessment: Benign

**UAP 2 — 0322Z (MGRS 39RXK6 vicinity)**
- UAP Physical State: Solid
- UAP Speed: ~440 MPH (estimated)
- UAP Signatures: Unknown
- UAP shape: Unknown (UNK)
- Maneuverability: None
- Intelligent control: No
- Observer assessment: Benign

Both observations: No effects on persons, no materials recovered.

## Significance

The THERMAL SHOWED COLD signature on UAP 1 is particularly notable: an object traveling at ~320 MPH with no detectable thermal emission is inconsistent with any conventional propulsion system. The near-simultaneous Iranian Air Defense guard call adds geopolitical context: these UAP encounters occurred while the aircraft was maneuvering near the Iranian border, raising the possibility the objects are Iranian drone or surveillance assets — or an unknown third category. Two encounters in 41 minutes over the same general area suggests a persistent presence, not a transient anomaly.
