# AARO MISREP D27 — Gulf of Oman UAP, Glowing Sphere with Pole/Bar, 2024-06-07

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 3rd Special Operations Squadron (3 SOS) / 27th Special Operations Wing / AFSOC / 379th AEW, from Al Dhafra AB (OMAM), UAE. Operation ENDURING SENTINEL. Classification: SECRET//NOFORN (more restrictive than FVEY standard). Declassification date: 20490606.

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 6–7 June 2024; takeoff 062100Z, land 070713Z; total 10 hours 13 minutes
- **Platform**: AFSOC 3 SOS / 27 SOW, Al Dhafra AB (OMAM), UAE; 1 aircraft
- **Sensors**: AN/DAS-1 (EO/IR), AIRHANDLER SIGINT, GMESH, SANTA FE; LINK 16
- **Mission**: ISR; NIB dhow scanning tasking (75 NM radius around start point)
- **Operations center**: 609th CAOC / USCENTCOM AFSOC
- **Note**: Aircraft was 7-lined (redirected) and did NOT arrive on station due to weather; conducted two NIB dhow-scanning taskings instead

## UAP Observation

- **DTG**: 070457Z JUN24; during RTB (return to base)
- **Event serial**: 060457ZJUN2024-CENTCOM
- **Observer location**: MGRS 40RFM60; altitude 23,999 FT MSL; speed 163 KTS; trajectory 294°
- **UAP description**: **"GLOWING HOT SPHERICAL UNIDENTIFIED OBJECT WITH A VERTICAL UNWAVERING CYLINDRICAL POLE/BAR ATTACHED ON THE BOTTOM OF THE OBJECT. POSS REFLECTION FROM THE OBJECT IN THE WATER."**
- **UAP maneuverability**: "FLYING STRAIGHT JUST OVER THE WATER AT SPEED OF 140 KNOTS"
- **UAP speed**: 140 knots; altitude: very low (just over the water surface)
- **UAP physical state**: Solid
- **UAP first coord**: 40RFM60; **last coord**: 40QFM92 (crossed MGRS zone boundary — moved south)
- **UAP response to observer**: NO CHANGE
- **UAP signatures**: None specified
- **Advanced capabilities**: NO; observer engagement: NO; assessment: BENIGN
- **Effects on persons/equipment**: NONE; no objects recovered

## Significance

The morphology is the headline: a glowing hot sphere with a vertical, unwavering cylindrical pole/bar at the bottom — with a possible reflection visible in the water below. This almost exactly parallels [[dow-uap-d25-mission-report-mediterranean-sea-2024-01-25]]'s "round diamond shape with straight, non-maneuverable probe/tail" (Mediterranean, January 2024, 33 SOS / 27 SOW). Both are AFSOC 27th Special Operations Wing missions; both describe a round/spherical object with a downward-projecting non-maneuverable appendage. The appendage pattern, appearing in two separate UAP reports from the same wing in the same calendar year, is without precedent in the non-AFSOC MISREPs in this batch. The "glowing hot" descriptor also contrasts with D23's cold thermal signature — suggesting distinct object types. The very-low altitude (sea-skimming with reflective return in the water) and the solid physical state with no advanced capabilities recorded adds to the anomaly profile.
