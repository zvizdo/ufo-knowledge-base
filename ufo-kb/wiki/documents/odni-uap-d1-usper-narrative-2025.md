# ODNI-UAP-D001 — USPER Narrative, Senior USIC Official

First-person witness narrative by an anonymized senior US intelligence officer ("USPER" = US Person) recounting a multi-hour UAP encounter at a classified western US test range, late 2025. Released by the Department of War under PURSUE Release 02 (2026-05-22) via ODNI. Companion document to the FBI 302 interview of the same witness, released earlier under PURSUE Release 01 (see [[fbi-western-us-late-2025-uap-investigation]]); together the two cover the same incident from structured-report and first-person-storytelling angles. On the war.gov manifest the ODNI entry is "PDF-paired" with 32 FBI photos (A001–A008, B001–B024) plus this USPER Statement.

## Headline Claims

- A senior US intelligence officer personally observed orange oval orbs "flare up" in a "T" formation directly off the helicopter's rotor disk during a late-2025 test-range UAP search — naked-eye observation, supplemented by FLIR/NVG from the pilots.
- Daytime aerial search of the mountain range — preceding the night encounter — turned up a "large cave entrance with no visible end in sight" in an area associated with the reported orb activity; pilot orbited but could not land safely. (NEW relative to the FBI 302.)
- The same orbs that approached the helicopter "appeared to break off" and pursue military fighter jets that subsequently arrived for a training mission, flaring sequentially above the jets in horizontal formation matching their flight path.
- Orbs split into two and a third object emerged at high speed; closest approach within 10 feet of the helicopter; helicopter "unable to match its speed" in pursuit.
- No photos were taken by the witness during the encounter: "I didn't take photos, as I was focused on assessing what it was and whether it posed a threat." (Explicitly addresses why the witness's own account is unaccompanied by imagery — the 32 photo PDFs in the release package were submitted separately, not by this witness during the encounter.)

## What's Revealed

- **Daytime pre-encounter context (page 1)**: search of the mountain range "for several hours" using "map of the earth" low-altitude routes; multiple debris finds attributed to rocket-test remnants on the test range; cave-entrance discovery; refueling at a "prepositioned tanker on the range" before being re-tasked.
- **Cave entrance (page 1)**: "large cave entrance with no visible end in sight" in an area of "reported orb activity"; terrain unsafe for landing; orbited for observation only; location noted but not subsequently revisited in this narrative.
- **Initial radar/FLIR sequence (page 1)**: JOC reports radar hits "several miles up range from our position"; ground team sees object on FLIR as "super-hot," low to ground, moving east then south, splitting into two and changing direction; object then rose from ground, "approached within ten feet of the helicopter, dropped below us, and then sped away."
- **T-formation flaring (page 2)**: at ~700 ft AGL, "two large orbs flare up side by side, close to the helicopter — stationary and just above the rotor disk to our right"; described as "oval-shaped, orange with a white or yellow center, and emitted light in all directions"; "a third orb flared up below the pair, followed by a fourth below that, forming a total of four or five in a 'T' formation under the original two"; "dimmed in reverse order"; entire event ~10–15 seconds.
- **Orbs-pursue-fighters sequence (page 2)**: fighter jets enter visual range at "about 23,00 feet AGL" (probable typo for 23,000 ft); "the same type of orbs appeared directly above the fighters... flared up one at a time in a horizontal formation, matching the jets' speed and flight path"; repeated several times across the jets' transit until landing.
- **Distinct triangle formation (page 2, NEW)**: separately from the T-formation, orange orbs "flaring up and down around us for several minutes, forming a distinct triangle before vanishing."
- **Post-mission affect (page 2)**: pilots and witness "virtually speechless after these observations" at the JOC after landing.

## What's Redacted

This release is a sanitized narrative — every geographic, facility, callsign, time-stamp, and personnel detail present in the FBI 302 (e.g. SITE CODE NAME, CALL SIGN 1, MILITARY AIRCRAFT, FEDERAL PARTNERS, MGRS coordinates, 2202–2241 timestamps, 5 fighter aircraft count) is absent here. The narrative discloses no classification banner; no `b()` exemption codes are stamped on the released text. The witness identity ("senior U.S. intelligence officer") is anonymized in the body itself. The "23,00 feet AGL" altitude appears to be a typo not a redaction.

## Notable Quotes

- "I — a senior U.S. intelligence officer — along with a colleague and two pilots, departed our Joint Operations Center (JOC) in a helicopter. Our mission was to investigate loud thuds heard in the mountains on the test range, which coincided with Unidentified Aerial Phenomena (UAP) sightings reported over the previous several nights." (page 1)
- "We discovered a large cave entrance with no visible end in sight. The terrain around the entrance offered no safe landing spot, so I instructed the pilot to orbit it several times for observation. We noted the location and then pressed on." (page 1)

## Open Questions Raised

- **Which test range?** Description matches a US military test range with mountainous terrain, prepositioned ground refueling, recurring "orbs/lights and thuds" reports, and authority to scramble fighter jets on training overflight. Candidate ranges in the western US (Nellis/Tonopah, White Sands, Dugway, Yuma) plausibly fit; no narrative detail discriminates. Cross-reference with [[fbi-western-us-late-2025-uap-investigation]] "western US" framing.
- **What is the cave?** A "large cave entrance with no visible end in sight" in an area of orb activity is suggestive but unresolved. The FBI 302 does not mention the cave; this is genuinely new from the ODNI document. No follow-up survey is referenced. The narrative makes no NHI/underground-base inference; the cave is noted purely as a physical-geography curiosity in an area of orb activity.
- **Why are the FBI and ODNI versions different?** The same witness apparently produced two artifacts: a structured FBI 302 (released as PURSUE Release 01) and a first-person narrative released through ODNI (this document, PURSUE Release 02). The narrative-style version omits the cave from the FBI version and includes the cave; the FBI 302 includes timestamps and witness/asset roles the narrative omits. Suggests parallel debrief processes; the narrative may have been prepared by the witness directly, the 302 by the interviewing agent.
- **Why no photos by the witness?** The witness explicitly says they did not take photos. The 32 photo PDFs paired with this narrative on the war.gov manifest (FBI Photo A001–A008, B001–B024) must therefore be from other observers (pilots, LP/OP, ground team) — consistent with the FBI 302's reference to a 24-photo evidence package, but the count mismatch (24 in FBI release vs 32 on war.gov manifest pairing) is unresolved.
- **What was the "successful test" the FBI 302 references?** The narrative does not mention it; only "loud thuds heard in the mountains on the test range" attributed to recurring UAP activity. Whether the day's classified test correlates with the UAP appearance is unaddressed in the narrative.
