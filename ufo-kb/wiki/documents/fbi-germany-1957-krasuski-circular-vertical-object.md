# FBI Detroit Case 100-26505: Krasuski Circular Vertical-Take-Off Object

FBI Detroit case file 100-26505, released under FOIA request 90290 (DOCID 34714592). Primary source: an 11/7/57 interview of [[wladyslaw-krasuski]] by SA Cassius Rathbun, prompted by an FBI Director URGENT teletype of 11-6-57 after Krasuski contacted [[robert-cutler]] (Special Assistant to [[dwight-eisenhower|President Eisenhower]]) during the November 1957 Levelland, Texas vehicle-stalling UFO wave.

## Primary Witness Account — 1944 Germany

[[wladyslaw-krasuski]] (aka Walter Krasuski), a Polish POW laboring at the agricultural estate Gut Alt Golssen approximately 30 miles east of Berlin, observed a large circular craft on a single occasion in 1944. His account:

- **Craft dimensions**: approximately 75–100 yards in diameter, approximately 14 feet in total height
- **Structure**: dark gray stationary top section (~5–6 feet), stationary bottom section (~5–6 feet), and a 3-foot rapidly rotating middle section that was blurred beyond resolution during operation
- **Rotation signature**: the rotating band emitted a high-pitched whine; Krasuski's tractor engine stalled twice when the craft was in operation — a classic [[electromagnetic-effects]] pattern documented across dozens of UAP encounters
- **Enclosure**: the craft launched from a circular earthwork enclosure approximately 100–150 yards in diameter with a 50-foot tarpaulin wall, visible as a flat-topped structure from outside
- **Launch sequence**: rose vertically from inside the enclosure, then moved slowly horizontally after clearing the wall
- **Ground infrastructure**: uninsulated copper cables approximately 1.5–2 inches in diameter ran from the enclosure to a concrete column — suggesting a fixed power supply or control connection rather than self-contained propulsion
- **Personnel**: SS guards were present throughout; approximately 16–18 Russian, French, and Polish POWs were working in the area and observed the event
- **Evidence removal**: after the war ended, Krasuski investigated the site — the cables had been removed and the enclosure area flooded, covering the launch circle

## 1957 FBI Investigation Context

The November 1957 Levelland, Texas UFO wave — multiple witnesses reporting engine stalling and radio failure when an oval luminous object passed overhead — triggered a spike in UFO-related communications to federal agencies. Krasuski, then residing in Detroit, mailed a letter to [[robert-cutler]] at the White House describing his 1944 observation. The FBI Director responded by URGENT teletype to SAC Detroit on November 6, 1957, ordering an immediate interview.

SA Cassius Rathbun conducted the interview the following day (11/7/57) at Krasuski's Detroit home. His assessment: "No indication of irrational or otherwise abnormal behavior" — placing Krasuski in the category of credible, grounded civilian reporters per [[credibility-frameworks]].

## 1966 Additional Material

The file also contains a 1966 letter from the Oklahoma UFO Research Association (OUFORA), asking the FBI for UFO sighting forms. The FBI Detroit response directed OUFORA to contact USAF Wright-Patterson — documenting the post-1947 information-routing protocol that funneled civilian inquiries away from the FBI and toward the [[us-air-force|Air Force]]'s [[project-blue-book|Project Blue Book]] apparatus.

## Significance

The Krasuski account is one of a small number of WWII-era HUMINT reports documenting what may have been a German experimental disc prototype at a specific identified location (Gut Alt Golssen). The copper cable infrastructure and enclosure design suggest a tethered or ground-powered test vehicle rather than a fully autonomous aircraft. The tractor stalling aligns with [[electromagnetic-effects]] signature patterns later compiled by researchers studying [[nazi-ufo-hypothesis]] and [[disc-shaped-craft]] reports.

The FBI's immediate response to the Levelland wave — URGENT teletype within one day of Krasuski's White House letter — illustrates the continued institutional attention to UFO vehicle-interference reports in 1957, parallel to the same collection apparatus documented in [[fbi-62-hq-83894-ufo-investigations-1947-1968]].
