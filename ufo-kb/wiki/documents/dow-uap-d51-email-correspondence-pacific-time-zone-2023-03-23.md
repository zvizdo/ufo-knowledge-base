# DOW Email D51 — AFOSI Classification Review, Pacific Time Zone Blue Triangle UAP (March 2023)

Email chain (2 messages), PURSUE Release 01. March 2023. Classification: SECRET//NOFORN original; UNCLASSIFIED approved for the summary via "derivative classification review."

## Content

An OUSD(I&S) Information Disclosure Analyst submitted a declassification/classification-review request to AFOSI for a summary of a UAP Intelligence Information Report (IIR).

**UAP summary (approved UNCLASSIFIED):**

- **Object**: Large blue featureless triangular object with a solid, unwavering silhouette; emitting powerful "whitish blue" light from multiple points along its perimeter
- **Behavior**: Hovering stationary near/above a national security facility for approximately 3 minutes; then moved higher in a "jerking" or "jumping" manner inconsistent with smooth jet propulsion
- **Total observation**: Approximately 8 minutes; nighttime
- **Location**: Pacific Time Zone (precise location redacted)
- **Reporter**: Civilian; observed via personal cellular device; stated the object did not appear to have a front or rear, no defined flight path, no vapor trail, no photo/data collection capabilities, and was not believed to be a drone
- **Resolution**: UNKNOWN (IIR and draft report attached to original email — not included in this FOIA release)

**Classification process:**

- AFOSI's standard IIR declassification process requires AFOSI Commander signature
- Instead, the [[afosi]] OSI CI Collections and Operations / Intelligence Oversight PM approved a "derivative classification review" — a faster pathway that treats the UNCLASSIFIED summary as a derivative product rather than requiring full declassification of the parent IIR
- The approving PM noted this was "a first" for that office
- Full IIR remains SECRET//NOFORN; only the summary is released

## Significance

1. **Civilian sighting adjacent to national security facility**: The proximity to a national security facility is what triggered AFOSI involvement in what would otherwise be a civilian UAP sighting — defines AFOSI's threshold for initiating an IIR
2. **Classification pathway documentation**: The email documents the "derivative classification review" mechanism used to surface AARO-relevant summaries from classified IIRs — the actual intelligence reports remain classified; only sanitized summaries are released
3. **Blue triangle variant**: The reported object is blue, not the canonical black/dark triangle of the [[black-triangle-uap]] archetype; "whitish blue" perimeter lighting is consistent with plasma field or luminescent emission descriptions
4. **Jerking/jumping motion**: The non-smooth "backing up" behavior is consistent with UAP kinematics reported in MISREPs (non-ballistic trajectory change)
