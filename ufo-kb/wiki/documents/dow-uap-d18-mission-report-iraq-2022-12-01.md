# AARO MISREP D18 — Iraq UAP, 482 ATKS, 2022-12-01

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 482 ATKS / 20 FW / 609 CAOC / ACC, from OKAS, Saudi Arabia. December 1, 2022. Classified SECRET//NOFORN; declassification date: 20471202.

- **Date**: 1–2 December 2022; takeoff 011206Z DEC22, land 020723Z; 19h 17m total; 18h 35m on-station
- **Platform**: 482 ATKS / 20 FW (note: 20 FW, not 432 AEW — different wing rotation); OKAS, Saudi Arabia; FMV (primary) + AH/BLASPHEMY (secondary — codename sensor)
- **Mission**: OP INHERENT RESOLVE; TARGET DEV (target development); scanning for HVI/POI signatures, POL, ADMS in suits, weapons, convoys/sentry, personnel IVO Baghdad, Iraq

## Significance

Three features distinguish D18 from the rest of the gov-release batch:

1. **"POSSIBLE UAP/UAV" dual assessment**: This is the only MISREP in the batch that explicitly combines both labels — acknowledging the adversary-drone possibility alongside the anomalous designation. In OP INHERENT RESOLVE context (counterterrorism HVI targeting over Baghdad), an Iranian or militia UAV is operationally plausible; the crew left both possibilities open.
2. **"DID NOT FOLLOW UAP"**: The crew made an affirmative decision not to deviate from their assigned HVI mission to investigate the UAP. This is the clearest example in the batch of a crew explicitly prioritizing mission continuity over UAP investigation.
3. **AH/BLASPHEMY sensor**: The secondary sensor is listed as "AH/BLASPHEMY" — a codename not seen in any other MISREP in the batch (others list AH/GMESH or AIRHANDLER). Its nature (SIGINT, ELINT, or other collection type) is unclear.
