# NASA Apollo 17 Crew Debriefing for Science — MSC-07632

MSC-07632; January 8, 1973; Science Requirements Branch, Planetary and Earth Sciences Division. Companion to MSC-07631 (technical crew debriefing). Partial OCR recovery — only the tail end of the document content is readable.

## Extractable Content

The recovered text covers scientific instrument results from Apollo 17, all cosmological in nature:

- **UV background survey**: Galactic latitude mapping (north and south galactic poles); spectrum observed "looks like the spectrum of a hot star" but no hot stars were in the field of view; most conservative interpretation: reflected light from galactic-plane hot stars via interstellar dust; possible extragalactic origin not excluded.
- **Coma cluster dark matter**: Searched for ionized hydrogen (Lyman-alpha at redshift); negative result — excludes ionized hydrogen as the binding agent for the Coma cluster; acknowledged as a "real mystery."
- **Lyman-alpha hydrogen in solar system**: Gary Thomas (U Colorado) interpretation — interstellar hydrogen streaming through the solar system; large Apollo dataset expected to clarify.
- **Earth UV spectrum**: Observation of Earth from outside.

No UAP observations or anomalous sightings are described in the recovered content. The document is a post-mission scientific instrument debrief with no aviation or encounter reporting component.
