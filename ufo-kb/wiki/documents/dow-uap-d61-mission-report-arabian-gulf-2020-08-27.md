# AARO MISREP D61 — Arabian Gulf UAP Formation, 482 ATKS, 2020-08-27

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 482nd Attack Squadron (482 ATKS) / 432nd AEW / 609 CAOC / ACC, from OKAS, Saudi Arabia. Declassification date: 20450301. The second in the five-MISREP 482 ATKS cluster from OKAS in 2020 (D60 Aug 8, D61 Aug 27, D62 Sep 16, D63 Oct 1, D64 Nov 2).

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 26–27 August 2020; takeoff 262307Z AUG20, land 272012Z; 21h 5m total; 20h 24m on-station
- **Platform**: 1 aircraft, 482 ATKS / 432 AEW; OKAS, Saudi Arabia; ANDAS4 (FMV) + AH/GMESH
- **Mission**: AREC; NAVCENT support; IRGCN/IRIN vessel characterization, UAS, pattern of life in Arabian Gulf / Strait of Hormuz / Gulf of Oman
- **Operations center**: 609 CAOC / USCENTCOM / ACC
- **FMV exploitation**: DGS1; weather: LIGHT CLOUD COVERAGE (prevented continuous tracking)

## Key ISR Observations (Non-UAP)

- **0611Z**: 1× small vessel at Greater Tunb Naval Port (MGRS 40RCQ3Q) — Iranian-controlled island
- **0700Z**: 1× probable IL-76 CANDID at Abu Musa Island AFLD (MGRS 40RCP02) — Iranian Air Force strategic transport
- **1742Z**: 1× probable Naser-class WAP (fast attack craft) + M/T vessel dead in water (MGRS 39RUN49); 2 adult males on rear deck

## Iranian Air Defense Guard Call

- **DTG**: 270532Z AUG20; observer at MGRS 40RCP78 (near Abu Musa Island), FL150, heading 018M
- **Caller**: IRANIAN AIR DEFENSE
- **Tone**: PROFESSIONAL; standard response; no mission impacts

## UAP Observation

- **DTG**: 271527Z–271529Z AUG20 (2 minutes)
- **Observer location**: MGRS 39RVM38 (Arabian Gulf, offshore)
- **UAP count**: Formation (number not specified)
- **UAP description**: "FORMATION OF FLYING OBJECTS TRAVELING NE-NW ALONG THE COAST"
- **UAP location**: MGRS 39RVM81 (coastal area)
- **Method**: 1.4a SENSOR (redacted — not standard FMV)
- **Trajectory**: NE-NW along the coast
- **Duration**: 2 minutes before PID lost in cloud cover
- **Gentext**: "FROM 1527Z TO 1529Z, [REDACTED] OBSERVED A FORMATION OF FLYING OBJECTS TRAVELING NE-NW ALONG THE COAST IVO 39RVM81[REDACTED]. [REDACTED] WAS TRACKING THIS FORMATION FOR APPROXIMATELY 2 MINUTES BEFORE PID WAS LOST IN CLOUD COVER. AIRCREW WAS UNABLE TO GAIN PID AGAIN ON THIS FORMATION."

## Significance

D61 is the **first formation (multi-object) UAP observation** in the gov-release D-series batch. The term "FORMATION OF UNK FLYING OBJECTS" appears in the Observed Activity Description field (the official classification); the narrative also uses "UAP." Two distinctive features: (1) the objects traveled NE-NW along the coast, suggesting directional, purposeful movement rather than hovering; (2) the sensor type is redacted (1.4a SENSOR) — unlike all other MISREPs in this batch which specify ANDAS4/FMV, suggesting a classified sensor made the detection. The Iranian military context is dense on this sortie: Iranian Air Defense guard call at 0532Z, IL-76 CANDID at Abu Musa Island, Naser-class WAP dead in water — the UAP formation was observed during an active IRGCN/IRIN pattern-of-life mission. D61 is the second in the five-MISREP 482 ATKS / 432 AEW cluster from OKAS in 2020; D60 (Aug 8) is the earliest, with a minimally documented 1× UAP observation.
