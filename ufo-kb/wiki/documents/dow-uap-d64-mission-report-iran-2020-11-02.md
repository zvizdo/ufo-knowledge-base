# AARO MISREP D64 — Arabian Gulf UAP, 2x Observations + Iranian Air Defense, 2020-11-02

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 482nd Attack Squadron (482 ATKS) / 432nd Air Expeditionary Wing / 609 CAOC / ACC, from Dhahran area (OKAS), Saudi Arabia. Declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison, USCENTCOM Chief of Staff, 16 March 2026. Declassification date: 20450301.

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 2–3 November 2020; takeoff 020608Z NOV20, land 030250Z; 20h 42m total
- **On-station**: 021015Z to 030113Z; 18h 55m on-station
- **Platform**: 1 aircraft, 482 ATKS / 432 AEW; OKAS, Saudi Arabia; callsign and asset type redacted
- **Primary sensor**: ANDAS4 (FMV); additional: AH/GMESH/VORTEX
- **Mission type**: AREC; NAVCENT support
- **Operation**: Classified (b)(1)1.4a; target deck: IRGCN/IRIN vessel characterization, UAS activity, pattern of life in Arabian Gulf
- **Operations center**: 609 CAOC / USCENTCOM / ACC
- **Weather**: HAZE PRECLUDED IMINT COLLECTION
- **FMV exploitation**: DGS1

## Iranian Air Defense Guard Call

- **DTG**: 021012Z NOV20; observer at MGRS 39RUN60[redacted], FL210, heading M 068
- **Caller**: IRANIAN AIR DEFENSE on GUARD frequency (frequency redacted)

- **Orders given**: Standard call; responded "STANDARD RESPONSE 1"; no impact to mission
- **Pattern**: Guard call 11 hours 31 minutes before first UAP observation

## Significance

D64 is the third MISREP from the 482 ATKS / 432 AEW unit at OKAS in the same operational year (alongside D62 September 2020 and D63 October 2020), establishing the Strait of Hormuz / Arabian Gulf as the most documented single-unit UAP cluster in the batch. The sequence — Iranian Air Defense guard call at 1012Z, then two UAP observations at 2143Z and 2148Z on the same sortie — continues the Iranian-proximity pattern seen in D62 (3 guard calls + 2 COMPLETE-impact EMI + UAP) and D63 (5 guard calls + UAP). The second UAP had a documented NW trajectory — directional movement, unlike the stationary or altitude-only descriptions in other reports. The haze precluding IMINT collection created a degraded-sensor environment, which parallels D10's dust-hindered FMV.
