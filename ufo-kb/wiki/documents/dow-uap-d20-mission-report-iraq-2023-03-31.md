# AARO MISREP D20 — Syria/Iraq UAP, F-16CM 2-Ship, 10–20x at FL600+, 2023-03-31

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 77th Expeditionary Fighter Squadron (77 EFS) / 378th Air Expeditionary Wing / 609 CAOC / AFCENT, from Prince Sultan Air Base (OEPS), Saudi Arabia. Operation INHERENT RESOLVE. Note: source PDF filename says "southern-united-states" but the document is unambiguously Iraq/Syria airspace. Classification: SECRET//NOFORN. Declassification date: 20480401.

## UAP Observation

- **DTG**: 312302:05Z MAR23 (2302Z, 31 March 2023)
- **Observer location**: MGRS 37SGA13[redacted], FL265, 293 kts
- **UAP count**: POSS 10X–20X (highest count in D-series batch)
- **UAP altitude**: FL600+ (above 60,000 ft — exceptionally high; above U-2 operational ceiling)
- **UAP location**: DATA MASKED (first-seen location redacted)
- **UAP behavior**: "OBSERVED SEVERAL BRIGHT OBJECTS MANUEVERING QUICKLY WEST TO EAST NE OF RLZ"
- **Sensor lock**: Targeting pod (TV mode) obtained on each object for approximately 20 seconds before object "WOULD BECOME DIM AND DISAPPEAR FROM TARGETING POD"
- **Formation**: "NONE OF THE OBJECTS WERE SEEN IN ANY FORMATIONS AS PREVIOUSLY SEEN IN PRIOR SORTIES" — implies prior UAP formation sightings by this unit, also unresolved
- **UAP signatures**: No
- **RF**: None recorded
- **Effects on persons**: NONE

**Analyst comment (verbatim):** "1.4a FLT COMPARED TARGETING POD BETWEEN POSS UAP AND STAR. RESULTS WERE DIFFERENT."

## Significance

Three features make D20 extraordinary within the D-series. First, the **FL600+ altitude** places these objects above the operational ceiling of most reconnaissance aircraft and at the edge of the stratosphere — atmospheric pressure at FL600 is ~1.7 kPa; no known conventional aircraft routinely operates there. Second, the **10x–20x count** is the highest in the batch, and the objects were not in formation — contrasting explicitly with prior sortie sightings that *did* involve formations (also unresolved, presumably in other MISREPs). Third, the **active star-discrimination** by the analyst: the targeting pod comparison between the UAP and a star yielded different results, ruling out astronomical false positives. The 20-second targeting pod lock before dimming and disappearance is consistent with the "camera evasion" and "SWIR-only" sensor-selective patterns documented in other MISREPs. The platform is a combat-armed F-16CM 2-ship (DCA mission, carrying AIM-120/AIM-9X), adding a tier of operational seriousness absent from ISR-only reports.
