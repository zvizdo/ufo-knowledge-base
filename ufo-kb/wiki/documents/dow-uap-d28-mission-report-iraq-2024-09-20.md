# AARO MISREP D28 — Iraq UAP During Live Fire, AC-130 16 SOS / 27 SOW, 2024-09-20

USCENTCOM Mission Report from SOTU 016 / 16 SOS / 27 SOW / 379 AEW / 609 CAOC, from OKAS, Saudi Arabia. September 20, 2024. Classified SECRET//REL TO USA, FVEY; declassification date: 20490921. PDF filename says "east-china-sea" — actual content is Iraq (Ayn Al Asad Air Base).

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 20–21 September 2024; takeoff 201740Z SEP24, land 210046Z; 7h 6m total; 3h 53m on-station
- **Platform**: SOTU 016 (AC-130 gunship); 16 SOS / 27 SOW; OKAS; sensors MX-20 + MX-25 (EO/IR targeting pods); AN/APN-241 radar; ALE-47 countermeasures; 716 chaff + 240 flares
- **Armament**: 30mm autocannon, 105mm howitzer, AGM-176 Griffin missile (PGM shot fired this mission)
- **Mission**: ARMED OVERWATCH; OP INHERENT RESOLVE; Ayn Al Asad Air Base (AAAB) ROZ RAINDROP (restricted operating zone); Iraq; AFSOC; 609 CAOC
- **Primary witnesses**: Weapons Systems Officer (WSO) and Combat Systems Officer (CSO) — both observed the UAP
- **Weather**: Not a factor

## UAP Observation

- **DTG**: 202027:59Z SEP24
- **UAP Event Serial Number**: 202027ZSEP2024-CENTCOM
- **DoD acquisition date**: 210000Z SEP24
- **Observer location**: MGRS 38SKC55... (Ayn Al Asad area), FL130, 170 KIAS, HDG 096 straight and level
- **UAP location**: First/last: MGRS 38SKC59... (within AAAB ROZ RAINDROP)
- **Physical state**: SOLID
- **Signatures**: "IR SIGNATURE DETECTABLE BY MX-20 & MX-25"
- **Assessment**: BENIGN

## Key Observation Details

The UAP was observed **during a live fire PGM shot** — specifically in the moment between AGM-176 release and munition impact:

- WSO and CSO observed an unidentified object "FLY THROUGH THE AIRCRAFT SENSORS AT A HIGH RATE OF SPEED" immediately after weapons release
- "UAP CREATED AN IR LENS FLARE ON THE MX-20 & MX-25 SENSORS, WHICH INDICATED A SIGNIFICANT HEAT SIGNATURE COMING FROM THE OBJECT"
- "UAP FLEW THROUGH [aircraft] SENSOR IN BETWEEN MUNITION RELEASE AND MUNITION IMPACT" (from UAP section)
- The crew maintained laser energy until the munition impacted — the AGM-176 hit its desired target despite the UAP passing through the sensor field
- Aircraft did not reobserve the UAP after the initial pass
- "UAP RESPONSE TO OBSERVER ACTIONS: NONE OBSERVED"
- "UAP ANOMALOUS CHARACTERISTICS: IT IS UNKNOWN AT THIS TIME WHETHER AN OBJECT DETACHED ITSELF FROM THE PRIMARY UAP IMMEDIATELY BEFORE LEAVING THE SENSOR FIELD OF VIEW"
- "PATH OF MOVEMENT APPEARED PREDETERMINED AND NOT IN RESPONSE TO [aircraft]'s DETECTION"
- UAP under intelligent control: field present but not filled
- No engagement, no recovery, no effects on persons or equipment

## Significance

D28 is among the most operationally significant UAP observations in the gov-release batch:

1. **Live fire context**: The UAP appeared during a live PGM shot at Ayn Al Asad — passing through the weapons sensor field between munition release and impact. Whether it was tracking the missile, traversing the airspace coincidentally, or drawn to the thermal event is unknown.
2. **Significant IR heat signature**: The IR lens flare on both MX-20 and MX-25 sensors simultaneously indicates an object emitting substantial heat — not an ambient-temperature solid but an actively radiating source. "Super-hot" thermal signature comparable to the [[fbi-western-us-late-2025-uap-investigation]] orb.
3. **Possible object detachment**: The anomalous note — "IT IS UNKNOWN WHETHER AN OBJECT DETACHED ITSELF FROM THE PRIMARY UAP" — was explicitly included in the formal MISREP, suggesting the crew or analysts observed something that might have separated from the UAP before it left the sensor field.
4. **"Predetermined" movement**: The explicit statement that the path was "not in response to [aircraft]'s detection" confirms no evasive maneuvering — the UAP's trajectory was autonomous and goal-directed.
5. **AFSOC 27 SOW**: D28 joins D25, D27, D33, D35 as a 27 SOW MISREP — five UAP reports across the Mediterranean, Arabian Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Aegean Sea, and Iraq from the same wing across 2023–2024. D28 is the only gunship MISREP in the batch (all others are ISR aircraft).
