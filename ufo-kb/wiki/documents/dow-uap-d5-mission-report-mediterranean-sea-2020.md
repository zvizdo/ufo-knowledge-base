# AARO Mission Report — Mediterranean Sea Dual UAP Observations, 2020 (D5)

AARO MISREP (D5). Two separate UAP observations logged on the same mission at 1354Z and 2243Z. Filename indicates "mediterranean-sea" but original PDF reportedly labeled "arabian-gulf." PURSUE Release 01.

## Observations

**Observation 1 — 1354Z**
- **Object count**: 1 UAP
- **Altitude**: FL160–170 (~16,000–17,000 ft)
- **Velocity**: 40 KTS (constant speed)
- **Behavior**: Steady, constant

**Observation 2 — 2243Z**
- **Object count**: 2 probable UAPs
- **Velocity**: ~278 KTS
- **Behavior**: Increased speed and changed direction south

## Significance

Two observations in one mission with markedly different velocity profiles (40 KTS vs. ~278 KTS) and different object counts (1 vs. 2) may represent separate objects or the same object at different flight phases. The direction-change south in Observation 2 mirrors patterns in other PURSUE Release 01 MISREPs (D4: east, D8: south). The low 40-KTS constant speed in Observation 1 is atypical — most reported UAPs show higher velocities.
