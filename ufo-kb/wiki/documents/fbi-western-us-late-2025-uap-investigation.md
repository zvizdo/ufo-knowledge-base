# FBI Western US Late 2025 UAP Investigation

FBI 302 interview of a senior US intelligence official (WITNESS 1) plus 24 photo PDFs submitted to AARO, covering a multi-hour UAP encounter at and near a classified facility (SITE CODE NAME) in the western United States, late 2025. Released under PURSUE Release 01. Classified SECRET//NOFORN.

## Mission Context

- **Classification**: SECRET//NOFORN; released under PURSUE Release 01
- **Participants**: WITNESS 1 (senior US intelligence official, FBI 302 subject); WITNESS 2 (senior US intelligence official, dropped off at western position ~2050hrs); two pilots from STATE PARTNER ORGANIZATION (helicopter CALL SIGN 1); FEDERAL PARTNERS 1–4
- **LP/OP**: Listening Post/Observation Post personnel (FEDERAL PARTNER 4) equipped with FLIR and NVGs, positioned ~4 miles east of SITE CODE NAME
- **JOC**: Joint Operations Center coordinating the operation
- **Origin**: Daytime aerial search of a MOUNTAIN RANGE west of SITE CODE NAME to investigate previous eyewitness reports of "orbs/lights" and "thuds as if something has fallen and hit the ground." The Office had conducted a "successful test" at the facility earlier that same day.

## UAP Observations Timeline

- **2202hrs**: Helicopter spotted a "possible aircraft on the horizon moving away to the west." LP/OP (FLIR) spotted an orb described as "**super-hot**" hovering at ground level, then headed toward the LP/OP bearing before turning east, then south at "a high rate of speed," and split into **two objects**. Helicopter directed south to intercept.
- **2207hrs**: 5 MILITARY AIRCRAFT entered airspace conducting a training mission; JOC contacted them to assist.
- **Post-2207**: Helicopter (FLIR, NVG, naked eye) could not locate the orb. LP/OP reported the orb gained elevation, **came within 10 feet of CALL SIGN 1**, then continued southeast past a road approximately 20 miles away. Helicopter unable to match speed, broke off pursuit. Co-pilot (NVG) observed "something emerge from the two objects and then travel in a different direction at a high rate of speed."
- **2218hrs**: CALL SIGN 1 awaiting instructions when pilots (NVG) and WITNESS 1 (naked eye) observed a **swarm of lights "too many to count"** moving in all directions but generally west and headed south. Military aircraft were 2 minutes out.
- **2227hrs**: CALL SIGN 1 lost visual of swarm. Near SITE CODE NAME, WITNESS 1 (naked eye) and pilots (NVG) spotted **two large orbs** appear in close proximity to the helicopter, west and above the rotor disk. Naked-eye description: "oval shaped, orange in color with a white or yellow center and emitting light in all directions." A **third orb flared up below** the two, then a fourth, then a fifth — 4–5 total. After a few seconds the orbs flared down in reverse order while appearing stationary, until visual was lost.
- **2228hrs**: 4–5 orbs appeared over the mountains **above the MILITARY AIRCRAFT**, flared up one at a time in a horizontal formation, then flared down in the opposite order after ~10–15 seconds.
- **2233hrs**: Similar horizontal orb formation observed to the **east** in the direction of a nearby town. Same sequential flaring up then down in reverse.
- **2241hrs**: Single orb flare up west of SITE CODE NAME over the mountain. Flared down after several seconds.

## Witness Comments (WITNESS 1)

- Pilots were recording but many sightings were above the helicopter, outside the FLIR camera angle, and thus not captured.
- Some phenomena not visible to naked eye but pilots called out sightings via NVG.
- **"The orbs appeared to break off from [CALL SIGN 1] and pursue the [MILITARY AIRCRAFT]"** — the single most operationally significant witness comment.

## Significance

This is the most recent firsthand UAP witness statement in the gov-release batch (late 2025) and the only FBI 302 interview in the release. Key unusual features:

1. **Orb-pursues-military-aircraft behavior**: The orbs actively redirected from the helicopter to pursue the arriving military aircraft — intentional, interactive, and potentially adversarial/surveillance behavior.
2. **10-foot helicopter approach**: An orb closed to within 10 feet of the helicopter (CE1 boundary), observed under NVG by the co-pilot.
3. **Sequential horizontal flaring display**: 4–5 orbs flaring up one at a time, then down in reverse order — a structured, non-random pattern repeated three times at different locations (west of helicopter, above military aircraft, to the east).
4. **Orb fission + emergence**: A single "super-hot" orb split into two; something then emerged from the two and traveled in a third direction — three distinct trajectories from one original object.
5. **Super-hot FLIR thermal signature**: "Super-hot" FLIR characterization contrasts with the COLD thermal signatures in other gov-release MISREPs (e.g., D23 Arabian Gulf); suggests active energy emission rather than ambient-temperature solid object.
6. **Classified facility context**: The incident occurred the same day as an unspecified "successful test" at the classified site — consistent with the [[ufo-nuclear-nexus]] pattern of UAP appearing at nuclear or advanced-weapons facilities during active test periods.
7. **PURSUE Release 01**: Novel disclosure provenance — not a standard FOIA or NDAA release; suggests a dedicated classified disclosure program parallel to AARO's MISREP release series.
