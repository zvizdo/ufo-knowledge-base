# USAF Air Intelligence Information Report IR 193-55

USAF Air Attaché cable (OPERATIONAL IMMEDIATE) from Prague to HQ USAF, Washington, reporting direct visual observation of two disc aircraft in Azerbaijan SSR by three US government witnesses, 4 October 1955. USAIRA reliability: **B-2 (Probably True)**.

## Primary Observation — 1955-10-04, 1910 hours

Three witnesses aboard a Soviet train traveling between Atjaty and Adzhijabul, Trans-Caucasus:

- **Senator [[richard-russell]]** (Georgia; Chairman, [[senate-armed-services-committee]]) — observed UAP 1 first; called the others to the window
- **Lt. Col. [[e-u-hathaway]]** ([[us-army|US Army]]; Senate Committee staff) — confirmed UAP 2 with clear view
- **[[ruben-efron]]** (Committee Consultant) — confirmed UAP 2 with clear view

### UAP Characteristics

Both objects appeared from a Soviet facility area ~1–2 miles south of the rail line, where searchlights were pointing vertically skyward:

- **Shape**: Round/circular; no protrusions, wings, or fuselage
- **Surface**: Outer surface revolving clockwise
- **Lights**: Two stationary interior lights positioned near the top
- **Propulsion signature**: Sparks/flame visible from the bottom
- **Flight path**: Vertical ascent to ~6,000 ft altitude, then high-speed acceleration northward
- **Interval**: UAP 2 appeared approximately one minute after UAP 1 from the same launch area

The craft morphology matches no known 1955 Western aircraft: the revolving outer surface, internal stationary lights, and bottom flame/spark emission pattern are distinct from contemporary Soviet jet or rocket designs.

## Soviet Information Control Response

Immediately after the observation, Soviet trainmen closed all window curtains and denied further window access. This coordinated, rapid response indicates the Soviet crew recognized the significance of what was observed — an independent corroborating datum suggesting the objects were not routine.

## Secondary Intelligence

The report appends additional Soviet military intelligence observed during the same delegation trip:

- **Dnieperpetrovsk airfield**: 42 unusual jet fighters observed on ramp, plus a multi-engine jet bomber type not matching known NATO catalogued aircraft
- **Moscow radar site**: Radar installation noted in approaches to Moscow
- **Soviet pilot encounter**: Soviet pilot Egorov reported an unusual aerial encounter (details redacted in released version)

These secondary items corroborate that the delegation had legitimate intelligence-collection access during the trip.

## Significance

The Russell sighting is exceptional on every credibility dimension:

1. **Witness rank**: A sitting US Senator and chairman of the [[senate-armed-services-committee|Senate Armed Services Committee]] is the most senior US official ever to file a contemporaneous UAP observation through official channels
2. **Access context**: Russell had classified access to the full scope of US military aviation — his inability to identify the craft cannot be attributed to ignorance of exotic programs
3. **Multi-witness**: Three independently positioned co-witnesses, all with documented institutional roles
4. **Chain of custody**: Contemporaneous sworn cable, OPERATIONAL IMMEDIATE precedence, formal USAIRA reliability rating
5. **Soviet reaction**: Immediate curtain-closing is an independent behavioral datum

The sighting remained classified for decades. No official explanation for the observed objects has been provided.
