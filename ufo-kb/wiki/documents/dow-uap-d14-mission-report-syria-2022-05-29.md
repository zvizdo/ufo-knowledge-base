# AARO MISREP D14 — Syria UAP, Russian Naval ISR Context, 2022-05-29

USEUCOM Mission Report from 50th Attack Squadron (50 ATKS) / 432nd Air Expeditionary Wing, departing Sigonella AB (LICZ), Sicily. Operations center: 603rd AOC / 609 CAOC. Declassification date: 2047-05-29. MDR 25-0094 through MDR 25-0099.

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 29–30 May 2022; takeoff 291355Z, on-station 291551Z, off-station 300616Z; total 20 hours 30 minutes
- **Platform**: 50 ATKS / 432 AEW ISR aircraft (1), Sigonella AB (LICZ), Sicily
- **Sensors**: SIGINT (AIRHANDLER), IMINT (ANDAS4 FMV + SAR)
- **Mission**: XREC / Target Development, 39 EEIs observed; OP HUMMER SICKLE IVO 41EMD (Eastern Mediterranean)
- **COCOM**: USEUCOM; MAJCOM: ACC; ATO E149

## ISR Context: Russian Naval Surveillance

The mission was ISR against Russian and Syrian military assets in the Eastern Mediterranean / Syria region. Observed targets (MGRS 36S–37S grid, Mediterranean/Syria/Lebanon coast):

- **Russian naval vessels** transiting: 1× Slava-class CG hull 055 (east); 1× Gorshkov FFG hull 461 (east); 2× Udaloy I DD hulls 626 and 564
- **Russian aircraft at Bassel Al-Assad AFD (36SYE68)**: 1× A-50U MAINSTAY D (AEW&C), 2× IL-38 (maritime patrol), 1× IL-76 (transport), and 1–2× probable Su-27/35
- **Syrian naval operations areas**: Latakia Naval HQ, Tartus Naval Base (Russian basing facility), coastal defense sites
- **Other targets**: Beirut military facilities, SIGINT facilities (Bamlkah), possible CNO facility

## RFAF SU-30 Reaction Event (2147Z)

At 2147Z (approximately 2.5 hours before the UAP observation), 1–3 aircraft including 1× possible RFAF Su-30 reacted to the observer. The Russian aircraft approached westward from the Syrian coast, flew **directly under** the observer's orbit at FL190 (observer at FL243), within approximately 5 NM, for approximately 2 minutes — then flew south ~10 NM and returned west. No impact to mission; observer did not visually acquire the aircraft. Identified via NTIG.

## UAP Observation

- **DTG**: 300117Z MAY22; observer at MGRS 36SXD99 area, FL240, 142 KIAS
- **Number**: 1× UAP
- **Behavior**: Flew north to northeast; followed "as long as possible"
- **UAP Physical State**: Not recorded
- **UAP Signatures**: No
- **Assessment**: Screener could **not** get a positive ID on the UAP
- **UAP first seen**: MGRS 37SBV28 area
- **Effects on persons/equipment**: Not recorded

## Significance

The D14 mission is the most geopolitically loaded in the AARO MISREP batch: the platform was conducting ISR against Russian Black Sea Fleet vessels and Russian Air Force aircraft at Latakia while flying under a RFAF SU-30 intercept — all in the context of the May 2022 Russian-Ukrainian war (Moskva sunk April 2022; heightened Russian naval posture). The UAP appearing ~2.5 hours after the SU-30 reaction, over the same Eastern Mediterranean operational area, adds a third-actor question: neither US nor Russian — though the MISREP is silent on assessment beyond "could not get a positive ID." The absence of BENIGN/assessment field (left blank) is itself notable compared to every other D-series MISREP.
