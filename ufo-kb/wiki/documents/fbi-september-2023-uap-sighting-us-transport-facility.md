# FBI Investigation — UAP Sighting at US Transport Facility, September 2023

FBI 302 interview records (Serials 3, 4, 5) of US-citizen first-hand witnesses plus an FBI Lab composite sketch dated April 30, 2024 with site-photo overlay. Incident: September 2023 at a US transport facility. PURSUE Release 01.

**Note on serial numbers**: The serial numbers in the PDF filenames do not match the serial numbers inside the documents. Serials 3, 4, and 5 are keyed to the document-internal designations.

## Witness Accounts

**Serial 3 — Witness 1 (Facetime interview)**

A group of US-citizen contractors was driving east to a test site in three vehicles (Ford F150, GMC AT4, sprinter van) to acquire data for **LiDAR testing**. As they drove through gates to a restricted area, the primary witness saw a **bright white stationary light** over the horizon through the top-right of the vehicle's windshield. The light then started moving to the right and disappeared. Duration: **~10 seconds**. Size appeared to stay constant throughout. The witness estimated the light was **10–20 miles away** and noted no vehicle interference. Witness considered the possibility it was a meteor "coming straight toward them and burning up in the atmosphere."

**Serial 4 — Witness 2 (primary witness; most detailed account)**

A 15-year employee (role and name redacted) at the facility who had restricted the airspace for upcoming tests at the time of the incident. At 7:02 am, contractors received the base's standard brief; between **7:15 and 7:30 am**, the group drove to their test site. While driving through a gate, the gate malfunctioned: it opened partway on three attempts, then opened fully on the fourth. **No prior or subsequent operational problems** with this gate.

As she drove through the gate, she looked up and observed:
- **Shape**: Cigar-shaped object
- **Color**: Metallic bronze
- **Location**: Southwest of her position, approximately **500–3,000 feet above the nearest tree line** (~1 mile SW of her position)
- **Movement**: Almost hovering, slowly moving **from east to west**; completely silent
- **Light**: Intense **diamond white** light with what appeared to be a **ring** around it, located on the **eastern end** of the object, pointing southeast — "like looking into the sun"
- **Size**: Length of **2–3 Blackhawk helicopters nose to tail** (~120–180 feet); width approximately 1.5 Blackhawks (~45–60 ft), though the intense light may have been obscuring part of the body

Witness had 15 years at the facility with extensive exposure to US military aircraft and drones and stated she had "never seen anything like it." She noted she would not have reported it alone. Several co-workers "made fun of her" afterward. No photos or video taken. A second contractor in the adjacent vehicle corroborated the sighting.

**Serial 5**: Fully redacted — only image placeholders remain; no extractable text content.

## FBI Lab Composite Sketch (2024-04-30)

The FBI Lab produced a composite sketch with a site-photo overlay dated April 30, 2024 — approximately 7 months after the incident. The sketch corroborates the eyewitness account by placing the described object in the reported spatial relationship to the facility.

## Significance

1. **FBI 302 formal investigation**: This is a formal FBI investigation of a UAP encounter — 302 interviews indicate a criminal/national security inquiry was opened. The FBI's role in UAP investigation (historically channeled through AARO for military incidents) here extends to a civilian transport facility.
2. **Multiple witnesses**: At least three 302 interviews; at least two confirmed eyewitnesses in adjacent vehicles. Corroborated.
3. **Lab composite sketch**: FBI Lab involvement indicates the Bureau treated this as a legitimate investigative matter serious enough to warrant forensic reconstruction.
4. **Gate anomaly**: The gate malfunction (intermittent, resolved on 4th attempt, no prior/subsequent issues) during the observation period is consistent with reported EMI-type effects associated with UAP proximity.
5. **LiDAR test context**: The crew was conducting LiDAR sensor testing at the time — sensors that might have captured data relevant to the object. No sensor data is referenced in the released documents.
