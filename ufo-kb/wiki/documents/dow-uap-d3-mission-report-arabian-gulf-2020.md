# AARO MISREP D3 — Arabian Gulf UAP Formation, 2020

Heavily redacted AFCENT Mission Report from the Arabian Gulf, 2020. Specific unit, operation, COCOM, platform, sensors, and exact date are all redacted as 1.4a. Classification: SECRET (caveats redacted); declassification date: 20480603.

## UAP Observation

- **Location/time**: Arabian Gulf, 2020; observation begins ~1736Z
- **UAP count**: 4× total in field of view; cloud coverage obstructed view and prevented following

**Sequential observation (27-second window)**:
- **17:36:22**: 1× UAP observed
- **17:36:30**: 2× UAP observed **side by side** (formation)
- **17:36:49**: 1× UAP observed

- **Physical state**: Redacted
- **Advanced capabilities**: NO
- **UAP Event Serial Number**: not assigned (dash)
- **Cloud coverage**: explicitly noted as obstruction to follow-on observation; "obstructed from following and getting a clear visual"

## Significance

Three features distinguish D3 from other MISREPs in the batch:
1. **4× simultaneous UAP in FOV** — one of the highest multi-UAP counts in the gov-release batch (alongside D10's 5×)
2. **Formation behavior**: "2X UAP OBSERVED SIDE BY SIDE" at 17:36:30 — only explicit side-by-side pairing in the batch
3. **Sequential appearance**: Objects appeared/disappeared across a 27-second window in sub-minute intervals — unusual temporal clustering
