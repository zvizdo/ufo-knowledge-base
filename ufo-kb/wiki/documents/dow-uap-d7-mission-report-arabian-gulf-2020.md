# AARO Mission Report — Arabian Gulf Balloon-Like UAP Weapons Track, 2020 (D7)

AARO MISREP (D7). Balloon-like UAP at 31,000 ft MSL traveling with winds. Crew obtained a weapons-quality radar track and performed a "next to shoot" (simulated intercept). Visual ID via TFLIR. References a prior similar report from the 48th Fighter Wing (RAF Lakenheath, UK). PURSUE Release 01.

## Observation

- **Location**: IVO 323's (Arabian Gulf grid/waypoint)
- **Object count**: 1 UAP
- **Altitude**: 31,000 ft MSL
- **Behavior**: Traveling with winds (no independent propulsion detected)
- **Appearance**: Balloon-like
- **Radar track quality**: Weapons quality 1 (sufficient for weapons employment)
- **Sensor**: TFLIR (Targeting Forward-Looking Infrared) — visual ID obtained
- **Prior reports**: Similar UAP previously reported by 48th Fighter Wing (RAF Lakenheath, UK)

## Significance

A weapons-quality radar track and simulated intercept ("next to shoot") distinguish this from standard passive-observation MISREPs — the crew was close enough and had sufficient radar contact to simulate engagement. The cross-reference to a prior 48 FW (Lakenheath) report suggests balloon-like UAPs were logged across multiple theaters in the PURSUE batch. Wind-traveling at 31,000 ft may indicate high-altitude balloon-type behavior, though standard meteorological balloons rarely achieve weapons-quality radar tracks.
