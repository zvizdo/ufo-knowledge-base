# AARO MISREP D65 — Arabian Gulf UAP ×3, ISR Platform, 2020-07-16

Standardized USCENTCOM MISREP for a 16–17 July 2020 AREC (Area Reconnaissance) mission supporting NAVCENT over the Arabian Gulf. Declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison, USCENTCOM Chief of Staff, 16 March 2026. Classification declassification date 2045-03-01.

## Mission Context

- **Operation**: NAVCENT support (operation name redacted)
- **Date**: 16–17 July 2020
- **Platform**: ISR aircraft (type redacted), 482nd ATKS, 432nd AEW; sensor: ANDAS4/AH GMESH
- **Operations center**: 609 AOC Det 1 / 609 CAOC (USCENTCOM)
- **Mission type**: AREC; Takeoff: OKAS (Kuwait); Total mission: 21 hr 17 min
- **On station**: 0552Z–0012Z (20 hr 20 min)
- **Context**: Pattern-of-life surveillance of Iranian vessels (PROB NASER, SAFIR KISH, HOUDONG)

## Significance

Three separate UAP observations within 8.5 hours during a continuous Iranian vessel-surveillance mission in the Arabian Gulf. The concentration of UAP observations during a NAVCENT ISR sortie is consistent with the broader 2020 Arabian Gulf cluster documented across the AARO MISREP series. The lack of detail in the UAP sections suggests either minimal observable characteristics or redaction of description fields pending further review.
