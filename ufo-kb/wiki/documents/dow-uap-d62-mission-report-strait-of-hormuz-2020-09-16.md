# AARO MISREP D62 — Strait of Hormuz UAP, Dual EMI Events, 2020-09-16

USCENTCOM Mission Report from 482nd Attack Squadron (482 ATKS) / 432nd AEW, departing OKAS (Saudi Arabia). 609th CAOC / NAVCENT support. Declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison, USCENTCOM Chief of Staff, 22 January 2026.

## Mission Context

- **Date**: 15–16 September 2020; takeoff 152302Z; land 161958Z; total 20 hours 56 minutes
- **Platform**: 482 ATKS / 432 AEW, OKAS; 1 aircraft
- **Sensors**: ANDAS4 (FMV primary), AIRHANDLER SIGINT, GMESH
- **Mission**: AREC/ISR ISO NAVCENT; Arabian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz, Gulf of Oman; IRIN/IRGCN pattern of life
- **Operations center**: 609th CAOC; COCOM USCENTCOM; MAJCOM ACC

## ISR Context: IRGCN and Abu Musa Island

- 1× IR SA-5 launcher on Abu Musa Island (0930Z; MGRS 40RCP03) — strategic Iranian military presence in the Strait
- 2× possible Houdong WPTG (Chinese-built IRGCN fast attack boats) docked pierside (39RXL60, 1321Z)
- 3 Iranian guard calls: 0408Z (FL180, tone Directive), 0421Z (FL180, tone Directive), 1141Z (FL040, 277°, GUARD freq 243.000)

## Two EMI Events (Causing Flight Path Deviations)

**EMI 1 — 1248Z–1259Z** (11 minutes)
- Type: UNKNOWN; Impact: COMPLETE; Mission impact: MEDIUM
- Location: MGRS 39RXK01; FL180, heading 290°, 89 KIAS
- Mission change: FLIGHT PATH DEVIATION; JSIR ID330412

**EMI 2 — 1414Z–1259Z** (27 minutes; regained 1441Z)
- Type: UNKNOWN; Impact: COMPLETE; Mission impact: MEDIUM
- Location: MGRS 39RVM92; heading 264°, 120 KIAS
- Mission change: FLIGHT PATH DEVIATION; JSIR ID330414

Both EMI events were logged as JSIR incidents. The sequence — 3 Iranian guard calls (0408Z–1141Z), then 2 EMI events causing flight path deviations (1248Z, 1414Z), then UAP observation (1732Z) — presents a pattern of escalating mission disruption before the UAP appears.

## UAP Observation

- **DTG**: 161732Z SEP 2020
- **Location**: IVO 39RVM51 (Strait of Hormuz / Gulf of Oman); observer at FL180, 90 KIAS
- **Method**: FMV (ANDAS4)
- **Observed activity description**: "UAP"
- **Gentext**: "AT 1732Z, [aircraft] OBSERVED A UAP IVO 39RVM51" — remainder redacted/cut off
- **UAP characteristics**: Absent from released portion

## Significance

D62 is the most operationally complex MISREP in the 2020 Strait of Hormuz cluster. The dual EMI events (JSIR IDs 330412 and 330414), each causing flight path deviations and COMPLETE impact to the affected system, occurring 73–173 minutes before the UAP FMV observation, parallel the D19 Syria MISREP pattern (APG-82 EMI 1 minute before UAP). The IRGCN SA-5 on Abu Musa Island and Houdong fast attack boats establish a dense Iranian military environment. Same 482 ATKS / 432 AEW unit as D63 Strait of Hormuz (2 weeks later, same base, same theater).
