# AARO Range Fouler Debrief D38 — Arabian Gulf, 14 May 2020 (Night)

US Navy SPEAR Range Fouler Debrief; ISR platform; Arabian Gulf; 14 May 2020, 20:40Z (night). Approximate contact location: ~28°N, 49°E (northern Arabian Gulf near Bahrain/Qatar coast). Contact altitude: 20,000 ft, altitude constant: Yes. Declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison USCENTCOM COS; MDR 26-0019; PURSUE Release 01.

## Observation

- **Date/Time**: 14MAY2020, 20:40:00Z (night)
- **Location**: Arabian Gulf, ~28°N, 49°E (working area grid 28314 / 49524)
- **Platform altitude**: 20,000 ft; altitude constant
- **Contact**: 1× round object; intermittent trackfile; no ATFLIR autotrack; no tally
- **Movement**: Yes (direction/speed not legible in OCR)
- **Narrative** (partial OCR): Object flew through the sensor FOV, appearing as solid white (TN/Black-Hot/Lin polarity). Crew temporarily lost the object as it "appeared to make erratic" movements; re-acquired shortly after. Object was above the water surface; ocean waves visible in background. Sensor operator continuously repositioned sensor to maintain track; object intermittently invisible against water clutter.

## Significance

The erratic maneuvering combined with the intermittent track against ocean-surface background suggests a low-altitude object capable of non-linear flight. The "waves of water visible" detail confirms the object was tracked at very low altitude above the water surface during the observation.
