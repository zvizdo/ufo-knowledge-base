# AARO MISREP D19 — Syria UAP, F-15E, 2023-02-21

Standardized USCENTCOM MISREP for a 21 February 2023 DCA mission over Syria. Declassified by MG Richard A. Harrison, USCENTCOM Chief of Staff, 8 October 2025. Classification: SECRET//NOFORN; declassification date 2048-01-20.

## Mission Context

- **Operation**: INHERENT RESOLVE (counterterrorism, Syria)
- **Date**: 21 February 2023
- **Platform**: F-15E, 2-ship; 389th Expeditionary Fighter Squadron (EFS), 332nd Air Expeditionary Wing (AEW)
- **Operations center**: 609th CAOC (AFCENT / USCENTCOM)
- **Mission type**: DCA (Defensive Counter Air)
- **JTAC callsign**: KINGPIN; Killbox: ESSA (IVO Shaddadi, Syria)
- **On station**: 0003Z–0340Z (3 hr 37 min); Last land: OJMS 0425Z

## EMI Event (Possibly UAP-Related)

- **DTG**: 210021Z–210024Z FEB23 (3 minutes)
- **Type**: Radar jamming
- **Affected system**: APG-82 radar; frequency 8.8–9.9 GHz
- **Aircraft altitude**: FL270
- **Location**: IVO Shaddadi
- **Mission impact**: PARTIAL
- **JSIR Report ID**: 340377
- **Working theory**: Turkish X-band jammer from SYR/TUR border area (ABRA of effect from Turkish X-band jammer)

## UAP Observation 1 — Primary (210025Z)

- **DTG**: 210025Z FEB23 — one minute after the EMI event ended
- **Location**: IVO Shaddadi
- **Altitude**: FL240
- **Count**: 3x POSS UAP
- **Description**: 2 white objects, IR significant
- **Radar returns**: None
- **Health effects**: None reported
- **Sensor**: WSV (Weapons Systems Video) produced
- **Advanced capability**: No radar cross-section detectable (radar-transparent or stealth)

## UAP Observation 2 (210135Z)

- **DTG**: 210135Z FEB23
- **Location**: IVO Shaddadi
- **Altitude**: FL210
- **Count**: 1x POSS BALLOON
- **Resolution**: Assessed as possible balloon; NFTR

## Significance

The primary observation is notable for two reasons: (1) three objects appeared simultaneously with no radar returns despite being visually IR-significant — consistent with the radar-transparent profile seen in other CENTCOM theater UAP reports; (2) the APG-82 radar jamming event ended at 0024Z and the UAP observation was logged at 0025Z — a 1-minute correlation. The MISREP attributes the EMI to a Turkish X-band jammer, but the temporal overlap with the UAP observation is documented rather than resolved.
