# NASA Apollo 12 Mission Transcript, 1969

Mission transcript excerpt: communications between Apollo 12 crew and Houston Mission Control (Nov 1969). Crew: Pete Conrad (CDR), Alan Bean (LMP), Dick Gordon (CMP). PURSUE Release 01.

## UAP-Adjacent Observations

**1. Particle/light observation (LM — AOT, dark quadrant)**

[[pete-conrad]] (CDR-LM): "When you look out the AOT in the dark quadrant? You can see these lights — particles of light, flashes of light just seem to come from — in this case, I'm looking in quadrant 1 which is the left one. It's coming from behind me, the left, and they're just sailing off in space. I was thinking they're dropping from my water boiler, but it looks like some of those things are escaping the Moon. They really haul out of here and just press off at the stars."

No Mission Control response beyond "Roger." The observation is unresolved; Conrad himself considers but doesn't commit to the water boiler explanation — specifically noting some particles appear to be "escaping the Moon."

**2. AGS "all 8's" anomaly**

[[alan-bean]] (LMP-LM) reports getting an "all 8's flash on both the address and the information registers at about one-fifth the brilliance of the normal numbers" pulsing every second on the Abort Guidance System (AGS). Houston explains it as EMI ("Fredo and I have both seen that phenomena...it's probably an EMI"). TRW has a workup on the problem.

**3. Tracking light anomaly** — tracking light appeared burned out; Dick Gordon couldn't locate the LM in the CSM sextant. Operational issue; not UAP-relevant.
