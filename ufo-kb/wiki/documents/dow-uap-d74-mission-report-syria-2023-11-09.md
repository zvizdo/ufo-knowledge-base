# AARO MISREP D74 — Syria UAP Spherical Object

Standardized USCENTCOM Mission Report (MISREP), document number 9381202. Released under USCENTCOM MDR 25-0072, recommended by MG Brandon R. Tegtmeier (USCENTCOM Chief of Staff) on 2 June 2025. Classification: SECRET//NOFORN; declassification date 2048-11-09.

## Mission Context

- **Operation**: INHERENT RESOLVE (counterterrorism, Syria)
- **Date**: 9 November 2023
- **Platform**: Redacted ISR aircraft (callsign, type, tail number all 1.4a); primary sensor FMV; SIGINT also available
- **Operations center**: 609th CAOC
- **Mission type**: ISR — Target Development
- **Mission duration**: Takeoff 0217Z; final landing after 2319Z (>22 hours on-station total across three ISR sorties)

## UAP Observation

At 2153Z while returning to base (RTB), the ISR platform observed one UAP:

- **Shape**: "Bouncy ball" — spherical, solid physical state
- **Approach**: Came from the south at near co-altitude with the aircraft
- **Behavior**: Dropped altitude and safely passed the aircraft
- **Speed**: Consistently ~424 knots for at least 7 minutes, then moved out of range
- **Emissions**: None
- **RF frequency**: None
- **Maneuverability**: No active maneuvering observed
- **Response to aircraft**: No response to observation; not interrogated
- **Observer assessment**: Benign; not under intelligent control; no threat to aircraft or public safety
- **Advanced capabilities flagged**: YES — consistent 424 knot airspeed for 7+ minutes in spherical form with no visible propulsion
- **Effects on aircrew**: None
- **Effects on equipment**: None
- **Materials recovered**: No
- **Third-party observers**: None

## Significance

The D74 observation is notable for the combination of consistent airspeed (424 kn for 7+ min) with no detected emissions, no propulsion signature, and the FMV sensor capture. The "bouncy ball" descriptor is consistent with the spherical orb morphology catalogued in multiple CENTCOM theater UAP reports. The AARO reporting chain includes this as an event requiring MG-level review and formal MDR release.
