# FBI September 2023 UAP Sighting — US Transport Facility

> FBI investigation of a UAP encounter at a US transport facility in September 2023. The investigation produced three FBI 302 interview records of US-citizen first-hand witnesses (released as Serials 3, 4, and 5) plus an FBI Lab composite sketch with a site-photo overlay corroborating the eyewitness reports.
